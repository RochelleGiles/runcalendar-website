Course

A 10km evening trail run starting from the cricket pavilion in Havant Park and heading down the old Hayling Billy Line, a disused railway, on a dead-flat coastal trail finishing at the beach. Views open out over the Isle of Wight and Portsmouth. Run as a social evening rather than a competitive race.

About

Explore the stunning coastal trails of Havant, Hampshire during the Dash X Beach Trail Run. This 10km summer evening event offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the beauty of the British seaside while challenging themselves on a scenic, off-road course. Hosted by the Dash Collective, this trail run is not to be missed by adventurous runners seeking a memorable experience.
