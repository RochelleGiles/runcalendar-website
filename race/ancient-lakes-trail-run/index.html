Course

Trail races on a cloverleaf of three singletrack loops sharing one central aid station at the Bishop Recreation Area near Quincy, Washington. Distances run from 10K to 50 miles, with about 1,700 feet of climbing spread over rolling ground and no single climb over 400 feet. The route passes a waterfall, basalt outcrops and canyon country with views to the Columbia River Gorge. The central aid station carries water, Gatorade, sweet and salty snacks and soup later in the day.

About

The Ancient Lakes Trail Run is a unique trail race in the Washington Desert, featuring spectacular trails through canyon-like gorges, ancient desert lakes, and cascading waterfalls. Organized by Run Super Series, it offers distances for various abilities with excellent support and swag at the Bishop Recreation Area near Quincy, WA.[4][7]
