Course

A day-long team relay of roughly 75 miles from the Omaha metro area to downtown Lincoln, run by teams of six to eight. Individual legs are three to five miles and the route follows trails, paved streets and gravel back roads. The finish line is at Memorial Stadium in Lincoln. The field is capped at 650 teams.

About

The Market to Market Relay is a team relay race that runs from Omaha to Lincoln, Nebraska, split into a series of legs handed off between teammates. It's one of the state's largest team relay events.
