Course

An 8-mile loop around the perimeter of Sam Houston Jones State Park, repeated to make up each distance. It is nearly all single-track, with continuous twists, exposed roots and several bridged water crossings, and about 125 feet of climb per lap. Aid is at the start/finish and again around mile 4 of the loop.

About

The 50K, 25K and 7.78-mile distances all share the same trail course near Lake Charles, Louisiana, named for the historic "No Man's Land" border region once disputed between Louisiana and Texas. Runners pick their distance and take on the same course.
