Course

Point-to-point road 5K on paved streets, described as fast and flat. Starts at North St and Thelma Ave near St Paul's United Church of Christ and finishes at Second St and Eastern Ave. The race leads off the Irish Fest parade and follows the parade route. Course marshals on route; water and snacks at the finish.

About

Welcome to Irish Fest: In cooperation with the Irish American Society of County Will, the Prairie State Road Runners proudly presents the annual Manhattan Irish Fest Parade 5k. This unique race starts a parade, runs along the parade route, and is included with festival activities. After competing on a fast and flat road race course, runners get admission to the main tent for awards, food and beverages.
