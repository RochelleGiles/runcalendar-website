Course

Point-to-point runs along the Mortimer Trail between the market towns of Kington in Herefordshire and Ludlow in Shropshire. The 30-mile ultra starts opposite the Burton Hotel in Kington; the Croft Half covers the second half of the route from the Riverside Inn at Aymestrey. Mixed trail, forest and road.

About

The Midsummer Mortimer Trail Run invites runners to explore the stunning Shropshire countryside on a challenging 30-mile Ultra or a more manageable 15-mile 'Half' distance. Weaving through picturesque villages and rolling hills, this event offers an unforgettable midsummer adventure for trail enthusiasts of all levels. Whether you're seeking a scenic long-distance challenge or a breathtaking half-day run, the Midsummer Mortimer has something to captivate every outdoor-loving runner.
