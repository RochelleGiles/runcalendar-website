About

The Eisleber Frühlingslauf is a spring road race through Lutherstadt Eisleben, the Saxony-Anhalt town where Martin Luther was born and died. Routes range from 7K up to 25K, taking runners past the historic old town at the heart of this UNESCO World Heritage site.
