Course

A loop out of the city into the Geneva countryside, through the villages and vineyards of the plateau east of the lake, then a long descent to the shore at Cologny and seven kilometres of quays past the Jet d'Eau to a finish on the Pont du Mont-Blanc. A half marathon, a six-leg relay and a Saturday-night 10K share the course.

About

Known as Switzerland's fastest, flattest marathon course, the Genève Marathon threads through the city's diplomatic quarter before finishing near the old town. Half marathon, 10K and 5K races run alongside the marathon.
