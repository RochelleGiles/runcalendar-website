About

A USATF-sanctioned trail ultra near Damascus offering 16, 30 and 50-mile options along the Iron Mountain ridgeline in Jefferson National Forest, following a former Appalachian Trail route. The 50-mile course climbs roughly 8,000 feet and finishes with a steep, technical descent.
