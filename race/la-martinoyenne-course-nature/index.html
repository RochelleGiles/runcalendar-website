About

La Martinoyenne is a multi-distance trail running event offering four different race formats through the scenic bocage vendéen countryside. The event features routes combining road and trail sections with varying elevation gains, including a spectacular viewpoint from Moulin des Bois hill. The event also supports breast cancer awareness through partnership with SOLINE association, donating €1 per participant.
