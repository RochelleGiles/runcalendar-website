Course

An evening Halloween-themed race on tarmac in the North Park of Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, run from 7:00pm. The 5K is two laps and the 10K four laps of the same flat, fast circuit — just 3m of elevation across the course — with a distance marker at every kilometre.

About

Chase The Moon 5K and 10K is an evening Halloween-themed race through Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park in Stratford, London, on a flat, fast tarmac circuit within sight of venues built for the Olympic and Paralympic Games.
