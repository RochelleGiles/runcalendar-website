About

Experience the exhilaration of the Viking Raid on Lindisfarne with the Run The Raid event. This challenging trail run takes participants on a journey through the stunning landscapes of the United Kingdom, retracing the steps of the legendary 793 AD invasion. Runners will be captivated by the rich history and breathtaking scenery as they push themselves to the limit on this unique and unforgettable course.
