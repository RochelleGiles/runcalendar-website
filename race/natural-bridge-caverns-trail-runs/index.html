Course

Starts underground in Pluto's Anteroom before climbing out to dirt jeep trails around the caverns property in Garden Ridge, with some pavement at the start and finish. Courses are technical in spots with steep gravel sections; the 5K runs a single loop and the 10K an out-and-back.

About

Experience the only race in Texas that starts in the belly of a cave, descending deep into Natural Bridge Caverns through Pluto's Anteroom. Runners tackle half marathon, 10K, or 5K courses featuring jeep trails, challenging hills, and diverse Hill Country terrain.
