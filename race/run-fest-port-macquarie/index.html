Course

All races start and finish at Town Green, Port Macquarie. The marathon is three laps (21.1 km then two of 10.5 km); the half, 10 km, 5 km and 3 km are single-lap out-and-back routes through Westport Park, Kooloonbung Creek Nature Park, Town Beach and Rocky Beach Lookout. Surfaces are footpath, sealed road, boardwalk and grass, with two aid stations reachable about every 2 km.

About

Explore the stunning coastal scenery of Port Macquarie at Run Fest, featuring a range of running and walking events to suit all abilities. From the challenging Half Marathon to the family-friendly 3km fun run, this event offers an unforgettable experience for runners seeking a picturesque and well-organized race in Australia.
