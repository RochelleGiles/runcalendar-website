About

Explore the historic routes of Sir Walter Raleigh's estate during the Walter Raleigh Round in the picturesque countryside of the United Kingdom. This challenging half marathon and 13.5km third marathon event offers runners stunning views and the chance to retrace the steps of one of England's most renowned explorers.
