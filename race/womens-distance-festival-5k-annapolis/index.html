Course

An open two-loop course through the Wardour neighborhood of West Annapolis, run on paved roads with some shaded sections. Starts and finishes at West Annapolis Elementary School on Melvin Avenue. Mostly flat with a few short hills and one wooden footbridge. Two water stops, one at the footbridge and one near the start/finish.

About

The Women's Distance Festival 5K is hosted by the Annapolis Striders, a running club founded in 1978, and run alongside the Run After the Women 5K in Annapolis, Maryland. It's open to women and girls of all abilities, from first-timers to competitive racers.
