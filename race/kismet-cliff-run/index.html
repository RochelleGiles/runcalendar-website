Course

Trail loops over the granite ledges of Cathedral and Whitehorse in Echo Lake State Park, Conway, with a common start and finish. The Little Beast 5-miler runs single and double track with two climbs and descents; the Beast of the East half adds a loop over North Moat Mountain via Red Ridge. Aid at 2.1 and 4.1 miles, plus water at 9.9 miles for the half.

About

Part of the Merrell USA Skyrunner Series, the Kismet Cliff Run climbs steep, rocky cliff terrain above Conway, New Hampshire. Runners choose the 5 mile or half marathon distance across technical mountain trail with sustained climbing.
