About

The HEART RUN is a Valentine's weekend race at Bolsa Chica State Beach in Huntington Beach, California, offering 5K, 10K and half marathon distances plus a children's dash. The half marathon runs two laps toward Dog Beach and back on a course described as almost entirely flat. A February festival expo runs alongside the race.
