About

Des Marais et des Bosses is a relay trail race in Saint-Herblain, Loire-Atlantique, organized by the Outdoor Club Herblinois. Teams of two or four runners cover a 10.5km loop through marshland and an old quarry near La Carrière, tagging off at the start of each lap until the full distance is complete.
