About

Join the area's finest runners for the 39th anniversary of this classic race and enjoy tasty chili made by Cleveland's finest Firefighters & Police Officers! The 5k course is ENTIRELY flat and fast, the chili is hot and delicious, and the atmosphere is electric – ensuring a fun and memorable experience for all runners and walkers!
