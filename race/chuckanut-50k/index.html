About

The Chuckanut 50k is a mountain ultra with technical terrain and variable weather. The lollipop course starts and finishes on with 10k on the Interurban Trail with smooth running to start and finish. The middle 30k is what this race is known for as you get to climb (5,000ft), traverse and descend the famous Chuckanut Mountain Ridge exposing runners to the elements as well as the beautiful pnw terrain.
