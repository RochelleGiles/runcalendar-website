Course

A flat out-and-back along the paved Green River Trail, starting and finishing at Three Friends Fishing Hole on Russell Rd in Kent. The half marathon adds a small loop at the halfway mark; the 10K turns around just past the mile 3.1 aid station. The route follows the river past golf course frontage.

About

Well-organized, professional, personable, safe and fun: it's what makes Orca Running races so well known. Our newest race will be no different! Join us on March 14th for the 4th annual Run the Green! The half marathon and 10K are out and backs on the Green River Trail, and along the way you'll enjoy scenery of the river and golf course. Flat and fast you won't want to miss this race. All participants receive a commemorative shirt, medal, free photo downloads, and post race snacks.
