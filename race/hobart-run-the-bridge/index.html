About

Lace up your running shoes and join the crowds at the Hobart Run The Bridge, one of Tasmania's premier annual running events. Participants journey together over the iconic Tasman Bridge, taking in stunning views of the Derwent River and Hobart's picturesque cityscape. This is a must-do event for runners seeking a unique and memorable running experience.
