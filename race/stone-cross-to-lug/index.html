About

Explore the rugged beauty of Ireland's Wicklow Mountains at the Stone Cross to Lug race. This challenging trail run takes runners on a scenic journey from the historic Stone Cross to the summit of Lugnaquilla, the highest peak in the region. With stunning vistas and a rewarding challenge, this event is a must-do for adventurous trail enthusiasts.
