Course

Rugged wooded trails around Clinton Lake, starting and finishing at Shelter 1, 812 N 1402 Rd, Lawrence. The 10K climbs Sanders Mound early and again near the end before returning on the Blue Trail; the 25K runs one loop and the 50K two. Three stocked aid stations at Lake Henry, Lands End and the start/finish; runners carry their own hydration.

About

A trail ultra hosted by the Lawrence Trail Hawks at Clinton Lake near Lawrence, Kansas, with 10K, 25K, and 50K distances. The singletrack course loops through wooded lakeside terrain known for its deceptively tough climbs.
