About

The PNC Atlanta 10 Miler & 5K is one of Atlanta's most popular running and walking events. Starting and ending at Atlantic Station, runners and walkers wind through Atlanta's Buckhead neighborhood. Celebrate your finish with family and friends at one of Atlantic Station's many retail and restaurant locations- several of which are offering discounts and specials on race weekend.
