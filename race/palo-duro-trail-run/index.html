Course

A looped course in Palo Duro Canyon State Park, starting and finishing at the Mack Dick Pavilion car park, with the longer distances repeating laps. Footing runs from rolling singletrack to sand and rock, with short but steep and technical ascents and descents. Four staffed aid stations carry water, soda, pickle juice, electrolyte drink and food from sweets and crisps to boiled potatoes and PB&J.

About

The Palo Duro Trail Run takes runners deep into Palo Duro Canyon State Park, known as the ''Grand Canyon of Texas,'' with 25K, 50K, and 50-mile distances across the canyon''s rugged red-rock trails.
