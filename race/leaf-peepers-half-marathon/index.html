Course

A half marathon on the original Waterbury/Duxbury course, restored after several years on a Perry Hill route. Aid stations sit at 3.5, 7 and 10.9 miles. A 5K is run alongside the half.

About

The Leaf Peepers Half Marathon and 5K, hosted by Central Vermont Runners, run through Waterbury, Vermont, timed for peak autumn foliage in early October along the Winooski River valley.
