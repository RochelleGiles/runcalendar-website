About

The Jungfrau Marathon is one of the most scenic and challenging running events in the world. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Swiss Alps, the course takes runners on a journey through picturesque villages, lush meadows, and up the slopes of the legendary Lauberhorn. With an elevation gain of over 1,000 meters, this is a true test of endurance and determination, making it a must-do for any serious runner.
