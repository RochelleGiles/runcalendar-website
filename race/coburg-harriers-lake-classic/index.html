Course

Usually run on the Merri Creek bike path, an undulating scenic path, starting and finishing at the Harold Stevens Athletic Track on Outlook Rd, Coburg North. A 10 km and a 5 km are offered, with morning tea afterwards. Fields are small, typically 60 to 90 entries.

About

The Coburg Harriers Lake Classic loops around Coburg Lake in Melbourne's northern suburbs. Runners choose the 5km or 10km distance on a flat course circling the lake reserve, hosted by the local Coburg Harriers athletics club.
