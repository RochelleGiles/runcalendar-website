Course

Two single-track trail races in the Oregon Cascades. The 14 mile starts and finishes at Hardesty Trailhead, climbing 3,300 feet in the first 4.5 miles, giving it all back over the next 4, then rolling for the last 5.5, with a four hour limit. The 5.5 mile forest-fiver runs from South Creek Trailhead to Hardesty on gradual grades and technical footing.

About

A challenging trail race in Oakridge, Oregon, climbing the singletrack of Mount Hardesty in the Willamette National Forest. The 14-mile and 5.5-mile courses test runners with steep, technical terrain.
