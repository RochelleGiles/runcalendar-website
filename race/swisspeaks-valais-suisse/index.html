About

SwissPeaks Trail is a point-to-point mountain race through the Swiss canton of Valais, running from the shores of Lake Geneva up to the high passes and glaciers of the Alps. Eight courses are offered, from a 10km Discovery to the 650km Odyssey, so the field ranges from first-time trail runners to multi-day ultra specialists.
