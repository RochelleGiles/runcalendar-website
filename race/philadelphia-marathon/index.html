Course

Fast city marathon starting at 22nd Street and the Benjamin Franklin Parkway beside the Philadelphia Museum of Art. The route runs through the historic district, out through Fairmount Park and along the Schuylkill River into University City and Manayunk, then back to finish at the Art Museum steps. Sealed roads throughout, starting at 7:00am.
