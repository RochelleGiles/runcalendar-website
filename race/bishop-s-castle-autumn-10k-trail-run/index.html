About

Explore the rolling hills and stunning vistas of Shropshire's countryside at the Bishop's Castle Autumn 10K Trail Run. This breathtaking 10km route showcases the natural beauty of the region, offering runners a challenging yet rewarding experience through picturesque landscapes. Hosted by Runningmonk Trail Events, this event is a must-do for trail enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable adventure.
