Course

A road half marathon starting and finishing in Jackson Park on Chicago's South Side, with exclusive access to a closed stretch of Lake Shore Drive and a run through historic Southside neighbourhoods. The route is flat and fast with skyline and Lake Michigan views. A 5K along the lakefront and a kids' run share the morning.

About

The Chicago Half Marathon and 5K run along Lake Michigan's lakefront path with the downtown skyline as a backdrop. The flat, fast course draws both first-time half marathoners and runners chasing a personal best in late September.
