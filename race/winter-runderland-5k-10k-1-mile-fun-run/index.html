Course

Starts and finishes at Richland County Elementary School on North Holly Road in Olney. The 5K and 10K both run counter-clockwise on the roads around the school, with chip timing and bibs for those two distances only. A one-mile family fun run is untimed.

About

The Winter RUNderland, hosted by the Richland County CEO Program in Olney, features 5K, 10K and one-mile fun run distances open to runners and walkers of all ages. A winter-themed festival with vendors, food trucks, games and activities follows inside the RCES gym, with T-shirts for registered participants and awards for top age-group finishers.
