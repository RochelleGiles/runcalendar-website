About

The Cincinnati Heart Mini-Marathon promotes cardiac and stroke health awareness, noting that cardiac arrests can happen anywhere, including at the finish line, and encourages participants to learn Hands-Only CPR. The two-day event includes a half marathon, 15K, 5K, and other races alongside a health and fitness expo.
