About

Nestled in the heart of the unknown, the Sanbe Trail offers runners a truly unique and challenging experience. Traversing rugged terrain and stunning natural landscapes, this event promises an unforgettable adventure for outdoor enthusiasts. Hosted by the Vertical Sanbe organization, the Sanbe Trail invites runners to push their limits and immerse themselves in the beauty of the unknown.
