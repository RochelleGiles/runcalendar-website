Course

All distances start in downtown Rockmart. The 5K is a flat, fast, USATF-certified out-and-back on the Silver Comet Trail and qualifies for the Peachtree Road Race; the one-mile fun run is an out-and-back through downtown, with a 100-yard Tot Trot for under-6s.

About

The Love Loud 5K is once again being produced by Crossview Community Church of Rockmart, GA as part of their Community Outreach Ministry. Our mission as a Church is to love our community and to do it in a BIG and LOUD way so the term to "LOVE LOUD" was born! All proceeds from this race will be used directly to fund Crossview's multiple community outreach efforts such as Back to School Backpacks & haircuts, Thanksgiving food boxes, and Christmas help for the needy just to name a few!
