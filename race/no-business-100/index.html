Course

A single 100-mile loop starting and finishing at the Group Camp in Pickett CCC Memorial State Park and running through the Big South Fork National River & Recreation Area on the Tennessee-Kentucky line. Almost entirely technical single-track past sandstone arches, rockhouses, waterfalls and the abandoned No Business settlement, with about 14,500 ft of climbing. The direction of the loop alternates each year.

About

No Business 100 is a trail ultramarathon through the Big South Fork National River and Recreation Area near Jamestown, Tennessee. The remote course crosses rugged gorges and forested ridgelines along the Tennessee-Kentucky border.
