Course

A 5K run/walk followed by a children's fun run, starting and finishing at the Hathaway Brown School on North Park Boulevard in Shaker Heights. The race is run on the sealed streets of the surrounding neighbourhood and is followed by a family carnival on the school grounds.

About

Carnival is the HB's largest and longest-running annual student fundraiser. In 1929, Latin Teacher and Associate Principal Grace Waymouth proposed a carnival to replace the Christmas bazaar fundraiser and it has continued ever since. Carnival is also the senior class' charitable gift to HB's celebrated community. Each year, the senior class picks a civic engagement partner to collaborate with and donate the funds raised from Carnival.
