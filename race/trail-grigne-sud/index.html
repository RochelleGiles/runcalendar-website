About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Grigne Sud on this challenging trail running event. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Italian Alps, runners will test their limits on a course that winds through lush forests, across rocky ridges, and over rolling hills. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to the sport, the Trail Grigne Sud offers an unforgettable running experience.
