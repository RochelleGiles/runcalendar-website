About

The Great Ennis 10K is a beloved 10km road race in the charming town of Ennis, Ireland. Runners will wind through the historic streets and scenic countryside, taking in the picturesque views that make this event a favorite among the local running community. Whether you're chasing a new personal best or simply looking to enjoy a scenic run, the Great Ennis 10K offers an unforgettable experience for athletes of all levels.
