Course

Starts and finishes in Wenzel Family Plaza in downtown Marshfield, with registration and packet pickup at the Second Street Community Center. The route mixes street and trail through downtown Marshfield neighbourhoods. Both distances are run or walked.

About

The Cabin Fever Run is a winter road and trail event staged by the Marshfield Area Run Club in downtown Marshfield, Wisconsin. It offers a 5K and a 3K, both open to runners and walkers, starting and finishing in Wenzel Family Plaza.
