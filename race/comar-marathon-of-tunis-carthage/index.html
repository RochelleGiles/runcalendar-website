About

The Comar Marathon of Tunis Carthage is a prestigious running event that takes participants on a scenic tour through the historic city of Tunis and the ancient ruins of Carthage. The course offers a unique blend of urban landscapes and archaeological wonders, providing runners with an unforgettable experience. The event is known for its vibrant atmosphere and the opportunity to explore the rich cultural heritage of the region.
