Course

A 5K loop climbing the steep Wildwood Trail toward the Mohawk Mountain summit, traversing to descend Timber Trail, then working the right side of the hill through Pine, Laurel and Deer Run before Victor's Way, a climb up Route 100 and Ledges back to the peak and a final descent of Mohawk Trail. The 5K gains 1,332ft; the 10K runs it twice. The half adds miles in Mohawk State Forest for 3,169ft, and the marathon doubles the half.

About

This trail race climbs the ski runs of Mohawk Mountain in Cornwall, following trails like Wildwood, Timber and Ledges with sections through Mohawk State Forest on the longer distances. It's part of Connecticut's Blue Blazed Trail Running Series and run cupless, so entrants carry their own hydration.
