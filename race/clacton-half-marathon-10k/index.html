About

Experience the seaside charm of the Clacton Half Marathon & 10K, held as part of the vibrant Clacton Carnival in Clacton-on-Sea, Essex. Runners will enjoy picturesque coastal views and a lively atmosphere as they tackle this challenging yet rewarding event in the heart of the United Kingdom.
