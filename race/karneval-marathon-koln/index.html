About

The Karneval Marathon in Cologne is a unique event that combines the passion for marathon running with the excitement of the Cologne Carnival. This annual race marks the start of the 'fifth season' in Cologne, offering a festive and lively atmosphere for runners and spectators alike. The DLV-certified course provides a scenic route through the city, allowing participants to experience the vibrant Carnival spirit while challenging themselves physically.
