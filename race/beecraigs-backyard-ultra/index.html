Course

A backyard ultra in Beecraigs Country Park near Linlithgow. Runners repeat a 4.167-mile loop every hour, on the hour, until one runner remains. The loop follows forest trail, gravel paths and undulating woodland tracks with about 150 m of climb per lap.
