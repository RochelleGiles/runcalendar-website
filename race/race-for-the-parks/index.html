About

Race for the Parks is a lapped race at Hudson Springs Park in Hudson, Ohio, staged by Western Reserve Racing. The route mixes non-technical groomed trail with adjacent neighbourhood roads, passing the park lake and woodland. Runners complete one loop for the 5K and two for the 10K.
