Course

Mountain trail races in Eryri starting from Rhyd-Ddu. Terrain is technical and rocky in places and fast grassy trail in others, with a Grade 1 scramble on the Snowdon SkyRace. Climb ranges from about 950m to over 3,900m depending on distance, the longest route following the Welsh 3000s. Between one and three aid stations by distance.

About

SkyRun Eryri is Wales' premier Skyrunning event, offering a range of challenging races from 16km to 55km through the stunning Snowdonia National Park. Runners will tackle technical, rocky trails and fast, grassy paths with breathtaking views of the Welsh 3000s peaks.
