About

A winter trail race at Montanay, just north of Lyon, on the farm tracks and wooded valleys of the Franc Lyonnais plateau. The 25 km Trail Givré and the 9.5 km Montanoise cross the protected Ruisseau des Echets valley, with views over the Val de Saône and the Monts d'Or. Held each February.
