Course

A point-to-point road relay starting at Harris Bridge Vineyard in Philomath and running backcountry roads alongside the Yaquina River into Newport, finishing at the Yaquina Bay Yacht Club on the historic Newport bayfront. Running teams of up to seven cover 67 miles; walking teams of four cover 33 miles.

About

The Barrel to Keg Relay is a team relay race through Oregon's Willamette Valley wine country, running roughly 67 miles from a winery near Philomath to a brewery. Proceeds support the Race to End Poverty charity. Teams cover the route in stages, passing through rolling farmland and vineyards along the way.
