Course

A Thanksgiving morning run starting and finishing at Grand Park in downtown Los Angeles, with the 5K and 10K looping through closed downtown streets. A 1-mile Dog Jog opens the morning at 7:30am, the 5K goes in two waves from 8:00am, the 10K at 9:00am and a quarter-mile Widdle Wobble at 10:15am.

About

Turkey Trot LA offers a 10K, 5K and 1-mile Dog Jog on Thanksgiving morning in Los Angeles. Runners and their leashed dogs turn out together before the holiday meal for the citywide community race.
