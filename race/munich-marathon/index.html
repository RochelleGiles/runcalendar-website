Course

A single-lap city marathon starting and finishing in the Olympic Park, running past the Siegestor and Marienplatz and through the English Garden and the Werksviertel. The half marathon also starts in the Olympic Park and the 10 km starts in the Werksviertel. The organiser does not state the surface.

About

The Munich Marathon is a vibrant urban running event that takes participants through the most beautiful neighborhoods of Munich. Runners experience an exciting one-lap course that winds through iconic city locations like Marienplatz, Sendlinger Tor, and the Werksviertel, culminating in a spectacular finish at the Olympiapark.
