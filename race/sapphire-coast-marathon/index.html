Course

The route follows the Amanzimtoti coastline on the KwaZulu-Natal South Coast. Three distances are offered: 10 km, 21.1 km and 42.2 km. The full marathon serves as a Comrades and Two Oceans qualifier.

About

A marathon in Amanzimtoti on KwaZulu-Natal's South Coast, recognised as a KZN Athletics 42.2km Championship and used by many runners as a Comrades Marathon qualifier. Distances include 10km, 21.1km and the full marathon.
