About

La Landsberg is a trail running event that takes place in the Pays de Barr region. The event offers various race distances that showcase the natural beauty of the area, including vineyards, forests, and scenic viewpoints. The organizers have focused on the essence of the sport, providing a challenging course without excessive amenities, allowing runners to connect with the natural environment.
