About

The Crooked Creek Ultra runs a 12.7-mile loop with over 2,300 ft of gain per lap at Camp Crooked Creek in the Harry S. Frazier Scout Reservation near Shepherdsville, Kentucky. Distances range from 5K to 100 miles, including triple-play multi-race options and free pacer entries for the ultras.
