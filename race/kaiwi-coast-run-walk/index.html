Course

The roughly four-mile course starts at Awāwāmalu (Sandy Beach Park) at sunrise, follows the Kaiwi coastline past Hālona and Hanauma Bay, crosses the pedestrian overpass at Kalanianaʻole Highway and Lunalilo Home Road, and finishes at the Hui Nalu Canoe Club on Maunalua Bay. Water, fruit and snacks at the finish.

About

The Kaiwi Coast Run & Walk follows Oahu's southeast shoreline from Sandy Beach Park toward Maunalua Bay. It is organized by a local nonprofit, and proceeds support conservation of the Ka Iwi coastline and the surrounding community.
