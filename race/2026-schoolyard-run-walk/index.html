About

The Schoolyard Run + Walk is a 5K run and a one-mile walk hosted by the Rich-Mar Rotary Club in Mars, Pennsylvania. Both events start and finish at the Mars Area school campus on Three Degree Road, with packet pickup held at a local brewery in the days beforehand.
