Course

A flat, fast and certified course on waterfront trail and service roads around Confederation Park in Hamilton, where every distance starts and finishes. Aid stations carry water and electrolyte drink — one on the 5K, two on the 10K, and roughly every 3 km on the half and full marathon, with dedicated feed stations for nutrition.

About

Road2Hope is Hamilton, Ontario's fall marathon weekend, offering marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K distances. It's a popular Boston-qualifying race held each November on the city's outskirts.
