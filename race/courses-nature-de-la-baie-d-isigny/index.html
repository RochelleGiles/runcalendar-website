About

This race starts and finishes near the harbour in Isigny-sur-Mer, Normandy, following coastal dikes, polders and Norman hedgerow lanes on mostly trail surface. Two distances, 10.5km and 17km, are offered, with the field capped at 500 runners, now in its 15th edition.
