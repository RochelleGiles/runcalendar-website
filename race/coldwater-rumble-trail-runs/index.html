Course

Looped desert course in Estrella Mountain Regional Park on crushed-granite singletrack, rocky ground, sandy washes and old jeep roads, rolling with no sustained climbing. Longer races combine 10-mile red loops with 27-mile blue loops; the back half crosses the sandy 1.5 to 2 mile Corgett Wash. Well-stocked aid stations on each loop.

About

Follow desert trails that wind through the foothills of the rugged and jagged Sierra Estrella mountain range. Many trails at Estrella Mountain Regional Park have recently been re-worked and there are several new miles of trail in use for this course.
