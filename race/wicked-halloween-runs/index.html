Course

A single loop repeated by distance - one for the 5K, two for the 10K, three for the 15K - inside Maybury State Park, Northville, with start and finish in the park. The surface is a smooth paved hike and bike trail eight feet wide, with two-way runner traffic at one point. Aid stations at every mile marker with water and Gatorade Endurance.

About

Join us this October at Maybury State Park in Northville, Michigan for the BEST Halloween-themed race packed with fall vibes, festive fun, and a few spooky surprises. Choose from the 5K, 10K, 15K, or bring the little goblins for the Kids Spooky Fun Run—there's something for everyone! Run through scenic trails bursting with color, eerie décor, and crisp autumn air. Costumes are encouraged, and prizes await the most creative looks.
