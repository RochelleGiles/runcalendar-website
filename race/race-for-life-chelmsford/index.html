Course

A 5K contained entirely within the grounds of Hylands Park, starting and finishing on grass. The route runs mainly on grass with a gravel and woodland path, over slight inclines and undulating ground. Grass and woodland sections can turn soft and muddy in poor weather.

About

Race for Life's 5K crosses Hylands Park, the Georgian parkland surrounding Hylands House near Chelmsford. It's a non-competitive run, jog or walk raising money for cancer research.
