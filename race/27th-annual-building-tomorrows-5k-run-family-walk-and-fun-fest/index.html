About

The 27th Annual Building Tomorrows 5K Run, Family Walk, and Fun Fest is a family-friendly event hosted by The Arc of Essex County to support individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities. It takes place at the South Mountain Recreation Complex, featuring a 5K run, fundraising walk, and fun activities for all ages.
