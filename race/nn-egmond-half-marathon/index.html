About

The NN Egmond Half Marathon is a picturesque seaside running event held along the North Sea coast in the Netherlands. Runners will wind their way through charming coastal towns, taking in stunning views of the dunes and crashing waves. With a rich history and a reputation for excellent organization, this is a must-do event for half marathon enthusiasts.
