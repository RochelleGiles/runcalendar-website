About

The CMC Education Foundation runs this 5K and fun run at Northeast High School in Clarksville, Tennessee. Both courses stay entirely within the Northeast complex, with the 5K starting at 8:30am and the fun run at 9:15am. Proceeds support Clarksville-Montgomery County school programmes.
