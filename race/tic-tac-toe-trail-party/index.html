Course

Multiple loops on the 1 North Adventure Trails in Blanchard, with game stations around the circuit and split times recorded every five miles. The organiser describes it as not especially technical but with real elevation for the region. An aid station sits at the start/finish, with water at the turnaround.

About

This race is a beginner friendly twist on the traditional trail run. It will be held at 1North Adventure Trails in Blanchard. TTTTP is a different format than your traditional race. It's designed to make running fun by utilizing a game you've known since childhood....tic-tac-toe. You will be timed and your distance will be recorded but there will be no age groups and no overall winners. The goal is 3 O's in a row but there will be other ways to win as well.
