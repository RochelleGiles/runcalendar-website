About

The West Clare Cancer Centre 8K Run/Walk takes runners on a picturesque journey along Ireland's stunning Wild Atlantic Way. This scenic event, set in the heart of County Clare, offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners and walkers of all levels, with proceeds supporting the vital work of the West Clare Cancer Centre.
