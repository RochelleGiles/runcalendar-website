About

Discover the picturesque Périgord Noir region of France at the Croisade en Périgord noir de Domme, a running event steeped in history. Runners will enjoy a scenic route through the charming medieval town of Domme, offering breathtaking views of the Dordogne River valley. This unique event promises an unforgettable experience for runners seeking a challenging yet beautiful race in a stunning French landscape.
