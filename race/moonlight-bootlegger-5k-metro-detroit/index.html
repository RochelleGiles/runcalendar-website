Course

A night 5K run entirely within Maybury State Park, on a mix of wide forest trail and flowing singletrack. The marked course is lit by more than 300 tea lights and runners carry headlamps. Start and finish are at the same point, with live music at the finish.

About

Runners step into the shadows at the Moonlight Bootlegger 5K, racing through the woods like old-school outlaws guided by headlamp and moonlight. This after-dark adventure at Maybury State Park blends trail running, community and celebration.
