About

The Stranda Fjord Trail Race offers an extraordinary trail running experience in the stunning Sunnmøre region of Norway. Runners can choose from multiple distance options ranging from 7km to 95km, traversing breathtaking landscapes including fjords, alpine ridges, lush forest trails, and mountain lakes. This event provides a unique opportunity to explore the dramatic Norwegian terrain while challenging personal endurance limits.
