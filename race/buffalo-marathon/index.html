Course

The marathon runs through downtown Buffalo and out along Lake Erie, passing the KeyBank Center, Forest Lawn Cemetery and the Frederick Law Olmsted-designed park system. The half marathon is flat and fast on a shorter loop taking in the downtown waterfront and its landmarks. A 5K run and walk is held over the same weekend.
