About

The Ultramaraton Babia Gora is an annual running event that takes place in the picturesque town of Lipnica Wielka, at the foot of the Babia Góra mountain. Runners can challenge themselves on various courses, including the Ultramaraton 6xBabia Góra, Ultramaraton 4xBabia Góra, and 2xBabia Góra SkyRace®, which offer stunning views and a true test of endurance.
