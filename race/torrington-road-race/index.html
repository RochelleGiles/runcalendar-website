Course

All distances start and finish in Coe Park, Torrington. The five-mile race is run on a former USATF-certified course, alongside a 2.85-mile walk and a one-mile fun run.

About

This 5-mile race starts at Coe Park in Torrington and includes a companion 1-mile fun run. Proceeds support the Torrington Trails Network and other local charities, with free t-shirts for the first 100 entrants.
