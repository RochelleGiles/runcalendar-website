Course

Starts and finishes at The Blue Crab Restaurant & Oyster Bar, 7900 Lakeshore Drive, New Orleans. The half marathon starts at 7:15am and the 10K and 5K at 7:30am. The organiser publishes downloadable course maps only, with no written route or surface detail.

About

The Big Easy Halloween Half Marathon, 10K & 5K is a fall running event in New Orleans starting from the Blue Crab Restaurant & Oyster Bar. It features a flat and fast course with a post-race party and includes a Kid's One Mile Fun Run for family fun.
