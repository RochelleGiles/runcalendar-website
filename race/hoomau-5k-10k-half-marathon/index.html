Course

All three distances start at the Wai Kai facility in Ewa Beach and head out on Keone'ula Boulevard as out-and-backs. The 5K turns just past Kapolei Parkway; the 10K and half continue onto Papipi Road along the Leeward coast, with the half adding Kamakana, Geiger and Roosevelt streets. Surfaces are 100 percent asphalt for the 5K, 87 percent for the 10K and 94 percent for the half.
