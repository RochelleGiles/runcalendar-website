About

The Trail do Zêzere is an exhilarating trail running event that takes participants through the breathtaking landscapes of the Zêzere River basin. Runners will traverse rugged terrain, conquer challenging ascents, and immerse themselves in the natural beauty of this remote and captivating region. With a rich history and a reputation for an unforgettable running experience, the Trail do Zêzere is a must-attend event for trail enthusiasts.
