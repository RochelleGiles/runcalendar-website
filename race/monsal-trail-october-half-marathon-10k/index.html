Course

Traffic-free out-and-back on the Monsal Trail, a former railway line through the Peak District, starting and finishing at Bakewell Station. The route passes through four tunnels and crosses the Monsal viaduct. Multi-terrain and undulating underfoot, with half marathon and 10K run on both the Saturday and the Sunday.

About

Experience the stunning Peak District landscape at the Monsal Trail October Half Marathon & 10K. Runners will traverse iconic trails and scenic vistas over two days, immersed in the natural beauty of this picturesque region of the United Kingdom. This event offers a unique challenge for runners of all levels, with both half marathon and 10K course options available.
