Kowen Winter Trails

Course

Start and finish at Wamboin Community Hall, 112 Bingley Way, Wamboin. Courses are out-and-back, opening with about 1.3km of sealed bitumen on Bingley Way before joining groomed dirt forestry trails and single-track in Kowen Forest. The marathon takes in the Mt Amungula ascent and the half marathon includes Mt Doom. No aid stations are specified.

About

Choose between a marathon, half marathon or a 12 km course and run through native forest and on groomed forestry trails with spectacular views of Canberra and surrounds. The Kowen Winter Trails marathon is an official qualifier for Six Foot Track.
