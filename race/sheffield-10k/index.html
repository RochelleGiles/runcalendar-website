Course

A city-centre road 10K starting on Arundel Gate in the heart of Sheffield at 09:30. The course is marshalled throughout with water stations at intervals and first aid alongside them, and is chip timed. Minimum age 15.

About

Lace up your running shoes and join the excitement of the Sheffield 10K, a rare chance to race through the iconic streets of this steel-forged city. Runners will wind their way past historic landmarks and vibrant neighborhoods, soaking up the energy of the Steel City crowd. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply enjoying the thrill of the run, the Sheffield 10K offers an unforgettable experience for athletes of all levels.
