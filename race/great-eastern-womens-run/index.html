Course

Starts and finishes at The Kallang in Singapore, running on closed city roads. The programme spans a 21.1km half marathon (elite and open), 10km, 5km, a 2km Mummy and Me and a 100m Princess Dash for young girls.

About

Held at the Singapore Sports Hub since 2001, the race offers a half marathon, 10km and 5km along closed city roads each November. Now in its third decade, it draws a women-only field from across Singapore and the region and is one of Asia's biggest running events for women.
