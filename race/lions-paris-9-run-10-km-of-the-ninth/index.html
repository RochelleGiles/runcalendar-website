About

The Lions Paris 9 Run is a 10K race through the vibrant 9th arrondissement of the French capital. Runners will wind their way past iconic Parisian landmarks, soaking up the energy of the city as they push towards the finish line. This annual event offers a unique opportunity to experience the heart of Paris on foot.
