About

The Leuven Marathon is an exciting running event that follows the success of the European Running Championships. The race offers three distances and traverses the vibrant heart of Leuven, with a start and finish at Sportoase. Marathoners will enjoy an additional loop through the Brabant Forests, accompanied by live music, DJs, and enthusiastic supporters.
