Course

A 10K run along the Chatham shore, starting at Monomoy Middle School on Crowell Road. The out-and-back course has been largely unchanged for decades. A two-person 5K relay is contested over the same course, capped at forty teams.

About

The Chatham Harbor Run is a 10K road race through the coastal town of Chatham on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, organized by the Cape Cod Athletic Club. The out-and-back course runs past Chatham Harbor with views of the town's working waterfront.
