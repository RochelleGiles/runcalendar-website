Course

About

The Hillsborough Castle and Gardens Running Festival is an exciting event that takes place in the beautiful grounds of the Hillsborough Castle and Gardens, the official residence of the Royal Family in Northern Ireland. The 10k and half marathon races are suitable for runners of all levels and offer the opportunity to enjoy the stunning surroundings on traffic-free paths.
