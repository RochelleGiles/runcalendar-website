Course

Starts and finishes at Colby Lake Park in Woodbury and links four connected lakes on sealed shared paths. The route has frequent turns and several road crossings, which are not marshalled. The half marathon is uncertified but wheel-measured at 13.11 miles.

About

Get ready to discover the scenic wonders of Woodbury at the Woodbury Lakes Half Marathon, 10k, and 5k – an exhilarating experience that takes you around four of the city's stunning lakes, seamlessly connected by paved trails. The journey kicks off at Colby Lake Park, where you'll set out on a captivating route.
