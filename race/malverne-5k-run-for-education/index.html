Course

A road 5K starting on Wicks Lane near H.T. Herber Middle School at 9:00 am and winding through Malverne, Lynbrook and Lakeview before finishing behind Malverne High School. A 200 m fun run for ages 1-10 is held alongside it.

About

The Malverne 5K run-walk for education is organized by the Malverne Educational and Fitness Foundation (MEFF). The net proceeds of the 5K run go to the MEFF, a not-for-profit 501(c)(3) organization that supports youth programs within the Malverne school district.
