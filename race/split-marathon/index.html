About

The Split Marathon route connects the most attractive city landscapes through a unique combination of history, Mediterranean nature, and urban beauty. The route starts along the picturesque Split Riva, where the waves whisper stories, and then dives into the historic heart of Split. You will run past the magnificent Diocletian's Palace and across the Braće Radić Square, and then through the lively urban center dominated by Hrvojeva and Zagrebačka streets. The route then leads across the Domovinskog Rata Street and Hercegovačka, connecting the modern parts with the green slopes of Marjan, the lungs of the city.
