Sydney Marathon

Sydney, New South Wales · Australia
RoadUrbanMarathon · 10K · 5K

The youngest World Marathon Major — a runner's honest guide to the bridge, the hills, the Opera House finish, and the ballot that gets you there.

Quick verdict

The most dramatic finish line in world marathoning, on a course that makes you earn it — 313 m of climbing, the Harbour Bridge at 2K and Oxford Street at 35K. Hilly, but not slow: 2026 brought a 2:04:42 course record and a wave of personal bests through the field, and a record 44,000 ran. Entry for Sunday 29 August 2027 was a free ballot, and results land on 3 November. Missed it? Travel and charity places are the other doors — save it as Interested and we'll keep you posted.

Course at a glance

313mClimbing
Net ↓83mDownhill
7 hrsCutoff

Point-to-point from North Sydney to the Opera House forecourt. Over the Harbour Bridge inside the first 3k, through The Rocks and Circular Quay, a long Anzac Parade out-and-back through the middle, then the city again for the finale down Macquarie Street.

Where you'll feel it

📍Oxford Street, kilometres 35-40.

The race is won and lost here. A grinding climb on tired legs just when you want it least — bank nothing on the early downhills, you'll spend it all on Oxford Street.

Best chance to push

The long descent to Macquarie Street — if you saved your legs on Oxford Street, the last 2k are free speed with the Opera House pulling you in.

Three things to know

  1. Entry is a free ballot and a random draw, open for three weeks from late September — entering early earns nothing. Results come out in early November. If you're planning for 2028, save the race as Interested and we'll tell you the morning the next ballot opens.
  2. This is a hilly Major. The bridge at 2K is the most exposed part of the course — wind and an early chill, then it is gone — and Oxford Street at 35K is where the race is decided. If your plan only has flat long runs, add hill repeats.
  3. Missed the draw? The travel and charity programs are the other doors, and both are named in the results email. The Mini Marathon runs the same weekend if you want the finish line without the hills.

16-20°C and late-winter sun. Cool and crisp at the 6:31am first wave; the bridge crossing is the coldest, most exposed stretch.

What runners wear

Singlet and shorts by 8am — but it's cold in the start pens. Bring a throwaway layer for Bradfield Park. Carbon plate shoes are everywhere, though this course rewards legs over stack height.

Typical finish times

Course records are 2:04:42 (Addisu Gobena, 2026) and 2:18:22 (Sifan Hassan, 2025); Peres Jepchirchir won the 2026 women's race in 2:18:31. A record 44,000 ran in 2026 — the big pack rolls in between 3:45 and 4:45, and the forecourt is still roaring at the 7-hour cutoff.

Worth the trip?

Yes — this is a World Marathon Major, the newest of them until Cape Town joins in 2027, and still the one with the finish photo nothing else on earth can match. If you are collecting the set, get in the ballot. It is free, and the draw is the same for everyone.
