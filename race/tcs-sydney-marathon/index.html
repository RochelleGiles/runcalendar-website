Course

Point-to-point from Miller Street in North Sydney across the Sydney Harbour Bridge, looping through Centennial Park and past Mrs Macquarie's Chair in the Royal Botanic Garden to finish on the Sydney Opera House forecourt. Drink stations every 5 km; +313 m of elevation gain with a net drop of 83 m.

About

The TCS Sydney Marathon presented by ASICS is the newest Abbott World Marathon Major. The course is one of the most scenic routes in the world, starting in North Sydney, crossing the Sydney Harbour Bridge, and finishing at the Sydney Opera House.
