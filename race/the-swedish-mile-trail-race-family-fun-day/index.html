Course

A well-marked, timed trail race on the trail system at the Vasaloppet Nordic Center, Mora. The 10km is described as rugged and carries one aid station. A 5km trail race, fun run and walk plus 1-mile and half-mile kids' races run alongside it.

About

One Swedish Mile = 10 Kilometers and a whole lot of family fun! The annual Swedish Mile Trail Race and Family Fun Day is a 10K timed trail race, 5K fun run/race, kid's race, and an afternoon of family-friendly activities to raise money for snow-making and operations at the Vasaloppet Nordic Center.
