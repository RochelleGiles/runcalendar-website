Course

A flat, fast, USATF-certified and Boston-qualifying road course through the Quad Cities, billed as five races, four cities, three bridges, two states and one island. The route follows the Mississippi River across state lines between Illinois and Iowa, taking in Arsenal Island and long stretches of riverfront.

About

The Quad Cities Marathon links Illinois and Iowa across bridges over the Mississippi River, passing through Moline, Rock Island, Davenport, and Bettendorf. Marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances are all on offer each September.
