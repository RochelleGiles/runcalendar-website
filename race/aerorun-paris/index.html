About

The Aerorun Paris is a unique running event that takes place at the Paris-Le Bourget Airport. Participants have the opportunity to run on the private airfield and experience the thrill of being surrounded by aviation. The event offers a range of distances, catering to runners of all levels, and promises a memorable experience with surprises along the way.
