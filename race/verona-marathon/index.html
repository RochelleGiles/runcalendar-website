Course

Starts on Corso Porta Nuova beside Piazza Bra and finishes in the piazza next to the Verona Arena, run as a single city loop (giro unico) rather than repeated laps. The route is World Athletics certified. The 21K and the Family Run share the same start and finish area.

About

The Verona Run Marathon is a prestigious running event that takes participants through the historic and picturesque city of Verona. Runners experience a scenic course that showcases the city's beautiful architecture and cultural landmarks. The marathon offers multiple race distances, making it accessible to runners of all levels and providing a memorable running experience in one of Italy's most beautiful cities.
