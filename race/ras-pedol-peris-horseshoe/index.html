Course

A self-navigated mountain race starting and finishing at the Padarn Lake Hotel in Llanberis. The full Peris covers 28 km with 2,600 m of climb over nine summits including Yr Wyddfa/Snowdon; the half covers 13 km and 1,375 m over three. Runners find their own line between summit checkpoints, so fell experience and navigation skills are required.

About

Ras Pedol Peris, also known as the Peris Horseshoe, is a fell race around the ridges above Llanberis in Snowdonia, north Wales, with Full and Half course options.
