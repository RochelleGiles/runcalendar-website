About

Swissalpine is a spectacular trail running and hiking event set in the heart of the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Sardona. Participants experience breathtaking trails along the Rhine Gorge, around Lake Cauma, and past impressive natural landmarks like Martinsloch and the Tschingelhörner peaks. The event offers multiple distance options for runners and hikers of all levels, celebrating alpine energy and natural beauty.
