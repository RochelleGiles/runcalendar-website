Course

Out-and-back road course starting at Frankston Pier on the Frankston Waterfront and following Nepean Highway to Eel Race Road, Carrum, before looping back to the start. Distances are 15km, 10km, 5km and a 1km kids race. Aid stations with water and sports drink are placed about every 2.5km and at the finish line.

About

The Frankston Running Festival is held on the Frankston waterfront in Melbourne's south-east, with 15km, 10km and 5km races plus a 1km kids race. All distances run an out-and-back along Nepean Highway between Frankston Pier and Carrum, alongside Port Phillip Bay. Aid stations are set about every 2.5km.
