About

The Leiria Half Marathon is a must-run event for any endurance athlete, set against the picturesque backdrop of the historic Portuguese city. Runners will wind through charming streets and scenic riverside paths, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for both seasoned veterans and newcomers to the distance. This annual race is a highlight on the regional running calendar, drawing participants from across the country to this vibrant community celebration of fitness and sport.
