About

Cajibío Trail Run is a trail race in the municipality of Cajibío, in Colombia's Cauca department, with routes of 21K, 14K and 7K through the surrounding Andean countryside. It is organized by Montaña Ancestral and draws trail runners from across southwestern Colombia.
