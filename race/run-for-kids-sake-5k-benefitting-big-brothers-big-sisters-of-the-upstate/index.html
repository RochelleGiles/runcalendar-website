About

Big Brothers Big Sisters of the Upstate hosts the Run for Kids' Sake 5K run/walk at Lake Conestee Park in Greenville, open to all ages, alongside a 400-meter kids' fun run. Proceeds support the organization's youth mentoring programs, with pre- and post-race snacks and vendors on site.
