Course

A point-to-point race through the southern Appalachian chain from Porters Gap to Lake Howard. The first 31 miles run on single-track, followed by a few miles of double-track and forest-service dirt before returning to single-track for the run in to Lake Howard.

About

A trail ultramarathon near Sylacauga, Alabama offering 50K and 50-mile distances on the Pinhoti Trail Series' forested singletrack through the Talladega National Forest. It draws Southeastern ultrarunners each September.
