Course

Single-lap 5k around Coopers Field in Bute Park, Cardiff, starting on the north side opposite Orchard Lawns and finishing on the south side by the Gorsedd Stone Circle. The route mixes tarmac paths and grassy fields and takes in two bridge crossings over the river; the grass start and finish are mostly flat and firm but can turn boggy in wet weather. An alternative route bypasses the bridges.

About

This Cancer Research UK fun run offers 3K and 5K distances at Coopers Field in Bute Park, the green space that runs through central Cardiff. Entrants can run, jog or walk, with all funds going to cancer research.
