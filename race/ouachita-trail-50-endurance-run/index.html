Course

Run on the eastern end of the Ouachita National Recreation Trail from Pinnacle Mountain State Park, west of Little Rock. Both races climb over Pinnacle Mountain before an out-and-back along the rolling north shore of Lake Maumelle. The trail is scenic, rocky single-track throughout. Now in its 36th running, with 50-mile and 50 km options.

About

The Ouachita Trail 50 Endurance Run is a challenging 50-mile ultramarathon through the beautiful Ouachita Mountains in Arkansas. Runners will experience a scenic and rugged course with over 10,000 feet of elevation gain.
