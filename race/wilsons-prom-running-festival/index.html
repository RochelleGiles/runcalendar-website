Course

All courses run in Wilsons Promontory National Park on coastal trail, with the event village at the Australian Commando Memorial on Ring Road, Tidal River. Routes take in beach sand, rocky coast and inland bush track, and include a water crossing that can reach waist deep.

About

Explore the untamed beauty of Wilson's Promontory National Park during the Wilsons Prom Running Festival, a weekend of exhilarating trail running just 3.5 hours from Melbourne. Challenge yourself on scenic routes that wind through rugged coastal landscapes and lush forests, with the opportunity to connect with a community of passionate runners.
