Course

A double out and back on the Elk River Hiking Trail, starting and finishing at the Outlet Channel Group Shelter on the north side of the Elk City Lake dam, Independence. Mostly single-track and very rocky, through limestone bluffs and rock formations. Nineteen aid stations, roughly 3.5 miles apart and never more than four.
