Course

A single-distance half marathon of 21.097 km through Málaga, certified by the RFEA and World Athletics. The organiser describes the course as completely flat and at sea level. The 2027 edition is capped at 14,000 bib numbers, up from 12,000 the year before.

About

The Malaga Half Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Spanish coastal city of Malaga, this race offers a challenging yet picturesque course that winds through the historic streets and along the Mediterranean shoreline. Runners can look forward to an unforgettable experience as they take on this scenic half-marathon challenge.
