About

The Run For Sweet Caroline honors Caroline Rhine, who died from a rare cancer called leiomyosarcoma. All registration and donation proceeds support sarcoma awareness, patient education, and research through organizations including the Sarcoma Alliance, the Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center, Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, and the Leiomyosarcoma Support & Direct Research Foundation.
