Course

A circuit of the Lac de Joux, the largest lake in the Swiss Jura, on walking paths that run along or above the shoreline and are unsurfaced over almost the whole loop. The full tour is 24km and the half circuit 12km, with walking, Nordic walking and a two-leg relay over the same route.

About

The Tour du Lac de Joux is a running event that takes place around the scenic Lake Joux in Switzerland. The course features a mix of pedestrian paths and trails, offering runners a beautiful and challenging experience. The event caters to runners of all levels, with different distance options available, including a 24km race, a 12km half-race, and children's races.
