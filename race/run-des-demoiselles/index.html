About

The Run Des Demoiselles is a unique female-focused solidarity running event taking place at the Château des Demoiselles vineyard. Participants complete a scenic 7-kilometer loop through vineyards and forests, with a special children's race also included. The event aims to raise funds for the Intercommunal Hospital of Fréjus Saint-Raphaël.
