About

Turtle Crawl is an annual road race on Jekyll Island marking the start of sea turtle nesting season on the Georgia coast. Runners choose a 10K, 5K or 1K fun run. Proceeds support the Georgia Sea Turtle Center through the Jekyll Island Foundation.
