About

The Copenhagen Half Marathon is one of the fastest and best-organised half marathons in the world — a flat, one-lap tour of the Danish capital's bridges, boulevards and waterfront, lined with crowds and bands the entire way. A World Athletics elite-label race where world records have fallen, and an equally perfect stage for a first half or a personal best.
