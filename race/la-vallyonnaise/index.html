About

Explore the historic streets and scenic riverside trails of an undiscovered French city at the annual La Vallyonnaise running event. This unique race offers runners the chance to discover a charming urban landscape, with a route that showcases the city's architectural gems and natural beauty. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a casual jogger, La Vallyonnaise promises an unforgettable running experience.
