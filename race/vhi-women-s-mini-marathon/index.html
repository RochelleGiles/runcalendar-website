About

The Vhi Women's Mini Marathon is a beloved 10km charity road race held annually in the heart of Dublin. Drawing thousands of participants, this iconic event celebrates the strength and determination of women, offering a vibrant and supportive atmosphere for runners of all abilities to challenge themselves and make a difference in their community.
