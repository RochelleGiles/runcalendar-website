Course

A single 7K loop of Flushing Meadows Corona Park in Queens, starting near the Queens Theater. Mile splits are marked and a timed segment between miles one and two is run as its own competition. A free kids fun run and a virtual option are also offered.

About

Run (or walk) with us through Flushing Meadows Corona Park, up to the Shea Stadium base paths, and back. Finisher medals for all. Post race catered by Mookie Wilson's Legacy Catering.
