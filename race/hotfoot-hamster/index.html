Course

Flat 500-metre crushed-gravel loop winding around the grounds of Nardini Manor, USATF-certified with effectively no elevation gain per lap. One main aid station sits directly on the loop. Runners repeat the lap for the duration of their timed event.

About

The Hotfoot Hamster tests endurance over 6, 12 or 24 hours on a flat, fast, custom-built gravel running track at the historic Nardini Manor. It is one of two annual events held on this unique, ultra runner-built course.
