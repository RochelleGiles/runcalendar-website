About

The AlUla Trail Race crosses the desert canyons and sandstone formations of AlUla in northwest Saudi Arabia, passing near the UNESCO-listed Hegra tombs and landmarks like Elephant Rock. Distances range from a 3K sunset run to the 100K Hegra Hundred ultra.
