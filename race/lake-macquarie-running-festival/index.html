About

The Lake Macquarie Running Festival follows the traffic-free Esplanade between Warners Bay and Speers Point, offering a half marathon, 10.5K and 4K along the lake foreshore. First held in 1984, it is one of Australia's longest-running road events, held 90 minutes north of Sydney.
