Course

Out-and-back trail race on the Buckeye Trail through Brecksville Reservation in the Cuyahoga Valley, starting and finishing at Oak Grove. Natural surface singletrack across several park areas, with a 7:00am start and a 10-hour limit. Five staffed aid stations plus additional water-only stops along the route.

About

25K DISTANCE: Point to Point from Pine Lane to Oak Grove – Experience the same trails as the 50k! The Buckeye Trail 50k was started in 1994 as Joe Jurczyk's informal birthday run/race. Joe has been running the Buckeye Trail since his college days – which predates the CVNP. The original course was an out and back starting at Oak Grove and going south to Pine Lane. It also included segments to Brecksville Road (the first course was actually longer than 50k!) The out and back course enabled all participants to see every other participant at some point along the course.
