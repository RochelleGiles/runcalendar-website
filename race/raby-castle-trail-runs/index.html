Course

Trail routes of 5km, 10km and half marathon across the 200-acre Raby Castle estate, on a mix of firm paths, woodland trails and grass with gentle undulations. The route passes ornamental lakes and woodland where red and fallow deer graze. Chip-timed, roughly 10:00 start, one dog per runner permitted, free parking on site.

About

Trail races of 5K and 10K through the historic parkland and deer park surrounding Raby Castle near Staindrop, County Durham, with runners crossing open parkland grazed by fallow deer since medieval times.
