Course

A trail running festival starting and finishing at Dungog Common, in the foothills of the Barrington Tops World Heritage National Park in the Hunter region. The courses run on flowing bush trail through forest, mountains, rural country and waterways. Distances offered are 5km, 10km, 20km and 40km. No aid station detail is published on the main page.

About

Run Dungog is a trail running festival in the Hunter Valley town of Dungog, at the foot of the Barrington Tops. Distances range from a 5km to a 40km ultra along local forest and farmland trails.
