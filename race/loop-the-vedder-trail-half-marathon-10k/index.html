Course

Run along the Vedder Rotary Trail beside the Vedder River, mixing fast flat gravel with winding single-track through woods on the river bank. Start and finish are at Petawawa Road and Dieppe Street in Chilliwack, opposite the entrance to Vedder Park, and the route passes the Blue Heron Nature Reserve and crosses the Keith Wilson bridge. The 21 km has three aid stations, the 10 km one at the turnaround.
