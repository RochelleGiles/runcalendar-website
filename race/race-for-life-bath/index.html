Course

Two laps of a 5k route through Royal Victoria Park and past the Royal Crescent in Bath. The surface is mostly road and paved path with some grass, with a mild incline at Cow Lane and about 88 m of total ascent. Start and finish are on grass and gravel that can turn muddy in wet weather.

About

Cancer Research UK's Race for Life 10K takes place in Royal Victoria Park, the Georgian park laid out below Bath's Royal Crescent. Runners and walkers follow a flat, traffic-free course through the park, raising funds for cancer research.
