Course

Four distinct loops of about five miles each on the dirt trails of North Park, with rocks and roots underfoot and possible mud or ice. Runners start and finish at the North Park Ice Rink building and can break indoors between loops. The Full Frozen Goat option is a separate continuous, unmarked and self-supported route of 20 to 25 miles.

About

Run 5, 10, 15, or 20 miles through unique loops within the trails of North Park. This is NOT a race. This is a fun way to explore North Park and be a part of the trail community. Group up with others or run solo. You will have 6 hours to complete 1-4 of the loops. Each loop is different and ~5 miles. Start and Stop at anytime. This is the Goat Loops Event Option. You can also choose a continuous 20+ miles route. This run begins at 7:15. This is the Full Frozen Goat Event Option.
