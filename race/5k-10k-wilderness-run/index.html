Course

Start and finish at Peace Ranch, 2570 Hoosier Valley Road, Traverse City. The 5K winds through hard-packed sand roads in the Pere Marquette Forest; the 10K adds notably challenging topography in the same forest. Refreshments are provided at the finish.

About

The 5K is like none other as it winds through hard packed sand roads through the beautiful Pere Marquette Forest. Our 10K has become a favorite of trail runners statewide and is known for its challenging topography and peaceful surroundings.
