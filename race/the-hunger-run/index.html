About

The Hunger Run, now past its 6th running, is a 5K and 1-mile event at Wolfe Park in Columbus, Ohio, supporting Neighborhood Services Inc. (NSI), a local food pantry and resource center. The race raises awareness about hunger and food insecurity in central Ohio and funds for NSI's work.
