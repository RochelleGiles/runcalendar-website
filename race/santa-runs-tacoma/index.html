Course

Half marathon, 10K and 5K starting and finishing at 815 Pacific Avenue in downtown Tacoma. The half is an out-and-back along the waterfront and the paved Ruston Way path to Point Ruston Waterwalk and Dune Peninsula Park, crossing the Wilson Way Bridge before returning downtown. The 10K covers the same opening waterfront stretch.

About

Santa Runs Tacoma is a holiday-season half marathon, 10K and 5K hosted by the Tacoma City Marathon Association each December, with a festive Santa theme drawing runners of all levels through Tacoma, Washington.
