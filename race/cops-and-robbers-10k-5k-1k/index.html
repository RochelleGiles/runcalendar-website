Course

Runners pick Team Cop or Team Robber, with robbers given a head start before the officers set off in pursuit. All distances start and finish on Convention Center Way at the Dixie Convention Center in St George, looping back to the same venue. Aid stations are set every one to four miles depending on the distance.

About

The St. George City Police Department is hosting their 5th Annual 10 Mile, 10K, 5K, and 1K! This is a Cops and Robbers themed event. When you register you can choose to be Team Cop or Team Robber. The Robbers will be given a head start before the Cops chase after them. This is a timed race and your time will start once you cross the start line no matter what team you choose. Each team member will be given a unique shirt and custom team swag!
