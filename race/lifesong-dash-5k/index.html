About

The LifeSong Dash 5K is a USATF-sanctioned road race and family fun day hosted by LifeSong Inc. to support adults with differing abilities. It features a competitive 5K for all abilities, a Junior Dash kids' run, and community activities while raising funds for the organization's mission.
