About

The Dark Trail is a night trail in Orgelet, in the Jura region. Set off with your sneakers and headlamp on one of our 3 courses, which have some great luminous surprises in store! The Dark Trail offers varied trails and a hilly playground, ideal for trying out night trail running for some, but also a great opportunity to prepare for the big winter night trails.
