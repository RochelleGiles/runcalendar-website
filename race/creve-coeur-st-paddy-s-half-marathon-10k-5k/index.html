About

The Creve Coeur St. Paddy's races circle Creve Coeur Lake on paved park trail, starting and finishing at Sailboat Cove in Maryland Heights, Missouri. Half marathon, 10K and 5K distances are chip-timed, with untimed 5K, kids and virtual entries offered alongside them.
