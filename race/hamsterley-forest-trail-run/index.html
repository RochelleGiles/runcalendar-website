Course

An off-road course on undulating, well-marked forest trails through Hamsterley Forest in County Durham. The route mixes shaded stretches beneath tall pines with clearings and open vistas, on forest tracks and winding singletrack that call for confident footing. The half marathon starts at 9:00am and the 10K at 9:30am.

About

An unforgettable trail running experience through the breathtaking landscapes of Hamsterley Forest. Runners traverse a mix of forest tracks, winding trails, and open vistas set within one of the North East's most picturesque woodlands. The event offers both half marathon and 10K distances through this stunning forest setting.
