Course

A certified road 5K starting and finishing on Monona Avenue at Memorial Park, Rutherford, with a 10am start. The route runs out along West Passaic Avenue past the Jackson Avenue intersection, with mile markers surveyed to the foot.

About

The Shamrock 'N' Run 5K is an annual race held in memory of Michael Cassidy, a beloved Rutherford teacher and father who passed away from glioblastoma. It takes place in the scenic neighborhoods of Rutherford, NJ, starting at 10 AM.
