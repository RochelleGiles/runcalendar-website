Course

Four laps of the Welcome Rock Trails circuit on Welcome Rock Station, each about 50 km with 4,000 m of climb, run in alternating directions for roughly 200 km total. Starts at the Garston Ski Hut on Nevis Road and finishes at the Welcome Rock Woolshed. Backcountry trail throughout, with navigation to 14 checkpoints a lap. Unsupported: no aid stations and no outside assistance.

About

Explore the untamed landscapes of New Zealand's South Island at the Welcome Rock Ultra, an unsupported individual ultra-adventure run through the rugged borderlands of Southland and Central Otago. Challenge yourself on this scenic and challenging course, where you'll immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the region and push your limits in an unforgettable adventure.
