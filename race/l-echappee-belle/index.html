About

L'Echappée Belle is a renowned running event that takes place in the beautiful Belledonne mountain range in France. The event offers a challenging and scenic course that showcases the stunning natural landscapes of the region. Runners can expect a thrilling experience as they navigate through the rugged terrain, with breathtaking views at every turn. The event is known for its warm and welcoming atmosphere, making it a must-attend for trail running enthusiasts.
