About

Dublin's annual St. Patrick's Day Leprechaun Road Race features family-friendly races including a 1-mile fun run/walk, 5K, and 10K to support local charities like Heart of Georgia Fellowship of Christian Athletes. All events start and finish at Dublin High School Old Gymnasium, with parking at Dublin Mall across the street.
