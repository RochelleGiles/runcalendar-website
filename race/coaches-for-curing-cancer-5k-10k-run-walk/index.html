About

Join the Fight Against Childhood Cancer! "DA" Coach Karl DeMasi and the Savannah Sports Report/Prep Sports Report are teaming up for the 10th Annual Coaches for Curing Cancer 5K/10K Walk/Run. This impactful event is dedicated to raising funds for childhood cancer research and supporting patients and their families.
