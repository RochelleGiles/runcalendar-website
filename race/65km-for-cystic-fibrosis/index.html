About

Conquer the scenic trails of Melbourne's iconic Princes Park and Royal Park in the 65km for Cystic Fibrosis, a challenging endurance event that pushes participants to their limits while raising crucial funds for a worthy cause. This unique running adventure invites athletes of all levels to join the fight against cystic fibrosis, offering an unforgettable experience amidst the city's natural beauty.
