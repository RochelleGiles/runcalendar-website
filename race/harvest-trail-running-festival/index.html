Course

Out-and-back running on the Chaparral Trail from the Onion Shed at Farmersville, over paved, dirt and crushed gravel surfaces. The route is flat and fast, roughly 130 ft of climb across a 15.5-mile out-and-back. Three aid stations serve hot and cold food, snacks, water, electrolytes, pickle juice and ice, with a water-only station between them.
