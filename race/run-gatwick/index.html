Course

A closed-road half marathon starting in Crawley town centre, running up to and around Gatwick Airport beneath the flight paths, then back via Manor Royal to finish on Southgate Avenue. Fast and flat throughout, with three water stations on the half and water at the finish. A 5K on closed roads through the town centre and a one-mile race start and finish alongside it at Southgate Park.

About

Run Gatwick is a must-attend event for runners in the UK, offering a challenging Half Marathon and a lively 5km race that start in the vibrant heart of Crawley town centre. Runners can look forward to a scenic course that showcases the best of the local area, making this an event not to be missed for those seeking an unforgettable running experience.
