Course

A 3.8-mile hill climb up the Danny On Trail at Whitefish Mountain Resort, starting at the Danny On Trailhead and finishing where the trail meets the cat track to the Summit House. Singletrack hiking trail throughout, with 2,200 ft of climbing and no descent - every finisher gets a one-way lift ticket back down. Runners, walkers and hikers all welcome.

About

Run, walk, or hike to the top of Whitefish Mountain Resort! A lung busting fitness challenge, or a casual family hike, the choice is yours! All ages and abilities welcome. Each entry receives a one-time lift ticket back down the mountain.
