Course

A circular 100km circumnavigation of Leeds along the Leeds Country Way footpaths, passing outlying villages, parks and nature reserves. Roughly 80% trail and 20% tarmac. Nine checkpoints run from 10km to 96km, with indoor aid stations and drop-bag facilities at Garforth (43km) and Woodkirk (77km). Even-year editions start and finish at St John's Community Church, Greengates.

About

An ultra-distance race covering the full 62-mile Leeds Country Way, a circular footpath that rings the city through farmland, woodland and suburban fringe on the outskirts of Leeds, completed within a single day.
