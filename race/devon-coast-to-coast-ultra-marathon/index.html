Course

A point-to-point crossing of Devon from Wembury to Lynmouth, following the 17-mile Erme-Plym Trail to Ivybridge and then the waymarked Two Moors Way across Dartmoor and Exmoor. The route crosses two national parks on a mix of waymarked path, road and open moorland, with navigation needed on the moor. Checkpoints are fully stocked with hot food and drink and a tracker is mandatory.

About

The Devon Coast to Coast Ultra & Marathon crosses the county from coast to coast, starting in Wembury on the South Devon coast. Distances range from a marathon up to a 112-mile ultra, organised by Climb South West.
