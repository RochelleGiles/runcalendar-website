Course

About

The Marine Corps Run for Freedom 5K is an annual event organized by the Corporal Reynolds Detachment of the Marine Corps League in Freehold, NJ. Participants can choose to run, walk, or ruck the 5K course, with options including in-person, veteran division, and virtual participation.
