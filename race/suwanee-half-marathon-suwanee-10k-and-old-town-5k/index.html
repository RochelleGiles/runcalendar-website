Course

Starts and finishes at Suwanee Town Center, 330 Town Center Avenue. The half runs Peachtree Industrial Boulevard and George Pierce Park, joins the greenway to McGinnis Ferry and turns at Suwanee Creek Park before returning over the boardwalk bridge. The 10K runs the greenway in reverse toward Suwanee Creek Park and the 5K covers Main Street into Old Town. Several aid stations; an alternate route is used if the greenway floods.

About

The Suwanee Half Marathon is a road and greenway event staged with the City of Suwanee, Georgia, and benefits the city's public arts programme. Runners choose a half marathon, a 10K or the Old Town 5K, all starting and finishing at Suwanee Town Center.
