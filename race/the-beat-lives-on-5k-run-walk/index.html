Course

Starts and finishes at Venetian Shores Park, 801 Granada Parkway, Lindenhurst. The 5K leaves the park onto Granada Parkway and loops the surrounding residential streets before returning to the park. A virtual option is offered over the following week.

About

The Beat Lives On 5K Run/Walk is held in loving memory of CJ Neumann at Venetian Shores in Lindenhurst. Proceeds benefit the Beat Lives On Foundation to support facilities raising awareness and aid in the fight against drug addiction.
