About

The All Egypt Running Tour is a sports and tourism journey in Egypt, now past its 16th running, comprising four stages of trail running or hiking across canyons, desert sands, and palm groves along the Nile. The final stage is the Pyramids Half-Marathon (21km, 10km, or 5km) at the Giza Pyramids, alongside cultural visits to temples and museums in Aswan, Luxor, and Cairo and a Nile cruise between sites.
