About

A summer running event held at Dow Diamond featuring a 5K run, 5K walk, and 1 mile run with complimentary lawn tickets to a Great Lakes Loons baseball game. Participants start in the outfield and finish at Lou E's Landing inside Loons Stadium, with racing, baseball, and entertainment throughout the evening.
