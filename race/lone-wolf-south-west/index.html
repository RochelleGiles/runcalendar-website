Course

A lapped 24-hour event in Haldon Forest, meeting at Exeter Racecourse, Kennford, where runners complete one loop per hour for up to 24 laps and choose their own distance from 10K through to ultra. The loop runs on undulating forest track, mixing stony tracks with single-track paths.

About

Unleash your inner adventurer at the Lone Wolf South West, a thrilling off-road running event in the untamed landscapes of the United Kingdom. Explore uncharted trails, navigate challenging terrain, and immerse yourself in the raw beauty of the great outdoors. This event promises an exhilarating experience for seasoned trail runners and outdoor enthusiasts alike.
