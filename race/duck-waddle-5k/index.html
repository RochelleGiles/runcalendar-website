Course

Out-and-back 5K on the Lake Estes Trail in Estes Park, running counter-clockwise around the lake. Starts on the trail east of the town parking structure and finishes on the trail west of the Lake Front parking area on the north side of the lake. Open to runners, walkers, strollers and dogs.

About

The Duck Waddle 5K is a spring time foot race that kicks off the Estes Park Rotary Club's Duck Race Festival. Runners, walkers, strollers, and friendly pets are all welcome to participate in this scenic race around Lake Estes.
