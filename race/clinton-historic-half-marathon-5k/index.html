Course

Half marathon and 5K through Clinton, Missouri, past the Anheuser-Busch building, Artesian Park and the downtown square. Mostly sealed streets with railway crossings, plus a stretch of pea gravel on the Katy Trail in the half. Four-hour limit on the half marathon.

About

All proceeds go to benefit Big Brothers Big Sisters of Henry County. The event features historic sites like the Anhauser-Busch Building, Artesian Park, and the Historic Downtown Clinton Square.
