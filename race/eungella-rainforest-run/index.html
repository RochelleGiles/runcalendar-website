Course

Entirely off-road courses through Eungella National Park and the Crediton Reserves, west of Mackay. The 5km is an out-and-back, the 11km a loop, and the 18km a remote run with about 590m of climbing and two river crossings at the Diggings. Expect rolling downhills, punchy climbs, uneven ground and wet feet, with the long course marked by pink tape, arrows and signage.

About

Embrace the thrill of off road running through the secluded corners of Eungella National Park & Crediton Reserves. An unforgettable journey on remote backroads, diverse terrains, steep climbs and fast-paced hidden dirt tracks.
