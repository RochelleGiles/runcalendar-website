Course

A hilly out-and-back through Medicine Bow National Forest on mostly dirt and gravel roads, described by the organiser as 100% unpaved for the half marathon and 77% for the marathon. The route starts at about 8,700 feet and drops to roughly 8,000 feet at the turnaround. The course closes at 4pm.

About

The Wyoming Marathon Races are held in the Medicine Bow National Forest west of Laramie, on hilly dirt and gravel forest roads at high altitude. The day carries a marathon, the Medicine Bow Half Marathon, a Rocky Mountain 50K and the Vedauwoo 5K.
