Course

Mountain trail runs from Frater Lake HQ in the Kaniksu country of north-east Washington, over 100K, 50 miles, 50K, 29K and a half-ish. The 50 mile combines an 18-mile out-and-back to Granite Peak with a 32-mile lollipop over Thomas Mountain, for about 9,700 feet of climbing. Mostly technical singletrack with switchbacks and mud; eight aid stations, some water-only.

About

Kaniksu Trail Runs is a weekend of trail running events held in the Lower Selkirk Mountain Range of Colville National Forest, WA, honoring the SERE Community. Events include 100km, 50 mile, 50km, 18 mile, half-marathon, and ruck races around Granite Peak.
