Course

Starts and finishes at Bodman Park, 120 Market Street, Middletown. The four-mile route runs along Cooper Road over rolling hills past horse farms and is mostly shaded. The 4 Mile starts at 8:30 AM, the 2 Mile walk at 8:35 AM and the kids races at 9:45 AM.
