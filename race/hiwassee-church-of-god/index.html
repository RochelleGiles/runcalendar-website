About

Our mission is to create an inclusive and welcoming environment where people from diverse cultures, economic and social backgrounds can come together to experience the love of Christ. We are committed to fulfilling the Great Commission. We will strive to love and serve our community through acts of compassion, justice, and mercy, and to empower our members to use their God-given gifts and talents to serve others.
