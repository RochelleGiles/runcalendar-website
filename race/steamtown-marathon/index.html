About

The Steamtown Marathon is a USATF-certified Boston Marathon qualifier held annually in Northeast Pennsylvania, running point-to-point from Forest City High School to Courthouse Square in Scranton. It features a fast downhill course with 955 feet of elevation drop through 14 communities and benefits St. Joseph’s Center.
