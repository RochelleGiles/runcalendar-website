Course

High-alpine point-to-point along the Elkhorn Crest, starting on an old mining road near Bourne and finishing in Sumpter. Smooth and rocky-technical singletrack gives way to two-track and forest service roads, with 11,000 ft of climbing on the 53-mile and strict aid-station cutoffs.

About

The Elkhorn Crest 50 is a high alpine trail ultra race along the Elkhorn Mountain ridgeline near Sumpter, Oregon, featuring panoramic views, technical singletrack, and over 10,500 ft of ascent. It offers a 53-mile distance and a 50K option, starting near Bourne and finishing at Sumpter fairgrounds.
