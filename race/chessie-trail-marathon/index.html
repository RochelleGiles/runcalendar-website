Course

Run on the Chessie Nature Trail, a historic rail corridor of smooth gravel and grass about ten feet wide following the Maury River, with a very gentle grade and no road crossings. The marathon covers two out-and-back loops from McKethan Park, the half one, and the 5K and 10K use shorter turnarounds past farmland and limestone cliffs.

About

This marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K follow the Chessie Nature Trail, a former Chesapeake and Ohio Railway line converted to a rail-trail along the Maury River near Lexington. The flat, crushed-gravel path runs through pastureland and forest with river views.
