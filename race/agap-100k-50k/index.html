Course

A multi lap course of roughly six mile loops at Camp Agape, Fuquay-Varina, with start and finish at the camp. The 50K is five loops and the 100K is ten. The surface is a mix of singletrack and gravel trail with some hills. Two aid stations on the loop.

About

The Agapé 100K and 50K is a trail ultra at Camp Agapé in Fuquay-Varina, NC, run over roughly six-mile loops of singletrack and gravel with rolling hills. The weekend includes on-site camping, two aid stations per loop, and a 24-hour cutoff for both distances.
