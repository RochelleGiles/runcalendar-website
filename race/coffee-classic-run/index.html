Course

Both distances start and finish at the Meadowlark Pavilion in Indian Springs Metropark, following the park's flat paved path. The 5K has a single water station at the turnaround around mile 1.5. The 8-mile course carries four water stations, at miles 1.5, 3, 4.5 and 6.5.

About

Celebrate National Coffee Day at Indian Springs Metropark with a timed 8 mile or 5K race. Participants receive a custom race hat, finisher medal, and coffee mug.
