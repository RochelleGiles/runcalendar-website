About

Over the Sky – Hospice de France is a Pyrenean skyrace above Bagnères-de-Luchon, part of the international Skyrunning circuit. Courses of 9km and 21.5km climb into high alpine terrain around the Hospice de France, testing runners on steep, technical mountain ground.
