Course

A six-person relay on a closed-road loop of about two miles through Cambridge city centre and several university colleges. Each runner covers one lap before handing over. The race starts and finishes on Queens' Green.

About

A team relay race through central Cambridge, its stages weaving past the historic colleges and the Backs along the River Cam. Teams of six runners hand off across the city centre course.
