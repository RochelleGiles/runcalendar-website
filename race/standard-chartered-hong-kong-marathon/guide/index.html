Hong Kong Marathon

Hong Kong · Hong Kong
RoadCity skylineMarathon · Half Marathon · 10K · 5K

Three bridges, two tunnels and a harbour crossing before breakfast. Asia's hardest big-city marathon, back on its classic route.

Quick verdict

Not a course for a personal best. Highway slopes, bridge climbs and January humidity make it slower than any other city marathon its size — and that is the point. The classic route over Tsing Ma Bridge returns in 2027, and 18,000 marathon places go by ballot in September.

Course at a glance

6:00amStart
6 hrsCutoff
18,000Marathon field

Out of Nathan Road in Tsim Sha Tsui before six, up onto the West Kowloon highway and over Stonecutters Bridge, through the Nam Wan Tunnel to the turn on Tsing Ma Bridge with the Ma Wan channel below, back across Ting Kau Bridge and down through the Cheung Tsing Tunnel, then the long drop into the Western Harbour Crossing under the harbour to Hong Kong Island and the last kilometres through Central and Wan Chai to finish in Victoria Park.

Where you'll feel it

📍Out of the Western Harbour Crossing, 36–38K.

The Western Harbour Crossing, around 35K. Two kilometres down into the tunnel, warm and airless, then two kilometres climbing back out into daylight with the finish still 5K away. Runners talk about the bridges; the tunnel is what breaks them.

Best chance to push

The last 4K on Hong Kong Island, from the tunnel exit along the harbourfront to Victoria Park, is flat and finally has a crowd.

Three things to know

  1. The public ballot opens Friday 11 September at 2pm Hong Kong time and closes 20 September, with results on 25 September. Save the race as Interested and we'll remind you the morning it opens.
  2. It starts at 6am and the marathon cut-off is six hours, so it is a dawn race run mostly on closed highways — no crowds for long stretches, just the harbour and the bridges.
  3. Non-local entry is US$90 and the expo runs 13–16 January. Stay in Tsim Sha Tsui: you walk to the start and take the MTR back from the finish.

Race-day weather

BallotEntry

Mid-January is Hong Kong's coolest month, 15–20°C, but the humidity rarely drops below 70 percent. Cool at the start, clammy by the tunnels.

What runners wear

Vest and shorts. Nothing warm — the start is mild and the tunnels are warmer than the air outside.

Typical finish times

Course records are 2:09:20 for the men and 2:26:13 for the women, both set in 2019 and slow for a race this size — which tells you about the course. 2027 is a record 78,000 places across the marathon, half and 10K (84,000 with Saturday's 5K and youth run); 18,000 of them run the marathon.

Worth the trip?

For the bridges, yes. Nowhere else lets you run over Tsing Ma and under Victoria Harbour on closed roads. Come to finish, not to race.
