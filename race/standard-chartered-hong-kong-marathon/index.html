Course

Point-to-point from Nathan Road in Tsim Sha Tsui to Victoria Park on Hong Kong Island, run almost entirely on closed highways: over Stonecutters Bridge, through the Nam Wan Tunnel to a turn on Tsing Ma Bridge, back across Ting Kau Bridge and through the Cheung Tsing Tunnel, then under the harbour in the Western Harbour Crossing before the last flat kilometres through Central and Wan Chai. Highway slopes, bridge climbs and January humidity make it slower than any other city marathon its size.

About

The Standard Chartered Hong Kong Marathon is a major annual running event in Hong Kong. It features races of various distances, including the full marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K.
