Course

The 10K and the Crawford Street Mile both start and finish at the Portsmouth Pavilion on Crawford Circle — a new location and a new course for this edition. The 10K is run as separate run/walk, competitive walk and wheeled-athlete divisions.

About

A 10K and one-mile road race along the Elizabeth River in downtown Norfolk, finishing near Crawford Street. Organized by the Tidewater Striders, the event has run for years as one of Hampton Roads' regular waterfront road races.
