About

The Chester Kiwanis 5K, now past its 30th running, is a 3.1-mile community race and an important fundraiser for the Kiwanis Club of Chester. One of the most popular runs in the Hudson Valley, it draws hundreds of runners and walkers who enjoy a hot breakfast while supporting the Kiwanis mission of serving children worldwide.
