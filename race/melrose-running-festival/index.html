Course

Held in Mount Remarkable National Park in the Southern Flinders Ranges, about three hours north of Adelaide. The 50km runs Wilmington to Mambray Creek, then on to Melrose, and a two-person relay splits it into 25km legs. 30km and 15km trail runs are also offered. The organisers call the terrain very tough, on mountain trails with substantial climbing and summit crossings.

About

The Melrose Running Festival is a trail race at the foot of Mount Remarkable in Melrose, South Australia, with distances from a 15km King of the Mount up to a 50km ultra marathon.
