Course

USATF-certified and relatively flat 5K over the residential streets of Smithtown on a police-controlled route with water stops. Start and finish are at Smithtown Elementary School, 51 Lawrence Avenue. A one-mile family fun run and walk starts at 9:00am ahead of the 9:30am 5K.

About

The Smithtown Rotary's Go for the Green 5K is a USATF-certified road race over the residential streets of Smithtown, New York, with a one-mile family fun run alongside it. Both start and finish at Smithtown Elementary School, and proceeds go to local charities and scholarships.
