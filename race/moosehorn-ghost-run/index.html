Course

Half marathon and relay starting and finishing near the Refuge Headquarters at Moosehorn National Wildlife Refuge, 103 Headquarters Road, Baring, Maine. The route runs on dirt and grassy refuge access roads with two paved crossings, meandering south through meadows and flowages via Snare Meadow and Moosehorn Ridge Roads before a closing three-mile loop back to headquarters. Two self-serve aid stations at about miles 4 and 9.1 double as relay exchanges. Field capped at 100.
