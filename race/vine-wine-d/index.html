Course

A trail course looping through the vineyards, meadows and wooded ground of Fenn Valley Vineyards during grape harvest. About

The Vine Wine'd is a scenic trail race through premium wine grape vineyards, meadows, and forested ecosystems at Fenn Valley Vineyards. The 5K is a timed challenging course while the 1 mile is untimed and easier, followed by a post-race party with live music, wine, cider, and food.
