About

The Tamaulipas Marathon is a running event that takes place in Matamoros, Tamaulipas, Mexico. The event offers various race distances, including a full marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K. The course showcases the scenic landscapes of the region and provides a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels.
