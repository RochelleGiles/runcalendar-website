Course

Off-road obstacle run over the Mid Ulster countryside at The Jungle, Moneymore, run through mud and water throughout. Obstacles include cargo nets, balance beams, A-frames, a barbed crawl and a four-lane slip and slide. Adults run 5K or 10K on the Saturday; kids and teens race 2K and 5K on the Sunday. Marshals and medics are posted at obstacles and lifeguards at the water features.

About

The "Rampage Weekender" returns to the stunning Mid Ulster countryside, challenging runners with gut-busting, unique obstacles and plenty of mud. Hosted by the renowned outdoor event organizers at The Jungle NI, this thrilling two-day running adventure promises an unforgettable experience for adrenaline-seeking participants.
