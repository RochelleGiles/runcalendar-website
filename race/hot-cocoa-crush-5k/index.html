About

Warm up your winter with a fun, festive run through the scenic paths of Eisenhower Park! Whether you're chasing a PR or just looking for some cozy vibes, this race is the perfect way to kick off the season. Cross the finish line and treat yourself to a steaming cup of hot cocoa and delicious treats from All Round Foods & Waterloo Sparkling Water!
