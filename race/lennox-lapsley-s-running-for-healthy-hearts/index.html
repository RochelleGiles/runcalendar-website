Course

A roughly 13-mile loop on the gravel grid west of Millboro. The race begins on 310th Ave about a mile from the finish and parking area, running south, then east on 300th St, north on 314th Ave and west on 297th St — a section-line dirt trail rather than a county road — before turning south on 310th Ave to finish. Water stations and portable restrooms throughout, with three relay exchange points.
