Course

Half marathon runners start on Union Avenue in Havre de Grace, cross the Thomas J. Hatem Bridge twice into Perryville and return to finish at Millard Tydings Memorial Park on a USATF-certified course. The 10K starts at Perry Point VA Medical Center (shuttled from Havre de Grace) and finishes at Tydings Park. The flat 5K stays in historic Havre de Grace with waterfront views, from Union Avenue to Tydings Park.
