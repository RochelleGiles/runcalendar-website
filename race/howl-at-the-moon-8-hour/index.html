Course

A timed ultra run on a 3.25-mile clockwise loop through Kennekuk Park, Danville, starting and finishing at the Laury Barn (Big Red Barn). The loop mixes grass, gravel and pavement and takes in Watermelon Hill, with a full aid station near the halfway point and another at the top of the hill. Eight-hour and four-hour run and walk divisions are offered.
