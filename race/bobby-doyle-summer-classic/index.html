Course

Five-mile road loop starting and finishing at Narragansett Middle School on South Pier Road, winding through Narragansett neighbourhood streets and paved footpath. The certified course has water stations at 1.42, 2.75 and 4.05 miles and at the finish. A two-mile fun run and walk starts alongside it.

About

The Bobby Doyle Summer Classic is a 5-mile road race in Narragansett, Rhode Island, part of the Rhode Island Grand Prix racing series along the state's southern coast.
