About

The Corrida Appelouse is a festive running event that brings together runners, spectators, and volunteers in a warm and friendly atmosphere. The event offers courses for all levels, from children's races to a 12 km main event, creating a comprehensive and inclusive sporting experience in the heart of Firminy.
