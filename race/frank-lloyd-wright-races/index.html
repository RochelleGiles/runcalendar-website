Course

A point-to-point road course through Oak Park's tree-lined streets, past the neighbourhood's Frank Lloyd Wright architecture. The 10K and 5K start east of Oak Park & River Forest High School at Ontario and Elmwood and finish at the high school at Erie and Scoville. A youth mile and a 50-yard dash for younger children run alongside.

About

The Frank Lloyd Wright Races take runners past the architect's Prairie School homes and studio in Oak Park, Illinois. The 10K, 5K, and Youth Mile route through the historic district each October, with proceeds supporting the Park District of Oak Park.
