Course

A timed lap race at Thorndon Park in Paradise, about 20km north-east of Adelaide, run on a 1.426km loop around the reservoir. An aid station sits by the start/finish line, so runners pass it every lap. Entrants choose a 6 day, 72 hour or 48 hour format and accumulate distance rather than racing a set course length.

About

The Australian 6 Day Ultra Marathon Festival is an iconic event held at Thorndon Park, located 20 km northeast of Adelaide in South Australia. Thorndon Park is a scenic public reservoir shared with native koalas, wildlife, and local walkers! This is an event full of rich history that will see you walk away stronger mentally and physically, and with amazing friendships that will last a lifetime.
