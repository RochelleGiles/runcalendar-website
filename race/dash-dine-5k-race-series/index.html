Course

Out-and-back 5K starting and finishing at the Boulder Reservoir main parking lot. The course heads east on paved Reservoir Road for about half a mile, then turns onto packed dirt along the reservoir dams before returning the same way. Evening race series held on consecutive Tuesdays; the dirt sections are noted as bumpy.
