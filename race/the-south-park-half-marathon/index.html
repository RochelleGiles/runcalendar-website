Course

Two loops on paved roads and paths in South Park, starting and finishing near the museum building on Brownsville Road. Consistently hilly with around 1,000ft of elevation gain, passing the horse track, the Game Preserve bison herd and the Oliver Miller Homestead. Six aid stations at miles 1.2, 3, 6, 6.5, 9 and 11.9 with sports drink, water, fruit snacks and granola bars.

About

The South Park Half Marathon is a two-loop road race in South Park Township, Pennsylvania, starting and finishing near the park museum. The hilly paved course climbs around 1,000 feet and passes the horse track, the park's bison preserve and the Oliver Miller Homestead.
