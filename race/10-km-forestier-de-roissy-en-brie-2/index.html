About

The 10 Km Forestier is a community running event in Roissy-en-Brie that brings together local runners and supporters. Now in its 33rd edition, the race supports charitable causes, with proceeds going to ARSLA to fight Charcot's disease. Participants enjoy a scenic forest route that challenges and inspires runners of all levels.
