Course

A loop at Hobsonville Point starting and finishing on De Havilland Drive, with the 10k and 15k running multiple laps past a decision point. Underfoot it mixes the hard-surface and gravel coastal track, concrete paths, an elevated boardwalk and footpaths through the parks.

About

Run the Point is an annual running event in Hobsonville, New Zealand that offers a scenic course along the coastline. The event is known for its beautiful views, friendly atmosphere, and the opportunity to challenge yourself with various race distances.
