Course

A 20 km loop on scenic equestrian trails through foothills terrain, starting and finishing at Spring Lake Ski Hill in Saddle Hills County. Each lap carries over 200 metres of climb; runners take one loop for the 20K, two for the 40K and three for the 60K, solo or as a relay team.

About

The Saddle Hills Ultra is a looped trail ultramarathon on the equestrian trails around Spring Lake Ski Hill in Saddle Hills County, Alberta. Runners choose 20K, 40K or 60K, solo or as a relay team.
