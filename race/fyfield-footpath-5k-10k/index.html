About

The Fyfield Footpath 5K & 10K is an off-road running adventure set in the picturesque Essex countryside. Explore scenic footpaths and trails as you challenge yourself over either a 5K or 10K distance. This unique event offers a chance to immerse yourself in nature and experience the beauty of the British countryside.
