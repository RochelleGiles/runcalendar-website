Course

Starts on JFK Promenade in Golden Gate Park and finishes on Bernice Rodgers Way in the park's southwest corner. The half loops around the Panhandle, back through the park, and out and back through Sunset Dunes; the 5K is a certified downhill route through the park. Finish line closes at 12 p.m.

About

The San Francisco Half Marathon, 10K & 5K is an annual running event held in Golden Gate Park and along the Great Highway. Participants enjoy scenic routes with ocean views, receiving long sleeve T-shirts, finisher medallions, and post-race festival amenities while supporting local charities.
