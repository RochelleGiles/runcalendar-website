Course

Starts and finishes at Phil Rogers Park, Rainbow Beach, running the Cooloola Coast on single track, fire roads and beach, past coloured sand cliffs, perched lakes, sand dunes and the historic lighthouse. Five checkpoints: three with water, electrolyte, fruit and snacks, two water-only. No support crews on course.

About

The Rainbow Beach Trail Running Festival offers runners a spectacular trail-running experience through the stunning Cooloola Coast. Participants will traverse diverse terrain including lush forests, perched lakes, rising sand dunes, and scenic coastal trails, with breathtaking views of towering sand cliffs and the expansive Coral Sea.
