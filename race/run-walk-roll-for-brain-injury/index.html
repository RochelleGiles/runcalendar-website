About

The Run, Walk, Roll for Brain Injury is an annual fundraising event held at Tyler State Park to support BIAPA and Camp Cranium for children with brain injuries. Participants can join a 5K run or 1-mile walk/roll to celebrate Pennsylvania's brain injury community.
