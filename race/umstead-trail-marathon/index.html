About

Traverse 26.2 miles through the 5,000-acre wilderness of William B. Umstead State Park. The demanding course features a dynamic blend of 25% technical single-track trails and 75% runnable fine gravel bridle paths. Prepare for a course that features roughly 2,400 feet of total elevation gain, that showcase the park's lush forests, tranquil lakes, and winding streams.
