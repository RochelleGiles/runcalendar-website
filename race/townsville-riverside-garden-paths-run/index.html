Course

Two loops starting and finishing at Pioneer Park on Riverway Drive, crossing Federation Bridge and following Riverside Boulevard through residential streets before returning along the riverside paths. The surface is a mix of sealed shared paths and grass, with short dirt and rock-causeway sections.

About

Explore the lush Riverside Garden Paths of Townsville, Australia during this scenic running event hosted by the Townsville Road Runners. Choose between a 7.5km or 4.7km course, both winding through the city's vibrant greenspaces and along the banks of the nearby river. This unique event offers runners a chance to experience the natural beauty of the region while challenging themselves on the trails.
