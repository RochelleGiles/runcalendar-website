Course

A 5K through downtown Somerset, Kentucky, with the start and finish on S. Maple Street off E. Mt. Vernon Street. Entrants gather at the Pulaski County Judicial Center courtyard. Run, competitive walk and ruck categories share the same route.

About

This 5K run/walk in Somerset, Kentucky, is part of the Lake Cumberland Walk/Run Series and supports veterans transitioning to civilian life through a 20-acre, 25-tiny-house community.
