Course

A fast, flat road course starting and finishing on North Bayshore Drive in Elk Rapids, with views over Lake Michigan. The 5K is an out-and-back on North Bayshore; the 10K adds a loop in and out of East and West Harbor Drive. There is no significant elevation change.
