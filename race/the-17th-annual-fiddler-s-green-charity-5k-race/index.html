About

The Fiddler's Green Charity 5K is an annual road race in Winter Park, Florida, starting and finishing at Mead Botanical Garden. Organised by a local Irish pub, it is followed by an after-party, and proceeds go to the Conductive Education Center of Orlando and Habitat for Humanity of Winter Park-Maitland.
