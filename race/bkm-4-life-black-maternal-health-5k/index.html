About

The BKM 4 Life Black Maternal Health 5K is a community 5K hosted at SSM Health DePaul Hospital in the Bridgeton area of St. Louis, Missouri. It is run by BKM4Life to raise awareness of Black maternal health. The organiser page currently carries only a recap of the most recent edition.
