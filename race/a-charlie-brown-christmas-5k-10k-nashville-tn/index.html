Course

Start and finish on the Shelby Park Events Lawn in Nashville, near the Reflection Sculpture. The 5K runs on sidewalk and street; the 10K adds trail sections. Both courses carry mile markers and several water stations. Time limits are 90 minutes for the 5K and 120 minutes for the 10K, with streets reopening at 9:00am.

About

Join us as we celebrate five amazing years of timeless holiday cheer, iconic characters, and heartwarming tradition on Saturday, December 12th at Shelby Park right in the heart of Nashville! The 10K kicks off the event at 7:15am followed by the 5K at 7:30am and the Snoopy Kids Dash at 9:00am.
