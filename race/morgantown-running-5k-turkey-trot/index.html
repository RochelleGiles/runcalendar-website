Course

A Thanksgiving-morning 5K starting on the blue track at the WVU Coliseum, running most of a lap before heading out onto Mon Boulevard, through neighbourhood streets and onto the rail trail for the last couple of miles to finish at the Seneca Center. Mixes track, road and rail-trail surfaces; a kids 800m fun run is also held.
