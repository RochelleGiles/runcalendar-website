Course

Point-to-point races along the Serpent Trail through the South Downs National Park, from Haslemere towards Petersfield. The northern 100k and 75k climb through woodland, glades and heathland over the Wealden Greensand Hills; the southern 50k, 20k and 10k start near Petworth on lower sandy trails and paths with short linking road sections. Coach transport runs from the finish venue to each start.

About

The Serpent Trail Races offer a scenic running experience through the South Downs National Park, featuring routes that traverse ancient woodlands, greensand ridges, and protected heathlands. Runners can choose from multiple distances, exploring the beautiful countryside of southeastern England.
