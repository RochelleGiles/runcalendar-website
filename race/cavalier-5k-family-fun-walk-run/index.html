About

5k Family Fun Run/Walk hosted by the CCES Alumni Association. All CCES alumni, students, parents, and friends are invited to participate in 2nd Annual Cavalier 5K! This Family Fun Walk/Run promises to be a fun event for the entire family! Come out and join us for the race, or be with us to help cheer participants on! All proceeds benefit student financial aid.
