Course

Multi-lap race on a 10 km figure-eight loop starting from Wet Gully Road about 1 km off the Ranfurly–Naseby Road, in New Zealand's highest town at roughly 600 m. Half of each lap runs on gravel forest road, climbing Hoffmans Dam Road and past Coalpit Dam; the other half follows the historic water race and Forest Walk on singletrack. Runners pass the start/finish hub every lap.
