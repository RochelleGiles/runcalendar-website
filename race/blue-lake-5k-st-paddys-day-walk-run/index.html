About

The Blue Lake Township Firefighters' Association hosts its annual St. Patrick's Day 5K run/walk and half-mile fun run for kids 9 and under, starting at 10:00 AM at the Blue Lake Twp Fire Department. All proceeds support the local fire department, with awards including trophies and medals in various divisions.
