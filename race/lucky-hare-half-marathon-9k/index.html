About

The Lucky Hare Half Marathon & 9K is a St. Patrick's Day weekend celebration race along the Great Northern Trail system from Kila to downtown Kalispell, Montana. The half marathon starts at Smith Lake Fishing Access, while the 9K covers the second half of the course, both finishing outside Brannigan's Irish Pub.
