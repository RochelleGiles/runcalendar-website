Course

Start and finish at North Penn VFW Post 676, 2519 Jenkintown Road, Glenside, Pennsylvania. The 5K is two loops on closed roads through Glenside with a few rolling hills; a one-mile fun walk runs alongside it. No aid station detail is published.

About

The annual 5k run/1 mile fun walk is held each April, and is the GGPA's largest single fundraiser to support the July 4th events. All proceeds from your participation in "Keep the Parade Running" will ensure the continuation of the time honored Glenside 4th of July events (parade, fireworks, kids program).
