Course

Both races start and finish at Preston Festival Field on Cunningham Lane. The 5K runs out along Springfield Road to a turnaround at 2.5K and back; the 10K heads north to Route 86, crosses the AuSable River by stone bridge, turns at Bowman Lane and returns via Fox Farm Road and Indian Rock Road. Water is on course at roughly 3K, 5K and 8K.
