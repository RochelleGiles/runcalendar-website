About

Sunshine Horses along with Willow Running will host our Annual Strawberry Shortcake 4K and Open House at our farm at 3721 VerPlank Road, Clay. The race will begin and end at the farm with strawberry shortcakes waiting at the end! There will be vendors, riding demonstrations, food, baskets, horses, and more! Come out and see who we are and learn about our mission while enjoying a day with your family and friends!
