Course

A USATF-certified 5K looping around Big Bear and Little Bear Lakes inside Century Park in Vernon Hills, starting and finishing in the park. It is mostly flat, with small hills and inclines on the southernmost loop.

About

The Loop the Lakes 5K is a USATF certified run/walk event through the picturesque Century Park Arboretum in Vernon Hills. Runners and walkers enjoy one of Lake County's best parks, supporting the Vernon Hills Park District Scholarship Fund.
