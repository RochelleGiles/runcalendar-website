About

The Oisans Trail Tour is a must-experience running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of the French Alps. Runners will test their endurance on a challenging course that winds through stunning mountain landscapes, offering a unique and unforgettable challenge. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an adventure you won't soon forget.
