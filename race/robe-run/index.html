Course

All races start and finish at Foreshore Reserve on Mundy Terrace in Robe, on South Australia's Limestone Coast, over a mix of coastal trail, beach and road including a 3 km stretch along Long Beach. The marathon is a double loop of the half marathon course. Distances run from a 1 km kids dash up to the full marathon.

About

The Robe Run showcases the stunning Limestone Coast of South Australia, offering a range of distances from a full Marathon to a 5km Fun Run. Explore the breathtaking landscapes and coastal trails of this picturesque region as you challenge yourself at this popular running event.
