About

Discover the charming streets of Aix-en-Provence at the annual Aix en Foulées running event. This scenic race winds through the historic city center, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the picturesque architecture and vibrant atmosphere of this beloved French destination. Whether you're a local or visiting from afar, Aix en Foulées promises an unforgettable running experience.
