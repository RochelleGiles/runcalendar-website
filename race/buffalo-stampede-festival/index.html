Course

A three-day sky running festival based at Pioneer Park, Bright in the Victorian High Country. The 100km runs from Bright to the Horn, the high point of Mount Buffalo, with roughly 60km inside the national park; the 42km SkyMarathon descends from Mount Buffalo back to Bright. Shorter races loop from Bright over Mystic, Emily's Spur and Clearspot on mountain and MTB trails.

About

The Buffalo Stampede is an exhilarating SkyRunning event that takes runners to the top of the world in the heart of Australia. Attracting top athletes from around the globe, this prestigious festival challenges participants with stunning mountain vistas where earth and sky converge. Whether a seasoned trail runner or outdoor enthusiast, the Buffalo Stampede offers an unforgettable experience.
