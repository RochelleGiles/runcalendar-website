About

The Iceland Backyard Ultra is a unique trail running event where participants need to complete a 6.7 km loop within an hour, with the last person standing declared the winner. The event encourages runners to enjoy the beautiful nature of Iceland and the camaraderie of fellow runners, with aid stations, facilities, and a lively atmosphere throughout the competition.
