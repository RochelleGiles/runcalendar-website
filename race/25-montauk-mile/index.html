Course

Point-to-point one-mile road race in Montauk, New York, starting a mile out and finishing at Lion's Field on the corner of South Erie and Edgemere Street. The men's mile goes off at 10.30am and the women's mile at 11am, followed by a free kids fun run. No dogs are allowed on the course.

About

In honor of John Conner, the 25 Montauk Mile is an annual running event featuring a men's mile, women's mile, and free kids fun run. The event takes place rain or shine at Lion's Field in Montauk, New York.
