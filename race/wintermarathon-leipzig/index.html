About

The Wintermarathon Leipzig is a must-run event for endurance athletes, taking place in the historic city of Leipzig. Runners will wind through the charming streets and parks, with the chance to take in the city's stunning architecture and vibrant culture along the way. This winter marathon offers a unique challenge for seasoned racers and newcomers alike.
