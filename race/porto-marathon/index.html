About

The Porto Marathon is a must-run event for any serious runner. Held in the historic city of Porto, Portugal, this scenic race winds through the charming streets and over the iconic bridges of this picturesque destination. Runners will be treated to stunning views of the Douro River and the city's stunning architecture as they push themselves to the finish line.
