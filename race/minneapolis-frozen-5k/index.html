Course

An officially timed 5K starting at 8:00am from Lake Nokomis Park on West Lake Nokomis Parkway in Minneapolis. A Dash and Splash option combines the 5K with a plunge into Lake Nokomis. Check-in runs the Friday evening before, or from 7:00am on race morning at the Plunge site.

About

The Frozen 5K is an officially-timed 5K race around Lake Nokomis to kick off a day of Polar Plunging! All participants receive a medal and a race t-shirt.
