About

Run Mate By Core Lean is a unique relay running event that takes participants on a scenic journey around Lake Geneva. Teams of 2 to 9 runners cover approximately 215 kilometers, experiencing the beautiful landscape day and night. The event offers a challenging yet inclusive adventure for runners of all levels, creating unforgettable memories and a festive atmosphere.
