Course

A Thanksgiving morning road race starting and finishing at Lakeshore Community Church on Latta Road in Rochester. The 10K, known as RwG 3.0, is USATF certified and run on open road shoulders; the 5K follows the same roads but is not certified. One water station sits at the halfway mark, and the field is capped at 800 runners.

About

Race with Grace is a 5K and 10K road race held in Rochester, New York, each November, drawing local runners and walkers to the community event.
