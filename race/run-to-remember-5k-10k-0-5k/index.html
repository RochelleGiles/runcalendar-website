About

The Run to Remember 5k/10k/0.5k is hosted and coordinated by Beard vs Beans to raise awareness and donations for the Alzheimer's Association of San Antonio and South Texas. It's a chipped and timed run that is open to all ages and all walks of life, including pets! We host vendors, sponsors, and those who sign up receive a t-shirt and run medal. Runners who place in various age groups will be awarded for their efforts in our run!
