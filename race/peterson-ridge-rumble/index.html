Course

Three distances on the Peterson Ridge and Metolius-Windigo trails near Sisters, mostly single track with some dirt road, and mud or snow possible. All start from a parking area on Hwy 242 west of Sisters and finish with a lap of the track at Sisters Middle School. Aid stations fall every three to seven miles.

About

The Peterson Ridge Rumble is a fun, low-key trail running event utilizing the Peterson Ridge and Metolius-Windigo multi-use trails near Sisters, OR. It is a fundraiser for the Sisters High School Cross Country team and Sisters Trails Alliance, offering three distances: 40 miles, marathon, and 20 miles, with dogs welcome.
