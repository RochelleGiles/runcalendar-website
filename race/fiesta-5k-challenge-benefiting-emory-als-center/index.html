About

The Fiesta 5k Challenge is a chip-timed Cinco de Mayo themed 5K at Cumming City Center in Cumming, Georgia, with a kids fun run and a virtual option alongside it. All proceeds go to the Emory ALS Center. The race is held in memory of Jon Blais, who died of ALS in 2005.
