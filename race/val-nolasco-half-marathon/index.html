Course

Starts at the Kapiolani Park bandstand on Kalakaua Avenue, turning up Monsarrat and Paki to climb over Diamond Head and along Diamond Head Road. Runners continue onto Kalanianaole Highway out to Wailupe Circle before turning back to the park, finishing at the Honolulu Marathon finish line in Kapiolani Park.

About

A half marathon run by the Mid-Pacific Road Runners Club each November in Honolulu, starting and finishing at Kapiolani Park. The course climbs over Diamond Head before an out-and-back along Kalanianaole Highway to Wailupe Circle.
