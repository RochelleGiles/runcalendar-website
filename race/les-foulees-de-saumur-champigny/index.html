About

Explore the scenic vineyards of Saumur-Champigny at the annual Les Foulées De Saumur Champigny running event. This picturesque race takes runners through the heart of this renowned winemaking region, offering stunning views and a unique challenge for both casual and competitive athletes. Whether you're a local or visiting the area, this event is not to be missed by running enthusiasts.
