About

Traprock 50 is a rocky New England trail ultra run on loops of the Metacomet Trail, offering a 17K and a 50K. Half of each loop is technical singletrack, with the remainder on rolling forest road and doubletrack. The 50K covers three loops under a nine-hour cutoff.
