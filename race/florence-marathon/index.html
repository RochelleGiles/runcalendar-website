About

The Florence Marathon is a renowned running event that takes runners on a scenic tour of the historic city of Florence, Italy. Participants can experience the stunning architecture, picturesque landscapes, and vibrant culture as they navigate the challenging 26.2-mile course. This iconic marathon is a must-do for any serious runner seeking an unforgettable European running adventure.
