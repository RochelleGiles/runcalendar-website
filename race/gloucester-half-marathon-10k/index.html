Course

Starts and finishes in Gloucester Park. The route runs through pedestrianised city-centre streets past Gloucester Cathedral, then onto a closed-road circuit through Gloucester and Quedgeley before joining the Gloucester & Sharpness Canal path.

About

The Gloucester Running Festival offers runners a scenic tour of the historic city, with the Half Marathon and 10K events starting and finishing at the picturesque Gloucester Park. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply looking to enjoy the beautiful surroundings, this event is sure to be a highlight of the running calendar for athletes of all abilities.
