Course

Point-to-point course on the Behind the Rocks Trail south of Moab, mixing dirt road, slickrock and sandy washes with technical doubletrack and jeep trail. Start and finish at the Behind the Rocks trailhead, reached by 13 miles of single-lane dirt road. Multiple aid stations; the race is cup-free, so runners carry a reusable container.

About

Because a girls weekend should include an exciting challenge, we present Thelma and Louise Marathon, Half Marathon, and 15K! Bring your best friends to Moab and experience remoteness of Moab's Behind the Rocks area. We've designed this course to be rugged and tough enough to be worthy of the name "Thelma and Louise."
