Course

All races start and finish in Sinks Canyon outside Lander, the corridor into the Wind River Mountains. The 50K is a full mountain run; the 18K climbs hard through meadow and forest singletrack; the 6K meanders along the Middle Fork of the Popo Agie River. A kids 1K out-and-back follows the same river.
