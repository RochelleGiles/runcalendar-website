About

The TRANSMARTINIQUE (10th edition) is a festival of 7 races (3 ultra trails and 4 trails) - a new event called 'Jung' La'. The TRANSMARTINIQUE allows runners to traverse the tropical forest, climb the steepest slopes of the island (Montagne Pelée and Vauclin), pass through food and vegetable plantations, and reach the white sand beaches of Sainte Anne.
