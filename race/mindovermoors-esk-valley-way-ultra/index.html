Course

A point-to-point run from source to sea, starting at Castleton with a 15-mile loop before tracing the River Esk to the coast and finishing at the shoreline. The route crosses moorland, woodland trail with stone steps, river valley and coastal path, following Esk Valley Way signs with marker tape where needed. Five checkpoints with food and drink; no cut-off time.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Esk Valley Way on the MindOverMoors Esk Valley Ultra, a 36-mile endurance challenge that takes runners from the source of the River Esk to the North Sea. This unique ultramarathon in the United Kingdom's picturesque countryside offers a scenic and rewarding test of mental and physical fortitude for seasoned trail runners.
