Course

Loop course at Howard Buford Recreation Area on Mt. Pisgah, starting at the horse arena staging area north of the northern trailhead. The 50K is four loops, the 25K two and the 15K one. Trails are flat in places, often wet or muddy, with steeper summit terrain. Aid stations carry water, energy products and snacks, with a transition station at the start and finish.

About

Expect wet & muddy conditions on the flat sections of Base Loop 1 and Base Loop 2. Steep Summit Loop has a short section on packed gravel road. The rest of the courses cover very well maintained trails of varying trail width and surfaces. Base Loop 1, 2 & Summit Loop all have small creek crossings.
