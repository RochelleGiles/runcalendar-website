Course

Starts and finishes at Area 1 of Lums Pond State Park, using portions of the Little Jersey and Swamp Forest trails. The route mixes single track with wider natural-surface trail and is relatively flat, with the half marathon covering the loop twice. Organisers describe it as suitable for first-time trail runners.

About

A trail half marathon and 10K near Bear, Delaware, run on natural-surface park paths. Both distances share a wooded, rolling course that trades pavement for dirt and roots.
