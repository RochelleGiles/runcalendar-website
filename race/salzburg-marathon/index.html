Course

A city marathon starting on the Staatsbrücke over the Salzach and finishing at Max-Reinhardt-Platz in the old town, run through the historic centre and along the river. A half marathon and a 10K city run start the same morning, with a relay, a women's run, a junior marathon and a breakfast run spread across the festival weekend.

About

The Salzburg Marathon is a must-run event for runners seeking an unforgettable race experience. Winding through the picturesque streets and historic landmarks of Salzburg, Austria, this marathon offers a scenic and challenging course that showcases the city's stunning architecture and natural beauty. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time racer, the Salzburg Marathon promises an exhilarating and memorable running adventure.
