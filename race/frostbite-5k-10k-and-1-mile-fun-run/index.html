Course

Starts and finishes at the Lelia Patterson Center, 1111 Howard Gap Road, Fletcher. The 5K and 10K are hilly road courses with U-turn points on Howard Gap, Jackson, Patty's Chapel and Hutch Mountain roads, which stay open to some traffic; the 10K has two water stations and the 5K one. The flat 1 Mile loops Fletcher Academy including a lap of the track.

About

The Frostbite races are a long-standing winter road event staged by iDaph Events at the Lelia Patterson Center in Fletcher, North Carolina. A hilly 5K and 10K run on open rural roads alongside a flat one-mile fun run, with an afternoon start.
