About

A fun, glamorous, women-focused running event held at Beach Central Park in Galveston, TX, featuring both a half marathon and 5K with festive atmosphere including tiaras, tutus, and a red carpet finish. Participants receive race apparel, finisher medals, tiara, sash, and celebrate empowerment and style.
