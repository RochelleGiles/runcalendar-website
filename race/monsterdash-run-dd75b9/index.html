Course

Starts and finishes at Louisville Community Park, 955 Bella Vista Drive. The 5K is a lollipop loop heading west on wide dirt trails, measuring 3.3 miles, with one water stop. The 10K is a figure-of-eight, west with the 5K then east on a dirt bike path toward Broomfield, with water at miles 1.2, 2.0, 4.5 and 5.0.

About

The MonsterDash Run is known for its fun kids races, costume contest, and post-race festivities with food, drinks, and scary music. Over 60% of runners come in costume to this unique afternoon race.
