Course

A multi-loop waterfront course starting and finishing at the American Veterans Memorial Pier on Shore Road in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn. Runners cover a half-mile out and back followed by three four-mile loops of the Shore Road Promenade. Fluid stations are roughly every mile. Start is 8am.

About

The Go Hard or Go Home Half Marathon, a Sammy Lazhir memorial run slated on the Saturday before the Big Game! This 13.1 mile journey around one of Brooklyn's most iconic waterfront parks will be as challenging as it is beautiful. Be the athlete you need to be! Challenge yourself like the athletes headed to the big game on Sunday and be your own MVP! Earn your Sunday trip to the hot wings table showing off your own victory hardware… your Go Hard or Go Home finisher's medal!
