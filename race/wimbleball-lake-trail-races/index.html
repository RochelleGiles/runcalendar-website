Course

Off-road trail course on the Exmoor shores of Wimbleball Lake, starting and finishing at the lake. The 10K follows almost the same route as the next day's triathlon; the 5K starts and finishes the same way but turns earlier. Rolling terrain with a return leg along the side of the lake. Canicross waves run alongside both distances.

About

Explore the stunning Wimbleball Lake Trail Races near Dulverton, Somerset. This off-road running event offers both 5km and 10km courses, allowing runners of all abilities to experience the beautiful natural scenery and challenging terrain of the UK countryside. Whether you're looking for a scenic training run or a thrilling race day, the Wimbleball Trail Races promise an unforgettable running adventure.
