Course

Point-to-point road half marathon in Cache Valley. It starts seven miles up Blacksmith Fork Canyon at the Hyrum electrical plant and runs down the canyon to finish at Zollinger Park in Providence. Aid stations are placed every three miles and staffed by local volunteers. The race counts toward the USATF Utah Road Racing Circuit.

About

The Top of Utah Half Marathon starts 7 miles up Blacksmith Fork Canyon at the Hyrum Electrical Plant and finishes at Providence Zollinger Park. It is part of the USATF Utah Road Racing Circuit with aid stations every 3 miles, finisher medals, shirts, and post-race prizes.
