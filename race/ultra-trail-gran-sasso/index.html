About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Gran Sasso mountain range in central Italy at the Ultra Trail Gran Sasso, a challenging and scenic ultramarathon event. Runners will traverse breathtaking alpine landscapes, testing their endurance and determination on a course that showcases the region's dramatic peaks and valleys. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or looking to take on your first long-distance challenge, the Ultra Trail Gran Sasso promises an unforgettable adventure.
