Course

Repeated 20.44-mile loops at Robbers Cave State Park, Wilburton, with about 2,549 ft of climb per lap. The going is steep and rocky with some rock scrambles and sections of roadway. The start/finish aid station takes crew and serves hot and cold food, snacks, electrolytes, pickle juice and ice; water-only stops sit at miles 3.6, 6.51 and 13.01.

About

OK Hundred is an ultra race at Robbers Cave State Park near Wilburton, Oklahoma, a park known for its history as an outlaw hideout. Runners choose the 40 mile, 100K, or 100 mile distance.
