Course

A backyard ultra on a looped trail circuit through Cosmeston Lakes Country Park, starting and finishing at the Medieval Village at Cosmeston, Penarth. Runners cover 4.167 miles every hour until one is left standing. Fast underfoot: compact gravel trail, woodland path and tarmac, and mostly flat. Event HQ has hot and cold water, plumbed toilets, charging points and indoor shelter.

About

The Medieval Backyard Ultra challenges runners to a unique endurance test at the scenic Cosmeston Lakes in Wales. Inspired by medieval lore, this "last one standing" event pushes participants to their limits as they loop a 4.167-mile course against the clock, with the last runner left standing crowned the champion. Runners seeking an extraordinary test of their mettle won't want to miss this one-of-a-kind challenge.
