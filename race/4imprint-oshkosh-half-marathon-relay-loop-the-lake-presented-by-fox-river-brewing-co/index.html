Course

Courses follow the Wiouwash Trail and the Oshkosh Riverwalk along the water. The half marathon can be run solo or as a relay; the Loop the Lake four-nautical-mile run/walk takes the Wiouwash Trail, turns onto the Heritage Trail past Rainbow Park and returns to Fox River Brewing Co.

About

The 4imprint Oshkosh Half Marathon, Half Marathon Relay, & Loop the Lake presented by Fox River Brewing Co. is the perfect New Years resolution spring race to train for. There is a distance available for everyone! Each course will take participants along the Wiouwash Trail and Riverwalk for scenic & serene views. Participants will enjoy post-race food & beverage including Fox River Brewing Co. beer to those 21 & older.
