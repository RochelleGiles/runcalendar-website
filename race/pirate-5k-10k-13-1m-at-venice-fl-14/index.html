Course

All distances start on the path behind the Nature Center at Shamrock Park in Venice, on a mix of concrete and asphalt. The 5K and 10K run a 3.11 mile out-and-back once and twice; the half marathon uses a 3.28 mile out-and-back run four times.

About

The Pirate 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is held at Shamrock Park in Venice, FL, featuring out-and-back courses on concrete and asphalt paths behind the Nature Center. The event starts at 8:00 AM with a 3.5-hour time limit and provides finisher medals.
