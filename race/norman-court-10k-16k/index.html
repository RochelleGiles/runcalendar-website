Course

A mixed-terrain race starting and finishing at Norman Court, West Tytherley, over rolling hills and countryside trails near Salisbury. The 16K sets off at 9:00am and the 10K at 9:10am. The route is fully marked, with marshals at key points and water stations on course, and runners are asked to finish inside four hours.

About

The Norman Court 10K & 16K is an exhilarating mixed terrain race set in the picturesque countryside of West Tytherley, Salisbury. Runners can choose between a 10K or 16K course, both of which promise breathtaking views and a challenging yet rewarding experience for athletes of all levels. This event is a must-do for anyone seeking an unforgettable running adventure in the heart of the United Kingdom.
