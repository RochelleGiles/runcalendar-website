About

Discover the stunning natural beauty of Rimouski as you conquer the Marathon de Rimouski. This iconic race takes runners on a scenic tour through the picturesque coastal town, offering breathtaking views of the St. Lawrence River. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time racer, the Marathon de Rimouski promises an unforgettable running experience.
