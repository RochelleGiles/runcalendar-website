Course

The 200-mile race is a loop from Juniper Hall at Dorking linking five national trails - North Downs Way, Vanguard Way, South Downs Way, Wayfarers Walk and St Swithuns Way. The 100-mile race runs point to point from Amberley Museum to Juniper Hall, roughly 84% trail and 16% road, with three checkpoints serving hot meals and drop bags and a 35-hour limit.

About

A winter ultramarathon along the North Downs Way, starting from Dorking near Box Hill. The 100 and 200 mile distances follow chalk ridgeline trail through the North Downs, testing runners through short, cold December days.
