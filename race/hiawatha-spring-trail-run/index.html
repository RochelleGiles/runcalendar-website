Course

An out-and-back on the Route of the Hiawatha rail-trail at Lookout Pass, Idaho, running through towering forest, rugged peaks and historic train tunnels. Spring brings rushing waterfalls, heavy greenery and long mountain views. The event offers 10K, 15K, 25K and 50K distances on the same corridor.

About

The Hiawatha Spring Trail Run follows the Route of the Hiawatha, a former railroad grade carried on high trestles and driven through long dark tunnels between Montana and Idaho. Spring runoff fills the waterfalls beside the trail. Distances range from 10K to 50K on a surface normally ridden by cyclists.
