Course

A point-to-point mountain crossing of the Lake District, starting at the Oddfellows Arms in Caldbeck at 8am and finishing at Cartmel Priory School. The 50-mile route runs via the Helvellyn ridge and the western shore of Windermere, with around 4,000m of ascent over rough fell terrain and river crossings. High sections are not waymarked and require navigation. Three feed stations serve the route.

About

Lakes In A Day is a challenging ultra-marathon that takes runners through the stunning Lake District in the UK. The course covers over 50 miles of rugged terrain, showcasing the region's beautiful lakes, valleys, and fells. Runners will be treated to breathtaking views and a true test of their endurance and determination.
