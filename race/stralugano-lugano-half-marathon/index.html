About

The Stralugano Lugano Half Marathon is a premier running event in the picturesque city of Lugano, Switzerland. The course showcases the stunning lakeside scenery and the charming Old Town, providing a memorable experience for runners of all levels. With a vibrant atmosphere and a strong community spirit, this event has become a must-do for endurance enthusiasts.
