About

Come join us for Greenville's first-ever Queen Anne's Revenge Ultra! The course is a 6.2-mile loop through River Park North, 5 times for the full 50k. Wanting to run with friends? Create a relay team of 2–5 runners and take on the 50k together! Each runner will pass a wristband for easy lap transitions. Not ready for the full 50k? Choose the 20k solo or 20k relay with a friend. Same course as the 50k but only 2 loops around the 6.2-mile course, on your own or with a teammate.
