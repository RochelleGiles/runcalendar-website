Course

A single-loop 5K run entirely inside Buffalo Park, starting and finishing at the park entrance on N Gemini Rd. The route follows the park's plowed path, which is typically snow-covered in late January. Water is available roughly every 1.5 miles and the race is chip-timed.

About

A winter 5K running event held at Buffalo Park in Flagstaff, Arizona, featuring a snowy course on plowed paths. The race offers both in-person and virtual participation options with chip timing, water stations, and awards for various age groups.
