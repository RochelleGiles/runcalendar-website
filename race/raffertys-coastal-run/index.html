About

Raffertys Coastal Run starts and finishes at Raffertys Resort at Cams Wharf on Lake Macquarie, New South Wales. Runners enter 15km, 22km or 36km courses crossing Wallarah National Park, Catherine Hill Bay and Munmorah State Conservation Area on single track, fire trail, rock platforms and beach. A kids fun run is also held.
