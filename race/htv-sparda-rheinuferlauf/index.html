About

The Rheinuferlauf in Duisburg has several hundred runners at the starting line. The field of participants is diverse, as is the range of events. From the everyman's run with 5 km, to the 10-kilometer race, the Volkslauf, to the half marathon with 21.1 km - the range leaves no distance wish unfulfilled.
