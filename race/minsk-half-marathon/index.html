About

The Minsk Half Marathon is a must-run event for runners seeking a scenic and challenging course through the historic city of Minsk. Boasting a route that showcases the city's stunning architecture and vibrant culture, this race offers an unforgettable running experience for participants of all levels. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply looking to enjoy the journey, the Minsk Half Marathon is sure to leave a lasting impression.
