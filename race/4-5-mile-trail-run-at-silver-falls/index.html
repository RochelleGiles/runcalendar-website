Course

A 4.5-mile loop at Silver Falls State Park, starting and finishing at the Howard Creek Horse Camp near Sublimity. The surface is mostly singletrack with a few gravel and paved sections, and there is an aid station at the start/finish of every lap. Runners choose anything from one to seven loops inside a six-hour window.
