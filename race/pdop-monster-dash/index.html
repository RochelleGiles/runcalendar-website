Course

A 5K run and a one-mile family walk from Ian Deutch Memorial Park in Pahrump, Nevada, held during the town's Pumpkin Days festival. Check-in is at 7:00am for an 8:00am start, and the course is shared by runners, walkers, wheelchairs and strollers alike.

About

The Monster Dash is a 5K run and 1-mile family fun walk that has long been part of PDOP Pumpkin Days in Pahrump, Nevada. Proceeds benefit the Pahrump Disability Outreach Program.
