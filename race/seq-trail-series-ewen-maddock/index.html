About

The SEQ Trail Running Series offers an exciting trail running experience across 8 epic locations in Southeast Queensland. Runners can choose between long and short course options, exploring scenic trail routes around Brisbane, Sunshine Coast, and Gold Coast regions. Each event provides a family-friendly atmosphere with coffee, charity BBQ, and a supportive running community.
