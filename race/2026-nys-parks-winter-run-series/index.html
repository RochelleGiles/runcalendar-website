Course

A four-race series of 5Ks on consecutive Sundays at Long Island state parks - Heckscher at East Islip, Robert Moses at Babylon, Sunken Meadow at Kings Park and Jones Beach at Wantagh. Every race is 5K and starts at 11am.

About

The NYS Parks Winter Run Series is a popular annual event featuring 5K races at four different state parks on Long Island. The series includes themed races with fun costume elements, and offers long-sleeve shirts and other giveaways to participants.
