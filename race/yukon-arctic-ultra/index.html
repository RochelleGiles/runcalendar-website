Course

All distances set off from Shipyard's Park in Whitehorse and follow the Yukon Quest trail north in winter. Options are a 32 km Muktuk Challenge, 150 km to Braeburn, 375 km to Pelly Crossing, and 480 km which extends to Pelly Farm on the Pelly River and returns.

About

The Yukon Arctic Ultra is an extreme winter challenge that takes place in the Yukon territory of Canada. Participants can choose from various race distances, including a 100-mile ultramarathon, a 300-mile race, and a 430-mile race. The event is known for its stunning scenery, challenging terrain, and extreme cold temperatures, making it a true test of endurance and resilience.
