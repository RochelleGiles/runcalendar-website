Course

A flat point-to-point ultra from the Riverside Museum in Glasgow to Edinburgh. The route follows the River Kelvin, Sustrans Route 754, the Forth and Clyde Canal and the Union Canal past the Falkirk Wheel and the Antonine Wall, then the Water of Leith into Edinburgh. Staffed checkpoints along the way and live GPS tracking.

About

2026 sees the return of the brilliant Scottish race G2E Ultra starting in Glasgow from the 'Hyper-Modern' Riverside Museum beside the Victorian Tall Ship Glenlee! G2E Ultra has live online GPS tracking from GB Race Tracker, a well marked route, great NEW venues & brilliant support at checkpoints along the journey, the G2E Ultra is a great, flat, fast-paced race, ideal for a personal best at the distance!
