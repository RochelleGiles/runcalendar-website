Course

A women's half marathon run at night, starting and finishing at Redondo Beach King Harbor and tracing the South Bay coast through Torrance and Hermosa Beach at sunset. The course is hilly for a road half, with a notable climb near mile 3 and about 460ft of total gain.

About

Nike's After Dark Tour brings a night half marathon to LA's South Bay, running along the coast through Redondo Beach and Hermosa Beach. Part of a global series created for women runners, it pairs the course with music and crowd support after dark.
