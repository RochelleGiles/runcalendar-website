Course

A Thanksgiving morning road race based at the Onalaska Omni Center on Riders Club Road, Onalaska. A single start line sends off four waves from 8am across a 5 mile run and a 2 mile fun run/walk. Dogs may take part on the 2 mile course only.

About

The Festival Foods Turkey Trot – Coulee Region is a Thanksgiving Day 2-mile and 5-mile race in Onalaska, Wisconsin, part of a Festival Foods-sponsored trot series held in cities across the state each November.
