Course

A chip-timed 5K around the grounds of Horse Creek Winery in Sparks, Georgia, run on grass and cross-country style terrain. Self-serve water stations sit roughly every 1.5 miles. A Sip & Stroll option covers the same course with no requirement to run, and every finisher gets a medal and a mimosa.

About

Matchmakers & Mimosas 5K is a run/walk at Horse Creek Winery in Sparks, Georgia, combining fitness with a post-race mimosa among friends in a winery setting.
