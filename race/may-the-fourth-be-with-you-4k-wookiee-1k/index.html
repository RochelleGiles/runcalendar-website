About

The May the Fourth Be With You 4K and Wookiee 1K is a Star Wars themed evening race in Perrysburg, Ohio, hosted by the Second Sole store at Levis Commons. The chip-timed 4K is run on flat paved roads; the Wookiee 1K is for children aged 11 and under. Overall male and female awards are given and the first 200 finishers receive a gift.
