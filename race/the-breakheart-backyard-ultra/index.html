Course

A 6.7 km (4.167-mile) backyard loop with roughly 560 ft of climb per lap, run almost entirely on mixed trail through a stone quarry and dense woodland. Each lap starts in the quarry, heads out on flat, wide woodland paths to a turnaround, then descends with climbs including BIG's Hill before a sculpture-trail loop back to the quarry finish. HQ has hot and cold water, snacks and a log fire.

About

The Breakheart Backyard Ultra is a grueling last one standing endurance event set in the stunning Cotswold countryside of Gloucestershire, UK. Runners will push their limits as they compete on a challenging loop course, with the last person standing declared the winner. This unique event promises an unforgettable test of mental and physical fortitude for seasoned ultrarunners.
