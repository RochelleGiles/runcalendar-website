Course

A trail race in Groton State Forest, starting from Boulder Beach State Park. Two routes are offered: a 25K of about 15 miles setting off at 9am and a 12K of about 7.2 miles at 9.30am, both on forest trail.

About

Groton Forest Trail Run winds through Groton State Forest, one of Vermont's largest blocks of public forest land, with 12K and 25K options on singletrack and forest roads near Ricker Pond.
