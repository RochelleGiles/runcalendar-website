About

The Pine Beach 5K, hosted by the Ocean Running Club, is a cherished community tradition celebrating the 100th anniversary of Pine Beach with a flat, fast course along the Toms River waterfront. It features a 5K run, 1 mile fun run, and new 5K ruck, promoting fitness and camaraderie with awards and post-race refreshments.
