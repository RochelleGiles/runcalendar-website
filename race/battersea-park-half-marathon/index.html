About

Battersea Park is a favourite among runners wanting to chase a PB! The course is totally flat, with long, wide paths to keep you on track from start to finish. After an initial out and back, the marathon route follows 11 laps of the park (5 laps for Half marathon) before finishing back in the Millennium Arena. The course is also great for spectating with easy access to all areas and dedicated spectator zones.
