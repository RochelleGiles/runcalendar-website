About

Battersea Park is a flat, PB-friendly venue beside the Thames in south-west London, and RunThrough stages 5K, 10K and half marathon races there on wide tarmac paths. The route laps the park from the Millennium Track, with every kilometre flagged and dedicated spectator zones.
