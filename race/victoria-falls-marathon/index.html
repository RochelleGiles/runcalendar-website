About

The Victoria Falls Marathon offers runners a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to experience the breathtaking natural wonder of Victoria Falls. This scenic race winds through the stunning landscapes surrounding one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World, providing a truly memorable challenge for athletes of all levels. Runners will be captivated by the roar of the mighty Zambezi River as they push themselves to the finish line.
