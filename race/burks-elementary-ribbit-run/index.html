About

The Johnnie Lay Burks Elementary Ribbit Run is a school fundraiser held at Johnnie Lay Burks Elementary on North Chase Street in Athens, Georgia. A 5K runs alongside a 1K Tree Frog Trot, a 0.1-mile race for small children and a virtual entry option.
