About

The Torlauf Dachstein attracts runners from all directions to Ramsau am Dachstein. Set against the spectacular backdrop of the Dachstein massif, the event combines the experience of nature, athletic challenge and community, running through impressive mountain landscapes for trail lovers, leisurely runners and team players alike.
