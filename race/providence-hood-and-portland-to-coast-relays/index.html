Course

Point-to-point 196-mile relay from the top of Mount Hood to the Pacific beach at Seaside, run by teams of 8-12 across 36 legs with 35 exchange points. Legs range from steep paved descents to rough gravel; the companion Portland to Coast walk covers 128 miles.

About

The Providence Hood and Portland To Coast Relay is the most popular and largest running and walking relay race in the world, annually drawing participants from over 40 countries and all 50 states. Known as the Mother of All Relays, the event takes 8 to 12 member relay teams 198 miles or 128 miles (from Portland) from the iconic top of Mount Hood to the beaches of the Pacific Ocean.
