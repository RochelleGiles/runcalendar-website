About

The Hamsterley Forest Winter Trail Races offer runners a spectacular winter trail running experience through the largest forest in County Durham. Participants will enjoy undulating trails with breathtaking views of the valley and forest, traversing well-marked footpaths and woodland tracks. The event provides an ideal opportunity for runners to challenge themselves in a beautiful natural setting while working off festive indulgences.
