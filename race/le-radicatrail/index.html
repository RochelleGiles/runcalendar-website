About

Le Radicatrail is a comprehensive trail running event with races ranging from 14km to 122km, taking place over two days in the beautiful Normandy region of France. The event offers a variety of challenges for runners of all levels, from a family-friendly 14km trail to an ultra-distance 122km race. The scenic courses showcase the stunning natural landscapes of the area, providing an unforgettable experience for participants.
