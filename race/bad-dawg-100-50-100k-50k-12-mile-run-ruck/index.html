About

This no-frills ultra near Fort Leonard Wood in the Missouri Ozarks repeats a 10-mile loop of groomed trail and gravel country roads, with about 2,500 feet of climb over its 50-mile distance. Distances range from a 12-mile run/ruck up to 100 miles, and proceeds provide shoes to Crocker school district students.
