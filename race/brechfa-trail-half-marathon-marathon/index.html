Course

A 13 mile lap of Brechfa Forest, run once for the half marathon and twice for the marathon. The going is mostly on forestry tracks rather than technical trail, passing open panoramic views, quiet pine plantation and waterfall-lined deciduous woodland. Start and finish are at Keepers Car Park. The route is fully marked with marshals and several aid stations offering water and snacks.

About

Explore the scenic Brechfa Forest on the Brechfa Trail Half Marathon and Marathon courses. This challenging yet rewarding event takes runners through the heart of the Welsh countryside, just 90 minutes from the city of Cardiff. Challenge yourself on these picturesque trails and experience the natural beauty of this stunning region.
