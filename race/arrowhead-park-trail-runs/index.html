Course

A marked 5K loop on the trails and grounds of Arrowhead Park, taking in lake and wooded sections. The 10K is two laps and the 4-Hour Enduro is as many laps as possible within four hours. Enduro starts at 8:00, 10K at 9:00 and 5K at 9:10. Aid station detail is not published.
