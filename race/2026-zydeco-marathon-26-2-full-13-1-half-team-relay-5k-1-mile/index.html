About

Zydeco Marathon is a two day running event in the heart of Cajun Country. Five distance options are available to accommodate just about everyone. On Saturday we have the one mile fun run and 5K. Sunday is the heart of the weekend with the full marathon, half marathon, and team relay events. The Zydeco Finish Line Festival on Sunday has live music, great food, and plenty of drink options to wrap up your weekend.
