About

Join us for an extraordinary Niagara running experience! All routes are entirely within Canada. Marathon runners will start at Rapidsview Park, before turning left onto the Niagara Parkway, heading towards Niagara Falls. Runners will see the roar of the iconic Horseshoe Falls, before turning around by Fallsview Casino, giving you some of the most amazing views you will find on any racecourse in the world!
