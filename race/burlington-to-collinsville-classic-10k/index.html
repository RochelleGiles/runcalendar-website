Course

A riverside point-to-point 10K from Burlington to Collinsville. The route starts on quiet local roads before joining the multi-use trail alongside the Farmington River, and is flat and shaded for most of its length. It finishes in downtown Collinsville.

About

The Burlington to Collinsville Classic 10K is a point-to-point road race in northwest Connecticut, organized by the Hartford Marathon Foundation. The course runs from the village of Burlington to Collinsville, a historic mill village on the Farmington River.
