About

The 11th Annual Coast to Crest is a flat and fast trail race featuring 10K and 5K distances in Del Mar, CA, with spectacular lagoon views and a downhill finish. The out-and-back courses are well-groomed, 100% dirt trails held in the San Dieguito River Park.
