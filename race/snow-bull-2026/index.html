Course

A clockwise 6.6 mile loop above the Sequatchie Valley near Whitwell, mixing single and double track, grass fields, mountain trails and old mining and logging roads. Each lap climbs about 1,600 ft and is largely runnable with some moderately technical rock and ridge sections. The 21K, 6-hour and 12-hour formats all repeat the same loop.

About

Camp Chet will be hosting Mad Cow Trek Club's Snow Bull 6/12 Hour and 21K Trail Runs. The course is a clockwise 6.6 mile loop of mixed single and double track, grass fields, spectacular views, mountain trails, and mining/logging roads. Each loop will have 1600ft of elevation and is largely runnable with some moderately technical sections.
