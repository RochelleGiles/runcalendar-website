About

MALOKARPATSKÁ VERTIKÁLA is a more or less friendly amateur organized ultratrail, suitable mainly as a training dose for similarly demanding events. The main specificity of the route is the alternation of relatively fast runnable sections with more or less "unrunnable" sections (slope, more demanding terrain), which distinguishes it from most similar events in our country. It is intended mainly for mountain runners, but certainly not only for them, given the time limit.
