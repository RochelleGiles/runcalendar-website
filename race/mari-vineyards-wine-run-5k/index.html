Course

A two-lap cross-country style 5 km run and walk starting and finishing at Mari Vineyards near Traverse City. The course runs through the vines and over grass with areas of uneven footing, and is billed as a fun run rather than a fast one. Runners may skip the second lap through the vines.

About

The Race Starts and Finishes at the Winery and includes vines and grass. This is a mostly cross country style route and has areas of uneven footing. If you're looking for a personal best 5k time this is probably not the best course for you, but if you're looking for the most fun time you've had at a 5k this one is for you!
