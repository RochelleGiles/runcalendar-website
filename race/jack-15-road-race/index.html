Course

A point-to-point 15.2-mile road race from Hooker and Main in White, South Dakota, to the Campanile in Brookings, following county roads. The race starts at 8:30am, with an early start available on request for walkers and slower runners; relay divisions split the route into 2, 3 or 5 legs.

About

An annual 15.2-mile road race from White, SD to Brookings, SD, making it the oldest road race in the Midwest with over 60 years of history. The race offers both individual and relay categories, with free shuttle service to the starting line.
