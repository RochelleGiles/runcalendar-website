Course

A loop course with water stations along the way, starting from East Bank Park at Okatibbee Dam near Collinsville, Mississippi. The 5K sets off at 5:30pm, with a 1 mile fun run for children ten and under alongside.

About

Join WLHS National Honor Society and WLHS Stars & Stripes Club in a 5K run/walk at Lake Okatibbee. Contributions will benefit military families at school and in the community. In addition to the 5K, there will be a shorter distance fun run for those 10 and younger.
