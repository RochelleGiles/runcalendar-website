Course

A single 11 km evening race, starting at 8pm on rue de la Première DFL beside the salle des fêtes in Obernai and finishing on the Remparts Maréchal Foch nearby. The route is a rolling loop around the historic centre, half road and half track, with about 150 m of climb and a notable ascent towards the Mont National at the 6 km mark.

About

The Les Onze Dobernai event is an opportunity to experience the special atmosphere of summer evening races while discovering or rediscovering the town of Obernai. The 11km course features a hilly route around the historic center, with a challenging 1km climb up the Rue de la Paix. Runners of all levels, from those used to flat courses to those who prefer hills, can compete against each other. The race finishes on the city's ramparts, near the town hall, providing a scenic and memorable end to the event.
