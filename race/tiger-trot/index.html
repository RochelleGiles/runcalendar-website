About

The Tiger Trot is an annual 5K run hosted by St. Thomas the Apostle School, held alongside a children's race over a quarter-mile or half-mile loop. The 5K runs on open roads and shared paths, and the event draws a few hundred runners and a large volunteer turnout each year.
