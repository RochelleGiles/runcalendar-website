Course

Four separate courses around Picton and the Queen Charlotte Sound, all finishing at the event hub on the Picton waterfront. The Link runs point-to-point from Anakiwa to Picton; The Snout is an out-and-back from Picton Marina to the end of the Snout; the 10km is an out-and-back between Picton and Waikawa marinas. Routes use native bush tracks and waterfront trails.

About

Marina 2 Marina is a scenic annual running event in the Marlborough Sounds that offers runners and walkers a unique opportunity to explore picturesque trails between Picton, Waikawa, and Anakiwa. The event features stunning panoramic views of the Marlborough Sounds, traversing native bush and waterfront trails with courses suitable for all fitness levels.
