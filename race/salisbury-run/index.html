Course

Out-and-back starting and finishing on grass at Carisbrooke Reserve, Salisbury, heading west along the Little Para River through parklands and under Port Wakefield Road via pedestrian underpasses. The 10 km turns at Strowan Park; the 5 km uses the Carisbrook Classic loop. Surface mixes bitumen and off-road path, mainly flat and shaded. Four water tables about every 2.5 km.

About

Nestled in the picturesque parklands of Salisbury, South Australia, the Salisbury.RUN is a beloved running festival that welcomes runners of all levels. Offering a range of distances to choose from, this family-friendly event promises an unforgettable experience amidst the stunning natural surroundings.
