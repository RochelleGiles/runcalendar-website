About

Discover the 2000 hectares of the Montmorency state forest, dotted with steep trails. Offer yourself a trail run in the Montmorency forest on one of the 8 events ranging from the children's race to 80 km, covering the entire Montmorency massif. Traverse the slopes bordering the massif, alternating between wet valleys and summits overlooking Paris. Support the Mutuelle la Mayotte center to provide quality support and education for children with disabilities.
