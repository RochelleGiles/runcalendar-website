Course

A rolling USATF-certified 10K road course that starts and finishes at Pruett's Market on Taft Highway, Signal Mountain. The route runs along East Brow Road above the valley. It serves as the 2026 RRCA State Championship 10K.

About

The Signal Mountain Pie Run is an annual 10K hosted by the Chattanooga Track Club on Signal Mountain, a plateau town above Chattanooga, Tennessee. Finishers are treated to pie at the race's end, a longstanding local tradition.
