About

The North Downs Ridge 50K Trail Ultra is a challenging and scenic trail running event in the heart of the United Kingdom. Runners will traverse the beautiful North Downs Ridge, taking in stunning views and challenging terrain along the way. This event is a must-do for trail running enthusiasts looking for a true test of their endurance and skills.
