About

The Dubai Marathon is a must-run event for any serious runner. Set against the stunning backdrop of Dubai's iconic skyline, this race takes participants on a scenic tour through the heart of this vibrant city. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply soaking in the electric atmosphere, the Dubai Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
