Dubai Marathon

Quick verdict

One of the fastest marathons in the world, on a flat coastal course: Jumeirah Beach Road, the Burj Al Arab three times and a course record of 2:03:34. About 4,000 run the marathon; 15,000 more run the 10K and the 4K Fun Run behind it. Pick it for a January marathon you can race in the sun.

Course at a glance

6:30Start
6 hCut-off
3 passesBurj Al Arab

Out-and-backs on closed coastal roads. The marathon starts beside the Dubai Police Academy in Umm Suqeim, runs west on King Salman Street to turn by Dubai Media City around 6 km, then comes back past Madinat Jumeirah and the Burj Al Arab onto Jumeirah Beach Road. It turns at Sunset Mall near 19 km, runs a second lap between the Burj Al Arab and Sunset Mall, and finishes in a grandstand on the Academy's parade ground. The 10K runs the Media City out-and-back; the 4K a short one on King Salman Street.

Where you'll feel it

📍The second lap, from 27 km, with the sun up

There is no hill. What you feel is the shape: four U-turns that break your rhythm, and a second lap between the Burj Al Arab and Sunset Mall that you have already run once, now in full sun. You start at about 14°C in the half-dark; by 30 km most runners are near 20.

Best chance to push

The last eight kilometres, from the Sunset Mall turn west along Jumeirah Beach Road: the Burj Al Arab grows at the end of the road, the crowds gather at Madinat Jumeirah and the grandstand waits at the Academy. It is flat all the way home.

Three things to know

  1. Entries are first come, first served, online only, with no ballot and no published closing date. Places cannot be transferred under any circumstances: running on someone else's bib means disqualification and a ban. There are no refunds, even if the race is cancelled.
  2. Collect your bib at The Agenda in Dubai Media City between Wednesday and Saturday; none are handed out on race morning. Take the Metro to Mall of the Emirates, which has opened at five for the race, and walk over the bridge to the Academy. Jumeirah Beach Road is closed: do not drive.
  3. Water every 2.5 km from 5 km, sports drink every 5 km from 15, and toilets every 5 km; the organiser lists no gels, so carry your own. The roads reopen at six hours: slower runners still get a medal and a time, on the footpath.

Race-day weather

Cool at the start, warm by the finish: about 14°C at the gun, into the low twenties by late morning, humid at dawn and drier as the sun climbs. Rain is rare, about one day in eleven in January.

What runners wear

Singlet and shorts from the start, a cap and sunglasses for the second lap, and racing flats: it is smooth tarmac and flat throughout.

Typical finish times

Course records 2:03:34 by Getaneh Molla in 2019, then the fastest debut in history, and 2:16:07 by Tigist Ketema in 2024, also on debut. The 2026 winners ran 2:04:00 and 2:18:31. Behind them about 4,000 ran the marathon and 16,000 the 10K and 4K, from 151 countries.

Worth the trip?

Yes, for a fast, flat January marathon in winter sun with the Burj Al Arab in view three times, in a city built for a long weekend. Abu Dhabi is five weeks earlier and Burj2Burj a week later. It is not a crowd-lined course: the beach road is wide, and the first hour is half-dark.
