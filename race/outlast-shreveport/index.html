Course

A six-hour last-one-standing format at 1North Adventure Trails Park. Runners set out on a 5K trail loop every hour, on the hour, and must finish each lap inside the hour to start the next one. The loop is natural-surface trail with dirt, roots, rocks and elevation change, plus short sections of gravel and pavement. Water and snacks are at the basecamp start/finish; the event is cupless.

About

Runners tackle a 5K loop on the trails at 1North Adventure Trails in Blanchard every hour, on the hour, for six hours. The format rewards steady pacing over raw speed, and anyone who misses the hourly cutoff is done for the day.
