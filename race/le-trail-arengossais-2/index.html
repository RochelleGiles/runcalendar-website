About

Explore the scenic trails of the Arengossian countryside at the Le Trail Arengossais 2. This challenging off-road running event offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region, with a route that winds through forests, fields, and rolling hills. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable adventure.
