Course

Starts and finishes at Glacier Rock Centre in Echo Valley Provincial Park near Fort Qu'Appelle, running the park's named singletrack animal trails - Owl, Eagle, Otter, Raccoon, Raven, Mink, Deer and Badger - along with wide cross-country ski trails, a stepped interpretive trail and a rock garden section. The 13 km carries about 400 m of climb and the 26 km doubles that loop; both have aid near 4 km and 10 km.
