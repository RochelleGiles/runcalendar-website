Course

A 5K run entirely on the ice of frozen Lake Mendota. The course starts off the end of The Edgewater's pier, heads out toward Picnic Point past the Memorial Union, then loops back to finish at The Edgewater. Participants may run, walk, skate, ski or snowshoe, timed or untimed.

About

The Frozen Assets 5K Run/Walk is a unique event where participants run, walk, or skate entirely on frozen Lake Mendota, starting and ending at The Edgewater in Madison. It raises funds for Clean Lakes Alliance to support lake improvement projects, education, and water quality monitoring.
