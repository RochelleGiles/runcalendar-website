About

Trans Aubrac is a captivating trail running event that takes adventurous runners through the stunning landscapes of the Aubrac region in France. Winding through rugged mountains and pastoral valleys, this race offers a challenging yet breathtaking experience for endurance athletes seeking an unforgettable journey. With a rich history and a reputation for stunning scenery, the Trans Aubrac is a must-do event for any trail running enthusiast.
