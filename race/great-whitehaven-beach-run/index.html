Course

Out-and-back on Whitehaven Beach, Whitsunday Island, using roughly 7 km of beach; the half marathon covers two out-and-back laps while the 10 km and 5 km run to their own turnarounds and back. Surface is hard-packed sand throughout. Competitors reach the start by chartered ferry from Port of Airlie or Hamilton Island Marina.

About

Experience the breathtaking beauty of Australia's Whitehaven Beach during the annual Great Whitehaven Beach Run. This unique event invites runners to traverse the pristine white sands and crystal-clear waters of this stunning coastal destination, offering a one-of-a-kind challenge and scenic backdrop for an unforgettable running adventure.
