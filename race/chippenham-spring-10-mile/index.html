Course

A predominantly fast and flat 10 mile road race starting and finishing on Chippenham High Street. The route leaves the town centre onto surrounding country roads before looping back through town. UKA/Run Britain measured and registered, with mile markers throughout and water stations at 3, 5.5 and 6.5 miles.

About

The Chippenham Spring 10 Mile in Wiltshire is one of the fastest 10-mile routes in the South West, offering runners a scenic and challenging course through the rolling countryside. With a reputation for being well-organized and attracting a mix of competitive and recreational runners, this event is a must-do for anyone seeking a springtime running adventure.
