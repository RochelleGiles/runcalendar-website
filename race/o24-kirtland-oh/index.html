Course

A fixed-time 24 hour trail race on a USATF-certified 1.0024 mile loop at Lake Metroparks Chapin Forest Reservation, about 30 minutes east of Cleveland. The loop is 93 percent crushed limestone and dirt and 7 percent asphalt, rolling with one main hill. Runners may cover any distance or time up to 24 hours.

About

O24 is a 24-hour fixed-time trail race at Lake Metroparks Chapin Forest Reservation in Kirtland, Ohio. Runners repeat a USATF-certified one-mile loop of crushed limestone, dirt and a short asphalt section for as long as they choose, up to 24 hours. Finishers of 100 miles or more receive a belt buckle.
