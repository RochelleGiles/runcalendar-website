Course

A single 10K on scenic trails through the Loseley Park estate near Guildford, winding across the grounds and neighbouring trails with panoramic views. Chip-timed and licensed under UK Athletics rules, with a minimum age of 16. All finishers receive a bespoke medal.

About

A British Landmark Events 10K around the grounds of Loseley Park, the Elizabethan estate near Guildford known for its walled garden and deer park. The course crosses parkland within sight of the historic house.
