About

The Trans Augrabies Stage Race is a multi-day trail run based in Augrabies, Northern Cape, near the granite gorge of Augrabies Falls on the Orange River. Runners can enter the full two-stage event or either stage individually, crossing semi-desert terrain typical of the Northern Cape.
