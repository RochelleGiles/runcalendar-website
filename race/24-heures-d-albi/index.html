About

The 24 Heures D'Albi is a captivating endurance challenge set against the picturesque backdrop of the historic city of Albi. Runners have 24 hours to complete as many laps as possible on a scenic course, pushing their limits and soaking in the vibrant atmosphere of this unique event. With a rich history and a reputation for camaraderie, the 24 Heures D'Albi offers an unforgettable experience for ultrarunners seeking a true test of their mettle.
