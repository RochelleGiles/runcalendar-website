About

The Buhl Fun Run, also known as the Sagebrush Days Run and Walk, will be held Independence Day, July 4 at 8 AM in Buhl. The race starts and ends at the Furniture Room parking lot located at 1104 Main Street. Registration begins at 7AM for the 10K run, 5K run and 5K walk. Awards are given to the top three males and females, the male and female in each age group and the overall winners.
