About

The Bilbao Night Running Fest is a thrilling after-dark running event that takes participants on a scenic tour through the historic streets of Bilbao, Spain. Runners will be treated to stunning views of the city's iconic architecture and landmarks as they challenge themselves under the glow of the night sky. This unique running experience is not to be missed by adventurous athletes seeking an unforgettable athletic challenge.
