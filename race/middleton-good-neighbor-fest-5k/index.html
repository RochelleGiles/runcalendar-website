Course

A 5K run/walk that wanders through the Pheasant Branch Conservancy in Middleton. Race-day registration and packet pickup are at Middleton High School, 2100 Bristol Street. A separate 5K walk and a kids 5K for ages 12 and under are held alongside the run.

About

The Middleton Good Neighbor Fest 5K is a run/walk held during Middleton, Wisconsin's Good Neighbor Festival, a community celebration on Bristol Street with live music, food, and family activities following the race.
