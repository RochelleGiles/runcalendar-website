Course

Starts at sunrise from the Harlequin Club in Groenkloof, Pretoria, with the longer distances setting off first at 06:03 and all routes finishing back at the club. Four distances are offered: 32km, 21.1km, 10km and 5km, over hilly terrain often used as a Comrades and Two Oceans tune-up.

About

The Sunrise Monster is a road race hosted by Harlequin Harriers, starting at Groenkloof Nature Reserve in Pretoria and taking in the area's hilly terrain across distances from 5km to 32km.
