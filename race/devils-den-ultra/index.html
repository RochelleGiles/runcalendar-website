Course

A 6.4-mile loop on Wright's Mountain in Bradford, climbing steeply to the peak at the start then dropping back down through woods to the start/finish. Footing ranges from easily runnable to steep, rocky and rooty, with one road crossing. Timed 30-hour and 12-hour formats run alongside a single-loop 6-miler.
