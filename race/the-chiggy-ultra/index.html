About

The Chiggy Ultra runs through Cape Chignecto Provincial Park near Advocate Harbour, Nova Scotia, within the Cliffs of Fundy UNESCO Global Geopark on the Bay of Fundy. Loop distances range from a 9K to a gruelling 153K.
