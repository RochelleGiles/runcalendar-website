About

The Chamber 5K on the Runway is a 5K on the Vero Beach Regional Airport taxiway and runway beginning at 5:00 PM. This professionally chip-timed event will offer a unique race opportunity on a real airport runway making it a perfect fit for runners, walkers and families at all fitness levels. The course is flat and completely paved making it stroller and wheelchair friendly.
