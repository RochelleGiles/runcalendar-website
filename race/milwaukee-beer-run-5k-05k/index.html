Course

A chip-timed 5k along the Oakleaf Trail, starting and finishing at the Bavarian Bierhaus Oktoberfest celebration on West Lexington Boulevard in Glendale. A 54-yard .05k is also run. Entry includes admission to the Oktoberfest at Heidelberg Park.

About

Join us during the final weekend of Milwaukee's Original Oktoberfest Celebration at The Bavarian Bierhaus. Entry to the festival on October 4 is included with your registration! Celebrate your race in true Oktoberfest style.
