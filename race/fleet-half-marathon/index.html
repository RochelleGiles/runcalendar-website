About

The Fleet Half Marathon in the United Kingdom is the ideal springtime challenge for marathon trainees and recreational runners alike. Renowned for its scenic rural course and supportive community atmosphere, this event provides the perfect opportunity to test your endurance and prepare for bigger races on the horizon.
