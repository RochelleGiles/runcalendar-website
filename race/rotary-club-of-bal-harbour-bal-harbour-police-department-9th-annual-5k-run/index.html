A single 5K run/walk at Haulover Park on Collins Avenue, starting at 7:30 a.m. The race is chip-timed, with finisher medals and shirts. Neither the village page nor the entry page describes course shape, surface or aid stations.

About

Join us for a meaningful morning at Haulover Park as we honor two incredible leaders through this community 5K run/walk. Participants will receive chip timing, a finisher medal, and an event shirt. Awards will be given to the top finishers.
