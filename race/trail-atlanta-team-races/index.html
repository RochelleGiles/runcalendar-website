Course

An overnight team trail relay at Georgia International Horse Park in Conyers, a venue from the 1996 Olympics. Teams loop smooth singletrack through Georgia pine forest and over bridge-covered streams alongside the Yellow River, with roughly 360 feet of gain per loop. Standard teams of eight cover 126.4 miles, with ultra and sprint team options alongside.
