About

The Karratha edition of the Chevron City to Surf for genU offers 4km and 12km courses starting and finishing at Searipple Park in Bulgarra. It runs as part of the Western Australian City to Surf series, which raises funds for disability services provider genU. Both distances set off together at 8:00am.
