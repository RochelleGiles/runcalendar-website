About

The Bord na Móna Tullamore Half Marathon is recognised as one of the best half marathons in Ireland, combining prestige with a warm, community-focused atmosphere. Its scenic route through Mucklagh, Killurin and Cloonagh mixes flat stretches with gently rolling hills.
