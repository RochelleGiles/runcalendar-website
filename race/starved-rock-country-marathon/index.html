Course

The marathon follows the Illinois River, Ottawa neighbourhoods and rolling terrain around Starved Rock State Park, with 16 aid stations. The half marathon starts inside the state park and is described as flat and fast. The 10K is an out-and-back on Route 71 along the river; the 5K follows part of the marathon route and finishes by the river.

About

The Starved Rock Country Marathon, Half-Marathon, 10K, and 5k are a "must-do" event for runners in central Illinois and beyond. These races blend challenging courses with awe-inspiring scenery, making them an annual favorite in the running community.
