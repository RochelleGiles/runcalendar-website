About

The Trail du Soldat de la Marne is an annual trail running event that takes place across multiple communes including Barcy, Chambry, Crégy les Meaux, Meaux, and Varreddes. The event offers multiple race distances and includes both running and walking options, making it accessible to runners and walkers of different skill levels.
