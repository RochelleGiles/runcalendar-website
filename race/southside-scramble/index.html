Course

A 10-mile trail loop through Southside Park in Atlanta, wheel-measured and using every trail in the park. Singletrack, fast descents, gravel-strewn stream crossings, powerline climbs, rock hazards and marsh sections run through pine and hardwood forest. The race starts at 8 a.m. from the north end of the car park.

About

Atlanta's only intown trail race, hosted at Southside Park, the city's sole trail system and one of its best-kept secrets. Runners will enjoy looping singletrack, roller coaster descents, gravel-strewn stream crossings, powerline climbs, rock hazards, marsh passings and wildlife sightings, right in their back yards. This 10-mile course through pine- and hardwood-shaded forests will leave them doubting they are inside the Perimeter. All proceeds from the race will benefit trail building and stewardship organization MTB Atlanta.
