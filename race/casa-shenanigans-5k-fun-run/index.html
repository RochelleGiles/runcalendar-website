About

Join us for the 9th Annual CASA Shenanigans 5K Fun Run, a family-friendly event dedicated to raising awareness and critical funds for children in foster care in Bell and Coryell Counties. Whether you walk, jog, or run, this event is for all ages and fitness levels—and every step makes a difference.
