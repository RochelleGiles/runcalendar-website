Course

Run almost entirely on gravel roads from Eagle Bluff Resort northeast of Tahlequah, Oklahoma, with views over the Illinois River and the J.T. Nickel Preserve. The ground is rolling and somewhat hilly, each climb paying back with a long descent. 100 miles, 100K, 50K, 25K and a 10-plus-mile option are offered, with aid stations staffed by experienced ultrarunners.

About

The Pumpkin Holler Hunnerd is a trail ultra through the wooded hills and hollows near Tahlequah, Oklahoma, in the heart of the Cherokee Nation. Distances range from 10 miles up to a full 100 miles, drawing ultrarunners from across the region each October.
