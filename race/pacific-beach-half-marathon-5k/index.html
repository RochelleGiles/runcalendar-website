Course

A two-day beach weekend in San Diego. The Sunday half marathon is point-to-point from Crown Point Park in Mission Bay to Thomas Avenue at the Pacific Beach Boardwalk, flat and fast on closed streets and sidewalk past SeaWorld, Hospitality Point and Mission Beach, with no sand. The Saturday 5K starts and finishes near 700 Thomas Avenue and mixes about 1.75 miles of sand with 1.35 miles of boardwalk.

About

The Pacific Beach Half Marathon & 5K runs through the Pacific Beach neighborhood of San Diego, taking in the coastline and beachfront boardwalk. A Kids 1K is also held on race weekend.
