About

The Big One is a 5K held on Super Bowl Sunday at Keller Town Hall in Keller, Texas, run on the town's trail network. A 1-mile untimed option and a free kids dash are offered alongside it. Finishers receive a medal, with extra medals for the top four in each age group.
