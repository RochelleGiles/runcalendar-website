Course

A looped singletrack course on the Magnificent Seven trail system north of Moab, starting and finishing in the Gemini Bridges parking area about 10 miles out of town. Runners cross technical slickrock and rocky climbs via the Gemini Bridges, Bride Canyon and Bull Run trails, with views to Arches National Park and the La Sal Mountains. Cupless aid stations at start/finish and on course.

About

The Dead Horse Ultra, from Mad Moose Events, runs on desert trails near Dead Horse Point State Park outside Moab, Utah, overlooking the Colorado River gorge. Distances range from 15K to 50 miles across red-rock terrain.
