Course

Flat, fast course on paved streets, starting and finishing at Two Rivers High School. The route runs around Point Beach State Forest and through Neshotah Park along the Lake Michigan shoreline before returning to the start. Aid stations with water and sports drink at miles 2, 4, 6 and 8. Relay legs are 3.96, 2.31 and 3.73 miles.

About

The Aurora Health Care Two Rivers 10-Mile course is flat, fast, scenic, and USATF certified. The race starts in front of Two Rivers High School, proceeds on paved streets surrounding Point Beach State Forest and includes a beautiful stretch through Neshotah Park and along the Lake Michigan shoreline.
