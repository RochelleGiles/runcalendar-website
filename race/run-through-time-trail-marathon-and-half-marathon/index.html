Course

Trail courses across the Arkansas Hills Trail System above Salida, on BLM and Forest Service land plus local roads, mixing dirt and jeep road with narrow, hilly single track between 7,000 and 9,000 feet. The start sits about 200 metres from the finish at the Salida Scout Hut on East Sackett Avenue, across the F Street bridge and railroad tracks. Foot travel only, with no crews or pacers.

About

The 21st Annual Run Through Time is a trail marathon and half marathon held in Salida, Colorado, featuring challenging courses on dirt roads and single-track trails with elevations ranging from 7,000-9,000 feet. The race takes place on the Arkansas Hills Trail System with fantastic views of Salida and the Arkansas Valley.
