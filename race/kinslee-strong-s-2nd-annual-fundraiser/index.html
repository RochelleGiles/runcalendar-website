About

Kinslee Strong is a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting children and families navigating the challenges of Pediatric Congenital Heart Defects (CHD). Our mission is to provide compassionate care, practical resources, and a strong community network to families facing the emotional, financial, and medical hurdles that come with CHD, while also supporting research and advancing treatments to improve outcomes for children everywhere.
