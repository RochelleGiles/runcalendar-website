Course

About

The SARR Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving Day tradition hosted by the San Antonio Road Runners, with 2-mile and 4-mile options plus a Kids' Pumpkin Dash. Now in its fifth decade, it is one of San Antonio's longest-running holiday races.
