About

Explore the lush landscapes of Wimbledon Common during the Dash For The Splash 10K, an exhilarating all-terrain race that takes runners on a scenic journey through this picturesque London park. Promising a challenging yet rewarding experience, this event invites runners of all levels to embrace the great outdoors and experience the thrill of an off-road 10K adventure.
