Course

Trail loops in Copper Breaks State Park near Quanah, starting and finishing at the Group Pavilion and picnic area. Footing varies from wide grass, rock and sandstone to singletrack dirt through the park trails and Bull Canyon, over slightly hilly country — about 2,500 feet of climb for the 50 mile. Aid stations number three on the 20K, seven on the 54K and nine on the 50 mile.

About

Chupacabra 50 is a trail ultra at Copper Breaks State Park near Quanah, Texas, crossing rugged canyon and badlands terrain. Distances include the 20K, 50 mile, and 54K.
