Course

All distances start and finish at Camas Meadows Golf Course on the western shore of Lacamas Lake, about 20 minutes from Portland. Surfaces mix asphalt, roadway and packed gravel or dirt. The half is largely flat but climbs steeply between miles 4.5 and 6.5 around Round Lake; the 15K, 10K and 5K follow the Lacamas Creek and lake trails over rolling to flat ground. Half and 15K have a 4.5-hour limit.

About

The cutest and most scenic Easter weekend race of the spring! Your favorite Spring-themed run/walk takes you along the banks of beautiful Lacamas Lake and Creek and the paths at Camas Meadows Golf Course in Camas, Washington. Located just across the River about 20 minutes from Portland, you'll love this tranquil, rare lakeside course and spring celebration!
