Course

Point-to-point races along the Ouse Valley Way through Huntingdonshire, on a mix of grass, gravel and tarmac paths. The marathon starts at Eynesbury at 09:30 with just 13m of elevation; the half starts at Godmanchester at 10:30 with 9m. Both finish at Earith.

Marathon42.2 km

About

A marathon and half marathon along the Ouse Valley Way from St Ives, Cambridgeshire, run by the Great Ouse Valley Trust and BRJ Run & Tri. The route follows the river valley through flat fenland countryside.
