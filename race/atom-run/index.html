Course

A 4km loop starting and finishing at the King Baudouin Stadium in Laeken, passing the Victor Boin training stadium, running under the Atomium and then through the hilly, green Laeken Park. The surface mixes running track, asphalt and wooded trail. The Kids race stays inside a secure loop between the two stadiums.

About

The Atom Run is a running event that supports a local charity in Laeken, Belgium. The event has been organized for over 40 years and helps provide material and moral assistance to those in need in the Laeken community. A portion of the event's profits are donated to the Laeken Entraide Center, which distributes food, clothing, and furniture to approximately 380 people per week.
