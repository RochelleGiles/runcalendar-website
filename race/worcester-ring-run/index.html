Course

A clockwise loop from Droitwich Rugby Club, following the Droitwich Canal onto the Worcester & Birmingham Canal down to Diglis, over Diglis Bridge, through Pitchcroft at Worcester Racecourse and back north along the canal. Mostly traffic-free towpath with a few minor tarmac lanes; three control points with water, drinks and food.
