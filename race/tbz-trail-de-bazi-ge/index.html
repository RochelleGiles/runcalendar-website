About

TBZ Trail de Baziege is a trail running event in Baziege, near Toulouse in Haute-Garonne. Its 'TBZ Survivor' format is a multi-lap race on an 8.2km loop mixing road and trail, starting from the town's zoo, with no aid stations on course.
