Course

Races start and finish at the Fire Station on Fifth and Commercial Streets, one block north of the town square in Stromsburg. The course is level and entirely on pavement. The 10K and 5K start at 8:00am and the 1-Mile Fun Run at 8:05am.

About

Stromsburg's Swedish Festival will sponsor a 10K (6.2miles) Race and 5K (3.1 miles) Race. Also available is a One-Mile Fun Run. The course is all level one and is entirely on pavement, beginning at the Fire Station, Fifth and Commercial Streets, one block north of the northeast corner of the town square, ending at the same place.
