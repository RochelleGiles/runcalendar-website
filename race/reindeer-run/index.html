About

Experience the magic of the Reindeer Run as you wind through the picturesque Patawolonga Creek in Holdfast Bay, Australia. This festive 5K event offers a scenic course perfect for runners of all levels, with the chance to spot wildlife and capture stunning views along the way. Join fellow running enthusiasts for a truly memorable holiday-themed adventure.
