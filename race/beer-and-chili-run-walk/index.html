Course

The 5K and 10K start together from the bridge on Bridge Street, Newport, and run out into the countryside of the Kuyahoora Valley. A water station sits at the 5K turnaround, and the post-run gathering is at the Masonic Temple in Newport.

About

The Beer and Chili Run/Walk, hosted by the Kuyahoora Kicker Running Club, is now past its 42nd running. The brisk run through the Kuyahoora Valley countryside is followed by a spirited post-run party celebrating running and friendship.
