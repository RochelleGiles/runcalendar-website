Course

A 241.8 mile point-to-point loop through the Utah desert with 31,892 ft of gain, crossing canyons, slickrock and two mountain ranges within sight of Canyonlands and Arches. Fifteen full aid stations, five of them sleep stations, and a 112-hour cutoff. Surfaces run from rocky desert trail to slickrock and forest road.

About

The Moab 240 is a 240-mile ultramarathon that loops through the canyon and mountain terrain around Moab, Utah, crossing the La Sal Mountains, Behind the Rocks, and sections near Canyonlands and Arches National Parks. It is one of the longest annual trail races in the country.
