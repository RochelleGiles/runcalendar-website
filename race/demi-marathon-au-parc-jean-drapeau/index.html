Course

Run inside Parc Jean-Drapeau on the islands in the St Lawrence off downtown Montreal, using the paved Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve motor racing track. Start and finish are at Espace 67 by the Jean-Drapeau metro. Refuelling stations with water and electrolytes are placed around the course and at the finish, with timing mats for splits.

About

A half marathon in Parc Jean-Drapeau, on Île Sainte-Hélène and Île Notre-Dame in the St. Lawrence River off Montreal, home to the former Expo 67 site and the Circuit Gilles Villeneuve F1 track. The event offers 5K, 10K, and half marathon distances.
