Course

A certified 5km loop through Commonwealth Park and Henry Rolland Park on the northern foreshore of Lake Burley Griffin, starting and finishing at Rond Terrace West near the southern end of Anzac Parade. The surface is a mix of bitumen path and sealed red gravel. Running starts at 5pm Saturday and closes at 8am Sunday; 20 laps for 100km and 10 for 50km. Aid is at the start/finish and at the Acton West Beach turnaround.

About

The Sri Chinmoy 100km Road Race in Canberra's picturesque Commonwealth Park offers runners a unique challenge and breathtaking views. Traversing the city's scenic streets and trails, this event celebrates the human spirit with a focus on personal growth and achievement. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking your first 100km, this race promises an unforgettable journey.
