Course

Starts at Booths Winery and runs out through the Warby Ranges and Warby-Ovens National Park on a mix of fire trail and single track. Five options from a 2K kids' run to a 50K ultra; the 25K and 50K take in the Kwat Kwat lookout. Climbing ranges from about 180m on the 6K to 1,600m on the 50K.

About

The Kwat Kwat Trail Run is held in the Warby Ranges of north-east Victoria, starting from Booths Winery. Courses run on fire trail and single track through Warby-Ovens National Park, with five options from a 2K kids' run to a 50K ultra. The longer races climb to the Kwat Kwat lookout.
