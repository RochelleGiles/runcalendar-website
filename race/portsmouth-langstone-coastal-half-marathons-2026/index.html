Course

A two-day weekend of half marathons on the Hampshire coast. Saturday's Langstone Harbour Half starts at the Ferryboat Inn on Hayling Island and follows muddy harbour trails to finish at Eastney Point. Sunday's Portsmouth Coastal Half is a fast, flat promenade loop around the eastern edge of Portsea Island, starting and finishing at South Parade Pier in Southsea.

About

A coastal running weekend in Hampshire pairing the Langstone Harbour Half Marathon on the Saturday with the Portsmouth Coastal Half Marathon on the Sunday. Runners can enter either race on its own or take on both as a weekend double, earning a bonus medal for completing the pair.
