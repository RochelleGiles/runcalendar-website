About

A 5K run hosted in partnership with the Michigan Department of Natural Resources, the Detroit Auto Show, and the Friends of the Outdoor Adventure Center, designed for all paces and ages. The course starts and finishes near Huntington Place along the Detroit Riverfront, with proceeds supporting new exhibits at the Outdoor Adventure Center.
