Course

A 10-mile and a 7km trail run in Carolina North Forest, starting from Municipal Drive in Chapel Hill. The courses are run on the forest's singletrack, with marked alternatives on the gravel roads if singletrack is closed. A self-timed window on the marked courses follows race day.

About

The Philosopher's Way Trail Runs features 7K and 10-mile races on the singletrack trails of Carolina North Forest in Chapel Hill, North Carolina. Participants can choose an in-person race or a self-timed option in the days that follow.
