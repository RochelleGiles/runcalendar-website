Course

About

5k Run/Walk Be a Champion for Local Kids! Join us for the inaugural Boys & Girls Club 5K, presented by Fleet Feet Treasure Valley, on Saturday, September 6th! Lace up your shoes and make a difference in the lives of young people right here in the Treasure Valley. Every stride you take in this community-focused 5K supports the vital programs of the Boys & Girls Club of Canyon County, empowering them to reach their full potential.
