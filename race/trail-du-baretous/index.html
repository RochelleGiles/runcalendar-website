About

With its magnificent autumn colors, the lower Baretous Valley will reveal all its riches in a pastoral environment. Tracks, streams, deep forests, steep paths, playful single tracks: everything is gathered for varied courses and balanced elevation gains. As usual, the meal that will be served to you (by reserving it during your registration) will benefit the Vaincre la Mucoviscidose association. And of course, the organization thanks to the municipality of Arette will support the fight against ALS.
