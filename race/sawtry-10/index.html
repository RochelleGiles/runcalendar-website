Course

A single loop starting and finishing at Sawtry Village Academy. Runners leave the school grounds, pass through the village and head out on quiet, gently undulating country roads past Hamerton Zoo and Aversley and Archers Woods before returning. Roads are open to traffic, with about 360ft of climb.

About

The Sawtry 10 is a scenic 10-mile road race through the picturesque rolling countryside of Cambridgeshire, UK. Runners will enjoy the tranquility of quiet country lanes as they take in the beautiful natural landscapes. This event is a must for those seeking a challenging yet rewarding running experience.
