About

Explore the stunning French Alps at the Trail du Galibier - Thabor, a challenging and scenic trail running event. Traverse the rugged terrain between the majestic peaks of the Galibier and Thabor mountain ranges, taking in breathtaking vistas at every turn. This unique race offers a true test of endurance and a chance to connect with nature in an unforgettable setting.
