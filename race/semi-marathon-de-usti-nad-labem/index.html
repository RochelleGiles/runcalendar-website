About

The Mattoni Ústí nad Labem Running festival is a popular annual running event in the city of Ústí nad Labem, Czech Republic. The event features a scenic half marathon course along the Elbe River, offering runners a chance to experience the beautiful natural surroundings of the region. The festival atmosphere and enthusiastic local support make this a must-do event for runners in the Czech Republic.
