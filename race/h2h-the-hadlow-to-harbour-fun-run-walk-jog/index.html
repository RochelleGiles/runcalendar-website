Course

Two point-to-point courses both finishing at Caroline Bay, Timaru. The 10 km starts on Hadlow Road at 9:30am and the 3 km from the Countdown car park at 9:45am. Toilets, water stations and St John staff are placed along the route. Free buses run from Caroline Bay to the Hadlow start between 8:00 and 8:15am.

About

Runners and walkers of all abilities welcome. Choose from either 3km or 10km, set your own challenges, party in fancy dress or walk your dog. Be in for prizes! Join us for a fun, family-friendly day out!
