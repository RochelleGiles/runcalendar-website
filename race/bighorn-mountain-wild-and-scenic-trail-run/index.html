Course

A remote out-and-back through the Little Bighorn and Tongue River country of the Bighorn National Forest, offered at 100 miles, 52, 32 and 18. The 100 mile covers 76 miles of technical singletrack, 16 miles of rugged two-track jeep road and 8 miles of gravel, with over 20,500 ft of ascent and a 35-hour limit. Expect steep climbs, hard descents and deep mud.

About

The Bighorn Mountain Wild and Scenic Trail Run is a point-to-point ultra through the Bighorn National Forest in Wyoming, following high alpine ridgelines and stream crossings. Distances range from 18 to 100 miles.
