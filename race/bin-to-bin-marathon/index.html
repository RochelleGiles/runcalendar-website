Course

Point-to-point between two CBH grain bins in the Western Australian wheatbelt, from Mount Walker to Narembeen. The marathon can be run solo, as a duo of 21.1 km each or as a four-person team of 10.5 km each; the half marathon covers the second half of the route. No surface or aid station detail is published.

About

Traverse the picturesque Eastern Wheatbelt of Australia in the Bin to Bin Marathon, running from the Mt Walker to Narembeen CBH grain silos. This unique event challenges runners to conquer the rolling hills and rural landscapes of this remote region, offering a one-of-a-kind experience for adventurous athletes.
