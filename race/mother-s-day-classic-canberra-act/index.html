Course

Start and finish at Lennox Gardens, Acton, Canberra. The course passes under Commonwealth Avenue Bridge towards Kings Avenue before looping back. The 10 km continues across Kings Avenue Bridge past Rond Terrace, loops Commonwealth Park and returns. Aid stations at about 3 km on the 5 km course and at 3 km and 7 km on the 10 km.

About

The Mother's Day Classic is a run/walk event held to support and fundraise for breast cancer and ovarian cancer research. Participants can choose to support one or both research areas while participating in a community fitness event.
