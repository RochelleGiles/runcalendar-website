About

This 5K and one-mile fun run start and finish at Peaceful Side Social Brewery and Craft Kitchen in Townsend, Tennessee, alongside the town Bigfoot Festival. The looped route mixes backroads and greenway. Entry numbers are capped because merchandise is ordered in advance.
