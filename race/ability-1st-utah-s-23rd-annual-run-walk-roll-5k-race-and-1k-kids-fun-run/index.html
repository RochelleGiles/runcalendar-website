Course

The 10K, 5K and Kids 1K all start from 1455 West 820 North in Provo, with course maps published online. The 10K sets off at 8:00 a.m., the 5K at 8:30 a.m. and the kids' run at 10:30 a.m. Runners, walkers, wheelchair users and power mobility users are all accommodated.

About

Join us in celebration of our 25th year of serving individuals with disabilities! We want you to be a part of our 23rd Annual Run, Walk, or Roll Race. This is an amazing opportunity for everyone, of every age and ability, to participate in a race that will benefit people with disabilities.
