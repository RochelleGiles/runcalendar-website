Course

A flat loop around Bedok Reservoir, starting and finishing at HomeTeamNS Bedok Reservoir in eastern Singapore. The 4 km covers one lap of the reservoir and the 8 km two laps, and a four-person relay splits 8 km into 2 km legs. The organiser calls the course flat and accessible, and the event starts mid-afternoon. Complimentary entries are offered for participants with special needs and their caregivers.

About

Run For Inclusion is Runninghour's flagship event pairing sighted volunteers with visually impaired runners and other athletes with disabilities. Marking over a decade in Singapore, it offers a 4km run and an 8km relay open to all.
