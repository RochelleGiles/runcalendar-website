About

Sierra Cazorla Trails is an annual trail running event that takes place in the beautiful Sierra de Cazorla, Segura y Las Villas Natural Park in Andalusia, Spain. The event features several race distances, including an Ultra, Marathon, Skyrace, and Short Trail, allowing runners of all levels to participate.
