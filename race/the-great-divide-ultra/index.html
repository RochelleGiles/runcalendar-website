Course

A looped trail course held entirely inside Mueller State Park, Colorado, on smooth single and double track with no technical footing. All distances start and finish at the Livery by the main parking area and share the same loop system; the 100 mile is six 14.8 mile loops plus a 10.5 mile loop. The course sits between roughly 9,190 and 9,740 feet, with crew-accessible aid stations every 4 to 6 miles and drop bag support.

About

Experience the spring-fed meadows, massive rocks of Pikes Peak granite, and sweeping views of Colorado's most spectacular mountains in the Great Divide Ultras. This Colorado early season 100m, 100k, 50k and 25k trail ultra takes place entirely on the beautiful rolling meadows of Mueller State Park. The beautiful looped course is 100% smooth single/double-track trail.
