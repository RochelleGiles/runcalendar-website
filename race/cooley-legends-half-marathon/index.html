About

The Cooley Legends Half Marathon is an off-road trail race on the Cooley Peninsula in Carlingford, Co. Louth, named for the mythical hero Cúchulainn. Runners tackle the peninsula's hills with views over Carlingford Lough and the Cooley Mountains.
