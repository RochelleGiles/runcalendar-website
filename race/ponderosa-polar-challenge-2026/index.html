Course

Three lap courses of 1, 2 and 3 miles at Zion Ponderosa Ranch Resort, run in either direction until the entrant reaches their distance; the 2- and 3-mile loops can be combined for a longer lap. A single home-base aid station at the start/finish of each loop carries all food and drink. Distances run from 5k to 150 miles alongside 12- and 24-hour timed challenges, over a 52-hour event window.

About

This is a unique endurance event set against the stunning backdrop of the Zion Ponderosa Ranch Resort right outside of Zion National Park. Whether you're looking for a fun walk/run or an ultra-marathon challenge you've found the right place. This race is about movement, adventure, and community—not just speed. With distances ranging from 5k to 150 miles, you decide how far you want to go within a 52-hour time limit.
