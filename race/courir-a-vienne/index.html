About

Courir à Vienne is an annual event known and recognized by the people of Vienne. It contributes to the life of the city and its influence. The organization and logistics are handled by the Club Léo Lagrange de Vienne in partnership with the City of Vienne, Vienne Condrieu Agglomération, the Département de l'Isère, the Région Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, local associations, and companies. The event aims to showcase the historical heritage of Vienne with its Gallo-Roman monuments and facades, as well as the natural heritage with the banks of the Rhône and its slopes, and the beautiful countryside in the Balmes Viennoises.
