About

Explore the scenic banks of the River Exe at the Exeter 10K River Run, organised by the Exeter City Community Trust. This picturesque urban course takes runners on a journey through the heart of historic Exeter, showcasing the city's beautiful natural surroundings. Whether you're a seasoned racer or a first-time 10K participant, this event offers a fun and challenging experience for all.
