Course

All-road circular marathon starting and finishing at Millennium House in Pensilva, running through the Draynes Valley, across the southern edge of Bodmin Moor and down the Fowey Valley. A hilly course with a hard climb between miles 24 and 25. Water stations at miles 3, 6, 9, 14, 17, 21 and 24, with special drinks available at 14 and 21.

About

The Cornish Marathon is Cornwall's only all-road marathon. The scenic circular out and back route takes runners along the Draynes Valley, through the 'Poldark' country of Bodmin Moor, past King Arthur's legendary Dozmary Pool, and Du Maurier's Jamaica Inn at Bolventor, with a challenging climb to Foredown between miles 24 and 25.
