Course

A 37-mile loop on Isaac's Tea Trail, starting and finishing at Allendale and running via Ninebanks, Alston and Nenthead. The route crosses moorland, dales and upland ground in the North Pennines AONB, with about 4,800ft of ascent. It is self-navigated but waymarked, and a three-leg relay shares the same course.

About

Explore the stunning North Pennines AONB on the Isaac's Tea Trail Ultra, a challenging 37-mile loop that takes runners through remote valleys, over rugged fells, and past historic tea houses. This unique event celebrates the legacy of Isaac Holden, a 19th-century tea merchant who established a network of trails to connect his customers, offering an unforgettable journey through the heart of northern England's breathtaking countryside.
