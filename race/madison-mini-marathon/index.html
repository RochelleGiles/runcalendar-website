Course

The half marathon and 5K start and finish on N Park Street just past Science Hall in Madison, with the kids run at Park and University. The route winds past Capitol Square, the Dane County Farmers Market, State Street, Camp Randall Stadium, the Kohl Center, the Arboretum and the UW campus. Half marathon at 7am, 5K at 7.15am, kids run at 10am.
