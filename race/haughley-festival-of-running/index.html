Course

A lapped trail race entirely inside the Haughley Park estate in Suffolk, on parkland and woodland paths with about 29m of gentle undulation per 5.3km loop. Runners choose a 9am or 11am start and repeat laps to their distance, with race numbers, water and the aid station all at the lap point.

About

The Haughley Festival of Running offers marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K distances on countryside routes around the Suffolk village of Haughley, organised by Zig Zag Running.
