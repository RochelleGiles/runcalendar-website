Course

A 5000m track race held at the Bill Jacob Athletics Track in Wagga Wagga — 12.5 laps of the outdoor synthetic track, starting and finishing in the same place. Runners are seeded into eleven pace-group waves from sub-17 minutes through to 45–55 minutes, with racing running from mid-afternoon into the evening.

About

Wagga 5000 is a 5000m road race that anchors a new running festival in Wagga Wagga, in the NSW Riverina. Organisers created the event to give the region a fixture that welcomes runners of all levels.
