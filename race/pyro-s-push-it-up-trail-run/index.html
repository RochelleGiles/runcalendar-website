About

A trail run held in memory of Capt. William H. "Pyro" DuBois at Grand River Park near New Castle, Colorado, at over 5,000 feet. Runners choose a 3.5K beginner or 7.7K intermediate course over rooty, rocky and uneven ground, with proceeds going to the memorial scholarship fund.
