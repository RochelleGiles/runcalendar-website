About

The Antelope Canyon Ultras is a trail running event through the stunning Arizona desert, featuring slot canyons, desert mesas, and views of Horseshoe Bend and Page Rim. Organized by Vacation Races, it offers distances from half marathon to 50 miles starting and finishing at the Page Sports Complex.
