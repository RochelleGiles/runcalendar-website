About

A community run at University City in Sharjah, staged by Al Thiqah Club for the Disabled with the Sharjah Sports Council: a 10K, a 5K, a 2.5K and a 1K on fully closed roads from 7 in the morning, with wheelchair races over 10K and 5K for people of determination.
