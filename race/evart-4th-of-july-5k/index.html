Course

Starts and finishes at Evart Elementary School on N Cedar Street, Evart. A single 5K loop for runners and walkers, with refreshments and music at the finish. Playing cards are handed out along the route for a best-poker-hand award.

About

Whether you hit your fastest time, a time that encourages more training, or it's just a fun time, the Evart 4th of July 5K will help to enrich your life. Come join and support the community of walkers and runners in Evart.
