Course

A backyard ultra at Richmond, North Yorkshire: a 4.167-mile loop with about 302ft of climb, run every hour on the hour until one runner completes a lap more than anyone else. The route crosses woodland and fields before joining an old rail track with views of Easby Abbey and the River Swale. Largely self-sufficient, though hot water, cold drinks, snacks and a microwave are provided between laps.

About

The Sting Backyard Ultra is a unique endurance event held in the heart of the Yorkshire Dales. Participants will embark on a captivating 4.18-mile loop through enchanting woodlands, sprawling fields, and along the historic River Swale, with the chance to encounter the legendary Drummer Boy. This open-ended race continues until only one runner remains, pushing participants to their limits and beyond.
