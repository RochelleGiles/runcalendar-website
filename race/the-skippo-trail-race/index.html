Course

Trail race in Castlewood State Park beside the Meramec River, starting and finishing at the Special Events Area staging ground. The longer distances run two 10K loops. Fairly flat by trail standards apart from the park's well-known stair climb. The 10K is held on the Saturday, the half marathon and 30K on the Sunday.

About
