About

Welcome to Starkey Market's first annual Miles that Matter 5K, 10K and 1 Mile Fun Run. Come join us at 3460 Starkey Blvd for a day filled with fun and fitness. Lace up your sneakers and get ready to run, walk, or jog for a cause. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or just looking for a leisurely stroll, this event is for everyone! Bring your friends, family, and furry companions along for a morning of community spirit and healthy living. Let's make every mile count together!
