About

Explore the scenic island of Jersey during the annual Hospice 2 Hospice Half Marathon. This unique event takes runners on a stunning tour through the United Kingdom's Channel Islands, raising funds for the Jersey Hospice Care organization. With breathtaking coastal views and a challenging course, this half-marathon offers an unforgettable running experience.
