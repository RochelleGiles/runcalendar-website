Course

Point-to-point routes along the South Cornwall coast path between St Anthony Head and Porthpean, all finishing at the Porthpean festival village. About 95% multi-terrain — uneven, often slippery coast path with stiles, gates, rocks, steps and hills — with brief road sections. Checkpoints roughly every 10 miles carry water and nutrition. Distances run 11, 20, 32 and 64 miles, plus a 24-hour five-mile-loop event.

About

Explore the rugged Roseland Peninsula at The Rat, an off-road running challenge that takes runners through ancient woodlands, remote coastlines, and rolling hills. This classic trail event from MudCrew offers a scenic and challenging course for seasoned trail runners, with stunning views of the Cornish countryside. Whether you're looking to test your limits or simply enjoy the natural beauty of this stunning region, The Rat is an event not to be missed.
