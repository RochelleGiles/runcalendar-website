Course

A point-to-point marathon from Sparta down the river trails of the Driftless region, finishing at Riverside Park in downtown La Crosse. Roughly 20 miles are crushed gravel bike trail and 6 miles are paved bike trail. The course is USATF certified and a Boston qualifier; a half marathon and 5K run alongside it.

About

The La Crosse Marathon is an annual running event that takes place in the beautiful city of La Crosse, Wisconsin. Runners can choose from a full marathon, half marathon, or 5K run/walk, each offering stunning views of the Mississippi River and the surrounding natural landscapes.
