About

A 100-mile mountain trail race on Mount Hood, Oregon, run as a crew-accessible lollipop loop starting and finishing at the Mt. Hood Meadows ski area. The course carries about 17,300 feet of climbing and the same again of descent, and is held on the first full weekend of July.
