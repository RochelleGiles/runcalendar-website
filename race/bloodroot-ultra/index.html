Course

Based at Riverside Farm in Pittsfield, with courses exploring trails in the Green Mountain National Forest. The 50K and 50 miler follow the Bloodroot Trail while the 100 miler and 10 miler use the Riverside Trails, over steep and rugged mountain terrain with three to five aid stations depending on distance.

About

The Bloodroot Ultra is an original Spartan Trail ultra event in Pittsfield, Vermont, in the Green Mountains, with distances ranging from 10 miles to a 200-mile race.
