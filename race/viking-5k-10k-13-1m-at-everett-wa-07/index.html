About

The Viking 5K, 10K and Half Marathon are lap-based road races held at McCollum Park Athletic Field in Everett, Washington. The 5K and 10K repeat a 0.78-mile loop two and four times; the half marathon repeats a 1.09-mile loop six times, under a 3.5-hour limit. Entry is online only, with no race-day registration.
