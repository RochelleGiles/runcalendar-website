Course

Point-to-point on County Road 250 alongside the Animas River, running 13.1 miles from Baker's Bridge to the Durango Fitness Club. USATF-certified road course at around 6,500 ft, not closed to traffic. Five aid stations roughly every two miles with water, Tailwind and snacks. The field is capped at 250 runners.

About

The Steamworks Half Marathon runs through Durango, Colorado, a mountain town in the San Juan Mountains, with the course finishing near Steamworks Brewing Co. in the city's historic downtown district.
