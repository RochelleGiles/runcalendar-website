About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Mourre Poussiou region at the annual La Draille De Mourre Poussiou running event. This challenging course takes runners through stunning natural landscapes, offering an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an exhilarating adventure in an idyllic setting.
