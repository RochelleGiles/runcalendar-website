Course

An out-and-back BM fell race of 17.1km with 560m of ascent, running parallel to the River Tees across the fells of Upper Teesdale. The route mixes road, track and open fell; the fell section has no consistent path or flagging, so navigation skills are needed. Starts and finishes at the Farmhouse Kitchen Cafe, Low Way Farm, Holwick, at 14:00.

About

A fell race across Cronkley Fell in Upper Teesdale, part of the North Pennines Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. Runners cross open moorland above the River Tees, one of England's wildest upland landscapes.
