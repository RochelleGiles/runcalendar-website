Course

An out-and-back 4 miles from RC Outfitters on SW Water St in Peoria, starting on the Rock Island Greenway before climbing to the Bob Michel Bridge pedestrian path over the Illinois River into East Peoria. Runners follow the Riverfront Trail under I-74 to a turnaround at 2 miles and retrace the route. Water is at roughly a third of a mile and at the turnaround.

About

The Bridge to Bridge is a 4-mile out-and-back road race in Peoria, Illinois, crossing the Bob Michel Bridge pedestrian path over the Illinois River into East Peoria. It has grown into one of the area's premier running events.
