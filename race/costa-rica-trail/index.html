About

Explore the stunning natural beauty of Costa Rica on the thrilling Costa Rica Trail. This iconic ultramarathon takes runners through lush rainforests, across scenic mountain trails, and past breathtaking vistas, offering a truly immersive experience in one of the world's most biodiverse countries. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or a newcomer to the sport, the Costa Rica Trail promises an unforgettable adventure.
