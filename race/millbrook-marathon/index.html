Course

A single-loop road marathon on quiet country roads through the rolling hills and farmland around Millbrook in eastern Dutchess County. The course is USATF certified and a Boston Marathon qualifier, and the hills are steady enough that the organisers bill it as a tough day out.

About

The Millbrook Marathon is a scenic and challenging race through the rolling hills of Dutchess County, New York. Runners will enjoy the beautiful countryside and small-town charm of Millbrook.
