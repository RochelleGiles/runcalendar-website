About

The Run for Water Trail Race climbs Sumas Mountain above Abbotsford, British Columbia, part of the wider Run for Water series that raises money for clean water initiatives in Ethiopia. It has become a highlight for trail runners drawn to the scenery of the Fraser Valley, with distances to suit a range of experience levels.
