About

The SchneeLauf Fun Run or Walk is a family-friendly event during Schnee Days weekend in Elkhart Lake, WI, offering 5K or 2-mile routes for runners and walkers. Registration occurs from 8:45AM to 9:45AM at the Quit Qui Oc clubhouse with a shotgun start at 10AM, and the first 100 pre-registered participants receive Schnee Days swag.
