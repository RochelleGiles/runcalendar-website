Course

A two-day 60-mile (97km) tour of the Isle of Arran. Day one runs 28 miles south along the coast on quiet paths, forest trails, boardwalk and some tarmac; day two covers 30 miles of virtually all off-road northern mountain terrain including Goatfell, with rough going underfoot.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Isle of Arran during the Rat Race Ultra Tour, a thrilling two-day off-road running adventure in Scotland. Traverse the island's diverse landscapes, from coastal trails to lush forests and towering peaks, as you push your limits and immerse yourself in the stunning natural surroundings.
