About

Run, party, and celebrate lakeside at the AdventHealth Lake Minneola Half Marathon–10K–5K! Enjoy a scenic race experience along the Lake Minneola Waterfront on the beautiful South Lake Trail. Choose your distance — the Half Marathon, 10K, or 5K — and soak up the energy with live music and entertainment along the course. After the run, the fun is just getting started! Join us for an epic lakeside after party featuring the Suncreek Brewery beer garden and the ultimate comfort food — hot mac 'n cheese.
