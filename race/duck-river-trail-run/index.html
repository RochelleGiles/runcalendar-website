About

The Duck River Trail Run 5k & 15k take place along the scenic trails of Chickasaw Trace Park located just northwest of the city of Columbia, Tennessee. Net proceeds from this event benefit Maury County Parks & Recreation and efforts to continue the preservation and care of Chickasaw Trace Park which provides a quality place for families and individuals to gather and recreate. Come out, run the trails, and enjoy the beauty of Chickasaw Trace Park!
