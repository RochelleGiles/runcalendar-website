Course

Timed laps of a 4.5-mile loop at Comlara Park north of Bloomington-Normal, run as 12-hour, 6-hour, 3-hour and 1-hour events plus a relay. Runners must finish each lap inside the hour to start another. The loop mixes singletrack with open grassy fire road, with the aid station at the start/finish each lap.

About

This trail running event offers timed races of 12 hours (Sunrise-Sunset last-person-standing format), 6 hours, 3 hours, and 1 hour on loops at Comlara Park. Organized by Lake Run Club, it takes place on mountain bike trails in Hudson, Illinois.
