Course

Trail races through the tallgrass prairie at Konza Prairie near Manhattan, Kansas. The 25K follows the Kings Creek Loop Trail across the prairie; a 10K is also offered, both starting at 7:30 am with aid roughly every three miles. The 25K carries a four-hour time cap.

About

The Konza Prairie Biological Station, located just south of Manhattan, KS, is a 8,600 acre site devoted to a 3-pronged mission of research, education and conservation. It is the only such site of its type that is located in the highly imperilled tallgrass prairie ecosystem.
