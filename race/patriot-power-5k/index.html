About

Around the world, folks in our military make great personal sacrifices and risk their lives to protect freedom and our way of life. On April 29th we will have an opportunity to show our appreciation and do our part. As part of this, EnerSys is sponsoring a 5k run on April 29th in Reading, Pennsylvania OR virtually to support a great Veteran's charity: Team RWB (Team Red White and Blue) Team RWB's mission is to enrich the lives of Veterans through social and physical activities as well as community service events. This is an organization with local chapters nationally that help Veterans recover from challenges by providing the comradery, challenges and sense of duty that they all require. Proceeds from this race will be donated Team RWB.
