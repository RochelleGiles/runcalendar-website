Course

Trail race held over three days in Rock Cut State Park, Rockford. The Friday evening 10K starts near the boat launch and is marked with signage, reflective tape and glow sticks; the 25K and 50K start from the Red Oak picnic area on Saturday and Sunday. Courses run on the park's trail network and are aid-supported.

About

This trail race threads the wooded hills and lakes of Rock Cut State Park near Rockford, Illinois. Runners choose the 10K, 25K, or 50K distance across singletrack and forest doubletrack, with rolling elevation making for a proper autumn trail challenge.
