About

Nordic Trail La Bresse is a trail running event held in the scenic La Bresse area. The event features a special children's trail challenge called 'Défi p'ptiot trailers' for ages 6-14, offering a fun and technical course. The event takes place during the French national holiday of Bastille Day, adding to its festive atmosphere.
