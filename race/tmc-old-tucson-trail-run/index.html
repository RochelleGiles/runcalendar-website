Course

Cross-country trail run over the dusty streets, horse trails and desert terrain of Old Tucson Studios near Tucson. The 8-mile adds a 4-mile desert loop through Gates Pass; the 4-mile stays on the Old Tucson course; a 1-mile family walk/run is also offered.

About

Saddle up and head to Old Tucson! The popular TMC Sunrise at Old Tucson Trail Run is back! Runners and walkers of all ages can choose either an 8-Mile Trail Run, a 4-Mile Run or family-friendly 1-Mile Walk/Run.
