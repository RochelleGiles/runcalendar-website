Course

All routes start and finish at the event village beside Blair Castle in Highland Perthshire and run on the estate's trails: forest tracks, riverside paths, rolling hills and open moorland, with real climbing on the longer distances. Five adult distances run across the weekend, from a 7km through to a 58km ultra, plus kids' races of 1, 2 and 3km.

About

Explore the stunning trails surrounding historic Blair Castle at the Blair Castle Trail Weekender. This scenic event offers runners a variety of distances, from a challenging 58km ultra to a beginner-friendly 7km route, through the picturesque countryside of the United Kingdom. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this weekend getaway promises an unforgettable running adventure.
