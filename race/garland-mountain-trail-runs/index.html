Course

Singletrack on the Garland Mountain Horse and Hike Trails at Waleska, Georgia. The 4 mile course rolls and flows for the first three miles then climbs for a mile back to the finish; the half marathon covers the same ground over a longer route. A dog-friendly 4 mile wave runs alongside. The field is capped at 300.

About

The Garland Mountain Trail Runs feature a 4 mile race and a 13.1 mile race on the fast and flowy singletrack trails of Garland Mountain Horse and Hike Trails. A portion of the proceeds will benefit the Friends of Garland Mountain Trails for continued trail maintenance and development.
