Course

Out-and-back mountain run starting and finishing at Awa Awa Rata Reserve, Pudding Hill. The first 4.5km runs through native bush before the route opens into rugged alpine terrain and climbs the Mt Hutt road to the ski field car park, then descends the same way. The 25km course climbs roughly 900m.

About

Run Mt Hutt in New Zealand offers a breathtaking and challenging course, with stunning mountain vistas and varied terrain that will test runners of all abilities. This scenic event provides an unparalleled opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region while pushing your limits on an unforgettable running adventure.
