About

The Chili Run is a 2-mile chip-timed winter race starting and finishing at Carolina Blue Smokehouse & Taproom in Pitman, NJ. Participants enjoy chili, beer or soda, a custom shirt, and support the HEADstrong Foundation charity partner.
