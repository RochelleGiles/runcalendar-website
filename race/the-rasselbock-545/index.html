About

The Rasselbock 545 is a unique time-based track running challenge held at the Horspath Athletics Track, just east of the historic city of Oxford. Runners will push themselves to the limit as they aim to cover as much distance as possible in 545 minutes, testing their endurance and mental fortitude on the iconic Horspath circuit. This event offers a thrilling and immersive experience for runners seeking an extraordinary athletic challenge.
