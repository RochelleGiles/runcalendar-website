About

The Semi Marathon de Binche is a must-run event for any seasoned runner. Set in the historic town of Binche, this scenic course winds through charming streets and picturesque countryside, offering a truly unique running experience. Runners can look forward to a challenging yet rewarding challenge, with stunning views and a lively atmosphere making this a truly memorable race.
