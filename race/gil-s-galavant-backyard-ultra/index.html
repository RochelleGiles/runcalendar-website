Course

A last-one-standing backyard ultra on a working cattle property at Wallumbilla North Road. Runners complete repeated 6.71km loops of the property, with a cattle bell signalling the start of each loop. The field is capped at 200 entries. Surface breakdown and aid station provision are not stated.

About

Gil's Galavant Backyard Ultra is a unique endurance challenge set in the rugged outback of Queensland, Australia. Runners compete in a "last one standing" format, pushing their limits on a looped course as they strive to be the final participant still moving. This event offers a thrilling test of mental and physical fortitude for seasoned ultrarunners seeking an unforgettable backcountry adventure.
