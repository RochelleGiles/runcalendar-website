About

The Millet XTrail Courchevel is a unique event in France that offers 6 different distances (11, 22, 33, 44, 55, and 66 km) with a single registration fee and a single start time. Runners can choose their distance during the race based on the time barriers, and will be reimbursed at the finish line based on the distance they completed. The event takes place in the exceptional landscapes of the Vanoise National Park, allowing participants to familiarize themselves with ultra-trail running in a beautiful setting.
