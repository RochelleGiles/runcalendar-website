Course

Starts on Bellamy Street outside the Cabot High School field house. The course returns into the field house car park, drops down the ramp into the stadium and finishes on the track after a short straight. Aid stations are not stated.

About

The Spring Fling has been run in Cabot since 1996. From 1998 to 2001, the race was part of the Arkansas Grand Prix Series. After a five year absence the Cabot Country Cruisers brought the race back into the Series starting in 2007.
