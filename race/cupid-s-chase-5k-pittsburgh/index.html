Course

A USATF-style certified, professionally timed 5K starting and finishing at the PNC Park Left Field Lounge, 115 Federal Street, on Pittsburgh's North Shore. Gun time is 10:00 AM. Runners, walkers, strollers and wheelchairs are all welcome.

About

The 17th annual Cupid's Chase 5K invites runners, walkers, rollers, and sponsors to raise funds for housing and employment opportunities for people with disabilities through Community Options, Inc. This inclusive, certified 5K event at PNC Park offers finisher medals, shirts, and a pet-friendly atmosphere.
