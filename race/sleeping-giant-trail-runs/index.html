Course

A single 25K loop starting and finishing at the Pavilion, with the 50K covering two loops. The first five miles are mostly runnable rolling singletrack, followed by a rock scramble climb of over 500 feet and technical footing on the White Trail, then a 1.5-mile carriage road descent and 0.2 miles of pavement to the finish. Two to three aid stations; cupless event.

About

A trail race through Sleeping Giant State Park in Hamden, mixing smooth carriage roads with technical, rocky singletrack. The 25K and 50K (two loops) climb more than 3,000 feet each lap over traprock ridges. Start and finish are at the park pavilion on Mount Carmel Avenue.
