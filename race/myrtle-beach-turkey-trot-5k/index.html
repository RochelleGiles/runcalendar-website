Course

A USATF-certified 5K through the Market Common district of Myrtle Beach, on the site of the former Myrtle Beach Air Force Base. Flat sealed roads, 8:00am start. No water stop on course; water and snacks at the finish. A free 50-yard Gobbler Kids' Dash follows at 9:00am.

About

The Myrtle Beach Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving-morning 5K in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, one of several Grand Strand turkey trots hosted by N.S. Promotions & Events each fall.
