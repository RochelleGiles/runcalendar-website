Course

A two-day festival. Friday's 5K and Kids 1K start and finish at Wingfield Park in Ruidoso. Saturday's marathon, half marathon and 47K/50K run from the Enchanted Vine by Noisy Water venue, with the 47K away at 6am, the marathon at 7am and the half at 8am. The 50K adds an optional extra 3K loop.

About

Ruidoso RunFest brings marathon, half marathon, 50K and 5K races through the mountain village of Ruidoso, New Mexico, with courses winding along forested trails and village streets in the Sierra Blanca foothills of Lincoln National Forest.
