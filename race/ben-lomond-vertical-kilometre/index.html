Course

An uphill-only climb of 3.9km gaining about 1200m, from near the Ben Lomond Woolshed to the summit of Ben Lomond above Queenstown. The route is entirely off-track and needs good footwork, balance and strength; poles are allowed. Runners descend afterwards on the Ben Lomond and Skyline tracks.

About

The Ben Lomond Vertical Kilometere event takes you from near the Ben Lomond Woolshed to the summit of Ben Lomond, climbing 1200m over 3.9km. The course is all off-track and good footwork, balance and strength is essential.
