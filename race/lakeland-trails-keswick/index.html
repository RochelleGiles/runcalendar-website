Course

All races start and finish under the gantry in Fitz Park, Keswick, with live music, commentary and food at the finish. The routes follow footpaths and bridleways on hard-packed trail with the occasional muddy section, including the boggy ground of the Glenderaterra Valley, and take in views of Derwentwater, Skiddaw and Blencathra. Expect a few hills.

About

Explore the stunning Cumbrian landscape at the Lakeland Trails Keswick event, offering runners a choice of 15km, 10km, and 5km trail runs through the picturesque Lake District. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or a newcomer seeking a scenic challenge, this event promises an unforgettable experience amidst the region's breathtaking natural beauty.
