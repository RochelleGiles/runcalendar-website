Course

A repeating 5K loop along the Scioto Mile beside the river in downtown Columbus, starting and finishing at Genoa Park, with 10K runners covering the loop twice. The route is marked throughout with arrows and cones. A free half-mile kids run is offered alongside.

About

A two-race spring training series along the Scioto Mile trail in Columbus, offering 5K, 10K, and 15K distances. Participants can complete any distance at both events and receive ASICS running shoes, a custom Ciele hat, race apparel, and finisher medals.
