About

The 4th annual Cherokee Chase is a 5K Race and One Mile Fun Run held at Etowah River Park in Canton GA. We are also excited to announce that the Cherokee Chase is an Atlanta Journal-Constitution Peachtree Road Race Qualifier! Please come out for this community-wide event which will include the race, bounce houses, prizes and much more family fun! Proceeds from the race will support student athletes and the Athletic Department at Cherokee Christian School.
