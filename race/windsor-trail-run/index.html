Course

Flat off-road trail course starting and finishing at Alexandra Gardens in Windsor, with 10K and half marathon options. Elevation gain is minimal - around 29 m for the 10K and 58 m for the half. The 10K sets off at 09:00 and the half marathon at 09:20.

About

Experience the Windsor Trail Run and enjoy a royal route through Alexandra Gardens. Flat, scenic, and perfect for all runners. Join us for the Windsor Trail Run series and run in the shadow of Windsor Castle on a smooth trail course.
