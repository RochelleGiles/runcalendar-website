About

The Courthouse O'Putnam 5K is a Hartford Marathon Foundation race through downtown Putnam, Connecticut, following the Putnam River Trail beside the Quinebaug River. A virtual entry and a FitKids run for ages two to eleven are offered alongside the 5K.
