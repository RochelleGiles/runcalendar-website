Course

Starts by the 6th Street Arch in downtown Redmond and finishes in Centennial Park. The route drops into Dry Canyon Trail, running between steep rocky walls and twisted juniper on the canyon floor, then climbs back out onto road for the run through the historic downtown.

About

Conquer the Canyon is a running event on Redmond's Dry Canyon Trail, featuring stunning high desert geological features and scenic views through the town center. It offers Half Marathon, 10K, 5K distances plus a 5K Doggie Dash, with start under the 6th Street Arch and finish in Centennial Park.
