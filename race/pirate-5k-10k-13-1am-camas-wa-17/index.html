About

The Pirate 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is a themed running event held at Lake Camas Heritage Park in Camas, WA. Runners complete out-and-back loops on scenic courses with a 3.5-hour time limit, featuring finisher medals, shirts, and a best costume contest.
