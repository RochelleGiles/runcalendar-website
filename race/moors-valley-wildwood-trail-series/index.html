Course

One lap of Moors Valley Country Park for the 10K and two laps for the half marathon, run on gravel forest paths with muddy sections and short inclines. Both distances start and finish in the park. The route is fully marked, marshalled and chip timed, with aid stations on course; runners bring their own cup.

About

A winter trail race set in Moors Valley Country Park and Forest in Dorset, run entirely on the park's gravel woodland paths. Half marathon and 10K distances are offered over the same lapped route, which passes through mature forest where deer and birds of prey are often seen.
