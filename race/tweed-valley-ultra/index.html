Course

A 67k and a 50k route from Glentress Peel Visitor Centre near Peebles, on forest paths, riverside tracks, moorland and minor roads rising to 520m. The 67k climbs 1,574m with checkpoints at 17k, 37k and 52k; the 50k climbs 1,145m with checkpoints at 19k and 35k.

About

The Tweed Valley Ultra is a multi-terrain ultra event set in the stunning Tweed Valley. The course offers a variety of terrain, including rolling hills, woodland trails, and riverside paths, providing runners with spectacular views of the surrounding countryside. This event is ideal for those looking for a challenging yet scenic ultra-running experience.
