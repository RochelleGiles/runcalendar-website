About

The Marathon de Nairobi is a premier running event set against the vibrant backdrop of Kenya's bustling capital city. Runners can expect a challenging yet scenic course that winds through the city's diverse neighborhoods, offering a unique opportunity to experience the sights, sounds, and energy of Nairobi. This event is a must-do for any distance running enthusiast looking to immerse themselves in the rich culture and running heritage of East Africa.
