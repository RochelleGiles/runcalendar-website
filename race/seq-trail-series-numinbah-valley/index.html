Course

Based at Numinbah Valley, with parking at 2201 Nerang Murwillumbah Rd. Runners choose an 8.8km short course or a 13.4km long course, with Strava routes published for each. Professionally timed, and a participant guide is released the Wednesday before the event.

About

SEQ Trail Series: Numinbah Valley is a trail running event in the Gold Coast hinterland's Numinbah Valley, near Springbrook National Park. Runners can choose a 13.4km long course or an 8.8km short course through forested valley terrain.
