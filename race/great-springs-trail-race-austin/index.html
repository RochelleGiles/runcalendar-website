Course

Multiple laps of a well-marked 6.5 mile loop at Southeast Metro Park, Del Valle, south-east of Austin. The loop mixes rough jeep road, single-track and double-track, with rocky descents, steep pinches and creek-bed crossings through mesquite and oak alongside Onion Creek, for roughly 640 feet of gain a lap. Two aid stations — start/finish and past halfway — carry water, Tailwind, snacks and fruit. Starts are staggered from 6:30am.

About

A trail race featuring over six miles of scenic trails with single tracks, rolling hills, and fast downhills in Del Valle, Austin. The event supports the Great Springs Project's mission to protect lands over the Edwards Aquifer recharge zone.
