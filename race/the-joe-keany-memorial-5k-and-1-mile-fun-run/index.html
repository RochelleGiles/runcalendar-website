About

The Joe Keany Memorial 5K and 1 Mile Fun Run is a USATF-certified course starting and finishing at North Country Road Middle School in Miller Place. Runners pass through historic Miller Place, down past Mount Sinai Harbor, and up the steep Cedar Drive hill before finishing back at the school.
