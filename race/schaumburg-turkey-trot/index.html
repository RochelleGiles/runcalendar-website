Course

Both races start and finish at Ned Brown Meadow in Busse Woods, Elk Grove Village. The certified half marathon runs entirely on paved path and road over rolling terrain, and the 5K uses paved surfaces in the same park. The half starts at 8:00 a.m. and the 5K at 8:20 a.m.

About

A chip-timed running event held on Thanksgiving Saturday at Busse Woods in Schaumburg, Illinois. Participants can choose between a 5K or Half Marathon distance, with fun run and kids categories available.
