Course

The marathon runs 11 laps of Battersea Park on a course the organisers describe as totally flat, starting at the Millennium Arena and finishing on the athletics track under the RunThrough gantry. The half marathon covers the same park circuit over fewer laps. A 1km children's race is run on the athletics track.

About

The Battersea Park Running Festival in London offers an exhilarating challenge for runners of all levels. Featuring both a Half Marathon and a full Marathon, this scenic urban event takes participants on a picturesque tour through the lush greenery of Battersea Park. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply seeking an unforgettable running experience, the Battersea Park Running Festival promises an unforgettable day on the roads.
