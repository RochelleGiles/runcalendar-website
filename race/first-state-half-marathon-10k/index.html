Course

Starts and finishes at the Deer Park Tavern on West Main Street in Newark, running out through the town centre, residential streets and paved trails on a USATF-certified course (DE22001JLW) with the turnaround at Barksdale Road. Three water stations sit at Devon Drive, Apple/Sunset Road and the turnaround, with toilets at five points.

About

The First State Half Marathon & 10K is an annual road race in Newark, Delaware — named for the state's nickname as the first to ratify the U.S. Constitution. The event offers both half marathon and 10K distances.
