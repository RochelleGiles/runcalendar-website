Course

A UTMB World Series mountain weekend based at Snowbird resort in the Wasatch, with start and finish at the resort base. The 50K climbs 3,450 m, the 30K 2,000 m and the 10K 500 m, with a short, near-vertical Summit race as well. High alpine ridgelines and wildflower meadows on technical mountain terrain.

About

Speedgoat Mountain Races is a UTMB World Series mountain trail event at Snowbird Resort in Utah's Wasatch Mountains, known for brutally steep climbs on ski-run terrain.
