About

The TreeTops Trail Race is an 8.5 mile singletrack trail race held in DuPont State Recreational Forest in western North Carolina. Runners climb close to 1,000 feet across rolling, rugged terrain, with two water stations on course and chip timing at the finish. It is run by iDaph Events.
