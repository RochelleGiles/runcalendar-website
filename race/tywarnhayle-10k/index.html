Course

A challenging mixed-terrain 10K from Mount Hawke Cricket Club near Truro, heading through countryside to the coast and taking in sections of coast path around Chapel Porth and Porthtowan before looping the historic Tywarnhayle Mine. Expect footpaths, technical descents, mud and a stretch through a river. A children's Tywarnhayle24 follows the main start.

About

A challenging multi-terrain 10K starting from Porthtowan, a beach village on Cornwall's north coast, mixing road, trail and coastal path. It draws runners from clubs across west Cornwall each October.
