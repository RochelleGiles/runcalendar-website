About

We are Upstate New York's premier half marathon that starts and finishes at the Oncenter in Syracuse. This popular event is enjoyed by thousands of runners each year and features great SWAG and a post race party with live music, a hot food breakfast and unlimited beverages (alcohol and non-alcoholic).
