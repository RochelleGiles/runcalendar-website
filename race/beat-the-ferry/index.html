Course

Out-and-back trail races on the South West Coast Path along the Purbeck peninsula, starting from Knoll Beach Cafe on Studland Beach with race HQ at Knoll House Hotel. The route passes chalk cliffs, Old Harry Rocks, Corfe Castle and the Purbeck hills. The 50K climbs 922 m via Grange Hill; the 25K turns at Corfe Castle for 556 m of climb.

About

An ultra along the Jurassic Coast between Swanage and Poole Harbour, timed to beat the chain ferry across to Sandbanks. The 50K and 25K routes follow the cliffs and beaches around Studland Bay.
