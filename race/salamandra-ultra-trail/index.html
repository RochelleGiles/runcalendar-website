About

Salamandra Ultra Trail is one of the most challenging and scenic ultra-trail running events in Poland. The 100 km course takes runners through the beautiful landscapes of the Silesian and Żywiec Beskids, offering unforgettable views and technically demanding sections. The event attracts top runners from Poland and abroad who compete for victory and course records.
