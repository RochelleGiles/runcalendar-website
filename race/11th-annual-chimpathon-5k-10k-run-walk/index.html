About

Save the Chimps opens its 150-acre sanctuary in Fort Pierce for a 5K and 10K run and walk, one of only three days a year the public is admitted. The flat, chip-timed course loops past the twelve island habitats where rescued chimpanzees live. Entry fees support the sanctuary's care work.
