Course

A 10K run across the Sunshine Skyway Bridge on the northbound lanes of Interstate 275, over lower Tampa Bay. The span carries runners more than 200 feet above the water, linking Pinellas, Manatee and Hillsborough counties. The field is capped at 8,000 and sold first come, first served.

About

The SunSky 10K is a road 10K in St. Petersburg, Florida, run along the waterfront recreation area beneath the towering Sunshine Skyway Bridge, with open views across Tampa Bay for the length of the course.
