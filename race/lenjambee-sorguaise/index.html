About

L'Enjambée Sorguaise is organized by the City of Sorgues in partnership with the Vaucluse Athletics Committee and the Sorgues Athlé 84 club. Participants cross the Ouvèze river via a Himalayan suspension footbridge, with the finish line and activity village set in the heart of the municipal park.
