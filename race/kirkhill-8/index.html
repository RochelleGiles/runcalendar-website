Course

An eight-hour timed race on a 4.3-mile loop run entirely on trails in Kirkhill Forest, starting and finishing at the same point each lap. Runners complete as many loops as they can inside the window and set their own target, whether that is 20 miles, a marathon, 50k or further.

About

The Kirkhill 8 is a thrilling trail race in the heart of Aberdeen, where runners push their limits over an 8-hour period. Participants can complete as many 4.3-mile loops as they desire, navigating the scenic trails and challenging terrain of the Kirkhill countryside. This event offers a unique test of endurance and determination for seasoned trail runners.
