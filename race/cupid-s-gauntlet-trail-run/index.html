Course

Loop trail courses around Stillhouse Hollow Lake near Belton, Texas — rocky and mostly wooded with exposed sections. The 10K is two short loops; the half, marathon and 60K run a ~13-mile loop one, two or three times. Aid stations with water, Gatorade and food.

About

Welcome to Cupid's Gauntlet, the trail race where love, grit, and sneakers collide! This isn't your typical stroll through the woods—this is an adventure designed to test your endurance, your sense of humor, and the opportunity to make a love connection with some rocks.
