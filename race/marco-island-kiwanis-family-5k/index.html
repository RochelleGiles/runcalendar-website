Course

A 3.1 mile course starting and finishing at Tigertail Beach on Marco Island. The route runs through the Hideaway Beach neighbourhood, taking in boardwalks, coastal preserve, a golf course and views over the Gulf.

About

The annual Marco Island Kiwanis Family 5K is a fun run/walk event starting and finishing at Tigertail Beach on Marco Island. It welcomes runners, joggers, and walkers with beautiful views along the course.
