Course

Laps of a 0.55-mile crushed gravel trail loop at Kimberley Park in Winston-Salem. The 5K is about six laps and the 10K just over eleven; there is also a one-mile fun run, a solo 48-miler and a 48-mile relay for teams of up to six.

About

Annual charity running event at Kimberley Park in Winston-Salem where participants run laps on a 0.55-mile crushed gravel trail loop to celebrate organizer Justin's birthday and raise funds for HOPE of Winston-Salem. Features 5K, 10K, 48-mile solo, relay, and 1-mile fun run options with post-race party, prizes, and family-friendly activities benefiting food insecurity relief.
