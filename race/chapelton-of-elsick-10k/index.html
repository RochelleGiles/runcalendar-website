About

The Chapelton of Elsick 10K is a scenic multi-terrain race starting and finishing in the charming town of Chapelton, Stonehaven. Runners will wind through picturesque countryside and quaint villages, offering a unique and memorable challenge for both seasoned athletes and newcomers alike.
