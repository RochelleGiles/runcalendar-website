Course

Starts at the Knysna Waterfront and heads out along the red brick road to George Rex Drive, climbing to the highest point on the East Heads before returning. The 21 km takes in that climb; a 10 km on flat ground and a 5 km fun run are also offered.

About

The Knysna Waterfront Half Marathon is organised by the Knysna Marathon Club and runs along the waterfront of the Knysna Lagoon on South Africa's Garden Route. Half marathon, 10km and 5km distances are on offer.
