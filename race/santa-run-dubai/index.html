About

The ninth Santa Run, on West Beach at Palm Jumeirah: a 5K, a 3K and a 1K along the shore, every runner in a Santa suit (it comes with the entry). Runners, walkers and families of all ages, with prizes for the best-dressed family and groups. Starts at 8 in the morning.
