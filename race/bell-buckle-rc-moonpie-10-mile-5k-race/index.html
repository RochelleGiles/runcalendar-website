Course

Both races start at 7:00am at the top of Hinkle Hill Road beside the Bell Buckle Post Office and finish in the parking lot in front of the downtown stores. Paved road loop with painted mile markers and directional signs. The 5K has one water stop past two miles; the 10 Mile has water at miles 2, 4, 6 and 8 with Gatorade at 6 and 8. Course closes 9:30am.

About

The Bell Buckle Chamber of Commerce RC-MoonPie 10 Mile Run is a certified course held annually in conjunction with the Bell Buckle RC-MoonPie Festival. It's one of the few remaining ten-mile races, since most similar events have shortened to 10Ks, making it distinctive for its length and endurance test. A 5K touring Bell Buckle's historic neighborhoods and tree-lined streets runs alongside it.
