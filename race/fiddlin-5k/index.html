Course

The 5K starts and finishes on the Otis Duck Green Way at 222 Bailey Street in Mars Hill, where parking is available. The route passes the Bailey Mountain Preserve. A virtual entry option is also offered.

About

The Fiddlin' 5K is an annual running event organized by the Madison County, NC Rotary Club. The event features live music from local Appalachian musicians and a scenic course through the Bailey Mountain Preserve.
