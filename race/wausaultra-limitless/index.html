About

WausaUltra Limitless is a Backyard Ultra event held at Sylvan Hill park in Wausau, WI, where participants run a 4.167-mile (6.707 km) loop every hour starting at 9 am until only one remains. It follows the format popularized by Lazarus Lake, challenging runners to push their limits on gorgeous mostly trail terrain.
