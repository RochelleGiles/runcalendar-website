About

The UAE's road championships in Al Ain, finishing at the Al Ain Cycle Track: a 5K and a 2.5K, each with an elite championship race and an age-group race behind it, from under-12s to masters, plus a 1 km fun run. Point-to-point: a bus takes runners from the finish out to the start.
