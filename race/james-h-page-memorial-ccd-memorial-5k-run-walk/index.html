About

In person or you may join us from around the world to remember Jim Page and celebrate Corn Capital Days in Olivia. Race is held on 2nd Street at Sunrise Park. Registration deadline is July 15th for T-shirts and race pack. Past proceeds have been donated to the Renville County Rebels, our Special Olympians for personalized Bowling uniforms, 5K Race t-shirts, Bowling, Pizza Party's, and their meals at their special events. Our Renville County Rebels award the medals to all of our race participants as they cross the finish line!
