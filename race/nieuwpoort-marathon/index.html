About

The Nieuwpoort Marathon is a must-do event for runners seeking a scenic and historic challenge. Set in the charming coastal town of Nieuwpoort, this marathon boasts a picturesque route that winds through quaint neighborhoods and along the stunning North Sea shoreline. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to soak in the vibrant atmosphere, the Nieuwpoort Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
