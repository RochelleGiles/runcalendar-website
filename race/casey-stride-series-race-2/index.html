Course

Laps of the open roads and paths around Casey Fields Regional Athletics Centre in Cranbourne East. There are no road closures, so all roads stay open to normal traffic. A drink refill station near the start/finish carries water and electrolyte and is reached roughly every 2.5km. Kids races over 2km, 1km and 400m run the same precinct.
