About

The Gran Trail Courmayeur is a captivating running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Italian Alps. Challenging trail runners to conquer stunning mountain landscapes, this race offers a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the Courmayeur region. Whether you're seeking an exhilarating adventure or a chance to push your endurance limits, the Gran Trail Courmayeur promises an unforgettable running experience.
