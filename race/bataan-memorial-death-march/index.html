About

The Bataan Memorial Death March is a marathon-distance event held on the high desert of White Sands Missile Range, New Mexico, honoring the American and Filipino soldiers who survived the World War II Bataan Death March. Many participants march in military gear or carry rucksacks across the sandy, rugged course.
