Course

All distances start at The Lawns at the Roundhouse on Roundhouse Road, Camps Bay. The 42 km TMC Classic circumnavigates Table Mountain with substantial climbing; the 27 km TMC Challenge climbs to higher ground above the Atlantic; and the 15 km TMC Baby stays on the scenic lower slopes and suits newcomers. Refuel stations are placed on all routes.

About

The Table Mountain Challenge runs three trail distances — 15km, 27km and 42km — on the slopes of Table Mountain above Cape Town. Expect sustained climbing, rocky footing underfoot and long exposed stretches, with the 42km the hardest of the three.
