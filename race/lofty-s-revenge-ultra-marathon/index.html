Course

Point-to-point. The 50km starts at Felixstow Reserve and finishes at the Mt Lofty summit; the 28km starts at Foxfield Oval, Athelstone and also finishes at the summit; the 10km starts and finishes at the summit. The organiser states the course is roughly 70% trail and 30% road. Aid stations are marked on the course maps and the 10km field is capped at 50 runners.

About

Lofty's Revenge is an exciting trail running event that takes participants from Felixstow Reserve to the summit of Mt. Lofty. The course features a mix of trails and roads, including the scenic Aqueduct Trail in Highbury with amazing views of Black Hill. The 50km ultra marathon combines approximately 70% trail and 30% road with 1,800m of elevation gain, offering runners a challenging and diverse running experience.
