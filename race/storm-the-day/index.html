About

This event is made for EVERYONE!!! You can do one loop or 50 loops….it is all up to YOU!! The clock begins at sunrise and you will have until sunset to complete the looped course as many times as you can. Tired after the 3rd loop….take a rest! Want to stop and eat lunch after your 10th loop…..go for it!! It is all up to you!!
