About

Miles for Meridian 5K, now past its seventh running, is a walk/run/roll event at Tioga Town Center in Newberry. Proceeds benefit Meridian Healthcare, which supports people affected by mental illness and substance use disorders through primary care, coordinated treatment and supportive services.
