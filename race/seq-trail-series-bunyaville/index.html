About

Explore the lush forests and winding trails of Bunyaville Regional Park during Round 5 of the South East Queensland Trail Running Series. This picturesque event offers runners a chance to challenge themselves on a variety of terrain, from gentle slopes to more technical sections, all while immersed in the natural beauty of this stunning location.
