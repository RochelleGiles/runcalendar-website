About

The Diagonale Du Mont Joly is a running event that takes place in Saint-Gervais Mont-Blanc, a captivating destination known for its stunning natural landscapes and rich cultural heritage. This event offers runners the opportunity to challenge themselves on a course that showcases the breathtaking scenery of the region, including the majestic Mont Joly. Participants can expect an unforgettable experience as they navigate through the picturesque trails and immerse themselves in the vibrant atmosphere of this unique running event.
