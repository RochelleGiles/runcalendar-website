About

The race runs on dirt roads and forest trails within the DCR Blue Hills Reservation, starting and finishing near the Houghton's Pond Visitor Center. Terrain includes small rocks and tree roots but avoids the reservation's most technical hiking trails. Proceeds benefit Friends of the Blue Hills, which maintains the reservation's trails.
