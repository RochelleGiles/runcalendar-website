About

The U.Trail Lamer Winkel is an ultra-trail through the Bavarian Forest around Lam, with routes up to 54km including the Osser-Riese leg past the Großer Osser peak on the German-Czech border. A shorter 12km Rookie-Trail suits newcomers.
