Course

An indoor 5K on a 200-metre track, 25 laps to the distance, at 521 Tuscarawas St W, Canton. The field is capped at 100 runners, started in heats between 6:30 PM and 8:15 PM.

About

A good old basic running race to check your Winter fitness! A timed indoor 5K with a BASIC event shirt and BASIC finisher medal. Your chance to run a Winter Race in shorts and a t-shirt inside on a PERFECT 200 meter track. Runners will be placed in heats with those of similar expected finish times. All ages and abilities welcome.
