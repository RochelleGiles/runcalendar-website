About

The 7Weekspoort Trail Run Challenge offers 5km, 10km and 18km routes from Ladismith in the Klein Karoo, held alongside the event's long-running mountain bike challenge. The courses cross dry Karoo farmland and foothill tracks beneath the Klein Swartberg.
