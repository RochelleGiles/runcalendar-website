Course

Races start at Center and Washington Avenues in downtown Bay City and run on sealed roads through the town, finishing on a long straightaway. The organisers describe the course as very flat and fast with no hills.

About

Join Bay Area Runners Club and Presenting Sponsor Jolt Credit Union at the 53rd running of the Bay City St. Patrick's Day Races - named by Runner's World as one of America's stellar St. Patrick's Day runs. Affordable, fun St. Patrick's Day weekend events for the family include a Race Expo presented by Covenant HealthCare, Leprechaun Races for the kids presented by Uptown Orthodontics, 5K Run, 5K Walk, 8K Run and Irish Double events.
