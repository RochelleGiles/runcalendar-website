Course

A point-to-point 100 miles from Bamburgh Castle in Northumberland to Edinburgh Castle. The first half is largely coastal trail along the Northumberland shoreline, becoming more urban path on the run into Edinburgh. Nine pit stops at 10, 22, 33, 43, 48, 60, 74, 83 and 93 miles carry water, food, toilets and medical support. A 48-hour window makes it walker-friendly at 2.5mph.
