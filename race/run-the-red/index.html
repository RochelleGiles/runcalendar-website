Course

A point-to-point course from the Whitehorse Creek Overlook to South Pass City, crossing Wyoming's Northern Red Desert — a maze of buttes, canyons, badlands and miles of open country. Offered as a 30K and a 50K.

About

Run the Red Desert is a 30K and 50K trail race through Wyoming's remote Red Desert near South Pass City, crossing wide-open high-desert terrain known for its wildlife and big-sky scenery.
