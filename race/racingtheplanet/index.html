About

Racingtheplanet is a series of ultramarathon events that take place in some of the most remote and challenging environments on Earth. The events offer a unique opportunity for runners to push their limits and explore stunning natural landscapes. The courses highlight the beauty and diversity of the host countries, providing an unforgettable experience for participants.
