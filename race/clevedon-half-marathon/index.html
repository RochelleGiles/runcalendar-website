About

Explore the picturesque country roads of Auckland's Clevedon region at the annual Clevedon Half Marathon. Offering 21.1km, 10km, and 5km distance options, this event provides a scenic challenge for runners of all levels. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply seeking a beautiful run through the New Zealand countryside, the Clevedon Half Marathon is not to be missed.
