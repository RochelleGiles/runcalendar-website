About

The Wings of Mercy Runway 5K is a fundraising event held at Linden Price's Airport to support Wings of Mercy East Michigan's mission of providing free air transportation to medical patients in need. Participants run or walk on the runway with the motto 'RUN SO OTHERS CAN FLY.'
