Course

A 7-hour challenge on the Grand Western Canal near Tiverton, run as repeated 6.55-mile out-and-back laps on flat canal towpath from Sampford Peverell Village Hall. Runners complete as many laps as they wish and may finish after any lap, so the distance is self-selected; a separate 10K runs on its own start time. Aid is at Burlescombe about 3.3 miles out and again at HQ on each return.

About

Lace up your shoes and join the Canal Shuffle, a unique 7-hour running challenge set along the scenic waterways of Sampford Peverell, near Tiverton in the United Kingdom. Runners will navigate a picturesque course that winds through historic canal-side paths, offering stunning views and the chance to connect with nature. This event promises an unforgettable experience for endurance athletes seeking a new adventure.
