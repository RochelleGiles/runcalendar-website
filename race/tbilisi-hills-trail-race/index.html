About

Explore the rugged trails of Tbilisi's iconic hills at the Tbilisi Hills Trail Race. This challenging event takes runners on a scenic journey through the lush green landscapes surrounding the Georgian capital, offering breathtaking views and an unforgettable running experience. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to the sport, this race promises an exhilarating adventure for all.
