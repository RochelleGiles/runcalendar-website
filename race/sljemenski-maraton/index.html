About

The Sljemenski Maraton climbs from central Zagreb up Mount Medvednica to the Sljeme summit, best known for hosting the Snow Queen Trophy World Cup ski races. Runners tackle a marathon or 14K route through forested slopes overlooking the Croatian capital.
