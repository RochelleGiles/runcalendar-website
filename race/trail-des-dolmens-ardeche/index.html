About

Discover the stunning trails of the Ardeche region at the Trail Des Dolmens Ardeche, a captivating running event that takes participants through a landscape dotted with ancient megalithic monuments. Runners will be immersed in the natural beauty of this rugged, scenic area as they challenge themselves on a course that promises breathtaking vistas and a unique cultural experience.
