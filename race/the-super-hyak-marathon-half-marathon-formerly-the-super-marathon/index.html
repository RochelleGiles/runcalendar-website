Super Hyak Marathon

Downhill marathon gliding beneath the Cascade Mountains

Course

A point-to-point course on the Palouse to Cascades State Park Trail, starting at the Hyak Trailhead and running through the Snoqualmie Tunnel before a long steady descent - 1,247ft net downhill over the marathon distance. The half marathon is run on the same trail.

About

Enjoy 26.2 miles of flat and then scenic downhill with a gorgeous view of the Cascade Mountains as you glide down the gravel trail on this point-to-point course. This is the first event of our 3-race Downhill Super Series.
