Course

A cross-country style course over the grounds of Andrew Jackson's Hermitage, crossing fields and grass with short sections of trail and paved road. The route passes Tulip Grove Mansion and the Hermitage Church before finishing at the Hermitage Mansion. The start is 8:00 a.m. and the race is not chip-timed.

About

The 7th President 7K takes runners through the grounds of The Hermitage, Andrew Jackson's historic home near Nashville, Tennessee. The 7K distance is a nod to Jackson's place as the seventh U.S. president.
