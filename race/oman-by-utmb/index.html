About

Oman by UTMB races through the Hajar Mountains around the historic city of Nizwa, once Oman's capital, crossing canyons and rocky desert terrain. Distances range from the 21.5K Scenic Ascent to the 154K Hajar Ultra, among the toughest courses on the UTMB World Series calendar.
