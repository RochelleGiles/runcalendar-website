Course

The 5K run/walk and the one-mile walk both start near the pond behind the McConnell Heart Health Center. The route crosses sections of the McConnell and Sports Medicine car parks, joins McConnell Drive to the back of the campus and then follows paved trail and path around the grounds. The 5K is flat apart from one short uphill approaching mile 2.

About

The McConnell 5K Run/Walk and 1 Mile Walk fundraiser benefits the OhioHealth Foundation and is held on the McConnell Heart Health Center campus in Columbus, Ohio. The race takes place on a combination of paved roads and a partially wooded looped trail, with the 5K mostly flat aside from one or two hills.
