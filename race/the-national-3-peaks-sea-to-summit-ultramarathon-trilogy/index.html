Course

Three separate sea-level-to-summit ultramarathon routes climbing Ben Nevis, Scafell Pike and Yr Wyddfa. Each can be entered on its own, or all three completed across the series to earn the Gold Trilogy Medal. The routes are purpose-designed by the organiser rather than following the standard walking lines.

About

The Rat Race Sea to Summit 3 Peaks trilogy takes you to the summit of the high points of England, Scotland and Wales along 3 spectacular and varied ultramarathon distance routes. Popular since time eternal, "the 3 peaks" probably need no introduction. But the way these routes have been laid down is a totally fresh take on this threesome of classic British mountains. With routes specially and uniquely designed by us, if you know Rat Race you'll already know that these will not be your standard days out on these tops.
