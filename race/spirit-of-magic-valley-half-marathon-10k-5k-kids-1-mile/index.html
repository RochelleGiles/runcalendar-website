Course

About

All races take place along the stunning Snake River Canyon Rim Trail in Twin Falls, Idaho, offering breathtaking views of the world-famous Perrine Bridge, the Evel Knievel Jump Site, and Shoshone Falls. Each race begins and ends at the iconic Shoshone Falls.
