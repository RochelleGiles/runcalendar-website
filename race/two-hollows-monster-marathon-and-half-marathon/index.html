Course

Held at Kennedy State Forest in Virgil, New York, on singletrack trails with challenging hills. Age-adjusted staggered starts are used so that runners converge at the finish. Marathon and half marathon distances are offered.

About

A trail running event held on well-maintained trails in Kennedy State Forest between Daisy Hollow and Babcock Hollow. The race features age and gender head starts on beautiful trails in the western section of Kennedy State Forest.
