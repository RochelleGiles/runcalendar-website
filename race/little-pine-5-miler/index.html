About

The Little Pine 5 Miler is a beginner trail race that is suitable for runners/hikers of all ages and skill levels. We use the Lakeshore Trail loop located in Little Pine State Park. Although it's called a 5 Miler, the distance will be about a 1/2 mile longer depending on the start/finish line for race day. All pre-registered participants will receive a tech shirt and we'll have some pizza, drinks, and snacks after the race so feel free to stick around. The event is held in conjunction with the Call of the Wilds Mountain Fest. ReVibe Gear is offering to sponsor all kids under the age of 14 who want to run the race.
