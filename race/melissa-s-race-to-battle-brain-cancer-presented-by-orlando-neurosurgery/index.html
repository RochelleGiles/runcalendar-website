About

Melissa's Race is a 5K run and walk in Sanford, Florida, presented by Orlando Neurosurgery and held each spring in memory of Melissa Vosburg. Entry fees support brain cancer research through the Melissa Vosburg Endowed Chair in Neuro-Oncology Research.
