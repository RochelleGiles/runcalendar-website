Course

A point-to-point road marathon from Lake Village, Arkansas to Greenville, Mississippi, with buses departing the EE Bass Cultural Arts Center on Main Street. The marathon carries a 7-hour cutoff; the event also hosts a half, half relay, 5K and the Delta Double Down combo.

About

The Mississippi River Marathon offers the only marathon in the Mississippi Delta and a variety of opportunities that no other race in the world can offer. Our goal is first, for people across the nation, especially in the Delta communities, to get excited about healthy lifestyles by supporting and participating in our running events. Second, this marathon was created to help all children receive an excellent education by donating funds raised to the Washington County and Southeast Arkansas Community Foundations.
