Course

Run through the Southern Kettles of Wisconsin, 100 percent trail apart from a few road crossings, with 65 miles of the course following the Ice Age Trail. Distances of 100 miles, 100K, 50K and 38 miles are offered. Entry is by lottery and previous years have filled immediately.

About

This classic ultra marathon was founded by Kevin and Kris Setnes, who directed it for several years before Tim "Timo" Yanacheck and Jason Dorgan took over as co-race directors. These ultra running pioneers built traditions in the Kettle Moraine 100 that carry on today, inspiring generations of trail and ultra runners.
