Course

A single 14km loop through the Scribbly Gums Conservation Area at Redlands Track Park, directly across from the event precinct at Hilliard State School, Alexandra Hills. Teams and solo runners repeat the loop alternating direction each lap - anticlockwise, then clockwise - handing over a timing chip at the precinct between laps. Underfoot is maintained conservation-area trail.

About

The Unbroken Team Trail Marathon is an intense endurance challenge set in the Scribbly Gums Conservation Area. Teams of four or two compete by completing multiple 14km loops, pushing their limits through day and night. Participants can choose between a standard team marathon or an ultra edition, making this a unique and challenging trail running experience.
