Course

Run on the Historic Railroad Trail beside Lake Mead, starting and finishing on Lakeshore Road in Boulder City. The route follows an old railroad line through the Six Tunnels towards Hoover Dam, with open views over Lake Mead. Half marathon, 12K, 5K and 1 mile are offered.
