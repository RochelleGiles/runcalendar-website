About

Anjou'R & Nuit is a trail running weekend in the Loire vineyards of Anjou, between the Château de Brissac and Château de Serrant near Blaison-Saint-Sulpice. Distances of 12km, 24km and 36km run through vineyards, woods and countryside around the two châteaux.
