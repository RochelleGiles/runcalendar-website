About

A Can't Miss Fourth of July Celebration Join us for this Fourth of July themed 5K | 10K starting and finishing in downtown West Linn, OR. This festive race will bring you to a finish line full of vendors, drinks, food, and of course, a hot dog eating contest! Stay after and enjoy the local shops and restaurants on the main street of West Linn.
