About

The Medellin Marathon is a must-do event for runners seeking an unforgettable experience. Set against the stunning backdrop of Medellin, Colombia, this race takes participants on a scenic journey through the city's vibrant streets and neighborhoods. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time racer, the Medellin Marathon promises an exhilarating challenge and the chance to explore one of South America's most captivating destinations.
