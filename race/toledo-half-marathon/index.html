About

The Media Maratón & 10K Toledo is a running event that takes place in the city of Toledo, Spain. The event offers both a half marathon (21.1 km) and a 10K race, providing runners of all levels the opportunity to challenge themselves and experience the beautiful city of Toledo. The course showcases the city's historic landmarks and scenic landscapes, creating a unique and memorable running experience.
