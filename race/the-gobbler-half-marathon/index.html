Course

The half marathon is a double out-and-back that is 98% paved road, starting and finishing at Mereworth Farm with the first 0.2 mile and last 0.1 mile on gravel. The route runs through Bluegrass horse country and carries 10 hydration stations. The 10K and 5K use similar routing.
