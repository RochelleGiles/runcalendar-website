Course

Run on the singletrack network at the Whitewater Center on the Catawba River west of Charlotte. The half marathon covers two laps of the East Main trail with over 1,000 feet of climbing; the 5K is a single shorter loop. Two aid stations, East Main with water only and Prairie Dog with water and food.

About

Eastbound Half Marathon & 5K Trail Race runs through the U.S. National Whitewater Center in Charlotte, North Carolina. Runners cover the center's wooded singletrack trails along the Catawba River.
