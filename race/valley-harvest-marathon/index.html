About

The Valley Harvest Marathon runs through Nova Scotia's Annapolis Valley near Wolfville, a region of orchards and vineyards on the Bay of Fundy's Minas Basin. Distances range from 5K up to the marathon, plus an ultra option, each October at harvest time.
