About

Explore the picturesque Grange Coastal region of Adelaide, South Australia during the Coastal Fun Runs' annual Grange Coastal Run. This low-key event offers runners of all levels a chance to experience the stunning seaside scenery and laidback atmosphere of this beautiful part of the country.
