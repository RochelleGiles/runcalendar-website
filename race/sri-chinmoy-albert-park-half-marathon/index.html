Course

Multi-lap loop on a 5km closed circuit around Albert Park Lake. All races start and finish at Palms Lawn, next to the Athletics Stadium. The 5km is one lap, the 10km two laps and the half marathon four laps plus a 1.1km add-on during the first lap. Drink stations with water and electrolyte every 2.5km.

About

Sri Chinmoy's Albert Park Half Marathon is an annual event in Melbourne, Victoria. The event is conducted on a scenic 5km closed trail circuit around Albert Park Lake, with full runner services.
