Course

Flat out-and-back 5km starting at Wollongong Boat Harbour, joining the sealed blue mile shared path along the coast before turning and returning to finish near the harbour. The route is entirely on sealed road and paved shared path. Start is 8:00am with check-in from 6:30am, and there are water stations, course marshals and on-course safety services.

About

The Blue-Mile 5 Fun Run is a scenic and flat 5km course starting at Wollongong Boat Harbour. Runners will enjoy the coastal blue-mile shared path, historic continental pools, and North Beach before turning around at the UOW Innovation campus and finishing on the closed-off Cliff Road. The event is open to all ages and fitness levels, with a fun and supportive atmosphere.
