Course

A single-lap 5K and a 10K at Rock Park, Barnstaple, beside the River Taw. The route mixes tarmac, paving, grass and woodland, with an incline and decline at the Long Bridge underpass where the path narrows. Start and finish are on flat, firm grass at the end of Ladies Mile.

About

Race for Life's Barnstaple event runs through Rock Park, a riverside park beside the River Taw in the heart of Devon. Cancer Research UK's charity 10K and 5K are run, jogged or walked on a flat, traffic-free course along the water, raising funds for cancer research.
