Course

Lap course in Burrawan State Forest on forest trails and tracks. The 21.1 km is one lap and the 42.2 km is two laps. The 12 km route passes the Old Bottlebutt Tree, which has a 600 m loop walking track around it near the Burrawan picnic area. The 7 km is a faster, flatter course. No aid station detail is published.

About

Explore the rugged and scenic trails of the Bottlebutt Bash Trail Run, a challenging and unique running event set in the heart of the Australian wilderness. This annual race invites runners of all levels to conquer the stunning natural landscapes, with stunning views and a thrilling course that will test your endurance and determination.
