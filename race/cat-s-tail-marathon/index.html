Course

Point-to-point trail marathon of about 26.5 miles from the end of Fox Hollow Road in Shandaken over Panther, Slide, Cornell and Wittenberg mountains to Main Street in Phoenicia. Rugged, technical Catskills terrain including the Cornell Crack; four aid stations; 7:00 am start with an 11:30 am cutoff at Winnisook (mile 9.5) and 5:30 pm at the finish.

About

The Cat's Tail Marathon is an approximately 26 mile point to point trail race in the Slide Mountain Wilderness Area of the Catskills, hilly, rugged and technical. The course summits four Catskills High Peaks – Panther, Slide, Cornell, and Wittenberg Mountains, as well as Giant Ledge, Cross Mtn, Moun​t Pleasant, and Romer Mtn, with almost 7000 ft of gain. The descents are even more challenging than the climbs!
