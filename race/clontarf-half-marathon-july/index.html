About

The Joe Duffy Group Clontarf Half Marathon offers runners a breathtaking tour of Dublin's scenic coastline, showcasing the natural beauty of Ireland. This popular event returns to the Clontarf area, providing a challenging yet rewarding course for both seasoned runners and those new to the half marathon distance.
