Course

An undulating but not hilly road 10K running anticlockwise from and back to the Market Square in Easingwold, past the Medieval Butter Cross and the Georgian houses of Uppleby with wide views across the Vale of York. The first half climbs more than it drops and the second half reverses that, with a short sharp rise just after 8km before the run home across Millfield.

About

Explore the picturesque streets and rolling countryside of Easingwold, just 12 miles north of York, at the annual Easingwold 10K. This undulating scenic route showcases the charming market town and its surrounding natural beauty, making it a must-run event for runners seeking a challenging yet rewarding experience.
