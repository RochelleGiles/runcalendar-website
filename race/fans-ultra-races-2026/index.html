Course

A timed event on a certified loop of under two miles on park paths at Normandale Lake Park, Bloomington. Runners repeat the loop for the duration they entered. Two aid stations on the loop supply water, sports drink, soda, fruit, sweets, bars, cakes and crisps, with pizza and soup added later in the event.

About

FANS Ultra Races is an annual event held at Normandale Lake Park in Bloomington, Minnesota. The event supports Pillsbury United Communities, a registered 501c3 non-profit.
