About

Hanover Township Office of Community Health is proud to host the Township's 11th annual Sprint 2 Spring 5K Run/Walk on the first Saturday in May. This event takes place at the Hanover Township Izaak Walton Youth Center, where participants can run or walk in the race, visit the sponsorship tables, enjoy complimentary snacks and beverages, and await to see if you placed for a medal at the medal ceremony!
