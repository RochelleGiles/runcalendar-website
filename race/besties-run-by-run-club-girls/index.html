Course

A scenic coastal course on the Long Beach beach path, run as a 5K, 10K or half marathon from 5400 E Ocean Blvd. Runners, walkers, stroller users and dogs are all welcome, with 3.5 hours allowed to finish. Two children's races, a half mile and a one miler, run mid-morning.

About

A celebration of sisterhood and female empowerment featuring 5K, 10K, and half marathon distances along Long Beach's scenic coastal path. The event includes chip timing, finisher medals, a live DJ, finish line party, and is designed with special 'Besties' duo registration options.
