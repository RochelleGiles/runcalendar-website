Course

Point-to-point along the Federation Trail between Romsey and Lancefield, a car-free rail-trail with hard-packed pea gravel underfoot. The 7.5 km starts at the Romsey Hotel and runs east over gentle rolling hills into a gradual descent to the Lancefield Hotel. The 15 km starts at Lancefield, loops around Lions Park near Romsey and returns, climbing steadily to the 10 km mark before dropping to the finish.

About

The Pub to Pub Run runs point-to-point between the country towns of Romsey and Lancefield in Victoria's Macedon Ranges, with the longer 15km 'Pub to Pub to Pub' course linking three pubs along the way.
