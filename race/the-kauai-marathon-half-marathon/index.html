About

The Kauai Marathon & Half Marathon is an annual running event held on the island of Kauai, Hawaii. The event features both a full marathon and a half marathon distance, as well as a Keiki (children's) run. Runners can enjoy the scenic beauty of Kauai's lush landscapes and coastlines during the race.
