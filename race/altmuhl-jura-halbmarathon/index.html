About

The Altmuhl Jura Halbmarathon is a scenic half-marathon that winds through the stunning Altmuhl Valley and Jura region of Germany. Runners will be treated to breathtaking views of rolling hills, limestone cliffs, and the winding Altmuhl River as they challenge themselves on this unique course. This event is a must for any runner seeking an unforgettable European running adventure.
