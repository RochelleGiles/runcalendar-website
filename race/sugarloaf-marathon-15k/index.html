Course

Point-to-point road race. The marathon starts at Cathedral Pines Campground, 945 Arnold Trail, Eustis, with race headquarters at Sugarloaf Resort in Carrabassett Valley. The 15K starts at Ayotte's Country Store, 1215 Carrabassett Drive, Carrabassett Valley. The course is USATF-certified and Boston-qualifying, with a 1,500-runner field limit.

About

The Sugarloaf Marathon and 15K run through the Carrabassett Valley beneath Sugarloaf Mountain in western Maine, following valley roads along the Carrabassett River on a rolling course each May.
