Course

An out-and-back road race on Highway 128 beside the Colorado River, starting and finishing at Lions Park in Moab with red rock walls and Arches National Park alongside. The highway closes to traffic for the race. Six aid stations with water and Gatorade Endurance, plus medical support on course and at the finish.

About

The Canyonlands Half Marathon offers a half marathon and 5-mile race through the red rock desert landscape surrounding Moab, Utah, near Canyonlands National Park. The event is organized by Mad Moose Events.
