About

Dave's 10-Miler was started in 1974 by Dave himself and has happened every year regardless of the unpredictable weather. Join us in this historic event and take on the county roads of Delta... who knows, you may even spot a yeti lurking in the fields.. The course begins and finishes at Pike Delta York Middle School and loops through the beautiful countryside of Delta. After the race, fill your belly with a great hot lunch and shower inside the Middle School. Choose between the 10 Miler or 10K, both will be sure to have you dancing with the Yeti!
