Course

A circuitous, well-marked route through the mostly unlit wooded South Loop of Knoxville's Urban Wilderness, using the Lost Chromosome and Helix trails. Start and finish at Anderson-South Head Start on Prospect Road. Run at night; the route is marked but not illuminated, and runners must carry a headlamp or torch.

About

Hann Jivin' in the Dark 6 Miler and Kids Trail Mile. This is a night race with a circuitous, well-marked route in the mostly unlit wooded environs of the South Loop of Knoxville's Urban Wilderness. The Kids Trail Mile is a miniaturized, less dark version of the adult course.
