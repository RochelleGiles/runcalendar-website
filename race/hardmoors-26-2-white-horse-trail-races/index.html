Course

Unmarked trail routes from Sutton Bank Visitor Centre near Thirsk on the North York Moors, navigated with the organiser's maps and route descriptions. The marathon runs about 29 miles with roughly 3,400 ft of ascent; the half is 16.5 miles and the 10K about 7. Checkpoints are cupless, so runners carry their own cup.

About

Explore the stunning North Yorkshire countryside at the Hardmoors 26.2 White Horse Trail Races. This challenging trail event offers a marathon, half marathon, and 10k distance, allowing runners of all levels to conquer the rugged terrain and magnificent views of the White Horse Trail. An unmissable adventure for anyone seeking an exceptional off-road running experience.
