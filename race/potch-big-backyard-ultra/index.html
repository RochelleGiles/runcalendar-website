Course

Run on a 6.706km loop at Lekwena Wildlife Estate, 10km outside Potchefstroom, over a mix of trail and road with about 80m of gain and loss a lap. Entries are a Backyard Half (four yards or fewer, 26.8km), a Backyard Marathon (seven yards or fewer, 46.9km) or the full unlimited Backyard Ultra.

About

Potch Big Backyard Ultra is a last-runner-standing backyard ultra race in Potchefstroom, North West. Competitors repeat a fixed loop every hour until only one remains, in the global Backyard Ultra format.
