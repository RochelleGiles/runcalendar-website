Course

Roughly 20-mile loops on the Switchgrass Mountain Bike Trail in Wilson State Park, starting and finishing at the same trailhead: one loop for the 20-mile, three for the 100K, five for the 100-mile and seven for the 140. Surface is rugged singletrack, marked with ribbons. Restrooms at the start/finish and at the Hell Hill aid station.

About

All runs will take place on the Switchgrass Mountain Bike Trail at Wilson State Park. The 100 has the most elevation gain and loss of any 100-miler in Kansas! If you're looking for a challenging course in the "flatlands" you've found it. The trail is easy-to-follow, rugged, single-track with very few trees/wooded areas. Beautiful views of Wilson State Park and Wilson Lake are abundant.
