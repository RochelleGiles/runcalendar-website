Course

A 6.7 km lap in Wyre Forest at Callow Hill near Bewdley, run on the hour every hour for up to 24 hours in last-one-standing format. The terrain is undulating forest track with stony sections and single-track paths. Runners set their own distance by lap count, from 10k through marathon and beyond; the closing lap is raced.

About

The Lone Wolf Birmingham is a last one standing endurance trail running event set in the picturesque Wyre Forest. Runners will push their limits on this challenging course, navigating the rugged terrain and natural beauty of the forest. This unique event is a true test of mental and physical fortitude, making it a must-try for experienced trail runners seeking an unforgettable adventure.
