About

The Run/Walk for WeSPARK is an annual 5K and 10K run and walk in Los Angeles raising funds for the WeSPARK Cancer Support Center. Entries are handled through RunSignUp, with pre-registration closing the day before the race and walk-up entry available on race morning.
