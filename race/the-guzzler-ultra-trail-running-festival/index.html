Course

Three of Brisbane's oldest reservoirs — Enoggera, Gold Creek and Lake Manchester — linked in the order they were built, starting and finishing around Mt Coot-tha. Courses are 99% trail, hilly and well marked. The 100km visits all three across Saturday night into Sunday; the 50km covers two, and the 21km and 10km each take in one on the Sunday.

About

The Guzzler Ultra Trail Running Festival takes runners through three of Brisbane's oldest reservoirs: Enoggera, Gold Creek, and Lake Manchester. Participants will explore the green heart of Brisbane at Mt Coot-tha, experiencing a historic trail running journey.
