About

The Life Time 305 Half Marathon & 5K presented by Baptist Health is all about local vibes, attracting visitors from all over the world and those who are fired up to call Miami their home. Once known as the 13.1 Miami Beach & 5K, this ultimate Miami experience is a cultural gem that will have you running like you live here. Count on a fast and coastal course with a fresh and vibrant Miami soul.
