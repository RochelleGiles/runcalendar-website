Course

Three sealed-road routes winding out through the neighbourhoods surrounding Waunakee, Wisconsin, with water stations on course. The Ted Tweed 10 Mile is the feature race, alongside a 5K run, a 2 mile walk and a kiddie run.

About

The WaunaFest Run is an annual event brought to you by the Waunakee Area Chamber of Commerce. It features a 10-mile run, 5K run, 2-mile walk, and kiddie run.
