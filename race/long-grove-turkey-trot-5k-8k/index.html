Course

Start and finish at 308 Old McHenry Rd, Long Grove, on a course the organiser says is almost entirely on paved streets. The route runs through historic downtown Long Grove and finishes under the famous covered bridge. The 5K sets off in two pace-based waves alongside the 8K.

About

The Long Grove Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving Day running event featuring chip-timed 5K and 8K races through historic downtown Long Grove, along with untimed 5K and kids fun run options. Participants finish under the famous covered bridge and enjoy post-race treats like Apple Haus donuts, hot apple cider, and beer.
