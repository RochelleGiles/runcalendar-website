About

The Cabourg Half Marathon is a picturesque coastal running event held in the charming seaside town of Cabourg, France. Runners can enjoy stunning views of the English Channel as they navigate the scenic route, making this a must-do race for those seeking a scenic and memorable half marathon experience.
