About

The Other NYC Marathon Rockaway Ocean Marathon is a full marathon race held on the scenic Rockaway Beach Boardwalk in Far Rockaway, NY. It offers runners a unique ocean-side experience as an alternative to the NYC Marathon, with a supportive community atmosphere.
