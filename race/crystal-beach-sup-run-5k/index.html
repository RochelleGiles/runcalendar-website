About

A multi-sport race at Crystal Beach on Florida's Pinellas coast, run as three separate entries: a 5K run, a 5K stand-up paddleboard race and a 10K combining both. Everything starts and finishes at Live Oak Park on Crystal Beach Avenue at 7:30 in the morning and wraps by 10. Proceeds go to Crystal Beach Community Enrichment.
