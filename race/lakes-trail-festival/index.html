Course

Point-to-point and loop trail courses based at Hawks Nest on the NSW north coast, running through Myall Lakes National Park. Each course follows existing tracks and trails, beaches, lake edges and single track, taking in forest tracks, sand dunes and rocky headlands through remote coastal country. Distances range from 12km to 100km, with a kids' 1km.

About

The Lakes Trail Festival runs through Myall Lakes National Park near Hawks Nest, tracing coastal dunes, forest trails and lakeshore paths. Distances range from a 12km taster to the flagship 100km ultramarathon.
