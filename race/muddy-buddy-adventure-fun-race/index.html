Course

An obstacle fun race in the Moutere Inlet at Tasman, where entrants choose one, two or three laps of roughly 1km each. The route runs through tidal mud and the organiser warns of sharp shells underfoot, so sturdy shoes taped to the feet are required. Sign-in runs from 10.30am for a 12.30pm start.

About

The annual Muddy Buddy Adventure Fun Race at Tasman School in New Zealand is the ultimate mud-packed adventure for all ages and abilities. Runners can expect to get down and dirty as they navigate obstacles and challenges on a course designed to push their limits. This unique event offers a thrilling experience for those seeking a fun, messy challenge in the great outdoors.
