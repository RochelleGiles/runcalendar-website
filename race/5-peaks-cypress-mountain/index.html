About

5 Peaks Cypress Mountain is a trail race held at the Cypress Mountain ski resort in British Columbia's North Shore mountains, near West Vancouver. Part of the long-running 5 Peaks trail series, it offers Sport, Enduro and Grit distance categories on the resort's summer trails.
