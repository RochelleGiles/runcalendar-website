Course

A five-stage ultra of roughly 130km over seven days in the Sahara around Merzouga, run semi-self-sufficient. Stages cross golden dunes over 150m high, rocky plateaus and wild valleys. Each evening runners camp under the stars or stay in a hotel, with showers and a traditional meal. Open to runners and walkers.

About

Explore the vast, awe-inspiring Sahara Desert on the Sahara Desert Trail Morocco, a captivating ultra-running event that takes participants through the heart of this iconic landscape. Challenge yourself across a breathtaking route that showcases the region's stunning natural beauty, from rolling dunes to rugged mountain passes. This bucket-list adventure offers runners an unparalleled opportunity to immerse themselves in the rich culture and traditions of Morocco.
