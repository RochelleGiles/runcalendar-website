Course

The half marathon starts at Family Park and finishes at Fawick Park in Sioux Falls. Aid stations are placed roughly every 2 miles with water, Gatorade and carbohydrate fuel, and pace teams run from 1:30 to 2:50. The Zippy Mile starts near Cherry Rock Park and shares the Fawick Park finish.

About

This spring we Skedaddle! Hosted by 605 Running Company, the Sioux Falls Skedaddle Half Marathon is more than just a race—it's a celebration of community, movement, and momentum in the heart of Sioux Falls, South Dakota.
