Course

A traffic-free 10K on relatively flat tarmac in Regent's Park, run as three laps from Race HQ on The Broad Walk. The route passes London Zoo and the boating lake and heads down the famous Broad Walk towards the fountain. A two-mile fun run covers one lap, with a junior run of about 700m alongside.
