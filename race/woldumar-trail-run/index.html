Course

Run entirely on natural surfaces with no pavement, across more than 180 acres of Woldumar Nature Center. The route passes through prairie, pine forest, wetland and hardwood forest and runs along the Grand River. Terrain is rugged and varied; pre-registration is required with no race-day entry.

About

Runners, walkers, and nature enthusiasts! You are invited to enjoy the beauty of fall on the trails at Woldumar Nature Center. Join us on Sunday, November 1st for the 18th Annual Woldumar Trail Run presented by Lake Trust Credit Union! The Woldumar Nature Association is a Lansing, MI based nonprofit that engages over 7,000 students in outdoor education each year. You'll enjoy the beauty of our prairie, pine forest, wetlands, and hardwood forest as you run along the Grand River on over 180 acres protected by the Association.
