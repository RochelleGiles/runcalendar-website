About

Join us for a 50k, 50k relay, 26.2m, 13.1m, 10k, 5k, or a 1-mile kids' run! This is a gorgeous place to run with views of Las Vegas and the south Las Vegas Mountains! We are excited to recycle our inventory from our awesome previous races! Since this is a flat course, it is great for a PR!
