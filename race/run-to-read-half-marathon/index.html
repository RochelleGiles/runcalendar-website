Course

A half marathon based at Prickett's Fort State Park near Fairmont, run as a double out-and-back on two rail trails. The first section is around five miles of pavement passing through the historic restored Meredith Tunnel; the second is crushed limestone with views over the Monongahela River.

About

A half marathon, 10K and 5K in Fairmont, West Virginia, now in its third decade of raising funds for children's literacy programs through the Run to Read organization, held each January in north-central West Virginia.
