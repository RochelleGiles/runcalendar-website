About

The Tralee 10 Mile Road Race in County Kerry, Ireland offers runners a scenic tour through the picturesque countryside. This event is part of the renowned Tralee Marathon Series, providing a challenging yet rewarding experience for both seasoned and aspiring long-distance athletes. Runners can look forward to a well-organized race with stunning views along the route.
