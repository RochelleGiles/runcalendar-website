Course

Both distances start and finish at Great Meadow Park in the Briar Chapel community and loop through its 900-acre trail network. Each course opens with just under half a mile of pavement before turning onto a mix of undulating double-track dirt and flowy singletrack with roots, rocks and short climbs. The 5K has one on-course aid station and the 10K three, plus one at the finish.

About

Welcome to the 2nd Annual Leftovers Trail Run, happening Saturday, November 28 — two days after Thanksgiving and our Birds of a Feather Trail Trot. Choose a 5K or 10K and enjoy the scenic, fun, and challenging single-track trails of Briar Chapel.
