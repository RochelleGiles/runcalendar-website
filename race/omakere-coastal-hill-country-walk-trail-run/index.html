About

The Omakere Coastal Hill Country Walk & Trail Run returns to Central Hawke's Bay on 17 October 2026. The event offers 6km coastal, 12km inland, or an 18km combination route, with post-race festivities including live music and local market stalls. It is hosted by the Omakere School & Community Association to support the school.
