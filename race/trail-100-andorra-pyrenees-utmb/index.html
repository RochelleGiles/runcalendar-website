About

Trail 100 Andorra by UTMB is an exciting trail running expedition into the mighty Pyrenees. The event offers multiple distance options to suit all runner levels, allowing participants to test their limits while exploring the beauty and rich cultural heritage of Andorra. The race features challenging elevation gains across stunning mountain terrain, with routes ranging from 10km to 105km.
