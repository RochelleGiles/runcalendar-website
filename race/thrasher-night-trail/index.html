Course

A night trail race on an 11km loop at Cave Creek Regional Park, starting and finishing at the Horse Staging Area group picnic ground and using the Slate, Quartz, Go John and Overton trails. The terrain is rolling desert singletrack with fast smooth sections and harder mountainous ones. The 33K runs three laps and the 22K two, through the start/finish each time. A stocked aid station sits at the start/finish plus one remote station passed every lap.

About

Thrasher Night Trail is a night trail running event held at Cave Creek Regional Park featuring fun and fast courses on rolling desert trails. Distances include 33K, 22K, 11K, and 5K, with chip timing, aid stations, and finisher awards.[1]
