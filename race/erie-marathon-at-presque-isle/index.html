Course

Flat, fast marathon run within Presque Isle State Park on the Lake Erie peninsula, starting and finishing at the Erie Runners Club Beach 1 Pavilion. The course is mostly asphalt with about a third concrete, and a grass verge runs alongside much of it. Total elevation change is under 250 feet. Boston qualifier with a six-hour limit.

About

The Erie Marathon loops the Presque Isle peninsula on Lake Erie, a flat, fast course popular with Boston Qualifier hopefuls. Half marathon and marathon relay options share the same lakeside route.
