Course

Out-and-back 10K on Blackpool's traffic-free promenade, starting at The Savoy Hotel on Queens Promenade, running south to the Pleasure Beach and back north to finish at The Savoy. The whole route is on paved promenade alongside the sea.

About

Lace up your running shoes and join the Beaverbrooks Blackpool 10K Fun Run, a scenic jaunt along the stunning coastline of this vibrant seaside town. Runners will enjoy a traffic-free route, taking in the iconic sights of Blackpool's promenade and beaches. Whether you're an experienced racer or a casual jogger, this event offers a fun and picturesque challenge for all.
