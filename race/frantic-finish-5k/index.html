Course

A 5K run of roughly three loops inside Olander Park, starting and finishing at Nederhouser Community Hall, 6930 W Sylvania Avenue, Sylvania, Ohio. Runners are set off in a staggered start based on their predicted finish time, so that in theory the whole field finishes together.

About

The Frantic Finish 5K is run in Olander Park in Sylvania, Ohio, over about three loops, starting and finishing at Nederhouser Community Hall. A brief club meeting and the traditional post-race meal and refreshments follow.
