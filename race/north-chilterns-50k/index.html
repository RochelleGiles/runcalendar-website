Course

A single 50km trail loop from the Putteridge Bury estate near Luton, climbing 798m over the North Chilterns ridgeline past Warden Hill and the Barton and Pegsdon Hills. The route is fully marked, capped at 250 runners, with a time limit of 8 hours 45 minutes.

About

The North Chilterns 50K Trail Run covers a unique loop course, exploring the best views and countryside in the area. Starting from Putteridge Bury, runners will traverse surrounding trails with stunning viewpoints including Warden Hill, Barton Hills, and Pegsdon Hills.
