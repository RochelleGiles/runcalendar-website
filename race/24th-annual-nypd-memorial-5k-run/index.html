About

This race is run in honor of all NYPD Officers who have died in the line of duty. There are more than 1,100 NYPD heroes whose names have been etched into the granite New York City Police Memorial Wall in Battery Park City. We gather yearly to honor these brave men and women who have made the ultimate sacrifice.
