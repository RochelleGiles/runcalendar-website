Course

Four out-and-back legs radiating from Meonstoke Village Hall in Hampshire's Meon Valley, on well-trodden public trails including the South Downs Way and old railway lines, with short road sections linking sections. Runners choose 10km, 30km, 50km or 70km. Checkpoints carry water and snacks, including vegan and dairy-free options.

About

Discover the scenic Meon Valley in Hampshire as you take on the Winter X, a mid-winter trail-running adventure. Conquer the challenging terrain and breathtaking landscapes of this unique event, set against the backdrop of the United Kingdom's picturesque countryside. Challenge yourself and experience the thrill of off-road running in the heart of winter.
