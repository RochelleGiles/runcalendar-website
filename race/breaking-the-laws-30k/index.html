Course

Three-leg 30K starting and finishing in North Berwick, East Lothian, with 640 metres of ascent over roads, trails and hill sections. The route runs to East Linton on the John Muir Way, climbs Traprain Law, then returns over North Berwick Law. Fully marshalled with hydration stations. Run solo or as a relay team of three, with a 10K walk alongside.

About

Breaking The Laws is a 30km mixed-terrain running event based in North Berwick, East Lothian, with 640 metres of ascent across road, trail and hill sections. It is fully marshalled and can be run solo or as a relay team of three. A scenic 10km circular walk around North Berwick and Balgone runs alongside it.
