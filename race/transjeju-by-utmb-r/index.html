About

Explore the stunning landscapes of Jeju Island at the TransJeju by UTMB® race. This challenging ultra-marathon traverses the volcanic terrain, offering runners breathtaking views and a truly immersive experience in nature. Whether you're a seasoned ultra-runner or seeking a new adventure, this event promises an unforgettable journey through one of South Korea's most captivating destinations.
