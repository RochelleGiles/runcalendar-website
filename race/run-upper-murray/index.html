Course

High-country trail races out of the Upper Murray, crossing farmland and native forest with Mt Elliot as the centrepiece. The 33K is a single loop with repeated climbs and descents finishing over the summit; the 16K is a 6km out-and-back to the top and the 9.5K packs 440m of climbing into a short, technical descent. A 3K kids race runs alongside.

About

Run Upper Murray is a trail event through the Thowgla Valley in Victoria's High Country, with distances from 3km to 33km following the upper reaches of the Murray River near its source in the alpine foothills.
