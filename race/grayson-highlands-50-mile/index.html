Course

Mountain trails through Grayson Highlands State Park in southwest Virginia, crossing high alpine balds grazed by the park's wild ponies. Climbing is steep and sustained: about 7,500 feet for the 50 mile and 4,500 for the 50K, with 25K and 8-mile options on the same terrain. Cutoffs run from 4 to 16 hours.

About

Grayson Highlands 50 Mile climbs through Grayson Highlands State Park in the Virginia mountains, sharing high alpine balds with the park's free-roaming wild pony herds. Distances range from an 8-mile taster to the full 50-mile mountain ultra, all starting and finishing near Mouth of Wilson.
