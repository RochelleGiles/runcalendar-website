About

With the mountains of Malcata, Mesas and Gata (Spain) as a backdrop, the Trail da Raia trails guarantee a total immersion in nature. The routes take us past ancient watermills, rock-carved tombs, a suspended bridge, and an athlete statue in honor of all who pass through. The rural paths rich in local experiences, the smugglers' trails, cross the border and make this adventure the best pretext to enjoy the vast landscapes, doing what we love most: running up and down hills amidst oaks, chestnuts, pines, eucalyptus, and broom.
