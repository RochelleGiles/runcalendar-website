Course

A double lap of the pathways around Alexandra Park, Hastings, with a few inclines, bridges and woodland sections and a maximum gradient of 15%. Surfaces mix hardstanding and woodland path; the start and finish are on grass that can turn uneven or muddy in the wet.

About

Cancer Research UK's Race for Life 5K runs through Alexandra Park, Hastings' Victorian valley park with its ponds, woodland and cascading gardens. The flat, traffic-free course is run, jogged or walked to raise money for cancer research.
