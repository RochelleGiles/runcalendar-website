Course

A trail race in Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park, starting and finishing at Bobbin Head about 30 minutes north of Sydney. Three distances of 12km, 22km and 45km are offered, plus a Triple Trail Challenge combining all three. The organiser describes sandstone cliffs, waterways and bushland on an extensive single-track network. Aid station detail is not stated.

About

The Jabulani Challenge offers runners a stunning trail adventure through the lush Ku-Ring-Gai Chase National Park, just north of Sydney. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of this protected wilderness as you take on the challenge of navigating the winding trails and diverse terrain. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Jabulani Challenge promises an unforgettable running experience.
