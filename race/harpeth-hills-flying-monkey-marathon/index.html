Course

Run in Percy Warner Park, Nashville, over rolling hills through dense woods. The course is uncertified and is not a Boston qualifier. The field is capped at about 300 runners, allocated by a weighted lottery.

About

The Harpeth Hills Flying Monkey Marathon runs on paved and gravel roads through Nashville's Percy Warner Park. Known for its relentless climbs, it's widely regarded as one of the toughest road marathons in the US, with a half marathon option too.
