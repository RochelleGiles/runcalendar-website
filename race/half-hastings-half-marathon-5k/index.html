Course

The half marathon starts at Hastings Middle School and finishes at Hastings College. The 5K starts near the Bronco statue at the Hastings College arena and finishes at the same place. The half carries six aid stations spaced roughly every two miles and the 5K carries two. Surface is not stated.

About

Half Hastings is a half marathon and 5K presented by Mary Lanning Healthcare, encouraging individuals, families and groups in Hastings, Nebraska, to get moving together. With multiple distances to run or walk, there's something for everyone.
