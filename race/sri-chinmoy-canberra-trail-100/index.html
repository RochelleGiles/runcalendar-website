Course

A 100km course through the hills, nature parks and open spaces of central, south and north Canberra, starting and finishing at the Rond Terraces at the foot of Anzac Parade on Lake Burley Griffin. It can be run solo or as a relay team of two to four.

About

The Sri Chinmoy Canberra Trail 100 is a 100km trail ultramarathon in the hills around Canberra, organised by the Sri Chinmoy Marathon Team, testing endurance over a single long day on the trails.
