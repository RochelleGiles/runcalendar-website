Course

Run entirely inside the Bellevue Underground Mine in the Crowsnest Pass, on a 250 m shaft at a 1% grade giving a 500 m out-and-back loop. The shaft is about 4 m wide and sits at a constant 0-2C year round. Runners complete as many loops as they can inside their chosen time limit.

About

A 24-hour timed race run entirely underground in a real coal mine near Bellevue, Alberta, with runners completing as many laps through the tunnels as they can in a full day.
