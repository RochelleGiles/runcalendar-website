Course

Evening 5K and 10K run on the roads in and around Dodger Stadium, with views over the Downtown LA skyline. Both courses finish with a cool-down lap on the warning track of UNIQLO Field inside the stadium. A Kids Fun Run and a virtual option are also offered.

About

The LA Dodgers Foundation Sunset Run takes runners onto the roads around Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles for a 5K or 10K as the sun sets over Chavez Ravine. Proceeds support the Dodgers Foundation's youth and community programs.
