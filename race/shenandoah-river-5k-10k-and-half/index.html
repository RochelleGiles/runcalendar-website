Course

Held at Shenandoah River State Park near Bentonville. The paved 5K is hilly with about 200ft of climb and starts at 9:30am, while a flatter trail 5K sets off at 10:45am. A 10K and a half marathon are also run.

About

The Shenandoah River 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon start near Bentonville, Virginia, close to Shenandoah River State Park. The courses follow the river valley in the shadow of the Blue Ridge Mountains.
