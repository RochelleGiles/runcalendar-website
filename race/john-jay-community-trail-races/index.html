Course

The 5k runs on the John Jay Community Trail at the John Jay campus in Cross River, New York, a wooded trail network that includes bridges, boardwalks and a causeway. A 4k walk/hike and a 200m kids dash use the same trails. Start and finish are at the campus.

About

The John Jay Community Trail Races are an annual event featuring a 5k trail race, 4k walk/hike, and 200m kids dash. The races take place on the John Jay Community Trail, a scenic trail system on the campus of John Jay High School.
