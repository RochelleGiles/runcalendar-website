Course

A cross-country 5K run/walk through the orchard at Akash Winery & Vineyards, Temecula, weaving back and forth between the tree rows. The surface is mostly well-groomed grass with some uneven patches. Two waves start at 9:00am and 11:00am; leashed dogs and running strollers are allowed.

About

This 5K run/walk event at Akash Winery & Vineyards combines running or walking with wine tasting in Temecula's wine country. Participants receive a T-shirt, shatterproof wine glass, finisher medal, chip-timed bib, online results, and a pour of wine.
