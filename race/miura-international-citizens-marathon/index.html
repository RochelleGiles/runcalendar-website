About

The Miura International Citizens Marathon has run on the Miura Peninsula in Kanagawa since 1981, starting and finishing on the sand at Miura Kaigan beach. The half marathon, 10km and 5km courses cross the Jogashima Bridge past fishing villages, farmland and rocky coastline with Mt. Fuji views.
