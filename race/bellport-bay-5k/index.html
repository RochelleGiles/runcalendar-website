Course

A measured 5K through the village of Bellport, starting and finishing at the Bellport Bandshell on Bellport Lane, with water stops on route. Strollers are not permitted. A free kids' fun run is held alongside.

About

The Bellport Bay 5K is an early-evening summer race organized by the Bellport Village Program Fund (BVPF), with a Fun Run for kids of all ages alongside it. Proceeds from registrations and sponsorships, after race-related expenses, benefit the BVPF.
