Course

The USATF-certified marathon and half marathon start and finish at Field of Dreams Stadium Track on Tashiro Road, running at about 3,900 feet through pecan orchards with views of the Organ Mountains and the Rio Grande. The course is flat and fast and is a Boston qualifier. A four-person relay, a 4.7K and a kids' dash use the same finish.

About

The State 47 Las Cruces Marathon takes its name from New Mexico's 1912 admission as the 47th state. Runners can choose the marathon, half marathon, 4.7K, or a four-person relay through Las Cruces in the high desert of the Mesilla Valley.
