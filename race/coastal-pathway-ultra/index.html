Course

An out-and-back course on the sealed North West Coastal Pathway in Tasmania, running from Latrobe to Ulverstone and back with the coast alongside for much of the way. Shorter distances turn earlier on the same pathway, and the 64km can be run solo or as a team relay.

About

The Coastal Pathway Ultra takes runners on a breathtaking journey along the rugged North West Coast of Tasmania. Featuring stunning ocean vistas and challenging terrain, this event offers an unforgettable ultra-running experience for adventurous athletes. Traverse picturesque beaches, winding trails, and dramatic cliffs as you push your limits in this stunning natural setting.
