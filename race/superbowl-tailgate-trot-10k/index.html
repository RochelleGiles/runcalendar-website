About

The Lehigh Valley Road Runners have staged this Allentown 10K on Super Bowl Sunday since 2002. The race starts and finishes at the club's Lehigh Parkway clubhouse and runs on roads that stay open to traffic, with intersections barricaded and marshalled by police and volunteers.
