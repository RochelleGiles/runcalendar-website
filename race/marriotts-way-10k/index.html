Course

A flat, mostly off-road point-to-point 10K following Marriott's Way, a disused railway line through the Norfolk countryside. It starts near race HQ at Woodgate Nursery in Aylsham and finishes in Reepham market place, on soft trail underfoot. Five waves set off at ten-minute intervals from 10:00.

About

A South Norfolk and Broadland council 10K along Marriott's Way, the disused railway line turned traffic-free path running out of Norwich. Now in its second decade, the race draws a large local field.
