Course

A lapped course in Prince William Forest Park starting from Cabin Camp 5. The first loop is 23 miles and subsequent loops 19.5 miles. Mostly singletrack with roots, rocks and short technical sections plus some fire road, rolling with no substantial climbs. Three stocked aid stations per loop plus three unmanned water points.

About

Devil Dog Ultras offers 50K, 100K, and 100-mile distances on single-track trails within Prince William Forest Park in Triangle, Virginia. Runners complete a 23-mile opening loop followed by shorter 19.5-mile repeat loops, passing aid stations named Camp Remi, Camp Gunny, and Camp Toofy. Entry is capped at 300 runners across all distances.
