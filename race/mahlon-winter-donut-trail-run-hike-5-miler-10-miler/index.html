Course

A true trail run on the rugged, mixed woodland terrain of the Mahlon Dickerson Reservation in Morris County, New Jersey. Runners follow trail-marker signage and are expected to be able to find their way back to the course if they stray. It goes ahead rain, snow or shine, over 5 and 10 mile distances.

About

The Mahlon Winter Donut Trail Run & Hike offers 5-mile and 10-mile trail running and hiking options through rugged, picturesque woodland trails at Mahlon Dickerson Reservation in Lake Hopatcong, NJ. Participants receive a long-sleeved shirt, medal, and donuts at the finish.
