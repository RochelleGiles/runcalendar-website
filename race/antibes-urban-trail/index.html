About

The Antibes Urban Trail is a unique running event that takes participants on a scenic journey through the picturesque city of Antibes. Winding through historic streets and along the stunning Mediterranean coastline, this challenging trail offers runners a chance to experience the beauty and vibrancy of this French Riviera destination.
