Course

A coastal road race starting at Club Mykonos Resort in Langebaan, with the Langebaan lagoon on one side and the open Atlantic on the other. Runners can walk to the start from the resort. Three distances are offered: 21 km, 10 km and 5 km.

About

The Mykonos Half Marathon starts at Club Mykonos on the shores of Langebaan Lagoon on South Africa's West Coast, offering half marathon, 10km and 5km distances for runners of all levels.
