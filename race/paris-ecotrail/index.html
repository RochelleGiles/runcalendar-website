About

The Paris Ecotrail is a premier trail running event that invites runners to explore the natural beauty of the Île-de-France region. With a variety of distances to choose from, the event caters to both beginners and experienced trail runners, offering a unique opportunity to experience the city's iconic landmarks and scenic landscapes.
