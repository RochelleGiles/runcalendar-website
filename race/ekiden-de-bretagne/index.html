About

The Ekiden de Bretagne is a captivating running event that takes place in the picturesque region of Brittany, France. This unique relay race challenges teams to navigate a scenic course, showcasing the stunning landscapes and rich cultural heritage of the area. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Ekiden de Bretagne offers an unforgettable experience for all participants.
