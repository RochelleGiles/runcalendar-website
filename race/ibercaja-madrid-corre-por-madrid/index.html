About

The Ibercaja Madrid Corre Por Madrid is a special edition of the annual running event, celebrating the Hispanic heritage and cultural diversity. The race will take place on the streets of Madrid, the heart of the Hispanic world, inviting runners from all over the world who share the pride and connection of the Spanish language and culture. Each stride will be a gesture of unity, and every kilometer a bridge that connects America to Europe.
