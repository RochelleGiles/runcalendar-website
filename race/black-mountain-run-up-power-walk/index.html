Course

The run climbs from the car park on Clunies Ross Street, Acton (575 m) to the Black Mountain summit (811 m) - about 236 m of climb over 2.6 km. It starts on flat 4WD trail beside the Botanic Gardens, follows Frith Road uphill, then joins the Summit Trail through Canberra Nature Park. The surface is a mix of trail and fire road.

About

Challenge yourself on the rugged trails of Black Mountain at the monthly Black Mountain Run Up & Power Walk in Australia. This unique event offers both a running race and a power walking option, allowing participants to conquer the steep inclines and stunning natural scenery at their own pace. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or a fitness enthusiast, this event promises an exhilarating outdoor experience.
