Course

Trail loops in the Dandenong Ranges above Melbourne, based at Kalorama. The 23km covers a single loop and the 46km ultra runs it twice; a shorter 12km twilight event and a kids' race use the same trails. The course is named for its repeated climbs and descents.

About

Explore the breathtaking Dandenong Ranges on the Roller Coaster Trail Run, a challenging mountain trail event just outside Melbourne, Victoria. Runners will navigate a scenic course filled with rolling hills, lush forests, and stunning views, making this an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an exhilarating adventure through the heart of Australia's natural beauty.
