Course

Point-to-point from Boyds Tower to Green Cape Lighthouse in Beowa National Park. The route crosses coastal forest, heathland, beaches and rocky headlands, and includes technical rock scrambles and exposed heathland sections. No aid stations are listed.

About

The WinterSun Light to Light Trail Run is a spectacular 33km coastal adventure along the stunning Sapphire Coast of New South Wales. Runners will be treated to breathtaking ocean vistas, lush native bushland, and the opportunity to experience the beauty of this remote Australian landscape. This event is a must-do for trail running enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable challenge and a connection with the natural world.
