Course

Starts and finishes at the Grandiosa Pavilion in Tradewinds Park on West Sample Road, Coconut Creek. Adult and youth divisions both set off at 7:00am and are timed from the start line to the finish. Walkers and strollers are welcome and line up at the back of the field.

About

The City of Coconut Creek Parks and Recreation Department hosts this annual USA Track & Field certified 5K race at Tradewinds Park, welcoming runners and walkers of all ages. Participants enjoy chip-timing, awards, long-sleeve shirts for the first 700 registrants, finisher medals, DJ music, breakfast, and butterfly-themed fun.
