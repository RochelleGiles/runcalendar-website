About

The Comboitise is a new trail running event organized by the Triple Effort Enthusiasts association. The 10km trail course will take runners through the scenic landscapes around Grenoble, celebrating the 10th anniversary of the association. The event promises an enthusiastic atmosphere and a challenging yet enjoyable experience for participants.
