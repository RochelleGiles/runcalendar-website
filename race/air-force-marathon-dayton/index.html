About

The Air Force Marathon starts and finishes at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base near Dayton, Ohio, taking runners past the National Museum of the US Air Force and its aircraft displays. It is one of the largest marathons in the state, with marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K distances.
