Course

Multi-distance trail event from Winchester, running the South Downs Way, Allan King Way, St Swithun's Way and the Itchen Way alongside the River Itchen. Predominantly undulating trail with road sections through the city centre. Distances of 14 miles, 19 miles, marathon and 50K share the route.

About

Explore the stunning Winchester countryside on the BigWayRound trail run. This event offers four distance options, allowing runners of all abilities to experience the breathtaking scenery of the United Kingdom's rural landscapes. Whether you're seeking a challenging adventure or a scenic tour, the BigWayRound promises an unforgettable running experience.
