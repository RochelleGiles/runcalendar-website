Course

A double out-and-back on dirt roads and trail from Buena Vista into the Collegiate Peaks of the Sawatch Range, run at altitude. The 50 mile covers the 25 mile loop twice, with the 50 starting at 6.30am and the 25 at 6.45am.

About

The Collegiate Peaks Trail Run (CPTR) is a premier trail running event in the heart of the Rocky Mountains. Runners will experience stunning alpine vistas, challenging terrain, and a welcoming community of outdoor enthusiasts.
