About

The Les Preautomnales is a trail running event organized by Passion Sports Nature, featuring multiple distance options including a challenging Maratrail with 1000m of elevation gain. The event offers a variety of trail running and Nordic walking routes to accommodate runners of different skill levels and preferences.
