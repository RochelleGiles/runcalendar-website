About

This 5.2-mile road race begins at The Woodshed on Main Street and finishes at the north end of Route 124, passing along Route 6A, Route 137, Tubman Road, Stony Brook Road and Lower Road through Brewster on Cape Cod. The race has run for over 45 years and draws more than a thousand runners each August.
