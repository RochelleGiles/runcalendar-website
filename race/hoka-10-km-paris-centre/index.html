About

The Hoka 10 Km Paris Centre is a must-do event for runners seeking an unforgettable urban challenge. Experience the thrill of racing through the heart of the City of Light, with a scenic course showcasing iconic Parisian landmarks. This HOKA-sponsored race offers a unique opportunity to explore the vibrant streets of Paris while pushing your limits.
