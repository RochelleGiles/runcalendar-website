About

The Montane Lakeland 100 is widely regarded as the premier ultra trail race in the UK, a 105 mile-class circular route through the Lake District starting and finishing in Coniston. The same weekend hosts the Lakeland 50, and both races take in relentless fell climbs, remote valleys and passionate village checkpoints.
