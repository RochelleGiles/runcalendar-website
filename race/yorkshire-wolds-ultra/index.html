Course

A circular 54km route starting and finishing on the edge of Pocklington and taking the quieter trails of the Yorkshire Wolds through Givendale, Bishop Wilton, Kirby Underdale, Thixendale, Huggate and Fridaythorpe. The course crosses hidden valleys and wild grass meadows for around 4,100ft of ascent, with four staffed checkpoints and three water stations.

About

Discover the stunning Yorkshire Wolds on this 55km ultra-marathon challenge. Runners will embark on a circular route starting and finishing in the charming town of Pocklington, taking in the rolling hills and picturesque landscapes of this beautiful region. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or looking to take on your first long-distance event, the Yorkshire Wolds Ultra promises an unforgettable adventure.
