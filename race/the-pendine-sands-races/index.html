Course

Run entirely on Pendine Sands beach, out and back on firm compacted sand once used for land-speed record attempts. Starts and finishes beside Barnacles Cafe at Sea View, Pendine. Distance markers every quarter mile; the half marathon is two out-and-back loops. Four-hour limit, with start times set by the tide tables and confirmed about two months before race day.

About

The Pendine Sands Races in the United Kingdom offer runners a unique opportunity to test their limits on the vast, windswept beaches of this stunning coastal location. With distance options ranging from an exhilarating Ultra to a scenic 5km, this event has something for every level of runner, promising an unforgettable experience amidst the breathtaking natural beauty of the Pendine Sands.
