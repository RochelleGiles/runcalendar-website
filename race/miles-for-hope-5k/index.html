About

Miles for Hope 5k is a 5k race in Troutman, NC, raising money for Acres of Hope's university fund. Acres of Hope is a school in Nebbi, Uganda that educates, houses, and feeds children from surrounding areas for free. They educate kids from young ages, all the way through secondary school (high school equivalent). Many of the kids who graduate from Acres of Hope have ambitions of further education to pursue careers such as doctor or computer science engineer. However, without the funds to send these kids to university, they will not have the opportunity to go. All of the proceeds from this 5k will benefit the fund for sending kids to university from Acres of Hope. The race welcomes people of all ages and run-times.
