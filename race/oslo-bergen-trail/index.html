About

The Oslo Bergen Trail is a captivating ultra-running event that takes participants on a breathtaking journey through the stunning landscapes of Norway. Spanning a challenging course that connects the vibrant cities of Oslo and Bergen, this event offers runners an unparalleled opportunity to experience the country's majestic fjords, rugged mountains, and serene forests firsthand.
