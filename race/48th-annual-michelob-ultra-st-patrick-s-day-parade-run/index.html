About

The great tradition of the Michelob ULTRA St. Patrick's Day Run as a rite of spring is back! One of the largest events of its kind in the St. Louis area, with more than 5,000 participants, the 5K Run travels the streets of downtown and midtown St. Louis before the St. Patrick's Day Parade, regularly noted as one of the top in the country.
