About

The Rural Kutxa San Sebastian Half Marathon is a popular running event that takes place in the beautiful city of San Sebastian. The course highlights the scenic landscapes and charming streets of the city, offering a unique and enjoyable experience for runners. The event is known for its great atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators.
