About

The event kicks off at 7:30 am with the Community Health Fair featuring health and wellness organizations offering giveaways and handouts. In addition to our health fair we are giving vendors opportunities to promote their businesses to the community through other opportunities including swag bag items, marketing and coupons. Local wellness businesses and providers will display their healthy programs and products during the event that is designed to benefit the community. The Community Health Fair features something for all ages. This annual event is part of an ongoing City effort to promote Mission Viejo's role as a Healthy Eating, Active Living (HEAL) city.
