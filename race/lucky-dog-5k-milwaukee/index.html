Course

A paved, generally flat out-and-back 5K starting and finishing at the Milwaukee Sports Complex in Franklin. Runners may take part with or without a dog, and the route offers open views of the surrounding Franklin countryside.

About

Lucky Dog 5K is a themed running and walking event supporting animal shelters like Rescue Gang. Participants enjoy scenic views in Franklin, WI, with options to run with pets, receiving t-shirts, medals, and finish line treats for humans and dogs.
