About

The Wut Wienerwald Ultra Trail is a trail running event that takes place in the Wienerwald region of Austria. The event offers various distances, including a 50km and 100km ultra-trail, allowing runners to challenge themselves on scenic trails through the beautiful Wienerwald landscape. The event aims to continue growing its international appeal, with participants from up to 20 different countries in recent years.
