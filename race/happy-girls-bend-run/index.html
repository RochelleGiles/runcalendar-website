About

Happy Girls Bend Run is a women-focused event starting and finishing at Riverbend Park in Bend, Oregon, with half marathon, 10K and 5K distances. The courses mix trails, groomed paths and sealed surfaces. Aid stations, medics and event staff are placed along the route, and all distances finish by early afternoon.
