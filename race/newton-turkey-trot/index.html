Course

Out-and-back 5K starting and finishing at Zervas Elementary School on Beethoven Avenue, running through Newton neighbourhoods and out along the Commonwealth Avenue carriage lane to Newton City Hall before looping back. USATF-certified. A one-mile kids' fun run goes off at 8:30am, the 5K at 9:00am.

About

Now in its 13th year, this USATF-certified 5K sells out annually with more than 1,800 runners. The course starts and finishes at Zervas Elementary School and turns back near Newton City Hall. All proceeds, with a $15,000 goal, support the Newton Food Pantry and Centre Street Food Pantry.
