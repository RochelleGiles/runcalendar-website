About

Trail Dix Huit 2 is organized by the Amicale des Sapeurs-Pompiers de Saint-Juéry in the Tarn region, offering a 10.8km trail and an 18km semi-night trail — distances chosen to represent the firefighters' emergency number 18. The event shares the organizers' training trails with participants of all ages and includes a fire truck exhibition for children.
