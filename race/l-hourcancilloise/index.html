About

L'Hourcancilloise is a festival of sport and nature that features a variety of running events, including a 10K, a half marathon, a bike and run challenge, and a night trail run. The event takes place in a unique setting, with the courses winding through forest trails and along the shores of the lake. The energy of the crowd and the festival atmosphere make this a special event for runners of all levels.
