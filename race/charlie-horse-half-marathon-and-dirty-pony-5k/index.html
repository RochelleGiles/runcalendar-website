Course

A mixed road, trail and country half marathon starting at Sleepy Hollow Athletic Club and largely following the Horse-Shoe Trail through Brecknock Township, mostly single track. Three aid stations sit at miles 3, 6 and 10. The companion Dirty Pony 5K loops the Plowville section before returning to the same finish.

About

Join us Memorial Day Weekend for the 28th running of the Charlie Horse Trail Half-Marathon or the Dirty Pony 5K to benefit Berks Co. Special Olympics. The majority of this race will be run on the Horse-Shoe Trail in Brecknock Twp, PA. It is well aided with 3 strategically placed aid stations at mile 3, 6 and 10.
