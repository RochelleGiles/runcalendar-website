About

The Trail Des Bulles is a popular trail running event held in the beautiful Château Moncontour - Vignobles Feray in Vouvray, France. The event features a variety of race distances, including a 15km, 21km, and 8km solo and duo races, catering to runners of all levels. Participants can enjoy the scenic vineyards and countryside while challenging themselves on the well-marked trails.
