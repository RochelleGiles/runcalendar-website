Course

A road-closed half marathon starting on Hart Field in the heart of Rugby, mixing town-centre streets with clear, gently undulating country roads. The route takes in sections of the disused Great Central Railway, now a nature reserve, before returning to town for the finish. Accurate kilometre markers and spectator-friendly vantage points throughout.
