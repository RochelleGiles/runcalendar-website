Course

Waymarked routes from Broad Oak Cricket Club in Linthwaite, Huddersfield, crossing the open moorland, reservoirs and Pennine trails of the Colne Valley. Signage begins around 3.5km in on the longer distances and is heavy over the final 4km, though basic navigation is still needed. Three staffed checkpoints at Holme Village, Wessenden Head and Clough Head serve hot and cold food.

About

A 50-mile ultra trail race through the Pennine moors above Huddersfield, known locally as the Scammonden route. Expect long climbs, reservoirs and remote moorland underfoot across West Yorkshire's Pennine watershed.
