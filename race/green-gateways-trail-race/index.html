Course

Trail race following the Green Gateways walking route, which links the parks and green spaces of north Leeds. Runners start at Station Top car park on the Chevin above Otley and finish at the Bridge United Reformed Church in Otley, passing Yeadon Banks, Yeadon Tarn and Danefield Woods; the marathon adds The Billing and a run along the River Aire.

About

Explore the lush Green Gateways of West Yorkshire's rolling hills at the Punk Panther Trail Race. This off-road adventure features three scenic trail runs departing from the charming town of Otley, offering a challenge for runners of all levels amidst the region's picturesque natural landscapes.
