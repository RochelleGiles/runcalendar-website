Course

A 5K on a mixed course of Michigan country dirt roads, paved trail and the streets of downtown Pinckney, starting and finishing at Townsquare Park on S. Howell St. A quarter-mile kids' fun run does two laps of the park.

About

A 5K and a quarter-mile kids' fun run in Pinckney, Michigan, held as a fundraiser for Light of the World Academy and now in its fourteenth year. The children's race runs two laps of Townsquare Park in the middle of town.
