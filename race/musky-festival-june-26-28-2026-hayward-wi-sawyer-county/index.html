Course

Road race through downtown Hayward held as part of the Musky Festival. Both the 5K and the 10K start and finish on Main Street in front of Frandsen Bank & Trust, running city streets through town. A short free kids' race loops a single downtown block beforehand.

About

The Musky 5k Run/Walk and 10K Run is a fun, family-friendly event with awards for top finishers. The course follows a route through the downtown and the City of Hayward starting and finishing on Main Street in front of Frandsen Bank & Trust.
