About

The Vedder Mountain Challenge is a trail race in British Columbia's Fraser Valley, climbing the forested slopes of Vedder Mountain near Cultus Lake. Runners choose between 15K and 35K distances on the mountain's singletrack trails, organized by Coast Mountain Trail Running.
