About

The Trail Du Vieux Semur is a running event that takes place in the historic town of Semur-en-Auxois. The event offers three different course options - the 10km Trail des Remparts, the 17km Trail du Vieux Semur, and the challenging 25km Trail du Vieux Semur XL. Runners will have the opportunity to explore the old town, its stairs, and gardens, as well as ascend the nearby hills for stunning views.
