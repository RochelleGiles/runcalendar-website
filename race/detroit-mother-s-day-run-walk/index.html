About

The Detroit Mother's Day Run/Walk is a women-focused race weekend in Detroit, Michigan, part of the Goddess Racing Series and formerly held in Ann Arbor. It offers 10K, 5K and one-mile run/walk options plus kids' races, with team categories for mothers, daughters and multi-generation groups.
