About

The Maitland River Run is a popular annual running event in the historic city of Maitland, New South Wales. The course takes runners along the scenic banks of the Hunter River, offering stunning views of the surrounding countryside. Participants can choose from a range of distances, making it an event suitable for runners of all abilities.
