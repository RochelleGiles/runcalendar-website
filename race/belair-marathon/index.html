Course

Out-and-back courses inside Belair National Park, starting and finishing at Karka Pavilion about 3 km into the park. The terrain is steep and constantly undulating on park trails. Manned aid stations are placed along the route, seven on the 50 km and progressively fewer on the shorter distances. The field is capped at 500.

About

The Belair Marathon offers runners a stunning tour of the lush Belair National Park in South Australia. Winding through the park's picturesque landscapes, this event provides an immersive experience for athletes seeking a challenging yet rewarding race. Runners can expect to be captivated by the natural beauty surrounding them as they push towards the finish line.
