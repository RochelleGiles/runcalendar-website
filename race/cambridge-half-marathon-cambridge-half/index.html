About

The Cambridge Half Marathon is a popular annual running event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the historic city of Cambridge. Featuring a flat, fast course and a lively atmosphere, this half marathon is a must-do for runners looking to explore the charming streets and landmarks of this iconic university town.
