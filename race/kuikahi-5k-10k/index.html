Course

Loop course starting at the keyhole area of Ala Moana Regional Park, running out around Magic Island and back along a straightaway on the mauka side of the park. The 5K is one lap and the 10K two. The surface is 81% asphalt and 19% concrete, with soft surface alongside 67% of the route. Aid stations roughly every mile with water, electrolytes and first aid.
