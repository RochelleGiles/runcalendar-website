Course

All races start and finish at the grandstand of Perth Racecourse and follow the banks of the River Tay, with views of Scone Palace and a stretch through the North Inch. The half marathon and 7.5km are Scottish Athletics certified; the 7.5km tracks the racecourse boundary along the New Kingsway and crosses the New Destiny Bridge before looping back. A one-mile fun run also runs.

About

A running festival in Perth, Scotland, offering a half marathon, 7.5K and one-mile fun run through the city on the banks of the River Tay. Perth holds city status as one of Scotland's newest cities.
