About

The Media Maratón Palas de Rey «Os 21 do Camiño» is organized by the Asociación Deportiva Cerne. The course alternates between dirt and asphalt and is characterized by multiple ascents, highly regarded by participants. Folklore groups liven up the atmosphere with bagpipes, with aid stations and irrigation points to deal with the heat, plus a photo contest for spectators.
