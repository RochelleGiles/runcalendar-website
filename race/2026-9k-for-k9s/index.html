About

Join K9s United at the Ring Power Corporate Headquarters complex for the K9s United 9K for K9s. This annual event includes the 1 mile fun run (untimed), the classic 5k and our challenging 9k! Both the 5k and 9k will be chip timed with awards for the top runners in each category. All the runs are open to runners and walkers of all levels.
