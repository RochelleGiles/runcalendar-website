Course

A 24-hour relay run on an undulating 8km trail lap inside Wasing Park, passing the 15th-century Church of St Nicholas and looping through Wasing Wood. Runners enter solo, in pairs, or in teams of three to twelve and cover as many laps as they can inside the day.

About

Endure24 Reading is the UK's biggest 24-hour trail running event, taking place at the beautiful Wasing Park in Reading. Runners of all abilities can test their limits on a challenging yet scenic course, with the opportunity to compete solo or in teams. This unique event offers an unparalleled endurance challenge amidst the stunning Berkshire countryside.
