Course

A backyard ultra run on a 6.71 km looped course on private land at Lake Towerrinning, Western Australia. Runners start one loop on the hour, every hour, until a single finisher remains. The trail loop is adjusted each year to suit conditions and can be firm or soft underfoot. A staffed aid station at the start/finish transition offers water, electrolyte, snacks and fruit for the first 24 hours, alongside runners' own supply tables.

About

Birdy's Backyard Ultra is an iconic endurance running event where participants run 6.71k loops on the hour, every hour, until only one runner remains. Located at Lakeside Camping, Lake Towerrinning, this event has become a legendary stage for extraordinary athletic achievements and has helped establish Western Australia as a premier ultra-running destination.
