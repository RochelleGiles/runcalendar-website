Course

Starts at Castle Park by Bangor City Hall and finishes in Ward Park, running out through the town centre and along the coast to Groomsport before returning to Bangor. Fairly flat and fast on roads, with a short hill around the 9 km mark. 10K only, starting at 10:15.

About

Previously called the Bangor 10K, the Seaton Classic 10K Road Race is hosted by the North Down Athletic Club and also serves as the NI & Ulster Championship 10K. The route starts at Castle Park near Bangor City Hall and heads through Bangor town centre before following the coast and countryside out to Groomsport.
