Course

Coastal trail races from Little Haven on the Pembrokeshire Coast Path, run over grass, trail, beach and clifftop coastpath. Terrain is graded moderate: undulating and exposed, past rugged cliffs, quiet coves and sandy bays. Courses are fully marked so no navigation is required, and the marathon and ultra are UTMB qualifiers.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Pembrokeshire coastline during the EnduranceLife Pembrokeshire trail run. Offering distances from 10K to Ultra, this event challenges runners to conquer the stunning natural landscapes of the United Kingdom's southwest. Whether you're seeking a scenic challenge or aiming to push your limits, the EnduranceLife Pembrokeshire has an option to suit your running goals.
