About

Songkhla Marathon is a long-standing running event in the southern region of Thailand, organized to promote health and boost the tourism economy in Songkhla province. The event is supported by the community and public-private organizations, and has gained popularity among athletes from Thailand and neighboring countries like Malaysia, Singapore, and Indonesia.
