About

The Course des Terrils is a unique running event that takes place in the scenic Raismes region of northern France. The course winds through the area's iconic coal mining terrils (slag heaps), offering runners a challenging and visually stunning experience. With various distances to choose from, the event caters to runners of all levels, from the 3km Family Run to the grueling 45km Contrebandière.
