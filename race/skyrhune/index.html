About

The Skyrhune is a challenging running event that takes place in a scenic mountain environment. The course features stunning views and a mix of trail and road running, offering a unique experience for participants. With a focus on camaraderie and personal achievement, the Skyrhune attracts runners of all levels who are seeking an adventure in the great outdoors.
