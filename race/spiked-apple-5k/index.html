Course

A two-lap cross-country style 5K at Indian Ladder Farms in Altamont, New York, starting and finishing near the cidery and brewery. The route has uneven footing and some inclines, and strollers are not recommended.

About

The Spiked Apple 5k is a fun and festive running event featuring awesome race swag, an amazing atmosphere, and guaranteed fun. Runners can look forward to a super soft t-shirt, finisher medal that doubles as a bottle opener, chip-timed race bib, souvenir cider glass, and delicious donuts.
