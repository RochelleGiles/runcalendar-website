Course

A road tour of Kansas City taking in the World War I Museum and Memorial, the Country Club Plaza, the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art, Waldo, Westport and 18th & Vine. Marathon and half marathon start together at 7am, the 10K at 7:30am and the 5K at 7:45am.

About

The Kansas City Marathon is an annual road race through Kansas City, Missouri, organized by the Kansas City Sports Commission. Runners can choose the marathon, half marathon, 10K, or 5K distance, with the race typically held each October.
