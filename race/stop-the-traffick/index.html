Course

Run entirely off-road on the cross-country course at Kalamazoo Christian Middle School on South 12th Street, which is also the start and finish. Grass and dirt trail underfoot, so strollers are discouraged. A one-mile costume fun run is held alongside the chip-timed 5K.

About

We are ready to do something. Stop the Traffick! is race festival that in partnership with Destiny Rescue is seeking to raise funds to rescue children from human trafficking. In this, the second annual event, we will begin the morning with a one mile fun run followed by a 5K race, both at Kalamazoo Christian Elementary (note that this course will be off-road and will use the cross-country course), followed by awards.
