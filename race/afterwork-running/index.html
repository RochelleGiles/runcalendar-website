About

AfterWork Running in Lyon is a unique opportunity for runners to explore the city after the workday ends. This evening event takes participants on a scenic route through the heart of Lyon, showcasing the city's landmarks and vibrant nightlife. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, AfterWork Running offers a fun and engaging way to stay active and experience the beauty of Lyon.
