Course

Starts at Blackbutt Reserve off Mahogany Drive, near the Queens Road junction in New Lambton. An out-and-back reserve course with sustained climbs and descents; long-course runners cover the medium loop twice. Club races start at 2.00pm and entry for the week closes Friday midnight.

About

Newcastle Cross Country Club's race at Blackbutt Reserve in New Lambton is part of a series held across the Hunter Region of NSW. The out-and-back course starts off Mahogany Drive near the Queens Road junction, with long-course runners covering the medium distance twice.
