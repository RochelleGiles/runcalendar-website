Course

A timed lap race from Friend Park Campground near Douglas, in the Medicine Bow National Forest. Each lap is about 9 miles with close to 3,000 ft of gain, summiting near 10,000 ft on rugged, technical and remote ground — dense pine, rocky mule deer paths, narrow ridgelines and granite outcrops. Entries are 24-hour, 12-hour rolling start, or a single summit.

About

The Laramie Peak Endurance Challenge is a single-summit trail race in Wyoming's Laramie Range, climbing to the peak's summit above 10,000 feet before returning to Douglas. It's a high-altitude test for trail and mountain runners.
