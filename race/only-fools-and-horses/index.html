Course

The 40k and 25k start and finish at Wedderburn Cottages in the Maniototo, crossing private land over open high country, following an ancient water race and climbing ridges with substantial elevation gain. The 16k walk starts and finishes at derelict buildings at the end of Laws Road. Surface split and aid station detail are not stated.

About

Only Fools and Horses is a one-of-a-kind running event set in the stunning Maniototo region of Otago, New Zealand. Runners will embark on a journey through the rugged backcountry, starting and finishing in the charming town of Wedderburn. This unique race promises an unforgettable experience for those seeking an adventure off the beaten path.
