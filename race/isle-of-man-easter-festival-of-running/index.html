About

The Isle of Man Easter Festival of Running offers a thrilling three-day racing experience on the picturesque island. Runners can challenge themselves across a series of scenic routes, taking in the island's stunning coastal landscapes and charming towns. This beloved annual event is a must for any runner seeking a unique and unforgettable running adventure.
