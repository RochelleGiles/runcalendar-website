About

Cusy'Hard is a sports and community event that celebrates endurance, nature, and camaraderie. Nestled between the Bauges massif, Lake Bourget, and Lake Annecy, the charming village of Cusy becomes the hub for this annual trail running and hiking celebration. The event offers a variety of courses suited for all levels, from beginner to expert, allowing participants to challenge themselves in a preserved natural setting of the Haute-Savoie region. With the support of dedicated volunteers, Cusy'Hard provides an unforgettable experience of personal achievement, discovery, and conviviality at the heart of the village.
