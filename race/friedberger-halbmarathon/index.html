About

The Friedberger Halbmarathon is a popular running event in the town of Friedberg, Germany. Runners will tackle the challenging Friedberger Berg, a hill with a 13% incline, and then enjoy the lively Halbmarathonbiergarten on the Marienplatz. The event offers a unique atmosphere and scenic course that showcases the charming town.
