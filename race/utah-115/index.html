About

UTAH 115 is a self-supported, multi-stage ultramarathon held near Kanab, Utah, in the canyon country of the Colorado Plateau. Runners carry their own gear and supplies between daily stages, with distances up to 115 miles across the full event. It is organized by Grand to Grand Ultra.
