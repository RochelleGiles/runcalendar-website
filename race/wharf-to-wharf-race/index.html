Course

A six-mile point-to-point on seaside streets from Portola Drive in Santa Cruz to Cliff and Wharf Road at the Capitola Wharf. The first mile is flat and fast, miles four to six roll gently past Pleasure Point and The Hook, and the last mile runs downhill to the finish.

About

One of California's most iconic road races, running six miles along the coast from the Santa Cruz Wharf to Capitola. Live bands line the course, and the point-to-point route finishes near Capitola Village.
