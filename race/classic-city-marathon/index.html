Course

The marathon and half marathon start together in historic downtown Athens and run through varied neighbourhoods, vibrant communities, university campuses and scenic greenways. The two courses split partway through, rejoin at several points, and share the last six miles. A 2-4 person relay covers the marathon route in legs of roughly 6-7 miles.

About

The Classic City Marathon, Half Marathon, and Marathon Relay is a community-focused running event in Athens, Georgia. Runners will experience the historic and vibrant city of Athens, with local entertainment, sustainability initiatives, and a warm post-race celebration.
