About

The Cederberg Traverse runs through the Cederberg wilderness in the Western Cape, from Sanddrif, with routes from 21km up to a 100-mile ultra and a 50km night stage. The trails cross rugged sandstone mountains, dry passes and remote rock formations far from any town.
