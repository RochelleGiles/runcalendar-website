About

The Crailsheimer Halbmarathon is a beautiful running event that takes participants through the Burgbergwald, mostly on forest and meadow paths. Runners can choose between a half marathon (21.1 km) or a 10.6 km route, and there is also a relay half marathon option. The event has a great atmosphere and is a popular preparation race for many runners.
