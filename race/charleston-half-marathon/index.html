Course

Starts on President Street in downtown Charleston and finishes at the RiverDogs' ballpark on Fishburne Street, on city streets throughout. The half marathon sets off at 7:00am, wheelchair athletes five minutes earlier, and the course closes at 10:30am.

About

The Charleston Half Marathon is a premier running event that showcases the historic charm and vibrant culture of Charleston, South Carolina. Runners will experience the city's iconic landmarks, picturesque neighborhoods, and stunning waterfront views as they navigate the scenic course.
