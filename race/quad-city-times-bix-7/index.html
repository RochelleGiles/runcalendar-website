About

The Quad-City Times Bix 7 race week is more than just a run—it's a legendary festival of speed, spirit, and community celebration! The excitement builds all week long, starting with The Bechtel Trusts Sr Bix on Tuesday, followed by the MercyOne Genesis Brady Street Sprints on Thursday, and the John Deere Shakeout Run and Arconic Jr Bix on Friday. Then, on Saturday, the city comes alive for the Quad-City Times Bix 7 and the Prairie Farms Quick Bix—the ultimate showdown on an electrifying out-and-back course through downtown Davenport.
