About

The Trail Urbain de Saint-Denis is a must-try event for trail running enthusiasts. Set against the historic backdrop of Saint-Denis, this urban trail challenge takes runners on a scenic tour through the city's vibrant neighborhoods and picturesque parks. With a mix of paved paths and rugged terrain, the course promises an exhilarating adventure for runners of all levels.
