Course

Starts and finishes at the Tremayne Shelter in Sailboat Cove, Creve Coeur Lake Park, on the sealed paths around the lake north-west of St Louis. The in-person course is fully wheelchair accessible, and a virtual option is offered alongside it.

About

A 5K run/walk event held in memory of Brian J. Smith, a dedicated volunteer for CHAMP Assistance Dogs. All proceeds benefit CHAMP Assistance Dogs, an organization that provides assistance dogs free of charge to those in need.
