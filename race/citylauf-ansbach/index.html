About

The Citylauf Ansbach is a running event that takes place in the city of Ansbach, Germany. The event features a scenic course that showcases the historic city center and its landmarks. Runners can participate in various distances, making it an inclusive event for both recreational and competitive runners. The event is known for its lively atmosphere, with music and entertainment along the course, creating a memorable experience for all participants.
