Course

Held at Brian Piccolo Sports Park in Hollywood, Florida, starting next to Cricket Field 2 just off the car park. The course is a flat 0.78-mile out-and-back loop, run twice for the 5K and four times for the 10K. A two-hour time limit applies and there is no on-site entry.
