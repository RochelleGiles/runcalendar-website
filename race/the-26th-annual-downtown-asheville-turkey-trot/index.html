Course

A USATF-certified 5K on Asheville city streets, starting at the island on Woodfin Street in front of the YMCA and finishing at Pack Square Park. The route climbs East Chestnut Street early on and loops through the historic Montford neighbourhood before returning via Orange and Central Avenues. A 1 mile Gobble Wobble runs alongside.

About

The 26th Annual Downtown Asheville Turkey Trot is a popular running event that takes place in Asheville, North Carolina. The event features a 5K race and a 1-mile fun run, with participants enjoying the scenic downtown area and supporting the local MANNA FoodBank.
