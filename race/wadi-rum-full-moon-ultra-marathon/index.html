About

The Wadi Rum Full Moon Ultra Marathon is run at night across the red sand and sandstone formations of Wadi Rum in the Jordanian desert, timed to coincide with a full moon. Distances from 5km to 50km cross open desert trails beneath dramatic rock towers.
