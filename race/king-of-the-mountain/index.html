Course

A loop course starting and finishing at Helena Valley Primary School, run anticlockwise up the mountain on the north side of the valley with a detour south of Helena Valley Road on the return. Includes steep ascents and descents and a creek crossing. No drinking water is provided on course, so participants carry their own. Start 8:00am with a 10:30am course cutoff.

About

Conquer the slopes at the King of the Mountain race in Western Australia. This challenging 16.1km course takes runners from Helena Valley Primary School to the scenic finish line at Fred Jacoby Park in Perth, testing their endurance and determination against the region's rugged terrain. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or seeking a new adventure, this event promises an unforgettable mountain running experience.
