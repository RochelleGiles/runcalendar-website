Course

A self-navigated trail race across the North York Moors and the Cleveland Heritage Coast. The marathon climbs to the highest part of the moors and holds that height for several miles over very exposed ground, with half marathon and 10K options alongside. Runners navigate from a supplied route description and maps, with marshals and foot sweepers on course.
