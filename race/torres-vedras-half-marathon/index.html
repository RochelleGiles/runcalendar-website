About

The Torres Vedras Half Marathon is a must-do event for runners seeking a challenging yet picturesque course. Set against the stunning backdrop of Portugal's historic town of Torres Vedras, this half marathon offers a unique opportunity to explore the region's charming streets and scenic countryside. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a newcomer to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
