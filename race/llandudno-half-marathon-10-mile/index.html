About

The Llandudno Half Marathon & 10 Mile race offers runners a scenic tour of the picturesque coastal town of Llandudno, United Kingdom. Set along the promenade, this event provides a stunning backdrop for participants to challenge themselves on either the half marathon or 10-mile distance. With a reputation for beautiful scenery and a lively atmosphere, this race is a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable running experience.
