Course

A single-lap anti-clockwise road loop starting and finishing by Fusion Tunbridge Wells Sports Centre on St John's Road, Southborough. The route passes through Bidborough, Penshurst, Fordcombe and Langton Green, entirely on tarmac with no off-road sections. Undulating and testing, with a 300ft climb up Spring Hill at Fordcombe and five water stations.

About

The Tunbridge Wells Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging race through the beautiful countryside of Kent. Known for its meticulous organization, value for money, and friendly marshals, the event has been voted the 'Most Improved Race in the UK' and one of the top half marathons in the country.
