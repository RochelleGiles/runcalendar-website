Course

A flat 5K on fully closed streets through the Lincoln Square and Ravenswood neighbourhoods of Chicago's north side, past tree-lined blocks and historic architecture. One of the few fully closed-street 5K courses in the city. A separate kids' race is also held.

About

Join us for the 30th annual Ravenswood Run presented by Fleet Feet X Altra Running, Chicago's favorite neighborhood running tradition. Since 1996, this iconic Chicago 5K race has brought runners, families, and neighbors together for a morning of fast racing, friendly competition, and community spirit.
