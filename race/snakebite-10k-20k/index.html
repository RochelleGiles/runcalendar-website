Course

A trail loop in McDowell Mountain Regional Park near Fountain Hills, Arizona, starting and finishing at the Youth Group Campground in the north of the park. The route follows the well-used Verde, Pemberton, Escondido and Minx hiking trails over gently rolling desert terrain; the 20K runs the loop twice.

About

Snakebite is a fast 10k or 20k run on trails in the beautiful McDowell Mountain Regional Park. Come enjoy the beautiful desert scenery, gently rolling hills, and mild weather!
