About

The Prince William Turkey Trot offers a timed 5K and an untimed Mashed Potato Mile through Manassas, starting and finishing at the Freedom Aquatic & Fitness Center on Thanksgiving morning. Proceeds benefit Hero's Bridge, a nonprofit supporting elderly veterans from WWII, Korea, and Vietnam.
