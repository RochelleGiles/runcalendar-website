Course

A two-day weekend based at Isla Blanca Park at the southern tip of South Padre Island. The half marathon runs on Sunday morning from Isla Blanca and is entirely paved, with no sand on course and a five-hour cut-off; a two-person relay shares the same route. Saturday's 5K/Turtle Trot and one-mile fun run start from the same park and do include sand sections.

About

The South Padre Island Half Marathon is a beach-town road race weekend on South Padre Island, Texas, with half marathon, 5K and 1-mile options. Courses run along the island's coastal roads near the Gulf of Mexico.
