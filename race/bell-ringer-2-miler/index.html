Course

A single two-mile loop around the lake at North Lake Park in Loveland, starting and finishing at the park. The race starts at 9:00 am and is run on park paths and roads. Water bottles are provided to entrants who register in advance.

About

Our community is the only city in the nation to follow the old tradition of ringing bells throughout the town in the early morning hours of Veteran's Day. This race will mimic some of those old traditions to honor our nation's veteran community. We are honored to be part of this 2-Mile event and look forward to the engagement of the families in Loveland. This event benefits Post 2000, which is created a Loveland Colorado Veteran's Assistance program.
