Course

A 41km figure-eight loop in Glenbervie Forest about 10km from Whangārei, starting and finishing at the Matariki Forestry headquarters on Maruata Road. Roughly 75% is maintained forestry road and 25% older unmaintained road, running through pine plantation and native bush over rolling hills. Longer distances repeat the loop; the 165km miler has a 32-hour cutoff.

About

Set in the rugged and breathtaking landscapes of Glenbervie Forest, managed by Rayonier Matariki Forests. The Northland 100 is an ultra-running experience like no other. This course takes runners deep into forestry land, offering a unique challenge through rolling hills, dense native bush, and wide-open logging roads.
