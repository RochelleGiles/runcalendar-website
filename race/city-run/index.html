Course

A lap event on the 7.5km Weir Loop along the River Torrens, starting and finishing at Karrawirra in central Adelaide. Shorter distances run a shortened loop and longer entries repeat it, with options from 1km through to 60km. One aid station sits at the start and finish of each loop with hydration, lollies and first aid, and a bag drop is provided.

About

GoodRunnings' City Run takes runners on a scenic tour through the heart of Adelaide, following the Linear Path and showcasing the city's iconic landmarks. This urban adventure offers a unique opportunity to experience the vibrant atmosphere of the CBD while challenging your endurance on a picturesque course. Whether you're a seasoned runner or newcomer to the sport, the City Run promises an unforgettable running experience.
