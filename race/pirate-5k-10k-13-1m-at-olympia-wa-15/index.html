Course

All distances start together at 8:30 am at Heritage Park, 5th Ave SW and Water St SW in Olympia. The 5K and 10K repeat a 1.56-mile out-and-back twice and four times respectively, while the half marathon repeats a separate, slightly longer out-and-back six times. The time limit is 3 hours 30 minutes.

About

The Pirate 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon takes place in Olympia, WA, offering scenic out-and-back courses for all levels of runners. Finishers receive medals, with packet pickup opening 30 minutes before the 8:30 AM start and a 3.5-hour time limit for the half marathon.
