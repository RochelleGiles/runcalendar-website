Course

A cross-country style route around Deal's Orchard near Jefferson, run as two laps giving a scenic tour of the farm and orchard. Footing is uneven in places and the organisers pitch it as suitable for runners and walkers alike. A shorter 1.5 mile walk is offered alongside the 5K.

About

The Spiked Apple 5K runs through Deal's Orchard in Jefferson, Iowa, a working apple orchard, timed for the autumn harvest season. Runners can look forward to fresh apples and orchard treats at the finish.
