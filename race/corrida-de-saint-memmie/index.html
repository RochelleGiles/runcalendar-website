About

The Corrida De Saint Memmie is a running event held in the town of Saint Memmie, France. The event features a main 8.8km race as well as shorter 3km and 1km races for younger participants. The event is open to both licensed and non-licensed runners, providing an opportunity for runners of all levels to participate.
