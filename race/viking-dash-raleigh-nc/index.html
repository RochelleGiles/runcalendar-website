About

Viking Dash Raleigh is a Norse-themed trail run held at Harris Lake County Park in New Hill, North Carolina, about 30 km south-west of Raleigh. It offers a 5K and a 10K on park trails, with race-day packet pickup at the park and a themed finisher medal.
