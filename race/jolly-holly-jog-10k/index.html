Course

A single-lap, undulating multi-terrain 10K from Hell Wath Nature Reserve in Ripon, mostly on footpaths. It runs via Whitcliffe Lane and Mackershaw Woods through Studley Deer Park and Plumpton Lane, returning through a steep woodland section to the finish.

About

The annual Jolly Holly Jog 10K is a scenic, multi terrain course of approximately 10km. It is an undulating route mainly on footpaths through the magnificent Studley Royal deer park. The start and finish is on Hell Wath Nature Reserve, next to the Panthers Football Club.
