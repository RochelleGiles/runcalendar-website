Course

Two laps from Mortimer Fairground through local woodland on footpaths and bridleways, about 85% off road. Undulating and uneven in places, with roughly 85m of climb and a 1hr45 limit; not suitable for buggies or wheelchairs. A separate canicross 10km starts alongside.

About

The Mortimer 10km is a new multi-terrain course that takes runners through the beautiful local woodlands, using footpaths and bridleways. The undulating and uneven course offers a challenging yet scenic experience for participants.
