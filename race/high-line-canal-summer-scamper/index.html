About

The third annual High Line Canal Summer Scamper, 5K for All, will take place on the evening of June 25 at Bible Park in Denver. Before the 5K enjoy a free 200m Ditch Dash for kids 8 and under. Then scamper along the Canal – whether that's running, walking or strolling – with a smile on your face, and celebrate post-race at the Canal Summer Fest with food, drinks and live music. There will also be summer nature-themed fun for all ages. It's the perfect way to cap off a wonderful summer evening.
