About

The Freedom For Fighters 5K Walk/Run Event is presented to Mountain Home, Idaho in honor of all who are crushed in spirit, specifically those who carry the hidden wounds of PTSD and Depression. This 8th annual event is brought to you by the Jason Barney Family and the City of Mountain Home.
