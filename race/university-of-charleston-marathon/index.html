Course

A fast, flat, USATF-certified and Boston-qualifying marathon through Charleston, looping around the state capitol, along the city's historic streets, across the Kanawha River and through the University of Charleston campus. A half marathon runs the same weekend, with a 5K on the Saturday.

About

A marathon, half marathon and 5K through Charleston, West Virginia, hosted by the University of Charleston. The event marks the state capital's first full marathon in nearly 30 years, with the shorter distances run alongside it.
