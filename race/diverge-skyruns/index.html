Course

Skyrunning courses on the mountains above Queenstown on Tasmania's west coast: the 50km Mt Lyell Skyrun, the 25km Mt Owen Skyrun and the 13km Little Owen Trail, alongside 10km and 5km airfield runs and the 5km Lynchford Express, which runs with the West Coast Wilderness Railway heritage steam train.

About

DIVERGE Skyruns is a mountain skyrunning event in Queenstown on Tasmania's rugged west coast, with routes up Mount Owen and Mount Lyell alongside shorter airfield and trail distances.
