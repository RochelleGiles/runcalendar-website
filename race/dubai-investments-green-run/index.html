About

A community run with an environmental theme in Dubai Investments Park: a 5K for adults and youth and a 3K for adults, youth and young children, both starting at 7 in the morning. Shirts are made from recycled materials, and there are trophies for the top family, school and organisation as well as awards for the 5K.
