Course

Starts and finishes at Cotanchobee Fort Brooke Park on the Tampa waterfront, staying beside the water for the whole route. The 5K, 10K and 15K cross the Platt Street Bridge and follow Bayshore Boulevard and its fitness trail, with views over Harbour and Davis Islands and past the city pirate ship. The 5K starts at 7:00 am, the 10K and 15K at 8:00.

About

The SKECHERS Hot Chocolate Run brings a 15K and 5K through downtown Tampa, with every finisher rewarded with a signature chocolate fondue station and toppings bar at the finish line.
