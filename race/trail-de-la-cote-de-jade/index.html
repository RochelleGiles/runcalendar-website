About

Explore the stunning Jade Coast on this challenging yet rewarding trail run. Weaving through lush forests and along dramatic seaside cliffs, the Trail de la Côte de Jade offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of this picturesque region. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or a newcomer to off-road running, this event promises an unforgettable adventure.
