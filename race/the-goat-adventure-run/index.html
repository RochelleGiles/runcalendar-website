Course

A roughly 20 km point-to-point across Tongariro National Park from Whakapapa to Turoa on the Round the Mountain track, with over 1,000 m of vertical ascent. Highly technical underfoot: mud, tussock, alpine streams, waterfalls and vertical rocky terrain through beech forest, herb fields, boulder-strewn rivers and old lava and scoria flows.

About

The Goat Adventure Run takes runners on a thrilling journey through the breathtaking landscapes of Tongariro National Park in New Zealand. Participants will conquer the iconic Round the Mountain track, trekking from Whakapapa to Turoa Ski Fields, immersed in the rugged natural beauty of this remarkable region. This event promises an unforgettable adventure for outdoor enthusiasts seeking a challenging and rewarding running experience.
