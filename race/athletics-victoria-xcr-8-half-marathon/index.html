Course

Multi-lap course on the paths around Lake Wendouree, Ballarat, starting and finishing near the foreshore. The half marathon covers the standard lake circuit plus one extra lap. Open athletes contest either 6 km or the half marathon; juniors run 6 km. No aid station detail is stated.
