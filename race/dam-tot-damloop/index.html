About

The Dam Tot Damloop is a scenic running event that takes participants on a scenic tour through the picturesque countryside surrounding Amsterdam. Runners can choose between an 8km or 16km course, allowing both casual and experienced athletes to challenge themselves amidst the beautiful Dutch landscape. This popular event is a must-do for any runner looking to explore the natural beauty of the region.
