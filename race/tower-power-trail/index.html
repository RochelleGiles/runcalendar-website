Course

Trail courses in the Our Nature Park around Clervaux, northern Luxembourg, starting by the church in Wëicherdange with the main race off at 17:00. Distances are 21 km, 10 km and 5 km on forest and field paths, with refuelling stations on each, plus 10 km and 5 km walks and free children's races.

About

Tower Power Trail is run in the Our Nature Park around Clervaux in northern Luxembourg, starting by the church in Wëicherdange. Runners choose between 21 km, 10 km and 5 km trail courses, with walking options over 10 km and 5 km and free races for children.
