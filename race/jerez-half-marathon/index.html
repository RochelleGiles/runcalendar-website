About

The XXVIII Medio Maratón de Jerez is a prestigious half marathon event held in the historic city of Jerez, Spain. The course takes runners through the charming streets and landmarks of Jerez, offering a scenic and challenging experience for both elite athletes and recreational runners. The event is known for its vibrant atmosphere, enthusiastic spectators, and the opportunity to explore the rich culture and heritage of this Andalusian city.
