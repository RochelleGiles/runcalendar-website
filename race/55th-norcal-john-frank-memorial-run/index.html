Course

Starts at Lake Redding Park on Benton Drive, Redding. The route follows the Sacramento River Trail and crosses two pedestrian bridges, the Diestelhorst Bridge and the Stress Ribbon Bridge. Distances are ten mile, three mile and one mile. Run as a USATF road grand prix event. Surface split and aid stations are not stated.

About

The NorCal John Frank Memorial Run is a USATF Pacific Association Road Grand Prix event featuring a 10-mile race along the scenic Sacramento River Trail and across two iconic pedestrian bridges in Redding, CA. It also includes 3-mile and 1-mile run/walk options, with free entry for kids 14 and under in the 1-mile race.
