Course

Club races start and finish at the SBSC Hall in Orleigh Park, West End, on a bend of the Brisbane River. Events are held fortnightly on Sunday mornings from 6am, and each one includes a 5km with longer distances offered alongside it during the peak season.

About

The Brisbane Road Runners 5K Championships is a must-run event for competitive runners in Australia. Hosted by the renowned Brisbane Road Runners Club, this race challenges participants with a scenic course through the heart of the city, offering a chance to compete for the coveted club championship title. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or looking to test your speed against fellow runners, this event promises an exhilarating and rewarding experience.
