Course

The 1 mile and 5K both start and finish at West Bridge Park on West River Street, Monticello. The route follows West River Street where festival spectators gather. The 1 mile starts at 7:30am and the 5K at 8:00am.

About

Monticello Riverfest is a community celebration featuring a 5K race, art in the park, beanbag tournament, street dance, parade, duck race, and more.
