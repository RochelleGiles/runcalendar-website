About

The Tateyama Wakashio Marathon runs along the Boso Peninsula coastline in Chiba, combining a flower-lined seaside road with inland hills and valleys around Tateyama City. Full marathon, 10km and 2km courses start and finish near the city, with views across Tateyama Bay toward Mt. Fuji.
