About

The event is a tribute to Pamela McDonald, an avid runner and mental health activist from George County, Mississippi, held in Downtown Lucedale. It promotes community, health, fitness, and fun, with all proceeds benefiting the Pamela McDonald Balanced Life Foundation for mental health awareness and scholarships.
