Course

A weekend of mountain trails from Pralognan-la-Vanoise, in the Vanoise national park. Four races are on offer: the Tour des Glaciers de la Vanoise over 73 km with 3800 m of climb, L'incontournable over 41 km with 2850 m, Pralo vu du Ciel over 25 km with 1750 m, and the Tour de l'Aiguille de la Vanoise over 15 km with 1100 m. The same 73 km circuit is also walked over two or three days.

About

The Week End Du Tour Des Glaciers De La Vanoise is an exciting trail running event featuring multiple challenging mountain routes in the Vanoise National Park. Participants can choose from several distances, including a 73km main trail with significant elevation gain, offering a true test of endurance and mountain running skills.
