Course

The marathon and half marathon run a double loop along the Lake Michigan shoreline in Kenosha, on sealed roads after earlier gravel sections were removed. The 5K uses a revised course. Marathon runners may take an early start 25 minutes before the 7:00am gun.

About

A beautiful course along the Lake Michigan coastline with a marathon and half marathon course that features amazing views of the lake and no more gravel sections, plus an updated 5k course. The race will feature a double loop marathon course, pacers leading groups from 3:30 to 6:00 for the marathon, and an early start option for marathoners that need a little cushion.
