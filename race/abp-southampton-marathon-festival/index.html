Course

City road race through Southampton taking in the docks, the waterfront and the city streets, passing St Mary's Stadium. The climb over the Itchen Bridge comes late in the race and is the hardest section of a course the organisers describe as not a flat PB course. Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K are offered.

About

The ABP Southampton Marathon Festival offers runners a diverse range of distances, from the full marathon to a 5km, through the vibrant streets of this historic coastal city. Featuring a scenic course that showcases the city's landmarks, this annual event is a must-do for runners seeking a memorable running experience in the United Kingdom.
