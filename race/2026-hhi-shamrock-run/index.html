Course

Loop course starting and finishing at Celebration Park, Hilton Head Island. The route runs out South Forest Beach Road to the bike path at Lemoyne Avenue, continues through Cardillo Parkway and the Providence Church area, then returns along the South Forest Beach Road path, turning onto Deallyon Avenue and Nassau Street to finish in the park.

About

The Hilton Head Island Shamrock Run is a 5K run and health walk held over St Patrick's Day weekend. The loop course starts and finishes at Celebration Park and follows South Forest Beach Road and the island bike path network.
