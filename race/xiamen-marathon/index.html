About

The Xiamen Marathon is a premier running event held in the picturesque coastal city of Xiamen, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the city's stunning scenery and vibrant culture. Winding through historic landmarks and scenic routes, this marathon provides a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants of all levels, making it a must-attend event for running enthusiasts.
