Course

Out and back on the Elk River Hiking Trail at Elk City Lake, starting and finishing at the Outlet Channel Group Shelter on the north side of the dam. Mostly single-track through limestone bluffs and rock formations, very rocky with no long hills. The 50K has nine aid stations, the 25K three, spaced roughly 3.5 miles apart.

About

A trail ultra at Elk City State Park near Independence, Kansas, with 25K and 50K distances across the rocky, boulder-strewn terrain of the Table Mound trail system above Elk City Lake. Part of the FlatRock Triple Crown series.
