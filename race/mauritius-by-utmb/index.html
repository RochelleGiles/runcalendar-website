About

The Mauritius By Utmb event is part of the UTMB World Series, offering a range of trail and ultra-trail running experiences around the world. The event takes place in the beautiful island nation of Mauritius, known for its stunning natural landscapes and diverse culture. The course highlights the island's diverse terrain, from lush forests to rugged coastlines, providing a challenging and scenic running adventure for participants.
