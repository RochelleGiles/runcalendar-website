About

Florida Running Company is locally owned and operated and hosts the best holiday-themed and family-friendly 5K races in Orlando, Windermere, Winter Garden, Clermont, Hamlin, and the Horizon West area. Renowned for creating fun, festive, and inclusive events, our races are a favorite among local families and runners of all levels. Every race is designed to celebrate the season while promoting health and community spirit. With professionally organized courses in some of Central Florida's most beautiful neighborhoods, Florida Running Company delivers unforgettable race day experiences. Discover why our events are considered the best races in Orlando and beyond!
