Course

All Beacon Run courses are out and back on the Lake Hefner trails, starting and finishing at the Lake Hefner East Wharf in Oklahoma City. The 15K turns around at the 7.5K mark. Aid stations are placed approximately every 2.2 miles with fluids and various fuels.

About

The Beacon Run is an annual trail and road race hosted by the Landrunners Oklahoma City Running Club at Lake Hefner's East Wharf. Runners can choose from 5K, 15K, and 25K distances along the lake in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.
