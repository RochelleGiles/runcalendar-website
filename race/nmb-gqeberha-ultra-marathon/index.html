About

The NMB Gqeberha Ultra Marathon is a 50km road ultra marathon through Nelson Mandela Bay (Gqeberha/Port Elizabeth), organised with Eastern Province Athletics. Runners can also enter the half marathon distance, with the full route closing city roads for race day.
