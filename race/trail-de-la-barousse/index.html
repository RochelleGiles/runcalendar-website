About

First run in Sarp as the Trail des Boudinaires, a nature race on the trails of the lower Barousse valley and Saint-Bertrand-de-Comminges, the event has since become the Trail de la Barousse, a mountain race starting in the upper valley villages of Sost and Ferrère. Over a full weekend, the team offers seven trail courses, three challenges, a club/company challenge, a Cani-Trail and a gourmet hike.
