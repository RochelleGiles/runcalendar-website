About

The Winter Weavers' Way Ultras showcase the beautiful Norfolk landscape, offering runners a scenic journey through picturesque villages, riverside trails, and historic stately homes. Participants can choose between a challenging 50km or 100km route that traverses the stunning Weavers' Way, culminating in an impressive finish at Cromer Pier. The event provides a perfect tour of Norfolk's diverse terrain, from marshes and rivers to charming countryside and coastal views.
