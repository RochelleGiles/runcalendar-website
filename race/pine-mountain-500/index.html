Course

A stair-climb race at Pine Mountain. Runners head down Kramer Drive to Lower Pine Mountain Road and Hibbard Street before turning onto Pine Mountain Road for the main climb of 500 stairs up Giant Pine Mountain. Single, double (Deuce) and quadruple (Extreme) climbs are offered, each also available as a Pine Muddin' variant that adds a trail loop before the stairs.

About

The Pine Mountain 500 is an extreme running event that challenges participants to conquer the 500 steps of the Pine Mountain in Iron Mountain, Michigan. Runners can choose from three distances: the 2.7-mile single loop, the 5.4-mile double loop, or the grueling 10.8-mile quadruple loop.
