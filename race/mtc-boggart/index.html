Course

A point-to-point ultra of 37.6 miles from the Gretna Chase Hotel in Gretna Green to the Kings Arms Hotel in Penrith, following the same line as the organiser's JOGLE race. It carries about 2,238ft of ascent and 1,427ft of descent across rural countryside, with four checkpoints en route. The course is not signed, so runners navigate with map and GPS.

About

The MTC Boggart is a single-day ultra of 37.6 miles running from Gretna Green in Scotland to Penrith in Cumbria, following the same line as Ultra Running's JOGLE stage race. Runners navigate the largely unmarked route themselves using map and GPS, passing four checkpoints across open rural countryside.
