Course

Start and finish at Halleck Park, 710 E Halleck Street, Papillion, Nebraska. A single 5K loops the park and joins the Papio Trail. The event is not chip timed and welcomes runners, walkers and ruckers.

About

Catch the wave and join us for the first annual Omaha Surf Wave Run 5k - a fun, family-friendly charity run in support of the Omaha Surf Soccer Club! Lace up your shoes and enjoy a scenic course around the Papillion Soccer Complex and Papio Trail as we come together to support youth athletics in our community.
