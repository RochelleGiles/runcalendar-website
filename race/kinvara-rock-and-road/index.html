About

Explore the rugged beauty of Ireland's west coast at the Kinvara Rock and Road event. This scenic race through County Galway offers a range of distances, from a challenging 3/4 Marathon to a fast-paced 10km, allowing runners of all levels to experience the stunning natural landscapes and charming local culture.
