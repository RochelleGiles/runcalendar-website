About

The Ibiza Trail Festival (Ibiza Trail Marathon) is a benchmark trail event at the Balearic, national and international levels, now past its 16th running. It offers Starter, Half Marathon and Marathon XL-format distances across the island's varied and technical terrain, with elevation gain to challenge runners. The festival combines the race with music, gastronomy and other activities.
