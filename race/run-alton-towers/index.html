About

Run Alton Towers is a unique running event held within the iconic Alton Towers Theme Park. Participants can choose from 5k, 10k, or half marathon distances, all starting and finishing among the park's incredible rides. The event offers a thrilling opportunity for runners of all abilities to challenge themselves in a spectacular theme park setting.
