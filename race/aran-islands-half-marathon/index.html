About

Explore the rugged beauty of Ireland's Aran Islands at the Aran Islands Half Marathon. This scenic race takes runners on a stunning journey through the windswept landscapes of Inis Mór, showcasing the island's dramatic cliffs, ancient stone walls, and rich cultural heritage. An unforgettable challenge for endurance athletes seeking a truly unique running experience.
