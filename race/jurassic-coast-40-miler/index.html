Course

Point-to-point along the South West Coast Path from Abbotsbury to Lulworth Cove, roughly 65km with the sea on the runner's right throughout. Rocky clifftop coastal trail with steep climbs and descents, waymarked by the Acorn sign. Checkpoints roughly every 10-20km with water, food and Coke. The start is reached by a 40-minute minibus from event HQ on Portland.

About

Explore the stunning Jurassic Coast of the United Kingdom at the Jurassic Coast 40 Miler. This challenging ultramarathon event takes runners on a scenic journey from Abbotsbury to Lulworth Cove, following the breathtaking South West Coast path. With dramatic cliffs, fossil-rich beaches, and picturesque villages along the route, this is a must-do event for adventurous endurance athletes.
