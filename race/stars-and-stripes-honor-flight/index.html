Course

Starts and finishes at Veterans Park on Lincoln Memorial Drive on the Milwaukee lakefront. The 5K turns right and loops the lagoon before heading out to the end of the pier; the one-mile walk turns left straight for the pier and back. A 5K ruck and a kids run are held alongside.

About
