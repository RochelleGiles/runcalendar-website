Course

A single-lap 44 km loop starting and finishing near Keswick, taking in England's highest peak. Roughly 90% singletrack with about 1,800 m of ascent, running through woodland and lakeshore trails to Seathwaite before an unmarked high mountain section over Scafell Pike's boulder fields and loose rock. Aid stations sit at Seathwaite (13 km and 29 km) and Watendlath Beck (37 km), with drop bags available.

About

A trail marathon incorporating England's highest peak. Based from Keswick, the route follows beautiful lakeshore trails, wooded valleys to spectacular high mountain trails, incorporating some of the most dramatic rock scenery in the Lake District.
