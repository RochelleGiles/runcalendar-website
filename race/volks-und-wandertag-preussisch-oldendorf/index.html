About

Explore the charming town of Preußisch Oldendorf at the annual Volks Und Wandertag running event. This community-driven race celebrates local culture, offering runners a scenic route through historic streets and picturesque countryside. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a casual jogger, this event promises an enjoyable experience for all.
