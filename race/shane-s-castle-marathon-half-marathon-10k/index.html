About

The Shane's Castle Marathon, Half Marathon & 10K is on 25 October 2026 in Co Antrim. The route is located within the Shanes Castle Estate making it virtually traffic-free. This stunning location is a great place to run - you will run where many of the epic scenes from Game of Thrones were filmed.
