Course

A 5K on the Withlacoochee State Trail, running along the shores of the Tsala Apopka chain of lakes and starting and finishing at Liberty Park in Inverness. Participants describe it as flat, straight and well maintained. The 5K sets off at 7:30 am, race walkers at 7:33 and the one-mile cakewalk at 7:36.

About

The Cake by the Lake 5K and One Mile Cakewalk finish back at Liberty Park in Inverness with finisher medals, live music, and festivities, capped off with the race's signature cake.
