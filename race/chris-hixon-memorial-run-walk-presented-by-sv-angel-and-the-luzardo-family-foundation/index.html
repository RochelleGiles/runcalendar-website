About

The Chris Hixon Memorial 5K is a run/walk on the boardwalk of Hollywood Beach, Florida, held around Chris Hixon's birthday to honor his memory as a friend, coach, and father. All proceeds support the Chris Hixon Athletic Scholarship Foundation.
