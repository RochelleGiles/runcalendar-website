About

A challenging 10k trail run at Ward Pound Ridge Reservation in Cross River, NY that climbs to the two highest points in the park (Raven Rocks Overlook and Fire Tower Summit). The event supports the Friends of Trailside Museum and Ward Pound Ridge Reservation and includes a virtual 100k challenge option.
