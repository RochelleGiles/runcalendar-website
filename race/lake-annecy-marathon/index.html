About

The Brooks Lake Annecy Marathon offers runners a breathtaking tour of the stunning landscapes surrounding the crystal-clear waters of Lake Annecy in southeastern France. With a course that winds through picturesque villages and along the lakeshore, this event promises an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels. Runners can look forward to taking in the region's natural beauty as they challenge themselves on this scenic and challenging course.
