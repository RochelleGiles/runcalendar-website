About

The Mohnesee Pokallauf is a scenic running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Möhne Reservoir in Germany. This popular race offers runners of all levels the chance to experience the beautiful natural surroundings while challenging themselves on an enjoyable course. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or new to the sport, the Mohnesee Pokallauf promises an unforgettable running experience.
