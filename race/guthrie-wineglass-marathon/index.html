Course

Point-to-point road marathon from Bath down the valley through Savona, Campbell (where the half starts), Coopers Plains and Painted Post to finish on Market Street in Corning. Fast, flat and net-downhill within BQ standards (under 1,500 ft drop); 6.5-hour limit; 8:15 am start. The 5K runs Saturday.

About

Celebrating its 45th year, the Guthrie Wineglass Marathon is a must-do destination race! A point-to-point course, the full marathon starts in Bath, NY, traveling the valley to Savona, Campbell (where the half marathon starts), onto Coopers Plains, Painted Post, and finishing on historic Market Street in Corning, NY. Runners will turn "Right on Bridge, Left on Market" where the golden tree lined street will guide them to the finish that is in their line of sight.
