Course

Four totally off-road courses on the mountain bike trails of the Kaiteriteri Mountain Bike Park, near Nelson. The half marathon, 16km, 8km and kids 3.2km all start and finish at the park and run entirely on purpose-built singletrack. Profits from the event go back into the Kaiteriteri Recreation Reserve.

About

The Milnes Beatson Kaiteriteri Gold is a running event with trail courses through the Kaiteriteri Mountainbike Park. It offers a Half Marathon, 16km, 8km, and Kids 3.2km options, all off-road. The event is supported by the Kaiteriteri Recreation Reserve, a 100% Crown-owned entity that invests all profits back into the Reserve for social, economic, environmental, and cultural purposes.
