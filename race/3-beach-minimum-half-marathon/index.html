3 Beach Minimum Half Marathon

RoadCoastal

Course

Coastal out-and-back half marathon starting and finishing at Nantasket Beach in Hull, Massachusetts, by the Mary Jeannette Murray Bath House, with a 7:30am start. The route is entirely on public road open to traffic, so runners keep to the right throughout. Aid stations with water and portaloos sit at Black Rock Beach, Sandy Beach and the former Cohasset Harbor Inn, each hit twice. The field is capped at 400 with a three-hour limit.
