About

The Free Care 5K is a fundraiser hosted by St. Barnabas Charities at their Gibsonia Campus on Meridian Road, featuring a USA Track & Field-certified 3.1-mile out-and-back course. It includes cash prizes, medals, team competitions, and a post-race party with raffle prizes to support the Free Care Fund.
