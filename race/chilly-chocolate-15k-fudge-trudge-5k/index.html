Course

About

The Chilly Chocolate 15K & Fudge Trudge 5K will start on North Broadway in Downtown Green Bay. Participants will enjoy running or walking through a winter wonderland and earning chocolate at the finish line. Participants will also enjoy post-race festivities at Pete's Garage. This event is a 4imprint Race Series event.
