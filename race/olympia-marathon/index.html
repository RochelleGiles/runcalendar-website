Course

A 42.195km loop from the Ancient Olympia Town Hall out through the villages of Koskina and Magira, past the archaeological site and over the bridge on the Kladeos River, by the Ancient Stadium and the Pierre de Coubertin monument, then on to Linaria and Agios Nikolaos before looping back. Starts at 9:00am.

About

The Olympia Marathon finishes inside the ancient stadium at Olympia, Greece, birthplace of the Olympic Games and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Runners can choose marathon, half marathon, 12K or 5K distances through the historic Peloponnese countryside surrounding the archaeological site.
