About

The Harmonie Mutuelle Amiens Urban Trail offers a unique 10 km running experience through the historic city of Amiens. Participants will explore the city's exceptional heritage, including 53 protected historical monuments and UNESCO World Heritage sites like the Notre-Dame Cathedral. The event showcases Amiens' rich cultural landscape, often called the 'Little Venice of the North' due to its numerous canals and floating gardens.
