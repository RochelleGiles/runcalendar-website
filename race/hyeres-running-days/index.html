About

Situated in the picturesque town of Hyères, France, the Hyères Running Days offers a unique running experience for athletes of all levels. Featuring both scenic coastal and historic urban routes, this annual event invites runners to explore the charming Mediterranean landscape while challenging themselves on a well-designed course. Whether you're seeking a scenic destination race or a chance to experience the vibrant local culture, the Hyères Running Days promises an unforgettable running adventure.
