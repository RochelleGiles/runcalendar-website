About

Skyline Scotland is a mountain and skyrunning festival based in Fort William, with courses crossing the ridges and scrambles of Ben Nevis, the UK's highest peak, and the neighbouring Mamores. Distances range from the 30K Ben Nevis Skyline to the 100-mile Skyline Scotland ultra, all through classic Highland terrain.
