Course

Held on the single-track desert trails of SARA Park above Lake Havasu. The half marathon and 13k climb out to the Pilot Rock Lighthouse on the lake shore, while the 7k stays within the park. An aid station sits on the beach and a four-hour limit applies to all races.

About

Havasu on the Rocks is a trail race in Lake Havasu City with half marathon, 13k, and 7k options, launched by the organizers of the Lizard Peak Scramble. All three distances explore SARA Park's desert terrain above Lake Havasu.
