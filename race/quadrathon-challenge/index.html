About

The Quadrathon Challenge offers an iconic 4-day running festival along the scenic Wild Atlantic Way in the Inishowen Peninsula. Runners can experience rugged coastal roads, stunning landscapes, and the hospitality of the local community while exploring the area around Malin Head, Ireland's most northerly point.
