Course

A lapped course based at the Tuscarawas Meadows shelter in Firestone Metropark, Akron. Entrants cover one, two or four loops to make 2, 4 or 8 miles, and may run, walk or ruck. The walking divisions are untimed.

About

This challenge honors Iraq combat veteran Anthony Salem, who died from addiction and the invisible wounds of war. It stands as a public declaration that no one should have to fight alone, raising awareness for veterans and individuals battling PTSD, addiction and trauma in recovery.
