About

Blue Sky Ascent & Round Trip is a mountain race in Idaho Springs, Colorado, that climbs the Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway, the highest paved road in North America, toward its summit above 14,000 feet. Runners choose the 14.5-mile Ascent or the 29-mile Round Trip.
