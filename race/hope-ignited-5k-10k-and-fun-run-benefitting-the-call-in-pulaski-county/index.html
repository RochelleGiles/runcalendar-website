About

The CALL in Pulaski County is ready to IGNITE HOPE within the community to bring awareness to the foster care crisis. This race will be a fundraising event for The CALL as we continue to recruit, train and support foster and adopt families. There are over 400 children and teens in foster care just in Pulaski County alone, and less than 150 homes open to care for them. The CALL is working to fill in those gaps to make sure no child is without a safe place to rest their head at night.
