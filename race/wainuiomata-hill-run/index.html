Course

Out-and-back hill run starting at Hugh Sinclair Park in central Wainuiomata. The 6.5 km Half Hill climbs to a lookout on Wainuiomata Hill and returns; the 10 km Full Hill continues over the hill before turning. Run and walk categories share the same course, alongside a buggy-friendly 6.5 km walk and a 1 km kids dash.

About

The Mitre 10 Mega Wainuiomata Hill Run offers 6.5km and 10km distances that climb Wainuiomata Hill, 195m above sea level, near Lower Hutt. It also includes a Buggy Half Hill 6.5km for families and a flat 1km dash for kids.
