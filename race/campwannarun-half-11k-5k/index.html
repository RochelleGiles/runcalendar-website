About

Run Campwannarun around beautiful Bald Eagle Lake and choose from a certified half marathon, 11K, or 5K. This spring course is ideal for prepping for Grandma's Marathon or securing an early corral placement for the Twin Cities Marathon. Enjoy scenic miles, frequent aid stations, free parking, and post-race breakfast.
