Course

A certified 10K at Goodwood, starting at the main grandstand with a 4km lap of the wide motor circuit, then a 3km section out on the surrounding country roads before returning to the circuit for the last 3km to the finish. Fast and almost entirely flat, on sealed surfaces throughout.
