Course

Coastal trail courses starting and finishing at Case Park, Horseshoe Bay Road, Bowen. Routes loop through beaches, headlands and lookouts, with rocky technical trail, steep climbs, fast descents, stair sections and soft sand, plus out-and-back spurs to lookouts. Terrain is uneven throughout.

About

The Rock 'N Reef Bowen Trail Run is a unique event that takes runners through the stunning natural landscapes of Bowen, Queensland. The course highlights the region's diverse terrain, including rocky trails, lush rainforests, and breathtaking coastal views. Participants can expect an unforgettable atmosphere as they challenge themselves on this picturesque trail run.
