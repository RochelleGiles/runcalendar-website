Course

An out-and-back segment of 0.52 miles each way at Roof Park: the 5K covers it three times and the 10K six times. Both start at 8:30am and carry a two-hour limit. Entry is by pre-registration only, with packet pick-up from 30 minutes to 10 minutes before the start.

About

The Viking 5K and 10K are run at Roof Park in New Cumberland, Pennsylvania, on repeated laps of a short out-and-back segment. Finisher medals go to all participants, with awards and a costume contest, and both races carry a two-hour time limit.
