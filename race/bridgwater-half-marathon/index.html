Course

About

The Bridgwater Half Marathon is a scenic 13.1-mile run through the picturesque Somerset countryside, taking runners on a journey through the charming villages of Chedzoy and Stawell. This captivating event offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels, showcasing the beautiful natural landscapes of the United Kingdom.
