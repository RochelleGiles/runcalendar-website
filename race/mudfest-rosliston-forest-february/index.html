About

Escape the winter doldrums and get down and dirty at Mudfest Rosliston Forest! This 4-hour running challenge in the scenic Rosliston Forest, just south of Burton-on-Trent, promises a muddy, exhilarating adventure through winding trails and obstacle-filled courses. Runners of all levels will relish the chance to test their mettle and embrace the elements in this unique off-road event.
