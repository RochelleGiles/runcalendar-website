About

The Trail des 7 Collines offers runners a beautiful journey through the Brionnais countryside. In its 8th edition, the event features two main trail running distances with varying elevation gains, showcasing the region's seven hills. Participants are rewarded with a homemade sweet and savory buffet at the finish line.
