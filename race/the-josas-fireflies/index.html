About

The Josas Fireflies is a popular running event in France that takes place in the charming town of Jouy-en-Josas. With over 500 runners on the starting line, the event is known for its lively atmosphere, glowing headlamps, and festive spirit. The scenic course offers a mix of terrain, including hills and single-track trails, providing a challenging yet enjoyable experience for participants.
