About

The Glenelg Classic is a 5km and 10km fun run and walk organised by the South Australian Road Runners Club. Both courses start at Wigley Reserve, Glenelg, and head north around the Patawalonga along the coastal path, with the 10km extending further before returning to the start. The 10km starts at 8:00am and the 5km at 8:15am.
