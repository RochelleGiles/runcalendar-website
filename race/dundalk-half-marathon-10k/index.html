About

The Dundalk Half Marathon & 10K takes runners on a scenic tour of the charming suburbs of Dundalk, County Louth, Ireland. Boasting a stunning route that showcases the local architecture and natural beauty, this event is a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable racing experience. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to enjoy the vibrant atmosphere, the Dundalk Half Marathon & 10K is sure to impress.
