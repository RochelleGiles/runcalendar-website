About

La Légende du Graoully is a legendary trail running event in Woippy, France. Runners will challenge themselves by taking on the mythical trail and immersing themselves in the legend of the Graoully, a fearsome dragon that once terrorized the region. The event offers various distances to cater to runners of all levels, from a 13K to an ultra-distance 55K. Participants can expect an exciting atmosphere with live music, fireworks, and post-race festivities.
