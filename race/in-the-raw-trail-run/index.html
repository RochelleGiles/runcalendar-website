Course

Marked trail courses in the Numinbah Valley, in the Gold Coast hinterland. Four distances start in sequence from the same event base, the 25km at 7:00am through to the 4km at 9:30am. Organisers advise walkers to enter the 15km rather than the longer courses, and runners must stay on the marked route throughout.

About

The stunning Numinbah Valley plays host to numerous adventures each and every year - and its no surprise with it's stunning and challenging terrain the perfect playground for trail runners, obstacle racers and adventure seekers. The Triple Crown Long Course (25km) and Short Course (15km) events will be accompanied by a 4km and 9.3km event for the young stars, fresh faces or wiser souls!
