Course

Starts and finishes at the Beaver Valley Community Centre in Thornbury, Ontario, on the shore of Georgian Bay. The 1km kids' run goes at 9:00am and the 21K, 10K and 5K at 9:30am. Course support on the half marathon runs for three hours.

About

The Thornbury Turkey Trot is a fall road race in the South Georgian Bay town of Thornbury, Ontario, with 5K, 10K and half marathon (21K) distances starting from the Beaver Valley Community Centre.
