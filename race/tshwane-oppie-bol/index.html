Course

A fast, flat road race held at the SANDF Sports Grounds on the corner of Hendrik Potgieter and Hendrik Alberts Roads in Centurion. Three distances are offered — 10km, 5km and a 1.6km family run — on level tarmac around the grounds. The race supports Disability Rights Awareness Month.

About

Tshwane Oppie Bol is a road race starting and finishing at Pilditch Stadium in Pretoria, with 10km and 5km distances. It's a popular fixture on the Tshwane running calendar for club and recreational runners heading into the summer season.
