About

The Monte Rosa Skymarathon is an epic mountain running event that challenges athletes through the stunning alpine landscape of Monte Rosa. Participants will experience breathtaking mountain terrain, high-altitude trails, and spectacular views of the surrounding peaks. This skyrunning event offers multiple race categories that test runners' endurance and mountain skills.
