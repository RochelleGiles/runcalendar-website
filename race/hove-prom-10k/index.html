Course

Two fast, pancake-flat laps along Hove Promenade, starting and finishing at the same point with a U-turn at each end. The surface is paved promenade, shared with cyclists and pedestrians, marked with arrows and cones and guided by marshals. Water station just before the U-turn and another at the finish.
