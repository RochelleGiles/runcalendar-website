Course

A 5.25km walk/run lap at Avondale University, with laps setting off on the hour every hour from 6pm for up to eight hours. Entrants complete as many laps as they choose. The event is run at night with glow sticks, fire pits and live entertainment on site.

About

The Illumin8 Challenge in Cooranbong, NSW, challenges entrants to complete up to 8 laps of a 5.25km walk/run course at night. The event raises money for the Eyes for India charity, which provides the gift of sight to under-served people in India.
