Course

Net-downhill road half marathon dropping 3,381 ft down the canyon into Heber Valley, finishing at Old Mill Elementary School in Heber City, with runners bused to the start. A 5K and an all-abilities half-mile event share the finish area. USATF-certified course.

About

Join us in experiencing the breathtaking beauty of Heber Valley as you race an all-downhill half marathon course, surrounded by stunning mountain views and the crisp canyon air of the Wasatch Mountains. Make Runtastic HEBER a tradition for your family. Enjoy an unforgettable race, a vibrant and welcoming atmosphere, and the opportunity to give back.
