About

The Stilfserjoch Stelvio Marathon is a breathtaking running event that takes place against the stunning backdrop of the Italian Alps. Runners will conquer the challenging Stelvio Pass, one of the highest paved roads in the Alps, offering unparalleled views and an unforgettable experience for endurance athletes.
