Course

Undulating tarmac road race on quiet, wide lanes around Woodchurch, near Ashford in Kent. Start and finish are at Woodchurch Green. The half marathon is a single lap of the countryside circuit and the marathon covers the same lap twice.

About

Explore the picturesque Kent countryside on a scenic, multi-terrain course at the Kent Spring Marathon & Half Marathon. This popular event offers runners a challenging yet rewarding experience, with rolling hills, winding paths, and stunning rural views throughout the route. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply enjoying the beautiful surroundings, this is a must-do event for runners in the United Kingdom.
