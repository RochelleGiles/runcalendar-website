About

The Feriascapade is a popular running event held in Dax, France. The event features a 10km official race that is part of the Landes Road Championship, as well as a non-timed 8.5km walk. Participants can look forward to over €25,000 in prizes awarded through a raffle, including a car and dream vacations.
