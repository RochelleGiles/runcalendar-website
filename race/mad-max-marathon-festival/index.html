Course

Based at the Broken Hill Racecourse. The 5km follows the racecourse parkrun course with a short extension; the longer distances add sections of dirt track alongside Racecourse Road, and the marathon is two laps of the half marathon course. Surfaces are a mix of racecourse track and unsealed outback dirt, described by the organisers as a little unpredictable.

About

Immerse yourself in the post-apocalyptic world of the Mad Max Marathon Running Festival, as you explore the rugged outback and historic city of Broken Hill, Australia. This unique event offers runners a thrilling challenge through the stunning desert landscapes that inspired the iconic Mad Max films. Embrace your inner road warrior and experience the adventure of a lifetime.
