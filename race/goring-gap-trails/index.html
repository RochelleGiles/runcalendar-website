Course

Predominantly flat trail routes along the Thames and through the villages of the Chiltern Hills, based at Goring. The 50 mile ultra runs on the Saturday; the 52K ultra and half marathon on the Sunday. Routes are waymarked and marshalled at tricky points, so no navigation is needed.

About

The Goring Gap Trails event features multi-terrain routes at the foot of the Chiltern Hills, using riverside trails along the river Thames. The event is designed for those new to ultra running, with support stations every 5-6 miles and long cut-off times.
