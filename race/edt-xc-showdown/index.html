Course

All races share a common start and finish at Aurora Sports Park. The 2K is entirely on grass; the 3K and 4K mix grass and hard-packed gravel with a couple of short crossings over a one-lane asphalt road. All courses are flat.

About

Whether you run for a team, as an individual, or you just want to try out the experience of running a cross country race, you are welcome to join us for the Elevated XC Showdown! Boys/Girls will run in combined races, and will be separated by age groups below. All courses utilize the same start/finish area. Courses are flat and VERY spectator friendly.
