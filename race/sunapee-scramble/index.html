Course

A two-lap up-and-down mountain race of roughly 9.3 miles with about 3,300 feet of climbing, both laps starting and finishing at the Mount Sunapee Base Lodge. Lap one climbs ski slope, service road and trail to the summit before a 1.75-mile descent; lap two climbs 3.5 miles by service road and the Newbury Trail, then takes the same descent back to the lodge.

About

The Sunapee Scramble is an up-and-down mountain race at Mount Sunapee in Newbury, New Hampshire, climbing the resort's ski slopes toward the summit and back. It's a short, steep test of vertical speed on one of the region's classic peaks.
