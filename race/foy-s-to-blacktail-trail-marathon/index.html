Course

A 26.2-mile trail marathon, billed as the only trail marathon in Northwest Montana, running from Herron Park toward Blacktail Mountain near Kalispell on single and double-track dirt trails with roughly 5,000 feet of climbing. The field is capped at the first 100 registrants.

About

The Foy's to Blacktail Trail Marathon is a challenging 26-mile trail race on single and double track dirt trails with over 5,000 feet of climbing in the Flathead Valley. It starts from trails spanning Herron Park to Blacktail Mountain in Kalispell, Montana.
