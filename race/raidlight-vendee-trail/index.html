About

The Raidlight Vendee Trail is a thrilling running event that takes participants through the picturesque landscapes of the Vendée region. Organized by the renowned outdoor gear brand Raidlight, this challenging trail run offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the area while pushing their physical limits.
