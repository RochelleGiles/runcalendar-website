Course

Run on the wide gravel carriage roads of the Rockefeller State Park Preserve, starting and finishing at historic Rockwood Hall. The route opens with Hudson River views before turning into woodland, crossing meadows and historic stone bridges. Non-technical underfoot but continuously rolling.
