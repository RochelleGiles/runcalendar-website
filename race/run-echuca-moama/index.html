Course

A loop through the twin river towns, crossing the Murray twice and covering both Victoria and NSW. The route passes historic paddlesteamers, winds through the Port of Echuca and finishes across the Old Echuca-Moama Bridge. The 21.1km is two laps of the 10km course; the 10km and 21.1km start at 8am and the 5km at 9:15am. Water stations are on course.

About

The Run Echuca Moama event takes place in the picturesque twin towns of Echuca and Moama, offering a scenic 21.1km course along the banks of the Murray River. Runners can enjoy the beautiful natural surroundings and the vibrant atmosphere of this community-driven event.
