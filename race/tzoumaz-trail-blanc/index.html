About

Tzoumaz Trail Blanc is a winter running event that offers two challenging courses - a 17km route with 880m of elevation gain and a 12km route with 660m of elevation gain. The event takes place in the scenic Tzoumaz region, providing participants with a unique and picturesque winter running experience.
