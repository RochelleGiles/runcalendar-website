About

The Yashada Pimpri Chinchwad Half Marathon is held around Pimple Saudagar in Pimpri-Chinchwad, near Pune, and takes its name from the Yashwantrao Chavan Academy of Development Administration. Alongside the half marathon, 10K and 5K, it includes a 3K Saree Run for women.
