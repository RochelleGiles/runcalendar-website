About

Górski Zimowy Maraton Ślężański is a challenging mountain running event that takes place in the beautiful Ślęża mountain range near the town of Sobótka, Poland. The event features both a full marathon and a half marathon distance, with stunning views and a demanding course that tests the endurance of participants.
