Course

Club race starting and finishing at the SBSC clubhouse in Orleigh Park, West End, on the club course beside the Brisbane River. The 5km, 10km and 15km start together at 6:00am and timing closes at 8:30am. Entry is online in advance only with no on-the-day registration; visitors are welcome and collect a temporary bib at the clubhouse. Runners carry their own water.

About

The Brisbane Road Runners Club kicks off the new year with a scenic race through the heart of Australia's vibrant riverside city. Runners can choose from 15km, 10km, and 5km distances, each offering stunning views and a challenging course. This popular annual event draws enthusiastic participants from across the region to celebrate the joy of running.
