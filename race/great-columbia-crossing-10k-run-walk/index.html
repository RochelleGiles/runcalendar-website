Course

Point-to-point road course across the Astoria-Megler Bridge from the Washington side to Astoria, Oregon, mostly flat with one steep climb to the bridge's peak. The bridge closes to traffic 8:30-11 a.m. and all participants must finish by 11:30 a.m.

About

Experience the glory of the Columbia River as you trek across the Astoria-Megler Bridge during this unique opportunity to walk/run across the bridge! This 10K offers a scenic adventure on a fairly flat road with one STEEP incline to the peak of the bridge. It is the longest continuous truss bridge in North America. Whether you're an experienced runner or looking for a family-friendly event, the Great Columbia Crossing 10K appeals to everyone.
