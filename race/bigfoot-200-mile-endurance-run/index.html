Course

A 207.9-mile nonstop, point-to-point traverse of the Cascades starting at Mount St. Helens, crossing lava fields left by the 1980 eruption, long mountaintop ridgelines, deep old growth forest and stream crossings. Around 45,563ft of ascent and 46,880ft of descent. Entry is by lottery.

About

The Bigfoot 200 is a 200-mile ultramarathon looping through the Gifford Pinchot National Forest around Mount St. Helens in southwest Washington, one of the sport's toughest 200-mile courses.
