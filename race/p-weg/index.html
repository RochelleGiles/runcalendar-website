About

The P Weg Marathon is a unique event in Plettenberg, Germany that combines hiking, running, and biking. The marathon and ultramarathon courses follow the P Weg, a loop around the city that includes four valleys and their surrounding mountains. The event has a special atmosphere, with participants celebrated by the Plettenberg crowd as they cross the finish line on a stage in the city center.
