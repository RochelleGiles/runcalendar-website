About

Organized for 23 years, this incredible 'King's Highway' will take you through Jordan from north to south for a crossing at the heart of the landscapes of this calm and welcoming country! The journey begins with a first stage on the shores of the Dead Sea, then a float bath in this particularly salty water. The next two stages take place in the canyons of Jordan near Dana and the Templar castle of Shubak. Then comes the magnificent day of tourist visit to the city of Petra, followed the next day by the stage that takes place in the very heart of this Wonder of the Ancient World. Finally, two days and two nights in the Wadi-rum desert, an extraordinary desert with sand of course but also its many canyons and sandstone cliffs, a setting that will be engraved forever.
