Course

A six-hour timed trail event at Beacon Hill Country Park, Leicestershire, run as repeats of a 3.3-mile loop. Runners complete as many laps as they like, from a single 5k lap to 50km or more. The loop climbs steadily for its first half on well-maintained crushed stone paths through mature woodland to a trig point, crosses a field and descends back to the start.

About

The Badger Challenge is a unique 6-hour running race held on an off-road loop through the scenic Beacon Hill Country Park in Leicestershire, UK. Runners will navigate a challenging 3.3-mile course, testing their endurance and exploring the beautiful natural surroundings. This event promises an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners and those seeking a thrilling new challenge.
