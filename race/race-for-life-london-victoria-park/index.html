Course

A flat 5k on tarmac paths and grass around Victoria Park, east London. It starts on hardstanding between the Hub Cafe and the Burdett Coutts fountain and finishes on grass, which turns soft in wet weather.

About

Race for Life is Cancer Research UK's charity fundraiser, with 3K and 5K options for walkers, joggers and runners of any pace. This London edition takes place in Victoria Park, the city's oldest public park, in the East End.
