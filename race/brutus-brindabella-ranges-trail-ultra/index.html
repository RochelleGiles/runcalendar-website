Course

Three courses on dirt roads and forest trails in the Brindabella Ranges west of Canberra. The 60km starts and finishes at Blue Range Hut, climbing Coree Summit Road and taking in the Slippery Dip descent; the 100km BTrail starts at Corin Dam and traverses Mt Ginini, Franklin and Aggie to Mt Coree. The 14km Knife climbs through radiata pine to Knife Point. Checkpoints carry sports drink and refreshments.

About

BRuTUS (the Brindabella Ranges ultimate Trail Ultra Spectacular) is a tough trail ultra in the mountains west of Canberra, with 60km, 100km and 14km options through steep, remote terrain.
