About

The P'tit Train Du Nord Marathon is known as the fastest course in America, featuring a linear route through breathtaking natural scenery. The race takes place in the beautiful Laurentides region, offering runners an exceptional autumn running experience along what appears to be a converted rail trail. The event has gained recognition for its high Boston Marathon qualification rate.
