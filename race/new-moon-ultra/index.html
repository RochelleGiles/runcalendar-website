Course

A night lap race on private MTB singletrack at Rowallan Park, Holts Road, Mackay, starting and finishing at Campsites 2 and 3. Runners set off on a 6.7 km lap every hour on the hour from 6pm, choosing one lap, six laps (40.2 km) or twelve laps (80.4 km). Terrain is remote and technical with changing visibility through the night.

About

Venture into the untamed wilderness of Queensland's Mackay region for the New Moon Ultra, part of the Off The Beaten Track Trail Run Series. Runners will be captivated by the stunning natural landscapes and the challenge of navigating remote trails under the ethereal glow of the new moon. This unique event promises an unforgettable adventure for seasoned ultrarunners and nature enthusiasts alike.
