Course

Looped courses starting and finishing at the Horse Staging Area in Cave Creek Regional Park, north of Phoenix. Single track and dirt road link the park with the Maricopa Trail, Tonto National Forest and Spur Cross Ranch Conservation Area, over rock, sand, gravel and cactus country. Aid stations fall every 3 to 7 miles; the event is cupless.

About

Elephant Mountain Trail Runs is a trail race series in Cave Creek, Arizona, hosted by Aravaipa Running. Distances from 12K to 50 miles cross rocky Sonoran Desert singletrack in the foothills north of Phoenix.
