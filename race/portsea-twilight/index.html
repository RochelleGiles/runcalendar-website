Course

Both courses start and finish at the Point Nepean Quarantine Station event village inside Point Nepean National Park, with the start line about 50m from the village. The 9.5km is undulating and the 5.5km is flat. The course is closed to vehicles and drink stations are placed roughly every 3km.

About

Portsea Twilight is an evening race held in Point Nepean National Park on the Mornington Peninsula. Runners choose a 9.5km undulating course or a flatter 5.5km, and a kids dash is also offered. Both distances start and finish at the Quarantine Station event village on a vehicle-free course with drink stations every 3km.
