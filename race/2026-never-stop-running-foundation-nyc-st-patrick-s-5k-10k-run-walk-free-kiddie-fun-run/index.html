Course

A USATF-certified loop through Flushing Meadows Corona Park on park pathways, starting and finishing near the Queens Theater on the north side of the park. Runners pass the Unisphere twice and circle the Fountain of the Planets. The 5K runs one loop and the 10K two.

About

This event features a USATF certified 5K and 10K run/walk through scenic Flushing Meadows Corona Park, starting and finishing near the Queens Theater. A free kiddie fun run dash starts at 9AM, with proceeds benefiting the Never Stop Running Foundation.
