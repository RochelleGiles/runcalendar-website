Course

Lapped routes on the paths and broad avenues of Greenwich Park, starting and finishing at the Bandstand: 1.5 laps for the 5K and three for the 10K, with a mini lap for the junior race. Each kilometre is marked with distance flags and the races are chip timed.

About

Multiple laps of historic Greenwich Park, home to the Royal Observatory and the Prime Meridian, with views across the Thames to Canary Wharf. Expect a short, sharp climb within an otherwise rolling course.
