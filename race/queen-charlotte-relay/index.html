Course

Five-person team relay of about 34.65 km run entirely on the sealed Queen Charlotte Drive from Picton to Havelock. Legs measure 5.1 km, 8.6 km, 8.1 km, 6.2 km and 6.65 km. The opening leg climbs steadily to Wedge Point, the middle legs undulate, and the last rises before dropping sharply to the finish at the Havelock Hotel.

About

The annual Queen Charlotte Relay is a 5 person team relay starting in Picton and finishing in Havelock. This is one of the oldest road relays in New Zealand, first held in 1982, and includes both running and walking categories.
