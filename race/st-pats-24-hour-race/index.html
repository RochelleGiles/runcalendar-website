Course

A looped timed race at St. Patrick's County Park in South Bend, Indiana, starting at 8:00am. Most of the loop is dirt and grass trail with three short asphalt crossings. 24-hour, 12-hour and 6-hour events are offered, each finishing with an optional quarter-mile out-and-back in the final half hour.
