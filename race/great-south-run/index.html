About

The AJ Bell Great South Run is probably the world's favourite 10 mile running event, with inspiring history, salty air and glorious sea views guaranteed. Portsmouth's supporters will keep your spirits and motivation up the whole way round, with blaring music, cheers, high fives, fist bumps all part of the incredible Great South Run experience. Whether you're chasing a new PB, raising money for a cause close to your heart, or soaking up that unbeatable South Coast energy, this is a run for everyone.
