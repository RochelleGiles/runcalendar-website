Course

Looped course in Freeport, Maine on the Wolfe's Neck Center and Freeport Conservation Trust trails. In-person distances of 10, 30 and 50 miles start in waves from 6:30am, with relay options over 10 and 30 miles; a virtual option runs over several weeks. Aid station detail is not stated.

About

This ultra in Freeport, Maine runs point to point between working farms, with 5, 10, 30, and 50 mile distances crossing the town's rural countryside. The course links two different farms rather than looping back to the start.
