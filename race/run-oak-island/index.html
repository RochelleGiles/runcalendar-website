Course

Two-day road event with start/finish at 4815 E Oak Island Drive. Saturday's 26.25-mile ultra and the marathon share a route past the arboretum with around 11 miles of ocean views and an Intracoastal bridge crossing near mile 23; two separate half marathons run east and west. Sunday holds the flat oceanfront 10K, 5K and a one-mile out-and-back on Dolphin Drive.

About

Run Oak Island is a two-day road race weekend on Oak Island, North Carolina, produced by Coastal Race Productions. Saturday holds the 26.25-mile 'world's shortest ultra', the marathon and two half marathons; Sunday brings the 10K, 5K and 1 Mile on flat oceanfront roads near the beach.
