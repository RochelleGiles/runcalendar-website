Course

A coastal loop from basecamp at Dunster, near Dunster Castle, taking in the Minehead section of the 630-mile South West Coast Path before heading over Exmoor. The full 50 km carries about 1,600 m of ascent, with 25 km and 10 km options on the same route. The route is fully signed with GPS tracking, rest stops roughly every 12.5 km and major stops every 25 km offering catering and medical cover.

About

Starting from the 'basecamp' in the shadows of Dunster Castle, the 50km course begins with the first Minehead section of the famous 630 mile Coastal Path. The coastal path provides fantastic views across the Bristol Channel towards South Wales in the distance.
