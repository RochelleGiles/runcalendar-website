About

The Trail Des Champignons is a unique trail running event set in the picturesque landscapes of Ardèche. Participants can choose from multiple trail distances and a randonnée, all designed to be run in duos or individually. The event offers a chance to explore the wild trails of the region, featuring scenic routes through rivers, forests, and countryside.
