Course

A fully accessible six-hour race at Heckscher State Park in East Islip, run as 3.1-mile loops from Field 2 — runners complete as many as they can, up to eight loops for a marathon. A 100m dash for all abilities runs alongside.

About

Inclusion in Motion is a six-hour endurance event at Heckscher State Park in East Islip, New York, where athletes of all abilities come together to promote inclusion and defy limits. Participants can run, walk, roll or push, with a 100-meter dash included alongside the main event.
