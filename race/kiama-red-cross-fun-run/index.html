Course

Three point-to-point courses along the Kiama coastline, all finishing at Surf Beach. The half marathon starts at Kiama Surf Beach at 7:00am, the 8km walk/run starts from James Oates Reserve at Minnamurra and the 5km from the northern car park at Bombo, both at 9:00am. No aid station or surface detail is published.

About

The Kiama Red Cross Fun Run is a community event through the coastal town of Kiama on the NSW South Coast, with 5km, 8km and half marathon options. Proceeds support the local Red Cross branch.
