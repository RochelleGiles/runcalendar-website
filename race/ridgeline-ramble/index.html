Course

A point-to-point trail run over scenic sections of the Ridgeline Trail in south Eugene, starting at Blanton Heights Trailhead and finishing at Frank Kinney Park. The 20K and 10K are almost entirely on trail, with a few road crossings and a short stretch facing traffic down Dillard Road before rejoining the trail. Four aid stations serve the course.
