Course

About

Grab a friend and trot in the woods this fall! Harbison State Forest is a hidden gem tucked just outside Columbia, SC. Spanning over 2,000 acres along the Broad River, it offers a network of diverse, well-maintained trails perfect for our fall celebration. You'll run through tall pines and hardwoods, cross peaceful creeks, and take on varied elevation—challenging enough to keep it interesting but welcoming for all experience levels.
