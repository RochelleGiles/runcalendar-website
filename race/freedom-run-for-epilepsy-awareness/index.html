About

The Freedom Run for Epilepsy Awareness is an annual charity road race that starts and finishes at Kailua High School on Oahu. Runners choose a chip-timed 5K or 10K, while a separate 1-mile walk lets participants raise awareness without a clock. Proceeds benefit the Epilepsy Foundation of Hawai'i.
