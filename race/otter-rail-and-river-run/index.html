Course

An undulating 10K from Tipton St John in Devon, heading downstream alongside the River Otter, through Harpford woods and along the old Sidmouth railway line, then back through fields out to Fluxton and along tracks into the village. Largely off-road. A roughly one-mile fun run runs alongside it, on an off-road route through grazed fields.

About

Explore the enchanting Otter Valley in Devon at the Otter Rail and River Run, a 10km scenic trail race that winds through picturesque countryside and along the banks of the River Otter. This unique event offers runners the chance to experience the natural beauty of this stunning region, with the route taking in historic railway lines and tranquil riverside paths. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Otter Rail and River Run promises an unforgettable running adventure.
