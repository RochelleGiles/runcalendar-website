About

The Matsue Castle Marathon is a full marathon and 4.4km fun run starting and finishing at the Matsue City General Sports Gymnasium in Shimane's prefectural capital. It was revived after a 14-year gap without a full marathon in the prefecture, and the race caps entries at 5,000 runners.
