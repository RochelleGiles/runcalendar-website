Course

Starts at the Center Hill Recreation Area overlooking Center Hill Dam, crosses the dam and follows roads into Edgar Evins State Park through a native pollinator corridor towards the Visitor Center. Road inclines make it demanding in both directions. Aid stations roughly every 1.5 to 2 miles.

About

Race Day: November 21, 2026 Join us for the 4th Annual Dam 5 Miler! This race starts at the Center Hill Recreation Area, which offers a scenic vantage point of the Center Hill Dam. The run continues over Center Hill Dam towards then enters Edgar Evins State Park. As you enter the State Park, a native pollinator corridor hugs the roads as you travel to the Visitor Center Office. The route in both directions has challenging points due to road inclines.
