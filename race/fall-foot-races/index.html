Course

The half marathon and 10K run point-to-point from the Cascade Lakes Welcome Center, dropping downhill on the bike path and joining the Deschutes River Trail to finish at Riverbend Park, losing about 500 feet and splitting roughly half pavement, half smooth wide trail. The 5K starts and finishes at Riverbend Park on a tour of the Old Mill and river parks.

About

CORK and Run Bend partner to host the Fall Foot Races, a Central Oregon autumn event combining newer additions — the Fall Foot Half and Fleet Foot 5K — with two of Oregon's oldest and most beloved races, the Bigfoot 10K and Little Foot kids run. Together they offer distance options for all runners and walkers.
