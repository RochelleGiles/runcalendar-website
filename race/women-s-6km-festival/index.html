Course

A point-to-point 6K finishing near Downtown Centennial Plaza in Canton. Participants are shuttled from the plaza to the start between 6:00 and 7:45 AM, with the elite race off at 8:00 AM and the community run at 8:05 AM. A 0.6 km Little Ladies Loop for ages 0-10 runs on a secure loop near the finish at 9:40 AM.

About

Join us this July for another year of celebrating women and girls of all ages and talents. We present a full morning festival combined with a Women's only 6K run / walk.
