Course

A trail course on the Sunshine Coast Hinterland Great Walk, starting and finishing at QCCC in Mapleton. The route weaves through tall forest, drops into deep gullies and follows ridge lines with long views out over the hinterland. Checkpoints are spread across each distance and drop bags are available for runners without a crew.

About

Conquer the Blackall Range on the Sunshine Coast hinterland at the Blackall 100, a challenging trail running ultra that takes runners across stunning mountain landscapes. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of this iconic Australian destination as you push your limits on a course that promises breathtaking views and unforgettable experiences. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a new adventure, the Blackall 100 delivers an unparalleled trail running challenge.
