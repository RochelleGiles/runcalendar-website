Course

A 520km stage race across the Northern Territory outback from Alice Springs to Uluru, run over 9 stages in 10 days. The route crosses West MacDonnell National Park, continues through Finke Gorge National Park and finishes in Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park. Underfoot is pebbly and sandy vehicle track plus sections of single track, run food self-supported.

About

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the heart of Australia's iconic Outback at the Track Outback Race. This self-supported, 520km foot race from Alice Springs to Uluru challenges runners with 9 stages of rugged terrain, stunning landscapes, and the opportunity to experience the raw beauty of the Australian wilderness.
