About

"A Warrant to Run" is hosted by the United States Army Warrant Officer (USAWOA) Longrifle Chapter. Warrant Officers are the few technical experts in their field, making up less than three percent of the Army. Our organization is comprised of both active and retired members with the purpose to recognize Warrant Officer excellence, provide mentorship and scholarship opportunities, and to represent the professionalism/time honored history of the Warrant Officer Cohort.
