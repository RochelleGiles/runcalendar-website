Course

The 5K starts at North Carroll Road and East Church Street and runs neighbourhood streets including North Avenue, Elm Drive, Anderson Road, Cleghorn Street, Russell Street and Myrtle Street before finishing at The Mill Amphitheater. The 1.5 mile route is a shorter loop via North Avenue and Elm Drive. Surface and aid stations are not stated.

About

We encourage women to take a holistic approach to prioritizing their health journey believing that it leads to healthier and happier lives for themselves, their families and their communities. Our mission is to empower women to prioritize their health through community engagement, physical activity and positive mental health. We support and provide resources to women to educate and empower them along their health journey so they can inspire future generations.
