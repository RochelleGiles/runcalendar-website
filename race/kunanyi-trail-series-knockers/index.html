Course

Event hub is the lookout near the Forest Road carpark on Knocklofty, West Hobart. Both courses run on a mix of wide, smooth firetrail and well-built single track, with some technical sections. Knockers Enduro is 11km and Knockers Sport 4km. No aid stations are described.

About

Knocklofty is the hill that West Hobart sits on, featuring an incredible network of bush trails close to the CBD. The Knockers Trail Runs offer beautiful trails with wide firetrails and single tracks, providing awesome views of kunanyi and the Derwent River.
