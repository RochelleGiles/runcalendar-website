About

The Fire on the Rocks 10K is a great intermediate level trail race held at Zindel Park in McElhattan PA. It's going to challenge each runner with lots of climbs and descents throughout the journey. The course will utilize local trails that are used for the Frozen Snot and Boulder Beast races. Expect about 1400 feet of climbing throughout the 6.6 miles.
