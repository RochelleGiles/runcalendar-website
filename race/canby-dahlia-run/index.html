Course

Starts and finishes at Swan Island Dahlias outside Canby, with the 5K and 10K following the same general route through surrounding farmland. The surface is mostly paved road with a short gravel stretch and a small grass segment into the finish. Water stations are placed along the course, with portable toilets at the start, midway and finish.

About

The Canby Dahlia Run's 10K and 5K courses pass Swan Island Dahlias, a working dahlia farm in the Willamette Valley near Canby, Oregon. The race is timed to the farm's late-summer dahlia bloom.
