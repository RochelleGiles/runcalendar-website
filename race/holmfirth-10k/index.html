Course

An undulating but very runnable 10K on quiet country roads around Hade Edge, near Holmfirth. The route starts on Dunford Road with a gentle climb to Harden, drops to Winscar Reservoir, climbs Winscar Edge and undulates back along Dunford Road before a fast final 2km into Hade Edge, finishing with a lap of the recreation ground. 211m of total ascent.

About

A 10K road race through Holmfirth in the Holme Valley, West Yorkshire, organised by Holmfirth Harriers Athletics Club. The route takes in the town before finishing back near the valley centre.
