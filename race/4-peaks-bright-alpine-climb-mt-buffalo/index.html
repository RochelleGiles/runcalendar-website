Course

Day 1 of the four-day Bright Alpine Climb. A point-to-point 10.5km ascent with 1345m of vertical gain, starting at Eurobin Creek Picnic Area on Mt Buffalo Road and finishing at the top of the steps at Buffalo Chalet. The route follows The Big Walk mountain walking track and includes four road crossings. A water-only stop sits at Mackies Lookout, about 4.6km from the finish.

About

The 4 Peaks Bright Alpine Climb is a challenging running event that takes participants up the scenic Mt. Buffalo in the Victorian Alps. The course features a 1,345m vertical climb over 10.5km, offering stunning views and landscapes along the way. This event is a test of endurance and determination, attracting runners from across Australia.
