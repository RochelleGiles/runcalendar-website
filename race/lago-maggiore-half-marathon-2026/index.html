About

The Lago Maggiore Half Marathon runs 21 km along the lakefront of Lake Maggiore, with the Borromean Gulf as a backdrop, from Stresa to Pallanza. It is home to a 59'06" clocking, the second-fastest half marathon time ever run in Italy, on a fast, record-breaking course.
