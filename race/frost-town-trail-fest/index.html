Course

Starts and finishes at the Cumming Nature Center. The 25K takes a single rugged loop over historic logging roads and the Rattlesnake Hill ridgeline, with a steep winding climb and descent and three aid stations about four miles apart; the 10K stays on the more runnable Nature Center trail system with far less climbing. A 3K fun run is offered for children.

About

A trail running event celebrating the RMSC Cumming Nature Center with challenging 25k and 10k courses through the Bristol Hills. Proceeds support environmental education programming at the nature center.
