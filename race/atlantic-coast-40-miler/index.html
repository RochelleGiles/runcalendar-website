Course

A 40-mile point-to-point along the South West Coast Path, starting at Fistral Beach in Newquay and finishing at St Ives Bay Holiday Park in Hayle. The acorn-waymarked route is rugged, with steep climbs and descents throughout. Checkpoints sit roughly every 10 miles, stocked with bars, snacks, filled rolls and hot drinks, and hot soup is served at the finish.

About

Explore the stunning Atlantic Coast of North Cornwall on this 40-mile endurance challenge. Starting at the iconic Fistral Beach in Newquay, runners will follow the rugged coastline south to the Hayle River Mouth, taking in breathtaking ocean vistas and charming seaside villages along the way. This epic event is a must for adventure-seeking runners looking to conquer one of the UK's most beautiful coastal routes.
