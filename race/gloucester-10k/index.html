Course

A predominantly flat, traffic-free 10K from Gloucester Park, run as one lap partially retraced over road, footpath and canal towpath. The route passes the Cathedral and heads through the Docks and Gloucester Quays before following the canal towpath back. Water stations are at 4K and 8K, with distance markers every kilometre and chip timing.

About

The Gloucester 10K is a classic road race through the historic streets of this vibrant UK city. Organised by the Rotary Club of Gloucester Quays, this popular event offers a scenic and challenging course, perfect for runners of all abilities looking to conquer a 10K challenge. With stunning views of the city's iconic landmarks, the Gloucester 10K promises an unforgettable running experience.
