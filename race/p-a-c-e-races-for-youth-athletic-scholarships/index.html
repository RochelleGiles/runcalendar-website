Course

Both distances start and finish at Ed Spear Park in Smiths Grove, running out through the communities of Smiths Grove and Oakland. Over 90 percent of the route is paved and the remaining 10 percent gravel, with moderate hills and several railroad crossings.

About

Warren County Parks and Recreation hosts the P.A.C.E. Marathon and Half Marathon, PACE standing for Parks and Community Events. The race lets participants experience the communities of Smiths Grove and Oakland, a rural section of Warren County, Kentucky. Marathon and half marathon participants receive a shirt, and finishers receive a unique medal featuring the two communities.
