Course

Based at Stage 88 in Parkes beside Lake Burley Griffin, with the whole course on paved walking paths. Entrants choose a 2km walk, a 5km walk or jog, a 10km timed run or a 10km elite run for those going under 60 minutes. The site opens at 7:30am and starts run from about 8:40am.

About

The Memory Walk & Jog: Canberra is a community event that brings together people of all ages and abilities to walk or jog in support of Alzheimer's Australia. The event features a scenic course through the heart of Canberra, offering participants the chance to enjoy the city's beautiful surroundings while raising awareness and funds for a important cause.
