About

The Ćićarija Trail Run project aims to revive old paths, promote the unexplored parts of Ćićarija, and have a good time. The races will pass through parts of the famous 100 miles of Istria trail and climb some of the most famous peaks of Ćićarija - Žbevnica, Gomila, Orljak, Županj vrh. The project includes a total of 6 races, each set in a small Ćićarija village.
