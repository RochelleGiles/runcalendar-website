Course

A flat, fast USATF-certified 5K with many corners through the Woodmont area of Tamarac. It starts at 77th Street on Pine Island Road, runs east on 75th and north on 80th, then loops back to Pine Island Road and south to the finish at City Hall.

About

The Tamarac Turkey Trot 5K is run on Thanksgiving morning from Tamarac City Hall in Tamarac, Florida, and has been held annually for more than four decades. The course is USATF-certified and there is no race-day registration, with entries taken online in advance.
