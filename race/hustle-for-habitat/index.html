Course

Both events start near the Dunvilla Trap Range at Pelican Rapids, running a grassy trail through farmland, along a river and past pollinator fields, with a section on gravel road. The organisers describe the 5K as closer to a trail run than a road run. The 1 Mile Strut starts at 8:30am and the chip-timed 5K at 9:00am.

About

The Hustle For Habitat is an annual running event organized by the Pelican River chapter of Pheasants Forever to support local habitat conservation projects. It features the Rooster Run 5K and 1 Mile Strut races held at the Dunvilla Trap Range.
