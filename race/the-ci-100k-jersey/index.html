About

The CI 100k is a challenging 100km race in Jersey, offering runners a chance to experience the beautiful and rugged trails of the island. The course features stunning coastal scenery, rolling hills, and technical terrain that will test the endurance and skill of participants. The event is known for its inclusive and supportive atmosphere, making it a must-do for trail running enthusiasts.
