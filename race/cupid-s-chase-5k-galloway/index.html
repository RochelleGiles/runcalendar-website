About

Cupid's Chase 5K Galloway is an annual fundraising race series by Community Options, Inc., celebrating their founding and supporting housing and employment for individuals with disabilities. It welcomes runners, walkers, strollers, and wheelchairs in a festive Valentine's Day event.[1][4]
