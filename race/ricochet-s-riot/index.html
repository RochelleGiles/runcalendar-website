Course

A mixed-terrain trail course at Parrie Haynes Ranch taking in rolling hills, pastures, stream crossings and stretches beside the Lampasas River. The longer races add technical rocky and single-track sections; the 50K runs two loops and the 25K one.

About

A trail race held at Parrie Haynes Ranch in Killeen, TX featuring rolling hills, stream crossings, and scenic views along the Lampasas River. The event includes multiple distance options and concludes with a beer mile, live band, food trucks, and a beer trailer.
