About

The 5 Km Et 10 Km Des Lions is a local running event in Plauzat featuring multiple race distances. The event includes adult races of 5 km and 10 km, as well as children's races called 'Courses des Lionceaux'. Approximately 300 competitors participate, with races offering competitive categories and a festive community atmosphere.
