Course

A city-street loop through San Francisco Chinatown, starting at 8:00 AM on Grant Avenue at Sacramento Street and finishing on Kearny Street at Sacramento. Mostly flat with a few short hills, mile markers on the right. The 5K runs the loop once and the 10K twice; walkers are welcome in the 5K but not the 10K.

About

An annual run staged by the Chinatown YMCA through the streets of San Francisco Chinatown, offering a 5K run/walk and a 10K run. Both start on Grant Avenue at Sacramento Street and finish on Kearny Street, the 10K covering the loop twice.
