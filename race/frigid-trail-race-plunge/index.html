Course

Two trail distances, 5K and 10K, run on the trail network at the U.S. National Whitewater Center outside Charlotte. The trails stay open to other runners, hikers and bikers during the race. Pre-entry closes a few days beforehand, with day-of entry available at a higher price, and a polar plunge follows the running.
