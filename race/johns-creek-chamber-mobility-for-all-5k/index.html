Course

The single 5K runs the USATF-certified Cauley Creek Course at Cauley Creek Park in Johns Creek. The race starts at 8:00 AM with check-in from 6:30. Leashed dogs and strollers are welcome, with strollers starting behind the timed runners.

About

Join the Johns Creek Chamber of Commerce for the Mobility for All 5K, a community-focused run and walk dedicated to promoting movement, inclusion, and overall wellness for people of all abilities. Set against the scenic backdrop of Cauley Creek Park, this event brings together families, businesses, and community partners for a meaningful morning of connection and impact. With generous support from Boston Scientific and Agape Family, the Mobility for All 5K highlights the importance of access to mobility, health, and supportive resources that strengthen our community. Whether you're an experienced runner, a casual walker, or cheering from the sidelines, all are welcome to participate and support this inclusive cause.
