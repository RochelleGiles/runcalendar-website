Course

A community morning of chip-timed 10K and 5K run/walks plus a 2K fun run on the streets of Tustin, run alongside 15K and 5K bike tours. The 10K starts at 7:00am, the 5K at 8:00am and the 2K at 8:45am, with the bike tours setting off first.

About

The Dino Dash is the Tustin Public Schools Foundation's annual fundraising race, now in its 31st year. Runners choose from a 10K, 5K, or 2K fun run through Tustin, California, with proceeds supporting local public schools.
