Course

Loop courses starting and finishing at Wingfield Park in Ruidoso, at about 6,900 feet. The 13.8-mile and 8.5-mile routes run on mountain trail with steep climbs and technical descents; the 4-mile course is roughly half paved and half trail. Refill stations sit at the park and at miles 5 and 9; the 4-miler has none, so runners carry their own.

About

Ruidoso Grindstone Trail Runs loops around Grindstone Lake in the Sierra Blanca foothills near Ruidoso, New Mexico, on singletrack and forest road, with 4-mile, 8.5-mile and 13.8-mile options through Lincoln National Forest terrain.
