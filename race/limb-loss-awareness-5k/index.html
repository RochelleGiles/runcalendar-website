Course

A 5K starting and finishing at Veterans Memorial Park in Kentwood, run alongside a 1-mile Resilience Race and a children's 1-mile fun run. The event is deliberately family-friendly and open to all abilities, with the 1-mile turning at a marked point on the same course.

About

The Limb Loss Awareness 5K is hosted by the City of Kentwood Parks & Recreation to raise awareness about limb loss and limb difference. It celebrates mobility, resilience, and community support at Veterans Memorial Park, welcoming runners, supporters, and sponsors.
