Course

Starts and finishes in downtown Houston near City Hall, with the 10K running along Dallas Street by Sam Houston Park. The 10K course is certified by the USATF Road Running Technical Council; a 5K fun run and walk starts from the same area.

About

A non-profit event organized by the Houston Area Road Runners Association. 100% of the proceeds are donated to the Houston Parks Department and other charities that support our running community.
