About

The Dublin Mountain Backyard Ultra is a unique running challenge set against the stunning backdrop of Ireland's Dublin Mountains. Runners will test their endurance on a looped course, competing to see who can complete the most laps within a 24-hour period. This event promises an unforgettable adventure for seasoned ultrarunners seeking a true test of their limits.
