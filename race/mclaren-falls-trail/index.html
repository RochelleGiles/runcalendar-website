Course

Trail loops around McLaren Falls Park overlooking Lake McLaren, ten minutes from Tauranga, on formed tracks through native bush. All three distances share a start and finish and the series avoids steep hills, so the course suits walkers as well as runners.

About

Just a short ten minute drive from Tauranga, McLaren Falls Regional Park is covered with pristine forest and well formed trails overlooking the stunning Lake McLaren. Alush with native bush and glowworms, this is one of NZ's most popular and visited Regional Parks.
