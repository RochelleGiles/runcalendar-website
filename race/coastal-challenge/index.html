Course

Point-to-point coastal run along Auckland's North Shore. Distances start variously at Takapuna Beach, Milford Beach, Browns Bay, Long Bay or Arkles Bay, and all finish at Windsor Reserve in Devonport. Runners cover sand, rock hopping and water crossings between beaches.

About

The Coastal Challenge is an iconic adventure run along Auckland's extraordinary North Shore coastline. Runners, walkers, and adventurers of all ages and abilities can experience the stunning beaches, rock hopping, and getting wet on this one-of-a-kind event in a one-of-a-kind city.
