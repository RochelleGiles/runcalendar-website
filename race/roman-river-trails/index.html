Course

Marathon and half marathon starting and finishing at Bannatyne Gym & Spa, Kingsford Park, Layer De La Haye near Colchester. The marathon runs through the Roman River valley with several river crossings; the half is one loop of the marathon course, taking in the villages west of Colchester. Predominantly hard-packed trail with sections of tarmac and grass footpath, hilly through the valley. Aid stations roughly every 10km.

About

The Roman River Trails event takes runners through the Roman River valley near Colchester, featuring predominantly hard-packed trail terrain with sections of tarmac and grass footpaths. The course crosses the Roman River multiple times and includes some hilly sections.
