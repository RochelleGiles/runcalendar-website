Course

A 10K loop starting and finishing at Queen Elizabeth School in Crediton, roughly 90% off road across footpaths, mowed grassland, private farmland and quiet country lanes. The route runs field perimeters, farm lanes and riverside sections and takes in the climb known as Breakheart hill. Organisers advise trail shoes as the ground can be muddy and slippery.

About

Crediton Crunch is a 10K road race hosted by the Crediton Running Network. The course heads out from this historic Mid Devon market town into the surrounding countryside, on quiet lanes typical of the area.
