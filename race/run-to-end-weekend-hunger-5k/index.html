About

The Run to End Weekend Hunger 5K is a community event at Seward Park in Seattle benefiting Backpack Brigade's mission to end weekend hunger for homeless and food insecure kids in King County. Participants can run, jog, or walk, with a kids dash option, featuring fun contests and prizes.
