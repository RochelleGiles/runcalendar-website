Course

Flat loop courses starting and finishing at Cesar Chavez Park in Laveen Village. The 5K and 10K mix concrete path and dirt trail through the park and Montana Vista, with a couple of stretches on paved street and a loop around Alvord Lake. The 1-mile fun run stays on paved path around the lake. Water at the start, finish and a midpoint station.

About

The Laveen Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving morning 5K and 10K run and walk based at Cesar Chavez Park in Laveen Village, Arizona. A shorter 1 Mile option makes it an easy outing for the whole family before the holiday meal.
