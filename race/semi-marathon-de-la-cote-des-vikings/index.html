About

The Semi Marathon De La Cote Des Vikings is a scenic running event along the picturesque Normandy coast. Runners will enjoy stunning views of the English Channel and the rugged cliffs of the Cotentin Peninsula. The course is challenging yet rewarding, with a mix of flat sections and gentle hills. This event offers a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the Normandy region while pushing your limits as a runner.
