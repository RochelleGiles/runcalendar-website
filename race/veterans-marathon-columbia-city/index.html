Course

Both races start at West Van Buren St and North Chauncey St in downtown Columbia City. The marathon is a two-loop Boston qualifier run on the half-marathon course; marathoners must finish the first loop by 11.15am or be moved to the half, because the roads reopen under permit.

About

Veterans Marathon is a marathon and half marathon held in Columbia City, Indiana, benefiting causes that support military veterans. The race also includes a companion 5K and draws runners from across northeast Indiana.
