Course

Run on the Gainesville-Hawthorne Trail, a paved rail trail. Both distances start and finish at First Magnitude Brewing in Gainesville, the half marathon at 7:30am and the 5k at 8:00am. The half marathon carries a 3.5 hour time limit.
