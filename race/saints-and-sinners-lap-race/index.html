Course

A timed lap race run on a 5km loop starting and finishing in the school courtyard at St Columba's College, Springwood. Each lap mixes winding single track, fire trail and road with about 110m of climbing. Runners take on as many laps as they can in three or six hours, solo, in pairs or in teams. Two aid stations sit on the loop alongside support at the race hub.

About

Saints and Sinners is a timed lap race in Springwood, in the NSW Blue Mountains. Held in 3-hour and 6-hour formats, entrants complete as many laps of the course as possible within the time limit, part of the Running Wild NSW trail series.
