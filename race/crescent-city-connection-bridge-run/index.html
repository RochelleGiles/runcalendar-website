Course

A single 6K run/walk that closes the Crescent City Connection to traffic. The course starts on the Westbank at Gretna, climbs the twin span across the Mississippi River and finishes downtown on the New Orleans side. The one sustained climb is the bridge itself, with skyline views from the top of the span.
