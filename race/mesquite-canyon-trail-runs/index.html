Course

Lollipop courses at White Tank Mountain Regional Park, starting and finishing at the Competitive Track parking area. Runners cover desert single track and dirt roads over rock, sand and gravel; the 50M and 50K add a technical Ford Canyon section with rock scrambling. Aid stations sit every 1.8 to 8.8 miles.

About

A trail running event featuring multiple distances from 8K to 50 miles on rugged, single-track trails within White Tank Mountain Regional Park. The 50 Mile and 50K courses include challenging terrain with rock scrambling through Ford Canyon and iconic White Tank formations.
