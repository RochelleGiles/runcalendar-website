About

A family-friendly 5K run and 2-mile walk through the holiday lights at Olin Park, following the shore of Lake Monona beneath the Madison skyline. Both events start at 5:00pm in step with the Holiday Fantasy in Lights display, and finish with food, drinks and music at the pavilion.
