Course

A high-altitude trail race at about 7,400 feet in Government Prairie, within the Kaibab National Forest near Parks. The 30K follows part of the historic Beale Wagon Road and carries a 4.5-hour cutoff. The 30K and early-start 18K/10K set off at 7:00am; the competitive 10K and 18K start at 8:00am. Snow is possible on the upper reaches.

About

The Soulstice Mountain Trail Run is organized by volunteers, including the unpaid race director, from Northern Arizona Trail Runners Association (NATRA LLC). As a result, 100% of the race proceeds benefit Big Brothers Big Sisters of Flagstaff youth mentoring programs. The race takes place in the stunningly beautiful Government Prairie grassland just west of Flagstaff.
