Course

A backyard ultra run on the Wolwefontein farm at Living Colour outside Darling, with the start and finish at the venue's Smoke House. Runners repeat a single loop on the farm's running trails until only one is left able to complete it within the hour. The circuit crosses open farmland with flowing lines and long views.

About

Living Colour Backyard Ultra is a last-runner-standing backyard ultra race hosted at the Living Colour venue in Darling, on the Western Cape's West Coast. Competitors repeat a fixed loop every hour until only one remains, part of the WannaDo Events backyard ultra series.
