About

The Memory Walk & Jog: Gold Coast is a community event that brings together people of all ages and abilities to raise awareness and funds for Alzheimer's Australia. The scenic course along the Gold Coast waterfront offers stunning views and a great atmosphere for participants to enjoy a leisurely walk or jog.
