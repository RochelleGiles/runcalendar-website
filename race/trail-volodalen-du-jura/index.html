About

The Trail Volodalen Du Jura (TVJ) is an annual running event held in the heart of the Jura region, along the shores of the Vouglans lake. Participants can choose from 5 different race distances, ranging from 10km to 60km, allowing runners of all levels to challenge themselves on the scenic Jura trails. The event offers a unique atmosphere and the opportunity to explore the beautiful natural surroundings of the Jura mountains.
