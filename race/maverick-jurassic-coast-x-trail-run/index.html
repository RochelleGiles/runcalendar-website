Course

The 100km starts at the Hardy Monument near Dorchester, follows the South Dorset Ridgeway to Osmington and joins the South West Coast Path past Durdle Door and Lulworth Cove before merging with the 52km route at Corfe. The 52km starts near Corfe Castle, crosses marshland to Old Harry Rocks and St Aldhelm's Head, and finishes at Burnbake Campsite.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Jurassic Coast on the Maverick Jurassic Coast X-Trail Run. This invigorating adventure takes runners around the Isle of Purbeck and along part of the famous UNESCO World Heritage site, offering stunning coastal views and challenging terrain. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience in the heart of the United Kingdom's breathtaking countryside.
