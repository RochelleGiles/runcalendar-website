Course

Three trail courses on the 250-acre grounds of Gator Wilderness Camp School, with the 5K, 10K and 15K each starting from a different point at 8:00am. The route runs on wooded trail under canopy through pine woods and shaded hammocks. Aid stations are placed along all routes. No pets or strollers.

About

Run through the diverse woods and shaded hammocks of Gator Wilderness Camp School grounds, situated on 250 pristine acres. Come to enjoy the beautiful scenery while testing your skill and stamina in a 5k,10k or 15k race. All race entry proceeds will benefit Gator Wilderness Camp School.
