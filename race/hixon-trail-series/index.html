About

The Hixon Trail Series, organized by the River City Running Club, offers a variety of trail events set against the scenic backdrop of Hixon Forest in La Crosse, Wisconsin. Our mission is to provide runners of all levels with opportunities to challenge themselves, connect with nature, and become part of a vibrant trail-running community.
