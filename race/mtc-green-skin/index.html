Course

A single-day point-to-point ultra of about 36 miles from the Talbot Hotel in Kidderminster to the Tudor House Hotel in Tewkesbury, following the organiser's JOGLE route at a reduced daily distance. Four checkpoints sit at roughly 9.7, 17.6, 25.9 and 35.5 miles. Runners must hold a minimum average of 4mph or be withdrawn.

About

MTC Green Skin is a 36-mile single-day ultra from Kidderminster to Tewkesbury, following the same route as Ultra Running Ltd's JOGLE event over a shorter daily distance. Four checkpoints break up the course, and runners must hold an average of at least 4mph to stay in the race.
