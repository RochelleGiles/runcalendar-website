Course

A point-to-point half marathon starting in Snow Canyon State Park and dropping through the park's red rock and lava landscape on a USATF-certified sealed road course with steep downhill sections. The 10K joins the half course inside the park; the 5K follows the paved Lava Flow Trail around Snow Canyon High School before joining the final mile. Water, Gatorade and gels on course.

About

The Snow Canyon Half Marathon runs through and around Snow Canyon State Park near St. George, Utah, known for its red and white sandstone cliffs and petrified sand dunes. Half marathon, 10K and 5K distances are offered.
