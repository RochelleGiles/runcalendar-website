Course

Crosses rarely accessed private high country on Flock Hill and Castle Hill Stations plus adjoining DOC land. The 30 km Full Burn starts on Flock Hill over limestone ridges and a bouldering field, descends Dry Gully into Broken River, works upstream then climbs through Castle Hill Station past Kura Tawhiti Reserve, finishing with a 380 m climb on Dixons Track in Craigieburn Forest Park. Also 20 km, 10 km and 5 km.
