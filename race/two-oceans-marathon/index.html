Course

A 56 km ultra around the Cape Peninsula from Newlands: south along the False Bay coast through Muizenberg and Fish Hoek, over Chapman's Peak Drive above the Atlantic, through Hout Bay, then the long climb of Constantia Nek before the run down to finish at the University of Cape Town. Two big climbs in the second half decide the race.

About

The Two Oceans Marathon is a 56km road ultramarathon around the Cape Peninsula, often billed as the most beautiful marathon in the world for its sweeping views from Chapmans Peak Drive and Constantia Nek. The April race weekend also hosts one of the largest half marathons in South Africa.
