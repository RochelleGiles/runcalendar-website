Course

A coastal road course starting on Marine Drive, Wollongong, circling the city's landmark lighthouse and harbour with open ocean views for much of the way, and finishing in Lang Park. The programme runs from a 2km Turbo through the Fitness 5km and Kerryn McCann 10km to the half marathon and marathon.

About

Run Wollongong offers runners a chance to experience the picturesque coastal city of Wollongong, Australia. This popular annual event features a full marathon, half marathon, and shorter race distances to challenge runners of all levels. With scenic routes and a lively atmosphere, Run Wollongong is a must-do event for any runner looking to explore a new destination.
