Course

The 5K starts at Richardson Lane and South Bay Avenue, runs south on South Bay Avenue to the beach, loops a grass island in the car park, then heads back north on South Bay Avenue, turning left onto Maple Avenue and right onto Monell Avenue to finish at the Islip Fire Department. Check-in opens at 7:30am for a 9:00am start.

About

The Islip Fire Department Turkey Trot 5K is an annual event that raises funds for the Islip Fire Department. The race takes place on the streets of Islip, New York, and is open to runners of all skill levels.
