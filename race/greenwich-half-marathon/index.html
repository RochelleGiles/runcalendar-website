Course

A flat, fast two-loop half marathon at Greenwich Point Park in Old Greenwich, running along the shoreline with coastal views for much of the route. A three-hour limit applies, about 13:44 per mile.

About

This capacity-limited half marathon starts and finishes at Greenwich Point Park on Long Island Sound, with entries deliberately capped to keep the race small. Proceeds support the Kids in Crisis charity, and finishers have a three-hour time limit to complete the flat, two-loop course.
