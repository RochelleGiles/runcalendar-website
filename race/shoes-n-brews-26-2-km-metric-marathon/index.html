Course

A point-to-point 26.2km run along the Schuylkill River Trail, starting at Conshohocken Brewing Company on East Elm Street and finishing under the Gay Street Bridge in Phoenixville. Water stations sit at miles 4, 6, 9, 11.5 and 14, with gels also at mile 9. A two-person relay splits the distance at mile 9 near Betzwood, and the field is capped at 200 runners.

About

Shoes 'N Brews 26.2KM Metric Marathon & Relay is a collaboration between The RUNegades (Shoes) and Conshohocken Brewing Company (Brews). The race and its relay option connect Conshohocken to an afterparty on Bridge Street in Phoenixville.
