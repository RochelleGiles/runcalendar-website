Course

About

The Vernal Equinox is a timed ultra at Majestic Farm, an equestrian property at Batavia, Ohio. Runners complete as many two-mile laps as they can within their chosen limit, on a loop of meadow, woodland and gravel road, with a heated barn as the start and finish of each lap.
