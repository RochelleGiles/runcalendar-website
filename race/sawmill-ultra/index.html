Course

A three-loop trail course in the Humboldt-Toiyabe National Forest at Lee Canyon, Mt Charleston, around 7,500 feet. The loops are 5.64, 8.2 and 4.3 miles, all run counterclockwise, and solo entrants repeat loops to reach their chosen distance. The event is cup-less: aid stations supply Gatorade and electrolyte gels and runners carry their own hydration.
