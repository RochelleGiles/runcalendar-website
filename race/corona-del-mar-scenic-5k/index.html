Course

About

The Corona del Mar Scenic 5K runs along the ocean bluffs of Corona del Mar in Newport Beach, California, taking in sweeping Pacific views before finishing near the village. The morning also includes a 2-mile fun walk, a Dolphin Dash and a timed youth race.
