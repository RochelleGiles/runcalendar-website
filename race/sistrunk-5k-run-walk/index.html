About

The Sistrunk 5K is an exciting race near downtown Fort Lauderdale on the Historic Sistrunk Boulevard. Come run or walk through history and have a great time. This race travels down the Historic Sistrunk Boulevard in the Dorsey Riverbend Neighborhood close to downtown Fort Lauderdale and Fat Village.
