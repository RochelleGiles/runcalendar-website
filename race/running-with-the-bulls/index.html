Course

Starts and finishes at Mountain View High School on East Brown Road in Mesa. The 5K runs on sealed surfaces through the surrounding neighbourhood and is accessible to wheelchairs, strollers and wagons. A half-mile Tiny Toro kids dash runs alongside it.

About

The 12th annual Running with the Bulls 5K is organized by the Student Council from Mountain View High School in Mesa, AZ. This community event features a 5K run/walk and a Tiny Toro Kid's Dash, with all proceeds benefiting non-profit charity programs, athletics, and academics at the school.
