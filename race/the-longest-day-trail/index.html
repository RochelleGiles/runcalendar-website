About

The Longest Day Trail is an exhilarating ultramarathon that takes runners on a scenic journey through the region's stunning natural landscapes. Challenging yet rewarding, this event tests the limits of endurance as participants traverse diverse terrains, from rugged mountain trails to serene forest paths. Runners of all skill levels are invited to experience the thrill of pushing their boundaries and immersing themselves in the beauty of the great outdoors.
