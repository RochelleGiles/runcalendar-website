About

Join us in Farmingdale for a charity 5K in which all proceeds will go toward the St. Baldrick's Foundation. Invite your friends and family to participate or cheer you on. Raffle baskets featuring local businesses will be available to help raise money for the charity. Your participation can save lives!!
