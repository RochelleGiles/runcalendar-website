Course

A late-afternoon trail run in the Herberton area, starting at 3:15pm with most runners home by 6:00pm. The route is at times technical single trail with significant elevation gain, over rougher ground than the regularly trodden trails nearer town. Water is available at the start/finish only, and runners are asked to carry their own bottles or packs.
