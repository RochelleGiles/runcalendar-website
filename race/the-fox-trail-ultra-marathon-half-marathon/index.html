Course

A point-to-point trail run through the Surrey countryside, with all three distances finishing together in Godalming. The 62K starts in Godalming at 08:00, the 41K in West Clandon at 09:30 and the 20K at Merrist Wood College, Worplesdon at 11:00; the 62K can also be run as a three-person relay. Routes are marked with trail flags, tags and arrows with marshals at the main junctions, and GPX files are supplied for self-navigation.

About

Explore the scenic Fox Way in Surrey during the The Fox Trail Ultra, Marathon & Half Marathon. This challenging event offers runners the choice of three distances, each winding through the picturesque countryside of the United Kingdom. Whether you're tackling the ultra, the classic marathon, or the half marathon, this is a must-do for trail enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable running adventure.
