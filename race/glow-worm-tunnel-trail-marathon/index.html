About

The Glow Worm Tunnel Trail Marathon is a weekend trail-running event based at Glen Davis Campground in the NSW Blue Mountains, with distances from 6K to a full marathon. The event relocated here after a landslip closed the usual Wolgan Road access to Newnes.
