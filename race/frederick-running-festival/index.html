Course

Starts and finishes at the Great Frederick Fairgrounds, looping out through historic downtown Frederick along Market Street and Baker Park and passing Hood College and the minor league ballpark. Seven aid stations carry water and Gatorade. A team relay, Twilight 5K and kids fun run join the half marathon.

About

The half marathon starts and finishes at the Frederick Fairgrounds, looping past Baker Park, Frederick Memorial Hospital and residential neighborhoods on gently rolling city streets. Field size is capped at 5,000 finishers, with a Saturday twilight 5K and kids' fun run rounding out the festival weekend.
