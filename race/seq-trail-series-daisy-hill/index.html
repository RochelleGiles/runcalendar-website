Course

Round 8 of the SEQ Trail Running Series, held on the bushland trails at Underwood Park, Priestdale, beside Daisy Hill Conservation Park. Two options are offered: a 12.6 km short course and an 18.5 km long course, with wave starts from 6:30am.

About

Explore the lush forests and rolling hills of Daisy Hill in the latest round of the South East Queensland Trail Running Series. This scenic course promises a challenging yet rewarding experience for trail runners of all abilities, with stunning natural landscapes to discover along the way. Don't miss your chance to be part of this thrilling event in the heart of the region.
