Course

A single 13.1-mile loop that is entirely dirt, gravel and singletrack, through the Highlands Ranch Backcountry Wilderness Area and the Douglas County East/West Trail. Start and finish are on Monarch Blvd in Lone Tree, with four aid stations and hilly, mountain-view terrain.
