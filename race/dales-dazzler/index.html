Course

A big loop out of and back to Skipton on established Dales trails. The route heads west to pick up the Pennine Way near Pinhaw Beacon and Thornton-in-Craven, with the longer distances taking in Malham Tarn and Arncliffe before joining the Dales Way at Starbotton. It then follows the Dales Way through Kettlewell, Grassington and Burnsall to Barden Bridge and returns to Skipton via Embsay.

About

The Punk Panther Dales Dazzler offers four off-road trail races through the picturesque Yorkshire Dales, with the start and finish in the charming market town of Skipton. Runners can immerse themselves in the stunning natural landscapes of the region as they tackle a range of scenic routes, making this an unmissable event for trail enthusiasts.
