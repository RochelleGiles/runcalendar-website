Course

A single loop of Lake Rotorua on a World Athletics certified sealed course, starting on Queens Drive in the Government Gardens and finishing at the Novotel Rotorua Lakeside. The route passes lakeside settlements, rolling countryside and geothermal ground. Twelve drink stations are on course, with personalised bottle stations at 9.3, 19.5, 27.8 and 37.0 km.

About

The Red Stag Rotorua Marathon is one of New Zealand's most scenic and iconic marathon events. Set against the stunning backdrop of Rotorua's geothermal landscape, this challenging course takes runners on a journey through lush forests and past steaming hot springs. With its unique natural beauty and welcoming community, the Rotorua Marathon is a must-do for endurance athletes seeking an unforgettable running experience.
