Course

A simple out-and-back on the Great Victorian Rail Trail, a converted railway line that is smooth, wide and largely flat, with only a few welcome undulations and a handful of road crossings. Distances run from a 2km primary school challenge through 5km, 10km and the half up to the full marathon.

About

The Mansfield Marathon follows the Great Victorian Rail Trail through Victoria's high country, with 5km, 10km, half marathon and full marathon distances on a gentle, largely flat trail, plus a short primary schools challenge.
