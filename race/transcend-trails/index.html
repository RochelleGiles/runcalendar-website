Course

Starts at Walyunga National Park and explores the Avon Valley north-east of Perth. Options run from a kids dash and 6km hike or run, including a 6km adaptive wheelchair event, up through 12km and 28km to 40km and 65km solo or team relay.

About

Explore the stunning Avon Valley in Western Australia at the Transcend Trails, featuring the iconic 65km Transcend Ultra. This challenging yet rewarding trail running event takes participants through the heart of the picturesque region, offering a truly immersive and unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
