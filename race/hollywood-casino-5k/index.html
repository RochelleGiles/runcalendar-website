About

Try your luck at the first-ever Hollywood Casino 5k in Rossford, Ohio on Friday, June 12th. Afterwards, hang out for the parking lot party featuring live music, food and beverage. You can also head into the casino to redeem a free play entry, courtesy of the Hollywood Casino. We also have the swag! Every participant gets an event shirt and a custom medal! Whether you're chasing a PR or just out for the fun of it, this race is all about good vibes, great food, and making a day of it at the Hollywood Casino Toledo.
