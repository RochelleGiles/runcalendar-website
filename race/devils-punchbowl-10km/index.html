Course

A guided evening trail 10km around the natural amphitheatre of the Devil's Punchbowl at Hindhead in Surrey, on good trails with plenty of hills and pauses at the high points for the views. The steeper climbs are usually hiked. Run as a sociable group rather than a race, at around 10 min/mile on trails.

About

The Devil's Punchbowl 10K takes place around this dramatic natural amphitheatre near Hindhead in Surrey, one of the largest in England and now a National Trust nature reserve.
