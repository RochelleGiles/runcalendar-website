About

Belle Ile en Trail is a running event held on the beautiful island of Belle-Île-en-Mer, the largest of the Ponant islands in Brittany, France. The event offers a unique opportunity to explore the island's stunning coastal trails and landscapes, providing a truly memorable experience for runners. The course highlights include breathtaking ocean views, rugged cliffs, and charming seaside villages.
