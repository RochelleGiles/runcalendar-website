Course

City courses starting and finishing at 731 Front Ave NW beside the 6th Street Bridge, crossing Grand River bridges through downtown Grand Rapids and its parks; the 10-mile heads out through Riverside Park. Both the 5K and 10-mile start at 8:00am on Sunday, with pacers from 7 to 12-minute miles in the 10-mile.

About

A premier running event in Grand Rapids that has been providing the community with a starting line to accomplish goals since 2002. Participants choose between a 10 Mile Run or 5K Run/Walk, running through downtown Grand Rapids and crossing multiple bridges in the city.
