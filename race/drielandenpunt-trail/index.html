Course

A two-day winter trail event at the Drielandenpunt, where the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany meet, with routes crossing all three countries. The longer distances run on the Saturday and the rest on the Sunday, and two Challenge options pair a Saturday leg with a Sunday one. The organiser bills it as the trail with the most climbing in the Netherlands, through land managed by Staatsbosbeheer.
