About

Explore the captivating Waitomo region of New Zealand on the Waitomo Trail Run, an exhilarating adventure that takes runners and walkers on an unforgettable journey through both underground and aboveground landscapes. Immerse yourself in the stunning natural beauty of this unique destination as you navigate a challenging course that showcases the area's remarkable caves, rolling hills, and lush forests.
