About

The 10 Km D Hirson is a popular running event held in the town of Hirson, France. The course takes runners through the scenic streets and surrounding countryside, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants. The event is known for its lively atmosphere and enthusiastic local support, making it a must-do for runners in the region.
