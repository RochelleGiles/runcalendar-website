Course

A two-lap 10K night trail run at Salcey Forest near Northampton, starting in the evening. The course is fundamentally flat and fully marked, mostly on crushed stone paths that can turn slippery and muddy in wet weather. A head or chest torch is compulsory.

About

Explore the enchanting Salcey Forest under the cloak of night at the Petzl Night Runner 10K. This thrilling trail run challenges runners to navigate the winding paths and terrain of the forest in the dark, offering a unique and exhilarating experience. Whether you're seeking an adrenaline-fueled adventure or simply a chance to connect with nature, this event promises an unforgettable journey through the serene beauty of the United Kingdom's wilderness.
