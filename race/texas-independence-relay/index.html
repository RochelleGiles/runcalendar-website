About

Experience your own epic victory at the Texas Independence Relay! Your 200 mile journey (give or take a few miles) traces the route of Sam Houston and the Texian Army back in 1835-1836. Start at Gonzales, Texas (home of the Come and Take It skirmish) and finish at the Water Works in Buffalo Bayou Park (which provides the very best close-up Houston Skyline view)!
