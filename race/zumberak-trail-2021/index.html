About

The Salomon Žumberak Trail is an international trail running event held in the Žumberak-Samoborsko gorje Nature Park, just 30 km southwest of Zagreb. Runners have the opportunity to earn ITRA points in trail running and qualify for the prestigious Ultra du Mont Blanc (UTMB) race.
