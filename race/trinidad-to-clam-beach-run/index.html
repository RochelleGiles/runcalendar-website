About

The Trinidad to Clam Beach Run is a 5.75-mile coastal race starting in Trinidad, CA, on an undulating road through redwoods, crossing the Little River at Moonstone Beach, and finishing on the sand at Clam Beach State Park. Participants enjoy a post-race beach party with chili, music, and a bonfire, suitable for all levels and ages.
