About

Explore the majestic Idre Fjäll region of Sweden at the Salming Idre Fjällmaraton. This challenging trail running event takes participants through the stunning mountain landscapes, offering breathtaking views and a true test of endurance. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or looking for a new adventure, the Salming Idre Fjällmaraton promises an unforgettable running experience.
