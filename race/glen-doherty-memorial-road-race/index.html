Course

Road race starting in front of Winchester Town Hall on Mount Vernon Street, Winchester, Massachusetts, at noon. The 5K is a single loop through the town and the 10K covers the same loop twice. Runners, walkers and ruckers are all welcome.

About

The Glen Doherty Memorial Road Race is held each September in Winchester, Massachusetts, honoring former Navy SEAL and CIA contractor Glen Doherty, a Winchester native killed in the Benghazi attack. Runners can choose the 10K or 5K distance.
