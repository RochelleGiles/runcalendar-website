About

The Kinmen Marathon is a prestigious running event held in the scenic Kinmen Islands of Taiwan. The course showcases the island's natural beauty, with runners passing by historic landmarks and stunning coastal views. This event attracts both competitive athletes and recreational runners, creating a vibrant and inclusive atmosphere.
