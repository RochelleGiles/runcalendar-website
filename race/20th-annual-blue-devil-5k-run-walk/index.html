Course

Run on the main campus of Kansas City Kansas Community College, starting from the car park at the Health Professions Building. The route loops through the campus grounds and past Lot H, which sits in the middle of the course.

About

Join us in the parking lot of the Health Professions Building as we walk/run on KCKCC's beautiful main campus! Participants are encouraged to run or walk to their ability level. Awards are given to the top overall male and female finishers, and the winner of each age group receives an award.
