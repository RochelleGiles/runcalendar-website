About

The Shoreham Wading River Community Programs Turkey Trot 5K Run/Walk is an annual event held in honor of Paul "Coach K" Koretzki. The race takes place on Thanksgiving Day and features a 5K run/walk as well as a 1 mile run for both youth and adults.
