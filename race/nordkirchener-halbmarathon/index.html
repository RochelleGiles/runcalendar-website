About

The Nordkirchener Halbmarathon takes place in the picturesque area around Nordkirchen Castle, offering runners a scenic route through the beautiful Schlosspark and the Obsen farmland. Participants will enjoy two loops through the landscape, with enthusiastic local support and a festive atmosphere along the course.
