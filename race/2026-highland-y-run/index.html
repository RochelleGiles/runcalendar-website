About

The Highland Y Run is more than just a race—it's a tradition, a celebration, and a lifeline for families in our community. For 43 years, the city of Highland has welcomed runners and race fans of all ages and abilities to this cherished event. Together, we've built something special: a race that not only inspires health and togetherness but also directly strengthens the community we call home.
