About

The Bangsaen42 Chonburi Marathon is a premier running event in Thailand that takes runners on a scenic course along the beautiful Bangsaen Beach. The event offers a challenging marathon distance as well as shorter races, catering to runners of all levels. Participants can expect a vibrant atmosphere and stunning coastal views throughout the race.
