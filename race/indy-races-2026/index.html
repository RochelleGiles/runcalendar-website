About

Indy Races' mission is to support families affected by pediatric illnesses by raising funds for organizations that operate primarily on donations and volunteers. Through the Indy Races, we aim to provide ongoing assistance to these organizations, enabling them to continue helping families in need. We are committed to promoting a healthy lifestyle and fostering a supportive community. We enjoy seeing people actively engage in outdoor activities and connect. It is truly inspiring to witness individuals unite for a common purpose, prioritizing their health while enjoying nature and participating in wellness-focused activities.
