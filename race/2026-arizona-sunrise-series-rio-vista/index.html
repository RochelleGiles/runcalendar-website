Course

Early-morning 5K on the sealed New River Trail System in Peoria, starting and finishing at Rio Vista Park. All asphalt and concrete, flat, on shared-use paths that stay open to the public during the race. Chip timed, capped at 800 entries, with a flexible early start from 5:30am.

About

Arizona runners don't stop running as the temperatures rise - we just get started a little earlier! Join us as we continue the 13th Annual Arizona Sunrise Series with our third leg at Rio Vista Park!
