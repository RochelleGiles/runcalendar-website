Course

A 6.1-mile climb with about 4,100 ft of vertical gain, starting under the Aerial Tram at the base of Jackson Hole Mountain Resort and finishing at the tram summit on Rendezvous Mountain. Runners take the Wildflower Trail to Rendezvous Lodge then the Cirque Trail to the top. The course is almost entirely singletrack.

About

The Rendezvous Mountain Hill Climb sends runners straight up the ski slopes of Jackson Hole Mountain Resort, from the valley floor at Teton Village to the summit of Rendezvous Mountain. Roughly 6 miles with relentless vertical gain, it's one of the steepest hill climbs in the US.
