Course

Out and back from Currumbin Valley into the Border Trails, climbing away from the coast to ocean views. The 6.6km is entirely on pavement; the 13.5km, 22km and 31km add progressively more trail, with the 31km taking in the steep Strawberry Hill climb.

About

The Wild Earth Border Bolt is set on bumpy but beautiful border trails in Currumbin Valley. The event offers trail running experiences with increasing difficulty across different distances.
