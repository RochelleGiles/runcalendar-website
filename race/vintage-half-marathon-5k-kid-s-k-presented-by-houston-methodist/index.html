Course

The race is based at Klein High School, 16715 Stuebner Airline Road, Klein, north-west of Houston, where all distances start and finish. A half marathon runs alongside a timed 5K, an untimed 5K and a kids 1K. The organiser publishes no route map, surface or aid station detail.

About

The Vintage Half Marathon, 5K & Kid's K is an annual running event in Klein, Texas, presented by Houston Methodist. The event features multiple race distances and is part of the Run Houston Race Series, with finisher medals and Voo-doo doughnuts at the finish line.
