Course

Held at Eastern Market Brewing Co in Detroit, the format is four quarter-mile laps with a hot dog eaten before each one. Waves start every 45 minutes between 10 am and 2 pm, and a relay option lets teams split the dogs and the laps.

About

The Hot Dog Mile is an annual event celebrating hot dogs and regular dogs, dreamed up by the runners of Eastern Market Brewing Run Club. Participants eat four dogs and run four 1/4 mile laps- it's as simple as that! All proceeds after race costs are donated to a local Detroit Dog Rescue!
