About

The Jeux des Masters de Vichy is a must-attend running event for seasoned athletes. Held in the picturesque town of Vichy, this prestigious race offers a challenging course through the scenic French countryside. Runners can look forward to a truly unforgettable experience as they compete against some of the best masters-level competitors from around the world.
