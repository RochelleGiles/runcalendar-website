Course

A two-day trail weekend at Falcon Ridge Ski Hill in Whiteshell Provincial Park, with every race starting and finishing at the chalet. Saturday runs the Classic Up and Down 8 km and 16 km; Sunday the Loop the Lake Marathon and the 28 km Better Half circle Falcon Lake. Courses mix singletrack, Canadian Shield slab, rock and root, winter trails and ski-hill sections. The marathon has aid at 14 km, 24 km and 35 km.

About

A trail race weekend in Whiteshell Provincial Park, Manitoba, with courses looping the shoreline of Falcon Lake. Distances range from 8K up to the 42.2K Loop the Lake Marathon through the park's forest trails.
