Course

A single undulating lap on fully closed roads, starting and finishing in Welshpool town centre on Broad Street. The route runs out along Berriew Road and Red Lane into the Powis Castle estate before returning to the High Street. There is a 90-minute cut-off.

About

The Welshpool 10K is a road race staged on the grounds of Powis Castle, a National Trust property in Powys, mid-Wales. The route takes in the historic parkland surrounding the castle before finishing back in the market town of Welshpool.
