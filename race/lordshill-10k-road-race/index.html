Course

A flat out-and-back 10K on road, starting and finishing at Oasis Academy Lord's Hill in Southampton, where race HQ handles number collection, bag drop and refreshments. The route gains only about 48m over the 10km, under 0.5% in total. Water stations are provided on route and at the finish.

About

The Lordshill 10K Road Race takes runners on a scenic tour through the quiet countryside lanes surrounding Southampton. This popular community event offers a challenging yet rewarding course, showcasing the natural beauty of the local area. Runners of all levels are invited to join in the celebration of running at this classic British road race.
