Course

A lapped trail race starting and finishing at Kielder Waterside on a 25km loop taking in the Lonesome Pine trail and the shores of Kielder Water. The 28K covers one loop and the 50K two, with the final laps of Bull Crag passing back through the event village before a finish at the lake's edge. On-course support and nutrition are provided, and cut-offs apply at 20km, 30km and the finish.

About

The Northumbrian Trail Runs is a premier trail running event set in the stunning landscapes of Northumberland, England. Runners will experience breathtaking views, challenging terrain, and a welcoming atmosphere that makes this event a must-do for trail enthusiasts.
