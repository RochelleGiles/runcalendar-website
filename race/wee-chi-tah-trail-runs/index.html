About

Wee-Chi-Tah Trail Runs sends runners onto the singletrack of Wichita Falls'' Wee-Chi-Tah Trail system, with 10K and 12-mile distances through the natural-surface trail network built for the city''s Hotter''N Hell trail and mountain-bike events.
