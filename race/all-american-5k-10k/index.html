About

The All American 5K/10K, now past its 15th running, is held annually on Armed Forces Day near the Frederick Brown Amphitheater in Peachtree City. It offers a 5K run/walk, a 10K run, and a virtual Phantom entry, presented by American Legion Post 50 and the Peachtree City Running Club. Proceeds support Post 50's Veteran and Youth programs serving Peachtree City, Tyrone and Senoia.
