Course

A point-to-point course starting at Shasta Dam in Shasta Lake City and finishing on the glass Sundial Bridge at Turtle Bay in Redding. Runners drop away from the dam, pass through Old Shasta around miles 10 to 18, then follow the paved Sacramento River Trail to the finish, crossing the historic Diestlhorst Bridge. Marathon, three-person relay and 5K are offered.
