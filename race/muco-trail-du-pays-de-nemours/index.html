About

A charity trail weekend in early March at Bagneaux-sur-Loing, south of Fontainebleau, organised by the Pays de Nemours running club in aid of cystic fibrosis research. Two routes: the 11 km des Coquelicots and the 22 km des Verriers, both through the woods and glass-making villages of the Loing valley.
