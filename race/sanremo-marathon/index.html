Course

Point-to-point along the Ligurian coast, run almost entirely on the Riviera dei Fiori coastal cycle-pedestrian path rather than open road. The marathon starts at the Sanremo Casino and runs out through Arma di Taggia, Riva Ligure, Santo Stefano al Mare and Ospedaletti before returning to finish in Sanremo. Flat and sealed throughout.

About

The Sanremo Marathon is a stunning coastal running event that takes runners along the picturesque Italian Riviera. Explore the historic city of Sanremo and its breathtaking Mediterranean scenery as you challenge yourself on this renowned marathon course. Whether you're a seasoned runner or looking for a new adventure, the Sanremo Marathon offers an unforgettable experience.
