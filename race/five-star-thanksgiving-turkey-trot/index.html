Course

A 5K loop starting and finishing at 3890 Johns Creek Parkway, Suwanee, with race-day check-in in the lot behind Northside Hospital. The organisers call the loop fairly flat with some of the roll typical of north Georgia; 10K, 15K and half marathon entrants repeat it. Every distance also has a virtual option.
