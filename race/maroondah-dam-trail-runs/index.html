About

Explore the breathtaking Yarra Ranges just an hour east of Melbourne at the Maroondah Dam Trail Runs. This scenic event is the first race in the prestigious Mountain Trail Series, offering runners a challenging yet rewarding experience through the region's lush forests and rolling hills. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable day on the trails.
