Course

A USATF-certified 3K starting at the St. Augustine Alligator Farm on Anastasia Island, running out through the Davis Shores neighbourhood and back to the Alligator Farm to finish. Water is available at the start, the one-mile mark and the finish. Strollers and costumes are welcome.

About

Run or walk to help save native wildlife species in Florida! All proceeds from this all-ages race will benefit a conservation organization to aid in the conservation and research of native wildlife. All participants will receive a Raptor Run 3K t-shirt, a finisher's medal, post-race refreshments, and a free child's admission ticket to the Alligator Farm for every adult ticket purchase.
