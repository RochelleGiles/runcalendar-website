Course

Both races start and finish at Pop's Knoll in Donald County Park. The loop runs on mostly double-wide trail with some singletrack and several climbs; the 5K gains about 300 feet and the 15K about 1,050 feet. The course is marked with arrows and pin flags, aid stations are on course and organisers advise carrying water.

About

The Donald Dash Trail Run is held at Donald County Park in Mount Horeb, Wisconsin, with 5K and 15K trail races plus a kids one-mile fun run. Courses loop from Pop's Knoll on double-wide and singletrack trail over hilly ground.
