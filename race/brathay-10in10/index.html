Course

Ten marathons on ten consecutive days, each one a lap of the Windermere Marathon course. Start and finish are at Brathay Hall, Ambleside, on the shore of Windermere, which doubles as the event base. The road circuit takes in the Devil's Elbow climb at mile 7, Newby Bridge at mile 13 and Rayrigg Road at mile 21.

About

The Brathay 10in10 challenges runners to conquer the stunning Windermere Marathon course in the heart of the United Kingdom's picturesque Lake District. Over the course of 10 days, participants will complete 10 laps of this scenic route, testing their endurance and dedication in a truly unique running event.
