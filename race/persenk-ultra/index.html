About

The Persenk Ultra is a thrilling 110km trail running challenge set against the rugged backdrop of the Rhodope Mountains in southern Bulgaria. Runners will navigate a demanding course through dense forests, rocky ravines, and high alpine meadows, testing their endurance and technical skills on the quest to conquer the "Wild Boar Ultra."
