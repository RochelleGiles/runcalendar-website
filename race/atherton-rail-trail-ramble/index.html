Course

Both distances start together at Prior's Creek Park, heading south-west along the Rail Trail toward Rifle Range Road, then onto the Mount Baldy and Mount Yabi walking tracks before returning to the start/finish. The 12.5km carries on north-east past the Men's Shed and over the highway bridge to Golf Links Road and BillyCart Brewery, rejoins the Rail Trail to a turnaround at Manthey Road, and retraces the route. Mostly trail with some road sections.

About

Atherton Rail Trail Ramble follows a section of the Atherton Tablelands Rail Trail, built along a disused railway corridor in Far North Queensland. Runners can choose a 12.5km or 5.9km course through the Tablelands countryside.
