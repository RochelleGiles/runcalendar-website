Course

A point-to-point 200-mile run along the crushed-gravel Cowboy Trail rails-to-trails route, starting at Ta Ha Zouka Park in Norfolk (Friday 6:00am) and heading west to Valentine across 200+ bridges. A companion 100-mile starts in Atkinson (Saturday 8:00am). Flat and non-technical, about 90% crushed gravel, with 84-hour and 32-hour limits.

About
