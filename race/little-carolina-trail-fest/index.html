Course

Held in Cleveland Metroparks Rocky River Reservation on a repeating 5K loop, run once or several times depending on the distance entered. Start and finish at the Lagoon Picnic Area on Valley Parkway. Evening start at 6:00pm with a shuttle from Frostville Museum from 4:15pm. Surface and aid station detail not stated.

About

We're heading south—Cleveland-style—for the Little Carolina run on June 20th! While we're not making the trip to the real Carolinas, we are bringing the southern vibes to the Cleveland Metroparks with this fun, summer evening race.
