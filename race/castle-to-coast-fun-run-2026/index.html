Course

A point-to-point 8.2 miles on Highway 1, starting at William Randolph Hearst Memorial Beach below Hearst Castle and finishing at Shamel Park in Cambria, with open Pacific views the whole way. Cuesta College volunteers marshal the route and shuttles return runners to the start.

About

LIMITED PARKING available at the William Randolph Hearst Memorial Beach parking lot. PLEASE CARPOOL! Shuttles are provided by the event staff to transport you back to the start. The course is a well marked point-to-point route. Cuesta College volunteers monitor the course.
