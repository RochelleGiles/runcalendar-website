Course

Out-and-back courses of 7km, 10km and 14km starting and finishing at Awakairangi Park in Upper Hutt, following the Hutt River Trail along the river's edge. The surface is roughly half well-formed bush trail and half sealed pathway, running through native bush and open parkland, over small bridges and past swimming spots with the surrounding hills in view.

About

Tucked alongside the Hutt River and winding its way through the heart of Upper Hutt, this trail offers a beautiful mix of riverside serenity and lush greenery. The course follows the river's edge, weaving past native bush, open parkland, and peaceful swimming spots, with the crystal-clear water never far from view. Keep an eye out for local birdlife as you cross small bridges and enjoy the changing scenery, from shaded forested sections to wide open spaces framed by the surrounding hills. Easily accessible yet rich in natural beauty, this trail is a favourite for both locals and visitors seeking a refreshing riverside escape.
