About

Great Paniisan Trail is an annual trail running race in the Sentul Highland area of Bogor district, West Java. The race offers two categories — Red Star (ITRA Endurance Point 2) and Black Star (ITRA Endurance Point 3) — both starting and finishing at Nasihin Parking Lot in Wangun, Karang Tengah Village, Sentul City.
