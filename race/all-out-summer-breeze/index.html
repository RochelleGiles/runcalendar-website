About

Join us for our 14th annual Summer Breeze run/walk along the Van Bibber Trail in Arvada, CO , where we celebrate community and inclusion while benefiting the Food Bank of the Rockies. This BOLDERBoulder qualifying event features relatively flat 5K, 10K, and half marathon courses that meander along the Van Bibber Creek Trail, offering breathtaking views of Arvada's open spaces and Table Mountain, making it the perfect setting to chase a personal record!
