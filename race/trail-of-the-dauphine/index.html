About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Dauphiné region at the annual Trail of the Dauphiné! This challenging trail running event takes participants through stunning mountain landscapes, offering a true test of endurance and a chance to immerse yourself in the natural splendor of this captivating corner of France. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or seeking a new adventure, the Trail of the Dauphiné promises an unforgettable experience.
