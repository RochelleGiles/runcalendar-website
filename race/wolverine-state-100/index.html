Course

A point-to-point crossing of Michigan's Lower Peninsula, starting at Bayfront Park in Petoskey at 5:30am Saturday and finishing at Bayview Park in Alpena by 2:30pm Sunday. The route mixes singletrack, gravel and limestone roads and quiet seasonal roads through forest and farm country, over packed dirt, sand, grass and pavement with roots, rocks and leaves underfoot. Aid stations every 5-8 miles.

About

Wolverine State 100 sends runners, relay teams and crews across Michigan's northern Lower Peninsula, racing from Petoskey to Alpena on a route built for grit, beauty and endurance.
