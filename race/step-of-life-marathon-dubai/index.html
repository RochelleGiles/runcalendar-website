About

A community run on Al Mamzar Beach organised by Dubai's Islamic Affairs and Charitable Activities Department, under the slogan "A Step Towards a Healthier Life": a 5K from 4:30 in the afternoon, then a 3K, a 1.5K, a 1 km juniors' race and a 500 m race for people of determination into the evening. A marathon in name only: the 5K is the longest race.
