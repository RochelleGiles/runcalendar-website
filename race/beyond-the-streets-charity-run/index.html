Course

Run along the LA River from Canoga Park on about three miles of uninterrupted paved path. The 5K covers half the path, the 10K is an out-and-back, and the half marathon covers the course twice plus an extra section.

About

We're making this event completely free because we want everyone to be able to show up, move their body, and be part of something good: no barriers, no pressure, no financial gatekeeping. But if you're able to donate, even a little, it makes a real difference at North Valley Caring Services
