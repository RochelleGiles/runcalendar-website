Course

Start and finish are at 23 Waitārere Rise Avenue, Waitārere Beach, with all courses run on flat, easy forest tracks inside Waitārere Forest. The routes suit runners, walkers and buggies alike. Timing mats record splits every 2.5 km, and aid stations are placed on the longer courses.

About

Discover the enchanting Waitārere Forest in New Zealand as you wind through the lush, verdant trails at the Waitārere Forest Run. Challenge yourself with distances ranging from a family-friendly 2km to a half marathon 21km, all while immersed in the serene natural beauty of this hidden gem. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this event offers an unforgettable experience for all.
