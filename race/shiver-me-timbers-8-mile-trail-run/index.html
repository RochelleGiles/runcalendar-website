Course

About

The Shiver Me Timbers is an 8 Mile mixed terrain run along the Coast Path in Torbay, Devon. The course starts at the Bay of Goodrington, and heads out across coastal trails, through woodlands across bays and coves, through a Grove and returns to Goodrington.
