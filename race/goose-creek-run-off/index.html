Course

A net-downhill road race through the Goose Creek valley south of Oakley, Idaho. The 10K starts on Goose Creek Road and runs point-to-point to the finish at Oakley City Park; the 5K covers the second half of the same route. Sealed road surface throughout.

About

The Goose Creek Run-Off is known for its retro eighties costume theme, with participants encouraged to dress in neon and leg warmers each year for a fun, throwback atmosphere.
