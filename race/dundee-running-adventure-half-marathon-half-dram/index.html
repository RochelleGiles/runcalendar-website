About

The Dundee Running Adventure Half Marathon takes in the Green Circular cycleway which surrounds the city of Dundee. Runners pass through wooded areas, parkland and along a lengthy stretch of the northern banks of the River Tay, ending opposite Barnhill Rock Gardens (Broughty Ferry).
