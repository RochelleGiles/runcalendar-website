Course

A USATF-certified road 5K starting on River Road and running down Derby Avenue past the community centre, under Route 8 and up a gradual rise past the Historical Society and Library before returning via Cedar Street and Derby Avenue. Total ascent is about 170 feet and the last half mile is long and flat. Water is available near miles 1 and 2.

About

This 5K in Seymour, Connecticut starts at Broad Street Park and benefits Seymour Pink's breast cancer charity. Runners can take part in person or complete a virtual option.
