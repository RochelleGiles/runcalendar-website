About

Located in the heart of the fabled Mississippi Delta, the Viking Half Marathon • 10K & 5K promises Southern charm, fast times, and an unbelievable race experience. From the time you arrive in Greenwood, you can expect great local culture, an array of fabulous food, and warm Southern hospitality.
