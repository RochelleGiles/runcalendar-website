Course

A 21.1km point-to-point through Sydney at dusk, starting at Rozelle Parklands and finishing at Tumbalong Park via the White Bay Ferry Terminal, Anzac Bridge, Darling Harbour and Pyrmont Bridge. Mixed underfoot: road, boardwalk, gravel paths, stairs and some narrow sections. Almost flat, 3m to 21m elevation.

About

Nike's After Dark Tour brings a half marathon through Sydney's inner west after dark, part of a global night-running series built to empower women runners, with pacers along the route and a festival finish line.
