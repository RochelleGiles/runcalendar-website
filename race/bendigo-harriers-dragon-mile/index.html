Course

A one mile street course through the centre of Bendigo, starting in Pall Mall near the Gold Monument. It is run as part of the Bendigo Easter Festival parade programme on Easter Sunday. The organiser does not state a finish point, surface detail or aid station provision.

About

The Bendigo Harriers Dragon Mile is a historic running event that takes place in Bendigo, Australia, as part of the city's iconic Easter Parade. This unique mile-long race showcases the athletic talent of the region and offers runners a chance to experience the vibrant community atmosphere of this beloved annual celebration.
