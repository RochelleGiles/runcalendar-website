Course

Runs through desert canyon terrain outside Boulder City, mostly on single track. Aid stations stock GU sports drinks, water and salty snacks, with hot meals served at base camp, and race-day entries are accepted until 30 minutes before the start. The 100-mile has a 30-hour cutoff.

About

Whether you're a trail-running veteran or just dipping your toes into the ultra-world, Bootleg Boogie promises an experience like no other. Our aid stations are stocked with everything you need to refuel and recharge, including sports drinks featuring GU, water, and salty snacks. And when night falls, if you are still out on the course, hot, hearty meals at base camp will keep you fueled and smiling. Enjoy stunning views of Lake Mead and the glitzy Las Vegas Strip in the distance, all while dancing along single track that will test your grit.
