About

The Bruintjieshoogte Ultra is a 50km road ultramarathon based in Somerset East, in the Karoo region of the Eastern Cape. Now in its 38th year, it's one of the area's longest-running ultra distance events, drawing runners from across the Eastern Cape each March.
