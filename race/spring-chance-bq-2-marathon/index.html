About

The Spring Chance BQ.2 Marathon is a Boston Qualifying race organized by Boston finishers, designed to help 300 runners achieve their qualifying times with a flat, fast loop course at Fabyan Forest Preserve West. It features a net downhill start, pace teams targeting times 3 minutes under BQ standards, and comprehensive support to maximize Boston entry chances.
