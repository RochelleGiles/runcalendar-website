Course

A flat urban loop starting on Elm Street at the KI Convention Center and finishing at the CityDeck in downtown Green Bay, with a net elevation change of only three feet. The course is entirely paved, the first 7.5 miles on concrete and the last 5.6 miles on asphalt along the Fox River Trail, passing the Broadway district and boulevards in Ashwaubenon and De Pere. A half marathon relay and a 5K run alongside it.

About

The Bellin Women's Half Marathon and 5K is an annual women's-only race through Green Bay, Wisconsin, organized by Bellin Health as part of its Titletown Wellness Race Series, drawing thousands of runners each October.
