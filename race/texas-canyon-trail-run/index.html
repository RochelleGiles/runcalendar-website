About

The Texas Canyon Trail Run is an annual trail running event hosted by the Amerind Museum featuring scenic 5K and 10K courses through Texas Canyon's spectacular rock formations, yucca fields, and cacti. Race registration includes free entry to the Amerind Museum and supports its mission of promoting knowledge of Native Peoples through research and education.
