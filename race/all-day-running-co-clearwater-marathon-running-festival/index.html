About

The All Day Running Co. Clearwater Marathon & Running Festival is home to America's #1 beach and is just 22 miles from Tampa on Florida's stunning west coast. All of our courses in the Clearwater Running Festival take you past some of the most beautiful scenery with spectacular views of the Gulf, Inner Coastal and city.
