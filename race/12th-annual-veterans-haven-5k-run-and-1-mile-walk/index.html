Course

Starts at Christ the King Church on Hopkins Avenue in Haddonfield and continues through the Camden County park system alongside the historic Cooper River. The 5K is chip timed and starts at 9:00 am, with a 1 mile walk running alongside it.

About

The Veterans Haven 5K Run and 1 Mile Walk is organized by American Legion Post 38 of Haddonfield and the Camden County Board of Freeholders to support veterans and combat homelessness. The 12th annual event features a 5K run and 1-mile walk in Haddonfield, New Jersey.
