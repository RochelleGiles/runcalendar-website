Course

A fast, largely traffic-free course on the paved Loop Trail system along the Columbia River at Wenatchee, with Cascade Mountain views. The route crosses the river on the Odabashian Bridge and passes through Confluence State Park; marathon runners cover the half-marathon loop twice. Walkers are welcome in every category.

About
