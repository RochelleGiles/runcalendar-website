Course

A 5K loop at Turtleback Rock Picnic Area in South Mountain Reservation, repeated up to ten times for the 50K. The loop mixes wide dirt and gravel carriage roads with technical singletrack over rocks and roots, including a rocky descent at mile one and a climb at mile 2.5. A single aid station sits at the start/finish, reached every 5K, with hot food, snacks, water and Skratch.

About

The Squatchy Leftovers is a trail running and hiking event held at South Mountain Reservation in West Orange, NJ. Participants can choose from 5K, 10K, 25K, 50K distances or a Kids 1M, supporting the non-profit Bigger Than The Trail.
