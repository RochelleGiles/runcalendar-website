Course

A scenic out-and-back along the South Lake trail, 1.5 miles in each direction. The 5K starts and finishes at Suncreek Brewery in downtown Clermont, Florida. One aid station sits mid-course and is passed twice. A virtual option is also offered.

About

The Clermont Police Department hosts the Honoring the Fallen 5K in downtown Clermont, Florida, now past its fifth running. Formerly known as the Conrad Buckley Legacy Scholarship 5K, the race honors fallen law enforcement officers, with proceeds benefiting Concerns of Police Survivors (C.O.P.S.) and the Code 4 Initiative.
