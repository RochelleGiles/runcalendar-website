Course

Run point-to-point on the Wild Azalea Trail through the Evangeline Ranger District of the Kisatchie National Forest outside Alexandria. The 10K, 13-mile, 27-mile and 50-mile options all use sections of the same singletrack, with self-service aid boxes placed roughly every four miles and trailheads at Woodworth and Twin Bridges. Bike categories share the 15, 27 and 50-mile routes.

About

The Wild Azalea Trail Challenge follows Louisiana's longest hiking and running trail through Kisatchie National Forest, with distances from a 10K up to a 50-mile ultra. The route runs through pine forest near Valentine Lake outside Alexandria.
