Course

An off-road forest race in Castlewellan Forest Park, with the start and finish on the grass beside the lake. The route runs on forest terrain past the park's famous maze with views to the Mourne Mountains, and distances may vary slightly given the off-road surface. As a winter race there is no water station on course.

About

Immerse yourself in the enchanting forest trails of Castlewellan, Northern Ireland, as you take on the annual Run Forest Run challenge. This beloved winter trail running event invites adventurous runners to explore the scenic landscapes and test their endurance through a captivating course winding through the heart of the Irish countryside.
