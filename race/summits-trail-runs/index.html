About

The Summits Trail Runs occur at Granby Ranch Resort in Colorado's beautiful Rocky Mountains. These challenging trail races offer a variety of distances ranging from shorter runs to ultra distances, each course showcasing sweeping mountain vistas, dense forests, and rugged alpine terrain. Runners experience varying elevation gains that test endurance as the routes twist through single-track trails, alpine meadows, and aspen groves. The event celebrates the spirit of human resilience and determination, attracting runners who crave physical challenge and mountain landscapes' serenity. With a strong community atmosphere, the Summits Trail Runs emphasize respect for nature and camaraderie among athletes. This race weekend is a memorable mix of adventure, grit, and the breathtaking beauty of Granby Ranch.
