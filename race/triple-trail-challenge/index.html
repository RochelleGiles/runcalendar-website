Course

A three-race Park City series. The Round Valley Rambler runs buff single and double track in Round Valley at 6,500-7,100ft, rolling high desert with long steady climbs and fast descents. The Jupiter Peak 25K is a 16-mile loop from Park City Mountain Resort at 7,000ft to the 10,000ft summit and back. The Mid Mountain 50K loops Deer Valley, holds the Mid Mountain Trail for nearly 20 miles, then drops through the Utah Olympic Park trails.
