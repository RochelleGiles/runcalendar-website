About

The Petra Desert Marathon invites runners to experience the stunning, rugged landscapes of Jordan's ancient city of Petra. Traverse a challenging course through dramatic desert vistas, with the iconic rock formations of this UNESCO World Heritage site as your backdrop. This unique event offers an unforgettable adventure for endurance athletes seeking an extraordinary running experience.
