About

Nestled in the heart of the Haut-Jura, the Course de la Passerelle takes place in Pratz, a charming Jura village surrounded by forests, viewpoints, and the Saint-Romain chapel. A exceptional natural setting between wild valleys, wooded trails, and panoramic views on the Bienne. Ideal to escape, breathe, and take on a sporting challenge in a local and warm atmosphere.
