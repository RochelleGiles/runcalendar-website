About

Run, walk, or jog through the Ashton Gardens at the Tulip 5k! This fan-favorite 3.1-mile race takes you through the Tulip Festival, passing by thousands of fragrant flowers along the way. All participants will receive a themed race shirt and commemorative medal. Perfect for families, the Tulip 5k is wheels-friendly: strollers and other running aides are welcome.
