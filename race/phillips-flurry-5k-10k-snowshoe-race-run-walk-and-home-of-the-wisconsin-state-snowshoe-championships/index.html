Course

Run in the Phillips School Forest, with the 5K and 10K on groomed ski trails and packed single track over rolling terrain with a few large hills. Snowshoe and run/walk divisions share the same courses, and the race hosts the Wisconsin State Snowshoe Championships.

About

The Phillips Flurry is an annual 5K and 10K snowshoe race/run/walk event held in Phillips, Wisconsin. It is the home of the Wisconsin State Snowshoe Championships.
