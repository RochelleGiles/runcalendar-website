Course

Trail courses along the ridgelines of Hassans Walls Reserve above Lithgow, in the western Blue Mountains. Runners cross dramatic cliff lines, ferny gullies and rocky ridges on a mix of singletrack, mountain bike trail and fire road. The 50km carries about 2,300m of climbing and the 42km about 1,956m, with shorter 30km, 22km, 10km, 5km and kids races.

About
