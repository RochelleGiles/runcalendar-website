Course

A point-to-point 10K along the canal towpath from The Bonded Warehouse in Stourbridge to the Brierley Hill waterfront. The route is mostly flat leafy towpath until the closing nine-lock climb. A small number of places are held back for entry on the day.

About

The 9 Locks 10K Challenge is a gritty, scenic, point-to-point run that takes you from Stourbridge to Brierley Hill along the historic canal route once powered by the Industrial Revolution. It's a fun, social event, but make no mistake: this is a genuinely challenging experience, especially when you hit the famous nine-lock climb in the final stretch.
