About

Mito Komon Manyu Marathon is a full marathon in Mito, Ibaraki Prefecture, Japan, named for the legendary Edo-era lord Tokugawa Mitsukuni, popularly known as Mito Komon, whose travels give the race its "manyu," or wandering journey, theme.
