Course

Courses loop through the Griffith University Logan campus and neighbouring Riverdale Park over a mix of dirt, grass, gravel and asphalt. Sharp turns, short steep pinches and long cambered sections break up any rhythm. The 2km colour run, 5km and 10km all start and finish on campus and suit runners and walkers alike.

About

The Griffith Sport Logan Fun Run is a community road and park event on the Griffith University Logan campus, south of Brisbane. Runners and walkers choose a 2km colour run, 5km or 10km, all crossing a mix of asphalt, gravel, grass and dirt through the campus and Riverdale Park.
