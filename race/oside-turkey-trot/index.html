Course

A flat, fast beachside 5K in Oceanside, starting on North Coast Highway and finishing at The Strand and Seagaze behind the amphitheatre. The route runs beside the ocean for much of its length. Two 5K waves, a 5K walk and two kids races share the same seafront corridor.

About

The O'side Turkey Trot's 5K and Kids 1-Mile take place on Thanksgiving morning in Oceanside, California, organized by the San Diego Running Company. Runners turn out along the coast before the holiday meal for the community race.
