Course

Two 5km loops through the vineyards of the Rathfinny Wine Estate at Alfriston, starting and finishing at the Tasting Room. The going is mainly grass, chalk and flint trail with undulating cambers and views over the South Downs. The field is capped at 75.

About

Explore the stunning vineyards and rolling hills of the South Downs National Park during the Rathfinny Christmas Run. This unique event guides runners through the picturesque Rathfinny Estate, offering a scenic and challenging course with stunning views of the English countryside. Whether you're looking for a festive fitness challenge or a chance to enjoy the great outdoors, this event is not to be missed.
