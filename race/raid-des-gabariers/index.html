About

The Raid des Gabariers is a demanding trail running event taking place in the gorges of the Dordogne River during winter. The race features challenging terrain with elevation gradients ranging from 8% to 12% across three distances. The event showcases unique and authentic locations throughout the Dordogne gorges, with routes being revised annually to provide new experiences.
