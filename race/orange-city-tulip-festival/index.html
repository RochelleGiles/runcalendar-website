Course

The 5K and the 1 mile walk both start at 8:05am on Arizona Avenue near the park in downtown Orange City. The route runs on city streets and finishes along Central Avenue, passing Northwestern College and the county courthouse. Aid station detail is not published.

About

The Tulip Festival Road Race is an annual event held during the Orange City Tulip Festival, featuring scenic routes through the community. It offers a 5K run/walk and a 1-mile fun walk starting near Windmill Park or downtown.
