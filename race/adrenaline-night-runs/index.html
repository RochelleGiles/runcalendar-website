Course

Night trail races starting and finishing at the Four Peaks Staging Area in McDowell Mountain Regional Park, Fountain Hills, Arizona. Rolling desert singletrack on the Escondido, Cinch and Scenic Ridge trails mixes fast smooth running with tougher mountain sections, returning along the Shallmo Trail. Longer distances repeat the loops, the 50K climbing about 2,163 feet.

About

Experience the beauty of McDowell Mountain Regional Park on an array of fun and fast courses at the Adrenaline Night Runs. Our 6K distance is perfect for first time night trail runners and the challenging 10K will push those ready for a new challenge.
