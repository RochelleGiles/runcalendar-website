About

The Altötting Half Marathon is a renowned running event located in the heart of Bavaria. Featuring 80% of its route on forest paths, it is considered one of the fastest natural running courses in Germany. The event offers multiple race distances and a vibrant atmosphere with entertainment like samba drummers and rock bands along the course.
