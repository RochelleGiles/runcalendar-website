About

Gavin's Gallop 5K isn't just another race on the calendar. It's a chance to stand beside families walking through something most of us can't imagine. All proceeds from this event go to Gavin's Gifts, a nonprofit helping families affected by Batten Disease, a rare childhood condition. Your registration helps provide financial assistance, home modifications, and care-related support for families who need it.
