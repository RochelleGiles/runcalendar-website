Course

Flat, fast 5-mile and 10-mile routes along Rhyl's seafront promenade, starting and finishing near the promenade car park. Run on paths and promenade with no road closures. The event has been postponed while sea defence construction continues in Rhyl.

About

A road race along the North Wales coast at Rhyl, offering 5 mile, 10 mile and half marathon distances on the same flat seafront course. A traditional club event popular with local Welsh running clubs.
