Course

A lapped course on the Granite Way footpath and cycleway out of Okehampton, Devon, with views over Dartmoor. Race HQ and start are at the Granite Way Cafe on Klondyke Road; the 10-mile is one lap and the 20-mile two, starting at 9am.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Granite Way in this challenging 10 and 20-mile running event hosted by the Exeter City Community Trust. Runners will wind through the stunning countryside of the United Kingdom, taking in breathtaking views and testing their endurance on this scenic course. Whether you're an experienced ultramarathoner or a dedicated 10-miler, this event offers an unforgettable running experience for all.
