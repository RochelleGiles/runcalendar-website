About

Tackle the legendary Sierre Zinal, a renowned mountain running event that takes participants on a breathtaking journey through the Swiss Alps. Runners will conquer five peaks over 4,000 meters, immersed in the stunning natural beauty of the Valais region. This iconic race is a true test of endurance and a must-do for any adventurous trail runner.
