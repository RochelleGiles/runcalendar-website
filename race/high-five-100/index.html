Course

A self-navigated 100-mile mountain route out of Lake City, Colorado, linking 24 mandatory proof points and checkpoints that must be reached in a set order. There is no marked course and no race-provided aid between the designated checkpoints, and runners pick their own line, so the distance covered varies from roughly 90 to 105 miles.
