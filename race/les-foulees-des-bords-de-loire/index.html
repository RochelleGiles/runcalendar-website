About

Explore the scenic banks of the Loire River at the Les Foulées Des Bords De Loire, a beloved running event hosted by the Racing Club Nantais. This picturesque route through the heart of the city offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty and historic charm of Nantes. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a casual jogger, this event promises an unforgettable running adventure.
