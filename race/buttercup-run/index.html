Course

All three routes start and finish at the Arlee Community and Fitness Center. The terrain is mainly flat, over a mix of pavement, gravel and trail. Same-day entry opens at 9am on race morning.

About

The Buttercup Run is a spring race through the Jocko Valley in Arlee, Montana, on the Flathead Reservation, raising money for Arlee school teacher grants.
