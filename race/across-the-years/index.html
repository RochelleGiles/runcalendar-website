Course

Fixed-time and fixed-distance races on a flat 2.27km (1.42-mile) loop circling the Peoria Sports Complex in Peoria, Arizona. The surface mixes dirt paths with asphalt and concrete, gaining about 23ft per lap. Runners start and finish at the complex, lapping continuously across the six-day window.

About

Across the Years is a multi-day ultramarathon in Peoria, Arizona, run on a looped course over fixed time periods from 24 hours up to six days. Runners tackle 100K, 100 mile, 200 mile, or marathon formats.
