Course

A single traffic-free lap of about 10 miles through the private Eridge deer park, on tracks, grass and woodland trails that are often muddy, with steep, rutted and narrow sections. The route passes three lakes, a Saxon hill fort with a Victorian folly, natural springs, former carriageways and ancient woodland, and includes stream crossings and climbs known as Heavenly Hill and the Slippery Slope.

About

Explore the picturesque trails of Eridge Park during the Eridge Park 10 Trail Challenge, an off-road race hosted by Tunbridge Wells Harriers. Runners will navigate approximately 10 miles of scenic countryside in the heart of the United Kingdom, testing their endurance on this challenging yet rewarding course.
