Course

A 100% paved point-to-point route through the high desert, starting at Romero's Park in Agua Fria and finishing at Reunity Resources Farm. Runners loop through Agua Fria Village near mile 4 then run out and back along the Santa Fe River to a turnaround at mile 9, the course high point at 6,883 feet, before dropping downhill to the finish. Total climb is 651 feet.

About

The Santa Fe International Half Marathon winds through the high-desert streets and historic adobe neighborhoods of Santa Fe, New Mexico, at over 7,000 feet elevation, with a 5K and relay option alongside the half marathon.
