About

The TMC A-Mountain Half Marathon starts and finishes beside Mercado San Agustin at the base of A-Mountain on the west side of downtown Tucson. The half circles the summit of Sentinel Peak before an out-and-back along the Santa Cruz Riverpath. A 4-miler, a family mile and a free FitKidz mile run alongside it.
