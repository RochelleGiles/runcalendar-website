About

This annual event is a fundraiser with proceeds supporting the construction and upkeep of Sharps Ridge Veterans Memorial Park. The event start point and ceremonial area is at the Lincoln Park Technology Trade School, 535 Chickamauga Ave. The courses proceed in a loop on the well marked hiking trails of Sharps Ridge that meander through largely wooded and hilly terrain. Participants can choose from three options for this event; a 5K timed trail run, a 1 mile family fun walk and a 10K Ruck Race. All events take place on the Sharps Ridge trails. Ample parking is available throughout the neighborhood at designated locations and shuttle service is provided for those who are unable to walk a few blocks.
