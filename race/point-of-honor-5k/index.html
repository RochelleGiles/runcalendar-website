Course

Starts and finishes beside the Depot Grille in Lynchburg, with most of the course running on the Blackwater Creek Trail. The exact routing is reviewed each year around trail works. A single 5K is offered, with separate adult and children's divisions.

About

Point of Honor 5K takes its name from the historic Point of Honor mansion overlooking the James River in Lynchburg, Virginia, and is hosted by the local Riverside Runners club as an annual community race.
