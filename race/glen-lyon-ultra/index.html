Course

A 31-mile ultra with 3,500 feet of climb, 85% of it off road with a tarmac section through the middle. It opens with a loop of Loch Lyon taking in about six river crossings - wet feet are guaranteed - then passes back through the start/finish for drop bags before climbing into remote Glen Lochay for high and low-level sections and returning to finish in Glen Lyon. Water stations at roughly 8, 20 and 26 miles with drop bags at halfway. An alternative Route B is used if the crossings become impassable. A shorter trail race runs alongside.

About

The Glen Lyon Ultra Marathon takes place among the hills of Glen Lyon and Glen Lochay in stunning surroundings. Water crossings, hills and mostly off-road, Glen Lyon has a distances of 31 miles and around 3,500ft elevation.
