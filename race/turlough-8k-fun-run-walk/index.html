About

The Turlough 8K is a scenic one-lap course through the charming town of Turlough, County Mayo, Ireland. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just looking for a fun fitness challenge, this event offers a chance to explore the picturesque Irish countryside while getting in a great workout. Runners and walkers of all levels are welcome to enjoy the community spirit and beautiful surroundings.
