Course

A loop trail race in Wompatuck State Park, starting and finishing at South Field. Each loop mixes about two miles of pavement and rail trail with unpaved, often muddy singletrack. The 10 miler runs one loop and the 50K three loops plus an extra mile, with a nine-hour cutoff and walkers welcome.

About

The Womp Romp takes place at Wompatuck State Park in Hingham, mixing paved rail trail with unpaved, often muddy singletrack. The 10 Mile is a single loop while the 50K repeats it three times plus an extra mile. The cupless, volunteer-run event benefits Friends of Wompatuck and Free to Run.
