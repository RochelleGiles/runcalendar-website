About

Nestled in the heart of Slovakia's stunning mountain landscapes, the Javornicka Stovka is a must-attend event for trail running enthusiasts. Challenging runners with a scenic and demanding 100km course, this race offers a unique opportunity to explore the region's breathtaking natural beauty while pushing the limits of their endurance. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or seeking a new adventure, the Javornicka Stovka promises an unforgettable experience for all.
