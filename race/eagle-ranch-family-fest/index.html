About

Run the Ranch is a 5K run and walk held on the grounds of Eagle Ranch in Flowery Branch, Georgia, as part of the charity's annual Family Fest. A kids' fun run and a 1.5-mile nature walk on wooded trail are held the same morning, and every entry includes admission to the festival.
