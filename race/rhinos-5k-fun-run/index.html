Course

Out-and-back 5K on the Lowell Riverfront Trail, starting and finishing at Lowell Riverfront Park in Everett. The trail is paved and mostly flat. Organisers note that upcoming construction may force a detour onto an unpaved section.

About

The Rhinos 5K Fun Run is hosted by the Snohomish Rhinos Rugby Club at Lowell Riverfront Park in Everett. All proceeds go directly to supporting the team.
