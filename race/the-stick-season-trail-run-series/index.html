Course

Winter trail series in the Glencoe area, with two distance options on each event day. Courses mix fast asphalt road with goat-busting singletrack and a lot of climbing, including a run through the 109 tunnel and a clockwise loop past the quarry wall. A shoe transition through the racers village lets runners swap between road and trail footwear.

About

Welcome to The Stick Season Trail run series! 5 different dates and routes of varying distances and terrains from fast smooth asphalt road to Goat busting single track with a lot of vert with a 1/2 marathon finalie! There will be something for everyone, and a shoe transition on the course through racers village? Yep. Will those Trail shoes out perform the Road Shoes on the next section? Swap em' in the transition! We go rain, shine, sun or snow, its gonna be epic! And something super fun to do in winter (Stick Season)
