Course

Run on the Rocks and Roots trails at Alum Creek State Park, starting from Hollenback Road in Lewis Center. Alternating north and south loops, the north clockwise and the south counter-clockwise, stacked up for the longer distances; the 6K takes a shortcut version of the north loop. Entirely on singletrack.

About

Join us at the Muddy Duck! Get down in the muck and run like a duck! We're not really sure what that line means but it sounds good so we are going with it! But for real, let's get muddy! With the coming of spring the foliage will be blooming, eggs will be hatching, and we'll be running!
