Course

A 5K through Abington Park, Northampton, starting and finishing at the top of the field. The route mixes pathways and grass over uneven terrain, is not wheelchair accessible and can become boggy in poor weather.

About

Race for Life's 5K runs through Abington Park, a historic park in Northampton home to Abington Museum. It's a non-competitive run, jog or walk raising money for cancer research.
