About

The 22nd Magdeburg Marathon is an annual running event that takes place in the city of Magdeburg, Germany. The event features exciting competitions, great activities, and unforgettable moments along the course, making it the event of the year. Runners can look forward to a scenic and challenging route that showcases the city's landmarks and natural beauty.
