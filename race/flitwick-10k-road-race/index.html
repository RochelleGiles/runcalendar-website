Course

A closed-road 10K starting and finishing in Flitwick, running out through the village outskirts, around Steppingley and back. The route is fast, undulating and entirely on sealed road. A free junior fun run follows the main race.

About

The Flitwick 10K is a fast and undulating 10km closed road race set in the heart of Bedfordshire, UK. This scenic event winds through the picturesque countryside, offering runners a challenging yet rewarding experience. Whether you're chasing a new personal best or simply enjoy the thrill of road racing, the Flitwick 10K is a must-do for runners of all abilities.
