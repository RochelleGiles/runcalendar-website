About

Explore the historic streets and scenic trails of Villeneuve lez Avignon during the annual Urban Trail race. This challenging yet rewarding event takes runners on a captivating tour through the charming French town, showcasing its picturesque architecture and natural surroundings. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or a newcomer to the sport, the Urban Trail de Villeneuve lez Avignon offers an unforgettable running experience.
