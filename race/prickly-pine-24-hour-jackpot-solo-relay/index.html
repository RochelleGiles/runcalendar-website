About

Prickly Pine Ultrarunning hosts a timed ultra in the Southern Kettle Moraine State Forest near Dousman, Wisconsin. Runners complete repeated loops on the Ice Age Trail, choosing three, six or nine mile loop options, over six, twelve or twenty-four hours. Solo and relay entries are offered.
