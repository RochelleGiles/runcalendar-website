Course

A figure-of-eight lap course: a 16km lap made of a 10km northern loop run clockwise and a 6km southern loop run anticlockwise, with the start and finish at the crossing point. The organiser describes the surface as pure single track through forest. An aid station sits at the finish junction and is passed every half lap.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of New Zealand's Dog Creek Timber Trail Ultra, a challenging mountain and forest running event featuring 100-mile, 100km, 50-mile, 50km, and 21km race options. Traverse lush native forests, wind through remote valleys, and ascend scenic mountain ridges as you test your limits on this iconic course.
