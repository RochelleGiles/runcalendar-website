Course

A 5K starting and finishing on tarmac in front of the Dubai Duty Free Grandstand at Newbury Racecourse, over a mix of hardstanding, gravel path and grass with only 18 m of climb. About 1 km runs on uneven grass that can be boggy.

About

Race for Life brings Cancer Research UK's charity 5K to Newbury Racecourse, the Berkshire venue better known for top-class National Hunt horse racing. Runners and walkers follow a flat course around the racecourse grounds to raise money for cancer research.
