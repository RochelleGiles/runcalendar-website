Course

An off-road course through the tulip fields at Wooden Shoe Tulip Farm near Woodburn, Oregon, run during the Tulip Festival. Distances are 5K, 10K and half marathon over farm tracks and grass, which can be muddy.

About

The Annual Wooden Shoe 5k/10k and 1/2 Marathon Tulip Trail Run is organized by and benefiting Oregon City Track and Field. It features an off-road course through the blooming tulip fields at the Wooden Shoe TulipFest, with festival admission included in registration.
