About

The ShamROCK Stampede is a St Patrick's Day themed run at the Douglas County Event Center in Castle Rock, Colorado. The 10K doubles as the RRCA Colorado State Championship and runs alongside a 5K and a free 1K kids' fun run, with an indoor health expo at the finish. Proceeds support mental health programmes for veterans and first responders.
