Course

Self-navigated ultramarathons through the Mendip Hills in Somerset, organised by Bristol's Town & Country Harriers. Two routes are offered: 50 miles with about 1,960m of ascent and 50km with about 1,200m. Runners find their own line between checkpoints rather than following a marked course.

About

Explore the stunning Mendip Hills on the Butcombe Trail Ultra, offering a challenging 50-mile and 50km ultramarathon through this picturesque region of the United Kingdom. Runners will be captivated by the diverse natural landscapes and challenging terrain as they push their limits on this renowned trail running event.
