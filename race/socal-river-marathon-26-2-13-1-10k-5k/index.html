Course

Point-to-point along the Santa Ana River Trail, primarily flat with a gradual 365 ft descent over the marathon; most gradient comes from passing under freeways and roads. The marathon starts at Yorba Linda and the half at Anaheim, both bussed out, while the 10K and 5K run out and back from the shared finish at Huntington Beach State Park. Aid stations sit roughly every two miles from Mile 3.

About

The SOCAL River Marathon is a multi-distance running event held in Huntington Beach, California, featuring full marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances. The event starts in Yorba Linda and finishes at Huntington Beach State Park.
