Course

Trail races through Table Mountain National Park in the Western Cape. The 42km spans Green Point to Constantia Uitsig across the spine of Table Mountain (2000m gain); the 33km (1800m), 21km (1000m) and 10km (411m) follow shorter routes through vineyards and fynbos with ancient single-track, steep climbs and technical descents.

About

The Cape Cobra Trail Marathon is run on the trails around Durbanville, north of Cape Town, with 10km, half marathon, 33km and full marathon routes. The courses link vineyard singletrack with rolling hill paths, including the section the organisers call the Vineyard Labyrinth.
