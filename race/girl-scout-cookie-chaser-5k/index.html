About

The Cookie Chaser 5K is a community run and walk organised by Girl Scout Troop 21513 in Roebling, New Jersey, with proceeds supporting Girl Scouts programming. The out-and-back course is open to runners, walkers and strollers, and entrants receive Girl Scout cookies alongside a shirt and post-race refreshments.
