Course

Point-to-point from the Ghost Canyon Getaway near Hermosa to the Borglum Story Museum in Keystone. Around three opening miles are packed dirt road before the route turns to pavement on a net-uphill profile with about 1,850 feet of gain and a downhill finish. Roughly six aid stations sit near miles 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 and 12, with a 4-hour limit.

About

A point-to-point half marathon in the Black Hills, starting near Hermosa and finishing at the Borglum Story Museum in Keystone, South Dakota. The route follows packed dirt road for about the first three miles before turning to pavement, on a net-uphill course with roughly 1,850 feet of gain and around six aid stations.
