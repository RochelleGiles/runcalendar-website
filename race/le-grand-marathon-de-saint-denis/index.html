About

Immerse yourself in the storied history of Saint-Denis as you tackle the iconic Les Voies Royales de Saint-Denis marathon. Weave through the picturesque streets and landmarks of this charming French town, with the magnificent Basilica of Saint-Denis serving as the stunning backdrop. Runners of all levels will delight in the scenic course and vibrant community spirit that make this event a must-do on the European running circuit.
