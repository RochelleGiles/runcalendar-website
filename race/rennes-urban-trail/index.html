About

Explore the historic city of Rennes like never before at the Rennes Urban Trail. This unique running event takes participants on a scenic tour through the vibrant streets and hidden gems of this charming French city. With a challenging route that showcases the city's architecture and culture, the Rennes Urban Trail offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
