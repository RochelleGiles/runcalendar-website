About

Les Foulees Lindoises is a community running event taking place around Sauveboeuf in Lalinde, featuring trails along the Drayaux stream. The event combines sport and solidarity, with all proceeds going to the ARSEP Foundation for Multiple Sclerosis Research. The event offers a family-friendly atmosphere with children's activities and a post-race solidarity lunch.
