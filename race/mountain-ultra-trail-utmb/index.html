About

The Mountain Ultra Trail is South Africa's UTMB World Series event, run in the Outeniqua Mountains above George on the Garden Route. Distances run from a 45km marathon up to the 168km flagship, all on steep mountain trail with heavy climbing and rocky, exposed terrain.
