About

Run Killarney offers runners the chance to experience one of the world's most breathtaking and scenic routes. Set against the stunning backdrop of Ireland's Killarney National Park, this event features both a Half Marathon and a 10K distance, giving competitors the opportunity to challenge themselves in a truly awe-inspiring setting.
