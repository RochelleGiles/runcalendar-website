About

Explore the picturesque Île de Batz island during the annual Tour de l'Île de Batz running event. This scenic coastal race takes participants on a tour of the charming French island, offering stunning views of the Atlantic Ocean and quaint seaside villages. With a route that showcases the island's natural beauty, the Tour de l'Île de Batz is a must-do for runners seeking a unique and unforgettable running experience.
