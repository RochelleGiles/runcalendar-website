Course

Five courses run through the Southern Drakensberg inside the Maloti-Drakensberg Park World Heritage Site, four of them using the Giant's Cup Trail as their backbone. Routes take in Sani Pass, the Twelve Apostles, Khanti Ridge, Hodgson's Peaks, Garden Castle, Rhino Peak and Langalibalele's Pass, crossing between South Africa and Lesotho.

About

Conquer the majestic Drakensberg Mountains at the Ultra-Trail® Drakensberg, a breathtaking running challenge that takes adventurous athletes through the heart of South Africa's stunning wilderness. Traversing diverse terrain and awe-inspiring vistas, this event offers an unparalleled opportunity for endurance enthusiasts to push their limits and immerse themselves in the serene beauty of the region.
