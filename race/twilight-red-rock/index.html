Course

An out-and-back on the paved Red Rock Canyon scenic drive, all distances starting at the drive's exit at roughly 3,800ft and running against the traffic flow. The 5K turns at 1.55 miles near Pine Creek, the 10K at 3.1 miles near Red Rock Wash and the half marathon at 6.55 miles near White Rock. The road stays open to vehicles and is coned to give runners about a quarter of the carriageway. The race runs into sunset and darkness.

About

Majestically framing the west side of the valley, Red Rock Canyon is the most notable natural treasure in the Las Vegas area. Commencing just before sunset, run through Red Rock Canyon while enjoying the stunning sunset as day falls into night. Sun is replaced by stars and moonlight guides the way. The omnipresence of the surrounding canyon under the moonlit starry night is magical! Joshua trees dance in the shadows and the neon lights of Las Vegas can be seen in the distance. An enchanted late summer evening run.
