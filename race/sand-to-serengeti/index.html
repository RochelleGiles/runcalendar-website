Course

A trail race in Green Lakes State Park. The 10-miler starts on the beach at Green Lakes, loops Round Lake and climbs into the Serengeti section before descending back to a beach finish; the 3-miler takes a shorter loop of the two lakes. One water-only aid station serves the 10-miler at miles 2 and 6.5, and runners are advised to carry hydration.
