About

The 10 Km Of The Trocadero is a scenic running event located in the iconic 16th arrondissement of Paris. Runners will explore the beautiful Parisian landscape, experiencing the city's stunning architecture and historic landmarks. The event is part of the Paris Masters Circuit, offering participants a unique urban running experience.
