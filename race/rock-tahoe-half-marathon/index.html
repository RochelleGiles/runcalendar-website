Course

Downhill point-to-point half marathon above Lake Tahoe, finishing at the Golden Nugget in Stateline. Runners are shuttled from the finish to the start, with waves from 7:30am and a four-hour limit.

About

The Rock Tahoe Half Marathon starts at Spooner Summit and descends over 800 feet along Highway 50 through the stunning east shore of Lake Tahoe. Runners finish at the lakeshore for a post-race celebration with live music and poolside recovery.
