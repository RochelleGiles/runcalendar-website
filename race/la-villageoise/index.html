About

La Villageoise is a local running event featuring nature trails through the countryside near Lyon. Participants can choose from multiple race distances including 18km and 10km runs, as well as walking routes. The event emphasizes community spirit, charitable support, and a festive atmosphere in the picturesque surroundings of Sathonay-Village.
