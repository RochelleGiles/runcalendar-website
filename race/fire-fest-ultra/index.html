Course

Races run laps of a 17.1-mile backcountry loop through Valley of Fire State Park from a shared staging area near Logandale, on sandy and rocky desert trail past sandstone formations and petroglyphs. Longer distances repeat the loop through the start/finish; timed 12- and 24-hour events run the same course.

About

Fire Fest Ultra is a weekend trail festival in Valley of Fire State Park near Logandale, Nevada. Distances from 5K to 100 miles, plus 12- and 24-hour timed events, run laps of a 17.1-mile backcountry loop past sandstone formations and petroglyphs, with camping and entertainment at the staging area.
