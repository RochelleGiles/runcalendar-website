Course

The 5K starts on the track inside the Wellington Dukes football stadium, leaves the stadium for a loop of the streets around town, then finishes back on the track. The Fun Run is four laps of the track. Both start and finish at the stadium on Dickson Street.

About

The Dukes 5K & Fun Run, now past its 5th running, is hosted by the Wellington High School Track and Field team as a fundraiser. It includes a 5K race and a 1-mile fun run.
