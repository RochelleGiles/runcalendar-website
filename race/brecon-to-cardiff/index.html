Course

A point-to-point 43-mile ultra following the River Taff from Brecon, in the Bannau Brycheiniog National Park, to a finish at Nantgarw on the outskirts of Cardiff. The first 30km runs on footpaths and forestry trails; from Merthyr the route follows a traffic-free, mostly tarmac cycle path to the finish. Five manned checkpoints, drop bags at Merthyr, 14-hour limit.

About

A point-to-point ultra marathon from Brecon, gateway to the Brecon Beacons, down through the Taff valley to Cardiff. The route follows the Taff Trail across the hills and countryside of South Wales on canal towpaths, forest tracks and quiet lanes before finishing in the Welsh capital.
