About

The Donautal Marathon offers runners a picturesque tour through the scenic Donautal region. Weaving along the banks of the Danube River, this challenging course presents stunning natural landscapes and a chance to experience the charming local culture. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply seeking an unforgettable running adventure, the Donautal Marathon promises an unforgettable experience.
