Course

A five-race series through Canberra's nature parks, each starting at 10am from a different venue - Chapman Oval, Webbs Picnic Area, Kitchener St in Garran, Hackett and Aranda Oval - on grass, fire trails, single track and rocky nature park tracks. Each race offers Short (2-3.6km), Middle (5-10.6km) and Long (9.6-21.1km) courses.

About

The Sri Chinmoy Canberra Trail Series is a multi-race trail running series held in Stromlo Forest Park, Canberra, offering Long, Middle and Short course options across several events through the year.
