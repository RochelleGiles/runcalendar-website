Course

Run at Silent Valley Mountain Park in the Mourne Mountains, taking in the reservoir and the Mourne wall with mountain views throughout. Ordinary running shoes suit the terrain, though the course is not passable with a buggy. The 10 mile has two water stations and the 10K one at the dam turnaround.

About

The scenic Dambusters race takes place in the Silent Valley Mountain Park in Annalong, County Down, offering 10 mile, 10K and 5K distances. Silent Valley sits within the Mourne area and is home to some of the most beautiful scenery in Ireland.
