Course

A cross-country route on mixed terrain, with the start and finish on grass. The tree-lined avenue at the beginning and end can carry loose gravel and potholes, and the ground becomes uneven in poor weather. The 5K and 10K both set off at 11:00.

About

Race for Life Bury St Edmunds takes place in Nowton Park, a 214-acre former estate parkland with an arboretum and a medieval deer heritage. The 10K and 5K routes cross its grounds for Cancer Research UK.
