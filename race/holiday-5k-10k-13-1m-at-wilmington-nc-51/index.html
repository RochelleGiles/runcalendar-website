Course

Start and finish at Halyburton Park, 4099 S 17th St, Wilmington, with an 8:00am start for all distances. Support is minimal: EMS is not stationed on site and water stations are limited to the finish area. Course shape and surface are not described by the organiser.

About

A festive holiday-themed running event in Wilmington, NC featuring three race distances with scenic out-and-back courses through New Hanover County. Participants can earn finisher medals and compete for best costume awards.
