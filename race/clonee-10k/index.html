About

The Clonee 10K, hosted by the Dunboyne Athletic Club in Ireland, is an evening road race that offers runners a scenic tour through the charming town. This community-driven event is a great opportunity to challenge yourself on a fast, flat course while enjoying the lively atmosphere and local hospitality.
