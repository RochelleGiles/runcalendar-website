Course

Race HQ and finish are at Guilden Sutton Village Hall, with the start about 500 m away. The 10K runs two loops - a first of 3.2 km then a larger circuit - with the opening 4.2 km undulating as it passes under and over the A55 before flattening out. A drinks station sits at 6.8 km and a couple of short rises lead to the finish.

About

Guilden Sutton 10K is a road race through the Cheshire village of Guilden Sutton near Chester, run as part of the Cheshire Grand Prix series organised by South Cheshire Harriers.
