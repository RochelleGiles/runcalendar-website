Course

The marathon and half run a scenic loop taking in Reservoir Park, the Whitehall Trail and Nick's Creek Trail, with the marathon covering it twice; the 5K circles Reservoir Park alone. Four aid stations stocked with water, sports drink and snacks serve the marathon route.

About

The Moore County Trail Marathon, Half, and 5K is part of the Sandhills Running "Miles 4 Moore" race series. The "Miles 4 Moore" race series is a charity run series that donates proceeds to support community wellness and resiliency. The proceeds from this event will go towards funding gym memberships, coaching, and nutrition consultations for low-income families and high school athletes.
