About

The Cradle Mountain Run offers runners a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to traverse Tasmania's iconic Cradle Mountain to Lake St Clair Overland track in a single day. Traversing the rugged and breathtaking landscapes of this world-renowned hiking route, participants will be challenged and rewarded with stunning vistas and a true test of endurance. This event is a must for adventurous trail runners seeking an unforgettable experience in the heart of the Australian wilderness.
