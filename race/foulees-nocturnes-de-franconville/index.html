About

The 10th edition of the 'Foulées Nocturnes de Franconville' is organized by the Sports Department in partnership with the Franconville Athlétisme Val-d'Oise (F.A.V.O) Association. The event takes place on the Jean Rolland Stadium and the streets of Franconville, offering 5km and 10km races for adults as well as shorter distances for children.
