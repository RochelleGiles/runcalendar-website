Course

A free monthly time trial in Dunedin, starting from 9 Kitchener Street. The August edition uses Course 2 along the sealed Te Aka Ōtākou shared path beside the harbour, flat throughout and wheelchair and pram friendly. Bibs are collected from 8:15am for a 9:00am start; 5 km and 10 km share the same route.

About

The Leith Harbour Free 5K & 10K is a free monthly community time trial in Dunedin. The event takes place at two different venues and runs once a month, usually alternating between Thursday evenings and Sunday mornings.
