Course

A looped 12 hour challenge at Saunder Springs Nature Preserve near Radcliff, entered solo, as a duo or as a quad team. The loop takes in shaded forest trail, rocky climbs, creekside path and the preserve's waterfalls, mixing technical footing with fast runnable sections. Running is from 8:00am to 8:00pm and the aim is to cover as many miles as possible.
