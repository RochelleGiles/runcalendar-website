About

The Grand Sand 15K is a trail race along the sandy beaches of Grand Marais on the shore of Lake Winnipeg, Manitoba, near the dunes of Grand Beach Provincial Park. Organized by On The Edge Running, the single 15K distance follows the shoreline's beach and dune terrain.
