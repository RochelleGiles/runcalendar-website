About

The Serengeti Migration Marathon takes runners on an unforgettable journey through the heart of Tanzania's iconic Serengeti National Park. Participants will have the chance to experience the wonder of the region's legendary wildebeest migration up close while pushing their limits on a challenging course amid the stunning African landscape.
