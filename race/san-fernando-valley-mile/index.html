Course

A straight, point-to-point road mile down Maclay Avenue in San Fernando, starting at 8th Street and finishing just before 3rd Street. Separate elite, sub-elite, youth and open waves run the same course; a quarter mile and 100m dash for younger children start further along it.

About

The San Fernando Valley Mile is a one-mile race open to runners and walkers of all levels, starting at 8th Street and Maclay Avenue and finishing just before 3rd Street in the heart of San Fernando. It features multiple waves including elite heats, open sub-elite, youth championships, and kids runs.
