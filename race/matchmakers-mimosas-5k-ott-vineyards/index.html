Course

A cross-country style route run in and around Ott Vineyards, over grass rather than road. The start and finish are at the vineyard, with water stations roughly every 1.5 miles and chip timing at start and finish. A Sip & Stroll option and a virtual 5K run alongside the timed 5K.

About

The Matchmakers & Mimosas 5K is a Valentine-themed running event held at Ott Vineyards in Ellijay, GA, featuring a 5K race through vineyard terrain, a non-competitive Sip & Stroll option, and a Virtual 5K. Participants receive a custom race shirt, finisher medal, and complimentary mimosa, with awards for top finishers in age groups and overall categories.
