Course

A backyard ultra run on a 6.7km off-road loop at That Place Mountain Bike Park beside the Whanganui River, about 20km north of Whanganui. One lap starts on the hour, every hour, and runners must be back in the corral before each bell; the Backyard 6 variant stops after six hours. Twenty-four laps covers roughly 160km.

About

The Awa Backyard Ultra brings the thrilling format of backyard ultra distance running to the scenic Whanganui region of New Zealand. Runners will test their endurance on a challenging loop course, vying to be the last person standing in this unique test of mental and physical fortitude. With a stunning natural backdrop, the Awa Backyard Ultra promises an unforgettable ultra-running experience.
