About

The Turbine Backyard Ultra is a last-one-standing event near Bishop Auckland. Entrants complete a 4.1-mile loop on the hour, every hour, until only one runner remains; each lap has about 400ft of elevation gain across farmland, single-track trail, and woodland dirt tracks.
