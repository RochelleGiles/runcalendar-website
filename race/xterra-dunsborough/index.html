Course

An off-road multisport weekend at Dunsborough in the Margaret River region, doubling as the XTERRA Asia-Pacific Championship. Alongside the full (1.5k swim, 30.2k bike, 10.5k run), sprint and super sprint triathlons, the programme includes standalone trail races: a 38km trail marathon, a 21km trail half and a 10km trail run.

About

Explore the stunning natural landscapes of Western Australia at the XTERRA Dunsborough, Australia's premier off-road triathlon event. Challenging athletes with a dynamic course that combines swimming, mountain biking, and trail running, this exhilarating race immerses participants in the rugged beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned off-road enthusiast or seeking a new adventure, the XTERRA Dunsborough promises an unforgettable experience.
