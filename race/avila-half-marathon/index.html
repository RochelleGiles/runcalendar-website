About

The Avila Half Marathon is a must-attend event for runners seeking an unforgettable challenge. Set against the stunning backdrop of Ávila, Spain, this race takes participants on a scenic journey through the historic city's winding streets and picturesque landscapes. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the sport, the Avila Half Marathon promises an exhilarating and rewarding experience for all.
