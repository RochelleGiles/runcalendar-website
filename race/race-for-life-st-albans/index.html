Course

A 5K through Verulamium Park, mostly on grass with tarmac and woodland sections and a few slopes. The start and finish are on uneven but firm grass, and there are inclines behind the athletics track on the old golf course that can be bypassed by an alternative route. Grass and woodland can turn muddy in poor weather.

About

Race for Life is Cancer Research UK's charity fundraiser, open to walkers, joggers and runners of any pace, with no minimum time. This St Albans edition is a 5K through Verulamium Park, built over the Roman city of Verulamium.
