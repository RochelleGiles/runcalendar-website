About

The Sparkassen Küstenmarathon is a must-run event for seasoned runners and newcomers alike. Situated along the stunning German coastline, this picturesque marathon offers breathtaking views and a challenging yet rewarding course. Runners can look forward to an unforgettable experience as they take on the scenic route and soak in the lively atmosphere of this beloved community event.
