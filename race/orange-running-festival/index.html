Course

Every race starts and finishes within Gosling Creek Reserve, south of the Orange central business district. A marathon, half marathon, 10K, 5K and a community mile all run from the one venue, alongside primary and secondary school mile dashes. The organiser publishes no surface or aid station detail on the event page.

About

Nestled in the heart of Australia, the Orange Running Festival offers a diverse range of run and walk events, catering to runners of all ages and abilities. Immerse yourself in the scenic landscapes of this vibrant regional hub as you challenge yourself through a variety of distances, making this an event not to be missed by endurance enthusiasts.
