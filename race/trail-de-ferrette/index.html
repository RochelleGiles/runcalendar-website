About

Explore the stunning trails of the Jura region at the annual Trail De Ferrette event. This challenging yet rewarding race takes runners through picturesque landscapes, offering breathtaking views and a true test of endurance. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or a newcomer to the sport, the Trail De Ferrette promises an unforgettable running experience.
