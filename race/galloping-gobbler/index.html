Course

Starts and finishes at the Hutzell Athletic Center on the University of Saint Francis campus in Fort Wayne, heading out through the historic Lindenwood Cemetery before returning to campus. A four-mile chip-timed road race on Thanksgiving morning drawing over 2000 runners.

About

The Galloping Gobbler is a Thanksgiving morning 4 mile race organized by the Fort Wayne Running Club, running through Fort Wayne, Indiana. The race has been a local Thanksgiving tradition for more than two decades.
