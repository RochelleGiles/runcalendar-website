About

The Richmond Harriers Tiger 5 is a thrilling 5,000-metre track race held in the scenic suburb of Clifton Hill, Victoria. Hosted by the renowned Richmond Harriers club, this event promises an exhilarating challenge for runners of all levels, with a fast and well-maintained track and a lively atmosphere to fuel your competitive spirit.
