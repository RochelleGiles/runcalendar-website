Course

Out-and-back from Norman Park in Gordonvale to the summit of Walsh's Pyramid and back, 12km for the full race. The route mixes sealed road along Mill Street and the old Bruce Highway with cane headlands and a steep mountain track to the summit. SES checkpoints sit at the base, halfway and the summit. Water is supplied only in emergencies, so runners must carry a hydration pack.

About

An arduous trail and mountain race to the peak of Walsh's Pyramid, in Gordonvale, Queensland. It began in 1959 as a challenge between two cane farmers.
