Course

Starts and finishes at the historic Alhambra Hall in the Old Village of Mount Pleasant, running the scenic streets past colonial architecture and harbour views. Runners, walkers, dogs and strollers are all welcome, with music, refreshments and a raffle afterwards.

About

The Old Village Harbor 5K is offered to residents and visitors each year in November. More than 200 runners participate in the race throughout the picturesque Old Village. This is a must for all newcomers, for seasoned runners, walkers and dogs.
