Course

A gravel course at Michael O Buchanon Park, Bowling Green, starting at 8:00am. The 5K and 10K repeat a 0.78 mile out-and-back loop; the half marathon repeats a 1.09 mile out-and-back segment six times. A 3 hour 30 minute limit applies.
