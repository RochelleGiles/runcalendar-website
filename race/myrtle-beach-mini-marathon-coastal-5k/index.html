About

The Myrtle Beach Mini Marathon & Coastal 5K is a popular running event that takes place in the beautiful coastal city of Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. Runners can choose from a 13.1-mile half marathon or a 5K distance, both offering scenic routes along the Atlantic Ocean.
