About

Run Melbourne is the city's largest winter running event, drawing thousands of participants to race through the iconic streets and landmarks of Australia's cultural capital. Runners of all levels can experience the thrill of closed-off urban courses, with breathtaking views of Melbourne's stunning architecture and vibrant atmosphere.
