About

Oktoberfest at Lake of the Ozarks is back with a .5k and a 5k. Yes, you read that right. In addition to a traditional 5k, we have a one-half K (roughly .31 miles for you non-runners). Starting and finishing at the Sanctuary Brewery in Osage Beach, this will be the 5k of the year!
