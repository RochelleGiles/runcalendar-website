Course

Based at Mueller Park in Bountiful, Utah, where the 7 mile, half and full marathon distances run anticlockwise loops and the 50K heads point to point across linked trail systems. Surfaces are 80% dirt trail, 19% dirt road and a paved start and finish. The 50K climbs about 8,600 ft, over 6,000 ft of it in the first 15.5 miles.
