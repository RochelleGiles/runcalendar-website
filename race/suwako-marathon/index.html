About

The Suwako Marathon is a half marathon that loops around Lake Suwa in Nagano Prefecture, starting from the Suwa Lake Event Plaza. Now in its 38th year, the race caps entries at 7,000 runners with a reserved bracket for Nagano residents, though it is not a JAAF-certified event.
