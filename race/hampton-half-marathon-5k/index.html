Course

Both races start together at 10:00am in Hampton Beach. The half marathon runs out through seaside neighbourhoods, quiet backroads and marshland with only about 130 feet of climbing, then returns along Route 1A on the oceanfront for the last three miles. The 5K shares the first 2.5 miles of that route.

About

The Hampton Half Marathon & 5K, also known as Half at the Hamptons, runs along the seacoast in Hampton Beach, New Hampshire. The early-spring race pairs a coastal course with a finish near the beach.
