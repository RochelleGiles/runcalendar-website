About

The HERS Breast Cancer Walk/Run is an annual walk, run and community expo in Fremont, California, now in its 27th year. Runners can choose the 10K or 5K, with a yoga session and vendor expo alongside, supporting HERS Breast Cancer Foundation's programs for people healing from breast cancer.
