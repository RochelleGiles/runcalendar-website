Course

Two races from Olympia Shelter in North Park, Wexford, Pennsylvania: a 10K on the road and a trail race of about seven miles, sent off as separate starts. Entrants may run either one or combine both as the Two Face Challenge.

About

Held annually in August in beautiful North Park from Olympia Shelter, this event is a dual race that combines a 10K Road race, followed by a 10K Trail race. Race day is typically one of the most humid days of the year in western PA. Join us and see what we mean by insane!
