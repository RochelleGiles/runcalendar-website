Course

Mostly paved and flat half marathon starting and finishing at Blue Cork Winery and Vineyard in Williamstown, New Jersey, with about half a mile run on vineyard property. Hydration and aid stations roughly every two miles, plus music and cheer stations. Starts at 8:30am with a 3.5-hour limit.

About

A scenic and flat 13.1-mile half marathon held at Blue Cork Winery in Williamstown, NJ, featuring mostly paved roads with a half-mile section through vineyard property. The race includes an after-party with complimentary wine, live music, and food trucks.
