About

Join 97.1 The Fan celebrities at the inaugural 9.71k The Race presented by The Ohio State Wexner Medical Center, at 8 a.m. on Saturday, June 20. The 9.71k The Race running and walking event will feature three events: 9.71k Run / Walk (6.03 miles), The Fan 5K Run / Walk (3.1 miles) and the Common Man Mile Walk.
