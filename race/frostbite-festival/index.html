About

The Frostbite Festival runs in early December each year, so you never know what kind of weather we'll get! There are two options for these out-and-back races: the 2 mile and 10 mile race. The races start simultaneously at 9:00 a.m. This is the final race of the season and the 10 mile race is last leg of the SRRC Triple Crown Challenge and Abe's 50K Throwdown!
