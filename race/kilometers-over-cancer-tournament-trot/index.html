About

The Kilometers Over Cancer Tournament Trot is a one-mile run and walk around the Greensboro Coliseum complex in Greensboro, North Carolina, now staged with a fitness festival. It raises funds for earlier breast cancer detection, and entrants pick an ACC school to compete for an annual cup.
