About

The Semi Marathon de Bolbec is a long-standing running event set in the heart of the Pays de Caux region. For 46 years, this race has been a beloved gathering for passionate runners, featuring a challenging and authentic course that showcases the local landscape. The event is known for its hilly terrain, warm welcome, and family-friendly atmosphere that attracts runners of all levels.
