Course

A 100-mile point-to-point along the Thames Path from Richmond New Waterfront in south-west London to Queen's College Sports Ground in Oxford. The route is flat and fast, roughly 75% groomed trail and 25% paved, with about 560 m of total climb. At least 13 aid stations serve drinks, gels and food, with hot meals at Henley and Goring. The limit is 30 hours.

About

A non-stop 100-mile ultra along the Thames Path towpath, one of Centurion Running's point-to-point classics, following the river from the edges of London out into rural Oxfordshire.
