About

Explore the picturesque Vallée de la Lys as you take part in the Foulées de la Vallée de la Lys, a beloved running event that winds through the stunning French countryside. This scenic race offers runners of all levels the chance to challenge themselves on a route that showcases the region's natural beauty, making it a must-do for any runner seeking a unique and memorable experience.
