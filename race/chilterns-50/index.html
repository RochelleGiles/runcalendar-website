Course

All distances start and finish at Henley Showground and loop through the Chilterns on historic trails, rolling hills and nature reserves. The 50 km carries about 920 m of ascent, the 25 km about 500 m and the 10 km about 235 m. Marquee rest stops with catering, seating and medical cover come roughly every 12.5 km. Participants may walk, jog or run.

About

Explore the picturesque Chiltern Hills at the Chilterns 50, a challenging ultramarathon through one of the most scenic regions of southern England. This point-to-point race takes runners on a scenic journey through rolling countryside, ancient woodlands, and charming villages, offering a true test of endurance and a chance to immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the Chilterns.
