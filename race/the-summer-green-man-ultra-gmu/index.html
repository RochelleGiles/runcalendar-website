About

The Summer Green Man Ultra comes in two formats – the 45 mile distance and the 30 mile distance. Both races are held on the same course on the same day and have the same checkpoints and finish, just with differing start locations. The event offers a prestigious GMU buckle for runners who complete both the Summer and Winter 45 mile events in the same year.
