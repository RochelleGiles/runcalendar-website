About

Join us at the YMCA of the Rockies in Granby for the 12th annual Snow Mountain Ranch Trail Running Festival. This event features 4 races with 2 different "challenges". Races will include a Half Marathon, 10K, 5K, and 50K. Challenge yourself with our popular "Moosehead Challenge" and "Baby Moose Challenge" by completing multiple events in a single day. Regardless of distance... these courses keep all runners surrounded by the changing colors, rolling hills, and picturesque views, making this location and courses one of the most beautiful courses we have to offer.
