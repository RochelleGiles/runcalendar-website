Course

The course starts at Victoria Lake in North Hagley Park, Christchurch, and runs on the park's paths. Participants park at the Botanic Gardens car park and cross the Armagh Street bridge to reach the start tent. The 5 km and 10 km runs and the 5 km walk share the same route.

About

The Zonta Yellow Rose Run & Walk in Christchurch's North Hagley Park offers runners and walkers a scenic 5km or 10km route through the city's beautiful green spaces. This annual event celebrates women's empowerment and raises funds for the Zonta organization's local and global initiatives, making it a meaningful and enjoyable experience for the whole community.
