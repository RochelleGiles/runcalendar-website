About

Trail di Monaccia is a trail running event in Monacia-d'Aullène, a village in Corse-du-Sud known for its traditional stone 'orii' shepherd shelters. Courses of 9K and 15K cross the surrounding Corsican countryside, with an 8.5K walking option.
