Course

All three distances start and finish at Los Poblanos Fields Open Space in Albuquerque's North Valley, with the Sandia Mountains and the Rio Grande valley as the backdrop. The race is timed to coincide with the Balloon Fiesta, so runners may see hot air balloons overhead, though the organisers note this depends on the weather.

About

The Great Balloon Chase sends runners out at dawn during Albuquerque, New Mexico's Balloon Fiesta season, with 5K, 10K and half marathon distances through the city's North Valley.
