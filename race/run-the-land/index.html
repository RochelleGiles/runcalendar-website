Course

Road courses through Cleveland and neighbouring suburbs, starting and finishing at Great Lakes Brewery on Market Avenue. Routes are marked, easy to follow and keep crossings to a minimum, with runners on sidewalks and obeying traffic signals. Aid stations sit every three miles on the marathon, 20-mile and half marathon.

About

This is a fully supported long run event featuring an East Side vs West Side rivalry challenge, with courses highlighting Cleveland and surrounding suburbs. It offers timed races using RaceJoy GPS tracking, aid stations every 3 miles, and finishes with craft beer while benefiting Greater Cleveland charities.
