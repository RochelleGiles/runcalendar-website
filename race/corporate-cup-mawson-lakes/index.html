Course

Lapped course following the path around the lake beside Mawson Lakes Boulevard, starting and finishing on the north bank at the corner of Mawson Lakes Boulevard and Main Street. One lap is 2.25 km and two laps 4.5 km. Timing cards are mandatory at this venue; toilets are at the nearby shopping centre.

About

The Corporate Cup Mawson Lakes Run/Walk follows the path around the lake beside Mawson Lakes Boulevard in Adelaide. Part of the Corporate Cup Series, it offers a 2.25km (one lap) and 4.5km (two laps) run/walk, open to individuals and teams of five to seven people.
