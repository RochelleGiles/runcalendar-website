Course

A Boston-certified marathon winding along the Spokane River, through pine forest and onto the paved Centennial Trail, with half marathon, 10K, 5K and a marathon team relay alongside. Race day base is North Bank Park. The marathon and relay start at 7:00am, the half at 8:00am, the 10K at 9:00am and the 5K at 9:45am.

About

A marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K in Spokane, Washington, following the Spokane River Centennial Trail past Riverfront Park. The route traces the river through the heart of the city.
