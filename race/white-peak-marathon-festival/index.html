Course

Linear marathon from Thorpe to Cromford Meadows on converted rail trails, run on firm compacted limestone and cinder. The route follows the Tissington Trail north to Parsley Hay, then the undulating High Peak Trail, joining the half marathon course at Friden. It closes with a steep mile-long drop to High Peak Junction and a canal towpath finish. Registration and parking are at Cromford Wharf, with coaches to the start.

About

Escape the crowds and explore the stunning White Peak trails in Derbyshire during the annual White Peak Marathon Festival. This off-road running event offers a challenging Marathon, scenic Half Marathon, and fast-paced 10km, all set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Peak District National Park. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply want to experience the thrill of trail running, this festival has something for every runner.
