About

This new event is an out-and-back 10 mile trail run along the picturesque River Wye starting in Monmouth and heading towards Symonds Yat passing the iconic Biblins Rope Bridge. Ideal for those wishing to step up from running a 10k or training for a spring half marathon. Beginner Friendly with a pancake flat course.
