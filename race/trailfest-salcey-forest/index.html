Course

Woodland laps in Salcey Forest, Northamptonshire, with all distances starting and finishing at the forest. The route follows well-maintained pathways of crushed stone and trodden forest floor with some grassland that softens after rain, avoiding pavement apart from two road crossings on the longer distances. Cupless event — runners carry their own containers.

About

Explore the picturesque trails of Salcey Forest at the TrailFest, offering a range of distances from 10km to a challenging 50km Ultra Marathon. This scenic event near Northampton provides a unique opportunity for runners to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the British countryside. Whether you're seeking a personal best or a scenic adventure, the TrailFest Salcey Forest has something for every trail enthusiast.
