About

This nighttime, glow-in-the-dark 5K benefits the Florida Disaster Foundation, providing direct assistance, funding, and support in disaster response, recovery, and relief efforts for natural emergencies. Challenge Coins are awarded to top male and female participants in age categories: 14 and younger, 15-29, 30-49, and 50 and older.
