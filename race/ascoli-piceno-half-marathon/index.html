About

The Ascoli Piceno Half Marathon is a scenic and historic running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Marche region in Italy. Runners will wind through the charming streets and medieval architecture of Ascoli Piceno, a picturesque town known for its rich cultural heritage and vibrant local community. This race offers an unforgettable experience for athletes seeking a challenging yet rewarding half marathon adventure.
