Course

Both distances run entirely on trails at Turkey Mountain Urban Wilderness, a 300-acre park of post oak, blackjack oak and hickory woodland south of Tulsa. Start and finish are at the lower parking lot on S Elwood Ave. The 5-mile course is technical and challenging; the 2-mile is less technical. Starts are in three pace-based waves.

About

Escape from Turkey Mountain is a trail race through the Turkey Mountain Urban Wilderness in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Runners choose a 2 mile or 5 mile route across rugged, wooded singletrack close to downtown.
