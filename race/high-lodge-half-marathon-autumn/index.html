Course

A circular trail half marathon through the heart of Thetford Forest, starting and finishing at High Lodge near Brandon. Underfoot is hard-packed trail, sand and grassy footpath through forest. This is a cupless race, with fresh drinking water available on course to refill your own bottle.

About

The High Lodge Half Marathon runs through Thetford Forest near Brandon in Suffolk, following forest trails around Forestry England's High Lodge visitor site.
