About

Trans Scania is a unique running event that takes participants on a scenic journey through the stunning landscapes of southern Sweden. Winding through the picturesque region of Scania, this race offers runners a chance to explore the area's rich history and natural beauty. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or a recreational runner, Trans Scania promises an unforgettable experience for all.
