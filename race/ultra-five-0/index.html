About

Explore the rugged beauty of New Zealand's Hunua Ranges at the Ultra Five-0, a challenging 50km ultramarathon trail run. This stunning event takes adventurous runners through lush forests, over rolling hills, and past cascading waterfalls, offering an unforgettable experience amidst the country's breathtaking natural landscapes.
