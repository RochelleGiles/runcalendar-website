Course

Starts and finishes at Wesley Bolin Memorial Plaza in front of the Arizona State Capitol in downtown Phoenix. The 5K, 10K and 15K run city streets past the Arizona Financial Theatre, the Sandra Day O'Connor Courthouse and the Footprint Center, through a plaza of war and state memorials. The 5K starts at 7:25 am, the 10K and 15K at 8:35.

About

The SKECHERS Hot Chocolate Run brings a 15K and 5K through downtown Phoenix, with every finisher rewarded with a signature chocolate fondue station and toppings bar at the finish line.
