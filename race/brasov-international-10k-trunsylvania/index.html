Course

A flat, fast road 10K through the streets of Brasov, run as part of the Brasov Running Festival. The elite women's and men's fields start separately in the morning and the open race follows later. The festival weekend also carries a 5K, a 3K fun run and children's races over 800 m, 1.2 km and 3 km.

About

tRUNsylvania 10K is an Elite Label certified race by World Athletics and ranks 6th globally among competitive road races, holding 3rd place worldwide in the 10K category. The event features one of the world's fastest courses, attracting top international athletes to Brasov annually. The flat and fast course is perfect for setting records, while also inspiring thousands of running enthusiasts who transform the city into a global athletics stage.
