About

Take a 3.1 mile tour of Findlay, Ohio on foot to celebrate our community in the one and only Flag City, USA. There'll be something for everyone, including a post-race beer from Findlay Brewing Co. (21 & older), onsite physical therapy from PT Link and activities for the kids by way of the Findlay YMCA!
