Course

Start and finish are at Werribee Park Mansion. The half marathon includes a flight of about 40 stairs roughly 500m from the finish, so it is not suitable for prams or wheelchairs. Drink stations sit at approximately every 2.5km. The half marathon starts at 8:15am with an 11:00am course cut-off.

About

The Werribee Mansion Run offers a unique opportunity to explore the majestic grounds of Werribee Park Mansion's heritage estate. Runners and walkers of all levels can enjoy a scenic course that combines historical charm with athletic challenge, featuring multiple distance options through a picturesque landscape.
