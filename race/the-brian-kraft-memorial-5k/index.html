About

The Brian Kraft Memorial 5K is a flat, USATF-certified road race on the parkways around Lake Nokomis in Minneapolis, run on Memorial Day morning from the Nokomis Community Center. It is owned and operated by USATF Minnesota and draws a competitive field alongside local club runners.
