About

Le Trail des Rochers is a night trail race in Nogent-sur-Oise, Hauts-de-France, starting and finishing at the Château des Rochers. Runners choose 30k, 15k or 10k loop courses through the forests of Nogent-sur-Oise and Montataire, with FFA Trail Bronze certification and staffed aid stations.
