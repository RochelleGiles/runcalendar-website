Course

A last-one-standing backyard ultra: runners complete a 6.7km lap every hour, on the hour, until a single runner remains. The loop runs through commercial pine forest near Hanmer Springs.

About

The Hanmer Springs Backyard Ultra is a grueling last-one-standing endurance race set in the stunning landscapes of Canterbury, New Zealand. Runners will test their limits as they complete laps of an undisclosed loop, with the last person standing declared the winner of this unique challenge.
