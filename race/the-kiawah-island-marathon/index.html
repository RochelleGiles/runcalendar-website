About

The Kiawah Island Marathon and Half Marathon start and finish in West Beach Village and tour the island's paved roads past maritime forests, marshes and island homes. The December race starts at 8 a.m. and is staged from Kiawah Island Golf Resort, with a limited number of entries available each year.
