About

The Ekiden du Stade Français in Paris is a unique and historic running event that brings together teams of runners to tackle a challenging relay course through the heart of the city. Steeped in French running tradition, this event offers an unparalleled experience for endurance athletes seeking a true test of teamwork and perseverance on the iconic Parisian streets.
