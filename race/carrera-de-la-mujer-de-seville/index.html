About

The Carrera De La Mujer De Seville is an annual running event that takes place in the capital city of Andalusia, Spain. The event attracts over 6,000 participants who complete the 6.6 km course through the streets of Seville. The race celebrates women's empowerment and features a post-race fitness festival.
