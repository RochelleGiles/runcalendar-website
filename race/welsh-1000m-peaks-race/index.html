Course

A bee-line mountain race from the coast at the head of the Conwy valley to the summit of Yr Wyddfa/Snowdon, climbing through steep cwms and over rocky summits and technical ridges. The ground varies from rough boulder and rock to runnable grassy slopes and pitched stone paths. Long and short courses are offered.

About

The Welsh 1000m Peaks Race starts at sea level in Llanfairfechan and climbs over the Carneddau range to the summit of Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon), Wales's highest mountain, before descending the other side. It is one of the oldest mountain races in Britain.
