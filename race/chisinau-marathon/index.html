About

The Chisinau Marathon is a premier running event in the capital city of Moldova. Runners can expect a scenic course through the historic streets and parks of Chișinău, showcasing the city's vibrant culture and architecture. Whether you're an elite athlete or a casual runner, this event offers an unforgettable experience for all participants.
