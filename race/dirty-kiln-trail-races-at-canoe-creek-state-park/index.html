Course

Trail races in Canoe Creek State Park at Hollidaysburg, mixing wide paths with winding singletrack, stream crossings and, as the organiser puts it, lots of mud. The short course climbs Moore's Hill and circles the lake; the long course covers the park a second time on hillier half-marathon terrain. Both start and finish at 205 Canoe Creek Road.

About

The Dirty Kiln Trail Races, now past its 13th running, are brought to you by Allegheny Trailrunners, Inc. at Canoe Creek State Park, named one of Pennsylvania's 'Twenty Must-See State Parks' for its industrial history, wildlife, and recreation.
