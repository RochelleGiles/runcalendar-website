Course

Both races run on County Highway 30A through the coastal 30A communities on flat paved road. The half starts at 7:00 AM and the 5K at 7:30 AM, the 5K as an out-and-back. The half has water and Gatorade at five stations plus the finish, the 5K at two. Roads reopen at 10:30 AM, after which remaining runners move to an adjacent paved path.

About

The 24th Annual SEASIDE SCHOOL™ Half Marathon + 5K Race Weekend is hosted by SEASIDE® and benefits the SEASIDE SCHOOL FOUNDATION™, supporting educational initiatives at SEASIDE NEIGHBORHOOD SCHOOL™ and Seacoast Collegiate High School. The main events feature a USATF-certified half marathon and 5K on a scenic out-and-back course along Florida’s Scenic Highway 30A.
