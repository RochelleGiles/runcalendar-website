Course

Flat 5K on scenic roads through the Lordship section of Stratford, starting and finishing at the Little Pub picnic lot with views over Long Island Sound. The field is capped at 250 runners.

About

This flat 5K winds through Stratford's Lordship neighborhood along the coast, benefiting Make-A-Wish Connecticut. Adults and youth can race the certified distance, while a free kids' fun run rounds out the morning event.
