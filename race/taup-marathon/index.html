Course

Courses run out along the Taupō lakefront and back, built to keep the lake and mountain views in sight for as much of the distance as possible. The marathon and half marathon are both AIMS certified. A kids' dash runs alongside the 5km, 10km, half and full.

About

One of New Zealand's longest-standing events. The views from Taupō's lakefront simply can't be beat. And these courses are built to showcase them at their best. With course options to suit all ages and abilities – and beautiful medals for all finishers – bring your friends and whanau and make some amazing memories together that'll last a lifetime.
