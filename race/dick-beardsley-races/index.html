Course

All races start at Peoples Park, 1324 Peoples Street, Detroit Lakes. The 10K covers 6.2 miles along the Detroit Lake shoreline and is mostly flat; the 5K also follows the lakeshore before returning to the finish. Water and Powerade stations number five on the half marathon, two on the 10K and one on the 5K.

About

The Dick Beardsley Races honor the Detroit Lakes native who famously battled Alberto Salazar in the 1982 Boston Marathon's 'Duel in the Sun.' Runners choose a half marathon, 10K or 5K through the lakes-region town of Detroit Lakes, Minnesota.
