Course

A mountain course at Red River, New Mexico, mixing singletrack, dirt road and short highway sections. The marathon starts and finishes in town, climbing the old stage road to a seven-mile loop on the Enchanted Forest ski trails and up The Face, a steep quarter-mile trailless ski slope. The half descends from the ski area to finish in town, some 800ft lower than its start.

About

The Enchanted Forest Trail Races run through the high-altitude pine forest of the Enchanted Forest Nordic ski area above Red River, New Mexico, with 13-mile and 26-mile options on singletrack and forest roads.
