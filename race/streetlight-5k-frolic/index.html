Course

A 5K on the roads of downtown Concord: south on Union Street to Oakland Avenue, around R Brown Elementary School, then right on Union and back to the finish. Starts and finishes at the Cabarrus County Courthouse. A one-mile fun run is held alongside.

About

An evening running event in Downtown Concord featuring a 5K race and a free one-mile Fun Run, with a festive frolic including inflatables, balloon artists, LED seesaws, and a silent disco with glow-themed activities.
