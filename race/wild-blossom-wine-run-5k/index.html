Course

The course starts and finishes at Wild Blossom Meadery and Winery and follows the path through Dan Ryan Woods. The organiser describes it as a flat course with no hills. The race is chip timed and started in waves through the morning.

About

This annual 5k running and walking event takes place at Wild Blossom Meadery & Winery in Chicago. Participants enjoy a flat, fast course through Dan Ryan Woods and receive a glass of wine at the finish line followed by a post-race party.
