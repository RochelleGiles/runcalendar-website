Boston Marathon

The one you earn — a runner's guide to the oldest and most coveted start line in the sport.

Quick verdict

The oldest annual marathon in the world, and the one most runners spend years earning. Boston is a hard, historic, downhill-then-heartbreak course where the race begins at mile 16. The most meaningful medal in running, on the most tactical course of the majors.

Course at a glance

Net ↓140mDownhill
Mile 20.5Heartbreak
8Towns

Point-to-point through eight towns, Hopkinton to Boylston Street. The opening four miles drop steeply — free speed that quietly wrecks your quads — then rolling miles to Wellesley's scream tunnel at halfway, the four Newton hills from mile 16, Heartbreak Hill cresting at 20.5, and the run home to the most famous pair of turns in running: right on Hereford, left on Boylston.

Where you'll feel it

📍The Newton hills, miles 16–21

Heartbreak Hill is only 27 metres of climbing — it breaks people because of what the first 16 miles did to their legs, not its gradient. Every second you steal on the early downhills is repaid here, with interest.

Best chance to push

Off Heartbreak, miles 21–26 are downhill and roaring — if your quads survived, the run to Hereford and Boylston is the greatest victory lap in the sport.

Three things to know

  1. A qualifying time is the main door. For 2027 that means sub-2:55 (men 18–34) or sub-3:25 (women), and in practice you need 5–8 minutes under your standard to survive the cutoff. The other two doors, as at every major: a charity place with a fundraising target, or an official tour package with the entry built in.
  2. Run the first 4 miles 15–20 seconds per k SLOWER than goal pace — the descent trashes quads and the bill arrives at Newton. Cushioned shoes earn their keep here.
  3. April in Boston is 5–17°C and famously unpredictable — heatwaves, headwinds and sleet have all happened. Prepare for anything.

Race-day weather

Patriots' Day, mid-April: 5–17°C on average and genuinely unpredictable — recent editions have served tailwinds, downpours and heat. Train through everything.

What runners wear

Layers you can shed, and the most cushioned racing shoe you trust — the eccentric downhill pounding is the course's hidden tax. Sunglasses regardless of forecast; the course runs east all day.

Typical finish times

Winners run 2:05–2:10 on the hills. A field of qualifiers means the pack is FAST — the median finisher here would be top-10% anywhere else, rolling in around 3:30–3:50.

Worth the trip?

It is the summit of the sport for amateur runners. Fly for it when you have the time in the bank, or take the package route and go sooner. Until then, let it be the goal that shapes your seasons; that is what Boston is for.
