About

The Reedsburg Area Medical Center Foundation is carrying on the strong tradition that VetFest has had over the past 16 years, simultaneously raising money for local veterans' organizations as well as the RAMC Foundation and its mission of supporting the Reedsburg Area Medical Center and the Reedsburg Area Senior Life Center.
