Course

Starts at River Front Park in North Little Rock and runs through riverside parks and shaded trails. Surfaces are mixed, taking in pavement, crushed granite and occasional dirt path, with water aid stations positioned along the course. Distances run from a kids' dash and one-mile up to the half marathon.

About

A summer half marathon at River Front Park in North Little Rock, Arkansas, starting at 7 a.m. and running through riverside parks and shaded trails on mixed pavement, crushed granite and dirt. Quarter marathon, 5K, one-mile and kids' dash options run alongside it, organised by Bigfoot Running Company.
