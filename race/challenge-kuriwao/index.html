Course

Start and finish are at Clinton Rugby Club on Hillfoot Road, on variable farmland with moderate to steep sections. Surfaces mix formed and unformed farm track, tussock country and rural road. The 21 km and 42 km cross both The Peak (about 620 m) and Three Rocks; the 10 km stays on gentler lower farmland. Aid stations sit at key junctions and runners carry their own hydration.

About

Challenge Kuriwao is a trail running event across variable farmland terrain with steep sections and unformed tracks. The courses offer spectacular views and include challenging routes for runners and mountain bikers.
