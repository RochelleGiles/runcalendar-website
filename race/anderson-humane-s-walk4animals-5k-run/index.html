About

Anderson Humane provides life-changing care for dogs, cats, small mammals and wildlife in need of a second chance, offering food, shelter and medical care. The Walk4Animals & 5K Run, based in Batavia, Illinois, helps fund this work and supports a more humane world for animals and the people who love them.
