Course

Road races starting and finishing at a race village near the heart of downtown Naperville. The half marathon gives a tour of central Naperville, taking in the lively downtown, historic sights and residential neighbourhoods, with a loop around Naperville North High School. The course is relatively flat.

About

This race weekend along Naperville's Riverwalk offers a half marathon, 10K, and 5K through the tree-lined DuPage River corridor and downtown streets. The flat course and October date make it a popular tune-up ahead of fall marathons.
