Course

A trail circuit hugging the hills around Otley, Ilkley and Guiseley, starting at Station Top in Otley and finishing at The Bridge United Reform Church. The route takes in Otley Chevin's Surprise View, the Cow and Calf Rocks above Ilkley and Lindley Wood Reservoir, with 1,700ft of climb on the half, 2,800ft on the 20-mile and 4,800ft on the ultra. Four staffed checkpoints with water and food, plus two timing points. Capped at 200 runners.
