Course

Lapped trail race in the Hill Country State Natural Area, with start and finish at the Group Lodge. The main loop is 25 miles over rugged, rocky terrain with a 5-mile shortcut loop also used as its own race. Distances run from 5 miles to 100 miles, plus ruck divisions.

About

The Cactus Rose Endurance Trail Race takes place in the Hill Country State Natural Area near Bandera, Texas, known as the 'Cowboy Capital of the World.' The technical, rocky trails test runners across distances from 5 miles to 100 miles.
