Course

A point-to-point mixed-terrain route from Mayflower Park at Town Quay, Southampton. The 15 mile finishes at Titchfield Abbey and the marathon continues to Boundary Oak School at Wickham. The route includes a boat crossing of the Hamble at Warsash and passes Netley Abbey and Royal Victoria Country Park.

About

The Big Pilgrimage is a point-to-point race finishing at the historic ruins of Titchfield Abbey in Hampshire, with marathon and 15-mile distances on offer across the Hampshire countryside.
