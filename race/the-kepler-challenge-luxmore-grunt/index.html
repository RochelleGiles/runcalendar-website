Course

Run on the Kepler Track in Fiordland National Park, with both races starting at the Lake Te Anau Control Gates. The 60 km course mixes flat sections, steep uphill climbs and long descents, supported by eleven checkpoints including the finish. The 27 km Luxmore Grunt starts from the same point an hour later.

About

The annual Kepler Challenge follows the 60km Kepler Track circuit, offering runners a challenging trail experience through Fiordland's stunning landscape. The route includes a steady climb to Luxmore Hut, undulating tops with views of South Fiord, and a spectacular descent through diverse terrain.
