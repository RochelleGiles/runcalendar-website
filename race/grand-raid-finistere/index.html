About

The Grand Raid du Finistère celebrates its 4th edition and continues to grow year after year. With six race formats, from the discovery format to the ultra, the event invites runners to explore the raw and contrasting landscapes of the Crozon peninsula: steep coasts, cliffs, pine forests & coastal trail (the famous GR34). Each event is designed as a complete adventure, accessible to all levels and desires. Driven by a warm atmosphere, the unwavering commitment of volunteers, and the support of a loyal audience, the Grand Raid du Finistère has now established itself as a must-attend event for trail running in Brittany.
