Course

A relay across Park City over mixed terrain, from trail to pavement and long climbs to fast downhills, with legs graded from beginner-friendly to expert. The route passes Olympic venues, local landmarks and neighbourhood schools, starting and finishing at The Basin Recreation Fieldhouse.

About

Running with Ed is a 27.6-mile team relay race fundraiser for the Park City Education Foundation that supports local public schools. Teams of 1-8 people take turns running segments of the course, with legs ranging from 1.3 to 5.7 miles.
