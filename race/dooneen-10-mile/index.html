About

The Michal Rejmer memorial Dooneen 10 Mile road race in Limerick, Ireland is a must-do event for runners seeking a scenic challenge. Hosted by the local Dooneen Athletic Club, this annual race honors the legacy of a beloved runner through a course winding through the beautiful Irish countryside. Runners of all abilities will enjoy the welcoming atmosphere and stunning views along this 10-mile journey.
