Course

Starts on Michigan Avenue in front of the Lansing Center and finishes near Grand Avenue at Adado Park. The route takes in Michigan Ave, Grand River Ave, the MSU campus and the Lansing River Trail. All races are chip-timed.

About

Capital City River Run is a road race in Lansing, Michigan, the state capital, held each September. Runners choose between a half marathon and a 5K, with courses passing through the downtown riverfront area.
