About

The FORE! Miler is the Memorial Tournament presented by Workday and benefiting Nationwide Children's Hospital and Eat. Learn. Play. Foundation. The event features a 4-mile tour of Dublin, Ohio, with a finish on the 18th green of the Memorial Tournament presented by Workday.
