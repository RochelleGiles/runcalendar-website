Course

A 5 km loop on hard-packed gravel and asphalt around the perimeter of Princes Park, with an add-on loop under Royal Parade following the old inner circle railway down towards the Zoo and back. Start and finish at Lawn 3, Princes Park. Drink stations with water and electrolyte every 2.5 km.

About

The Sri Chinmoy Princes Park Run in Melbourne, Victoria offers a scenic and serene running experience through the beautiful Princes Park. With 20km, 15km, 10km, and 5km options, this event caters to runners of all levels, providing a challenging yet rewarding opportunity to connect with nature and the running community.
