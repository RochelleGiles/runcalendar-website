About

The Lucerne Marathon is a popular running event that attracts participants from all over Switzerland and the world. The half marathon distance is the most popular, with over 7,000 runners taking part, making it one of the largest half marathons in Switzerland. The scenic course takes runners past the city's famous landmarks, including the Kapellbrücke and the Verkehrshaus der Schweiz, providing a unique and unforgettable running experience.
