Course

The half marathon is a road race down the Spearfish Canyon Scenic Byway beside Spearfish Creek, starting near Savoy. The 5K starts and finishes at Spearfish City Park, running through the campground onto a bike path to the canyon car park before returning via the byway.

About

This signature race is an annual event which benefits abused and neglected children through the Northern Hills Area CASA Program. We serve 8 counties of western South Dakota. Join us and be a hero for the CASA Cause!
