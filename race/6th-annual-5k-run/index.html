About

We invite you to join us for Zoe Ministries' 6th annual RUN4ZOE, in historic downtown Cleveland, Mississippi to raise awareness and resources for our work in Kenya. Runners of all ages, strollers, and dogs are welcome. This is great event for the whole family!
