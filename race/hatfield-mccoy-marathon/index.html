Course

A point-to-point marathon starting at Food City in South Williamson, Kentucky and finishing in Williamson, West Virginia, running a scenic rural Appalachian route that climbs Blackberry Mountain around mile 7. Twenty-one water stops line the course. Two half marathons, a 5K and a kids run share portions of the route.

About

The Hatfield-McCoy Marathon runs through the Tug Valley on the West Virginia-Kentucky border, historic home of the famous Hatfield and McCoy family feud. The event offers a marathon, two half marathons - Blackberry Mountain and River Road - and a 5K through the Appalachian foothills around Williamson, WV.
