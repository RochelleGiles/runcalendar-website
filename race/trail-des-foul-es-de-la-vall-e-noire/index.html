About

Explore the picturesque Vallée Noire region at the annual Les Foulées de la Vallée Noire running event. This scenic race winds through the rolling hills and lush forests of this lesser-known corner of the country, offering a unique challenge for runners of all levels. Whether you're seeking a scenic training run or a competitive challenge, this event promises an unforgettable experience in a beautiful natural setting.
