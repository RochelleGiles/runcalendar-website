Course

All three distances start and finish at Wave Country on Two Rivers Parkway, Nashville, and run entirely on paved road or paved trail. Time limits are 1 hour 30 for the 5K, 1 hour 50 for the 10K and 3 hours 30 for the half marathon, so walkers are catered for.

About

A St. Patrick's Day themed running event offering Half Marathon, 10K, and 5K distances in Nashville, TN. Features chip timing, fun run categories, finisher medals, and supports Special Olympics.
