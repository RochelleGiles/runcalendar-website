Course

Ten loops of the classic Van Cortlandt Park cross-country course after an initial lap of the flats, over rocky, rooty terrain for roughly 2,200 ft of total climbing. Aid stations sit no more than about 1.5 miles apart with water, sports drink, snacks and fruit, and runners may leave personal drop bags on course.

About

The VCP50K is an ultramarathon designed for novices to complete safely while still challenging experienced runners chasing a fast time. The course is ten loops of the classic Van Cortlandt Park cross-country course after an initial lap of the flats, over terrain with some rocks and roots.
