Course

Rocky dirt single-track in Garner State Park at Concan, entered by the special event gate. Distances are built from a shared loop; the 10K lap climbs to the top of Old Baldy Mountain and back for about 1,500 feet of gain, while the 5K runs a modified loop with around 600 feet. Aid sits at the start/finish and at Baldy on the 10K loop, with hot and cold food, water, electrolytes and ice. Runners must carry water.

About

Challenging ascents, epic views, and great vibes await at the Frio Trail Runs. This tough yet scenic course features over 1,400 feet of elevation gain per 6-mile loop, with participants traversing rocky and dirt single-track trails in the beautiful Garner State Park.
