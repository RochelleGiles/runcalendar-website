About

The Tours Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner exploring the stunning Loire Valley region of France. Weaving through the historic city of Tours and its picturesque surrounding countryside, this challenging yet rewarding course offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty and rich cultural heritage of this renowned wine-producing area.
