About

Trail Mix Races is held at Camp Decorah, north of Holmen, Wisconsin, on the Black River in the heart of the Driftless Region. Camp Decorah is the starting point of the Bluffland Traverse, a route running the length of La Crosse County along the Mississippi River bluffs, and the race offers trail running and mountain bike racing through the property's northwoods terrain.
