About

The Wop Challenge Bilbao Bizkaia is an exhilarating running event that takes place in the scenic Basque region of Spain. Runners will be challenged to navigate a unique course that showcases the area's stunning natural landscapes and rich cultural heritage. Whether you're an experienced endurance athlete or a casual runner, this event promises an unforgettable experience.
