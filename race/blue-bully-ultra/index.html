Course

A three-day trail weekend on the slopes and forest trails of Blue Mountain Resort above the Bruce Peninsula. Friday brings the 5 km Stampede, Saturday the 50 km and 25 km Drive plus a kids' race, and Sunday the Round-Up, a three-hour timed event on a 2.5 km loop.

About

The Blue Bully Ultra runs across the ski trails and forests of Blue Mountain Resort in Ontario's Blue Mountains, with 25K and 50K distances plus a shorter 5K. Expect steep climbs and technical descents on the region's highest terrain.
