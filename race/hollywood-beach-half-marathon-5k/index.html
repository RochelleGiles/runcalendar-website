About

The Hollywood Beach Half Marathon & 5k is a scenic beachfront race starting at Hollywood North Beach Park in Hollywood, Florida. The course runs along the iconic Hollywood Beach Broadwalk, featuring pristine beaches, shops, restaurants, and entertainment.
