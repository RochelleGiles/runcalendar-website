About

Pull out your vintage concert t-shirts and join us for a fun event featuring the rock and roll sounds of the 80's, hosted by Rock N Brews at PrairieFire Center in Overland Park! There will be lots of heavy metal and hair bands featured in the soundtrack of this event and Rock N Brews is cooking up a post race breakfast buffet that is sure to rock! All participants receive a heavy metal medal and compete for high voltage age group and overall awards! Cool rock and roll event shirts will be available for only $7. Hearts for RMHC is partnering with us on this one! Don't miss this one!
