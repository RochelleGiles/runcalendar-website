About

Unite To Run is a 5K fundraiser supporting Unite for Health, a non-profit organization committed to reducing maternal and child mortality in underserved rural areas of Africa. It functions as a timed race for elite runners, and a peaceful route for morning walkers, to highlight the importance of community and making a difference.
