Course

Point-to-point along the Jurassic Coast footpath from west to east, starting near Charmouth and finishing at Shell Bay car park, Studland. Terrain ranges from coastal footpath to beaches and tracks with exposed cliff sections, and includes a full circuit of Portland. Checkpoints roughly every 10 km supply water, squash, snacks and filled rolls, with hot food at halfway.

About

The ONER Ultra is a challenging single-stage 85 mile ultramarathon across the legendary Jurassic Coast World Heritage Site. The course involves relentless climbs, dramatic cliffs, technical trails, wild weather, long night sections, and the constant battle against the coastline itself.
