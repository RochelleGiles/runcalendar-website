Course

Fixed-location relay: teams of two to eight rotate through repeated 5K legs to complete 50K, so no support vehicle is needed. Solo 50K and 25K runners cover the same loop. The organiser describes the terrain as flat and gives no aid station detail.

About

The NEWEST day-long relay in the Midwest. The Nebraska 50k Classic Relay requires no van, leg distance is the same for everyone, and the course is flat as a pancake! Teams consist of 2-8 members. Team members will take turns completing a 5k leg totaling a distance of 50k. Walkers are definitely welcome! Wanna fly SOLO? You can do a SOLO 50K or the newly added SOLO 25K. Totally your call. This is a day made for you!
