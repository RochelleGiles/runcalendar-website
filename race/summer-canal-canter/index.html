Course

Both races start at Tiverton and run along the Grand Western Canal Path. The organisers describe the courses as flat and fast, with roughly 45 feet of climb over the 10k and 90 feet over the half marathon.

About

The Summer Canal Canter is a flat half marathon and 10K in Tiverton, run entirely on the Grand Western Canal Path. It raises money for The Friends of the Grand Western Canal.
