Course

Three out-and-back races - 25K, 50K and 50 mile - all starting from St Helena Park in Canal Fulton, Ohio and running along the Ohio & Erie Canal Towpath Trail. Turnarounds are at the edge of Massillon, at Navarre and at Bolivar respectively. The towpath is flat and unsealed, with aid stations spaced along it.

About

The Get Lucky 50/50 Race offers 50-mile, 50k, and 25k distances along the Ohio Erie Canal Towpath Trail. The races start and finish at St. Helena Park in Canal Fulton.
