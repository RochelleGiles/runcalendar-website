About

The Monte Challenge is a thrilling adventure race that combines running, cycling, and off-road mountain climbing in the rugged and scenic landscapes of Donegal, Ireland. Participants will embark on a challenging journey through the wilds, testing their endurance and navigational skills as they conquer the natural terrain. This event promises an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts seeking an exhilarating and rewarding challenge.
