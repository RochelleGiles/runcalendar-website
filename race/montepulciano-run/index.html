About

Montepulciano Run is a three-day running festival set among the beautiful vineyards and olive groves of Tuscany's wine country. The event features both competitive and non-competitive races through the historic Renaissance town center and surrounding countryside, with routes winding through the famous Noble Wine vineyards. Participants can enjoy the stunning architecture, ancient flavors, and beautiful wine cellars of one of Tuscany's most picturesque regions.
