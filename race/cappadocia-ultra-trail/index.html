About

Cappadocia Ultra Trail is a running event that takes place in the stunning Cappadocia region of Turkey. Runners can choose from several team-based distances, including a 119km ultra and a 63km medium trail, allowing them to explore the breathtaking valleys and rock formations of this unique landscape. The event offers a chance to experience the rich culture and natural beauty of Cappadocia while challenging oneself in a team-oriented adventure.
