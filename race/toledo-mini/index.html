About

Toledo's next Running Chapter, born from a dynamic partnership between Toledo Metroparks and Run Toledo. This signature Half Marathon and 10K brings athletes and the community together on a course that showcases the city's remarkable transformation. For the first time, runners will experience the Maumee River and the expanding Glass City Riverwalk as the centerpiece of a major downtown running event, with the Riverwalk nearing 50% completion and emerging as the heart of Toledo's revitalized waterfront.
