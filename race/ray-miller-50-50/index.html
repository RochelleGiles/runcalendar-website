Course

A trail race in Point Mugu State Park in the Santa Monica Mountains, with a large proportion of singletrack and ocean views for much of the route. The 50 mile carries about 14,000ft of climbing; the 50K and 30K each about 6,000ft. Aid stations are spread along the course.
