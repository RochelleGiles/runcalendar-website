Course

A road race based at Vaal Mall on the corner of Rossini Boulevard and Barrage Road in Vanderbijlpark. Four distances are offered — 32km, 21.1km, 10km and 5km — on flat tarmac roads along the Vaal River corridor. The 32km serves as a long training run ahead of the summer marathon season.

About

The ACTOM Turbo Machines Run starts and finishes at the Vaal Mall in Vanderbijlpark, Gauteng, sponsored by engineering firm ACTOM. It offers a 32km, half marathon, 10km, and 5km for runners of all levels.
