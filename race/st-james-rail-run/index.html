Course

Starts and finishes at the St. James Railroad Depot Museum, 301 Tiell Drive. An evening programme of staggered starts: kids half-mile at 6:30pm, one mile at 7:00pm, four-mile walk at 7:15pm and four-mile run at 7:45pm. Held rain or shine.

About

The run/walk is held annually in conjunction with St. James Rail Road Days. This is a fun family event for all ages which is held rain or shine.
