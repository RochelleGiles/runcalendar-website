Course

Club races start and finish at the SBSC Hall in Orleigh Park, West End, on the club's standard riverside course, repeated for the longer distances. Every meeting includes a 5 km plus longer options up to 30 km in peak season. Members and visitors pre-register online from the Monday before each race until 7pm on the Saturday night.

About

Brisbane Road Runners Club's race includes a 20km, 10km and 5km distance. This event is part of a series of events every second Sunday during the year, comprising a range of distances to satisfy all needs and abilities.
