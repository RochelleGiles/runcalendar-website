Course

Out-and-back on Foxton Beach. The start and finish line is on the beach in front of the Surf Club building; participants head north on hard-packed sand to a turnaround refreshment station, with a different turnaround per distance. Race-day registration from 6:30am, staggered starts from 7:00am, and all finishers must be in by 11:00am because of the tide.

About

Explore the picturesque Foxton Beach during the Footprints In The Sand Fun Run, featuring a challenging Half Marathon, 10km, and 5km fun run/walk. This scenic New Zealand event offers runners of all levels the chance to experience the stunning coastal landscapes and enjoy a unique seaside adventure.
