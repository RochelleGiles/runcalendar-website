Course

A point-to-point half marathon on paved road from the Stilson Lot in Wilson to a finish on Spring Gulch Road, reached by shuttle only. The route follows Teton Pass Highway, Main Street and jogging paths before crossing the Snake River pedestrian bridge. Largely flat with about 1,267 ft of gain and a gradual climb over the closing miles. A 5K is also offered.

About

The Grand Teton Half Marathon is a Vacation Races event in Wilson, Wyoming, following a downhill, point-to-point course with views of the Grand Tetons. A 5K is also offered.
