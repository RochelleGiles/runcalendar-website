Course

Run in the North Fruita Desert on world-class single track, starting and finishing across from the 18 Road parking area. All distances open on fast, flowing singletrack before taking on the steep climbs and descents of the Zippity-Do-Da Trail. Water is available at the start/finish and every aid station; the event is cupless, so runners carry their own bottle or bladder.

About

Located in Fruita, Colorado, just a quick drive from the Front Range, this is the ultimate Racecation Adventure!! Known for its world class single track, the trails of Fruita are a hidden gem for trail running. Enjoy sweeping views of the San Juan Mountains and the Colorado National Monument as you run through the high desert. All distances begin on fast, flowing singletrack and work their way to the steep ups and downs of the Zippity-Do-Da Trail. If you love perfect running weather and stunning views, this race is for you!
