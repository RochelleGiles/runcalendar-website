Course

Backyard ultra format: a 6.7056 km loop started on the hour, every hour, until one runner remains. The loop combines road, grass, trail and beach sections, is fully marked and has minimal elevation change. Part of the route runs through bush with no street lighting, so headlamps are required overnight. Start and finish at Doug Jennings Park, Main Beach.

About

The inaugural Gold Coast Backyard Ultra challenges runners to push their limits on the sun-soaked beaches of Main Beach, Australia. This unique event tasks participants with completing a 6.7km loop continuously until only one runner remains, testing their mental and physical fortitude in a battle against the clock and each other.
