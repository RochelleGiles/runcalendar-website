About

The 4th Annual Elizabeth PBA #4 Leprechaun 5K Run/Walk benefits the Elizabeth P.A.L. and features bib pickup at Warinanco Sports Center, a holiday hat for the first 300 participants, live bagpiper music, and post-race refreshments.
