About

The Festival Des Templiers is a trail running event set in the scenic Grand Causses region, offering runners a unique experience in a preserved natural landscape. The event celebrates the beauty of the local terrain, with the iconic Pouncho summit as a backdrop. Participants can immerse themselves in the local environment while enjoying a challenging and scenic trail running experience.
