Course

Runs over North Menan Butte near Menan, Idaho, one of the world's largest dormant tuff volcanoes, climbing to the rim and dropping through the caldera. Surfaces are dirt, mud, sand and slick rock, with views of the Tetons and the Snake River. The 30K covers both volcanoes and the 55K is the full ultra distance.

About

Spitfire Ultra Trail Challenge invites runners to conquer North Menan Butte's rugged volcanic terrain with sweeping Teton views. Race options include 5k, 12k, 30k, and 55k distances on diverse trails featuring sand, volcanic rock, and slick rock.
