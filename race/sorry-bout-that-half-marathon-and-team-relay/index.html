Course

One large clockwise loop starting and finishing at the Mission Valley Aquatic Center in Polson, with an uphill start, a long downhill through the middle miles and an uphill finish. It runs on bike paths and roads through town with no road closures, so traffic is live. Two aid stations and one portable toilet on course.

About

The Sorry 'Bout That Half Marathon takes place in January in Polson, Montana, at the southern tip of Flathead Lake -- a deliberately wintry test that its tongue-in-cheek name pokes fun at.
