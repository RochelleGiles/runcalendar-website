About

The Marathon des terroirs du Valais BCVS is more than just a race - it's a unique experience that celebrates the spirit of the Valais region. Featuring festive aid stations and a carnival-like atmosphere, this marathon offers runners an unforgettable journey through the scenic vineyards of Switzerland.
