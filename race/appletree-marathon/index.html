Course

A flat, fast single-loop Boston qualifying course starting and finishing on Officers Row in front of the Grant House at Fort Vancouver National Historic Site. The route passes Fort Vancouver, the Army Barracks, Pearson Airport and the historic Old Apple Tree, and runs along the Columbia River. A 5K Sunset Run is held on the Saturday evening.
