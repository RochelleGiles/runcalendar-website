About

Ultra-Trail Snowdonia by UTMB is a UTMB World Series race based in Llanberis, with distances from 25K up to 100 miles through Eryri (Snowdonia) National Park. Courses climb into the mountains around Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon), Wales's highest peak.
