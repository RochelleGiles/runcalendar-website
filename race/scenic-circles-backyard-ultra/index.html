Course

A backyard ultra at Centennial Reserve in Timaru, starting at 10am. Runners repeat a 6.7056 km loop on shared public tracks — open throughout to walkers, dog walkers and mountain bikers — and must be back inside the hour to start the next lap. Self-supported: there are no aid stations and runners bring all their own food and drink.

About

The Backyard Ultra format is simple, run 6.7056 kms every hour, on the hour until you are the last person standing. All you need to do is be in the corral at the start of every hour for the bell and be back in time for the next bell. The winner is the last person to complete a loop. This is a self-supported event in that you must provide all your own food and fluid requirements. There is no aid station provided.
