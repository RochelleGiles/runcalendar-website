About

Get ready to run for your kolaches, Houston, because the 8th Annual 'Kolache Factory 5K/10K/Kids Dash' is back and better than ever! Now with six consecutive sold-out annual events - join us as we pay tribute to one of Texas' defining pastries - the kolache! Whether you're a seasoned runner, just starting out or even walking the distance, we've got the perfect race for you.
