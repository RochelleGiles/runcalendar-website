About

The Trail Des Alpes Maritimes is an annual trail running event that takes place in the beautiful Sospel region since 2008. Runners can choose from three different courses, ranging from 11km to 21km, each offering stunning views of the surrounding mountains and valleys. The event is known for its challenging terrain, scenic routes, and welcoming atmosphere, making it a must-do for trail running enthusiasts.
