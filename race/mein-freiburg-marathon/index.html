Course

A loop through Freiburg im Breisgau starting at the Seepark and running out through Dietenbachpark and the Wiehre district, along the Dreisam river and into the old town past the Münster cathedral. Around 200 m of climb, with live bands at the aid stations along the way.

About

Mein Freiburg Marathon is a rolling course through Freiburg im Breisgau, the gateway city to the Black Forest. Marathon, half marathon and 10K distances share the same start and finish in the city centre.
