About

Le VAULX TOUR TRAIL is above all a great human adventure, organized by the association 'La VAULX TOUR TRAIL', from the association 'Ecole en Pays de Vaulx'. A bit crazy bet of parents for their children! The idea was born within the association to drive a renewal, a dynamic on the territory through values such as sport and nature. This event is meant to be unifying. GATHER & SHARE are our motivations!
