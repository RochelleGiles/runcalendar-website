Course

A flat, fast lapped course through the Redlynch Valley, starting and finishing at Glenoma Park in Cairns. The 30k and 10k run multiple laps on fast, flat and largely shaded paths with good spectator access. Distances are 50k, 30k, 10k and a 5k, alongside 1k and 2k children's events.

About

The Cairns 50 Community Running Festival is a two-day running celebration featuring multiple events through the scenic Redlynch Valley. The premier event is a challenging 50km ultra-marathon that takes runners through stunning rainforest and picturesque cane fields. The festival offers a variety of distances to accommodate all fitness levels and includes a dedicated Family Funday, making it a true community event.
