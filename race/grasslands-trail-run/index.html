Course

Multi-loop trail race on the colour-coded trail network of the LBJ National Grasslands, starting and finishing at TADRA Point near Decatur. The loops roll through pine and oak forest and open grassland, with about 45 miles of non-overlapping trail. The race is cupless, so runners carry their own containers to refill at aid stations.

About

The Grasslands Trail Run is an ultra-distance trail race held in the Lyndon B. Johnson National Grasslands near Decatur, Texas. Distances range from 15K up to 100 miles, taking runners across the area's grassland and forest trail network.
