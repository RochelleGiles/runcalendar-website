About

The Mountain Do Desafio Pedra Branca is a trail running event where you will face the Morro da Pedra Branca on high-altitude routes. Forests, trails, dirt roads, and lots of climbing - you choose the distance of 25km, 15km, 9km, or 4km to challenge yourself!
