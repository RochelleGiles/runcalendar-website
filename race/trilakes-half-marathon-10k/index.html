About

The TriLakes Half Marathon & 10K on 12 December 2026 is based in Caherlistrane, County Galway. Both routes are set in the surrounds of the famous Knockma Duathlon Hill - don't worry it isn't part of the event! The start line is 0.5km away from the registration and finish line at Caherlistrane GAA Grounds. The races are chip timed.
