Course

Out-and-back along the Grand Western Canal towpath from Sampford Peverell in mid Devon, turning at Tiverton. The surface is hard-packed gravel canal path and the route is exceptionally flat. Water stops at the Tiverton turnaround and midway out, with an extra stop at halfway for marathon runners before their second lap.

About

Experience the serene beauty of Mid Devon's winding waterways at the Grand Canal Canter Autumn edition. This scenic running event offers a Marathon, Half Marathon, and 10K, inviting runners of all levels to explore the region's stunning natural landscapes along the historic Grand Canal. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply seeking an unforgettable outdoor adventure, the Grand Canal Canter promises an unforgettable autumn challenge.
