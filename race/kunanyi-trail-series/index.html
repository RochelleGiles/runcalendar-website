Course

The Cascade Slide round is a loop starting and finishing at the Cascade Brewery Bar gardens in South Hobart, on the lower slopes of kunanyi. The Enduro and Sport routes use the Cascade Track, Main Fire Trail, Slides Track, McRobies Gully and Jurassic Shark trails through woodland and rainforest gullies. The Explorer course uses Old Farm Road and the Golden Gully ridgeline.

About

A series of trail rounds staged on the lower slopes and gullies of kunanyi, each based at a different trailhead around Hobart. Courses run on fire trail and single track through woodland, rainforest gully and ridgeline, with Enduro, Sport and shorter Explorer options at each round. Season passes cover the full series.
