Course

Held in Sleepy Hollow State Park, 2,600 acres of hardwood forest and prairie grassland around the 410-acre Lake Ovid. The route circles Lake Ovid and several smaller lakes on lightly hiked and biked ground, mostly grass-covered, with some elevation change.

About

The Legend Trail Run is a trail race in Laingsburg, Michigan, held each October. It offers 5 Mile, 10 Mile and Half Marathon distances, drawing trail runners from across mid-Michigan for a fall race on local trails.
