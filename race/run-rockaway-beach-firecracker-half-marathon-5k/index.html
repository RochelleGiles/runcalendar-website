About

The RUN ROCKAWAY BEACH FIRECRACKER HALF MARATHON & 5K is a festive Independence Day race held on the scenic Rockaway Beach boardwalk. Participants can choose between a half marathon or 5K, with bib pickup at 6:30 AM and start at 8:00 AM at Beach 94th Street.
