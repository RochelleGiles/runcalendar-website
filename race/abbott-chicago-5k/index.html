About

The Abbott Chicago 5K is a 3.1-mile scenic run through downtown Chicago, past iconic landmarks, serving as a shakeout run for the Bank of America Chicago Marathon weekend. It starts in Grant Park with a 45-minute time limit at a 15-minute per mile pace, open to runners 8 years and older.
