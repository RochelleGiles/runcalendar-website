About

The Gopherdillo Trail Run is a trail race at Lake Manatee State Park in Bradenton, FL, featuring 5K and 10K distances through diverse landscapes of sand, grass, and paved roads. Runners experience scenic views of Lake Manatee, local wildlife like armadillos and gopher tortoises, and native birds amid lush Florida vegetation.[1]
