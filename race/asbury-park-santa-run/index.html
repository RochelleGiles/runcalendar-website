About

The Asbury Park Santa Run is a festive 5K event where participants dress as Santa and run or walk through the streets of Asbury Park in support of the local non-profit Lunch Break. Runners finish into one of four after parties at legendary music venues, with registration including a Santa suit, chip-timed results, and after-party access for those 21+.
