Course

The half marathon is point-to-point on sealed road down the Cardrona Valley, starting about 21 km up Cardrona Valley Road from Wanaka and finishing at Pembroke Park in Wanaka. Water and electrolyte stations sit at 5 km, halfway and 5 km from the finish. The field is capped at 500.

About

Explore the stunning Southern Lakes region of New Zealand at the annual Southern Lakes Half Marathon. This challenging yet picturesque course starts just below the Cardrona Ski Field entrance and winds its way through breathtaking alpine landscapes, finishing in the charming town of Pembroke Park. Whether you're a seasoned runner or looking for a scenic challenge, this event offers an unforgettable running experience.
