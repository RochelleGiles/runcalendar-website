About

Bull City Running stages this relay on the dirt oval at Occoneechee Speedway in Hillsborough, North Carolina. Teams of two or four cover 20 laps between them, handing off in any order and after any number of laps rather than running set legs. Entries are taken in all-male, all-female and co-ed divisions.
