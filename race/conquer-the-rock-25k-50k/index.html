About

So, you wish to walk among the clouds? Then lace up, for this journey takes you over the mighty backs of Pinnacle Mountain and the legendary Table Rock, two of South Carolina's tallest guardians of stone. These trails? They ain't gentle. They're steep, rocky, and relentless—but also stunningly beautiful. Every switchback, every climb, every gasping moment rewards you with panoramic views most only dream of. And when your legs tremble at the summit, the mountains whisper: You are strong. You are worthy.
