About

The 5K Run, Walk, and Stroller Roll is an event for the ENTIRE family. It's all about creating lasting memories- therefore, a stroller or child is NOT required to participate. We encourage you to walk, run, or roll at your own pace, so invite a family member or a friend to join in.
