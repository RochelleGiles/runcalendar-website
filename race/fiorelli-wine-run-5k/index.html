Course

A two lap route around the vineyards at Fiorelli Winery near Bradenton. The going is cross-country style on uneven off-road ground, suitable for both runners and walkers. Entrants finish with a glass of wine, a commemorative glass and a medal.

About

The Fiorelli Wine Run 5K is a unique event that combines a scenic 5K run/walk through a winery with a refreshing glass of wine at the finish line. Participants will receive a commemorative t-shirt, wine glass, and finisher medal.
