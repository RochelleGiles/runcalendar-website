Carman's Fun Run Melbourne

Melbourne, Victoria · Australia
Fun runUrbanHalf Marathon · 10K · 5KEntries open

Melbourne's bayside fun run: closed roads past the Brighton bathing boxes, a half to Black Rock, and a 5K walk the dog can join.

Quick verdict

Melbourne's bayside fun run: four distances on closed roads along Beach Road, past the Brighton bathing boxes, in aid of Breast Cancer Network Australia. The 10K is the biggest race, about 2,700 strong, and the half runs one flat out-and-back to Black Rock. A women's run for three decades, open to everyone since 2022.

Course at a glance

6:30Half starts
8:3010K starts
km 4Bathing boxes

Out-and-backs on closed roads, starting on Head Street and finishing inside Elsternwick Park. Every route runs down St Kilda Street to the sea at Middle Brighton, where the 5K turns at 2.5 km. The 10K carries on along the Esplanade past the Dendy Street bathing boxes and Green Point to turn on Beach Road at Hampton. The half keeps going past Sandringham Harbour and the Red Bluff cliffs to turn at Black Rock, around 10.5 km, and comes home the same way.

Where you'll feel it

📍The return from Black Rock, kilometres 11 to 20

There is no hill to speak of. The half's test is the way home: ten kilometres back up Beach Road, open to the bay, with the sun up and the breeze building. Half runners also need to pass 16 km by 9:55 to stay on the road.

Best chance to push

The last kilometre and a half, off the bay and up St Kilda Street into Elsternwick Park, is flat and straight. On the 10K, the turn at Hampton puts the bathing boxes and the city skyline ahead of you all the way home.

Three things to know

  1. Entries stay open online until 30 minutes before each race, so there is no rush. Name transfers cost a fee and close at midnight on the Friday before; distance changes close the same day. No refunds, but withdraw by late January and you keep a credit.
  2. Collect your race pack at Carman's in Huntingdale on Thursday or Friday, at the park on Saturday, or on race morning from 5:30. The Sandringham line stops at Elsternwick, a ten-minute walk, but early Sunday trains are sparse. Bag drop is in the village.
  3. Water every two to three kilometres on the half, sports drink at 6.5 and 16 km, and pacers from 1:25 to 2:20 for the half and 40 to 70 minutes for the 10K. The 5K walk at ten is untimed, and dogs on leads are welcome.

Race-day weather

Mild and early: about 15°C for the half's 6:30 start, in the half-light, and the high teens by nine. February afternoons reach the mid-twenties, the bay breeze builds through the morning, and it rains on about one day in six.

What runners wear

Singlet and shorts, a cap for the return leg and sunscreen before you leave home: the course is open to the bay with little shade. The organiser asks you to leave the headphones at home.

Typical finish times

No course records are kept, and the half's route is new. The 2026 winners ran 1:06:27 and 1:13:06 for the half and 30:32 and 36:57 for the 10K. Half of each field finished inside 1:55 for the half, 61 minutes for the 10K and 34 for the 5K.

Worth the trip?

Yes, if you are in Melbourne: a flat morning on closed bay roads with a cause attached, a distance for everyone in the house and a walk the dog can join. It is a community fun run rather than a destination race; come for the bathing boxes and the village, not the clock.
