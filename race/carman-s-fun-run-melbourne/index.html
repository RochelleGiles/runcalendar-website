Course

A bayside road event starting and finishing at Elsternwick Park in Brighton, running along Melbourne's foreshore. The half marathon starts at 6:30am, the 10K at 8:30am and the 5K at 9:30am, and there is also a Pink Lady 5K walk. Proceeds support Breast Cancer Network Australia.

About

The Carman's Fun Run is back in Melbourne, Australia, supporting the Breast Cancer Network Australia (BCNA) with a vibrant and community-focused event. Runners of all levels can enjoy the scenic course and celebrate wellness while raising funds for a meaningful cause. This annual tradition offers a chance to get active, connect with others, and make a difference.
