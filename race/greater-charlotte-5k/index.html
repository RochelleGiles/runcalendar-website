Course

A single-loop 5K run/walk on the packed gravel cross-country course on the grounds of Historic Rural Hill, Huntersville. Start and finish sit near the cultural centre where the post-race activities are held.

About

The pre-race activities (packet pick-up, race night registration, company gathering etc) and post-race party will take place at the main picnic shelter and adjacent grass located within the confines of Clanton Park. We will have plenty of banquet and picnic table seating for all teams and team members.
