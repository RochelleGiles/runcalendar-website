Course

The half marathon starts at 9th Street and the boardwalk, runs south through Ocean City toward Longport and returns north to finish at the Music Pier. Roads and boardwalk throughout, with a roped runner's lane on the boardwalk section. Seven water stations give ten drink opportunities, with gels at stations four and six. The 10 Mile follows the same route turning at 23rd Street; the 5K is an out-and-back entirely on the boardwalk.
