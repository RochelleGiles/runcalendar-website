Course

Held at Cauble Creek Vineyard near Salisbury. The 5K run/walk has two water stations plus wine tastings along the route and must be completed within 90 minutes; the 1K tasting walk visits three sampling stations.

About

The Cauble Creek Vineyard Winerun is a fun and unique running event that combines the love of wine with the thrill of a race. Participants can choose between a 1K tasting walk or a 5K run/walk, both of which offer the opportunity to sample delicious wines from the Cauble Creek Vineyard.
