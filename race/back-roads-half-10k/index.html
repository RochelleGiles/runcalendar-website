Course

Rolling rural roads outside Red Lodge with views of the Beartooth Mountains. The surface is mostly gravel with some paved sections and one off-trail descent. Overall elevation gain is minimal and the half marathon is net downhill. Packet pickup is at Red Lodge Ales.

About

The Back Roads Half & 10K takes place in Red Lodge, Montana, a small town at the base of the Beartooth Mountains near the entrance to the Beartooth Highway.
