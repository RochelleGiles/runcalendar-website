Course

Start and finish at 200 Portofino Way in Redondo Beach. The waterfront route passes the historic Redondo Beach Pier, the King Harbor marina and the Wyland whale mural. A 10K, a 5K run/walk, buggy and stroller divisions, a combined Big Game Day Challenge and a kids 1K are offered.

About

The Redondo Beach Super Bowl Sunday 10K/5K Run/Walk is a long-standing tradition in the South Bay, one of the oldest races there and among the largest events in Los Angeles County, expecting over 7000 participants from elite athletes to weekend warriors. It features a famous Costume Contest, 2-day Health and Fitness Expo, Post Race Party, and ocean views, with proceeds benefiting the Redondo Beach Chamber of Commerce programs.
