About

The Park2Park Half-Marathon course starts and ends at the Ottawa County Fairgrounds and includes scenic views of Lake Macatawa, Holland State Park, and Lakeshore Drive. September temperatures are just right for a half! The 5k run through Waukazoo Woods also starts and ends at the Ottawa County Fairgrounds. Enjoy a weekend in friendly and quaint Holland, Michigan, located on the west coast of the state, right along Lake Michigan, just 2 1/2 hours north of Chicago. Holland is the home of Tulip Time, wooden shoes, Hope College, Holland State Park, vibrant downtown shopping and eating, and the Park2Park!
