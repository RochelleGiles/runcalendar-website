Course

Half marathon and 10K courses starting and finishing at 801 Front Loop in Buena Vista, on trails winding through sagebrush and ponderosa pines above the Arkansas River, with views across to the Collegiate Peaks. The half marathon starts at 8:00am and the 10K at 9:00am.

About

A women's trail race in Buena Vista, Colorado, with half marathon and 10K distances starting and finishing near the Arkansas River at Front Loop. Courses wind through sagebrush and ponderosa pine below the Collegiate Peaks, and the event is run by Trail Sisters to grow women's participation in trail running.
