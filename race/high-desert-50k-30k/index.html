Course

Loop courses on rolling desert dirt roads outside Ridgecrest, California, running between about 2,400ft and 3,500ft. The 50K and 30K start and finish at the Cerro Coso Community College gym car park at 7:00am; the 10K starts at 8:00am through the old Goldbug mining area and joins the 30K route at Gracie's Mansion aid station.
