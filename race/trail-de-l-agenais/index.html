About

The Trail de Lamontjoie is a trail running event staged by SU Agen Triathlon at Lamontjoie in Lot-et-Garonne, south-west France. The organiser offers three courses of 6.5 km, 14 km and 21 km, plus a walking route, on the wooded tracks and farmland around the village. Entry is handled through the club's own website.
