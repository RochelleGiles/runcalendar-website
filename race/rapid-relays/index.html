Course

Four-hour lapped relay on the Culden Faw Estate at Henley Showground, Hambleden, near Henley-on-Thames. Racing runs from 11am to 3pm and entrants complete as many laps as they can as solos, pairs or teams of three or four. The loop is mostly woodland on mixed terrain, not especially hilly, with one climb at about 2.5km followed by a fast descent. Race village at the start-finish with catering and first aid.

About

The Rapid Relays in Henley-on-Thames is an exhilarating time-based running challenge where participants have 4 hours to complete as many 4km laps as possible. This unique event tests both speed and endurance, making it an exciting test for runners of all abilities. The scenic riverside location provides a beautiful backdrop for this test of running prowess.
