Course

A single loop starting in front of the Mirage Country Club and winding through Port Douglas — the main street, the Crystalbrook Superyacht Marina, up Flagstaff Hill and back past the Sheraton Grand Mirage. Every distance finishes with 3.6km on the hard-packed flat sand of Four Mile Beach.

About

Run Port Douglas welcomes runners from around Australia and across the globe to Tropical North Queensland. The 2km, 5km, 10km and 21.1km half marathon courses all finish on the famous Four Mile Beach in Port Douglas.
