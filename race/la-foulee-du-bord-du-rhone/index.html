About

Experience the beauty of the Rhône River at the annual La Foulee Du Bord Du Rhone running event. This picturesque race takes runners along the banks of the iconic Rhône, offering stunning views and a unique opportunity to explore the natural surroundings. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this event promises an unforgettable experience.
