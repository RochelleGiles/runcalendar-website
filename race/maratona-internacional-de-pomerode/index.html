About

The Maratona Internacional de Pomerode runs over two days in Pomerode, Santa Catarina, a southern Brazilian town known for its German heritage. A 6km race is held on the Saturday evening, and the half marathon and marathon both start at 5:30am on the Sunday. A free children's race runs alongside them.
