Course

A point-to-point route from Black Canyon City to Flagstaff, crossing Sonoran desert, canyon country and ponderosa forest, and linking the Black Canyon National Recreation Trail, Prescott Circle Trail and Flagstaff Loop Trail. The organiser gives the surface as 45% single-track, 46% double-track and 9% pavement.

About

A 250-mile point-to-point ultramarathon connecting iconic trails and towns across central Arizona, starting in Black Canyon City and ending in Flagstaff. The race traverses diverse landscapes including the Sonoran Desert, Bradshaw Mountains, and Coconino National Forest with 40,667 feet of elevation gain.
