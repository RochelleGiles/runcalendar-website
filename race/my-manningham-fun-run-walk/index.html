About

Lace up your running shoes and join the community at the My Manningham Fun Run Walk in the beautiful Westerfolds Park in Templestowe. This family-friendly event offers both a running and walking course, allowing participants of all fitness levels to enjoy the scenic surroundings and get active together. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your fitness journey, this event promises an enjoyable day of exercise and camaraderie.
