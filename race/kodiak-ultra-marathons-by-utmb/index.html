Course

Mountain trail races with the start and finish line in downtown Big Bear Lake. Courses climb through high desert, semi-arid and subalpine terrain, taking in the top of Sugarloaf Mountain and the descent into Siberia Canyon, with views over Big Bear Lake throughout. The 100M runs on the Friday and the 100K, 50K, 21K and 10K on the Saturday of a two-day festival weekend.

About

The Kodiak Ultra Marathons by UTMB take place in the San Bernardino Mountains around Big Bear Lake, California, as part of the UTMB World Series. Distances range from a 10K to the full 100-mile Kodiak Ultra, drawing trail and mountain runners from across the country.
