Course

Starts and finishes at the Group Camp by Yellowwood Lake, next to the Jackson Creek trailhead, and follows sections of the 42-mile Tecumseh Hiking Trail through Yellowwood State Forest near Nashville, Indiana. Demanding singletrack forest terrain, run in autumn foliage.

About

The Tecumseh Trail Challenge follows the Tecumseh Trail through the forests of Brown County, Indiana, near Nashville. Runners can choose the marathon, 50K, half marathon or 8 mile distance on rugged, wooded singletrack.
