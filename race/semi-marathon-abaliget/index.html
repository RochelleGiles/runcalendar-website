About

The Pécs-Orfű-Abaliget Run is a comprehensive running event that takes participants through the scenic landscapes of Pécs, Orfű, and Abaliget. The course highlights include the beautiful natural surroundings, with the event offering a range of distances to cater to runners of all levels. The event is known for its vibrant atmosphere and community spirit.
