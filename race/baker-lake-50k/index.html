Course

An out-and-back on Baker Lake Trail #606 and #610, a wide path contouring the eastern shore of Baker Lake through old-growth forest, starting and finishing at Kulshan Campground in the Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest. The trail rolls through countless short rises and descents and crosses the Baker River suspension bridge along with several slippery bridges and log crossings. Five aid stations carry water, with fuller support at the mile 15.6 turnaround.

About

Baker Lake 50k is a trail race organized by NW Endurance Events near Concrete, Washington. The course follows forested trail along the shore of Baker Lake in the North Cascades, in view of Mount Baker, with 25K and 50K options.
