Course

Trail race on singletrack over Bauxite Ridge, starting and finishing at the McKee Employee Recreation Center in Collegedale. The 15K has two aid stations, at 2.5 and 7 miles; the 8K has one, at 2.5 miles. Both distances finish with a final quarter-mile on the lower track.

About

Box Turtle Express Trail Races take place on the wooded singletrack of the Box Turtle trail network in Collegedale, Tennessee. Runners choose between 8K and 15K distances through rolling forest terrain.
