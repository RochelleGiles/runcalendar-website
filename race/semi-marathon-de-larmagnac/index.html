About

The Semi-Marathon de l'Armagnac is now past its 19th running, drawing runners from the region and across France for its scenic and festive atmosphere. The route winds through the Bas-Armagnac countryside between roads, vineyards, and paths, passing through the cellar of the Château de Ravignan and the Ognoas estate before finishing in the bullring of Villeneuve-de-Marsan to the rhythm of local brass bands.
