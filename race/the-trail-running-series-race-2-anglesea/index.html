Course

All courses start on the sand of Anglesea Main Beach, from Anglesea Riverbank Park, and run loop routes through coastal and hinterland trails in the Great Otway National Park. Surfaces include beach sand, single track, double track and clifftop trails on the Surf Coast Walk. Water points at 11.3km and 22.7km on the Long course and at 11.2km on the Medium; none on the Short course.

About

Race 2 of Rapid Ascent Trail Running Series is held at Anglesea on the Surf Coast in Victoria. Courses of 27.6km, 16.2km, 8km and a 4.3km Friendly start on Anglesea Main Beach and run coastal and hinterland trails, including single track and clifftop sections of the Surf Coast Walk.
