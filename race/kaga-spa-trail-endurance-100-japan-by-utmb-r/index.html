About

The Kagaspa Trail Endurance 100 by UTMB is a trail running event set in the stunning natural beauty of Yamanaka Onsen in Ishikawa Prefecture, Japan. The course offers breathtaking 360-degree views of the surrounding mountains, blanketed with rhododendron and beech forests, providing a truly inspiring stage for trail runners. The event is part of the UTMB World Series, bringing the magic and collective spirit of the UTMB to Japan.
