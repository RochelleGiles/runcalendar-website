Course

A three-loop 5K on mixed terrain starting and finishing at Yip Field on High Street in Central Falls. Check-in opens at 7:45am and the race starts at 9:00am, rain or shine. Dogs and baby carriages are not permitted on the course.

About

The Flynn's Flyers 5K Run/Walk is a community race staged by the Central Falls School District at Yip Field in Central Falls, Rhode Island. Runners and walkers cover three loops of a scenic mixed-terrain course, and every finisher collects a shirt and a medal.
