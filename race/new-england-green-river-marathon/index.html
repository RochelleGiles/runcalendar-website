Course

Point-to-point marathon from Marlboro, Vermont to Greenfield, Massachusetts, following the Green River valley and passing through an 1870s covered bridge. The course is almost entirely road, rolling but net downhill with roughly 1,400 feet of elevation loss. USATF-certified (MA18003JK) and rated among the fastest marathon courses in New England.

About

The New England Green River Marathon is a marathon-only race held each August in Greenfield, Massachusetts, in western Massachusetts. It draws a small, dedicated field of marathoners each summer.
