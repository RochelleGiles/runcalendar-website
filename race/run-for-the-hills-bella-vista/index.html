Course

A lapped cross-country course on the grounds of Bella Vista Farm in Sydney's north west, finishing on Bella Vista Oval. The 3.7km is one lap, the 7.4km two and the 11km three.

About

Run for the Hills takes place at Bella Vista Farm Park in Sydney's Hills District, offering distances from a family fun circuit up to an 11km challenge run. The heritage farm park provides open parkland for the multi-distance event.
