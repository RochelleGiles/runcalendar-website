Course

About

The Weymouth Woods 25K/50K Trail Run is held at Weymouth Woods-Sandhills Nature Preserve on trails filled with roots and sand under a canopy of longleaf pine trees. It offers 25K and 50K distances in a cupless event with aid stations, t-shirts, and finisher medals.
