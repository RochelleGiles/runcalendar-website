Course

Multi-lap loop course in a park at 500 Lee Road, Wayne. Runners start in the centre of the park, run a prologue partial lap out to the perimeter, then repeat a clockwise park loop of about 1.25 miles, finishing at the Tot Lot Pavilion. Eight-hour time limit and walker-friendly.

About

This second annual event is now the final leg of the ChesCo Winter Series (<https://runsignup.com/Race/PA/Wayne/ChesCoRaceFest> has information on the entire series). The park loop is a fairly flat 1.25 miles and you have the option of a 1/2 Marathon, Full Marathon, 50K, or 4 Person Marathon Relay. The races start in the center of the park and do a prologue partial lap to the perimeter and then loop the park clockwise finishing at the Tot Lot Pavilion. We will have lots of great snacks, drinks, and pizza. It will be a fun and festive atmosphere.
