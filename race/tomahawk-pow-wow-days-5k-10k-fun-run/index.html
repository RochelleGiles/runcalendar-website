Course

All races depart Veterans Memorial Park on the Wisconsin River. The 5K is a basically flat looped course that follows the 10K route for about 2.4km before detouring through school property, while the 10K runs a riverside route out toward Kings Hill. Surfaces are park and town roads plus school grounds. The 5K and 10K start together at 8:30 AM.

About

The 49th annual Pow Wow Days 5K, 10K, 1 Mile Fun Runs take place in conjunction with the annual 4th of July Lion's Day Festivities in Tomahawk. All races depart from Veterans Memorial Park on the beautiful Wisconsin River.
