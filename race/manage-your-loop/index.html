About

Manage Your Loop (Gère Ta Boucle) is a multi-lap trail race near Chailles in the Loir-et-Cher, not far from Château de Chambord. Runners repeat a roughly 7.5km forest loop, choosing 22km (3 laps), 45km (6 laps) or 90km (12 laps), with a 185km overnight option also available.
