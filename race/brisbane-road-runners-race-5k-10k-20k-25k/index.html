About

Explore the scenic streets of Brisbane at the annual Brisbane Road Runners Race, offering distances from 5K to 25K. This community-driven event welcomes runners of all levels to challenge themselves on a picturesque course through the heart of the city. Whether you're aiming for a new personal best or simply want to enjoy the vibrant atmosphere, this race has something for everyone.
