About

The 4 Paws Marathon in Christchurch's Bottle Lake Forest offers runners a unique opportunity to explore the scenic off-road trails alongside their canine companions. This dog-friendly event invites both human and four-legged athletes to tackle the challenging yet rewarding course, making it a must-try for adventure-seeking runners and their best furry friends.
