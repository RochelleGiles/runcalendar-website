Course

Starts and finishes at Wayne Bunker Family Park on West Alexander Road in northwest Las Vegas. The out-and-back route runs northwest along the Western Beltway Trail beside the 215, climbing gradually at roughly 100 ft per mile from 2,710 ft before turning and returning downhill toward views of the Strip.

About

The Rockin' Reindeer is a December holiday race in Las Vegas organized by Jusrun and held each year in tribute to Troy's Legacy, honoring the local running community. Runners choose from 10K, 5K and 1 mile distances, many decked out in reindeer antlers and holiday gear for the festive run.
