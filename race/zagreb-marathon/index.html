About

The Zagreb Marathon is a premier running event set against the stunning backdrop of Croatia's vibrant capital city. Runners can explore the city's historic landmarks and picturesque streets as they push themselves to the finish line of this challenging yet rewarding course. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a dedicated runner looking for a new adventure, the Zagreb Marathon offers an unforgettable experience.
