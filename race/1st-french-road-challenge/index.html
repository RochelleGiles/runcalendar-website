About

This road race takes place in Cernay-la-Ville within the Haute Vallée de Chevreuse Regional Natural Park, a forested area southwest of Paris. It offers a marathon and a 10km event, both run as a single loop, alongside a half marathon distance. The event markets itself as one of the least expensive marathons in France.
