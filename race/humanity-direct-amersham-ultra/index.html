Course

A one-day 50km ultra starting and finishing at Dr Challoner's Grammar School, Amersham, looping through the Misbourne and Chess valleys in the Chiltern Hills. The route mainly follows footpaths and bridleways with a short road section, comprehensively marked with tape, paint and arrows. Four checkpoints stocked with water and sweet and savoury food.

About

Challenge yourself in the Chiltern Hills at the Humanity Direct Amersham Ultra. This scenic ultra marathon event offers distances of 50km, 25km, and 10km, allowing runners of all abilities to explore the stunning natural landscapes of the United Kingdom. Whether you're seeking a personal best or simply a beautiful running adventure, the Humanity Direct Amersham Ultra is an event not to be missed.
