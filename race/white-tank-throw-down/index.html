Course

The 30K combines flat perimeter trails with mountain sections through Ford Canyon twice, Mesquite Canyon and Willow Canyon, taking in light scrambling and sand for about 2,500 ft of gain. The 10K stays on the flatter perimeter trails plus a less technical stretch of Ford Canyon for around 600 ft. Two aid stations on the 30K, one on the 10K, with drop bags at the start.

About

Join Thunderbird Trail Runners for the inaugural White Tank Throwdown at White Tank Mountain Regional Park in Arizona! This challenging trail race offers two distances on a mix of fast perimeter paths and rugged mountain terrain. Expect stunning desert views, technical sections, and a true test of endurance.
