About

The Shamrocks and Shenanigans 5K is a family-friendly race celebrating the holidays with fun for all ages, featuring a course along closed roads of Forest Park Drive in Queens. Participants receive custom beanies, full-color bibs, medals, treats, and drinks, with costumes encouraged and kids under 12 racing for $25.
