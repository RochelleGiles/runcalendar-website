Course

Lapped course of 5.275km laps around the Bannockburn Sluicings near Cromwell, starting and finishing at Mt Difficulty Winery, 73 Felton Road, Bannockburn. The half marathon is four laps and the full marathon eight. Solo, team, youth team and kids team entries are offered over the half.

About

The Rustic Run & Walk is an easy to moderate fun-oriented event that compliments the extreme Mt Difficulty Ascent held on the same day.
