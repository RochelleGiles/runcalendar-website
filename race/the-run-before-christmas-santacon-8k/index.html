Course

Starts and finishes at Red Mill Museum Village, 56 Main Street, Clinton. The route runs Main Street to Leigh Street, over Old Rt. 22, into Franklin Township across the bridge, then onto Lower Lansdowne Rd and the Lansdowne Trail back to the Red Mill. It mixes street and trail; the organiser bills the distance as 8-ish kilometres.
