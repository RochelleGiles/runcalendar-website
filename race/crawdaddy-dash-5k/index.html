Course

Road 5K starting and finishing in the Rosewood neighbourhood of Columbia, close to City Roots farm. The route is run on pavement and is described by the organiser as smooth and fast, a local favourite for quick times. Race-day check-in is at 1005 Airport Blvd and the start is at 8:00am.

About

A 5K race in Columbia, South Carolina that kicks off the legendary Rosewood Crawfish Festival. Participants of all ages are encouraged to join, with registration including free entry to the festival.
