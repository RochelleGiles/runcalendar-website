About

The Gax 100 Miles is an epic ultramarathon challenge set in an undisclosed, remote location. Runners will push their limits over 100 grueling miles, navigating rugged terrain and unpredictable weather as they strive to conquer this legendary event. With a reputation for stunning scenery and an unforgettable atmosphere, the Gax 100 Miles is a must-do for any seasoned ultrarunner.
