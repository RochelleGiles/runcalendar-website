Course

A lapped event on smooth tarmac park paths through Victoria Park's open lawns and tree-lined avenues, starting and finishing at St Marks Gate. The 5K is 1.5 laps, the 10K three laps and the half marathon 6.5 laps, all almost flat — 17m of climb over the half. Accurate kilometre markers and marshals at every key point.

About

A RunThrough multi-distance event around Victoria Park in East London, the city's oldest public park known locally as the People's Park. Half marathon, 10K and 5K routes loop the park's paths and lake.
