Course

Trail race from Shelter 8 in Shawnee Mission Park, Shawnee, combining the park's two separate trail systems. The 10 mile uses one system; the 20, 40 and 50 mile distances run both the north and south shore trails. Footing ranges from smooth and runnable to very technical with roots, rocks and rolling hills, mostly under tree cover.

About

Tale of Two Trails runs through Shawnee Mission Park in Johnson County, Kansas. It receives its name because it combines two separate trail systems in the same park. The 10M race is the only course that runs on one system. The 20M, 40m, and 50m are run throughout both Shawnee Mission's North and South shore trail systems, connected by a brief amount of paved trail. Each loop is 20M long.
