About

Explore the picturesque Westwind Trail, a challenging yet rewarding running event through the heart of the unknown countryside. Runners will be treated to stunning vistas and a unique course that tests their endurance, making this a must-do event for any trail enthusiast. With a rich history and a reputation for an exceptional participant experience, the Westwind Trail is sure to leave a lasting impression.
