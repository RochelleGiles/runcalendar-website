Course

Natural-surface trail run on the Trans Canada Trail through Pinawa, Manitoba, taking in the Old Pinawa Trail, Westdal Way and the Alice Chambers Trail and crossing a suspension bridge. The 14km starts from Pinawa Dam Provincial Park and the 5km from Suspension Bridge Road; both finish at the Pinawa Golf Course clubhouse.

About

A trail race through Pinawa Dam Provincial Park in Manitoba, past the ruins of one of Canada's early hydroelectric dams on the Winnipeg River. Distances are 5K and 14K.
