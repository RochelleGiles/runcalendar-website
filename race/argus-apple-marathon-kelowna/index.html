Course

A flat, fast loop from Kelowna City Park, certified by Athletics Canada and a Boston qualifier. The route runs north through downtown on paved roads and bike path, returns to the park, then heads south on the Abbott Street bike path beside Okanagan Lake and loops near Okanagan College. The half runs one loop and the marathon two; the 8 km splits off earlier onto Ellis Street. Marathon and half start at 7:30am, 8 km at 8:15am.

About

A marathon, half marathon and 8K through Kelowna, British Columbia, in the apple-growing country of the Okanagan Valley. Part of the Kelowna Apple Race Series held each fall.
