Course

A USATF-certified course at Des Moines Water Works Park, run largely around a wide open field. The 5K is a single loop; the 10.1K covers that loop twice. Start and finish are both inside the park.

About

Race for Hope DSM is Iowa's first and largest brain tumor awareness fundraiser, raising over $700,000 for brain tumor research. It features Kids Fun Run, 2K Walk, 5K Run/Walk, and 10.1K Run at Water Works Park in Des Moines, Iowa.
