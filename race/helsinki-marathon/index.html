About

The Björn Borg Helsinki Marathon is a must-run event for seasoned runners and newcomers alike. Winding through the historic streets of Finland's vibrant capital, this scenic course offers a unique opportunity to experience the city's rich cultural heritage while challenging your physical limits. With stunning views and an enthusiastic local crowd, the Helsinki Marathon promises an unforgettable running adventure.
