Course

Backyard-ultra format at Vista View Park, Davie. Runners repeat a 2.083-mile loop, one loop started every 30 minutes, up to 48 segments and 100 miles over 24 hours, with each loop to be finished inside 30 minutes. A nutrition and aid station and bathrooms sit at the start and finish. Solo and 2-4 person relay entries.

About

The Runner's Depot Endurance Challenge is a 24-hour endurance event where participants run a 2.0834-mile loop every 30 minutes, completing as many as possible up to 48 segments for 100 miles. It offers individual and relay team options with finisher medals for 25, 50, 75, and 100 miles.
