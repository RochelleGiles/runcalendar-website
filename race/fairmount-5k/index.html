Course

Starts and finishes at Fairmount Elementary School, 15975 West 50th Avenue, Golden, Colorado. A 5K run/walk is offered alongside a sub-one-mile Fairmount Flash fun run and a warrior-style Ninja obstacle course for ages five and up.

About

The Fairmount 5k at Fairmount Elementary welcomes students, staff, families, and the wider community for a run, walk, and fun run. Afterward, participants are invited to stay for music and treats.
