About

The You Yangs Trail Running Festival is a two-day trail event in the You Yangs Regional Park near Little River, Victoria, run by Trailsplus as race three of its Mountain Trail Series. Courses are loops over wooded bike trails and open rocky tracks with a climb toward Flinders Peak. Distances range from 5km to 100 miles.
