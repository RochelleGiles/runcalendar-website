Course

Out-and-back from Bath House Beach in Barmouth, taking in coastal paths, forest tracks, rocky ascents and open mountain. The longer courses climb to the summit of Cadair Idris before returning along the coast. Routes are flagged and signed with marshals at major junctions.

About

Snowdonia Sea2Sky is a trail ultra run by RunThrough Trails from the coastal town of Barmouth up into the Snowdonia mountains, with 12K, 25K and 54K distances on offer.
