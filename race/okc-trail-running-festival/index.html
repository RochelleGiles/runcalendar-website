Course

Equestrian singletrack at Liberty Lake, Guthrie, Oklahoma, with technical and rough sections and about 360ft of climb per 10-mile loop. The 15K, 35K and 50K repeat a 15K loop from the start/finish; the 5K is an out-and-back. Three aid stations per loop, at the start/finish, Liberty (mile 5.2) and a water-only point at mile 7.7. Cupless event.
