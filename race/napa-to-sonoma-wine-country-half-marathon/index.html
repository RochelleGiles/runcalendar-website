Course

Point-to-point half marathon starting at Cuvaison Estate Wines in Napa and finishing at Sonoma Plaza, passing rolling vineyards, ranches and farmland. Six aid stations sit at miles 2, 4.2, 6.4, 7.7, 9.5 and 11.4 with water and electrolyte, energy gel at mile 7.7, and medical support at three of them. The accompanying Rose 5K starts and finishes at Sonoma Plaza the day before.

About

A point-to-point half marathon through the vineyards of California's wine country, running between Napa and Sonoma. The weekend also includes a companion Rosé 5K and finishes with a wine festival at the finish line.
