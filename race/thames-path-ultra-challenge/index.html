Course

Linear route following the Thames towpath upstream from Bishops Park in Fulham to a finish at Henley-on-Thames, passing Richmond, Hampton Court, Runnymede and Windsor. Flat riverside towpath with around 350m of climb over the full 100km. Options range from 22km quarters to the full 100km, run continuously or over two days. Rest stops roughly every 12.5km.

About

The Thames Path Ultra Challenge is a self-paced trail run or walk along the Thames towpath. The full 100km distance follows a point-to-point route from Putney Bridge upstream through Richmond, Hampton Court and Runnymede to a riverside finish in Henley-on-Thames.
