About

Feeling the luck o' the Irish? Lace-up and chase the rainbow in Denver's favorite Irish-themed run! Join us at Washington Park for some shenanigans! Runnin' of the Green (ROTG) is a family-friendly 7K and 2 Mile race/walk celebrating Irish traditions, Saint Patrick's Day, and Spring! The event promises to be a day filled with Irish fun, live music, awards, and even cold beer.
