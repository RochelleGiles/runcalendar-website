About

Organized by the Boston Athletic Association, this 5K starts near Boston Common, runs up Commonwealth Avenue through Kenmore Square, and kicks off Patriots' Day Weekend alongside the B.A.A. Invitational Mile. About 10,000 runners take part each year.
