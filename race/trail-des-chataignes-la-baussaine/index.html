About

Trail des Châtaignes is a forest trail race in La Baussaine, Brittany, organized by the Bouge à La Baussaine sports association. The event offers three distances - 9, 16 and 30 km - through the surrounding woodland each November, drawing a couple hundred local runners across the distances.
