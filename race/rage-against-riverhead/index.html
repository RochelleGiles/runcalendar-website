Course

A 5km lap course in Riverhead Forest, a commercial pine forest, with the base at the shed just inside the forest at the corner of Barlow and Ararimu Valley Roads. Each lap mixes smooth flowing single trail with wider dirt tracks, plus about 50m of gravel road. Solo runners and relay teams of two, four or six complete as many laps as possible in 8, 12 or 24 hours. No aid stations and no water on course.

About

Rage Against Riverhead is a lapped timed trail race in Riverhead Forest, Auckland. Runners complete as many 5km laps as possible in 8, 12 or 24 hours, entering solo or in relay teams of two, four or six. Each lap combines smooth single trail with wider dirt tracks, and no water or aid is provided on course.
