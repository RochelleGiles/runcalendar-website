Course

The 50K circles the Pattee Creek watershed over several mountain summits with around 8,000 feet of climbing, finishing in Missoula's University District. The 25K covers the second half of that course along the House of Sky Ridgeline Trail. Less than two miles of the route is pavement; the rest is smooth single track.

About

City to Sky Trail Races start in downtown Missoula, Montana, and climb into the mountains above the city, with 25K and 50K distances that trace singletrack from the valley floor to high ridgelines.
