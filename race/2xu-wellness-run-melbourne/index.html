Course

A flat bayside course on asphalt along Beach Road in St Kilda. The 1km, 5km and 10km start and finish at Catani Gardens. The half marathon starts at Albert Park Lake, loops the lake, then joins Beach Road and runs past Luna Park to finish at Catani Gardens.

About

The 2XU Wellness Run takes place along the St Kilda foreshore in Melbourne, raising funds for the mental health charity Beyond Blue. It offers 5km, 10km and half marathon distances plus a junior fun run.
