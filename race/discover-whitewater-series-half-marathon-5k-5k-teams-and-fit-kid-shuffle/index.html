About

The Discover Whitewater Series is a Half Marathon, 5K, 5K Teams, and Fit Kid Shuffle held annually in Whitewater, WI every September. The race promotes the City of Whitewater and highlights its greatest features, along with encouraging a healthy lifestyle for people of all ages.
