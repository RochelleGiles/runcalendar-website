About

Mémo Run 66 is a charity run and walk in Perpignan supporting the Pôle Alzheimer Pyrénées-Orientales. Runners can choose a 5K or 8K course, with a 5K walk also open to all, raising funds and awareness for people living with Alzheimer's disease and their carers.
