Course

A backcountry mountain course in the Elkhorn Mountains near Helena, starting and finishing at the Willard Creek Trailhead. Technical singletrack, primitive roads and creek crossings, with not one step on pavement. Eight staffed aid stations on the 50-mile route; 50km and 13.1-mile distances also run.

About
