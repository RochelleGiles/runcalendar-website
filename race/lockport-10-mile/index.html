Course

Road race based at the Historic Palace Theatre in Lockport, NY. The 10 Mile starts on East Avenue at 9:00 AM; the 5 Mile starts at Slayton Settlement Road at 9:45 AM. Water stop at the five-mile mark with bathrooms at miles 4 and 7; a 10/5 relay option is also offered.

About

The Lockport 10 is one of the oldest road races in Western New York, run each February from the Historic Palace Theatre on East Avenue in Lockport. It offers a 10-mile race, a 5-mile race and a 10/5 relay, with online entry through RunSignup and race-morning registration at the theater.
