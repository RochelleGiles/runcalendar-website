Course

Starts and finishes at East Valley High School on East Wellesley Avenue, Spokane Valley, Washington. Two road distances are offered: a five-mile run that serves as a second-seed qualifier for Bloomsday, and a three-mile run.

About

The annual Rapid Rabbit Run is a major fundraiser supporting the Senior All Knighter graduation party. With 2 race lengths to choose from and in-person and virtual options, there's something for everyone!
