Course

An out-and-back trail half marathon starting and finishing in Wendover, following The Ridgeway National Trail out to Whiteleaf Hill Nature Reserve where an undulating forest loop awaits. Fully marked, entirely on trail, 21.1km with about 526m of climb. Capped at 350 runners with a four-hour limit.

About

The Chiltern Ridge Half is a trail half marathon along The Ridgeway National Trail in Buckinghamshire. Starting and finishing in Wendover, the route takes runners along a stunning stretch of trail to Whiteleaf Hill Nature Reserve, where an undulating forest loop awaits.
