Course

Start and finish are together under the arch at the east side of Church Ranch Office Park, 10170 Church Ranch Way, Westminster. The courses follow public trails along the Big Dry Creek Trail; the surface material is not stated. Aid stations average 1.2 miles apart with water, electrolytes and gels.

About

Ho-ho-holiday fun for all abilities! The only December half marathon in the Denver area with a wonderfully flat course and great views of the Front Range. Groups can run different distances from 5K to half marathon, and we permit dogs and strollers on-course as long as you follow basic trail etiquette.
