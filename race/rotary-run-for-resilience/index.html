About

Join us for the 3rd Annual Rotary Run for Resilience 5k Run/Walk, presented by Rotary Club of Queen Creek! Our Club's focus is to bring awareness and teen mental wellness resources to the community. Bring family and friends to cheer you on and meet local vendors and mental health providers.
