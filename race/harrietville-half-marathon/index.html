Course

A point-to-point race down the Ovens valley in the Australian Alps between Bright and Harrietville. The organiser lists separate start points at Germantown, Smoko, Stoney Creek Fish Farm and Howards Bridge for the different distances, with the 21.1 km away at 8:00am, the 10.5 km at 8:30am, the 5 km at 9:45am and the 2 km at 10:15am.

About

The Harrietville Half Marathon takes place in Harrietville, the gateway town to Mount Hotham in Victoria's Alpine region, with 5km, 10km and half marathon distances through the surrounding high country.
