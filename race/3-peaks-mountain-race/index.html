About

The 3 Peaks Mountain Race in Dunedin, New Zealand is a challenging endurance trail run organized by the Leith Harrier & Athletic Club. Runners will test their mettle as they conquer three distinct mountain peaks, each offering breathtaking vistas and demanding terrain. This event is a must for trail enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable adventure in the stunning New Zealand wilderness.
