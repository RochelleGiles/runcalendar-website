Course

The 5K and 10K wind through Camp Susque's mountain trails with rocks, mud and roots underfoot, gaining 469 ft (5K) or 938 ft (10K), with one water station on course. The 1-mile fun run/walk stays on flat grassy surfaces on a circuit around camp.

About

Our annual Trail Run occurs during our yearly Harvest Festival. Hit the trails in support of Susque! Susque's 5k trail run will challenge you as you wind through the hills, streams, and various areas at Susque. During the course, you will gain 469 feet of elevation and enjoy great views of the camp below. The 10k course is the same as the 5k course, just twice! For those who want to enjoy the day at Susque, but are looking for something a little less intense, the Fun Run will provide an easy, flat, approximate 1-mile circuit to enjoy the sights and sounds of Susque in the fall.
