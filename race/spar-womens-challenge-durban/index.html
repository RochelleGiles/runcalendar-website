Course

Women-only road race run along the Durban beachfront, with 10km and 5km options. Race pack collection is at Kings Park Outer Fields. The 10km starts at 08:00 and the 5km at 09:00. Fully sealed urban roads and promenade.

About

SPAR Women's Challenge Durban is the Durban leg of South Africa's long-running, women-only road race series. It offers 5km and 10km distances through the city and is one of several city stops held around the country each year.
