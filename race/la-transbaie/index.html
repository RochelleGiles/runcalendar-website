About

Explore the stunning landscapes of the Transbaie region at the annual La Transbaie running event. This iconic race takes runners on a challenging yet breathtaking journey through diverse terrains, offering a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the area. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a newcomer to the sport, La Transbaie promises an unforgettable adventure.
