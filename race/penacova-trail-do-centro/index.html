About

The Penacova Trail Do Centro is an annual running event organized by Carlos Sá Nature Events in Penacova, Portugal. The event features several competitive trail running distances, including a 46km Ultra Trail, a 30km Trail, and a 17km Trail Sprint, in the natural surroundings of the region.
