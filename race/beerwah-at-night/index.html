Course

A night trail race at the base of Mt Beerwah in the Glass House Mountains National Park, with staggered evening starts from 4pm and all races finishing by midnight. The 10 km climbs 294 m and the half marathon 586 m; the marathon is two laps of the half course, anti-clockwise then clockwise, for 1,172 m of gain. Trails are marked with reflective flagging and red arrows. No entries on the day.

About

Strap on a headlamp and run in the dark at Mt Beerwah. This popular event kicks off the trail-running season in Queensland, enabling runners to enjoy their sport in the cool of night. If you have never run trail in the dark before, this event around the base of Mt Beerwah is the perfect introduction.
