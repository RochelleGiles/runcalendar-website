First Light Marathon

Gisborne · New Zealand
RoadCoastalMarathon · Half Marathon · 6KEntries open

Gisborne's beaches and grassy headlands at the world's first sunrise: a marathon, half and 6K from Midway Surf Club.

Quick verdict

A hill race in a marathon's clothes. You start on the boardwalk at Midway Surf Club in the dark, climb short, steep grass hills above the Pacific as the sun comes up, and finish back on the beachfront. Sand, dirt, single track and road, and never more than 500 runners.

Course at a glance

Midway BeachStart & finish
1,206 mClimb (marathon)
8 hTime limit

Along the beachfront from Awapuni and round the harbour, then over the Kaiti headland to Sponge Bay. The half turns for home there. The marathon carries on up the coast to Wainui and Okitu, climbs into the farm hills behind, drops into the river valley and climbs again to 270 m around 31 km, then runs down through Whataupoko and the town to the beach.

Three things to know

  1. Bib pick-up is at Midway Surf Club the evening before, or at the start from 5am. The bag drop is staffed; label your bag with your name and bib number.
  2. Water and energy drink about every 4 km, and one gel station on the marathon around 25 km. Poles aren't allowed. You can change distance until 8pm the night before, not on the day.
  3. Runners from overseas enter through the organiser's five-day Gisborne package, with a pōwhiri on arrival. New Zealand residents can enter the race on its own.

Race-day weather

Peak summer: around 14°C before the sun is up, mid-20s and humid by late morning, and rain on about six days in a typical January. Sunscreen, a cap and sunglasses.

Worth the trip?

Yes, for sunrise over the Pacific from a grassy hilltop and a finish on the sand. Go for the day out rather than a time: 1,200 m of climbing and soft ground make it a run to enjoy, not a PB course.
