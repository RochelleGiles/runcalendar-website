About

The Trail Urbain Verdunois offers runners a unique urban trail experience through the vibrant streets and parks of Verdun. Explore the city's historic architecture and lush greenspaces as you challenge yourself on this scenic course. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable adventure in the heart of the city.
