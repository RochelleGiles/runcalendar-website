Course

All distances start and finish at The Lakes Community Center, 17520 Elizabeth Lake Rd, Lake Hughes. Starts are staggered from 5:30 am for the 100 mile through to 7 am for the 30K and 50K. The 100K and 100 mile carry Western States qualifying standards.

About

Leona Divide is a trail ultramarathon through the Angeles National Forest in the San Gabriel Mountains near Lake Hughes, California, with 50K, 50-mile, and 100-mile distances plus shorter 30K and 100K options. Runners cover rugged high-desert and chaparral terrain with big elevation swings.
