Course

Run on the Pine Line Trail, a former railroad corridor with a crushed-granite surface. The marathon and relay are out-and-back; the half marathon and 10K are point-to-point with a shuttle to the start. All distances finish near Centennial Parkway at the north end of Medford. The route is mostly flat and fast.

About

The Pine Line Marathon in Medford, Wisconsin, runs on the crushed-granite surface of the historic Pine Line Trail, a former railroad corridor. The mostly flat course is popular with runners chasing fast times amid Northwoods scenery.
