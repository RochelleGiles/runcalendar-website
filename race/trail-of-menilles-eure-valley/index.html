About

The Trail de Ménilles Vallée d'Eure event takes place in the picturesque Eure Valley, offering runners of all levels a chance to discover the authentic and dynamic town of Ménilles. Situated just an hour from Paris and 45 minutes from Rouen, this trail running event promises a magical atmosphere as it coincides with the Ménilles Christmas market.
