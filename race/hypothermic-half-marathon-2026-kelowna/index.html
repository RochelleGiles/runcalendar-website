About

The Hypothermic Half Marathon is a winter running event organised by the Running Room, designed to keep runners motivated and active during the colder months. Held in multiple Canadian cities, it is known for its friendly atmosphere, unique winter challenge and community spirit; the Kelowna edition welcomes runners of all levels to a scenic, invigorating race.
