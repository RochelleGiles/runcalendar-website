Course

A point-to-point marathon starting at Rochester International Airport and finishing in downtown Rochester. The route runs county roads and wooded sections, joins the Zumbro River trails, then city streets to the finish, with thirteen water and nutrition stations; chip timed.

About

The Med City Marathon, presented by ActivePT, is an annual race weekend in Rochester, MN, now past its 29th running. The weekend features a full marathon, half marathon, 20 miler, 5K, relays, back-to-back challenges and a kids run.
