Course

A 12-leg team relay finishing in the coastal community of Seabrook, Washington. Running teams of four to six cover 58.2 miles from Front Street in Pacific Beach; walking teams cover 38.4 miles starting at Humptulips. The organiser describes the route as fairly flat but does not state the surface.

About

The Les Schwab Tires Seabrook Relay is a fairly flat, fast, and beautifully scenic team journey with a looped course starting and ending in the charming coastal community of Seabrook.
