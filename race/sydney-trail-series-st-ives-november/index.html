Course

Lapped bush trail racing starting and finishing at St Ives Showground, using the trails of St Ives Wildflower Gardens. The longer courses push into Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park over steep, technical terrain with scenic single trail along Cowan Creek. The November race adds a 30km to the 7km, 10km and 24km.

About

Sydney Trail Series St Ives returns with bush trail distances from 7km to 30km through the natural bushland around St Ives on Sydney's upper north shore, part of a multi-race trail series through the year.
