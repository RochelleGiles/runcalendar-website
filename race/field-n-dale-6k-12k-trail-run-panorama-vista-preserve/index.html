Course

Two courses at Panorama Vista Preserve on the Kern River, starting and finishing at 901 E Roberts Ln, Bakersfield. The 6K is flat and fast on mainly compacted dirt with some asphalt, running along both banks of the river. The 12K is a harder trail run that climbs the bluffs and includes a section of single track.

About

Join us for this unique and exciting trail run through the Preserve. The 6K course is FLAT & FAST! Mainly compacted dirt & some asphalt, running along both sides of the mighty Kern River. The 12K course is a Challenging Trail Run, which climbs the bluffs and a portion follows single track trails.
