About

The Badger Mountain Challenge is an ultramarathon event featuring trail runs on Badger and Candy Mountains in southeastern Washington. It offers five distances: 15k, 55k, 50 miles, 100k, and 100 miles, with ruck options for 15k and 50 miles.
