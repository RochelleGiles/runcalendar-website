About

Puglia by UTMB races through the gravine landscape of southern Italy, linking the coastal Salento region with the ancient cave city of Matera and its UNESCO-listed Sassi. Distances span from a 10K up to the 145K I Sassi di Matera, the longest race on the calendar.
