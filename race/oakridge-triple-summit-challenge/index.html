About

Join us in scenic Oakridge, Oregon for the Oakridge Triple Summit Challenge. Located in the Pacific Northwest's Capital of Mountain Biking, Oakridge has nearly 500 miles of densely forested trails. Truly a trail running and mountain biking paradise! Come run on one, two or all three amazing mountainous trails equaling a total of 60k of racing!
