About

The Wine Run 5K crosses Chattooga Belle Farm, a 200-acre working farm and distillery in the Blue Ridge foothills of Long Creek, South Carolina. The course rolls past orchards and vineyards with sweeping mountain views and finishes with a glass of wine.
