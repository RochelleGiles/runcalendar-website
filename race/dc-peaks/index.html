Course

A Wasatch Front mountain ultra above Davis County, run over two days: the 100 miler sets off on the Friday, with the 50 mile, 55K and 25K following on the Saturday. The 50 mile starts from Wilderness Park in Kaysville, the 55K from Centerville and the 25K from Mueller Park, and all share the same pattern of long climbs and sustained descents.

About

DC Peaks is a mountain ultra based in Kaysville, Utah, climbing into the Wasatch Range peaks above Davis County. Distances range from 25K to 100 miles, with steep singletrack and significant elevation gain typical of Utah's front-range ultra races.
