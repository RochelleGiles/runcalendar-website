About

The Last Mile is a year-end running event held at Lake Crabtree Park in Morrisville, NC, featuring 2 Mile, 4 Mile, and 6 Mile races with a mass start at 8:00 AM. It promotes healthy lifestyles as families run out the old year, including virtual options and prizes for course records and referrals.
