Course

A roughly 10 km multi-terrain loop on the Lake Vyrnwy Estate, with race HQ, start and finish on private land at Llanwddyn. The route blends fast footpaths, grassed sections, stony tracks and stretches of tarmac, with a sustained climb and a fast descent.

About

The Lake Vyrnwy 10K Trail runs along the shore of Lake Vyrnwy, a Victorian-era reservoir in mid-Wales known for its ornate straining tower and dam. It forms part of the wider Lake Vyrnwy Running Festival, which also includes a half marathon.
