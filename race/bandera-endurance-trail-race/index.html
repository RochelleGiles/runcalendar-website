Course

Lapped trail race in the Hill Country State Natural Area near Bandera. The main loop is a counter-clockwise 50k, with the 100k running two full laps and the 50k one. The trail is rugged and rocky throughout, with aid at Lodge, Equestrian, Chapas, Boyles, Nachos and YaYa.

About

The Bandera Endurance Trail Race is a HOKA-sponsored ultra through Hill Country State Natural Area near Bandera, Texas, with 100K, 50K, and 25K distances. The rocky, technical course is a Golden Ticket qualifier for the Western States 100.
