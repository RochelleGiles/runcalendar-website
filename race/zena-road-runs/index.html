Course

Starts and finishes at Noble Mountain Tree Farm, 5115 Bethel Heights Road NW, Salem. The 3 Mile is a hilly out-and-back on gravel and packed dirt roads; the 6 Mile is a double out-and-back on mixed pavement and gravel; the 15K is a loop through wine-country roads. All courses close at noon.

About

The Zena Road Runs is a running event held at Noble Mountain Tree Farm in Salem, Oregon, featuring three races starting at 10:00 AM. It offers a historic 15K loop that has been run since 1969, along with 6-mile and 3-mile options on scenic roads.
