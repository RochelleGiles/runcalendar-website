About

The Trail De Fort Boyard is a thrilling running event that takes place on the beautiful island of Oléron, France. Runners will have the opportunity to explore the island's stunning natural landscapes, including the iconic Fort Boyard, as they tackle this challenging trail course. With a mix of scenic routes and diverse terrain, this event promises an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
