About

The Colmar Marathon is a captivating running event that takes place in the charming Alsatian town of Colmar, France. Runners will have the opportunity to explore the picturesque streets and historic landmarks of this vibrant community as they push themselves to the finish line. With a scenic course and a rich cultural atmosphere, the Colmar Marathon offers an unforgettable experience for endurance athletes.
