Course

Runs the flowing, well-groomed singletrack of the Tsali Trail System on a peninsula in Fontana Lake, Nantahala National Forest, with rolling trails and views of the lake and Great Smoky Mountains. The 50K starts at 8 a.m. with aid-station cutoffs at Murphy's Branch, the trailhead and Crossroads; the 30K and 8K follow at 9:00 and 9:30.

About

The Tsali Ultra offers a 50K, 30K and 8K on the well-groomed singletrack of the Tsali Trail System beside Fontana Lake in Nantahala National Forest, near Bryson City. The January race regularly sells out before registration closes, and the rolling course carries views of the Great Smoky Mountains.
