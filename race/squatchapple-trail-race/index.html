Course

Repeating-loop format at South Mountain Reservation, starting and finishing at the Tulip Springs trailhead. Three loops are used: Pink 4mi of mostly unpaved carriage road with some singletrack, Checkered 5.5mi primarily carriage road, and Blue 7mi of more technical singletrack with rocks and roots. The 11mi is Pink plus Blue; the 50K covers all three twice. One aid station at start/finish, reached after every loop.

About

A community-focused trail running and hiking event held at South Mountain Reservation featuring multiple distance options for runners and hikers of all ages and paces. The event is part of Sassquad Trail Running's series of trail parties in New Jersey and New York.
