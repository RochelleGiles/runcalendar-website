Course

All races run on the Catherine Valley Trail System, a well-maintained former rail trail out of Montour Falls. Routes climb slightly on the way out and drop gently on the return to the finish. The programme spans a Friday night 5K plus a Saturday 18-miler, half marathon, 10K and 5K.

About

Run this fall in the beautiful Finger Lakes Region of New York State! One of the premier Willow Running events is in Montour Falls (Watkins Glen), NY. There will be various race distance on the beautiful Catharine Valley Trail to choose from including the 18M, a great training run for those gearing up for a fall marathon, Half Marathon, 10K & 5 K distances. The trail is an old Rail Trail that is well maintained all year and easy to navigate with a slight elevation gain heading out and a slight elevation decline heading back to the finish line.
