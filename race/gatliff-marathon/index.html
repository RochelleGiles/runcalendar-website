About

The Gatliff Marathon is an annual challenge walk, jog or run of 25 or 50km mostly on footpaths and tracks in the Kent, Surrey and Sussex countryside. The challenge is to complete the event in under 10 hours, following the route directions and checking in at each of the checkpoints.
