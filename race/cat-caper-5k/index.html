About

The Cat Caper 5K is a run/walk fundraiser in Westerville benefiting the Cat Welfare Association, now past its 19th running. The festive event features a raffle and vendors alongside the run. Proceeds support food, medical care, shelter, adoptions and spay/neuter services for the community.
