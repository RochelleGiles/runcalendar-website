Course

A ten-mile multi-terrain race over private land in the rolling hills of mid Devon, taking in hills, woods, trails and river crossings. About 70% of the course is off-road and it is usually wet and muddy in March. Starts from a field beside Templeton Village Hall.

About

The Feighan Fury is a challenging 10-mile multi-terrain running race around the picturesque rolling hills of mid Devon. Runners will tackle a variety of surfaces as they wind through private land, offering a unique off-road adventure in the heart of the British countryside. This event is sure to test the endurance and agility of participants.
