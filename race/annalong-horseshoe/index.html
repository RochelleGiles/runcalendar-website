Course

A checkpoint fell race in the Mourne Mountains, about 20 km with roughly 2,000 m of climb. From the Carricklittle track runners visit Chimney Rock, Donard, Commedagh, Cove, Lamagan and Binnian's north tor in order, choosing their own lines between checkpoints, and finish back at Carricklittle.

About

A mountain race in the Mournes starting and finishing at Annalong, County Down, tracing a horseshoe-shaped route over the surrounding peaks. Organised by the Northern Ireland Mountain Running Association, it is a serious fell running test over open mountain terrain.
