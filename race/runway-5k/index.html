Course

A 5K run on an active runway and taxiway at Centennial Airport in Englewood, Colorado, one of the busiest general aviation airports in the world. The course is flat asphalt airfield pavement, closed to aircraft for the morning of the race.

About

The Runway 5K is an annual running event held at Centennial Airport. Runners and walkers of all levels are welcome to participate in this scenic race along the airport's runways.
