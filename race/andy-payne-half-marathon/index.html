Course

A point-to-point half marathon from the Andy Payne Statue in Foyil to the Claremore Museum of History, with the 5K starting at Claremore Lake and finishing at the same museum. Both courses are USATF sanctioned and certified on sealed roads. Runners are bussed to the start from the First Baptist Church car park.

About

The Andy Payne Half Marathon and 5K honors Cherokee runner Andy Payne, winner of the 1928 transcontinental Bunion Derby footrace along Route 66. Races start in Claremore, Oklahoma, with half marathon, 5K, and one-mile fun run options.
