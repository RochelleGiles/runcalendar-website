Course

A 7 km circuit along the northern bank and the refurbished southern bank of the Yarra River through Burnley and South Yarra, starting and finishing at Loys Paddock Reserve on the Main Yarra Trail. The course is flat to undulating with kilometre markers throughout, and refreshment stations offering water and electrolyte sit at 2.5 km intervals. Longer distances repeat the circuit.

About

The Sri Chinmoy Yarra Trail Run is a scenic, flat to undulating 7km loop course along the Yarra River in the heart of Melbourne. The event offers a fun and friendly atmosphere with age group awards, chip timing, and a pancake breakfast.
