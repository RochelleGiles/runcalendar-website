Course

A single loop around Franklin Park Golf Course in Dorchester, with start and finish beside the golf clubhouse at One Circuit Drive. The chip-timed 5K goes off at 9:00 am and a second wave follows for joggers and walkers. Aid station detail is not published.

About

This Franklin Park race has grown into Boston's largest Thanksgiving Day race, regularly selling out. The chip-timed 5K starts and finishes near the golf clubhouse, welcoming strollers, wheelchairs and leashed dogs, with awards for costumes and fastest dog.
