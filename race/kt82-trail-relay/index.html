Course

Team relay of about 82 miles broken into 18 legs of 2.5 to 7.5 miles, following the Katy Trail west from St Louis County through Augusta and Marthasville to finish in Hermann City Park. Most of it is crushed-limestone rail trail beside the Missouri River bluffs, with four legs on tighter singletrack in St Charles County. Staggered starts from 5:00am.
