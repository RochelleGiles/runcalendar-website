Course

A point-to-point coastal hike along Sydney Harbour and the eastern beaches, walked in teams. The 50km starts at Middle Head, the 30km at Rushcutters Bay and the 20km at Vaucluse, with all distances finishing at Coogee. The route links headlands, hidden bays and rugged coastal track, including the Bondi to Coogee walk.

About

Coastrek Sydney is a team-based charity hiking challenge along Sydney's harbour and eastern coastline. Teams of two to four choose a 20km, 30km or 50km route, all finishing at Coogee, and fund-raise for mental health charity Beyond Blue as they go.
