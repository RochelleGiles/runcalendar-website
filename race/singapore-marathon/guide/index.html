Singapore Marathon

Singapore · Singapore
RoadUrbanMarathon · Half Marathon · 10 km · 5 km

Southeast Asia's Gold Label marathon, run in the dark at 4:30 in the morning past the lit skyline of Marina Bay.

Quick verdict

A flat, fast course in the slowest conditions in world marathoning. Thirty degrees and tropical humidity are the race; the route is the reward. Local places are gone for 2026 and international entry closes 30 September, so the decision is this month.

Course at a glance

4:30amStart
7 hrsCutoff
55,000Field

Flag-off at the F1 Pit Building at 4:30am, out along the Marina Bay waterfront past Marina Bay Sands and the Supertrees of Gardens by the Bay, through the city centre and past the Merlion, then the long straight stretches east before returning to finish inside the National Stadium at Kallang. Flat throughout, with the only rises on the bridges and flyovers.

Where you'll feel it

📍There isn't one — the flyovers are the only rises worth the name.

Sunrise. It arrives around 7am with about two hours run, and the temperature climbs with it. The runners who fade here went out at their cool-country pace.

Best chance to push

The first 25K, in the dark, along the water. The course is fast and the air is as cool as it will be all day.

Three things to know

  1. International slots only for 2026, closing 30 September or sooner if they sell out. Save it as Interested and we'll remind you before the deadline.
  2. It is a Gold Label race that 55,000 people ran in 2025, with a 7-hour cut-off. Aim for the time you'd run on a hot day at home plus fifteen minutes, and you'll enjoy it.
  3. The marathon runs Sunday 6 December; the half marathon and 10K are on other days of the weekend, so a group can each pick their distance and still watch each other.

Race-day weather

26–30°CDecember
80%+Humidity
GoldLabel

Early December is monsoon season: 26–30°C, humidity above 80 percent, and a real chance of a downpour. The 4:30 start is the organiser's answer to the heat, and it works until dawn.

What runners wear

As little as you can. Light vest, shorts, a cap for the sun after 7am. Practise drinking on the move — you will need every station.

Typical finish times

Course records are 2:11:25 for the men (2009) and 2:28:54 for the women (2019). Most of the field finishes between 4:30 and 6:00 — an hour slower than they would run at home.

Worth the trip?

If you want a marathon in a great city with the logistics of a great city, yes. It is the easiest big race in Asia to get to and the hardest to run fast. Treat the time as a footnote.
