Course

Flag-off at the F1 Pit Building at 4:30am and out along the Marina Bay waterfront past Marina Bay Sands and Gardens by the Bay, through the city centre and past the Merlion, then long straight stretches east before returning to finish inside the National Stadium at Kallang. Flat throughout, with the only rises on bridges and flyovers; the heat and humidity, not the course, decide the day.

About

The Singapore Marathon is a prestigious running event that attracts tens of thousands of runners from around the world. The event features a diverse range of race categories, allowing participants to challenge themselves on the streets of Singapore. The course showcases the city's iconic landmarks and vibrant atmosphere, creating an unforgettable experience for runners and spectators alike.
