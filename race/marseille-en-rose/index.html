About

Explore the vibrant streets of Marseille during the annual Marseille En Rose running event. This unique race takes participants on a scenic tour through the historic city, showcasing its stunning Mediterranean coastline and iconic landmarks. Whether you're a seasoned runner or looking for a new challenge, Marseille En Rose promises an unforgettable running experience.
