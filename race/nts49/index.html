About

NTS49 is an ultra trail race in Slovakia's Low Tatras, following the wartime Cesta hrdinov SNP (Route of the SNP Heroes) used during the 1944 Slovak National Uprising. Runners choose between a 49km and a 103km mountain route starting near Telgárt.
