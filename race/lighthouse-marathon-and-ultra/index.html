Course

Point-to-point coast path races finishing at Godrevy: the marathon starts at Pendeen and the 35-mile ultra at Land's End, taking in the Longships, Pendeen and Godrevy lighthouses. Underfoot is rock, sand and tarmac, with about 1,200m of climb for the marathon and over 1,800m for the ultra. Registration is at the Sunset Surf Cafe in Gwithian, with buses to the start.

About

A marathon and 35-mile ultra along the north Cornwall coast from Hayle, passing Godrevy Lighthouse -- the beacon said to have inspired Virginia Woolf's 'To the Lighthouse'. Expect cliffs, coves and rugged coast path underfoot.
