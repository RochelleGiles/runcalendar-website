Course

An out-and-back half marathon starting and finishing at Seeley Lake Elementary School, combining highway and non-highway sections. Runners must clear the highway portion, around mile 5, by 12:15 PM. Winter conditions vary considerably; strollers and dogs on non-retractable leashes are permitted.
