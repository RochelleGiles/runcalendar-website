Course

Starts and finishes at New Belgium Brewing on Craven Street, Asheville. Runners head out and back along the paved West Asheville River Arts District greenway, turning near French Broad River Park where the single water stop sits. Relay teams of four or two hand off at the brewery between 5K legs.

About

The Beer City Relay is a team relay race where teams of 4 or 2 run 5K loops to complete a 20K total at New Belgium Brewing in Asheville, NC. Single runner 5K and 10K options are available, followed by an after-race party with beer for participants 21+.
