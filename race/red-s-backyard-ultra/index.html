Course

Backyard ultra starting at 8:00am at Integral Energy Park, Kembla Grange, with a 6.7 km loop set off every hour. The loop follows a NSW state cross-country course in two sections, the first clockwise and the second anti-clockwise, and is relatively flat and non-technical over a mix of manicured grass and trail. Each runner is allocated a personal aid station in the event hub.

About

Red's Backyard Ultra is an intense last-one-standing endurance event where runners complete a 6.7056 km loop every hour until only one runner remains. Located near Sydney, this unique race tests runners' physical and mental limits, challenging participants to push beyond their perceived boundaries in a grueling test of endurance.
