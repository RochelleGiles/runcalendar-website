Course

Starts near the sound shell, runs through the Azalea Ley Homestead and around most of Manning Park, then climbs the 192 Manning Stairs to ocean views before dropping into bush tracks and returning to the start. The 10km adds a second climb of the stairs and further trails. Expect steep sections and loose gravel. 5km and 10km are offered, run or walk.

About

Manning Park Trail Run is held in Manning Park Reserve, a bushland reserve in suburban Perth, with 5km and 10km trail distances through its network of tracks.
