A running weekend on the Cleethorpes seafront

Save to my season

Course

A marathon and a multi-terrain half marathon run on both days from the Haven Cleethorpes Beach holiday park at Humberston, with massed starts at 9am and relay teams on the Saturday. The lapped course is flat and almost traffic-free, following the park's access roads with a couple of hundred metres of off-road on each lap.
