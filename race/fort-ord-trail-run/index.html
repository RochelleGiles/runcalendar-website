Course

Trail race in Fort Ord National Monument, starting from the Turn 10 Shelf area at WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca. Most of the course is non-technical dirt trail and fire road, with some rutted sections and sand-dune-like climbs. The event is cupless: aid stations carry no cups and runners must carry a bottle. Aid stations stock gels, snacks, electrolyte drink and first aid.

About

Inside Trail Racing's February event in Fort Ord National Monument, on the former Army post above Monterey Bay. Runners take on 10K, 25K and 50K distances over dirt trails and fire roads across the coastal hills. The 50K runs on the Saturday, with the 25K and 10K following on the Sunday.
