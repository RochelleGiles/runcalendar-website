Course

A short, hard category B fell race of 5.2 km with about 800 ft of ascent, starting near the Crown Hotel on Lever Park Avenue in Horwich. The route climbs straight up to Rivington Pike and returns by the same ground.

About

The Rivington Pike Fell Race is a short, steep fell race from Horwich up onto Rivington Moor to the historic Pike Tower, a stone folly with views across Lancashire. It is organised by Horwich R.M.I. Harriers and is a regular fixture on the Lancashire fell-running calendar.
