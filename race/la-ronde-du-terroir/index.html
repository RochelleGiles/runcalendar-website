About

Explore the picturesque French countryside at La Ronde Du Terroir, a beloved running event that showcases the natural beauty of the Combe et Crêtes region. This scenic course winds through rolling hills, charming villages, and lush vineyards, offering runners a unique and unforgettable experience. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or an adventure-seeking runner, this event promises an unforgettable day immersed in the rich culture and stunning landscapes of France.
