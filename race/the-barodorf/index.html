Course

A point-to-point run between the Barossa Valley and Hahndorf in South Australia, across vineyards, rolling hills, native forest and heritage trails of the Adelaide Hills. Distances run from 16km to 120km. The direction alternates by year: even years head south from the Barossa to Hahndorf.

About

The Barodorf is a point-to-point trail ultramarathon running from the Barossa Valley to Hahndorf in the Adelaide Hills, with distances from 16km up to 120km.
