About

The MLK 5K/10K, presented by Blessings In A Bookbag in partnership with Howe2Run Marc Cameron, is held on Martin Luther King Jr. Day in Savannah and is now past its 15th running. The race raises funds and awareness for child hunger, with timing by Endurance Race Service, finisher medals for all participants, and an open format welcoming runners, walkers, skaters and dogs.
