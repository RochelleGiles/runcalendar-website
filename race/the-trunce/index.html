Course

A short off-road evening race of just over 4 miles from the playing fields at Oxspring, near the Wagon and Horses. The route takes in three river crossings and about 400ft of climb and descent. Entry is on the day only, registering before 6pm for a start around 6:45pm.

About

A trail race across the Pennine countryside around Penistone, organised by Penistone Footpath Runners. Known among regulars for its boggy going underfoot, it's built a loyal following as one of South Yorkshire's quirkier trail challenges.
