Course

Two-day festival. Saturday holds trail events, including a 60km point-to-point from Bridgewater Lakes along the Great South West Walk to the Portland foreshore, almost all single track with short beach sections and nine aid stations. Sunday holds road events - marathon, half marathon and 10.5km quarter marathon - with the half following the second half of the marathon course.

About

The Portland 3 Bays Running Festival offers an option to suit every runner. Saturday features trail events along the Great South West Walk, including a spectacular coastal trail run with single track and beach sections. Sunday hosts road events including a historic Marathon, Half Marathon, and a flat, fast Quarter Marathon.
