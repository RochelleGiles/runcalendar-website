About

Le Défi du Fort L'Ecluse is a unique trail running event set in an exceptional historical site. Runners traverse spectacular landscapes near the classified site of the Vierge de Léaz overlooking the Rhône river and the Ecluse gorge. The race's highlight is a challenging passage through the incredible underground gallery of Fort l'Ecluse, featuring 917 stairs.
