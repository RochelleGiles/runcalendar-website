Course

Half marathon, quarter marathon, 5K, one mile and a kids dash all start and finish at Walker Place Park in Bossier City. The route weaves past lakes and stands of trees on a mix of pavement, crushed granite and occasional dirt path. Aid stations along the way provide hydration.

About

Get ready for the "Day of the Dead Half Marathon: Parks Edition"! Starting at 8 a.m., this 13.1-mile race takes you through some of the most stunning parks in the region—where every step is a celebration of life and tradition. The peaceful, picturesque surroundings create a unique running experience, allowing you to immerse yourself in the tranquility of nature. Crossing the finish line, you'll be welcomed into a lively post-race celebration at the rally tent.
