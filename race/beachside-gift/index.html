About

The Beachside Gift is a premier running event held along the picturesque coastline of Australia. Runners will experience a challenging course with stunning ocean views, passing by iconic landmarks and lively beachside communities. This event is known for its vibrant atmosphere and enthusiastic local support, making it a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable experience.
