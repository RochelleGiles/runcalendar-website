About

Cupid's Chase 5K is a national race series celebrating the founding of Community Options, raising funds to provide housing and employment opportunities for individuals with disabilities. Now past its 18th year, the series welcomes runners, walkers, and participants with strollers or wheelchairs, held each February in Derry.
