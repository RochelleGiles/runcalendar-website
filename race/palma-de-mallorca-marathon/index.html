About

The Palma de Mallorca Marathon offers runners a stunning coastal course through the historic city of Palma, showcasing the island's breathtaking Mediterranean scenery. This premier running event invites athletes of all levels to experience the vibrant culture and stunning architecture of Mallorca as they conquer 26.2 miles along the island's picturesque shores.
