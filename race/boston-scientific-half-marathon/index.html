About

The Boston Scientific Half Marathon in Clonmel follows a flat, scenic route taking in country and urban areas, with the start and finish in the centre of Clonmel. Organised by Clonmel Athletic Club, it is a popular build-up race for those training for the Dublin City Marathon.
