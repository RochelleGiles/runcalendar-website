Course

Run on the Bristol & Bath Railway Path, a 100% tarmac former railway line including a 450m tunnel and restored signal boxes. Most distances are out-and-back from Green Park Station in Bath; the 25km is point-to-point, finishing at Bristol Temple Meads. Between two and nine aid stations depending on distance.

About

Explore the historic Bath & Bristol Railway Races this spring, following the scenic route of the original train line from Green Park Station in Bath to Bristol Temple Meads. Runners of all levels can experience the beautiful countryside and charming towns along this unique course, making for an unforgettable running adventure in the United Kingdom.
