About

A popular 15 km trail run open to all those who are not afraid to get their sneakers dirty, set in the exceptional setting of the Abbaye de Trizay. The event also includes a pedestrian walk of 8 to 13 km for companions and walkers who wish to share the moment. A portion of the proceeds will be donated to a local charity.
