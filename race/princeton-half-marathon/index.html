Course

A USATF-certified course held entirely within Princeton, starting at Paul Robeson Place at 7:00 AM. The route passes Princeton Battlefield, the Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton University and Lake Carnegie. Aid stations are not described; runners are asked to carry their own water to reduce waste.
