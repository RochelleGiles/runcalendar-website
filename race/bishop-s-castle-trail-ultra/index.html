Course

A 50 km trail ultra from Fox Holes Castle Camping in Bishop's Castle with about 5,000 ft of climb, mixing quiet back roads, forestry, meadows and single-track on parts of the Shropshire Way, Offa's Dyke Path and the Kerry Ridgeway. Three aid stations sit at miles 9, 15 and 24.

About

This challenging 50K trail ultra takes runners through the stunning landscapes of Shropshire, featuring approximately 5,000 feet of elevation. The route traverses tranquil back roads, serene trails, lush forests, and vibrant meadows, showcasing the breathtaking beauty of the region. Participants will explore parts of the Shropshire Way, Offa's Dyke path, and the historic Kerry Ridgeway, experiencing some of the most scenic trails in the area.
