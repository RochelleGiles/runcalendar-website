Course

A 6.71km loop starting and finishing at Butler Green in Arrowtown, run once every hour on the hour until one runner remains. The loop follows the Arrow River Trail, described as predominantly hard-packed trail plus a winding single track through the bush. Runners cross Bush Creek twice and use two pedestrian bridges over the Arrow River.

About

The Arrowtown Backyard Ultra is a last-person-standing race run on a 6.71km loop from Butler Green in Arrowtown. Runners must complete one loop each hour, on the hour, and are eliminated when they fail to start or finish a loop in time. The loop follows the Arrow River Trail and crosses Bush Creek twice.
