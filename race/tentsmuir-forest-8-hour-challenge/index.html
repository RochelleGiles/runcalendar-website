Course

An eight-hour lapped challenge on a 4.366-mile loop, run entirely on trail paths through Tentsmuir Forest and almost flat underfoot. Runners complete as many loops as they can: three laps makes half-marathon distance, six a marathon, seven or more an ultra.

About

An eight-hour timed running challenge on trails through Tentsmuir Forest, a coastal nature reserve on the Fife coast near Tayport. Runners complete as many laps of the forest and dune trails as possible within the time limit.
