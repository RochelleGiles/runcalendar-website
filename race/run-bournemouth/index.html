Course

A seafront festival on the Bournemouth coast. The half marathon runs along the coastline past Boscombe and Bournemouth piers and finishes at Pier Approach. The flat 10K follows the same seafront route, and the 5K starts on Bournemouth Prom at dusk under lighting and finishes in the Lower Gardens.

About

Run Bournemouth is a premier running weekend in the heart of the vibrant coastal town of Bournemouth, United Kingdom. Featuring a variety of distances including the Bournemouth Half Marathon, 10K, 5K, and Junior Races, this event offers something for runners of all levels to challenge themselves against the stunning seaside backdrop.
