About

This annual trail running event in Cublize offers two timed race courses - a 12km trail with 250m of elevation gain and a 24km trail with 750m of elevation gain. The event also features a timed Nordic walking course and a free-paced walking option. Runners and walkers can enjoy the scenic trails, forests, and lake views in the summer season.
