Course

Flat 5K, 10K and half marathon routes run almost entirely on the footpaths of Clapham Common, passing the bandstand, with the same start and finish for each distance. Every kilometre is marked with distance flags. The half sets off at 9am, the 5K and 10K at 9.30am.

About

Multiple laps of Clapham Common, a large green space in south London, on a flat, fast course. A popular starting point for runners building toward a first 10K or half marathon.
