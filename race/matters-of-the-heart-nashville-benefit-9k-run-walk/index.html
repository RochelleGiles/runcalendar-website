Course

The course is contained entirely within Hadley Lillard Park, on a park path. The 9K completes ten loops of the course and the 1 mile walk completes three. Participants count their own loops and keep right except when passing.

About

Runners and walkers of all ages are invited to come together to raise awareness of community resources, such as the Elam Mental Health Center, community development projects, and local businesses that support the well being of North Nashville.
