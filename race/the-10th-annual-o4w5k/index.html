Course

A 5K on closed city streets through Atlanta's Old Fourth Ward. The course starts and finishes on North Avenue opposite Ponce City Market, passing Historic Fourth Ward Park and the Martin Luther King Jr. Historic District. Runners are coned into one lane on North Avenue at the start and keep right on Glen Iris. A Kids Fun Run precedes the 5K.

About

The O4W5K starts in Historic Fourth Ward Park and runs through Atlanta's historic Old Fourth Ward neighborhood. The race can be run in person or completed virtually from anywhere.
