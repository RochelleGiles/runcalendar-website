Course

A 5K loop on the trails at Caw Caw Interpretive Center, west of Charleston. Footing shifts between hard-packed trail with roots, grass and boardwalk, with occasional stretches of uneven ground, and there are water stations on course. The race starts at 8:30am with a kids' dash alongside it.

About

Unleash your wild side and hit the trails during Where the Wild Things Run Trail 5K and Kids Dash at Caw Caw Interpretive Center. After the 5K, we invite kids ages 3-7 of all abilities to dash across the finish at the Wild Kids Dash.
