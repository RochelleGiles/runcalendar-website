About

This walk/run is held the first Saturday of October starting at McMinnville High School's Wortman Stadium. The 5k course is a down-and-back loop heading south toward Linfield University and finishing at the MHS stadium with cheerleaders and the high school band welcoming finishers. There's also a track loop option for younger students.
