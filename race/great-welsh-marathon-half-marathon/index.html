About

The Great Welsh Marathon and Half Marathon is South Wales' oldest and most beloved running event, taking place in the stunning Carmarthenshire countryside. Runners of all levels can challenge themselves on the scenic, rolling course, with the option to tackle the full marathon or the half marathon distance. This is a must-do event for any runner seeking an unforgettable experience in the heart of the Welsh countryside.
