About

The Run Your Tutu Off 5K event is perfect for runners and walkers of all levels while supporting Furniture Share's BEDS FOR KIDS program. BEDS FOR KIDS provides a bed, pillow, sheets and blankets, for children in emergency situations who often sleep on the floor. Children not having a bed result's in a poor night's sleep, affecting their ability to focus in school and thrive. Through our program, we provide over 1,500 Beds for Kids each year. Ongoing support of the program is crucial as that number continues to grow.
