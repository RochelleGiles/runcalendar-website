Course

Three looped courses of 7km, 10.3km and 14.5km through the Whakarewarewa Redwoods on the edge of Rotorua, run entirely on well-formed forest trail — wide avenues linked by flowing singletrack over soft forest floor and fern undergrowth. Climbing is modest, from about 86m on the short course to 236m on the long.

About

Just minutes from Rotorua's centre, the towering California Redwoods create a trail network unlike anywhere else in New Zealand. The course winds through these majestic giants, their soaring trunks filtering dappled light onto soft forest floors and fern-filled undergrowth. With a mix of wide avenues and flowing singletrack, every turn brings a new perspective of this enchanting forest. Famous for its beauty and accessibility, the Redwoods are a playground for walkers, runners, and riders alike — a truly iconic trail destination.
