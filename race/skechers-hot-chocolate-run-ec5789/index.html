Course

Point-to-point through Philadelphia, starting at Eakins Oval on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and finishing near the Rocky statue at the Philadelphia Museum of Art. Routes run along the Schuylkill River past the Washington Monument; the 5K turns around near Historic Strawberry Mansion. The 5K starts at 7:15am and the 10K and 15K at 8:00am.

About

The SKECHERS Hot Chocolate Run in Philadelphia offers 5K, 10K, and 15K distance options along the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and Schuylkill River. It's a treat-themed race for chocolate lovers of every pace.
