About

The Carney's Cares Weekend Extravaganza is a multi-day event featuring a 5K run, polar plunge, and festive activities like Beef and Beer and Kegs and Eggs at Carney's in Cape May. It supports local nonprofits such as the Cape May Food Closet through fun, community-oriented fundraising.
