Course

Point-to-point 14 km from the corner of Park and College Streets in central Sydney to Queen Elizabeth Drive at Bondi Beach. The route follows city roads through the Kings Cross Tunnel and over Heartbreak Hill at the halfway mark before dropping to the beach finish.

About

The Voltaren City2Surf Sydney is the world's largest fun run, attracting over 80,000 participants each year. The iconic course takes runners on a scenic journey through the heart of Sydney, showcasing the city's stunning harbor views and iconic landmarks. With a vibrant atmosphere and a focus on community and camaraderie, the City2Surf is a must-do event for runners of all abilities.
