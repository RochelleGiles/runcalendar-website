About

The Runningworks Event Series features multiple running events throughout Perth, including trail runs, road races, and duathlons. Events take place at iconic locations like Lark Hill, Canning River, Bibra Lake, and Champion Lakes Regatta Centre. The series offers diverse race experiences from evening party ultras to technical trail marathons, with courses designed to be spectator-friendly and family-oriented.
