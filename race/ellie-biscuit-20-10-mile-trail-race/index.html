Course

Two laps of a 10-mile loop in Point Washington State Forest, starting near the Eastern Lake trailhead. The course mixes singletrack with forest road over rocks, roots, stumps, mud and deep sand. The 10-mile covers one loop, the 20-mile two.

About

The Ellie Biscuit was created to fill a gap in the 30A area, which previously had no trail races of 10 miles or longer. The idea began as a conversation among friends and has grown into an established fixture near Inlet Beach, offering 10-mile and 20-mile distances.
