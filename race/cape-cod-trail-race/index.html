Course

An all-trail event in the Frances A. Crane Wildlife Management Area, starting from 754 Nathan Ellis Highway in Falmouth, Massachusetts. Runners complete laps of a single loop course: one lap for the 10K, two for the 20K and three for the 30K.

About

Held at the Crane Wildlife Management Area in East Falmouth, this all-trail race combines fast field sections with hilly, technical singletrack in the middle stretch. Runners share the trails with hikers, bikers, and horseback riders, and the event partners with Pride on Foot.
