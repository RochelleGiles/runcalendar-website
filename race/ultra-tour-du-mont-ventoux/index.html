About

The Mont Ventoux is a mythical mountain, known worldwide especially thanks to the epics of cyclists and its incredible scenarios it could offer. But the Mont Ventoux is above all the Giant of Provence, which can be seen from very far away at an altitude of 1910m, as there is no summit as high within a 50 km radius. By running the UTMV, you will always have an eye on the Mont-Ventoux, it will be a true totem, you will see it from all sides, in all directions and it will not let you go until the finish line. The UTMV is one of the few Ultra Trails in France to offer the tour of a mythical mountain, which will amaze you forever.
