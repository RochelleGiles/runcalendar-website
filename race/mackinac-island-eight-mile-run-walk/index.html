Course

A clockwise loop of Mackinac Island's car-free perimeter road, starting and finishing at Mission Point Resort. The first mile passes the harbor and downtown before the route follows the Straits of Mackinac shoreline; flat, with three water stations roughly every two miles. Runners and walkers have three hours, though the finish stays open for the last finisher. 9:30am start.

About

One of the oldest and most scenic races in Michigan, the Mackinac Island Eight Mile follows the perimeter of Mackinac Island with breathtaking views of the Mackinac Bridge and historic sites. The race features a fast and flat course with over 1,800 participants and includes running, walking, and virtual race options.
