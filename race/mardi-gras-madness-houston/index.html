About

Mardi Gras Madness is a Mardi Gras-themed 5K and 10K held at Oyster Creek Park in Sugar Land, Texas, on the Saturday before Shrove Tuesday. The event also offers a 1-mile run and a kids dash, with a rally tent for music, snacks and the awards ceremony.
