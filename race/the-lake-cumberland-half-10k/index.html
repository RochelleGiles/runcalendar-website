Course

A point-to-point half marathon starting on the Houseboat Capital of the World Bridge in Bronston, crossing Lake Cumberland and running through Burnside and Ferguson to finish at Somerset Community College. The 10K joins the same route at Jacksboro Street in Ferguson. Aid stations sit near miles 2.5, 5.3, 8.1, 9.8 and 11.6, and the course is not closed to traffic.

About

A half marathon and 10K in Somerset, Kentucky, run along the shoreline of Lake Cumberland, one of the largest reservoirs in the eastern United States and a popular Kentucky boating and fishing destination.
