Course

Roughly 95% singletrack through Strouds Run State Park near Athens. The 50K runs the Thunderbunny Trail, loops Dow Lake and crosses the Athens Ridge before three late climbs at Finger Rock, Hope Drive and Athens Trail. The 25K and 11K are shortened versions over the same trails, each with about a mile of pavement. Multiple aid stations on all distances.
