About

Les Foulées Mézidonnaises is an event that welcomes all types of runners, from beginners looking to start a physical activity to the most competitive. It offers various running practices such as road running, trail running, cross-country, and track. The event also has an Athletics School starting from 6 years old to practice all disciplines of athletics, from running to throwing.
