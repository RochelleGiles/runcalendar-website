Course

A 24-hour lapped relay at Bramham Park near Leeds, run as solo, pairs or teams of three to twelve over an 8km lap. The circuit follows wide, runnable trails through a mix of forest and open heathland, passing the 18th-century Temple of the Lead Lads and the Roundhouse. Camping on site runs across the weekend.

About

Running Solo, in Pairs, or Teams of up to 12, you'll have 24 hours to wrack up as many laps of our stunning 8km trail course as you can. Whether you're a first-time fun runner or a seasoned ultramarathoner, the only question that matters is how far will you go?
