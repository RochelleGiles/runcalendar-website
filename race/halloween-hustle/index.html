Course

Twilight and night event with two loop courses starting and finishing at the criterium circuit. The 2.5km Wolf Pack Prowl uses the criterium track and Casuarina Grove Circuit inside the national park. The 6.2km Zombie Shuffle adds dirt paths, single-track switchbacks and a dirt road with a small creek crossing, with a drink station at the crest. Torches are compulsory.

About

The Halloween Hustle is a twilight and night trail run held on a criterium circuit and adjoining national park trails on the Gold Coast. Runners choose a 2.5km junior loop or a 6.2km loop taking in single-track, dirt roads and a creek crossing. All entrants must carry or wear a torch.
