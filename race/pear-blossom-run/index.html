Course

Flat, fast out-and-back road races starting and finishing at Oakdale and West 8th in Medford, Oregon, held on the second Saturday of April. The USATF-certified 5K has a 60-minute limit and the 10-mile, which carries one hill, allows 160 minutes. One-mile and two-mile Mayor's Cup fun runs use flat out-and-back courses within the city.

About

The Pear Blossom Run is the Rogue Valley Family YMCA's largest fundraising event, featuring 5K and 10 Mile races along with the Mayor's Cup Fun Runs. It brings together runners and walkers from Oregon and beyond in Medford as part of the annual Pear Blossom Festival.
