Course

Teams cover 100km along most of the MacLehose Trail and connecting paths within a 48-hour limit, starting at Pak Tam Chung in Sai Kung and finishing at Harrow International School in Tuen Mun. Nine checkpoints and one control point mark the route.

About

The original Trailwalker event, first run in 1981 by British Gurkha soldiers, now Oxfam's flagship 100km fundraising trek along the MacLehose Trail through the New Territories.
