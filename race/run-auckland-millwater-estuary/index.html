Course

Lapped course starting and finishing at Metro Park, Millwater, north of Auckland. The 10.5 km and 5 km run a single lap and the half marathon runs two. Roads stay open to traffic throughout and entrants must stay on the footpath for the whole course.

About

Run Auckland: Millwater Estuary is part of The Athletes Foot Run Auckland Event Series, featuring multiple race distances around the Millwater Estuary. The event offers a half marathon, 10.5km, 5km and kids dash options, making it suitable for runners and walkers of all abilities. The course takes participants through the scenic Millwater area with a multi-lap format for longer distances.
