Course

The course uses existing mountain bike park trails at Kooralbyn in Queensland's Scenic Rim, running on single track and dirt through forest with lake and mountain views. Terrain is challenging, and the routes are fully marshalled and course-marked.

About

Explore the stunning Kooralbyn region in South East Queensland at the On The Edge Trail Running Series. This challenging yet picturesque event showcases the area's rugged terrain and breathtaking natural landscapes, offering runners an unforgettable off-road adventure.
