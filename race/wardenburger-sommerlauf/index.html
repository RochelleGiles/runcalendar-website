About

The Wardenburger Sommerlauf is a popular running event that offers a variety of race distances, including a 400m Bambini run, 1.5km Pax-Schülerlauf, 5km FFM - Walking/Nordic-Walking, 5km LzO-Jedermanns lauf, 10km Öffentliche Lauf, and a 10-mile EWE 10-Meilen-Lauf. The event is known for its scenic course and welcoming atmosphere.
