Course

Run on Antelope Island in the Great Salt Lake, with the lake on one side and the island's mountain spine on the other. Unlike many Utah marathons it is not a downhill course — the route rolls throughout. Marathon, half, 10K and 5K distances all run on the island.

About

The Antelope Island Marathon runs on Antelope Island, a state park in the Great Salt Lake reached by causeway from Syracuse, Utah. The course crosses open, rolling terrain shared with a free-roaming bison herd, with marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K distances.
