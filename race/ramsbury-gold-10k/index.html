Course

A multi-terrain 10K through the Ramsbury Estate in Wiltshire, starting beside the Tap Room and taking in local trails, woodland and footpaths before finishing back at the brewery. The course is 85 percent off road with around 180 m of climb, and has two water refill stations. A canicross wave runs the same route.

About

The Ramsbury Gold 10K is a scenic trail running event set in the beautiful surroundings of the Ramsbury Estate. Runners enjoy a multi-terrain course that winds through local trails, woodland, and footpaths, offering a challenging yet accessible route for runners of all abilities. The event is unique for its Canicross-friendly approach and the memorable finish at the Ramsbury Brewery.
