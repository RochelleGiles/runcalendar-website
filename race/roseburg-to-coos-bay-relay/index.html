Course

Point-to-point relay on rural roads in southern Oregon. Running teams start at KPIC-TV in Roseburg and walking teams at Laverne Park in Coquille, with both finishing at the Coos Bay History Museum. Course markings are minimal; facilities along the route are portable and flush toilets.

About

The Roseburg to Coos Bay Relay is a 68-mile, 5-person running team relay or a 24-mile, 4-person walking team relay with a staggered start based on predicted finishing times. The scenic point-to-point course follows quiet country roads along a river, featuring waterfalls, big trees, and serious hills.
