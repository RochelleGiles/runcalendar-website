About

The Spectacle running festival returns to the picturesque region of Whakatu-Nelson, New Zealand, offering trail running and road racing options for runners of all abilities. Explore the stunning natural landscapes of the region as you challenge yourself on diverse courses, making this an event not to be missed for outdoor enthusiasts. Whether you're seeking a scenic trail run or a fast-paced road race, The Spectacle has something to captivate every runner.
