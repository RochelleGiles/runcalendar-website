About

The Green Door 5K is an annual road race at Harry Myers Park in Rockwall, Texas, offering 5K, 10K and one-mile fun run distances. The course loops the park and nearby neighbourhoods. Proceeds support Children's Relief International's Green Door Project, which builds homes for families in poverty.
