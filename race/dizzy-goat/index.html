Course

Timed trail runs of 3, 6 or 12 hours on a roughly 3-mile wooded loop in the Omaha, Nebraska area, where runners complete as many loops as they can within their chosen time. Proceeds support local trail systems.

About

Proceeds from Dizzy GOAT go into the general GOATz fund to help the local trail systems. The G.O.A.T.z are also committed to providing our time and energy to help better the Omaha area trail system.
