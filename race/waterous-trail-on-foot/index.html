About

The WTF 50 & 100 Miler is the original 100-mile trail race in Western Australia, held annually on the long weekend in September. The 100-mile, 50-mile and 50km races depart together at 6am on the Saturday, based at Nanga Bush Camp and run in three out-and-back legs predominantly on the Munda Biddi trail between Jarrahdale and Dwellingup.
