Course

Point-to-point over Rim Rock Drive through Colorado National Monument: the marathon leaves Red Canyon Overlook at 7:30 am and the half the Saddlehorn entrance at 9:00 am, both finishing at the Fruita Community Center. Intermediate cutoffs apply; select aid stations take gear drops.

About

The Rim Rock Marathon and Half Marathon course follows the famous Rim Rock Drive through the Colorado National Monument, giving runners beautiful views of the Grand Valley. The views along the race course are unmatched and perfect for runners looking for something MONUMENTAL.
