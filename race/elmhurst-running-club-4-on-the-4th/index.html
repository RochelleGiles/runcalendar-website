Course

A USATF-certified four-mile road course (IL-19004NM) through the Elmhurst University campus and downtown Elmhurst. The route is primarily flat with three small hills, with mile markers clearly signed and water stations at the start, finish and near the 1.5 and 2.5-mile marks. A kids' 1K runs out and back on Prospect Avenue.

About

The Elmhurst Running Club's Fourth of July four-mile race through the streets of Elmhurst, Illinois, paired with a kids' 1K. Now marking two decades, it is a long-standing Independence Day tradition for the Chicago suburb.
