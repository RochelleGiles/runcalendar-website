Course

Trails through the Fort Assiniboine Sandhills Wildland Provincial Park in Alberta, on ancient moss-covered sand dunes, single track and an old wagon trail through boreal forest. The route drops into valley bottoms with steep climbs and fast descents and runs beside Clearwater Creek and the Athabasca River, passing old trappers cabins and grave sites.

About

The Klondike Ultra is an off-road endurance event in the Fort Assiniboine Sandhills Wildland Provincial Park, north-west of Edmonton in Alberta. Distances run from a 6 km dash up to a 100 km ultra, on sand dunes, single track and a historic wagon trail through boreal forest.
