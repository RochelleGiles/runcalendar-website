About

The Tdls Siberian Night Trail Ultra Trail is a challenging ultra-trail event that takes runners through the wild landscapes of the Molignée, Bocq Valley, and Hermetton regions. With distances ranging from 35km to an impressive 160km, this event offers a unique and immersive experience, with runners navigating through deep forests, snowy peaks, and illuminated villages. The event features well-equipped aid stations and a celebratory pasta party at the finish line, making it a must-do for those seeking to push their limits in a festive and unforgiving atmosphere.
