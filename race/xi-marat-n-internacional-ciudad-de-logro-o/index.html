Course

A road race through the streets and neighbourhoods of Logroño, capital of La Rioja. The marathon and half marathon start and finish on the Paseo de El Espolón; the 10K starts at the roundabout between Avenida de Lobete and Obispo Fidel García. The race sets off at 09:00, with cut-offs of 5 hours for the marathon, 2h30 for the half and 1h30 for the 10K.

About

The Maratón Ciudad de Logroño winds twice through the streets of Logroño, capital of Spain's La Rioja wine region, with the half marathon covering a single lap of the same circuit and a 10K completing half of it. Live music and local Rioja hospitality line the route at numerous entertainment points along the way.
