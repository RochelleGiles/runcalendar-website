About

The Foulées Charentonnaises is a running event that takes place in the picturesque town of Charenton-le-Pont, near the Bois de Vincennes in Paris. This 41st edition promises an exciting and eco-responsible experience, with a focus on health and sport for all. The event offers a range of distances, from the dynamic Mile to the challenging 15km, catering to runners of all levels. Participants can look forward to a vibrant atmosphere, passionate volunteers, and an opportunity to push their limits in a beautiful setting.
