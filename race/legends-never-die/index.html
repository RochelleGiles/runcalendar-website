Course

Single-track forest and alpine trails out of Oregon Gulch near Easley Hot Springs, north of Ketchum. The half marathon follows the Big Wood River, climbs the Oregon Fox Connector and loops back for a fast finish, gaining 2,450 ft to a high point of 7,600 ft. The 55K adds circuits around Fox Peak, with 6,781 ft of gain and a 8,650 ft high point. Snow is possible in early October.

About

A fall trail running event held in the mountains of Sun Valley featuring a half marathon and 55km ultra marathon on scenic single-track trails. The event supports local charities and takes place during peak autumn colors in the Idaho backcountry.
