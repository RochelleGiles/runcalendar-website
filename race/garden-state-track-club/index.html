About

Garden State Track Club is a New Jersey running club with youth, open, elite and professional squads. It hosts the Garden State 10 Miler race day at Colonial Park in Franklin Township, offering a 10 mile course, a 5K, a youth mile and a relay, and also stages the Garden State Open indoor track meet.
