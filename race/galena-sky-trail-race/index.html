Course

A timed race on the grounds of Chestnut Mountain Resort, Galena. Runners repeat a 2.91 mile loop, with a 0.6 mile short loop available from 3:30pm; start and finish are at the resort base, passed between laps. The surface is mainly singletrack with a short crushed gravel section, some grass and one sidewalk crossing. One aid station.

About

The Galena Sky Trail Race is a timed trail event on the slopes of Chestnut Mountain Resort in Galena, Illinois. Runners cover as many 2.91 mile loops as they can within four or eight hours, on singletrack above the Mississippi River. A finisher meal is served at the resort restaurant.
