Course

Start and finish at Barwon Valley Park on Barrabool Road, Belmont, following riverside paths along the Barwon River. The 10 km starts at 8:00am and the 5 km run/walk at 8:45am. Drink stations on the 10 km sit at about 1.5 km, 3.5 km, 6.6 km and 8.5 km.

About

Lace up your running shoes and join the River Run Geelong, a scenic event that winds through the picturesque Barwon Valley Park. Runners can choose between a challenging 10km run or a more relaxed 5km run/walk, offering something for all fitness levels. This community-focused event is a must-do for anyone seeking a beautiful, riverside running adventure.
