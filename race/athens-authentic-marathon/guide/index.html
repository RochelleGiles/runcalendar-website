Athens Authentic Marathon

Athina, Attica · Greece
RoadHistoricMarathon · 10K · 5K

The road the distance is named after — and the longest climb in major marathoning waiting in the middle of it.

Quick verdict

You run the original course and finish on marble where the 1896 Olympics ended. You also climb for twenty-one unbroken kilometres to get there. This is a pilgrimage, not a personal best. Treat it as one and it is the best day's running in the sport.

Course at a glance

21 kmClimb, km 10–31
248mHigh point
8 hrsCutoff

Point-to-point from Marathon town to Athens. A loop past the Marathon Tomb, coastal through Nea Makri, then inland and upward through Pikermi and Pallini to a high point of 248 metres near Agia Paraskevi at 31 km — then eleven kilometres of descent into the city and onto the track of the Panathenaic Stadium.

Where you'll feel it

📍Nea Makri to Agia Paraskevi, kilometres 10 to 31.

Twenty-one kilometres of it, never steep enough to justify walking, and it simply does not stop. The first five kilometres fall away downhill, so you arrive at ten ahead of pace and spend the next two hours giving it back.

Best chance to push

From 31 km, when the course crests and tips 155 metres down into Athens. Genuinely fast — but only for runners whose quads survived the climb.

Three things to know

  1. Do not spend the first ten kilometres. They drop 26 metres and feel free; the block from 18 to 31 km then climbs 256. Run the opening 10 to 15 seconds per kilometre slower than goal and you will be passing people at 30.
  2. Entry is a 36-hour window once a year. All 25,000 places for 2026 went in under 36 hours and the €165 package went in six. That package is the only tier carrying priority entry for the following year.
  3. The gun is 08:30, half an hour earlier than 2025, and buses leave central Athens around six. Plan on a five o'clock alarm and two hours waiting at the battlefield in the cool before you run.

Race-day weather

17–22°CAthenian autumn
8:30amStart
25,000Runners

Early November in Attica runs 17 to 22°C with real humidity on the coastal opening. You climb into a rising sun for two hours, and by 25 to 31 km you are in the warmest, most exposed part of the day.

What runners wear

Singlet and shorts; this is a warm race, not an autumn one. Throwaway layers for the long wait at Marathon, a cap for the exposed middle, and nothing that traps heat on the climb.

Typical finish times

The men's record is 2:10:34 from 2023; the women's 2:31:06 has stood since 2010. The mass field averages around 4:30, and the course models roughly ten minutes slower than a flat one. Eight hours, loosely enforced.

Worth the trip?

Yes, once, for the history and the stadium. Not for a time — you will run slower here than almost anywhere. Go knowing that, and the last 170 metres on Olympic marble will be the best of your running life.
