About

Explore the historic streets and scenic trails of Belves during the annual Trail Urbain De Belves. This urban trail running event offers a unique challenge as participants navigate the charming medieval town and surrounding countryside. With stunning views and a lively atmosphere, this race is a must-do for trail enthusiasts looking for an unforgettable running adventure.
