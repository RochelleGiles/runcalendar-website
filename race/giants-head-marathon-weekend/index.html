Course

Hilly trail routes through the Sydling and Cerne valleys, starting and finishing at the village hall in Sydling St Nicholas, Dorset. Steep climbs and sharp descents on a fully marked course with well-stocked aid stations. Marathon and 10K run on the Saturday, half marathon and the LittleFeet Bell Race on the Sunday.

About

Conquer the iconic Giants Head in Sydling St Nicholas, Dorchester during this captivating weekend running event. Tackle the challenging marathon route amidst the stunning Dorset countryside, or opt for the half marathon and relish the breathtaking views. This popular event offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
