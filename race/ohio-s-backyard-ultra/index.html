Course

Last-person-standing format at McChesney Ridge Athletic Club, with a new yard started each hour. The 4.17 mile day course is two loops of mixed double-track trail, gravel, grass and dirt with 450-475 feet of gain; the night course is an out-and-back on road with 150 feet of gain.

About

Ohio's Backyard Ultra is part of the 'Road to Big's' series, serving as a Bronze Ticket Race feeding into the Ode to Laz Backyard Ultra. In this last-person-standing format, runners must complete a 4.1667-mile loop every hour until only one remains.
