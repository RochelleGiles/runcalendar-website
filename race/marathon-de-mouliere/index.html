Course

Entirely off-road racing in the Moulière forest near Montamisé, a 7,000-hectare massif, on 100% forest paths. The 42.17km marathon (462m of climb, run solo, in pairs or as a trio) alternates direction from one edition to the next; a 20km trail with one aid station, an 8km discovery trail and two children's races complete the programme.

About

The Marathon De Mouliere is a unique running event that takes place in the vast and ancient Moulière forest in France. Organized by three local running clubs, the marathon and other races offer runners the chance to explore this scenic and historic landscape, passing by legendary sites like the Gouffre du Grand Soubis and the Fosse aux Loups. With a 100% trail course and the beautiful autumn foliage as a backdrop, this event promises an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
