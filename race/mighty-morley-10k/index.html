Mighty Morley 10K

Morley, England · United Kingdom
RoadUrban

Course

A chip-timed 10K on fully closed roads around the historic streets of Morley, starting on Wide Lane near the town centre and finishing by the Town Hall. The undulating course has two out-and-back sections and an unusual three-way crossover at Morley Bottoms. Fully marshalled and signposted, with a drinks station on course.
