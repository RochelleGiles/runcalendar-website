Course

Three courses on Inverary Station at the base of the Moorhouse and Peter Ranges. All start near Blondini Stream, climb to a plateau, then descend through stream valleys with single-track sections to finish near the station road. Terrain is rugged rocky trail, open tussock and matagouri, with multiple creek crossings on the longer routes. Aid at 6 km and 15.8 km.

About

The Canterbury Winter Trail Run Series includes events at different locations near Christchurch. This leg is based at Inverary Station near Mount Somers, delivering a true Canterbury high-country adventure through rugged rocky trails, open tussock and matagouri country, with multiple creek crossings on the longer routes.
