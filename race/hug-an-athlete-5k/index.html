Course

Run on the Belvidere Park District Kishwaukee Riverfront paths, starting on the bike path near the Doty Park Pavilion and crossing the river into Belvidere Park. The paths are sealed and ADA accessible, with a water station at about 1.2 miles and markers at miles 1 and 2.

About

A 5K run/walk/roll event benefiting the Belvidere Park District's Special Recreation Pirates Special Olympics athletes. Participants meet Special Olympics athletes at the finish line for hugs, fist bumps, and high fives.
