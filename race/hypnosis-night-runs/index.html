Course

Loops of rolling desert singletrack from the Estrella Mountain Competitive Track, run through the night under starlight among saguaro cacti. The trail mixes fast smooth sections with steeper mountainous ground. Distances run from 6K to 52K.

About

Hypnosis Night Runs is an overnight trail race hosted by Aravaipa Running in Goodyear, Arizona, part of the Insomniac night-running series. Runners head out after dark for distances from 6K to 52K across Sonoran Desert terrain west of Phoenix.
