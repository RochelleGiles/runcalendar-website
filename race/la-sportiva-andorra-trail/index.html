About

The La Sportiva Andorra Trail offers runners a breathtaking adventure through the stunning mountain landscapes of Andorra. Traversing rugged terrain and challenging ascents, this event promises an unforgettable experience for trail enthusiasts seeking to push their limits in a beautiful natural setting. Runners will be captivated by the diverse landscapes and the chance to explore the hidden gems of this remarkable region.
