Course

Run at Duncarron Medieval Village near Carron Valley on a 5km circuit of forest road, singletrack and trail beside the reservoir. The terrain is non-technical and runnable. Runners complete as many laps as they can in five hours, starting in daylight and finishing after dark. A Knights Quest half marathon and a children's race also run.

About

The Iron Keep Medieval Trail Running Festival is a trail running weekend in Denny, Stirlingshire, Scotland, featuring a Half Marathon and a timed 5 Hour Siege endurance event, organised by Outsider Events.
