About

Escape to the stunning Queyras mountains for the Trail des Étoiles, a captivating trail running event that takes participants on a journey through a breathtaking alpine landscape. Runners will be awed by the stunning vistas of snow-capped peaks and serene alpine lakes as they challenge themselves on this scenic course, making this a must-do event for trail enthusiasts.
