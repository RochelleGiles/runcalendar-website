About

Glow the Distance for MATS is a chip-timed evening 5K starting at 8:00pm from the Morristown Utilities parking lot in Morristown, Tennessee. The course has mild elevation changes and can be run or walked, and funds raised support the MATS Self-Sufficiency Program.
