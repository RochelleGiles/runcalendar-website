About

Alerte Zombies is a themed night trail through Nœux-les-Mines' Loisinord leisure park, taking in a tree nursery and the former colliery grounds before the finish. Runners cover about 12 km after dark, weaving past zombie-themed zones, and a team orientation race for pairs and small groups follows the next day.
