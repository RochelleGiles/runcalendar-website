Course

Both distances start and finish at Welcker's Point Shelter in Peninsula State Park, opening on a two-mile gravel path before turning onto single-track and multi-use trail. The 30K climbs and descends the Niagara escarpment several times, taking in Sven's Bluff and Eagle Bluff; the 14K splits off near mile 5 for a milder route. Four aid stations serve the 30K and two the 14K.
