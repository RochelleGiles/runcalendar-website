About

The Silesia Marathon is a must-attend event for runners seeking a challenging and scenic course. Set against the backdrop of the picturesque Silesian region, this marathon offers a unique opportunity to experience the rich history and natural beauty of the area. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your journey, the Silesia Marathon promises an unforgettable running adventure.
