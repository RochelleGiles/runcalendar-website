Course

Undulating multi-terrain forest trails through Bedgebury National Pinetum and Forest at Goudhurst, with start and finish at the pinetum. The 10 and 5 mile routes share the same woodland tracks, which can be very muddy after wet weather. A junior run uses the same venue.

About

The Bedgebury Forest 10 & 5 Mile races run through Bedgebury National Pinetum near Goudhurst in Kent, home to one of the world's most complete conifer collections.
