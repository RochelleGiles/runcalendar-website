Course

An overnight loop event at Ratalang Reserve, Bashams Beach, run on a 5.275km lap. The 12-hour endurance run starts at 6:00pm and can be entered solo or in pairs, while the Midnight Marathon (8 laps) and Half-Assed Marathon (4 laps) start at 10:00pm. All formats cover the same reserve loop.

About

Embark on a captivating overnight endurance challenge at Run Ratalang in the picturesque town of Port Elliot, South Australia. This 12-hour trail race offers the unique opportunity to conquer a Midnight Marathon or Half Marathon, testing your limits under the stars. Immerse yourself in the serene natural surroundings as you push your boundaries and experience the thrill of this truly exceptional event.
