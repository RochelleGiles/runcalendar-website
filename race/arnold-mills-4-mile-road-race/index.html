Course

Four-mile road loop starting and finishing in front of the North Cumberland Fire Station, run on neighbourhood streets through Arnold Mills via Nate Whipple Highway, Abbott Run, Quaker Road and Sun Valley Drive before turning back onto Arnold Mills Road for the finish. Run in conjunction with the town's Independence Day parade.

About

A Fourth of July road race through the historic village of Arnold Mills in Cumberland, Rhode Island, held alongside the town's long-running Arnold Mills Parade. The four-mile course is a fixture of the community's Independence Day celebrations.
