Course

A trail series based at the MOE Dairy Farm Outdoor Adventure Centre on Dairy Farm Road, on the edge of Singapore's Central Catchment nature reserves. Routes run technical forest singletrack and the rolling Mandai pipeline trails, with a 21km half and a 30km ultra. Bag deposit and toilets are available at the start venue.
