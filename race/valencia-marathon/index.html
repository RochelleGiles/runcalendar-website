Course

One loop at sea level from the Plaça de la Marató beside the City of Arts and Sciences: out past the Veles e Vents building and the Marina in the first 5K, the Malvarrosa seafront, then back through the city — Avenida del Puerto, the old town past the Plaza del Ayuntamiento and the Torres de Serranos, the Turia gardens — to the finish on the blue carpet laid across the water at the Museu de les Ciències. About 62 m of climbing in 42.2 km.

About

The Valencia Marathon is one of the fastest marathons in the world, run on a pancake-flat course through the historic centre and finishing on the famous blue carpet at the City of Arts and Sciences. A World Athletics Platinum Label race, it is held on the first Sunday of December and has hosted multiple world record performances.
