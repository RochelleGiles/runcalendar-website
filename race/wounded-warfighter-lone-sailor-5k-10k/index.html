About

For over 30 years, Baldwin Park was home to Naval Training Center Orlando, and in celebration, the Central Florida Navy League holds our annual race at the former "Grinder." Join us for a run around the "Grinder" to honor Orlando's rich military heritage and support our sea service members and their families, as well as our chosen charities.
