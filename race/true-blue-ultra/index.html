Course

Multiple laps of a looped course on the Australind Foreshore south of Perth, partly on public footpaths, starting and finishing on the grass reserve north of the picnic area. Aid stations sit at the start, 3.0 km, the 6.25 km turn point and 9.25 km, supplying Tailwind, Coke and water. Distances run from 6 km up to 100 km.

About

The True Blue Ultra is a scenic running adventure along the foreshore in Australind, Western Australia. Formerly known as the Australia Day Ultra, this event offers runners a stunning coastal course through the heart of the Australian bush. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or new to the distance, the True Blue Ultra promises an unforgettable challenge amidst the natural beauty of the region.
