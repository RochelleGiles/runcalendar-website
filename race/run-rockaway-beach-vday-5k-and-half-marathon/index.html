Course

Both distances run on the Rockaway Beach boardwalk in Far Rockaway, starting at the Beach 108th Street boardwalk at 9:00 AM. The 5K covers one loop and the half marathon two complete loops. Both courses are USATF certified.

About

The Rockaway Track Club presents a Valentine's Day themed Half Marathon and 5K on the boardwalk at Rockaway Beach. Participants receive a t-shirt, medal, and free beer at Healy's Pub after completing USATF certified courses.
