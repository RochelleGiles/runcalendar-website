Course

A 5K run and walk on the loop of Grand Boulevard in Corona, the circular street that carried the Corona Road Races from 1913 to 1916. The course follows the full circle through the centre of the city.

About

The Corona 5000 is a 5K road race organized by the City of Corona, California, drawing residents together in Riverside County for a hometown community celebration.
