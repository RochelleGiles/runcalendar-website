Course

A 5-mile course through Emerson Point Preserve on Snead Island, Palmetto. Most of the route is hard shell-packed winding trail through beaches, lagoons, salt marsh, mangrove swamp and coastal hammock, finishing along a paved 1.5-mile park road. Start and finish sit about 0.1 miles apart inside the preserve.

About

The Manatee River Run 5 Mile, now past its 24th running, is hosted by the Bradenton Runners Club at Emerson Point Preserve on Snead Island in Palmetto, Florida. It serves as the RRCA Florida State Championship for the 5-mile distance.
