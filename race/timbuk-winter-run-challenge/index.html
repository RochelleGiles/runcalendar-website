About

The Timbuk Winter Run Challenge takes place at Timbuk Farms in Granville, Ohio. All races start and finish at the Timbuk Farms Garden Center on a hilly, looped course over paved and gravel roads through a Christmas tree farm, with chilly winter weather to match. Participants warm up afterward in the greenhouse with soup and hot chocolate.
