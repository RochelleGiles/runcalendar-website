Course

An 8.6 mile out-and-back starting and finishing at Kersting Court in Sierra Madre. Runners climb Baldwin and Mira Monte Avenues on pavement before joining the Mount Wilson Trail and turning at Orchard Camp. Water stations are stationed at First Water and Orchard Camp. Kids fun runs of 1.0 mile and 0.6 mile are also held.

About

The Mount Wilson Trail Race climbs from downtown Sierra Madre up the historic Mount Wilson Trail into the San Gabriel Mountains foothills. First run in the early 1900s, it is one of the oldest trail races still held in the United States, with an 8.6-mile course.
