About

The Swedish Alpine Ultra® is a captivating running challenge that takes participants on a stunning journey through the untamed wilderness of Sweden's majestic alpine region. Runners will push their limits as they navigate rugged mountain trails, taking in breathtaking vistas at every turn. This unique event offers an unparalleled opportunity to experience the raw beauty and exhilaration of ultra-distance running in one of Europe's most awe-inspiring natural landscapes.
