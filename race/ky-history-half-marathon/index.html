Course

The half marathon starts and finishes on Broadway Avenue near the Old State Capitol in downtown Frankfort, on roads through the historic district. Six hydration stations serve water and SWORD, and runners pass them multiple times during the race. A 10K and 5K are also offered.

About

A half marathon, 10K and 5K in Frankfort, Kentucky, organized by the Kentucky Historical Society. The course passes historic sites around the state capital, including the Kentucky State Capitol.
