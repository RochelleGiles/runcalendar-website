Course

Starts at TOPEC on Hydro Road south of Burgess Park, near New Plymouth, on a mix of road, reserve trail, farmland and forest running up to Lake Mangamahoe. Options are a flat 2.5 km, a flat 5 km, a 5 km lake challenge that climbs to Lake Mangamahoe, and a 9.5 km combining both 5 km courses. Not suitable for pushchairs.

About

The Nexans Fun Run/Walk in Inglewood, New Zealand offers a scenic summer event for runners and walkers of all levels. Set in the beautiful Taranaki region, this annual series provides a fun and social atmosphere for participants to enjoy the outdoors and explore the local community.
