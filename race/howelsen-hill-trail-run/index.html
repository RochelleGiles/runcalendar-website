Course

Loop trail race on the Howelsen Hill network above Steamboat Springs, offered over 8 miles and 4 miles. Starts at Olympian Hall, climbs the Bluff's and Lupine trails, then descends MGM and Meadow back to the ski area base. Entirely on singletrack, with one aid station at the top of the climb; runners are asked to carry their own bottle.

About

Let the trail races begin!! This is a challenging trail run on the scenic single track trails of historic Howelsen Hill, Steamboat's outdoor playground. The trails of Emerald are beautiful this time of year with abundant aspen trees and wildflowers.
