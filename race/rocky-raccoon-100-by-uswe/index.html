Course

Looped course at Huntsville State Park: the 100-mile runs five 20-mile laps and the 100K a 2-mile short lap plus three full laps, starting from Raven Lodge by the lake. Gently undulating pine-needle trails with roots and wooden bridges, roughly 1,250 feet of gain per lap, and full aid-station support.

About

Known as the perfect trail running course for veteran runners wanting a 100mile P.R., as well as the perfect race for rookie ultra runners wanting a good setup to accomplish their goal. Expect frequent and incredible (known around the world) aid station support all day and night. Generous cut-offs. A fun atmosphere for family, friends, and awaiting crew members with 5 x 20 mile beautiful loops around the lake, over wooden bridges, and through towering East Texas pines. Gently undulating trails on a bed of pine needles.
