Course

Held in Weequahic Park, Newark, mostly on well-maintained paved paths through wooded sections bordering the lake, and including a section of rubber track. Three support stations offer Gatorade, water and gels. The layout lets spectators see runners at both the start/finish and the turnaround.

About

The March Madness Half Marathon, 10K and 5K are held in Weequahic Park in Newark, New Jersey. Courses loop around the park's lake on paved paths, offering runners multiple laps to reach the half marathon distance in this historic city park.
