About

Duo Trail is a unique adventure, in pairs or solo! You love running, walking, and sharing an unforgettable experience in nature? Duo Trail is made for you! Two courses adapted to all levels: 11 km for runners and hikers, and 25 km for experienced trail runners, for a real sporting challenge. Come with a friend, a loved one, or try the adventure solo! Awards for duos and solos (men, women, mixed).
