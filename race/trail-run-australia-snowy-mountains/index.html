Course

Point-to-point courses through the Snowy Mountains. The UTRA50 and TRA30 start at Claypits Park, Jindabyne; the TRA21 from Paddy's Corner and the shorter events from Lake Crackenback Resort. All distances finish at Lake Crackenback Resort & Spa. Terrain is predominantly alpine singletrack along Lake Jindabyne and the Thredbo River, with a short sealed section (49.2km trail to 2.4km tarmac on the 50k).

About

Trail Run Australia Snowy Mountains takes runners through spectacular mountain scenery, offering challenges for trail runners of all ages and abilities. The event features multiple distances and a free kids' mud rats run, set in the beautiful Snowy Mountains region.
