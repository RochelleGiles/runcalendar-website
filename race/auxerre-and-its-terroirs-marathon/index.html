About

The Auxerre And Its Terroirs Marathon is a gastronomic and sporting event at the heart of the Auxerrois region. With 4 accessible distances, everyone can find their pace and take on their own challenge, whether for performance, pleasure, or atmosphere. The unique and varied courses take you through the AJA stadium, two iconic châteaux, and the vineyards and bucolic landscapes of the Auxerrois. But the effort is always accompanied by comfort, as you discover the flavors of the terroir at the gourmet aid stations.
