Course

All races are based at Peddler's Village in Lahaska, Bucks County. The programme is an 8K and a 5K run, a Pot o' Gold two-mile fun run/walk and a one-third-mile Little Leprechaun kids' run.

About

This festive St. Patrick's Day celebration in picturesque Peddler's Village! Whether you're chasing PRs in the 8k or 5k, running with the little leprechauns, or enjoying the Pot o' Gold 2-mile fun walk/run, there's something for everyone. Dress in your best green, snag awesome swag, and stick around for hot chocolate, great vibes, and a lucky good time!
