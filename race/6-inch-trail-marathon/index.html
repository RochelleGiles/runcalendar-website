Course

A point-to-point run south along the Munda Biddi Trail through jarrah forest, finishing at Lions Park in Dwellingup. The marathon (about 47.5 km) starts at Goldmine Hill near North Dandalup and the half (about 23.3 km) starts where the trail crosses Nth Spur Rd. Two checkpoints at 23 km and 34 km carry water, energy drinks, fruit and lollies.

About

6 Inch Trail Marathon runs 46km point-to-point along the Munda Biddi Trail from North Dandalup to Dwellingup, about 80km south of Perth in Western Australia's jarrah forest country. One of WA's longest-running trail races, it has built a loyal following in the state's trail community.
