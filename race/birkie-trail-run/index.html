Course

Rolling courses on the American Birkebeiner trail system from the Birkie Trailhead in Cable, Wisconsin. The marathon and half climb Mt Telemark with singletrack sections; the 10K mixes ski trail, two-track and a little singletrack. Final marathon finish cutoff is 3:00 pm.

About

The Birkie Trail Run races on the American Birkebeiner trail system from the Birkie Trailhead in Cable, Wisconsin. Marathon, half marathon, 10K, 5K, relay and Nordic Trek options follow rolling ski trails and singletrack, with the longer courses climbing Mt Telemark.
