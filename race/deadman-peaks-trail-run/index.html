Course

A high desert course on the Continental Divide Trail through the Rio Puerco Valley near Cuba, New Mexico. It is mostly singletrack with some jeep trail, underfoot running from sand to rock and slickrock, over rolling hills between 6,350 and 7,400 feet.

About

Deadman Peaks Trail Run is a trail running weekend near Cuba, New Mexico, in the high country of the Santa Fe National Forest, offering distances from 8 miles up to 55K on singletrack and forest road, alongside the ultra-distance Backbone 100.
