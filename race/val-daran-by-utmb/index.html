About

Trail running at the heart of the Pyrenees. HOKA Val d'Aran by UTMB® is preparing for its sixth edition, returning for the fifth consecutive year as a European UTMB® World Series Major, which is the European final of the circuit. Runners who complete the corresponding distances will double their chances of obtaining a place in the lottery to participate in the UTMB® World Series Finals 2027, the three iconic HOKA UTMB® Mont-Blanc distances (OCC® 50K, CCC® 100K, and UTMB® 100M).
