Course

A six-hour timed challenge run as repeated laps at Daventry Country Park in Northamptonshire, taking in woodland, open grassland and the reservoir shore. Runners complete as many laps as they wish between 09:00 and 15:00, so the event suits both first-time ultra distances and a social day out.

About

The Squirrel Challenge is a 6-hour running event that allows runners of all abilities to participate. Runners can complete as many 5k laps as they wish within the time limit, making it suitable for those looking to run further than ever before or just enjoy a social event with friends.
