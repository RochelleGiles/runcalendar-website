Course

A loop through the tree-lined Burns Park neighbourhood of Ann Arbor, close to the University of Michigan campus. The 5K sets off at 8:30am and the 10K at 8:40am, with a half-mile fun run for younger children afterwards.

About

This event is a volunteer operation, led by parent volunteers from the Burns Park Elementary PTO. The Burns Park Run is the largest PTO fundraiser of the year, and helps support various school initiatives. The event is known for its family-friendly atmosphere, with 5K and 10K races that annually draw between 1,000 to 1,500 runners ages 5 to 90 years, and a separate 1/2 mile Fun Run around the park for the youngest participants.
