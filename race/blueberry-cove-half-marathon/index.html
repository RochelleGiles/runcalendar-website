Course

A 13.1-mile road loop around the St. George peninsula, starting and finishing at the 4-H camp. The first half runs rural back roads past coves and gentle hills; the second half passes Port Clyde, Marshall Point Lighthouse and Drift Inn Beach. Mile markers each mile, water stations every 2 miles, four porta-potty locations. Capped at 250.

About

The Blueberry Cove Half Marathon starts near the Blueberry Cove 4-H camp in Tenants Harbor, Maine, and follows coastal roads along the St. George peninsula on the state's midcoast.
