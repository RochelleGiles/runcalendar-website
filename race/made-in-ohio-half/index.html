About

The Made in Ohio Half Marathon, 10K, and 5K start near the Towpath Trail's Lake Avenue Trailhead in Massillon, Ohio. The half marathon and 10K cover rolling hills before finishing along the flat Towpath, while the 5K runs the flat Towpath section only.
