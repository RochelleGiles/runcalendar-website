Course

Starts and finishes next to the Day Barn and Library area of Draper City Park. The route runs east to 1300 East, south to 13200 South, west to Fort Street, then north before turning onto the Willow Creek Trail back to the start — a mix of city streets and trail. Walkers welcome and leashed dogs allowed, starting behind the main group.
