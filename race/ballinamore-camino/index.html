About

Explore the picturesque landscapes of County Leitrim, Ireland, during the Ballinamore Camino. This mixed-terrain trek offers routes of 36km, 24km, and 16km, allowing runners and walkers of all levels to immerse themselves in the region's natural beauty. Whether you're seeking a challenging adventure or a leisurely stroll, the Ballinamore Camino promises an unforgettable experience.
