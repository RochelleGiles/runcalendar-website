Course

A single day of races in the Mendip Hills above Cheddar Gorge, from 1km up to a marathon. Courses mix open fields and flat stone paths with more technical rocky sections and cliff-top path along the gorge. The half marathon and marathon climb Beacon Batch, the highest point on the Mendips. Routes are comprehensively marked with feed stations en route.

About

Explore the breathtaking Cheddar Gorge in southwest England at the Cheddar Gorge Challenge. This unique running event offers a range of fun run distances as well as the Omnium Challenge, a multi-distance test of endurance and skill. Runners will be treated to stunning views of the dramatic limestone cliffs as they wind through this picturesque natural wonder.
