About

The Shoe of the Trebbia Valley is a trail running event that takes place in the picturesque Trebbia Valley. Runners will experience stunning scenery and challenging terrain as they traverse the trails of this beautiful region. The event offers a unique opportunity to connect with nature and push personal limits.
