About

The Course De La Citadelle is a long-running running event that takes place in the city of Wormhout. The event features a variety of race distances, including a semi-marathon, 10km, and 5km, allowing runners of all levels to participate. The course showcases the historic citadel and surrounding scenic landscapes, providing a unique and memorable running experience.
