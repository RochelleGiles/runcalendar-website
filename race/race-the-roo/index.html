Course

A progressive elimination race over laps of just over 1km, based at the Caloundra Triathlon Club on Arthur Street, Caloundra. A pace-setter known as the Roo runs each lap faster than the last; any runner the Roo finishes ahead of is out. Placings are decided by how many laps each runner stays in front.

About

Race the Roo is a running event with a twist, hosted by Caloundra Triathlon Club in Landsborough. Runners complete 1.1km elimination laps, with the last-placed runner eliminated each round until a winner remains.
