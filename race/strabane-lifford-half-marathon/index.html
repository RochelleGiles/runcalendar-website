Course

A cross-border half marathon on roads, starting at the Alley Theatre in Strabane and finishing at Melvin Sports Complex. The 13.1-mile route takes in both Strabane in Northern Ireland and Lifford across the border in Donegal. Entry is open to individual runners, wheelchair athletes and three-person relay teams.

About

The Strabane Lifford Half Marathon is a unique cross-border running event organized by Derry City and Strabane District Council. Runners will experience the stunning natural beauty of the Irish countryside as they traverse the route between the towns of Strabane and Lifford. This event offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels.
