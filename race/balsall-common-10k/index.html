Course

Starts and finishes at Balsall Common Primary School on Balsall Street East. The 5K and 10K set off together at noon, with mini runs of about 400m for ages 4 to 11 at 11:00. Junior, family and adult 5K entries are offered alongside the 10K.

About

A 10K and 5K fun run through the village of Balsall Common in the Solihull borough of the West Midlands. A long-running community event, it draws local running clubs and families each September.
