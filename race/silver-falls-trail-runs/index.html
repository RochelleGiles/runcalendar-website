Course

Loop courses based at the South Falls Day Use Area of Silver Falls State Park, mixing singletrack past the park's waterfalls with paved and gravel segments; longer distances add backcountry trails and a creek crossing. The half runs Sunday, marathon and 50K Saturday, with strict park cutoffs.

About

Silver Falls Trail Runs features a 5K, 7 Miler, 1/2 Marathon, Marathon, and 50K at Silver Falls State Park in Silverton, Oregon. The courses are known for their scenic beauty and challenging terrain.
