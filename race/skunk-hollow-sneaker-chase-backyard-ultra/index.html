Course

Both formats run a 4.17-mile lap from the Casper Mountain Trails Center. The 8 mile race is two laps, run clockwise in even years and anticlockwise in odd years. The backyard ultra repeats the same lap on the hour until one runner remains, with lap direction decided by a coin toss each round.

About

The Skunk Hollow Sneaker Chase runs on a 4.17-mile lap starting and finishing at the Casper Mountain Trails Center in Wyoming. Entrants choose an 8 mile race over two laps, or a backyard ultra in which runners repeat the same lap on the hour until one remains. Lap direction alternates by year for the 8 miler.
