About

The Ultra Loop in Delamere Forest is an inclusive and flexible 8-hour running event that challenges participants to push their limits. With no set distance, runners can cover as little or as much ground as they desire, making it an exhilarating and personalized endurance experience in the heart of the United Kingdom's stunning natural landscapes.
