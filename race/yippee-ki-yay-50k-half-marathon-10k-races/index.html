About

The Yippee-Ki-Yay races run out-and-back on the Wabash Trace Nature Trail from Iowa West Foundation Trail Head Park in Council Bluffs. Held each October, the event offers a 50K, half marathon and 10K on a gently graded crushed-limestone rail line through wooded Iowa countryside.
