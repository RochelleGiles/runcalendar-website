Course

Half marathon, 10K and 5K on marked forest trails at Alsea Falls Recreation Area, about 10 miles west of Alpine. The distances share terrain until the 5K splits off at 1.9 miles, and all finish back at the start. One aid station sits at mile 7, so runners are asked to carry their own water and calories.

About

The Spring Fling Trail Run is an early-season meeting in the Alsea Falls Recreation Area, in Oregon's Coast Range. Half marathon, 10K and 5K options all run on forested trails, and entrants finish to pizza and a custom pint glass.
