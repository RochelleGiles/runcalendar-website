Course

Road courses out of the township of Cleve on South Australia's Eyre Peninsula. The half marathon runs essentially straight, with views to the Mangalo hills and across the district, while the 10km and 5km take in rolling hills, farmland and the town itself. The kids' dash is a lap of the town oval.

About

The Cleve Running Festival is an annual event that brings together runners of all levels to experience the beautiful countryside of South Australia. The event features a half marathon, 10km, and 5km race, offering something for everyone. Runners can expect a scenic and challenging course that showcases the region's natural beauty, as well as a welcoming and supportive atmosphere.
