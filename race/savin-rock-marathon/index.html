Course

A USATF-certified road marathon and half marathon at West Haven, with roughly a third of the route running along Long Island Sound. The first four miles are flat, five hills follow over the next five miles, and the closing four miles are downhill and flat. Certified as a Boston qualifier.

About

A marathon and half marathon along the Savin Rock shoreline of West Haven, Connecticut, once home to a bustling seaside amusement park. The course follows the Long Island Sound coastline through this historic beach neighborhood.
