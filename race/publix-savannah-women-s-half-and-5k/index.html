Course

Both the half marathon and the 5K start and finish at Forsyth Park and are run on closed city streets in Savannah, with published road closures and designated spectator crossing points. Course maps are published separately; aid station locations are not listed.

About

The Publix Savannah Women's Half Marathon and 5K aren't just races – they are an entire weekend packed with exciting activities, immersive experiences, and plenty of fun! Grab your girlfriends and take advantage of what race weekend has to offer with these activities across Savannah.
