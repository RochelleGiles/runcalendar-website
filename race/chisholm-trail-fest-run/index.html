About

The Chisholm Trail Fest Run is a half marathon at Caldwell, Kansas, held alongside the town's Chisholm Trail Fest. The route follows the historic Chisholm Trail over rocky ground and red dirt hills, and the same 13.1 mile distance is also offered as a two to four person team relay.
