Course

A coastal 10 mile and 5 mile pair starting and finishing at Palm Bay School in Margate, running along the Thanet Coast in East Kent with sea views for most of the way. The 10 mile follows a modified version of the established Alan Green Thanet 10 route. Advance entries close 1pm the Wednesday before; on-the-day entry may be available.

About

The Thanet Coastal 10 & 5 Mile follows the coastline of the Isle of Thanet, Kent, starting and finishing near Margate. Organised by Sporting Events UK, it offers two distance options along the North Kent coast.
