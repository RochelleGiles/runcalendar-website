Course

All three distances start together at 7:00 a.m. from 3rd and Oak Street in downtown Bonner Springs, Kansas, with parking available near the start. The race is run on the streets of the town during the Tiblow Days festival, rain or shine, and awards are presented near the finish.

About

Celebrating 48 years, the annual Bonner Springs Rotary Club Tiblow Trot 2K (1.24 mile) Fun Walk, 5K and challenging 5-Mile Run takes place during Tiblow Days — Bonner Springs largest annual community festival. Come join us and be a part of #TiblowTrot.
