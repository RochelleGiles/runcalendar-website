Course

Three looped trail courses around Castle Hill, starting and finishing at Belgian Gardens State School in Townsville. Terrain is dry rocky singletrack with climbs, descents and exposed rock scramble sections. One water point on course, with hydration, fruit and massage at the finish.

About

The Castle Hill Trail Run is designed to get people into trail running, featuring flowy single track, challenging climbs, rewarding descents and spectacular views over the city and Magnetic Island. The trails offer stairs and rock scrambles that provide a sense of achievement for participants.
