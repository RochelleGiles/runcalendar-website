Course

An out-and-back on the Great Allegheny Passage at Ohiopyle, entirely on hard-packed trail topped with crushed limestone; road shoes are fine. The 5K turns at 1.55 miles, the 10K at 3.1 and the half at 6.55, with the marathon covering the half-marathon loop twice. Aid stations with water and Gatorade roughly every two miles, and the marathon is Boston-qualifying.

About

The Great Allegheny Ohiopyle Race Festival offers a marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K along the scenic Great Allegheny Passage Rail Trail in Ohiopyle, PA. Participants enjoy flat, fast courses with stunning waterfalls, bridges, and natural beauty, plus swag including t-shirts, medals, and socks.
