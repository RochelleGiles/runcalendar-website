Course

An anti-clockwise loop from Streatley-on-Thames: east along the Thames, north up the Grand Union Canal, then back down the Oxford Canal to the start. Mainly rural towpaths and footpaths with some urban stretches. Nine checkpoints roughly every 25 miles provide food, toilets, sleeping tents and medical cover, the larger ones indoors with hot meals.

About

The Thames Ring 250 is an iconic 250-mile trail race through the heart of the United Kingdom. Starting and finishing in the charming village of Streatley-on-Thames, this challenging event takes runners along the banks of the River Thames, offering stunning views and a true test of endurance. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or looking for your next big adventure, the Thames Ring 250 promises an unforgettable journey through the British countryside.
