Course

A 21.1 km circular course through several Ettlingen districts, starting at the Horbachpark pavilion. The first kilometre climbs steadily through forest towards the Spessart, topping out near 400 m, about 250 m above the town, just before halfway. The second half runs mostly downhill with views over the Rhine plain before rolling to the finish at the Horbachsee. Surfaces are well-made paths, some gravel and some asphalt, with aid every 3 km.

About

Experience the scenic Swe Halbmarathon Ettlingen, a beloved half-marathon event that winds through the charming town of Ettlingen. Runners will enjoy a picturesque course showcasing the area's natural beauty, making this a must-do event for any endurance enthusiast. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply seeking an enjoyable challenge, the Swe Halbmarathon Ettlingen delivers an unforgettable running experience.
