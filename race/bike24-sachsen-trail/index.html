About

The Bike24 Sachsen Trail is a trail running event in the Erzgebirge region of Saxony, Germany. The event offers various race distances, from a short Fun Trail to an Ultra Trail, allowing runners of all levels to participate. The scenic course takes runners through the beautiful natural landscapes of the Erzgebirge, providing a challenging and rewarding experience for all participants.
