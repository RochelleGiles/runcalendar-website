About

The Ljubljana Marathon is a prestigious annual running event in the capital city of Slovenia. The course takes runners through the historic city center, showcasing the beautiful architecture and landmarks. With a vibrant atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators, the Ljubljana Marathon offers an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
