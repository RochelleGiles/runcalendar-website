Course

Three courses along the Deschutes River, all starting and finishing at Riverbend Park in Bend. The half marathon carries five aid stations (around miles 2.8, 4.8, 7.5, 9.2 and 12.1), the 10K two and the 5K one, each stocked with water, electrolyte drink and gels. Half starts at 9:00am, 10K at 9:15am, 5K at 9:30am.

About

A long-running spring road race in Bend, Oregon, held on courses that follow the Deschutes River from a start and finish at Riverbend Park. Runners choose between 5K, 10K and half marathon distances, with aid stations on every course.
