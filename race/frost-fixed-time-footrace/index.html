Course

Lapped trail event at the Caribou Bog Outdoor Center in Orono, Maine. Runners pick between a blue loop and a longer red loop and repeat laps for points, with a 5 km at 1pm and a six-hour fixed-time event running 2pm to 8pm. Aid station detail is not stated.

About

Hosted by the Marsh Island Trail Runners in Orono, Maine, this fixed-time footrace lets participants cover as much ground as they can within the set time, rather than racing a fixed course distance.
