About

This trail race circles a lake near Châtellerault before heading into forest singletrack. The 16km course, 'Le Kermit', includes two lake laps and 115m of elevation gain, while the 24km 'Le Rainette' adds more technical single-track. A 5km 'Color Run' with coloured powder is also offered.
