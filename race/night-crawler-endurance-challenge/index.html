Course

An overnight timed lap race on forested trail atop Casper Mountain at about 8,100 ft, starting and finishing at the Nordic stadium by the Casper Mountain Trails Center. Each lap is roughly 9.4 miles with about 1,200 ft of climbing. Entries are 4-hour or 12-hour solo, or a 12-hour four-person relay, running from 6:30 PM to 6:30 AM.
