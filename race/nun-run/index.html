About

Taking place in the scenic Muhlenberg area, the all-macadam course winds through the grounds of Holy Guardian Angels Regional School and Gethsemane Cemetery. The 5K course does include inclines. Start and Finish are located at HGA Regional School. Families encouraged, strollers permitted, and pets welcome.
