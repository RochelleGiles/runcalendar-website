Course

A single-lap road 5K on fully closed, quiet countryside lanes at Desborough, Northamptonshire. The roads are wide and smooth-surfaced, leaving room to overtake. The field is capped at 400 runners and the race starts at 7pm.

About

Enjoy a scenic sunset 5K road race in the heart of Northamptonshire. Challenge yourself to Beat The Sunset as you race against the clock on a fast, flat course. This unique evening event offers a fun and atmospheric running experience for all abilities.
