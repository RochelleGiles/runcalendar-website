About

The Belfast City Women's 10K is a female only event open to runners and walkers aged 15 and over. The 10km route is based from the iconic Titanic Quarter, finishing at Ormeau Park. It soaks up some of the city's greatest attractions, such as the SS Nomadic, the towering Harland and Wolff Cranes, the Great Light and the Thompson Dock along the Maritime Mile.
