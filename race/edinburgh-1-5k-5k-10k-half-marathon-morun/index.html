Course

A lapped road course starting and finishing on Queen's Drive in Holyrood Park. Each lap climbs for the first 2km before levelling out. The 5k is one lap, the 10k two laps and the half marathon four laps. The route is dog, buggy and wheelchair friendly.

About

The Edinburgh MoRun is a fun and inspiring running event that brings together runners of all levels to support men's health through Movember. Participants can choose from multiple race distances including 1.5k, 5k, 10k, and a half marathon, all taking place in the scenic Holyrood Park. The event combines fitness, fundraising, and community spirit with a playful atmosphere of fancy dress and camaraderie.
