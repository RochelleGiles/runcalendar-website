About

The Trondheim Marathon is a popular running event that attracts over 14,000 participants. The event is known for its fantastic atmosphere, with the city coming alive to support the runners. The course showcases the beautiful scenery of Trondheim, making it a must-do for runners looking for a scenic and challenging marathon experience.
