About

The Trail Nocturne des Étoiles de Gimel is a night trail event that takes place in the beautiful Pilat region. Runners can discover the stunning landscapes and views of the Alps while running on trails, wild paths, and groomed tracks. This intimate event with only 300 participants offers a warm and friendly atmosphere, with hot soup and refreshments provided.
