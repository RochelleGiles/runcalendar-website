Course

A 5K run and a one-mile memorial walk held entirely inside East Goshen Township Park in West Chester, Pennsylvania, looping the park paths from a start/finish by the park entrance. Not chip-timed, but a clock runs at the line, and walk-ups are welcome on the day.
