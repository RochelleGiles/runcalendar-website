Course

Coastal races starting and finishing at The Meadow Playing Fields in Woolacombe, North Devon, run on public footpaths, private land and rugged coastal terrain including beach and dunes. Twelve water stations serve the course. The marathon starts about 09:30, the half at 10:00 and the 10K at 10:30, staggered to avoid bottlenecks.

About

A marathon, half marathon and 10K starting from the beach at Woolacombe, running through the North Devon Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The route takes in coastal paths and rolling Devon countryside.
