About

The Wings for Life World Run comes to Auckland with an app-based catcher car virtual run. Experience the thrill of this global event, where runners race against a virtual vehicle that slowly catches up to them. Lace up and join this unique challenge in the beautiful city of Auckland, New Zealand.
