Course

Course runs through the vineyards at Ste. Chapelle Winery near Caldwell, Idaho. The 5K run/walk carries two water stations on course plus water at the finish and allows 90 minutes. The 1K tasting walk includes three sampling stations with a tasting at the finish.

About

Ste Chapelle Winery, Idaho's oldest winery, offers an impressive selection of premium wines set against the stunning backdrop of the Snake River Valley and Owyhee Mountain Range. With its charming Chateau Tasting Room and breathtaking sunsets, it's a must-visit destination. Join the Wicked Wine Run for a fun-filled day of running or walking through the scenic vines, followed by a celebration of wine, music, and friends.
