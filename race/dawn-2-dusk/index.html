Course

A circuit race based at Hoërskool Gerrit Maritz in Akasia, Pretoria North. The 100-mile event starts at 16:00 on the Friday and the 12-hour event at 06:00 on the Saturday, both run as repeated laps of the same short loop. The fixed base means aid and drop bags are reached every lap.

About

Dawn 2 Dusk is an overnight ultra-distance circuit race held on a repeating short course near Pretoria, tackled as a continuous 161km effort from dusk into the following day. It draws ultrarunners training for 100-mile and multi-day events.
