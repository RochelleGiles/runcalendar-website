About

The TMC Arizona Distance Classic is a scenic running event in Oro Valley, Arizona, featuring a Half Marathon, Quarter Marathon, and 5K. It celebrates the healthy active lifestyle, benefiting St. Luke’s Home, with views of the Catalina Mountains.
