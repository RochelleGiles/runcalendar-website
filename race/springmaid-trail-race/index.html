Course

Run on the trail network of the Anne Springs Close Greenway, starting and finishing at the Lake Haigler entrance in Fort Mill. The half marathon sets off at 8:15 am, the 10K at 9:00 am and the 5K and Dog Jog at 9:30 am. A 5K Hike Challenge follows at 9:35 am.

About

Be a part of the storied tradition at the beautiful Anne Springs Close Greenway for an unforgettable experience of trail racing fun. Dedicated to the Mary Warner Mack Dog Park, the entire family, even your furry best friend, is encouraged to participate in the Springmaid Trail Race!
