Course

Looped course on private trails at Beaumont Reservation near High Ridge, Missouri, rolling through wooded Ozark-foothill terrain with repeated hills. The direction alternates by year, clockwise in odd years and counter-clockwise in even. Distances from 25K to 100 miles share the same loop, including a night 40 miler.

About

The Shippey Endurance Runs is a Midwest winter ultra marathon held on rugged trails at Beaumont Reservation in High Ridge, Missouri. It offers challenging distances including 100mi, 100k, Night 40mi, 20mi, and a 100mi 5-person relay with significant elevation gain.
