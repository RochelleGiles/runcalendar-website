Course

Lapped course on the Shaw Cherry Hill Farm loop in Gorham, Maine, with the start and finish at the farm. The organiser describes the loop as flat and non-technical; the 50 km runs 12 laps, the 25 km six and the 10 km two and a half. Aid station detail is not stated.

About

A farm-based ultra in Gorham, Maine, held each September across the fields and trails of Cherry Hill Farm. Runners can choose from 10K, 25K, or 50K distances, all starting and finishing at the working farm just outside Portland.
