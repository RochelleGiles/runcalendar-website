About

The Laguna Phuket Marathon is an event to remember! Not only is it the largest sporting event in Phuket, Thailand, but it is also a highly-regarded marathon by participants from all around the world. With high attention and meticulous planning to meet international standards, this event has achieved certification by the IAAF AIMS (Association of International Marathons and Distance Races) since its very first year. This prestigious recognition has further qualified the Laguna Phuket Marathon to become a pathway to the illustrious Boston Marathon, attracting professionals and running enthusiasts alike from all corners of the globe. Be part of the marathon that truly lives up to its name and experience the ultimate running challenge in one of the most beautiful destinations on earth!
