Course

Both 5K races start on the driveway at Stewart Lodge in Mendon Ponds Park. One 5K is run on pavement and the other, a 5K-ish, is entirely on trails, so proper footwear is recommended. A kids half-mile is set on the day according to conditions.

About

Enjoy a wonderful winter day in the park with this new addition to the Monroe County Winterfest Festival. Choose from 5k road or 5k-ish trail and sign the little ones up for the kids race. Post race, stick around and enjoy all the offerings Winterfest has to offer the public. Come experience the best in winter activities at Winterfest. Perfect family friendly way to spend in the park this winter.
