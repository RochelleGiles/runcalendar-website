The Wensum Way Trail

Dereham, England · United Kingdom
TrailRiver

Course

A lapped race on a 5.3km out-and-back loop from Billingford Lakes on North Elmham Road, over a mix of tarmac paths, grassland and rugged trail. The half marathon is five laps as standard and the ultra nine, adjustable on race day. One aid station at base camp; the event is cupless. Starts at 09:00 (six-hour) and 11:00 (four-hour).

About

A Zig Zag Running multi-distance trail event based in Dereham, following the Wensum Way path through the River Wensum valley. Options range from 5K up to a full marathon.
