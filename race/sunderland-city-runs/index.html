Course

A flat, fast road course that runs through the centre of Sunderland and out along the seafront, crossing two of the city's landmark bridges. All three distances - half marathon, 10K and the Active Sunderland Big 3K - share the same route corridor.

About

Explore the vibrant streets of Sunderland during the Sunderland City Runs, a premier running event featuring a scenic Half Marathon, a lightning-fast 10km, and a lively Saturday evening 5km. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your journey, this celebration of the sport offers a course that showcases the city's historic landmarks and stunning coastline.
