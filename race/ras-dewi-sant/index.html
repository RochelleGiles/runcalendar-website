Course

A half marathon starting and finishing at the Cathedral steps in St Davids, run mainly on the Pembrokeshire coast path with country lanes and hill footpaths in between and a climb of Carn Llidi. Finishers come back up the 39 steps of the 12th-century cathedral. One feed station at Whitesands Beach around halfway.

About

A marathon, half marathon and 10K named for Wales' patron saint, run from St Davids in the Pembrokeshire Coast National Park. The route follows dramatic clifftop coast path around Britain's smallest city.
