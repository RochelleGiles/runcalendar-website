Course

Based at the field beside the JCC pool at 40 Clyde Street, Asheville, with the 5K starting from the pavilion opposite the pool and one aid station just after the first mile. A Splash+Dash pairs a 200-yard or 800-yard pool swim with the same 5K, and a kids 1K loops the field.
