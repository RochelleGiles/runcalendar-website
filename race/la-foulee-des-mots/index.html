About

The Traces de Vies association is organizing its very first solidarity run in Bourgogne Franche-Comté. This festive and sporting event aims to highlight hospital biography. By participating in the Foulée des Mots, you contribute to the deployment of hospital biography and this mission imbued with humanity to allow the other to exist fully and beyond illness or end of life.
