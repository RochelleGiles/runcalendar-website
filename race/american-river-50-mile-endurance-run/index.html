Course

A point-to-point run along the American River to a finish in Auburn. The 50 mile starts at Folsom Point at 6am, the 25 mile at Beals Point at 7am and the 10 mile at Rattlesnake Bar at 9am, all following the river corridor. Well-stocked aid stations and marshals are set along the route.

About

The American River 50 traces the Western States Trail along the American River from Folsom to Auburn. Runners follow single-track and fire road above the river canyon, climbing steadily in the final miles. It's a long-standing Western States 100 qualifier.
