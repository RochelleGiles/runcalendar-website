Course

Start and finish at the Waldbuehne on the Hartheberg at Kurort Hartha, with every distance run on forest paths and forestry roads through the Tharandter Wald. The longer races climb roughly 275 to 460 metres. Distances run from a 400m Bambini race up to 30km, with a 10.5km Nordic walking option.

About

Nestled in the serene Tharandter Wald forest, the Tharandter Wald Lauf offers runners a truly immersive experience. Traverse winding trails surrounded by lush greenery and breathtaking scenery as you challenge yourself in this picturesque setting. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or seeking a unique running adventure, this event promises an unforgettable journey through the heart of the Tharandter Wald.
