About

Join One Tribe Foundation as we bring the community together to celebrate Texas Independence Day with a 5K race and celebration! The 3rd Annual Texas Independence Day 5K will be at a new location, The Epic, which is a large campus in Grand Prairie with miles of trails, restaurants, a beautiful lake and more! Wear your favorite TEXAS gear or red, white & blue and feel free to race, run, ruck, or walk the beautiful race course around a lake. Bring your family, invite your company and come together . Enjoy music, refreshments and a Vendor Village with companies and organizations from all over the state of Texas.
