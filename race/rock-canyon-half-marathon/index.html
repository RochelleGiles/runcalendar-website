Course

Starts and finishes at the City Park Pavilion in Pueblo, dropping quickly to the Arkansas River trail. The half runs west along smooth path with dirt sections to the nature center and an open field, then returns the same way. Five water stations on the half; the Mini Rock Canyon 5K has one near its turnaround.

About

The Rock Canyon Half Marathon is a long-running December half marathon and 5K in Pueblo, Colorado, marking its 40th anniversary in 2026. The course runs through Rock Canyon near Lake Pueblo.
