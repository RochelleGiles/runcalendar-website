About

The Wellington Marathon is a scenic, supportive, and safe running event that offers something for everyone. With a flat and fast course, the event is perfect for both experienced runners and those new to the sport. Runners can choose from distances including the full marathon, half marathon, 10K, and a kids' mile, making it an inclusive event for the whole family.
