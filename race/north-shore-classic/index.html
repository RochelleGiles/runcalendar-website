Course

Point-to-point road course starting at St Johns and Central Ave in uptown Highland Park and finishing between St Johns and Elm Place. The half marathon runs tree-lined streets through Ravinia and Fort Sheridan over rolling hills; the 5K runs neighbourhood loops including a climb up Egandale Road. Both courses are USATF certified.

About

The North Shore Classic is a half marathon, 5K and Kids Dash celebrating community and sportsmanship through the picturesque streets of Highland Park, on Chicago's scenic North Shore.
