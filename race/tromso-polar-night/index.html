Course

Run in the dark of the polar night on the lit roads of Tromsøya island, out and back along the coast from the city centre. Mostly flat and often on packed snow and ice, with the northern lights a possibility overhead.

About

The Tromsø Polar Night Marathon is a captivating running event held under the enchanting glow of the Arctic's winter night sky. Runners traverse a scenic course through the charming Norwegian city of Tromsø, surrounded by breathtaking fjords and snow-capped peaks. This unique race offers an unforgettable experience for adventurous athletes seeking a challenge in a stunning polar landscape.
