About

The Seoul Half Marathon is a must-run event for distance enthusiasts, offering a scenic tour through the heart of the vibrant South Korean capital. Runners will weave their way past iconic landmarks and through bustling city streets, all while soaking in the electric atmosphere of this global metropolis. This event promises an unforgettable challenge and an immersive cultural experience for athletes of all levels.
