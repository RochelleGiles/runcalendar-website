About

The Barretstown Half Marathon is a fast, scenic, certified route in Naas, Co. Kildare, starting and finishing at Naas Racecourse. The route heads towards Kill, past Kerdiffstown Park, through Sallins and along the canal, before returning via Main Street from the Harbour to finish back at Naas Racecourse. The event raises funds for Barretstown, supporting children affected by cancer and other serious illnesses.
