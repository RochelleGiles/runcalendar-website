Course

A flat, fast circular road half marathon broadly circling Farnborough airport, starting and finishing under the aircraft hangar arch in Farnborough Business Park. The 2027 route takes in roads around the Exhibition Centre, the inner lane of the A325 and Aerospace Boulevard, largely traffic free though with traffic flowing on the opposite carriageway in places. An 800m kids run is held alongside.
