Course

A timed-format event on a roughly 5.1 km forest loop in Beaudry Provincial Park west of Winnipeg, much of it running alongside the Assiniboine River. Every loop passes the single start/finish and its supported aid station, which serves hot food such as ramen, broth, perogies and quesadillas on the longer events. Formats run 6, 12, 24 and 48 hours, with a 10K Sunset Fun Run on the Friday evening.
