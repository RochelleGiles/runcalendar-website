Course

A two-day trail meeting based at La Chapelle-Erbrée near Vitré in Ille-et-Vilaine, on the Haute Vilaine lake and the surrounding Breton countryside. Trail distances run from 5 km to 30 km, with a 32 km combined challenge and free Nordic walking routes of 7 km and 18 km. Proceeds go to the cystic fibrosis charity Vaincre la Muco.

About

A group of volunteers have been organizing this Trail Muco event for nearly 20 years to raise funds in the Pays de Vitré region to fight against cystic fibrosis. Runners and walkers of all ages are welcome to test their nature through the various races and walks offered.
