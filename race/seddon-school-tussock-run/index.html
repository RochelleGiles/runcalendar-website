Course

All three courses start and finish at Seddon School on Redwood Street. The 5km follows farm tracks before joining the other courses on the way to the finish; the 10.5km heads south on Marama Road to the vineyard-covered river terraces above the Awatere River. The 15km climbs Star Hill for 360-degree views over the Awatere Valley, Cape Campbell and Mt Tapuaenuku before crossing farmland and vineyard near the river.

About

The Seddon School Tussock Run in Marlborough's Awatere Valley is a fundraising fun run for the school, with 5km, 10km or 15km distances to choose from. The 5km and 10.5km courses suit mountain buggies, but the 15km does not.
