Course

Both races start at the Diversey Event Space, 2611 N Cannon Drive, Chicago. The route runs the trails within Lincoln Park before heading south along the Lakefront Trail. Every mile is marked, with half marathon splits at 5km, 10km and 10 miles, the 10km split taken at the 6.55-mile turnaround.

About

The Haunted Halloween Half & 5km is a costumed road and path race staged by F3 Running in Chicago''s Lincoln Park. Both distances start at the Diversey Event Space and head south along the city''s Lakefront Trail. The half marathon is chip timed, with splits recorded at 5km, 10km and 10 miles.
