Course

Run on the Swamp Fox Passage of the Palmetto Trail through the Francis Marion National Forest near McClellanville, South Carolina. The course is single and double track with rooty sections, fire roads and two very short paved road crossings. Distances run from 10K to 100 miles with generous cut-offs.

About

The Swamp Fox Ultra takes place on the Swamp Fox Passage of the Palmetto Trail just outside Charleston, SC. The race offers distances ranging from 10k-100miles with generous cut off times which means there's something for everyone!
