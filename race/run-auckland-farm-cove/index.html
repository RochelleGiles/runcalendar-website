About

Run Auckland: Farm Cove is a running event that takes place in the scenic Farm Cove area of Pakuranga, Auckland. The event offers 5km and 10km race distances, as well as a kids dash, allowing runners of all ages and abilities to participate. The course showcases the beautiful coastal landscape of the region, providing a picturesque backdrop for the race.
