Course

Looped trail routes from Staveley Recreation Ground into the Kentmere valley, on short sections of tarmac, grassy footpaths and stony bridleways with some stiles. The half marathon extends over Green Quarter Fell and Longsleddale before rejoining the shorter trails. All routes finish with a steep climb up Reston Scar and a 1km descent into the village. Water at 7km on the 10K and at 12km and 17.5km on the half.

About

Starting and finishing at Staveley Recreation Ground, the 5K Kentmere Sport Trail, 10K Kentmere Trail Run, and the Trail 1/2-ish Marathon courses follow fully marked and marshalled footpaths and bridleways, taking in panoramic views of most of the southern Lakeland fells. The trails climb out from Staveley above the Kentmere valley, before returning along the valley itself past idyllic tarns and through ancient woodland, climbing Reston Scar before the descent back into Staveley village.
