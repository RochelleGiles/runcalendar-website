Course

A five-race weekly series on the trails of Mt Pisgah, in the Howard Buford Recreation Area and Mt Pisgah Arboretum near Eugene, Oregon. Legs range from about 3 to 8 miles: some climb steeply through open prairie to the summit, while the bottomland routes beside the river are flat and fast.
