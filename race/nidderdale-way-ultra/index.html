About

Explore the stunning Yorkshire Dales on the Nidderdale Way Ultra, a challenging 50-mile trail race through picturesque countryside and rolling hills. This classic ultramarathon event offers runners a chance to test their endurance on a scenic route, taking in quaint villages and breathtaking natural landscapes along the way.
