About

The Turkestan Marathon is a must-experience running event that takes runners on a scenic journey through the historic city of Turkestan, Kazakhstan. Offering a unique opportunity to explore the region's rich cultural heritage, this marathon showcases stunning architecture and breathtaking landscapes that will captivate participants. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Turkestan Marathon promises an unforgettable experience.
