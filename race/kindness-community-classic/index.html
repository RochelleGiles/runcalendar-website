Course

The 5-mile and 5K races start and finish at the Tomah High School track, with food and drink at the finish. The shorter events stay on site: Jackie's Jog is run on the high school track and the 100-yard Angel Run on the football field.

About

The Kindness Community Classic is a series of races for all ages and abilities held as a fundraiser to support neighbors and community in Tomah, Wisconsin. Events start and finish at Tomah High School Track with pre-race festivities.
