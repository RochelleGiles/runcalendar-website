Course

Starts at the Mt Holdsworth DOC campsite in Tararua Forest Park and finishes at Holdsworth Lodge 500 m up the road. The 24 km is run in both directions between Jumbo (1,405 m) and Holdsworth (1,470 m), climbing steeply to exposed tops above the tree line before descending past huts and down the valley; the anti-clockwise route starts flat and fast into a very steep technical climb. A 12 km Hooper Loop stays mainly in the bush on graded tracks.

About

The Jumbo Holdsworth & Hooper Loop Trail Races offer a challenging and scenic running experience in the heart of New Zealand's stunning natural landscapes. Featuring multiple distance options, the event caters to runners of all levels, from the Podium Direction and Jumbo to Holdsworth routes to the Hooper Loop Trail Race. Participants can expect breathtaking views, a well-marked course, and a supportive community atmosphere.
