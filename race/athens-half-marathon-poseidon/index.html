Course

Flat, fast waterfront courses around Faliro Bay on the Athens coast, all starting and finishing in the same coastal zone. The half marathon is joined by a quarter marathon, a 5 km road race, a 5 km power walk and a kids run, and the half can also be run as a relay.

About

The Poseidon Athens Half Marathon is a vibrant running event held in Faliro Bay, Athens. Runners from around the world gather to participate in multiple race distances while raising awareness about diabetes and obesity. The event promotes a healthier lifestyle through exercise and community engagement.
