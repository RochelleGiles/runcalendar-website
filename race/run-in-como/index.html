Course

The half marathon sets off from the centre of Como on a FIDAL-certified 21 km road course. A 5 km race is held alongside it, offered in both competitive and non-competitive versions as well as a faster "5K Fast" field. The organiser publishes no aid-station detail.

About

The Run In Como is a must-experience event for runners seeking a scenic and historic challenge. This half-marathon race takes participants through the picturesque streets and lakeside promenades of the charming Italian city of Como, offering stunning views of the iconic Lake Como and the surrounding Alps. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Run In Como promises an unforgettable running adventure.
