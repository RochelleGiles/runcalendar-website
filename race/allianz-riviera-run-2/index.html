About

The Allianz Riviera Run offers a complete and immersive visit of the surroundings and backstage of the Allianz Riviera stadium. The 4KM race is the perfect solution for those who want to focus on the essentials, while the Family Run 2KM is the ideal opportunity to introduce the little ones or the less experienced to running while discovering the magnificent Allianz Riviera venue.
