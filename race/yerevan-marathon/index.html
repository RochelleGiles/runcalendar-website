About

Yerevan Marathon is the main running event organized by the Arm Marathon Foundation in Armenia. The race takes runners through the beautiful central streets of Yerevan and the Hrazdan River gorge, offering a scenic and challenging course. The event aims to promote a healthy lifestyle and sports tourism in Armenia, and is open to runners of all abilities.
