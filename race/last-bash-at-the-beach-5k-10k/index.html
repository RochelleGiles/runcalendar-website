Course

A certified boardwalk course starting and finishing at Rockaway Beach Playground on Beach Front Rd. The 5K runs out and back, the 10K covers two loops, both from a 9:00 AM start rain or shine. Water stops sit at the turnaround and finish, with public fountains roughly every quarter mile and toilets near the start and on course.

About
