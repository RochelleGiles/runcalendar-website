Course

One big loop of the city: down the Champs-Élysées, along Rue de Rivoli past the Louvre to Bastille, out to the Bois de Vincennes for a long green loop through halfway, then back along the Seine — under the quays in a series of tunnels, past the Eiffel Tower at 30K — into the Bois de Boulogne and a final straight up Avenue Foch to finish beside the Arc de Triomphe. About 164 m of climbing, most of it the ramps out of the tunnels.

About

The Paris Marathon is a renowned annual running event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the heart of the City of Light. Participants will have the opportunity to race past iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and the Louvre as they challenge themselves on this historic 26.2-mile course. The Schneider Electric Paris Marathon is a must-do event for any runner seeking an unforgettable European running experience.
