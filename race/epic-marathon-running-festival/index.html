Course

Races start on Cormier Road outside the EPIC Event Center in Green Bay, on flat courses running through Ashwaubenon, Green Bay, Allouez and the Brown County trail system, passing Lambeau Field. Marathon, half and relays start at 7:00 a.m., the 5K at 7:45 a.m. and the kids' dash at 10:00 a.m.

About

The EPIC Marathon & Running Festival features a marathon, half marathon, relays, 5K, and kids' run starting and finishing at EPIC Event Center in Green Bay, WI. The event includes live music along the courses and a free post-race concert with artists like Lita Ford and Brian Vander Ark.[1][5]
