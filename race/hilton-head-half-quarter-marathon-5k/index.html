Course

Starts and finishes at Harbour Town in the Sea Pines Resort. The route mixes paved roads, paved leisure paths and hard, smooth natural paths through the Sea Pines Forest Preserve, and is flat and fast throughout.

About

The Hilton Head Half, Quarter Marathon and 5K wind through Sea Pines on Hilton Head Island, South Carolina, taking in the island's maritime forest, lagoons and coastal scenery. It's held each November as one of the island's marquee running events.
