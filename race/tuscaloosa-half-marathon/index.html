About

The Visit Tuscaloosa Half Marathon is run in Tuscaloosa, Alabama, alongside a 5K and a one-mile fun run. The half marathon carries a three and a half hour time limit and the 5K an hour and a half. Entry is handled through the race own RunSignUp site.
