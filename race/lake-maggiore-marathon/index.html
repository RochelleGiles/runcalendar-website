Course

Point-to-point along the western shore of Lake Maggiore, starting in Arona and finishing in Verbania-Pallanza. The route is flat and fast on sealed lakeside roads, with eight refreshment points from 5.8 km and sponging stations every 5 km. Boats shuttle runners between the finish and the start. Field capped at 900 for the marathon; 5h30 time allowance.

About

Get ready to live a unique experience along one of the most scenic routes in Italy! 42 km of pure emotion from Arona to Pallanza, running overlooking Lake Maggiore, crossing the wonderful Borromean Gulf. The sporting event also offers distances for every runner: Half Marathon, 33 KM and 10 KM. LMM, to overcome your limits or simply to run in a dream setting.
