About

Explore the stunning Quiberon Bay on the Trail de la Baie de Quiberon, a captivating trail running event that showcases the breathtaking coastal scenery of this picturesque region. Runners will traverse rugged trails, rocky cliffs, and pristine beaches as they immerse themselves in the natural beauty of this unique location, making this a must-do event for trail enthusiasts.
