Course

The 5K is run entirely inside the Coral Springs Sportsplex on Sportsplex Drive, covering 13 laps of the track. The race starts at 9am with on-the-day registration from 7:45am, and players and coaching staff join for games alongside it.

About

Coral Springs Charter hosts the Panthers Home Run Hustle 5K at Coral Springs Sportsplex, joined by players and coaching staff for fun games alongside the race. The event supports Coral Springs Charter Baseball and helps advance the program.
