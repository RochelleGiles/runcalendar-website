Course

A 5K walk or run through Clinton's historic Olde Towne, covering a mix of asphalt and the district's original brick streets. The course starts at 7:00am on East Leake Street and remains open to traffic, so runners may share the road.

About

A 5k walk/run through Clinton's historic Olde Towne that is part of the Red Brick Roads Music & Arts Festival. Participants receive a race t-shirt and a one-day pass to the festival.
