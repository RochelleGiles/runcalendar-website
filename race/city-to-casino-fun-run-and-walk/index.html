Course

Point-to-point along the Derwent foreshore in Hobart, finishing at Wrest Point. The 12km starts at MyState Bank Arena at 8:00am, the 7km run and walk at Cornelian Bay at 8:10am, and the 2.7km for children aged 12 and under opposite the Supreme Court steps in Salamanca Place at 7:55am.

About

City to Casino is Tasmania's oldest fun run, held in Hobart since 1973, running from the city centre out along the Derwent to Wrest Point over 2.7km, 7km and 12km distances. It remains one of the city's biggest community events.
