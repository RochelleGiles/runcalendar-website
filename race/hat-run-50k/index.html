Course

Two identical loops covering nearly all of Susquehanna State Park, with roughly 4,300 ft of elevation gain. The surface is single-track trail mixed with open fields, dirt roads and a small amount of blacktop. Four stream crossings require wading. Start, finish and race HQ are at Steppingstone Farm Museum, with multiple staffed aid stations.

About

Now in its fourth decade, the HAT Run 50K starts and finishes at the Steppingstone Farm Museum in Susquehanna State Park near Havre de Grace, Maryland. The course mixes singletrack trail with open fields and dirt roads, with minimal pavement, and is a season-opening staple for mid-Atlantic ultrarunners.
