Course

A 10km and 5km run on open roads around Coolin at Priest Lake, all distances starting and finishing at Coolin Community Park. The roads stay open to traffic, so runners keep to the roadside. A kids dash is held alongside.

About

Welcome to the 48th annual Priest Lake Fun Run, where runners can test their endurance in the family-friendly atmosphere that is the Priest Lake Spring Festival. Stay all weekend and enjoy the parade, great food, vendors and a craft fair.
