Course

Lap course on a closed loop at War Memorial Drive, Park 10 Warnpangga, North Adelaide, which is also the start and finish. Lap length is given as 2200m on the organiser site and 2175m on the entry page. One aid station is provided, along with electronic timing, first aid and toilets. The organiser notes the surface makes the event ineligible for IAU labelling.

About

The Adelaide 24 Hour Festival returns to the 'Uni Loop' in North Adelaide, offering runners a unique challenge to push their limits over a full day and night. Held at the scenic Park 10, this event invites athletes of all abilities to tackle laps around the loop, with support and camaraderie fueling them through the miles. Whether running solo or as part of a team, participants can expect an unforgettable endurance experience in the heart of South Australia.
