Course

A point-to-point ultra along the Cotswold Way, based at Cirencester with a halfway hub at Wotton-under-Edge. The full 100km runs continuously or over two days, with shorter 76km, 53km, 47km, 30km and 24km options. Rolling hills, villages, footpaths and trails, with about 2,250m of climb over the full route. Rest stops roughly every 12.5km and major stops every 25km with catering and medical cover.

About

The Cotswold Challenge is a mass-participation ultra along the Cotswold Way, starting from a basecamp at Cirencester. Entrants choose anything from a 10km up to the full 100km, tackled in a single continuous push or split across two days with a halfway stop at Wotton-under-Edge.
