About

Explore the picturesque Sentiers Bressuirais trails in the heart of the Bocage region. This challenging yet rewarding race offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in the serene natural beauty of the area, with stunning countryside vistas and winding paths to discover. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, the Sentiers Bressuirais event promises an unforgettable running experience.
