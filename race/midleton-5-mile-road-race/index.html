About

The Midleton 5 Mile Road Race is an annual running event organized by the Midleton Athletic Club. The race takes place in the community of Midleton, Ireland, and is open to runners of all abilities. The course highlights the scenic local area, and the event has a welcoming and supportive atmosphere for participants.
