About

The MadCity Ultras started in 2007 and is comprised entirely of volunteers. The Local Organizing Committee (LOC) is recognized by the USATF as a trusted and dedicated group that has hosted the 100K & 50K National Championship race for the past 12 years. The objective is to highlight the Madison area running community.
