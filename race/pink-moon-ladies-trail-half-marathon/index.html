Course

A women-only out-and-back trail half marathon starting at 4pm from Rock Lane, Sutton Scarsdale. The route runs through Sutton Spring Wood then climbs to the Five Pits Trail, undulating to a turning loop before returning the same way. One aid station sits at the turnaround, with refreshments at the finish. A 3.5-hour limit applies, with an optional early start.

About

Now past its second running, the Pink Moon Ladies Trail Half Marathon is part of the Lunar Trails race series, taking its name from the full moon in April. The out-and-back route, typically 20-30 minutes longer than a usual half marathon, runs through Sutton Springs Wood and climbs to the Five Pits Trail, with an aid station at the turning loop and cake, hot drinks and a campfire waiting at the finish.
