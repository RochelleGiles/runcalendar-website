Course

An out and back on asphalt at Eagle Lakes Community Park, Naples, starting and finishing by the tennis courts. The 5K runs a 3.11 mile out and back once and the 10K twice; the half marathon runs a 3.28 mile out and back four times. Aid stations are not described.

About

The Luau 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is a themed running event held at Eagle Lakes Community Park in Naples, Florida, featuring an asphalt out-and-back course. Runners of all levels participate in a fun atmosphere with finisher medals, costume encouragement, and a 3.5-hour time limit.
