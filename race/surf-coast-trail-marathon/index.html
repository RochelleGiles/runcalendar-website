Course

Point-to-point coastal trail run finishing at Fairhaven Surf Lifesaving Club. The marathon starts at Torquay at 8.30am; the half marathon starts at Point Addis and the 8km at Urquharts Beach, both at 11am. The route follows single track past Bells Beach, the Point Addis and Anglesea clifftops and Split Point lighthouse. Cup-free event, so runners carry their own hydration receptacle.

About

The Brooks Surf Coast Trail Marathon takes runners along the Surf Coast Walk, starting in Torquay and finishing in Fairhaven. The course features iconic locations like Bells Beach, Point Addis cliff tops, Anglesea, and Split Point lighthouse, offering a unique trail running experience through surf beaches and Otway rainforest.
