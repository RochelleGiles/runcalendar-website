Course

Multi-lap trail event at Pineland Farms on mostly wide, well-groomed double-track trails with rocks, roots and ruts; not highly technical. The 25K is one lap, the 50K two laps and the 100K four laps of the same loop. The 25K, 50K and 100K have two water stops per lap plus a start/finish aid station with water, energy products and snacks.

About

The Trail Festival at Pineland Farms is a multi-distance trail race across the working farm and trail network of Pineland Farms in New Gloucester, Maine, with looped distances from 5K up to 100K over rolling New England terrain.
