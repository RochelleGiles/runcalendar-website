About

Run through the historic streets of Stratford-upon-Avon, the birthplace of legendary playwright William Shakespeare, in the annual Shakespeare Marathon & Half Marathon. This scenic, road-closed course takes runners on a captivating journey through the charming town, offering a unique opportunity to experience the rich literary and cultural heritage of this iconic British destination.
