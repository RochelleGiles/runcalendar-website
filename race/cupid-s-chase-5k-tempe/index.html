About

Cupid's Chase 5K is an annual national race series by Community Options, Inc. that celebrates its founding and raises funds for housing and employment opportunities for people with disabilities. The Tempe event welcomes runners, walkers, rollers with baby strollers or wheelchairs, and is held at Kiwanis Park.
