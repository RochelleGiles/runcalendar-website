Course

Starts at Knightstown on Valentia Island with the Atlantic on all sides, Dingle Bay and the Blaskets to one side and the Skelligs to the other. The organisers call the course challenging. The half marathon has water stations at about 10km and 15km and the 10K one at halfway.

About

This is your chance to run in what are undoubtedly Ireland's most scenic races. Starting from Knightstown on Valentia Island, you are surrounded by the Atlantic on all sides. Enjoy the views of Dingle Bay and the Blaskets to your right and the Skelligs to your left, as you experience a challenging course not to be missed.
