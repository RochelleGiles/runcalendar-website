Course

Starts in the open field behind the Red Top Mountain State Park Lodge parking lot and heads into woodland along the shoreline of Lake Allatoona. The route links the Sweet Gum, Homestead, Campground, Iron Hill and Visitor Center Loop trails, mixing single track and double track over rolling and steep hills with minimal roots and rocks. Distances of 3.1 and 8.85 miles are offered.
