About

Nestled in the heart of the French Alps, the Semi Marathon Nevache Val Des Pres Briancon offers runners a breathtaking challenge amidst stunning mountain scenery. This historic event takes participants on a scenic journey through the picturesque valleys and villages surrounding the town of Briancon, providing a unique and unforgettable running experience. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a passionate trail enthusiast, this semi-marathon is sure to captivate and inspire.
