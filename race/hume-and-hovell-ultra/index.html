Course

Trail courses on sections of the Hume & Hovell Walking Track, which follows the 1824 route of explorers Hamilton Hume and William Hovell. All distances start and finish at Henry Angel Trackhead near Tumbarumba and run predominantly on single track. Aid stations range from two on the 22km to ten on the 100km.

About

Set on the historic Hume and Hovell Track near Tumbarumba, this ultra offers 22km, 50km and 100km distances through rugged mountain ranges, forests and pine plantations, passing Buddong Falls, Paddys River Dam and Mannus Lake. Its 100km distance has counted toward the AURA national trail championship.
