About

The WUU2K Wellington Urban Ultra Marathon is a winter trail event on the hill trails surrounding Wellington, New Zealand. It offers 65km and 45km races starting from Khandallah and a 21km starting at Owhiro Bay. Each distance is capped at 250 entrants and compulsory gear applies.
