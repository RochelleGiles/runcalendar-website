Course

An out-and-back on the paved Shark Valley loop road in Everglades National Park, run to and from the Miccosukee Indian Village. The whole course is sealed and flat, surrounded by open Everglades wetland, with the 50K adding sections of Loop Road. Fully stocked aid stations and water stops are backed by race monitors and tribal police.

About

The Miccosukee Glades Runner Ultra runs to and from the Miccosukee Indian Village on the Shark Valley loop in Everglades National Park. Distances include 5 kilometers, 16.5 miles, and 50 kilometers, all on paved road, hosted by the Miccosukee Tribe.
