Course

The half marathon is an undulating route through historic Kirkcaldy, taking in Beveridge Park, Dunnikier Park and Ravenscraig Park before a coastal path section past Dysart and a finish along the refurbished Waterfront. The 5K and 10K trail races start from Dunnikier Park over grass, tarmac, gravel, soft trail and field edge through woodland and golf course. A children's fun run runs alongside.

About

The Kirkcaldy Parks Running Festival is a must-attend event for runners seeking a scenic and challenging course. Winding through the picturesque parks and green spaces of Kirkcaldy, this festival offers a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the local area while pushing your limits on the run. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a casual jogger, this event has something for everyone to enjoy.
