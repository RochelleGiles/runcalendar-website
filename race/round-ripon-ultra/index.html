Course

A 35-mile circular route starting and finishing at Studley Roger Village Hall near Ripon. The course crosses trail, woodland and moorland in the Nidderdale Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty before a closing downhill run through Studley Royal deer park. Four checkpoints and additional water stations serve the route, with savouries and toilets at checkpoint three around 19 miles.

About

The Ripon Ultra is a challenging long-distance running event hosted by Ripon Runners, offering runners an opportunity to test their endurance through the scenic landscapes of North Yorkshire. The event attracts both local and regional runners who are passionate about trail and ultra-running. Participants can expect a well-organized course that showcases the beautiful countryside around Ripon.
