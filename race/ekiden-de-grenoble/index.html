About

The Ekiden De Grenoble is a unique and historic running event that takes place in the picturesque city of Grenoble, France. This team relay race showcases the stunning scenery of the French Alps, offering runners a challenging and scenic course through the heart of the city. Whether you're an experienced runner or new to the sport, the Ekiden De Grenoble is an unforgettable experience not to be missed.
