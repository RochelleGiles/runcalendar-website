Course

An accurately measured four-lap 10K (plus 40m) in Beddington Park, starting and finishing opposite the Pavilion Cafe. The surface is 97% pathway and 3% field, making it a potential PB course. Water at several points, with registration, changing rooms, toilets and baggage at Beddington Park Cafe and ample parking.
