About

Explore the picturesque streets of Porto Vecchio, Corsica, during the annual 5 Km De Porto Vecchio race. This scenic course winds through the historic old town, offering stunning views of the Mediterranean coastline. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your journey, this event promises an unforgettable running experience in a breathtaking setting.
