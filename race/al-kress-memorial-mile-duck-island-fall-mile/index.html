About

The Al Kress Memorial Mile/Duck Island Fall Mile is a low-key event with multiple race distances, including a competitive walk, girls mile, boys mile, and adult mile. It is part of the SFTC KFOM, Adult Mile series and K&Q series, and qualifies for Tennessee state records.
