Course

The run events start and finish on the lawn at Broadwater Parklands, Southport. The course uses flat sealed highway road surface. Both the 10km and the 5km start at 6:00am. No aid station detail is published.

About

Scenic coastal views, a lively atmosphere, and a challenge for runners of all abilities - the Gold Coast 10 and 5 events are the perfect addition to the renowned Gold Coast Triathlon weekend. Set against the picturesque backdrop of Australia's Gold Coast, these races invite participants to push their limits and soak in the vibrant energy of the local community.
