Course

A point-to-point-feeling loop starting and finishing at Silver Hill Meadow in West Windsor. The 100 miler runs roughly 68 miles of rolling dirt road, 30 miles of horse trail and 2 miles of pavement for 17,000 feet of climb; the 100K covers 41 miles of dirt road, 20 of horse trail and 1 of pavement for 9,000 feet. No single major climb, but relentless small ones.

About

The Vermont 100 Endurance Race is one of the four races in ultrarunning's Grand Slam, covering 100 miles of dirt roads and horse trails through the Green Mountains near West Windsor, Vermont. A concurrent 100K follows the same course.
