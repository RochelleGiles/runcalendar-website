Course

A single 10-mile road course starting at Laurelton and Park in Long Beach. The route runs the city streets, taking in Connecticut Avenue and Oceanview before finishing on the Boardwalk at Laurelton Boulevard. Flat throughout, with ocean frontage for the closing miles.
