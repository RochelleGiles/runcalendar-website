Course

A point-to-point team relay of about 72 miles, starting at 2 Rocket Drive in Asheville, North Carolina and finishing at Hampton Station in Greenville, South Carolina. Teams of three to six share 12 legs of 3 to 9 miles, running from the Appalachian peaks down to the foothills and through craft breweries and small southern towns. Staggered starts begin around 5:00 am.

About

The Ville to Ville Relay celebrates the beautiful region from Asheville, NC to Greenville, SC. It is a 72-mile team running event traveling from the peaks to the foothills of the Appalachian Mountains, through local craft breweries, and into quaint southern towns.
