About

The Greymouth Harriers Two or Four Person Relay on 10 October 2026 is run along the West Coast Wilderness Trail from Kumara to Greymouth. The race starts outside the Kumara Theatre Royal Hotel and finishes beside the Grey River at the Blaketown Rugby Clubrooms. The relay is split into 4 legs of 8km, 5km, 6km and 6.6km which you can compete as a 2 Person Team (run or walk 2 alternate legs each) or a 4 Person Team of 4 (run or walk 1 leg each).
