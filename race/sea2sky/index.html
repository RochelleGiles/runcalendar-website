Course

An evening race from Newcastle's promenade in Co. Down: a road section leads onto trails and open mountain that climb gradually through the Mourne foothills to around 5K, with views over the town, before descending via Donard Park and road to finish back on the promenade. One water station at about 5K on the 10K route.

About

A 10K and 5K trail and road race starting at the seafront in Newcastle, County Down, and climbing toward the foothills of the Mourne Mountains. The route combines coastal parkland with rising forest and hill trails.
