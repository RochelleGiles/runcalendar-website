Course

Road races on rolling country roads through southern Adams County, starting and finishing at the Eisenhower Conference Center outside Gettysburg. The 10K and half marathon cross the Mason-Dixon line into Maryland and back, passing farms and wooded back roads. Courses stay open to light traffic with marshals directing runners.

About

The Gettysburg Festival of Races combines three historic races in one weekend just outside the Gettysburg battlefield. The course winds through wooded back country roads, past fields and farms, and crosses the Mason-Dixon line into Maryland.
