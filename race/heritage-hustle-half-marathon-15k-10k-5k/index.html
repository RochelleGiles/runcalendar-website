About

The Heritage Hustle offers a half marathon, 15K, 10K and 5K from Heritage Park in Olathe, Kansas. The half marathon and 15K start at 7:00am and the shorter races at 7:30am. It is run by Johnson County Park and Recreation District and caters to a wide range of abilities.
