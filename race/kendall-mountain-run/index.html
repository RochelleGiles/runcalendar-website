Course

A mountain loop starting in downtown Silverton, Colorado at 9,318 ft. Runners climb jeep roads to a final 300-foot scramble onto the 13,066 ft summit of Kendall Mountain before descending to finish at Memorial Park. The 12-mile course carries on over the back side of the peak; the 11K turns at Deer Park.
