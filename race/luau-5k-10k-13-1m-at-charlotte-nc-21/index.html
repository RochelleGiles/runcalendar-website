Course

A multi-lap greenway race starting and finishing at Mallard Creek Playground, opposite Mallard Creek Elementary School. The 5K and 10K run a 1.56-mile loop once or twice; the half marathon runs four laps of a slightly longer 1.64-mile loop on the Clarks Creek Greenway. There is a hill at the start/finish.

About

The Luau 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is a themed running event starting at 8:30 AM at Mallard Creek Playground in Charlotte, NC. Participants enjoy finisher medals, costume contests, and out-and-back courses with a 3.5-hour time limit.
