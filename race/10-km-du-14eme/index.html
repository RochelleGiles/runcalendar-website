About

The 10k du 14e is an annual running event organized by the Office du Mouvement Sportif du 14ème and the Mairie du 14e arrondissement. The race takes place in the streets of one of the most art deco neighborhoods of the French capital, offering runners a scenic and enjoyable experience.
