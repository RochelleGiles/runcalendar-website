About

The Lamoille Canyon Half Marathon & 5K is a scenic race through the stunning Ruby Mountains in Nevada. It features a half marathon starting at 6:00 am, a 5K at 7:00 am, and a free kids K at 8:00 am, with all participants receiving a shirt.[1][2]
