Course

About

The 45th annual running of the Steamboat Marathon is a scenic race through the Yampa Valley alongside the Elk River with snow-capped Rocky Mountains as backdrop. It is a certified Boston Qualifier race ranked as one of the Top 10 Destination Marathons in North America.
