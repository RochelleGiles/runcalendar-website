Course

Starts and finishes at Union Meadows recreation complex in Union Township, Pennsylvania. The organisers bill the half marathon as flat and fast: it loops the Berm Trail, then follows the river trail out to Douglassville and back. Water stations and portable toilets sit at miles 3, 7 and 9.

About

Spring Ahead with the perfect race to kickstart the season! The FLAT, FAST, Spring Ahead 1/2 Marathon to benefit Schuylkill River Greenways will start and finish at Union Township's Union Meadows recreation complex. After the race, all runners over 21 will receive a free beer at the Fork & Ale!
