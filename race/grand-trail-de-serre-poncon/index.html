About

The Grand Trail De Serre Poncon is a unique trail running festival in the heart of the French Alps. Runners will experience exceptional panoramas, breathtaking views of the Serre-Ponçon lake, and challenging trails along the mountain ridges. This event is not just a race, but an adventure where personal achievement meets the beauty of the alpine landscapes.
