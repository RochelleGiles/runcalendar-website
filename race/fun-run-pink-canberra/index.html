About

The Canberra edition of Fun Run Pink is an exciting part of the Ramsay Health Care Triathlon Pink Series, taking place at the picturesque Stromlo Forest Park and Leisure Centre. Runners can look forward to a vibrant atmosphere, as they challenge themselves on the scenic course and support a meaningful cause - all while enjoying the beautiful natural surroundings of Australia's capital.
