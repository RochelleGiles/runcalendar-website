About

The Hot2Trot Cottage Grove Run/Walk is a community race featuring half marathon, 5K, and kids 1K events at Fireman's Park. Proceeds benefit the Cottage Grove Fire Department, welcoming runners of all levels including elite athletes and firefighters.
