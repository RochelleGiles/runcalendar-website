Course

Half marathon, 5K run/walk and a kids 1K, all starting and finishing at Fireman's Park in Cottage Grove. The half sets off at 7:00am, the 5K at 7:45am and the kids 1K at 7:30am. Race support on course ends at 10:30am. The organiser does not state the surface or aid-station placing.

About

The Hot2Trot Cottage Grove Run/Walk is a community race featuring half marathon, 5K, and kids 1K events at Fireman's Park. Proceeds benefit the Cottage Grove Fire Department, welcoming runners of all levels including elite athletes and firefighters.
