Course

A snowshoe race with a mass start at 10:30am from the CCC Camp on County Route 90 near Mannsville. The course mixes groomed trail, packed singletrack and virgin-snow singletrack past trees, creeks and stone walls. There are no aid stations on course and food is served afterwards.

About

The Stone Wall Snowshoe Race is a snowshoe racing event held in Winona State Forest. It features 5K and 10K distances on well-marked trails suitable for all participants.
