About

Ocean City's annual OC CON event features a costumed 1-mile Super Hero Run for all ages starting at 9 a.m. on the Boardwalk at the Music Pier, followed by kids' Super Hero running events at 9:30 a.m. Participants can enjoy the OC CON Comic Book and Memorabilia Show afterward at the same location.
