Course

A 21-mile loop of the Ardgartan Peninsula starting and finishing at Ardgartan Forest Park near Arrochar, 90% off road over a mix of tarmac, forestry trails and open hill. The route runs out to Lochgoilhead before climbing back to the Rest and Be Thankful and descending to the finish, with about 3,500 ft of ascent. Water checkpoints at 7 and 13 miles.

About

Experience the stunning natural beauty of the Scottish Highlands at the Ardgartan Trail Race. This challenging 21-mile course winds through the picturesque Ardgartan Forest Park, offering runners breathtaking views and a true test of endurance. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable adventure in the heart of Argyll.
