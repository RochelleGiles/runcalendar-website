About

The Reykjavik Marathon offers runners a unique opportunity to explore the stunning natural beauty of Iceland's capital city. Winding through the heart of Reykjavik, this iconic race showcases the city's captivating blend of modern architecture and breathtaking landscapes. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time participant, the Reykjavik Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
