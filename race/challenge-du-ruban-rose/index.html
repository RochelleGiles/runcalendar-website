About

The Challenge Du Ruban Rose is a popular annual running event held in Bordeaux, France. The event features both a walk and a race, with participants coming together to support breast cancer prevention and screening. The course takes runners along the scenic quays of Bordeaux, offering a beautiful backdrop for the event. The organizers have made efforts to reduce the event's environmental impact, encouraging participants to bring their own reusable water bottles.
