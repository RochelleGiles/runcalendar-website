Course

Three colour-coded trail loops - Green, Blue and Black - starting and finishing at Red Hill Auditorium, private property open only on event day. The courses feature sustained climbing and technical descent. Water refill stations are on course and at the finish, and the route is marked with blue flagging tape and arrows.
