Course

A lapped course based at York Racecourse on Knavesmire Road. The first 2km of each lap is pathway and the remainder grass, mainly flat with one incline, and it can be muddy after rain. The 5k is one lap, the 10k two laps and the half marathon four laps plus an extra 1.1k section. Not suitable for wheelchairs.

About

The York 1.5k, 5k, 10k & Half Marathon MoRun is a must-attend event for runners of all levels in the heart of York. Enjoy a scenic route through the city's historic streets and beautiful parks, with options for a range of distances to challenge yourself. This annual running festival is a highlight on the local calendar, offering a fun and inclusive atmosphere for the whole community.
