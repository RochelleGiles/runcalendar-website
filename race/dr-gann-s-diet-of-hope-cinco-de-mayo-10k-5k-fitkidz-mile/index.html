Course

Begins and ends at Cholla High School in Tucson, winding through Starr Pass in the foothills of the Tucson Mountains. The route gives mountain scenery and wide views over the Tucson basin. Rider and chair athlete divisions are offered at both 10K and 5K.

About

The 44th annual edition of Tucson's most festive 10K and 5k winds its way through Starr Pass in the foothills of the Tucson Mountains. Run or walk the gorgeous course with beautiful mountain scenery and great views of the Tucson basin. Afterwards, join us for a Cinco de Mayo Celebration featuring a live mariachi band.
