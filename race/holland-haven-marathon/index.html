Course

Point-to-point marathon from Grand Haven to downtown Holland, tracking the Lake Michigan shoreline. The half marathon is a mostly flat course on paved paths, and the 8K is flat and run as an out-and-back.

About

Holland Haven Marathon is a road race held each September in Holland, Michigan, a city on the Lake Michigan shoreline. It offers marathon, half marathon and 8K distances for runners at different levels.
