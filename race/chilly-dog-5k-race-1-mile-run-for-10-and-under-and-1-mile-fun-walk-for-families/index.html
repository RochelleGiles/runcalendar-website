About

The Chilly Dog 5K is a family-friendly race starting with a train ride north from the Northern Central Railway station in New Freedom, PA. Participants run or walk back to the finish line at the station, with options for a 5K race, 1-mile run for kids 10 and under, and 1-mile fun walk.
