About

The Granada Half Marathon is a must-run event for any endurance enthusiast. Set against the stunning backdrop of Spain's historic city of Granada, this challenging 13.1-mile course winds through the city's picturesque streets and offers breathtaking views of the majestic Sierra Nevada mountains. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Granada Half Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
