Course

Point-to-point road ultramarathon of about 90km between Pietermaritzburg and Durban in KwaZulu-Natal. The 2027 race is the 100th edition and the 50th Down Run, starting in Pietermaritzburg and finishing in Durban. Sealed road throughout, with refreshment stations at regular intervals along the route.

About

The Comrades Marathon is an iconic ultra-marathon race held annually in South Africa. Known as 'The Ultimate Human Race', it covers a distance of approximately 90 km between the cities of Durban and Pietermaritzburg. The race is renowned for its challenging terrain, rich history, and the incredible camaraderie and spirit of the runners.
