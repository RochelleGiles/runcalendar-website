About

Les 21 kms de Casteljaloux is a November road race in the Lot-et-Garonne, run from the spa town of Casteljaloux through the surrounding Landes de Gascogne countryside. A 21.1 km and a 10 km race are timed, and a shorter charity walk starts alongside them.
