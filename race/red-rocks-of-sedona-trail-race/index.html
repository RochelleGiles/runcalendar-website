Course

A point-to-point trail race on Sedona's southside, starting at the United Methodist Church and finishing at Verde Valley School (the 2.5 miler excepted). The route crosses high desert slickrock and winding singletrack with dramatic elevation shifts and fast technical descents; the Hi-Line Trail features on all three longer distances, giving exposed ridgelines above Cathedral Rock. The course is fully marked with GPX files published, and aid stations are stocked throughout.
