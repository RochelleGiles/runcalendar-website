About

Hosted in collaboration with Macclesfield Harriers and previously known as 'Run Macc Fest', we are excited to announce the return of the 5k, 10k & Half Marathon this October! You will enjoy a fully road closed half marathon, 10k and 5k races, starting and finishing in the historic town of Macclesfield and heading out into the Cheshire countryside.
