Course

Starts and finishes at Kelly's Run Nature Preserve on an entirely off-road course, taking in creek crossings and steep climbs. A 10K and a 5K are offered, plus a free kids trail race of about 1.5 miles.

About

Experience one of the best events for trail running in the area! Offering two great choices for runners to experience the trails and parts of the Conestoga Trail along with the beauty of Kelly's Run Nature Preserve and the Pinnacle Overlook. Plus our FREE Kids Trail Run the Conestoga "Little Crusher" gets the kids an opportunity to experience some trails and makes a fun day for the whole family!
