Course

A forest course through and around the Buggenhoutbos, starting and finishing at Erkridoplein in Opstal. The 8 km, 14 km and 21 km all run on woodland paths and quiet lanes in and around the wood, with a kids' run alongside.

About

The Bosmarathon is a unique running event with a course that does not have any laps. Participants can expect a personalized bib number with a chip, a finisher's medal for the half marathon, a lively atmosphere with music and performances after the race, and aid stations along the way. The event also offers amenities like toilets, changing rooms, kilometer markers, and a secure bag check.
