Course

Britain's flattest big marathon, 54 m of climbing in 26.2 miles: from Bridgewater Way beside Old Trafford out through Stretford and Sale to the loudest crowds of the day in Altrincham at miles 7–8, back through Timperley, Sale Moor and the quiet far turn at Northenden, then Chorlton, Hulme and the roar of Oxford Road to finish outside Whitworth Hall at the University of Manchester.

About

The adidas Manchester Marathon is now the fourth largest marathon in Europe, making it a must-attend event for any serious runner. Set in the vibrant city of Manchester, UK, this iconic race offers a scenic course that winds through the heart of the city, providing a unique and unforgettable running experience.
