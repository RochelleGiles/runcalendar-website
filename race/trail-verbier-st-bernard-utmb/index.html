About

Discover the stunning Trail Verbier St Bernard by UTMB®, a challenging trail running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Swiss Alps. Runners will navigate rugged mountain terrain, passing through picturesque alpine villages and conquering demanding ascents, all while taking in the region's awe-inspiring natural beauty. This event offers an unforgettable experience for adventurous trail enthusiasts.
