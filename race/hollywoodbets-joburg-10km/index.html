Course

A city road race starting and finishing at the corner of Rand Show Road and Nasrec Road in Johannesburg, beside the Nasrec expo grounds. Both the 10km and 5km set off at 07:30 on closed tarmac streets. Race packs are collected at the same venue.

About

The Hollywoodbets Joburg 10km is hosted by Hollywood Athletics Club through the streets of Johannesburg, with a 10km road race and a shorter 5km option. It's grown into one of the city's most popular road running events.
