Course

A USATF-certified 5K starting and finishing on the track at The Citadel in Charleston. Runners complete a lap of campus, pass through the Summerall gate for a lap of Hampton Park, then return through the gate for a final campus lap. The course is sealed road and track, and is stroller friendly.

About

Enjoy a family friendly, pet friendly 5K run/walk down the beautiful historic campus of The Citadel located just outside the heart of Charleston. The course is also stroller friendly for those who want to bring the whole family. You will see the academic buildings, the barracks and the beautiful view of the Ashley River.
