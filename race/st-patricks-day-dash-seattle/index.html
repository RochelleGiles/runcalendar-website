Course

A city 5K starting at Seattle Center beside the Space Needle, heading south on 7th Avenue and looping through the Amazon campus and South Lake Union past the Spheres, with an out-and-back on Aurora Avenue before finishing at the Seattle Center International Fountain. A 1K Leprechaun Lap for under-10s loops within Seattle Center.

About

The St. Patrick's Day Dash is an annual 5K through downtown Seattle each March, drawing thousands of runners dressed in green. First held in the 1970s, it has grown into one of the largest St. Patrick's Day races in the United States.
