Course

A mostly flat, multi-terrain seven miles starting and finishing within the grounds of Kirkstall Abbey. The route takes in riverside paths and the canal towpath as well as road and the abbey grounds. Undulating in places and not suitable for wheelchairs. Field capped at 300.

About

A 7-mile multi-terrain race starting and finishing at the ruins of Kirkstall Abbey, a 12th-century Cistercian monastery on the River Aire in Leeds. The route follows the Leeds-Liverpool Canal towpath.
