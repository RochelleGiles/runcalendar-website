Course

A USATF-certified road course through historic downtown Wilmington, taking in much of the central city. The marathon is a Boston Marathon qualifier. Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K are offered, with a virtual option.

About

The Delaware Running Festival is Wilmington's spring marathon weekend, organized by Corrigan Sports Management. Runners can choose from marathon, half marathon, 10K, or 5K distances through Delaware's largest city each April.
