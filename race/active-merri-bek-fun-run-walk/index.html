Course

Starts at Coburg Olympic Swimming Pool and follows the Merri Creek Trail through Melbourne's northern suburbs. Options are a 10 km run, 5 km run and 5 km walk, all on the shared-use creek path. Day-of registration is available at the pool on race morning.

About

Explore the picturesque Merri Creek Trail in Coburg, Victoria during the Active Merri-bek Fun Run & Walk. This scenic event offers a 10km run, 5km run, or 5km walk, inviting runners and walkers of all levels to enjoy the natural beauty of the area. Whether you're looking for a challenging workout or a leisurely stroll, this community-focused event promises an enjoyable experience for the whole family.
