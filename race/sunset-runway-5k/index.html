About

The Sunset Runway 5K is more than just a race—it's an experience unlike any other. As the sun dips behind the rolling hills of Broome County, runners and walkers take off down the wide-open runway of the Greater Binghamton Airport, surrounded by panoramic views of the Southern Tier. The setting creates the perfect backdrop for golden-hour photos, with planes, clouds, and a fiery sky painting a scene you'll want to capture and remember. The flat, fast course makes it fun for both competitive racers and casual participants, while the atmosphere is pure celebration.
