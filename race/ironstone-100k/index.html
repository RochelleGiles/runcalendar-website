Course

A 63.4-mile point-to-point from Canoe Creek State Park, Hollidaysburg to Greenwood Furnace State Park, McAlevy's Fort. The route links three named trail sections over a mix of singletrack, rail trail and dirt road, with only short stretches of asphalt.

About

The Ironstone 100K is a test of endurance on the wildest trails of the Ridge and Valley of Pennsylvania. Runners will face a challenging course with over 20,000 feet of elevation gain, traversing rugged terrain and technical singletrack.
