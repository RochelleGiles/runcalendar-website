About

Join us Memorial Day weekend for the 6th Annual Maricopa Memorial Day 5k sponsored by ACP Painting! Run or walk our spectator-friendly course at the beautiful Copper Sky Recreation Center in Maricopa, AZ. This is a chip-timed event. All participants will receive custom finishers' medals and an event tech shirt. In addition, an award ceremony to follow the event for 1st-3rd Overall Male/Female Winners, Male/Female age group winners, and Top Co-Ed Teams will be held. 100% of profits will benefit local non-profit Veteran's organizations.
