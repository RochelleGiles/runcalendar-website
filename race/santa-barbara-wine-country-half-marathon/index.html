Course

Point-to-point half marathon starting at the corner of Atterdag Road and Laurel Avenue by Hans Christian Andersen Park, running through Los Olivos, over Corkscrew Hill and past more than seven vineyards to finish in the centre of Solvang. A relay and a 5K are also offered.

About

The Santa Barbara Wine Country Half Marathon starts in downtown Solvang, California, and runs through the vineyards and rolling hills of the Santa Ynez Valley. A 5K and half marathon relay are also offered.
