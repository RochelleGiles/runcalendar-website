Course

A flat, fast course on the park roads of Menomonee Park with a few small rises. The 5K is one lap and the 10K two. A two-mile walk, a 5K ruck and a free 1K children's race are also offered. Parking must be bought in advance from Waukesha County Parks.

About

The Flannel 5K and 10K are winter races held in Menomonee Park, Menomonee Falls, Wisconsin, with entrants encouraged to wear flannel. The course is flat and fast on park roads; a two-mile walk, a 5K ruck and a free children's race are also offered.
