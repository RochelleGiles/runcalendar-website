Course

A snowshoe race at Mountain Creek in Vernon. Entrants ride the cable car to the summit, strap on snowshoes and run or walk the 5K along the mountain ridge. An untimed last-mile option runs alongside it for beginners and first-time snowshoers.

About

A snowshoe running event at Mountain Creek where participants ride a lift to the mountain top and choose between a 5k ridge course or a shorter final-mile option. The event welcomes runners, walkers, and first-time snowshoe users.
