Course

The half marathon and 5K start together at William Allen High School on North 17th Street in Allentown and run a road course through the city. The half marathon course reopens to traffic after three hours under city regulations. A separate youth run is held the day before at Lehigh Parkway.

About

Discover why runners keep returning to this local favorite. The Half Marathon has a pioneering spirit that focuses on the wants and needs of the runners participating in the event. With the well-being of the participants always in mind, the race has always kept up with the cutting edge of racing technology and practices.
