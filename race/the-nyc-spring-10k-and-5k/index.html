Course

A looped course in Riverside Park South, entered near West 84th Street. The 5K covers one loop and the 10K two, starting and finishing at the same point. The route passes over the Freedom Tunnel above the Hudson. Water stations are placed roughly every 1.5 miles.

About

A scenic running event through Riverside Park on Manhattan's Upper West Side, featuring the seldom-seen Freedom Tunnel and views of the Hudson River. The course consists of 1 loop for the 5K and 2 loops for the 10K through the 150-year-old park designed by Frederick Law Olmsted.
