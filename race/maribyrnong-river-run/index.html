Course

Run entirely on the sealed asphalt path along the Maribyrnong River in Melbourne's west, out of Footscray Park. The course is flat and pram and wheelchair friendly, with drink stations every 2.5 to 3 km. The field is capped at 1,400 and the half marathon and 10 km carry a 2 hour 45 minute cut-off.

About

Explore the scenic Maribyrnong River on foot at the Maribyrnong River Run in Footscray Park. This picturesque event offers a range of distances, from a challenging 21.1km half-marathon to a family-friendly 5km run/walk, making it an ideal choice for runners of all abilities. Experience the beauty of this vibrant Melbourne suburb as you navigate the riverside trails.
