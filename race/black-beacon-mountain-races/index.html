Course

Mountain trail races across the Black Mountains and Bannau Brycheiniog National Park, run over a long weekend. The 100 mile, 50 mile and 21 mile races start and finish at Cusop Hall, Hay-on-Wye; the 50km Ultra Bach starts at Llangynidr village hall. Trails are fast and not overly technical. Checkpoints carry water, energy drinks and snacks, with drop bags on the longer races.

About

Conquer the rugged trails of the Brecon Beacons at the Black Beacon Mountain Races in the United Kingdom. This challenging multi-day event offers four long-distance trail races through the breathtaking landscapes of the Bannau Brycheiniog National Park, testing runners' endurance and adventurous spirit. Prepare to be captivated by the stunning natural scenery as you push your limits on this unforgettable mountain running adventure.
