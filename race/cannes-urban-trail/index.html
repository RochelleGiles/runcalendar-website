About

The Cannes Urban Trail offers several courses for accessibility to all! It's an opportunity to discover Cannes in an original way by crossing iconic places of the city. Cannes, the capital of outdoor sports, lives up to its name! Between timed courses, walking courses, and children's courses, there will be something for everyone. The playgrounds are varied between sand, road, trails, stairs, ... there's no time to get bored!
