Course

A 3.1-mile walk/run in Haulover Park, Miami Beach. The course starts on the south side of Atlantic Way near Haulover Inlet, runs north past the lighthouse, turns back toward the Ritz Carlton and returns to the inlet to finish.

About

The 4th annual Pelican Run 5K is a 3.1-mile walk/run event held at Haulover Park in Miami Beach. All proceeds support wildlife rehabilitation and environmental education programs.
