About

Quota of Indiana, PA is hosting its 10th Annual 5k fundraising event, both in-person and virtually. With over 50 active members, we have strived to benefit our community for the last 70 years. Quotarians are known especially for their service to deaf, hard-of-hearing and speech impaired individuals, and disadvantaged women and children. The Quota 5k Run/Walk is an event that promotes healthy living and the funds raised benefit our scholarships - the Gold Scholarship and the IUP Speech and Hearing Scholarship.
