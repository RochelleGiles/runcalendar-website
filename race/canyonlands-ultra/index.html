Course

A looped trail ultra on the Behind the Rocks Trail outside Moab, along the border of the Behind the Rocks Wilderness Study Area. Terrain ranges from smooth doubletrack to sandy washes and technical doubletrack; the 50 mile runs two loops, the 125K three and the 100 mile four, with shorter distances using part of the same loop. Cupless aid stations.

About

The Canyonlands Ultra, put on by Mad Moose Events, runs on slickrock and desert trails near Canyonlands National Park outside Moab, Utah. Distances span from a half marathon to 50 miles across the region's exposed red-rock terrain.
