Course

A 170-mile point-to-point ultra linking two Scottish long-distance trails, starting at Milngavie station and running the West Highland Way (94 miles) then the Great Glen Way (72 miles) with a short tarmac connector between them, for around 24,000ft of ascent. The route is fully waymarked. Support crews can meet runners at set checkpoints, with marshalled remote checkpoints handling drop bags. Entry requires prior completion of a 70-mile-plus race.

About

Experience the stunning landscapes of Scotland's Highlands on the By Way of The Glen, a 170-mile foot race between Glasgow and Inverness. This challenging ultramarathon takes runners through the picturesque glens and along the banks of tranquil lochs, offering breathtaking vistas at every turn. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or seeking a new adventure, the By Way of The Glen promises an unforgettable journey through the heart of the Scottish wilderness.
