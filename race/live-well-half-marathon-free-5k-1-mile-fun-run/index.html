Course

The USATF-certified half marathon and 5K both start at Town Common in Greenville, the half at 7:30am and the 5K at 9:00am, with a one-mile fun run in between. Four water stops serve the half; the 5K has one. A 3:30 limit applies to the half.

About

Kinetic Physical Therapy and Wellness is hosting an event with local sponsors and support to help promote the health of the Pitt County area! We want to give competitive and first time runners a chance to experience the benefits of exercise and healthy living with a half marathon and a FREE 5k and 1 mile Fun Run!
