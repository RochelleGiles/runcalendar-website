Course

A certified rolling loop of about 3.1 miles starting and finishing at the Fortified Hills Baptist Church car park, taking in Old Dallas Road, Holland Road, Cook Road and Brinkley Road. The 8:00am start is open to runners, joggers and walkers, and strollers are allowed.

About

Miles for Moms is a 5K and family fun festival in Dallas, Georgia, raising funds for Paulding Pregnancy Services and its Earn While You Learn and car seat safety programmes. The certified course is open to runners, walkers and strollers.
