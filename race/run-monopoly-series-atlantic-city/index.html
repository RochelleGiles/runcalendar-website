Course

An out-and-back on the Atlantic City Boardwalk, starting and finishing at the Boardwalk at Metropolitan Avenue. The surface mixes boardwalk and paved road. Aid stations sit at Kennedy Plaza, Jackson Avenue, Oriental Avenue and New Hampshire Avenue, the last two passed twice.

About

The Run Monopoly Series - Atlantic City is an officially licensed MONOPOLY-themed running event held in the birthplace of Monopoly. Runners race distances including half marathon and 5K along the boardwalk, passing legendary streets with coastal energy.
