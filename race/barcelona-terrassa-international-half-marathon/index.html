About

The Barcelona Terrassa International Half Marathon is a popular running event held annually in Terrassa, Spain. The course is a 21.097 km loop that starts and finishes at the Parc dels Catalans, offering runners a scenic tour of the city. The event attracts both local and international participants, creating a vibrant and inclusive atmosphere.
