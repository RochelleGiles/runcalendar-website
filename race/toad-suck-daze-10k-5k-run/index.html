Course

Both the 10K and the 5K begin and end on the Conway High School West Campus at 2300 Prince Street, Conway. The courses are USATF certified and run on city streets around the school as part of the Toad Suck Daze festival weekend.

About

The Toad Suck Daze 10K/5K is a road race in Conway, Arkansas, held each spring as part of the long-running Toad Suck Daze festival, named for the historic Toad Suck Ferry landing on the Arkansas River.
