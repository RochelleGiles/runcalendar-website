Held at Kapiolani Regional Park in Honolulu, with the ekiden relay and the 5K fun run sharing the same park circuit. Race briefing is at 7:00am and the ekiden and 5K start together at 8:00am, with a kids challenge at 10:30am. Packet pickup runs over the two days before the race.
