Course

A twelve-leg relay across central Oregon's high desert, with views of Smith Rock, Prineville Reservoir and the Crooked River. Running teams of four to six cover 67.5 miles from Brasada Ranch Road at Powell Butte; walking teams cover 43.7 miles starting from Crooked River Park in Prineville.

About

The Les Schwab Tires High Desert Relay is a one-day team relay event allowing participants to immerse in majestic mountains and big, clear skies. Each leg features spectacular views from the Cascade Mountains and Smith Rock to the Prineville Reservoir and the Crooked River.
