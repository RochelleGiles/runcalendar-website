Course

Lollipop course on the south side of Blue Marsh Lake: 5.5 miles out, a 4.5-mile loop, then 5.5 miles back, with 50K runners completing it twice and drop-bag access at mile 15.5. Start and finish at the Day Use Area car park, 1420 Palisades Drive, Leesport. The surface is roughly 80 percent single track and 20 percent gravel road, with aid stations about every 3 miles.
