About

The Santa Eularia Ibiza Marathon invites runners to experience the stunning scenery of Ibiza's east coast. Winding through picturesque villages and along the Mediterranean shoreline, this event offers a unique challenge for both seasoned marathoners and those seeking an unforgettable running adventure. Runners will be captivated by Ibiza's natural beauty as they push themselves to the finish line.
