Course

A point-to-point downhill half marathon starting at the top of Dry Fork Canyon and finishing in Vernal on paved road, with eight aid stations carrying water, sports drink and first aid. The 5K runs from Maeser Elementary School to Uintah High School, and the kids' half-mile starts and finishes at Uintah High School.

About

The Runtastic DINO is a road race at Vernal in Utah's Uinta Basin fossil country. The half marathon is a point-to-point descent from Dry Fork Canyon into town, with a 5K and a children's race alongside it. The organiser currently lists the event as on hiatus, with no edition scheduled.
