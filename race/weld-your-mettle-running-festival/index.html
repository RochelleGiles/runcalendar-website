Course

Start and finish on private land at Woodland Home Marketplace near Eaton, Colorado. The course is a four-mile horseshoe segment run two miles out and back, repeated for the longer distances. The surface is groomed trail and dirt and mostly flat. Two aid stations serve the loop, one at the start/finish and one at the turnaround, both with crew access.
