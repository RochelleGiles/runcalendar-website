Course

A half marathon on Staten Island starting at 8:00 am, with views over New York Harbor and the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge. Water and Gatorade stations are placed along the route, with a Maurten gel depot at mile 7. Runners are advised to take the Staten Island Ferry from Manhattan to reach the start.
