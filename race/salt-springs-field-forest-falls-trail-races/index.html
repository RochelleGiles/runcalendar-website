Course

Technical trail courses starting and finishing at Salt Springs Park, Montrose. The 5K runs almost entirely on trail through old-growth forest; the 10K adds a flat, fast wetland section plus three steep climbs and technical descents. The 50K is five loops of the 10K, over 7,000ft of gain, with an aid station at each loop start.

About

The Salt Springs Field, Forest & Falls Trail Races are held at Salt Springs Park near Montrose, Pennsylvania. Runners choose a 5K, 10K or 50K over technical singletrack through old-growth forest, waterfalls and wetland, with the 50K run as five loops.
