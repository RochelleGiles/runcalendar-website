Course

Four interconnected singletrack loops at Kanopolis State Park - Horsethief Canyon, Prairie Trail, Alum Creek and Big Bluff - taking the north half of each loop outbound and the south half back, with around ten metal gates per loop. Start and finish are at the Corral Shelter. The marathon covers three loops, the 21-mile skips Big Bluff and the 50K doubles it; manned aid sits near 13 and 18 miles.

About

The Rockin K Trail Runs take place at Kanopolis State Park near Ellsworth, Kansas, offering 50K, marathon, 21-mile, and 4-mile distances. Entry across all distances is capped at 120 runners.
