Course

A flat bayside course on the fully closed roads of Beach Road, starting and finishing at Elsternwick Park in Brighton. The marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K courses are AIMS-certified. Waves start from 6:00am and all runners must be off the course by 11:00am.

About

Previously known as the Sandy Point Half Marathon, the Brighton Beach Marathon is a scenic fast and flat bayside course through Brighton, Hampton and Sandringham. The event offers multiple race distances along Melbourne's iconic coastline.
