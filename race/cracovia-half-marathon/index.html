About

The Cracovia Half Marathon has become a mass event of international renown over the past decade. The characteristic finish line in the darkened largest sports and entertainment hall in Poland - TAURON Arena Kraków - is a unique setting that remains in the memory for a long time! Runners who have already made their debut in the Cracow half marathon know that such a finish, with the cheers of the fans gathered in the arena, is a real treat and a shot of endorphins.
