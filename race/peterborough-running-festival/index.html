About

Set on a 5km loop of hard-standing paths within the beautiful Nene Park, this flat and fast course is perfect for runners aiming for a new personal best. Just a few miles from the city centre, Nene Park is one of the region's largest and most loved green spaces. With scenic meadows, peaceful lakes, and woodland trails, it's a favourite for both local visitors and runners alike. Whether you're racing the 5K, 10K or Half Marathon, this stunning route has something for everyone.
