About

Explore the rich industrial heritage of the West Midlands at the Birmingham Black Country Half Marathon. This point-to-point course winds along the picturesque canal path, connecting the vibrant cities of Wolverhampton and Birmingham. Runners can immerse themselves in the area's unique history and scenery as they tackle this challenging yet rewarding half marathon.
