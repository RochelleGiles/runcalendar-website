About

This years race is a hybrid event at scenic Vanburen Park, 2607 Connors Road, Baldwinsville NY 13027 just off rt 690 west at the Vanburen Road exit. The race includes the challenging hill veterans have nicknamed 'Schneider Hill'. The venue is a favorite training facility of area high school cross country teams.
