Course

Trail run on Genesis Church property in York, starting and finishing at 1405 Seven Valleys Road, with roughly 56 feet of elevation change. The 5K run starts at 8:00am, the one-mile walk at 8:10am and a quarter-mile kids fun run at 9:00am. No aid station detail is published.
