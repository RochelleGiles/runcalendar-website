Course

About

The Grace Race 5K & 1 Mile Run/Walk, hosted by Grace Community Church Boca, benefits children and youth in the community. All proceeds support 4KIDS of South Florida, Trail Life USA Troop 6:33, and the outreach efforts of Amped Student Ministries.
