About

The Faultline Ultra is an adventure that will take your breath away. With 6 challenges for every adventurer, the event offers trail bliss ranging from 161km to shorter distances, all designed to inspire and provide the best after-match stories. The event weaves through the best trails from Kāpiti, the Hutt river trail, Belmont, Porirua, Karori, and Wellington City, making it a true trail running paradise.
