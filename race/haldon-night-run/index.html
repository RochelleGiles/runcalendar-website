Course

After-dark trail run in Haldon Forest, Devon. Single-loop 10K and 5K courses start and finish at the same arch, marked throughout with reflective poles and arrows, and both run slightly over their nominal distances. Head torches required. Shorter 2K and 1K runs are offered for children, plus a 10K canicross wave.

About

Explore the enchanting trails of Haldon Forest at night during the annual Haldon Night Run. Runners can choose between a challenging 10km loop or a more manageable 5km course, both winding through the serene woodland landscape under the glow of the moon. This unique event offers an unforgettable running experience in the heart of the United Kingdom.
