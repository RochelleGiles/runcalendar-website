Course

About

The Mangum Marathon and Half Marathon is a rustic, no-frills road race on the quiet rural roads around Ellerbe, North Carolina. Runners start and finish at "The Dog Pen" and can expect long stretches of solitude, rolling terrain and minimal course markings. January conditions are typically cold, and the event is largely self-supported.
