About

Explore the stunning landscapes of Beppu and Ōita at the Marathon De Beppu Ita, a captivating running event that showcases the natural beauty of this region. Runners will traverse a scenic course through hot springs, lush forests, and seaside vistas, offering a truly unforgettable experience for participants. This event is a must-add to the calendar for any runner seeking a unique and visually-striking marathon challenge.
