Course

A 47-mile (75 km) clockwise circuit of Lake Windermere starting and finishing at Low Cunsey Farm near Ambleside, with a 6am start. The route is mainly trail and crosses five summits, with only small sections following quiet roads. Five feed stations plus a halfway aid station at Troutbeck.

About

The Lap is a 47-mile ultra trail race that circles Windermere, England's largest natural lake, starting and finishing in Ambleside in the Lake District. It runs clockwise or anticlockwise depending on the edition.
