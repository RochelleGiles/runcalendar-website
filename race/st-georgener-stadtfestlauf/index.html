About

St. Georgen im Schwarzwald is a beautiful mountain town in the heart of the Black Forest. The town's train station is the highest two-track station in Germany at 806 m. Shaped by economy, tradition, culture, and commitment in harmony with innovation and progressive change, St. Georgen is a place where living is still affordable. Moreover, you are directly surrounded by beautiful nature. Relaxation starts right at your doorstep!
