Course

Starts and finishes at Clipston Recreation Ground on Station Road, Market Harborough, climbing straight out of the village into the Northamptonshire countryside before dropping towards Kelmarsh and looping back. The route runs mainly on bridleways with short stretches of minor country road and a footpath section on the return, and is hilly with about 237 m of ascent. Both races start at 9am with a 3.5-hour limit.

About
