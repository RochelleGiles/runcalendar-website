Course

A multi-loop trail ultra on a 13.4km purpose-built loop at Umdoni Park, Pennington. The route runs flowing singletrack through indigenous coastal forest with open fairways, dune paths, beachside stretches and elevated viewing decks. Two aid stations serve each loop for all multi-loop events.

About

Umdoni100 is a trail ultra based at Pennington on the KwaZulu-Natal South Coast, offering 80km and 160km (100-mile) distances through the coastal Umdoni region.
