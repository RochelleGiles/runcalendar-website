Course

Evening races on a 5km loop at the Auckland Domain, starting and finishing at the Grandstand. The 10km runs the same loop twice. Walkers set off at 5:30pm and runners at 6:00pm, every Thursday through daylight saving.

About

The Y Marathon Club's Summer Series offers 5K and 10K timed races in the Auckland Domain every Thursday during Daylight Saving. It's the perfect way to challenge yourself and stay active.
