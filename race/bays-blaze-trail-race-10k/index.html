Course

A trail 10K starting at Bays Mountain Park. It opens with a floating-bridge crossing of the reservoir, then follows singletrack through the Cherry Knobs, Cross Ridges, Back Hollow, Chinquapin and Lake Road sections. The surface is singletrack over rocky terrain and rolling ridgelines in hardwood forest, starting at 6:00 PM.
