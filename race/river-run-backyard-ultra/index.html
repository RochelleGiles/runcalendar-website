Course

Backyard-ultra format: a flat 6.7 km loop following the Tukituki River, with a new lap starting on the hour from 9:00am until one runner remains. Every lap starts and finishes at event HQ, the A&P Showgrounds in Waipukurau. Surface mixes limestone path and dirt single track that can be slippery or muddy in wet conditions. HQ has covered space, toilets, power and water.

About

The River-Run Backyard Ultra is New Zealand's only free Backyard Ultra event. Runners will take on a 6.7km loop course, running one lap every hour until they can no longer continue. The event takes place in the picturesque town of Waipukurau, offering a scenic and challenging course for ultrarunners to test their limits.
