About

The Szczecin Night Marathon is a unique nighttime running event that transforms the city into a vibrant running festival. Runners experience Szczecin's most iconic landmarks, including the Port Gate, Royal Gate, Philharmonic Hall, Pomeranian Dukes' Castle, and the industrial Szczecin Shipyard, creating an unforgettable urban running experience.
