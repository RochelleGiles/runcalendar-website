Course

A hundred-mile loop course in Stub Stewart State Park near Buxton, Oregon, through towering forest and along scenic rail trails, with just over 7,000ft of climbing. Start and finish at the Hill Top Day Use Area, and more than 25 crew access points. Run over 24 and 25 April; a two-person relay is also offered.

About

Welcome to Stub Stewart State Park…. it is such a gem of the Oregon State Park system! We are grateful to be able to utilize this amazing outdoor adventure hub as our home base for what we hope becomes one of the Premier 100 mile Ultramarathons in the Pacific Northwest!
