Course

A flat, officially certified half marathon starting and finishing on the Boulevard in San Sebastian, and listed on the Spanish athletics federation calendar. Water stations sit at 5K, 10K, 15K and 20K, with extra refreshments at 15K and water, isotonic drinks and snacks at the finish.

About

The San Sebastian Half Marathon is a picturesque running event along the stunning Basque coastline of northern Spain. Runners will take in breathtaking ocean views and historic architecture as they challenge themselves over the 13.1-mile course through the charming city of San Sebastian. This popular race is a must-do for any runner seeking an unforgettable European running experience.
