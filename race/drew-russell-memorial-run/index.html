About

This 5K Run/Walk honors Captain Drew Russell, a WMU ROTC alumnus and Vicksburg High School graduate from Scotts, Michigan, who was killed in action in Kandahar Province, Afghanistan. He is remembered as a stand-up man with a great sense of humor who always looked out for his friends and family.
