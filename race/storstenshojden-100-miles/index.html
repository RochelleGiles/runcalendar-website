About

Storstenshöjden SkogsUltra is a 100 km race with 4,100 meters of elevation gain on a technical course in the Kilsbergen mountains in the middle of winter. The course consists of a 60 km loop with a mix of marshes, hiking trails, and off-trail sections, followed by two laps of a 21 km loop with a technical mountain ultra character. Participants must be self-sufficient, as there are no aid stations, drop bags, or transportation provided.
