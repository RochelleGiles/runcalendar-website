Course

A 5-mile lap of the trail network at Lake Fairfax Park, run once for the 5-mile and twice for the 10-mile, with views over the lake. Mostly smooth singletrack with a few short rocky, rooty sections and little pavement; no long climbs but constant short ups and downs, about 300ft of gain per lap.

About

Part of EX2 Adventures' Fall Backyard Burn series, this 5-mile and 10-mile trail race winds through the wooded trails of Lake Fairfax Park in Reston. It is one of several stops in a fall trail-running series held across Northern Virginia parks.
