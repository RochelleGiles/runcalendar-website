About

The McLeans Forest Trail offers a scenic running experience weaving through beautiful old grown pine forest and along river banks. The flat course treats runners to stunning natural scenery and pristine farmland, with terrain consisting of 85% well-formed track/forestry road and 15% river stone pathways.
