About

The Founder's Day Run, presented by the Union R-XI Foundation, is now past its 47th running. Since the Foundation took over the event, the race has returned to its roots with an evening start during Union's annual Founder's Day Festival. It is the oldest and longest-standing road race in Franklin County and one of the oldest in the St. Louis metro area, known for being a tough course.
