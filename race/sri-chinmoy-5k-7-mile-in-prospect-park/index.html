Course

A scenic loop course in Brooklyn's Prospect Park, run counterclockwise on the pedestrian lane of Park Drive through forest and meadow with a few hills. The 5K covers just under one loop and the 7-mile two full loops, both starting and finishing at Lincoln Road Lawns.

About

The Sri Chinmoy 5K & 7-Mile Race, now past its 9th running, takes place in Brooklyn's Prospect Park on a scenic loop course. The 5K and 7-mile events start simultaneously.
