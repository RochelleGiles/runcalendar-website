Course

Coastal routes on the Wirral starting and finishing at Fort Perch Rock in New Brighton. The half marathon and 10K are largely flat, turning at Seacombe Ferry and returning by Marina Bridge and the Clown roundabout towards Moreton; the half includes a 200m off-road section before the turn. The marathon route is being redrawn for 2027.

About

Explore the picturesque Wirral Peninsula at the Love2run Wirral Marathon, a community-driven event hosted by the Paul Lavelle Foundation. This scenic course winds through charming villages and along the stunning Mersey Estuary, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of this stunning region of the United Kingdom.
