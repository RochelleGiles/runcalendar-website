About

This event is for all ages and abilities, whether you're in it to win or out for a fun Sunday morning walk. Come out and take a step to help kids in Grand Junction by participating in the 2.5K, 5K, or 10k distance. *The really littles, 4 & under, can participate in the free Hatchling Hustle!
