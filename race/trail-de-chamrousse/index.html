About

The Trails des Lacs de Chamrousse-Belledonne (TLCB) is a running event that takes place in the beautiful mountain town of Chamrousse, France. The course showcases the stunning natural landscapes of the Belledonne mountain range, with runners traversing scenic trails and lakes. The event is known for its challenging terrain and breathtaking views, making it a must-do for trail running enthusiasts.
