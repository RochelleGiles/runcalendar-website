Course

Six-hour looped timed event through Sherwood Pines forest in Nottinghamshire, run over two days with day one on the Saturday and day two on the Sunday. Runners complete as many laps of the forest loop as they like within the time limit, at whatever pace they choose.

About

Immerse yourself in the vibrant colors of autumn at the Rasselbock Run in Sherwood Pines Forest, Nottinghamshire. This two-day running event offers a scenic journey through the lush, historic woodland, providing a unique and captivating experience for runners of all levels. Embrace the crisp, refreshing air and the beauty of the changing seasons as you tackle this challenging yet rewarding course.
