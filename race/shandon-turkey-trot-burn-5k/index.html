About

The Shandon Turkey Trot & Burn 5K is a community race organized by the Shandon Neighborhood Council, welcoming runners and walkers through the beautiful tree-lined streets of the Shandon neighborhood in Columbia, SC. Proceeds benefit neighborhood improvements, with a flat, USATF-certified 5K course starting near Craft and Draft, capped at 1000 participants.
