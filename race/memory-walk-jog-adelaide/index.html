Course

The course starts and finishes at Wigley Reserve in Glenelg and is flat and wide, following a paved walking path for its entire length. A short grassy section leads from the start archway onto the path. The route narrows where it passes the weir.

About

Memory Walk & Jog Adelaide invites the whole family to a morning of fitness and fundraising at the picturesque Wigley Reserve in Glenelg. Lace up your sneakers and join this community event supporting a meaningful cause, with both walking and jogging routes available to suit all abilities. Enjoy the scenic coastal views and camaraderie as you get active and give back.
