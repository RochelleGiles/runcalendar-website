Course

Out-and-back dirt trail course starting and finishing at First Dam / Canyon Entrance Park in Logan. The route follows the Bonneville Shoreline Trail toward Green Canyon and returns to the start. Water stops sit at the 5K turnaround and at a second point on the 10K route.

About
