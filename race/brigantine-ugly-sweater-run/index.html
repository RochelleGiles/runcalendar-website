About

Green Whales' ugly sweater run on Brigantine island, held on Small Business Saturday: a 10K, a 5K, a kids' one-mile fun run and a one-mile walk for all ages, all starting and finishing at the Brigantine Community Center on 42nd Street. The 10K starts at 9:30, the 5K at 9:55 and the mile events at 10:00. Afterwards there is hot cocoa, coffee and desserts for runners and volunteers, with ugly sweater contests for everyone.
