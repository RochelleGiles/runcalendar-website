Course

One continuous 200-mile loop around Summit County, Colorado, run mostly on singletrack with stretches of infrequently travelled forest road, hiking trail, 4WD road and recreation path. The low point sits above 9,000ft and the course reaches close to 13,000ft, gaining and losing roughly 40,000ft overall. Field capped at 75 runners, 109-hour cutoff, and entrants must have finished a 100-miler.

About

The Summit 200 is an ultramarathon through Summit County, Colorado, tracing high alpine trails across the Rocky Mountains near Keystone and Frisco. The inaugural edition covers roughly 200 miles of rugged mountain terrain.
