Course

The 5K and 10K start together at KC Summers Toyota at 6:30am, with a separate 1 mile fun run from YMCA Park at 8:30am. The routes run through Lytle Park and the surrounding streets of Mattoon. A virtual option is also offered.

About

Join us for Mattoon's favorite tradition! The 39th annual Run for the Bagel will be held on Saturday, July 18th. Participants choose to walk or run 5k or 10k distances that wind through Lytle Park & around Mattoon. Celebrate with Lender's Bagels at the finish line!
