About

Matawan Borough's Turkey Trot, in its 20th year in 2026, is held on the Saturday after Thanksgiving. Runners and walkers share a certified 5K course that starts and finishes on Main Street, and families are welcome. Online entries close about a week before the race; you can still enter in person on race morning. Net proceeds support the borough's parks, beautification projects and recreation programs, and the race collects for the Matawan Food Pantry.
