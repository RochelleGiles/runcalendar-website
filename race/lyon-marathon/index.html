About

The Lyon Marathon is a prestigious running event that takes place in the vibrant city of Lyon, France. The course showcases the city's stunning architecture and scenic riverbanks, offering runners a unique and memorable experience. With a lively atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators, the Lyon Marathon is a must-do event for runners of all levels.
