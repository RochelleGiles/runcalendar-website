Course

A 36.8 km Lake District horseshoe fell race with about 2,290 m of climb, starting and finishing at Ennerdale Scout Camp near Ennerdale Bridge. Nine checkpoints take in Great Borne, Red Pike, Black Beck Tarn, Green Gable, Kirk Fell, Pillar, Haycock, Iron Crag and Crag Fell. Only the opening and closing sections are flagged, so runners need to navigate.

About

The Ennerdale Horseshoe is a demanding long-distance fell race in the Lake District, circling the Ennerdale valley over remote, rugged fells. Rated among the toughest routes in British fell running, its roughly 23-mile horseshoe carries serious ascent with few chances to bail out.
