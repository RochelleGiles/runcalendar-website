Course

Starts and finishes at Gulf Beach Recreation Area in Milford, running along the shoreline and over the Silver Sands State Park boardwalk. The USATF-certified course is flat throughout. Five aid stations stocked with water and Gatorade Endurance sit at miles 2.75, 4.75, 6.75, 8.75 and 10.75, with toilets at the start/finish and at four points on course.

About

This USATF-certified half marathon runs along Milford's Connecticut shoreline, starting at Gulf Beach Recreation Area. The flat, fast coastal course regularly sells out and has served as the RRCA Eastern Region Championship race.
