Course

A six-hour lapped challenge in the grounds of Hardwick Hall, Derbyshire, on a 6.03km loop with a hill each lap. Runners complete as many laps as they choose, with four laps making a half marathon and seven a marathon. The route crosses working farmland and is often muddy or boggy; trail shoes are recommended.

About

The Hardwick Hobble - Winter is a scenic running event held in the picturesque grounds of Hardwick Hall in the United Kingdom. Participants can enjoy a challenging course that winds through the historic estate, offering stunning views of the countryside. This event is a great way to start the new year with a healthy and invigorating outdoor activity.
