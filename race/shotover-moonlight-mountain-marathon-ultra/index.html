Course

A point-to-point mountain course on Ben Lomond Station near Queenstown, starting near the Shotover River and finishing at Moke Lake. Runners follow sheep tracks, historic gold mining water races and high country mustering routes through tussock, mountain ridges, beech forest and creeks, with a scree slope descent, a ladder climb beside a waterfall and a tunnel on the valley floor.

About

Nestled in the breathtaking landscapes of Queenstown, New Zealand, the Shotover Moonlight Mountain Marathon & Ultra offers runners a truly unforgettable challenge. From the iconic 56km Ultra to the scenic 10km trail run, this event caters to a range of skill levels, inviting participants to conquer the rugged terrain under the glow of the moon.
