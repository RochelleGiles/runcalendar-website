Course

Starts outside Lucas Oil Stadium and winds through downtown Indianapolis before entering the stadium through the players' tunnel to finish on the 50-yard line. Sealed city streets throughout, with a 1K Blue's Fun Run for children alongside the 5K. Strollers and wheelchairs are permitted.

About

The Colts 5K starts and finishes at Lucas Oil Stadium, home of the NFL's Indianapolis Colts, with runners crossing the finish line inside the stadium. The event also features Blue's Fun Run, a 1K for kids alongside the Colts mascot.
