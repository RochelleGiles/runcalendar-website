About

The Argentan Half Marathon is a diverse and well-organized running event that takes runners through the scenic countryside and forests around the town of Argentan. The course features a single loop with a 4km stretch through the Gouffern Forest, as well as passages through several charming villages. Runners can enjoy aid stations every 5km and free access to the local aquatic center after the race.
