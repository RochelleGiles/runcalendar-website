Course

A certified out-and-back half marathon starting by the Duck Pond in St. Vital Park at 8am, running Perimeter Road, River Road and the Red River bike path, then out along University Crescent, Sifton Road and Cloutier Drive before returning to the park. The 5K Fun Run covers two laps of Perimeter Road inside St. Vital Park and starts shortly after the half.
