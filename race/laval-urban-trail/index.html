About

The Laval Urban Trail is a captivating running event that takes participants on an exhilarating journey through the vibrant streets and hidden gems of the city. Winding through a unique urban landscape, this race offers runners an opportunity to explore Laval's rich history and diverse architecture while challenging themselves physically. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or a newcomer to the sport, the Laval Urban Trail promises an unforgettable running experience.
