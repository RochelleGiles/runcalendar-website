Course

A chip-timed 5K based at Atypical Brewery and Barrelworks on East Central Avenue in Minot. A course map is linked but the organiser gives no route, surface or aid station detail. The beer mile runs four quarter-mile laps on a coned course beside a sectioned-off beer zone.

About

We love running, dogs, and beer and we wanted turn those loves into a way to give back to the animal rescue organizations in our community. This race is fun and social and is NOT a qualifier for the World Championship (yes that's a real thing).
