About

The Chartreuse Backyard Ultra is a unique running event where participants run a 6.7 km loop every hour until only one runner remains. It's an opportunity for runners to challenge themselves and push their limits, whether it's completing a half-marathon, a full marathon, 100 km, or even 100 miles in 24 hours. The event takes place in the beautiful Chartreuse region, offering a scenic and challenging course for the participants.
