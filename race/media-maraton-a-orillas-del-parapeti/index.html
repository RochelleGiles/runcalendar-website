About

Explore the stunning landscape of Bolivia's Parapetí River at the Media Maraton A Orillas Del Parapeti. This half-marathon event takes runners along the banks of the tranquil Parapetí, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains and countryside. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a beginner looking for a scenic challenge, this race promises an unforgettable running experience.
