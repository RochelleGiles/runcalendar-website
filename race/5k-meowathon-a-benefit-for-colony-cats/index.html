About

The 5K Meowathon is a benefit for Colony Cats, an all-volunteer 501(c)(3) non-profit that addresses cat overpopulation in central Ohio through public awareness and spay/neuter efforts. All proceeds from the race support the organization's work, and a children's fun run is held alongside it.
