About

The 13th Annual Samuel F Pearson Memorial Day Event is a 5K run/walk held in honor of Sam Pearson, a Piqua native and Army soldier who made the ultimate sacrifice. Funds raised support scholarships for Piqua High School seniors through the Piqua Education Foundation.
