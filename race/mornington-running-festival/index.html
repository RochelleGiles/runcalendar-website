Course

The course runs along the Esplanade from Mornington to Mt Martha and back, on sealed road closed to traffic, with Port Phillip Bay alongside for most of the way. All distances share the same out-and-back route.

About

The Mornington Running Festival takes place on the picturesque Mornington Peninsula, offering a scenic course that winds along the Esplanade from Mornington Park to Mt Martha and back. All finishers receive a medal and a free donut.
