About

The Boise Marathon is an annual summer road race in Boise, Idaho, organised by the charity team SoleKIDs. It offers marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K events plus a virtual option, with finisher medals and shirts for all entrants. The June date is chosen for one of the longest daylight days of the year.
