About

The Hokkaido Marathon is a must-run event for outdoor enthusiasts, showcasing the breathtaking natural beauty of Japan's northernmost island. Runners will wind through scenic landscapes, including lush forests and stunning coastlines, providing a truly immersive experience for participants. This event is perfect for those seeking a challenging yet rewarding running adventure in a one-of-a-kind destination.
