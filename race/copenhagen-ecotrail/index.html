About

The Copenhagen Ecotrail is a unique running event that takes participants on a scenic journey through the beautiful city of Copenhagen. The course highlights the city's green spaces, historic landmarks, and sustainable initiatives, offering runners a one-of-a-kind experience. With a focus on environmental responsibility, the event promotes a healthy and eco-friendly lifestyle.
