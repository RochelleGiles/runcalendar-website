Course

A point-to-point road marathon starting at the Shops at Wailea, running south to turn back at the Fairmont, then down onto South Kihei Road through oceanfront Kihei Town and north along Highway 30 to finish outside Launiupoko Beach Park. The entire course is asphalt or sealed shoulder bike path, mostly 4 to 8 feet wide, and is certified as a Boston qualifier.

About

The Maui Oceanfront Marathon runs the south and west coast of Maui each January, with a marathon, half marathon, 6-miler and 5K on the programme alongside a Thursday cookie run. The certified marathon course is a Boston qualifier, run entirely on asphalt beside the ocean.
