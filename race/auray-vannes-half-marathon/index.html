Course

Point-to-point half marathon starting at 10:00 on the RD17 in the Kerfontaine zone at Auray-Pluneret and finishing on the athletics track at the Stade de Kercado in Vannes. Aid stations come at 4.8km (Le Bono), 8.2km, 12.2km, 16.3km and 18.5km, plus the finish. A three-hour time limit applies. A 10km, a 5km and a nordic walking event run alongside.

About

The Auray Vannes Half Marathon offers runners a scenic tour through the charming coastal towns of Auray and Vannes in Brittany, France. Participants can expect beautiful views of the region's historic architecture, rolling hills, and the sparkling waters of the Gulf of Morbihan as they challenge themselves on this picturesque 13.1-mile course.
