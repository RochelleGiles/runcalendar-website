Course

Three mountain trail courses start and finish at Mont Sion in Saint Blaise, on the Salève massif south of Geneva: La Thuile over 17.8 km, Les Pitons over 24.8 km and La Croisette over 37.8 km. The routes are ITRA-measured.

About

The Trail du Salève is a captivating trail running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Salève mountain range near the France-Switzerland border. Runners can expect a challenging yet rewarding course that winds through scenic alpine landscapes, offering breathtaking views and an unforgettable outdoor adventure. This event is a must for any trail enthusiast seeking an exceptional running experience.
