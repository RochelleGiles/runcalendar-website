Course

A 10KM starting on Cartwright Street by Performance Works on Granville Island, looping over the Burrard Bridge onto the Seawall, turning at Science World and returning along the Seawall to finish just off Island Park Walk. Paved throughout and flat, on closed seawall path and city street; the seawall can be slippery when wet. A kids run follows at 8:50am.
