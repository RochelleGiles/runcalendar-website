About

The Love the Run You're With 5K is a run/walk in New Braunfels, Texas, where participants enter in pairs and dress as a well-known duo with a partner, friend or pet. Proceeds go to the Steve's Pantry Food4Kids programme, which supplies food and hygiene items to local children and families.
