Course

A 5K run on the grounds of the Stratford Ecological Center nature preserve near Delaware, Ohio. The course is mostly nature trail with some sections of crushed gravel, starting and finishing on the Stratford property.

About

The Fantastic Frigid 5K Series is held twice each winter in partnership with Stratford Ecological Center near Delaware, Ohio. Participants can sign up for either date or both.
