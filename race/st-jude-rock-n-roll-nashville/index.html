Course

The marathon and half marathon start at 8th and Broadway and finish at Nissan Stadium. The 10K and 5K start at 8th and Demonbreun with the same finish, and the 1 Mile both starts and finishes at Nissan Stadium. Courses run through downtown Nashville on relatively flat road.

About

This unique event is jampacked with authentic Nashville music at every turn. Prepare to be immersed in music culture from the Health & Fitness Expo to the finish line when you join us for the St. Jude Rock 'n' Roll Running Series Nashville. Experience a legendary tour of Music City, passing iconic landmarks like Broadway, Music Row, The Gulch, the Country Music Hall of Fame, and more.
