About

The Bol Dair De Saint Avertin is a thrilling running event set in the picturesque town of Saint-Avertin. Runners will have the chance to explore the charming local streets and scenic countryside as they challenge themselves on this historic course. With a focus on community and camaraderie, this event offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
