Course

A six-hour challenge run on 5km laps from the Green Table Cafe at Dartington Hall, Totnes, running 9am to 3pm. The lap is mixed terrain over gravel, grass and a little tarmac, often muddy, with one hill and otherwise reasonably flat. Water and feed stations and a covered rest area are at the start/finish.

About

A six-hour lapped trail challenge on the Dartington Hall estate near Totnes. Runners complete as many 5km laps as they can between 9am and 3pm, on gravel, grass and short tarmac sections with one hill per lap. There is a canicross category and a covered rest area with a wood-burning stove.
