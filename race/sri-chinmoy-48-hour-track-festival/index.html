Course

Fixed-time track races held on the certified 400m floodlit MONDO surface at the AIS Athletics Track in Bruce, Canberra. Runners circle the same loop for their chosen duration, with electronic lap counting and live online results. Track certification has lapsed pending resurfacing, so records cannot be claimed.

About

The Sri Chinmoy 48 Hour Track Festival offers a variety of track races from the Marathon to the 48 Hour Ultra-Marathon at the world-class AIS Athletics Track in Canberra. The event features a rich history and provides a challenge for runners of all levels.
