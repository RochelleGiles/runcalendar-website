Course

Starts and finishes at Fresh Fields Village on Seabrook Island, setting off from Village Green Lane at 8:00am and looping the flat island roads. Parking is off site at Mullet Hall on Johns Island with shuttle buses to the start, about eight minutes away.

About

The Bohicket 5K and Half Marathon wind through Seabrook Island, a private barrier island resort near Charleston, South Carolina known for its maritime forest, marsh and golf courses. The race is held each November on the island's quiet resort roads.
