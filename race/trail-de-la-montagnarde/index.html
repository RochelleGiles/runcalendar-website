About

Now past its 30th running, the Trail de la Montagnarde has echoed with the steps of runners, the laughter of children, and challenges met under the sky of Ablain-Saint-Nazaire for three decades. It offers a 1km and 2km Kids race, a 9km walk or run, a 16km challenge and a 30km course.
