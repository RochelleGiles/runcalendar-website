About

The 5K Foam Fest is a globally recognized fun run series that brings together the excitement of a 5-kilometer course with the thrill of over 25 unique obstacles. Participants can expect to tackle the world's largest inflatable waterslide, multiple slip-and-slides, mud pits, and navigate through an astonishing 2.5 million cubic feet of foam. The event is designed to be accessible and enjoyable for people of all fitness levels, emphasizing fun and camaraderie over competition.
