About

The 8th Annual Earth Day Trail Run is a family-friendly trail running and walking event held at Pennypack Ecological Restoration Trust to celebrate Earth Day and raise funds for land preservation. It features scenic courses on grass and gravel with rolling hills, starting and ending at the headquarters, with options for all levels including a 1-mile family trail.
