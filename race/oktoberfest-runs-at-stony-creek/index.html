Course

All distances start and finish at the Baypoint Beach tent inside Stony Creek Metropark and loop around the park lake. The route follows the park asphalt hike/bike path, which stays open to the public and runs two-way, with several parking-lot driveway crossings. Aid stations sit roughly every mile with water, Gatorade Endurance, gel and Pepsi.

About

Oktoberfest Runs @ Stony Creek is a road race at Stony Creek Metropark in Shelby Township, Michigan, held each October alongside an Oktoberfest celebration. It offers 30K, half marathon, 10K and 5K distances for a range of runners.
