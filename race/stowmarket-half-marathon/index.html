Course

A rural course through the Suffolk countryside, run on a mixture of B-class roads and quiet single-track country lanes. The half marathon starts at 10:00 am with the 10K setting off shortly afterwards. Water stops are provided at regular intervals along the route.

About

Stowmarket Half Marathon & 10K runs through the Suffolk countryside surrounding the market town of Stowmarket, organised by local club Stowmarket Striders. The route mixes quiet lanes and villages typical of mid-Suffolk on its way back into town.
