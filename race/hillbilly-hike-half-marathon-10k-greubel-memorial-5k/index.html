Course

Point-to-point road races finishing in Carlisle. The half marathon starts in Indianola with a gentle downhill before levelling out, running through town for about a mile and a half then out into oak woodland; the 10k starts at Summerset State Park and the 5k near the Summerset Trailhead. All three set off at 8:30am with buses to the start lines.
