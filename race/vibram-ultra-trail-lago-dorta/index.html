About

The Vibram Ultra Trail Lago Dorta (UTLO) is a premier trail running event that takes runners on a scenic journey through the stunning landscapes surrounding Lake Orta in Italy. The course features challenging terrain, breathtaking views, and a welcoming atmosphere that attracts runners from around the world. This event is known for its Vibram-developed trail running technologies and the opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region.
