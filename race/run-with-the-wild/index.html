Course

A 5K and 10K on the paved Historic Columbia River Highway State Trail, starting from the Mark O. Hatfield West Trailhead near Hood River with views over the Columbia River Gorge. The sealed surface is wheelchair and stroller accessible, and walkers and dogs are welcome.

About

Run With The Wild is a community-oriented 5K and 10K run/walk suitable for all ages, families, and dogs along the ADA-accessible Historic Columbia River Highway State Trail in the Columbia River Gorge. 100% of proceeds support the Wildland Firefighter Foundation with fun activities like auctions, raffles, and Smokey Bear meet-and-greet.
