Course

An overnight team relay on the mixed trails around Zion Ponderosa Ranch outside Orderville, Utah: red dirt singletrack, doubletrack, equestrian and jeep trails. Teams of eight rotate through three loops covering 124.8 miles in total, with 15,016 feet of climbing. Ultra teams of four cover the same course.

About

Ragnar Trail Zion is a two-day, one-night trail relay race where teams camp at Zion Ponderosa Ranch and run scenic loops over 120 miles total. Runners complete three loops each—Green (4 miles), Yellow (4 miles), and Red (7 miles)—totaling about 15 miles per participant with stunning views and village amenities.
