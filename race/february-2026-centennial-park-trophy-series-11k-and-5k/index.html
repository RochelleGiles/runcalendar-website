Course

A paved course along the Platte River, starting and finishing at Centennial Park on South Decatur Street. The start is handicapped: slower 11K runners go at 9am, faster 11K runners about 30 minutes later, and the 5K about 15 minutes after the first wave.

About

February will bring us to the South Platte River trail at Centennial Park for our 11k and 5k race. Depending on the year, it can be either 50 degrees and sunny or cold and snowy!! Always a fun time either way!!
