Course

An old-school mud-and-dirt trail run on the singletrack of Lincoln Parish Park, a well-regarded trail network outside Ruston. The 10 mile race is one full loop, the 5 mile a half loop and the 20 mile two laps, all starting and finishing at the park. Aid stations sit at the start/finish and near miles 4 and 6.

About

This is an "Old School" style mud, dirt, blood and tears trail run at Lincoln Parish Park in Ruston, Louisiana. LPP is one of the best kept trail secrets in Louisiana. This race is 3 distances. We will have a 5 mile, a 10 mile, and a 20 mile race.
