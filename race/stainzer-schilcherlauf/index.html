About

The Schilcherlauf is a running event taking place in the Weststeiermark region of Austria. The event emphasizes both competitive racing and fun, with their motto 'Bei uns is imma a Hetz' (We always have fun). The race features multiple competitions and includes side activities and refreshment stations along the course, creating a unique atmosphere for both competitive athletes and recreational runners.
