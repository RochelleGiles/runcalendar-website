About

Explore the picturesque roads of Bernes-sur-Oise at the annual Tour de Bernes, a beloved running event that winds through the charming French countryside. This challenging yet scenic course offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region while pushing their limits. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a casual runner, the Tour de Bernes promises an unforgettable running adventure.
