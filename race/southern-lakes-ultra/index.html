Course

Six-stage multi-day ultra of roughly 250 km run over seven days through the ranges between Wanaka and Queenstown, finishing in Queenstown. Individual stages run from about 30 km to 50 km with one 80 km stage. Terrain is a mix of DOC single trail, hard-pack trail, mountain bike trail and 4WD road, with multiple river crossings and remote backcountry. Cut-offs apply at each stage.

About

Explore the breathtaking Southern Lakes region of New Zealand in this 7-day, 6-stage multi-day ultra. Runners will traverse stunning landscapes, from rugged mountains to serene lakes, in this challenging yet rewarding event hosted in the heart of Central Otago. The Southern Lakes Ultra offers an unforgettable adventure for endurance athletes seeking an exceptional running experience.
