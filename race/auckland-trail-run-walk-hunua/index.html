Course

Starts at Upper Mangatawhiri Campsite, Waterline Road, Hunua. Courses combine single-track trail and gravel road and stay largely within the valley, with stream crossings and undulating sections. Aid stations: one on the Short, three on the Mid and Long, and four on the Super Long.

About

The Auckland Trail Run & Walk takes place in the stunning Hunua Ranges, the largest native forest area in Auckland. Participants will experience a variety of trail conditions including single track, bush trails, gravel paths, and stream crossings. The event offers multiple course distances with challenging terrain that will test runners of all skill levels.
