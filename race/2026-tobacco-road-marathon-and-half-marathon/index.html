Course

The marathon and half marathon start together at 7:00am from the race site at USA Baseball and Thomas Brooks Park in Cary and run mostly on the unpaved American Tobacco Trail, with some road sections. The trail carries two-way running traffic and parts of the road sections have no usable shoulder.

About

The Veradigm Tobacco Road Marathon and Half Marathon feature over 20 miles of the American Tobacco Trail, with a flat and fast course ideal for personal records and Boston Marathon qualifiers. Organized as a non-profit event in Cary, NC, it supports partner charities through runner participation.[1][5]
