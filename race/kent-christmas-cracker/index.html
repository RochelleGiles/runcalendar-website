Course

Two full clockwise laps of Betteshanger Country Park near Deal, entirely traffic-free and run on flat tarmac paths. Start and finish are together inside the park. A 2-mile fun run and a 1km junior race use the same venue.

About

The Kent Christmas Cracker is a festive road race in Deal on the Kent coast, home to Deal Castle, one of the coastal artillery forts built for Henry VIII.
