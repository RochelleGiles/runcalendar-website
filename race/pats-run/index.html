About

Pat's Run is an annual 4.2-mile race through Tempe, Arizona, in honor of former NFL player and U.S. Army Ranger Pat Tillman. The distance reflects his jersey number 42, and the race finishes on the field at Sun Devil Stadium, benefiting the Pat Tillman Foundation.
