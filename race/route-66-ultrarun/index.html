Course

Point-to-point along the longest unbroken stretch of Historic Route 66: the 140-mile runs Seligman to Topock66, the 100-mile starts at Peach Springs. All paved highway, descending from 5,258 ft at the start to 477 ft at the Colorado River, over Sitgreaves Pass at mile 111. Time stations at Peach Springs, Kingman and Oatman.

About

A 140-mile ultramarathon (with a new 100-mile CenturyRun option in 2026) following the longest remaining unbroken stretch of Historic Route 66 from Seligman to Topock66, Arizona. The race celebrates the history of Route 66 and includes solo, relay team, and journey runner divisions with a 60-hour cutoff for the 140-mile race.
