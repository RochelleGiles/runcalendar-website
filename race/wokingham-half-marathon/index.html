Course

Fast, flat half marathon on fully closed rural roads through Wokingham town centre, starting and finishing at Cantley Park. UK Athletics certified with bib-tag timing and pace runners. Water stations on course; race village has catering, massage, changing and showers. A Mini Mile runs alongside. Cut-off 3 hours 15 minutes.

About

The Wokingham Half Marathon is a fast, flat 13.1-mile race set in the charming town of Wokingham, UK. Runners can enjoy closed roads and stunning countryside views as they challenge themselves on this scenic course, which is perfect for setting new personal bests.
