Course

Free 5K starting and finishing at East Jersey Old Town Village on River Road, inside Johnson Park, Piscataway. Run as two loops of an out-and-back along Johnson Drive, which is closed to traffic. Surface is paved park roads and paths, and the race is chip timed. A 1 mile fun run/walk is also offered.

About

The Revolutionary Celebration Run is a free 5K and one-mile fun run and walk at East Jersey Old Town Village in Piscataway, New Jersey, kicking off local Independence Day celebrations.
