About

The MY Fun Run Mount Eliza Festival offers a scenic running experience through the picturesque Mount Eliza Regional Park on the Mornington Peninsula. Runners of all levels can enjoy the beautiful natural surroundings as they take on this community event, making it a must-do for those looking for a unique and engaging running adventure.
