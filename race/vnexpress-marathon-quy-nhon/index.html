About

The VnExpress Marathon in Quy Nhon offers runners a picturesque seaside course along the stunning central Vietnamese coastline. Featuring a route that showcases the region's natural beauty, this event is a must for those seeking a memorable running experience. Runners can expect a challenging yet rewarding challenge as they take in the breathtaking ocean views throughout the race.
