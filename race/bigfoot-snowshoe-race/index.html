Course

A hilly off-trail snowshoe race through wooded terrain near Traverse City, with logs to jump and branches to duck. Both 5K and 10K options are offered on the same course. If snow does not materialise, the course is run in trail shoes, and snowshoe rental is available on the day.

About

Take a snowy jaunt through the woods with plenty of logs to jump and branches to duck! Gather pre-race in the cozy Timber Ridge Nordic Center and stick around after the race to warm up around a roaring fire with a bowl of chili.
