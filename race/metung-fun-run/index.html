About

The Metung Fun Run is a community event in Metung, Victoria, offering a 10km run, a 5km run, a 5km walk and a 1km kids colour dash. It raises funds for Metung Primary School and is supported by the Lions Club of Metung. Entry is online, with prices rising closer to race day.
