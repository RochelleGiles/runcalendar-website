About

Ultra Trail Whistler by UTMB offers a trail running experience in the Whistler Blackcomb resort. Runners explore terrain from evergreen forests to alpine vistas, taking in views of Black Tusk and mountain lakes across multiple race distances that showcase the rugged landscape of Whistler.
