Course

A two-loop road course through central Montpellier, passing the Antigone district, Verchant, Grammont and the Hotel de Ville before finishing on Avenue Foch at the Arc de Triomphe. Total elevation gain is about 225 m. Aid stations sit at kilometres 5, 10, 15, 20, 22, 26, 31, 36 and 41, with an 8 a.m. start and a six-hour limit.

About

The Montpellier Marathon is a must-do event for runners seeking an unforgettable experience in the heart of southern France. Weaving through the historic streets and scenic landscapes of Montpellier, this marathon offers a captivating challenge for athletes of all levels. Runners can look forward to taking in the city's vibrant culture and architecture as they push towards the finish line.
