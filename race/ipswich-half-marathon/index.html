Course

Starts and finishes at Portman Road, home of Ipswich Town FC, running down Duke Street and through the Ipswich Waterfront before heading along Cliff Lane and around the River Orwell. The route returns via Wherstead Road and Grafton Way to finish back at the stadium, on roads and paved waterfront throughout.

About

The Ipswich Half Marathon offers runners a unique opportunity to race through the historic streets of Ipswich, United Kingdom. Starting and finishing at the iconic Portman Road stadium, this event provides a scenic and challenging course that showcases the city's beautiful architecture and vibrant atmosphere. Whether you're an experienced half-marathoner or a beginner looking for a new challenge, the Ipswich Half Marathon is sure to deliver an unforgettable running experience.
