Course

USATF-certified 5,000m on the flat, paved park path loop through Hudson Mills Metropark at Dexter. Mass start with chip timing, water on course, and no pacers, bikes or outside assistance. The run can be entered on its own or as the running half of the hybrid strength-and-speed competition.

About

Competitors will complete two scored events: 1. Deficit Deadlift (with DefiPlate™ + stiff bar) and 2. 5K Run (USATF-measured course at Hudson Mills Metropark). Final placement is determined by combined scoring.
