Course

The 5K and 10K start and finish at Wiard's Orchard, Ypsilanti. Roughly one mile runs on gravel roads inside the orchard, with the remainder on asphalt roads and a cross-country course in Rolling Hills County Park. A mix of paved and dirt paths leads to the finish. One aid station on the 5K course, two on the 10K.

About

Run Scream Run is a Halloween-themed trail race in Ypsilanti, Michigan, held every October 31. It offers 10K, 5K and 1 Mile distances, with costumes encouraged as runners mark the holiday on the trails.
