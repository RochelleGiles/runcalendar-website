Course

Half marathon and 5K based in Cheshire, Connecticut. The half runs through Cheshire and Hamden with considerable stretches on the historic Farmington Canal Trail, while the 5K is a flat neighbourhood loop with few turns. The field is capped at 2,000 and wheelchairs are not permitted on the half marathon course. Kids' fun runs of a quarter and half mile run alongside.
