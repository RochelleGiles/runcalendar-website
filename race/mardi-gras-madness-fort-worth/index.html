About

Mardi Gras Madness is a Mardi Gras-themed running event held at Cravens Park in Arlington, Texas, in the Dallas-Fort Worth metro. It offers a kids dash, one mile, 5K and 10K over mixed park and greenbelt surfaces, with beads handed out at the aid stations.
