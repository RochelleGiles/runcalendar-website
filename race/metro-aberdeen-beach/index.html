About

Explore the scenic Aberdeen coastline at the Metro Aberdeen Beach 10K! This popular annual race takes runners on a scenic tour of the city's picturesque beachfront, offering stunning views of the North Sea. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, the Metro Aberdeen Beach 10K is the perfect event to challenge yourself and soak in the beauty of this vibrant Scottish city.
