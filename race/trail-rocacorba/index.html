About

Trail Rocacorba climbs from Canet d'Adri, near Girona, to the Rocacorba sanctuary, a summit famous among professional cyclists as a training climb. Runners choose from 19.6km, 30.2km, or a full 38.2km route through the hills of Catalonia's Girona province.
