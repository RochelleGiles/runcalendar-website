About

Rumble In The Jumble is a six-hour timed lap race staged by Circuit Running Events at Northampton Active in Northamptonshire. Runners and walkers cover as many laps as they can of a flat out-and-back route along a disused railway line, with 5K, 10K and half marathon milestones recorded along the way.
