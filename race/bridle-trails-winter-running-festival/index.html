Course

Every distance runs the same 5.2-mile loop of trail around the perimeter of Bridle Trails State Park in Kirkland, mostly clockwise. Winter conditions leave sections rough, rutted or muddy, and the route is marked with streamers and glow sticks. The course closes at 4:30pm.

About

The Bridle Trails Winter Running Festival takes place in Bridle Trails State Park, a forested equestrian park in Kirkland, Washington. Distances range from a 5 mile up to a 50K, run solo or as a relay.
