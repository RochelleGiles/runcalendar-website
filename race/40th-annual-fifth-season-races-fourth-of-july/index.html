About

The 40th Annual Fifth Season Races: Fourth of July event features a 8K and 5K race, as well as kids races, in Cedar Rapids, Iowa. The races start and finish in Greene Square Park, with the 8K route running an out-and-back along 3rd Ave SE, 19th St SE, and Grande Avenue SE, and the 5K following a similar but shorter course.
