About

The Trail Des Pierres Sacrees is a running event that takes place in France. The event features multiple race distances, including a 12km, 18km, and 30km trail run, as well as children's races and an eco-friendly hike. The event is known for its beautiful natural scenery and the opportunity to explore the region's sacred stones.
