Course

Point-to-point trail courses along the Cape to Cape coastline in Western Australia. The 80km runs from Hamelin Bay to Howard Park Winery and the 42km from Yallingup to the same finish, both solo or as relay legs. Runners cross towering Karri forest, cliff-top coastal trail, granite domes and long stretches of soft white sand, with technical footing and rock scrambles throughout.

About

The Margaret River Ultra Marathon is a premier trail running event in Western Australia that showcases the stunning scenery of the Margaret River region. The course features a mix of challenging terrain, including rugged coastlines, lush forests, and rolling vineyards, making it a must-do event for trail running enthusiasts.
