About

Join US FOR THE FIRST ANNUAL WORLD SNOW "FROSTY FOX" 5K. Run a scenic course along the St. Croix River, enjoy internationally renowned snow sculptures, and finish with a breathtaking view of Stillwater's iconic Lift Bridge. Festivities include a separate kids' races and a fun mascot race!
