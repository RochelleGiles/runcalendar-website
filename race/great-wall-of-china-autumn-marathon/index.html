Course

Runs on and beside the Wall itself: sections of steep, uneven stone steps on the Wall, joined by country roads and village lanes between. The Wall's climbs come early and are met again late in the race, so it is slow, hard and unlike any other marathon.

About

Conquer the legendary Great Wall of China at the annual Autumn Marathon! This breathtaking race takes runners on a challenging journey through the iconic ancient fortifications, offering stunning vistas and a one-of-a-kind experience. Runners will be inspired by the historic setting as they push their limits on this bucket-list adventure.
