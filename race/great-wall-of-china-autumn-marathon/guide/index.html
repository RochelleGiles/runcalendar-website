Great Wall of China Autumn Marathon

Beijing · China
RoadMountainsMarathon · Half Marathon · 10 km · 5 km

Not the famous one: a wild, unrestored wall you reach by ladder, in a field of under two hundred.

Quick verdict

Forget the postcard. Three-quarters of this marathon is tarmac, the wall section is reached by climbing a ladder, and fewer than two hundred people start. Come for a stretch of unrestored wall almost nobody has run — not for the race the name suggests.

Course at a glance

500mClimbing
10 hrsCutoff
~180Runners

A loop on mountain road north-west of Beijing in Yanqing, with two separate wall segments for the full marathon and one for everything shorter. Roughly 32 km is paved, 4 km is single track through forest, and 6 km is on the wall itself. About 500 metres of climbing all day.

Where you'll feel it

📍The approach track and the ladder, before the wall.

Not the wall — the animal track that gets you there. A winding single-file path wide enough for one person, ending at a ladder you climb to reach the ramparts. Then long, very steep staircases on stone nobody has repaired. You queue, you scramble, you do not run.

Best chance to push

Everywhere else. Three-quarters of this course is ordinary tarmac at ordinary rhythm; if you are fit, the road kilometres are where the time comes back.

Three things to know

  1. Wear trail shoes, not road shoes. The organiser's own advice is explicit — road shoes slip on the trail sections. Pick something road-capable with grip, not a burly mountain shoe, because 76% of the day is tarmac.
  2. Budget six and a half to seven and a half hours. The 2026 field averaged 7:23, the last ranked finisher took 10:00:14, and eight men were timed out. The 10-hour cutoff applies to every distance, even the 5 km.
  3. There is no field cap and it has never exceeded 185 starters, so entry is not a race. It closes 8 October with payment due the 9th. Book flights first, enter second.

Race-day weather

2–8°COn the wall
8:00amStart
90 minCoach to start

The venue is mountainous and colder than Beijing city suggests: runners record about 2°C at eight in the morning, with hard wind once you are exposed on the ramparts. Late October is dry and clear, which is the compensation.

What runners wear

Trail shoes with a road-friendly sole, and a windproof layer for the wall. Throwaway warmth for the assembly: you leave the hotel at 05:30 for an 08:00 gun and spend that wait standing in near-freezing air.

Typical finish times

Winners run around 4:30, which is what this terrain does to a marathon. The 2026 field averaged 7:23, the last ranked finisher came home in 10:00:14, and the 10-hour cutoff is enforced. Finish rate is still about 95%.

Worth the trip?

If you want wall almost nobody runs, yes. If you are picturing 42 km of ramparts, no — that is the May race at Huangyaguan, and you should book that instead. This one is small, cold, wild and three-quarters tarmac.
