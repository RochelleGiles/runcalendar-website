Course

A loop through Central Park starting on Central Park West just north of West 90th Street and finishing near Bethesda Terrace by the 72nd Street transverse. Runners enter the park at West 72nd Street and follow West and East Drive via the 102nd Street transverse. Four fluid stations sit between miles 1 and 6, with misting stations alongside them.

About

Run through Central Park every June, the Mastercard New York Mini 10K began in the early 1970s as the world's first women-only road race and remains a landmark event for women's running.
