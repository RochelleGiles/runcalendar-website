About

A scenic, challenging road race set in the heart of West Cork, starting from Clonakilty Rugby Club. The evening race features a rolling course through Shannonvale and Lyre, with a fast finish back towards the West Cork Technology Park. Chip-timed and suitable for both competitive runners and those looking to enjoy a well-organised local race.
