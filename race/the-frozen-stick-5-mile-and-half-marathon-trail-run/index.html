Course

Both distances start and finish at LaSalle School in Glencoe, returning to an indoor race village. The 8 miler is largely flat on asphalt paths through Glencoe and the Al Foster Trail with one singletrack loop up Bluffview; the half marathon adds climbs on Bluff View and Rock Hollow. Both pass the aid stations twice.

About

Welcome to The Frozen Stick Trail Run! The Frozen Stick is a stand alone event and also the FINALIE to the Stick Season Trail Run Series. Using the same route of discontinued Frozen Feet, Rockinator Racing keeps the 15 year tradition alive! With 2 distance options, the Frozen Stick starts at LaSalle Middle School, heads up and through beautiful Bluffview, Rock Hollow and Zombie Trails.
