Course

Loop course starting and finishing at 196 Main Street on the Lynchburg town square. Rolling Moore County terrain including the Whiskey Hill climb from around mile 4, roughly a mile of gradual ascent then a short very steep pitch, a gradual descent from mile 9 to 12 and a flat final mile. Seven water stops between miles 2.7 and 11.9. Field capped at 1650.

About

A half marathon held in Lynchburg, Tennessee that winds through rural Moore County and passes the Jack Daniel's Distillery. The course is known for a memorable hill between 3.5 and 4 miles into the run.
