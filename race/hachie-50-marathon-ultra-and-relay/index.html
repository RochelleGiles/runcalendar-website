Course

A 10-mile out-and-back loop repeated five times for the 50-mile and the relay; marathoners cover a slightly shortened version three times. Start and finish at Getzendaner Memorial Park on South Grand Avenue, Waxahachie. The organiser states the surface is 90 percent concrete sidewalk and 10 percent dirt and gravel. Flat and tree-lined throughout.

About

The Waxahachie Running Club and the Midlothian Running Club join forces with the Waxahachie Lions Club to sponsor the Hachie 50 in Waxahachie, Texas, benefiting the Waxahachie Lions Club's support of local children and schools in Ellis County. The race is held in Getzendaner city park, mostly closed to traffic, on a fast, flat, tree-lined out-and-back course.
