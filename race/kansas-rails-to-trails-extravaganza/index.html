Course

An out and back on the Prairie Spirit Trail, a rails-to-trails route through a string of small towns, starting and finishing at Celebration Hall, 220 W 17th Street, Ottawa. Mostly crushed limestone with asphalt paths through each town. Turnarounds differ by distance, the 100 mile reaching Iola and the 50 mile Garnett. Aid roughly every five miles.

About

An ultra in Ottawa, Kansas, run along the Prairie Spirit Trail, a rail-trail built on a former railroad corridor through the Kansas countryside. Distances range from the marathon up to 100 miles on the trail's flat, crushed-limestone surface.
