Course

A fast, flat single-loop urban half marathon starting and finishing on Pleasant Street outside City Hall in downtown New Bedford. The route runs through the city's North and South Ends with views of the waterfront, Clarks Cove and the harbour. USATF-certified with a three-hour limit and six aid stations carrying water, Gatorade and toilets.

About

This point-to-point half marathon starts and finishes on Purchase Street in New Bedford, passing harbor waterfront views and the Whaling National Historic Park before turning inland. Organized by the Friendly Sons of Saint Patrick since 2009, proceeds fund community scholarships.
