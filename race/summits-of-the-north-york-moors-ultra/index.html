Course

A circular 120-mile ultra starting and finishing at Goathland Village Hall, taking in the ten highest hills of the North York Moors for around 16,000ft of ascent. The route crosses hills and moorland on ancient tracks and trods, following stretches of the Cleveland Way, Esk Valley Walk and Lyke Wake Walk past burial sites, medieval boundary stones and Bronze Age earthworks. The field is capped at 70 and the time limit is 35 hours.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the North York Moors on the Summits Of The North York Moors Ultra, a challenging 120-mile trail run starting and finishing in the charming village of Goathland near Whitby. Challenge yourself to conquer the rolling hills and scenic vistas of this stunning national park, as you push your limits on this unforgettable ultramarathon adventure.
