About

Trail de la Lucerne d'Outremer passes La Lucerne Abbey, a well-preserved 12th-century Norman abbey, through the countryside of the Manche near the Bay of Mont-Saint-Michel. Runners choose the 15K or 10K route through farmland and woodland.
