Course

A point-to-point downhill 5K from Fish Hatchery Road to Performance Park on the River Walk, with a free shuttle from the Estes Park Visitor Center to the start. The first mile rolls gently on a quiet road; miles two and three run on the detached Fall River Trail, with about 100 yards of single-track dirt. The route passes the historic Elkhorn Lodge amid autumn aspen.

About

With the Fall Season, Estes Park shines in colors and wilderness experiences! Herds of Elk begin to roam the meadows and open fields; while sounds of bugling Bull Elk call out their territory. Join us as we acknowledge this unique season at the RUT RUN 5K as part of 'ELK FEST'. Kick starting the weekend of events, the 5K (3.1 miles) race offers a short and scenic tour of fall colors, river views, and possible Elk encounters (from a safe distance).
