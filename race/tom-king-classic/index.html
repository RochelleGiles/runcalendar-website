About

The Tom King Classic is an annual running event featuring a Half Marathon and 5K Run/Walk starting and finishing at Cornelia Fort Airpark. The races follow certified, fast, and flat out-and-back courses through Shelby Park, downtown Cumberland River's east bank, Davidson Street, and Shelby Bottoms Greenways.
