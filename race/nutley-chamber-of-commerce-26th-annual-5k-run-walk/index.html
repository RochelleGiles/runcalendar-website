Course

Starts and finishes on the Park Oval field at Nutley High School, looping out through the town streets via Chestnut, Whitford, Satterthwaite, Washington, Centre and Franklin. The organiser calls it slightly challenging, with one water station at the halfway point.

About

An annual 5K run/walk event hosted by the Nutley Chamber of Commerce that includes a kids fun run and short sprint. Approximately 350 participants from Nutley and surrounding towns compete on a town-wide race route starting and finishing at Nutley High School Park Oval.
