About

Explore the stunning vineyards and rolling hills of Sicily's countryside at the Trail delle Cantine, a captivating trail running event that showcases the region's natural beauty. This challenging course winds through scenic landscapes, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the breathtaking vistas and rich winemaking heritage of this picturesque corner of Italy.
