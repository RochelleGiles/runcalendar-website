About

Explore the historic streets and vibrant urban landscapes of Lens, France at the Lens Lievin Urban Trail. This unique running event takes participants on a captivating journey through the city's rich cultural heritage, showcasing its architectural gems and lively neighborhoods. With a challenging yet rewarding course, the Lens Lievin Urban Trail offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
