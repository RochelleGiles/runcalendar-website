Course

Starts on Hound House Road in the Hurtwood Forest near Guildford, following the Greensand Ridge and its escarpments over sunken sandy and rocky footpaths, mud and brooks. Trail shoes are required, and there are road crossings plus a short back-road section. The 21K has water stations at 5K, 13.5K and 18.5K, the 15K at 5K and 10K.

About

The Hurt Half Marathon is a challenging trail running event that takes place in the beautiful Hurtwood forest near Guildford, UK. Runners can expect to encounter a mix of terrain, including rocks, mud, and babbling brooks, as well as some lung-busting hills.
