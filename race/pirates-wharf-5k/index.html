Course

A 5K on the crushed-stone paths of Pirate's Wharf Park on Whitehaven Road, Quantico. Walking is permitted within a 1 hour 15 minute limit; dogs and strollers are not allowed. Food, drinks and a DJ follow the finish.

About

Pirate's Wharf 5K runs through Pirate's Wharf Park, Wicomico County's newest park, along the waterfront near Quantico, Maryland. The Eastern Shore Running Club hosts the race each October, drawing runners from across the Eastern Shore.
