About

The SS&A City2City Run Walk takes runners on a scenic journey through the twin cities of Albury and Wodonga, showcasing the natural beauty of the region. This popular annual event challenges participants with a scenic course that winds through the heart of these connected communities, offering a unique opportunity to explore the area on foot.
