About

The Chablis Marathon is a captivating running event set against the picturesque backdrop of the Chablis wine region. Runners will have the opportunity to experience the stunning vineyards and charming villages of this renowned French destination as they challenge themselves on the marathon course. With a focus on the region's rich history and culture, the Chablis Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
