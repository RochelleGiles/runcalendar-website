Course

Starts and finishes in Grant Park, setting off near the Art Institute of Chicago and Cloud Gate and finishing by Buckingham Fountain. The road course runs past Millennium Park, the downtown riverfront, Soldier Field and the Field Museum. Distances share the same start area.
