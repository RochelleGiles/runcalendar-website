Course

A marked trail race starting from the village of Talybont-on-Usk in the Brecon Beacons, with panoramic views over the hills. The routes are marked with trail flags, tags and arrows, with marshals at major junctions and serviced aid stations. GPX files are supplied for those navigating themselves.

About

The Brecon Beacons Trail Runs take runners on a scenic journey through the rolling hills of the Brecon Beacons in the United Kingdom. Participants will follow winding trails and paths, immersed in the stunning natural beauty of this picturesque region. Whether a seasoned trail runner or newcomer to the sport, this event offers an unforgettable experience for all.
