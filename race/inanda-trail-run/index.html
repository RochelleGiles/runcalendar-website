Course

Starts and finishes at eNanda Adventure Park on the shore of Inanda Dam. Courses of 5 km, 12 km and 18 km mix purpose-built lakeside singletrack with dirt roads and cattle tracks, climbing roughly 219 m, 452 m and 576 m respectively. One to three aid stations depending on distance; walkers are welcome on the 5 km and 12 km.
