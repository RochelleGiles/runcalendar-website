About

The Sticker 5 Mile Race is a picturesque run through the rolling hills and country lanes of Sticker, Saint Austell. Runners will be treated to stunning views as they navigate the undulating course, making this a must-do event for those seeking a scenic and challenging race experience in the United Kingdom.
