About

The Tails & TaTas Breast Cancer Awareness 5k Run/Walk, in partnership with Swope Family of Dealerships, will be held in a hybrid format allowing both virtual and in-person participation. The start/finish line for the dog-friendly run will begin at the Swope Toyota Dealership and wind through Freeman Lake Park. We have also added a 1-mile fun run for the 12 and under group.
