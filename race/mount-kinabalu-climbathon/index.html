About

Conquer the iconic Mount Kinabalu at the annual Climbathon, a thrilling running challenge that takes participants through the stunning landscapes of Malaysian Borneo. Runners will push their limits as they ascend the towering peak, with breathtaking views and a sense of accomplishment awaiting those who reach the summit. This prestigious event attracts athletes from around the world, making it a must-do for any adventurous runner.
