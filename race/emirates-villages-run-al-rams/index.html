About

The sixth of seven Emirates Villages Run races, each in a different village: a 10K, a 5K and a 1.5 km family run from Al Rams Children's Park north of Ras Al Khaimah, across the Al Rams bridge onto Saraya Island and back. Mostly paved, with the odd patch of gravel or wind-blown sand. The 10K starts at 4 in the afternoon.
