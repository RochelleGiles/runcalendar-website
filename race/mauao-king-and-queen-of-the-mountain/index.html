About

Conquer the iconic Mauao (Mount Maunganui) in the 78th annual King and Queen of the Mountain race, hosted by Athletics Tauranga. This challenging vertical ascent tests the strength and endurance of runners as they scale the majestic coastal peak, rewarded with stunning views over the Bay of Plenty region of New Zealand.
