About

This is a paved loop trail located outside the Milner Recreation Complex. You will be running around the Dottie West Park, which consists of baseball fields, outside fitness center, skate park and a pool. There will be outdoor restrooms that you will run by every loop. The loop is .78 mile and 43 feet of gain each loop.
