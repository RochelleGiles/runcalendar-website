Course

All distances start and finish at Hill's Resort on the west side of Priest Lake, winding through cedar groves and along the lakeshore. The organisers call it gravel running: roughly 80% smooth dirt forest road, under 10% rocky jeep-style forest road and under 10% paved road open to traffic. Aid stations are well stocked.

About

Fall colors, fresh mountain air, and plenty of space to run wild! Priest Lake gravel running courses start from Hill's Resort on the west side of Priest Lake, winding through lush cedar groves and along the lakeshore of Priest Lake. Break free of the confines of pure road running and embrace the varied terrain and flexible lines of Priest Lake's gravel forest roads.
