Course

A road race starting at the Cape Town City Hall and finishing on the Grand Parade, running out through District Six, the Bo-Kaap and past the Castle of Good Hope. Four options share the route: a 21.1 km half marathon, a 10 km run, a 10 km big walk and a 5 km fun run.

About

The Slave Route Challenge follows landmarks from Cape Town's slave history, including District Six, on a route of 5km, 10km and a half marathon through the city. The race honours the memory of those forced along this route centuries ago.
