Course

A multi-loop out-and-back on a fast asphalt greenway at Horse Fork Creek Park in Owensboro, with minimal elevation change. The roughly 1.5-mile loop runs along a creek, past two lakes and under trees; the 5K is one loop, the 10K two, the half five, the marathon nine and the 50K eleven. Aid every mile with water, sports drink and gels.

About

Welcome to the 3nd Annual RYR Endurance Race Weekend. The weekend will host a series of races including a competitive marathon for those striving to qualify for races such as Boston, a casual marathon with a generous time limit (9 hours), an ultramarathon (50K), a half marathon, a 10K, and a 5K. We have a race for everyone. We are excited to provide the first certified marathon and half marathon courses in the area.
