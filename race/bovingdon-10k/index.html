About

The Bovingdon 10K is a premier road running event hosted by Bovingdon Football Club and Gade Valley Harriers in the United Kingdom. This scenic course winds through the picturesque countryside, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply enjoying the beautiful scenery, the Bovingdon 10K is not to be missed.
