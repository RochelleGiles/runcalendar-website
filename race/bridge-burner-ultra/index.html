Course

A single loop from Centennial Park in Canon City, following the Arkansas River through the Tunnel Drive tunnels, then climbing into Royal Gorge Mountain Park on singletrack to about 7,109 ft, crossing the bridge and descending through Temple Canyon. Surface is a mix of singletrack and dirt road. Aid stations carry Tailwind, gels and warm food.

About

The Bridge Burner Ultra is a trail running event featuring a scenic single-loop course starting in Canon City, Colorado, along the Arkansas River, through historic Tunnel Drive tunnels, and up to 7,109 feet in Royal Gorge Mountain Park. It offers distances of 35 Mile/50K, 35K, and 10K with approximately 4,400 feet of elevation gain for the longest distance.
