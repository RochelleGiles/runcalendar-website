Course

A 20-mile road race on the lanes around Ashby-de-la-Zouch, with race headquarters at the Ashby Leisure Centre & Lido on North Street. Organised by runners for runners, with cut-offs at 10 miles (2h20) and 15 miles (3h30) and around 4h45 to finish.

About

A 20-mile road race through the rural lanes around Ashby-de-la-Zouch in Leicestershire. Long popular as a tune-up for spring marathons, it draws runners from clubs across the Midlands each March.
