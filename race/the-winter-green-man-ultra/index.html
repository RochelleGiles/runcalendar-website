Course

An anti-clockwise circuit of the Community Forest Path through the woodland ringing Bristol, often muddy in winter. The 45-mile route starts and finishes at Ashton Park School; the 30-mile starts at The Lock Keepers, Keynsham and finishes at Ashton Park. Four stocked checkpoints with hot food; 3,100ft of climb over 45 miles and 1,900ft over 30. Self-navigated from GPX and written directions.

About

The Winter Green Man Ultra offers runners a scenic 30 or 45 mile challenge through the beautiful city of Bristol, UK. Held twice a year, this event showcases the region's stunning natural landscapes and historic architecture, providing an engaging and picturesque ultrarunning experience for participants.
