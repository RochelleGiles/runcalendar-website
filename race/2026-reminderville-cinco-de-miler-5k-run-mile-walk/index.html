About

The Reminderville Athletic Club and the City of Reminderville host the Reminderville Cinco De Miler 5K Race and Mile Walk, a full-blown Cinco de Mayo celebration in Twinsburg, Ohio. Participants can expect upbeat energy and a festive atmosphere, whether running, walking or cheering from the sidelines.
