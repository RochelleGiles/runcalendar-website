About

Between the plains of Beauce and the hills of Perche, the Loir is a natural border. By following it, one understands that Marcel Proust could have admired these places conducive to literature. The 17 km Trail Contre le Temps Perdu or TCTP will be, for our regulars, a race quite different from the previous editions and always full of surprises!
