About

Certified as a USATF 10K, this cross-country race benefits local food pantries and soup kitchens as part of the Long Island Track and Field series. It starts at Park Avenue School and finishes near Lily Flanagan's pub in Babylon Village, over a mostly flat, wooded course with only brief pavement.
