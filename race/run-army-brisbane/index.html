Course

A road course starting on Grey Street at South Bank. The 10km crosses the Story Bridge and William Jolly Bridge; the 5km uses the Goodwill and Victoria bridges. Both pass city landmarks including ANZAC Square and Queensland Police Headquarters before returning to South Bank.

About

Run Army Brisbane is a unique military-themed running event featuring Australian Army elements including the Royal Australian Artillery with M777A2 howitzers on course and Bushmaster vehicle support. The event takes place at South Bank with entertainment from the Australian Army Band, offering participants a distinctive running experience alongside military displays and equipment.
