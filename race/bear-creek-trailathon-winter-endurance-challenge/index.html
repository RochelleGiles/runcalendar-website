Course

A lapped trail event at Bear Creek in Robbins, North Carolina, using a 1.3 mile and a 3.1 mile wooded loop that runners may mix as they choose. Entrants entering the 6-hour or 12-hour challenge cover as many loops as they can within the window, with no minimum distance.

About

The Winter Endurance Challenge is a timed, loop-style endurance event with 6-hour and 12-hour options on 1.3-mile and 3-mile trail loops at Bear Creek in Robbins, NC. It is a charity fundraiser with no minimum distance requirement, awarding top mileage achievers.
