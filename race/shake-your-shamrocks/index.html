Course

An out-and-back starting and finishing at Lucky Chucky's Beach Bar & Grill in Oconomowoc. The course heads east on Valley Rd to Silver Cedar Rd before joining the Lake Country Trail and turning back, with two water aid stations en route. A 5K, 10K, 5K ruck, two-mile walk and a 1K kids' race are offered.

About

We are all a little Irish on St. Patrick's Day and if you're like us, you prepare for St. Patrick's day all year long through a rigorous training plan, continual practice, and self sacrifice. We also run a bit so we're prepared for the Shake Your Shamrocks 5K, 10K run and 2K walk.
