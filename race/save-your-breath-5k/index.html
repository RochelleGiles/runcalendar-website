Course

Fast, relatively flat out-and-back 5 km on the rail trail, starting and finishing at the Kennebec Valley YMCA, 31 Union Street, Augusta, Maine and turning at the Adirondack chairs in Hallowell. The course is USATF certified. A timed walk starts at 8am and the 5 km at 8:30am. Aid station detail is not stated.

About

A community 5K run and walk in Augusta, Maine, raising funds for lung health awareness. The race draws local participants each fall for a cause-focused morning through Maine's capital city, with proceeds supporting local health programs.
