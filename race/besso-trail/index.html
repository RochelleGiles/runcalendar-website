About

An extreme alpine trail from Zinal, at the head of the Val d'Anniviers in the Swiss Valais. The 59 km course links the valley's five mountain huts on old, half-forgotten alpine paths, crossing two glaciers and passing above 3,000 m four times, with about 5,800 m of climbing. Held in early September.
