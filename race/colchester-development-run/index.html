About

The Colchester Development Run is a road race held in Colchester, a small Eastern Cape village at the mouth of the Sundays River on the Sunshine Coast, near Addo Elephant National Park. Runners can choose between 21.1km, 10km, or 5km distances, with proceeds supporting local development.
