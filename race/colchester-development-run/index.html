About

The Colchester Development Run is an Eastern Province Athletics road race at Colchester, the village at the mouth of the Sundays River near Addo Elephant National Park. Runners choose between 10km and 5km, with a 1 mile race as well, all starting from the Mackay Bridge Angling Club.
