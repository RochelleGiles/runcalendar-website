Course

A USATF-certified point-to-point through the Amish farmland around Bird-in-Hand, largely on quiet paved lanes with short unpaved sections that can be muddy after rain. Water and sports drink are offered at eleven points on the half marathon, with six rest stops along the way. A 5K runs the same morning.

About

A half marathon, 5K, and kids fun run through Lancaster County Amish Country featuring authentic horseshoe finisher medals made from real horseshoes. The race is known for its community atmosphere, fully supported course with water stations, and post-race celebration including a free BBQ picnic.
