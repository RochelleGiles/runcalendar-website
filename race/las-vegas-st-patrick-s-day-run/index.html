Course

Run on the Historic Railroad Trail in Lake Mead National Recreation Area, about 18 miles from Las Vegas. The half marathon extends toward Hoover Dam and passes through six railroad tunnels; shorter distances use out-and-back sections of the same rail-trail. Water and Gatorade are provided on course.

About

The Las Vegas St. Patrick's Day Run is a family-friendly event held in historic Boulder City at Lake Mead National Recreation Area. Runners enjoy scenic routes on the Historic Railroad Trail with views of the largest man-made lake in the US.
