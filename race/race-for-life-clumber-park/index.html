Course

A single-lap 10K through the National Trust parkland at Clumber Park near Worksop, following wide gravel paths and tarmac estate roads through ancient woodland. Underfoot it mixes hardstanding, gravel, grass and woodland path, with gentle curves between mature trees and roaming deer.

About

Race for Life's Nottinghamshire event runs through Clumber Park, National Trust parkland famous for its avenue of lime trees and its lake. Cancer Research UK's charity 10K is run, jogged or walked through the historic estate, raising funds for cancer research.
