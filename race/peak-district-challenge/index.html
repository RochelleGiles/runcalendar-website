Course

A two-day challenge event in the Peak District offering 25km, 50km, 75km and 100km routes with time limits from 9 to 24 hours. The 25km Copper route is a circular from Hathersage to Chatsworth and back via Curbar and Froggatt edges; the 75km Silver route links Hope Valley, Ladybower, Millersdale and Bakewell before returning to Hope Valley.

About

The Peak District Challenge is a trail run and trek organised by Wilderness Development, offering 25km, 50km, 75km, 100km and 100-mile distances. The mixed-terrain routes are anticlockwise loops starting at Hathersage, with final routes revealed to entrants seven days before the event.
