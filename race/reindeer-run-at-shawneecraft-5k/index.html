Course

The 5K starts and finishes at ShawneeCraft Brewery, crossing a bridge onto the historic Shawnee Inn golf course and running its cart paths and fairways plus a wooded trail section before returning to the brewery. There is a one hour limit and no late starts; the course suits neither strollers nor dogs.

About

Join this holiday 5K race at ShawneeCraft Brewery and the historic Shawnee Inn and Golf Course for the 4th annual holiday themed race! Participants will run over a beautiful bridge onto the historic golf course and enjoy this scenic course which includes cart paths, fairways, and a wooded trail before finishing at the brewery.
