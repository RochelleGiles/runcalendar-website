About

Explore the stunning Coombabah Lake Conservation Area on the Gold Coast during the Coombabah Trail Run. This scenic event offers both a 5.5km and 12.5km course, allowing runners of all levels to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of this protected wetland environment. Whether you're seeking a leisurely walk or a challenging trail run, the Coombabah Trail Run promises an unforgettable experience.
