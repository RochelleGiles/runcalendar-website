About

The Glen of Aherlow Trail Run offers runners a picturesque adventure through the stunning natural beauty of Ireland's Glen of Aherlow. This challenging yet rewarding event winds through lush forests, rolling hills, and breathtaking mountain vistas, providing an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this race is sure to leave you inspired by the stunning Irish landscape.
