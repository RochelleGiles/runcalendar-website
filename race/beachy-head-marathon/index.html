Course

Off-road races starting and finishing on Eastbourne seafront at the start of the South Downs Way, crossing the South Downs National Park. The marathon, half marathon, ultra (about 52.6km) and 10K share the downland course; the 10K loops out to Belle Tout Lighthouse and back, and the half covers much of the marathon route.

About

One of the biggest off-road marathons in the UK returns on 24-25 October 2026 for a 46th year! Formerly known as the Seven Sisters Marathon, it is popular for its scenic and challenging route through the South Downs National Park countryside. Great for runners, joggers or walkers looking to get fit.
