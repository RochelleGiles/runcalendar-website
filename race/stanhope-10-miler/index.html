Course

Starts in Stanhope with a hard climb up Softly Bank towards Bollihope, reaching about 1,300ft, then runs on through Hill End and back through Stanhope to finish at Stanhope Showfield. Undulating and multi-terrain, roughly 90% road and 10% grass and trail. Entry on the day is not available.

About

The Stanhope 10 Miler runs through Weardale in the North Pennines Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, following country roads and trails around the market town of Stanhope in County Durham, northern England.
