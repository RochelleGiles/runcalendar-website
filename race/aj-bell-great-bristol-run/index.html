About

The AJ Bell Great Bristol Run is a popular running event featuring 10k and half marathon routes through Bristol's scenic cityscape. Runners experience stunning views, inspiring history, and vibrant street art while exploring the city's most famous landmarks. The event attracts thousands of participants each year, offering a challenging yet enjoyable running experience for athletes of all levels.
