About

The Freckleton Half Marathon is the UK's oldest half marathon, taking runners on a scenic tour through the picturesque Lancashire countryside. This historic event offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels, with a course that winds through charming villages and past stunning natural landscapes. Whether you're a seasoned half marathoner or new to the distance, the Freckleton Half is a must-do for any runner looking for an unforgettable race.
