About

After four editions marked by conviviality, sharing and celebration, the Bougarber Parents' Association (APEB) is pleased to announce the fifth edition of the 'La Bougarbersienne' trail. The sporting event aims to finance school outings for the children and bring the village together around the strong values of sport: solidarity, self-transcendence and team spirit. The evening will continue in a festive and warm atmosphere, with catering and refreshments on site to extend the exchanges and smiles. As every year, the 'Vaincre la mucoviscidose' association will be our partner, and this edition will also have the pleasure of supporting the ARTC (Association for Brain Tumor Research).
