About

Immerse yourself in the breathtaking landscapes of France's Auvergne region at the Trail des Sources de la Loire. This challenging trail run takes participants on a journey through the picturesque countryside, following the winding path of the Loire River's headwaters. Runners will be captivated by the diverse terrain, from lush forests to rugged mountain vistas, making this event a must-do for any trail enthusiast.
