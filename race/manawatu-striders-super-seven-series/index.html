About

The Manawatu Striders Super Seven Series is a beloved community running event held in the heart of New Zealand's Manawatu region. Over seven consecutive Tuesday nights, participants can challenge themselves on a variety of scenic routes, making this a must-do for local and visiting runners alike. Whether you're looking to push your pace or simply enjoy an evening jog with friends, this series has something for everyone.
