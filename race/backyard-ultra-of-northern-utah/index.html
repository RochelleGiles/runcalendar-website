Course

Backyard ultra run on a 4.1 mile repeating loop using the Mantua Reservoir Trail. The start/finish corral sits in the west parking area off Main Street in Mantua, with the aid station alongside it stocked with standard ultra fare. The surface is firm dirt with some gravel. Runners continue lap after lap with no set finishing distance.

About

The Backyard Ultra of Northern Utah isn't just another Backyard Ultra — it's a perfect 4.1 mile Backyard Ultra loop. Set against the breathtaking backdrop of Mantua Reservoir, Utah, this event will bring runners together from across the country to take on a challenge they'll never forget.
