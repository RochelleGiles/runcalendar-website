About

The Tidewater Striders Turkey Trot offers a 10K and a one-mile run/walk starting and finishing at the YMCA on South Boulevard in Virginia Beach. The certified 10K loop weaves through neighborhood streets before returning to the YMCA, while the mile runs out and back along South Boulevard. Proceeds support the club's scholarship fund.
