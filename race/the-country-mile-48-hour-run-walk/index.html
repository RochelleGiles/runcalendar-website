Course

A single 4.167-mile looped course, repeated as many times as the participant wants within 48 hours. The organiser describes the surface as 95% wide dirt paths with some grass and light gravel, with directional arrows at each turn. Start, finish and the sole water station are at the U-Pick Stand at Beechwood Farms, with an indoor bathroom there and portable toilets around the loop.

About

We couldn't be more pleased to welcome 300 strong-hearted folks out here for a weekend filled with dirt, distance, and some good ol'-fashioned farm fun. This here patch of land—Beechwood Family Farm—is one of the Upstate's prettiest spots, and they've kindly opened up their fields so y'all can stretch your legs and chase dreams from 50K all the way up to a big ol' 150 miles.
