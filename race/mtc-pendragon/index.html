Course

A point-to-point 46-mile route on National Cycle Route 8 from Llanidloes to the River Wye Activity Centre at Glasbury, on cycle path apart from about a mile leaving Llanidloes. Around 1,271 m of ascent. Five checkpoints roughly every 8-9 miles with food and drinks. Cup-free event capped at 40 runners.

About

MTC Pendragon is a mystery-route ultramarathon in the Mount to Coast series, starting from Llanidloes in the Cambrian Mountains of mid-Wales. The 74km course is kept secret until race day, taking runners deep into remote Welsh hill country.
