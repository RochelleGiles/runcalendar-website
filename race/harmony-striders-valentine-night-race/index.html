About

The Harmony Striders Valentine Night Race is an evening road race in Welkom, hosted by the Harmony Striders club, named for the Harmony Gold mining company based in the Free State Goldfields. Runners can choose between a marathon, 16km, 10km, or 4km distance, run after dark around Valentine's Day.
