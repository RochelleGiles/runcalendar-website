Course

A modified out-and-back loop on closed streets through downtown Lexington, starting and finishing on Short Street between the two courthouses. The route runs out East Main, turns 180 degrees around the median at Bassett Avenue, and returns via Martin Luther King Boulevard. There is one hydration station.
