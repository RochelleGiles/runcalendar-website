Course

About

The Razorback Run takes adventurous runners on a journey through the breathtaking landscapes of Victoria's Alpine National Park. Offering 3 challenging distances, this event invites athletes to push their limits amidst the region's dramatic peaks and valleys. Whether a seasoned trail runner or newcomer to the sport, the Razorback Run promises an unforgettable experience in one of Australia's most spectacular natural settings.
