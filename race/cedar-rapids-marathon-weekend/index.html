Course

A flat, fast road marathon on wide paved streets, starting in the heart of downtown Cedar Rapids and winding through historic neighbourhoods, business districts, riverside paths and city parks. The course has minimal elevation gain, long straight stretches and only gentle turns.

About

At the Cedar Rapids Marathon, our mission is to deliver a world-class race experience that reflects the energy, resilience, and community spirit of Cedar Rapids. We believe a marathon should be more than just a race — it should be a celebration. A celebration of personal goals, of community pride, and of everything that makes this city special.
