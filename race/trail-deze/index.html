About

Explore the picturesque trails of Eze during the annual Trail d'Èze running event. This challenging course winds through the scenic Riviera countryside, offering stunning views of the Mediterranean coastline. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail d'Èze promises an unforgettable running experience amidst the natural beauty of the region.
