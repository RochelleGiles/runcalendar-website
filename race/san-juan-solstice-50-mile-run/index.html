Course

Single counter-clockwise 50-mile loop starting and finishing at Town Park in Lake City. Mostly single-track with some jeep road, running between roughly 8,671 ft and 13,334 ft. Seven aid points: Alpine Gulch, Williams Creek, Carson, Divide, Slumgullion, Vickers and the finish. 16-hour limit.

About

The San Juan Solstice 50 Mile Run is a challenging and scenic ultramarathon that takes place in the beautiful San Juan Mountains of Colorado. Runners will experience stunning alpine landscapes, technical trails, and the thrill of conquering this demanding 50-mile course.
