About

This is the Grand Finale of the Track Shack Running Series presented by AdventHealth. The 49th annual event is a local tradition. With a 10k, 2 mile and kids' run the whole family can participate. Run through the beautiful neighborhoods of Winter Park. The 10k course features slight inclines, tree canopied roads, historic homes and views of the Chain of Lakes.
