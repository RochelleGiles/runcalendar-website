Course

A multi-terrain 10K that is about 75% off road, winding through local trails, woodland and single-track footpaths near Yattendon. It starts by the Tap Room and finishes back at the Renegade Brewery, with 140m of climb and a two-hour cut-off. Two water refill stations on course; the race is cupless.

About

The Renegade 10K run takes place in the beautiful surroundings of the stunning Yattendon Estate, home to the Renegade Brewery. The multi-terrain course winds through local trails, woodland, and footpaths.
