Course

Start and finish at Victor Ashe Park, Knoxville. The 5K loops from the pavilion onto the greenway to the back of the school and returns to the start. The half marathon is three loops of a 4.37-mile greenway course with a four-hour limit, and relay teams each run one loop. Aid stations are passed nine times over the route.

About

Get into the holiday spirit with either a half marathon or 5K run Saturday, December 12th, 2026 from Victor Ashe Park in Knoxville, TN. Events benefit the Sunshine Ambassadors - Enriching the lives of people with disabilities through dance! Christmas Rock-n-Roll music will abound from Victor Ashe Park and along the routes.
