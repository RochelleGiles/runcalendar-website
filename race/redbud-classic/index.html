Course

Road 5K and 10K run through the Nichols Hills and Classen Curve districts of Oklahoma City. Both courses are USATF sanctioned and certified, and runners who leave the certified route are disqualified. The running events are held on the Sunday of the two-day festival, with cycling on the Saturday.

About

The Redbud Classic offers 10K and 5K running races through Oklahoma City each spring. It anchors a long-running community fitness festival that also includes a cycling ride, kids' races, and family activities on race weekend.
