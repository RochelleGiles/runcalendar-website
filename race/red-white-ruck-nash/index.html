About

Memories of Honor's Red, White & Ruck is a fun, patriotic, competitive, and noncompetitive ruck designed to unite the military community, Gold—and Blue-Star Families, and the civilian population. This event is all about honoring the service, sacrifice, and lives of our service members and fallen heroes. Whether you're a seasoned Rucker, new to the experience, or walking without weight, this charity event welcomes individuals of all ages and fitness levels.
