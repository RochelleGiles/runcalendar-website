Course

Loop courses through Pat Sin Leng and Lam Tsuen country parks in the north-eastern New Territories, on country trails, ancient village paths and reservoir tracks with technical climbs. Distances run over two days: 8km and 21km on the Saturday, 31.5km and 43.5km on the Sunday, the longest taking in five major peaks.
