About

The Trail Des Combrailles is a popular running event that takes place in the scenic Combrailles region of France. The course features challenging terrain and stunning natural landscapes, offering a unique and rewarding experience for runners. The event attracts participants from all over the country and is known for its vibrant atmosphere and sense of community.
