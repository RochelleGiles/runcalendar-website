About

The Peanut Butter Jelly Jog is an annual 5K road race in Graham, North Carolina, starting and finishing at Trailhead Church. The course loops through residential streets and the town centre and is chip timed. It is run by SAFE Alamance, which supports child abuse prevention in the county.
