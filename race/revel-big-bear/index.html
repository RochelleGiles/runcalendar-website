Course

A point-to-point downhill road marathon and half marathon dropping out of the Big Bear mountains through the San Bernardino National Forest to the foothills at Redlands. The grade is a steady smooth descent on sealed road, with a large net elevation loss that makes it a popular course for personal bests.

About
