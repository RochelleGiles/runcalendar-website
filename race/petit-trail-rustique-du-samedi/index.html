About

The PTRS is a real 10 km trail with 300 meters of elevation gain. An engaged trail, 100% nature, with winding paths, schist paths, field crossings, roots, streams, undergrowth, open landscapes... in short, a very varied trail! The course will be marked with signs and ribbons attached to the trees, and stewards will also be there for safety. Several vehicles will be distributed along the course for safety, as well as volunteers on mountain bikes.
