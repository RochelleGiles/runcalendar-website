Course

A road course beginning and ending in Lubbock's historic downtown, with the full marathon run as a Boston qualifier. The route weaves out through the city's cotton-and-cattle heritage districts, its live-music quarter and the redeveloped downtown blocks before returning to the same finish. Half marathon, 10K, 5K and a kids run share the morning.
