Course

A night race on looped dirt-trail courses from Mountain Vista High School in Highlands Ranch. The 12-hour solo, 3-person relay and 50K run a 10.3-mile loop with about 870ft of climb per lap; the 6-hour, 3-hour and 5-person relay use a 5.3-mile loop with about 420ft. The organiser describes it as roughly 98% dirt trail, loose and rocky underfoot, with aid at Outlaw and the start/finish.

About

Chase the Moon is a 12-hour team relay and solo ultramarathon, run overnight under the light of the full moon in the Highlands Ranch Backcountry trail system (we promise some of the best views of the metro and front range). Run as a solo ultrarunner, or get your fellow moon-chasing friends for a 3-person or 5-person relay team.
