Course

A 20.4-mile point-to-point along the Causeway Coast, taking in the basalt columns of the Giant's Causeway itself. Mixed grass, trail, beach and coastpath underfoot with about 2,276 ft of climb, past cliffs, sandy beaches and castle ruins. Rated moderate; a UTMB qualifier.

About

Explore the legendary Giant's Causeway on this captivating 20-mile trail run along Ireland's stunning coastline. Immerse yourself in the dramatic, otherworldly landscapes of this UNESCO World Heritage site as you challenge yourself on this unique endurance event. Runners of all levels are invited to experience the beauty and magic of this iconic natural wonder.
