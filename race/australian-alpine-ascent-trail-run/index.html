Course

All distances start and finish at The Stables, Perisher, in Kosciuszko National Park, on alpine trails in the Snowy Mountains. The 80km, 55km and 27km runs set off in the morning with finishers expected between 6pm and 8pm depending on distance.

About

Conquer the Australian Alps at the Australian Alpine Ascent Trail Run, a challenging and scenic event through the stunning landscapes of Kosciuszko National Park in New South Wales. Choose from three distance options - 80km, 55km, or 27km - as you push your limits and take in breathtaking views of towering peaks and alpine meadows.
