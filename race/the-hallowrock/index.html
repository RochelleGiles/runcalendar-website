Course

An evening out-and-back on the Western Beltway Trail, starting and finishing behind the PKWY Tavern on West Flamingo Road in Las Vegas. From 2,710 ft the course climbs gradually at roughly 100 ft per mile to the turnaround, then returns downhill with views of the Strip. Aid stations are provided on course.
