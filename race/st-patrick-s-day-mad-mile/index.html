About

The Sneaker Store's Jessica Cooper St. Patrick's Day Mad Mile presented by Standard is the best way to kick off a great St. Patrick's Day! Bring your family, friends, and your best Irish outfit, and get ready to dash down Genesee Street just ahead of the Utica St. Patrick's Day Parade. With a built-in cheering crowd, this one-mile course with elevation drop will get you revved up to enjoy the day's festivities.
