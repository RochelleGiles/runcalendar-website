Course

A looped trail race at Farmdale Reservoir near East Peoria, an 800-acre area maintained by the US Army Corps of Engineers. The course mixes meandering single-track, service road and open prairie, with all loops returning to a start/finish aid station where crew access is allowed. Distances run from 10K up to 100 miles.

About

Farmdale Trail Run offers 10K, half marathon, 30-, 50-, and 100-mile distances on wooded singletrack at Farmdale State Recreation Area near East Peoria, Illinois. Runners tackle looping trails through rolling forest terrain each October, with options for first-time trail racers through seasoned ultrarunners.
