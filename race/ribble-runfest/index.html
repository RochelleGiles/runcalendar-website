Course

A lapped course on hard-surface paths through Preston's riverside parkland, starting near Avenham Park and running along the River Ribble through Miller Park to a turnaround at Upper Farm Nature Reserve before returning to the finish. The 10K is one loop plus a short out-and-back; the half marathon is three loops, the marathon six and the ultra seven. A six-hour time limit applies to every distance.

About

Ribble Runfest offers marathon, half marathon and 10K distances starting from Avenham Park in Preston, with routes following the River Ribble. It's part of Lancashire's road-race calendar and draws everyone from first-time marathoners to PB-chasers.
