Course

The 100K starts and finishes at Bayfront Festival Park; the 50K runs point-to-point from Oldenburg Point in Jay Cooke State Park to Bayfront Park; Sunday's half marathon and 10K finish at Spirit Mountain's Grand Avenue Chalet. Aid stations sit 2 to 7.4 miles apart on rocky, rooty terrain.

About

The Wild Duluth Races include the 50K and 100K ultramarathons, the Harder 'n Heck Half Marathon and the Terribly Tough 10K, each following the Superior Hiking Trail through the city of Duluth. Runners enjoy spectacular views of Lake Superior from the rugged, rocky, rooty trail, feeling like they're in a wild place despite running mostly within Duluth's city limits.
