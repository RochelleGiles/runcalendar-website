About

The Flytex 10KM Paris 15 is a unique 10-kilometer running event traversing the 15th arrondissement of Paris. Runners will explore iconic locations including Quai de Seine, Beaugrenelle, Institut Pasteur, and Parc Georges Brassens. The event offers a flat, fluid course with just 49 meters of elevation gain, making it suitable for both experienced runners and enthusiastic amateurs.
