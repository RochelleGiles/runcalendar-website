Course

Point-to-point and loop courses along the Sapphire Coast. The TRA30 runs from Short Point Recreation Reserve, Merimbula to Tathra SLSC (22.9km trail, 5.6km beach, 1.4km tarmac, three aid stations); the TRA18 from Hobart Beach Campground to the Tathra foreshore; the TRA7 loops out and back from Tathra. Courses cross coastal forest, beach and clifftop, passing the historic Tathra Wharf.

About

Dive into an exhilarating adventure along Australia's Sapphire Coast, where wild coastlines, untouched wilderness, hidden lagoons, and expansive beaches await. The centrepiece of this running festival is the iconic 28k event, where participants embark on an unforgettable journey from the picturesque town of Merimbula to the historic Tathra Wharf, winding through expansive coastal vistas on pristine trails that showcase the very best of the Sapphire Coast.
