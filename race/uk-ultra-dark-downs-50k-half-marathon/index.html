Course

A dusk-till-dawn loop starting and finishing at Plumpton Racecourse near Lewes, setting off at 18:30 with a 12-hour limit. The route runs 95% trail and 5% road across the rolling South Downs National Park, with about 4,400ft of ascent. Four staffed aid stations serve the course, which is fully marked with highly reflective signage for running in darkness.

About

The UK Ultra Dark Downs 50K is a Dusk till Dawn loop in the South Downs, starting and finishing at Plumpton Racecourse. Runners will have 12 hours to complete the race, with 95% trail and 5% road terrain, featuring 4 aid stations and a fully marked course.
