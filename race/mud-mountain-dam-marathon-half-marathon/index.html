Course

Point-to-point. The marathon starts at Mud Mountain Dam Park near Buckley and finishes at the historic McMillin Bridge on a fully paved route through farmland and forest tracing the Carbon River. The half marathon starts in South Prairie and follows the paved Foothills Rail Trail along the Carbon River to the same finish.

About

The Mud Mountain Dam Marathon & Half Marathon is a scenic downhill race through farmlands and forests, tracing the path of the Carbon River. The marathon course features a net elevation loss of 1,201 feet, while the half marathon has a net loss of 339 feet. Both races finish in front of the historic McMillin Bridge.
