Course

A double loop on South Ridge Drive by Pie Traynor Field in North Park, starting and finishing at the Harmar Pavilion up the hill from the North Park swimming pool. The 1-mile fun run goes at 6:00 PM and the 5K at 6:30 PM, with a combined Seelos Double for younger runners.

About

The One Mile Fun run will begin at 6:00 PM and the 5K will begin at 6:30 PM. This family friendly event will take place in beautiful North Park at the Harmar Pavilion located close to the North Park Swimming Pool on South Ridge Drive.
