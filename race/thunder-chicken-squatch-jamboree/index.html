Course

Technical singletrack with rocks and roots through the New Jersey Highlands, starting and finishing at the Kittle Picnic Area in Stokes State Forest. The 17-miler climbs about 1,500 ft with a full aid station at mile 7.65 and water at 13.5; the 10K climbs 600 ft with water at mile 3; the 5K climbs 325 ft with no on-course aid.

About

Based at the Kittle Picnic Area in Stokes State Forest, this jamboree offers 5K, 10K, and 17-mile trail distances plus a weighted ruck division, over rocky, root-covered singletrack typical of the New Jersey Highlands. It offers discounted entry for military service members.
