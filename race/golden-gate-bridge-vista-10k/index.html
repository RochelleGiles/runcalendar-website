Course

A clockwise loop from the USSSF Monument parking lot at El Camino del Mar, descending past Seal Rock and along the Lands End Trail past Eagles Point toward Baker Beach, reversing at the Sand Ladder. Mixed paved and dirt/gravel surfaces with a 15-minute-per-mile pace limit.

About

A challenging 10K trail race featuring an out-and-back course with hills, stairs, and scenic views of the Golden Gate Bridge and San Francisco Bay. The race starts and finishes at the USSSF Monument in the parking lot at Seal Rock Drive and El Camino del Mar.
