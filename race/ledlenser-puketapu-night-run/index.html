Course

Night trail run on loops along both sides of the Tūtaekurī River, starting and finishing at Puketapu School. The surface is limestone riverside trail, with the longer loops crossing the Waiohiki and Puketapu bridges. Sections are lit and marshalled, and headlamps are required. The full loop is also offered as a three-person relay.

About

The Ledlenser Puketapu Night Run in Hawke's Bay offers 17km, 12km and 6.25km distances along the Puketapu Loop. It can be run individually or as a three-person relay with legs of around 5.5km, 5.25km and 6.25km, and is run by the Puketapu School Parents' League as a fundraiser for Puketapu School.
