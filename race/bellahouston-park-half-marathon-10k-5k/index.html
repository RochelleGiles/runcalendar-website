Course

A 5km loop in Bellahouston Park, one lap for the 5K, two for the 10K and four plus a short extra section for the half marathon. Kilometre markers, directional arrows and marshals throughout, with a water station at the halfway point of the loop.

About

Lace up your running shoes and join the Bellahouston Park Half Marathon, 10K & 5K in Glasgow's vibrant Southside. This picturesque event takes place in the stunning Bellahouston Park, offering runners of all levels a scenic route through the lush green space. Whether you're aiming for a new personal best or simply want to enjoy the beautiful surroundings, this community-focused race is not to be missed.
