Course

A multi-loop format at Anclote Gulf Park where every race starts and finishes. The 5K runs two 0.78-mile loops and the 10K four; the half marathon uses a separate 1.09-mile loop repeated six times. An 8am start with a 3 hour 30 minute course limit.

About

The Viking 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is a running event held at Anclote Gulf Park in Holiday, Florida, featuring out-and-back courses with multiple laps for each distance. Finishers receive medals, and awards are given for the best costumes, with a 3.5-hour time limit.
