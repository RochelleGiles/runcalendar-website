Course

Point-to-point courses run from San Francisco into the headlands, with runners shuttled from the Palace of Fine Arts to the start. The 50 mile and 50K finish at Rodeo Beach, while the 25K and 15K finish at Crissy Field. Courses are subject to land-agency permit approval and may be modified before race day.
