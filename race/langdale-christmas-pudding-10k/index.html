Course

A 10K on the quiet Lakeland roads of the Great Langdale Valley, starting and finishing at the New Dungeon Ghyll Hotel event field. One water station sits at 5.6km. The roads stay open to other traffic, so runners keep left and no more than two abreast. There is a two-hour limit and a 50-minute cutoff at halfway.

About

Lace up your running shoes and join the festive fun at the Langdale Christmas Pudding 10K! This scenic race winds through the stunning Great Langdale Valley in the heart of the Lake District, offering stunning views of the surrounding fells. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a newcomer to the sport, this event promises a memorable holiday experience for all.
