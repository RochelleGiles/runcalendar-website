Course

A repeating 15.5-mile loop through forest and rolling red-clay hills, with about 1100 feet of climb per lap. Roughly three quarters is undulating, technical single-track over roots and rocks, with the rest equestrian path, gravel and a short road section; water crossings appear after rain. Aid sits at the start/finish plus miles 5, 9 and 14, with drop bags at the last two.

About

Screaming Monkey is a trail ultra near Keithville, Louisiana, offering 100-mile, 100K, 50K, 25K and 10K distances on the same course. Runners choose their distance and take on the trails of northwest Louisiana together.
