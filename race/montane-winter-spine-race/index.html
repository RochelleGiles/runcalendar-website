About

A non-stop 268-mile race along the entire Pennine Way, from Edale in the Peak District to Kirk Yetholm on the Scottish border. Run every January, it crosses exposed moorland and mountain terrain in the depths of the British winter, widely rated as one of the world's toughest ultras.
