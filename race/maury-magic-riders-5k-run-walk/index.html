Course

The 5K winds through Maury County Park on Maury County Park Drive in Columbia, using a mix of park roadways and nature trails. The organiser describes the pathways as easy to moderate and suitable for both runners and walkers. No aid station or route shape detail is published.

About

The 4th Annual Maury Magic Riders 5K is a scenic run/walk through Maury County Park featuring park roadways and nature trails. The event supports the Maury Magic Riders Therapeutic Horseback Riding Program, which provides equine-assisted therapy to individuals with disabilities.
