Course

Starts and finishes at the Memorial Coliseum in Fort Wayne, running downtown along the St Joe River, across town on Main Street and then onto the city's paved trail system. Flat and fast; marathon runners follow the half route for 13 miles before heading north for a second half almost entirely on paved trail.

About

The Fort Wayne Marathon and Half Marathon is an annual marathon and half marathon in Fort Wayne, Indiana, organized by the Fort Wayne Running Club. Runners choose between the full 26.2-mile marathon or the 13.1-mile half marathon distance through the city.
