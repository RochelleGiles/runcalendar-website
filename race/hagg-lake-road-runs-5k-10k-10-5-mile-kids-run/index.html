Course

The 5K and 10K are out-and-back from Bobcat Cove at Henry Hagg Lake; the 10.5-mile runs once around the lake counter-clockwise. All courses use the paved park road, with runners kept to the bike lane, and there are four aid stations. A free quarter-mile kids' run is also held.

About

This popular recreational haven boasts big water and big vistas. Hagg Lake Park plays host to four events: 5k, 10k, 10.5 Mile and a Kids' run. All feature gentle hills and natural beauty!
