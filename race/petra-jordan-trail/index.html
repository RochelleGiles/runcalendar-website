About

Located in Western Asia, amidst a conflict zone, Jordan is nonetheless a haven of peace where all the magic of the East is concentrated. Localized between two seas, the Red Sea and the Dead Sea, Jordan is a small country engulfed by desert and long inhabited exclusively by nomads. This area of the globe is a crossing point between the Mediterranean world and the East, where many civilizations have succeeded each other and left behind magnificent remnants, among the most important in the Middle East. The treasures are numerous in this country known for housing one of the seven wonders of the modern world, with the sublime Petra. The Petra Jordan Trail is an intense immersion in the wild and diverse nature of this small country and an exploration of Eastern culture.
