About

The 5K Run/Walk is held on the Saturday morning of the annual Thibodaux Firemen's Fair, starting and finishing at the fairgrounds entrance on Tiger Drive. The route passes through Thibodaux's historic Downtown District. Youth entries are sponsored free of charge, and pre-registered adults receive a food and drink voucher for the fair.
