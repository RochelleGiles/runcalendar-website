About

The Saukville Scare is a Halloween-themed 5K run/walk starting and finishing at Grady Park in Saukville, Wisconsin. Alongside the main 5K there is a separate free race for ages 12 and under, plus a children's trick-or-treat fun walk that follows a shortened route with four treat stops.
