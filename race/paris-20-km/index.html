Course

Point-to-point 20 km on closed Paris roads, starting on the Pont d'Iéna and finishing beneath the Eiffel Tower. The route runs through western Paris past the Trocadéro, the Arc de Triomphe, the Bois de Boulogne, Roland-Garros and the Maison de la Radio, with bands and music groups along the way.

About

The Harmonie Mutuelle 20km de Paris runs from the Pont d'Iéna through western Paris and finishes at the Eiffel Tower. The route takes in the Trocadéro, the Arc de Triomphe, the Bois de Boulogne and Roland-Garros, with bands stationed along the way. Its 48th edition is held in October 2026.
