Course

A timed trail event on a 4.4-mile loop through Little Bennett Regional Park, Clarksburg. Runners cover as many loops as they can within a 9-hour or 5-hour window, solo or as a relay, with a shorter "More Than a 4-Miler" option. One fully stocked aid station on the loop, and a chili bar at the finish.

About

Froggy Hollow 5 & 9, presented by Altra, is a trail race in Clarksburg, Maryland, offering 5-mile and 9-mile distances on wooded singletrack. Charm City Run organizes the event each winter, drawing local trail runners for a low-key winter outing.
