Course

Point-to-point along the North Carolina Outer Banks. The 100 mile starts at Historic Corolla Park and the 100K at Jennette's Pier, Nags Head; both finish at The Wreck Tiki Bar. The route is entirely paved and flat, with multiple aid stations.

About

Aye, ye ask why we call it Blackbeard's Revenge? Pull up a chair, Matey, and hear this tale... The Outer Banks be a fickle beast—temps can swing from 80 to 20, the wind'll whip ye sideways, and though the roads be flat, they stretch on like the horizon, testin' yer spirit. Plunderin' these parts (er, runnin') is a grand adventure—but make no mistake, she'll make ye earn yer reward!
