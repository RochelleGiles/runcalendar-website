Course

A six-hour lapped challenge in the grounds of Hardwick Hall, where entrants complete as many 5.66km loops as they can from a 9am start. Four laps make a half marathon, eight a marathon and nine or more an ultra. The signposted loop crosses working farmland with one hill per lap and can be muddy, so trail shoes are advised. A stocked base camp at the start-finish serves food and drink each lap.

About

Explore the historic grounds of Hardwick Hall in Derbyshire during the Hardwick Hobble Autumn event. Challenge yourself with a 6-hour running challenge that winds through the scenic countryside surrounding this iconic Tudor estate. Runners of all levels will enjoy the unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of this picturesque setting.
