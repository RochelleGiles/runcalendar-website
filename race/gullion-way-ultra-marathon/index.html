Course

A 64km loop following the Gullion Way around the Ring of Gullion volcanic formation in County Armagh. Roughly 90% quiet rural road and 10% trail, and notably hilly, traversing Slieve Gullion, Black Mountain and Camlough Mountain and passing Clontygora Court Grave and Moyry Castle. Capped at 50 runners.

About

The Gullion Way Ultra Marathon offers a challenging 64km run along an historic route with stunning mountain views. Runners traverse scenic landscapes including the Ring of Gullion, passing historic sites like Clontygora Court Grave, Moyry Castle, and Slieve Gullion Forest park.
