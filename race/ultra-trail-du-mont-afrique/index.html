About

The Ultra Trail du Mont Afrique runs as four consecutive loops starting from Corcelles-les-Monts near Dijon, named Serpent, Ouf, Plateau and Combes, each with its own cutoff time. Runners can tackle the full course solo or split it as a relay team of two to four, with the route winding through the wooded hills of Mont Afrique in Burgundy.
