Course

Starts and finishes at Occasions, 125 E. North Lake Road in North Platte. The route follows the Whitaker's Way trail system, which incorporates the NPPD Canal Trail, out to the scenic overlook south of town and back. Offered as a 5K and a 10K. Surface and aid station detail are not published.

About

This unique event honors North Platte fitness pioneer and mayor Jim Whitaker and celebrates the value of trails to our community! The race begins and ends at Occasions, 125 E. North Lake Road, and takes you on a scenic tour of Whitaker's Way, the trail system that incorporates the NPPD Canal Trail and travels to the scenic overlook south of North Platte.
