Course

Closed-road course starting on Neilson Road in Gateshead and passing the Millennium Bridge, BALTIC, The Glasshouse, the Tyne Bridge and the Swing Bridge before finishing with a lap of the track inside Gateshead International Stadium. Marathon starts at 9am and the half marathon and 10K at 9:25am, with a six-hour cut-off set by the road closures.

About

Explore the iconic cityscapes of Newcastle and Gateshead as you challenge yourself in the Newcastle-Gateshead Marathon, Half Marathon, or 10K. This picturesque multi-distance event showcases the best of North East England, with stunning views of the River Tyne and its historic bridges. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or looking to conquer your first 10K, this is an unforgettable running experience not to be missed.
