Course

An undulating half marathon on fully closed roads, starting and finishing at Warwick Racecourse and running out through the Warwickshire countryside. The final 2 km is slightly downhill into the finish. A 1 km junior race is held alongside the main event.

About

The Warwick Half Marathon takes in spectacular, scenic views of the Warwickshire countryside. Based from the Warwick Racecourse, the undulating course takes place on fully closed roads and is suitable for runners of all abilities.
