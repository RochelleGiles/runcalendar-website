About

Boston's Run to Remember offers a half marathon and 5-mile race on Memorial Day weekend, starting and finishing in Boston's Seaport. Organized by Millennium Running, the route passes Boston Common, the Public Garden, Harvard, Boston University, and Fenway Park, honoring fallen law enforcement officers and first responders.
