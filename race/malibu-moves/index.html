Course

Coastal road races starting and finishing at Zuma Beach in Malibu. The half marathon, 10K and 5K are all out-and-back on the Pacific Coast Highway, rolling but well graded, with ocean views for the whole route. A 1-mile Zuma Dash and a 1K Kids Run complete the weekend.

About

Malibu Moves is a running festival in Malibu, California, with routes along the Pacific Coast Highway near Zuma Beach. Distances range from a 1K kids run to a half marathon along the Southern California coastline.
