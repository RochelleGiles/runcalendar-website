Course

A point-to-point night trail half marathon through Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park, starting at Golden Jubilee Field and finishing at St Ives Showground. Flowing singletrack, technical climbs and fire trail with several road crossings and waterfront sections, 556m of climbing. Four-hour limit.

About

The Night Edition of the Sydney Trail Half Marathon takes runners along bush trails after dark, one of several seasonal editions of this trail half marathon held around Sydney through the year.
