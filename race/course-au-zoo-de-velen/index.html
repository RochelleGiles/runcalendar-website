About

The Course Au Zoo De Velen in Velen, Germany offers runners a unique opportunity to experience a scenic race through the local zoo. Participants can expect a challenging course that winds through the zoo's diverse animal habitats, providing an unforgettable and one-of-a-kind running adventure. This event is a must-do for nature and running enthusiasts alike.
