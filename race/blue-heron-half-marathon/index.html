Course

Run along the South Platte River at Riverdale Regional Park in Brighton. The half is mostly flat with about 3.5 miles of smooth dirt, looping Mann-Nyholt Lake before a slight climb south to a 1.2-mile loop at Valente Lake and a downhill return. The 5K is flat and fast, roughly half paved and half smooth dirt.

About

The Blue Heron Half Marathon and 5K are held at Adams County Regional Park along the South Platte River in Brighton, Colorado, tracing the riverside trails of Riverdale Regional Park.
