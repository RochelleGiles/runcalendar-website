Course

Point-to-point along the Somerset Rail Trail, a converted railway corridor in the Brisbane Valley. The 8km starts at Vernor and the 3km at Lowood, both setting off at 9am and finishing together at Fernvale Sports Park. Run or walk, and the event shares the day with 20km and 44km rides.

About

Somerset Rail Trail Classic follows a section of the Brisbane Valley Rail Trail at Fernvale, taking in rolling country and riverside trail along the way. There are 3km and 8km run/walk options for all fitness levels.
