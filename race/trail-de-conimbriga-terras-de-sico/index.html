About

Explore the ancient ruins of Conimbriga and the stunning Sicó region at the Trail De Conimbriga Terras De Sico. This challenging trail running event takes participants through a picturesque landscape of rolling hills, lush forests, and historic sites, offering a unique and unforgettable running experience.
