Course

A half marathon on roadways and paved trails, described as relatively flat. The route passes Cape Girardeau landmarks including the Bill Emerson Bridge, the Common Pleas Courthouse and Old St. Vincent's Church, and finishes at the riverfront by Century Casino. The start is listed at 777 North Main Street.

About

The Muddy River Half Marathon is a half marathon in Cape Girardeau, Missouri, run on a relatively flat course of roadways and paved trails. The route passes downtown landmarks and finishes at the Mississippi riverfront. In-person, relay and virtual entries are offered.
