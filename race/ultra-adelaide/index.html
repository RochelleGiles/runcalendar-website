Course

Point-to-point route running from the Adelaide Hills down into the city, finishing beside Adelaide Oval at Pinky Flat. The 100 mile course has ten manned full-service aid stations, including Rocky Creek, Mylor, Mt Lofty, Cleland, Norton Summit, Montacute and Gorge Road. Shorter distances use sections of the same route.

About

Ultra Adelaide is a trail ultramarathon running from the Adelaide Hills into the city and finishing beside Adelaide Oval. Distances range from 21km and 34km through 50km and 100km to a 162km 100-mile event, plus a 100km teams relay. The longest course is supported by ten manned aid stations.
