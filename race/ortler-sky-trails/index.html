About

Ortler Sky Trails is partly a fundraiser for research on Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis. The event offers a variety of skyrunning challenges around the Ortler mountain, including a double marathon, a marathon, a glacier trail, and a vertical race. Runners can expect a high-altitude experience with stunning views, glacier crossings, and exposed mountain trails.
