Course

All races start near Shelter #1 at Holston River Park in Knoxville at 9:00 a.m. The 5K and 10K run a 0.78 mile out-and-back, twice and four times respectively, while the half marathon repeats a separate 1.09 mile out-and-back six times. A three and a half hour time limit applies.

About

The Luau 5K, 10K, & 13.1M is a festive running event held at Holston River Park in Knoxville, TN, featuring out-and-back courses with multiple loops for each distance. All finishers receive a themed medal, and costumes are encouraged in a fun, luau-themed atmosphere.
