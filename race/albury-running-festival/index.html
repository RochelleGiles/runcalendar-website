Course

Start and finish at Hovell Tree Park, South Albury. The organiser describes a fully pathed, undulating course on the banks of the Murray River. Water stations carry water and Tailwind Nutrition: eight on the half marathon (2, 4.3, 6, 8.9, 12.3, 14.6, 16.5 and 19.2 km), four on the 10 km and two on the 5 km.

About

The Albury Running Festival is run on the banks of the Murray River in South Albury, New South Wales, starting and finishing at Hovell Tree Park. The programme offers a half marathon, 10 km, 5 km and a 2 km family dash on a fully pathed, undulating course.
