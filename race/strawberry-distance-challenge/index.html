About

The Strawberry Distance Challenge is a 5K and 10K run at the Walden Lake Polo Fields in Plant City, Florida, with a 1-mile option and a 5K ruck. It raises funds for The Shade Tree and local youth organisations. Finishers are given fresh strawberries, bananas and water.
