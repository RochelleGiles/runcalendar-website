Course

Begins and finishes at the Muny Festival Grounds in Forest Park, using an open-start format with staggered start times. The course has been modified for 2026 and updated maps are still to be published. Now in its 47th year, it is the region's longest-running half marathon.

About

The Saint Louis Half Marathon and 5K runs through Forest Park in St. Louis, one of the largest urban parks in the United States and home to the St. Louis Zoo, Art Museum, and Science Center. It is organized by the St. Louis Track Club.
