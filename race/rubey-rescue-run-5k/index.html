About

Join us on Sunday, October 4 for a Halloween themed 5K run or walk beginning at Koch park and winding through the Marolt foot path and open space with your furry friends, or without! This is a timed race with cash prizes for top 3 female and male finishers. All net proceeds benefit the Friends of the Aspen Animal Shelter. Please have your pets come in costume! There will be prizes for best costumes and raffle prizes, food trucks, etc.
