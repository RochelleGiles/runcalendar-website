Course

Starts from the retail hub of Central Park in Fredericksburg, runs through the downtown shops and restaurants and climbs Hospital Hill to the finish. A single half marathon distance, with an Esprit de Corps Challenge pairing it with the Saturday Semper Fred 5K. Runners must hold 17:30 per mile.

About

The Marine Corps Historic Half is a half marathon and 5K held each May in Fredericksburg, Virginia. The course winds through the city's historic downtown, home to Civil War-era buildings and monuments. Marines and volunteers support runners along the route.
