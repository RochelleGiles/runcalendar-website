Course

Trail courses from Mésigny in Haute-Savoie, crossing the countryside of Sallenôves, Chilly, Sillingy, Contamine-Sarzin and La Balme-de-Sillingy. Three routes are offered: 27 km with 1000 m of climb, 17 km with 540 m and 11 km with 330 m, alongside a 9 km walk and free children's races. Water stations are placed every two kilometres, with starts staggered from 8:30am for the 27 km to 10:20am for the 11 km.

About

Mésy'Trail is a trail-running event in Mésigny, Haute-Savoie, with three routes: the 27km Trail de Chamaloup, a 17km and an 11km, through the foothills of this Alpine region.
