About

The Sunset Series: Princes Park is a unique running event that takes place in the heart of Melbourne, Australia. Runners will enjoy a scenic course through the picturesque Princes Park, with stunning views of the city skyline and the setting sun. The event is known for its lively atmosphere and community spirit, making it a must-do for runners of all levels.
