Course

A road festival starting at Jezzine Barracks on The Strand and looping through Townsville's CBD and waterfront. The marathon, half marathon and 10km share sections of open road with traffic; the 5km and 2.5km courses are traffic free. Drink stations sit at roughly 5km intervals with water every 2.5km.

About

The McDonald's Townsville Running Festival offers a diverse range of races for runners of all abilities, from the challenging marathon to the family-friendly 2.5km Hero Run. Set in the vibrant city of Townsville, Australia, this event promises an unforgettable running experience with stunning coastal views and a lively atmosphere.
