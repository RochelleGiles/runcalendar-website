Course

The 10 miler, half marathon and 5K all run on the Virginia Capital Trail out of Richmond, the paved route that links Richmond to Williamsburg along the James River. The race is cup-less, so runners carry their own bottle for the water stations. A Mummy Mile is held alongside.

About

This 10-mile, half marathon and 5K race follows the paved Virginia Capital Trail out of Richmond along the James River, a route connecting Richmond to Williamsburg. The flat, traffic-free trail is popular with cyclists and runners year-round.
