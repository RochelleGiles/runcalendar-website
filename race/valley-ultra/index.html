Course

Runs from the Castle Hill basin through the Craigieburn Valley in inland Canterbury, passing in the shadow of Mt Cheeseman. Three options share the same alpine valley terrain: the 54 km Whole Hog, the 24 km Piglet and the 10 km Teacup.

About

Explore the rugged landscapes of New Zealand's Craigeburn Valley at the Valley Ultra, an annual running event that challenges athletes with its stunning natural terrain. Traversing through a picturesque alpine setting, this ultramarathon offers runners an unforgettable experience as they push their limits amidst the breathtaking vistas of this remote corner of the country.
