Course

Three loops of about 1.03 miles on the Seaside Heights boardwalk, starting and finishing at the Beachcomber, 100 Ocean Terrace, and heading south toward Sawmill and Stockton Avenue. Runners keep to the ocean side of the boardwalk when heading north. A 1-mile fun run runs alongside.
