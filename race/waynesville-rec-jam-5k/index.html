Course

Starts and finishes at the Waynesville Recreation Center on Vance Street, touring the town greenway. Flat and fast with roughly 60 feet of climb, and open to walkers and strollers. A one-lap fun run on the centre track runs beforehand.

About

Rec Jam 5K runs along a greenway in Waynesville, North Carolina, celebrating the 25th anniversary of the town's Parks and Recreation Center. It's a community race that benefits local parks and recreation programs in the town.
