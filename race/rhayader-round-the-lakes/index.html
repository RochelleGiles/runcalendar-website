Course

Road races of 10K and 28.5K around the Elan Valley reservoirs above Rhayader in mid Wales, with lakeside views throughout. The 2027 edition uses a revised route designed to cut out unnecessary road crossings, and the longer race is shortened from the previous 30K.

About

Rhayader Round the Lakes offers 10K and 30K road races starting from Rhayader, known as the outdoors capital of Wales, with routes heading out past the Elan Valley reservoirs in the hills of mid-Wales.
