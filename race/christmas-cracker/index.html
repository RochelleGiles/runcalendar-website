About

The Punk Panther Christmas Cracker offers three off-road trail races through the scenic Yorkshire countryside, departing from the historic town of Otley. This festive event challenges runners to conquer challenging terrain and stunning winter landscapes, making it a must-do for trail enthusiasts seeking a unique holiday running experience.
