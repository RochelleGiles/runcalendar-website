Course

A 10.3km point-to-point trail race from the top of Selworthy Beacon down to Minehead Beach, on technical trails, woodland paths and sweeping descents that call for trail shoes. A youth relay runs the same line in five legs. Water at the event hub, at each relay changeover and at the finish; runners bring their own cup.

About

A trail race from high ground above Minehead down to the sea, on the edge of Exmoor National Park. Organised by Channel Events as part of its calendar of Exmoor running events.
