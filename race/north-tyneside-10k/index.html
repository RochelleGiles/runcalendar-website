Course

A coastal point-to-point along the North Tyneside shoreline, passing the working Fish Quay, the River Tyne and Tynemouth Priory and Castle before a long stretch of coast road to the finish at St Mary's Lighthouse in Whitley Bay.

About

A 10K road race in North Shields, on the North Tyneside coast where the River Tyne meets the North Sea. Organised by North Tyneside Council, it's grown into one of the region's most popular community 10Ks.
