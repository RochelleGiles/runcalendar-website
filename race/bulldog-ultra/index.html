Course

Run entirely within Malibu Creek State Park on singletrack and fire road, with long steep climbs and ocean and canyon views. The 50K starts at 7:00am and the 25K at 8:00am from the park at 1925 Las Virgenes Road, Calabasas. Aid stations are stocked with Skratch Labs hydration; canyon temperatures can reach triple digits.

About

The Bulldog Ultra runs through Malibu Creek State Park in the Santa Monica Mountains, climbing the notorious Bulldog Motorway fire road. The 50K and 25K courses cover rugged singletrack and fire roads through canyon terrain once used as the M*A*S*H filming location.
