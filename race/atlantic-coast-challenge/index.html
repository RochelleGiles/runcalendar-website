Course

Three consecutive marathon-distance days along the South West Coast Path, from near Padstow to Land's End, and entrants may take on one, two or all three. Day one runs to Perranporth (42.2km), day two to St Ives Bay (42.2km) and day three from Lelant to Land's End (44.3km). The acorn-waymarked coast path is rugged with steep climbs and descents, with checkpoints roughly every 10km.

About

The Atlantic Coast Challenge is a scenic and rewarding 3 marathons in 3 days race in North Cornwall. Participants run or walk a total of 78.6 miles over 3 days along some of the wildest coastlines in Britain, starting near Padstow and finishing at Lands End.
