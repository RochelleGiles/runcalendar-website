Course

Point-to-point marathon across Bermuda, starting at the Royal Naval Dockyard at 7:00am and finishing at Barr's Bay Park in Hamilton. Mostly sealed island roads, with roughly 4.3 miles on the Bermuda Railway Trail through Somerset and Southampton. Runners join the half-marathon course at mile 14 past Horseshoe Bay and Warwick Long Bay.

About

The Bermuda Marathon is a must-do event for running enthusiasts, taking place on the picturesque island of Bermuda. Runners will have the chance to explore the island's stunning natural beauty as they tackle this challenging course, with breathtaking ocean views every step of the way. This event is a true test of endurance and a unique opportunity to experience the Bermuda Triangle Challenge.
