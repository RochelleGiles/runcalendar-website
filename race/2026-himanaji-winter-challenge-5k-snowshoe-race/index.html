About

The Himanaji (hi-ma-NA-ji) Winter Challenge is a 5k snowshoe race hosted by Tara Lila at the Ripco Road Park Unit in Oneida County, Wisconsin. Himana (adj.) " meaning "snowy", and " Aji (n.) " meaning "racecourse". We hope this challenge will inspire you to test yourself physically as you explore the beautiful Northwoods in the winter.
