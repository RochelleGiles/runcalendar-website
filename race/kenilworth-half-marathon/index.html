Course

A single closed-road loop starting and finishing at the clock tower in Kenilworth, running through the grounds of the 12th-century castle before heading out into the Warwickshire countryside. The course is undulating throughout. The 5K shares the route to mile 2, then branches off for its own finish.

About

The Kenilworth Half Marathon is a must-run event for experienced and aspiring runners alike. This scenic route winds through the picturesque town of Kenilworth, offering stunning views and a challenging course that will push participants to their limits. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply seeking an enjoyable running experience, the Kenilworth Half Marathon is sure to deliver an unforgettable race day.
