Course

A point-to-point 100.38-mile run through the Talladega National Forest, from Pine Glen Campground near Heflin to Lake Howard on the Sylaward Trail. The route is 83 miles of single track, 17 miles of jeep road and 2 miles of pavement, taking in Cheaha Mountain, the highest point in Alabama, with around 28,000 feet of total elevation change.

About

A point-to-point 100-mile ultramarathon through the Talladega National Forest on Alabama's Pinhoti Trail, one of the Southeast's most storied trail ultras. Runners face rugged, mountainous singletrack and significant elevation gain each November near Sylacauga.
