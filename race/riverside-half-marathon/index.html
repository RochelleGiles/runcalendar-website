About

Explore the picturesque Riverside route as you take on the challenge of the Riverside Half Marathon. This scenic race winds through the heart of an historic city, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty and cultural landmarks along the way. With a course that caters to runners of all abilities, the Riverside Half Marathon is a must-do event for any avid distance runner.
