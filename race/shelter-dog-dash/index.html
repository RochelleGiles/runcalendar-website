Course

Starts and finishes at Freestone District Park on East Juniper Avenue in Gilbert. Check-in opens at 8:00am, the 5K goes off at 9:00am and the one-mile dash at 9:10am. Dogs run with their owners; a virtual option is also offered.

About

One Love hosts the Shelter Dog Dash, a 5K and 1-Mile Run/Walk at Freestone Park in Gilbert, Arizona, now past its fifth running. The event raises funds for vulnerable dogs in Maricopa County.
