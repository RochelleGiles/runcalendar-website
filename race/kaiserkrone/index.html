About

The Kaiserkrone Trail is an epic trail running event that takes place in the stunning Wilder Kaiser mountain range in Austria. Runners can choose from five breathtaking distances, including an ULTRA, SKYRACE, MEDIUM, and SHORT course, as well as a Kids race, offering a challenge for runners of all levels. The event promises an unforgettable connection with nature and stunning alpine scenery.
