About

Patagonia Bariloche by UTMB races through the Argentine Andes around San Carlos de Bariloche, with courses circling Nahuel Huapi Lake and climbing toward peaks like Cerro Catedral and Tronador. Distances range from the 33K Nahuel Huapi to the 130K Tronador, through classic Patagonian mountain terrain.
