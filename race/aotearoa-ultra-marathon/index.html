About

The Aotearoa Ultra Marathon will be held on Banks Peninsula and Christchurch, encompassing some of the most picturesque settings of Canterbury. Starting in Little River, you will experience Pigeon Bay, Port Levy, scale Mt Herbert and experience amazing views of Akaroa and Lyttleton Harbours as you make your way to Christchurch's Halswell Quarry and the Finish.
