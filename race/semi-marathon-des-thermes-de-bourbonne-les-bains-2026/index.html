About

The Semi-Marathon des Thermes is a running event featuring multiple race formats including individual half marathon and relay options. The course consists of multiple loops through Bourbonne les Bains, with relay teams having the option to run in traditional relay format or 'Escadrille' style where runners complete portions together. The event includes youth races on the day before the main event.
