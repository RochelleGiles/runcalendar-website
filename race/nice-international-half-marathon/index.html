About

The Nice International Half Marathon is a must-run event for distance enthusiasts. Nestled in the picturesque coastal city of Nice, France, this half marathon showcases stunning Mediterranean views and a challenging course that winds through historic neighborhoods. Runners of all levels will enjoy the vibrant atmosphere and the opportunity to experience the unique charm of this iconic French Riviera destination.
