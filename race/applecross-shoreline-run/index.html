About

The McNally Applecross Shoreline Run is a West Australian Marathon Club event held along the Applecross foreshore in Perth. It offers 15km, 7.5km and 3km distances, all starting at 7:00am from Tompkins Park. Entry numbers are capped under council approval, and registrations close once the cap is reached.
