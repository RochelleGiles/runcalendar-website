About

The Ultra Sud Canigou Montagne is a running event that takes place in the heart of the Canigou Massif, a natural gem of the Catalan Pyrenees. With its unique course, the event invites runners to discover the stunning landscapes and rich heritage of this exceptional mountain region. The organizers' commitment is to provide a superb course that allows participants to fully enjoy the pleasure and performance of their run.
