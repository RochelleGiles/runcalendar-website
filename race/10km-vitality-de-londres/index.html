About

The 10km Vitality de Londres is a must-run event for any avid runner in the heart of one of the world's most iconic cities. Weave through the historic streets of London, taking in the city's stunning landmarks as you challenge yourself over the 10km distance. This popular annual race offers an unforgettable running experience for participants of all levels.
