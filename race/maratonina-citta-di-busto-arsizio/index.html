About

The Maratonina Citta Di Busto Arsizio is a popular running event in the city of Busto Arsizio, Italy. The race offers a challenging course that showcases the city's historic landmarks and scenic surroundings. Runners can expect a lively atmosphere and enthusiastic local support as they compete in this annual event.
