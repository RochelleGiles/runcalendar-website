Course

A timed looped event in South Mountain Reservation, South Orange, using four different trail loops; runners complete as many loops as they wish inside their time window. Terrain mixes hard-packed carriage trails with technical singletrack over rocks and roots, with under 0.20 miles of pavement plus two marshalled road crossings. A single aid station sits at the start/finish and is staffed from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
