Course

All distances start and finish at North Shore Park on the downtown St. Petersburg waterfront, just north of the Vinoy. The courses are very flat, running waterfront neighbourhoods and the paved Pinellas Trail rail-trail, with an out-and-back section on the marathon. Gatorade and water at numerous aid stations, plus fresh oranges on the marathon and half courses.

About

The St. Petersburg Distance Classic offers a marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K from North Shore Park on the downtown waterfront. The courses are flat, taking in waterfront neighbourhoods, historic streets and the paved Pinellas Trail. Live musicians and spectators line parts of the route.
