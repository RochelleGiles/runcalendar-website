Course

Hourly 5K loops at Elm Creek Park in Maple Grove, running from 7am to 5pm: complete one loop every hour for ten hours to reach 50K, or stop at any point. Mass start at 7am, then hourly waves from the start/finish, where the single aid station offers water, ice and snacks.

About

The Baby Backyard 5K-50K ULTRA is a community fun run with a focus on community, philanthropy, and achieving fitness goals. All of our race registrations are donated to the Children's Minnesota Kids Cancer Fund.
