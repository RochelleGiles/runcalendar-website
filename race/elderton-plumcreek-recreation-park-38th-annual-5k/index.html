Course

A fast, slightly rolling USATF-certified 5K through the streets of Elderton, Pennsylvania, starting and finishing at E. Saltwork and N. Lytle Street beside Elderton State Bank. Splits are called at one and two miles and water is available on course. Check-in, refreshments and awards are at Plumcreek Recreation Park on Calhoun Street.

About

The Elderton Plumcreek Recreation Park 38th Annual 5K Run/Walk is a hometown tradition set in a charming small-town setting. Often described as a fast, slightly rolling course! Runners and walkers of all ages enjoy scenic streets, friendly faces, and the simple joy of a community coming together.
