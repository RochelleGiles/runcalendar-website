About

The Run In Park event takes place in the beautiful Heillecourt Park, offering runners a scenic and peaceful course through the lush greenery. This community-driven event welcomes runners of all levels, providing a fun and inclusive atmosphere for participants to enjoy the outdoors and challenge themselves.
