Course

Starts and finishes at the Neuadd Arms Hotel in Llanwrtyd Wells at 10:30am. The route is mainly off-road over challenging mountain terrain in mid Wales, with a shorter 12k option alongside the half marathon. It is run as a cupless event, so runners carry their own reusable container.

About

The Ron Skilton Memorial Half Marathon is a trail race through the Cambrian Mountains around Llanwrtyd Wells in mid Wales, held in memory of local runner Ron Skilton and raising funds for motor neurone disease research.
