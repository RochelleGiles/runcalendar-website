Course

All distances start and finish at Windsor Reserve in Devonport, running through the suburb's heritage streets and along the waterfront past its beaches, with views across the harbour. Aid stations are spaced roughly every 3–5km and toilets are available on course.

About

The Devonport Half Marathon is a scenic running event located in the picturesque coastal suburb of Devonport, Auckland. Runners enjoy stunning views of the harbor and surrounding landscapes while challenging themselves on a well-organized course. The event attracts both local and international runners seeking a memorable running experience in New Zealand.
