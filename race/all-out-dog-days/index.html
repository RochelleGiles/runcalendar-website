About

Get ready for a fantastic mid-season challenge and a sun-soaked adventure at our 14th Annual Dog Days 5K, 10K, and half marathon, proudly benefiting Lifeline Puppy Rescue! Held in a central location convenient to both Denver and Boulder, our courses go along the scenic Big Dry Creek Trail as it winds northeast. You'll enjoy breathtaking views of the Flatirons on the horizon while you run.
