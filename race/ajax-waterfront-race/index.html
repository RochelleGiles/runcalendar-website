About

A 5 km, a 15 km and a 20 km challenge that runs both, from Rotary Park on Lake Driveway West along the Ajax waterfront trail on the shore of Lake Ontario, with a 1 km kids' run. In its second year in 2026, supporting Community Living Ajax Pickering and Whitby.
