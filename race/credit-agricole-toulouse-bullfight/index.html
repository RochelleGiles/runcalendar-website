About

The Credit Agricole Toulouse Bullfight is a unique running event that takes place in the heart of Toulouse, France. Participants have the opportunity to race through the historic streets and plazas of this vibrant city, with the iconic Toulouse Cathedral and the Garonne River as the stunning backdrops. This event offers a one-of-a-kind experience for runners seeking an exciting challenge in a picturesque setting.
