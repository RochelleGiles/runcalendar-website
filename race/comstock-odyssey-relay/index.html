Course

A roughly 30-mile relay covering legs 31 to 36 of the full Reno-Tahoe Odyssey course. Teams of up to six start in Virginia City at Exchange Point 30 and run through to the Finish Line Festival in Reno; two- and three-person teams may also enter.

About

The Comstock Odyssey Relay is a 30-mile team relay through Virginia City, Nevada, run as part of the Reno-Tahoe Odyssey series. Teams cover ground on the historic Comstock Lode, the 19th-century silver-mining district between Reno and Lake Tahoe.
