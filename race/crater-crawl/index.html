Course

A rough, strenuous desert course following a jeep road to the top of a shield volcano and around the Crater Rings, with steep ups and downs and exposure to desert heat. The 10K circles the volcanic pit craters; a shorter 2.9-mile out-and-back reaches the East Crater viewpoint. Three hydration stations along the way.

About

The Crater Crawl, also called Run Around the Rings, is a trail race in Mountain Home, Idaho, hosted by the Idaho Outdoor Association. It offers 2.9-mile and 6.2-mile distances through the high desert of southern Idaho.
