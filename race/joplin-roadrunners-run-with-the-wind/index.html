Course

A USATF-certified 25K point-to-point between Sarcoxie High School and Carthage High School. The direction is set by the wind and announced the day before, so either school can be the start. Runners park at the finish and are bussed to the start. Relay teams split the course into 8, 2 and 5-mile legs, with several water stations en route.

About

Missouri's iconic USATF-certified 25K point-to-point road race from Carthage or Sarcoxie, depending on wind direction. Can be run solo or in relay teams of 3 with uneven legs of 8 miles, 2 miles, and 5 miles.
