Course

A point-to-point 136-mile race from the Rice Lake boat launch near Whitewater heading north on the Ice Age Trail through the Southern Kettle Moraine State Forest. The route mixes groomed wide trail, singletrack, bike paths and road connectors, gaining about 11,148 feet, with nine stocked aid stations and a 55-hour limit. Friday 11:00am start.

About

Echo 136 is a 136 mile trail event in Whitewater, Wisconsin, held on the Ice Age State Trail through the Southern Unit of the Kettle Moraine State Forest. The course follows a mix of wide groomed trails, single track, bike paths and road connectors and is set up to be completed within a 55 hour time limit.
