About

Explore the scenic trails of Pécharmant at the annual Trail de Pécharmant. This captivating event takes runners through the picturesque Périgord region, known for its rolling hills, lush forests, and charming villages. With a challenging course and stunning natural surroundings, the Trail de Pécharmant offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
