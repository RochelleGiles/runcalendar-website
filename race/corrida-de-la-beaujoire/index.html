About

The Corrida de la Beaujoire is an annual running event organized by the ALPAC in Nantes, France since 1983. The event offers a unique course around and within the Beaujoire Stadium, attracting a large and enthusiastic crowd. It is a community event that also supports a charitable cause, with a portion of the registration fees being donated to a charitable organization.
