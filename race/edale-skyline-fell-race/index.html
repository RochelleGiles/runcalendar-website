Course

A Category A long fell race on the high, exposed ridges around Edale, run on an unmarked course over open moorland. Registration and the 10:30 start are at Edale Village Hall. Runners must carry map and compass — GPS navigation is banned — and prove recent Category A fell racing experience to enter.

About

A classic fell race circling the ridges above Edale in the Dark Peak, taking in around 4,500 feet of climbing over 21 miles. Organised by Dark Peak Fell Runners, it's one of the toughest and most respected races on the fell-running calendar.
