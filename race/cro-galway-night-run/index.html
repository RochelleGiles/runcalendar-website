About

The Croí Galway Night Run is a beloved annual 5km walk/run along the picturesque Salthill Promenade in County Galway, Ireland. Runners and walkers of all abilities come together under the night sky to enjoy the stunning coastal views and vibrant atmosphere of this popular community event.
