Course

A flat out-and-back along Auckland's Tamaki Drive waterfront, starting and finishing at St Heliers and turning in downtown Auckland near The Cloud and the Ferry Building. The sealed, traffic-free route is closed to cars throughout. Aid stations with water and sports drink sit roughly every 2-3km.

About

New Zealand's biggest city and most famous running venue play host to the country's premier half marathon event. The ASICS Waterfront Half Marathon features a cast of thousands, a breathtaking setting and an unforgettable tour of the Eastern Bays and all the way into Downtown Auckland, featuring landmarks like The Cloud and the famous Ferry Building.
