About

The Trail Du Haut Clunysois is a running event that takes place in the beautiful region of Dompierre les Ormes. The event offers several race distances, allowing runners to explore the scenic trails and landscapes of the area. Known for its convivial atmosphere and well-organized logistics, the Trail Du Haut Clunysois is a must-do event for trail running enthusiasts.
