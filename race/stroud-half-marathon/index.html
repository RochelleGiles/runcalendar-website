Course

Road half marathon starting and finishing on Cainscross Road beside Marling School field in Stroud, running out into the Gloucestershire countryside below the Cotswold escarpment. Not flat, but regarded as a fast course. Water stations at 4.3, 9 and 11 miles, with volunteer pacers at eight target times.

About

The Stroud Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging race through the picturesque Cotswold countryside. Runners will be treated to stunning views as they tackle the hilly terrain, making this a must-do event for any half marathon enthusiast. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to enjoy the scenic route, the Stroud Half Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
