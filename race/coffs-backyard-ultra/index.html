Course

A 6.706km yard repeated on the hour, combining a 1.3km loop of the Coffs Harbour showground on bitumen with a 5.406km out-and-back on the Coffs Creek Walkway. Roughly 75% is smooth shaded gravel path, 10% wide boardwalk and 15% bitumen. Each lap climbs and drops only about 20m, so pacing stays consistent lap after lap.

About

The Coffs Backyard Ultra invites runners of all abilities to test their limits on an unknown course in the heart of Australia. This unique event challenges participants to complete laps of an undisclosed distance, continuing until only one runner remains standing. Whether you're a seasoned ultra-marathoner or new to the sport, the Coffs Backyard Ultra promises an unforgettable adventure.
