Course

All races start from Merthyr Mawr House and run the estate's trails on the South Wales coast, taking in hills, valleys, a river crossing and the Big Dipper, the highest sand dune in Wales. The half marathon and marathon extend onto clifftop paths along the Glamorgan Heritage Coast. Entries close on 5 July 2027 or when full.

About

The Rabbit Run Wales offers runners the unique opportunity to experience the private grounds of the Merthyr Mawr Estate in South Wales. This trail race through the picturesque countryside provides a challenging and scenic course for runners of all levels. Whether you're seeking a new adventure or a chance to immerse yourself in the natural beauty of Wales, the Rabbit Run is not to be missed.
