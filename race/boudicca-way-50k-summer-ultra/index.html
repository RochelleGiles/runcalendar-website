Course

Point-to-point 50km ultra following the Boudicca Way long-distance path from Hope Church in Diss to Trowse Common on the edge of Norwich. Well-marked trails with a generally good running surface, passing through historic South Norfolk villages including Saxlingham Nethergate, Shotesham and Caistor Roman Town.

About

The Boudicca Way 50K Summer Ultra is a beginner-friendly ultramarathon along the historic Boudicca Way Long Distance Path. The route winds through ancient South Norfolk villages where Boudicca, the Queen of Iceni, once ruled, passing through historic settlements and parallel to the old Roman Road.
