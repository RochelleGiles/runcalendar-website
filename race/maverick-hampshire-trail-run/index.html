Course

Three trail routes — 5km, 16km and 24km — from the Matterley Basin Grand Prix track near Winchester, out into the South Downs National Park. Fast hard-packed bridleways and flowing singletrack over undulating chalk downland with punchy climbs and a few technical descents, passing the River Itchen and Cheesefoot Head.

About

The Maverick Hampshire Trail Run takes place in the beautiful South Downs National Park, offering runners a challenging and scenic trail experience. Participants will explore trails surrounding Winchester, traversing undulating hills with panoramic views across the National Park. The route features stunning winding trails past the River Itchen and through woodland, finishing at the iconic Cheesefoot Head.
