Course

Loops of the trail system at Brown's Woods in West Des Moines, starting at 9:00 am. The 5K runs the outermost loop, the 10K two loops and the 15K three. Single and double-track dirt deep in the woods, with a hard, uneven surface, ruts, roots and slippery wooden bridges and steps.

About
