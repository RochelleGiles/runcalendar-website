About

Ultra Trail Stokely Creek winds through the wilderness near Algoma Mills in Northern Ontario. The trail event offers distances of 170.9k, 85.5k and 56.4k across rugged, scenic backcountry terrain, offering a demanding test of endurance for trail runners.
