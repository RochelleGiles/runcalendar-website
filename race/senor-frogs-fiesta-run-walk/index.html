About

The Señor Frogs Fiesta Run/Walk is a 5K at Broadway at the Beach in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, a waterfront entertainment complex on the Intracoastal Waterway. The race has a Cinco de Mayo fiesta theme with music and prizes at the finish.
