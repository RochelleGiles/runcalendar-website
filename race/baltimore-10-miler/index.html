Course

An out-and-back on the roads of north Baltimore, starting and finishing at Druid Hill Park and turning at Lake Montebello, where the path is flat and the roadways wide and newly paved. The route shares many roads with the Baltimore Marathon course and uses a wave start. Runners have three hours to finish, an 18:00 per mile pace.

About

The Kaiser Permanente Baltimore 10 Miler starts and finishes at Druid Hill Park, home of the Maryland Zoo, following wide, newly paved roads borrowed from the Baltimore Marathon route out to Lake Montebello. The race has drawn more than 40,000 finishers since it began, with wave starts and a 3-hour time limit.
