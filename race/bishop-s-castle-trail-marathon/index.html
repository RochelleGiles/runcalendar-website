Course

Starts from Montgomery Road in Bishop's Castle at 07:00 and runs the Shropshire hills, following sections of the Shropshire Way through the Nipstone and Stiperstones National Nature Reserves and up onto the Long Mynd. The 50-mile option repeats the first 26.2 miles of the marathon then adds a circular 24 miles through the Clun Valley on the Shropshire Way and Offa's Dyke Path.

About

Runningmonk's Bishops Castle Trail Marathon is a circular adventure along the stunning Shropshire hills. The course follows sections of the Shropshire Way, through to the Nipstone and Stiperstones National Nature Reserves, up onto The Long Mynd, with endless views of the surrounding areas.
