About

The Elopura Marathon is Sandakan's very own marathon, featuring full, half and 10km categories. With an exciting route through Sandakan town and its surroundings, this race will be memorable for all runners. Elopura, meaning 'beautiful city', is the old name for Sandakan and a name befitting this town on the east coast of Sabah.
