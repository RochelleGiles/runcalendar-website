About

360º The Challenge is a 265km ultra-endurance race that circles the volcanic Canary Island of La Gomera, from San Sebastián de la Gomera through the island's mountainous interior and coastline. It is one of the toughest multi-day ultra formats in the Canary Islands, testing endurance, navigation and recovery over several days on the move.
