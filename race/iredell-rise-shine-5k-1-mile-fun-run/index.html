About

The Iredell Rise & Shine 5K and 1 Mile Fun Run are held at Jennings Park in Statesville, North Carolina, organised by Iredell County Parks and Recreation. Both events start at 9am and follow the park's nature trails and paved sidewalks, and strollers are welcome.
