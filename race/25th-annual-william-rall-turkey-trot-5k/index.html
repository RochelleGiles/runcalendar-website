Course

The 5K starts and finishes in the parking lot at the side of Lindenhurst Middle School on S. Wellwood Avenue, with a free fun run setting off half an hour earlier. Parking is not available at the school itself, so runners are directed to the surrounding side streets. The race is held rain or shine.

About

Join us for the 25th Annual William Rall Turkey Trot 5K in Lindenhurst, New York. This family-friendly event features a 5K race, a free fun run, and an after-party at Sand City South.
