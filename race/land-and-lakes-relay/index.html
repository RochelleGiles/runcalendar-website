Course

A 61-mile point-to-point road relay from Warren Wilson College in East Asheville to Fonta Flora Brewery at Whippoorwill Farm near Lake James. The route runs east through Swannanoa, Black Mountain and around Lake Tomahawk, follows much of the Geyser Growl Half Marathon course into Old Fort, then passes through downtown Marion. Twelve legs of 3.3 to 7.7 miles are shared by teams of four to six, on public roads open to traffic.

About

Welcome to YEAR FOUR of the Land and Lakes Relay. The race will once start again at Warren Wilson College in East Asheville and finish at the amazing Fonta Flora Brewery-Whippoorwhill Farm in Nebo, near Lake James. Now is the time to get your team together to run and celebrate with us.
