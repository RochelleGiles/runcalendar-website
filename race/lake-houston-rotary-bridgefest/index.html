Course

A chip-timed 5K and an adaptive-friendly one-mile race, both starting and finishing at the Lake Houston Family YMCA on West Lake Houston Parkway, Kingwood. The one-mile sets off at 8:00am and the 5K at 8:30am, with parking and a shuttle from Creekwood Middle School.

About

The iconic BridgeFest event has supported the Lake Houston area community for over 35 years. It brings hundreds of community members together for entertainment and run/walks designed for all ages.
