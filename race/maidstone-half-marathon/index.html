Course

A measured and licensed half marathon starting and finishing in Mote Park, Maidstone. The route opens steadily through the park, climbs gently to the Pilgrims Way and out onto quiet lanes, then returns on a confident descent to the finish back in the park. Undulating rather than flat, with a 3.5-hour cutoff. No buggies or dogs.

About

The Maidstone Half Marathon starts and finishes in Mote Park before heading out into the Kent Downs. Organised by Maidstone Harriers, the course was redesigned to take in more of the county's hilly countryside.
