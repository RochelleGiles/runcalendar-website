About

Sri Lanka, known as the 'Pearl of the Indian Ocean', is fast developing into an ideal destination for recreational activities. Its tropical weather, white sandy beaches, warm waters of the Indian Ocean, numerous inland waterways, picturesque landscapes, and challenging natural terrain all contribute to making it one of the finest recreational destinations in Asia. The Colombo Marathon is the latest addition to the long list of activities that can be enjoyed on the island.
