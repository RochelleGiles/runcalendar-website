Course

A coastal trail event based at Eastbourne, running the chalk cliffs of the Seven Sisters above the English Channel. Four distances share chalk and forest trails over rolling downland, with heavy climbing — 1,276 ft of ascent over the 10K rising to 5,266 ft on the ultra. Marathon and ultra carry UTMB qualification.

About

The EnduranceLife Sussex trail runs explore the dramatic cliff-ridden coastline on a mix of forest and chalk trails. Starting from Helen Garden in Eastbourne, the courses showcase the stunning coast that shoulders the towns of Eastbourne and Seaford.
