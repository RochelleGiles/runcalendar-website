Course

Run in and around Dolamore Park near Gore in Southland, on undulating bush tracks and across neighbouring farmland. The 5 km follows the Whiskey Creek Track, an undulating and challenging bush track, and the 2 km takes a gently undulating bush track through the park; a 15 km run and several mountain bike distances share the same country.

About

The annual MLT Moonshine Trail in New Zealand's scenic Southland region is a premier mountain bike and off-road running event, challenging athletes with stunning landscapes and a unique nighttime atmosphere under the glow of the moon. Runners can explore the rugged backcountry trails and experience the thrill of this one-of-a-kind adventure race.
