About

Explore the stunning Monts de Flandres region at the Nord Trail Monts de Flandres (NTMF) event. This challenging trail run takes runners through the picturesque hills and forests of this lesser-known corner of northern France, offering breathtaking views and a true test of endurance. Whether you're an experienced trail enthusiast or new to the sport, the NTMF promises an unforgettable running adventure.
