About

The Course Des Lumieres is a unique running event that takes place in the beautiful city of Bordeaux, France. The event offers participants the opportunity to experience the city's stunning architecture and landmarks while running through the streets at night, illuminated by the city's lights. The course highlights the city's historic charm and provides a memorable atmosphere for runners of all levels.
