About

Beaujo Run'in Rock is a themed fun run held during the Beaujofest music festival in Saint-Georges-de-Reneins, in the heart of the Beaujolais wine region. Runners choose from short distances around 6.6km, 1.3km or 700m, with a lively festival atmosphere and live rock music at the finish.
