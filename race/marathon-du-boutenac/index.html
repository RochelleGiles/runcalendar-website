About

Nestled in the picturesque Corbières region, the Marathon du Boutenac offers runners a truly unique experience. Winding through stunning vineyards and rolling hills, this event showcases the breathtaking natural beauty of the local landscape. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, the Marathon du Boutenac promises an unforgettable challenge and an opportunity to explore one of France's most captivating wine-producing areas.
