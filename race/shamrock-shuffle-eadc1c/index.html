Course

Three races through Harmony, Pennsylvania: a hilly half marathon that climbs Textor Hill Road, a rolling 5K on the town streets, and a one-mile out-and-back. All start and finish on Main Street. Runners can enter one race, two, or take on all three as a triple challenge.

About

Welcome to the Shamrock Shuffle, an annual event dedicated to supporting the Rotary Club of Zelienople. Choose from three exciting race options: the challenging 13.1-mile half marathon, the family-friendly 5K, or the delightful 1-mile race. For the ultimate challenge, embrace the Triple Challenge and conquer all three races, totaling 16.1 miles of thrilling running.
