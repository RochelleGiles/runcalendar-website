Course

Point-to-point with staggered starts all finishing at Pelican Park. The marathon leaves Bethanga Hotel at 8:00am, the half marathon Bullioh at 9:30am and the 10km Old Tallangatta Lookout at 10:30am, with the 3km run starting at Pelican Park. Marathon aid stations sit at roughly 7, 14, 23, 30 and 37km.

About

Explore the stunning High Country Rail Trail as you make your way from Koetong to Tallangatta in the Pub to Pelican Marathon. This scenic Australian event takes runners through the beautiful landscapes of Victoria, offering a unique challenge for both seasoned marathoners and those seeking a memorable running experience.
