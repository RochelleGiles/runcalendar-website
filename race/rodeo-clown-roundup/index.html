Course

Starts and finishes at Karbach Brewing Company, 2032 Karbach Street, Houston, on a flat chip-timed course. The 10K runs two loops and the 5K one loop, both starting at 9:00 AM, with a Kids and Family 1K at 10:45 AM. Surface and aid stations are not stated.

About

The Rodeo Clown Roundup is a fun-filled, chip-timed 5K and 10K race at Karbach Brewing Company to kick off Houston's rodeo season. Participants receive a finisher medal, event t-shirt, and free beer (21+), with post-race activities like Singo Bingo and brunch.
