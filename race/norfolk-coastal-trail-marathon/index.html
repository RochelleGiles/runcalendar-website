Course

Point-to-point races along the waymarked Norfolk Coast Path, both finishing at Morston. The marathon starts at Holme-next-the-Sea and the half at Burnham Overy Staithe, on mainly off-road ground, with refreshment checkpoints carrying water, squash and snacks. Distances are approximate as the route is not UKA-measured; entries cap at 200 marathon and 300 half.

About

A trail marathon and half marathon along the Norfolk Coast Path, starting from the harbour town of Wells-next-the-Sea. The route follows salt marsh, dunes and coastal path with the North Sea alongside.
