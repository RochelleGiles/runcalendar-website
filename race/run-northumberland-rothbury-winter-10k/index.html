Course

A certified, measured road 10K on undulating Northumberland lanes. The race starts at the middle school in Rothbury with a downhill first mile, runs out along Whitton Bank Road and Carterside Road, turns back before Little Tosson and finishes at Riverside Park.

About

A winter 10K road race starting in the Northumberland market town of Rothbury, on the River Coquet at the edge of the Cheviot Hills. The route takes in the surrounding countryside before returning to the town for a festive finish.
