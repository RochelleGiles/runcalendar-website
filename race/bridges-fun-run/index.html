Course

A fun run starting and finishing at Barrack Square in central Perth, crossing several of the city's bridges with views over the Swan River and the city skyline. A 10km, a 5km and a 300m kids dash are offered; online entries close at midday on the Thursday before race day unless the event sells out first.

About

The Bridges Fun Run in Perth, Australia is an annual 5km and 10km event organized by the West Australian Marathon Club. Participants enjoy scenic views as they traverse the city's iconic bridges, making this a must-do for runners seeking a unique and scenic running experience.
