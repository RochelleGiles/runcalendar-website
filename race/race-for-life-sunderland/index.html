Course

The 10K is a double lap of the 5K course through Herrington Country Park on Wearside, parkland reclaimed from a former colliery spoil heap. The course is flat and closed to traffic, and may be run, jogged or walked.

About

Cancer Research UK's Race for Life 10K runs through Herrington Country Park, Wearside parkland reclaimed from a former colliery spoil heap. The flat, traffic-free course crosses open grassland and lakes, raising funds for cancer research.
