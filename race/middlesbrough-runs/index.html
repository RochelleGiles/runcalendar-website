Course

A flat and fast traffic-free route through Middlesbrough town centre on closed roads, taking in the town's landmarks and passing the Riverside stadium. Water stations are positioned on the route and again at the finish, with chip timing, marshalled junctions and a baggage drop.

About

The Middlesbrough Runs in the United Kingdom offer two exciting races - the Middlesbrough 10K (Tees Pride 10K) and the Ali Brownlee 5. Runners can experience the vibrant energy of this historic northern town while challenging themselves on scenic routes that showcase the best of the local area.
