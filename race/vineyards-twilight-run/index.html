Course

A twilight run through the King Valley vineyards at Milawa, taking in three wineries and finishing at the Brown Brothers cellar door. The half marathon runs Brown Brothers to Sam Miranda via John Gehrig and back, the 10km comes from Sam Miranda to Brown Brothers, and the 5km goes out to Milawa Cheese and returns. The half starts at 5:45pm and the 10km and 5km at 6:00pm.

About

Explore the picturesque vineyards of Milawa, Victoria, as you take on the Vineyards Twilight Run. Hosted by the renowned Brown Brothers Winery, this unique running festival offers runners a chance to experience the serene beauty of the Australian countryside at dusk. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a casual runner, this event promises an unforgettable evening of camaraderie, stunning scenery, and a celebration of the great outdoors.
