Course

A full or half lap of the historic Cambridge boundary, mixing roads, footpaths and trails through parts of the city runners rarely see. Around 30 percent of both courses is off-road and there are several reasonably sized hills. Both start at the University of Cambridge Sports Centre; the half finishes on Coldham's Common and the marathon returns to the Sports Centre. Five aid stations serve drinks and food.

Full Marathon42.2 km

About

Explore the historic boundaries of Cambridge on the Cambridge Boundary Run. This marathon and half marathon event takes runners on a scenic tour through the city's picturesque streets and surrounding countryside. With a unique course showcasing the vibrant culture and natural beauty of this beloved university town, the Cambridge Boundary Run is a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable challenge.
