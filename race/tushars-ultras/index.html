Course

High-altitude singletrack in the Tushar Mountains, starting and finishing at Eagle Point Resort's Skyline Lodge above Beaver at 10,500 ft. Courses hold between 9,500 and 10,500 ft with peaks over 11,500 ft, crossing pine forest, alpine meadow, avalanche chutes and exposed ridge. Climbing ranges from 3,981 ft for the half to 16,392 ft for the 100K.

About

A mountain ultramarathon series in the Tushar Mountains near Beaver, Utah, offering 100K, 70K, marathon, and half marathon distances across high-altitude volcanic terrain, hosted by Aravaipa Running.
