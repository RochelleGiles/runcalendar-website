Course

Trail racing on Orcas Island in the San Juans, through old-growth forest with long climbs and views over the surrounding islands and Puget Sound. The 50K runs on the Saturday and the 25K on the Sunday, with a Double option for those taking on both. Reaching the start requires a ferry to the island.

About

The Orcas Island 50K is a trail ultramarathon through Moran State Park on Orcas Island in Washington's San Juan Islands, climbing to Mount Constitution, the archipelago's highest point, with lake and forest views along the way.
