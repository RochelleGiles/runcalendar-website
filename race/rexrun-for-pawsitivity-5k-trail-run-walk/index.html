Course

A trail 5K starting at 7am from the Arapahoe County Fairgrounds in Aurora. The route runs over natural grassland, through gently flowing creek beds and along dirt trails, with native wildlife often visible along the way.

About

At the heart of the 4th Annual RexRun for PAWSitivity is a powerful purpose: to inspire hope, promote mental well-being in children, and strengthen the bond between our community and the K9s and Officers who protect it.
