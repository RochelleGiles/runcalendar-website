Course

A 5K and 10K based at Little Elm Park on the shore of Lewisville Lake north of Dallas, with a 1-mile run and kids' dash alongside. Courses run through the park and adjoining shared-use paths, which stay open to other park users during the race. A published 5K course map is available.

About

Shake off the winter chill and welcome the sunshine with the Spring Fling Race Series — the hottest 5K / 10K event of the season! Lace up, grab your crew, and join us for a morning full of music, medals, and Bigfoot magic. From our littlest Yetis in the Kids Dash to seasoned runners chasing PRs, there's something for everyone!
