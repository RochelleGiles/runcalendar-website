Course

Sealed city paths starting on Main Street at QUT, running through the QUT grounds and over the Goodwill Bridge to South Bank. The 3km and 10km retrace the same route back to finish at Queensland Parliament House. The 110km is run overnight as repeated 5km loops that also take in the paths around the Brisbane City Botanic Gardens.

About

The Darkness to Daylight Challenge is a unique running event that takes participants on a journey from darkness to daylight. Held in Australia, this event celebrates the resilience and strength of the human spirit, as runners push through the challenges of the night to emerge into the light of a new day. The course highlights the natural beauty of the local landscape, offering a scenic and inspiring experience for all who take part.
