About

Celebrate love, friendship, and community at the first-ever CBH Hearts Across the Valley Love Run! Hosted by CBH Homes and the Treasure Valley YMCA, this festive event invites runners and walkers to enjoy a scenic 1-mile or 5K course along the Boise Greenbelt. Whether you're racing with your sweetheart, your family, or flying solo, this run is all about spreading joy and heart-healthy fun.
