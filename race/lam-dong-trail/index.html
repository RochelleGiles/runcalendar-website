About

Lam Dong Trail is not just a running event, but a journey that connects people with nature and themselves. Each step on the trail is a reminder of the vibrant life force of the primeval forest - a strength that, if understood, loved, and explored, will reveal the inner power within you. It is a place where people find peace, energy, and inspiration amidst the pristine natural surroundings.
