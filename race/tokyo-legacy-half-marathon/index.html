Course

Starts and finishes on the track at the National Stadium in central Tokyo. The single half marathon loop runs out through Fukumachi, Suidobashi, Jimbocho, Kanda and Nihonbashi with two turnaround points before returning to the stadium. The road course is certified by World Athletics and the JAAF. Wheelchair athletes start at 8:00am and the first runners' wave at 8:05am.

About

The Tokyo Legacy Half Marathon starts and finishes at the Japan National Stadium, the main venue of the Tokyo Olympic and Paralympic Games, and follows the Tokyo Paralympic marathon course through central Tokyo. Now past its 4th running, it fields elite, semi-elite, and para-athlete categories alongside the general entry field.
