Course

Road race festival based at Promised Land State Park in the Pocono Mountains, run entirely on paved asphalt roads through the surrounding state forest. Marathon and half marathon start at 7:30am from the Upper Lake overflow car park, with 5K and 10K following. The marathon is a certified Boston qualifier.
