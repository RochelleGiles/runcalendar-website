Course

A scenic four-mile loop following the Musconetcong River past streams, pastures and historic buildings. The course is mostly flat and open to both runners and walkers, with walkers starting ahead of the runners.

About

Annual fundraiser featuring a scenic 4-mile loop course along the Musconetcong River that runners and walkers can complete. Funds support the Musconetcong Watershed Association's education, River Watcher program, hiking trips, and river protection initiatives.
