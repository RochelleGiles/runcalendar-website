Course

The course runs through Te Hiku Forest, a 17,000-hectare pine plantation established to stabilise the coastal sand dunes. Widely spaced pines and an open forest floor give a forgiving surface, cushioned by pine needles underfoot. Races start on West Coast Road at Waipapakauri.

About

Te Hiku Forest is a 17,000-hectare pine forest with an unusual, evenly spaced landscape that creates a magical running atmosphere. Runners can choose to compete or enjoy a leisurely run with friends and family.
