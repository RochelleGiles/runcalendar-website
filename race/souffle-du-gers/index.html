About

This half marathon starts in Auch, Gers, with the 10km departing separately from the hamlet of Boucagnères. Both distances are FFA-homologated and count toward French Championship qualification, run on a mostly flat, asphalt course. A timed 10.5km Nordic walk also takes place on natural trails.
