About

Choose from the 5K, 1 Mile Fun Run/Walk, or by taking a chilling plunge into the Atlantic Ocean. Race participants are not required to plunge but are encouraged to heat up with the run/walk and cool down with the Polar Plunge – because some people are just worth freezing for! Live music, vendors, food trucks and games make this a one-of-a-kind special event not to be missed!
