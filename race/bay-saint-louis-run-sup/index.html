Course

An out-and-back along the beach sidewalk from the Washington Street Pavilion in Bay Saint Louis, paired with a one-mile stand-up paddle that heads south parallel to the shoreline and returns. Entrants may run the 5K alone, do both legs, or split them as a two-person relay. Paddlers bring their own board.

About

Join us for a one-of-a-kind coastal race in beautiful Bay Saint Louis, Mississippi—ranked in the Top 5 of America's Best Coastal Small Towns by USA Today! Now in its fourth year, the Bay Saint Louis Run+SUP combines a 5K run along the scenic beach sidewalk with a 1-mile stand-up paddleboard (SUP) race.
