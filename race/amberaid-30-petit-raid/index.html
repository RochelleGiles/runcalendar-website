Course

A figure-of-eight trail route in the Amber Valley, Derbyshire, starting and finishing at Ashover Village Hall near the eastern edge of the Peak District. The full Amberaid covers 31 miles with a nine-hour limit over punchy climbs, woodland trail and open views; the Petit Raid runs the 20-mile Northern Loop.

About

A 31-mile trail run through the scenic Amber Valley in Derbyshire, near the eastern edge of the Peak District. The route follows a figure-of-8 path featuring punchy climbs, engaging descents, expansive views and pleasant woodland trails. The event offers two distance options with the Petit Raid covering the Northern Loop, making it one of the most promising small ultra trail races in England.
