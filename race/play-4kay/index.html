Course

The 5K run/walk begins and ends at Monarch Stadium on the Methodist University campus in Fayetteville, setting off at 9:00 am with registration from 8:00 am. The organiser describes it as a fun run rather than a qualifying race and publishes no route or surface detail.

About

The women's basketball program, in partnership with the Women's Basketball Coaches Association, hosts the annual Breast Cancer Awareness "Play 4Kay" 5K in Fayetteville, North Carolina, to benefit the Cape Fear Valley Health Foundation's Friends of the Cancer Center and the Kay Yow Breast Cancer Fund.
