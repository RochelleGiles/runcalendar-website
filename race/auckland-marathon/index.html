Course

Point-to-point from King Edward Parade in Devonport to Victoria Park in central Auckland, crossing the Auckland Harbour Bridge. Rolling terrain through the first half gives way to a flat, fast second half with two small bridge rises. Checkpoints at Smales Farm, Onewa Road, St Heliers, Mission Bay, Okahu Bay and Quay Street.
