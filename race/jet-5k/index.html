About

The JETÉ 5K is a fundraiser run benefiting the students of the USF School of Theatre and Dance by supporting performance, production costs, and student travel. It is a family-friendly event held at the USF Tampa Campus Arts Complex featuring tutu-wearing participants, a kids' 1-mile boogie, and awards.
