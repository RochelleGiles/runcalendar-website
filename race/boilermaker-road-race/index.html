Course

A 9.3 mile point-to-point on closed Utica streets, starting on Culver Avenue near Beechgrove Place and finishing on Court Street at Varick Street outside the Matt Brewery. The rolling, USATF-certified road course is supported by 19 water stations and 34 aid points. A 5K, wheelchair 15K and kids' races run the same weekend.

About

The Boilermaker is a 15K road race through the streets of Utica, New York, named for the city's blue-collar factory workers. Founded in 1978, it has grown into one of the largest 15Ks in the country, finishing with a legendary post-race party at the F.X. Matt Brewery.
