Course

Point-to-point 10K through Sproul State Forest starting at the Western Clinton Sportsmen's Club. The route climbs roughly 3 miles to the top of McCloskey Mountain then drops about 3.7 miles to the finish. Technical single-track with stream crossings. A shorter 5K shares the venue.

About

The Sproul 10k is a challenging trail race through the mountains of Sproul State Forest, featuring 3 miles of uphill climbing to the top of McCloskey Mountain followed by a technical descent. Organized by PA Trail Dogs, it is touted as Pennsylvania's toughest 10k.
