About

Celebrate The Healing Place of New Hanover County's anniversary by run/walking our 5K on Saturday, February 7th. The Trudge commemorates the 3 miles that our clients walk every day in their recovery journey. After your 5K, stay for the big anniversary celebration which includes free food (brunch), community vendors, and more. Brunch includes fruit, yogurt, breakfast sandwiches, and coffee.
