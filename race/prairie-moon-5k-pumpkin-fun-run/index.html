Course

An out and back from Prairie Moon Waldorf School, 1853 E 1600 Rd, Lawrence, running the gravel 1600 Road in front of the school out to the Mud Creek levee trail and returning. Sturdy-tyred strollers are allowed and the school grounds can be muddy.

About

A fall-themed 5K fun run in Lawrence, Kansas, hosted by the Prairie Moon Waldorf School. The community event embraces a pumpkin and autumn theme, with proceeds supporting the school's programs.
