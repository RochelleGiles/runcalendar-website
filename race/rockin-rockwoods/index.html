Course

Out-and-back on 95% singletrack through Greensfelder County Park, taking in Rockwoods Range and Rockwoods Reservation west of St Louis via the Green Rock, DeClue, Fox Run, Eagle Valley and Deer Run trails. Surfaces range from hard-packed dirt to loose, uneven rock, with roots and creek crossings on steep, technical ground.

About

Rockin' Rockwoods is a trail race held at Rockwoods Reservation near Wildwood, Missouri, a Missouri Department of Conservation area known for rugged, rocky terrain. Runners choose between a 53K ultra and a half marathon distance.
