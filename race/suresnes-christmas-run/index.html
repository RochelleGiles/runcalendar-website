About

Prepare for the end-of-year festivities and do a good deed by running for the Telethon. Sign up for the 5km and 2km races! The event features a short but beautiful 5km course through Suresnes as the city gets ready for the holiday celebrations. There will be marching bands and athletic workshops to enjoy along the way, and participants are encouraged to come in costume (Yeti, Grinch, Santa Claus, etc.) to add some spice to the day.
