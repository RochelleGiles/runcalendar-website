Course

An out-and-back on the paved Eaton Trail, starting and finishing at the Mt View Shelter in Woodward Park, Fresno. The route is mostly flat and is billed as a fast course. A 12K, 5K, two-mile walk and kids run are offered.

About

Reyes Running events present this unique event at Woodward Park in Fresno, featuring a course to the famous Burrito Hill. Participants enjoy 12K, 5K run/walk, 2 mile walk, kids races, burritos, hot chocolate, medals, and awards at the finish.
