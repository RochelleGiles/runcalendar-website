Course

Point-to-point across the Motatapu, Soho and Glencoe high-country stations. The marathon starts at Motatapu Station near Glendhu Bay on Lake Wanaka, reached by mandatory bus, and finishes at Wilcox Green in Arrowtown. Mostly firm 4WD farm track with gravel and grass and numerous stream crossings; the last 6 km follows the Macetown Track above the Arrow River. Four self-service aid stations.

About

The Motatapu is an iconic trail running event in the stunning landscapes of New Zealand's Southern Alps. Traversing the Motatapu, Soho and Glencoe Stations between the adventure towns of Wanaka and Queenstown, this challenging course offers runners a truly unforgettable experience amidst breathtaking mountain vistas.
