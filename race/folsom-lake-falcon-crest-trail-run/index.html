Course

Starts at the Falcon Crest car park / Monte Vista Trailhead in El Dorado Hills, on dirt, rock and single-track with as little pavement as possible over rolling ups and downs. The 5K is a counter-clockwise loop; the 10K runs it then reverses it; the 8 mile extends along Brown's Ravine Trail to a turnaround at New York Creek; the half continues to Fitch Way. All start at 8:00am with a four hour cutoff.

About

These trails are one of my favorite places to train on and visit. This race is run in the beautiful, awesome foothills of El Dorado Hills. This area has incredible oak trees, green grass and is loaded with single track trails paralleling Folsom Lake. The trails are fast to run on and have some technical sections too—lots of fun. Some shaded areas and lots of exposure as well.
