Course

A flat community run based at Rancho Simi Community Park in Simi Valley, starting beside the tennis courts. The 5K, 10K and half marathon all use the same park and neighbourhood roads and paths, and a Kids Fun Run is held alongside them.

About

The Fredrick Michael Gibson Walk/Run for Depression Awareness is a 5K, 10K and half marathon fundraiser in Simi Valley, California, benefiting the Free Clinic of Simi Valley and raising awareness for depression and mental health.
