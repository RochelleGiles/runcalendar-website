About

Explore the stunning scenery of Eibenstock, Germany, at the Le Marathon Des Trois Barrages D Eibenstock. This challenging race takes runners through the picturesque landscape surrounding three historic dams, offering a unique and unforgettable experience for endurance athletes. With a beautiful course and rich history, this event is a must-do for runners seeking an adventure beyond the typical road marathon.
