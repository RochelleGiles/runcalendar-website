Course

A 6 km (about 3.6 mile) trail race on the White Oak Mountain trails on the Southern Adventist University campus in Collegedale, Tennessee. The route runs on rolling, hard-packed mountain bike trails, with a steep climb early and a rolling descent over the final 2.5 miles. The field is capped at 175 runners.

About

Come join us for the 17th annual McKee Southern 6 Trail Race presented by Southern's Outdoor Adventure Program! Everyone and anyone is invited to run in the six kilometer trail race and compete for cash prizes. The event will take place on the campus' White Oak Mountain trails in Collegedale, Tennessee.
