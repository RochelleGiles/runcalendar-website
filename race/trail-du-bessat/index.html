About

Explore the scenic trails of the Bessat region at the annual Trail Du Bessat race. This challenging event takes runners through the rugged, picturesque landscapes of the Auvergne mountains, offering stunning views and an unforgettable running experience. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail Du Bessat is sure to push your limits and leave you eager to return.
