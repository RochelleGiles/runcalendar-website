About

Utacch Ultra Trail Amanecer Comechingon is one of the oldest and most challenging ultra-trail races in Argentina, crossing the Sierras Grandes mountain range of the Comechingones region. The event offers several distance options, including the iconic 100-mile race, which takes runners to the summit of Cerro Champaquí, the highest peak in the province of Córdoba.
