Course

Run on the Dawes Range section of the Boyne Burnett Inland Rail Trail, starting and finishing at the Grand Hotel in Many Peaks with Barrimoon siding as the far point. The 50k is an out-and-back taking in the rail trail tunnels; the 23k Uphill climbs to Barrimoon, the 23k Downhill shuttles to the top and returns, and the 12k Taster is a short out-and-back near the start.

About

The Boyne Valley 50 runs along the Boyne Burnett Inland Rail Trail through Queensland's Dawes Range, passing the historic Kalpowar Tunnels, with 12km, 23km and 50km distance options.
