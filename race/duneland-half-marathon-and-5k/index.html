Course

An out-and-back along the paved Prairie Duneland Urban Bike Trail, starting and finishing at Dogwood Park in Chesterton. Flat and smooth the whole way, with directional signage at the turns. The half marathon and 5K share the same trail.

About

The Duneland Half Marathon and 5K takes place in Chesterton, Indiana, gateway to Indiana Dunes National Park. Runners can choose the half marathon or 5K distance through the surrounding Duneland region.
