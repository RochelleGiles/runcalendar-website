Course

A point-to-point road marathon starting at the Wex'liem Community Building on the Lummi Nation and finishing in central Bellingham. The half marathon, 10K and 5K all start and finish at Depot Market Square on Railroad Avenue.

About

A marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K starting and finishing in downtown Bellingham, Washington, with routes along Bellingham Bay and Puget Sound. The marathon is a certified Boston Marathon qualifier, drawing pace groups aimed at BQ times.
