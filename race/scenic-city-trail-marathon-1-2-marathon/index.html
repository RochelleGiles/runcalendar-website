About

The Scenic City Trail Race is run on the trail network at Raccoon Mountain above Chattanooga and is organised by Wild Trails, the city's trail protection nonprofit. The route is largely non-technical and forgiving underfoot, which suits first-time trail runners as well as those chasing a fast time.
