Course

A point-to-point 10K on closed city streets, USATF certified. It starts in front of the Superdome, runs through downtown into the French Quarter past Jackson Square, Cafe Du Monde and the French Market, then heads down Esplanade Avenue into City Park to finish on Lelong Avenue outside the New Orleans Museum of Art. Flat and fast, with water stations and bands from miles 1 to 5.

About

The Crescent City Classic is a 10K road race through downtown New Orleans, starting near the Central Business District and finishing in City Park. Run every spring since 1979, it draws thousands of runners and walkers.
