About

Giant Summer Run is the second event in the Historic Highway Hustle Series, a three-race celebration honoring the legacy of America's Mother Road. Set along iconic Route 66, this summer race invites runners and walkers of all ages to experience the rich history, small-town charm, and community spirit that helped shape the open road.
