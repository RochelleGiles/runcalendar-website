About

Explore the stunning landscapes of Madeira Island at the Ecotrail Funchal-Madeira Island event. This captivating trail running race takes participants through the island's lush forests, rugged coastlines, and picturesque villages, offering a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of this Portuguese gem. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a nature enthusiast, the Ecotrail Funchal-Madeira Island promises an unforgettable adventure.
