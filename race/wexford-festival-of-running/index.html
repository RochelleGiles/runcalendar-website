About

The Wexford Festival of Running is a multi-distance ultramarathon event held on the grounds of Johnstown Castle, Co. Wexford. Runners choose from 10K, 25K, 50K, or 100K distances around the historic castle estate, making it one of Ireland's biggest ultra festivals.
