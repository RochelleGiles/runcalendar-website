About

The Stop the Silence 5K Walk/Run is the only national Black Breast Cancer walk/run, drawing more than 4,000 participants annually in Houston. It honors Black women fighting breast cancer and raises funds for the Sisters Network's Karen E. Jackson Breast Cancer Assistance Program.
