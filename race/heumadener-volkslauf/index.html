About

The Heumadener Volkslauf offers runners two course lengths (5.5 km and 10.3 km) plus a half marathon over 21 km. The course is a long, rather flat loop through Heumaden, Riedenberg and Kemnat, with start and finish on the sports grounds of Untere Hasenwedel in Heumaden.
