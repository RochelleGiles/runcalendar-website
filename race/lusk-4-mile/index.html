About

The Lusk 4 Mile is an annual road race organized by the Lusk Athletic Club in County Dublin, Ireland. This community-driven event offers runners a scenic 4-mile course through the charming town of Lusk, providing a perfect challenge for athletes of all levels. With a lively atmosphere and the support of the local community, the Lusk 4 Mile is a must-attend event for runners seeking an enjoyable and rewarding racing experience.
