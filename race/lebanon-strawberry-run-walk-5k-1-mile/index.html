About

The Lebanon Strawberry Run/Walk - 5k & 1 Mile is a family event during Lebanon's Annual Strawberry Festival, preceding the Grand Parade and benefiting the East Linn Christian Cross Country and Track & Field teams. Both races follow the parade route through town on a flat, fast out-and-back course lined with cheering spectators.
