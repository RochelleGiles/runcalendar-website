Course

The marathon is a loop around Dyes Inlet through the Manette, Illahee, Silverdale, Chico and Brownsville neighbourhoods, starting at Evergreen Rotary Park, Bremerton. The 10K also starts at Evergreen Rotary Park; the half marathon starts at Newberry Hill Heritage Park, Silverdale. It is mostly run on open roads with rolling to intense hills. Aid stations every 2 to 4 miles.

About

The Bremerton Marathon offers a stunning and challenging course that loops around Dyes Inlet, taking you through scenic neighborhoods like Manette, Illahee, Silverdale, and Brownsville. With views of the waterfront, lush forests, and on clear days, Mount Rainier and the Olympics, this race showcases the best of the Pacific Northwest. Expect rolling hills and open roads, making it a great challenge for both seasoned runners and first-timers. Aid stations will be available every 2-4 miles. Join an unforgettable race experience!
