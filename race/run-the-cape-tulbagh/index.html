Course

Three-day staged trail run based at De Stilte near Wolseley in the Tulbagh Valley: 7km on day one, 19km on day two and 5km on day three, 31km in total. Routes cross Cape fynbos, pine forest and wheat fields, including private paths open only for the event. The field is capped at 80 and walkers are welcome.
