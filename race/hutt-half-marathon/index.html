About

The Hutt Half Marathon is one of the Hutt Valley's premier running and walking events, held in Lower Hutt on a flat, scenic out-and-back course along the Hutt River. It offers half marathon, 10 km, 5 km and 1 km Cool Kids Kilometre distances.
