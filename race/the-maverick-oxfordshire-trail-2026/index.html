Course

Starts and finishes at Stonor Park near Henley-on-Thames, with 22km, 13km and 7km routes through the historic estate and the surrounding Chiltern Hills. Fully marked woodland trail with steep pitches reaching 22%. The 13km route has one aid outpost and the 22km has two; the 7km has none.

About

The Maverick Oxfordshire Trail sets off from the 850-year-old Manor House at Stonor Park. The estate is home to one of Britain's most ancient deer herds, with fallow deer that have grazed here since medieval times, and runners may also spot the rare red kite, once saved from national extinction in the area.
