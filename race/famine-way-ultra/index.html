About

The Famine Way Ultra is an epic point-to-point ultramarathon that takes runners on a challenging 105-mile journey through the stunning Irish countryside, from Dublin to Strokestown, Co Roscommon. Tracing the historic Famine Way, this unique event offers an unforgettable adventure, showcasing Ireland's breathtaking landscapes and commemorating an important chapter in the nation's history.
