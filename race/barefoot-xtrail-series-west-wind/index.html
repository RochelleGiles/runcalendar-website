Course

Three courses run anti-clockwise from a common HQ at the Meridian West Wind farm. Terrain ranges from pine-needle tracks through forest to coastal trails and ridgeline running. The long course adds rock-hopping along the Cook Strait coast, steep ridgeline climbs, native bush and a creek valley; the medium and short courses omit the coastal section.

Long Course17.4 km
Medium Course11.4 km
Short Course7.2 km

About

The Barefoot XTRAIL Series West Wind race takes place at Meridian Wind Farm, offering unique trail running experiences through diverse landscapes including pine forest tracks, coastal trails with Cook Straight views, and running amongst 110-metre wind turbines.
