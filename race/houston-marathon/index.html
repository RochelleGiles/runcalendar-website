About

The Chevron Houston Marathon is the largest winter marathon in the United States, run each January on a fast, flat loop through the neighbourhoods of Houston. Race weekend also features the Aramco Houston Half Marathon, one of the most competitive half marathons in the country, plus the We Are Houston 5K.
