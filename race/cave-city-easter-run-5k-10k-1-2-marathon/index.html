Course

All distances start and finish at Cave City Park at 9 am. The first three-quarters of a mile is sealed before the course turns onto rolling gravel back roads; the 5K and 10K are out-and-back while the half marathon runs a lollipop. The organiser describes it as difficult and challenging, with aid stations throughout.
