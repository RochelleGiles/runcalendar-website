Course

The course winds through the Lakewood area of Mandan, starting at the Mandan Firefighters Training Grounds. Competitive 5K and 10K races are held alongside a family fun 5K and a half-mile kids race. A virtual option is also offered.

About

Through the Smoke Road Race is an annual 10K and 5K road race in Mandan, North Dakota. It is open to runners and walkers of all abilities and draws a mix of local and regional participants each year.
