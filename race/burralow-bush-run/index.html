Course

Starts and finishes at Bilpin Fruit Bowl in the Hawkesbury foothills, on fire trail and natural terrain through national park. The 16km Devil's Dash crosses Devils Wilderness with 184m of climbing; the 34km drops to Burralow Campground, climbs to Kurrajong Heights, then runs an out-and-back to Bowen Mountain. Aid stations every 3-6km with water, fruit, lollies and chips.

About

The Burralow Bush Run is the only trail running event in the Hawkesbury, in the foothills north-west of Sydney, run in collaboration with the Hawkesbury Trail Runners. It starts and finishes at the Bilpin Fruit Bowl on fire trails through national park land. The event is non-profit, with all funds raised going to the local Rural Fire Service.
