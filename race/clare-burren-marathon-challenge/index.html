About

Explore the rugged beauty of Ireland's Clare Burren on the Clare Burren Marathon Challenge. This spectacular trail event offers marathon, half marathon, and 10km distances, winding through the stunning Burren landscape. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience.
