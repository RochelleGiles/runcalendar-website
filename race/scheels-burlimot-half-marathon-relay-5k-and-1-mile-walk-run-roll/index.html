About

A running event that showcases the beautiful communities of Burlington and Minot, ND featuring a scenic 13.1 mile course that follows the Souris Valley along dedicated running/biking paths. The event includes multiple race distances and is organized by Run with Heart and Sole, a Minot-based business promoting community participation through quality running events.
