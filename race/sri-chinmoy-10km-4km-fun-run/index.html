Course

Starts and finishes at Acton Park on the signposted Sri Chinmoy 10 km Recreational Running Course, following the sealed shared cycle path along the northern shore of Lake Burley Griffin. The course is measured and marked. On-site registration runs at Acton Park from 7:30am to 8:20am.

About

Sri Chinmoy conducts a bi-annual 4km and 10km lakeside fun run/walk event in Canberra. Since 1984, the Sri Chinmoy Marathon Team has conducted these races along the cycle path on the northern shore of Lake Burley Griffin, starting and finishing at Acton Ferry Terminal.
