Course

Starts just outside Edinburgh at 11am and finishes inside Musselburgh Racecourse, Scotland's oldest. The route is flat and fast, running the East Lothian golf coast before a closing section on the racecourse. Hydration and energy gel on route, with hydration and nutrition at the finish.

About

The Scottish Half Marathon is a flat, fast half marathon held in Edinburgh. It draws personal-best chasers alongside first-time half marathoners taking on their first 13.1 miles, with support from a large field of runners of all abilities.
