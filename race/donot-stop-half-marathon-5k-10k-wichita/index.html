About

The DoNot Stop Half Marathon (5k/10k) - Wichita is a family-friendly spring running event offering 5K, 10K, and half marathon distances with creative swag, finisher medals, and a fun, inclusive atmosphere for all paces. Participants enjoy donuts, youth pricing, and virtual options, with packets shipped post-race.
