Course

An out-and-back for every distance from Joseph City Park in the Wallowas, at 4,200ft. The half marathon runs three paved miles, then six miles of maintained dirt road climbing to about 5,100ft, then three paved miles back, so it is hilly throughout. Views take in the Wallowa Mountains, Eagle Cap Wilderness and the Seven Devils. Water at miles 3, 6.5 and 10.

About

The Hurricane Creek Half Marathon is a hilly and challenging out-and-back course starting at Joseph City Park in Joseph, Oregon. It features a mix of paved and dirt roads with significant elevation gain from 4200 ft to 5100 ft.
