About

The Quad Dipsea is a point-to-point-and-back trail ultramarathon that covers the famous Dipsea Trail four times between Mill Valley and Stinson Beach, California. The route climbs over Mount Tamalpais and includes the iconic Dipsea Steps, with roughly 28 miles and significant elevation gain.
