Course

Start and finish at Boyd Lake State Park in Loveland, Colorado, run on paved walkways with about three-quarters of a mile off-road on the south loop. Aid stations offering water, snacks and electrolyte drink are shared by both distances.

About

The NoCo Half Marathon & 10K is a scenic lakefront running event held at Boyd Lake State Park in Loveland, Colorado. Runners enjoy a flat course on paved walkways with post-race craft beer, food, medals, shirts, and professional timing.
