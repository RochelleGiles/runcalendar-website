Course

An overnight road relay of about 206 miles from the Jim Beam Distillery in Clermont to Lexington Brewing & Distilling Company, run over two days and one night through the rolling Bluegrass hills. The route passes distilleries including Maker's Mark and Town Branch, with pop-up tastings on course. Teams of 12, ultra teams of 6, or a one-day sprint.
