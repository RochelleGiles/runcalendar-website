Course

A 30-mile fully-tracked ultra starting and finishing at Morland Village Hall near Penrith, with around 3,000 ft of climbing. The route is predominantly off-road mixed terrain through the Eden Valley towards the Pennines and Howgills, passing stone circles, waterfalls, low fells, rivers, meadows and historic villages. Four checkpoints with water and snacks; 12-hour cutoff.

About

Explore the rugged landscapes of Cumbria in the Morland 'Marathon' Trail Ultra, a challenging 30-mile journey with 3,000 feet of elevation gain. This mixed-terrain race invites adventurous runners to test their mettle on winding trails, scenic hills, and breathtaking vistas that make this event a true test of endurance and a must for any trail enthusiast.
