Course

Road race starting and finishing on the Benjamin Franklin Parkway in front of the Philadelphia Museum of Art — the finish is at the foot of the steps, not up them. The 10 Mile takes in Mount Drago, a steep sustained climb, while the 5K stays flat. Both start from 2600 Benjamin Franklin Parkway, the 5K at 7:00am and the 10 Mile at 8:00am.

About

Rocky Run follows the film's training route along Kelly Drive and the Schuylkill River before finishing atop the Philadelphia Museum of Art's "Rocky Steps." The 5K and 10-mile distances trace scenes from the Rocky movies.
