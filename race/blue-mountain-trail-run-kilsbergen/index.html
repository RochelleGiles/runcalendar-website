About

Blue Mountain Trail Run is a trail race in Kilsbergen, about 22 km west of Örebro, starting and finishing at First Camp Ånnaboda. Four courses follow the Bergslagsleden trail and the mountain-bike trails around Ånnaboda, over roots, rocks and boardwalks across streams, with a final climb to Sånnabodahöjden before the finish: Blankhult, a 21 km long loop on technical trail (527 m of climbing); Alvars stig, 15 km of singletrack throughout (400 m); Trolldalsvägen, the classic 10 km through Trolldalen (230 m); and Östra Ånnabodasjön, a 6 km loop around the lake for first-time trail racers (100 m). There is no cut-off: the organisers wait for everyone at the finish. Run by Garphyttans IF since 2023.
