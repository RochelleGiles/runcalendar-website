About

The Les Foulées De Laeroport is a unique running event held in an unexpected location - the grounds of a bustling airport. Runners of all levels can experience the thrill of racing through the normally restricted areas, taking in the sights and sounds of this dynamic transportation hub. This annual event offers a one-of-a-kind challenge and an unforgettable experience for any avid runner.
