Course

Flat, fast city half marathon starting at 22nd Street and the Benjamin Franklin Parkway and finishing at Eakins Oval. Run entirely on sealed city roads through central Philadelphia and along the river drives. A companion 5K is run the following morning over the same start and finish.

About

The Philadelphia Distance Run is one of the country's longest-running half marathons, following the Schuylkill River through Fairmount Park past Kelly Drive and the Philadelphia Museum of Art.
