About

We are super excited to be hosting the 2nd Annual Cupid Run for Hope 5K to support our local Mental Health Court, or MHC program. As part of the Cherokee Judicial Mental Health Community Council, we aim to raise awareness and support for our Cherokee Judicial Mental Health Court program with participants' needs.
