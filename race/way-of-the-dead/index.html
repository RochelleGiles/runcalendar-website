Course

A 50km east-to-west traverse of Dartmoor from Trenchford Reservoir car park to Tavistock Golf Club, with 1,327m of climb and a ten-hour limit. The line crosses wooded paths, fields, bridlepaths and some tarmac, and parts of it need navigational experience. Three checkpoints double as feed stations at roughly 8, 13 and 23 miles, GPS trackers are issued and runners may enter solo or in pairs.

About

An ultramarathon crossing Dartmoor National Park from east to west, covering 50 kilometres of remote moorland, tors and open access land. Organised by Wild Running in support of Devon Air Ambulance.
