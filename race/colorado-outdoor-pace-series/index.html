Course

Raced on the Colorado State Patrol car training track on top of South Table Mountain, entered from South Quaker Street. Access is a dirt road climbing to the summit where pavement resumes, and the racing itself is on the paved track. Six to eight events across the winter at 5K, 10K and 15K plus 45, 60 and 90 minute formats, all starting at 6pm.

About

This is a race series that will take place from January to March at the Colorado State Patrol car training track on South Table Mountain. The series will consist of a minimum of six and a maximum of eight different foot races of various distances. Athletes can choose to participate in one race or participate in the whole series.
