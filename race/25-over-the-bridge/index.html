Course

A generally flat and fast road course through Hampton Bays, with the one real challenge the climb over the Ponquogue Bridge. The route takes in water views across the Atlantic, Shinnecock Inlet and Shinnecock Bay before returning through residential streets. Aid is provided on course. Both 5K and 10K start at 9:00 a.m.

About

The Hampton Bays Lions Club's 14th annual running event features a unique course highlighting breathtaking views along the Atlantic Ocean, Shinnecock Inlet, and Shinnecock Bay from the Ponquogue Bridge. The race includes both a 10K run and 5K run/walk option with a generally flat and fast course that crosses the iconic Ponquogue Bridge.
