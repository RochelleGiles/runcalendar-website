About

The Imperial Trail of Bizy is a running event that takes place in the scenic surroundings of the Bizy Castle. Runners can choose from various distances, including a 46km ultra-trail, a 34km trail, a 23km trail, and an 11km trail. The event also features a Nordic walking option and a Kid's Trail, making it a family-friendly affair. Participants can expect a challenging course with stunning views and a welcoming atmosphere.
