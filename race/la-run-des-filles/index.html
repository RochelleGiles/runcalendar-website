About

La Run Des Filles is an annual running event held in the town of Isle sur la Sorgue. The event is dedicated to women and focuses on accessibility, allowing both experienced runners and those new to the sport to participate. The course is a 2.5km loop that can be run or walked at one's own pace, making it an inclusive and enjoyable experience for all. The event is known for its great atmosphere, with a vibrant village area and various activities and entertainment.
