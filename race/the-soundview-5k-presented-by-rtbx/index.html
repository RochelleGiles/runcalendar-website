About

THE SOUNDVIEW 5K is a 5000 meter race within Soundview Park in the Bronx. The paved course is flat and fast, with one hell of a hairpin turn! You'll run through an urban green space with playgrounds, soccer & baseball fields, and an amphitheater. Oh, and you'll also run along the Bronx River!
