Course

A flat half marathon, half relay and 5K starting and finishing at the IMON Arena on Admiral Sheehy Drive, Dubuque. The half marathon route takes in the Bee Branch and the Mississippi riverfront, with the 5K on a separate course. A free kids mile is held alongside.

About

The MVRA Benefit Classic is a long-running half marathon and 5K in Dubuque, Iowa, hosted by the Mississippi Valley Running Association along the bluffs above the Mississippi River. Proceeds benefit local running scholarships and community programs.
