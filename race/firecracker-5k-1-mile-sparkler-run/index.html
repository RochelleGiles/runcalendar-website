About

Join us for a 5K and/or 1 Mile Run/Walk in the heart of Annandale during one of the greatest Independence Day celebrations in the state of Minnesota! Annandale's Firecracker 5K and 1 Mile Sparkler Run/Walk is a family-friendly yet competitive event with both courses beginning and ending in the heart of Annandale's 4th of July Celebration near Annandale Municipal Park.
