About

The St. John Applefest Road Race is an annual event held as part of the Applefest festival at St. John the Evangelist Catholic Church in Fenton, MI. It features 5K run and walk options, a 1-mile Apple Core race, and a double challenge combining the 1-mile with the 5K.
