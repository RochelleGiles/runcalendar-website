Course

Point-to-point road race through the Buller Gorge on the West Coast. The marathon starts at Hawks Crag, climbs about 7km to a turnaround near Berlins, then runs down the undulating Buller Gorge Highway to finish at Victoria Square in Westport. The half marathon starts separately near the Ohikaiti River Bridge.

About

Explore the stunning Buller Gorge in New Zealand during the Buller Gorge Marathon, featuring a challenging 42.2km course, a Half Marathon, Marathon Team Relay, and a 10km run. Runners can immerse themselves in the region's breathtaking natural beauty as they navigate this scenic event, making it a must-do for endurance enthusiasts.
