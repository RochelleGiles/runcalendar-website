About

The Atlas Quest is much more than just a race. It is an event at the heart of a timeless place, where memories have a special meaning. Participating in the Atlas Quest offers an exceptional experience: four days of immersion in the splendor of the High Atlas, where every step reveals a new discovery and every evening at the base camp strengthens the bonds between the participants and with the land that welcomes us. A rare and incomparable moment of life.
