Course

Start and finish at Rosalind Park in central Bendigo. Routes pass the Ulumbarra Theatre, the Dai Gum San Chinese Precinct and the Queen Elizabeth Oval on sealed city streets and park paths. The organiser describes the courses as good quality hard surfaces with plenty of shade.

About

The Bendigo Ford Fun Run is a popular annual running event in the heart of Bendigo, Victoria. The course takes runners through the scenic streets and parks of this vibrant regional city, offering a challenging yet enjoyable experience for participants of all levels. With a focus on community, the event attracts runners from across the state and beyond to come together and celebrate their love of running.
