Course

An out-and-back on closed sealed roads. The course starts at Gumbuya World and heads north along Brew Road towards Tynong Quarry, turning at the halfway mark before finishing inside the park. Volunteer-staffed water stations on course carry water and Tailwind.

About

Explore the natural beauty of Victoria at the Gumbuya World Running Festival! This scenic event offers a range of distances, from a challenging 10km race to a fun 2km Family Dash, allowing runners of all levels to enjoy the stunning surroundings. Whether you're seeking a personal best or a leisurely run with loved ones, this festival promises an unforgettable experience.
