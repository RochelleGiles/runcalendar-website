Course

175-mile, 36-leg relay for teams of 12, running from Twin Bridges to Virginia City, Montana, largely on road including the Pioneer Mountain Scenic Byway. Rolling hills give way to a crossing of the Pioneers, with about 13,000 feet of cumulative climb and 11,000 feet of descent.

Join our 180-mile relay adventure through the rugged Pioneer Mountains. From Twin Bridges to Virginia City, you'll face the elements, test your endurance, and forge lasting memories.
