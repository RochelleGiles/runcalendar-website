About

The Spijkenisse Spark Marathon is a popular running event that has been held for 19 editions. The marathon course takes runners through the scenic streets and neighborhoods of Spijkenisse, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants. The event is known for its vibrant atmosphere and strong community support.
