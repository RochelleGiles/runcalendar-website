About

The Izzo Legacy proudly celebrated its 2nd annual Izzo Impact Grant Award event on November 5th. This event highlights and embodies the mission of The Izzo Legacy Foundation — bringing people together and supporting organizations that make a meaningful difference in communities across Michigan. Giving back, creating opportunities, and making a lasting impact is at the heart of the work of The Izzo Legacy Foundation.
