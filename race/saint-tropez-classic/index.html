About

The Saint-Tropez Classic is a scenic running event taking place in the picturesque coastal town of Saint-Tropez. Runners can choose between 16 km and 8 km distances, with a simultaneous start on the port. The event also features a special 'Course des Minots' for children, making it a family-friendly running celebration.
