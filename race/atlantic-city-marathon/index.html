Course

The marathon and half marathon run on Absecon Island through the streets of Atlantic City, Ventnor, Margate and Longport, taking in the Atlantic City Boardwalk. The start is at 1900 Boardwalk, Atlantic City. The marathon and half run on the Sunday; the 10K and G. Larry James 5K are held the previous day.
