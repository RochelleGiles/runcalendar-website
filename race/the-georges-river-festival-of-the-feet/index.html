Course

All races are held inside Mirrambeena Regional Park, starting and finishing at the Western Districts Joggers and Harriers clubhouse, Georges Hall. Courses follow paved pathways along the Georges River and around Lake Gillawarna with short sections on grass; road shoes are advised. The 7km is an undulating loop. Drink stations at 3km, 5km, 10km, 12km, 17km and 19km.

About

The Georges River Festival of the Feet is an annual fun run staged by Western Districts Joggers and Harriers in Mirrambeena Regional Park, Georges Hall, Sydney. Entrants choose 3km, 7km, 14km or 21.1km on undulating sealed paths beside the Georges River and Lake Gillawarna.
