About

The Kiwanis Club of Sioux Falls presents its annual fundraiser with a new name and distance options: Lace Up for Kids 5k, 10 Mile, & Bake Sale to support kids and youth in our community. Come get your steps in with this fun run/walk and follow it up with a bake sale or just stop out for some sweet treats without running!
