About

The event will take place at Bunker Hills Golf Course in Coon Rapids. This event is a family-friendly luminary-lit run and walk. We will kick off the night with games and activities for all ages, including face painting, decorating a luminary, glow station, and more! There will be drawings and awards for the largest team, most fundraised, and more! The goal of the event is to raise awareness of what youth who are homeless may experience when the darkness of night sets in.
