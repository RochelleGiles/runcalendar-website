About

Explore the rugged beauty of Ireland's Cooley Peninsula at the Annaloughan Loop 10K Trail Run. This new event takes runners on a challenging yet scenic course through the region's rolling hills and lush forests, offering a unique off-road adventure. Whether you're an experienced trail enthusiast or looking to try something new, the Annaloughan Loop promises an unforgettable running experience.
