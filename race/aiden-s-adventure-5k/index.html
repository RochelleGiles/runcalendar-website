About

Aiden's Adventure 5K is a run/walk event held in loving memory of Aiden, a joyful 5-year-old lost in a tragic accident. All proceeds benefit Care and Share of Southern Colorado and Catamount Institute, featuring a 5K route, kids 1-mile dash, and family activities.
