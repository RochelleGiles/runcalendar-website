Course

Point-to-point 5K starting at 980 MacArthur Causeway on the east side of Watson Island and finishing at 1 Washington Avenue in Miami Beach. The route is a flat, scenic, water-lined causeway road crossing to South Beach. Runners must hold a minimum 20-minute-mile pace, and no shuttles run between the start and the finish area.

About

The Life Time Tropical 5K is a family-friendly 5K race that serves as the kick-off and preview of the #MiamiFamous weekend, suitable for runners of all levels. It starts on the MacArthur Causeway on the east side of Watson Island at 7:30 AM and is limited to 100 participants who must maintain a 20-minute per mile pace.
