Course

The course starts on the road beside the Beaver fire station and finishes in Irvine Park, with the 10K running the same loop twice. Roads are not closed to traffic for the event. A one mile Elf Run is held alongside the 5K and 10K.

About

Kick off your holiday season by helping us stop youth hunger! Proceeds from this race will go toward our youth feeding programs in Beaver County. Wear your Santa hats & Christmas gear for this fun & festive 5k/10k.
