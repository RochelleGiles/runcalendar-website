Course

Three distinct trail loops in Rocky Gap State Park run in order: Rocky Trail (6.5mi), Canyon Overlook (4.5mi) and the Lakeside Loop (5.2mi). The 25K covers each once, the 50K twice, solo or as a two to three person relay. Singletrack and doubletrack with roots, rocks and stream crossings; base camp aid at the Mountain View Pavilion start/finish between each loop.

About

The Rocky Gap 25K, 50K and Relay Trail Run winds through Rocky Gap State Park in the Allegany County mountains near Flintstone, Maryland, circling Lake Habeeb on rugged, hilly singletrack trails.
