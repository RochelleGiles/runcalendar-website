About

Organized as part of the Lynchburg Ultra Series and now run by Winding Way Run Co., Terrapin Mountain sits beside the George Washington National Forest with the Blue Ridge Mountains as a backdrop. The 50K follows a multi-loop course climbing nearly 7,000 feet, while the half marathon covers a single loop.
