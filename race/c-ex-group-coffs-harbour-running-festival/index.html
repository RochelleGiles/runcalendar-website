About

The C.ex Group Coffs Harbour Running Festival offers a variety of races to challenge runners of all levels. Set against the stunning backdrop of Coffs Harbour, Australia, this event features a Marathon, Half Marathon, 10km & 5km Fun Runs, and a 3km Family Fun Run/Walk, ensuring there's something for everyone to enjoy.
