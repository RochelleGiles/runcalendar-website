About

The Mt Esja Ultra is a challenging and scenic trail running event nestled in the heart of Iceland's rugged landscape. Runners will wind their way through the stunning Esja mountain range, taking in breathtaking views of volcanic peaks, glacial rivers, and lush meadows. This unique ultra-distance race offers an unforgettable adventure for endurance athletes seeking an extraordinary running experience.
