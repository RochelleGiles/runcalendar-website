Course

Flat road course on the city streets of Waite Park, starting and finishing in the ConnectAbility of MN car park on 3rd Street South. The 5K loops through 3rd, 31st, 4th, 28th, 2nd, 33rd and 5th, with a water station and music at halfway. A shorter 1K run, walk or roll follows a chalk-marked route.

About

Join us for ConnectAbility of MN's Break the Barrier 5K race/walk, an inclusive race/walk where everyone is welcome and everyone belongs. Whether you're running, walking, rolling, or strolling, this event is about breaking down barriers and building community.
