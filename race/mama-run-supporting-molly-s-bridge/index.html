Course

The course starts and finishes at Coastal Cathedral Church and winds through the Berwick Plantation area on the edge of Savannah. A 500-yard kids' fun run is held alongside the 5K and 10K.

About

The 5th Annual Mama-Run / Walk for Molly's Bridge offers a kids Fun-Run (500 yds), 5K (3.1 mi), and new 10K (6.2 mi) options. Participants can run or walk as fundraisers to support single mother-led families, with prizes for top fundraisers.
