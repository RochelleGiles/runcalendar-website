About

Les Foulées Aixoises is an annual running event offering multiple race distances through the scenic town of Aixe-sur-Vienne. Participants can choose between 6 km and 15 km routes, with an additional 8 km walking option. The event supports the Téléthon charity and provides a welcoming atmosphere for runners of all experience levels.
