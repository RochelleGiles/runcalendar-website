Course

Courses set off from Rue Jolliet at the foot of Mont-Saint-Hilaire, with a Halloween theme and staggered starts between 8.30am and 11.45am by distance. Refuelling stations with water and Brix electrolytes are placed around the route and at the finish, with toilets beside some of them.

About

A road race at the foot of Mont Saint-Hilaire, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and Monteregian Hill in Quebec's Montérégie region. The event offers 1K, 5K, 10K, and 15K distances.
