Course

Flat 5K on the streets of Pensacola Beach, starting and finishing near Bamboo Willie's at the Casino Beach boardwalk parking lot. The course is marked with cones, arrows, police and volunteers. Race-morning packet pickup runs 6:30-7:30 at the start. Aid station detail is not published.

About

The Crawfish 5K on Pensacola Beach is one you don't want to miss! The Course is flat flat flat and will be the scenic Beach 5K route on the streets of Pensacola Beach and will be extremely well marked with cones/police/arrows/and volunteers. Post race party & awards ceremony will be at Bamboo Willies with live music and great food. Remember, it's ok to "suck" at this race!
