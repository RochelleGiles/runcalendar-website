Course

Rural back-road courses starting at 390 Cummins Falls Lane near Cummins Falls State Park outside Cookeville, Tennessee, following the Blackburn Fork State Scenic River. The marathon, half and 10K feature steep hills, with aid roughly every 2-3 miles. The marathon and half start at 8:00 AM with an eight-hour cutoff; the 10K and 5K start at 9:00 AM.

About

These four races are very scenic with the marathon, half marathon, and 10K routes following the Blackburn Fork State Scenic River for a good portion of the route. The three longer distance races are very challenging with steep hills and great scenery. All four routes are run on the picturesque rural back roads of Jackson County, Tennessee, and are sure to be enjoyed by traditional marathon runners and trail runners alike. This race is a qualifier for the Boston Marathon.
