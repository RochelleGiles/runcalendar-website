About

The Oxford Half is a popular running event that takes participants on a scenic tour of the historic city of Oxford. The course showcases the city's iconic landmarks, including the dreaming spires of the university colleges and the River Thames. With a lively atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators, the Oxford Half is a must-do event for runners looking to experience the charm of this beautiful English city.
