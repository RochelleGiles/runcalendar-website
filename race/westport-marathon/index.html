About

The Westport Marathon offers runners a scenic tour through the picturesque countryside of County Mayo, Ireland. In addition to the full marathon distance, this event also features a Half Marathon and a 10km race, providing options for runners of all abilities. Runners can look forward to a challenging yet rewarding course that showcases the natural beauty of this stunning Irish region.
