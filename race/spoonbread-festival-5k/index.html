Course

A flat, paved 5K run or walk on the Berea City Trail, starting and finishing at the Russel Acton Folk Center on West Jefferson Street. One water station sits on the route and runners pass it twice. A one-mile fun walk sets off just after the 5K.

About

A 5K and one-mile run through Berea, Kentucky, held during the town's annual Spoonbread Festival. Berea is known as Kentucky's Folk Arts and Crafts Capital, home to working artisan studios and Berea College.
