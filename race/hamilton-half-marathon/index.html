Course

Starts and finishes at Hamilton Gardens on the south side of the city. The course runs riverside along the banks of the Waikato River on the sealed Te Awa River Ride pathway, winding through parks, landmarks and historic neighbourhoods. Distances of 21 km, 10 km and 5 km are open to both runners and walkers.

About

Join us for a running event that starts and finishes at the stunning Hamilton Gardens with riverside running along the banks of the Waikato River, winding through parks, landmarks and historic neighbourhoods. The event offers a 21KM half marathon, 10KM and 5KM options for both runners and walkers.
