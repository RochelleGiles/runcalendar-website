Course

Run almost entirely inside State Forest State Park, between the Never Summer and Medicine Bow ranges near Cameron Pass. Starts and finishes at the Gould Community Center with staggered pre-dawn waves. Surfaces range from singletrack and high alpine rock to jeep and logging roads. The 100K covers 61 miles with 14,250 ft of gain; the 60K is 36.5 miles with 8,850 ft.

About

A mountain ultramarathon based in Gould, Colorado, held in State Forest State Park near the Never Summer mountain range. Runners choose the 100K or 60K distance across high-altitude forest and alpine trails.
