Course

The race begins at Fiddler's Green, 19 Temple Street, Worcester, and finishes at the Hibernian Cultural Center. A 1K Kids Fun Run is held alongside the 5K, and a virtual entry is offered. There is no race-day registration.

About

The Canal Diggers 5K is a road race in Worcester, now past its 20th running. It starts at Fiddler's Green and finishes near the Hibernian Cultural Center, followed by food, live entertainment and refreshments, with a 1K Kids Fun Run alongside the main event.
