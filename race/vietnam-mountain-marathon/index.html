Course

Point-to-point and loop courses through the mountains around Sapa in northern Vietnam, running on single track off the beaten path through bamboo forest, rice-terraced valleys and villages of several ethnic groups. Every distance finishes at Topas Ecolodge. The 100-mile course covers 161km with 8,800m of climbing.

About

The Vietnam Mountain Marathon is centred around Sapa and is one of the most prestigious trail races in Asia, welcoming thousands of runners from dozens of countries. The course climbs into the mountains through bamboo forests and rice-terraced valleys, passing small villages of various ethnic groups along the way.
