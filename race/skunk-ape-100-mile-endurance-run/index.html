Course

A roughly 50-mile out-and-back on the Florida National Scenic Trail from Santos Trailhead on the Marjorie Harris Carr Cross Florida Greenway at Ocala, with 100-milers covering it twice. Underfoot it is singletrack, sand, roots and short climbs with some paved trail, running through sand pine scrub, longleaf pine and the Ross Prairie WMA. About 1,562ft of gain per lap; 7am start and a 36-hour cutoff.

About

A 100-mile ultramarathon trail race held in the Marjorie Harris Carr Cross Florida Greenway near Ocala, Florida. The event features a 50-mile out-and-back course on the Florida National Scenic Trail and paved paths, with 100-milers completing the course twice.
