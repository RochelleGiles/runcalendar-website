About

Luck O' the Lake is an exciting St. Paddy's Day event set against the stunning backdrop of Lake Minnetonka in Excelsior, MN. Participants can embrace the Irish spirit as they race along the scenic shores in a 5k and 1-mile run, enjoying the perfect blend of fitness, fun, and a touch of green in this picturesque lakeside celebration. End at Excelsior Brewing Company and enjoy a locally brewed beer and post race snacks while watching the Rince na Griene Irish Dancers.
