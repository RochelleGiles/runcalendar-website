Course

About

The L.A. Chinatown Firecracker Run/Walk sends runners through the streets of Los Angeles's historic Chinatown, with 5K and 10K options alongside a Kiddie Run and dog-friendly Paw'er Run. Now in its 48th year, it is one of the city's longest-running community road races.
