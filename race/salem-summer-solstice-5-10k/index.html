Course

An evening race starting and finishing at the pavilion beside car park 3 at Minto Brown Island Park, Salem. Both courses are flat and set in parkland: the 5K runs entirely on paved trails, while the 10K adds a small loop on open trail before returning to sealed path. The 10K starts at 7:00pm and the 5K at 7:10pm.

About

The Salem Summer Solstice 5/10K is a longtime early-summer road race in Salem, Oregon, hosted by the Willamette Valley Road Runners. Runners choose between the 5K and 10K distance on a course through the city, timed to the start of summer.
