Course

Technical trail routes from Caffyns Farm near Lynton into Exmoor National Park, with long steep climbs and descents. The courses take in the South West Coast Path above the Bristol Channel, the Valley of Rocks, ancient woodland, moorland and river valleys. Aid outposts carry fruit, cake, crisps, gels and electrolyte; runners carry their own cup under a plastic-free policy.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of Exmoor National Park at the Maverick Exmoor X-Trail Run. This challenging off-road event takes runners along the stunning South West coastal path, offering breathtaking ocean views and savage climbs that will test even the toughest trail enthusiasts. Hosted by the renowned Maverick Race series, this is a must-do event for any adventure runner seeking an unforgettable experience.
