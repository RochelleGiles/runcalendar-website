About

The Lyricantrail offers several distances to satisfy all runners, from beginners to the most experienced. The 14km trail provides a glimpse of the natural wonders and elevation changes of Larchant, making it ideal for newcomers before tackling the longer version. The 25km trail is a challenging course with technical sections, perfect for seasoned trail runners.
