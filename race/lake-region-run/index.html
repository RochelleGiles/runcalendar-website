Course

Road courses starting and finishing near Dr Allen Magnuson Park in Fergus Falls. The 10 mile loops around Lake Alice, past the Regional Treatment Center, around the M State campus and through Butterfly Park; the 5K runs up Mill Street and around Lake Alice; the 1 mile is an out-and-back on Mill Street. The 10 mile can also be run as a two-person relay.

About

The Lake Region Run is held each June in Fergus Falls, Minnesota, offering a 10-mile race, a two-person 10-mile relay, a 5K and a one-mile. The courses run on city roads around Lake Alice and the M State campus. The spread of distances suits casual walkers through to competitive runners.
