About

The Vienna Turkey Trot starts and finishes at the Vienna Volunteer Fire Department on Center Street South, drawing runners through Vienna's residential streets each Thanksgiving morning. The USATF-sanctioned race benefits the James Madison High School Band and the Vienna Volunteer Fire Department.
