Course

Four laps of a two-mile grassy trail loop at Lakewood Nature Preserve, with rolling hills. Start and finish are at The Barn. Water is available at the finish line. The eight miles can be run solo or split as a relay.

About

Run fast. Drink hard. Don't get bit. What do you get when you combine trail running, random beers, and your most competitive (and possibly least coordinated) friends? The Cottonmouth Beerlay, baby! This wild race at the beautiful Lakewood Nature Preserve is back for another year of glorious chaos, beer-fueled miles, and unforgettable memories.
