About

The 46th Annual South Salem Memorial Day Races feature a 10K, 5K, Mile Fun Run, and Pee Wee Dash. The 10K and 5K courses are U.S.A. Track and Field certified, allowing running times to be accepted as records and nationally ranked. A portion of the proceeds goes directly to support local families affected by Alzheimer's disease through the Alzheimer's Association Hudson Valley.
