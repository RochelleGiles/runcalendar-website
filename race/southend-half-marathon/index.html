About

The Southend Half Marathon offers runners a scenic coastal route along the picturesque shores of East Beach, Shoeburyness. This fast, flat, and traffic-free 13.1-mile challenge provides an exhilarating experience for runners of all levels, with stunning views of the United Kingdom's beautiful coastline.
