About

Join Us for the 5K & Polar Plunge at Robert W. Crown Memorial State Beach. Ready to brave the waters for Special Olympics Northern California? Join the Bay Area 5K & Polar Plunge, a fun dip, skip or jump into the Bay for a great cause! Don't want to get wet? Register as a chicken and you'll be excused from plunging but still qualify for the incentive rewards and other festivities.
