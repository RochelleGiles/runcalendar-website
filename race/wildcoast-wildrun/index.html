Course

A three-stage, 112 km point-to-point run along the Wild Coast from Kei Mouth to Hole-in-the-Wall. Stages of 44.2 km, 35.2 km and 34 km cover beaches, grassy headland trails and coastal forest, with nights in rustic hotels and lodges along the route.

About

A three-day stage race along South Africa's wild, undeveloped Wild Coast in the Eastern Cape. Runners cross remote beaches, river mouths, forests and coastal cliffs between overnight camps, on terrain rated among the country's toughest trail challenges.
