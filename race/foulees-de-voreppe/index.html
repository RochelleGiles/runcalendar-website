About

The Foulees De Voreppe is a running event that offers several trail and road race distances, including a 23km trail, a 15km trail, a 7.5km trail, a 10km road race, and a 5km road race. The event is known for its challenging yet scenic courses that take runners through the beautiful countryside around Voreppe. The event also supports the Dynamoov association, which creates unique opportunities for people with disabilities.
