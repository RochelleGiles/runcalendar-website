Course

A women-only road race starting and finishing at the Hoërskool Diamantveld rugby field in Kimberley, with 10km and 5km options both setting off at 08:00. The route runs on flat city roads, with water points every 3km.

About

The Kimberley Harriers Ladies 10km Race is a women-only road race in Kimberley, Northern Cape, hosted by the Kimberley Harriers running club. It offers 10km and 5km distances and celebrates fitness and friendship among women runners.
