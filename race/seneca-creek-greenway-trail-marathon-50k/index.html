Course

Singletrack dirt trail over rolling hills in Seneca Creek State Park, starting and finishing at the Nuthatch Pavilion in Gaithersburg. Marathon and 50K run together until mile 27, where runners either finish or add a loop of Clopper Lake for the ultra distance. Expect mud, ice, rocks, roots and slick crossings, and about 2,200 ft of climb. Drop bag at the Berryville Road aid station.
