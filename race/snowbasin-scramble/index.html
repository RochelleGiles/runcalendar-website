About

Snowbasin Resort is a year-round recreation destination featuring world class skiing, mountain biking, and hiking. Spartan Trail racers will take advantage of steep slopes and rugged winding singletrack as they accumulate as much as possible of the mountain's 3000 vertical feet in a short but grueling 10k. Come prepared for the altitude and the most epic views on the Spartan Trail calendar.
