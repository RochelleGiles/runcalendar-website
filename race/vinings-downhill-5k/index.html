About

The Vinings Downhill 5K is a point-to-point road race from Mt. Wilkinson down to the Riverview Village area of Vinings, Georgia. A Peachtree Road Race qualifier organised by the Vinings Cumberland Rotary Club, it raises money for children's charities.
