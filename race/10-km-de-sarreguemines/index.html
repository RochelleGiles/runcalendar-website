About

The 10 Km De Sarreguemines is an urban road race run on IAAF measured asphalt roads. The race features a two-wave start system to ensure smooth race flow, with protected intersections and a hydration station at the 5km mark. The event attracts elite runners with prize money for top finishers and includes special primes for first male and female runners passing specific checkpoints.
