Course

Backyard ultra on a 6.72km loop. The course follows the scenic drive trail beside the Murray River, then moves onto pavement beside the bridge to New South Wales as an out-and-back turning at the Blue Tree, before returning to climb the hill. Surface is mixed trail and pavement. Base aid includes electrolyte, water, fruit, hot soup and noodles, plus a camp kitchen.

About

The 2 Rivers Backyard Ultra challenges runners to push their limits on a scenic course along the banks of the Murray and Campaspe rivers in Echuca, North Victoria. Featuring a unique "last runner standing" format, this event tests mental and physical endurance as participants compete to complete a new 6.7km loop every hour until only one remains. An unforgettable test of human potential in a stunning natural setting.
