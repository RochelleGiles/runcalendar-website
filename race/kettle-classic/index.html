Course

Non-competitive routes through the Kettle Moraine starting from the Lad Lake campus at Dousman, Wisconsin. A 5K challenge run and 5K walk cover natural terrain on and around the campus, with a 2-mile family walk also offered. Rest stops and SAG support are provided and all events finish by 12.45pm.

About

The Kettle Classic is an annual running event that takes place in the beautiful Kettle Moraine region of Wisconsin. Runners of all levels can enjoy the scenic trails and challenging terrain as they push themselves to new personal bests.
