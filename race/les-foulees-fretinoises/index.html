About

Les Foulées Fretinoises is a running event that takes place in Fretin, France. The event features both a 5km and a 10km race, offering something for runners of all levels. The course takes participants through the scenic streets and countryside of Fretin, providing a challenging yet enjoyable experience for all participants.
