About

The Downtown Dash 5k is a fun run/walk event held in Minneapolis, MN, starting at 2:00 p.m. from 100 West River Parkway, crossing the Stone Arch Bridge and following the W River Rd trail with a turnaround point. A portion of net proceeds supports Appetite For Change, a non-profit building health, wealth, and social change in North Minneapolis.
