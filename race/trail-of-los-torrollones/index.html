Course

Two trail routes through the semi-arid Monegros landscape of Huesca, both starting and finishing in the village of Sodeto. The long race covers 17 km on the Virgen del Castillo route and the short race is an 11 km circuit around La Gabarda. A race for children runs alongside.

About

The Trail of Los Torrollones runs through Los Monegros, the semi-arid badlands of Aragón, starting from the small village of Alberuela de Tubo in Huesca province. Routes of 11km and 17km cross the region's stark, sun-baked terrain.
