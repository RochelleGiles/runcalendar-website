Course

All distances start at Jamison Park, 285 Meadowlark Drive, Winston-Salem. The 5K and 10K share a 3.11-mile out-and-back run once and twice; the half marathon uses a separate 3.28-mile out-and-back covered four times. All start together at 8:00 AM under a 3 hour 30 minute limit, and entry is online only with no race-day registration.
