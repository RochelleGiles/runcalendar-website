About

The Riverway 5K, now past its 1st running, is a non-profit race in downtown Conway, South Carolina, benefiting Winyah Rivers Alliance. The scenic route starts near the City's water tower by the Waccamaw River, with proceeds funding conservation efforts, water quality monitoring, and environmental education programs.
