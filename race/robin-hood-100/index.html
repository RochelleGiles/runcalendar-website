Course

Loop course starting and finishing at South Wheatley Village Hall near Retford, Nottinghamshire. The route takes in two laps of Sherwood Forest, sandstone villages, Creswell Crags, the Welbeck Estate, Clumber Park and canal towpath, mostly on forest trails and footpaths. Relatively flat with steady undulations. Basic refreshments at regular intervals and at the finish.

About

This is a fully marked ultra on nice underfoot, runnable and scenic trails. The route heads into Sherwood Forest to take in Robin Hood's homeland before returning back to South Wheatley. Relatively flat with no major climbs, just steady undulations on occasion, make it a 'fast' course.
