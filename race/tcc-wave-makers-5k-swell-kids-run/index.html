About

Make waves in the fight against cancer! Join us for the inaugural Tulane Cancer Center Wave Makers 5K & The Swell Kids 1/2 Mile Run on Saturday, January 17, 2026, in Audubon Park. Every step is more than just distance covered — it's a stride toward awareness, compassion, and a cure. By signing up, you're not just participating in a race — you are a Wave Maker, helping to turn individual effort into a fight against cancer.
