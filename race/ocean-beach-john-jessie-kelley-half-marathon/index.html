Course

A well-marked and supported road half marathon starting and finishing at Ocean Beach Park in New London, out along rural roads through New London and Waterford with panoramic views over Long Island Sound. The finish area opens onto a three-quarter-mile sand beach and boardwalk, so runners are told to bring swimwear.

About

The Kelley Course is a 13.1-mile loop on paved roads, starting and finishing at Ocean Beach Park in New London. The route is mostly flat with a few rolling hills and one long climb around mile eight, supported by eight water stops along the way.
