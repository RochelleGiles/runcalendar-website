Course

Point-to-point route on the Palouse to Cascades State Park Trail, a converted rail corridor through Central Washington that includes railroad trestles and tunnels. Starts at Renslow Trestle, with relay exchanges at Kittitas Trailhead and Kiwanis Park in Ellensburg, and finishes at Thorpe Trailhead.

About

Experience the beauty and spirit of Kittitas County as you run through breathtaking natural landscapes and charming small towns along the historic Palouse to Cascades State Park Trail. This unique course follows the former Milwaukee Road railroad corridoroffering runners an unforgettable journey through tunnels, bridges, and scenic backcountry terrain.
