Course

All distances start and finish at Grant Park, 501 Lake Drive, South Milwaukee. The half marathon, 10K and 5K run within and around the park, with an untimed 1 mile and a kids dash alongside them. A virtual entry option is also offered.

About

The Golden Harvest Half Marathon, 10K, 5K, and 1-mile fun run take place in South Milwaukee, Wisconsin, a Lake Michigan shoreline suburb, part of a Golden Harvest race series held in several Midwest cities each fall.
