About

The Ikara Ultra invites runners to explore the stunning Ikara-Flinders Ranges in South Australia along the renowned Heysen Trail. Set in the heart of the outback, this challenging ultra-marathon offers a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in the rugged natural beauty of the Australian landscape.
