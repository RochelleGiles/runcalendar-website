About

Saint Mont Vignoble en Course is organized by the winegrowers of the appellation with the help of around 150 volunteers, and is now past its 7th running. The event in Saint-Mont brings together runners, mountain bikers and walkers, who take on their respective courses in an afternoon of sport and conviviality.
