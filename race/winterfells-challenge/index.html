About

The cold bites harder up north—and that's exactly where the Winterfells Challenge lives. This Strava-based battle sends runners across rolling frozen miles with two ways to prove themselves: the Full, a 13-mile gauntlet with 6 brutal climbs, or the Mini, 6 miles featuring 3 hill tests that still hit like a hammer. Every climb, every mile, every shiver earns points toward glory on the leaderboard. No crowds, no cowbells—just you, the frost, and the hills daring you to rise above them. Earn your points, your pride, and your frostbite-free bragging rights.
