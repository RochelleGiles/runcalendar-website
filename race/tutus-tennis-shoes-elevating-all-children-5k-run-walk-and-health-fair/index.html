About

The Greater Tampa Chapter of Jack and Jill of America, Incorporated invites your participation in our annual Tutus and Tennis Shoes: Elevating ALL Children 5K Run/Walk and Health Fair. This health and wellness event includes a scenic 5K route in historic downtown Tampa, a Children's Fun Run, and a Free Community Health Fair. All participants will receive a race t-shirt and Finisher's Medal.
