Course

Run at the Parc del Fòrum in Barcelona on a lapped course, with 5 km covering one lap and 10 km two laps. Runners choose their distance on the day. The race is free to enter and carries no timing or classification.

About

BR Barcelona is part of the Beer Runners network, a social running community that pairs a group run through the city with a post-race celebration. More than 25,000 runners take part in Beer Runners events across Spain, combining exercise with sharing a beer afterwards.
