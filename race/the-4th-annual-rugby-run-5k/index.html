About

A 5k run and a 1-mile fun walk to benefit the Downingtown Rugby Club and All 4 Paws Rescue. The Downingtown Rugby Football Club is a non-profit sports organization dedicated to playing, developing, and promoting rugby in the Downingtown Area for everyone from Grades K through 12.
