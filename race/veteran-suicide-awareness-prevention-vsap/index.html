About

The VSAP helps build community which is one way to help combat suicide. Montana consistently has one of the highest suicide rates in the nation, per capita. This event will help raise awareness and promote prevention about this issue by bringing the community together to memorialize those lost, letting Veterans, friends, and families know they are not alone.
