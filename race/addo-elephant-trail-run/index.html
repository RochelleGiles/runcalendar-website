Course

Point-to-point routes through the wilderness of Addo Elephant National Park on single track, 4x4 and old jeep tracks with stretches of dirt road, including many river crossings and long climbs with views from the top. The 100 Mile starts at the park's Main Camp, the 50 Mile and 50 km at Nyathi Gate and the 21 km at Hayterdale Trails; all four finish at Zuurberg Mountain Village.

About

The Addo Elephant Trail Run takes place inside Addo Elephant National Park in the Eastern Cape, one of the few races run alongside free-roaming elephants and other wildlife behind game-park fencing. Distances range from 10km to a 76km ultra through the reserve's valley bushveld.
