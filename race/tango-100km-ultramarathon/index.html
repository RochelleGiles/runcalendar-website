About

The Tango 100km Ultramarathon crosses the Tango Peninsula in Kyoto Prefecture, a mountain-climbing ultra along Japan's Sea of Japan coast. The 100km and 60km courses take runners over steep coastal hills and through small fishing towns of the peninsula.
