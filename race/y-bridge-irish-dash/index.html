About

Celebrate St. Patrick's Day weekend with a race experience you do not want to miss. The Y-Bridge Irish Dash invites runners and walkers of all ages and abilities to enjoy festive fun in the heart of Zanesville. This fully paved course will take participants through Downtown Zanesville, across the world-famous Y-Bridge, and over the Muskingum River for a one-of-a-kind race day experience.
