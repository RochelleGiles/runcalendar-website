Course

A point-to-point coastal half marathon following the Pilgrim's Trail along the Wales Coast Path from Cwt Tatws to Aberdaron on the Llyn Peninsula. The 21km route climbs 345m and descends 366m, staying between sea level and 81m, with under 15 per cent of it flat. Fully marked and signed with on-course support stations.

About

Embark on a scenic pilgrimage through the captivating landscapes of North Wales at the North Wales Pilgrim Half Marathon. This challenging yet rewarding course takes runners along the picturesque coastline and through the rolling countryside, offering breathtaking views that will make every stride an unforgettable experience.
