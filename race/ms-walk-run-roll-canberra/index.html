About

Lace up your running shoes and join the MS Walk Run + Roll event in Canberra, Australia, as you support Australians living with multiple sclerosis. This family-friendly day out offers the chance to get active and make a difference, with a variety of distance options to suit all fitness levels. Explore the scenic local area while raising crucial funds and awareness for this important cause.
