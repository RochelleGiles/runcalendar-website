Course

Multi-terrain race over private farmland at Dummer Down Farm, Hampshire, starting and finishing at the farm taproom. The 10km is a single loop that is 80-90% off road across trails, woodland, fields and short sections of local road; the half marathon covers the loop twice. One to three cupless water stations on course.

About

The Dell 10K takes runners on a scenic multi-terrain journey through the picturesque Hampshire countryside. Offering both a 10km race and a Half Marathon, this event promises an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels, showcasing the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're seeking a challenging workout or a leisurely tour of the local landscape, the Dell 10K has something to offer every participant.
