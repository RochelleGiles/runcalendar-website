Course

All three races start and finish at St Georges Square in Luton town centre, on closed, traffic-free roads. The half marathon takes in the grounds of Luton Hoo; the 5K and 10K routes stay out of the estate. The courses are hilly in places, with the climbs spread evenly rather than concentrated.

About

Love Luton RunFest offers half marathon, 10K and 5K distances through the town of Luton, Bedfordshire. The multi-distance event is organised as part of the town's Love Luton community initiative.
