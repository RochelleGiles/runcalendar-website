About

The Menorca Half Marathon is an annual running event organized by Elitechip SL, Club Menorca Atletisme, the FAIB Delegation in Menorca, the Department of Sports of the Consell Insular de Menorca, and the Ajuntament de Ciutadella de Menorca. The event features a 21.1 km half marathon, a 10 km race, and a popular run, showcasing the beautiful island of Menorca.
