Course

A 50-mile trail ultra starting and finishing in Malham in the Yorkshire Dales, with about 2,593 m of ascent. The route takes in Janet's Foss, Gordale, Malham Cove, Malham Tarn, Fountains Fell and sections of the Pennine Way. Five checkpoints support the course.

About

A 50-mile ultra across the Yorkshire Dales, starting from the village of Malham beneath its famous limestone cove. The route crosses remote moorland and dale country on the Pennine watershed.
