About

The Stockholm Marathon is a renowned running event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the heart of Sweden's capital city. Participants will have the chance to experience the city's stunning architecture, vibrant culture, and lively atmosphere as they push themselves to the finish line. This iconic race is a must-do for any seasoned or aspiring marathon runner.
