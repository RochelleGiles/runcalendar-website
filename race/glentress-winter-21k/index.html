Course

A 21k and a 10k route starting and finishing at Glentress Peel Visitor Centre near Peebles. The courses run on hard-packed gravel forest roads, twisting singletrack, mountain bike trail sections and open moorland, climbing to 572m. The 21k has 900m of ascent and hydration stations at 6k and 12k; the 10k has one at 6k.

About

Glentress Forest is described as the 'Gateway to the Tweed Valley Forest Park'. The area features mile upon mile of waymarked trails including forest walks, woodland singletrack and heather clad moorland, making it a fantastic location for trail running. The Glentress Winter 21k offers a challenging course with stunning panoramic views.
