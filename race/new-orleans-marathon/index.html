About

The New Orleans Marathon offers a marathon, half marathon, 5K and marathon relay on a flat loop through City Park and along Lake Pontchartrain. The course starts at the New Orleans Museum of Art, passes Bayou St. John and the lakefront, and finishes on Roosevelt Mall in the park.
