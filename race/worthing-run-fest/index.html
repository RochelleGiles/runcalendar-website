Course

Flat, fully closed-road seafront festival at Worthing, with a half marathon, 10K and family mile starting through the morning. The half and 10K are accurately measured and licensed by Run England. The route runs along the promenade past Worthing Pier, with the finish area at the Beach House.

About

The Worthing RUN-FEST is a must-attend running festival in the heart of the United Kingdom. With a scenic Half Marathon, a fast-paced 10km, and a fun Family Mile, this event offers something for runners of all levels to enjoy. Whether you're chasing a new personal best or simply looking to soak up the vibrant atmosphere, the Worthing RUN-FEST promises an unforgettable weekend of fitness and community.
