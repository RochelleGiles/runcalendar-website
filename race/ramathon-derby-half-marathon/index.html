Course

A flat, fast city half marathon starting and finishing at Silk Mill Park beside Derby Cathedral, taking in Pride Park Stadium on the way round. Water stations and energy stations are spaced along the route with medical cover near each. Pace groups run for a range of finish times.

About

The Ramathon is Derby's city half marathon, staged each summer on closed roads through the city centre and out past Pride Park Stadium. The route is flat and fast, drawing club runners chasing times alongside a large charity field, with pace groups and crowd support along the way.
