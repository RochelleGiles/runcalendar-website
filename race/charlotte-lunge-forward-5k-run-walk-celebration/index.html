About

The Charlotte LUNGe Forward 5K is a run, walk, and celebration event that brings together survivors, caregivers, and the community to honor those impacted by lung cancer. Proceeds support the Lung Cancer Initiative's efforts for survivors, caregivers, health care providers, and researchers.
