Course

The marathon starts on Exeter Quayside and follows the Exe Estuary; the half marathon joins at Dawlish Warren. Both then take the sea wall to Dawlish and the South West Coast Path to Teignmouth, crossing Shaldon Bridge before finishing at Babbacombe Downs. Mainly flat tarmac and gravel paths early on, turning to hilly coastal trail.

About

The City to Sea Marathon and Half Marathon offer runners a scenic coastal route along the Exe Estuary and South West Coast Path. The marathon starts in Exeter and follows the estuary to Dawlish Warren, where the half marathon begins. Both routes then continue along the sea wall and trails to the finish line at Babbacombe Downs, providing stunning views of the English countryside and coastline.
