About

The Lois & Larry C. Bolden 5K Fun Run/Walk is an annual event organized by Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc.®, Theta Theta Omega Chapter and 20 Pearls Foundation, Inc. to honor the legacy of Lois and Larry Bolden, two pioneering figures in the Las Vegas community.
