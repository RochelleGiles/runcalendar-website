Course

A 5K walk/run on the UCLA campus, starting and finishing at Wilson Plaza, 120 Westwood Plaza. The morning opens at 7:30am with a Fit Family Expo, activity tents and a kids zone, and the 5K starts at 9:00am on campus roads and paths.

About

The LA Cancer Challenge 5K is an annual walk/run in Los Angeles benefiting the Hirshberg Foundation for Pancreatic Cancer Research, held in partnership with UCLA. Now in its third decade, the event raises funds for pancreatic cancer research and patient support.
