About

The Südtiroler Erdäpfllauf (Potato Run) is a scenic 17.6km running event from Bruneck to Sand in Taufers at the foot of the Ahrntal glaciers. The course takes runners between castles through beautiful surroundings in South Tyrol, offering participants a unique racing experience in the Italian Alps.
