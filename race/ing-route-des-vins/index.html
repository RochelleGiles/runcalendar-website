About

The Route du Vin is Luxembourg's oldest road race, taking runners along the Luxembourg Moselle vineyards on a 21.1km half marathon. The route starts in Remich, heads to Stadtbredimus, passes back through Remich toward Schengen, and returns to Remich for the finish.
