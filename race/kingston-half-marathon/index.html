Course

Starts and finishes at Kingston Market Place, running out past Hampton Court Palace on a fast, flat route that mixes traffic-free riverside paths with road sections. The half marathon is UKA-certified and clearly marked. Water stations sit at 4.7, 8.6 and 11 miles and at the finish, with course toilets at 1, 5 and 8.6 miles.
