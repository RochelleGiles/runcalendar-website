Course

A half marathon loop starting and finishing on Usk Way outside the University of South Wales Newport campus, on road and pavement. The route crosses the SDR Bridge and Newport Bridge, passes the Clarence Place cenotaph and runs out along Caerleon Road to Caerleon village before returning by the River Usk, Millennium Bridge and river path. Water and toilets at three, six, nine and twelve miles.

About

A half marathon through Newport, South Wales, a city on the banks of the River Usk best known for its historic Transporter Bridge. The course mixes riverside path and city streets on generally flat terrain.
