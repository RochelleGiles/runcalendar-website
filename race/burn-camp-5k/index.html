Course

Staged at Barnes Park in Kaysville with the start area at Pavilion 1. The 5K is a two-loop course with three aid stations per loop serving water and electrolyte drink, starting at 9:00am. A one-mile kids run and walk covers a single loop with three aid stations and starts at 10:30am. Surface is not stated.

About

The Burn Camp 5K raises funds for the University of Utah Burn Center's camp for young burn survivors. The race is staged at Barnes Park in Kaysville, Utah, where the 5K runs two loops with three aid stations per loop. A one-mile kids run over a single loop follows later in the morning.
