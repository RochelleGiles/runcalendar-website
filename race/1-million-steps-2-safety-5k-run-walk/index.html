About

The 1 Million Steps 2 Safety 5K Run/Walk is hosted by the Louisiana Highway Safety Commission to promote pedestrian safety during Pedestrian Safety Month. The event features a 5K run/walk, resource fair, and jambalaya cook-off at the Louisiana State Police Headquarters in Baton Rouge.
