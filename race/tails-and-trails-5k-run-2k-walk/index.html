Course

A cross-country style course on the rolling grassy trails of Main Line Animal Rescue's farm at Chester Springs, opened to runners only for this event. Dogs are welcome on course. Runners set off at 9:00 AM and walkers at 9:15 AM from the same farm start.

About

Join us on Saturday, May 9, at Main Line Animal Rescue (1149 Pike Springs Road, Chester Springs, PA) for the Tails and Trails 5K Run/2K Walk. Enjoy music, raffle baskets, a 50/50 drawing, adoptable animals, a beverage garden, games, local vendors, and more.
