About

Nestled in the picturesque Norwegian countryside, the Ringeriksmaraton offers runners a truly unique and breathtaking experience. Winding through the stunning landscapes of the Ringerike region, this marathon challenges participants with a scenic yet demanding course that showcases the natural beauty of the area. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Ringeriksmaraton promises an unforgettable adventure.
