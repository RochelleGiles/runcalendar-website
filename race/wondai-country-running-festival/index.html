Course

Two course types from a common base near Wondai Town Hall. The Saturday 11.1km and 21.1km Forest Trail races run cross-country through McEuen forest on undulating trail through spotted gum and wattle woodland. The Sunday 5km, 10km, half and full marathon use the South Burnett Rail Trail, a fully sealed bitumen surface, with SES marshalling road crossings. Toilets and drink stations sit at regular intervals.

About

The Wondai Country Running Festival offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the stunning natural beauty of rural Australia. Set against the backdrop of the Wondai Country Festival, this event invites participants to test their limits on a course that winds through picturesque countryside, showcasing the vibrant local community.
