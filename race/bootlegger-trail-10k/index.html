About

The 8th annual Bootlegger 10K trail run will be held on Sunday, February 22nd, on upper Creve Coeur Park's Bootlegger's Run trail, which gets its name from the area's notorious reputation during Prohibition. Today, Creve Coeur Park has regained its pre-Prohibition notoriety as a beautiful locale for enjoying nature. The beautiful Bootlegger 10K course is primarily single-track trail and includes plenty of turns, switchbacks, and hills.
