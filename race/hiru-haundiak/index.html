About

Hiru Haundiak is one of the pioneering mountain ultra races in Spain, organized by Sociedad Excursionista Manuel Iradier since 1987. The course connects three of the most emblematic Basque mountain peaks: Gorbea (1481m), Anboto (1331m), and Aizkorri (1523m), each located in different Historical Territories and natural parks. The challenging route traverses from east to west between Araia and Murgia.
