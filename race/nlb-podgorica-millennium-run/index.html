About

Through the bustling boulevards and quiet streets, from the old town center to the new settlements growing every day, across the center, Block Five, City Quarter, Zagorič, Maslina, Donja Gorica, Zabjelo, Ljubović and Stara Varoš, we connect the parts of Podgorica into one story that is run. Our goal is for you to get to know every meter of Podgorica. Welcome to the unique Podgorica Marathon.
