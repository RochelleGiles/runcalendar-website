Course

Lapped races on Cheshire country lanes starting at Mickley Hall and finishing at the event village beside the Hack Green Secret Nuclear Bunker. The half marathon runs two loops with under 200 feet of climb and the 20 miler three loops with under 300 feet, making both flat and fast. Roads are not closed. Two water stations serve each lap.

About

The Secret Half is the perfect race for getting that PB! It's got the same elevation gain as Crewe parkrun. The Secret 20 miler, with only 280ft of climb over 20 miles, is ideally placed for a spring marathon - only 5 weeks before Manchester and 6 weeks before London, perfect to see how your marathon prep is going!
