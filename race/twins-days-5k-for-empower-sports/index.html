About

A 5K run and walk held on the Sunday morning of the Twins Days Festival in Twinsburg, Ohio, starting and finishing at Dodge Intermediate School opposite the festival site. Proceeds go to Empower Sports, which runs sports and exercise programmes for children and adults with physical and cognitive disabilities. A short kids' fun run is held on the track.
