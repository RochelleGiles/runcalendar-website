Course

A one-mile loop on city streets circling Elmhurst University, relatively flat. It starts on Elm Park Avenue with a gentle downhill into a chicane section, runs north on Grace Street past a halfway clock, turns east on Alexander Street past the university stadium, then rises gently on Prospect to a flat finish at Wilder Mansion.

About

The Elmhurst Twilight Mile is a race for athletes who want to race on a fast 1-mile circuit in front of large crowds and on the same course the elite cyclists raced. It features Open, Elite Men, and Elite Women races powered by the Elmhurst Running Club.
