Course

A USA Track and Field certified 5K road course through downtown Brookings and surrounding residential streets, passing Pioneer Park, the children's museum and Hillcrest Park. The race starts on 4th Street and finishes at the courthouse on 6th Avenue. The course is marked and run on lightly trafficked streets.

About

The Beef & Eggs 5K is the 22nd annual running event held by the Prairie Striders Running Club in Brookings, SD. It features a 5K race starting at 8:00 AM followed by a free kids fun run at 9:00 AM for children 12 and under.
