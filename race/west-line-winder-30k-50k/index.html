Course

Loop courses starting and finishing at E. Main St and N. Court St in downtown Buena Vista. The 50K runs 30.6 miles with 4,300 ft of gain — 22 miles singletrack, 7 miles forest/gravel road, 1.5 miles pavement, high point 9,500 ft, 11-hour cutoff. The 30K is 18.1 miles, 2,450 ft, 88% singletrack, 10-hour cutoff.

About

With glorious views of the Collegiate Peaks, these races feature miles of buttery single track and forest roads, perfect for everyone from the first time trail runner to the veteran ultramarathoner. Although the Arkansas Valley is famously known for its high alpine peaks, it's a well kept secret that there's a stash of arid, desert style trails just outside of Buena Vista.
