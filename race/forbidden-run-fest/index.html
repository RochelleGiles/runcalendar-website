Course

Starts and finishes at Houston Playground, 900 Grakyn Lane, in Wissahickon Valley Park, Philadelphia. The half marathon, 10 miler and 10K run mostly on the smooth gravel of Forbidden Drive with rolling single-track sections and a short paved stretch. Aid stations sit 2-3 miles apart: one on the 10K, three on the 10 miler and four on the half.

About

The Forbidden Run Fest is a trail running event held in Wissahickon Valley Park, Philadelphia, celebrating the park's natural beauty and historic trails. It features races on a mix of smooth gravel Forbidden Drive and technical single-track sections, limited to 750 runners.
