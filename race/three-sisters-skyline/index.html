Course

The 50K runs to the edge of the Three Sisters Wilderness through Ponderosa pine, past ground scarred by wildfire, alpine meadow and alongside a mountain stream. The half marathon is a point-to-point with a net downhill that produces fast times. Both run on mountain trail in the Oregon Cascades.

About

The Three Sisters Skyline 50K and half marathon climb into the Cascade Range near Sisters, Oregon, with alpine trail views of the Three Sisters volcanoes. Expect rugged, high-elevation singletrack.
