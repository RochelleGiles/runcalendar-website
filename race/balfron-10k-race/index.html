Course

Out-and-back 10K on closed roads through the Stirlingshire countryside, starting and finishing at Balfron Campus with an 11am start. The course is measured and run under a Scottish Athletics permit, and is open to runners aged 15 and over.

About

The Balfron 10K is a scenic closed-road race through the beautiful Stirlingshire countryside. Open to runners of all abilities, the event starts and finishes at Balfron Campus and is run under a Scottish Athletics permit.
