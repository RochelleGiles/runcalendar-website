About

The Media Maratón Vera is organized by the Club de Atletismo Sureste and the Ayuntamiento de Vera, and is now past its 11th running. Athletes from across the province of Almería tackle an urban circuit of two laps to complete the 21.097-metre half marathon distance, starting and finishing on Avenida Puerto Rey.
