Course

A double out-and-back on the path beside the Barwon River, starting at Barwon Valley Park in Belmont, running to Breakwater, then out to Fyansford and back. Drink stations and toilets are placed at points along the route. The organiser does not state the surface beyond describing it as a riverside path.

About

The annual Geelong Half Marathon is held on a fast, scenic course around the beautiful Barwon river in Geelong. The Half Marathon course runs along the Barwon River, from Belmont, to Breakwater and then up to Fyansford and back to Belmont.
