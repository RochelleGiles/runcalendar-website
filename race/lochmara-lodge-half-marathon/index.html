About

The Lochmara Lodge Half Marathon is a point-to-point race in the Marlborough Sounds. Runners and walkers board a catamaran at Picton for a one-hour cruise to the start at Anakiwa, then follow the course to the finish at Lochmara Lodge, where prizegiving and lunch are held. Entry is capped at about 200.
