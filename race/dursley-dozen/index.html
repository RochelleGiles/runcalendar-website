Course

Starts on May Lane in Dursley and finishes on pedestrianised Parsonage Street at the Market Hall. The 12-mile route climbs steeply up Hill Road through woodland onto Stinchcombe Hill Golf Course, runs undulating trail to Drakestone Point, drops sharply to Waterley Bottom and climbs again around Breakheart Quarry. Underfoot is muddy, stony and rooted woodland path with short road sections and just over 2,000 ft of climbing.

About

The Dursley Dozen is a challenging 12-mile multi-terrain running race set in the picturesque town of Dursley, Gloucestershire. Runners will test their endurance on a scenic course that winds through the rolling hills and countryside of this charming corner of the United Kingdom. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or looking for a new adventure, the Dursley Dozen offers an unforgettable running experience.
