Course

Start and finish at Eric Creary Park on the north side of Shellharbour Surf Club, 115 Junction Road. The marathon is a two-loop course described as relatively flat; the half marathon covers one lap of the same route, and the 10 km and 5 km are single-lap courses. Drink stations with water and energy drink sit roughly every 2.5 km, with hydration at the finish.

About

Run Shellharbour on the stunning South Coast of New South Wales offers a range of distances to challenge runners of all levels. From the grueling 42.2km marathon to the family-friendly 1km kids' run, this event has something for everyone. Runners can soak in the beautiful coastal scenery as they make their way through this picturesque seaside town.
