Course

An out-and-back on the North Fork Road beside the Flathead River, starting and finishing at the Big Creek Outdoor Education Center. The surface is undulating gravel road with gentle inclines, about 2,300 feet of climbing across the 50 miles. The route runs through Northern Rockies country on the edge of Glacier National Park.

About

Le Grizz is a 50-mile ultramarathon run on gravel roads along the North Fork Flathead River near Glacier National Park, through remote grizzly bear habitat in northwest Montana. A relay, 15K and 5K are also offered.
