Course

A road race through the streets of Mangaung, starting and finishing at the Lemo Mall basement parking entrance on Dolphyn Street. Four distances are offered, from a 5 km fun run to the full marathon, which is a Comrades qualifier. The organisers expect a field of more than 6,000.

About

The Lemo Mall Marathon is a marathon-distance road race in Bloemfontein, capital of South Africa's Free State province. Held each May, the race is part of the city's road-running calendar and draws participants from across the province.
