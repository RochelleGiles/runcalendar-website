Course

Runs the Ullswater Way around the lake in the Lake District on rocky and gravel trail paths. The ultra adds the Lowther and Dalemain loops to a full lap; the Trail 20 is a straight circuit of the lake; the Trail 10 covers the final ten miles back to Glenridding. Ultra and 20 start at Jenkins Field, Glenridding, the 10 at Pooley Bridge, with feed stations along the route.

About

The Montane Ullswater Way Trail follows sections of the Ullswater Way, the waymarked path that circles Ullswater in the Lake District. Trail 10, Trail 20 and Trail Ultra distances take in the fells and shoreline around the lake.
