About

The Hermitage Saint Joseph Half Marathon is a picturesque running event set against the backdrop of historic architecture and stunning natural landscapes. Runners will wind through charming streets and tranquil trails, offering a truly unique and memorable experience for both seasoned athletes and newcomers alike. This annual race is a must-do for any runner seeking a scenic challenge in an idyllic European setting.
