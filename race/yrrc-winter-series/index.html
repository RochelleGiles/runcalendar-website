Course

A winter series of nine road and park races held at venues around York County, Pennsylvania, from November to February. Main events range from 5K to 10 miles, with a 1-mile race starting 30 minutes before each. Courses can change at short notice for weather or road works.

About

Want to run some hills around York County this winter with 200 – 300 of your best friends? If so, with nine race venues around the county, the York Road Runners Club Winter Series is for you. Each venue hosts a main event from 5K up to 10 miles as well as a one mile run.
