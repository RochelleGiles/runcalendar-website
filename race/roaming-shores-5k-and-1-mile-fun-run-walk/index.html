Course

Both races are flat loops around Roaming Shores Village, starting and finishing at the Clubhouse parking lot on Roaming Rock Blvd. The 5K loops the village and the 1-mile follows Roaming Rock Boulevard. Both start at 8:00 AM, with day-of registration from 7:00 to 7:50 AM.

About

The Roaming Shores 5K and 1-mile fun run take place over Independence Day weekend in Roaming Shores, Ohio, on flat loops around the lakeside village. Proceeds benefit local Safety Services and the STARS Club.
