Course

A chip-timed 10K and 5K through the countryside around Dromtrasna near Abbeyfeale, County Limerick, with a 4K walk and a 2K kids' run. The kids' race goes at 10:00am and the main events at 11:00am. Proceeds go to Milford Hospice.

About

The Dromtrasna Challenge is an annual charity run through the countryside near Abbeyfeale in County Limerick, with 10K and 5K options. It began as a community fundraiser under the banner 'Take That Step' and has grown into a fixture on the local racing calendar.
