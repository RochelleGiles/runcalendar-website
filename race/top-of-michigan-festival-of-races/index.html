Course

Races run as out-and-backs from Petoskey's waterfront, starting at Bayfront Park and following the Little Traverse Wheelway non-motorised trail along Little Traverse Bay. The route is mostly paved, with some sections on grass and a short off-road stretch on dirt.

About

The Top of Michigan Festival of Races is a multi-distance road event based at Bayfront Park in Petoskey, Michigan. Half marathon, 10K and 5K fields run out and back along the Little Traverse Wheelway beside Little Traverse Bay, on a mostly paved route.
