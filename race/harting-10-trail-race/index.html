Course

A ten-mile multi-terrain route from South Harting over private tarmac lanes, hard gravel tracks and paths. It climbs to the top of the Downs, drops towards Compton, runs on to Chalton and Ditcham Park School, then picks up a stretch of the South Downs Way before descending to finish on the sports field. A 2hr15 time limit applies.

About

A challenging multi-terrain 10 mile trail race in beautiful countryside in and around the South Downs National Park. The terrain includes private lanes, gravel tracks, bridleways and footpaths.
