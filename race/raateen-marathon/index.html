About

Raatteen Maraton is a historically significant marathon event that takes place along the historic Raate Road battlegrounds in Finland. The event serves as a tribute to war veterans who fought for Finnish independence, combining athletic achievement with historical remembrance. The course traverses emotionally significant terrain that holds deep meaning in Finnish military history.
