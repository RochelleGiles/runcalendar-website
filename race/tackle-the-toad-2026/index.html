About

Tackle the Toad is a trail ultra hosted on Toad Mountain / Morning Mountain just outside Nelson, British Columbia. The event focuses on mountain trail running through cedar forests, technical singletrack and exposed alpine ridge running with expansive views of Kootenay Lake.
