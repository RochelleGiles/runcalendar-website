About

Discover the rugged beauty of Inis Meáin, one of Ireland's Aran Islands, at the annual Inis Iron Meáin running event. Challenge yourself on a scenic course that winds through the island's unique limestone landscape, offering stunning views of the Atlantic Ocean. This community-driven race celebrates the island's rich cultural heritage and invites runners to immerse themselves in its tranquil, island atmosphere.
