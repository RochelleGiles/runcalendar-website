Course

The half marathon is a single loop starting near 8,700ft and climbing above 9,500ft through the Arapaho National Forest, almost entirely on remote dirt roads, with aid roughly every 3.5 miles. The 5K uses dirt roads and trails near the Fraser Rodeo Grounds.

About

The Byers Peak Stampede is a trail half marathon and 5K near Fraser, Colorado, run on dirt roads through the Arapaho National Forest at altitude. It is organised by the Shining Stars Foundation, and proceeds fund outdoor programmes for children affected by cancer.
