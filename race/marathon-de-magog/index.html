Course

Road races beside Lac Memphrémagog in Magog, Québec, in the Eastern Townships, run for the autumn colours. The marathon, 10km and 5km start at Parc des Braves on rue Principale Ouest; the half marathon starts at the Bowen/Des Pins intersection. A 1km children's race starts and finishes at Parc des Braves.
