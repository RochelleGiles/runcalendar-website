About

The Backside Trail Marathon and Half, organized by Quest Outdoors, runs through Cherokee Park the day after Louisville's mini marathon on the roads. The single-track trails run through one of the original parks designed by Frederick Law Olmsted, and runners who complete both races over the weekend are classed as Double Down entrants.
