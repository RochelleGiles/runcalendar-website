Course

Run entirely on gravel and forest service roads through Rothrock State Forest near Boalsburg, with no singletrack at any point. The rolling course is marked throughout and served by aid stations at the relay transition zones. Offered as a solo ultra or as a team relay over the same route.

About

Tussey mOUnTaiNBACK sends runners and relay teams along Tussey Ridge in central Pennsylvania's Rothrock State Forest, a point-to-point ultramarathon on rolling gravel roads that finishes after dark.
