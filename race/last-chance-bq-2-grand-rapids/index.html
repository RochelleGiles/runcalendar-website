Course

Six-plus laps of a 4.05-mile paved loop at Secchia Meadows, Millennium Park, Grand Rapids, crossing a footbridge and winding along the Grand River each lap. The USATF-certified course (MI18031MN) is mostly flat with a gradual rise between miles 2.5 and 3.5 of each loop. 7:00am start with a soft 4.5-hour limit.

About

A flat, multi-lap marathon designed for runners chasing Boston qualifying times, held on a 4.05-mile paved loop at Millennium Park's Secchia Meadows in Grand Rapids. The USATF-certified course crosses a footbridge and follows the Grand River each lap, with a 7:00am start and a soft 4.5-hour time limit.
