Course

An Everesting-format race on Cannonsburg Ski Hill at Belmont, Michigan: repeated short loops up the Cannonsburg Quad ski run and down the Double run, plus optional two-mile wooded singletrack loops runners can take any time. The 100-miler carries over 30,000 ft of climbing and the 50-miler over 15,000 ft. Base camp aid comes round every 0.6 miles.

About

The Midwest Everest is, without a doubt, the most difficult 100-mile run in West Michigan. With over 30,000 feet of ascent and an equal amount of descent, runners should prepare to have their legs, mind, and spirit challenged.
