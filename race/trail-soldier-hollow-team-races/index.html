Course

A team trail relay based at the Soldier Hollow Nordic Center near Midway, Utah, at around 5,400 ft in the Wasatch Mountains. Teams cover 106.4 miles over three looped trails — smooth singletrack, rolling doubletrack and a rugged ridgeline — with views over Heber Valley, Mount Timpanogos and Deer Creek Reservoir.

About

Conquer the ridgelines of Soldier Hollow with sweeping views of Mount Timpanogos and Deer Creek Reservoir. Just an hour from Salt Lake City, this Olympic venue blends epic trails with alpine beauty.
