About

The Ogwen Yr Helgi Du Race (the Black Hound) is a thrilling mountain race set against the dramatic backdrop of Eryri's Ogwen Valley in Wales. Runners will tackle epic trails and technical terrain as they push themselves to the limit in this challenging yet rewarding event. With stunning natural scenery and a unique course, the Ogwen Yr Helgi Du Race promises an unforgettable running experience.
