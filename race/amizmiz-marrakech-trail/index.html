About

The Amizmiz Marrakech Trail is an exciting off-road running event set against the stunning backdrop of Morocco's Atlas Mountains. Runners will traverse scenic trails and remote villages, immersing themselves in the rich culture and breathtaking landscapes of this unique region. This challenging yet rewarding race offers an unforgettable adventure for trail enthusiasts.
