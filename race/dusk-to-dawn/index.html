About

Conquer the night at the Dusk to Dawn running event in Toogoolawah, Australia. Runners of all levels can challenge themselves on a 1km loop course at the local Showgrounds, with timed and distance-based races through the evening hours. This unique event offers an exhilarating opportunity to push your limits under the stars.
