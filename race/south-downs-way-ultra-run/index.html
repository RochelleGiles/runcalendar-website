Course

A 30-mile trail ultra starting and finishing at Harting Down in West Sussex, taking in some of the best of the South Downs Way and the country around it — Beacon Hill, Cocking Down and the ancient yews of Kingley Vale — with sections of the Sussex Border Path, Shipwrights Way and Staunton Way. Hilly, with steeper climbs most runners hike.
