Course

Three road-based distances from Washington City, Utah. The point-to-point half marathon starts north of Coral Canyon and runs mostly on asphalt and paved trail, with one short dirt and gravel stretch on the Cottonwood Trail, before dropping to the Virgin River and finishing at Sullivan Park. The 10K and 5K are out-and-backs from Sullivan Park along the Virgin River Trail and boardwalk.

About

Dogtown Races is a multi-distance running event held in Washington City, Utah, featuring half marathon, 10K, 5K, and kids races on mixed road and trail terrain. The races start at different times from Sullivan Virgin River Soccer Park and finish at Staheli Family Farm, benefiting local charities like the Homeless Animal Rescue Team.
