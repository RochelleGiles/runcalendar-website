About

The Hennepin Hundred is Illinois' only point-to-point, all-trail ultramarathon, a true Illinois adventure from Sterling, Illinois on the Rock River to Colona, Illinois near the mighty Mississippi, along the historic and beautiful Hennepin Canal. Flat and fast, the 50- and 100-mile courses follow the Hennepin Canal State Trail, Illinois' longest multi-use trail, across historic locks and lift bridges, past the autumn corn, picturesque farms and postcard towns of northwest Illinois.
