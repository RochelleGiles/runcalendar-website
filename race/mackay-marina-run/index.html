Course

Starts at Mackay Marina and runs through the coastal city of Mackay on a course the organiser describes as flat. The 3km Kids Dash runs from the entrance to the Marina Wall down the coastline onto the Marina Breakwater, finishing at the boat ramp end of Marina Village, with one water station.

About

Experience the scenic Mackay Marina Run in Queensland, Australia. This popular event offers a range of distances, including a challenging Half Marathon, a 10km race, a 5km fun run, and a 3km kids dash, making it an enjoyable event for runners of all levels. Participants can take in the stunning coastal views as they make their way through this picturesque location.
