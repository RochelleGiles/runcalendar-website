Course

Twelve-kilometre trail race along the Skyline Trail in the Blue Hills Reservation at Milton, Massachusetts. The route is rocky, rooty singletrack with repeated short steep climbs over the reservation's hills.

About

The TARC Blue Hills Skyline Trail Run covers 12K on the rocky, rooted Skyline Trail through Blue Hills Reservation, just south of Boston. Organized by the Trail Animals Running Club, the course crosses several of the reservation's rugged summits.
