Course

Five-mile road race starting at Silk Mill Park by Cathedral Green in Derby at 09:30. It follows the Ramathon half marathon route through the city centre for the first two miles, turns out towards Pride Park Stadium and returns to finish back at Silk Mill Park.

About

The RAM 5 Mile is a closed-road race through the streets of Derby, run alongside the Derbion Ramathon half marathon as part of Run For All's summer race weekend. It's a shorter, more approachable distance for runners chasing a fast time or a first race.
