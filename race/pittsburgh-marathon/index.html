About

The Pittsburgh Marathon runs through downtown Pittsburgh and several of its riverside neighborhoods, crossing bridges over the city's three rivers. Runners can choose the full marathon, half marathon, a relay, a 5K, or a 4-mile race, all on the same race weekend.
