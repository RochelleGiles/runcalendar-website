About

Foulées des Battages runs through Gaillan-en-Médoc in the Gironde, in the heart of the Médoc wine region north of Bordeaux. Runners choose the 15K, 8K or 3.5K route past vineyards and countryside, held around the traditional harvest 'battages' festival.
