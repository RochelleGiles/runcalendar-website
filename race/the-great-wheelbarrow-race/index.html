Course

Run over three days along the Wheelbarrow Highway in Far North Queensland. Day one covers Mareeba to Dimbulah, day two Dimbulah to Almaden with a lunch stop at Petford, and day three Almaden to Chillagoe, where the finish presentations are held. Teams stay overnight at the end of the first two stages.

About

Teams of runners push a wheelbarrow in relay along the Wheelbarrow Highway in Far North Queensland, covering the route over three days each May. Entry is by team rather than by individual, with overnight stops in Dimbulah and Almaden before the finish at Chillagoe.
