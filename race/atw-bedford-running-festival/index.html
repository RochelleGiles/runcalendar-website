Course

A flat, fast, traffic-free course based in Bedford town centre. The festival runs across two days and offers 5K, Mini Miles, a Twilight 10K, a half marathon and a 20-mile race; the 20 starts at 9:30am on the Sunday alongside the half marathon.

About

The ATW Bedford Running Festival is a must-attend event for runners seeking a scenic and challenging course. Set against the picturesque backdrop of Bedford, this festival offers a unique opportunity to push your limits and enjoy the vibrant running community. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a beginner, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
