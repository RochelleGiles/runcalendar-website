Course

A 24-hour ultra starting with a mass start at noon from the Nova Centre in Prestatyn and heading south along the Offa's Dyke Path. There is no fixed finish line: runners cover as much of the trail as they can and stop wherever they have reached at noon the following day.

About

24 Hours South is a timed ultra-endurance race along the Offa's Dyke Path, the ancient earthwork marking the historic border between Wales and England. The route heads south from Prestatyn on the North Wales coast for as much distance as runners can cover in 24 hours.
