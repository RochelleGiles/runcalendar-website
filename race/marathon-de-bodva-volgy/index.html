About

Explore the serene Bódva Valley as you test your endurance at the Marathon de Bódva-Völgy. This picturesque race winds through the rolling hills and charming villages of northeastern Hungary, offering runners a unique challenge amidst stunning natural scenery. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or looking to conquer your first 26.2, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
