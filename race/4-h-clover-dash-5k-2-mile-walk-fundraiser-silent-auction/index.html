About

Anoka County 4-H is hosting our annual 4-H Clover Dash 5K and 2-mile walk fundraiser and silent auction. Run or walk to support this fundraiser for Anoka County 4-H! Funds will support healthy living programs, community service projects and scholarships for youth. We also have a virtual option for those who want to participate on their own or don't live in the area.
