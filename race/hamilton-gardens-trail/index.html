Course

Start and finish are both inside Hamilton Gardens. The course meanders through the gardens, heads out to the Waikato River and follows the undulating river banks through native bush. Distances are 6km, 12.3km and 16.5km. The organiser states the route is on 100% paved track.

About

Located in the Centre of Hamilton and nestled on the side of the mighty Waikato River, Hamilton Gardens offers picturesque views and wonderfully manicured gardens. Both the start and finish line are sat within the gardens with the course meandering throughout the gardens out towards the Waikato River. You will then trail along the undulating river banks lined with lush native bush.
