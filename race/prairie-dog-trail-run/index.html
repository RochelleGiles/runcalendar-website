Course

A trail race run entirely inside Prairie Moraine County Park at 6705 Wesner Road, Verona, over 170 acres of restored prairie and oak savanna on varied, well-maintained trails. Both a 5K and a 10K route are offered. On-site restrooms, an aid and first-aid station, and post-race refreshments are provided.

About

The Prairie Dog Trail Run follows wooded and prairie trails at Prairie Moraine County Park in Verona, Wisconsin, home to one of the area's most popular off-leash dog parks, with 10K and 5K distances on offer.
