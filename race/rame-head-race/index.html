Course

A circular 10.6 km lap around the Mount Edgcumbe peninsula, starting and finishing at Mount Edgcumbe, Cremyll. The route runs mostly on coast path with a good mix of hills and flatter, faster sections; the half marathon covers two laps and the marathon four. The course is fully marked and marshalled with kilometre markers throughout.

About

The Rame Head Race is a stunning 10.6K run through the breathtaking Mount Edgecumbe Peninsula in Cornwall, United Kingdom. Participants will be treated to stunning coastal views as they wind their way around this single loop course, making for an unforgettable running experience. Whether you're looking for a challenging race or a scenic tour of this picturesque region, the Rame Head Race is not to be missed.
