Course

Hybrid road and off-road course starting and finishing at Huntly Speedway on McVie Road. The 21.1 km route runs through and around Lake Kimihia, loops Lake Hakanoa, then follows parts of the original Huntly course back to the Speedway. The organiser states the half marathon is 14 km off-road and 7 km road. Toilets at start/finish and at 10 km.

About

The BAYLEYS Huntly Half Marathon is New Zealand's oldest and most iconic half marathon event. The new hybrid course takes runners through the scenic areas of Huntly, including the Huntly Speedway, Lake Kimihia, and Lake Hakanoa, offering a unique and memorable running experience.
