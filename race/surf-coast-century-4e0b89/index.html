Course

Point-to-point coastal ultra from Anglesea taking in clifftop trail below sea cliffs, remote beach running including Bells Beach at dawn, and single track through wildflower heathland and hinterland forest. The 100 km carries about 1,800 m of climb and is run solo or as a relay of 2 to 4; the 50 km covers legs 3 and 4. Seven stocked aid checkpoints support the course.

About

The Surf Coast Century takes place on the beaches of Anglesea, Victoria. Runners can tackle the 100km solo or as a relay team, or take on the 50km, crossing trail below sea cliffs, remote beaches and wildflower hinterland.
