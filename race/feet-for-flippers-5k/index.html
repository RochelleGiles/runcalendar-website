Course

A USATF-measured 5K starting promptly at 10:00am, with parking in the Buzzards Bay Recreation Area lot off Main Street. Chip timing is provided, and a virtual 5K option runs alongside.

About

This charity 5K starts at the National Marine Life Center in Bourne and raises funds for the nonprofit's work rehabilitating cold-stunned sea turtles and treating sick or injured seals. Age-group awards are presented after the race, which runs rain or shine each October.
