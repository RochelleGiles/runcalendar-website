Course

A modified point-to-point on the Snoqualmie Valley Trail, starting on SE North Bend Way and finishing at Meadowbrook Farm in North Bend. The surface is entirely unpaved, smooth hard-packed dirt and gravel, with a gentle climb over the first four miles and about nine miles of net downhill to the finish.

About

A half marathon in North Bend, Washington, following the flat, converted rail bed of the historic Iron Horse (Palouse to Cascades) Trail through forest along the Snoqualmie River valley. Organized by Orca Running.
