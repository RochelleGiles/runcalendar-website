About

The Traversee des Dentelles is a trail weekend based in Gigondas in the Vaucluse, running through the limestone ridges of the Dentelles de Montmirail and the vineyards below them. The programme spans two days and five events, from the short Petite Gambade and Gambade and a sport walk up to the Traversee itself and the Maratrail des Dentelles.
