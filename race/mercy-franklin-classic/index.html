Course

All distances start and finish on the Downtown Public Square in Franklin. The 10K goes at 6:30 AM, the 5K at 8:00 AM and a 1K kids run at 9:00 AM, with a 15K combo for those running both. The course takes wheelchair entrants, and aid stations and toilets are set around the square.

About

A Labor Day morning road race on the Downtown Public Square in Franklin, Tennessee, raising funds for Mercy Community Healthcare. Entrants choose a 10K, a 5K, both together as a 15K combo, or a 1K kids run, and wheelchair athletes are accommodated.
