About

The Woodstock Warrior Race & Relay is a 100-mile, 50-mile, or 10-mile ultramarathon held at Seven Bends State Park in Woodstock, Virginia, named for the horseshoe-shaped bends the North Fork of the Shenandoah River carves through the Massanutten mountains.
