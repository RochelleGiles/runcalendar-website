About

Come celebrate our 13th annual Bacon5Kegs and the Bacon and Brews Festival on INTERNATIONAL BACON DAY, Saturday, September 5th & 6th. The festival will Feature Music, Unique Bacon Dishes, Craft Beer, a 5K Run/Walk(Sept 6th), and Kite Flying! There's something for everyone.
