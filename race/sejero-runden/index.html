Course

Three road routes on the island of Sejerø — a 5 km blue route, a 10 km yellow route and a half marathon red route — all following the island roads through open countryside. Start and finish are on the athletic field at Sejerø School, which provides changing rooms, bag storage and swimming. A clubhouse alongside has tents, toilets and refreshments before and after the race.

About

Explore the picturesque island of Sejerø as you take on the Sejerø Runden, a must-run event for any seasoned or aspiring runner. This scenic route winds through the island's charming countryside, offering stunning views of the Baltic Sea and the chance to immerse yourself in the local Danish culture. Whether you're looking for a challenging race or a scenic tour, the Sejerø Runden is sure to leave a lasting impression.
