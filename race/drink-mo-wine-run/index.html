Course

Starts in the hilly town of Augusta and tours its historic streets before dropping onto the flat Katy Trail. The 5K and 10K turn around on the trail and return; the half marathon continues into Klondike Park on wooded trails. Surfaces are mixed paved and trail. Water and electrolyte drink are on course for all distances.

About

The Drink MO Wine Run starts and finishes at Mount Pleasant Winery in Augusta, Missouri. Routes climb through the town's historic streets before dropping onto the flat Katy Trail; half marathoners add wooded trail in Klondike Park. Water and electrolyte drink are provided on course.
