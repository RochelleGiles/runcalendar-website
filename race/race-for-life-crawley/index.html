Course

A double-lap 5K around Tilgate Lake and Nature Reserve, crossing tarmac hardstanding, grass, gravel and woodland. There are a few inclines, declines and uneven surfaces on the woodland paths, and the start and finish are on uneven, sloping grass that can turn soft in wet weather.

About

Race for Life is Cancer Research UK's charity fundraiser, with 10K and 5K options for walkers, joggers and runners of any pace. This Crawley edition takes place in Tilgate Park, known for its lakes and woodland nature reserve.
