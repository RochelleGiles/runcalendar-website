About

The Sole to City Half Marathon is a point-to-point race starting at Second Sole on Route 224, running through scenic Mill Creek Park, and finishing in downtown Youngstown. It features a celebration village at Penguin City Brewing with a 2-Mile Run/Walk and free Kids Run for children 12 and under.
