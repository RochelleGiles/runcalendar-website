About

The first Tuggeranong Running Festival, at Lake Tuggeranong District Park in Greenway on Sunday 7 February 2027: a half marathon, a 10 km, a 5 km and a 2 km family dash. The 10 km starts at 6:30, the half marathon at 6:55, the 5 km at 8:30 and the family dash at 9:15, with bib collection from 5:30 on race morning. Every finisher gets a medal. Organised by Race Hub Australia; online entries close on 11 January.
