About

The Windsor Half Marathon offers runners a scenic tour through the historic town of Windsor, with the iconic Windsor Castle serving as a stunning backdrop. This annual event is a must-do for runners seeking a challenging yet picturesque course, set against the beautiful landscapes of the United Kingdom.
