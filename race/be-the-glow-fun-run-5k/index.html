About

The Be The Glow Fun Run-5K is a family-friendly community event with a powerful purpose. Families near and far come together for one common goal: fighting food insecurity in our community. This annual fundraiser directly supports Lotz of Blessings' food insecurity programs, including Pack-A-Snack, Brown Bag Buddies (PB&J), and Blessings Boxes.
