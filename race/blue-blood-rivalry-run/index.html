About

The Blue Blood Rivalry Run is a 5K fun run/walk/dribble around the streets and paths of Herndon Park next to the American Tobacco Trail in Durham, NC. It supports TROSA, a nonprofit providing treatment and recovery services for individuals with substance use disorders, with all profits donated to the charity.
