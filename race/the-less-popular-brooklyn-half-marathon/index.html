About

The Less Popular Brooklyn Half Marathon is a small-field alternative to the borough's larger spring half, staged by Trimara Sports in Bay Ridge. Runners complete three loops of the Shore Road Promenade beside the Narrows, starting and finishing at the American Veterans Memorial Pier.
