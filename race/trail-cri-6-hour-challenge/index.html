About

Experience the stunning natural beauty of Ireland's Kippure Estate during the Trail Criú 6 Hour Challenge. This captivating trail running event invites adventurous runners to push their limits on scenic, winding paths through the lush, rolling hills of County Wicklow. Whether you're seeking a personal challenge or a chance to connect with the vibrant trail running community, the Trail Criú 6 Hour Challenge offers an unforgettable day on the trails.
