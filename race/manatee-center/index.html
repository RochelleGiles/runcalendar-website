About

The Friends of the Manatee Center 5K is an annual run in Fort Pierce, Florida, pairing a five kilometre race with a one mile event and a children's run. It is organised by the Friends of the Manatee Center and raises funds for the centre's environmental education work on the Indian River Lagoon.
