About

The St. Patrick's Day 5K is an annual themed chip-timed run/walk open to all ages and abilities, with proceeds benefiting the Santa Rosa Recreation & Parks Scholarship Fund. Participants receive a finisher medal and mug, followed by a post-race celebration featuring beer or root beer, costume contest, and activities.
