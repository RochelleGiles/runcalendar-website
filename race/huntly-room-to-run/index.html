Course

All races start in Huntly's town square, pass under the Simpson Building arch and run past Huntly Castle along a tree-lined avenue before crossing the Deveron. The 5K uses roads and paved footpaths; the 10K adds gravel and mud trails through Bin Forest; the half marathon mixes road with forestry roads around Bin Wood and several hills. All finish on Deveron Street.

About

Huntly Room to Run is a 10K road race starting from The Gordon Schools in the Aberdeenshire town of Huntly. The route mixes road and trail as it loops through the town and surrounding countryside.
