Course

A single sustained climb of the historic Mount Mansfield Toll Road: 4.3 miles with 2,564 feet of ascent, finishing high on Vermont's highest peak. The unpaved auto road is tackled by runners, hikers and cyclists in the same event, and a kids fun run is held alongside.

About

Race to the Top of Vermont climbs the historic Toll Road on Mt. Mansfield, Vermont's highest peak, from the base near Stowe to a finish near the summit ridge, gaining significant elevation over 4.3 miles.
