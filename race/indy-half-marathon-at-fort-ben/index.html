Course

Winds through historic Lawrence and Fort Harrison State Park on the northeast edge of Indianapolis, with a few hills breaking up plenty of flat stretches. Courses are USATF certified and the race starts at sunrise over the park.

About

The Indy Half Marathon at Fort Ben runs through Fort Harrison State Park, site of the former Fort Benjamin Harrison Army post in Indianapolis. Runners can choose the half marathon, 10K or 5K through the park's wooded grounds.
