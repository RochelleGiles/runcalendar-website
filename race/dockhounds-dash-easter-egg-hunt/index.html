Course

A very flat two-lap course with only about three feet of elevation change, starting and finishing at home plate inside WBC Park in Oconomowoc. The laps run around the Pabst Farms Business Park and through the Wisconsin Harley-Davidson site before a final crossing of the ballpark field.

About

Join Silver Circle Sports Events and the Lake Country DockHounds for the 4th Annual DockHounds Dash and Easter Egg Hunt! This one-of-a-kind race lets you experience WBC Park like never before, with a course that starts and finishes at home plate—a dream come true for runners and baseball fans alike!
