Course

The half marathon and 5K both run through Busse Woods Forest Preserve in Elk Grove Village. The half marathon starts at 7:00am and the 5K at 8:00am. Finishers receive blinking light medals.

About

A festive summer running event featuring a half marathon and 5K race with a Christmas theme. The event includes unique swag such as sublimated tech shirts and blinking light finisher medals, with multiple water stops and post-race celebrations.
