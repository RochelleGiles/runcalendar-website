Course

The half marathon runs around Marco Island and over the Goodland Bridge; both races start near Physicians' Regional on San Marco Road, the half at 7:00am and the 5K at 7:15am. A three-hour limit applies because of road restrictions, and strollers and dogs are not allowed.

About

The Marco Island Bridge Run Half Marathon is an annual running event that takes place on the beautiful island of Marco Island, Florida. Runners will have the opportunity to experience stunning views of the Gulf of Mexico and the Marco Island Bridge as they make their way through the course.
