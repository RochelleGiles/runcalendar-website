Course

A point-to-point road half marathon down Big Cottonwood Canyon, starting at Silver Fork Lodge at about 7,700 feet and dropping roughly 3,000 feet over some 11 miles of paved canyon road. USATF sanctioned, with a 6 AM start and aid stations at miles 2, 5, 7, 10 and 12. Shorter 10K and 5K options run the same canyon.
