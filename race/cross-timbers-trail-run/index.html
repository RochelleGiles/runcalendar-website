About

The Cross Timbers Trail Run, now past its 45th running, bills itself as the oldest trail run in Texas. The rugged course follows the shoreline of Lake Texoma on the Texas side of the Texas-Oklahoma border, known for some of the best running temperatures Texas sees all year.
