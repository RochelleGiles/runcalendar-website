Course

Entirely off-road on ranch roads and single-track at Figueroa Mountain, Los Olivos. Two ten-mile loops meet at a central hub: the pink loop climbs about 2,100 feet, the yellow about 1,600 feet. Aid stations sit roughly every five miles and the route is ribbon-marked throughout.

About

Born to Run Ultra Marathons is a multi-day trail ultramarathon held in Los Olivos, California, in the Santa Ynez Valley. Runners can choose distances from 10 miles up to 100 miles across the event's several days. The race takes place on trails in the region's oak-studded foothills.
