Course

Trail races through the indigenous forests and mountain passes of Magoebaskloof near Tzaneen, with river crossings. Five distances from 10 km to 100 km run over three days; the 30 km is predominantly singletrack and the 60 km mixes singletrack with forestry roads.

About

The Hi-Tec Magoebas Ultra is a trail ultramarathon through the forested Magoebaskloof mountains near Tzaneen, Limpopo, with distances from 10km up to 100km. It has grown into a celebrated community trail event in the region.
