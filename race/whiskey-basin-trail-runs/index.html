Course

Runs on the Prescott Circle Trail from Watson Lake Park, Arizona, at around a mile of elevation. Underfoot is a mix of desert trail, pine and juniper forest, fast singletrack, technical granite and steep scrambles. The 92K covers the full loop and the shorter races take sections of it, with climbing from about 647ft on the 10K to 6,100ft on the 92K.

About

Trail running event taking place in and around Prescott, Arizona along the scenic Prescott Circle Trail that encompasses the entire town. Features multiple distance options from 10K to 92K with a mix of desert trails, rocky scrambles, and winding single track.
