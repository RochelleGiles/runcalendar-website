About

The Ilkley Half Marathon is a scenic 13.1-mile road race through the picturesque countryside of Ilkley, West Yorkshire. Runners will be treated to stunning views of the rolling hills and charming villages that make this region of the UK a popular destination for outdoor enthusiasts. This event is a must-do for any half marathon enthusiast looking for a challenging yet rewarding race experience.
