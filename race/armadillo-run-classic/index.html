About

The Armadillo Run is a premier event featuring flat, fast, scenic, and nationally certified 5K and 10K courses with chip timing. With a rich history, the event continues to draw several hundred runners and their families each year to enjoy the city of Oldsmar as they race along the shores of Tampa Bay.
