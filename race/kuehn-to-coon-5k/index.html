Course

A single 5K trail race at Kuehn Conservation Area near Earlham, Iowa. The course is a loop entirely on single and double track, passing through prairie, woodland and floodplain beside the Raccoon River.

About

Let us show off Kuehn Conservation on Saturday morning April 18th! The 5K trail race will take you through the prairie trails along the upland areas, as well as the woods and prairies along the Racoon River bottom within the park. Hosted by the Adel Rotary Club, the race proceeds help fund their local service projects.
