Course

A 6.71 km undulating fire-trail loop starting and finishing at the Blue Range Hut corral in the Brindabella Ranges west of Canberra. The lap takes in a rocky slope, wide forest road and named features including Mt GUMBY and Gumbilical Lane. Runners set off on the hour, every hour, until one remains.

About

The Great Ultra Mediocre Back Yarder (GUMBY) is a traditional last one standing race in the Brindabella Ranges to the west of Canberra. Runners complete a 6.7km lap on the hour every hour until only one person remains, with everyone else receiving a DNF.
