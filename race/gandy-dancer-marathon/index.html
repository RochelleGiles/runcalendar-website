About

The Gandy Dancer Marathon is run on a flat limestone surface of the Gandy Dancer State Trail, an old railroad bed between Luck and Frederic, Wisconsin. The race offers multiple distances including a full marathon, half marathon, 5K, marathon relay, and kids fun run, with proceeds benefiting the local volunteer fire department.
