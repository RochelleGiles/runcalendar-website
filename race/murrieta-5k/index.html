Course

A flat 5K on closed streets that encircles downtown Murrieta, running along Historic Route 395 and through the old main street with the foothills of former ranch country alongside. The race is based at Lily Kelly Ministries on Washington Avenue and is open to runners, walkers, strollers and dogs.

About

The Murrieta 5k is a community run and walk starting on Washington Avenue in Murrieta, California, now in its second year. Timed runners, walkers, strollers and leashed dogs are all welcome, and a virtual entry is offered; proceeds go to Lily Kelly Ministries.
