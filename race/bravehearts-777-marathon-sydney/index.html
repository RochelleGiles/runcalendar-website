About

The Bravehearts 777 Marathon is a unique and challenging event that takes runners on a journey through the iconic landmarks of Sydney. Covering a distance of 777 kilometers, this marathon is a true test of endurance and determination. The course showcases the city's stunning natural beauty, from the iconic Opera House to the serene harbor, providing runners with an unforgettable experience. The event's atmosphere is electric, with enthusiastic supporters lining the streets to cheer on the participants, making this a must-do event for any marathon enthusiast.
