Course

Repeating 5.2-mile loop in Swiss Valley Nature Preserve with about 875 feet of climb per lap; the 25K runs three loops and the 50K six. Rugged trail with creek crossings and steep inclines. Staggered starts at 7:00 am (50K), 8:00 am (25K) and 9:00 am (5.2 miler).

About

A trail running event featuring three distance options set against the backdrop of Swiss Valley Nature Preserve in Dubuque County, Iowa. The course includes creek crossings, steep inclines, and rugged terrain with approximately 750 feet of elevation gain per loop.
