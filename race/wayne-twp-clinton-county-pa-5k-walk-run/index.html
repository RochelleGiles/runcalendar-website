About

Fun 5K Walk/Run along the Susquehanna River in quaint McElhattan PA. Profits will go to Wayne Township Recreation Committee, which funds projects at the Wayne Township Nature Park and other parks located in Wayne Township in Clinton County PA. The parks provide walking paths and covered pavilions along with parking and bathroom availability during the regular season. The Rec Committee organizes Easter and Christmas events, Halloween fun and hayrides, and other celebrations throughout the year to local residents. This event is in conjunction with the America250PA celebration.
