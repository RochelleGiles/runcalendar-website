Course

A flat course through Burswood Park alongside the Swan River, with every distance finishing inside Optus Stadium rather than at a street finish line. The 4km dash, 10km, half marathon and marathon all share the same parkland and riverside route, the longer distances covering more of it.

About

The Perth Running Festival in Burswood, Western Australia offers a scenic and challenging course for runners of all levels. Featuring the iconic Perth Marathon as well as other distance options, this event is a must-do for those seeking a premier running experience in a vibrant city setting.
