About

An ultra trail event in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands near Howick, South Africa, with 20 Miler, 30 Miler, 50 Miler and 100 Miler distances. Routes cross Karkloof's forest, plantation and grassland trails in the hills above the Karkloof Valley.
