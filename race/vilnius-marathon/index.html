About

The Vilnius Marathon is the largest running event in Lithuania, attracting thousands of participants from around the country and the world. The course takes runners through the historic streets of Vilnius, showcasing the city's stunning architecture and landmarks. The event has a vibrant atmosphere, with enthusiastic spectators cheering on the runners and creating a memorable experience for all involved.
