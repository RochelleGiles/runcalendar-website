About

The NZ Ultramarathon Festival is a multi-day endurance event featuring a 2km loop course with zero elevation around Victoria and Albert Lakes in Hagley Park. The fully catered event allows participants to run, walk, and rest at their own pace, passing through 'The Hub' for food, drinks and rest after each loop. Set in the heart of Christchurch near the Botanical Gardens, this world record breaking course offers athletes a unique challenge in a supportive community atmosphere.
