Course

A loop course through South Minneapolis, starting and finishing at Beard's Plaisance on Upton Ave S. Runners follow the paved lakeside parkways around Lake Harriet and Bde Maka Ska. The field is capped at 1,500 entrants.

About

The City of Lakes Half Marathon winds along running paths that circle Minneapolis's chain of urban lakes, a route local runners know well from year-round training. The single half-marathon distance finishes in the heart of the city.
