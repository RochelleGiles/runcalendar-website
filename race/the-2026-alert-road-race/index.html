Course

All three races start and finish at Alert Covenant Church, 15820 Green Randolph Road, Leonardville. The 10K and 5K courses include both paved and gravel segments. The 1 Mile is an out-and-back on all-paved road. Water stations are available on all three courses.

About

This popular rural tradition will once again be held at the Alert Covenant Church, 15820 Green Randolph Road in Leonardville, Kansas--Rain or Shine! (It's Kansas--be prepared for both!) Come for the friendly competition and fellowship, stay for the post-race breakfast provided by the Alert Ladies!
