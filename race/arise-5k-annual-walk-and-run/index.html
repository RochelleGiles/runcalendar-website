About

The ARISE 5K Annual Walk/Run is a USATF-certified 5K oceanfront course in Santa Barbara that brings together hundreds of participants from the community, ages 6 to 90. The event benefits the Santa Barbara Rescue Mission's Bethel House Women's Residential Treatment Program.
