Course

Well marked and marshalled footpaths and bridleways starting and finishing at Coniston Sports and Social Club. The route runs from the shoreline of Coniston Water up through the historic Coppermines Valley workings, on hard-packed scenic trail with the occasional muddy section and one testing climb on the 16K routes, under the Old Man, Swirl How, Wetherlam and Dow Crags. A 10K, a 16K challenge, a 16K race and on-the-day fun trails for under-12s.

About

The event takes place from the shores of Coniston Water into the shadows of the Old Man, passing through the historic Coppermines Valley. The course follows well marked and marshalled footpaths and bridleways, offering panoramic views of Coniston Water and surrounding peaks including the Old Man, Swirl How, Wetherlam and Dow Crags. A carnival atmosphere is guaranteed with live music, race commentary, food and drink at the start and finish at the Coniston Sports and Social Club venue.
