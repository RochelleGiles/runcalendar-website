Course

A trail course at Fox Hill Nature Preserve, West Bend, winding through deciduous forest, restored prairie and glacial moraine terrain. The 10K is two laps of the 5K loop. A 1-mile trail walk and a Kiddos 1K run alongside. The course is not suitable for strollers or dogs.

About

Get outdoors and join CLCF in its 4th Annual Race For Open Space 10K Trail Run, 5K Trail Run/Walk, and 1-mile Trail Walk at CLCF's Fox Hill Nature Preserve! Your participation will help support CLCF's mission to protect the natural environment of the Cedar Lakes region.
