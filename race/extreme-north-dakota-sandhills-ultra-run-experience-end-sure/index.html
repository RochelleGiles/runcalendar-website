Course

A 25K, 50K and 100K starting and finishing at Jorgens Hollow Campground near McLeod, about 45 minutes southwest of Fargo. The routes follow the North Country Trail through the Sheyenne National Grasslands, crossing open prairie, pockets of forest and rugged rolling sandhills. The event is capped at 120 runners.

About

The Extreme North Dakota Sandhills Ultra Run Experience is North Dakota's first ultramarathon, run through the Sheyenne National Grasslands southwest of Fargo. The route mixes open prairie, forest pockets, and rugged rolling sandhills across its 50K and 100K distances.
