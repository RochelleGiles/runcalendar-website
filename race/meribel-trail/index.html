About

The Asics Méribel Trail is a mountain running weekend above Méribel in the Trois Vallées, Savoie. Eight formats are spread across three days, from a 500 m children's race and an open vertical kilometre to 5 km, 10 km, 25 km and 42 km trails on alpine paths.
