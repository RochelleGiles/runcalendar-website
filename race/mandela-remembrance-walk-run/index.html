About

The Mandela Remembrance Walk & Run, hosted by the Nelson Mandela Foundation each December, starts at the Union Buildings in Pretoria (Tshwane). Choose a 21 km run, a 10 km run or a 5 km walk. Race packs are collected beforehand, in Houghton, Johannesburg, or on the Union Buildings lawns the day before; there is no collection on race day.
