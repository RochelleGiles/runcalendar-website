Course

A perimeter tour of Lory State Park northwest of Fort Collins, starting and finishing at the Soldier Canyon Picnic Area. The 13.3-mile course covers about 12 miles of dirt singletrack and 1 mile of dirt road, climbing roughly 1,500 feet between miles two and six before a fast descent and rolling final third. Runners have 5.5 hours to finish.

About

A trail half marathon taking place on the wonderful trails of Lory State Park to the northwest of Fort Collins, Colorado, offering a perimeter tour with breathtaking views of Horsetooth Reservoir and the Rocky Mountains. The course covers 13.5 miles with approximately 2,000 feet of vertical gain/descent, featuring a mix of dirt roads and technical singletrack terrain.
