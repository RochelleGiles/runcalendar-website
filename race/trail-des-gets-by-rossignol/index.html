Course

A mountain trail race above Les Gets, running from open mountain pastures onto ridge trails with views of Mont Blanc and the Roc d'Enfer, and through shaded woodland. The route alternates technical sections with smoother stretches. The programme includes adult races, a junior race, children's races and a vertical challenge.

About

Explore the scenic trails of the French Alps at the Trail Des Gets by Rossignol, a captivating running event that winds through the picturesque village of Les Gets. Runners of all levels can immerse themselves in the stunning mountain landscapes and challenge themselves on the diverse terrain, making this a must-experience event for outdoor enthusiasts.
