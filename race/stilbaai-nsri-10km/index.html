Course

Starts and finishes at the NSRI building on Main Road, Still Bay West, at 07:00. The route follows quiet residential streets beside the Goukou lagoon and the beachfront, and the 10km adds a 3km loop on the hiking trail through the Melkhout forest in the Skulpiesbaai reserve. A shorter 5-6km option covers the road section only.

About

Explore the scenic seaside town of Stilbaai, South Africa, during the annual NSRI 10km run. This picturesque coastal event raises funds for the National Sea Rescue Institute, challenging runners with stunning ocean views and a course that winds through the charming local streets. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a newcomer to the sport, this community-driven race offers an unforgettable running experience.
