About

The Grand Raid des Pyrénées is a prestigious ultra trail event set in the Pyrenees mountains, based in Vielle-Aure. Independent from the World Trail Majors circuit, it focuses on its authentic identity and connection to the local territory, embodying values of engagement, ambition and responsibility while offering an exceptional mountain running experience.
