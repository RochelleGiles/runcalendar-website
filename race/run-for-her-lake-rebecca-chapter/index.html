About

Join us at our flagship chapter to walk, jog, or Run for HER. Together, we affirm the dignity of marginalized women and fundraise to help provide the resources they need to thrive. Whether you prefer a quiet journey or deep community connection, you'll find your place here. On event day, we all come together—celebrating, encouraging, and standing strong for the cause!
