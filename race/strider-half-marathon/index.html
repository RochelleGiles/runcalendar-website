Course

A paved, mostly asphalt half marathon starting and finishing at the Estabrook Park Beer Garden near the central car park in Milwaukee. The route loops through Estabrook Park then runs an out-and-back section on the Oak Leaf Bike Trail. The field is capped at 1,000 entries.
