Course

A double-race weekend in Somerset offering two point-to-point 50-milers, or both back to back as a 100. The Flat 50 runs canal, river and coast paths with a pancake-flat first half and an undulating second (about 2,128ft of climb); the Hilly 50 crosses rugged hills, ancient woodland and towering sea cliffs. Mostly trail with cycle-path and village sections.

About

Explore the stunning West Country of the United Kingdom at the West Country Ultra event. This challenging double-race weekend features two different 50-mile courses as well as a grueling 100-mile ultra, all set against the picturesque backdrop of Somerset. Whether you're a seasoned ultra-runner or looking to push your limits, this event offers an unforgettable running experience.
