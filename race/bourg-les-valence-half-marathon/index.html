About

The Bourg Les Valence Half Marathon is a picturesque running event set against the stunning backdrop of the French countryside. Runners will wind through charming villages and scenic landscapes, making this a must-do for any endurance athlete seeking a memorable European race experience. The course offers a challenging yet rewarding challenge for runners of all levels.
