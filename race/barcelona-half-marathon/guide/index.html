Barcelona Half Marathon

Barcelona · Spain
RoadUrbanHalf MarathonSold out

Europe's second-biggest half: a flat, fast loop from the Ciutadella to the Columbus monument, out through Poblenou and home along the seafront past the Port Olímpic.

Quick verdict

The fastest half marathon course on the calendar and the second biggest in Europe: 40,000 runners, 71 metres of climb in 21 kilometres, a Gold Label start list and the 56:42 that was briefly the fastest half ever run. Entries go in a week each May. Pick it for a February personal best with a city attached.

Course at a glance

71 mClimbing
8:30Start
2 h 45Cut-off

One urban loop from Passeig Picasso beside the Ciutadella. The first two kilometres run the harbour front to the Columbus monument, then the course turns inland up the Paral·lel to its western edge near Plaça d'Espanya at 4 km. The Gran Via brings you back east in one 2.5 km straight to the Arc de Triomf at 7 km, Poblenou follows past the Torre Glòries, and from 18 km the Avinguda Litoral carries you along the sea past the Port Olímpic to the finish on Passeig de Pujades.

Where you'll feel it

📍The Gran Via rise, kilometres 5 to 7

The only rise is the Gran Via up to the Arc de Triomf between 5 and 7 km, gentle but long. After it the Poblenou grid turns 25 corners in nine kilometres and breaks the rhythm you built on the straights. On a windy day the seafront at 18 km is where you feel it.

Best chance to push

The Avinguda Litoral from 18 km: three flat kilometres along the sea past the Port Olímpic, the biggest crowds of the morning, then the finish straight on Passeig de Pujades beside the park. More personal bests are set here than anywhere else on the course.

Three things to know

  1. Entry is first come, first served, no ballot, no charity places: 40,000 bibs sold in a week in May 2026. The next edition opens in May. Official bib transfers and the organiser's resale marketplace close on 15 January.
  2. Collect your bib at the expo on the Thursday, Friday or Saturday; nothing is handed out on race morning. Starts are staggered by the pace you gave at entry, pens are colour-coded, and the bag drop is split three ways by bib colour around the Ciutadella.
  3. Water and Aquarius at 5, 10, 15 and 17.5 km, gels from 10, and ten pace groups from 1:20 to 2:05. The cut-off is a generous 2 h 45 from the last starter. No showers at the finish; the party moves to the Poble Espanyol at one.

Race-day weather

40,000Field

Cool and usually dry: 7 to 9°C at the 8:30 start, 11 to 13 by the finish, rain on about one day in seven. The sea keeps the air humid; 2025 ran at 11°C with no wind.

What runners wear

Singlet or short sleeves and shorts, a throwaway layer for the pen, and racing flats: it is tarmac throughout and certified to the metre.

Typical finish times

Course records 56:42, the fastest half ever run when Jacob Kiplimo set it in 2025, and 1:04:01 for Loice Chemnung in 2026. Behind them 36,000 ran in 2026, 40 per cent women, 40 per cent from abroad, most of the field under 35.

Worth the trip?

Yes, for the fastest half you are likely to run and a city that treats the race as a festival: 25 cheer points, live television from first runner to last, and a finish beside the Ciutadella ten minutes' walk from the old town. Enter in May or you will be watching.
