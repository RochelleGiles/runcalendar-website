About

The Barcelona Half Marathon is a must-experience event for runners seeking a scenic and iconic urban challenge. Weaving through the vibrant streets and landmarks of the Catalonian capital, this race offers a unique opportunity to explore the city's rich history and stunning architecture while pushing your limits. Runners can expect an unforgettable journey through one of Europe's most captivating destinations.
