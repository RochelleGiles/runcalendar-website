Course

Single and double track trail courses around Whitefish. The 50K covers about 31 miles with over 6,300 feet of climbing. The Sunday races explore the Beaver Lakes area, with roughly 2,000 feet of gain for the half marathon, 750 feet for the 10K and 500 feet for the 5K.

About

The Whitefish Trail Blazer runs on the Whitefish Trail, a forested singletrack network near Whitefish, Montana, maintained by Whitefish Legacy Partners. Distances from 5K to 50K loop through the woods on the trail system near Whitefish Lake.
