About

The Phoenixville Run is a hybrid race held rain or shine at Reeves Park, offering in-person and virtual options. Runners can choose the traditional 5K and 10K, along with a Fun Run for kids 12 and under; virtual challenge participants can complete their distance anytime during the event window.
