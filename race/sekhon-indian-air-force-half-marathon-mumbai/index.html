About

The Sekhon Indian Air Force Half Marathon is the IAF's flagship running event, named for Flying Officer Nirmal Jit Singh Sekhon, the only Param Vir Chakra recipient in Indian Air Force history. Mumbai is one of seven cities hosting the marathon's second edition, with 21.1K, 10K and 5K options.
