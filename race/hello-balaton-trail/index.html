About

The Hello Balaton Trail offers a challenging trail running experience through the volcanic hills and hidden paths of the Balaton-felvidék region. Runners will explore scenic routes featuring witness hills, vineyards, and breathtaking landscapes of the autumn Balaton area, with multiple distance options to challenge trail runners of different skill levels.
