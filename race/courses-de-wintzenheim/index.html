About

The Courses de Wintzenheim is an annual running event held in the town of Wintzenheim, France. The event offers various race distances, including a 5km, 10km, and children's races, providing an opportunity for runners of all levels to participate. The scenic course showcases the beautiful natural surroundings of the Vosges region, creating a memorable and enjoyable experience for both participants and spectators.
