About

Explore the picturesque Surrey countryside on the Y2Y North Downs Trek/Run. This stunning event follows the iconic North Downs Way, offering runners a chance to immerse themselves in the region's natural beauty. Whether you prefer a leisurely trek or a challenging run, this event promises an unforgettable experience for all outdoor enthusiasts.
