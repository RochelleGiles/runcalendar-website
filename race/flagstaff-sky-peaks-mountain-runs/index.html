Course

Mountain trail races from Arizona Snowbowl. Saturday's races climb the 3.4-mile Agassiz loop toward 11,500 feet, the 50M then following the Arizona Trail north while the 50K heads south over Schultz Pass and the Weatherford climb; 12- and 6-hour events repeat the Agassiz loop, and Sunday's 20K runs point-to-point from Fort Valley Trailhead. Aid at White Horse, Hostetter, Lockett Meadow and Schultz Pass.

About

A multi-day mountain running event held at Arizona Snowbowl featuring races that climb to 11,500 feet, making it Arizona's highest footrace. The weekend includes a hill climb on Friday, multiple distances on Saturday (50M, 50K, 26K, 11K, 5K, and timed events), and mountain races on Sunday.
