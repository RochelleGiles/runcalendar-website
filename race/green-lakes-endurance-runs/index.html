Course

An 8-mile loop at Green Lakes State Park, starting and finishing at the Tulip Hill picnic area. The loop mixes single and double track trail, grassy paths, dirt roads and a short asphalt section, with two steep wooded climbs over roots and stones plus rolling meadow. Two staffed aid stations and one water drop keep runners within 3.75 miles of aid.

About

Green Lakes Endurance Runs takes place in Green Lakes State Park near Syracuse, New York, known for its two vividly green glacial lakes. Runners choose the 25K, 50K, or 100K distance on wooded trails around the park.
