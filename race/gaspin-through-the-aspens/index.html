Course

Starts and finishes at Tibble Fork Reservoir in American Fork Canyon, Utah. The 25K and 50K run out and back to a summit pass with a ridgeline loop; the 50-mile branches off onto remoter terrain after about 31 miles before returning. Roughly 95% singletrack through aspen stands and high mountain terrain.

About

Gaspin' Through the Aspens is a mountain trail race in Alta, Utah, offering 50-mile and marathon distances through the Wasatch Mountains. Runners tackle flowy singletrack, steep technical climbs and rugged descents beneath sweeping alpine views on this demanding, rewarding course.
