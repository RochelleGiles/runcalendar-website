About

The Portsea Bay to Surf events will showcase the best of Point Nepean National Park and will encompass three separate event formats, including a 9km run for athletes, a 4.5km run for beginners, and a 4.5km walk for families and individuals to explore the iconic scenery of the Mornington Peninsula.
