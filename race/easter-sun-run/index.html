About

The Easter Sun Run is the 31st annual event held at Sedgwick County Park in Wichita, featuring a 10K race and Jeff Wenzel 2-mile run/walk. Participants receive T-shirts and finisher medals, with cash awards for top finishers and a family fun zone including an Easter Egg Hunt.
