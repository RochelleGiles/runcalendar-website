Course

A flat, AIMS-accredited coastal road course starting and finishing at Foreshore Park car park in Newcastle, relocated from The Station because of construction. Both the marathon and half marathon run the Honeysuckle Foreshore and pass Nobbys Beach and Newcastle Beach, with spectator support and entertainment along the way.

About

The Newcastle Marathon and Half Marathon follow a flat coastal route along Honeysuckle Foreshore, Carrington and Nobbys before finishing near Newcastle Beach. Organised by H Events, the course is being remeasured for AIMS accreditation with the start moving to Foreshore Park.
