Course

Starts at Sunset Point parking lot and traverses Mt Nebo twice on rocky, technical singletrack via the Lizard Tail, Rim, Miller's Goat and Ox Pull trails. The half finishes after the Sunset connector climb; the 20-mile adds the Chickalah loop before a final push to the finish. Aid stations every 4-6 miles.
