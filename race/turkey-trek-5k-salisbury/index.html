About

The Turkey Trek 5K is a Thanksgiving-week race hosted by Wicomico County Recreation & Parks in Salisbury, Maryland, with a costumed "Mr. Gobble Goble" fun challenge alongside the timed run, drawing families out for a pre-holiday run each year.
