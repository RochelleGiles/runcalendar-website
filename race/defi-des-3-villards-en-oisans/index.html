About

Nestled in the picturesque Oisans region, the Défi des 3 Villards en Oisans challenges runners with a breathtaking course that winds through the stunning alpine landscapes. Featuring a rich history and a reputation for an exceptional participant experience, this event offers a unique opportunity to test your endurance amidst the majestic peaks and valleys of this captivating corner of France.
