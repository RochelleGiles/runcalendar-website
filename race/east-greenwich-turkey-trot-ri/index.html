Course

The certified 5K takes in Main Street, the East Greenwich waterfront and the hill district, with a one-mile fun run following it. The 5K starts at 8:30am and the fun run at 9:30am. Race-day registration and bib pick-up are at the Armory, 6 Main Street.

About

The East Greenwich Hill and Harbour Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving-week 5K and 1-mile fun run through East Greenwich, Rhode Island's historic waterfront neighborhood. It's organized by the Main Street Association.
