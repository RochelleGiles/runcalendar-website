Course

Marathon on quiet North Essex roads from Halstead, taking in rolling hills, woods and farmland. The course keeps runners on low-traffic roads for safety, with just a 400m off-road stretch in the final mile. Start is 9:30am.

About

The Halstead & Essex Marathon takes runners on a scenic 26.2-mile journey through the picturesque North Essex countryside. Organized by the Halstead Road Runners club, this event offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels, showcasing the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, the Halstead & Essex Marathon promises an unforgettable running adventure.
