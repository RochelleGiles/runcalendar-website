Course

Start and finish are at the same point at Church Ranch Office Park, 10170 Church Ranch Way, Westminster. The route follows the Big Dry Creek Trail southwest, the half marathon passing Standley Lake below the Front Range. Trail surface varies by distance and a stroller/wheelchair advisory is given. Aid stations average 1.4 miles apart with water, electrolytes, gels and first aid.

About

All-Out Beat the Heat is a road race where the Big Dry Creek Trail meets Standley Lake Regional Park in Westminster, Colorado, supporting the Adaptive Adventures mission. The half marathon follows the Big Dry Creek Trail southwest with views of Standley Lake against the Front Range. It is an official BOLDERBoulder qualifying event.
