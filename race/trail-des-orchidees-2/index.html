About

Trail des Orchidées is a multi-day trail and trek event in Chiang Dao, northern Thailand, at the gateway to the Golden Triangle. Over four stages, runners cover roughly 100km through orchid-filled mountain forest beneath Doi Chiang Dao, one of Thailand's highest peaks.
