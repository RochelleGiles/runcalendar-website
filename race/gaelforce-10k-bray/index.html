About

The Gaelforce 10K Bray Run in County Wicklow, Ireland is part of the Gaelforce 10K Trilogy series, offering runners a scenic and challenging course along the stunning Bray coastline. Participants can expect stunning ocean views and a lively atmosphere as they push themselves through this picturesque seaside town.
