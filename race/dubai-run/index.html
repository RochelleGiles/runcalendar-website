About

Dubai Run closes the iconic Sheikh Zayed Road to traffic for one morning, letting runners and walkers take over the city's main skyscraper-lined highway. Held as part of the Dubai Fitness Challenge, it offers 10K and 5K distances through the heart of downtown Dubai.
