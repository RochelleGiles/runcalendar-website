Course

A twilight running festival on the NSW Mid North Coast, following a riverside course that winds around the sights of Camden Haven as the sun sets. Half marathon, 10km and 5km are offered alongside kids' 1-mile and 3km races. The organiser publishes no surface or aid station detail on the main page.

About

RunHaven is a half marathon, 10km and 5km event around Laurieton on the Camden Haven coast of the NSW Mid North Coast, run in the late afternoon so runners finish as the sun sets over the water.
