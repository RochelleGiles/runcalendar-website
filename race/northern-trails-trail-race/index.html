Course

Piedmont trail racing out of Northern Guilford High School near Lake Townsend and Lake Brandt, Greensboro. Courses run on well-groomed hiking trail with some mountain-bike singletrack, over 5-mile, 10-mile and 17-mile options. Ruck and virtual variants are offered for each distance.

About

Hit the spring trails with us in March for one of the most beautiful trail races in Piedmont. Northern Trails is a North Carolina tradition starting and ending at Northern Guilford High School in Greensboro, NC! This is a great beginner race for new trail runners, a fantastic prep race for those gearing up for an ultra, and a perfect goal race for those looking for a fast time!
