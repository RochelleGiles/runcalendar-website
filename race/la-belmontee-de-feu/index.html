About

La Belmontée de Feu is a semi-nocturnal trail race in Belmont, Bas-Rhin, organized by the local ski club in the Vosges' upper Bruche valley. Runners cover technical trails between Fouday and Belmont, finishing above 1,000m elevation, often in cold, wet, or snowy November conditions.
