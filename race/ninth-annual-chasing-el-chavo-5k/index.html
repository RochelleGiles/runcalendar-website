About

Join us for the 9th Annual Chasing El Chavo 5K, a fiesta-themed race like no other! Inspired by our mango habanero hard cider, El Chavo, Chasing El Chavo invites patrons of all ages to join one of Blake's favorite traditions. This fiesta-themed race is perfect for runners and walkers of all levels, offering a fun and festive atmosphere as you navigate the gravel trails of our family-owned orchard. Whether you're racing to the finish line or taking a scenic stroll, the stunning orchard views and vibrant vibe will keep you energized every step of the way.
