About

La Transju' Trails is a trail running weekend in the Jura mountains of eastern France, staged by the organisers of the Transju cross-country ski race. The programme spans distances from 5 km up to a 110 km ultra, with 82 km, 65 km, 42 km, 25 km, 15 km and 10 km races alongside a youth event and a 15 km hike.
