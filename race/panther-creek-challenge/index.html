Course

Trail races through the woods and along the lake at Panther Creek State Park, run as circuitous forest loops. The half marathon climbs around 1,800 feet and the 10K around 1,500 feet, with two aid stations that runners pass three times. A kids trail mile is also offered.

About

The Treadin' Trodden Trails team is excited to travel thirty-five miles northeast of Knoxville to Panther Creek State Park again to this verdant and wondrous paradise of woods and lake for an arduous romp through the forest. The longer distance option will be near a half marathon in length, and the shorter race will cover 10k. Both trails will definitely present runners with a good test of their fitness, as well as offer a delightful tour of the park.
