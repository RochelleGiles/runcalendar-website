Course

A single loop of Beveridge Park in Kirkcaldy, alternating between the perimeter pavement and the grass, passing the boating lake and play parks. Surface is tarmac footpath and grass, with some steep sections reaching a 6 percent gradient. The start and finish sit on flat firm grass that can turn soft or muddy in wet weather.

About

Race for Life's 5K takes place in Beveridge Park, a landscaped Victorian park in Kirkcaldy on Scotland's Fife coast. Entrants run, jog or walk the course at their own pace, with proceeds going to Cancer Research UK.
