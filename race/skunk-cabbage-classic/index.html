About

The Skunk Cabbage Classic is Ithaca's iconic spring tradition featuring 10K and half-marathon courses through Cornell campus, scenic rail trail, and Ellis Hollow countryside. The event starts and finishes at Barton Hall with pre- and post-race festivities.
