Course

Starts and finishes at the Dixon Lake Picnic Area in Daley Ranch, Escondido. The half marathon runs one rolling trail loop; the 50K covers the same loop twice, with cutoffs of 4.75 hours at the end of loop one and 9.5 hours overall. Three stocked aid stations serve each race; the 50K starts at 7:00 AM and the half at 7:30 AM.

About

This race will take place in the Daley Ranch area, with the Start/Finish line for all distances at the Dixon Lake Picnic Area. The 50K will run a 25K loop two times, that will have 3,159' of gain (963m) per loop, which is 6,318'/1,926m total for the course. The half marathon will consist of a single loop with 2,706' of gain (825m). There will be three fully stocked aid stations available on this course.
