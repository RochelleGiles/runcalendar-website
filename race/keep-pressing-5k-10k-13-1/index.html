Course

All three distances start and finish at 200 Breckenridge Street in downtown Henderson and head out along Garnett Street. The 5K turns back on the same roads; the 10K extends onto Satterwhite Point Road and Spellman Loop; the half marathon adds Hedrick Drive and Kerr Lake Club Road. Hospitality stations are at Back to Eden and Kerr Lake Country Club.
