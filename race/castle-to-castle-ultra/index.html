About

The Castle to Castle Ultra is a challenging ultra-running event that takes runners from Clitheroe Castle to Lancaster Castle, covering the scenic countryside of the United Kingdom. The course features diverse terrain and stunning landscapes, providing an unforgettable experience for participants.
