Course

A point-to-point road half marathon that starts in the middle of the 25 de Abril Bridge over the Tagus and finishes in central Lisbon by the Jerónimos Monastery in Belém. Fast and largely downhill off the bridge, with a three-hour limit and a 20,000-runner cap. Part of the SuperHalfs series.

About

The EDP Lisbon Half Marathon starts on the 25 de Abril Bridge, closed to traffic for the race, and runs along the Tagus to finish beside the Jerónimos Monastery in Belém. It is one of the fastest half marathon courses in the world and a leg of the SuperHalfs series, with a field capped at 20,000 and a three-hour limit.
