Course

A mostly flat city loop starting on the Joseph-Beuys-Ufer beside the Rhine and finishing on the lower Rhine embankment by the Altstadt. The route runs north past the trade fair and Nordpark, crosses the Oberkasseler bridge for a loop through Oberkassel, then returns via the Königsallee, the zoo park and the Medienhafen.

About

The Dusseldorf Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner. Set against the stunning backdrop of Germany's vibrant cultural capital, this race takes participants on a scenic tour through the city's historic streets and along the picturesque Rhine River. With a reputation for excellent organization and an enthusiastic local crowd, the Dusseldorf Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
