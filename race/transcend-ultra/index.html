Course

Point-to-point through the Avon Valley, starting at Boongarup Pool in Walyunga National Park and finishing at Cobbler Pool near Toodyay. Run as five legs (12.6, 13.3, 11.5, 16.4 and 12.3km), with the shorter distances starting partway along the route. Terrain is singletrack, mountain-bike and fire trail, gravel track, bushland, paddock and creek crossings. Aid stations at Avon Ridge Estate Park, Paruna Wildlife Sanctuary and Valley Campground.

About

Transcend Ultra is a challenging trail running event that takes runners through the beautiful Swan Valley and Toodyay regions of Western Australia. The course features a mix of scenic trails, rolling hills, and stunning landscapes that offer a truly immersive experience for participants. The event is known for its welcoming community, well-organized logistics, and the opportunity to push personal boundaries.
