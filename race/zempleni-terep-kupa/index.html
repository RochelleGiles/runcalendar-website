About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Zemplén region at the annual Zempleni Terep Kupa trail running event. This challenging course winds through the rolling hills and dense forests of this scenic area, offering a unique and immersive running experience for adventurous athletes. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable day of competition and camaraderie in a stunning natural setting.
