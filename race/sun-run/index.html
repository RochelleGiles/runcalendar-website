About

The Sun Run is one of Sydney's most popular family-friendly community sporting events, offering a scenic course and a vibrant atmosphere for runners of all levels. Set against the backdrop of Australia's iconic landscapes, this annual event invites participants to experience the city's natural beauty while challenging themselves on the run.
