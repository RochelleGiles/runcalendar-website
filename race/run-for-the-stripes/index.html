Course

Start and finish are at the Philadelphia Zoo, 3400 W. Girard Ave, Philadelphia. Entrants choose a 5K run or a one-mile family walk, and a virtual option is offered at a distance of the runner's choosing. No route, surface or aid station detail is published.

About

The 14th annual Run For The Stripes is a 5K run and 1 mile family fun walk held at the Philadelphia Zoo to raise awareness and funds for neuroendocrine (NET) cancer research. Proceeds support NET research at the University of Pennsylvania Abramson Cancer Center.
