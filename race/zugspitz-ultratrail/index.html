About

The Zugspitz Ultratrail is a challenging and scenic mountain running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Bavarian Alps. Runners will traverse rugged trails and conquer the slopes of Germany's highest peak, the Zugspitze, for an unforgettable ultra-distance adventure. This race offers a unique opportunity to experience the breathtaking natural beauty of the region firsthand.
