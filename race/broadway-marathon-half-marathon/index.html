Course

Both distances start and finish on Broadway High Street in the northern Cotswolds, climbing first to Broadway Tower before crossing to Snowshill and Stanway. The marathon then heads on towards Winchcombe and Sudeley Castle and the hardest hill of the day on the escarpment; the half rejoins at Stanway for gently undulating fields and woods back to Broadway. The majority of the course is trail or unpaved track, with short road sections, marshalled at key points.
