Course

A road race starting on Main Street in the village of Lake Placid and finishing inside the 1980 Olympic Oval. The route runs on sealed roads out through the Adirondack countryside with mountain views and lakeside stretches; the marathon covers two laps of the half marathon loop. A 10K is also offered.

About

The Lake Placid Marathon, Half Marathon, and 10K take runners through the Adirondack village of Lake Placid, New York, past Mirror Lake in the shadow of the High Peaks.
