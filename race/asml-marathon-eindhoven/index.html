Course

City-centre road marathon starting and finishing on Montgomerylaan in the heart of Eindhoven, run in waves. The officially measured and Atletiekunie-certified route passes the Philips Stadium, the Evoluon and the High Tech Campus before returning through the city centre and green parks.

About

Traditionally, the ASML Marathon Eindhoven takes place in the second weekend of October. The largest two-day sport event in the province of North Brabant has international allure and is a fantastic event for young and old. The ASML Marathon Eindhoven is more than just a sport event, it is #onzetrots. The ASML Marathon Eindhoven stands for connection, innovation and is a running event for and by the city, where more than 35,000 runners get moving and over 200,000 enthusiastic spectators carry you to the finish line on the bustling Vestdijk. An experience never to be forgotten!
