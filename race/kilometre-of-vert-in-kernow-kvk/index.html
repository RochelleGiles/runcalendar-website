Course

Laps of a 7.5-mile loop from Perranporth Airfield near St Agnes in Cornwall, predominantly trail, with about 1,000 m of total ascent over the solo distance of roughly 15 miles. Teams split the route into two 7.5-mile legs, swapping at the venue. Aid station at the venue and about 3.5 miles into each loop.

Solo/Relay15 km

About

The Kilometre of Vert in Kernow or KVK is the first and only vertical ascent based race in the South West. The course features two figure-of-eight loops covering 15 miles of inland hills and coastal running with significant climbs and descents.
