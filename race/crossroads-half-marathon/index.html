Course

Run on the Middlesex Greenway, a 3.6-mile paved asphalt tree-lined path through Metuchen, Edison and Woodbridge, covered several times to make up the 13.1 miles. Start and finish are at Greenway Park, 691-707 Middlesex Ave. Water sits at the start/finish and near miles 2.9 and 4.3.

About

The 7th annual Crossroads Half Marathon is a fast and relatively flat 13.1-mile race on the Middlesex Greenway trail in Metuchen, NJ. Hosted by Crossroads Running Club, it starts at 8 a.m. from Greenway Park with finisher medals for all participants.
