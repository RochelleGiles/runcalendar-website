Course

A point-to-point course of about 14.5 miles, mostly downhill, from Diamondfield Jack Campground to Third Fork Campground. The first three miles follow the rough jeep and ATV track of Deadline Ridge Road; the remaining ten-plus miles are singletrack. Entry is capped at 200 runners.

About

This point-to-point ultra half marathon runs through high-desert canyon terrain south of Twin Falls, starting near Diamondfield Jack Campground on Rock Creek Road and finishing at Third Fork Campground. The trail is marked with flagging through open sagebrush country, and the last 10.5 miles have no water station.
