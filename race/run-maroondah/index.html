Course

All events start and finish at HE Parker Reserve in Heathmont and run laps of a course following the Dandenong Creek Trail — one lap for the 5 km, two for the 10 km and three for the 15 km. The kids 1.2 km dash covers 800 m of the trail plus a 400 m lap of HE Parker Oval.

About

Run Maroondah is a community fun run hosted by Maroondah City Council in Heathmont, in Melbourne's outer east, with 5km, 10km and 15km distance options plus a short kids' dash.
