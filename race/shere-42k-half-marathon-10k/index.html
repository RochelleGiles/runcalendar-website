Course

A multi-terrain trail event in the Surrey Hills, with all three distances starting and finishing at Shere Recreation Ground. The 10k climbs north through Oaken Grove and back; the 21k and 42k follow the North Downs Way over Ranmore, Leith Hill Tower, Holmbury Hill and St Martha's Hill. Aid stations: one on the 10k, three on the 21k, five on the 42k.

About

A Surrey Trek and Run multi-terrain event starting from the village of Shere in the Surrey Hills, with 10K, half marathon and 42K options. The routes climb into the North Downs countryside surrounding the village.
