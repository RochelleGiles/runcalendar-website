About

Often imitated, but never duplicated. The Fat Boy 5k is now in cities across America (Las Vegas, New Orleans and more), but the original started right here in Baton Rouge, LA. With the Fat Boy growing (pun intended) the Original Fat Boy Running Club has real reason to celebrate. And THE Fat Boy knows how to celebrate!
