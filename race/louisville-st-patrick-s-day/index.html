Course

Mostly flat course starting in front of Louisville High School, 1201 S. Nickelplate Street. The route winds through Metzger Park and along the Nickleplate Trail before finishing back at the high school. The 5K starts at 10:00am and the half-mile fun run at 9:30am.

About

The Louisville St. Patrick's Day 5K is a mostly flat race in Louisville, Ohio, run by the Louisville High School cross country program. It starts outside the high school, passes through Metzger Park and along the Nickleplate Trail, and finishes back at the school.
