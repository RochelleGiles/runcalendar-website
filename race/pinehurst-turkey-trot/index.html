Course

Starts and finishes at the Village Arboretum on Rassie Wicker Drive and runs through the streets and residential neighbourhoods of Pinehurst, in the North Carolina Sandhills. The Little Gobbler one-mile fun run goes off at 8:30am and the 5K at 9:00am.

About

North Carolina's oldest and most scenic Turkey Trot, featuring a 5K race and 1-mile fun run through the beautiful streets and residential neighborhoods of Pinehurst. Participants are encouraged to wear festive turkey attire and enjoy a community celebration of fall, family, and fun.
