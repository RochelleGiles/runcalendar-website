Course

The marathon is an AIMS-certified 42.195km course starting and finishing at the Lakes Entrance foreshore, with aid stations approximately every 4km. The organisers describe the route as a mix of sealed road and off-road trail, with the surface breakdown listed as still to be confirmed. The festival also runs 2km, 5km, 10km and half marathon events from the same foreshore precinct.

About

The Lakes Entrance Running Festival is based along one of Victoria's most iconic coastal landscapes. The event offers a scenic journey on a mix of smooth road stretches and natural off-road trails, with sweeping views of the lakes, ocean, and bushland.
