About

The Marion Arts Festival, now past its 33rd running, is a one-day event in Uptown Marion, Iowa, that ranks among the top fine art fairs in the United States and draws thousands of art enthusiasts. It includes a half marathon and 5K run/walk through the festival's uptown district.
