About

The Lincoln Memorial Garden Trail Race is an 8K, just under five miles, run on the trail network inside the garden on the shore of Lake Springfield, Illinois. A kids fun run of three short distances circles the pond beforehand. Entry proceeds support the garden, a 100-acre site of restored prairie, woodland and wetland.
