About

The Chattahoochee Road Race features fast 5K and 10K courses that are mostly flat with a 0.75-mile downhill finish, making it one of Georgia's quickest races. Runners enjoy a festive finish line party with free beer, food, live music, custom medals, and quality t-shirts.
