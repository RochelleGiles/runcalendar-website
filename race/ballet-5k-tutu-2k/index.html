About

The Ballet 5K & Tutu 2K is an annual fundraiser for the New Jersey Civic Youth Ballet, starting and finishing at Hackettstown Riverfront Park. Entrants choose between the 5K and a shorter 2K, both run on crushed gravel and paved bike paths. Tutu-themed teams are encouraged and compete for prizes.
