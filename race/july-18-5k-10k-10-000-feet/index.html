Course

Runners ride Gondola One from Vail Village and start at Mid-Vail at 8am. The 10K is 25% gravel road and 75% singletrack, gaining 925 feet to a high point of 10,977 feet. The 5K is 40% gravel road and 60% singletrack, gaining 488 feet. Aid stations sit at mile 1.5 and, for the 10K only, at Wildwood at mile 4.3; water is available at both.

About

The 10@10 returns to its original route on Grand Traverse. The race starts 10,000 feet above sea level, on the beautiful Vail Mountain. Racers will be challenged as they traverse over Vail Mountain and across the legendary back bowls of Vail. The average grade of the 10K is 7 percent with an elevation gain of nearly 1,200 feet, while the shorter 5K is a tad less challenging and allows runners the chance to experience racing at an impressive altitude. Both courses provide incredible views of the Vail Valley, the Gore Range and the beauty of wildflowers in full bloom.
