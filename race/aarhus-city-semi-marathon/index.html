Course

A fully closed half-marathon route through central Aarhus, starting and finishing in the city. The course passes the ARoS art museum, the Concert Hall, the Town Hall tower, the cathedral, Dokk1 and Tivoli Friheden on city streets.

About

The BESTSELLER Aarhus City Half is a vibrant urban half marathon that takes runners through the heart of Aarhus. Participants will experience a scenic route showcasing the city's most iconic landmarks, including ARoS Kunstmuseum, Aarhus Domkirke, and Tivoli Friheden. With over 60 entertainment hotspots and around 50,000 enthusiastic spectators, this event offers an electrifying atmosphere that makes running through the city an unforgettable experience.
