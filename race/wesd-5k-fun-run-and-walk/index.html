About

The Sixth Annual WESD 5k Walk/Run Event takes place at ASU West Campus in Glendale, Arizona. The event includes a 5k run/walk for ages 11 and up, a Kids Dash for ages 10 and under, a health fair, and awards for top finishers in each age division.
