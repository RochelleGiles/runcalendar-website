About

Kullamannen by UTMB runs along the Kullen peninsula on Sweden's south-west coast, based in the seaside village of Mölle. Courses climb through the cliffs and beech forests of the Kullaberg nature reserve, ranging from the 22K North Shore to the 173K Ultra 100 Miles.
