Course

Point-to-point 31 km run starting on Belltrees Road and climbing to the Woolooma Glass House at the summit of Mt Woolooma at roughly halfway, then descending to the finish. The surface is mixed: sealed and unsealed road, fire trails, river crossings, cattle grids and steep rocky sections. Four water refill checkpoints are set on course, with toilets at the summit.

About

The Woolooma Classic Adventure Run is a unique running event with the inspiring tagline 'doing it for those who can't'. This adventure run offers participants a challenging and scenic course through the Australian landscape, emphasizing community spirit and supporting a meaningful cause.
