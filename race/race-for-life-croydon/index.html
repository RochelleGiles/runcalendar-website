Course

Routes of 3K, 5K and 10K around Lloyd Park in South Croydon, starting and finishing on the grass playing fields beside Lloyd Park tram stop. The terrain is mixed, over grass and woodland with some short climbs and descents, and the organiser warns it can turn muddy and slippery in poor weather.

About

Race for Life's Croydon event is held in Lloyd Park, a green space in South Croydon. Cancer Research UK's charity 10K, 5K and 3K are run, jogged or walked on a flat, traffic-free course, raising funds for cancer research.
