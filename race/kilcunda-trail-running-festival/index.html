About

Escape the city and challenge yourself on the rugged coastal trails of the Kilcunda Trail Running Festival. Runners will be treated to stunning ocean vistas and challenging terrain just 90 minutes from Melbourne, making this event a must-do for adventurous trail enthusiasts. Don't miss your chance to experience this unique running experience on the Bass Highway.
