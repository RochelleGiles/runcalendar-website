Course

About

The Trillium Trek Trail Run & Walk is organised by the Elachee Nature Science Center on the trail network of the Chicopee Woods Nature Preserve near Gainesville, Georgia. Runners and walkers choose a half marathon, 10K or 5K, and entry fees support Elachee's environmental education work.
