Course

A mountain trail race above Vail crossing several ski resorts. The half marathon runs 78% singletrack and 22% dirt and gravel road with about 2,844 ft of climbing and five aid stations. The 5K is a separate point-to-point, 80% singletrack and mostly downhill, run without aid stations.

About

The Arrowhead Half Marathon & 5K crosses the Arrowhead, Bachelor Gulch and Beaver Creek Ski Resorts on an extensive network of mountain trails. The half marathon climbs through aspen groves to the summit of Bachelor Gulch Mountain before descending the Arrowhead Mountain Trail, while the 5K runs point-to-point from The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch to Arrowhead, mostly downhill.
