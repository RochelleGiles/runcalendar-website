Course

A trail course based at Bickley Outdoor Recreation Camp in the Bickley Valley east of Perth. The route uses single track with sustained climbs and descents, taking in the Quad Cruncher ascent and a section past the Victoria Dam lookout. All distances start and finish at the camp and mandatory gear applies.

About

The RunningWorks 3 Valleys event in Australia offers runners a scenic and challenging course through the stunning 3 Valleys region. With 10km, half marathon, and full marathon distance options, as well as a team relay, this event caters to runners of all levels looking to experience the natural beauty of the Australian countryside.
