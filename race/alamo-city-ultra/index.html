Course

A looped course on the natural trails of Eisenhower Park, with rocks and roots underfoot. The 50K runs six loops, the 25K three, the 10K two and the 5K one. Aid stations sit at miles 3.1 and 5 for the longer distances, plus a finish-line station for the 10K. The field is capped at 400 runners.

About

This is a fun and challenging mile course that features 425 feet of elevation amongst lots of rocks and roots run on the natural trails. Some of the route runs on the greenway trail to the finish area.
