Course

An undulating seven-mile multi-terrain race starting and finishing at Old Leo's Rugby Club, through woods and parkland in Adel Woods, Scotland Woods and Meanwood Park. The route passes under the Seven Arches and the Slabbering Baby and through a tunnel beneath the Leeds Outer Ring Road. Trail or fell shoes are recommended.

About

The Meanwood Valley Trail Race follows the Meanwood Valley Trail out of Leeds, a green corridor along Meanwood Beck linking the city centre to parks and woodland to the north, raising funds for Leeds Children's Charity.
