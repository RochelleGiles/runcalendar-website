Course

Road fun run along Wellington's waterfront. Participants start in the city and follow the harbour around the bays to finish at Kilbirnie Park. An 8.4km course and a half marathon are offered, with an optional shorter family cut-through available on the day. Sealed roads and waterfront promenade throughout.

About

Take part in the capital's largest fun run - 8.4km or Honda Half Marathon - walk, jog, run or roll from the city around the bays to Kilbirnie Park. Grab your friends, whanau or colleagues for a fun run like no other. Your course, your choice with an optional whanau-friendly shortcut on the day for our mini athletes.
