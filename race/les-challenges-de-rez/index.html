About

Les Challenges de Rezé is organized by ASBR Athlétisme, now past its 16th running. The event offers multiple race formats on a course alternating between road and wide pedestrian paths, taking runners past local heritage sites including the Jaguère valley and the Classerie and Butte de Praud chateaux.
