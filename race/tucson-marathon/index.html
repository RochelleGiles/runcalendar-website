Course

A point-to-point, largely downhill road course through the Santa Catalina foothills. The marathon and 50K start at Biosphere 2 in Oracle and the half at Golder Ranch in Catalina, with all distances finishing at Pima Community College Northwest Campus in Tucson. The surface is paved road and bike path, net 1,493ft of descent over the marathon, with about 16 aid stations en route.

About

The Tucson Marathon is a point-to-point course that begins in Oracle, Arizona, and descends toward Tucson, making it one of the fastest, most downhill marathon courses in the country. The event also offers a half marathon, 50K ultra and marathon relay.
