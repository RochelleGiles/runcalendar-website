About

The Waisman Whirl Run, Walk & Roll for All Abilities offers a 5K and a 1-mile course in Madison, Wisconsin, open to runners, walkers and wheelchair users. Proceeds benefit individuals and families affected by developmental disabilities and neurodegenerative diseases.
