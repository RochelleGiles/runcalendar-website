Course

A 100-mile route from Waverton Village Hall near Chester, following the River Dee over the Old Dee Bridge and out along the North Cheshire Way over Helsby and Frodsham Hills, then the Sandstone Trail through Delamere Forest, Beeston, Peckforton and Bickerton. Checkpoints at Dunham Hill, Hapsford, Peckforton, Beeston and Tilston village halls, with support runners allowed from halfway.

About

A non-stop 100-mile (and 50-mile) ultra following canal towpaths from Chester through the Cheshire countryside. The flat, waymarked route runs through the night with checkpoints along the way.
