Course

The marathon is a four-lap out-and-back course to Cannonvale Beach, run on footpaths and boardwalks along the Airlie Beach foreshore. There are three drink stations per lap, with sports drink at two of them. All festival races are held along the Airlie Beach and Cannonvale foreshores.

About

The Airlie Beach Marathon Festival is held over a weekend in July at Airlie Beach in the Whitsundays region of North Queensland. Races run along the Airlie Beach and Cannonvale foreshores on footpaths and boardwalks, and include a marathon, half marathon, 5km, mile, 2km and 1km. Entries are open to ages 4 and up.
