About

The Marathon Objectif Autonomie takes runners on a stunning coastal journey through the picturesque towns of Perros-Guirec and Paimpol. With breathtaking ocean vistas and a focus on sustainable practices, this event offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels. Discover the rich history and natural beauty of Brittany as you challenge yourself to conquer this renowned marathon.
