About

The Ryan Walsworth Spear-It 5K is a fun walk/run held in memory of local asparagus farmer Ryan Walsworth, kicking off the National Asparagus Festival activities. The event starts at Silver Lake Chicken Shack in Mears, MI, with registration at 7:30 AM and race start at 9:00 AM.
