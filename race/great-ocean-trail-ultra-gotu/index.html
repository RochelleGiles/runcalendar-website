About

The Great Ocean Trail Ultra (GOTU) is undoubtably Australia's most iconic oceanside ultra, weaving its singletrack magic alongside the most famous coastline our continent has to offer. Staying true to the original GOW100 from which it has grown, GOTU is a grassroots ultra outing that will change your life.
