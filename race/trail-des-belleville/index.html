About

Explore the picturesque trails of the Belleville region at the Trail des Belleville, a challenging and scenic running event. Weaving through rolling hills and lush forests, this off-road adventure offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the area. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail des Belleville promises an unforgettable experience.
