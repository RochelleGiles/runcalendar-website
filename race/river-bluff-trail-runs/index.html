Course

Runs the Missouri River bluffs in St Joseph, linking Krug Park and River Bluff Trail Park, with start and finish at the McArthur parking lot on Douglas MacArthur Drive. The 50K covers two 15.5-mile loops for roughly 4,000 feet of climbing. Surfaces are smooth dirt trail with roots and rocky sections plus crushed gravel path. Aid stations every four miles or so carry water and snacks.
