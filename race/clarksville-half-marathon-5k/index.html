Course

USATF-certified, chip-timed road courses through downtown Clarksville, starting and finishing at 102 Public Square. The half marathon is off at 7:00am with a 3.5-hour cutoff (4.5 hours for the ruck division) and the 5K at 7:30am; both take in city views along the way.

About

The 2nd Annual Clarksville Half Marathon, 5K, and 1-Mile Race is presented by Hankook Tire Manufacturing Tennessee. The USATF-certified courses take runners through downtown Clarksville, scenic parks, and iconic landmarks.
