About

The Florida Endurance Challenge invites runners to test their limits on the scenic grounds of Florida Manor in Killinchy, Ireland. This challenging event offers a chance to push your endurance in a stunning natural setting, surrounded by the rolling hills and lush landscapes of the Irish countryside. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a new adventure, the Florida Endurance Challenge promises an unforgettable running experience.
