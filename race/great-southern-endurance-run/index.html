Course

Distances of 14km, 28km, 56km, 50 mile (solo and relay) and 100 mile in the Victorian Alps. All start and finish at the Hotham Alpine Base Function Centre at Mt Hotham except the 28km, which starts at Harrietville and finishes at Mt Hotham. The 56km uses the Bon Accord track, Bungalow Spur, Mt Feathertop and the Razorback; the 50 and 100 mile are predominantly single track including the Australian Alps Walking Track.

About

The Great Southern Endurance Run is a mountain ultra in the Victorian Alps, based at Mt Hotham Alpine Resort, with the 28km starting from Harrietville. Distances range from 14km to 100 miles, including 50 mile solo and relay, 56km and 28km options. Courses use high country trails such as Bungalow Spur, Mt Feathertop and the Razorback.
