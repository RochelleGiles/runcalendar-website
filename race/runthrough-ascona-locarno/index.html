About

Experience the stunning natural beauty of the Ticino region as you run through the picturesque towns of Ascona and Locarno. This scenic trail running event offers a challenging yet rewarding course that winds through lush forests, past tranquil lakes, and alongside the majestic Swiss Alps. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, the Runthrough Ascona Locarno is an unforgettable adventure not to be missed.
