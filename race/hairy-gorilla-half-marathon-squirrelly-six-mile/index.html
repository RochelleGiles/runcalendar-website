Course

A trail race run entirely inside John Boyd Thacher State Park at Voorheesville, starting and finishing at 1 Hailes Cave Road. The first 1.75 miles cross gentle, flat grass field before the course turns to singletrack with technical sections. The half marathon and Squirrelly Six start together.

About

The Hairy Gorilla Half Marathon & Squirrelly Six (.3*) Mile is part running race, part costume party, and is truly among the most unique events you'll ever do! Be on the look out for gorillas that have gone bananas, nutty squirrels, graves with your own names on them, and other inanity to keep you smiling and enjoying the beautiful views that await!
