About

The Elstertal Lauf in Germany offers runners a scenic and challenging course through the picturesque Elstertal valley. This long-running event features a route that winds along the banks of the Weiße Elster river, providing a stunning backdrop for an unforgettable running experience. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a recreational runner, the Elstertal Lauf is a must-do event for those seeking a unique and rewarding challenge.
