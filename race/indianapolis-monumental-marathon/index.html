Course

A fast, flat 26.2 miles winding through downtown Indianapolis and a string of historic neighbourhoods and cultural districts, passing through the Indiana State Fairgrounds between miles 8 and 9. Sealed city streets throughout, with a half marathon and 5K on the same morning.

About

The Indianapolis Monumental Marathon is named for downtown's Soldiers and Sailors Monument on Monument Circle. Runners can choose the marathon, half marathon or 5K through the streets of Indianapolis.
