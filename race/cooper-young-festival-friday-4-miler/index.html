Course

Starts at Cooper and Central in the Cooper-Young neighbourhood of Memphis and runs on neighbourhood streets. The race is held on the Friday evening of the Cooper-Young Festival with a 7:00 PM start and finishes at a block party.

About

The Cooper Young Festival Friday 4 Miler is a road race through the Cooper-Young Historic District of Memphis, Tennessee, run the Friday evening before the annual Cooper-Young Festival street fair kicks off.
