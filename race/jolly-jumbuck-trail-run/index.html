Course

Off-road courses climbing Jumbuck Hill, marked with blue flagging tape and Perth Trail Series arrows. Runners choose a Green, Blue or Black course and can drop to an easier option where the routes intersect, with the Black and Blue sharing single track. The first and last 2km use shared-use trail where horses may be encountered, and the route passes billabong country. Runners must carry at least 500ml of water.

About

Jolly Jumbuck is a winter trail race in the Perth Trail Series, run on single track and shared-use trail over Jumbuck Hill. Entrants pick a Green, Blue or Black course to suit their experience, and can step down to an easier route where the courses meet. All runners carry their own water.
