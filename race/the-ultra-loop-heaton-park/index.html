Course

An eight-hour timed event at Heaton Park in Manchester: run as many 5.6km loops as you can, with no upper limit. The loop mixes road and trail past the Boating Lake and Heaton Hall and through woodland streams, climbing 262 ft a lap. In 2027 runners can enter the Saturday, the Sunday, or both.

About

The Ultra Loop in Heaton Park, Manchester offers runners an inclusive and flexible challenge. With no limit on distance, participants can push themselves for 8 hours on this picturesque urban course, making it perfect for ultrarunners and endurance enthusiasts alike.
