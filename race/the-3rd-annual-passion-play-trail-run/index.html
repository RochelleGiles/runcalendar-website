Course

Run on the Great Passion Play grounds above Eureka Springs. The 5K stays on the higher ground and crosses the passion play stage past the tabernacle; the 5-mile starts at the Christ of the Ozarks statue and links the Genesis, Holy Roller, Passover and Revelation trails before finishing through the Old City of Jerusalem gates. The half marathon joins both loops. Aid stations on each course.

About

A trail race on the grounds of the Great Passion Play at Eureka Springs, Arkansas, with a 5K, a five-mile, a half marathon and an all-road fun run. The courses wind past the Christ of the Ozarks statue and the site's Holy Land replicas on singletrack through the Ozark hills.
