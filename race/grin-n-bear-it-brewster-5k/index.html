About

Join us for the Second Annual Brewster Teachers' Association 5K Run/Walk event, a community event designed for runners and walkers of all ages and abilities! Set in the scenic Hudson Valley, this 5-kilometer race will take you through picturesque trails, tree-lined streets, and open spaces in our beautiful Brewster community. It will offer both a challenge and enjoyable experience for participants. Whether you're an experienced runner looking to set a new personal record or just want to enjoy a fun, active day outdoors, this event is perfect for you!
