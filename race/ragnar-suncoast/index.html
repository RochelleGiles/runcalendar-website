Course

Two loops starting and finishing at the Clearwater Beach Sheraton Resort on Sand Key, covering about 127 flat miles from sunrise to sunset with no night running. The route follows palm-lined roads and the Pinellas Trail, taking in Fort De Soto Park, St. Pete Beach, Madeira Beach, Belleair Beach, Safety Harbor and the Tarpon Springs Sponge Docks.
