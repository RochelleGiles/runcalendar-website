About

The Drogheda 10K is a beloved annual road race hosted by the Drogheda & District Athletic Club in Ireland. Runners of all levels will enjoy the scenic course through the historic town of Drogheda, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for those seeking to conquer a 10K distance.
