Course

A single 6.2km lap of the Mount Panorama motor racing circuit at Bathurst, starting and finishing on the circuit itself. The course is run entirely on the sealed bitumen race track and passes Hell Corner, Brock Skyline and The Esses. No on-course aid stations are listed; coffee and food are sold at the start area from 6:30am.

About

The Mount Panorama Punish is a 6.2km run around the Mount Panorama motor racing circuit at Bathurst, New South Wales. Runners complete one lap of the sealed track, passing Hell Corner, Brock Skyline and The Esses. The event is supported by Bathurst Regional Council, with onsite camping available over race weekend.
