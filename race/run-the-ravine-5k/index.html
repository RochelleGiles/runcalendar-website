Course

A fully paved 5K starting on the St. Croix Lutheran Academy campus, winding through Thompson and Kaposia Parks and Simon's Ravine before returning to the school. Comfortable for walkers and mildly testing for runners. A half-mile kids' fun run goes off at 10:00 a.m. and the 5K at 10:30 a.m.; leashed dogs are welcome.

About

Get ready to conquer Simon's Ravine for the 5th Annual Run the Ravine 5K at SCLA! This well-maintained, fully paved trail traverses through the woods of Kaposia Park and is considered perfect for walkers and somewhat challenging for runners. Dogs (on leashes) are welcome!
