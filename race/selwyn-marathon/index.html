Course

The marathon and half marathon start and finish at Lincoln Events Centre, Meijer Drive, Lincoln; the marathon covers a 21.1km loop twice, returning to the Events Centre between laps. The 10km is an out-and-back. Sealed road course certified to World Athletics standards, with the full and half passing through Rossendale Wines vineyard off the Akaroa Highway.

About

The Selwyn Marathon offers runners a unique challenge through the scenic landscapes of Lincoln, New Zealand. Held on the King's Birthday long weekend, this event invites participants to explore the region's rolling hills and charming countryside while pushing their endurance to the limit. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or an ambitious newcomer, the Selwyn Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
