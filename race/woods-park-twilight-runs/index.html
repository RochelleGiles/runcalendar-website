Course

An evening meet at Woods Park in Lincoln run cross-country style on grass, over multiple laps of the same flat loop. The Wild Things 1 mile for ages 12 and under goes off first at 5:45pm, followed by the Woods Park 5K. Faster runners lap slower ones, so the course operates on a keep-right-except-to-pass rule.

About

Woods Park Twilight Runs is an evening 5K and 1 Mile race held at Woods Park in Lincoln, Nebraska. Run as the sun goes down, it's part of the WellPower Movement race series, with the wooded park setting giving the twilight start a quieter feel.
