About

Please join us as Central Valley hosts the Warrior 5k Run/Walk. The event will take place at the Walter R. & Lois A. Sylvester Wellness Center, located at Central Valley High School, 160 Baker Road Extension, Monaca, PA 15061. The course will include Baker Road to CCBC, CCBC to Center Grange Elementary, and back to the Central Valley HS Campus, ending at the Wellness Center. This event will raise money in order to provide health and wellness equipment to our community members and students of the Central Valley School District. Our vision is to create healthier opportunities for our Central Valley Community by connecting wellness education with recreation.
