About

Promise Land 50K++ is a mountain trail ultra near Bedford, Virginia, part of the David Horton-founded Lynchburg Ultra Series alongside Hellgate and Holiday Lake. First run in 2001, the technical singletrack course climbs through Blue Ridge terrain with a field capped at 300 runners.
