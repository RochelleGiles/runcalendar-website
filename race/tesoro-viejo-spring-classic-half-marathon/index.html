About

The Tesoro Viejo Spring Classic Half Marathon offers runners a scenic course through lush meadows, shaded trees, rolling hills, and wetland preserves in the Tesoro Viejo community. Participants enjoy amenities like gender-specific tech shirts, finisher medallions, hot cinnamon rolls, and a post-race festival.
