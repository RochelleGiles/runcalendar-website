Course

Runs through Mill Creek Park past two lakes, Lanterman's Mill, the falls and the cascade. Start and finish at the Wick Recreation Area, McCollum Road. The organiser states the half marathon route contains 19 hills. Surface and aid stations are not stated.

About

This is the race to come and challenge yourself. You have 13.1 miles to complete 19 hills, and the weather is as much a part of the mystique of this race as the course itself. Join the new generation of runners and challenge yourself on this classic course!
