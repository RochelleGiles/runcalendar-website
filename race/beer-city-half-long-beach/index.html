Course

Starts at Shoreline Aquatic Park on the Long Beach waterfront, loops the park and heads up the LA River Trail before returning. The half marathon, 10K and 5K share the same routing over sealed park paths and riverside trail. A one-mile Beer Stroll and a beer mile also run on the day.

About

The Beer City Half™ Long Beach features races along the waterfront and up the LA River Trail at ShoreLine Aquatic Park, celebrating Long Beach's beer culture. It includes a post-race Beer City Festival with local brews, music, food, and events like the Beer Stroll and Beat the Beast Beer Mile.
