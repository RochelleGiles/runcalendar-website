Course

A 28 km mountain fell race over seven summits of the Paps of Jura with 2,370 m of climbing, run on open, pathless hill ground. Widely regarded as one of the toughest British hill races at the distance, it demands hillcraft and navigation as much as endurance. Starts are at 10:00 and 10:30.

About

The Isle of Jura Fell Race climbs the island's iconic Paps of Jura, a trio of quartzite peaks visible across the Inner Hebrides. Starting and finishing in Craighouse, it's one of Scotland's classic long-standing fell races.
