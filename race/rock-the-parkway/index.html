Course

A USATF-certified half marathon and 5K along historic Ward Parkway in Kansas City, run on roads closed to traffic. The half marathon course is fairly flat with a long downhill finish and is considered one of the fastest in the area. Numerous aid stations offer water and sports drink. Half marathon runners must hold a minimum 14-minute-per-mile pace under city permit.
