About

The Women's 5K Run Prague, sponsored by Adidas, is a must-attend event for runners seeking an exhilarating urban challenge. Set against the stunning backdrop of Prague's historic city center, this race offers a unique opportunity to explore the city's iconic landmarks while pushing personal boundaries. Runners of all levels are invited to join in the celebration of women's athleticism and community.
