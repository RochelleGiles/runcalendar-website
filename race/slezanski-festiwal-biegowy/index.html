About

The Slezanski Festiwal Biegowy is a mountain running event that features a marathon, half marathon, and 10K races. The challenging courses with steep climbs and technical descents capture the character of the Sleza mountain. The cozy atmosphere of the Sobótka mountain hut, which hosts the start and finish, adds to the sense of satisfaction for the runners. The friendly and passionate running community makes this a popular event that runners enjoy returning to.
