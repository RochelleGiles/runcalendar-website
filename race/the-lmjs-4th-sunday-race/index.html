Course

Laps of the paved path around Lake Merritt - one for the 5K, two for the 10K, three for the 15K - starting and finishing on the meadow beside the Sailboat House at Adams Point. The loop is flat and water is available. The 15K starts at 8:45am, the 5K and 10K at 9:00am.
