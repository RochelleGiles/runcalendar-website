About

XTERRA Conquer the Crater is a multi-sport event featuring triathlons, duathlons, and trail runs in Greater Sudbury, Ontario. The event uniquely supports the Northern Cancer Foundation's Miles Against Cancer Fund, with 100% of proceeds dedicated to improving survival rates and quality of life for children impacted by pediatric cancer in Northeastern Ontario.
