Course

Closed roads around Plymouth Hoe plus paved paths through Hoe Gardens, starting and finishing on hardstanding by the RAF Memorial on the Hoe Promenade. Mostly tarmac, with some compact grass and uneven patches. Steep pulls at Lambhay Hill, around 6 percent gradient, and Cliff Road, for 62m of climb overall.

About

Race for Life's 5K takes place on Plymouth Hoe, the historic clifftop parkland overlooking Plymouth Sound and the English Channel. Entrants run, jog or walk the course at their own pace, raising funds for Cancer Research UK.
