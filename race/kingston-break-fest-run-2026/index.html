Course

A flat, fast lapped course along the Thames at Kingston, running past Hampton Court Palace. The 8 mile is a single lap through Kingston, the 16 mile adds a shorter second circuit, and the 20 mile runs two laps plus a further 3.9 miles out on the river towpath. Held annually since 1998.

About

Following the iconic 'breakfast run' format, the Uber Boat by Thames Clippers Kingston Breakfest Run offers distances of 8 miles, 16 miles and 20 miles. Formerly known as the Kingston Spring Race Day run, it follows a single 8-mile loop through Kingston, with the 16-mile adding a shorter second circuit and the 20-mile running two laps plus a further stretch on the river towpath.
