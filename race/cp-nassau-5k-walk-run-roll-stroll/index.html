About

A 5K walk, run, roll and stroll held at Eisenhower Park in Westbury, New York, raising funds for the Cerebral Palsy Association of Nassau County. The course is open to runners, walkers and wheelchair users alike, and the morning event draws teams and family groups from across Long Island.
