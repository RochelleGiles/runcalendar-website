About

The Taupo Ultramarathon is a challenging 100km running event set against the stunning backdrop of New Zealand's Lake Taupo. Runners will traverse diverse terrain, from lush forests to rugged volcanic landscapes, as they push their limits in this iconic ultramarathon. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a new adventure, the Taupo Ultramarathon offers an unforgettable endurance challenge in a breathtaking natural setting.
