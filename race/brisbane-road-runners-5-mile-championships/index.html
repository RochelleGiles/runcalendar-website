About

The Brisbane Road Runners 5 Mile Championships offers runners a chance to compete for their club's top prize on a scenic course in Australia. This premier event features a challenging 5-mile race as well as a 4km distance, making it an ideal choice for runners of all levels. With a focus on camaraderie and competition, this race is a must-do for any runner in the Brisbane area.
