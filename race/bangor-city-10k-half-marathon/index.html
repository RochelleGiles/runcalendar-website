Course

Starts and finishes in Bangor city centre, running out to the coast and along Garth Pier over the Menai Strait with views of Anglesey and Snowdonia, before turning onto woodland paths. Both the 10K and half marathon share this mix of road and woodland path. Water stations sit at roughly every three miles.

About

The Bangor City 10K & Half Marathon is a premier running event in the United Kingdom. The course takes runners through the scenic streets of Bangor, offering stunning views of the surrounding countryside. With a vibrant atmosphere and a supportive community, this event is a must-do for runners of all levels.
