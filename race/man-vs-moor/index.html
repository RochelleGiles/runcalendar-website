Course

An obstacle trail event on the Chains in Exmoor National Park, where runners cross remote moorland and natural obstacles including Pinkery Pinnacles, Green Mile and Hoaroak Hiatus. Every route includes an open water swim of roughly 75 to 100 m, with a dry alternative for those who prefer it. Options are a 5 km Light, a 10 km Classic and a 10-mile Ultimate.
