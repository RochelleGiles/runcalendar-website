Course

An after-dark trail race from Delamere Forest Visitor Centre in Cheshire, one lap for the 10K and two for the half marathon. The course runs on well established forest trail with some potentially muddy and slippery sections, marked throughout with reflective signs. Half marathon starts at 6pm, 10K at 7pm; head torches required.
