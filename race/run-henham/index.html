About

Lace up your running shoes and join the festivities at Run Henham, a picturesque event set in the heart of Suffolk. Runners of all ages can choose between a challenging 10km race or a family-friendly 1km option, offering something for every fitness level. With scenic routes winding through the charming countryside, this event promises an unforgettable experience for runners seeking an escape from the everyday.
