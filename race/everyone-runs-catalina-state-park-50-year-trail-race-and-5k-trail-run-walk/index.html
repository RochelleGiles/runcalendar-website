About

A trail running event at Catalina State Park in Tucson featuring a 9.3-mile 50-Year Trail Race and a 5K trail run/walk on the Nature and Bridal Trails. The event includes homemade Mexican breakfast, free professional race photos, quality finisher shirts, and age group medals for participants.
