Course

A flat road marathon and half through Miami, finishing alongside the water at Bayfront Park. The half marathon shares the marathon finish, deviating only in the last 200 metres. A Tropical 5K is run the day before.

About

The Life Time Miami Marathon and Half Marathon runs through downtown Miami and across Biscayne Bay via the MacArthur Causeway into Miami Beach, passing the Art Deco district before returning to the finish near downtown.
