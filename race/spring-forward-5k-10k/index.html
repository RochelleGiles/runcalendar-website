Course

A single loop for the 5K and two loops for the 10K inside Morris Central Park, on a mix of grass, pressed gravel and dirt with some hills. Start and finish at Morris Central Park on North Cottage Street, Morris Plains. Water stations sit at roughly 1.5 and 3 miles.

About

The Spring Forward 5K & 10K is a mixed terrain race featuring grass, pressed gravel, dirt, and hills at Morris Central Park, supporting the Morris County Park Alliance. It offers a 5K single loop and a new 10K option with two loops, open to runners of all abilities with finisher medals, shirts, and post-race festivities.
