Course

Twisty, relatively smooth and fast trail running through mixed hardwood forest and fields at Rosaryville State Park, Upper Marlboro. The half marathon uses the 9-mile perimeter trail plus interior and wide horse trails; the 10K and 5K use shorter combinations of the same. Nine aid stations on the half, four on the 10K, two on the 5K.

About

An off-road trail race through Rosaryville State Park near Upper Marlboro, Maryland, hosted by EX2 Adventures. Runners choose from half marathon, 10K and 5K distances across wooded park trails.
