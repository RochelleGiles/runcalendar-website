Course

Four trail races at Chester Woods County Park near Eyota, Minnesota — 5K, 10 mile, 20 mile and 50K — all run on the park's trail network. The 50K starts at 6:00 am, the 20 mile at 7:00 and the 5K and 10 mile at 8:00. The organisers impose no cutoffs on any distance.

About

Storm the Woods - Mason Run, now past its 19th running, is part of the Storm Trail Race Series at Chester Woods Park in Eyota, MN. It carries on the legacy of the Mason Run with 5K, 10 mile, 20 mile and 50K distances across Chester Woods' rolling trail terrain.
