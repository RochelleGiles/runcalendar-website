Course

Starts and finishes on Southampton Common, with the half marathon, 10K and 5K run as laps of the Common. The route passes through woodland, grassland and open parkland and past ponds, wetlands and lakes. Water is provided on each lap and at the finish, with portable toilets on the route. The half marathon has 155 m of climb and a three-hour cut-off.

About

RunThrough's summer running festival on Southampton Common, a Site of Special Scientific Interest in the middle of the city: a half marathon at 09:30, then a 10K and a 5K, with a junior race at noon. The route starts and finishes on the Common and runs through woodland, grassland, ponds, wetland and lakes; the half climbs about 155 m in all. Chip-timed, with a medal for every finisher.
