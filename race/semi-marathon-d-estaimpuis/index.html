Course

All races start and finish at the Parc de Bourgogne, Place de Bourgogne in Estaimpuis. The half marathon sets off at 9:30 with water and food stops every 5 km and at the finish, a four-hour limit and pacers at 1:30, 1:45, 2:00 and 2:15. The 10 km race starts at 10:00 with refreshments at halfway and at the finish. A 10 km free walk leaves the same start between 8:00 and 10:00.

About

The Semi Marathon d'Estaimpuis is a road race in Estaimpuis, in the Belgian province of Hainaut close to the French border. It offers a 21 km half marathon and a 10 km race, both setting off from the Parc de Bourgogne, with a 10 km free walk alongside them.
