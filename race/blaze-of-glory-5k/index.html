Course

An out-and-back 5K on rural Bucks County roads, starting at 9am on Meetinghouse Road near Hunsicker Lane and turning onto Curly Hill Road for the bulk of the route. The turnaround sits past a gentle dip, with a water station near mile 1, and runners return the same way to finish.

About

The Blaze of Glory 5K is hosted by the Plumsteadville Volunteer Fire Company on rural Bucks County roads. Proceeds support the local volunteer firefighters.
