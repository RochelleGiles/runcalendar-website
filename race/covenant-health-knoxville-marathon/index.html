Course

A USA Track and Field certified Boston qualifier through Knoxville, Tennessee, run over rolling terrain with most of the elevation change in the first half and few genuinely flat miles. Marathon, half marathon and four-person relay start together at 7:30 am on the Sunday; the 5K is run the evening before. Eighteen aid stations serve the marathon and eight the half.

About

The Covenant Health Knoxville Marathon is an annual running event held in Knoxville, Tennessee. The event features a variety of race distances, including a full marathon, half marathon, 4-person relay, 5K, and a kids run.
