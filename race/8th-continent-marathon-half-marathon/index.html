Course

Laps of a 5.28 km loop starting and finishing at the Domain Grandstand in Auckland Domain: eight laps for the marathon, four for the half. Two aid stations, one on the start line and one near the museum, both with water.

About

Escape to the 8th Continent for an unforgettable running adventure at the Auckland Domain in New Zealand. Conquer the challenging yet picturesque marathon or half marathon routes, surrounded by lush greenery and breathtaking views. This unique event offers a one-of-a-kind experience for runners seeking an extraordinary challenge amidst the natural beauty of the region.
