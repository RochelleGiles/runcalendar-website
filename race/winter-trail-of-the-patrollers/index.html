About

The Winter Trail of the Patrollers is a captivating trail running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Swiss Alps. Runners will traverse a challenging course through the serene winter landscape, immersed in the rich history and traditions of the region's ski patrol. This unique event offers a thrilling opportunity to experience the beauty and adventure of the mountains in the heart of winter.
