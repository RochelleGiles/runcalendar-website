Course

Point-to-point running on the South West Coast Path in south Devon, roughly 75% off-road and technical in places. The 50km starts at Kingswear and finishes at Shaldon by way of Berry Head, Brixham, Goodrington, Torquay Harbour and Babbacombe, with four aid stations. The half marathon starts at Preston Green in Paignton and joins the ultra route at Torquay Harbour, with two aid stations.

About

Experience the breathtaking beauty of the South West Coast Path in Devon during the Total Coastal Ultra & Half Marathon. This scenic event offers runners the choice of a challenging 50km ultra or a half marathon route, both showcasing the rugged coastal landscapes that make this region a runner's paradise. Whether you're seeking an epic adventure or a scenic tour, the Total Coastal promises an unforgettable experience.
