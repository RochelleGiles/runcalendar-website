Course

A 4.167-mile loop just outside Crickhowell in the Black Mountains, repeated on the hour in last-one-standing format over 12 hours. The loop is roughly half trail and half tarmac, with one gradual climb and a short steep section; the rest is relatively flat and fast.

About

The Trails & Tarmac 12 Hour Summer Back Yard Ultra is a last one standing endurance race in the Black Mountains, Wales. Competitors must consecutively run a 4.2 mile lap in less than one hour, every hour, with the final lap being a unique sprint race in reverse direction.
