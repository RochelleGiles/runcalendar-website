Course

Mixed-terrain race starting and finishing at the Whitewater Center, running trail sections and the concrete whitewater channels themselves, drained for the event. The course includes steps, drop-offs, inclines, uneven footing and wet concrete. The run for children starts at 9:30am, the 1 mile at 10:00am and the 5K at 10:30am.

About

A trail-to-channel mixed terrain foot race through the channels of the U.S. National Whitewater Center without the rapids. The event includes a 5K, 1-mile run, and kids run (ages 9 and under), followed by a hot chocolate bar.
