Course

Three-day point-to-point stage race on sections of the Bibbulmun Track. The Karri long course covers 33-39km per stage; the Tingle short course covers 17-24km per stage. All stages start and finish within 30 minutes drive of Denmark, WA, with bus transport between stages. Surface is walking-track singletrail with coastal deviations past Greens Pool and Elephant Rocks; checkpoint food and drinks are provided.

About

The Run Bibbulmun Track Stage Race is a three-day point-to-point trail race on sections of the Bibbulmun Track near Denmark, Western Australia. Runners choose the Karri long course at 33-39km per stage or the Tingle short course at 17-24km per stage. All stages start and finish within 30 minutes of Denmark, with bus transport and on-course checkpoints.
