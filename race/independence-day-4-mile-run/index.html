Course

A flat, fast certified course through the streets of Bellmore with only nine turns. The start is on Pettit Avenue, 100 m west of the Bellmore Firehouse and opposite the LIRR station, with the finish on Grand Avenue. A 1-mile youth run for under-13s is held alongside the 4-mile.

About

This annual 4-mile race is dedicated to the memory of our friend Matthew Warkala. It's a fun, family-friendly event that brings the community together to celebrate Independence Day.
