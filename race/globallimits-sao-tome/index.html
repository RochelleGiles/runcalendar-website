About

Explore the tropical paradise of São Tomé at the Global Limits ultramarathon! This stunning island nation off the west coast of Africa hosts a challenging and breathtaking race through lush rainforests, past pristine beaches, and up rugged volcanic peaks. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or a first-time adventurer, the Global Limits São Tomé event promises an unforgettable experience amidst the island's unique natural beauty.
