Course

Starts and finishes at Knysna High School sports field on Waterfront Drive, with the marathon away at 07:00 and the half at 08:00. Both routes climb into the indigenous forest above the town on gravel forestry roads, opening onto a view over the Knysna lagoon and The Heads before the descent to the finish. The race is run as part of the Knysna Oyster Festival.

About

Explore the serene Knysna Forest as you challenge yourself at the Knysna Forest Marathon. This iconic South African event takes runners through the lush, verdant landscapes of the Garden Route, offering a scenic and unforgettable running experience. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, the Knysna Forest Marathon promises an unparalleled adventure in nature.
