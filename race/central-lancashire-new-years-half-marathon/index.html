Course

Fast single-lap half marathon on gently undulating rural roads through the Central Lancashire countryside, starting and finishing at the Marriott Hotel in Broughton, Preston. Around 75 metres of climbing across the 13.1 miles, with wide open views. Water stations roughly every three miles and at the finish. Entry limited and open to runners aged 17 and over.

About

An amazing opportunity to burn off those festive calories. The Central Lancs New Year Half Marathon runs through some very picturesque countryside and villages. The course is flat(ish). Entry is strictly limited to 600 competitors and always sells out, so be sure to get your entry in early.
