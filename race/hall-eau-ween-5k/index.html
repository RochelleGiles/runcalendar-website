Course

About

Get ready for 3 p-EAU-int 1 miles of Halloween fun as you dash through the festively lit bridges and streets of downtown Eau Claire! Whether you're a seasoned runner or pushing a stroller full of goblins, this night is sure to be a hauntingly good time for all. Costumes are encouraged — so grab your witches, werewolves, and superheroes and join us for an unforgettable evening of fright and delight!
