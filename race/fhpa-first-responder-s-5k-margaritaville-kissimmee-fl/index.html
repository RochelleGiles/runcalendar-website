About

The FHPA First Responder's 5K is a run and walk at the Promenade at Sunset Walk in Kissimmee, Florida, raising funds for the Florida Highway Patrol Advancement association. The single 5K route loops the Margaritaville Resort Orlando complex and is chip-timed.
