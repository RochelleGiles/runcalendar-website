About

A half marathon across Dubai's skyline, from one landmark to another. The course is fast and mostly flat, with music every kilometre and the sun coming up as you run. The start is 06:30 in batched corrals by expected finish time, with a three-hour cut-off, and runners must be 16 or older on race day. More than 15,000 runners from over 170 nationalities finished the last edition. A mini marathon for children aged 3 to 12 runs the day before.
