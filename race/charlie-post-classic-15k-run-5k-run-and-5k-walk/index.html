About

The Charlie Post Classic 15K and 5K run and walk is the Charleston Running Club's signature winter event. This run/walk honors the memory of Dr. Charlie Post, a well-known Charleston runner, triathlete, and family physician who volunteered his time and medical talents to the running community.
