Course

An out-and-back fun run on the Animas River Trail, starting at Rotary Park in Durango and heading north 1.55 miles to just before 32nd Street before returning to the start. There is no chip timing or prizes. It is run as a zero-waste event and runners are asked to carry their own water.

About

The Earth Day 5K is a community run in Durango, Colorado, organized around Earth Day by Durango Nature Studies and the Durango Running Club to celebrate the outdoors.
