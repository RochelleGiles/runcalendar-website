Course

The marathon starts and finishes on Castray Esplanade, with the finish in front of Princes Wharf. Roughly 20 drink stations run from the 3.6km mark to 41.6km carrying water and electrolyte, with special needs points at designated places. The marathon cut-off is six hours. Course shape and surface are not stated by the organiser.

About

The Hobart Marathon Festival offers runners a stunning course along the picturesque Constitution Dock, Derwent River, and historic Salamanca Wharves. Soak in the vibrant waterfront atmosphere as you wind through this iconic Tasmanian city, with the majestic Mount Wellington as the backdrop. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time runner, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
