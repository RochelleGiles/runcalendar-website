About

The Three Forts Challenge is a challenging trail running event featuring steep climbs, rough tracks, and scenic routes through the South Downs. Runners pass iconic landmarks like Chanctonbury Ring and Cissbury Ring, enjoying panoramic countryside views while navigating mixed terrain.
