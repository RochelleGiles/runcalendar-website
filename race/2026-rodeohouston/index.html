Course

Point-to-point through downtown Houston, starting at Walker and Bagby Streets and finishing on Allen Parkway near Eleanor Tinsley Park, ahead of the 10:00 AM Downtown Rodeo Parade. The wheelchair 10K starts at 9:00 AM, 10K waves at 9:10 and the 5K at 9:35, all on paved city streets.

About

The Rodeo Run presented by ConocoPhillips is a 5K/10K walk/run/wheelchair race that precedes the Downtown Rodeo Parade in Houston. Proceeds benefit the Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo, supporting education in Texas.
