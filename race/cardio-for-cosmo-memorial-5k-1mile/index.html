About

Memorial 5K run and 1-mile walk honoring fallen Detective Steven T. Cosmo, organized by Burlington Township Police FOP Lodge #84. Supports suicide prevention for law enforcement and military, PTSD treatment for veterans, and scholarships for students interested in related fields.
