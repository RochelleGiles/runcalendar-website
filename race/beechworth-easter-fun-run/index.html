Course

All three distances start from Baarmutha Park in Beechworth. The 12km, 8km and 3km courses run on a mix of road and gravel through the historic goldfields town, with the 3km taking in a lap of the Beechworth Golf Course.

About

Explore the historic town of Beechworth, Australia during the Easter Fun Run, featuring picturesque trail runs of 3km, 8km, and 12km through the region's scenic landscapes. Hosted by the Beechworth Football Netball Club, this annual event offers a unique opportunity to experience the Golden Horseshoes' natural beauty while challenging your running abilities.
