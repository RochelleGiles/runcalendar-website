Course

A three-day trail stage race based at the Beulah Shelter in Greensfelder County Park, 4515 Hencken Road, Wildwood, Missouri. Stage one covers about 21 miles across Greensfelder and Rockwoods Range, stage two about 26 miles over the full Beulah Trail with the steepest climbs, and stage three about 25K of flowing singletrack. Expect steep grades, rocks, roots and creek crossings throughout.

About

St. Louis's only three-day trail running stage race — the Thunder Chicken 100 Stage Races! Hosted at Greensfelder County Park (Beulah Shelter) in western St. Louis County, Missouri, this event was created for adventure seekers who want a full weekend of challenge, community, and unforgettable trail running. Participants come together for tough races, great fellowship, and the chance to experience some of the very best trails STL has to offer!
