About

Run, jog, walk, dawdle and even push a stroller - this fantastic community fun run has something for everyone. For the serious runners, the 10km run is sure to challenge, while both the 2km and 5km routes will give you a real sense of accomplishment. Best of all, join in for a good cause. All proceeds raised will be donated to charity - 50% to KYDS Youth Counselling, 25% to Hornsby Kuringgai Women's Shelter and 25% to Mentoring Men.
