Course

A point-to-point run along the full length of the Cape to Cape Track in Western Australia's South West, between Cape Naturaliste and Cape Leeuwin. The 100-mile covers the whole 160km from Augusta to Yallingup; the 50-mile takes a section of it. Underfoot is dramatic limestone clifftop, long stretches of soft coastal sand and ancient Karri forest.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of Western Australia's South West region in the new Cape2Cape Ultra Marathon. This challenging event offers both a 100-mile and 50-mile ultra course, taking runners through stunning coastal landscapes and lush forests. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a new adventure, the Cape2Cape promises an unforgettable running experience.
