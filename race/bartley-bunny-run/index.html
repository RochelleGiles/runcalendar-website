Course

About

Come enjoy a beautiful trail run at Bartley Ranch Regional Park. This is a majority dirt trail with a slight hill to the scenic overlook of Audrey Harris Park (windy hill lookout). The trail starts at Bartley Ranch Regional Park, goes through Anderson Park trail, back up to windy hill, and ends at Bartley Ranch Regional Park.
