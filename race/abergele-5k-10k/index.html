Course

A flat, fast route starting on the promenade at Abergele/Pensarn on the North Wales coast. The 5K starts at 9:30am and the 10K at 11am, and runners may enter both and race the double on the same day.

About

The Abergele 5K & 10K offers participants the unique opportunity to run in both distances on the same day. This double-distance challenge takes place in the scenic Welsh coastal town of Abergele.
