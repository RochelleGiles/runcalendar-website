Course

A figure-of-eight route around the Gower Peninsula from a basecamp at Penrice Castle, taking in golden beaches, limestone cliffs, wild moors, ridges and woodland. The 100km splits into a Western loop (50km, ~1200m ascent) and an Eastern loop (50km, ~1000m ascent), with shorter 75km, 50km, 25km and 10-mile options. Rest stops roughly every 12.5km and major stops every 25km with catering and medical cover.

About

Conquer the rugged beauty of the Gower Peninsula in this self-paced ultra challenge. Runners and walkers will embark on a scenic trail adventure starting from Penrice Castle in Swansea, immersing themselves in the stunning coastal landscapes of this Welsh gem. Whether tackling the full distance or a shorter route, participants will be rewarded with breathtaking views and a true test of endurance.
