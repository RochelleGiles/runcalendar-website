About

Explore the stunning landscapes of Gran Canaria on the Sky Gran Canaria Beyond The Coast Sky Tour. This challenging running event takes participants on a breathtaking journey through the island's mountainous terrain, offering unparalleled views of the Atlantic Ocean and the rugged coastline. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or seeking a new adventure, this event promises an unforgettable experience amidst the natural beauty of Gran Canaria.
