About

The Ultra Trail Du Vercors is a challenging trail running event set in the beautiful Vercors region of France. The course features stunning mountain landscapes, technical trails, and significant elevation gain, making it a true test of endurance for participants. The event offers multiple race distances, catering to runners of all abilities, and is known for its welcoming atmosphere and stunning scenery.
