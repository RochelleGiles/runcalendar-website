About

The ABP Newport Marathon boasts one of the flattest marathon courses in Europe, making it a popular choice for runners seeking a fast and scenic race through the charming streets of Newport, Wales. With a route that showcases the city's historic landmarks and stunning coastal views, this event offers an unforgettable running experience for participants of all levels.
