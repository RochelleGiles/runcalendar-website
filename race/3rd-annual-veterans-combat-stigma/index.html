About

Join us for a meaningful and motivating run/walk to combat stigma and promote mental health awareness! The race kicks off and finishes at Northampton Community College's Bethlehem Campus, following a scenic, mostly flat, and fast point-to-point route through local neighborhoods on paved roads. It's perfect for both seasoned runners and casual participants!
