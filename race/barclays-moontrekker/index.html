Course

An overnight event on Lantau Island starting from the Mui Wo football pitch at 8pm and finishing at Pui O Beach. The 40km and 30km routes climb to the summit of Lantau Peak before sunrise; a 20km introductory route for first-time participants leaves out the peak.

About

Barclays MoonTrekker is an overnight trekking and running event across Lantau Island, raising funds for The Nature Conservancy's conservation work in Hong Kong.
