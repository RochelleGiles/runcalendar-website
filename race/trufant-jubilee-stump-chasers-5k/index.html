About

Come join us for the Annual "Stump Chasers" 5K Run/Walk during the Great Trufant Jubilee! This event will take you on a slightly challenging course. We will register at the north entrance/parking area of the famous "Trufant Flea Market" then proceed up 4th Street to Kohler to the start. Then you will travel over country roads, and back to the registration area. There will be a water station along the route. Light snacks and water at the end. Both the runners and walkers will take off together. All ages and abilities are encouraged to join us!! Raffle prizes possible for all, both runners and walkers!
