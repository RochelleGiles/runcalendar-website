Course

The 15K is an out-and-back on back-country roads near the Little River; the 7K is a loop over the same terrain without the out-and-back section. Both start and finish at Peaceful Side Social in Townsend. Water stops on course are passed once by the 7K and twice by the 15K.

About

The Townsend 15K and 7K is a road race in Townsend, Tennessee, near the entrance to Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Runners choose between the two distances on rural roads through the foothills.
