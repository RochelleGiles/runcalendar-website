About

The Marathon Höga Kusten is a stunning running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of Sweden's High Coast region. Participants can challenge themselves on a scenic course that winds through the area's dramatic fjords, forests, and coastal landscapes. This annual race is a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable adventure.
