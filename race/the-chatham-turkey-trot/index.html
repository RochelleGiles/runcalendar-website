About

The Chatham Turkey Trot is an annual Thanksgiving Day event featuring a 1 Mile Family Fun Run and a USA Track & Field certified 5K Race through the scenic Highlands section of Chatham Township, NJ. It raises funds for first responders, veterans, and diabetes research, having collected over $365,000 since 2009.
