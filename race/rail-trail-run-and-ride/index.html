Course

Runs along the North East Rail Trail out of Scottsdale, on the old railway formation's flat, gentle grades through Tasmania's rural north-east. The 54km starts at the Old Scottsdale Railway Station at 8am; the 27km, 14km, 7km and 2km start later from the event village, with bus transport out to the remote starts.

About

Rail Trail Run and Ride is a multi-distance running and riding event in Scottsdale, Tasmania, following the flat, gentle grades of the North East Rail Trail through the state's rural north-east.
