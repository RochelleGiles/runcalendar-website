About

The STRUTT with your MUTT 5K is a dog-friendly trail run/walk event supporting Adoption First Animal Rescue. It features a 5K fun run, 1 mile fun walk, post-race festivities including food trucks and vendor village, with all participants receiving medals and goodie bags.
