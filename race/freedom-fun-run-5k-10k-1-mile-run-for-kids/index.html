About

Come celebrate FREEDOM with us at the 4th Annual Freedom Fun Run. All races begin at Brophy Park, and end in the same location. Enjoy the coolness and beauty of Lake Darling, Cowdry & Brophy Lake and the rolling hills of the Brophy Bike Trails on your way to the finish line!
