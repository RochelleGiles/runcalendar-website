Course

Coastal road and shared-path course on Perth's northern beaches, finishing at Tom Simpson Park in Mullaloo. Six distances are offered: 2 km, 5 km, 10 km, 21.1 km, 32 km and the full 42.2 km marathon.

About

Own the sunrise on the Sunset Coast and kick your run and fitness goals on this stunning course navigating the coastal shared path! With walk/run and race distances to suit all ages and abilities to choose from, commit and get your spring off to the best start. Whether you are competitive, out for fitness or fun with you family and friends, get your entry in early to capitalise on early-bird entry pricing.
