About

Run the Night Dublin is an all-ages, community night run through the vibrant city center of Ireland's capital. Powered by the Dublin City Council, this 5-mile event offers runners of all abilities the chance to experience the city under the glow of the evening lights. With a focus on bringing the community together, Run the Night Dublin promises an engaging and unique running experience.
