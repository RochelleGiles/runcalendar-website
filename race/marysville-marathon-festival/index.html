Course

Trail festival based at Gallipoli Park in Marysville, Victoria. Saturday runs a Kids Dash, KQOM 7km and 14km and a 100km ultra; Sunday runs 4km, 10km, 21km, 42km and 50km. Courses follow valley trails and forest tracks around the Steavenson River, taking in fern gullies, waterfalls and lookouts, with climbs and technical descents.

About

The Marysville Marathon Festival is a two-day trail running event based at Gallipoli Park in Marysville, Victoria. Saturday hosts a 100km ultra plus KQOM 7km and 14km races and a kids dash; Sunday hosts 4km, 10km, 21km, 42km and 50km events on forest tracks and valley trails.
