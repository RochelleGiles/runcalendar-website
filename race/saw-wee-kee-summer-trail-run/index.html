Course

Run entirely on the natural soft-surface trails at Saw Wee Kee Park, with twists, turns and short climbs, and conditions that can be muddy. Each race in the series uses a different course. Runners must carry their own hydration; water stations are provided for refilling.

About

The Saw Wee Kee Summer Trail Run is part of the Oswegoland Park District’s trail run series at Saw Wee Kee Park, featuring a 4-mile natural course with twists, turns, and elevation changes. Runners are timed using bibs at staggered intervals and receive seasonal swag and a collectible medal piece.
