Course

A lapped course from the Red Lodge Mountain basecamp on Ski Run Road: three laps for the half marathon, six for the marathon, one for the Hot Lap. Singletrack, ski access road and a little bushwhacking, climbing roughly 900 to 1,200 feet per lap, with a snack table at basecamp.

Marathon42.2 km
Half Marathon21.1 km

About

A throwback to how running used to be and we don't mean uphill both ways (although the course might feel like that!). This casual race goes back to the roots of trail running: good company, good terrain, and good beer. Make yourself at home at the start/finish basecamp and get ready for the race of your life.
