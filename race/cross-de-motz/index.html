About

Cross de Motz: One of the most spectacular, friendly and competitive running events in the region. This outdoor event takes place at the Espace Sport et Nature du Fier-Motz with a course on natural terrain including lawn, forest, gravel paths, paved roads and muddy paths. There is a race for each age group starting from 3 years old, with distances of 0.25 km, 0.5 km, 1 km, 2 km, 5 km, 7 km, & 12 km. Athletes can participate in more than one race, both in individual and/or team categories.
