Course

Run almost entirely on the Jordan River Parkway Trail, starting and finishing at East River Front Park in South Jordan, Utah. The route is flat and well marked with flags, ribbons and trail marshals. Marathon starts at 6:30am and the half marathon at 6:45am.

About

The beautiful Jordan River has plenty to offer for a Marathon. Just like our Half Marathons that run along the Jordan River we have taken what we feel is the best course for running and scenery. This course takes place almost entirely on paved trails with no running on or crossing the road at all. This course will be green and beautiful during the Spring with flower trees in full bloom. You will fall in love with the Jordan River Parkway trail after this one!
