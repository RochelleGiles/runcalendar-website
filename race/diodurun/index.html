About

Diodurun is a unique trail running challenge featuring 8 loops of 6.7 km with 150m elevation gain. Runners must complete each loop within one hour, with a new start every hour, testing both physical endurance and strategic recovery. The event takes place in the historic area of Diodurum, a former Gallo-Roman territory, blending athletic challenge with local archaeological heritage.
