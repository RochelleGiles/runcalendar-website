Course

Point-to-point trail race on the Green Gateways route linking the parks and green spaces of North Leeds, from Station Top car park on the Chevin to the finish in Otley. It passes Yeadon Banks, Yeadon Tarn and Danefield Woods through parkland and forest; the marathon adds The Billing and a riverside stretch along the River Aire.

About

The Punk Panther season curtain raiser. With a number of distances from half-marathon to ultra, this trail race (formerly the North Leeds Greenway) follows the route of Walkers are Welcome (Otley) Green Gateways book. If you would like a copy of the guide book please email us admin@punkpanther.co.uk. The walk sets itself apart thanks to its city-centre setting. Green Gateways Greenway is a little-known trail that unites the parks and public green spaces around Leeds. Despite taking runners close to the heart of this Northern Powerhouse, the race remains substantially off-road – much of the return route is along the Meanwood Valley Trail – making it easy to forget one is so close to this bustling city.
