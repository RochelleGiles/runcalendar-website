Course

A 3.1-mile north woods loop starting and finishing in Crosslake Town Square, with the start line near Rafferty's. The route is stroller friendly and open to leashed dogs, and there is a 5K ruck division alongside the run/walk. The kids race is two laps of Town Square.

About

Join us as we celebrate 12 years of running fun! Don your green apparel and get ready to have some fun on this St. Patrick's Day fun run/walk. All ages are welcome in the 5k!
