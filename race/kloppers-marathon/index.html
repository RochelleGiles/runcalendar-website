Course

The marathon, half marathon and 10 km start at the corner of Zola Budd Street and Kings Way in Bloemfontein and run on tarred city streets past the City Hall, hospitals and schools, finishing on the paved sidewalk just beyond the entrance to the Advocates' Chambers. A 5 km fun run is also offered.
