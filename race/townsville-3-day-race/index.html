Course

Three separate timed runs on consecutive days, each starting and finishing at Strand Park, Townsville. Day 1 loops through Jezzine Barracks, the Esplanade and Sir Leslie Thiess Drive; Day 2 runs inland to Pallarenda Road, turning near Town Common; Day 3 crosses Captains Creek and follows canal paths back to the foreshore. All courses use street footpaths and promenades.
