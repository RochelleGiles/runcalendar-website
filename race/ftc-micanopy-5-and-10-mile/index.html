Course

Starts in historic Micanopy and runs on unsealed dirt and lime rock roads beneath moss-draped oaks. The 5 mile is a relatively flat out-and-back; the 10 mile repeats that section then adds a hilly loop south of town. Water at 2.5 miles, and again at 7.5 miles for the 10 mile.

About

The FTC Micanopy 5 and 10 Mile is a Florida Track Club road race near Gainesville, Florida, with 5-mile and 10-mile options on quiet, rolling country roads each February. The course loops around and through the small town of Micanopy.
