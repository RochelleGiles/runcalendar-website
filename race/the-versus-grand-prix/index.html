Course

Two anti-clockwise laps of the Kyalami Grand Prix Circuit in Midrand on wide tarmac with sweeping corners, starting and finishing at the circuit. Two water points on route; the post-race gathering is in the pit garages.

About

The Versus Grand Prix is a 10km road race held entirely on the Kyalami Grand Prix Circuit in Midrand, Gauteng. Runners complete two laps of the Formula One-grade racetrack with the road closed to cars.
