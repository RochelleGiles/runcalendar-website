Course

An 8.2-mile loop run counter-clockwise from near the Visitor's Center in Greensfelder Park, over rolling hills and technical trail including the Eagle Valley climb, finishing at the yellow gate. Aid stations sit at 3 miles and 6.2 miles. Marathon entrants add a 2.4-mile Dogwood Trail loop before joining the main circuit.

About

Come get loopy with us! The DeClue Loops Trail Race will be held Oct. 10, 2026 in scenic Greensfelder Park in Wildwood, MO. The DeClue Trail is a 8.2-mile loop that begins and ends near the Visitor's Center in the heart of Greensfelder Park. Runners will run counter-clockwise starting and finishing near the trailhead.
