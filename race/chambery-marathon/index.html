About

The Chambery Marathon offers a stunning running experience between lakes and mountains. Runners can enjoy a fast, flat course that passes through the historic city center and along a scenic cycling path between Chambéry and Aix-les-Bains. The event provides a welcoming, family-friendly atmosphere for runners of all levels.
