Course

A point-to-point coastal trail race from Redcar to Whitby along the King Charles III England Coast Path, passing the 'Circle' sculpture and a string of fishing villages. Underfoot is mud, sand, rock and woodland, with around 3,263ft of climbing over the cliffs. Four marshalled checkpoints supply water, snacks and soft drinks; the cutoff is 10 hours.

About

Explore the stunning North East coast of the UK during the Charm Bracelet Marathon & Half Marathon. This off-road trail running event takes you along the scenic Cleveland Way, offering breathtaking views of rugged cliffs, rolling hills, and charming seaside towns. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your fitness journey, this unique challenge promises an unforgettable experience.
