Course

Mountain trails through the Serra de Tramuntana above Sóller, with distances from a short trail race to a full ultra. Rocky, technical paths, dry-stone terraces and long climbs to the ridges, with the Mediterranean below for most of the way.

About

Mallorca is the essence of the Mediterranean. An island with thousands of corners to discover and get excited about. Mallorca is the sea, the land, and the air. Nature in its purest state. A race at the heart of the Mediterranean, with a great variety of landscapes. Four distances in the 100M, 100K, 50K and 20K categories, which will cross the UNESCO World Heritage Serra de Tramuntana Natural Site. An extraordinary adventure in a unique setting.
