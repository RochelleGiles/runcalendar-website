Course

A loop course at Harris Lake County Park, one loop for the 5 mile and two for the 10 mile. The route opens with about a mile of wide dirt road before turning onto singletrack with lake views. The organiser rates the terrain 2+ out of 5 with minimal roots and rocks. Aid stations sit every 1 to 2 miles.

About

Offering 5 and 10 mile course options that include outstanding lake views, mellow single-track, and scenic open field running, the 5th Annual Common Rush Trail Run at Harris Lake is a super-fun, mid-summer trail run at one of the most beautiful parks in the Triangle.
