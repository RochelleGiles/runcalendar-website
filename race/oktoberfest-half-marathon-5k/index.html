Course

Both distances start and finish at the Hi-Wire Beer Garden in Asheville's River Arts District. The half marathon loops out through the district, past areas still recovering from Hurricane Helene, and into historic neighbourhoods and greenways; the 5K stays mostly on the River Arts District greenway. Aid stations on course, three-hour limit.
