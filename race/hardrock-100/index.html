About

The Hardrock Hundred Mile Endurance Run is a 100-mile loop through the San Juan Mountains of Colorado, linking the old mining towns of Silverton, Telluride and Ouray with an average elevation over 11,000 feet. Finishers must kiss the Hardrock, and entry via its famously oversubscribed lottery is one of the hardest tickets in ultrarunning.
