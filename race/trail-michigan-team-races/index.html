Course

An overnight trail relay at Hanson Hills Recreation Area near Grayling, run on smooth single-track through wooded bluffs. Teams cover 134.4 miles and 17,216 feet of climb between them, averaging 16.8 miles and 2,152 feet per runner. Eight-person, four-person ultra and three-person sprint formats are offered.
