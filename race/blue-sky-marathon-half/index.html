Course

Starts at the Blue Sky Trailhead west of Fort Collins (half at South Bay Pavilion), running between Horsetooth Mountain Park and Devil's Backbone on 100% dirt and roughly 90% singletrack, from smooth trail to technical rock gardens. The marathon (Saturday) has 8 aid stations and intermediate cutoffs; the half (Sunday) has 5.

About

Taking place on the trails between Horsetooth Mountain Park and the Devil's Backbone Open Space, the Blue Sky courses take you on a north-south tour of the easternmost hogbacks of Northern Colorado's Front Range mountains. With a moderate amount of elevation gain and loss and a variety of terrain, the route offers a little bit of something for everyone.
