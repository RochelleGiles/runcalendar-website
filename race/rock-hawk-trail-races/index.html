Course

Run on the rolling Miller Park and Ridgeline trail systems at Castle Rock, Colorado. The organisers describe the singletrack as not overly technical but consistently climbing, with long views across the ridge. Four distances share the trail network, from a 5K up to a 50K.

About

Held on the gorgeous, thrilling and rolling trails of Castle Rock's Miller Park & Ridgeline Trail systems. While not overly technical, these trails will challenge and excite, as you soak up the amazing views. This is an excellent race for both first-time and veteran trail runners, and provides a nice test for those who are training for longer summer events.
