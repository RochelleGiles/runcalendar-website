About

The Color Run Luxembourg by Baloise is a vibrant and exhilarating event that celebrates the joy of running. Participants are doused in a rainbow of colored powders as they race through the scenic streets of Luxembourg, creating a truly unforgettable experience. This family-friendly event is perfect for runners of all levels, inviting everyone to embrace the spirit of community and have a blast getting colorful.
