About

Explore the breathtaking Trail de la Vallée de Joux, a challenging yet rewarding running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Jura Mountains in Switzerland. This scenic trail race takes runners through picturesque alpine landscapes, offering a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail de la Vallée de Joux promises an unforgettable running adventure.
