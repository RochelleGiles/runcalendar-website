Course

Gravel-and-trail run through the ancient woodland of the New Forest National Park. Routes are largely flat and untechnical on moderately smooth tracks with a gravel surface, though mud and short steep pitches appear in places. Three distances: roughly 22km (+172m), 12km (+111m) and 6km (+31m).

About

The Maverick New Forest Gravel Run explores the stunning New Forest National Park's ancient woodland trails in Hampshire. The terrain is moderately smooth and generally covered with a gravel surface, featuring fast, flat and untechnical routes.
