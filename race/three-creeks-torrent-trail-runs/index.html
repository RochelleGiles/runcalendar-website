About

The Three Creeks Torrent Trail Runs is a trail race sponsored by Columbia Track Club at Three Creeks Conservation Area south of Columbia, Missouri. The course features approximate 10-mile loops that are 80% single track trail with creek crossings and 950 feet of elevation gain per loop.
