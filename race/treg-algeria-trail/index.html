Course

Staged desert racing out of Timimoun, an oasis on the edge of the Grand Erg Occidental in the Algerian Sahara. Three distances are offered: the 182 km Ultra Algeria Trail, the 106 km Algeria Trail and the 50 km Algeria Marathon. The event runs from 29 November to 7 December.

About

The Treg Algeria Trail is a trail running event in one of the most beautiful oases of the Sahara, on the edge of the Grand Erg Occidental. Whether you are a seasoned desert runner or looking for a first experience, the Treg Algeria Trail has a race for you - from the 182km Ultra Algeria Trail to the 50km Algeria Marathon. After the effort, the serenity of the Timimoun oasis will allow you to recover in peace.
