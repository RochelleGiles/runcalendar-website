About

The Seawitch 5K Classic is the centerpiece of Rehoboth Beach's Seawitch Racing Festival Weekend, run alongside a costumed 1-mile Fright Walk each Halloween season. Now in its 36th year, it's one of the Delaware beaches' longest-standing running traditions.
