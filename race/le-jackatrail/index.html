Course

Trail races in Parc regional Montagne du Diable at Ferme-Neuve in the Laurentians, with 1 km, 3 km, 5 km, 10 km, 25 km, 42 km and 65 km courses on marked forest singletrack. The organisers describe the routes as demanding but accessible, and provide aid stations on the longer distances, drop-bag service, free shuttles and medical staff.

About

An ultra-trail race in Parc régional Montagne du Diable in Quebec's Laurentides region. Distances from 5 km to 65 km cross forested mountain terrain north of Ferme-Neuve.
