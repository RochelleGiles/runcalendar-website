About

A clucking good time to kickstart your fitness goals and ring in the New Year with a dash of fun and motivation! Dust off your running shoes, put on your chicken-themed best, and join us in a clucktastic experience at Oviedo Mall's Chicken Run! All levels or walking and running are encouraged to participate. Proceeds support Victory Track club and various local youth sports programs.
