Course

A flat and fast 5K on closed, traffic-free roads through Middlesbrough town centre, run alongside the Middlesbrough 10K and passing the Riverside stadium. Water is provided on the route and at the finish, with chip timing, marshalled junctions and a baggage drop.

About

The Ali Brownlee 5K celebrates the 'Voice of the Boro', beloved radio broadcaster Ali Brownlee, known for his passionate Middlesbrough F.C. commentary and his community fundraising work. The event features a flat, fast course starting and finishing at Centre Square.
