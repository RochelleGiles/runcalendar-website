About

The Magnetic 10in10 Marathon Event in Townsville, Australia challenges runners to complete 10 full marathons over 10 consecutive days. This unique endurance test takes place in the stunning coastal region, offering breathtaking ocean views and a truly unforgettable running experience for seasoned athletes.
