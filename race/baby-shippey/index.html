Course

Run on the Beaumont property over gravel, powerline and ATV paths, with rugged rock traverses, fast jeep roads and steep climbs and descents across primitive ground. The Half covers 13.05 miles with about 2,390ft of gain, including a narrow gravel switchback and two powerline sections; the 10K shares the first three miles, splits off, then rejoins for the last 1.5. Aid at 3 and 8.2 miles on the Half, at 3 miles on the 10K.
