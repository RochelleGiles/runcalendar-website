About

Lexington Habitat for Humanity's Shamrock Shuffle is a chip-timed 3K run and walk through downtown Lexington, Kentucky, starting outside the Old Fayette County Courthouse. It is open to all ages and abilities and raises funds for the affiliate's housing work.
