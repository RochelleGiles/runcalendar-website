Course

Starts and finishes at Roundhill Campsite, Brockenhurst, with 21.6k, 16.7k and 10k routes on wide, hard-packed gravel paths through ancient woodland and heathland. Slightly undulating, with only about 185m of climb over the half marathon. Marked with pink arrows and tape; water stations on course. Passes a disused WWII airfield and grazing New Forest ponies.

About

Starting from Roundhill Campsite, the route is on wide, hard packed gravel pathways that weave through magnificent forests, ancient woodland and beautiful heathland. The course is slightly undulating / relatively flat and the terrain is well maintained and solid underfoot trails.
