About

The Nassfeld Mountain Skytrails is Austria's southernmost skyrunning and trail running event, held at the Nassfeld Pass. The challenge is to conquer four trails of varying difficulty around the pass, crossing rugged rocks and green meadows with views spanning the Italian and Austrian sides.
