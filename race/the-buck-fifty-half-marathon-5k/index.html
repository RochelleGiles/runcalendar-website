About

We are kicking off a new event for you this July 5th, and what a race and challenge it will be. After many conversations with our Buck Fifty Runners the one thing we continually hear is that the half marathon distance is one of their favorites. Like most things with The Buck Fity this will not be an easy race as it has 1,000 feet of elevation gain over the 13.1 miles. For you 5K runners we do have a nice flat track so it's a great chance for a personal record!
