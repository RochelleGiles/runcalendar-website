Course

A 6.7km loop through the Otematata Wetlands, repeated on the hour under backyard ultra rules from a 9am Saturday start. The surface is firmly packed fine gravel with about 200m of single track and some grass, and the loop is close to flat. Runners pass views of the wetlands, Lake Aviemore and the surrounding mountains, with toilets at the start corral and near 4km.

About

The Best Dam Backyard Ultra is a unique endurance running event where competitors attempt to complete a 6.7km loop within each hour. Set in the scenic Otematata Wetlands, runners challenge themselves to complete as many laps as possible, with the last person standing declared the winner. The course offers minimal elevation, well-marked trails, and stunning views of Lake Aviemore and surrounding mountains.
