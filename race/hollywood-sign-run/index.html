Course

Out-and-back trail courses in Griffith Park, Los Angeles, all starting and finishing at the same park staging area. The 8-mile route climbs to the Hollywood Sign viewpoint with views of downtown LA and Griffith Observatory; shorter 8K and 5K options turn earlier. Aid stations with medical staff are placed along the course.

About

The Hollywood Sign Run is a trail running and hiking event in Griffith Park featuring scenic out-and-back courses to the Hollywood Sign Viewpoint. Participants enjoy panoramic views of Downtown Los Angeles, Griffith Observatory, and the iconic Hollywood Sign, with options for all levels.[2]
