Course

A 30-hour timed ultra on an irregular 2.5-mile loop in Roosevelt Forest, over New England roots, rocks and rolling hills with about 201 ft of gain a lap. Entrants pick 26.2 miles, 50K, 50 miles, 100K, 100 miles or simply run as far as they can; 40 loops makes 100 miles. The pavilion is the sole aid station, stocked day and night.

About

The Forbidden Forest Ultra is a 30-hour timed race on a 2.5-mile loop through Roosevelt Forest in Stratford, on typical New England terrain of roots, rocks and rolling hills. Runners choose their own target distance, from a marathon up to 100 miles, with a finisher buckle for completing the full 100.
