About

Fun people + beautiful trails + good food = a great day. This is a clothing optional 5K trail run/walk through the woods, trails, and meadows of Carolina Foothills Resort. You may choose to run the 7.3K at no additional cost. If you run the 7.3k, you will also be entered into the 5K. Please choose only one race length. We give awards for first, second, and third place winners in the 5K as well as a special finisher's medal for those who complete the 7.3K Mike Ward Challenge.
