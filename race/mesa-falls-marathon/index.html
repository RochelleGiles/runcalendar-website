Course

The marathon is point to point, starting on Scenic Highway 47 in Targhee National Forest near the Warm River headwaters and finishing on the track at North Fremont High School in Ashton. It takes in a pedestrian path at Lower Mesa Falls, a tunnel underpass at Bear Gulch and a railroad bed section, and is fully paved from mile 17. The shorter races start and finish at the school track.
