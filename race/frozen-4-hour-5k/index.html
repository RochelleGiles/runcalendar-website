Course

Start and finish at the Lester-Amity chalet. Runners cover a 5K main loop for the first three hours, then switch to a half-mile secondary loop for the final hour, marked at each 0.1-mile increment. Runners may enter and leave the course only at the start/finish. The race runs 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

About

The Frozen 4 Hour & 5K is a winter trail running event in Lester Park, Duluth, MN, featuring a timed 4-hour race where runners complete as many 5K loops as possible on snowpacked trails. A single 5K option is available for those preferring one lap.
