About

Experience the breathtaking coastal scenery of Australia's Great Ocean Road at the annual Great Ocean Road Running Festival. Runners of all levels can challenge themselves on courses that wind through the region's stunning natural landscapes, from rugged cliffs to tranquil beaches. This popular event offers an unforgettable running adventure along one of the world's most scenic coastal drives.
