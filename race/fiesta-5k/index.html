About

Come out and celebrate Cinco de Mayo with the Fiesta 5K. All participants will receive a shirt and FINISHER MEDAL! After the race, show your bib and finisher medal to Compadres in Painesville and receive 3 free tacos with your order! The course runs through the Fairgrounds and a neighborhood.
