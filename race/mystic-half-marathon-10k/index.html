About

The half marathon and 10K start at Olde Mistick Village and wind through the historic seaport downtown of Mystic, Connecticut, tracing the coastline before finishing near the water. It's one of the Hartford Marathon Foundation's flagship shoreline races, drawing runners from across New England.
