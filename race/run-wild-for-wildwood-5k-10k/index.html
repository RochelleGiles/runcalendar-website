About

The 6th annual Run Wild for Wildwood 5K & 10K is a scenic run/walk event at Wildwood Park in Harrisburg, PA. The course follows the Wildwood Way Trail around the lake and the Towpath Trail along the Pennsylvania Canal, supporting the park and community.
