Course

Run on the wicked trails of Brown County, Indiana, over vicious 600-foot ridges. The organiser calls the 10K the world's most difficult, warning runners to expect double their road time and triple the effort. Distances run from 10K through marathon and 50K to 50 miles, plus a 100K team relay.

About

Dances with Dirt Gnaw Bone is a trail ultra series in Brown County, Indiana, near Nashville, held each spring across hilly, wooded terrain typical of the region's state parks, with distances from a 10K up to a 50-mile ultramarathon.
