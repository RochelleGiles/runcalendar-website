Course

Out-and-back courses on relatively flat paved park paths from Wilket Creek Park on Leslie Street, running through the Sunnybrook Park pathway system. Both distances share the start and finish; the 8K goes at 9:00am and the 5K at 9:15am, in waves two minutes apart. The paths are not closed to the public.

About

Part of the Toronto Women's Run Series, the PUMA Toronto Women's Fall 8K/5K is a women's road race held each autumn in Toronto. It offers both 8K and 5K distances, drawing a large field of runners of all paces to close out the series' racing season.
