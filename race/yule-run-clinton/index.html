Course

The 5K and 8K both wind through the streets of historic Olde Towne Clinton and along Clinton Boulevard, following the route of the town's Christmas parade. Sealed roads throughout, with a 7:00 a.m. start timed so runners finish before the parade begins.

About

The Yule Run Clinton, presented by Continental, is a holiday-themed race through the streets of historic Olde Towne Clinton, Mississippi, continuing into the town's annual Christmas parade route along Clinton Boulevard.
