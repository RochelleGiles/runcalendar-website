Course

A 6.79-mile loop in Ponderosa State Park beside Payette Lake near McCall, mostly flowy single-track and rocky rolling trail with some double-track, gravel and short pavement; roughly 640 ft of climb per loop. Offered as 20K, 30K and 50K.

About

The Sasquatch Shuffle is a trail ultramarathon near McCall, Idaho, hosted by Endless Trails. Runners can choose 20K, 30K, or 50K distances through the forested terrain of central Idaho each October.
