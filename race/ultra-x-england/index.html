Course

Trail ultra starting in Edale and running through Peak District ridgelines, moorland trails and limestone valleys. Distances of 110km (single or two stage), 60km, 50km, 25km and 12km, with the 110km and 60km starting Saturday and the shorter races Sunday. Checkpoints provide water, snacks and hot drinks, with medical support on course and at the campsite.

About

Ultra X England offers a variety of challenging ultramarathon and trail running experiences in the scenic Peak District National Park. Runners can choose from a 110km ultra, a 110km 2-day option, or shorter single-stage races ranging from 60km to 12km, providing a test for athletes of all levels. This event promises an unforgettable journey through the rugged natural beauty of one of the UK's most stunning national parks.
