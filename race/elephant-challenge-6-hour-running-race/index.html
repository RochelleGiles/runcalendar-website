Course

Six-hour lapped race on a 3.3 mile off-road loop in Salcey Forest, Northamptonshire. Runners complete as many laps as they choose within the time limit. The surface is crushed-stone path and well-trodden forest floor, with narrower tree-lined sections that turn muddy in wet weather. Snacks are available at the lap point on each circuit. Canicross entries are accepted.

About

Challenge yourself at the Elephant Challenge, a 6-hour running race through the scenic trails of Salcey Forest near Northampton, UK. Runners will tackle a challenging 3.5-mile off-road loop, testing their endurance and determination over the course of this unique event. Whether you're aiming for distance or personal records, the Elephant Challenge promises an unforgettable running adventure.
