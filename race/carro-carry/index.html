About

The Carro Carry event is a unique running experience that takes participants on a scenic course through the picturesque towns of Carro, la Couronne, and Carry. The course highlights the natural beauty of the region, offering runners a chance to immerse themselves in the local culture and landscape. With a welcoming atmosphere and a focus on community, this event promises an unforgettable running adventure.
