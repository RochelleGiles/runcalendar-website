About

Embrace the spooky season at the Exeter Halloween 5 & 10 mile, hosted by the Exeter City Community Trust. This thrilling event takes runners on a scenic tour through the historic streets of the United Kingdom, with the chance to dress up in costume and enjoy the festive atmosphere. Whether you're chasing a personal best or just looking for a fun challenge, this race is sure to delight.
