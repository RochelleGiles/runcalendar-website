About

The Himan Trail Run Race takes runners through the heart of Oman's stunning Hajar Mountains. With over 164km of built trails and a total elevation gain of 9,599m across the races, this event offers a challenging and breathtaking experience for trail runners of all levels. Participants will have the chance to explore historic sites, beautiful farms, and scenic mountain vistas as they push themselves to the limit.
