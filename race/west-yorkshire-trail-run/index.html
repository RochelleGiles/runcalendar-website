Course

Trail loops on the Harewood House estate near Leeds, starting and finishing at the house. Routes run out along Wall Side Plantation and climb to Sugar Hill, pass the Emmerdale set and Eccup Reservoir, then return through the forest trails of Carr Plantation and Piper Wood. On-course water and refreshments.

About

Explore the stunning grounds of Harewood House in West Yorkshire during the annual West Yorkshire Trail Run. This off-road event offers a challenging Half Marathon and a 13km distance, allowing runners of all levels to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the British countryside. With rolling hills, picturesque landscapes, and the grand stately home as a backdrop, this is a must-do event for trail running enthusiasts.
