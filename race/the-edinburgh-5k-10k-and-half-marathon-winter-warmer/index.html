Course

A hilly lapped road course around Holyrood Park, starting and finishing on the Parade Ground at Queens Drive opposite St Margaret's Well. The 5K is one lap, the 10K two and the half marathon four. The half starts at 09:00 and the 5K and 10K at 10:00. Buggies are permitted and the course is wheelchair accessible.

About

A chance to lace up those shoes, get motivated, focus on training during winter and run this awesome 5k, 10k and half marathon run with us. There will be loads of great entry benefits including full race chip timing, a super cool medal, snacks, drinks, some surprise goodies and a load of fun, smiles and support along the way.
