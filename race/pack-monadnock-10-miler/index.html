Course

A point-to-point mountain race starting on Highland Street near Wilton-Lyndeborough High School and finishing on the summit of Pack Monadnock. The route is mostly paved with gravel sections, climbing then rolling along Sand Hill and Davisville Roads to Burton Highway, with a water stop at mile 5.1. The last 1.3 miles switchback steeply up through Miller State Park.

About

The Pack Monadnock 10 Miler is an annual road race hosted by the Gate City Striders in Wilton, New Hampshire. The 10-mile course runs through the foothills of Pack Monadnock in the Wapack Range. Runners of all levels are welcome to take on this scenic slice of southern New Hampshire.
