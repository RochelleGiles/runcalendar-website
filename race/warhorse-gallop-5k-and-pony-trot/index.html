Course

A multi-terrain 5K run around the Charles D. Owen High School campus, 99 Lake Eden Road, Black Mountain, starting and finishing in the school stadium. The 1K Pony Trot for children aged 10 and under is run on the track. Aid stations and the surface split are not stated.

About

The Warhorse Gallop 5K and Pony Trot is a fundraiser event held at Charles D. Owen High School in Black Mountain, NC, supporting the sophomore class and Black Mountain Home for Children. It features a scenic 5K race for runners and a 1K kids fun run for ages 10 and under.
