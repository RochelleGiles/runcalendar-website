About

The Clun Valley Trail Half Marathon follows a route of familiar hills, stiles, forests and stream crossings, meandering through the scenic Clun Valley with some of the most spectacular views in the area. The course is clearly marked and staffed with marshals, and there is no cut-off time, letting everyone run at their own pace.
