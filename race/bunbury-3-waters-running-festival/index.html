Course

Distance-certified road courses around Bunbury's waterfronts, taking in the Leschenault Inlet, Koombana Bay and the Indian Ocean foreshore, with a stretch through the Big Swamp Nature Reserve. Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K all run over the weekend from the same festival hub.
