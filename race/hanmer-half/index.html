Course

The 21 km climbs Conical Hill for views over the basin before dropping into the running trails of Hanmer Heritage Forest. The 10.5 km Heritage Traverse is flat, starting and finishing at the Hanmer Springs Village Green and winding through century-old woodland. Both run on forest trail.

About

A run like no other—towering 100-year-old trees, crisp alpine air, and golden autumn colours set the scene for an unforgettable day on the trails. The Hanmer Half is fun, and seriously scenic, winding through the stunning Hanmer Heritage Forest with breathtaking mountain views.
