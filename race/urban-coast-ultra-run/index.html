Course

Run on the rolling coastal shared paths of Perth's sunset coast, starting and finishing at Mullaloo. The 64 km ultra covers the stretch between Mullaloo, City Beach and Mindarie, and can be entered solo or as a two-to-three person team. Shorter 10 km, 21 km and 27 km options are also offered.

About

The Urban Coast Ultra Run takes runners along the beautiful WA sunset coast. Participants can run the 64km Ultra solo or in a relay team, enjoying a course of rolling coastal paths with stunning views.
