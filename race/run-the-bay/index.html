About

Run The Bay in New Zealand's picturesque Bay of Islands offers runners a scenic and challenging course along the stunning coastline. Formerly known as the Bay of Islands Run/Walk Festival, this event is part of the popular Sport Northland Run/Walk Series, providing an opportunity to experience the natural beauty of this stunning region.
