Course

A hill-focused trail race in the Surrey Hills, starting and finishing at Stepping Stones car park below Box Hill. The 12.5-mile route links repeated climbs through woodland, heathland and open fields to gather roughly 1,000m of ascent, with some technical scrambling onto the summits.

About

Box Hill Vertical KM climbs this Surrey landmark, made famous as the punishing climb used in the London Olympics road cycling races, in the North Downs near Dorking.
