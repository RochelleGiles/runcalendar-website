Course

Out-and-back on the Harding Truck Trail, a dirt forest-service jeep road, starting and finishing at Tucker Wildlife Sanctuary in Modjeska Canyon. The 50K and 30K climb toward Santiago and Modjeska peaks, gaining 5,926 and 3,482 feet; the 15K is a shorter out-and-back with 2,088 feet of gain. Stocked aid stations are placed along the route.

About

A trail ultramarathon (15K, 30K and 50K) through Modjeska Canyon in Orange County's Santa Ana Mountains, climbing singletrack in Cleveland National Forest with canyon and ridgeline views. Organized by Aravaipa Running.
