About

The Urban Trail d'Orange is a running event held in the city of Orange, France. The event features three different trail races - the Trail d'Auguste (12km), the Trail de la Vierge (5.8km), and the Trail de Daudet (3.6km). Runners can also choose to participate in a 6km or 12km walking event. The event is known for its scenic urban course that showcases the historic landmarks and architecture of Orange.
