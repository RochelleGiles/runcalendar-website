About

The Almaty Half Marathon is a must-run event for any endurance enthusiast. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Tian Shan mountains, this race takes runners on a scenic tour through the vibrant city of Almaty, Kazakhstan. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply soaking in the incredible scenery, the Almaty Half Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
