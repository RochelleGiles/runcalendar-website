Course

Roughly 3.5 miles on the trail system at Landen/Deerfield Park, starting and finishing near the restrooms. Densely wooded, winding dirt and mud singletrack with flat sections alternating with hills and elevation changes of up to 50 feet, plus two calf-deep creek crossings.

About

Start out your St. Patrick's Day Weekend with a hardy workout. Course consists of approximately 3.5 miles of mostly densely wooded, winding dirt/mud trails within Landen/Deerfield Park. Elevation changes of up to 50 feet with two calf-deep creek crossings.
