Course

Lapped singletrack at Rocky Hill Ranch in Smithville, through pine forest and wildflower meadows with stream crossings and bridges. The hills are short and steep rather than long, giving a roller-coaster feel between faster open sections. The 10K and 25K are single loops, the 50K two 15.5-mile laps and the 50-miler three of 16.67 miles, with aid every 2.8 to 4.5 miles.

About

A lapped trail race at Rocky Hill Ranch near Smithville, Texas, offering distances from 10K up to 50 miles, plus ruck divisions and a youth mile. The singletrack rolls through pine forest and wildflower fields with short, steep climbs and frequent stream crossings rather than sustained ascents.
