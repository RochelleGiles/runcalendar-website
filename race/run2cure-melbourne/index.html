Course

Run at Princes Park in Carlton, on the park's running tracks among its ovals and lakes — a new venue for the event in 2026. Start and finish are at the park on Royal Parade, with the programme running from 6:30am. Distances span a 1km children's walk, a timed 5km and 5km walk, 10km and the half marathon.

About

Run2Cure Melbourne is a charity fun run raising funds for childhood cancer research through Neuroblastoma Australia, with distances from a 1km Little Heroes dash up to the half marathon.
