Course

Three routes in the Tararua Range north of Wellington. The 36 km Southern Crossing is point-to-point from Kaitoke in Upper Hutt over the tops to Ōtaki Forks; the 24 km Kime Climb and 13 km Field Dash are out-and-back from Ōtaki Forks. Terrain is technical mountain trail with roots and exposed tops, with LandSAR crews supporting the course.

About

Tackle the rugged terrain of the iconic Tararua Mountain Race (TMR) in New Zealand's North Island. This challenging event takes runners through the stunning Tararua ranges, just north of Wellington, offering breathtaking views and an unforgettable alpine adventure.
