Course

Starts and finishes on the Salem Lake trail beside the playground in Winston-Salem. The 7 Miler runs the full loop around Salem Lake on the greenway trail; the 5K is an out-and-back on the same trail. A free one-mile walk is also offered.

About

The Feet for the Street 5K & 7 Miler is an annual running event created to raise awareness and financial support for the Winston-Salem Street School, a private alternative high school serving at-risk youth. The 8th annual event takes place at Salem Lake in Winston-Salem, NC.
