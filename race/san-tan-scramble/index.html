Course

Looped trail courses in San Tan Mountain Regional Park, Queen Creek, all starting and finishing at the park. Rolling single track threads through saguaro-studded mountains and valleys, with pitches of steep rocky trail and stretches of sandy wash. The 50K runs three laps of the 17K loop, alternating direction, for 3,876ft of climb. Aid every 2.2 to 5.1 miles; the race is cupless.

About

The San Tan Scramble is a trail race at San Tan Mountain Regional Park near Queen Creek, Arizona, organized by Aravaipa Running. Distances range from 5K to 50K across the park's desert trails and foothills.
