Course

Courses from The Adobe Resort in Yachats over Cape Perpetua's coastal trails. The 50K covers 31.1 miles with 5,700 ft of climbing, opening with a 2.7-mile beach section and the Oregon Coast Trail before dirt singletrack through old growth; hard cutoffs at Cummins (12:45 pm) and Perpetua Campground (3:00 pm) with a 5:00 pm finish. 50K/20M Saturday, half/10K Sunday.

About

Oregon's crown jewel coastal trails! The event features incredibly scenic 50K and 20 miler races on Saturday, along with half marathon and 10K races on Sunday. Each course includes practically everything the Oregon Coast has to offer!
