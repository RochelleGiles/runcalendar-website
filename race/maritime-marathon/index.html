Course

The marathon and half marathon share a course along the Lake Michigan shore, starting and finishing at the UW Green Bay-Manitowoc campus. The marathon runs out to Two Rivers and turns at Port Sandy Bay before returning the same way; the 5.75-mile Nautical Run turns near the Maritime Museum. A four-person relay uses the marathon route with exchanges at Two Rivers and Port Sandy Bay.

About

This out-and-back course features amazing views of Lake Michigan's shoreline as you run from Manitowoc to Two Rivers. Come experience the Maritime Capital of Wisconsin! The Marathon is a race for runners by runners. The course is flat, fast and a Boston Qualifier.
