Course

A fast, flat 5K starting and finishing on Methodist Street in Niantic, running past the off-season cottages of the Pine Grove community. HMF FitKids runs of a quarter, half and one mile are held alongside, and a virtual 5K option is offered.

About

A 5K road race through the shoreline village of Niantic in East Lyme, Connecticut, part of the Hartford Marathon Foundation's St. Patrick's Day race series. The March event draws more than a thousand runners each year.
