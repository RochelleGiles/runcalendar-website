Course

A closed-road race that starts and finishes on the historic Silver Jubilee Bridge, crossing the River Mersey and the Manchester Ship Canal between Widnes and Runcorn. Every kilometre is marked with distance flags. Both the 10K and the half marathon set off at 09:00.

About

Explore the picturesque Jubilee Bridge in Runcorn, Cheshire, as you challenge yourself at the Jubilee Bridge 10K & Half Marathon. This closed-road event offers runners of all levels the chance to conquer a scenic 10km or 21.1km course, showcasing the stunning architecture and landscapes of this historic region.
