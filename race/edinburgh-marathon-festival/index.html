Course

A point-to-point road marathon starting on Potterrow beside the University of Edinburgh's McEwan Hall and running east to a coastal finish. The route passes Greyfriars Kirkyard, the Royal Mile, St Giles Cathedral, Edinburgh Castle, Princes Street Gardens, the Scottish Parliament, Arthur's Seat and Holyrood before reaching Musselburgh's historic golf course and Gosford House. It drops around 90 metres to sea level and is otherwise very flat. Half marathon, 10K, 5K and junior races run across the same festival weekend.

About

The Edinburgh Marathon Festival returns to the historic streets of Scotland's capital, offering a range of distances for runners of all abilities. Featuring stunning city views and a vibrant atmosphere, this premier running event is a must for those seeking an unforgettable challenge in a picturesque setting. Runners can choose from a variety of race options to suit their fitness level and goals.
