Course

A point-to-point 42-mile ultra along the St Peters Way, starting from The Pleasance car park in Chipping Ongar at 8:30am and finishing at the Chapel of St Peter-on-the-Wall, Bradwell on Sea. The route mixes muddy trails, fields and rural tracks with short road sections through villages, ending along a raised sea wall. Four staffed checkpoints; basic navigation required.

About

The St Peters Way Ultra Marathon is a challenging endurance event that takes runners through the picturesque countryside of the United Kingdom. The course highlights the stunning natural landscapes and offers a unique opportunity for runners to push their limits in a scenic and supportive environment.
