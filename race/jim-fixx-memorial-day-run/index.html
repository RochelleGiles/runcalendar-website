Course

The 5K starts on Greenwich Avenue at East Elm Street and runs through central Greenwich, taking in Bruce Park before finishing on the track by Havemeyer Field behind Greenwich Town Hall. A separate kids race is held alongside it. Entry is online only.

About

Held in Greenwich since the late 1960s and renamed after author Jim Fixx, whose bestselling books helped launch the 1970s running boom, this Memorial Day road race through the town draws around 200 runners each year.
