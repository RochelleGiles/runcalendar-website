About

The Yellowstone Independence Day 5K Run/Walk is a fundraiser organized by the Yellowstone Running Club, held annually on July 4th at Sacajawea Park in Livingston, Montana. It features a scenic route along the Yellowstone River, chip timing, t-shirts for early registrants, and supports the club's mission and local youth cross country team.
