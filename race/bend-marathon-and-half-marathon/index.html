Course

Scenic courses through West Bend, Oregon, taking in neighbourhood parks, ponderosa pine forest and the banks of the Deschutes River before finishing in central Bend. About

Come run the Bend Marathon, Half Marathon, 10k and 5k. These are spectacularly scenic courses through West Bend emphasizing everything that makes Bend, Oregon great. You'll cruise around neighborhood parks, through ponderosa pine forests, along the mighty Deschutes River, and finish in a beautiful iconic Bend location.
