Course

About

Escape the crowds and tackle the rugged beauty of the Esk Valley in Cumbria during the Esk Valley Epic Trail Ultra. This 36-mile off-road challenge takes runners through stunning landscapes, from the tranquil River Esk to the dramatic fells of the Lake District. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or looking to push your limits, this event offers a true adventure in the heart of the British countryside.
