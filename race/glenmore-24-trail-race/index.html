Course

A 4-mile loop in Glenmore Forest near Aviemore, run repeatedly from noon Saturday to noon Sunday. Laps start at The Hayfield and head out on forest trails past Loch Morlich, with a water stop at 2 miles and a short hill between miles 2 and 3; roughly 400ft of climb per lap. A 400m loop is available for the final hour.

About

Tackle the rugged trails of the Glenmore Forest in the heart of the Scottish Highlands at the Glenmore 24 Trail Race. Runners can take on this challenging 12- or 24-hour ultra event, surrounded by the stunning natural beauty of the region near the adventure hub of Aviemore. With a mix of technical singletrack and forest roads, this race offers an unforgettable endurance challenge for trail enthusiasts.
