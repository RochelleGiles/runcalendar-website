Course

One round of the Run The Vines series, run on farm tracks and vineyard roads on a Western Cape wine estate near Paarl. Three routes are offered — 5km, 10km and 17km. The 5km and 10km may be walked; the 17km is run-only and carries cut-off times.

About

This stop on the Run The Vines series takes place at Fairview wine and cheese estate near Paarl, home to the farm's landmark goat tower. Runners can choose from 3km, 5km, 10km and 17km trail distances through the vineyards.
