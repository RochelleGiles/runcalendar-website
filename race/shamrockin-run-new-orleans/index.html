Course

Start and finish at the Blue Crab Restaurant and Oyster Bar on Lakeshore Drive, on the New Orleans lakefront about 6 miles from downtown. The half marathon is flat, with roughly 200 ft of cumulative gain, and crosses one bridge twice. Half marathon aid stations sit about every 2 miles, with gel at miles 6 and 8.

About

The Shamrockin Run New Orleans is a fun and festive running event that celebrates St. Patrick's Day in the heart of the Crescent City. With a variety of race distances, including a half marathon, 10K, 5K, and Leprechaun Fun Run, there's something for runners of all levels.
