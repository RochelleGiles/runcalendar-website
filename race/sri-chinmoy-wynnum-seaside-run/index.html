Course

Starts at Bayside Park, Manly, at the corner of the Esplanade and Fairlead Crescent, and runs out and back along the traffic-free bike paths of the Wynnum foreshore before climbing into Elanora Park. Fast and flat, with markers every kilometre. The 2.5km turns at 1.25km, the 5km at 2.5km and the 10km at 5km; the half marathon runs the 10km route twice.

About

Sri Chinmoy Wynnum Seaside Run takes place along the Moreton Bay esplanade in Wynnum, Brisbane's bayside. Organised by the Sri Chinmoy Marathon Team, it offers 2.5km, 5km, 10km and half marathon distances for all levels.
