Course

A point-to-point 50K on wilderness singletrack. Runners meet at the finish area and are bussed to the start at 7:30am for an 8:00am start. The route climbs and descends into Soddy Creek, Possum Creek and Rock Creek. Aid provided on course is minimal.

About

The Upchuck 50K is a rugged trail ultramarathon in Soddy-Daisy, Tennessee, organized by Wild Trails. The technical singletrack course is known among Chattanooga-area trail runners as one of the toughest 50Ks in the region.
