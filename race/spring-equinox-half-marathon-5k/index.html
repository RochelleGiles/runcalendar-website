About

The Spring Equinox Half Marathon & 5K features an out-and-back course starting and finishing at Deppen Brewing in Wellington, CO, with a mix of half dirt and half asphalt roads for the half marathon and a flat asphalt 5K around neighborhoods. The event offers chip timing, multiple aid stations, finisher medals for the half, and supports local schools and sports teams.
