About

The Milton Keynes Festival of Running is a premier running event offering multiple race distances through the city's scenic routes. Featuring flat, fast courses and experienced pacers, the event attracts runners of all levels from beginners to seasoned marathoners. Participants can choose from 5K, 10K, Half Marathon, and 20 Mile distances, all starting at the iconic Xscape venue.
