Course

A ten-leg team relay along the Ridgeway National Trail, starting at Ivinghoe Beacon in Buckinghamshire and finishing in Marlborough, leaving the Ridgeway about two miles before Overton Hill to cross Fyfield Down. Legs range from 5.4 to 11 miles and the route is scenic, hilly and off-road throughout. At least two legs must be run by women, and teams arrange their own route planning and support.

About

The Ridgeway Relay is a team relay organised by Marlborough Running Club. It is run over ten legs, the legs varying in length from 5.4 miles to 11.0 miles. The route leaves The Ridgeway itself some 2 miles before Overton Hill, and continues across Fyfield Down to the finish in Marlborough. The route is scenic, hilly and off-road.
