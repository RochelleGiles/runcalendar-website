About

Since its inception, the Kansas Half Marathon has helped offset costs and increase access to affordable health care services for those that are uninsured/underserved in Douglas County. We kindly invite you to partner with us for this annual signature event. Together, we can engage all people, regardless of fitness level, to run with a purpose; supporting health care for all.
