Course

Courses run inside Cougar Mountain Bike Park, starting on the lower field of the sports grounds. The 7km short course is 40% pine and native bush single trail, 40% harvested forest single trail and 20% gravel forest road, with one drink station at 4.1km. The first climb, Humpty Dumpty, comes after about 1km of wide flat trail.

About

The Cougar Trail Run is held at Cougar Mountain Bike Park in Tokoroa, South Waikato, on forest trails. Runners and walkers choose a 21.1km long course, 14.5km mid course or 7km short course, plus a Trail Tigers Kids Dash. Drink stations are on course and participants carry their own cup or hydration pack.
