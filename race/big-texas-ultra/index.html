Course

Dirty, rocky single and wide track at Flat Creek Crossing Ranch beside Pedernales Falls State Park near Johnson City, where every distance starts and finishes. The 50K loop carries about 2,200 ft of climbing and the 15K out-and-back about 850 ft. A main aid station sits at the start/finish with three more on course, all serving hot and cold food, snacks, water, electrolytes, pickle juice and ice.
