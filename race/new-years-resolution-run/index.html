Course

Both distances start at Pack Square in downtown Asheville. The 5K runs north into Montford; the 10K continues into the Grove Park area. Each finishes by descending historic Spruce Street back to Pack Square. City streets, mostly open to traffic, with water and snacks on course.

About

The New Year's Resolution Run is a 10K and 5K held on January 1st in Asheville, North Carolina, nestled in the Blue Ridge Mountains, organized by iDaph Events to kick off the new year of running.
