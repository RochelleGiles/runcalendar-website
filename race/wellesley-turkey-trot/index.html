Course

USATF-certified 5K loop starting and finishing at Linden Square on Linden Street in Wellesley, Massachusetts, on Thanksgiving morning. The route runs the residential streets of Crest Road, Central Street, Weston Road and Washington Street, climbs College Road for about three-quarters of a mile through the Wellesley College campus, and passes the Boston Marathon Scream Tunnel. A kids' fun run of up to a mile is held alongside it.
