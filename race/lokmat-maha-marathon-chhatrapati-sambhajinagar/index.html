About

Lokmat Maha Marathon Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar is the Sambhajinagar leg of the multi-city Maha Marathon series run by the Lokmat media group across Maharashtra, alongside sister editions in Kolhapur, Thane and Nashik. It offers 21K, 10K, 5K and 3K distances.
