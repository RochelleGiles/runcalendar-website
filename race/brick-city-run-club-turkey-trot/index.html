About

A free Thanksgiving morning run and walk from Military Park, hosted by the Brick City Run Club with the City of Newark and the Newark City Parks Foundation. Every event is one mile: the Newark Community Mile, the faster Turbo Turkey Mile and a kids and family run, and you are welcome to do more than one. It is a community celebration rather than a race. Entry is free but you must register; bring non-perishable food or toiletries for the collection. Check-in opens at 8:00 for a 9:00 start, and parking is limited, so walk, share a car or take public transport.
