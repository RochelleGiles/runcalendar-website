Course

An out-and-back road course leaving from Walker Place Park in Bossier City, shared by the 5K, 10K and half marathon. The organisers describe it as not completely flat but nowhere near hilly. Water stations sit roughly every 1.5 miles, and runners need to hold at least a 16-minute mile to finish.

About

The Salute to Veterans 5K, 10K and Half Marathon in Bossier City honors military veterans with a fall race weekend in northwest Louisiana. Runners choose from three distances on courses through the Shreveport-Bossier City area.
