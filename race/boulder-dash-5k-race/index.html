Course

The course runs through an operational hard rock quarry in Forsyth County, the only day of the year the public is let in, passing the working quarry face, rock deposits and processing plant. The route is re-drawn each year and includes loose gravel underfoot. Finishers come back to the Martin Marietta Spring Fling with music and food.

About

The Boulder Dash 5K offers runners a rare look inside an operational hard rock quarry in Forsyth County, Georgia — the only day of the year the public is let in. The route passes the working quarry face, ancient rock deposits and the massive equipment used to process the rock, with loose gravel underfoot throughout.
