Course

A six-hour timed event run on a 3.3-mile lap of Coombe Abbey Park, Coventry, where entrants complete as many laps as they choose. Four laps make a half marathon and 8.5 a marathon. The relatively flat loop is mostly stone pathway through mature woodland and past waterways, with an open grassland meadow section that can be muddy in wet weather.

About

The Kingfisher Challenge invites runners to test their endurance on a picturesque 3.3-mile off-road loop at Coombe Abbey Park in Coventry. This 6-hour race offers a unique opportunity to push your limits in a stunning natural setting, with the chance to soak up the tranquil beauty of the park's surroundings as you strive to rack up the miles.
