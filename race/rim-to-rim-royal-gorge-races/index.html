Course

All races start from Parking Lot B at Royal Gorge Bridge and Park near Cañon City and cross America's highest suspension bridge. The trail half marathon starts 7:30 am and the 8-mile trail run 8:00 am on trails; the stroller-friendly 5K road run starts 8:30 am.

About

The Rim to Rim Royal Gorge Races feature a 5K road run, 8-mile trail run, and half marathon trail run through scenic foothills near the Royal Gorge Bridge, culminating with a crossing of America's highest suspension bridge. All races start from Parking Lot B at the Royal Gorge Bridge & Park and offer stunning views of Colorado's landscapes.
