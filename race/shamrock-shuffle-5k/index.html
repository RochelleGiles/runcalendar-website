About

The Walters State Shamrock Shuffle is a 5K run and walk on the college's Morristown campus in East Tennessee. The course circles the campus and College Square Mall, mixing flat stretches with moderate inclines, and starts in front of the College Center Building. Medals go to the top finishers in each age group.
