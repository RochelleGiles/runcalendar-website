About

Explore the stunning Jura Mountains at the Trail De Lours, a challenging and scenic trail running event. Winding through pristine forests and over rolling hills, this race offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or a newcomer to the sport, the Trail De Lours promises an unforgettable adventure.
