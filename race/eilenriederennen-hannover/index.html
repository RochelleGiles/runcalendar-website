About

The Eilenriederennen in Hannover is a must-attend event for runners seeking a unique and historic race experience. Winding through the scenic Eilenriede forest, this event offers a challenging yet picturesque course that showcases the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a recreational runner, the Eilenriederennen promises an unforgettable running adventure.
