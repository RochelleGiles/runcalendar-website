Course

An 8K trail run at Lakeside Park in Piedmont, starting and finishing near the baseball diamonds. The course links the Every Minute and All Day, Baby trails, beginner-friendly singletrack through the woods with minimal roots, a few gradual climbs, switchbacks and steady undulation. A central aid station is passed twice, and a 90-minute limit applies.

About

The Ice Breaker 8K is back and Square One Events will be hosting it for the 5th time at Lakeside Park in Piedmont, SC. The trail system at Lakeside Park is ideal for beginners looking to test out their trail shoes or even advanced runners looking to find some speed around the various switchbacks and turns!
