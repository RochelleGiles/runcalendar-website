About

The Life Style Sports Fastlane Summer Edition in Clondalkin, Ireland offers runners an exciting challenge with its 3/4 Marathon, Half Marathon, and 10K distance events. Set against the backdrop of the Emerald Isle, this popular race series promises an unforgettable experience for athletes of all levels seeking to test their limits on the open road.
