Course

A running tour of Tulsa on a 25-mile city loop, with a 1.2-mile out-and-back added for the marathon; runners cover one lap for the marathon and up to four for the 100 miles. The terrain is generally flat to gently rolling with sidewalk for most of the route. The course is not marked, so runners navigate from turn-by-turn directions. Start and finish at RunnersWorld Tulsa.

About

The Tulsa Urban Adventure offers marathon, 50-mile and 100-mile distances entirely on the streets and trails of Tulsa, Oklahoma. It's one of a small number of ultra events run through an American city rather than in the backcountry.
