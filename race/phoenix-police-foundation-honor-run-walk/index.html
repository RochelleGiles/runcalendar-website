About

The Phoenix Police Foundation's Honor Run/Walk takes participants through downtown Phoenix, passing the Memorial Wall at Phoenix Police Headquarters that honors every Phoenix officer who made the ultimate sacrifice. The event offers a 5K run and a 1-mile Honor Walk in tribute to fallen officers.
