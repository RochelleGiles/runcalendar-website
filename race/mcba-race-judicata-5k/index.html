Course

Two separate routes at Chaparral Park in Scottsdale, Arizona: a 5K run/walk and a 1 mile fun run, both mapped on Strava. Wheelchairs, strollers and leashed dogs are welcome; skates, skateboards and motorised devices are not. The course may change if the race director judges it unsafe.

About

The MCBA Race Judicata 5K is a fundraising event organized by the Maricopa County Bar Association to support domestic violence shelters. It features a 5K run/walk and a 1 Mile fun run/walk held at Chaparral Park in Scottsdale.
