About

Run Doral is a Half Marathon, 10K, and 5K running festival brought to you by Leon Sport and RaceTime. This event is the only half marathon in Doral! The event will include a timed race, age group awards, a great spectator section, live music, starting and finishing at Doral Meadow Park.
