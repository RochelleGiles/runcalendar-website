Course

The 10K is run on Memorial Day morning in Boulder and finishes inside Folsom Field at the University of Colorado, where finishers are cheered by a stadium crowd. Runners are set off in waves seeded by predicted finish time.

About

The BOLDERBoulder 10K is Boulder, Colorado's long-running Memorial Day tradition, with wave starts designed for walkers, joggers and racers of every pace.
