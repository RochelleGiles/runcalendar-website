About

The American Legion Post 14 Birthday Bash 5K is run along Cross Lake on South Lakeshore Drive in Shreveport, Louisiana, starting and finishing in the Post 14 car park. A 5K, a one-mile walk or fun run and a half-mile children's run are offered, followed by a birthday celebration with food and sponsor stalls. Funds raised go to veterans, service members and their families.
