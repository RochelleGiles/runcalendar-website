Course

Flat out-and-back on the Wellington waterfront, starting and finishing at the Freyberg Pool car park. The 10 km heads south to the wind needle and turns; the 5 km turns earlier. Start 8:30am, with an optional 8:15am start for those expecting over 65 minutes. Entry is paid in cash on the day or by prior bank transfer. No aid stations described.

About

The Honest 10 is an accurately measured 5km and 10km fun run held once a month around the Wellington waterfront. Both distances follow a flat out-and-back course starting from Freyberg Pool in Oriental Bay.
