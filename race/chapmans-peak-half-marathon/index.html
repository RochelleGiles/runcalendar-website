About

Hout Bay Harriers' road half along Chapman's Peak Drive, closed to cars for the morning: out of Hout Bay towards Noordhoek and back, about 350 m of climb with the high point above the sea at 163 m. The 12 km was dropped in 2026 and the half alone remains; the 2026 edition sold out.
