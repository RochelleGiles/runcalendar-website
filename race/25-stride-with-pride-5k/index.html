About

The Stride With Pride 5K Run/Walk is held at Belmont Lake State Park in West Babylon, New York. Runners and walkers cover a single 5K route inside the park with a 9am start. Proceeds go to the North Babylon school district LGBTQ clubs at middle and high school level.
