Course

A wooded trail course in the Bonnet Carre Spillway, starting from the Boat Launch Pavilion. The surface is unpaved and uneven with quick turns, short 4 to 6 foot rises and drops and several wooden bridge crossings, maintained by local mountain bike volunteers. The 5K is one full loop and the half marathon repeats it; water stops sit around the loop and the route is flagged and signed throughout.

About

The Armadillo Dash 5K and Half Marathon Trail Run take place in Norco, Louisiana, in the River Parishes region along the Mississippi. Runners choose between the two distances on trail courses organized by Lazy Hound Racing.
