Course

A primitive winter trail run from the Scuppernong Trailhead in the south unit of Kettle Moraine State Forest, usually following area snowmobile trails. The route is set as loops or out-and-back segments and changes each year with trail and weather conditions. One aid station at the start/finish shelter carries food and fluids; runners must bring their own containers for refills.

About

The John Dick Memorial 50K is in remembrance of a former club member who was an all-around athlete, and enjoyed the trails in the Southern Kettle Moraine.
