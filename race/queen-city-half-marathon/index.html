Course

A half marathon starting and finishing in downtown Marquette, with seven aid stations at miles 2, 3.1, 5, 7.75, 9, 10.25 and 12, and GU Roctane gel at the 7.75-mile station. The companion Breakwater 5K is run as a fast out-and-back.

About

The Queen City Half Marathon, presented by Embers Credit Union, is a fast, chip-timed race through downtown Marquette neighborhoods, with finisher shirts and medals for all participants.
