About

The Marathon du Nouvel An Pilsen is a must-run event for any endurance enthusiast. Set against the stunning backdrop of Pilsen, this historic race invites runners to ring in the new year with an unforgettable journey through the city's picturesque streets and landmarks. Whether you're tackling the full marathon, half-marathon, or 10K, this event promises an exhilarating challenge and an unparalleled celebration of the running community.
