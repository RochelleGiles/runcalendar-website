Course

The half marathon, 10K and 5K run out from Simsbury along the Farmington River and through surrounding farmland, with the half marathon covering two loops and passing through the town centre. Roads are closed for the races.

About

Organised by the Hartford Marathon Foundation, this race offers a half marathon, 10K and 5K along paved rail-trail through the Farmington Valley in Simsbury, just north of Hartford, on a course that stays largely flat and traffic-free.
