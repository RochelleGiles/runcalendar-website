About

The FLATS Trail Half Marathon is a 13.1-mile trail race along the shores of beautiful Forest Lake in Kirksville, Missouri, featuring hills, rocks, roots, fast singletrack, and water crossings. All proceeds support the Forest Lake Area Trail System for trail building and maintenance in Adair County.
