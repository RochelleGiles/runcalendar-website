About

The Norwegian Foot March & Trail Run, sponsored by the Vessey AUSA Chapter of Minnesota, is a signature military running and fitness event. It pairs a 30K foot march with 30K and 5K trail run options, open to the public, military families, and Junior and Senior ROTC programs, alongside a business expo and live music.
