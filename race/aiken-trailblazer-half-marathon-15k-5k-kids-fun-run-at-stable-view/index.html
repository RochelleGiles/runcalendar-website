Course

Trail races over the thousand-acre Stable View equestrian property near Aiken, through longleaf pine forest and rolling hills. Half marathon and 15K start at 8:00 am, the 5K at 9:00 am. Half and 15K water stops sit near miles 2, 5.5, 7.5 and 9.75; the 5K has water at mile 1.5. Held rain or shine.

About

Celebrate Spring with a Half Marathon, 15K, 5K, and Kids Fun Run on the trails at Stable View! Lace-up for a thrilling adventure - run or walk - at scenic Stable View equestrian facility, surrounded by a thousand acres of enchanting forests and rolling hills. Earn more than just your finisher's medal here; celebrate your triumph with a stylish t-shirt, indulge in top-notch post-race grub from The Bistro @ Stable View, and enjoy the luxury of a hot shower post-run.
