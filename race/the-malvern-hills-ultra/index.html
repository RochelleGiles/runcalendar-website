Course

An out-and-back along the Worcestershire Way, with every distance finishing at Abberley Village Hall and the shorter races starting from intermediate checkpoints; the 47-mile route turns at British Camp. Mixed, well-signposted trail through the Malvern Hills. Five checkpoints on the 47-miler supply water, food and facilities, with fewer on the shorter distances.

About

The Malvern Hills Ultra is a challenging ultra-running event that follows the Worcestershire Way, offering multiple distance options through scenic landscapes. Runners will experience significant elevation and self-navigation across varied terrain.
