Course

Run from Churchill Park on the Rockingham Foreshore in Western Australia, along what the club calls one of WA's most scenic coastal courses. Both the 10km and the 5km offer an iconic beach leg, so the route mixes paved path with sand. A 0.3km children's race runs alongside.

About

The Rockingham Coastal Classic is a road race along the coast at Rockingham, south of Perth, hosted by the West Australian Marathon Club, with 5km and 10km distances.
