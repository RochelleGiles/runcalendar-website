About

The Cadillac Rotary Club will host its 27th Annual STRIDE FOR S.T.R.I.V.E. 5k Event on Memorial Day, hosting hundreds of athletes. This event is successfully run each year through collaborative efforts by Cadillac Rotarians and other key community members.
