About

The RISE Run brings the San Angelo community together for a day of fellowship, movement, and gratitude as we use our God-given bodies to do fun, hard things. Whether you choose the 1-mile walk/jog, 5K, or 10K, each course offers a relaxed, inviting atmosphere that encourages both enjoyment and personal challenge. Runners and walkers of all levels are welcomed to push a little further than they normally would—surrounded by neighbors, friends, and uplifting energy. After crossing the finish line, participants can enjoy a festival-style celebration with food trucks, games, drinks, live music, and more.
