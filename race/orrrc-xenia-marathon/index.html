Course

Marathon, half marathon and 10K all starting and finishing at the Xenia YMCA. The marathon carries 11 water and sports-drink aid stations spaced roughly every two miles, plus five gel stations. Shawnee Park and Xenia Station are named as the main spectator points on course.

About

One of Ohio's longest-running marathons, staged on the flat, paved Little Miami Scenic Trail from Xenia. The tree-lined rail-trail carries the marathon, half marathon and 10K, making it a favourite for a fast spring time.
