About

The annual Galway Women's Mini Marathon is a celebration of Irish heritage and female empowerment. Held in the vibrant city of Galway, Ireland, this event invites women of all ages and abilities to come together and conquer a 10K course through the charming streets and scenic countryside. Whether you're an experienced runner or just starting your fitness journey, the Galway Women's Mini Marathon provides a supportive and inclusive atmosphere to challenge yourself and make lifelong memories.
