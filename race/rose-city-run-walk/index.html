About

The Rose City Run & Walk features a 10K Run, 1 Mile Fun Run, and 5K Walk held annually in Thomasville, Georgia, associated with the Rose Festival. The 10K and 1 Mile events start on North Broad Street near the U.S. Post Office, while the 5K Walk begins at the Thomasville Center for the Arts.
