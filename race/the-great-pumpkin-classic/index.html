Course

Starts and finishes on the driveway beside Trumbull High School. The rolling 5K climbs through the first mile, drops sharply to 1.3, then faces the Laurie Road hill before a downhill final stretch; over 75% follows the original 1991 route. A flat 1.7-mile health walk and kids' runs on the track are also held.
