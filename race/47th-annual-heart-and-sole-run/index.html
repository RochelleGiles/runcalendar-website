Course

The 10K starts at St. Francis Catholic School, 2202 Colton Blvd, Billings. The 5K and 2-mile health walk start near St. Vincent Healthcare at Grandview Park. All three courses finish at Dehler Park. The organiser publishes no surface or aid station detail.

About

People of all ages and abilities are encouraged to take part! The proceeds go towards the YMCA Annual Campaign and improving the Billings trail system through Billings TrailNet.
