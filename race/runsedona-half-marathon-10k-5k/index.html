About

One of the most beautiful races in one of the most beautiful places. The courses take you on a tour of the breathtaking Red Rocks, famous for their enchanting iron-clad formations. RunSedona Half Marathon, 10K, and 5K is consistently ranked as one of the best events in the USA.
