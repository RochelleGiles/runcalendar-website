Course

A single loop of the South Downs National Park starting and finishing at Plumpton Racecourse in East Sussex, setting off at 6am Saturday with a 34-hour limit. The route is 95% trail and 5% road over rolling hills, with around 12,000ft of climbing and 12 aid stations on a marked course.

About

The South Downs 100 miles is a single loop trail race through the beautiful South Downs National Park. Runners will begin at the fantastic venue of Plumpton Racecourse before encountering an iconic and beautiful but challenging route. There is over 12,000 ft of elevation, with rolling hills, stunning views, 12 aid stations and a 34 hour cutoff time.
