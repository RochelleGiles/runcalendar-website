Course

A 42-mile point-to-point ultramarathon along the West Highland Way from Tyndrum to Fort William. The organisers give the surface as 95% trail and 5% road. The route is well marked and needs no navigation, with checkpoints at 7, 17, 27 and 35 miles.

About

An ultramarathon along the West Highland Way from Tyndrum to Fort William in the Scottish Highlands, covering 42 miles of mountain and glen scenery including Rannoch Moor and Glencoe. It forms the second half of the full West Highland Way route.
