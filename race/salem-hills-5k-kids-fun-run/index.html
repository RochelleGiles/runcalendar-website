Course

Loop course through the Willamette Valley Vineyards estate at Turner, on a mix of paved lanes, gravel roads and dirt tracks. The route runs past the Into the Woods campground and takes in several steep ascents and descents before a final climb up the vineyard's main drive. Water stations are placed along the way.

About

The tentative 5k course will take runners and walkers through the Estate property, including the lower Bernau Block - Willamette Valley Vineyards' original planting. You'll pass through "Into the Woods," Willamette's camping, RV, and outdoor facility, and finish with a challenging and rewarding climb up the vineyard's iconic drive.
