About

The Parma Marathon is a premier running event in the heart of the Food Valley region of Italy. Runners will experience a scenic course that showcases the historic city of Parma, known for its rich culinary heritage and stunning architecture. The event offers a vibrant atmosphere and the opportunity to challenge oneself while enjoying the beautiful surroundings.
