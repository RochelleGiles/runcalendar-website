About

Join us at Etowah River Park as we lace up and hit the pavement to honor the bright, beautiful life of Breanna "Bree" Chadwick. This special day is more than a race—it's a celebration of Bree's smile, spirit, and the legacy she left behind. Every step we take helps raise awareness for organ donation and supports kids in our community.
