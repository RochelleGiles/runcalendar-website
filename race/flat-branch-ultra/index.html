Course

Flat, fast out-and-back on the MKT and Katy rail trails from Martin Luther King Jr Memorial Park on West Stadium Boulevard in Columbia. The 50K runs about 7.5 miles down the MKT before joining the Katy Trail towards Rocheport beneath the Missouri River bluffs, passing the Burr Oak at mile 8.5; the 25K turns back before it. Field capped at 125.
