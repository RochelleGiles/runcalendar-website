Course

Start and finish at the large pavilion in Dow Park, Deer Park. The course is an out-and-back repeated by lap: twice for the 5K, four times for the 10K and six times for the half marathon. Overall time limit is 3 hours 30 minutes. Course maps are provided as MapMyRun links. Aid station and surface detail are not published.
