About

The Marlow Half Marathon and Frankenstein & Marlow 7 are popular annual charity runs organized by the Marlow Striders in the picturesque town of Marlow, UK. Runners can choose between a challenging half marathon or a 7-mile route, both offering stunning views of the River Thames and surrounding countryside. This spooky-themed Halloween event is a must for runners seeking a unique and memorable running experience.
