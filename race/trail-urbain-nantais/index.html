About

The Trail Urbain Nantais is a unique running event that takes participants through the heart of Nantes, showcasing the city's vibrant urban landscape. The course highlights the city's historic landmarks, parks, and waterfront, offering runners a one-of-a-kind experience to explore Nantes on foot.
