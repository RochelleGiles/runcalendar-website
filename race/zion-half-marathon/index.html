Course

A point-to-point half marathon from Virgin to Springdale, finishing in front of Cable Mountain Lodge at the south gateway to Zion National Park. Runners start on a partially closed road, then move onto a paved path running parallel to it through Grafton and Rockville. Six cupless aid stations with water, electrolyte and gels.

About

The Zion Half Marathon, from Vacation Races, runs a net-downhill course along State Route 9 toward Springdale, Utah, finishing near the entrance to Zion National Park. The point-to-point route passes red-rock canyon scenery along the way.
