About

Explore the picturesque boulevards of Colomiers at the annual Les Boulevards De Colomiers running event. This scenic race takes participants on a journey through the charming streets and parks of this vibrant French town, offering a unique and memorable running experience. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, this event promises an enjoyable challenge amidst the beautiful surroundings.
