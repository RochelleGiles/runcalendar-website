Course

Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K all start and finish at New Park Showground in Brockenhurst, in the heart of the New Forest. Junior races and a woodland walk run alongside them. Tracks are narrow in places and the courses are not suitable for wheelchair access.

About

The New Forest Marathon is a unique running event set in the stunning New Forest National Park. The course takes runners through picturesque villages, ancient woodlands, and open heathland, offering a truly memorable experience. With a passion for delivering an exceptional atmosphere, this event caters to runners of all ages and abilities.
