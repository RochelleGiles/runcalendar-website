Course

Single-track racing on the Uwharrie Trail: the 8-mile runs point-to-point from Jumping Off Rock to the Highway 109 trailhead, the 20-mile continues to Wood Run trailhead, and the 40-mile runs out-and-back with a turnaround at Wood Run. Time limits are 3, 6 and 12.5 hours respectively.
