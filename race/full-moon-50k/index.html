Course

Starts at historic Camp Ouachita in Perryville and runs at night on rugged forest roads through the Ouachita National Forest, some of it following 1930s railroad logging grades. The undulating route takes in the Lake Sylvia, Lake Winona Vista and Crystal Mountain areas, with well-stocked aid stations along the way.

About

Full Moon 50K and 25K are night races in Perryville, Arkansas, timed to start under a full moon. Runners head out after dark and finish before sunrise, with both distances sharing the same start and finish line.
