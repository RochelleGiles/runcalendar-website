Course

Starts and finishes at the National Sailing Academy on Portland and follows the South West Coast Path around the island. The marathon runs two loops of Portland; the half marathon and 10K cover single loop sections. The route is unmarked — runners navigate from a supplied GPX file — and the terrain is exposed coastal trail rather than flat and fast.

About

A coastal trail event based at the Weymouth and Portland National Sailing Academy, offering a marathon, half marathon and 10K. Runners follow the South West Coast Path around the Isle of Portland on exposed, demanding terrain, navigating by GPX rather than course markers. Entries are capped at 500.
