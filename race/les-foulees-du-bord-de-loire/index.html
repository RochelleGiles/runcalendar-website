About

Experience the scenic beauty of the Loire River at the Les Foulées Du Bord De Loire running event. This annual race takes participants along the picturesque riverbanks, offering a unique opportunity to explore the natural landscapes of the region. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your fitness journey, this event promises an unforgettable experience for all.
