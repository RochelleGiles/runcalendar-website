About

The University of South Carolina Aiken hosts its Homecoming 5K Run on campus, now past its third running, to benefit the Sandy Rogers and Scotty Richardson Memorial Scholarship. The scholarship honors two Aiken police officers killed in the line of duty and is awarded by the sociology program to students pursuing a career in criminal justice with demonstrated financial need.
