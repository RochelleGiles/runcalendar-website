Course

A single-loop closed-road half marathon starting at Central Park, Dartford. The route runs out to South Darenth and through the Kent countryside before returning to Dartford town centre, finishing on the athletics track in Central Park Arena. Undulating throughout, entirely on sealed roads.

About

The Dartford Half Marathon offers runners a fast, closed-road course starting from the Central Park Arena. Situated in the United Kingdom, this event promises an exciting challenge for seasoned runners and newcomers alike, with a scenic route through the local area. Runners can look forward to a rewarding experience as they take on this popular half marathon.
