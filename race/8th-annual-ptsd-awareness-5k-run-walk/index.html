About

It's our 8th Annual PTSD Awareness 5K! And we have something new and exciting!! We will be introducing a running chair that will allow disabled individuals to participate in these events. Help us in our efforts to overcome PTSD by supporting and participating in this event. Show your support by honoring those that have lost the battle to PTSD, those who are currently battling and those who have survived the battle. Through PERSEVERANCE, TENACITY, SUPPORT and DETERMINATION we can overcome PTSD.
