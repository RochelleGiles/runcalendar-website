About

The Viking 5K, 10K, Half Marathon, and Marathon starts at 8:00 AM next to the Wilson Park Recreation Center in Temple, TX. It features out-and-back courses for 5K and 10K, with longer distances looping multiple times, finisher medals, and a 6-hour time limit.
