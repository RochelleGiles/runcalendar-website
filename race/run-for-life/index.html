Course

Starts at 09:00 inside the Monza autodrome, under the circuit's starting lights, and runs over the new track, the old banked circuit and the surrounding Parco di Monza. Distances on offer are 21 km and 10 km competitive plus 10 km and 5 km non-competitive. The organiser does not state the finish point or the surface split.

About

Run for Life takes place in the Parco di Monza, the historic royal park surrounding the Reggia di Monza and the Autodromo Nazionale motor-racing circuit. Five route options let runners of every level explore the park while raising funds for its upkeep.
