Course

Two laps of a route starting and finishing near the cider house entrance at New Paltz, touring the surrounding orchards. The organisers warn of uneven footing and slight inclines; runners and walkers are both catered for.

About

A 5K running event held at Brooklyn Cider House in New Paltz, New York, combining running or walking with scenic views and cider tasting. The event features a festival atmosphere with food, vendors, and recovery options at the finish line.
