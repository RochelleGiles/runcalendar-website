Course

Starts and finishes at Bakewell Station in the Peak District, running out and back along the Monsal Trail on a former railway line through four tunnels and over the Monsal viaduct. Traffic-free and undulating on a multi-terrain trail surface. Half marathon and 10K over a two-day weekend.

About

The Monsal Trail Half Marathon & 10K take place on iconic trails in the Peak District. Traffic free and with a flat-ish surface, the route follows a former Midland railway line through four railway tunnels and across the spectacular Monsal Viaduct.
