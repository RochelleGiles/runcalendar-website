About

The Trail de la Ria et de l'Océan is a scenic running event located in Brittany, offering participants a unique trail experience through diverse terrains including coastal paths, sand beaches, woodlands, and local agricultural trails. Created in 2009, the event has grown from 470 initial participants to over 1,600 runners and hikers, attracting participants beyond Brittany's borders.
