Course

A remote wilderness course in the Pioneer Mountains of central Idaho, with over 23,000ft of climbing across the 100-mile distance. The route strings together steep climbs and sustained high-alpine sections with long unsupported stretches between its twelve stocked aid stations. Shorter 30K and 60K races share the same mountain terrain.

About

The Standhope Ultra Challenge is a mountain ultramarathon in the Pioneer Mountains near Mackay, Idaho, with 100-mile, 60K and 30K options. Courses climb remote, high-alpine terrain far from any town, testing runners on rugged trail through central Idaho's backcountry.
