Course

A fully marked course through coastal terrain south of Florence: open sand dunes dotted with tree islands, old-growth rainforest, stream fords and remote ocean beach. Both the 50K and the shorter option start and finish at Honeyman State Park, three miles south of town, with full-service aid stations on course.
