Course

A 2km and 5km run and walk around the Gladstone Marina Parklands, starting on Bryan Jordan Drive, Callemondah at 7:00am with registration from 6:15am. Entry is taken on the morning only and is cash on the day; Gladstone Road Runners members enter free.

About

The Gladstone Road Runners hold their annual Australia Day Fun Run around the Marina Parklands in Central Queensland. Runners and walkers choose between a 2km and a 5km course, entering on the morning of the race. Random prizes are drawn and cash goes to the overall winners.
