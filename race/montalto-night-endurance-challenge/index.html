About

The Montalto Night Endurance Challenge offers runners a unique opportunity to test their limits in the stunning surroundings of the Montalto Estate in Ballynahinch, Co. Down, Ireland. This captivating event challenges participants to push their endurance to the max as they navigate the estate's picturesque trails under the cloak of night.
