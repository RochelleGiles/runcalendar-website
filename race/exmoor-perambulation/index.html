Course

A self-routed circular course over remote Exmoor moorland, following the 1818 boundary of the Royal Forest of Exmoor from the Pinkery Centre at Simonsbath. The route is unmarked and crosses open access land and private land with livestock, so proficient map and compass navigation is required. Eight checkpoints support the 30-mile and 15-mile routes.

About

A 15 and 30-mile trail event named for the historic perambulation -- the ancient custom of walking the boundary of Exmoor Forest. Organised by Channel Events across the moorland of Exmoor National Park.
