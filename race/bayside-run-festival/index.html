Course

Out-and-back along the Botany Bay foreshore path, starting and finishing at Depena Reserve in Sandringham. Runners head north past Ramsgate Beach to a turnaround before Monterey, then return south on the same path. The route is flat and fast with open water views throughout.

About

The Bayside Run Festival is Sydney's next big community running event, taking place along one of Australia's most scenic coastal courses. Runners of all levels can enjoy the breathtaking ocean views and lively atmosphere as they take on this picturesque challenge in the heart of the city. This annual festival offers a unique opportunity to experience the beauty of the Australian coastline while participating in a fun, inclusive running event.
