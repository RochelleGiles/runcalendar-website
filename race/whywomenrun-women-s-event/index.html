Course

The course starts and finishes at the Auckland Domain Grandstand and follows the paths around the Domain, with water stations along the way. Walkers and runners set off at separate times, and a kids dash runs alongside the 5 km and 10 km.

About

The Y Marathon Club's annual WhyWomenRun event in New Zealand draws both competitive racers and casual runners/walkers for a celebration of women's running. This inclusive, empowering event offers a chance to challenge yourself on the course while connecting with a supportive community of female athletes.
