Course

All events are based at 450 12th Street in Mountain Lake, Minnesota, and run on town roads. The afternoon programme opens with the 1 and 2 mile fitness walks at 3:00 p.m., followed by two kids' races, the Mountain Lake Mile at 4:15 p.m. and the four mile race at 4:30 p.m.
