About

CLASH Endurance proudly presents the Daytona Beach Half Marathon & 5K. The course will begin at Daytona International Speedway and travel by Daytona Beach landmarks such as: Bethune-Cookman University, Riverfront Esplanade, Jackie Robinson Ballpark, and the Motorsports Hall of Fame. Then back to finish at One Daytona.
