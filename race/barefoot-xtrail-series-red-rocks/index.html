Course

Start and finish at the Owhiro Bay Visitor Centre on the Wellington south coast. Courses head west along flat coastal trail toward Te Kopahou, then climb through Te Kopahou Reserve onto exposed ridgelines before descending back to the coast. Surfaces are rugged coastal track and technical inland trail. No aid stations are mentioned.

About

Explore the rugged natural beauty of New Zealand's Red Rocks during the Barefoot XTRAIL Series, an annual off-road trail running event that challenges runners with stunning coastal scenery and technical terrain. This unique race invites participants to experience the thrill of running barefoot or in minimalist footwear, connecting them more closely with the land as they navigate the trails of Owhiro Bay.
