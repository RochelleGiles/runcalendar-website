Course

Starts at Husky Stadium and runs Seattle city streets over the University Bridge, which is raised partway through the race — runners try to cross before it goes up, and those who miss it wait for it to lower before finishing. An 8K run and wheelchair race, a three-mile walk, a one-mile fun run and a toddlers' Diaper Derby all share the start.

About

Beat the Bridge starts at Husky Stadium in Seattle and raises money for type 1 diabetes research through Breakthrough T1D. The morning offers an 8K run and wheelchair race, a three-mile walk, a one-mile fun run and a Diaper Derby for toddlers, with runners racing to cross the University Bridge before it lifts.
