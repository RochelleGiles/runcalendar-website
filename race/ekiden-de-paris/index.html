About

The MAIF Ekiden de Paris is a unique running event that takes place in the heart of the City of Light. This team relay race features stunning views of iconic Parisian landmarks as runners traverse the historic streets. With a rich history and a vibrant atmosphere, the Ekiden de Paris is a must-experience event for runners looking to explore the French capital.
