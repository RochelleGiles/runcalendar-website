Course

A repeating loop through Brady's Run Park, mixing winding single-track with scenic gravel road as it crosses hills and streams and skirts Brady's Run Lake. Technical sections alternate with runnable ground and the climbing is steady rather than steep. The 100 mile is eight laps and the 100K five, with aid stations around the loop and long night sections for the ultra distances.

About

The Rabid Raccoon 100 is a trail running event held at Brady's Run Park in Beaver County, Pennsylvania, featuring winding single-track trails, scenic gravel roads, hills, streams, and lake views. It offers multiple distances including 100 Mile and 100k ultras, a relay, and an obstacle course race, testing endurance with aid stations and participant perks.[1][2]
