About

The Journey to Heal Ministries Hope Run is a 5K and 1.5-mile run/walk in Wilmington, North Carolina, held in support of survivors of human trafficking. A virtual option runs alongside the in-person race. Proceeds fund the ministry's survivor care and community awareness work.
