About

The Tussock Traverse is an iconic alpine trail run through the breathtaking landscapes of Tongariro National Park in New Zealand. Runners can choose from 32km, 21km, 12km, or 6km distances, allowing them to challenge themselves amidst the stunning volcanic terrain and rolling tussock fields. This event offers a truly unforgettable running experience for all levels of trail enthusiasts.
