About

La Frileuse is a nature running event organized by the ECORUN team, held on the training site of the elite corps of the French Gendarmerie and the French Army, offering a unique opportunity to run on a military site. The event features 20km, 10km and 5km races plus non-timed walking events, alongside demonstrations from the Gendarmerie and French Armed Forces.
