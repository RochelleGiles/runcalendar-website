Course

Held at Weston Park in Canberra on dirt tracks and bike paths. A 6 km handicap event and a 3 km event are open to women and girls, with boys aged 12 and under welcome in the 3 km, plus a 2 km mini-jog. Starts are 8am from October to April and 9am from May to September.

About

The Women's and Girls' Jogalong is a monthly community run held by Canberra Runners at Weston Park on the first Sunday of each month. It offers a 6 km handicap race, a 3 km event and a 2 km mini-jog, and is free to members with a small entry fee for others.
