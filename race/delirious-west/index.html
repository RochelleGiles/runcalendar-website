Course

A point to point course from Northcliffe to Albany following the Bibbulmun Track, over forest, coastal cliff, dune and beach. The 200 miler covers 348.4km with 7,905m of ascent and 20 aid stations; the 100 miler covers 178.4km with 3,785m and 10 aid stations. The 200 and 100 mile fields are each capped at 100 runners.

About

The course follows the Bibbulmun Track from Northcliffe to Albany, through karri forest, dunes and coastal cliff tops. The 200 mile event covers 348km with almost 8,000 metres of climbing and 20 aid stations, and shorter 100 mile, 60km, 34km and 8km options run on sections of the same route. Both mile fields are capped at 100 runners.
