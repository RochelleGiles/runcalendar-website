Course

An undulating ten miles on public roads starting and finishing at Millpool Car Park in Looe. The route heads through West Looe and out along the coastal road to Talland, loops around the Killigarth and Porthallow area, then returns via Talland for the run home. Water stations sit at miles 3, 6 and 9.

About

The Looe 10 Miler is an annual multi-terrain 10 mile race organized by the Looe Pioneers in the picturesque coastal town of Looe, United Kingdom. Runners can expect a challenging yet breathtaking course that winds through scenic countryside and along the rugged Cornish coastline, making this event a must-do for runners seeking a unique racing experience.
