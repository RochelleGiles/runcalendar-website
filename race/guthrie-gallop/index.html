About

The Guthrie Gallop is an annual 5K and 10K in Sayre, Pennsylvania, starting at the historic Sayre Theatre and running local streets through the Athens and Sayre boroughs. A kids' fun run is held alongside, and proceeds go to the Guthrie Cancer Care Fund.
