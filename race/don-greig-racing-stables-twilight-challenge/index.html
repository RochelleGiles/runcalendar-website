About

The Don Greig Racing Stables Twilight Challenge is a unique running event held in New Zealand. The course takes runners through scenic countryside, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants. With a focus on community and camaraderie, this event provides a memorable atmosphere for runners of all levels.
