Course

A USATF-certified, rolling circuit through Valencia's paseos and bridge overpasses, with sections along the South Fork River Trail. The 10K adds a dirt equestrian trail between miles 3.25 and 4.85. Start and finish at Valencia Town Center.

About

Mardi Gras Madness is a fun and festive running event that takes place in Valencia, California. Runners can participate in a 5K or 10K race, and the event features live DJ music, a beer garden, and prizes for the best Mardi Gras-themed costumes.
