Course

The 5K and 10K run through Big Foot Beach State Park following the park's hiking trails, the 5K covering one lap and the 10K two. Two water stations are on course and strollers are welcome. Organisers note the route may change with weather conditions.

About

The Super Hero Shuffle 5K/10K Run/Walk, now past its 13th running, supports families affected by childhood cancer. Runners and walkers, many in costume, take on a scenic route through Big Foot Beach State Park's hiking trails, and strollers are welcome.
