About

The Hank Kabel Sarcoma Foundation is a 501(c)3 organization whose goal is to raise money and awareness for canine cancer. By supporting this foundation, you are not only raising money for dogs with cancer, but also for people with cancer, since clinical trials in dogs help decide medicines that are effective in human cancers as well.
