Course

Starts and finishes near the log cabin at the White Hall Mansion in White Hall Park. The 5K is an out-and-back on the access road plus a loop of the park and lake area, run on paved trail and roadway surfaces, with one or two water stops. Quarter-mile races for children are held alongside.

About

The Fostering Hope 5K is an annual family fun event held at White Hall Park in Richmond, KY, benefiting youth in foster care through support for Orphan Care Alliance. It features a scenic 5K run/walk, kid's dash, toy ride race, and virtual option with awards and T-shirts for participants.
