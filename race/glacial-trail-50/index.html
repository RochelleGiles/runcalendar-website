About

The Glacial Trail 50 is Wisconsin's fall ultra, held in the Northern Kettle Moraine State Forest. Starting in Greenbush, runners will take a connector trail from town out to the Ice Age Trail, which comprises most of the course. Hopefully fall colors will be at their peak!
