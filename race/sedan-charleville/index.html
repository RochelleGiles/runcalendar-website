About

The Sedan-Charleville is the oldest city-to-city race in France, founded in 1906. The historic event connects the cities of Sedan and Charleville-Mézières along a scenic 23.6-kilometer route through the Ardenne region. Organized by Courir en Ardenne, this race is a beloved local tradition supported by hundreds of passionate volunteers.
