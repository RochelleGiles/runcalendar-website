About

Join us at our yearly Run/Walk event to help support our community mission. Our mission is to educate and empower the community regarding the health and safety of our young people with a focus on alcohol and other drugs. For over a decade now we hold a Run/Walk event called Run 2 Save Our Youth as a way to support the programs our coalition provides to the community.
