About

The Belfast City 5K Fun Run takes participants on a flat, family-friendly route through the centre of Belfast, Northern Ireland, starting at City Hall and finishing on Donegall Place. Run, jog or walk it - the event is organised by the Belfast City Marathon team and is open to all ages and abilities.
