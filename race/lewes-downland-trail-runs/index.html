Course

Two off-road routes over the Sussex Downs, both starting at Landport Bottom Meadow near Lewes. The 5-mile loops out to Blackcap and back over historic Mount Harry; the 10-mile heads into quieter parts of the Downs and returns through the wooded valley of Ashcombe Bottom. Underfoot is grass, chalk and flint track and woodland path, narrow and uneven in places.

About

A Lewes AC trail event on the South Downs above the town, with 10-mile and 5-mile options across open downland. The routes climb the chalk hills surrounding Lewes.
