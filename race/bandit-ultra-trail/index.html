Course

Mountain trail races starting and finishing at Corriganville Park in Simi Valley. The routes climb through Rocky Peak, Las Llajas and Tapo Canyon on rugged mountain trail and fire road, with big climbs and mountain, city and ocean views. Aid stations are spaced through the course, five of them on the 50K.

About

The Bandit Ultra Trail climbs Rocky Peak in the Santa Susana Mountains above Simi Valley, with 15K, 30K and 50K distances on rugged Southern California singletrack and fire road.
