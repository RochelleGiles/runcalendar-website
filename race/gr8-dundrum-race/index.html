Course

Starts and finishes on Main Street, Dundrum. The 8.4-mile route mixes road, beach and boardwalk through Murlough Nature Reserve with views to the Mournes; the 3.5-mile route is on road only.

About

The Gr8 Dundrum Run starts in Dundrum, County Down, Northern Ireland, on the shore of Dundrum Bay near the sand dunes of Murlough National Nature Reserve. It is organised by Murlough Athletics Club.
