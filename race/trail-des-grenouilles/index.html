About

The Trail des Grenouilles is organized by the Anoue de Pezou festival committee, featuring two courses, 8km and 19km, held at night with full autonomy required of runners. The event takes place in Pezou and the surrounding communes of Lignières, Renay, La Chapelle Enchérie, and Saint-Firmin-des-Prés.
