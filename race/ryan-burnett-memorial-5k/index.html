About

The Ryan Burnett Memorial 5K takes place at the Cookeville Cross Country Facility on Whiteaker Springs Road in Cookeville, Tennessee. The 3.1-mile course covers paved roads, a lap of the track, and finishes on the Cookeville Cavaliers Cross Country team's grass course, with awards and door prizes for finishers.
