Course

A single rural loop starting and finishing at Welland Academy in Stamford. The route passes through Little Casterton, Tolethorpe, Ryhall, Careby, Pickworth and Great Casterton on undulating country roads with several modest hills. The field is capped at 750 runners.

About

Join us to help celebrate 32 years of the fabulous 30k. The Stamford Striders' St Valentine 30K Race is the flagship event organised by the Club. It is run each year on the Sunday closest to St Valentine's Day. It is recognised nationally as a tough event, providing a good training race for those planning a spring marathon.
