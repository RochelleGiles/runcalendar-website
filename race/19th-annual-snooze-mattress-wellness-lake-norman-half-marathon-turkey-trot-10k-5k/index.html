About

Looking for a fun-filled Thanksgiving activity the whole family can enjoy? Look no further than the Lake Norman Turkey Trot in Cornelius, NC! This annual event offers families from Mooresville, Huntersville, Denver, Davidson, Hickory, Statesville, Charlotte and beyond a chance to bond, stay active, and give back to the community, all while celebrating the holiday spirit.
