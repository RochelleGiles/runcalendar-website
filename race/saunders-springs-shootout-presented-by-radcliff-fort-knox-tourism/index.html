Course

Trail races at Saunders Springs Nature Preserve in Radcliff, Kentucky, run on courses that are 99% singletrack and mix scenic woodland with steep, punchy climbs. 5K, 10K and 25K trail runs start together in the morning, with a free kids trail run afterwards.

About

The Saunders Springs Shootout is a two-day event at Saunders Springs Nature Preserve in Radcliff, Kentucky: Saturday brings mountain biking endurance races in solo, duo, and four-person team classes, and Sunday brings 5K, 10K, and 25K trail runs. Both run and bike courses are almost entirely singletrack.
