About

The Guadix Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging race set against the stunning backdrop of the Andalusian countryside. Runners will wind through the historic city of Guadix, passing by ancient landmarks and enjoying breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains. This event is a must-do for any runner seeking an unforgettable Spanish running adventure.
