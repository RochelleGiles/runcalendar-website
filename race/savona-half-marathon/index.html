Course

Starts and finishes on via Paleocapa in central Savona, setting off at 9.30am on sealed city and coastal roads. The route is FIDAL-certified and listed on the World Athletics global calendar, with aid stations along the way and timing checkpoints at 10 km and 15 km.

About

The Savona Half Marathon is a scenic coastal running event that takes runners through the beautiful Ligurian coastline. The race route winds through Savona, Albissola Marina, Albisola Superiore, and Vado Ligure, offering participants stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea. Recognized by World Athletics and Fidal, this event attracts both competitive and recreational runners.
