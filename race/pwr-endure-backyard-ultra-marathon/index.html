About

Runners, test your mettle at the PWR Endure Backyard Ultra Marathon, set in the historic grounds of Tockington Manor in South Gloucestershire, UK. This unique event challenges participants to complete laps of a 4.167-mile loop, with the last runner standing declared the winner. Embrace the spirit of camaraderie and endurance as you push your limits on this scenic course.
