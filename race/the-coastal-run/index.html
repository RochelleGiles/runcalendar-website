About

The Coastal Run from 16 to 17 October 2026 is held along the east coast of the South Island and includes a 5km, 10km and Half Marathon distance to choose from. Taking in stunning scenery, the rolling courses are all out-and-back routes starting and finishing at the iconic Caroline Bay.
