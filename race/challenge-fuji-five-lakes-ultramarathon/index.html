About

The Challenge Fuji Five Lakes Ultramarathon starts and finishes at Fuji Hokuroku Park in Fujiyoshida, Yamanashi, at the foot of Mt. Fuji. Runners choose from 118km, 100km, or 63km courses that wind past the Fuji Five Lakes. First held around 1990, it is one of Japan's longest-running ultramarathons, drawing up to several thousand entrants across its distances.
