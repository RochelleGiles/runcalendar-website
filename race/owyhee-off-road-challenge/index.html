Course

About

Ready to experience fresh air, wide-open spaces, and beautiful landscape? Take a trip to Adrian, Oregon (just 54-miles northwest of Boise, Idaho) and experience the rugged beauty of the Owyhees and the hospitality of a small-town. The Owyhee Off-Road Challenge 55k, Half Marathon, 10k, & 5k offers breathtaking views of the Snake River and Owyhee Valleys with the friendliest volunteers this side of the Rockies. Best of all, every step you take helps support a great cause! All proceeds go toward purchasing equipment for the new Adrian School District track and funding programs that benefit local youth. Join us for an unforgettable adventure—run, explore, and make a difference!
