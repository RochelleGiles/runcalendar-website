About

The Marathon d'Amman is a must-do event for runners seeking an unforgettable experience in the heart of Jordan's vibrant capital. Weaving through the historic streets and landmarks of Amman, this race offers a unique opportunity to explore the city's rich culture and stunning architecture while challenging yourself physically. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a dedicated runner, the Marathon d'Amman promises an unforgettable journey.
