About

Celebrating 5 years of supporting teachers! Join us at Cypress Gardens on Saturday, April 25 for our certified 5k featuring a unique mixed terrain course that winds its way through the beautiful blackwater swamp. The race kicks off at 8 a.m., rain or shine! We'll be raising money to directly benefit local teachers through our Bright Ideas educational grant program.
