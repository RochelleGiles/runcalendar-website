About

The Saunders Lakeland Mountain Marathon is a two-day mountain navigation competition (or race) that is held annually in the Lake District for teams of two runners. The event comprises 6 linear courses of varying lengths and technical difficulty and one 'score' course where competitors choose which controls to visit over a 2 day total of 12 hours. Competitors must carry lightweight camping and safety equipment and demonstrate competence in map and compass navigation in the mountains.
