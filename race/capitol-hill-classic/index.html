Course

Both races start and finish in front of Peabody Primary School on Capitol Hill. The 10K runs west past Stanton Park to the US Capitol, the Supreme Court and the Library of Congress, out along East Capitol Street and around Lincoln Park towards RFK Stadium, then turns on the Whitney Young Bridge and returns via C Street NE. The 3K loops Stanton Park and East Capitol Street, with a Kids Fun Run after the 10K.

About

The Capitol Hill Classic is an annual 10K and 3K road race through the historic Capitol Hill neighborhood of Washington, D.C., organized to benefit the Capitol Hill Cluster School's public schools.
