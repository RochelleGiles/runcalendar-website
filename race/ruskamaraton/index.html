About

Ruskamaraton is a beloved running event in Lapland that has been celebrating athletics, joy, and the beautiful autumn landscape since 1984. Held annually in September, this event brings together running enthusiasts in the picturesque setting of Levi. The marathon has become a cherished tradition, attracting around 2,000 participants who enjoy the unique autumn scenery and community spirit.
