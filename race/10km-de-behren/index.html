About

The 10km De Behren is a must-do event for runners seeking a scenic and challenging race. Weaving through the charming town of Behren, this annual 10k showcases the region's natural beauty and rich history. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a newcomer to the sport, the 10km De Behren offers an unforgettable running experience.
