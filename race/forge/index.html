Course

A backyard-ultra format: one 4.167-mile loop started each hour for twelve hours, twelve loops totalling fifty miles. The loop is a circuit with a short out-and-back in the middle, on very flat dirt roads south of the Swinomish Casino, with one softer sand section. There is no shade on course. The event runs 7:00am to 7:00pm.
