Course

A 50K starting and finishing at Lake Chabot Marina, running a large loop through the East Bay Regional Parks out to Skyline Gate and back. One of the oldest continuously run 50Ks in the United States, with aid stations staffed by local running clubs.

About

Skyline 50K Endurance Run follows the Skyline National Recreation Trail through Anthony Chabot and Redwood Regional Parks in the Oakland hills, climbing wooded ridgelines with views over the San Francisco Bay.
