About

The Trail De La Tour En Alsace is an iconic all-female trail running event in the Alsace region. The event offers two course options, a 13km loop with 450m of elevation gain and a 20.5km loop with 730m of elevation gain, allowing participants of all levels to challenge themselves in the beautiful and diverse Champ du Feu massif. The event promotes values of women's empowerment, environmental protection, and solidarity.
