About

The Ingelheimer Halbe is a popular half marathon event held annually in the city of Ingelheim, Germany. The scenic course takes runners through the historic city center and along the banks of the Rhine River, offering stunning views of the surrounding vineyards and countryside. The event is known for its lively atmosphere, with enthusiastic spectators cheering on the participants throughout the race.
