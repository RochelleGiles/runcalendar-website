About

The 15th International Marathon "Alexander The Great" is a prestigious running event held in Thessaloniki, Greece. The course takes runners through the historic city, showcasing its rich cultural heritage. With a vibrant atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators, this marathon offers a unique experience for both elite athletes and recreational runners.
