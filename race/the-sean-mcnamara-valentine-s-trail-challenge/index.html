Course

About

The Sean McNamara Valentine's Trail Challenge, hosted by USOLE, honors longtime USOLE member Sean McNamara at Schiller Woods in Chicago, now past its tenth running. The course follows the Des Plaines River Trail through wooded, often snowy scenery, offering a good introduction to trail running for first-timers.
