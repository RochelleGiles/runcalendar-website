Course

Out-and-back four-miler on Sabino Canyon's main road in Tucson, running from the canyon entrance past Bear Canyon to a turnaround at the Seven Falls trailhead and back the same way. Sealed canyon road on a steady climb out and a fast return.

About

A 4-mile road run held at Sabino Canyon in Tucson, Arizona, starting at 5:05 AM. The event is organized by Everyone Runs and includes amenities such as free Mexican breakfast, free photos, and tech or cotton shirts for participants.
