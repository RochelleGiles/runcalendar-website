Course

An out-and-back dirt course on Black Mountain in San Diego, run entirely off-road. The half marathon climbs about 2,700ft over technical singletrack and service road; the 10K and 5K are less technical on groomed dirt, with roughly 960ft and 670ft of gain. Field is capped at 300 runners.

About

The Black Mountain Turkey Burner is a trail race in San Diego, California, held on Thanksgiving weekend on Black Mountain Open Space Park. Runners can choose a half marathon, 10K, or 5K distance across hilly singletrack trails.
