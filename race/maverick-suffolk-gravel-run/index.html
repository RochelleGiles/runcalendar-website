Course

Three routes of 9km, 16km and 23km start and finish at Wantisden Hall near Woodbridge, in the Suffolk AONB. The courses are flat forest and gravel trails through woodland, sandy in places. The half marathon and middle routes each have two aid stations serving snacks and electrolyte drinks; the short route has none.

About

Discover the hidden gems of Suffolk's picturesque countryside as you tackle the Maverick Suffolk Gravel Run. This captivating event takes you on a scenic journey through the Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, challenging runners with a mix of gravel trails and undulating terrain. Whether you're an experienced gravel enthusiast or looking for a new adventure, this event promises an unforgettable experience amidst the region's natural splendor.
