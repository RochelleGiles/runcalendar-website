Course

Courses of 21km, 10km, 5.5km and a 2km childrens race in Bottle Lake Forest, run on gravel roads, pine-forest trail with needles underfoot, shared paths and grass. The longer distances run two laps, with aid stations at 5km and 15km on the 21km. Sections are shared with mountain bikers.

About

Experience the stunning trails of Bottle Lake Forest in Christchurch, New Zealand at the Forest Run Fest. Offering four epic distances from a 2km Kids' run to a challenging Half Marathon, this event invites runners of all levels to immerse themselves in the serene beauty of the forest. With a variety of distances to choose from, the Forest Run Fest promises an unforgettable adventure for the whole family.
