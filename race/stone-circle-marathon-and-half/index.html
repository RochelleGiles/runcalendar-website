Course

A looped multi-surface course starting and finishing at Gargrave Village Hall in the Yorkshire Dales — one loop for the half, two for the marathon. The route crosses fields and footpaths, follows canal towpath and takes in a short section of country road, passing the Broughton stone circle. Three refreshment stations per loop. Marathon starts 9:30am, half at 10:00am; long-distance walkers welcome.

About

The Stone Circle Marathon & Half runs multi-terrain loops from Gargrave, North Yorkshire, crossing fields, footpaths, canal towpaths and country roads to the Broughton Stone Circle. The marathon covers two laps of the route, the half marathon one.
