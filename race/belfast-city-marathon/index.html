About

The Belfast City Marathon is one of the premier running events in the UK and Ireland, offering a scenic and challenging course through the historic streets of Belfast. Certified by the Association of International Marathons and Distance Races (AIMS), this iconic event attracts runners of all levels, from elite athletes to enthusiastic amateurs, making it a must-do for any distance running enthusiast.
