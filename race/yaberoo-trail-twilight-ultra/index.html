Course

An out-and-back on the Yaberoo Budjara Heritage Trail through Perth's northern coastal bushland, starting and finishing at Burns Beach Road at the Blue Mountain Drive intersection and heading north. Groomed trail with three manned aid stations (Hester Ave, Rheinhold Place and start/finish) carrying water, Tailwind, cola, lollies, crisps and fruit. All distances finish by midnight under a 12-hour cutoff.

About

The Yaberoo Trail Twilight Ultra takes place along the historic Yaberoo Budjara Trail, which translates to 'Land of the peoples North of Perth'. The course winds through beautiful coastal bushland and limestone trails, offering runners a unique experience through unchanged landscapes that exist alongside the suburbs. This trail event holds special significance as it runs along paths used by the Whadjuk peoples for tens of thousands of years, connecting the swan estuaries to Yanchep.
