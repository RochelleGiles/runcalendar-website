About

Explore the stunning natural beauty of the Font-Romeu region in southern France at the Trail De Font Romeu. This challenging trail run takes participants through breathtaking mountain landscapes, offering a unique opportunity to connect with nature and test your endurance. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable adventure in one of Europe's most picturesque outdoor destinations.
