About

Urban Race Narbonne is now past its 5th running, organized by the Comité d'Animation Voltaire Joffre in the streets and along the banks of the Robine in Narbonne. It offers an 18km timed race, a 9km timed race, a 9km untimed walk/hike, and a 4.5km untimed walk/hike, with proceeds supporting the association Il était une fois les Rooses, which helps women with cancer in the Aude region.
