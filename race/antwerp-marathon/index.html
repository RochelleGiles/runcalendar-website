About

The Antwerp Marathon is a must-run event for any endurance enthusiast. Winding through the historic streets and iconic landmarks of Belgium's vibrant port city, this race offers runners a unique and captivating experience. Featuring a scenic route along the Scheldt River, the Antwerp Marathon celebrates the city's rich culture and stunning architecture.
