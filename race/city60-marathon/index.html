About

A 60 km road ultra through Pretoria on 16 December, the Day of Reconciliation, starting and finishing at Church Square. Billed as a Comrades and Two Oceans qualifier and a last long run of the year; a 10 km and a 5 km start later in the morning, and the 60 km can also be run as a four-leg relay.
