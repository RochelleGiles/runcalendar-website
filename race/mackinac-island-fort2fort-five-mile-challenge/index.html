Course

A five-mile loop starting at the Sally Port gate inside Fort Mackinac. Runners head to Arch Rock via the Leslie Trail, climb to Fort Holmes, then descend on Garrison and Custer Roads to finish on the lawn below Fort Mackinac. The route mixes sealed island roads with established trail.
