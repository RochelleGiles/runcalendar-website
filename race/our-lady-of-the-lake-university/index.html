Course

Starts at the Main Building front walk on the OLLU campus, 411 SW 24th Street, San Antonio, and runs through the surrounding neighbourhood including a stretch around Elmendorf Lake. Start time is 7:30. The organiser notes the final route is subject to confirmation of the official course map. Aid station detail is not published.

About

A family-friendly spring event held on Our Lady of the Lake University's campus that combines fitness and fun as part of Fiesta San Antonio celebrations. Participants run or walk through the historic campus and Lake Elmendorf Park trails while being showered with confetti, with all proceeds benefiting scholarships for OLLU students.
