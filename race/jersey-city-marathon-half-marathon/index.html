Course

A flat, fast USATF-certified road course starting at 7am and running through Jersey City's waterfront and its varied neighbourhoods. The route includes narrow sections, rail-track crossings and two crossover sections. It is a Boston qualifier, and the organisers note the 2027 course may change.

About

The 4th Annual Jersey City Marathon and Half Marathon is a USATF-sanctioned event on a fast, flat, certified course starting at 7 AM in Newport. Runners experience Jersey City's diverse neighborhoods and it qualifies for the 2027 Boston Marathon.[1]
