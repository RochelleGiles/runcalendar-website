Course

A 24-hour timed event on a repeating 3.1-mile loop at Bear Lake near Lapeer, Michigan; runners complete as many laps as they choose. The loop takes in rolling hills on singletrack and wider trail, some grass and a few bridge crossings.

About

Year 9! 24 hours of FUN! This means you can run, walk, go fast, go slow! Do as many loops as you want or can! All profits from the race are given to Lions Bear Lake Blind Camp (501c3). In 8 years we have raised over $66,000! to the Camp.
