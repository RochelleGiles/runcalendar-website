Course

Starts on Charles Street beside Boston Common and finishes on the Common. Urban road loop through Boston and Cambridge, crossing the Longfellow Bridge at mile one, out along Memorial Drive beside the Charles River, then back over the Massachusetts Avenue Bridge through Kenmore Square and over the Boston Marathon finish line on Boylston Street. Water at miles 1, 2, 4 and 5.

About

The Boston 10K is organized by the Boston Athletic Association and presented by Mass General Brigham Cancer Institute. The loop course starts near Boston Common, runs along the Charles River and through Kenmore Square, and draws about 10,000 runners from roughly 97 countries each year.
