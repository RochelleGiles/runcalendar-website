Course

Off-road race at Spanish Lake County Park, north of the Confluence Conservation Area. The route starts on paved trail around Spanish Lake then turns into double track, gravel and single track around upper Sunflower Lake. The 9-mile runs two laps, the 9K one.

About

The Nippy Niner Trail Race is a winter trail run at Spanish Lake County Park near St. Louis, offering 9-mile and 9K distances on a mix of paved trail, double track, gravel and singletrack.
