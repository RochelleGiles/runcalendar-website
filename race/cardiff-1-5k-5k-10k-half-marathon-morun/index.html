Course

Flat lapped runs in Bute Park, Cardiff, starting and finishing at Coopers Field, mainly on tarmac paths. The 5K is a single lap, the 10K two laps and the half marathon an opening 1.1km loop followed by four full laps. Dog friendly and suitable for buggies and wheelchairs, with a 1.5K mini run for children after the main races.

About

The Cardiff MoRun is a fun and inspiring running event supporting Movember and men's health awareness. Participants can choose from multiple race distances including 1.5k, 5k, 10k, and half marathon, creating an inclusive event for runners of all levels. The event takes place in the scenic Bute Park and encourages runners to dress up, have fun, and support a great cause.
