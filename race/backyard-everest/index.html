Course

A backyard-style hourly format in Black Hill Conservation Park, Athelstone. Runners repeat a 5.3 km loop gaining 369 m of vertical on the hour, for up to 24 hours, with an aid station on the loop. Seven summit milestones mark progress; all 24 laps total 8,856 m of climb, the height of Everest.

About

Backyard Everest is a backyard-ultra style race in Adelaide where runners repeat a 5.3km lap on the hour until they have collectively climbed the elevation of Mount Everest, or are the last one standing.
