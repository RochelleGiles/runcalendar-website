Course

Loop course starting and finishing at the Rialto Theatre on Kenmore Boulevard. Runners head west then east along Kenmore Boulevard, connect to the Summit Lake Trail heading south, cross bridges onto the Towpath, loop the north shore of Summit Lake, then return to Kenmore Boulevard for the finish. A mix of urban street and trail/towpath sections.

About

The Kenmore Cowbell 7K is a family-friendly running event held in Akron, Ohio, featuring a unique 7K distance that combines natural and urban experiences. It kicks off at 8 a.m. at the historic Rialto Theatre on Kenmore Boulevard and includes Adult 7K Run/Walk, Student 7K Run/Walk, BLVD Mile, and spectator options.
