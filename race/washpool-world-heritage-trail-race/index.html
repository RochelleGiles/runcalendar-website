Course

All distances start and finish at Mulligans Hut in Gibraltar Range and Washpool National Parks. The 50 km runs the Gibraltar-Washpool World Heritage Walk singletrack on Saturday; the 25 km follows Little Dandahra Creek trail and the 9 km the Tree Fern Forest Walk on Sunday, through rocky outcrops and rainforest. Elevation gain is 1,250 m, 470 m and 240 m.

About

Explore the stunning Washpool World Heritage Trails in the rugged wilderness of New South Wales. This challenging yet breathtaking event takes runners through the picturesque Washpool and Gibraltar Range National Parks, showcasing the region's diverse landscapes and unique natural wonders. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or seeking a new adventure, this event offers an unforgettable experience in a UNESCO-protected natural wonderland.
