Course

A trail race run over three days from a race village at the Coliwoo Bukit Timah Fire Station, with the 25km and 80km starting instead from Pepys Road Carpark. Distances range from 20km up to multi-day 120km and 165km formats on Singapore's forested nature-reserve trails.

About

Singapore's flagship trail ultra offers distances from 20km up to a non-stop 165km, routing runners through the forested trails of the Central Catchment Nature Reserve. It draws competitive ultrarunners from across the region each June.
