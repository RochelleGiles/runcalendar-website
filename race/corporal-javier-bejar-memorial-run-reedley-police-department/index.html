About

Join us as we honor the life and legacy of Corporal Javier Bejar, a Reedley Police Officer, Marine Corps veteran, and beloved member of our community who made the ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty. This memorial run is more than a race—it's a tribute to courage, service, and community. Every stride taken represents our gratitude for Corporal Bejar's dedication and the enduring impact he left behind.
