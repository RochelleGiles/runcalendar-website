About

The Louisville Triple Crown of Running, presented by The Courier Journal, is one of the region's premier running series celebrating the city's vibrant running community and iconic landmarks for more than four decades. Now in its 41st year, this beloved tradition features three signature spring events: the Whiskey Row 5K, Derby City 10K, and The University of Louisville 10 Mile.
