Course

Starts at the top of Mt Nebo, in the parking lot near the pool and camping area about three miles west of Dardanelle on Highway 155. The route is an approximately 14-mile trail run over the mountain, finishing at the pavilion by the pool where refreshments are served. A low-key AURA Ultra Trail Series event where runners keep their own time.

About

Mount Nebo Trail Run is a 14-mile trail race in Dardanelle, Arkansas, following the rim trail around Mount Nebo, a flat-topped mountain with sweeping views of the Arkansas River Valley below and the surrounding Ozark foothills.
