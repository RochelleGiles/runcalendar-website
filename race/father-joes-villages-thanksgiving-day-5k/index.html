Course

The 5K starts at the Cabrillo Bridge in San Diego's Balboa Park and finishes at the Plaza de Panama, where the post-race festival is held. Timed, untimed and virtual entry options are offered, with a free category for children five and under.

About

The Thanksgiving Day 5K takes place in San Diego on Thanksgiving morning, drawing runners before the holiday meal. Father Joe's Villages, the race's beneficiary, provides homeless services and permanent housing across the city.
