Course

Trail loop from Scorton village into the Forest of Bowland Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, offered over 15k and 10k. The 15k climbs and drops about 1,230 ft. A water station sits at roughly 4.3 miles with a bottle collection point 100 metres beyond it.

About

The Scorton Trail Run is a scenic running event set in the Forest of Bowland, an area of outstanding natural beauty. Runners can choose between 15k and 10k distances, winding through rolling trails, lush woodlands, and picturesque Lancashire countryside. The event offers a challenging yet enjoyable experience for runners of all levels, showcasing the stunning landscape of the region.
