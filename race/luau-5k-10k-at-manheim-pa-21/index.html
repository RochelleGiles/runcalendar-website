Course

Run on the Lancaster Junction Trail, starting and finishing at 99 Champ Blvd in Manheim. The course is a shaded 1.04-mile out-and-back segment repeated to make up the 5K or 10K, all on compact gravel rail-trail.

About

The Luau 5K & 10K is a fun running event on a shaded compact gravel course at Lancaster Junction Trail in Manheim, PA. Participants receive finisher medals in a festive atmosphere with 5K and 10K distances.
