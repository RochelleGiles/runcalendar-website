Course

Both distances start and finish at Sky Valley Resort and run on mountain singletrack around Sky Valley, passing waterfalls and climbing Rabun Bald, the second-highest point in Georgia. The 50K runs Saturday at 7:00am and the 25K Sunday at 9:00am. An aid station matrix for both distances is published in the runner handbook.

About

The Sky To Summit Trail Race is a challenging ultra-marathon event featuring a 50K and 25K distance. The course takes runners through the beautiful Appalachian mountains, offering stunning views and technical terrain.
