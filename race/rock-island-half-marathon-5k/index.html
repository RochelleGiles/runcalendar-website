Course

An out-and-back on the Rock Island Spur of the Katy Trail, starting and finishing at the Rock Island Trail Head on W. Commercial St in Pleasant Hill, Missouri. The route is flat former railbed the whole way, with both the 5K and half marathon turning back on the same path.

About

Join us for this fifth annual event on the Rock Island Trail on July 26! Starting from Pleasant Hill, MO, the race features an out and back half marathon and 5K course to the southeast on the Rock Island Spur of the Katy Trail. There will be hydration stations and mile markers along the course and all participants will receive a custom finishers medal and race shirt. The number of registrants will be limited for this event and we ask all half marathons participants to finish in no longer than 3 hours.
