About

Get ready to don your most outrageous holiday sweater and join us for The Medina Ugly Sweater 5K & 1 Mile Fun Run! This festive event is all about fun, fitness, and giving back to the community. All net proceeds will support the Helping Hand of Humboldt, making every step you take a step toward helping those in need. We are also thrilled to announce that Santa Claus will be making an appearance during the event.
