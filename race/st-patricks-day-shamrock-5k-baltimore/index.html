Course

A fast, downhill point-to-point 5K through central Baltimore, starting at Charles and Mulberry Streets and finishing at Power Plant Live. The course is certified and friendly to walkers, and a virtual option is offered. Post-race celebration at the finish.

About

The Altra Kelly Benefits St. Patrick's Day Shamrock 5K is a certified 5K road race in Baltimore, Maryland. It is held each March as part of the city's annual St. Patrick's Day Parade weekend celebrations, drawing runners in green for the occasion.
