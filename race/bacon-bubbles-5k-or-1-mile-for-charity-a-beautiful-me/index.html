About

Family-friendly fun run with bacon & bubbles! A Beautiful Me is excited to host our 7th Annual 5K or 1 Mile Run/Walk FUNdraiser along the St. Clair River. Participants can look forward to raffle baskets for adults and kids, chip timing, great awards, snacks, beverages, and a lot of fun AND bacon!
