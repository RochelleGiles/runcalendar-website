About

Trail dei Cimbri runs between Monte Pizzoc and the Cansiglio forest plateau above Vittorio Veneto, in the Venetian Prealps. The 25K and 50K routes climb through beech woods named for the historic Cimbri community who settled the Cansiglio in the Middle Ages.
