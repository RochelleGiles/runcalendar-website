Course

A 3.6-mile evening run on the dirt Bonneville Shoreline Trail, starting from the pavilion stairs at Orson Smith Park in Draper. The route climbs 472ft, crosses the Bear Canyon suspension bridge and looks out over Corner Canyon and Draper City. Dog-friendly, run as a group.

About

This course has you running the incredible Bonneville Shoreline trail which offers great views of Corner Canyon and Draper City. You will cross the iconic Bear Canyon suspension bridge that is arguably the most popular hike in Draper.
