About

Conquer the historic slopes of Gergovie at the annual La Romagnatoise race. This challenging event takes runners on a scenic tour through the picturesque French countryside, offering stunning views and a chance to test their mettle against the challenging terrain. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or just seeking a unique running adventure, La Romagnatoise promises an unforgettable experience.
