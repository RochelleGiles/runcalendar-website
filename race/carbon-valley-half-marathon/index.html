About

The Carbon Valley Half Marathon is run from Centennial Park in Frederick, Colorado, between Denver and Fort Collins. The course takes in the town's wood carvings, ground connected to its former coal mines, and Milavec Lake Park. A 5K, a team relay and a fun run are also offered.
