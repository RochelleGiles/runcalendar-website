Course

A single city-centre loop starting and finishing at Jubilee Square. The route runs through central Leicester into Victoria Park, weaves through the University of Leicester campus and passes the Museum & Art Gallery and Leicester Cathedral. Sealed road and path surface, with water stations and pacers on course.

About

The Leicester 10K is a popular running event in the heart of the United Kingdom. The scenic course takes runners through the historic city, showcasing its beautiful architecture and vibrant atmosphere. Participants can expect a well-organized event with a lively crowd cheering them on to the finish line.
