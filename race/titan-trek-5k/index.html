About

The Titan Trek 5K is hosted by the Ball Chatham Glenwood PTO at Glenwood Elementary School in Chatham, Illinois. It offers a 5K run and walk, a separate category for K-12 students and a one-mile fun run, with proceeds supporting the school district.
