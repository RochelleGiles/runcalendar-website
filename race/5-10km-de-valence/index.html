About

The 5 and 10 km de Valence is a running event held in the city of Valence, France. The event features both a 5 km and a 10 km race, offering a challenge for runners of all levels. The scenic course takes participants through the streets of Valence, showcasing the city's historic landmarks and picturesque surroundings.
