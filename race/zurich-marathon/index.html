Course

A road marathon from the Quaibrücke along the shore of Lake Zurich to a turn at Meilen and back to the finish on Sechseläutenplatz. A half marathon in the early afternoon and a 10 km in the late afternoon share the same start and finish on race day.

About

The Zürich Marathon takes runners along the shores of Lake Zürich and the Limmat river before finishing in the city's historic Altstadt. Marathon, half marathon and 10K runners all follow the same lakeside and riverside route through the Swiss city each spring.
