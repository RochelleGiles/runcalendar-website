About

The New Delhi Marathon is an annual running event that brings together runners from all walks of life. The event offers various race distances, catering to both seasoned marathoners and casual runners. The course showcases the vibrant city of New Delhi, with participants running past iconic landmarks and experiencing the city's unique energy and culture. The event is known for its festive atmosphere, where runners come together to celebrate their passion for running and the spirit of sportsmanship.
