Course

A fast, flat single-lap 10K on closed city-centre roads. Starts on Riseholme Road and finishes beside Lincoln Cathedral, passing the Roman Walls and the gardens around the city. Water stations at several points, marshalled throughout.

About

The City of Lincoln 10K is one of the biggest and best-loved running events in Lincolnshire, UK. Runners of all levels will enjoy the scenic course that winds through the historic city, offering stunning views of Lincoln Cathedral and the surrounding countryside. This popular event is a must-do for any runner looking to challenge themselves in a beautiful setting.
