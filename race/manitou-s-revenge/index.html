Course

A 53-mile point-to-point across the Catskills, starting at 5 am from a private property on Big Hollow Road in Maplecrest and finishing at the Parish House in Phoenicia. The route mixes mountain trail with paved and dirt road, ending along Plank Road. Nine water and aid stops sit between miles 2 and 52, and three mandatory cutoffs are enforced.

About

Manitou's Revenge is a 53 mile ultramarathon through the Catskills beginning in Windham, N.Y. on the northern Black Dome Trail and then mostly following the Long Path from Acra Point all the way to downtown Phoenicia, N.Y. Forget what you know about 50-milers. Manitou's Revenge is a different beast — feral, unforgiving, and wholly indifferent to your pain. 53 miles and 15,000 vertical feet of jagged Catskill wilderness, you'll claw through technical climbs, stagger down rock-strewn descents, and scramble on all fours more times than you'll want to admit.
