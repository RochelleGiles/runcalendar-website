About

The Estrela Road Challenge invites runners to explore the stunning landscapes of the Estrela Mountains in Portugal. This scenic road race winds through picturesque villages and rugged terrain, offering a truly unforgettable experience for endurance athletes. Whether you're seeking a personal challenge or a chance to immerse yourself in nature, the Estrela Road Challenge promises an exhilarating and memorable running adventure.
