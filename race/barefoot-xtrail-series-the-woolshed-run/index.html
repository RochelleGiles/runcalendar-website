Course

Based at Belmont Regional Park, Wellington. Three courses of 16.36 km, 11.23 km and 5.13 km run through pine and native forest plus open, well-formed track, with climbs to viewpoints and technical descents on the Horokiwi Bridleway. No aid station detail is stated.

About

Explore the scenic trails of Belmont Regional Park at the Barefoot XTRAIL Series: The Woolshed Run. This off-road adventure challenges runners to test their limits on a course winding through the picturesque New Zealand countryside. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event offers an exhilarating way to experience the great outdoors.
