Course

Run and walk courses on the Whitehorse tracks above Waimate, using Naylors Track, Garlands Track, Point Bush Road and the Big Easy trail network. Distances run 1.5km, 5km, 13km and a 21km that climbs Whitehorse Hill for 582m of gain. Underfoot is established walking and mountain-bike track through tree-lined gullies and open vistas.

About

The Veterinary Centre Whitehorse Big Easy is a running event held in the scenic Otago region of New Zealand. The course takes runners through stunning landscapes, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants. With a focus on camaraderie and enjoying the great outdoors, this event provides a unique opportunity to connect with the local community and explore the natural beauty of the area.
