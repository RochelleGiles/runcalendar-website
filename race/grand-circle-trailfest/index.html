Course

A three-day stage race across southern Utah and northern Arizona, run point to point on a different trail each day: 15.7 miles through the Bryce Canyon hoodoos on the Thunder Mountain and Golden Wall trails, 10.4 miles of rim running from Smithsonian Butte to Wire Mesa above Zion, and 13.2 miles of sand through the Antelope Canyon slot canyons. Entirely off-road.

About

Grand Circle Trailfest is a trail running festival based in Kanab, Utah, a town often called the gateway to Utah's 'Mighty 5' national parks. Runners can choose from several trail distances through the surrounding red-rock canyon country, put on by Vacation Races.
