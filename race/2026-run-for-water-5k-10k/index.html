About

The Run for Water 5K // 10K is an annual event in Abbotsford, British Columbia, dedicated to raising funds for clean water projects in rural Ethiopian villages. The event has grown into a major community gathering, uniting runners, families and supporters for a meaningful cause, and is known for its inclusive atmosphere welcoming participants of all ages and abilities.
