About

The Bravehearts 777 Marathon Gold Coast is one leg of a seven-marathon, seven-state, seven-day series raising funds for child protection charity Bravehearts. Each leg is run as repeated laps of a measured loop course, with marathon, half marathon and shorter distance options.
