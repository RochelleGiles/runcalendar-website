Course

A 6.7km loop from Neilsons Beach Park on Fred Courtice Ave, with laps setting off on the hour, every hour. Daytime loops run on gravel trails through Barolin Reserve with some concrete and asphalt approaches. From 6pm the same path is run in the dark and a headlamp is required.

About

The Bargara by Night 12hr Backyard Ultra is a unique endurance challenge hosted by the Bargara Triathlon Club in Australia. Runners will navigate a scenic loop course under the stars, with the last person standing after 12 hours of continuous laps declared the winner. This "last one standing" event tests the limits of mental and physical resilience in a stunning coastal setting.
