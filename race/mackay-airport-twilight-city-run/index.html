Course

Out-and-back on Mackay city roads. Starts at the Whale Bones on Bluewater Quay, River Street, crosses north over the Forgan Bridge and runs along Harbour Road to a turnaround opposite BP Moss Marine, then returns the same way to finish at the Whale Bones. No aid stations are listed.

About

Escape the everyday and experience the magic of the Mackay Airport Twilight City Run. This 5km event invites runners of all abilities to explore the city streets under the glow of twilight, with the option to run, walk, jog, or even push a stroller. Discover the unique charm of this coastal Australian destination as you make your way through the lively course.
