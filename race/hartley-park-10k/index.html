About

The Hartley Park 10K is a winter trail race in the Duluth Winter Trailrunning Series, run on packed fat bike trails through Hartley Park in Duluth, Minnesota. The route showcases the park's winter topography and views, with conditions ranging from firm and icy to soft snow.
