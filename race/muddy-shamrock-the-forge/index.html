Course

A lapped trail race on the single and double-track mountain bike trails and gravel paths of The Forge and adjoining trail systems in Lemont. Underfoot is dirt, grass, rocks, mud, roots and stumps, with several short but steep climbs and descents; gravel sections are mostly flat. Half marathon runners complete two loops and quarter marathon runners one, from a common start/finish.

About

A trail half marathon and quarter marathon on the single- and double-track mountain-bike trails around the historic Lemont Quarries in Illinois. Footing varies between dirt, grass, rock and roots, with several short but steep technical climbs and descents. One aid station serves the half marathon.
