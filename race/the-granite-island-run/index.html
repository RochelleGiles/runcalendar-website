About

The Granite Island Run takes place at Victor Harbor, around an hour south of Adelaide, with 10km, 5km and a kids dash to choose from. Courses cross the causeway to Granite Island, taking in tram lines, gravel trails and short sharp climbs, with views across the south coast.
