About

The Cross De La Semine is a running event that takes place in the Haut-Rhône region, featuring various race distances. The event is known for its scenic course and the participation of dedicated runners and walkers. It is organized by the Comité des Fêtes de St GERMAIN SUR RHONE and is part of the Challenge des Trails du Haut-Rhône.
