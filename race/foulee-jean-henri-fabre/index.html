About

The Foulée Jean-Henri Fabre honours the entomologist Jean-Henri Fabre, who lived and worked at the Harmas de Fabre estate in Sérignan-du-Comtat. The race offers 12km, 6km and 1km distances through the Vaucluse countryside.
