Course

The half marathon is a USATF-certified loop on closed paved roads in Peninsula State Park, starting and finishing near Nicolet Bay Beach. It follows the Green Bay shore and climbs the Niagara Escarpment past Eagle Bluff Lighthouse, with flat early miles, steep mid-race climbs and a downhill finish. Nine aid stations serve water and sports drink. The 5K is an out-and-back on Shore Road.

About

The Door County Half Marathon and Nicolet Bay 5K take place inside Peninsula State Park near Fish Creek, Wisconsin. The half marathon is a USATF-certified loop on closed park roads along the Green Bay shoreline; the 5K is an out-and-back to Eagle Bluff Lighthouse. Both finish near Nicolet Bay Beach.
