About

The Wakefield Roaster is a popular running event in the Nelson region of New Zealand. The course takes runners through scenic countryside and offers both a 10km and 5km distance, catering to runners of all levels. The event has a friendly, community atmosphere and is a great way to explore the beautiful Wakefield area.
