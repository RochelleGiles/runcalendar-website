Course

Starts at sea level by Caernarfon Castle at 06:45 and finishes at Bron y Fedw Uchaf, Rhyd Ddu, taking in Yr Wyddfa by the Watkin Path and South Ridge. The route runs 29 miles with about 5,600 ft of ascent over coastal trail and rugged mountain ground, with a 20.5-mile option available from the second pit stop. Three pit stops support the course.

About

The Rat Race Sea to Summit 3 Peaks trilogy takes you to the summit of the high points of England, Scotland and Wales along 3 spectacular and varied ultramarathon distance routes. With routes specially and uniquely designed by Rat Race, these will not be your standard days out on these tops.
