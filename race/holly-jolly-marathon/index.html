Course

A mostly flat two-loop course on the Camarillo bike path, starting and finishing at Pleasant Valley Fields Park. The route is closed to motor traffic throughout and is USATF certified (CA18033RS). Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K are offered.

About

The Holly Jolly Marathon is a holiday-themed race weekend in Camarillo, California, offering marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances each December for runners of all paces.
