About

Run the City is an exhilarating urban running event that takes participants on a captivating tour through the heart of the city. Weaving past iconic landmarks and vibrant neighborhoods, this race offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the energy and diversity of the local community. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a casual jogger, Run the City promises an unforgettable running adventure.
