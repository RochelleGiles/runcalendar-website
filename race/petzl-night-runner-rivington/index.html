Course

A night trail race from Rivington and Blackrod High School near Bolton, run entirely by head torch on rough, boggy and rocky moorland ground that is demanding even in daylight. The route is fully marked with reflective signs. Trail or fell shoes with aggressive tread are required. Half marathon starts 17:30, 10K 17:50.

About

Petzl Night Runner Rivington is a headtorch trail race to Rivington Pike and beyond, in the hills above Bolton in Lancashire, part of the Epic Events night-running series.
