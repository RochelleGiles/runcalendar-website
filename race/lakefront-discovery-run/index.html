Course

About

The Lakefront Discovery Run in Milwaukee offers 15K and 5K distances plus a free kids run, with participants free to run, walk, skip or hop the course. Awards go to age group and overall winners, plus a 'Best Costume' prize, and finishers—costumed or not—receive free beer.
