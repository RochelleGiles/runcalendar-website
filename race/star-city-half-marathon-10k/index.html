About

The half marathon and 10K start at Rivers Edge North park in downtown Roanoke and are part of Virginia's Blue Ridge Triple Crown series alongside two other regional races. Finishers gather for live music, food vendors and complimentary beer at the after-party, and kids can join a separate fun mile.
