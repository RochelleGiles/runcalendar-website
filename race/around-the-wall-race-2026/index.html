About

The "Around The Wall Race" is a celebrated 10K road race that takes place in the vibrant city of Vancouver, BC. This annual event invites both seasoned athletes and casual runners to experience the breathtaking beauty of Stanley Park's iconic Seawall. Participants are treated to a unique blend of coastal and urban vistas, including stunning ocean views, majestic mountains, and lush green landscapes, all along a paved and accessible route.
