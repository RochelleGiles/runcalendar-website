About

The October Trail Menorca is a trail running event that takes place along the southern and southwestern part of the Camí de Cavalls on the island of Menorca. The event offers three different race distances - 32KM, 19KM, and 11KM - allowing runners of all levels to participate and enjoy the stunning coastal scenery.
