Course

A 50km course run as five legs, solo or as a 2- or 5-person relay, with every leg starting and finishing in the stadium area of the Canmore Nordic Centre in the Rockies. Total climb is about 1,595m, the legs varying sharply in difficulty. Water stations on course carry sports drink and gels.
