Course

Point-to-point on paved country roads from Perry to Berkley to Rippey in central Iowa, starting outside the Perry Grand 3 Theatres at 9:30am and finishing at the Rippey COOP. Mostly flat with gradual hills and no shelter from the weather. Support is minimal: one water station at halfway in Berkley.

About

The Perry – Berkley – Rippey (PBR) Half Marathon is an Adventure Run from the town of Perry to Berkley to Rippey. On the first Saturday of February, every year, come be one of "those" runners who wish to see if they are strong enough to take on an Iowa winter!
