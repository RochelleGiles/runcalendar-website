Course

A two-day team endurance challenge in the Grampians National Park over the King's Birthday long weekend, covering 12 to 35km each day. The route is entirely off-road, mixing single track with open four-wheel-drive tracks across hills and flats, and organisers change it each year to showcase a different part of the range. Teams of 3-8 travel together or rotate at checkpoints.

About

Tackle the rugged terrain of Australia's Grampians National Park in the annual Serra Terror team challenge. This unique event tests the endurance and teamwork of participants as they navigate the park's dramatic landscapes, with stunning scenery and a true sense of adventure around every turn. The Serra Terror is a must for any runner seeking an unforgettable challenge in one of Australia's most breathtaking natural settings.
