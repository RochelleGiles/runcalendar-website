Course

A two-hour lapped challenge on a one-mile loop at Afton, run between 8am and 10am. Entrants take on as many laps as they can in runner or rucker divisions, and may run, ruck or walk at their own pace. The winner in each division is whoever covers the most distance.

About

The Mount Afton Challenge is a two-hour lapped event at Afton Village in Concord, North Carolina, hosted by the F3 men's fitness group as a fundraiser. Entrants cover as many one-mile laps as they can, running, rucking or walking, with separate runner and rucker divisions won on distance rather than time.
