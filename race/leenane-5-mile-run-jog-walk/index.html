About

The Leenane 5 Mile Run, Jog, Walk takes place in the picturesque Irish countryside, offering a scenic route for runners, joggers, and walkers of all abilities. Hosted by the local Irish Red Cross branch, this annual event is a beloved tradition in the Leenane/Maam region, drawing participants from near and far to enjoy the beautiful natural surroundings.
