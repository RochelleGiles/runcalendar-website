Course

A loop course held entirely within Rockland Lake State Park, with start and finish in the park. The marathon is USATF certified and sanctioned, and World Athletics certified. Portable toilets are sited around the loop, with permanent restrooms near the parking lots.

About

The McKirdy Micro Races are marathon time-trial events at Rockland Lake State Park, Valley Cottage, New York, built around helping runners hit Boston Marathon and Olympic Trials qualifying standards. The 2027 running is the Breaking 3 marathon on a certified park loop.
