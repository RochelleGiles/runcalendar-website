Course

A two-day women's trail event based at the Fairy Knowe Hotel in Wilderness on the Garden Route: 15km on day one and 16km on day two, on trails through SANParks and CapeNature areas. Entry is capped at 50 and packaged with accommodation, a sunset beach walk and canoeing on the Touw River.

About

Trail Girl Wilderness is a women's trail running event held over two days in Wilderness, a Garden Route town on South Africa's southern coast known for its forests, lakes and beaches.
