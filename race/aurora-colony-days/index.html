Course

Starts and finishes at Aurora Park. The 5K heads clockwise to a turnaround and retraces its steps back to the park on sealed roads. The kids' 1K runs two laps of the park loop, away from road traffic. Both distances are chip-timed.

About

The Aurora Colony Days 5K and Kids 1K Fun Run is part of the annual Aurora Colony Days festival in historic Aurora, Oregon. It features a 5K main event and a kids' 1K fun run through scenic landmarks, suitable for runners and walkers.
