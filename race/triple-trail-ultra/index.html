About

The Triple Trail Ultra includes a 50km or 100km ultramarathon or 25km trail run through the stunning landscapes of Kent, featuring Kent’s three most iconic trails: the North Downs Way, Greensand Way, and Weald Way. The event offers rolling hills, ancient woodlands, serene orchards, and historic landmarks like Leeds Castle and the Cobham Mausoleum.
