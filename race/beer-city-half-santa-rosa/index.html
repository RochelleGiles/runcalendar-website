Course

All events start and finish at A Place to Play park in Santa Rosa. The half marathon is a figure-eight with an out-and-back on paved bike paths, gravel trail and brief road shoulders; the 10K loops the Peterson Creek and Santa Rosa Creek trails; the 5K is an out-and-back on the Patterson Creek Trail. Stated gain is under 120 ft on every course.

About

The Beer City Half™ Santa Rosa is a running event featuring half marathon, 10k, 5k distances, and a 1-mile Beer Stroll, starting at A Place to Play around Santa Rosa Creek. It includes post-race beer tasting from local brewers, a finisher's medal, shirt, and custom tasting glass, followed by a festival with games and entertainment.
