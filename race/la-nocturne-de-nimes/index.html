About

La Nocturne de Nîmes is a charitable running event that takes place in the historic city of Nîmes. Participants run through beautifully illuminated gardens, places, and monuments, creating a magical nighttime atmosphere. The event is organized by the Lions Club Nîmes Alphonse Daudet and aims to support and raise awareness for people with visual impairments.
