About

Lokmat Maha Marathon Thane is the Thane leg of the multi-city Maha Marathon series run by the Lokmat media group across Maharashtra, alongside sister editions in Kolhapur, Nashik and other cities. It offers 21K, 10K, 5K and 3K distances through Thane.
