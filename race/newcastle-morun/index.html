Course

A lapped, flat course in Exhibition Park, Newcastle, on a mix of path and tarmac. The 5k is one lap, the 10k two, and the half marathon an initial 1.1-mile loop followed by four full laps. Dog friendly and fine for buggies, though some sections are bumpy and it is not suitable for wheelchairs. A 1.5k Mini MoRun runs for children alongside.
