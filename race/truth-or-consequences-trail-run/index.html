Course

Lapped trail course starting and finishing inside Serpentine National Park, marked with flagging tape and course arrows. Colour-graded options run one lap (Green, Blue, Black) or two for the Double Black, which carries support stations at roughly 8.6km, 18.6km, 25km, 33.6km and 43.6km. Free parking with a shuttle from Karnup Road.

About

The Truth or Consequences Trail Run offers a challenging trail running experience through Serpentine Falls National Park. The course features diverse terrain including steeps, flats, single track, fire trail, ruts, and pea gravel, providing a comprehensive trail running challenge.
