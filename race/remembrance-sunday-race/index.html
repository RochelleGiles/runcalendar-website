Course

A traffic-free, undulating looped trail course starting and finishing outside the Batemans Tower Cafe on Promenade Way, Brightlingsea. Each lap is 10.54km and runners complete as many as they wish within six hours, so half marathon, marathon and ultra distances are all possible. Aid at the end of every lap and at around 5km.

About

The Remembrance Sunday Race in Brightlingsea, Essex offers runners a variety of distances to choose from, including a 10km, Half Marathon, Marathon, and 6-Hour trail event. Set against the picturesque backdrop of the United Kingdom, this event provides a unique opportunity to honor those who have served while challenging yourself on the scenic courses. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Remembrance Sunday Race has something for everyone.
