Course

Start and finish at the Massey University Recreation Centre, Palmerston North. Courses use the He Ara Kotahi pathway and the riverside walkway, with crossings of the He Ara Kotahi pedestrian bridge and Fitzherbert Bridge; most distances include out-and-back sections. Marked drink stations and toilets are placed along each course.

About

The Manawatu Striders Marathon and Half Marathon in Palmerston North, New Zealand offer a scenic, challenging course through the Manawatu region. Runners can choose between the full 42.2km marathon or the 21.1km half marathon, with both events starting and finishing on the Massey University campus. This popular annual event is a must for endurance athletes looking to conquer the hills and landscapes of the Manawatu.
