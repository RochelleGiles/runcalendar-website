About

Lace up your sneakers and join the Memory Walk & Jog in the heart of Melbourne's iconic Albert Park. This scenic morning event combines fitness, fun, and fundraising, offering participants a chance to support a meaningful cause while enjoying a scenic route through the picturesque park. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a casual jogger, this event promises an unforgettable experience for the whole community.
