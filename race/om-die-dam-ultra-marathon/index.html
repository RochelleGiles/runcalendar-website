Course

Based at Hartbeespoort High School in North West province, with the route looping around Hartbeespoort Dam. The 50 km ultra starts at 06:00 and the 21 km half marathon at 06:00 from R104 NECSA Gate 3, with the 8 km fun run at 06:45. Entries for the 50 km and 21 km are online only, with no late entry at registration.

About

Om Die Dam Ultra Marathon loops around Hartbeespoort Dam in North West province and is a popular Comrades Marathon qualifier. The 50km ultra, half marathon and 8km options draw runners preparing for the Comrades ultra later in the year.
