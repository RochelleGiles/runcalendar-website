About

Donuts and Running - I vote yes! eTown in Jacksonville, Rad Ringos Gourmet Mini Donuts and Florida Race Day are excited for this sweet race. Join us March 21st the 8:00 am start of the Annual Donut Run. The races takes place in the awesome new neighborhood of eTown which is close to 9B and 1-95.
