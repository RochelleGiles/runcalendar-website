Course

The 10K starts and finishes at The Peak, Stirling Sports Village, on the club's traditional fast route with views of Stirling Castle, the Wallace Monument and the Ochil Hills. Fun runs of 2K and 3.5K follow sections of the same route.

About

The Podfather Stirling 10K is a road race through the streets of Stirling, Scotland, organised by Central Athletic Club, with 3.5K and 2K fun runs held alongside the main 10K distance.
