Course

Lap-based courses inside Albany Lakes Civic Park, Auckland. The 5 km, 10 km and 15 km are built from repeats of a 5 km circuit taking in the upper lake, with an Art Bridge crossing used as the alternate route to the finish. Turn-by-turn course detail is not yet published.
