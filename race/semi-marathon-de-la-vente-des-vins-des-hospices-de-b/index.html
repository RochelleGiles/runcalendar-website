About

Fort of its previous editions, the Beaune wine sale half marathon has become over time a sporting event and a must-attend event for many regional, national and international runners. Each year, more than 5,000 participants, mainly from the Bourgogne, Franche-Comté, Île de France and Rhône Alpes regions, take to the courses of the Half Marathon (21.1 km) and the Foulées (10.4 km).
