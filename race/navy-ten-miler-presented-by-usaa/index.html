Course

Out-and-back along the Chula Vista Bayfront, starting and finishing at Bayside Park, with a loop of Bayfront Park and a turnaround near Main Street, passing the San Diego Bay National Wildlife Refuge. Runners must finish the ten miles within 2 hours 45 minutes.

About

The Navy Ten-Miler-Presented by USAA brings together athletes, service members, veterans, and civilians to honor the Navy's core values of Honor, Courage, and Commitment. Whether you're a seasoned runner or searching for your next challenge, this is your call to RUN NAVY.
