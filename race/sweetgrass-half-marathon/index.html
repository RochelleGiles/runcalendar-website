About

The Mount Pleasant Recreation Department hosts the Sweetgrass Half Marathon and 10K, now past its sixth running. The flat, fast course starts at the Mount Pleasant Recreation Complex in South Carolina and runs through the Carolina Park neighborhood, with marsh and pond views along the way.
