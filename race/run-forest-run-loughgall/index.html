Course

Run through Loughgall Country Park in County Armagh on forest paths around the estate, passing the golf course and the lake. The surface is a mixture of tarmac and gravel paths. Start and finish are beside the main Golf Reception, with the 10K off at 11:00am and the 5K shortly after. No water stations are provided on course.

About

Run through the stunning forests of Loughgall, Ireland at the annual Run Forest Run event. Immerse yourself in the serene natural surroundings as you tackle this challenging trail run, perfect for those seeking an adventurous winter workout. Discover hidden paths and scenic vistas that make this one of Northern Ireland's most popular off-road running experiences.
