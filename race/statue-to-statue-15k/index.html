Course

A point-to-point road 15K starting near the Vulcan statue and finishing close to the Statue of Liberty, winding through the neighbourhoods of Mountain Brook. The first six miles run gently downhill before two hard climbs, then a fast mile and a half of descent to the line.

About

A 15K road race organized by the Birmingham Track Club through the hilly streets of Mountain Brook, Alabama, held each April. It's known among local runners as one of the area's more challenging road courses.
