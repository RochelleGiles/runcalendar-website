About

Our annual run/walk/roll event is fun for the whole family–whether you are a walker or a runner. The run/walk/roll celebrates the determination and successes of the teen mothers at Florence Crittenton Services. Miles for Moms is a nearly 3-mile run that starts at FloCrit's campus and goes through Huston Lake Park. Participants have the opportunity to fundraise as individuals and create fundraising teams to raise money to support the teen mothers and their children at Florence Crittenton Services.
