Course

A 5k through the fields and woodland of Stanmer Park on cross-country terrain with steep inclines. The surface is a mix of tarmac, grass and gravel. The start and finish sit on grass that is flat to slightly sloped and firm underfoot, though it can turn soft or muddy in wet weather. Not wheelchair accessible.

About

Race for Life's 5K runs through Stanmer Park, parkland at the foot of the South Downs near Brighton, home to Stanmer House. It's a non-competitive run, jog or walk raising funds for cancer research.
