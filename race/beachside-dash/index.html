Course

A certified coastal course starting at Dolls Point on Botany Bay, linking the St George area to the Sutherland Shire. The longer distances cross the Captain Cook Bridge and turn at Shark Park before returning. Sealed foreshore path and road, flat apart from the bridge approach.

About

The iconic Beachside Dash returns to the picturesque shores of Dolls Point Beach in Sydney, offering runners a unique opportunity to dash along the scenic coastline. This beloved event features a range of distances, from the challenging Half Marathon to the family-friendly 2km, making it an ideal choice for runners of all levels.
