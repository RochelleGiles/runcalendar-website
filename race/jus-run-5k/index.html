Course

Starts and finishes behind the PKWY Tavern on West Flamingo Road in Las Vegas, running northwest along the Western Beltway Trail beside the 215. From 2,710 ft the course climbs about 100 ft per mile to the turnaround, then returns downhill. Strollers and dogs are permitted.

About

Jus Run 5K is an annual 5K and 1 Mile community run/walk in Las Vegas, Nevada, hosted by Jusrun to build community and support local outreach programs.
