Course

A flat, fully closed-road 10K on Littlehampton's seafront, starting and finishing on The Promenade. The route passes the town's colourful beach huts, its award-winning architecture and the UK's longest bench, with a water station at 5.2km. Headphones are permitted. A separate Family Mile runs alongside.

About

The Littlehampton 10K is a community event that has been running for over 20 years. This speedy and flat 10km route passes through residential Littlehampton, Rustington, and Mewsbrook Park, attracting a variety of participants from corporate teams and running clubs to individuals and groups.
