Course

A two-mile looped out-and-back along the shore of Lake Washington in Magnuson Park, Seattle, starting at 8am just north of the boat ramp across the wood foot bridge. Held on the first Saturday of every month in all weather. Entry is free and taken at the start line, with no advance registration.
