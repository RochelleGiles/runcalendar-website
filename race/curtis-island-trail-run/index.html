About

The Curtis Island Trail Run is a scenic running event that takes place on the beautiful Curtis Island in Australia. The course highlights the island's natural beauty, with runners traversing through lush forests, along coastal trails, and past stunning vistas. The event offers a unique and challenging experience for runners of all levels, creating a memorable atmosphere for participants.
