About

The Caramulo 100 is a challenging and scenic ultra-running event set in the picturesque Caramulo mountains of Portugal. Runners will be treated to stunning vistas and a demanding course that winds through lush forests and quaint villages, making this a must-do event for any trail enthusiast. With a rich history and a reputation for exceptional organization, the Caramulo 100 is sure to leave a lasting impression on all who participate.
