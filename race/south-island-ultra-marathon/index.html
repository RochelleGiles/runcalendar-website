Course

Point-to-point along the West Coast Wilderness Trail, a Grade 2 mountain bike trail passing lakes, reservoirs, rivers, water races and swing bridges. All four events finish on the Hokitika beachfront. The 100 km starts at Greymouth, the 54 km at Old Christchurch Road, the 24 km at Lake Kaniere and the 6 km at Kaniere township. Assembly is at the Beachfront Hotel, Hokitika.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of New Zealand's South Island at the South Island Ultra Marathon. This challenging event takes runners through the stunning West Coast Wilderness Trail, offering breathtaking coastal vistas and lush native forest landscapes. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a new adventure, the South Island Ultra Marathon promises an unforgettable experience in one of the world's most picturesque natural settings.
