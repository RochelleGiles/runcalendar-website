Course

Races start and finish at Natural Chimneys Park in the Shenandoah Valley. The 100M is a 167.4 km loop with 6,400 m of climbing on singletrack, gravel and forest roads over Wolf Ridge, Grindstone Mountain and Elliott's Knob; the 100K, 50K and 21K use Narrow Back Mountain ridgelines. The 100M starts Friday 6:00pm; shorter races run Saturday morning.

About

A 100-mile mountain ultramarathon through Virginia's Allegheny Mountains, starting and finishing at Camp Shenandoah. The course follows the Great North Mountain ridgeline and Wild Oak Trail, including a steep climb to Elliott Knob. Shorter 50K, 100K and 21K distances are also offered.
