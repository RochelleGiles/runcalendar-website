About

Trail des Roches takes place in Poncin, in the Ain department of Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, organized by the Raid Fémin'Ain association. Three distances are on offer - 10, 21 and 31 km - crossing a mix of woodland and open countryside outside the town. The 10 km route climbs around 280 m.
