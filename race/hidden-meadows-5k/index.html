Course

Evening 5k loop on the Corner Canyon trail system, linking the Hoof N Boot, Pork U Pine and Hidden Meadows trails. The route covers about 3.3 miles with roughly 457 ft of climb on dirt singletrack, starting and finishing at the pavilion on the east side of the Peak View Trailhead car park. No dogs are permitted.

About

Hidden Meadows is a great trail system that uses Hoof N Boot, Pork U pine, and the Hidden Meadows trail system to make a terrific loop. You will be rewarded to great views of the mountains and both Utah and Salt Lake Counties.
