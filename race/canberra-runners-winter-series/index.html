About

The Canberra Runners Stromlo Forest Park race is part of the club's Winter Series, with 2km, 4km, 6km and 8km distances. The Winter Series runs on Saturdays from late April until mid-September, with races over 2km, 3-5km and 6-10km on bike paths, cross country and dirt roads.
