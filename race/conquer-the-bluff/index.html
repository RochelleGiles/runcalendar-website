Course

An out-and-back climb starting and finishing on a vacant lot on Eagle Bluff Road behind the Eagle Gas and Food Market. Runners head toward the mountain on paved road, turn onto a gravel road with switchbacks leading to a trail summit, then return downhill by the same route. Total gain is about 930 feet. No aid station detail is given.

About

Conquer the Bluff is a 4.1 mile out-and-back climb at Jacksboro, Tennessee, gaining about 930 feet to a summit above Eagle Bluff Road. The route leaves pavement for a switchbacking gravel road and trail before returning downhill on the same line.
