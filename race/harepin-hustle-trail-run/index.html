Course

Lollipop course at Lake Crabtree County Park, with a shared out-and-back stem of about 2.1 miles leading into the trail-network loops. Start and finish on the lakeside lawn. Forested single-track with roots and rocks. Aid stations every one to two miles.

About

The 3rd Annual Harepin Hustle Trail Run returns this spring with newly routed 10K+ (7.3 miles) and Half Marathon courses that start and finish at Lake Crabtree and wind through the trail network of Wake County's newly acquired 286 property. While much of the course is different from years past, the spirit of the race remains the same — a challenging, fun, and engaging day on the trails.
