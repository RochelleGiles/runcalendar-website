About

The Elm Street Athletic Festival features a 10-mile race, 5K, 1-mile youth run, and a mile walk for brain tumor survivors, healers, heroes, and families along Greensboro's historic Elm Street and central boulevards. It supports brain cancer patients by raising funds for the Brain Tumor Network and Aware Coaching.
