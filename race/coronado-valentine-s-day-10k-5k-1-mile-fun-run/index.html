Course

Starts and finishes at Tidelands Park, Coronado, on a flat course running along San Diego Bay and past the island's historic homes. Three hydration points sit at miles 1.4, 3.5 and 4.7, each with water and electrolyte drink. The 1 Mile starts at 7:30 AM and the 5K and 10K at 8:00 AM.

About

The Coronado Valentine's Day races are held each February at Tidelands Park on Coronado island, San Diego. Runners choose a 10K, a 5K or a 1 Mile fun run on a flat bayside course, with couples entries available. The event is timed for the morning of Super Bowl Sunday.
