Course

Self-guided 13 mile run clockwise around the Letchworth Garden City Greenway, starting and finishing at Standalone Farm. Runners navigate from supplied route maps, instructions and a GPX file. There are road crossings with marshals who direct but cannot stop traffic, and a three hour limit.

About

The Greenway Challenge invites runners to explore the stunning Garden City Greenway in Letchworth, United Kingdom. Covering approximately 13 miles in a clockwise direction, this scenic event offers a unique opportunity to experience the area's natural beauty and tranquility. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Greenway Challenge promises an unforgettable journey through the heart of the countryside.
