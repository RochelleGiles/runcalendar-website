Course

A 14-mile road loop around Coniston Water in the Lake District, starting in Coniston village at 11am and passing Bowmanstead, Brantwood and Brown Howe. Run entirely on road and organised by a volunteer committee from the village, it draws around 1,600 runners.

About

The Coniston 14 is a road race around Coniston Water in the Lake District, taking in the quiet lanes and villages that ring one of Cumbria's most famous lakes, with the Old Man of Coniston rising above the course.
