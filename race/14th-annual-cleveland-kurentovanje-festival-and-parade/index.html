About

The Kurent Dash 5K opens Cleveland Kurentovanje, a Slovenian carnival festival held in the city's St. Clair-Superior neighbourhood. Runners set off at 10am from the Slovenian National Home on St. Clair Avenue, with registration and packet pick-up from 9am. The festival parade and performances follow the race through the afternoon.
