Course

A three-day mountain festival on Kunanyi / Mt Wellington above Hobart, running 19-21 March. The Vertical Kilometre opens on Friday, the long events (84km, 66km solo and relay, 46km Alpine Marathon and 25km Mountain Run/Walk) run Saturday, and the Foothills 5km/9km/14km and kids races close it on Sunday. The 84km climbs and descends close to 5,000m.

About

Kunanyi Mountain Run is a three-day trail festival on Kunanyi/Mt Wellington above Hobart, now past its 5th running. The Palawa, Tasmania's Aboriginal people, welcome trail runners to the mountain, with events suited to seasoned ultra runners and newcomers alike.
