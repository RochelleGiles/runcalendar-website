Course

A trail 50K and 25K starting and finishing at 3058 State Route 3 South near Loudonville, in and around Mohican State Park. The organiser warns of many hills and over 4,800 feet of climb, with a river crossing on route and several aid stations; the Covered Bridge is the last aid point with five miles to go.

About

Trail race held in Mohican State Park with hilly terrain, river crossings, and loops. The 50k starts at 7:30 AM and 25k at 9:00 AM, offering buckles, shirts, and post-race meals.
