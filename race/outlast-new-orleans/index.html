Course

A six-hour last-one-standing format at Fontainebleau State Park in Mandeville. Runners cover a 5K trail loop every hour, on the hour, and must finish inside the hour to start the next lap. The loop is rugged natural surface with dirt, rocks, roots and elevation change, plus occasional gravel and pavement. Water and snacks sit at basecamp; the event is cupless and runners may set up their own tent between laps.

About

OUTLAST New Orleans is held at Fontainebleau State Park in Mandeville, Louisiana. Runners rack up laps on wooded trails within a set time limit, testing endurance and pacing rather than racing a fixed distance.
