About

5inco De Miles takes place at Chula Vista Bayfront Park on the shore of San Diego Bay, with a 5K, Masters Mile, Kids' 1K Fun Run, and the Milla de Oro one-mile race as part of a Cinco de Mayo weekend celebration.
