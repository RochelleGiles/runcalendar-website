Course

The marathon and 10K start on Lake Street outside Newfound Memorial Middle School in Bristol and finish in Kelley Park. The 10K heads north on Route 3A to a turnaround and back. The marathon runs out on Route 3A to North Shore Road at 8.3 miles, through Hebron village to a turnaround past 13 miles, then back along the west shore of Newfound Lake to the Kelley Park finish.

About

The New Hampshire Marathon starts and finishes in Bristol and follows roads around Newfound Lake in the state's Lakes Region. Runners can choose the marathon, half marathon or 10K, all sharing the same lakeside course.
