Course

Held in Big Spring State Park, which sits on a bluff about 200 feet above the surrounding plain, with 160 to 200 feet of elevation change per loop. Trail distances run counter-clockwise loops and the paved-road distances run clockwise. Aid stations sit at the 1.25-mile road checkpoint and the 2-mile trail checkpoint, with the main support at the start and finish.

About

The Together Trail Race is held in Big Spring State Park, Texas, over Valentine's weekend, and offers both trail and paved-road distances on looped courses. Entries support Tate's Place, a local charity. Runners, joggers, hikers and walkers are all catered for.
