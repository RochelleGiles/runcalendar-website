Course

A flat, fast road course through Launceston, promoted by organisers as Australia's quickest. The half marathon carries a World Athletics Elite Label, the only Australian event to hold one, and is joined by 10km, 5km and one-mile races sharing the same festival precinct.

About

The McGrath Launceston Running Festival is one of Tasmania's premiere running events. The festival offers races for all ages and abilities, featuring a fast, flat road course with multiple distance options.
