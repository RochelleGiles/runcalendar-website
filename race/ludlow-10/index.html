About

The Ludlow 10 is a 10km mixed terrain race set in and around the picturesque town of Ludlow, Shropshire. The routes offer sections of quiet lanes, public footpaths and off road sections, including the Whitcliffe Common area with undulating ground, interesting climbs and descents, and a challenging ascent of the Donkey Steps.
