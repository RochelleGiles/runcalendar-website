About

The Festitrail is the sporting event organized as part of the Autrans International Festival - Mountain, Cinema & Culture! At the heart of a major cultural event in the mountain world, this race is the first white trail of the season if the snow is there! With or without the white carpet, it's a special atmosphere in an exceptional setting. Many cultural proposals are organized in parallel with the sporting event. You will have the opportunity to explore the magnificent trails of the Vercors on a course of about 20km, for 630 meters of elevation gain individually or in relay. And as every year, we don't forget anyone, with the children's courses.
