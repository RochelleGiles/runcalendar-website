About

Unleash the adventure at the 34th Annual Furry Skurry 5K – a paw-some day of heroic fun alongside your four-legged companions! Join us in a race that'll have tails wagging and spirits soaring, as you and your furry sidekicks dash through the trails at St. Leonard in Centerville. But that's not all – brace yourselves for a doggone good time with a marketplace tailor-made for both you and your pets. Our food trucks are ready to serve up delicious bites that will have you drooling (and maybe even your pups too). From pup-ular games to activities that'll have tails wagging, this day promises to be filled with thrills and spills, all while celebrating the incredible bond between you and your pets.
