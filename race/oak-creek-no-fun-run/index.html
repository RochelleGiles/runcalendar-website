About

This 5K run/walk takes participants through the streets of Oak Creek, looping through scenic Oak Creek Mountain Park, and finishes downtown just before the annual Labor Day Parade. Proceeds benefit the Alzheimer’s Association of Colorado in memory of long-time competitor Larry Brooks.
