About

Lace up your running shoes and join the Laufsporttag Kempten, a beloved annual running event in the heart of Kempten. Runners of all levels will enjoy the scenic route through the picturesque Allgäu region, known for its stunning mountain landscapes. Whether you're chasing a new personal best or simply soaking in the vibrant community atmosphere, this event offers an unforgettable running experience.
