About

The Cairns Port Douglas Trail Ultra takes runners on a breathtaking journey through the lush, tropical landscapes of northern Queensland. Winding through dense rainforests and along the stunning coastline, this challenging ultra-marathon offers a unique and unforgettable experience for endurance athletes. With stunning natural scenery and a supportive community of fellow trail runners, the Cairns Port Douglas Trail Ultra is a must-do event for those seeking an adventure-filled running experience.
