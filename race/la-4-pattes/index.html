About

La 4 Pattes is a running event in pairs, where each duo must cross the finish line together. Two adult courses are offered, 6KM or 12KM through hiking trails, roads, riverbanks, and forests. And two parent-child courses of 1KM or 2KM depending on the age to experience it as a family. The whole team welcomes you at the starting village of Les Frigos in Metz (Porte des Allemands) where you can enjoy a guinguette atmosphere and lively stands. La 4 Pattes donates 10% of each registration to a charity.
