About

Escape to the snow-capped peaks of the Semnoz mountain range for the exhilarating Le Trail Blanc Du Semnoz. This scenic trail race takes runners on a breathtaking journey through the pristine winter landscapes of the French Alps, offering a unique challenge for endurance athletes. Prepare to be captivated by the stunning vistas and the thrill of conquering this alpine adventure.
