Course

All distances start on Marktplatz Square in the middle of Fredericksburg. The half marathon runs one large loop out onto Crabapple Road, rolling hills with long Hill Country views, then back to the square; it is USATF certified. The 10K and 5K set off from the same start on town roads.

About

The Wanderlust Half Marathon, 10K and 5K are part of ScallyWompus's Texas Hill Country Series, run through the countryside around Fredericksburg, Texas, a town in the heart of Texas wine country known for its rolling hills and vineyards.
