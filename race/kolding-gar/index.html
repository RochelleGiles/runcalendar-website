Course

About

Kolding Half is a half marathon through the centre of Kolding in southern Jutland, with 10 km, 5 km and one-mile races alongside it. The route takes in Marielundskoven, the Slotssøen lake and views of Koldinghus castle over gently rolling ground, finishing above Kolding Fjord.
