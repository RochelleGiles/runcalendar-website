Course

A 10km trail run, walk or slog starting and finishing at Birch Grove School on the west side of Tofte. Run on the Sugarbush Trail Association's wilderness trail system, where footing can be wet and muddy. Kids' sprints and a youth mile are also held.

About

The Tofte Trek is an annual 10K run/walk event held on July 4th in Tofte, Minnesota. The event also includes kids sprint races and a 1-mile youth race.
