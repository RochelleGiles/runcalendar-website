Course

Point-to-point half marathon on rural Cheshire roads through four villages, starting at Helsby Community Sports Club and finishing at Hornsmill Primary School, a five-minute walk from race HQ. UKA certified, with water stations at miles 5, 8 and 10 and at the finish; no gels on course. Strict three-hour cut-off.

About

The Four Villages Half Marathon offers runners a scenic tour through the picturesque Cheshire countryside, winding through the charming villages of Dunham on the Hill, Mouldsworth, Manley, Alvanley, and Helsby. This scenic road race provides a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels, showcasing the natural beauty of the United Kingdom's rural landscapes.
