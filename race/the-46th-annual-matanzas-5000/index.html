About

The Matanzas 5000 is Ancient City Road Runners' long-running 5K through historic St. Augustine, Florida, billed as one of the fastest courses in the state. The flat USATF-certified route starts and finishes at Francis Field, running the bayfront and downtown streets past Flagler College.
