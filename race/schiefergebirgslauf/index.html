About

The Schiefergebirgslauf is a captivating running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Schiefergebirge region. Runners will traverse a picturesque route through rolling hills and charming villages, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants of all levels. This annual race is a must-attend for runners seeking a unique and scenic adventure.
