Course

Starts at the Woodlands Center at Camp Agapé, running down a gravel road to the Gerhard Cabin and a loop of the grassy field there, then back up a steep pitch on the same gravel toward the campground loop and Kidd Field. The gravel turns to pavement through the camp entrance gates and the closing stretch is mostly downhill, with gravel to the finish. Aid stations are not stated.

About

Race for ACE! is a 5K run and walk held at Camp Agapé near Fuquay-Varina, North Carolina, on a mixed gravel and paved course through the camp grounds. It raises funds for the Agapé Center for Environmental Education, and a virtual option and basket raffle run alongside the live race.
