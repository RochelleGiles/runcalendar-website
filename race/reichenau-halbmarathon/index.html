About

The Reichenau Halbmarathon is a stunning half-marathon event set against the picturesque backdrop of an idyllic German island. Runners can look forward to a scenic course winding through charming villages and along the shores of Lake Constance, offering breathtaking views at every turn. This popular race is a must for any runner seeking a truly memorable and scenic racing experience.
