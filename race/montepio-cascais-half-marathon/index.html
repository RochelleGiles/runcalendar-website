About

The Montepio Cascais Half Marathon, powered by Montepio Associação Mutualista, is organized by HMS Sports in partnership with the Cascais Municipal Chamber and the CCD Cascais. The event is held on Cascais Bay, with a Half Marathon (21.1 km), 10K, 5K and Children's Race.
