Course

All distances use an out-and-back at Cousler Park, York, repeated to make up the distance. The 5K and 10K use a 0.78-mile base leg, or 1.56 miles out and back, covered twice for the 5K and four times for the 10K. The half marathon uses a 1.09-mile base leg covered six times. All events start at 8:00 AM.

About

The Viking 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is a themed running event held at Cousler Park in York, PA, organized by US Road Running. It features out-and-back courses with loops: 5K (two loops), 10K (four loops), and Half Marathon (six loops of 1.09 miles each), starting at 8:00 AM with a 3.5-hour time limit.
