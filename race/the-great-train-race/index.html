About

The Great Train Race in Devonport, Tasmania is an exciting running event for the whole family. With distances ranging from a 400m dash to a challenging 7km course, this unique event allows runners of all ages and abilities to experience the thrill of racing along the historic train tracks. Whether you're looking for a fun day out or a competitive challenge, the Great Train Race has something for everyone.
