Course

A scenic, fully paved course winding across the grounds of The Greenbrier resort in White Sulphur Springs, offered as a half marathon, 10K and 5K. The half has a 3.5-hour limit and the course is wheelchair and handcycle accessible.

About

The Greenbrier Half Marathon takes place in White Sulphur Springs, West Virginia, home to the historic Greenbrier resort in the Allegheny Mountains. The event also offers 10K and 5K distances through the surrounding resort grounds and town.
