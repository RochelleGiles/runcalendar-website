Course

The half marathon is point-to-point, starting on Highway 177 near I-70 and running the northbound lane into Manhattan. From there it passes through downtown, around City Park and through Aggieville onto the K-State campus, finishing at Bill Snyder Family Stadium. The 5K starts and finishes at the stadium.

About

Come run with us for the 12th Annual Bill Snyder Highway Half and 5K presented by Orthopedic and Sports Medicine Center! Enjoy Manhattan's premiere half marathon. As we do with all our races, we strive to provide a memorable experience for all participants while raising money for a great cause.
