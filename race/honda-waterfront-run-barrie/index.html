About

The Honda Waterfront Run follows the shoreline of Kempenfelt Bay on Lake Simcoe in Barrie, Ontario, with half marathon and 5K distances along the city's waterfront trail each June, drawing a mix of first-time half marathoners and seasoned local runners.
