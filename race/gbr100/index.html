About

The GBR100 is Australia's newest 100km trail ultra, taking runners through the stunning landscapes of Far North Queensland. Traversing a remote and challenging course, this event offers an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners seeking to conquer the rugged beauty of the Australian outback.
