Course

Loop courses starting and finishing at Colossal Cave Mountain Park, using sections of the Arizona National Scenic Trail near Vail, 24 miles southeast of Tucson. The trail is well built and rolling, high desert running with wide-open views, and was laid out by Tucson Trail Runners as a relatively fast trail ultra. Aid stations are well stocked and the event is cupless.
