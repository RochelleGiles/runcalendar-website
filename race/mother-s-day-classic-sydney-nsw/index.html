Course

Start and finish in The Domain, Royal Botanic Garden Sydney, a 34-hectare heritage-listed open space about five minutes from the CBD. The course passes the Art Gallery of New South Wales, the Andrew Boyd Charlton pool and Mrs Macquarie's Chair. Drink stations are on the route and at the finish, with water refill in the event village.

About

The Mother's Day Classic is a national run and walk held each Mother's Day to raise funds for breast and ovarian cancer research. The Sydney event is one of the largest in the series, drawing thousands of walkers and runners across its short course options.
