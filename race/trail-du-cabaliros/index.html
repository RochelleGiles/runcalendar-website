About

The Trail du Cabaliros is a mountain trail race in the French Pyrenees, covering 22 kilometres of ascents and descents around Cauterets. Runners tackle rugged, high-altitude terrain with sweeping views across the surrounding peaks, a demanding test of endurance and technical trail running.
