Course

A point-to-point 100-miler along the Ozark Highlands Trail through the Boston Mountain segment of northwestern Arkansas, starting at Haw Creek Falls Recreation Area. The trail is rocky and steep through hardwood, cedar and pine forest, with creek crossings, bluffs and sandstone outcrops. Multiple aid stations, the longest gap about 12 miles. 38-hour limit.

About

Hare Mountain 100 is an ultramarathon held in the Ozark Mountains near Hagarville, Arkansas, offering 100-mile, 50-mile, 50K, and 25K distances on rugged, remote mountain trail through the surrounding national forest.
