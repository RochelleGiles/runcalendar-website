Course

Start and finish at Waterfront Park TurfMutt Great Lawn South, 231 E Witherspoon St, Louisville. The route passes Historic Main Street, Waterfront Park, Louisville Slugger Field and the Yum Center. The 5K starts at 7:30am and the 10K and 15K at 8:00am. The published course map marks the start and finish, the splits and the aid stations.
