Course

The course crosses the LSU campus by way of the LSU Lakes, Sorority Row, Dalrymple Drive, the LSU Law Center, the Student Union and Parade Grounds, the Bell Tower, the Campus Quad and the Indian Mounds. Runners come down Field House Drive and into Tiger Stadium to finish on the 50-yard line under the WIN bar.

About

The Our Lady of the Lake Tiger 10K runs through the LSU campus in Baton Rouge, taking in the LSU Lakes, Sorority Row, the Bell Tower and the Parade Grounds. A 5K and a kids mile for ages 3 to 12 are held alongside the 10K.
