A series of five evening trail races in Forest Park, Portland, run on alternate Wednesdays with a different 4-7 mile course each time. Races start at 6:15pm with bib pickup from 5:30pm. Route maps go up the Monday before each race, and one round each series is a Mystery Route where the trails stay secret until race day.

About

The Portland Trail Series - Spring is a low-key series of five evening trail running races held every other Wednesday in Forest Park, Portland. Each race covers a different challenging course ranging from 4-7 miles, starting at 6:15 p.m., with an awards party after the final race.
