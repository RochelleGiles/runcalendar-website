About

The Dallas Marathon is one of Texas' longest-running marathons, passing downtown Dallas and White Rock Lake on its way to the finish. The December race weekend also includes half marathon, 10K, 5K and 50K ultra options as part of the BMW Dallas Marathon Festival.
