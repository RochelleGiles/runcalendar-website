Course

Point-to-point along the Thames Path from Abingdon Road, Oxford to Henley-on-Thames, following the river bank for almost the whole route. Runners pass Sandford Weir, Culham, Cholsey, Streatley, Whitchurch-on-Thames and Reading. Four checkpoints at roughly 12 to 13 mile intervals supply water, sandwiches, sweets and dried fruit.

About

The MTC Oxford Ultra is a challenging 49-mile ultramarathon along the picturesque Thames Path, taking runners from the historic city of Oxford to the charming town of Henley-on-Thames. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the English countryside as you push your limits on this scenic and rewarding ultra-distance event.
