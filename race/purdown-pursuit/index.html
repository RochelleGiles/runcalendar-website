Course

A looping 10km through parkland and woodland at Purdown, with race headquarters at Countess Walk, Stapleton, Bristol. The organisers describe the route as carrying plenty of short, sharp hills. It is run on a summer evening with a 19:15 start.

About

The Purdown Pursuit is a 10km looping race through parkland and woodland in Bristol, featuring lots of challenging hills. It is a popular off-road event organized by the Town and Country Harriers running club, known for their welcoming and inclusive atmosphere.
