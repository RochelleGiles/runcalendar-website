About

The Ninja 5K, 10K & Half Marathon is an exciting race event held at Halyburton Park in Wilmington, NC, featuring scenic out-and-back courses for runners of all levels. Participants can earn finisher medals with a 3.5-hour time limit for the half marathon starting at 8:00 AM.
