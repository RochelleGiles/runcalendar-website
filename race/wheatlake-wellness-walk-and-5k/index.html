Course

The 5K starts and finishes at the Big Rapids Bandshell behind Big Rapids Middle School, heading north along the Riverwalk trail before circling back. The route is primarily flat and wheelchair accessible, with strollers welcome. Runners start at 9:30 a.m. and walkers follow immediately after.

About

The Wheatlake Wellness Walk and 5K, presented by Corewell Health Foundation West Michigan, raises funds in the fight against cancer. It offers both in-person and virtual participation options.
