About

The Ultra Trail des Sources (UTDS®) is an ultra-endurance running event organized on a permanent marked course located in the Ardennes, in eastern Belgium (Europe). Covering 80 KM and 2350 m+, this event connects the municipalities of Spa, Theux, Stoumont & Trois-Ponts. Dedicated to athletes who want to take on an 'ultra trail', it is also intended for trail runners who wish to cover this course, in relay, in teams of 2 to 4 runners. An exceptional experience to discover all the attractions of a raw and authentic nature and immerse yourself in a preserved natural environment, in the heart of the magnificent Pays des Sources...
