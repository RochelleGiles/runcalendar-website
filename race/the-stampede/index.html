About

Welcome to one of the ONLY trail races within Austin city limits. Embark on a challenging adventure through a network of well-traveled trails, each filled with twists, turns, creeks, and demanding climbs. This is the 1st race of Austin's iconic Rogue Trail Series. Choose between the 10K, 20k, & 30K distance options to test your limits and immerse yourself in the beauty of the natural surroundings.
