Course

A figure-of-eight trail loop of about 10 miles and 1,000ft of climb at Monte Sano State Park, run once for the 10-miler, twice for 20 miles and three times for the 50K. The loop takes in the South Plateau, Firetower and Family Bike trails before descending through Logan-Keith and climbing the Sinks. One stocked aid station at the John Scoble Memorial Pavilion start-finish.

About

Dizzy Fifties is a trail ultra hosted by the Huntsville Track Club in Huntsville, Alabama, run on the rugged North and South Loop trails each November, with distances from 10 miles up to 50K.
