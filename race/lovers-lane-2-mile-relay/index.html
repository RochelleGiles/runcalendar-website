Course

About

The Lovers Lane 2 Mile & 4 Mile Relay is a Valentine's-themed running event held at Bridgeway Station in Simpsonville, SC. Participants can run solo in the 2-mile race or team up for the 4-mile partner relay, with races starting at 6:00 PM.
