Course

An out-and-back 5K starting at the Wyoming Pure building outside Wheatland. Runners head west on paved Slater Rd for about three quarters of a mile, then south on dirt road and two-track before turning back northeast through pasture and down Slater Rd to the finish. Sweeping prairie views over a working ranch, with Laramie Peak in the distance.
