Course

Courses are loops starting and finishing at Peter Depena Reserve, Dolls Point, beside Botany Bay. The route runs on concrete foreshore and cycle paths with a short grass section at the start, and is flat apart from a rise over the Captain Cook Bridge across the Georges River. Distances use different turnaround points and lap counts on the same path network.

About

The Sri Chinmoy Marathon Team presents a series of 4 events at popular running venues around Sydney throughout the year, for your fitness, enjoyment and self-transcendence. Each event features 3 distances: a short 4km or 5km, a medium 7km or 10km, and a longer Half-Marathon. In addition, at the Royal National Park, a full Marathon (42.2km) is offered.
