Course

All distances start and finish at Coupeville Elementary School, 6 South Main Street, Coupeville. The route crosses Ebey's Prairie and Crockett Prairie inside Ebey's Landing National Historical Reserve, combining seaside bluffs, dense forest and open farmland. Underfoot it is a mix of dirt and paved trail rather than closed road.

About

This USATF-sanctioned race crosses Ebey's and Crockett prairies within Ebey's Landing National Historical Reserve on Whidbey Island, mixing dirt and paved trails with views across Puget Sound and, on clear days, distant peaks including Mount Rainier and the Olympics. Distances range from 5K to a half marathon.
