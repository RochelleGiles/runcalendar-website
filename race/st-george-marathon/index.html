Course

Point-to-point road marathon descending from the Pine Valley Mountains through red-rock country to downtown St. George. 7:00 am start; runners must pass mile 23.1 at Snow Canyon Parkway by 1:15 pm and the finish closes at 2:15 pm (7:15). Entries capped at 5,000 marathon, 2,000 half, 1,000 5K. Boston qualifier.

About

The St. George Marathon is a point-to-point road race descending from the Pine Valley Mountains through red-rock country to a downtown finish in St. George, Utah. A Boston qualifier capped at 5,000 marathon entries, race weekend also includes a half marathon and 5K.
