Course

Starts near the 1790 Double Crib Barn and follows the lane onto the Sunrise Meadow trail, crossing field and trail through Blackacre Nature Preserve past an ancient White Oak, Cattail Pond, the Spring House and Dragonfly Pond. The 4-miler finishes at an arch; the 8-miler runs a second loop. Rocky in places, with farm animals on the route.

About

A trail race through Blackacre State Nature Preserve, a historic homestead and working farm on the edge of Louisville. The 4- and 8-mile courses cross wooded trails, open pasture and past the preserve's historic farm buildings.
