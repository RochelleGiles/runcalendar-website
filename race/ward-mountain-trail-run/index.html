Course

Loop courses starting and finishing at Group Site A, Ward Mountain Forest Service Campground, on a mix of dirt roads and single-track. Aid stations scale with distance: none on the 2-mile, one on the 5.5, two on the 8.5, and three on the 13.3 and the marathon.

About

The Ward Mountain Trail Run is an annual event organized by the Ely Outdoor Enthusiasts group, held on the scenic trails of the Ward Mountain Forest Service Campground. It offers multiple race distances, including a marathon.
