Course

Point-to-point run from 'the Caboose' in Kalamazoo to South Haven along the Kal-Haven Trail State Park pathway, about 33.5 miles and gently downhill. Ten aid stations provide food and hydration. Two-person relay teams exchange at Bloomingdale, the halfway point; teams of three to six split the route into six legs.

About

The Kal-Haven Trail Run covers roughly 33.5 miles of the Kal-Haven Trail State Park pathway, running point-to-point from Kalamazoo to South Haven in Michigan. Runners enter as solo athletes or in relay teams of two, or three to six, and ten aid stations support the gently downhill course.
