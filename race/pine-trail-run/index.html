Course

Races run along the Mogollon Rim at about 6,000 ft in Tonto National Forest, mostly on trails built by the Pine Strawberry Fuel Reduction crew and the Arizona Trail Association. Runners are bused from the Red Barn in Pine to starts at Pine Trailhead or Camp Lomia; all courses finish at the Red Barn. The 50K has at least three aid stations with portable restrooms. Field capped at 100 per race.

About

An annual trail race in the Ponderosa Pine forest near Pine, Arizona that supports forest health initiatives. The event offers multiple distance options and raises funds for sustainable trail development and forest education.
