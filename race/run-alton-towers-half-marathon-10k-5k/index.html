About

Run Alton Towers is the first event of its kind at the iconic Alton Towers Resort and will start and finish amongst the rides within the estate. Following the paths and roads within Alton Towers Resort you can take on the 5k, 10k or half marathon distance. This exciting new course provides the perfect opportunity to chase down a chip timed personal best.
