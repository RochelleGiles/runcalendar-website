Course

A trail 5K held in Blydenburgh County Park. The course mixes dirt roads and hard-packed trails with a few sandy spots and is described as not overly technical. Bathrooms sit near the finish line, with hydration and refreshments at the finish.

About

This Halloween season, pumpkin spice things up at the Great Pumpkin Dash 5K at Blydenburgh Park! Make your way through the scenic trails and walk away with some killer swag as a token of your achievement as well as some tasty treats from All Round Foods and Waterloo Sparkling Water!
