Course

A fast, gently downhill road course through Mesa, starting on N. Ellsworth Road and finishing at Riverview Park on Paseo Lago Vista. The marathon drops nearly 1,000ft net over about 19 turns, the half around 200ft, passing cactus desert and citrus groves along the way. Marathon, half and 10K all start at 6:30am.

About

The Mesa Marathon offers marathon, half marathon and 10K distances in Mesa, Arizona, each February. The route winds through East Valley neighborhoods in the Sonoran Desert, a short drive from Phoenix.
