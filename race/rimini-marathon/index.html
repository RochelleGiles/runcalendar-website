About

The Rimini Marathon is a vibrant running event taking place in the picturesque coastal city of Rimini. Runners will experience a scenic course along the beautiful Adriatic coastline, showcasing the city's stunning landscapes and energetic atmosphere. The event offers multiple race distances and attracts both local and international participants.
