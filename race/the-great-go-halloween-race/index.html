Course

About

The Great GO! St. Louis Halloween Race offers a half marathon, 10K, 5K, and 1-mile fun run with a Halloween twist. It's a must-attend fall event for serious runners and families alike, whether you're aiming for a personal best or just enjoying the festive atmosphere!
