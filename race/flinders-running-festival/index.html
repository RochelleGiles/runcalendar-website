Course

Out-and-back twilight course starting and finishing at Flinders Golf Club, Cook St Park, with a coastal outlook. Water and sports drink stations sit at roughly every 2.5 km and at the finish. The 10 km starts at 5:00pm, the 5 km at 5:15pm and the 1 km kids race at 6:15pm.

About

The Flinders Running Festival in the picturesque coastal town of Flinders, Victoria offers runners a scenic and challenging event. Featuring a 10km and 5km course, plus a 1km Kids' fun run, this festival is perfect for runners of all ages and abilities. Soak in the stunning views of the Mornington Peninsula as you make your way through this unique running experience.
