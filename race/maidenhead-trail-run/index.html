Course

A flat course on soft trail in Summerleaze Park, Maidenhead, looping the pathways alongside Maidenhead and Cookham Commons and Summerleaze Lake. The route is marked with trail flags and arrows. A 5k, 10k and half marathon are on offer.

About

The Maidenhead Trail Run offers a 5K, 10K or half marathon through the scenic Summerleaze Park trails. It's a relaxed, flat summer race suited to all levels.
