About

The Hardmoors 200 is a grueling 200-mile trail ultra traversing the rugged landscapes of the United Kingdom, from the historic city of Kingston upon Hull to the picturesque town of Helmsley. Runners will be challenged by the demanding terrain and stunning vistas of the North York Moors, making this event a true test of endurance and determination for the trail running enthusiast.
