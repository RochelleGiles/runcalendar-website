About

Celebrate 40 Years of the Women's Classic! Grab your girlfriends, family, and running buddies and join us for the 40th Anniversary of The Athlete's Foot Women's Classic — one of the happiest, most empowering events on the WAMC calendar. Whether you're running, walking, or dancing your way to the finish line, this is your day to move, smile, and celebrate women supporting women — all while raising funds for Breast Cancer Care WA.
