Course

A fast, flat loop starting on Bancroft Street by the University of Toledo North Tower, running through the UT campus and Ottawa Park, west into Ottawa Hills, then along the paved University Parks bike trail through Sylvania and Wildwood Metropark before finishing on the field inside the Glass Bowl stadium. Smooth paved roads throughout; USATF certified.

About

The Mercy Health Glass City Marathon is a fast, flat USATF-certified marathon in Toledo, Ohio, known for high Boston qualifier rates and finishing inside the University of Toledo Glass Bowl Stadium. It features a shared start with the Owens Corning Half Marathon and North Star BlueScope 5-Person Marathon Relay.
