Course

A point to point run down the Overseas Highway across more than forty islands. The 100 mile starts at Key Largo Community Park, the 50 mile at Marathon Garden Club and the 50K at Big Pine Key, all finishing at Higgs Beach, Key West. The surface is road throughout. There are no official aid stations: a crew is mandatory and runners resupply commercially.

About

KEYS100® is a 100-mile point-to-point running race from Key Largo to Key West. Both teams and individuals compete. There is also a 50-mile option from Marathon to Key West for individuals and teams and a 50-kilometer individual race from Big Pine Key to Key West. All races finish at Higgs Beach on the Atlantic Ocean in Key West.
