Course

Out-and-back on the paved Cape Fear River Trail greenway, starting and finishing at the Cape Fear Botanical Garden in Fayetteville. The 10K uses part of the Linear Park Trail and one covered trestle, with an aid station at the 2.83-mile turnaround; the half passes under two covered trestles and a waterfall, with aid at roughly 3.1 and 10.37 miles.

About

The Cape Fear River Run features a scenic 10k and half marathon along the Cape Fear River Trail in Fayetteville, NC. Participants can also tackle the River Rage Double challenge by completing both races for a special medal.
