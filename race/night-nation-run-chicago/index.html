Course

A 5K night run and walk staged at Soldier Field in Chicago, with the route passing through a series of party zones featuring DJs, light shows and glow-gear giveaways. The organisers describe it as a participation event rather than a course for setting a personal best.

About

The Night Nation Run is the World’s 1st Running Music Festival, blending a music festival with a fun run. Participants experience a music-filled course with live DJs, light shows, bubble zones, neon and black lights, finishing at an epic main stage after party with top headliner DJs.[1]
