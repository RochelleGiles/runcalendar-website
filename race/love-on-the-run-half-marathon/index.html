Course

Half marathon, 10K and 5K all start and finish at The Plaza at Toyota Music Factory in Irving. The route is flat and fast, running along Las Colinas Boulevard and the Campion Trail. All courses are USATF certified.

About

Love on the Run Half Marathon is a Valentine's Day Weekend tradition in North Texas, offering half marathon, 10K and 5K run/walk distances centered in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. It draws couples, families and friends together each February.
