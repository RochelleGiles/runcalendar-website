Course

Multi-lap event on a GPS-measured trail loop of about 3 km, starting and finishing beside the Lark Hill Sportsplex at Port Kennedy. The surface is mostly compact limestone with around 200 m of soft sand each lap, minor undulations and a short climb into the start/finish. Resupply is at the start/finish every lap with fruit, cola, hydration drinks and water.

About

The RunningWorks Lark Hill Party Ultra in Western Australia offers runners a unique challenge with 50km and 100km ultra marathon options, as well as a 25km run, all on a scenic 3km loop course. Hosted in the beautiful coastal town of Port Kennedy, this party-themed event promises a lively atmosphere and stunning natural scenery to fuel your endurance.
