Course

A five-course trail event in the Vosges, starting from the Grand Ballon massif in Alsace. The headline distance is 80 km, running through the high Vosges between the Grand Ballon and the Hohneck. Mountain trail underfoot throughout.

About

Conquer the iconic Grand Ballon mountain in this challenging yet breathtaking marathon. Runners will be rewarded with stunning vistas of the Vosges region as they push their limits on this scenic course. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a determined newcomer, the Marathon du Grand Ballon offers an unforgettable running adventure.
