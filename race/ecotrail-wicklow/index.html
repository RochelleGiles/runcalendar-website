About

EcoTrail Wicklow offers runners a unique opportunity to explore the stunning natural landscapes of County Wicklow, Ireland. Traversing picturesque trails and rolling hills, this event showcases the region's breathtaking scenery and rich biodiversity. Runners of all levels can immerse themselves in the peaceful serenity of the Irish countryside while challenging themselves on this unforgettable course.
