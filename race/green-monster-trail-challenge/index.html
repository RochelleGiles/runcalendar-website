Course

A mountain trail course in the Tioga State Forest, starting from 176 Straight Run Road, Wellsboro. The 15K climbs 1,700ft, the 25K 4,000ft and the 50K 7,800ft. The field is capped at 350 runners and the event welcomes hikers as well as runners.
