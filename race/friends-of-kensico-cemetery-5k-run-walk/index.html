Course

Starts and finishes at Mineola Lake inside Kensico Cemetery, Valhalla. The 5K crosses the road, climbs Uncas Avenue, continues around Highland Drive and loops back around Mineola Lake to the finish; walkers may take a 1.7-mile route via Seneca Avenue to the Tower and back via Tecumseh. Turns are signposted and both routes have water stations.

About

Join Friends of The Kensico Cemetery for a 5K Run and 1.7-mile Walk through scenic paths adorned with fall foliage, lakes, and stunning architecture. Enjoy a catered breakfast, exciting raffle prizes, and great camaraderie.
