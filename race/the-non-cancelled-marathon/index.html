Course

All distances run on the flat, USATF-certified Lake Country Trail, starting from Roosevelt Park on South Main Street in Oconomowoc. The marathon is four out-and-backs between Roosevelt Park and Valley Road, the half two, the 10K one to Highway 67 and the 5K one to Silver Lake Street, so spectators can see runners up to eight times from one spot. Ruck and relay categories are offered alongside.

About

The Non Cancelled Marathon began as a solution for cancelled races and has grown into a destination for runners chasing a PR or a Boston qualifier. The flat, fast, USATF-certified course is designed for speed, letting spectators cheer runners up to eight times without changing position.
