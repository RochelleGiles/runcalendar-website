About

Tepec Trail runs through Huasca de Ocampo, a Pueblo Mágico in Hidalgo state famed for the towering basalt columns of the Prismas Basálticos. The race is a stop on the Golden Trail World Series circuit, with routes from 12K up to a 40K.
