About

The Westonbirt House Easter Half Marathon & 10K offers a picturesque run through the grounds of a historic manor in the heart of the United Kingdom. This beginner-friendly event is perfect for runners of all levels seeking a scenic spring challenge, with routes winding through the estate's renowned arboretum and gardens.
