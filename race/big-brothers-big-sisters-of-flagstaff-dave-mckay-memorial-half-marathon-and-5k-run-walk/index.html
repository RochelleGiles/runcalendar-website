Course

Starts and finishes at Thorpe Park in Flagstaff, climbing onto Observatory Mesa on urban trail and dirt roads between 6,957 ft and 7,731 ft, with views of the San Francisco Peaks. The 5K shares the same Observatory Mesa terrain.

About

The 2nd oldest half marathon in Arizona, known for its scenic and challenging course through the high country with monsoon season views. All proceeds benefit Big Brothers Big Sisters of Flagstaff's youth mentoring programs.
