Course

Loop course on private land at Natural Bridge Campground, Slade. The 10K climbs about 1,500 ft over 6.2 miles of hilly trail, with three water fill stations on course. The 5K is run over the same terrain.

About

A trail run and walk in the Red River Gorge area of eastern Kentucky, held on private land at Natural Bridge Campground in Slade. Runners choose between a 5K and a hillier 10K, with a virtual option also offered. Entries close the day before the race and there is no race-day registration.
