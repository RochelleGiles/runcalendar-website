Course

Two identical loops in Willow River State Park, Hudson: the 10-miler runs one lap, the 20-miler two. Grass and gravel singletrack over rolling terrain with roots, ridgelines and open meadows. Aid station about 6.75 miles into each loop with water, gels and wrapped snacks, plus a second station for 20-milers at the lap change. Cupless event.

About

The Willow 10 & 20 Miler is a trail race in Willow River State Park at Hudson, Wisconsin. The 20-mile race runs two loops from a 7:00am start and the 10-mile one loop from 8:00am, both on grass and gravel trail over rolling terrain. The event is run cupless.
