About

The Medal Madness 5K & Half Marathon is held at Halyburton Park in Wilmington, NC, featuring a paved out-and-back course. Participants can choose between a 5K (out-and-back twice) or Half Marathon (out-and-back six times), with finisher medals awarded and a 3.5-hour time limit.
