About

The Blarney Blitz is Central Illinois' St. Patrick's Day-themed 5K run/walk, held on the Riverfront Trail along the Illinois River in Peoria. Runners are encouraged to wear costumes, with a celebration afterward at Kelleher's Irish Pub.
