About

Welcome to St. Joseph, Missouri! The Show Me Your Free State Marathon and Ultra is designed to showcase the stunning beauty of the "Show Me State" and the "Free State," where Missouri and Kansas converge along the Missouri River valley. Our goal is to provide all participants an unforgettable running experience in a welcoming environment that they and their supporters will cherish forever. All proceeds will benefit Saint Joseph School District Cross Country and Track & Field programs.
