Course

A point-to-point marathon starting in Wépion, with shuttle buses out from Namur Expo. The route follows the meanders of the Meuse and the Sambre through Profondeville and finishes back at Namur Expo in the city centre. A half marathon, a 10k, a kids run and a handisport race share the weekend.

About

The Baloise Namur Marathon is a premier running event in Belgium, offering a challenging course that showcases the picturesque city of Namur. Runners can choose from the full marathon, half marathon, or 10K Kick Cancer race, each providing a unique experience and stunning views along the way. The event is known for its vibrant atmosphere and strong community support, making it a must-do for runners of all levels.
