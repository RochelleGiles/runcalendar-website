Course

Single 50-mile loop starting and finishing in Goring-on-Thames, Oxfordshire, taking in the Chiltern Hills on 80% trail and 20% road. The route uses sections of the Thames Path National Trail and the Chiltern Way, passing Stonor Deer Park, Turville and Christmas Common, the high point of the course. Five aid stations along the route.

About

The Centurion Chiltern Wonderland 50 takes runners on a stunning journey through the picturesque Chiltern Hills, a designated Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty in southern England. Boasting challenging terrain and breathtaking vistas, this iconic ultramarathon event offers a truly unforgettable experience for endurance athletes seeking an adventure in the great outdoors.
