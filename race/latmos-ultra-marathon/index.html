About

The Latmos Ultra Marathon is a trail race around Lake Bafa in Turkey's Muğla province, passing the ancient ruins of Herakleia at the foot of Mount Latmos. Runners can choose from a 45km ultra, a 17km or a 10km distance along the lake's historic shoreline.
