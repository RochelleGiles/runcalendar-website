About

Nestled in the picturesque French Alps, the Lake Annecy Half Marathon offers runners a breathtaking tour of the region's stunning natural beauty. Trace the shoreline of the crystal-clear lake and wind through charming villages, all while soaking in the serene mountain vistas that make this event a true bucket-list experience for endurance athletes.
