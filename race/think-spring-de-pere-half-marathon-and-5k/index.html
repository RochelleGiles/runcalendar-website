Course

Half marathon starting and finishing at De Pere Middle School, 700 Swan Road, heading through countryside before joining the Fox River Trail and passing St. Norbert Abbey, then returning to the school. The 5K covers a shorter version of the same loop. Water stations roughly every 2 miles on the half, with Gatorade and Gu at mile 7; the 5K has one at mile 1.5.

About

On Pace puts on two races each year – Run De Pere on the West side in November and Think Spring in April on the East side. Think Spring half marathon athletes will begin from De Pere Middle School and take in the peaceful beauty of the countryside before meeting up with the Fox River Trail and heading back toward town. A few miles along the Fox will bring runners through the St. Norbert Abbey, and back to De Pere Middle School for an epic finish and post-race extravaganza.
