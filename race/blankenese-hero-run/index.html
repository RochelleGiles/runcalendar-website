About

The Blankenese Hero Run is an exciting annual running event that takes place in the picturesque coastal town of Blankenese. Runners will enjoy a challenging course that showcases the stunning natural beauty of the region, with stunning views of the Elbe River and the surrounding hills. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a recreational runner, this event offers a truly memorable experience for all.
