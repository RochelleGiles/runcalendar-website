About

The Mispo Talvemaraton is a small winter road marathon in Vändra, Estonia, organised by the MISPO health club each December. A looped 7 km course on well-kept roads starts and finishes at Vändra stadium, with marathon, half marathon, 14 km and 7 km run/walk options and a sauna at the finish.
