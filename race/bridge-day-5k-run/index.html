Course

A point-to-point 5K that starts just north of the New River Gorge Bridge, runs across the bridge itself, then winds through town to the finish at North Court Street by the Fayette County Courthouse in Fayetteville. Run on open streets and capped at 600 entrants; no walking on the bridge for the first mile under Bridge Day rules.

About

A 5K in Fayetteville, West Virginia, held during Bridge Day - the annual festival when BASE jumpers and rappellers descend from the New River Gorge Bridge, one of the longest steel arch bridges in the Western Hemisphere.
