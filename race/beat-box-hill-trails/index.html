Course

Run on the trails of Box Hill at Tadworth, Surrey, taking in ridgelines, woodland paths, chalky climbs and flowing singletrack. The 50K and 31K start at 08:00 and the 21K at 09:30. Routes are marked with trail flags, tags and arrows with marshals at major junctions, and GPX files are issued in advance. An event village at the finish serves food and drink.
