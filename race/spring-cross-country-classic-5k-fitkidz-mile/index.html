Course

A free cross-country 5K on the rolling natural trails of Lincoln Regional Park in Tucson, with separate women's and men's races plus a co-ed walk and a FitKidz mile. The course runs over uneven terrain with loose surfaces, rocks and ruts.

About

The Spring Cross Country Classic 5K is hosted by the Southern Arizona Roadrunners on the rolling trails of Lincoln Regional Park in Tucson. It's a free, retro-inspired cross-country event with no shirts or medals, team competition for groups of three, and a pancake breakfast afterward.
