Course

A 5K run and walk at Arnolds Park on Lake Okoboji, part of the University of Okoboji Winter Games. Entrants and walkers start together at the State Pier at Arnolds Park Amusement Park, with check-in from 8:00am at the Majestic Pavilion and a 9:00am start.

About
