Course

The 5K and 10K start together at the historic barn at Thousand Hills Ranch and share the first three miles on a mix of paved and dirt ranch roads through cattle country. The 5K then returns to the barn while the 10K continues on an out-and-back through the ranch before finishing at the same point. Water stations on both courses.
