Course

A two-day mountain navigation event for teams of two in the Mourne Mountains, with an overnight camp and full self-sufficiency between days. Courses run between 35 km and 55 km across open mountain, with teams visiting a set number of control markers; a one-day Score class collects points within six hours instead. Entry is capped at 200 teams.

About

The Mourne Mountain Marathon is a captivating team-based navigational challenge set amidst the stunning landscapes of Northern Ireland. Runners will push their endurance and navigation skills to the limit over two thrilling days in the Mourne Mountains, immersed in the region's natural beauty. This event promises an unforgettable adventure for seasoned trail runners seeking a unique test of their abilities.
