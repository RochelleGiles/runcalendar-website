About

The Zachary M. Martin Memorial Run/Walk Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, is named for PFC Zachary Matthew Martin, US Marine Corps. It raises funds to help veterans and military members struggling with PTSD, and to support families who have lost military loved ones to suicide.
