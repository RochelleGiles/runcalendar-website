About

Explore the stunning landscapes of Richmond Park in southwest London at the Richmond Park Trail Half Marathon. This off-road running event offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of one of the largest urban parks in the world, with rolling hills, dense woodland, and scenic lake views throughout the challenging 13.1-mile course. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running adventure.
