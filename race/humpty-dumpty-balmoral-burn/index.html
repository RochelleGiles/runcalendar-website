About

The Humpty Dumpty Balmoral Burn is a long-standing running event in Australia that raises funds for the Humpty Dumpty Foundation. The course features a challenging uphill climb, offering a unique and rewarding experience for participants. The event's atmosphere is lively and celebratory, with a strong sense of community and camaraderie among the runners.
