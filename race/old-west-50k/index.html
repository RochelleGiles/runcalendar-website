Course

Desert trail courses through the Anza-Borrego backcountry, all starting at Stagecoach Trails RV Resort and Campground. The 50K and marathon set off at 7:00am with about 2,500ft of gain and loss; the half marathon starts at 7:30am with around 670ft.

About

Old West Trail Runs offers a 50K, marathon and half marathon on backcountry trails around Shelter Valley, near Julian, California, climbing through high-desert chaparral and mountain terrain east of San Diego.
