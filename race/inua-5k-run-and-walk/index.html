Course

A loop entirely inside the Orlando park system. Runners start in Lower Blue Jacket Park and follow park sidewalks, cross Lakemont Avenue onto the paved trail encircling Lake Baldwin, then return to Blue Jacket Park to finish. Sealed sidewalk and paved trail throughout; a free kids run is also held.

About

The Inua 5K Run and Walk, now past its tenth running, takes place in Blue Jacket Park in Orlando, Florida, to raise funds for Inua Partners in Hope. The race offers both in-person and virtual participation.
