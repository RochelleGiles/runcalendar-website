About

This is the Livermore Granada Booster's main fundraiser of the year, and all proceeds go towards the Livermore Granada Boosters Olympian Scholarship program. This event is open to all ages and abilities. We host this event to encourage awareness and appreciation for fitness, as well as to foster community pride. All participants receive a Race T-shirt, Medal and Post-Race Refreshments.
