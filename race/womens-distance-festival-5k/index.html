Course

Starts and finishes at Optimist Park in Tallahassee, running on neighbourhood roads through Indian Head Acres. Women start at 8:00 a.m. and men follow at 8:05 a.m. A one-mile option is offered alongside the 5K.

About

Women's Distance Festival 5K is a Gulf Winds Track Club race for women runners in Tallahassee, Florida, part of a longtime national series promoting women's distance running.
