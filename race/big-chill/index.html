About

Join us for the 21st Annual Winter Run Series—Wisconsin's largest running series! This exciting series event takes you through the heart of Milwaukee and its scenic suburbs, showcasing the city's charm and beauty. Spanning six events throughout the winter season, it's the perfect way to stay active, embrace the winter months, and celebrate your favorite holidays. Bring the whole family and choose between the 5K or 10K distances.
