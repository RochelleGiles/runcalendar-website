Course

Rugged 50K loop run mainly on the singletrack Gerard Hiking Trail through Oil Creek State Park, repeated according to distance. Race headquarters, start, finish and a major aid station are all at Titusville Middle School. The route is marked with reflective flags, signage and cones for night running. Climbing is steep and near-constant.

About

Oil Creek 100 Trail Runs winds through Oil Creek State Park in Titusville, birthplace of the US oil industry, on rugged, hilly singletrack. Distances range from 25K to the full 100-mile event on interlocking loops.
