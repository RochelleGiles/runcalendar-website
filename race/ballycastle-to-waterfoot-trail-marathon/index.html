About

Explore the rugged beauty of Antrim's coastline at the Ballycastle to Waterfoot Trail Marathon. This point-to-point run takes runners on a scenic journey through the picturesque villages and stunning natural landscapes of Northern Ireland's Causeway Coast. Challenge yourself on this challenging yet rewarding trail marathon that showcases the region's renowned wild beauty.
