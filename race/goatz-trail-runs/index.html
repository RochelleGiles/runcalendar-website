Course

Loops of trail and grass around Glenn Cunningham Lake in north-west Omaha, starting and finishing by the marina and boat ramp. The 50 mile runs five laps of a 10 mile loop; the 50K and 21 mile run three and two laps of a 10.5 mile loop, with 10.5 mile and 5 mile options over shorter versions. Two stocked aid stations sit at the start/finish and mid-loop; runners carry their own hydration.

About

G.O.A.T.z Trail Runs is a trail race weekend in Omaha, Nebraska, with distances from 5 miles up to 50 miles, including 10.5 mile, 21 mile, and 50K options. It draws both first-time trail runners and experienced ultrarunners.
