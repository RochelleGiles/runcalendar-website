Course

Laps of the Coastal Carolina University campus at Blanton Park in Conway, over 10K (two 5K laps), 5K and a one-mile hustle. Mixed sealed surfaces of road and footpath, with a water station at halfway on the 5K loop.

About

Ed Piotrowski, Coastal Carolina University, Teal Nation and Apex Promoz host this 1-mile, 5K and 10K race in the Carolinas to mark the start of hurricane season each year. The event is streamed live for those who can't attend in person.
