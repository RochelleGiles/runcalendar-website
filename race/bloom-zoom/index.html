Course

About

Bloom & Zoom is a spring charity race at WakeMed Soccer Park in Cary, North Carolina, offering 8K, 5K and Fun Mile distances plus virtual options. Proceeds support local charities, and the event welcomes runners, walkers, strollers and wheelchair users.
