About

Tackle the ultimate vertical challenge at the Kilometre Vertical De Fully in the heart of Switzerland. This grueling race takes runners on a relentless ascent, climbing over 1,000 meters in just 5 kilometers of pure elevation gain. With stunning alpine vistas and a rich history, this event promises an unforgettable test of endurance and mountain running prowess.
