About

The Raider Athletic Booster Club is honoring Troy Nelson, Raider Super Fan in our second annual 5k & Fun Run. Troy loved all things Sunnyvale Athletics and we loved his cheer of "D-D-D Defense." We couldn't think of a better person to honor in our first #RaiderRun than this avid super fan.
