About

The Garrafrauns Dunmore 6K is an annual 6km road race starting in Garrafrauns and finishing in Dunmore, North East County Galway. It is held each year on the Monday of the August Bank Holiday Weekend as part of the Dunmore Annual Festival Sport Day.
