About

The Mattoni Running Festival Ústí nad Labem is a premier running event in the Czech Republic, offering a variety of race distances for runners of all levels. The scenic course takes participants through the historic city of Ústí nad Labem, showcasing the region's natural beauty and vibrant atmosphere.
