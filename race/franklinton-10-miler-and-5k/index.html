About

Whether you're getting ready for that spring half-marathon or looking to test your winter training in a 5K, the Franklinton 10 Miler and 5K are for you. We have race options of 5K and 10 miles. Join us on April 4th for something fantastic, the Franklinton 10 Miler and 5K. This race is all on the paved asphalt Scioto Greenway Trails, it is _not_ a trail run.
