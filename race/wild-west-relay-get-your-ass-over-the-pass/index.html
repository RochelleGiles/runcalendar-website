Course

A 200-mile point-to-point team relay from the Budweiser Brewing facility in Fort Collins to Steamboat Springs Ski Resort, crossing Colorado mountain passes and open country largely on roads. Teams start in waves between 5 and 7am Friday and finish between 8am and 4pm Saturday. Roads stay open to traffic throughout, so runners must yield to vehicles.

About

The Wild West Relay is a scenic point to point running adventure across Colorado. Starting in Fort Collins, teams of any size or individuals, run over mountain passes, through open spaces and on roads to the finish line 200 Miles away in Steamboat Springs, Colorado. Done solo, or in teams of two up to twelve, this race is about teamwork, camaraderie and the experience of a lifetime.
