Course

The marathon starts in Harpur Avenue heading north towards Liverpool Road, with the 42.2km run as two laps. Refreshment stations roughly every 3km carry water and Coke, and tables on the second lap add snacks and food for marathon runners. Distances are 42.2km, 21.1km, 10km and a 5km family run, all on road.
