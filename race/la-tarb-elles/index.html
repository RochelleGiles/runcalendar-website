About

Explore the picturesque Tarbès Pyrenees region at the annual La Tarb'Elles running event. This scenic trail race takes runners through stunning mountain landscapes, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for athletes of all levels. Whether you're seeking a scenic training run or a competitive challenge, La Tarb'Elles provides an unforgettable running adventure in an awe-inspiring natural setting.
