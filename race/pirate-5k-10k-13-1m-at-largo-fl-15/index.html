Course

All events start at 8:30 am at Eagle Lake Park, 1800 Keene Rd, Largo. The 5K and 10K are run as repeats of a 1.56-mile out-and-back; the half marathon repeats a 1.64-mile out-and-back four times. A 3 hour 30 minute time limit applies to every distance.
