About

Join us for the 6th annual Miles for Midwest 5K Run. All proceeds support the mission of Midwest Food Bank Georgia. The 5K course will begin at the Midwest Food Bank Facility in Peachtree City, and take you on a scenic run along the cart paths. There will be family-friendly post-race activities, such as an inflatable and delicious frozen treats! Tour our facility to learn how you can be involved at Midwest Food Bank to serve our local community.
