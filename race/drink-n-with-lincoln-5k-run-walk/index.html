Course

A single 5K run/walk starting in downtown Petersburg at East Douglas and 7th Street, run alongside the town's Street Fest. Race-day entry is taken at Hand of Fate Brewery between 8:00am and 9:00am. The organiser does not describe the route, surface or aid stations.
