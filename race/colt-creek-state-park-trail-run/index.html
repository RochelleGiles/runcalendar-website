Course

Well-maintained trails through Colt Creek State Park near Lakeland, Florida, winding among tall pines, dense forest and lakeshore with occasional creek crossings. Terrain is largely flat: about 104 feet of climb for the 5K rising to 330 feet for the half marathon. Aid stations sit every 2.5 miles on the longer distances.

About

Whether you're aiming for a personal best or reveling in the joy of the run, our race at Colt Creek State Park promises an unmatched blend of fitness, camaraderie, and natural beauty. Picture towering pine trees, flat terrains, and serene lakes—all set against the backdrop of pristine wilderness.
