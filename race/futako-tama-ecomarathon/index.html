About

The Nikotama Ecomarathon is a running event held in Futako Tamagawa, Tokyo, Japan. The course highlights the scenic Seta Picnic Square and offers a variety of distances including a half marathon, 10K, and a parent-child 5K run. The event has a focus on sustainability and environmental awareness, making it a unique and special experience for runners.
