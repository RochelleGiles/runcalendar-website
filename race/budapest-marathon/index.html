Course

Starts and finishes on Pázmány Péter Promenade by the ELTE Buda campus, running a single large city loop on paved streets and Danube quays. The route crosses between Buda and Pest over the Chain, Liberty and Árpád bridges, takes in Andrássy Avenue and Margaret Island, and includes several turnaround points. Twelve refreshment stations from 4.1 km to 40.9 km.

About

The SPAR Budapest Marathon is Hungary's largest road marathon, run on a single city loop along both banks of the Danube. Runners cross between Buda and Pest over the Chain, Liberty and Árpád bridges and pass Andrássy Avenue and Margaret Island. Shorter distances and relays share the weekend.
