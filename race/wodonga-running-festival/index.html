Course

A fully pathed, flat and fast course on the paths between Willow Tree Park and Gateway Island, starting and finishing at Willow Park in Wodonga. The half marathon goes at 8:00am, the 10 km at 8:20am, the 5 km at 10:00am and the 2 km Family Dash at 10:30am, with water stations at several points on the route.

About

The Wodonga Running Festival takes place in Wodonga on the Victorian side of the Murray River, opposite Albury. It offers 5km, 10km and half marathon options plus a short family dash.
