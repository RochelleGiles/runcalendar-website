About

The Bauers Megabackyard Funrun in Waterville, Co. Kerry is an endurance challenge like no other. This 'last person standing' event takes place on an undisclosed private estate, where runners must complete multiple laps of an unknown course to outlast their competitors. Prepare to be pushed to your limits in this unique, off-the-grid running experience.
