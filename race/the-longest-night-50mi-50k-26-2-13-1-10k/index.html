About

The Longest Night is an overnight lap race at Lakewood Nature Preserve in Greenville, South Carolina, held on the December solstice weekend. Runners cover a 1.5-mile grassy loop on a fixed 25-minute lap schedule from 6 p.m. until morning, with distances from 10K (4 laps) to 50 miles (34 laps).
