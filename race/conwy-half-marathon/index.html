Course

An out-and-back road half marathon starting and finishing on Conwy Quayside below Conwy Castle, off at 9.30am. The route runs beside the Conwy river to Deganwy and West Shore Llandudno, loops the undulating Great Orme between miles 5 and 7, then retraces to the quay. Water stations roughly every three miles.

About

The Conwy Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging race that takes runners through the historic town of Conwy and along the stunning North Wales coastline. The course features stunning views of the Conwy Castle, the River Conwy, and the Snowdonia mountain range, making it a truly memorable event for both participants and spectators.
