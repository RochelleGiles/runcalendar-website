Course

Starts and finishes at The Amphitheatre in central Bristol, running beside the Avon and onto the Frome Valley Way, through Eastville Park and along the old Bristol and Bath Railway path, then back under the Clifton Suspension Bridge via Leigh Woods. Largely paved: the 55 km is about 30% unpaved, the 25 km about 18%.

About

Beyond Trails is a series of trail running events, taking place in different major cities in the UK. They all start and finish in a city centre and will take you beyond the tarmac, to the trails. These events cater for challenge walkers and those new to trail running with the 25km trail distances, while also providing a serious challenge and a great training event for experienced ultra runners with the 55km distances.
