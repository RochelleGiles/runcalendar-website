About

Experience the vibrant city of Perth as you take on the challenging Accura Perth Half Marathon. This scenic course winds through the heart of Western Australia's capital, offering stunning views of the Swan River and the city's iconic landmarks. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a newcomer to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running adventure.
