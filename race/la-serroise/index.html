About

La Serroise is organized by BLABL'ARDITS featuring multiple trail running events through scenic singles and wooded areas. The event includes a 15.5km nature trail with 372m elevation gain, shorter discovery courses, timed walks, and children's races. The afternoon concludes with a meal organized by the AS Pont Long Rugby School.
