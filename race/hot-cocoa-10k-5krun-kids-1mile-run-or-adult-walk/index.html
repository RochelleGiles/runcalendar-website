Course

From the Nederhouser Building at Olander Park the 5K crosses Sylvania Avenue into the Farmbrook neighbourhood, picks up the University Parks Trail to McCord Road, heads north through Monarch Meadows and returns to circle Olander Lake to finish. The 10K extends along the University Parks trail to Holland Sylvania before rejoining the 5K course; the 1 Mile is a single loop of the lake.

About

A great family event that will help chase away the winter blues! Our Hot Cocoa 10K/5K/1Mile Race is being held in partnership with Olander Park. The race starts at 9am and there will be multiple corals for a safe start. In addition to the 5K and 10K, we are having a 1 mile race for our elementary age runners.
