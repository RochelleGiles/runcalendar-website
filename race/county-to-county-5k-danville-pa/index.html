Course

A flat, horseshoe-shaped 5K starting at FQ Hartman Park in Danville. The route runs down Front Street and across the Danville/Riverside bridge over the Susquehanna, then along D&H Avenue past the Merck Cherokee plant, loops a soccer field and green space, and retraces to the start. Runners cross into Northumberland County halfway over the bridge.

About

Bells will be ringing on this fast and flat horseshoe-shaped 5K course. Start at the FQ Hartman Park Area in Montour County and dash straight down Front Street to the Danville/Riverside Bridge. Take a right and cross the bridge that stretches across the Susquehanna River, otherwise known as "Muddy Waters." You enter Northumberland County halfway across the bridge. Take another right and run down the D&H Avenue to the Merck Cherokee Plant. Make a left around a grassy area of a soccer field and green space, and head back on the Avenue D&H and retrace your steps back to Montour County and the FQ Hartman Park Area. The scenery is amazing, and no matter the weather, you can't help but feel the excitement of running through the counties and crossing paths with the other race participants (high fives welcome).
