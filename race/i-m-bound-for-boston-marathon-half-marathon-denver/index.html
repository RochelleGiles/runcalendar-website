Course

A USATF-certified one-mile loop around Northfield High School in Denver, run 26 times for the marathon and 13 times for the half before a final sprint to the finish. The organiser describes it as flat and fast and pitches it as a Boston qualifier, with mile-split clocks positioned along the loop. The race starts at 6:30 am and the course closes at 10:30 am.

About

I'm Bound for Boston is a marathon and half marathon run on a flat one-mile loop at Northfield High School in Denver. The USATF-certified course is aimed at runners chasing a Boston Marathon qualifying time, and virtual entries are offered alongside the in-person races.
