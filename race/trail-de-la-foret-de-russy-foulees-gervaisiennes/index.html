About

Explore the scenic trails of the Russy Forest at the annual Foulées Gervaisiennes trail run. This challenging yet rewarding event takes runners through the heart of the picturesque French countryside, offering stunning views and a true test of endurance. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to off-road running, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
