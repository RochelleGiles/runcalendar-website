Course

A point-to-loop course starting at Geneseo Central School and heading out through downtown Main Street before returning for a three-quarter lap of the school track to finish. The 13.1-mile route runs 40% paved and 60% dirt roads through rolling countryside. Seven aid stations carry water, five of them also sports drink.

About

The Oak Tree Half Marathon and 5K take place in Geneseo, New York, a village in the rural Genesee Valley in the Finger Lakes region, home to SUNY Geneseo. The race includes a half marathon and 5K run/walk.
