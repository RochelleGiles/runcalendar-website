Course

A single loop starting and finishing at Drost Park in Maryville. The route runs on public streets and the MCT bike trail. The race is chip-timed, and donut stations along the route grant 30-second time reductions.

About

An annual 5K run/walk hosted by the Maryville Police Association that starts and finishes at Drost Park. All proceeds benefit the Maryville Police Department Good Will Fund to help members of the community.
