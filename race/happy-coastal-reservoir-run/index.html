About

The Happy Coastal (Reservoir Run) is a scenic, low-key running event set against the picturesque backdrop of Happy Valley in Adelaide, South Australia. Organized by Coastalfunruns, this community-focused race offers runners of all levels a chance to enjoy the tranquil reservoir trails and stunning coastal views that make this region a popular destination.
