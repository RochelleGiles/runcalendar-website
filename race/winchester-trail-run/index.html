Course

Off-road loop starting and finishing at Sparsholt College just outside Winchester, running the tracks, bridleways and footpaths around the Farley Mount landmark. Rolling rather than hilly, with tree-lined sections and wide views over the Hampshire countryside. Two routes: 21.4km half marathon (248m ascent) and a 12.1km medium route (144m). On-course refreshments and water stations; the event is plastic-free.

About

Now past its fifth running, the Winchester Trail Run has grown into a popular Hampshire trail event drawing well over a thousand participants. It offers scenic routes, off-road adventure and a motivating start to the New Year.
