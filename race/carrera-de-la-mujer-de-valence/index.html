About

The Carrera De La Mujer De Valence is a running event that celebrates and empowers women. The course highlights the beautiful city of Valencia, offering participants a chance to experience the vibrant atmosphere and scenic routes. This event is special as it transforms messages of encouragement from participants into donations for the Spanish Federation of Childhood Cancer Families.
