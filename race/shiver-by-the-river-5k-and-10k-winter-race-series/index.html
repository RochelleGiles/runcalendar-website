Course

A wheel-measured loop at Jim Dietrich Park in Reading, run four times through the winter as a series. The 5K covers the loop once and the 10K twice, starting and finishing in the same place. The road stays open to traffic during the race. Registration is indoors on the morning, with snacks at the finish.

About

This is a popular winter race series that draws approx. 400 runners per month because of its friendliness, great price, devotion to running in almost any type of weather, indoor registration, a wheel measured course and various snacks!!!
