Course

Road courses in Lincoln, Maine, starting at the corner of West Broadway and Main Street and finishing at Prince Thomas Park on Lake Street. The route heads south down Route 2 towards Enfield and is described by the organiser as flat, straight and fast. Water stations are set along the route with a personal bottle table at each. There is no time limit.
