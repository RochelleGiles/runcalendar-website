Course

Single-track mountain trails through meadows in the Gunnison National Forest, starting and finishing at Crested Butte Community School. The 50-mile climbs 8,301 ft and tops out at 11,291 ft; the 55K climbs 4,567 ft and the 35K 2,280 ft. Multiple aid stations are set along the course, with staggered starts from 6:00 a.m.

About

Crested Butte Ultra features 35K, 55K, and 50 Mile trail races through Gunnison National Forest, showcasing wide open views, mountain meadows, ridges, wildflowers, and fall foliage. Runners enjoy flowy singletrack trails in the quintessential mountain town of Crested Butte, Colorado.
