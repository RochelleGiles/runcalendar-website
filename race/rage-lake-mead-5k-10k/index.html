About

Scenic out-and-back 5K and 10K runs along Lake Mead National Recreation Area, passing Boulder Beach campground and River Mountain Loop Trail in the Mojave Desert near Las Vegas. Part of the larger Rage multi-sport festival including triathlon, duathlon, and aquabike.
