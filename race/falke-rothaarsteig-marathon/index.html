Course

Mountain trail marathon in the Schmallenberg Sauerland, opening with a 3.5km warm-up before climbing a feeder path to the Rothaarsteig ridge trail at around 742m. The route runs through meadows and forest past Trudes Sonnenbank, a suspension bridge and forest sculptures, then descends the Latrop valley to finish at the Fleckenberg sports field. Marathon 838m of climb; the 22.6km courses 421m.

About

The Falke Rothaarsteig Marathon is a captivating running event that winds through the stunning Rothaargebirge mountain range in Germany. Participants can expect a challenging yet rewarding course that showcases the region's natural beauty, making this a must-do event for trail running enthusiasts.
