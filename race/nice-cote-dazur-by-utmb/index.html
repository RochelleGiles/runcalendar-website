About

Nice Côte d'Azur by UTMB® offers a spectacular journey from the mountains to the Mediterranean Sea. The event features varied landscapes across the Nice Côte d'Azur metropolis with routes offering stunning panoramic views between sea and mountains. Runners can choose from four different distances to match their abilities, making it accessible for various skill levels.
