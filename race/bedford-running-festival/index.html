Course

A flat, fast, traffic-free course based in Bedford town centre. The festival runs across two days and offers 5K, Mini Miles, a Twilight 10K, a half marathon and a 20-mile race; the 20 starts at 9:30am on the Sunday alongside the half marathon.

About

Lace up your running shoes and join thousands of enthusiasts at the ATW Bedford Running Festival, Bedfordshire's most popular weekend of running. Explore the scenic countryside of this historic town as you take on a variety of distances, from a family-friendly 5K to a challenging half marathon. Whether you're an experienced runner or just starting out, this event offers an unforgettable running experience for all.
