Course

A trail course at Paloma Community Park in Peoria with staggered starts - half marathon 8:00am, 10K 8:15am, 5K 8:30am. Aid stations carry water and Gatorade: nine on the half, three on the 10K and one on the 5K, with gels for half runners. Strollers and wheelchairs are not advised.

About

Join StartLine Racing as we host our 2nd annual Arizona Rocks! Half Marathon - 10K - 5K trail run! This event will be taking place on January 25th at Paloma Park in Peoria. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or just curious to try something new, this event is perfect for everyone! Get ready to experience the beauty of nature, challenge yourself on some amazing trails, and connect with our awesome running community! Positive vibes only as we conquer the trails at Peoria's newest community park!
