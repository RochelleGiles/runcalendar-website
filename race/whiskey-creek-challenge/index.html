Course

Timed events run as repeated loops of the 5km Whiskey Creek trail at Dolamore Park, ten minutes from Gore. The loop runs through native bush, and entrants choose how long they will keep going rather than racing a set distance.

About

Based at Dolamore Park, just 10 minutes from Gore, in beautiful native bush, running loops around the 5km Whiskey Creek trail. We have 4 events that will push you to your limit and beyond. A three, six, twelve and an ultimate 24 hour challenge. We're thrilled to have you join our adventure!
