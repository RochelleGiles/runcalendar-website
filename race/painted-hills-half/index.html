Course

Point-to-point from the Painted Hills Unit of the John Day Fossil Beds to the town of Mitchell, with shuttle buses from the Mitchell gymnasium to staggered starts. The half marathon opens on loose gravel, switches to paved road mid-course and finishes on pavement, climbing about 800 ft; the 10K is roughly half gravel with around 600 ft of gain. Water and support stations are placed along the route.

About

The Painted Hills Half Marathon, 10K and 5K run through the John Day Fossil Beds' colorful striped clay hills near Mitchell, Oregon. Courses cross high desert terrain with sweeping views of the badlands.
