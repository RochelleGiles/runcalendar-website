About

Explore the scenic trails of the Cimetière region at the annual Trail De La Cimentiere event. This challenging course winds through picturesque landscapes, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the local area. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable adventure for participants of all skill levels.
