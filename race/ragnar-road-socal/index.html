Course

An overnight road relay of about 196 miles from Silver Strand State Beach in San Diego to Huntington Beach, run over two days and one night. The coastal route passes through La Jolla, Encinitas and Cardiff-by-the-Sea and is rated moderately challenging with some sustained climbing. Teams of 12, ultra teams of 6, or a one-day sprint of 5.
