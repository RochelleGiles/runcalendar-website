About

The Wangaratta Marathon & Fun Runs is celebrating its 15th annual event on a fast, flat and scenic course in beautiful Wangaratta. The event offers multiple distances to accommodate the whole family, making it a celebration for both locals and visitors. The 2km Primary School Challenge provides young runners under 12 with a professionally timed race experience, while the scenic course showcases the beauty of the region.
