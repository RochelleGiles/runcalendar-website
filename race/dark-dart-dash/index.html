Course

Single-lap night routes of 10K, 5K, 2K and 1K on the Dartington Estate trails, starting and finishing at the Green Table Cafe with starts between 5.30pm and 6.45pm. The terrain is gravel, grass, a little tarmac and often heavy mud. Head torches are mandatory and trail shoes advised. Water and feed stations on course.

About

The Dark Dart Dash is a night trail running event featuring single lap, mixed-terrain courses with gravel, grass, tarmac, and potential mud. Participants must bring a head or chest torch and are recommended to wear trail shoes.
