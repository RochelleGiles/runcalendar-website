About

The Puzzle Dash 5K is held at Columbia Woods Park in Barberton, Ohio, supporting autism awareness and benefiting Kids First at Tops School. The route runs from Columbia Woods to Norton City High School and back, with a separate accessible short course inside the park for children with disabilities.
