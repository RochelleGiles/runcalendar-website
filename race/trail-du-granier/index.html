About

The Trail du Granier is an exhilarating trail running event that takes participants through the stunning natural landscapes of the region. Runners will navigate challenging terrain and breathtaking vistas as they push their limits on this scenic course. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail du Granier offers an unforgettable running adventure.
