Course

Out-and-back course starting and finishing at Canteen Brewhouse on Aztec Rd NE in Albuquerque, following the North Diversion Channel Trail. Mostly flat, with some elevation change where the route passes under major streets. Aid stations at the 5K turnaround and at several points on the longer distances, with restrooms at the start, finish and turnaround.
