About

The Summer Turkey Trot is a 4 mile road race starting at Tricky's Hotel, Bar and Restaurant near Redruth in Cornwall, organised by Cornwall Athletic Club as Round 8 in the Cornwall Grand Prix Series. Now past its 50th running (counting both its Summer and Winter races), it is a historic and much-loved fixture.
