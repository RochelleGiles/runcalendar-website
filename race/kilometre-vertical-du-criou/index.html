About

Conquer the Kilometre Vertical Du Criou, a thrilling mountain running challenge that takes participants on a grueling ascent through stunning alpine landscapes. This iconic event offers runners the chance to test their mettle against the towering slopes of an unknown, yet captivating location. Whether you're a seasoned mountain runner or seeking a new adventure, the Kilometre Vertical Du Criou promises an unforgettable experience.
