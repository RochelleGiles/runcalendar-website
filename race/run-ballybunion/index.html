Course

All races start outside Kilcooly's on Main Street, Ballybunion, and finish at the playground on Cliff Road. The 5K and 10K are out-and-back along the same coast road past Doon Church and Doon East. The half marathon runs beyond those turns, arcs round and rejoins the R551 for the return. Roads throughout, with water at three miles and every three miles after.

About

Run Ballybunion is a road race weekend on the north Kerry coast, offering a half marathon, 10K and 5K. All three set off from Main Street in the village and finish at the playground on Cliff Road, running out along the coast road past Doon Church.
