Course

Mountain trail racing through the Jura massif from Oyonnax in the Ain, over steep forest paths, ridges and summit panoramas. Distances run from 13 km up to a 170 km solo or relay, with climbing from about 350 m to 9,000 m depending on the race. Terrain is rocky and rooted throughout.

About

The Tiger Balm® Ultra 01® is more than just a race - it's a true challenge, a test of resilience, courage, and passion. Runners from all backgrounds come together to push their limits and conquer a demanding yet deeply rewarding terrain. Through mountains, forests, and rugged paths, every stride becomes a victory over oneself. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or a young athlete seeking new athletic challenges, there's an event for you, ranging from 10 to 170 kilometers, with the option to have a pacer or support team. Lace up your shoes and get ready to give it your all. Together, we'll share thrilling emotions and celebrate the sport in all its dimensions. Welcome to the world of the Tiger Balm Ultra 01.
