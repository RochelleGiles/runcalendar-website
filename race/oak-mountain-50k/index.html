Course

Runs through Oak Mountain State Park with no repeating loops, predominantly on forested singletrack hillside trail, including two ascents to the Double Oak Mountain summit. Start and finish at the Dogwood Pavilion, South Trailhead. The 25K and six-mile events follow their own shorter routes.

About

A trail race in Oak Mountain State Park near Pelham, Alabama, offering 50K, 25K and 6-mile distances on the park's wooded singletrack. Part of the Pinhoti Trail Series, held each April.
