Course

An off-road course on the Carmi Trails, starting from the Beaverdell Road parking lot above Penticton, with the 8.2 km off at 10am and a 3 km fun run alongside it. One aid station serves the 8.2 km. The race was formerly known as the Campbell XC Race.
