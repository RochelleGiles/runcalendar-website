About

The Ultra Trail Del Moscato d'Asti is a challenging running event set in the picturesque Piedmont region of Italy. Runners will experience the stunning landscapes of the Moscato wine country, with the course highlighting the region's natural beauty and rich cultural heritage. The event offers a unique atmosphere and an opportunity for participants to push their limits in a breathtaking environment.
