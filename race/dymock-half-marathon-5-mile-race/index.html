Course

Road races on quiet rural lanes near the Gloucestershire and Herefordshire border, starting together at 09:00 from Dymock Cricket Club. The half marathon is hilly and undulating, with descents to balance the climbs. The 5 mile shares the first two miles before looping back to Dymock for a fast finish. Both routes are fully marked and marshalled with water stations.
