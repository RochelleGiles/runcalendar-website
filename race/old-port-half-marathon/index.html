Course

Single-loop half marathon through Portland along Casco Bay, finishing with a block party in the Old Port. The 5K is also a single loop, starting on Spring Street downtown, running to the waterfront via Commercial Street, joining the Eastern Promenade Trail, then returning via Fore Street and Middle Street. Half starts 7:00am, 5K at 7:35am.
