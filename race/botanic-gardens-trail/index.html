Course

Trail loops through the Auckland Botanic Gardens and neighbouring Tōtara Park, following formed garden paths and native bush tracks. The series is set up for walkers as well as runners, with no steep hills on any of the three distances, which all start and finish at the same point.

About

The Botanic Gardens Trail is a scenic running and walking event that explores the beautiful Auckland Botanic Gardens and Totara Regional Park. Participants can choose from three course lengths, meandering through lush gardens and native bush, offering a magical experience for trail runners and walkers of all levels.
