About

The Oak Forest-Crestwood Area Chamber of Commerce, in partnership with the City of Oak Forest, hosts the Oak Forest Fleadh 5K, now past its 16th running. The race precedes the Fleadh street festival, an annual Irish celebration held along Cicero Avenue in Oak Forest.
