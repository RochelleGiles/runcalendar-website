About

Coloma River Races is a non-profit that offers a challenging and unique trail race experience. This hidden gem offers adventurous terrain and breathtaking views–all in Sacramento's backyard. The Salmon Falls 50K is a point to point ultra distance trail race. The course will run the majority of the trails in the Cronan Ranch area before winding down to finish at Folsom Lake.
