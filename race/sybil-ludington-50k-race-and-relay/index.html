Course

A road ultra retracing 31 of the 40 miles of Sybil Ludington's 1777 ride through Putnam County. Starts and finishes at the VFW in Carmel, New York, at 7am, on rolling paved back roads. A two-person relay option shares the same course.

About

The Sybil Ludington 50K is organized by Putnam County and the Taconic Road Runners, retracing part of Sybil Ludington's 1777 Revolutionary War ride through Putnam County, New York.
