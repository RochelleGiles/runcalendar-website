About

The Kilomètre Vertical de Saint-Martin-Vésubie is an iconic event for trail running and mountain challenges enthusiasts. The 4.86 km technical course with a positive elevation gain of 1,170 meters takes participants from the heart of the village to the summit of La Palu at an altitude of 2,142 meters, offering breathtaking scenery in the heart of the French Alps.
