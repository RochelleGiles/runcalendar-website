About

The Prairie Striders Indoor 5K is an annual indoor race held on the SDSU track at the Sanford-Jackrabbit Athletic Complex in Brookings, SD. It features two heats for runners and a non-competitive walk option, with proceeds supporting the Prairie Striders/Phil LaVallee Memorial Scholarship.
