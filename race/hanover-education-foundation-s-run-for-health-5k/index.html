About

The Hanover Education Foundation's Run for Health 5K is a USATF-certified 5K race and one-mile fun walk held to support education initiatives. The event starts at Whippany Park High School with check-in at 7:00 AM, 5K at 8:30 AM, and fun walk at 8:15 AM.
