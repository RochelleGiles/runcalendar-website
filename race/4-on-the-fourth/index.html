Course

Lollipop course run on paved trail. Starts at Colby Lake Park in Woodbury, heads north over the Wilms Lake rollers, climbs a hill near Summit Pointe Park, then returns through a tunnel to finish back at Colby Lake Park. Self-supported with no aid stations, so runners carry their own water and nutrition.

About

A spirited 4-mile run that kicks off Independence Day festivities with a scenic lollipop-style course along paved trails starting and finishing at Colby Lake Park. Participants enjoy a post-race celebration with music, snacks, awards, and patriotic decorations.
