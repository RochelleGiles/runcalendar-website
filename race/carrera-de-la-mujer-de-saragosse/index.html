About

The Carrera De La Mujer De Saragosse is an exciting running event that celebrates the strength and spirit of women in the historic city of Zaragoza, Spain. This vibrant race attracts thousands of participants who come together to conquer the streets, creating an electric atmosphere of camaraderie and empowerment. Whether you're an experienced runner or new to the sport, this event offers a unique opportunity to be part of a powerful community of women athletes.
