Course

Run at the Fews Ford access of Eno River State Park on Cole Mill Road, Durham. Both the 11-mile and 6-mile routes are technical singletrack over rocks, roots and uneven ground, with fallen debris and sustained climbs and descents that demand careful footing.

About

Eno River Run is a trail race in Durham, North Carolina, following paths near the Eno River. Runners can choose the 11-mile or 6-mile distance through the rocky, wooded terrain typical of the area.
