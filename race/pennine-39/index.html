Course

A linear 37-mile route along the Pennine Way through the Cumbrian and Durham Pennines, from Bowlees to Alston YHA. It passes Low Force and High Force, Cauldron Snout, Cow Green Reservoir and High Cup Nick before traversing Great Dun Fell and Cross Fell. Checkpoints are at Dufton and Greg's Hut. The trails are wild but navigation is mostly easy on waymarked path.

About

A 39-mile ultra across the North Pennines starting from Alston, England's highest market town. The route crosses remote moorland and open fell country in the North Pennines AONB.
