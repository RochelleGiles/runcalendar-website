Course

An indoor track meet held on a rolling schedule from 10:00am to 3:00pm at the University of Pennsylvania's Jane and David Ott Center. Events run over 3000m, 1600m, 800m, 400m, 200m and a distance medley relay, in open, masters and high-performance sections. The meet is USATF sanctioned and World Athletics certified.

About

The PRTC Indoor Classic is an all-comers indoor track meet hosted by the Philadelphia Runner Track Club at the University of Pennsylvania's Jane and David Ott Center. Runners race a range of distances from the 200m to the mile, including the distance medley relay.
