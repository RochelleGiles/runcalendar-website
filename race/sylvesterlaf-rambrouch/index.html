About

The Sylvesterlaf Rambrouch is a unique running event set against the picturesque backdrop of Rambrouch, Luxembourg. Runners of all levels can challenge themselves on a scenic course that winds through the town's charming streets and countryside. This long-standing tradition is a must-do for anyone seeking a memorable end-of-year running adventure.
