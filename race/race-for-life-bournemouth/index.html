Course

An out-and-back along the Bournemouth seafront, starting on hardstanding and running mainly on tarmac paths towards Boscombe Pier and along Boscombe promenade before turning and returning to Pier Approach. Flat throughout; surfaces can be slippery when wet and sandy in windy weather.

About

Race for Life brings a non-competitive 5K and 10K to Bournemouth's seafront, between the town's piers. Entrants of any fitness level run, jog or walk to raise money for cancer research.
