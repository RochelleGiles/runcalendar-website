Course

Starts and finishes at Kentucky Wesleyan College, running through the campus and the surrounding neighbourhoods on a flat road course. The 5K runs one loop and the 10K two. The 10K starts at 8:00am and the 5K at 8:05am.

About

The Chad Lake Foundation's Run for Reform 5K/10K, now past its 2nd running, starts and finishes at Kentucky Wesleyan College. Proceeds support the foundation's work bringing awareness and resources to those affected by incarceration.
