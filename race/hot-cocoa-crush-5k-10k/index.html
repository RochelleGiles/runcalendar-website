Course

A multi-loop course inside Flushing Meadows Corona Park, starting and finishing on the north side of Queens Theatre. The 5K runs two loops and the 10K four loops of the same circuit. Hydration and post-race refreshments are at the finish, with portable toilets nearby.

About

Join us for the Hot Cocoa Crush 5K & 10K at Flushing Meadows Corona Park, Queens! Dash through one of NYC's most iconic parks. Whether you're chasing a PR or just here for the cozy vibes, this winter run is the perfect mix of fun, fitness, and festive cheer! Choose either a 5K or 10K and be greeted at the finish line with some delicious hot cocoa and treats courtesy of All Round Foods & Waterloo Sparkling Water!
