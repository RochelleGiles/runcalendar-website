About

The Grand Trail des Lacs et Châteaux's winter edition explores the Hautes Fagnes nature reserve around Waimes, Belgium, tracing forest trails past lakes and historic castles. Runners choose between the 12km, 22km and 38km routes through this high-moor landscape near the German border.
