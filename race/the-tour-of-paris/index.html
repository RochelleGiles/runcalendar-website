About

The Tour of Paris is a must-attend event for runners seeking a unique and captivating experience. Weaving through the iconic landmarks of the City of Light, this race offers a picturesque journey that showcases the best of Parisian culture and architecture. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a recreational runner, the Tour of Paris promises an unforgettable challenge and a chance to explore the stunning streets of one of the world's most beloved cities.
