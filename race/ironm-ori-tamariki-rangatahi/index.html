About

IRONMĀORI Tamariki & Rangatahi is a walk and run event for young people held at 4 Hardinge Road, Ahuriri, Napier. Participants choose a 1km, 3km or 5km course. It runs on the same day as the IRONMĀORI Kaumātua event, ahead of the Quarter and Half distances the following day.
