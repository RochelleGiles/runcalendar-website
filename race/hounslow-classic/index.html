Course

Blue Mountains sky run on dirt tracks and trails above Blackheath, taking in the Grand Canyon track, Govetts Leap, Evans Lookout, Perrys Lookdown and Lockleys Pylon. The course is steep and technical, running clifftop trail along sandstone escarpments and dropping into canyon trails. A 17 km and a 42.2 km marathon distance are offered across the race weekend.

About

The Hounslow Classic is a trail race from Blackheath in the Blue Mountains of New South Wales, run on clifftop tracks and canyon trails above the Grose Valley. Entrants take on either a 17 km or a marathon course, both steep and technical, with a kids race alongside them.
