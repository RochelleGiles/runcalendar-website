About

The Seoul International Ultra Trail Running Race is a challenging and scenic ultra-marathon event that takes runners through the stunning landscapes surrounding the vibrant city of Seoul, South Korea. Traversing a mix of rugged trails and urban landscapes, this race offers a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty and cultural richness of the region while pushing the limits of endurance.
