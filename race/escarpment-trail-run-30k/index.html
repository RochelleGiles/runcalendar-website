Course

A 30km point-to-point along the Escarpment Trail in the northern Catskills, from Elm Ridge Parking Lot in Windham to North/South Lake at Haines Falls. All singletrack with about 4,800ft of climb and 4,400ft of descent over two Catskill High Peaks, extremely rocky underfoot with boulders, downed trees, gullies and hidden roots, some hand-over-fist climbing and cliffside sections. No road crossings.

About

The Escarpment Trail Run is a 30K (18.6 miles) remote, rugged, technical trail race through the northern Catskill Mountains from Windham to North Lake in Haines Falls, NY. Established in 1977, it features nearly 10,000 feet of total elevation change, requires qualifying standards, and is designed for experienced mountain runners only with a 6-hour cut-off.
