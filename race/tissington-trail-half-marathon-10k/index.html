Course

An out-and-back on the traffic-free Tissington Trail, a former railway line running between Ashbourne and Buxton through the Derbyshire Dales. The outward leg climbs gently and the return is slightly downhill. Half marathon and 10K are offered across a Saturday and Sunday weekend.

About

Explore the picturesque Tissington Trail in Derbyshire during this popular half marathon and 10K weekend event. Run along the tranquil, traffic-free trail through the stunning Peak District countryside, with an out-and-back route offering scenic views at every turn. Whether you're chasing a new personal best or simply want to enjoy the beautiful surroundings, this event is not to be missed.
