Course

Point-to-point 13.1 miles along the Lake Superior shoreline. Starts north of Duluth on Scenic Highway 61, follows the lakeshore, then runs through downtown to finish in Canal Park. Paved road, rolling hills with a net downhill. Water and Powerade at miles 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12; fresh fruit near miles 6 and 10.5.

About

The Garry Bjorklund Half Marathon is a picturesque, point-to-point 13.1-mile race along the shores of Lake Superior, starting north of Duluth, MN, running along Scenic Highway 61, through downtown Duluth, and finishing in historic Canal Park. It features rolling hills and a net downhill elevation, held annually as part of Grandma's Marathon weekend.
