About

The Suzuki Ayuko Cup Honokuni Toyohashi Half Marathon starts and finishes at Toyohashi Municipal Athletic Stadium in Aichi Prefecture. The JAAF-certified 21.0975km course runs through the city center and along the Toyokawa River levee before returning to the stadium. The race caps entries at 5,000 runners.
