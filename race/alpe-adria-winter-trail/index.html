Course

Point-to-point winter trail across Val Saisera on packed snow, starting in Valbruna village and finishing at the Kile Alpine Resort. The 18 km Saisera Crossing climbs 741 m, opening fast along the valley, steepening through the middle and returning quickly to the finish. Microspikes are mandatory; the cut-off is 5h07.

About

The Alpe Adria Winter Trail takes runners on a stunning journey through the snow-capped peaks and valleys of the Alpe Adria region. Explore the breathtaking landscapes of this tranquil winter wonderland as you challenge yourself on a course that showcases the natural beauty of the area. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable alpine adventure.
