About

The Zurich Marató Barcelona features a flat, fast urban course through streets filled with centuries of history and modern vibrancy. The Mediterranean city transforms on race day with thousands of spectators creating an electric atmosphere that carries runners beyond their limits. This premier running event showcases Barcelona's essence as an open, vibrant city while offering participants a chance to chase their personal goals on a course designed for speed.
