About

The Mandalika 100 is a running event held in the beautiful Mandalika Tourism Area in Lombok, Indonesia. The event features several race distances, including a 119 km ultra, a 53 km, and a 25 km race. Runners will be treated to stunning coastal views and lush green hills as they make their way through the course.
