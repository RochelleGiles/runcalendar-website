Course

Runners circle a certified flat one-mile loop at the Sussex County Fairgrounds in Augusta. The surface is mostly pavement with roughly five percent crushed gravel, and the loop is lit at night. A live display shows laps completed and current position.

About

An ultramarathon event held at Sussex County Fairgrounds featuring multiple race distances from 6 hours to 144 hours (6 days). Runners complete a certified 1-mile flat loop on pavement and crushed gravel with flexible start times throughout the week.
