About

The Belfast Bay Run 10K on 4 October 2026 is a closed-road 10km race set in the iconic Titanic Quarter. The route starts and finishes in this world-renowned district with its rich maritime heritage. Runners are taken along scenic docklands and waterfront roads with stunning views across Belfast Bay.
