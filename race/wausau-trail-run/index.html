Course

A Friday evening lap race on the single-track mountain bike and ski trails of Nine Mile County Forest, south-west of Wausau. Start and finish are in the main venue area, with each lap running through the Dirt Town trail network. Entirely off-road; 5K is one lap, 10K two and 20K four.

About

The Wausau Trail Run is a Friday evening event that begins as the sun sets, with lights required on the course. After finishing, runners can stay for music, food and beer at the Nine Mile venue.
