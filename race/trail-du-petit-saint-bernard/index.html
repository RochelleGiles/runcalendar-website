About

The Trail du Petit Saint Bernard is a thrilling mountain running event that takes adventurous athletes through the stunning landscapes of the French Alps. Challenging technical trails and breathtaking vistas make this a must-do race for trail running enthusiasts looking to push their limits in a beautiful natural setting.
