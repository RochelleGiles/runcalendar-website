Course

Flat, fast point-to-point along the Thames from Walton Bridge, Walton-on-Thames, to Hampton Court Bridge. Road and path at the start before joining the Thames towpath, with a mix of flat path, road, gravel and grass underfoot. England Athletics certified, fully marshalled, with pacemakers and water at about 3, 5.5 and 8.5 miles and at the finish.

About

Explore the stunning Surrey countryside along the banks of the River Thames at the River Thames Half Marathon. This scenic course winds through the picturesque town of Walton-on-Thames, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned half-marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
