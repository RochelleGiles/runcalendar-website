About

The Evl Halbmarathon Leverkusen is a must-run event for any half-marathon enthusiast. Set against the backdrop of Leverkusen's vibrant cityscape, this race offers a challenging and scenic course that winds through the heart of the community. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the distance, the Evl Halbmarathon Leverkusen promises an unforgettable experience that will leave you inspired and eager to conquer the next challenge.
