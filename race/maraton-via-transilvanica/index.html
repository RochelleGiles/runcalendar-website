About

Explore the stunning landscapes of Transylvania at the annual Via Transilvanica Maraton. This captivating race takes runners on a scenic journey through the region's rolling hills, dense forests, and historic towns, offering a truly unforgettable experience for endurance enthusiasts. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the sport, this event promises to challenge and inspire.
