Course

The half marathon runs two loops around Boulder Reservoir, the 10K a single loop, and the 5K uses a separate route. The course is flat, with roughly 200 feet of climb, and stays mostly on dirt trails with views west to the mountains. All distances start and finish at Boulder Reservoir.

About

The Boulder Rez Half Marathon is a spring race at Boulder Reservoir in Colorado, run alongside a 10K, a 5K and a half marathon relay. The half covers two loops of the reservoir on mostly dirt paths, with roughly 200 feet of climb and views west to the Front Range.
