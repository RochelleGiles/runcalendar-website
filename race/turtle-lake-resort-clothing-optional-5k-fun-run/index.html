About

Our Annual 5K Fun Run differs from most others because, at Turtle Lake Resort, clothing is optional for our event. Wear clothing if you wish or join in with most of our competitors and wear nothing. Our 5K is run entirely on our private grounds. After the fun run, stay for the day and enjoy our outdoor or indoor pool or hot tub. We have a dance scheduled for the race day that you are welcome to stay for at no additional cost beyond your 5K registration fee!
