About

The Trail Des Monts De Vaucluse is an exciting trail running event that offers challenging routes through the beautiful Monts de Vaucluse region. Organized by BCI Athlétisme, this fourth edition provides passionate runners with unique trail experiences, exploring surprising and scenic routes that showcase the natural beauty of the area.
