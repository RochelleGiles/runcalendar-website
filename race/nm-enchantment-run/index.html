Course

An out-and-back along the scenic roads and ditch banks of Albuquerque's North Valley Bosque. The race starts at Reginald Chavez Elementary on Mountain Rd NW and finishes at Bike In Coffee at Old Town Farm, with the 15K away at 9am and the 5K at 9.30am.

About

The NM Enchantment Run offers 15K, 10K and 5K distances through Albuquerque, New Mexico, nicknamed the Land of Enchantment. It's organized by Bodies Race Company as part of its busy fall race calendar in the high desert.
