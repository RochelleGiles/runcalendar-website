Course

All three races start and finish at Lampasas Middle School and are chip timed. Five water stops are placed around the course for hydration and basic first aid. Parts of the course remain open to vehicular traffic during the race. Separate course maps are published for the 10K, 5K and one-mile events.

About

The Toughest 10K in Texas sends runners along hilly rural roads around Lampasas, in the Texas Hill Country. Now in its fifth decade, the race also offers a 5K and 1-mile fun run alongside the signature 10K. It has built a reputation as one of the state's most demanding road races.
