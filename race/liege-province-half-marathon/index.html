Course

Starts on the Esplanade Roi Albert on Île Monsin in Liège and finishes by the Coronmeuse eco-district on the Albert Canal waterfront. From 2026 the first and last three kilometres of both the half marathon and the 10km are reversed, so the race starts on wide roads and ends on narrower waterside paths. Around 6,000 runners take part.

About

The Liège Province Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging race through the picturesque landscapes of the Liège region. Runners will wind their way past historic landmarks and rolling hills, offering a unique and memorable running experience. This event is a must-do for endurance athletes seeking a new adventure in a stunning European setting.
