Course

A 50K on the mountain trails of Golden Gate Canyon State Park, with long climbs and descents, switchbacks and stretches that narrow to singletrack. The route takes in Windy Peak and passes five aid stations. The organiser advises hiking poles for the climbs and asks runners not to cut switchbacks.

About

The Golden Gate Dirty 30 is a 50K trail ultramarathon through Golden Gate Canyon State Park near Black Hawk, Colorado, climbing through high-altitude forest and canyon terrain with substantial elevation gain over rugged mountain trails.
