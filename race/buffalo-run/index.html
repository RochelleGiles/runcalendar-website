Course

Rolling singletrack across Antelope Island in the Great Salt Lake, through desert grassland with sweeping lake views and herds of wild bison on the course. Climbing runs from about 1,687 feet on the 25K to 3,038 feet on the 50K and 3,428 feet on the 50 mile.
