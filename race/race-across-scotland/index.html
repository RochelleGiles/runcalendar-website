Course

A 215-mile coast-to-coast ultra along the Southern Upland Way, from Portpatrick Village Hall in Dumfries and Galloway to Cockburnspath Village Hall in the Scottish Borders. The route crosses mountain trail, valley, forest and historic ways through the Southern Uplands. Twelve checkpoints supply water and food, with five bothies as emergency shelter, inside a 100-hour limit.

About

A multi-day, 215-mile coast-to-coast ultra across Scotland, starting from the harbour village of Portpatrick in the south-west. Runners cross the country's hills and glens over several days to reach the finish.
