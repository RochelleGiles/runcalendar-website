Course

Three loop courses start and finish at Belmont Recreation Reserve in Belmont Regional Park. Routes use native bush single track, open hillside and farmland tracks. The long course climbs the valley floor through bush to the Kilmister track and continues across farmland to the Boulder Hill summit before descending, with a final climb back to base.

About

Battle at Boulder Hill is a trail race in Belmont Regional Park near Lower Hutt in the Wellington region. Three loop courses of roughly 8km, 11km and 17km start and finish at Belmont Recreation Reserve, using native bush single track, open hillside and farmland tracks, with the long course reaching the Boulder Hill summit.
