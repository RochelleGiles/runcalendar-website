About

Trail du Sanglier is a spring trail race in the Haut Beaujolais hills of eastern France. Runners choose between 10 km, 14 km, 28 km and 40 km courses, the longest climbing around 1,800 metres, and a children's race, La course des Marcassins, is held alongside.
