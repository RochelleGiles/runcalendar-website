Course

Course runs through Old Town Florissant, starting and finishing at 50 Rue St Francois Street on the Duchesne Home Association grounds. The Shamrock Run/Walk starts at 9:05 AM and the Leprechaun Dash for children aged 8 and under at 8:45 AM.

About

The Florissant St. Patrick's 5K Run tours scenic Old Town Florissant, showcasing the area's history along a challenging course. It runs alongside the city's St. Patrick's Parade and Festival.
