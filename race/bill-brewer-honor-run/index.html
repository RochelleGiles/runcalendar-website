About

The Bill Brewer Honor Run, now past its 4th running, offers a 5K run/walk and a 10K run in Williamsburg. It honors fallen Clermont County Deputy Sheriff's Detective Bill Brewer, who was killed in the line of duty and was a Williamsburg High School graduate and Athletic Hall of Fame athlete.
