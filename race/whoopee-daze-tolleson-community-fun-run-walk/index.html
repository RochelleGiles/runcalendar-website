About

A community fun run/walk held as part of the Whoopee Daze Festival, Tolleson's most storied hometown event presented for over 60 years. The event takes place at Tolleson Veterans Park and is part of a three-day festival featuring a parade, carnival rides, food trucks, and family activities.
