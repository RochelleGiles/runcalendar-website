Course

Starts and finishes at the Haltom City Senior Center, 3201 Friendly Lane, Haltom City. The Fun Run sets off at 8:30 AM and the 5K at 9:00 AM, with timed and untimed 5K entries. The organiser publishes course maps but no written route description.

About

The Haltom Stampede is an annual February 5K and Fun Run staged by the City of Haltom City, Texas, and now past its fortieth running. It starts and finishes at the Haltom City Senior Center and offers timed and untimed 5K entries alongside an untimed fun run.
