Course

Loop races in Cornerstone Park, Henderson, Nevada: timed divisions and the standard 100-mile, 100K and 50-mile run a 2.35-mile loop, while the 100-mile short course and USATF 100 Mile Road Championship use a certified 1.19-mile loop. Surface is roughly 95% asphalt and concrete with a short crushed-gravel section. Overall course cutoff is 2:00 PM Sunday.

About

Jackpot Ultras is a multi-day ultrarunning festival at Cornerstone Park in Henderson, Nevada, with 6, 12, 24 and 48-hour timed races plus 50-mile, 100K and 100-mile fixed distances on short paved loops. It hosts the USATF 100 Mile Road Championship on a certified 1.19-mile loop.
