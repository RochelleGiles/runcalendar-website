Course

Point-to-point course on the northern section of the Bibbulmun Track across the Darling Scarp, run between North Bannister and Kalamunda. Distances are 100 miles, 100km, 50 miles (Boar), 50km (Sow), 23km (Pork Chop) and a 5km kids event. The long courses are remote, meeting a road only twice on the 100 miler.

About

The Feral Pig Ultra is a point-to-point trail race on the northern Bibbulmun Track through the Perth Hills and Darling Scarp in Western Australia. Distances range from 23km to 100 miles, plus a 5km kids event. The long courses are remote, with aid stations only where the track meets a road.
