Course

A 5K winter course through Hillview Recreation Area near Hinton, with check-in and parking at the Center for Outdoor Learning and a 10:00am start. Runners use snow boots or snowshoes depending on conditions, and snowshoes can be borrowed at the event.

About

Beat the winter blahs with a 5K race at Hillview Recreation Area near Hinton, IA, where participants can run, walk, or snowshoe. The event starts at 10am and is open to all levels of adventure seekers.
