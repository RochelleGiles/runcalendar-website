Course

Start and finish at Charles Paterson Park, Perth. Runners head west along the foreshore to separate 5 km and 10 km turnaround points, then return and cross the Boorloo Bridge, continue along the foreshore and loop back over the Matagarup Bridge to the finish. Water refill stations are provided in the event village.

About

The Mother's Day Classic is a fun run/walk event held annually on Mother's Day in Australia. The Perth & WA event offers both major and local events, with participants receiving a race bib and a finisher's medallion. The event celebrates mothers and raises funds for breast cancer research.
