About

The Dawn to Dusk Endurance Run is a unique event that challenges runners to push their limits over the course of a day. Runners can choose from a variety of distances, including a 6-hour, 8-hour, 15.5-hour, and 24-hour race, all taking place on a scenic 4.5-mile loop.
