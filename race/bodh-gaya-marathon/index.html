About

The Bodh Gaya Marathon is a unique running event set against the backdrop of the historic Bodh Gaya region, home to the sacred Mahabodhi Temple where the Buddha attained enlightenment. Runners can expect a challenging course winding through picturesque landscapes, offering a truly immersive experience steeped in spiritual and cultural significance.
