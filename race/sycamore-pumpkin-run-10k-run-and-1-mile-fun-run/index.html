Course

The 10K starts at Kishwaukee Drive and Route 64, runs west on State Street through downtown Sycamore, south on Somonauk Street, east on Bethany Road and north on Airport Road, then into Sycamore Park to finish at the golf course. Three water stations, near miles 2.5 and 4 and at the finish. The 1 Mile Fun Run loops inside Sycamore Park.

About

The Sycamore Pumpkin Run is a beloved annual event that celebrates the fall season with a 10K run and 1 mile fun run. Runners can enjoy the scenic course, energetic atmosphere, and festive costumes.
