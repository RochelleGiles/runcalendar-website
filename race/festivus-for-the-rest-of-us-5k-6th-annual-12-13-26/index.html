Course

Out-and-back of 3.10 miles on public roads, starting and finishing at the New Florence Fire Hall, 177 13th St. The first half has a net climb and the second a net descent. One water station sits at the 1.45-mile mark. Roads stay open to traffic, with marshals stopping traffic at the Route 711 crossing.
