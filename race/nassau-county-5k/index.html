About

The pre-race activities (packet and tee shirt pick-up, race night registration, company gathering etc) and post-race party (free pizza, free beer etc) will all be staged in the grass/picnic area next to the tennis courts within Eisenhower Park field/lot #2. The 5k run/walk will all take place on the certified "banana loop" and yellow recreation trail course with the start/finish at field/lot #2.
