About

The Georgina Marathon & Half-Marathon is a premier running event in the heart of Georgina, Ontario. With a scenic course showcasing the natural beauty of the region, this race offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply enjoying the camaraderie of the running community, this event promises an unforgettable race day atmosphere.
