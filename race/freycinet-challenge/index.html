About

The Freycinet Challenge is an annual multi-sport race across the stunning Freycinet Peninsula at Coles Bay, on the East Coast of Tasmania. The event is marking its 26th edition and promises to be an unforgettable experience for athletes and adventure enthusiasts alike, with disciplines including Kayaking, Road Cycling, Trail Running, and Mountain Biking.
