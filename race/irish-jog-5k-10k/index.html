Course

Out-and-back road course starting at Rath Avenue and Loomis Street in Ludington. The 5K route is flat and the 10K route includes hills. Both distances are run on roads and start at 9:00am.

About

The RUNLUDINGTON Irish Jog is a St. Patrick's Day-themed running event open to runners and walkers of all ages and abilities. Participants enjoy 5K and 10K distances starting at 9 AM in downtown Ludington with a festive atmosphere.
