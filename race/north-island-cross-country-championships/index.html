About

The North Island Cross Country Championships are held at Spa Thermal Park in Taupo, New Zealand, hosted by the Taupo Harrier Club. The championships include races for Under 10, Under 12, Under 14, Under 16, Senior and Masters age groups. Entries are taken online in advance, with late entries accepted at registration.
