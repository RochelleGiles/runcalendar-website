About

Spring Fling – San Antonio is a spring road race held at O.P. Schnabel Park in San Antonio, Texas. Part of the Spring Fling Race Series, it offers a kids dash, a one-mile run, a 5K and a 10K, with a themed shirt and finisher medal for participants.
