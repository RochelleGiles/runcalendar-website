Course

A category BS evening fell race of 8 km with 360 m of climb, starting at 18:45 from the Red Lion in the square at Whitworth. The route crosses open moorland and navigation skills are needed. Full FRA body cover and a head torch are compulsory, and entry is taken on the day as well as in advance.

About

This fell race is organised by Rochdale Harriers & AC as part of the South Pennines fell-running calendar, based in Rossendale, Lancashire.
