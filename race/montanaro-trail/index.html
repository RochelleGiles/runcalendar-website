Course

A mountain trail race in the Pistoiese Apennines above San Marcello Piteglio. Three routes are offered: La 50 over 50 km with 3,550 m of climbing, La 25 over 25 km with 1,700 m, and La 12, a 12 km Nordic walking route run almost entirely on trail. The courses follow forest paths and streams along the Apennine ridge.

About

The Montanaro Trail runs through the Tuscan-Emilian Apennines around San Marcello Piteglio, in the mountains above Pistoia. It offers 12K, 24K and 50K distances on mountain trails.
