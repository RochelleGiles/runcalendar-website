Course

A fast, flat coastal half marathon starting and finishing at Lytham Green beside Lytham Windmill, run out and back along the seafront with open sea views. The route stays open to the public, so runners share it with traffic, pedestrians and cyclists and are asked to keep left throughout. Water stations sit at regular intervals.

About

Get ready to take on one of the most scenic and atmospheric half marathons in the UK. Staged along the stunning Fylde Coast, the Windmill Half Marathon is more than just a race – it's a journey through iconic landmarks, coastal beauty, and the unbeatable camaraderie of the running community.
