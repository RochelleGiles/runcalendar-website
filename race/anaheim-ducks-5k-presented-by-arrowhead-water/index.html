About

The 4th annual Anaheim Ducks 5K presented by Arrowhead Water is a family-friendly run/walk event at Great Park Ice in Irvine benefiting the Anaheim Ducks Foundation. Participants receive a race t-shirt, finisher's medal, and souvenir bib, with options including Ducks game tickets.
