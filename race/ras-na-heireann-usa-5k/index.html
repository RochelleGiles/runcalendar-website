Course

Wheel-measured 5K on paved city streets, starting and finishing in Davis Square, Somerville. The route passes through Teele Square, along Powderhouse Boulevard, through Ball Square and past Tufts University and Trum Field, finishing on Highland Avenue. The course is mostly flat.

About

The Ras na hEireann USA 5K is a St. Patrick's season road race held in Davis Square, Somerville, Massachusetts, celebrating Irish-American heritage in the greater Boston area.
