Course

A half marathon, 10K and 5K starting and finishing at the Papillion Landing Campus Field House, winding through town on a mix of hard-surface trails and city streets. The half and 10K courses are USATF certified. Aid stations roughly every two miles carry water, Powerade, ice and toilets.

About

The Annual Papillion Half Marathon will begin at 7:30 am with the 10K and 5K to begin shortly after. Start and Finish will be at the Papillion Landing Campus (Field House, 1022 W. Lincoln Street). During the races, activities will include: DJ and Music, Food and Service Vendors and much more! Stick around and enjoy the day and watch the awards ceremony at the end of the races.
