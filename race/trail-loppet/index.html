About

Our most popular trail running event of the season, the Trail Loppet on Sunday is a day of rugged, urban trail running in Minneapolis's largest regional park. The Half Marathon, 10K and 5K races in Theodore Wirth Park are the ultimate urban trail running experience, and all participants will be greeted after the race with music and a free Pryes Brewing drink.
