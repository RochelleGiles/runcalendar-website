About

Year after year, we are deeply moved by the incredible support from our community for this meaningful cause. Families and children living with disabilities and medical challenges hold a special place in all our hearts. It's truly inspiring to witness the profound impact Peter has had—not only in raising awareness of this disorder but also in empowering and uplifting those with other disabilities.
