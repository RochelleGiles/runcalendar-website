About

La Compiegnoise is an annual running event held in Compiègne, France. The event features a variety of race distances, including a marathon, half marathon, and 10K, allowing runners of all levels to participate. The scenic course takes runners through the picturesque streets and parks of Compiègne, providing a unique and enjoyable experience for both participants and spectators.
