Course

Multiple laps of the Tan Track, the well-known 3.83km circuit around Melbourne's Royal Botanic Gardens. Start and finish are at the Pillars of Wisdom on Alexandra Avenue. The four-person relay and the individual marathon both cover 11 laps, with solo options over three laps (11.49km) and a single lap (3.83km).

About

The Sri Chinmoy Tan Team Relays take place on Melbourne's iconic Tan Track, the loop around the Royal Botanic Gardens, with teams tackling 4km and 12km legs plus a full marathon relay distance.
