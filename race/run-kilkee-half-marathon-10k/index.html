About

The Run Kilkee Half Marathon & 10K offers runners a stunning coastal challenge along the rugged Loop Head Peninsula in County Clare, Ireland. Participants will take in breathtaking ocean vistas as they race through charming seaside villages and along the dramatic cliffs of this picturesque region. Whether you're seeking a scenic half marathon or a scenic 10K, this event provides an unforgettable running experience.
