Course

An evening race around the Sandringham Estate and the surrounding roads and trails, starting and finishing within the estate grounds. The organiser notes the route is provisional and cannot be run outside event day because it crosses Sandringham private land. Toilets are positioned on the route and at the Visitor Centre, with water at the finish.

About

Experience the picturesque grounds of Sandringham Estate, the Royal Family's winter retreat, during the annual Run Sandringham 10K. This scenic race through the historic parklands offers runners a unique opportunity to explore the stunning countryside that has hosted the British monarchy for generations.
