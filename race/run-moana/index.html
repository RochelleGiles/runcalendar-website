About

RunMoana is a coastal running festival on South Australia's Fleurieu Peninsula, based at Moana Surf Club. The event runs on fully closed roads and offers a half marathon, 10 km, 5 km and a 2 km kids run. All finishers receive a medal, with refreshments served at the surf club afterwards.
