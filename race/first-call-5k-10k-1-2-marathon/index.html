Course

All distances start at the E.B. Shurts Building in Waukesha and head west on the Fox River Trail, also using the Glacial Drumlin Trail. The course is flat and fast on sealed rail trail; the half marathon runs three loops and the 5K and 10K shorter configurations of the same route.
