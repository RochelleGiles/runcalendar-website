About

Always Aim High Events are proud to host the historic and iconic Nick Beer Llandudno 10k. The event, which is in its 34th year, has previously been run by the North Wales Road Runners. This not-for-profit event will continue to support local charities and the North Wales Road Runners Club. Starting on the iconic Victorian promenade in Llandudno, the route takes runners around the awe-inspiring Great Orme with its stunning views of Eryri (Snowdonia), Anglesey and beyond. With hundreds of runners participating year after year, it is a fabulous start to the running season.
