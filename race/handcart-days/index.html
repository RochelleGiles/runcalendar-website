Course

All distances start and finish at the South Davis Recreation Center in Bountiful. The half marathon is mostly flat with slight inclines, running about five miles of the Legacy Trail plus city streets through Farmington, Centerville and Bountiful. The 5K is flat with one aid station at halfway; a 1K loops the facility grounds.
