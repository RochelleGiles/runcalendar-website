Course

An out-and-back on flat, mostly paved roads north along Raucholz Road, starting and finishing on the track at St Peter Lutheran School in Hemlock. The 10K includes a stretch of hard-packed gravel road and turns later than the 5K, with both returning via Lunney Road to finish around the track.

About

The holidays are coming once again! We invite you to join St Peter Lutheran School and Dig Deep Race Events for the 14th Annual running of the St Peter Turkey Trot on a flat, country road course in Hemlock to prepare you for the feasting that is to come!
