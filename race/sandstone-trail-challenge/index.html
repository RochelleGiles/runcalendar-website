Course

Point-to-point 32 mile (52km) crossing of the Sandstone Trail from Whitchurch to Frodsham, taking in three counties and about 1,268m of climb. The route mixes woodland trail, forest track, canal path and open ridgeline, passing Bickerton Hill, Beeston Castle and Delamere Forest with the Cheshire plain below. Five fully stocked checkpoints.

About

The Joe Beswick Sandstone Challenge is a point-to-point journey across one of the most inspiring long-distance trails in the North West. Organised by Helsby Running Club, it's a welcoming event that takes runners through three counties and thousands of years of history in one day.
