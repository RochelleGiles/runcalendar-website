About

Will Run for Tacos is a spring road and towpath race in the Tremont neighbourhood of Cleveland, starting and finishing outside The Southside. The course runs north on West 11th Street before joining the Towpath Trail, with turnarounds for each distance. A kids Taco Dash precedes the main events.
