About

Immerse yourself in the serene Jarrah forests of Dwellingup, Western Australia, during the Mighty Jarrah Trail Run. Tackle challenging terrain as you wind through the picturesque trails, surrounded by the towering Jarrah trees that give this event its name. This unique running experience offers a chance to connect with nature and push your limits in a stunning natural setting.
