About

A December 10 km in the 7th arrondissement, run on what the organiser calls the flattest course in Paris, with the Eiffel Tower over the start. It starts at 9:00 on the first Sunday of December. Entries open in May and fill; once they have, the organiser's official bib exchange on Klikego is the way in.
