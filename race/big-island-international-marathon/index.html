Course

The marathon is run as two out-and-backs along the old coast road north of Hilo, starting and finishing at the Hilo Hawaiian Hotel. The half marathon, 10K and 5K share the same coastal route on sealed road. Packet pickup and the expo are held at the host hotel the day before.

About

The Big Island International Marathon runs along the old coast road north of Hilo on Hawaii's windward side. The marathon covers two out-and-backs from the Hilo Hawaiian Hotel, with a half marathon, 10K and 5K sharing the same coastal route. The race has been held for close to thirty years.
