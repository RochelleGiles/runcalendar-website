Course

The half marathon and 10K start and finish at the same point inside Boiling Springs State Park, the half rolling over moderate hills for the first four miles and mostly flat after that, with the 10K turning around near 4.5 miles on the same course. The 5K stays wholly within the park over a few rolling hills. Six water stops on the half, three on the 10K and two on the 5K.

About

The Lion Heart Half Marathon is a half marathon, 10K, and 5K road race held annually in Woodward, Oklahoma, now in its 13th year. Runners choose from three distances starting and finishing in the same northwest Oklahoma community.
