About

The Snowball Series is a winter running series in St. Louis, now past its 46th year, comprising five races that tour different areas of the city, including a one-mile race around Lafayette Square. Runners can join the full series or sign up for individual races, with the series designed to encourage fitness and fun through the coldest months for all ages and abilities.
