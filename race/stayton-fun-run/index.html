Course

Courses run on Stayton streets and the Stayton middle school trail system, across asphalt and cross-country surfaces. The 10K includes close to 3km of groomed trail with about 135 feet of climb through the trail section.

About

An annual 4th of July running and walking event held in Stayton, Oregon since 1983 that celebrates fitness and family tradition. The event features multiple distance options on asphalt and cross-country surfaces suitable for competitive runners, fun runners, and walkers.
