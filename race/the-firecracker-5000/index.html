About

The Firecracker 5000 is a 5K Run/Walk hosted by the West Mansfield Conservation Club on July 4th at 8:30AM. The 5K run kicks off the full day of Independence Day events. Start your July 4th with us for a morning run. The start and finish is at the West Mansfield Conservation Club. After the race enjoy a day full of festival activities for all ages and wraps up at dark with a fantastic fireworks show.
