Course

Two out-and-back trail courses on Te Mata Peak. The 12.8km starts at Havelock North Primary, climbs through Tainui Reserve to the summit and returns by the same route; the 3.5km Tainui Trail starts at Tainui Reserve, climbs into the trails and returns along Joll Road to finish at the school. Both are trail underfoot with steady climbing.

About

The Gourmeats Peak Trail Blazer is a trail run for all abilities in Havelock North. Run or walk, team or individual, corporate or school, super fit or not so, young and old - you are covered. This is a 100% fundraising event supporting Havelock North Primary School and the Hawke's Bay Rescue Helicopter Trust.
