About

Fujairah's running weekend on the UAE's east coast, from Fujairah Festival Square: a new road half marathon, a 10K, a 5K and a 3K on the roads, and an 11 km trail run that starts from the Al Ittihad Road parking and heads into the hills behind the town. Starts run from 6:30 in the morning, with a free run for people of determination at 8:30.
