Course

A 24-hour lapped mountain race on Yr Wyddfa, starting and finishing at the base and repeating the ascent and descent of Wales' highest peak for as many laps as possible; the solo record stands at eight. Marshals and Mountain Leaders support the route. Entered solo or as a relay team of up to five, with an event village offering food, rest areas and a silent respite zone.

About

Yr Wyddfa (Snowdon) is the highest mountain in Eryri (Snowdonia) and England and Wales. It peaks at an impressive 1,085 m (3,560 feet). Starting and finishing at the foot of this iconic mountain, your challenge is to ascend and descend Yr Wyddfa as many times as possible in 24 hours.
