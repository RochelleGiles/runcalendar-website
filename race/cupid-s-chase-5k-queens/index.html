About

Cupid's Chase 5K Queens is an annual race series event that welcomes runners, walkers, and rollers (including wheelchairs and baby strollers) to raise funds for Community Options, Inc., supporting people with disabilities. The Queens event takes place in Flushing Meadows Corona Park with a USATF certified course.
