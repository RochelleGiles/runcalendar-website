About

Join us for the 2nd annual Fire and Ice 5K Race and 1 Mile Fun Run in Joplin, MO, an event designed especially for dog lovers and their furry companions. This unique race offers a shorter route, perfect for both seasoned runners and newcomers eager to see how their dogs perform in a racing environment. Whether you choose the 5K race or the 1 Mile Fun Run, you and your dog are in for an exciting day. For those who can't attend in person, a virtual option is also available.
