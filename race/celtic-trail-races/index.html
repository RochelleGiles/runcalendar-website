Course

A 34 mile ultra and a 26 mile marathon run circular routes from Dare Valley Country Park, while the 13 mile half is linear from Resolven Rugby Club to the same finish. The courses cross the Afan Forest and Rhigos mountains on ancient woodland tracks, remote single track and the Coed Morgannwg Way, with sharp climbs, river crossings and 5,850ft of ascent on the ultra.

About

Explore the enchanting Celtic Trail Races, a winter wonderland of trail running through the rugged landscapes of the United Kingdom. Challenge yourself with a 50km Ultra, full Marathon, or Half Marathon, and experience the festive spirit of this unique running adventure. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the region as you navigate the scenic trails.
