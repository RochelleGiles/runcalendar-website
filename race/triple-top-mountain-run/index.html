Course

A 19km mountain course crossing three peaks - Van Dyke, Claude and Roland - on technical single track. The listed start is Mt Roland Regional Reserve at 5:00am, and the course finishes on Claude Road after a descent of the Mount Roland face track. No aid station information is published.

About

Since 1991, this technical single-track race has crossed three Tasmanian peaks -- Van Dyke, Claude and Roland -- to become one of the country's most iconic trail events. The 19-kilometre course finishes on Claude Road below Mount Roland's face track, with a course record of 1 hour 57 minutes.
