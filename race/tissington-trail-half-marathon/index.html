Course

A point-to-point half marathon along the length of the old disused railway line of the Tissington Trail, running traffic-free from Parsley Hay to Ashbourne. The multi-terrain surface descends gradually the whole way, giving good PB potential, with views over the Derbyshire Dales throughout.

About

Explore the picturesque Tissington Trail in Derbyshire, UK, during this point-to-point half marathon event. Run along the traffic-free trail, taking in the stunning countryside scenery as you make your way from Parsley Hay to the charming town of Ashbourne. This unique running experience is perfect for those seeking an scenic, off-road challenge.
