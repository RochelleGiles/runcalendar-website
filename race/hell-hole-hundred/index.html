About

The Hell Hole Hundred is a trail ultramarathon held in Bethera, South Carolina, named for the surrounding Hell Hole Swamp in the Lowcountry near Francis Marion National Forest. Runners choose from 10K up to 100-mile distances on the low, flat, wooded terrain typical of the region.
