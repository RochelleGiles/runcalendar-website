Course

A triangular course at Mount Maunganui combining three ascents and descents of Mauao with three anti-clockwise laps of the mountain base track. Starting and finishing at the main beach, the route takes in stone stairs, the 4WD track and the North Face track, plus boardwalk, farm paddock, dirt track and coastal path. Run solo or as a team of three.

About

The Foundation Clinic Mauao Half Marathon is a community focused fun run/walk which traverses one of the Bay Of Plenty's most iconic natural attractions, Mauao (Mt Maunganui). Whether a competitive off road Mountain Goat runner or someone just looking for a challenge with breath-taking scenery, the event appeals to all. The course involves ascending and descending Mt Maunganui three times and three laps around the Mt Maunganui base track.
