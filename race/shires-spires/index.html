Course

Circular multi-terrain routes from Naseby Village Hall through the Northamptonshire countryside, mixing trail with road and passing the Holdenby, Althorp and Cottesbrooke estates. The direction alternates each year, clockwise in even years and anti-clockwise in odd. The 10K is run on road; the half, three-quarter, marathon and 35 mile ultra share the trail course.

About

Explore the picturesque Northamptonshire countryside at the Shires & Spires running festival. Choose from a challenging 35-mile ultra, classic marathon, or shorter 10K and half marathon distances, all showcasing the region's historic landmarks and rolling hills. This unique event offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
