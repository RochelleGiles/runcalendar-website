About

Run by Bold Coast Runners on the Cutler Coast Public Reserve in Down East Maine, this trail race covers 10.5 miles or 50K along some of the state's most rugged Atlantic sea cliffs. The reserve is considered one of Maine's wildest stretches of coastline.
