Course

A road race based at Marks Park Sports Club on Orange Road in Emmarentia, Johannesburg. The 42km and 21km start and finish at Marks Park at 06:00; the 10km and 5km start at Roosevelt High at 06:30 and finish at Marks Park, with a free bus back afterwards. Rolling suburban tarmac through the northern suburbs.

About

The Zone 3 Marathon, presented by Run Zone, is an annual road race through Johannesburg's northern suburbs, with marathon, half marathon, 10km, and 5km distances to suit runners training through the South African spring.
