About

This family-friendly event is a movement to empower youth in our community who are aging out of foster care, facing homelessness, or navigating life without a strong support system. All proceeds go directly toward providing critical services like mentorship, housing support, GED tutoring, career coaching, therapy, legal assistance, and so much more.
