Course

A scenic, flat and fast 2.95-mile paved path around Rockland Lake, smooth and free of bumps, cracks or potholes, roughly half shaded and half exposed. The 24 and 12-hour races run the full loop clockwise; the midnight marathon opens with a 2.62-mile partial loop then eight full circuits counter-clockwise; the sunset half runs a 1.31-mile out-and-back plus four loops counter-clockwise.

About

This event offers 24-hour, 12-hour, midnight marathon and sunset half marathon distances at Rockland Lake State Park, about 45 minutes north of New York City. The flat, fast lake loop draws runners chasing personal records across all four distances.
