Course

Singletrack loop courses at Marshall Mountain, starting and finishing in the ski-area base. Each loop shares the same climb to the ridge and descends by a different trail, closing with a short Local Glory Loop through the base area. The 25K covers about 15.4 miles with 3,600 ft of gain; the Moose 5 Miler has 1,200 ft. Two aid stations, one at the start/finish and one at the top of the climb.

About

Join us this May for the second Annual Marshall Mountain Revival! Marshall Mountain has long been a hub for outdoor adventure in Missoula, and the Marshall Mountain Revival is a way to honor its legacy and create a new springtime tradition. Missoula County has been hard at work building new trails at Marshall, and our looped courses do their best to showcase these offerings.
