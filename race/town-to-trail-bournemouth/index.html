Course

An eight-mile loop starting and finishing by Bournemouth Square, at the Pagoda in Middle Gardens. Around 70% is traffic-free, running out through Victorian gardens and ornamental bridges, the waterside paths at Coy Pond and the woodland of Pug's Hole before rolling back through Meyrick Park. Mixed surfaces with light undulation and only 81 m of climb; one aid station at mile 5 in Meyrick Park.

About

Town to Trail Bournemouth is a mixed-terrain, beginner-level 8 mile trail run. The looped course starts by Bournemouth Square and heads out to the wide paths and trails of Meyrick Park and beyond, gaining only 81 metres of elevation in total.
