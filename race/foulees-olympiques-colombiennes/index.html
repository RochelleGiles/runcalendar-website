About

The Foulées Olympiques Colombiennes is a unique running event that takes place in the heart of the historic city of Colombes, France. Runners will have the chance to experience the vibrant culture and scenic beauty of this Olympic host city as they tackle a challenging course through the streets. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this event promises an unforgettable experience for all.
