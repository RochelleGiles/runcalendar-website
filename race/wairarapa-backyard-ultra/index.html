About

The inaugural Wairarapa Backyard Ultra is a last one standing endurance race set in the rolling hills of Masterton, New Zealand. Runners will test their limits as they compete in this unique format, circling a short loop repeatedly until only one remains. This challenging event offers an unforgettable experience for ultrarunners seeking a true test of their mental and physical fortitude.
