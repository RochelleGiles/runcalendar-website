Course

A road marathon starting and finishing at Ibirapuera. The 42K starts on Av. Pedro Alvares Cabral by the Ibirapuera Obelisk and finishes at Praca Ibrahim Nobre, passing the Viaduto Onze de Junho, the Cidade Jardim bridge, the USP campus and the Janio Quadros tunnel. A separate 5K runs the day before from the Campo de Marte arena.

About
