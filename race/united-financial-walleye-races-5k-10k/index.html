Course

Both races are out-and-back on the Great Lakes Bay Regional Trail System, starting and finishing near the tennis courts behind the Freeland SportZone. The 10K runs 3.1 miles out to a turnaround; the 5K turns at 1.55 miles. A youth 5K, an untimed 5K walk and a one-mile kids fun run are also held.

About

United Financial Credit Union presents the Freeland Walleye Festival Race, drawing several hundred participants to run or walk the USATF-sanctioned 10K and 5K on the Freeland Rail Trail and road courses, alongside a Kids Fun Run.
