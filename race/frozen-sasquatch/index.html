Course

A winter trail race in Kanawha State Forest near Charleston, opening with a steady climb to the top of the mountain and running mostly on single track through the hills, often snow-covered. A 25K loop is run once for the 25K and twice for the 50K.

About

A 50K and 25K trail ultramarathon through Kanawha State Forest near Charleston, West Virginia, held each January by West Virginia Mountain Trail Runners on wooded winter trail through the forest's ravines and ridgelines.
