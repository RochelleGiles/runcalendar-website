Course

Both races are out-and-back on sealed seafront roads, starting and finishing at Pollok Beach, Summerstrand. The 10 km is women-only and starts at 07:00, the 5 km at 07:30. A 250 m differently-abled event is also held.

About

The SPAR Women's Challenge Gqeberha is one leg of South Africa's long-running national series of women-only road races. Runners choose between 10km and 5km distances through Gqeberha, with thousands of women of all ages taking part each year in one of the country's biggest running series.
