Course

A ten-mile scenic road course through Narragansett, run in the evening with the walk setting off at 5pm and the run at 6pm. The town caps the field at 4,000 and the race has sold out early in recent years.

About

The Blessing of the Fleet Road Race is a 10-mile road race in Narragansett, Rhode Island, hosted by the Narragansett Lions Club along the town's coastline.
