About

Kimbia Kenya is the first multi-stage trail running event created by Exaequo Voyages. The event aims to showcase the best of Kenya, offering participants a unique cultural and human adventure in addition to the sporting challenge. The course takes runners around Lake Elementaita, through rural areas, and past local schools, allowing for immersion in Kenyan life. Kimbia Kenya has grown over the years, now attracting nearly 500 participants, and is known for its welcoming spirit and support from the local communities.
