Course

Races set off from the village of Espelette and cross the Basque hill trails over two days, Friday evening and Saturday. The line-up runs to a 7.5 km night race, a 30 km, a 21 km, a 15 km and a non-competitive 7 km, with Nordic walking and hiking events of 15 to 16.7 km alongside. The organiser publishes no aid station or elevation detail on the main pages.

About

Explore the stunning Basque Country on the Course Des Crêtes Du Pays Basque, a captivating running event that winds through breathtaking mountain landscapes. This challenging yet rewarding course offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in the region's natural beauty while pushing their limits on a scenic, undulating route.
