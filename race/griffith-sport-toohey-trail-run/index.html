Course

All-terrain course starting and finishing at Griffith University's Nathan campus and running out through Toohey Forest, described by the organiser as going from the bitumen to the bush. The 5 km, 10 km and 21.1 km routes mix sealed paths with forest trail and climb steadily throughout. Water stations sit on course and at the start/finish.

About

The Griffith Sport Toohey Trail Run takes on an all-terrain, bitumen-to-bush challenge from Griffith University's Nathan campus in Brisbane South through the Toohey Forest. Designed for seasoned competitors and novice trail runners alike, it offers 21.1km half marathon, 10km and 5km courses.
