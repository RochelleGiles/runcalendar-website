Course

A point-to-point 50-mile route following National Cycle Route 8 from Caernarfon to Barmouth on the coast, run mostly on made-up cycle path. Around 1,465 m of ascent. Five checkpoints at 10.1, 18.1, 27.2, 35.6 and 44 miles with sandwiches, cake and drinks. Cup-free event with a minimum 4 mph pace and mandatory kit.

About

The Mount to Coast Cerridwen is a quadrennial event, part of the Mount to Coast End to End Challenge Series. The 50-mile race takes runners from Travelodge Caernarfon to the seaside resort of Barmouth, following the scenic National Cycle Route 8, and forms Day 2 of the Welsh end-to-end challenge, CYMRUN. Finishers receive a bespoke race medal.
