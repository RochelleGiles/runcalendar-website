About

The Beacons Ultra is a challenging trail and mountain running event set in the magnificent Brecon Beacons. Runners experience a diverse and unpredictable course that can present conditions ranging from sunshine to freezing temperatures and misty landscapes. The event welcomes athletes of all abilities, from world record holders to first-time ultrarunners, creating a uniquely inclusive and friendly atmosphere.
