Course

A lapped course around Canterbury Agricultural Park in Hillmorton, Christchurch, run with your dog. One lap is 2.5 km, with the 5 km and 10 km doing two and four laps; obstacles for dogs and handlers are set along the way. Starts are staggered from 10:00am and every finisher is timed.

About

The Pawsome Run is a dog-friendly fun run/walk based at Canterbury A&P Park in Christchurch. Choose from a 2.5km (1 lap), 5km (2 laps) or 10km (4 lap) run or walk with your dog, with a few obstacles thrown in for both dogs and humans. Every human finisher receives a Pawsome Medal and every dog a Pawsome Dog Tag.
