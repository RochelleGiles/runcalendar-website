Course

A point-to-point 50K from the Sonoran Desert shore of Lake Pleasant up into the ponderosa pines of the Bradshaw Mountains. The first half runs on rolling smooth dirt forest roads; the back half is rough four-wheel-drive jeep trail climbing steeply to 6,563ft at mile 28.7 before a final 600ft descent to the Crown King Saloon. 6,828ft of gain, 11-hour limit.

About

Run Arizona's classic 50K footrace from Lake Pleasant to the old mining town of Crown King. You'll start on the shores of beautiful Lake Pleasant, run through the heart of the Sonoran Desert and climb your way through scrub brush, junipers and ultimately to the cool pines of the Bradshaw Mountains! From Spines to Pines, the finish line at the historic Crown King Saloon awaits you.
