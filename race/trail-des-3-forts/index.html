About

The Trail Des 3 Forts is a coastal trail running event along the Côte d'Opale. Created by passionate runners for passionate runners, the race takes participants through scenic coastal paths, steep cliff trails, and stunning coastal landscapes. Runners will experience a unique journey from Le Portel to Écault, passing through Équihen-Plage, showcasing the beautiful maritime environment.
