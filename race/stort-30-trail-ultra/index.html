Course

Flat out-and-back along the River Stort and Lee navigations, starting and finishing at Hockerill Sports and Social Club in Bishop's Stortford and turning at 15 miles. Mostly grassy trail and graded gravel footpath with about half a mile of tarmac at each end; muddy in wet weather. Checkpoints every five miles with water, squash, snacks and food.

About

The Stort 30 Trail Ultra is a challenging 30-mile ultramarathon that takes runners along the picturesque River Stort, winding through the border region of Hertfordshire and Essex in the United Kingdom. Traversing varied terrain, this scenic event offers an immersive trail running experience for endurance athletes seeking a scenic and rewarding adventure.
