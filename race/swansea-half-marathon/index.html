Course

A flat, fast half marathon on fully closed roads, starting and finishing in the heart of Swansea and running out towards Mumbles with about five miles of coastline. The route passes Swansea Arena, Brangwyn Hall, Swansea Castle and the Swansea Museum, and climbs just 36 metres over the whole distance. UKA licensed and AIMS certified. A five-mile race and a family fun run run alongside it.

About

The Swansea Half Marathon is an iconic running event that takes participants on a scenic coastal route from the city to the sea. The fast, flat course is ideal for first-time runners, wheelchair athletes, and those chasing a personal best. Runners will enjoy stunning views of the Welsh coastline and an energetic atmosphere with entertainment zones and spectator support throughout.
