About

The Foulées Baudelloises is an annual running event taking place in the charming town of Saint-Baudelle. The race features a flat 10 km course through the town streets, offering participants a scenic and enjoyable running experience. Now in its 15th edition, this event attracts runners of various ages and skill levels.
