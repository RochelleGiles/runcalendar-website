Course

Held at the Georgia International Horse Park in Conyers, with an afternoon start. The 5K run/walk has two water stations on course plus one at the finish. A shorter 1K tasting walk carries three wine sampling stations and a fourth at the finish.

About

The Wicked Wine Run is a fun and unique running/walking event that combines the love of wine with the thrill of a race. Participants can choose between a 1K tasting walk or a 5K run/walk, both of which feature wine sampling stations along the course. The event also includes an after-party with music, games, and more wine.
