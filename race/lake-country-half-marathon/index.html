Course

A point-to-point road course starting at Village Green in downtown Oconomowoc and finishing at City Beach on Lac La Belle. Runners head north along the isthmus between Fowler Lake and Lac La Belle, west through the Village of Lac La Belle on countryside roads, then back along the lake's western edge for a flat finish. Four aid stations; the course closes three hours after the start.

About

The Lake Country Half Marathon and 8 Mile are road races through Oconomowoc, Wisconsin, a city built among the lakes of Waukesha County's Lake Country region, run the same weekend as the Lake Country Challenge triathlon.
