Course

The course finishes at Elephant Hill Estate & Winery. It combines sealed road with hard-packed limestone cycle trail through orchards and past vineyards, plus a section along the Napier waterfront and quiet country roads. The organiser describes it as flat. Neither the homepage nor the course overview states the start town or aid station placement.

About

Take in the best highlights of the Hawke's Bay region, with flat easy running through local cycle trails, quiet country roads and past vineyards and orchards - all finishing at the impressive Elephant Hill Estate & Winery where you can celebrate your efforts in style.
