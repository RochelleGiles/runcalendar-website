Course

Point-to-point on the Arizona Trail from Flagstaff toward the Grand Canyon, passing Hart Prairie, Cedar Ranch, Moqui and Hull aid stations. The 55K covers roughly the first 35 miles of the 100-mile route with about 2,800 feet of gain; the 100-mile gains roughly 7,000 feet and runs overnight into Sunday.

About

This point-to-point ultra runs along the Arizona Trail from Flagstaff toward the Grand Canyon, tracing the old mail and passenger line between the two. Runners choose a 100-mile or 55K distance, joined by a 100-mile mountain bike race and relay divisions; the 55K covers roughly the first 35 miles of the route.
