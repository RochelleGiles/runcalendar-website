Course

About

A St Patrick's Day road race weekend in Manhattan, Kansas, pairing the Marvin Hachmeister 10K with the Shamrock 2 Mile run and walk. Both courses are USATF certified and start and finish in the Aggieville district. The event raises funds for local causes under the Fill the Ville banner.
