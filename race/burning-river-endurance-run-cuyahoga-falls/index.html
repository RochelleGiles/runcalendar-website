About

The Burning River Endurance Run is a point-to-point 100-mile trail ultra between Willoughby Hills and Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio, crossing Cuyahoga Valley National Park and the Buckeye Trail. One of the country's longer-running 100-milers, it also offers 50-mile, relay and marathon options on the same course.
