Course

About

This race starts at the Hacienda Entrance of Almaden Quicksilver Park. The 30K, Trail Marathon, and 50K travel into the Sierra Azul Open Space Preserve. The 30K will summit Bald Mountain, while both the Trail Marathon and 50K will take you up the exclusive Mt. Umunhum.
