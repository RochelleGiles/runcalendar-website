Course

Both the half marathon and the One Medal 5K start and finish at the Event Center at Balloon Fiesta Park in Albuquerque, on a relatively flat and fast course looking out to the Rio Grande, the cottonwood bosque and the Sandia Mountains. Water stations with water, Gatorade and ice sit roughly every 1.5 miles and never more than 2.5 miles apart.
