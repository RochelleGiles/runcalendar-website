Course

A loop course circling the 130-acre Meadowbrook Park and prairie in Urbana. Both the 5K and 10K start and finish at the Garden Pavilion near Race Street and run on mixed terrain, combining park paths with natural prairie trail before returning to the start.

About

Organized by Grand Prairie Friends, this 10K and 5K run crosses restored tallgrass prairie near Urbana, Illinois, named for the rattlesnake master plant native to the preserve. The November race supports local prairie conservation.
