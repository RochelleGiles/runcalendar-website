About

Come celebrate at the ELITEFEATS SUMMER PARTY & 5K! Where the run is optional but the party is mandatory! Enjoy a timed run/jog/walk on the USATF certified course at Eisenhower Park - Field 2 and after-party. Come with a change of clothes if you plan to run and stick around for music, plenty of food and refreshments.
