About

The Marathon Rugenbrucken is a must-run event for any seasoned runner or those seeking a scenic challenge. Situated in an idyllic coastal region, this marathon offers runners the opportunity to take in stunning views of the Rugen Bridge and the Baltic Sea as they push themselves to the finish line. With a rich history and a reputation for an exceptional participant experience, the Marathon Rugenbrucken is a race that should be on every runner's bucket list.
