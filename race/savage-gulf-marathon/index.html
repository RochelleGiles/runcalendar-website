Course

Loop course through the Savage Gulf State Natural Area with two steep descents into the gorge and two climbs back out. Rocky, technical trail with stream crossings, bluff-edge sections and old-growth forest. The field is limited to the first 200 entries, and marathon entrants must have completed a previous marathon or longer.

About

Billed as one of the toughest marathons in the Southeast, this trail race runs through the rugged gorges and hardwood forest of Savage Gulf State Natural Area on the Cumberland Plateau. Expect steep climbs, rocky terrain and dramatic overlooks.
