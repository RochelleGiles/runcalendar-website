Course

The 5K loops from the Campbell Community Center bike path along the south bank, over the Frohnmayer footbridge, through Alton Baker Park, back onto the bike path and across the DeFazio bridge to the finish. The 10K adds an out-and-back northwest along the bike path, turning near Valley River Center. Paved bike paths throughout.

About

Kick off Valentine's season on a sweet note at the Cupids Chocolate Chase Presented by Euphoria Chocolate Company! Enjoy a festive run or walk with friends, family, or your favorite Valentine—and savor a handcrafted Euphoria chocolate waiting for you at the finish line. You won't want to miss the two-person team competition, perfect for pairs looking to add a little friendly fun to their run! Event proceeds will benefit Bags of Love, a local nonprofit supporting children in need. Whether you're racing solo, with a sweetheart, or bringing the whole crew, join us for a fun-filled celebration of movement, community, and delicious local treats.
