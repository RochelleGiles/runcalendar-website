About

Ideally located, in a green setting 5 minutes walk from the city center, the 100km course consists of a 4km loop to be covered 25 times, using the pedestrian paths of the Metz lake and the Grand Pâtural park. A 50km individual and a 100km relay team of 2 to 10 runners are also offered to runners who will set off in the wake of the hundred-borners to share with them the first 12 loops and set foot in the Beyond-the-Marathon.
