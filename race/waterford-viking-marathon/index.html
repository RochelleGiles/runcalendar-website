About

The annual Waterford Viking Marathon takes runners on a journey through Ireland's oldest city, offering a unique opportunity to experience the rich history and culture of this Viking settlement. With a route that starts and finishes in the heart of Waterford, this event promises an unforgettable running experience for participants of all levels.
