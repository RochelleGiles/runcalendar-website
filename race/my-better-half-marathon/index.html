Course

A lapped course along the Lake Washington shoreline at Seward Park. The half marathon is two laps, a 6.9-mile lap with a steep hill followed by a flat 6.2-mile lap; the 10K pairs a 3.7-mile hilly lap with a 2.5-mile flat one. The 5K takes in a short steep hill early on. Aid stations every 2 to 3 miles with water and nutrition.

About

My Better Half Marathon runs on the Lake Washington shoreline at Seward Park in Seattle, with half marathon, 10K, 5K and a two-person half marathon relay. The lapped course pairs a hilly loop with a flat one, so runners meet the climbing early.
