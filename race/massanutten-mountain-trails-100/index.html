About

The Massanutten Mountain Trails 100 sends runners on a rocky, technical figure-eight loop across Massanutten Mountain's ridgelines in Virginia's Shenandoah Valley, starting at Caroline Furnace Lutheran Camp. First run in 1995 and organized by the Virginia Happy Trails Running Club, it carries a 36-hour cutoff.
