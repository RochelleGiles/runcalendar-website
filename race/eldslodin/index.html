About

Embark on a thrilling Viking-inspired journey at Eldslodin, an exhilarating running event that takes you through the mysterious, untamed landscapes of Iceland. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a newcomer to the sport, Eldslodin promises an unforgettable experience that will test your limits and ignite your Viking spirit.
