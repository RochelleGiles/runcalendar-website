About

The Riverbanks Of Conflans is an annual running event organized by the PLM Conflans Athlétisme club and the city of Conflans. The event features a 15km and a 5km race, both taking place on the public roads of Conflans Sainte-Honorine. Runners will get to discover the city's unique features and scenery, including the banks of the Seine river.
