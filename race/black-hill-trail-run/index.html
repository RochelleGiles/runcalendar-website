Course

A figure-of-eight trail course starting and finishing at the Scout Hut in Bere Regis, with the 5K and 10K setting off together and splitting at around 3.5km. The route is entirely on well-worn trails, with boggy and steep sections, low branches, tree roots and the occasional rabbit hole. Trail shoes are recommended. There is no water on the route; water is provided at the finish.

About

Explore the picturesque trails of rural Dorset at the Black Hill Trail Run. This scenic event offers both a 10km and 5km course, allowing runners of all levels to challenge themselves amidst the natural beauty of the British countryside. Whether you're seeking a scenic training run or a chance to conquer a new personal best, the Black Hill Trail Run is an unforgettable running experience.
