Course

Trail festival on the Deene Park Estate near Corby, run over two days. Distances are a 10K, a half marathon and a 25K, with the Quest for the Golden Carrot combining all three into 56.1K. The organiser describes a real mixture of surfaces, woodland and undulations.

About

The Run Rabbit Trail Festival invites runners to explore the stunning grounds of Deene Park Estate near Corby, UK. This unique event offers a variety of challenging trail runs through the picturesque countryside, perfect for seasoned trail runners and those seeking a scenic outdoor adventure. With a focus on community and camaraderie, the Run Rabbit Trail Festival promises an unforgettable weekend in the great British outdoors.
