About

The Hardmoors 30 is a challenging 31-mile trail ultra that traverses the stunning Cleveland Way and Cinder Track. Runners will navigate cliff top paths and scenic landscapes, passing through picturesque locations like Ravenscar, Hayburn Wyke, Crookness, and Scalby Mills near Scarborough.
