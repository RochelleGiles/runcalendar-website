Course

Starts at the Vilano Beach Community Center and Park and winds through the Vilano Beach community, crossing the Vilano Bridge with views over the Intracoastal and the beaches. The 10K adds a loop through the grounds of the Florida School of the Deaf and the Blind. Both distances finish at North Shores Community Park.

About

The Vilano Bridge 5K & 10K Run presented by Reiter Roofing is a popular annual event that takes runners across the iconic Vilano Bridge in St. Augustine, Florida. The race offers both a 5K and 10K distance, providing a challenge for runners of all levels.
