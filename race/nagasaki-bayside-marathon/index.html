About

The Nagasaki Bayside Marathon runs along the waterfront of Nagasaki Bay in Nagasaki, Japan, starting and finishing at Nagasaki Water's Edge Park. Half marathon and 10K distances follow the harbor, giving runners coastal views of the bay throughout the course.
