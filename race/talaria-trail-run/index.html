Course

A 4.5-mile loop of the Bob Trail around the 90-acre Trap Pond in Trap Pond State Park, starting and finishing at the same point. The route runs through hardwood forest, crosses the dam at Raccoon Pond and uses boardwalk bridges over freshwater swamp. A 2-mile walk is offered alongside the race.
