Course

A fast, flat course based at Colwick Park on the grounds of the historic Nottingham Racecourse, using scenic lakeside paths and wide open trails over a mix of road and gravel. Kilometre markers and marshals feature throughout, with chip timing and post-race refreshments at the finish.

About

The Nottingham Running Festival will take place at the iconic Nottingham Racecourse, one of the oldest racing facilities in the country and host of major events in both horse and greyhound racing. The course also takes you along the wide trail paths of Colwick Country Park and around the park's stunning lakes!
