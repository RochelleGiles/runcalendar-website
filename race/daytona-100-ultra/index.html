Course

A point-to-point road race down Florida State Road A1A, the ocean road, from Jacksonville Beach to Ponce Inlet just south of Daytona Beach, finishing at the historic Ponce Inlet Lighthouse. The 100 mile has a 31 hour limit; the 50 mile and 50K start further south at Flagler Beach and must finish by noon on the Sunday.

About

The Daytona 100 Ultra runs from Jacksonville Beach to Ponce Inlet, Florida, offering 50K, 50 Mile, 100 Mile and 100 Mile Relay distances along the coast each December. It's a point-to-point challenge for ultrarunners chasing beachside miles.
