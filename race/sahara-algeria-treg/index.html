Course

A five-stage desert race covering about 222 km with 2,000 m of climbing, on the edge of the Tassili n'Ajjer in the southern Sahara. The event runs from 3 to 12 December and finishes at the Djanet oasis. The field takes in competitive runners, leisure runners and endurance hikers alike.

About

The Sahara Algeria Treg is a trail running event in one of the most beautiful regions of the South Sahara, on the edge of the Tassili des Ajjers. Whether you are an experienced desert runner or looking for a first-time experience, this event is made for you. With 5 stages covering 222 km and 2000 m+, the event caters to both fast runners seeking a challenge and endurance hikers. After the effort, the serenity of the Djanet oasis will allow you to recover in peace.
