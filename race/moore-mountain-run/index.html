Course

Run in Moore Park above Klamath Falls, with over 90% of the course on high-quality singletrack and sweeping views of Upper Klamath Lake and Mount Shasta. The half marathon carries about 1,600 ft of climbing, while the 5K is short but technically challenging with little elevation gain. A 30K is also offered.

About

The Moore Mountain Run's 30K, half marathon and 5K climb the forested Moore Mountain trail system above Klamath Falls, Oregon, hosted by the Linkville Lopers running club, with singletrack switchbacks through high-desert forest.
