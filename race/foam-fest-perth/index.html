About

The 5K Foam Fest is an exciting obstacle course event featuring 25 unique obstacles designed to get participants wet, muddy, and covered in foam. Participants will navigate through challenges like inflatable waterslides, slip and slides, mud pits, and foam zones, creating an unforgettable and adventurous running experience.
