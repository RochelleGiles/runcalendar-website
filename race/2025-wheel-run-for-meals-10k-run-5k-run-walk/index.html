Course

A flat, chip-timed out-and-back starting at Maple Avenue and Shore Drive in Beloit. The route crosses the Ken Hendricks Memorial Bridge then follows the Steve Gregg Memorial Path along the Rock River to Riverside Park and back. Both the 5K and 10K start at 9:00 a.m.

About

Wheel Run for Meals is a 10K run and 5K run/walk, that benefits Beloit Meals on Wheels. Whether you are an elite athlete, run for fun, or just looking for a challenging walk, this race is all three. Our scenic course begins at our new location on the corner of Maple Avenue and Shore Drive, over the Ken Hendricks Memorial Bridge, then picks up the Steve Gregg Memorial Path on the Rock River and around the lagoon at Riverside Park and back. This is a very flat and fast course.
