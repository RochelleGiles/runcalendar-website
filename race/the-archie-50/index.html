About

The Archie 50 is a 50km mountain trail race held on the same Victorian High Country weekend as the Oscars100 Hut 2 Hut Challenge, based at Mt Buller. It is one of five events in the Hut 2 Hut Trail Festival, which also takes in a 23km, a 10km, a night run and Bob's Round.
