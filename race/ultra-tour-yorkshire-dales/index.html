Course

Self-navigated ultra starting and finishing at the Devonshire Institute in Grassington, with 100K, 50K and 25K routes through the Yorkshire Dales past Malham Tarn, Buckden Pike and sections of the Dales Way. Underfoot is a mix of runnable trail, boggy moorland and open route choice between checkpoints. Marshals are minimal and map reading and fell navigation are required.

About

Discover the stunning landscapes of the Yorkshire Dales on the Ultra Tour Yorkshire Dales (UTYD) trail challenge. Offering routes of 100km, 50km, and 25km, this event invites runners to explore the picturesque countryside and challenging terrain that make this region a beloved destination for outdoor enthusiasts. Whether you're seeking a grueling ultra-distance adventure or a more approachable trail run, the UTYD has an option to suit your running goals.
