Course

Loop from the Project Chimps sanctuary in Morganton, Georgia, combining the entrance gravel road with the Green, White and Yellow forest trails. Off-road underfoot throughout, with roots, rocks, stairs and steep pitches for over 700 feet of climbing. A shorter one-mile Knuckle-walker route uses the gravel road and the East Loop only.
