Course

All three races start and finish at the south end of Bender Fields Park. The 5K runs city streets through downtown Lynden; the 10K adds a longer residential loop taking in the new Lynden trail to the hanging bridge; the half marathon covers the 10K route plus a further loop beyond the main perimeter before returning to the park.

About

A community run and walk through Lynden, Washington, starting and finishing at the south end of Bender Fields Park. Half marathon, 10K and 5K distances are run on city streets and local trail, chip timed, with recognition for the leading finishers.
