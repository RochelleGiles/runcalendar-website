Course

All races start and finish in Lons-le-Saunier in the Jura, climbing the limestone reculées above the town on forest trails, vineyard tracks and the old PLM rail path. Routes take in the Ermitage de Conliège, Baume-les-Messieurs, Montaigu and a long flight of steps, with aid stations on course. Glass is banned at aid stations, so runners carry their own cup or flask.

About

The Trail des Reculées runs out of Lons-le-Saunier into the limestone amphitheatres of the Jura, climbing between vineyards, forest and clifftop viewpoints. Four races share the morning, from a 13km introduction to a 55km route over Baume-les-Messieurs and Montaigu, each capped at a few hundred runners.
