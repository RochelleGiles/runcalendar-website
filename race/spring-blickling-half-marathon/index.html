Course

Starts and finishes at Blickling Hall and loops the Norfolk estate. The route is multi-terrain, mixing road, adopted road and hard-wearing tracks, and is uneven and narrow in places.

About

This is probably one of the most scenic Half Marathons in the UK. The race starts and finishes right in front of the iconic Blickling Hall, there couldn't be a better start and finish line!
