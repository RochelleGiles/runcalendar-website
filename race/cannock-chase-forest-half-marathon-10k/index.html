About

Runners will be enchanted by the Cannock Chase Forest Half Marathon & 10K, set amidst the stunning natural beauty of the United Kingdom's Cannock Chase. Challenging woodland trails wind through this picturesque forest, offering a unique and rewarding racing experience for competitors of all levels. Whether tackling the half marathon or the 10K, participants can expect a scenic and unforgettable event.
