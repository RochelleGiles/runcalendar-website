Course

Starts at the Memorial Park Monument by the Robert H Storz Drive roundabout in Omaha. Runners head east around the roundabout, turn onto Underwood Avenue and loop the park once for the 2.5K walk or twice for the 5K.

About

Memorial Park Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving morning 5K held at Memorial Park in Omaha's Dundee neighborhood. It's a neighborhood tradition each November that draws families out before the holiday meal.
