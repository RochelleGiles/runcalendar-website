About

Touroparc Run is a unique running event that takes place inside a wildlife park, offering runners an extraordinary experience of racing alongside various animals. Participants can choose from multiple race distances, including a 20km, 10km, and 5km route that winds through the park's scenic paths. The event combines athletic challenge with wildlife conservation, supporting Touroparc Conservation's efforts to protect endangered species.
