Course

A cross-country style 10K on the trails around Needwood Lake, starting and finishing at Needwood Lake Circle in Derwood. The organiser describes an undulating course over hills, mud, grass and trail. A quarter-mile Young Run precedes the 10K.

About

MCRRC's Lake Needwood Trail 10K follows wooded trails around Lake Needwood in Rock Creek Regional Park near Derwood, Maryland, part of the club's autumn cross-country series with a mix of dirt paths and rolling terrain.
