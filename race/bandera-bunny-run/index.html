About

The Bandera Bunny Run/Walk 5K is an annual community event organized by the Arthur Nagel Community Clinic to raise funds for low-income patients needing specialist care. The race starts and finishes at the Village of Hope Events Center in Bandera, TX, welcoming runners and walkers of all ages with prizes awarded in multiple categories.
