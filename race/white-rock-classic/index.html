Course

Both distances start and finish at Shores Lake Pavilion and run largely on well-maintained forest roads with sections of singletrack. The 50K loops north on county roads to the White Rock Mountain Rim Trail for views around the mountain, then returns on the Ozark Highlands Trail; the 25K takes a shorter line using the same Rim Trail and Ozark Highlands Trail sections. Support is minimal and there is no entry fee.

About

The White Rock Classic is a 25K and 50K trail ultramarathon in the Ozark National Forest near Cass, Arkansas, organized by the Arkansas Ultra Running Association. The course climbs rugged forest trails around White Rock Mountain.
