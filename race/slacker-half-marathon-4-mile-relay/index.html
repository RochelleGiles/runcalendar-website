Course

Point-to-point downhill course starting near the Loveland Ski Area at about 10,880 ft and descending to the finish in Georgetown at 8,400 ft. The route runs on a mix of dirt and paved roads past wetlands, waterfalls and old mining sites, with views of the Continental Divide. Water stations are placed throughout and stay open until the last finisher.

About

Seize the chance to earn your bragging rights as a true Slacker! Embark on this exhilarating journey starting at the base of Loveland Ski Area, perched at an impressive elevation of 10,880 ft. Brace yourself for an unforgettable descent to 8,400ft, followed by post-race festivities in charming historic Georgetown. Picture an abundance of freebies, locally crafted beer, post race pampering, and entertainment to celebrate your achievement!
