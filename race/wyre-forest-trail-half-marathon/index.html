Course

A trail half marathon and shorter Super Seven through the Wyre Forest, starting and finishing at the Callow Hill Visitor Centre near Kidderminster. The route takes in the forest's ancient trees and landscapes and rolls over many contours, demanding sure footing on the climbs and descents. Fully signed and marshalled with four water and sweet stations, medical support and a tail runner.
