About

The Valmalenco Ultradistance Trail is a captivating ultra-running challenge set against the stunning backdrop of the Italian Alps. Runners will test their endurance on a demanding course that winds through breathtaking mountain scenery, offering a true test of both physical and mental fortitude. This event promises an unforgettable adventure for seasoned ultramarathoners seeking a new challenge.
