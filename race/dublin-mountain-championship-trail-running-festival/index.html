About

The Dublin Mountain Championship Trail Running Festival is a must-attend event for trail enthusiasts. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Dublin Mountains, this challenging course offers runners a chance to test their mettle on diverse terrain, from rugged trails to breathtaking vistas. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this festival promises an unforgettable running experience.
