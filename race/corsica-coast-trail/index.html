About

The Corsica Coast Trail is a multi-stage trail running event along the stunning Corsican coastline. The course features diverse terrain including rocky coastal paths, lagoons, coastal waterways, small coves and large open bays, offering runners a unique opportunity to discover Corsica's preserved shoreline. Participants can experience the wild and natural beauty of Corsica under the autumn sun, traversing challenging seaside trails and breathtaking beaches while enjoying the special camaraderie that only a multi-stage trail event can provide.
