About

The Sparkassen Metropolmarathon is a popular running event in the Nuremberg metropolitan region, connecting the cities of Fürth and Nuremberg with attractive running routes. The event features a variety of races, including the marathon, half marathon, 10K, and a kids' marathon, offering something for runners of all levels. The course showcases the scenic landscapes and historic landmarks of the two cities, creating an enjoyable and memorable experience for participants.
