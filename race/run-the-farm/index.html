Course

A loop starting and finishing at the farmyard at Muscoot Farm, Katonah. The fall course opens with about half a mile of farm road, then roughly a mile of singletrack, two wider forest-meadow sections near miles 1.5 and 2, singletrack again from mile 2 to about mile 4, then meadows and farm roads to the finish. Direction reverses between the spring and fall editions.

About

Run The Farm is a series of trail races at Muscoot Farm in Katonah/Somers, NY. The races include a 5 mile trail race and two "Kids Run The Farm" age appropriate trail races.
