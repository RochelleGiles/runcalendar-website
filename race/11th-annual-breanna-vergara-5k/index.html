About

The Breanna Vergara 5K & Color Run, now past its 11th running, is the biggest annual fundraiser for The Breanna Vergara Foundation, held on the tree-lined streets of Miami Lakes, Florida. The event honors Breanna "Bre-Bre" Vergara and funds initiatives promoting awareness and early detection of heart conditions in children.
