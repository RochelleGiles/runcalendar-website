Course

A single loop of about five miles run entirely off road, through as much muck, mud and water as the organisers can find. Expect hills and multiple water crossings, with the depth at each crossing depending on the weather in the days beforehand.

About

Westchester's oldest trail race, hosted by the Taconic Road Runners Club at Yorktown Heights, New York. The five mile single-loop course runs off road through mud, hills and several water crossings. How deep those crossings run depends on the weather in the days before the race.
