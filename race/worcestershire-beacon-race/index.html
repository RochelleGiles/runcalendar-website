Course

A demanding all-terrain race of about 7 miles over the Malvern Hills, with steep ascents and descents on varying, often unstable ground. The route mixes muddy grass, rocky woodland trail, open hillside and a rocky descent from the Worcestershire Beacon summit at 1,395ft, with a level section circling the quarries. Single-file pinch points on the sharp climbs, and views over four counties and into Wales.

About

A fell race from Malvern up to the Worcestershire Beacon, the highest point in the Malvern Hills at over 400 metres. The course climbs steeply out of the town before an out-and-back return down the same ground.
