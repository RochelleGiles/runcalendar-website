Course

Run from the Grand Targhee resort base at Alta on the mountain's high-elevation ridgeline trails. The 50K goes on the Saturday; the 22K and 10K climb Peaked Mountain on the Sunday through wildflower meadows and aspen groves with views across to the Tetons. A 1K kids race loops the base area.

About

The Grand Targhee Wildflower Run climbs and descends trails around Grand Targhee Resort on the western slope of the Teton Range in Alta, Wyoming. Runners choose the 10K, 22K, or 50K distance through alpine meadows in full summer bloom.
