Course

Trail routes from Treverton College near Mooi River, on a mix of open grasslands, pockets of forest, rolling farmland and dams. The long course opens with a steady climb across grassland into forest; the medium course flows back down through small forests and past dams. Aid stations run from one on the short course to three on the long. No compulsory kit.
