Course

Races are run on the high-alpine trails above Palisades Tahoe in Olympic Valley, from the Ascent vertical climb through 11K, 18K, 23K and 46K distances. Youth and kids races and the multi-race Triple Crown and Iron Face challenges are held over the same weekend.

About

The Broken Arrow Skyrace sends runners up the steep ski slopes of Palisades Tahoe in the Sierra Nevada, part of the international Skyrunning circuit. Distances from 11K to 46K climb technical alpine terrain with major vertical gain.
