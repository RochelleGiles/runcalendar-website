Course

Out-and-back from Pine Terrace, Darlington along the Heritage Trail, described by the organiser as an open, mostly gravel trail. A slight incline runs out to the half marathon and 8 km turnarounds, giving a downhill return to the finish. Water stations are on course and runners are encouraged to carry their own hydration.

About

The Darlington Half Marathon in Australia is a must-run event for runners seeking a scenic and challenging course. Hosted by the West Australian Marathon Club, this event offers both a 21.1km half marathon and an 8km option, catering to runners of all abilities. Runners can expect a picturesque route through the heart of Darlington, showcasing the region's natural beauty.
