About

The Carrum Coast Guard Around the River Fun Run is an annual community event supporting local marine rescue services. Participants can choose between 6 km and 12 km running or walking routes, with a special children's race for under 7's. The event offers a family-friendly atmosphere and supports the vital work of the Australian Volunteer Coast Guard.
