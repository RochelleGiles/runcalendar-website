About

Dash with a Splash funds will benefit Everyone Cooperating to Help Others' (ECHO) housing and homeless programs. ECHO is a nonprofit, faith-community sponsored charity organization. ECHO is dedicated to meeting housing, food and other basic human needs and addressing the barriers to self-sufficiency.
