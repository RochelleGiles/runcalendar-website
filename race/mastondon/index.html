Course

Roughly 10 miles on the Maston Trail Network, BLM land between Tumalo and Redmond in Central Oregon. Start and finish are at the Newcomb trailhead and the course is largely singletrack through juniper high desert, with views of the Deschutes River and the Cascades. Relatively flat at about 400ft of elevation change, with one aid station near 4.25 miles.

About

FootZone is proud to produce the popular early season 10ish mile Trail Race: Mastondon! This race takes place on the BLM trail network called Maston in between Tumalo and Redmond in the shadow of Cline Butte. There are views of the Deschutes river, mountains of Central Oregon, and is just a cool trail through the juniper trees and high desert landscape. A relatively flat course with only about 400' of elevation change, this race is a good early season event to see how the engine is running without too much demand on the early season legs.
