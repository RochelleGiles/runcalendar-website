About

The IIT Bombay Half Marathon runs through the institute's leafy Powai campus, one of Mumbai's greener corners near Powai Lake and the Sanjay Gandhi National Park. The annual event draws more than 4,000 runners across 21.1K, 10K and 5K distances.
