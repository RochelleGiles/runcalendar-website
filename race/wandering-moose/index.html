Course

A net-downhill point-to-point starting high on the Kettle Valley Railway grade above Kelowna at about 1,250m and finishing at the Okanagan Recreation Center. After flat KVR running the route drops onto loamy, technical singletrack through Myra-Bellevue Provincial Park, with roots, rocks and creek crossings. 12km, 25km and 35km options plus a 1km kids race; two checkpoint aid stations on the longer distances.
