About

Ultra Race Romania is a challenging 250km (155 miles) multi-stage self-supported race divided into 6 stages through Romania's raw wilderness. The event combines ultrarunning with local culture and traditions, featuring post-race activities including visits to the famous Peles Castle and Brasov. Participants carry all their survival supplies for the week-long adventure in a unique setting where legends meet.
