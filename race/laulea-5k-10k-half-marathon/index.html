Course

Loop course starting and finishing at Wai Kai in Ewa Beach. Runners head out on the makai lane of Keone'ula Boulevard to a turnaround at Waipana Street; the 5K returns the same way while the 10K and half marathon continue through Hoakalei onto Geiger Road, the half extending to Coral Sea Road. Surfaces are asphalt and concrete throughout. Aid stations roughly every mile with water and electrolytes.
