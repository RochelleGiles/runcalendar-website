Course

Lapped course on the 3.8km Red Star Raceway motor circuit beside the N12 outside Delmas. The marathon is just over 11 laps of flat tarmac with no shade, with support tables and toilets set around the track.

About

Red Star Marathon is a road race near Delmas, Mpumalanga, run in the vicinity of the Red Star Raceway motorsport circuit. Entrants can choose 5km, 10km, 21km or 42km distances on the highveld roads around the venue.
