Course

Run from the Johnstown trailhead of the TJ Evans Bike Path in Johnstown, Ohio, with the marathon, half, quarter marathon and 5K starting in staggered waves from 6:30am. Timing ends and the course closes at noon, giving the marathon 5.5 hours and the half 4.5.

About

Join USA Race Timing & Event Management for a festive Christmas in July celebration at the Halfway to the Holidays Marathon, 1/2 Marathon, 1/4 Marathon, 5k, and Kids Fun Run! Embrace the holiday spirit early with jolly decorations, cheerful music, and fun-themed surprises while racing along scenic courses. Whether you're chasing a personal best or just enjoying the festive vibes, this event promises holiday cheer and fitness fun for all ages. Don your best holiday attire, and don't miss the chance to celebrate halfway to the most wonderful time of the year!
