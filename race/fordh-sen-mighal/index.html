Course

A multi-terrain point-to-point of about 11 miles following St Michael's Way from Lelant to Marazion, taking in Trencrom and Ludgvan. Registration is at St Uny's Church in Lelant with the start at Lelant railway station at 10am and a five-hour cut-off.

About

Fordh Sen Mighal (Cornish for St Michael's Way) follows the ancient pilgrim route across Cornwall from St Ives towards St Michael's Mount, one of the oldest trails in Britain, run each December by Bys Vyken Events.
