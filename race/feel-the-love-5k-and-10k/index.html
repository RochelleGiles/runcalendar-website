About

The Feel the Love 5K and 10K is a Valentine's weekend road race at Wilson Farm Park in Wayne, Pennsylvania, hosted by the Bryn Mawr Running Club. Both distances run from the park, with a kids fun run beforehand and a virtual 5K option. Proceeds go to the Parkinson's Disease Foundation at Jefferson Hospital and the club.
