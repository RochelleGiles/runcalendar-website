About

The Hanover 7 Miler is part of the York Road Runners Club Winter Series, featuring a main 7-mile event and a one-mile fun run starting 30 minutes earlier. The race starts at 9:00 AM from 600 Fairview Dr., Hanover, PA 17331, with series points awarded based on age group placement.
