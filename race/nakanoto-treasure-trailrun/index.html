About

Nakanoto Treasure Trailrun crosses the hills of Nakanoto Town on Japan's Noto Peninsula in Ishikawa Prefecture, an area recognized by the FAO as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System for its traditional Satoyama and Satoumi landscapes. Runners choose between a 25.7km and a 47.8km trail course through this rural terrain.
