Course

A single flat loop of South Park, starting and finishing on the main field. The majority of the route runs on grass, with the middle section following hardstanding paths past the lake and the play park. The grass can turn muddy in wet weather.

About

Race for Life Darlington is held in South Park, opened in 1853 as one of England's earliest public parks and still home to its Victorian lake. The 5K and 3K routes cover the grounds for Cancer Research UK.
