About

The Steinenberger Waldlauf is a scenic trail running event through the peaceful forests surrounding the charming town of Steinenberg. Runners of all levels can enjoy the picturesque routes, with options ranging from a 5K to a challenging half-marathon. This community-driven race is a highlight of the summer running season, drawing participants from near and far.
