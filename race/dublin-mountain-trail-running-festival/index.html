About

The Dublin Mountain Trail Running Festival (DMTF) will be held in a new location with all new courses from 9 to 10 October 2026. Now based from Ballinascorney, distances include 120km (~4400m elevation), 60km (~ 2200m elevation) and 20km (~ 760m elevation). The 120km course consists of two loops of the 60km course.
