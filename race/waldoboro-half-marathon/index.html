Course

Road half marathon starting at 1885 Atlantic Hwy, Waldoboro, Maine and finishing at Odd Alewives Farm Brewery, run on local roads without crossing Route 1. The organiser bills it as New England's hardest half, with about 1,400 ft of gain, 45 percent of the course uphill and seven hills over 10 percent. There are no official water stations, so runners carry their own fluids.
