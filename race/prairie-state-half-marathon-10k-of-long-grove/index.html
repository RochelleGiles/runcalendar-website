Course

Start and finish at Brothers Field on Old McHenry Road, Long Grove. The courses run almost entirely on paved streets through downtown Long Grove and the surrounding neighbourhoods, passing under the historic covered bridge. The half marathon and 10K are USATF certified. Water stations run throughout, with sports drink on the half marathon.

About

You're invited to the 5th annual Oktober Fest After Party at Brothers' Field featuring gourmet hot-off-the-grill brats and beer (21+). You can also run/walk a 5K distance by signing up for the Oktoberfest 5K Run/Walk instead of the Prairie State Half Marathon & 10K. Both races will take place on the same day at the same time.
