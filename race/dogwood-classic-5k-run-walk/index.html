Course

A USATF-certified loop course on Cherokee Boulevard, beginning and ending at the west parking lot at 2250 Cherokee Boulevard. The 5K wave runs between 8:00 and 9:15am and the kids run at 7:30am. No hill or aid station detail is published.

About

The Knoxville Track Club stages this USATF-certified 5K as a loop on Cherokee Boulevard in west Knoxville, starting and finishing at the west parking lot. A kids run plus virtual 5K and kids mile options are also offered, and the race counts toward the club Grand Prix series.
