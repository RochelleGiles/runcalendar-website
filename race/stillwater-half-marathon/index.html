About

The Stillwater Half Marathon takes participants along the beautiful Gateway Trail and Brown's Creek Trail, through historic Stillwater, ending at Pioneer Park with panoramic views of the St. Croix River valley. It offers half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances on a fully paved course.
