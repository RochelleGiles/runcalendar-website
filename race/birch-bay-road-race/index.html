Course

A road race along the shores of Birch Bay, starting and finishing at North Bay Community Church in Blaine. Aid stations with water, gels and electrolyte drink sit roughly every two miles on the 15K and 30K; the 5K carries none on course. Portable toilets are placed every 2 to 3 miles.

About

Birch Bay Road Race offers a 5k, 15k and 30k distance–something for everyone! Run along the beautiful shores of Birch Bay at the Birch Bay Road Race! You'll spot eagles and herons as you run or walk along these amazing courses. Birch Bay is a seaside village close to the Canadian border, just two hours from Seattle and an hour and a half from Vancouver, BC.
