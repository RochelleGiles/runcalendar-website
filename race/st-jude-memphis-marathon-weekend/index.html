Course

Road races through central Memphis, passing the Beale Street entertainment district and running through the St. Jude Children's Research Hospital campus, where patients and families line the course. Marathon and half share an 8 a.m. start with a 6.5-hour course limit; the 10K and 5K start at 7 a.m.

About

St. Jude Memphis Marathon Weekend unites athletes, volunteers and supporters from across the country for a common goal — to support the lifesaving work of St. Jude. Families never receive a bill from St. Jude for treatment, travel, housing, or food — so they can focus on helping their child live.
