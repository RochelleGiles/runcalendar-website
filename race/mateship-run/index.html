Course

All races start and finish on the foreshore in front of the Currumbin RSL, 165 Duringan Street, Currumbin. Distances are 11.11km, 5km and a 2.5km junior event. Four aid stations along the route carry water and sports drink, including near Currumbin Creek Northside Beach, the Dune Cafe and the Thrower Drive boat ramp.

About

Mateship Run is a Currumbin/Palm Beach community event raising funds for RSL Queensland's veteran services. The signature distance is 11.11km, a nod to the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month, with 5km and 2.5km options also available.
