About

Explore the urban landscape of Caudry at the annual Urban Trail de Caudry! This unique running event takes participants on a scenic tour through the city's historic streets and hidden alleyways, offering a one-of-a-kind challenge for adventurous runners. With a mix of paved and off-road terrain, the Urban Trail de Caudry promises an exhilarating and unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
