About

The Wheeler Mission Drumstick Dash is a Thanksgiving morning run through the Broad Ripple neighborhood of Indianapolis. Runners can choose the 2.6 mile or 4.3 mile distance, with proceeds benefiting Wheeler Mission's work with people experiencing homelessness.
