About

The Skukuza Half Marathon runs through Kruger National Park, sharing the bush with elephant, lion, rhino, buffalo and leopard. Park rangers and a helicopter patrol the route for safety, and the roughly 1,500 to 2,000 runners also have a 5km fun run option. Entry proceeds support Kruger's anti-poaching efforts.
