Course

The 18km Grand Traverse is a loop starting and finishing at the Redwood Street Gateway Carpark, Blenheim, on forest tracks in Wither Hills Farm Park (Forest Hills, Gentle Annie, Split Apple, Mt Vernon, Cobb Cottage and Mapp tracks). The 11km Fun Run/Walk follows a similar loop and the 2km Kids Challenge is out-and-back on Mapp Track. No water is provided on course; three water points sit slightly off route.

About

Mt Vernon Grand Traverse, an 18km hill run up Mt Vernon following the boundaries of the Wither Hills Farm park. With 950 metres of climbing and ascent to 422 metres this is a challenging run!
