About

The Darrel Chapman Fun Run offers runners a scenic tour of Lismore, NSW, with a choice of distances including a 21km half marathon, 10km fun run, and 4km fun run/walk. This community event celebrates the life and spirit of Darrel Chapman, a beloved local who inspired countless people to get active and enjoy the great outdoors. Runners of all levels are welcome to join in the fun and camaraderie.
