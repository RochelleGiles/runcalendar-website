Course

A three-day west-to-east stage race along the Jurassic Coast: Charmouth to Portland, Portland to Lulworth Cove, then Lulworth Cove to Shell Bay at Studland, including a full circuit of Portland. Coastal footpath, beaches and tracks with sustained hills. Self-navigated, with checkpoints roughly every 10 km.

About

Explore the dramatic Jurassic Coast of Dorset on the Jurassic Coast Challenge, a captivating endurance event along the scenic Dorset Coast Path. Runners will be treated to spectacular ocean vistas and challenging hills as they navigate this stunning natural landscape, making for an unforgettable adventure.
