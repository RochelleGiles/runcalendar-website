About

Step into the embrace of nature at the Lake Conestee 8k Trail Race, where the earth beneath your feet is alive with the rhythm of the wild. This race is more than just a physical challenge—it's an invitation to reconnect with the natural world. Set within the tranquil beauty of Lake Conestee Nature Park, the race offers a soulful experience for all runners, whether you're a seasoned athlete or a casual wanderer.
