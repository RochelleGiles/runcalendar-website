About

Lavy des Cimes is a vertical kilometer race on the western slope of Mont Semnoz above Annecy, climbing roughly 1,030m over 4.7km. Friends of a local trail runner founded the event as a mountain tribute race, and it now draws several hundred entrants each early December.
