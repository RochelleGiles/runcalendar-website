About

The first Vučko Trail 100+ km trail. Great effort and hard work has been put into it so it could see the light of the day-precisely, 8 years. Now we invite you to test your abilities and let us guide you through this unforgettable journey. Bjelašnica and Visočica , Bosnia and Herzegovina have been connected and separated by Rakitnica on this place through milleniums and centuries. Is there enough fear, hope and respect in you for VT 100k?
