Course

Starts and finishes on Vermijo at the Colorado Springs Pioneers Museum. The marathon heads west on Colorado Ave, drops into America the Beautiful Park, then follows the Greenway Trail north through Monument Valley Park — cement for the first mile, hard-pack pea gravel, then paved surface. Marathon, half, 5K and Kids K.
