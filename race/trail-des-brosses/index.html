About

Trail des Brosses runs through the forests around Chantraine in the Vosges, following varied singletrack over rocks and scree at a moderate difficulty. The race is centred on the Equ'crin d'Olima equestrian centre, keeping runners off tarmac and directly in the surrounding woodland. Distances range from a night race up to 50 km.
