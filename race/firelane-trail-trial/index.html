Course

An out-and-back time trial on Firelane 1 in Forest Park. Runners climb 2.23 miles to the top, tag a gate and descend the same route for 4.45 miles in total, with grades over 20 percent in places. The course crosses the Wildwood and Leif Erikson trails. Start and finish are at the Firelane 1 lower trailhead near 4332 Northwest St Helens Road.

About

A time-trial trail race on Firelane 1 in Forest Park featuring an out-and-back course with 10 runners starting every 10 minutes in wave format. The 4.45-mile roundtrip course includes 1,266 feet of elevation gain and loss, with the fastest time winning.
