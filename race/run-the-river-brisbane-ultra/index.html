Course

A looped riverside course based at the Queensland Maritime Museum in Brisbane, running laps along the Brisbane River with night-time city views. The 100 km can be run solo or as a team relay, and a Midnight Marathon runs on the same loop.

About

Explore the stunning Brisbane River on foot at the Run The River Ultra, featuring a challenging 100km solo race, a 100km team event, and a captivating Midnight Marathon. Runners will immerse themselves in the vibrant city's natural landscapes as they push their limits along this picturesque Australian course.
