Course

A 10km off-road run starting and finishing at the Taylors Mistake beach car park, about 15km east of Christchurch, on the Godley Head peninsula. The route uses well-maintained coastal track, wooden boardwalks, steps and gates, plus a grassed farm track with uneven, rutted 4WD sections, rocks and tussock. A Kids Dash of roughly 700-1200m is also held.

About

The Boulder Bay Classic is a 10km off-road run on the Godley Head peninsula near Christchurch, starting and finishing at the Taylors Mistake beach car park. The route follows well-maintained coastal track, wooden boardwalks and steps, and a grassed farm track. A Kids Dash of roughly 700-1200m is also held on the day.
