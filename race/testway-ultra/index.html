About

A point-to-point trail ultra along the Test Way, from the Linkenholt Activity Centre near Andover down the chalk stream valley to Totton on the edge of Southampton Water. Two distances, 50 miles and 40 miles, run by And-Over Trail Events and now past its tenth running. Entries are on SiEntries.
