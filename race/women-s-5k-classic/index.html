About

We are an all-volunteer, grassroots organization that founded the Women's 5K Classic back in 1993. Our mission is two-fold: To promote fitness among women; To support women with Breast & Gynecologic (cervical, ovarian, uterine, vaginal & vulvar) Cancers and their families and to contribute funding for related programs and research in the Lehigh Valley.
