Course

Starts in front of the Red Rock Canyon visitor center and finishes in the village of Blue Diamond. The route threads Aztec sandstone canyons and washes past Calico Hills and Sandstone Quarry, crossing Red Rock and Cottonwood Valley; the 50K splits off after Blue Diamond over the 'Hurl' climb. Aid stations sit 5.5 to 8.5 miles apart.

About

The Red Rock Canyon 100k | 50k is an endurance foot race in the shadows of the Red Rock Escarpment just outside of Las Vegas, NV. The course will take runners from Red Rock visitor center through ancient Aztec sandstone canyons & washes; across quarry's & around limestone formations millions of years old. The 100k course spans the entire distance of Red Rock and Cottonwood Valley before reaching the bottom of Satan's Escalator and returning to the town of Blue Diamond to finish.
