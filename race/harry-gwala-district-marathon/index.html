Course

A point-to-point road race finishing at Umzimkhulu TVET College. The 42.2km starts on the R612 at Highflats and the 21.1km at Ixopo, while the 10km and 5km start at the college itself. Four distances are offered across the Harry Gwala District of KwaZulu-Natal.

About

Harry Gwala District Marathon is an annual road race through South Africa's Harry Gwala District in KwaZulu-Natal, based around Umzimkhulu. It offers marathon, half marathon, 10km and 5km distances and has become a fixture for the region's top distance runners.
