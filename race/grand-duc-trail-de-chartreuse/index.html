About

Immerse yourself in the breathtaking trails of the Chartreuse Massif at the Grand Duc Trail race. This challenging yet scenic event takes runners through the heart of the French Alps, offering stunning vistas and a true test of endurance. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or seeking a new adventure, the Grand Duc Trail promises an unforgettable mountain running experience.
