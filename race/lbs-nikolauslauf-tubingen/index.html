About

The LBS-Nikolauslauf in Tübingen is a beloved annual running event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the historic university town. Participants can look forward to a festive atmosphere, with the race taking place in the weeks leading up to Christmas and featuring a unique Nikolaus (Santa Claus) theme. This is a must-do event for runners seeking a fun and memorable running experience.
