Course

Unmarked trail routes from Chop Gate Village Hall in the North York Moors, navigated with the organiser's printed maps and route descriptions. The 28-mile marathon has about 5,600 ft of ascent and the 16.5-mile half about 3,630 ft; checkpoints carry refreshments.

About

Tackle the rugged Wainstones Trail in North Yorkshire at the Hardmoors 26.2 Trail Races. This challenging event offers a Marathon, Half Marathon, and 10km distance, showcasing the stunning moorland landscapes and technical trails that make the Hardmoors series a must for adventurous runners. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply want to immerse yourself in the wild beauty of this corner of the UK, this is an event not to be missed.
