Course

A two-day 66-mile out-and-back along the North Downs Way, running Farnham to Redhill on day one and back on day two. The trail crosses woodland, rolling fields and villages on centuries-old tracks, and in winter ranges from frozen ground to deep mud. Aid stations, hot meals and luggage transfer are provided; single-day entries are also available.

About

The Pilgrim Challenge is a captivating ultra-marathon event that takes runners through the stunning landscapes of the United Kingdom's legendary North Downs Way. Participants can choose between a one-day 33-mile course or a more grueling two-day 66-mile challenge, offering an unforgettable test of endurance against the backdrop of this historic trail.
