About

La Nuit Blanche du Pilat is a unique night trail running event set in the magical winter landscape of the Pilat Massif. Runners experience a breathtaking nocturnal trail through snow-covered forests and mountains, creating an unforgettable winter running adventure that challenges participants with cold temperatures and potentially snowy conditions.
