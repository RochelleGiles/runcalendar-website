About

Explore the stunning Yorkshire Dales at the Malham Half Marathon, part of the renowned Due North Trail Series. This scenic half-marathon course winds through the picturesque village of Malham and the surrounding natural beauty of North Yorkshire, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for trail running enthusiasts.
