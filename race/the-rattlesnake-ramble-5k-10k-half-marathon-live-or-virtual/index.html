Course

Three trail distances inside China Camp State Park, all starting from the Miwok Meadows staging area in Santa Venetia. About

The Rattlesnake Ramble is a trail race in China Camp State Park near San Rafael, California, with 5K, 10K and half marathon distances starting from the Miwok Meadows staging area. The courses are technical in places and include singletrack, and each distance can also be entered virtually.
