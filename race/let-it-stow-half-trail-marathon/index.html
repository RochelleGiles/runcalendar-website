Course

A half marathon on well-trodden trails through the Mid-Suffolk countryside, starting and finishing at Stowmarket Football Club on Greens Meadow. The route mixes woodland, meadows and quiet country lanes with a number of road crossings, and is marked with arrows and hi-vis tape. Cupless race: water for refills at start and finish only.
