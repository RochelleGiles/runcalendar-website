Course

A point-to-point marathon on rough woodland trail from the Lake Superior Zoo in Duluth to the Munger Trailhead in Carlton, with 2,969 ft of ascent and 2,566 ft of descent. The route covers half of the Minnesota Voyageur Ultramarathon course plus extra mileage, over rugged singletrack with roots, rocks and bridges that turn slippery.

About

The Eugene Curnow Trail Marathon is run over rough woodland trails on a point-to-point course from the Lake Superior Zoo in Duluth, MN, to Carlton, MN. The course encompasses one half of the Minnesota Voyageur Trail Ultramarathon, plus a bit extra to reach 26.2 miles. NMTC introduced the race in 1992 as a thank you to Voyageur volunteers and an introduction to the course for those exploring the 50 miler. The race has become wildly popular in its own right.
