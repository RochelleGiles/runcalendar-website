Course

A straight, flat point-to-point course running north on Long Beach Boulevard. Both races start at Roosevelt and Pershing Avenue in Holgate at 10:00 AM; the 18 Mile finishes at Barnegat Lighthouse State Park and the 12K at 48th Street opposite the St. Francis Community Center. Water and sports drink every two miles, with restrooms at major stations and medical support on course and at the finish.
