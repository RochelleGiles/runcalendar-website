Course

Autumn 10K and 5K through the rolling Berkshire Hills around Great Barrington, Massachusetts, timed for peak fall foliage. Both races start at the Kilpatrick Athletic Center at Bard College at Simon's Rock, the 10K at 9:20am and the 5K at 9:40am.

About

Run for the Hills is a 10K and 5K in the Berkshires of western Massachusetts, organized by the Great Barrington Land Conservancy to support its conservation work.
