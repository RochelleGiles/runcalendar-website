Course

A night race on the Soldiers Trail and Highlands Trail loop at Fort Tuthill County Park, winding through ponderosa pine above 7,000 feet before topping out on a mesa under the stars. Footing ranges from narrow singletrack to wide forest path. The 27K runs two circuits, the 13K and 6K one.

About

Blackout Night Runs is an after-dark trail race at Fort Tuthill County Park near Flagstaff, Arizona, organized by Aravaipa Running. Runners choose the 6K, 13K, or 27K distance through high-elevation pine forest, with headlamps required once the sun goes down.
