Course

The 5K starts and finishes at Wild State Cider, 2515 W Superior Street, in the Lincoln Park district of Duluth, running 3.1 miles through the neighbourhood and past Bent Paddle Brewing. The quarter-mile Reindeer Run for children goes off at 9:30am and the 5K at 10:00am.
