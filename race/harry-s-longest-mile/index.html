Course

Last-one-standing format run as one-mile loops on the 800 m horse racing track at Kilmore Racing Club, about 55 minutes north of Melbourne. The sand and dirt surface is described as firm and fast underfoot. Runners complete as many rounds as possible until eliminated, with the cutoff dropping ten seconds each round.

About

A last-one-standing style race where entrants run 1 mile before the cutoff time. If they beat the cutoff, they run it again. With every round, the cutoff time drops by 10 seconds, and if they don't reach the finish line in time, they're out. The event is based on a 800m horse racing track at Kilmore Racing Club - each 1 mile (1.6km) round is 2 laps of the track.
