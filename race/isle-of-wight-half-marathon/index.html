Course

Two laps of about 13.1 miles starting and finishing in Appley Park at Ryde, running out through Bullen Lane, Goddard's Farm Lane, Attrill's Lane and Nettlestone with a bridleway stretch through Whitefield Woods. Surfaces mix tarmac road with off-road gravel track and the course is undulating throughout. Water stations sit at roughly 2, 6, 7.5, 9.5 and 11 miles.

About

The Isle of Wight Half Marathon is a popular event organized by Ryde Harriers. The course is undulating and covers various scenic areas of the island, including Ryde, Bullen Lane, Goddard's Farm Lane, and Appley Park.
