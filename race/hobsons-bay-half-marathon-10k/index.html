Course

A flat, sealed course running along the Hobsons Bay foreshore on wide, smooth shared paths. Start and finish are at Logan Reserve in Altona, in Melbourne's west. The half marathon sets off at 8:00am and the 10km at 8:15am.

About

The Hobsons Bay Half Marathon & 10k runs along the Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail beside Port Phillip Bay in Altona, west of Melbourne, offering flat, scenic bayside views for both the half marathon and 10km distances.
