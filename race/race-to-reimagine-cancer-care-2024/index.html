About

Join the Abington Health Foundation Women's Board for the Race to Reimagine on our certified course, starting and finishing at the Sidney Kimmel Comprehensive Cancer Center - Asplundh Cancer Pavilion in Willow Grove, PA! Runners and walkers are welcome and all proceeds from the race will support the center. We believe no one should ever face a cancer diagnosis alone.
