About

RunThrough Trails Morzine-Avoriaz is an ultra-distance trail event through the Portes du Soleil in the French Alps, based around the resorts of Morzine and Avoriaz. Runners choose from a vertical kilometre race and 25km, 50km and 100km routes across high alpine terrain.
