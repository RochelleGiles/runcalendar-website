About

The Formentera All Round Trail is a captivating running event that takes place on the picturesque island of Formentera, off the coast of Spain. Runners will have the chance to explore the stunning natural landscapes of this Mediterranean paradise, traversing a challenging course that showcases the island's diverse terrain and breathtaking coastal views.
