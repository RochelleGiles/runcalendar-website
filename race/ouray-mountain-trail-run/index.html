Course

A single 13-mile loop starting and finishing at Fellin Park in Ouray, climbing 4,180 feet through the San Juan Mountains. Three aid stations are on course, with a mandatory check-in at the second, around seven miles, inside three hours. Start is 7:30 a.m.

About

This is a fundraiser for the Ouray School, hosted by the school's parent and teacher organization, PATT, and the high school Outdoor Education Class. We have financially supported the students and teachers with equipment and on trips to Moab, Denver, Paris, and the Dominican Republic.
