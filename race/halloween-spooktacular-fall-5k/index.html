About

This is your chance to experience autumn at its absolute best – on foot through the historic Olmsted Brothers-designed landscape of Planting Fields! Get ready to lace up and hit the scenic trails at Planting Fields amid the vibrant colors of the season at our 5th Annual Fall 5K, a fall tradition for all ages and fitness levels.
