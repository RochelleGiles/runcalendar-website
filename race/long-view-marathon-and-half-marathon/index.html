Course

Point-to-point road race from Fort Collins to Loveland, fully paved. The marathon starts at Edora Pool Ice Center and the half at Fossil Creek Park; all distances finish at Fairgrounds Park in Loveland. Nine aid stations serve the marathon, four the half.

About

Showcasing a unique city-to-city trail connection, The Long View Marathon and Half Marathon runs from Fort Collins to Loveland. Together, Gnar Runners and Sweetheart City Racing bring a unique collaboration to Colorado's running scene in this one-of-a-kind race event.
