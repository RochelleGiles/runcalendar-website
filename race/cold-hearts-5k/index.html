Course

A USATF-certified, chip-timed 5K through Philadelphia's Fairmount Park. It starts at 8:30 AM on Avenue of the Republic, between the Please Touch Museum and the Smith Memorial. The route was rerouted off its original Schuylkill River line along MLK Drive after a water main break.

About

The Cold Hearts 5K is a winter road race through Philadelphia's Fairmount Park, organised by Philadelphia Runner. Entry includes a race shirt, a finisher medal and a post-race party, and the organisers publish optional training plans for runners building towards the distance.
