Course

Fixed-time and fixed-distance runs on a certified 0.99-mile concrete and paved-sidewalk loop around the Turf Picnic area at Estrella Mountain Regional Park, Goodyear. Start and finish sit on the loop, which is almost flat at roughly 6ft of gain per lap. A fully stocked aid station is directly on course with a water point at the far end, staffed around the clock. Headlamps are required after dark.

About

Fat Ox Endurance Runs is a multi-day ultramarathon at Estrella Mountain Regional Park in Goodyear, Arizona, with 50K, 100K, and 100-mile distances across Sonoran Desert terrain.
