Course

A 2.2 km climb of Mt Ainslie starting from Treloar Crescent in Campbell, behind the Australian War Memorial, rising about 240 m to the summit. Held at 12.45pm on the first Tuesday of each month with no entry fee and no pre-registration.

About

Conquer the iconic Mt Ainslie in Canberra with the thrilling Mt Ainslie Run Up & Power Walk. This monthly challenge invites runners and walkers to push their limits on the scenic mountain trails, offering stunning views of the nation's capital. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or a fitness enthusiast, this event promises an unforgettable outdoor adventure.
