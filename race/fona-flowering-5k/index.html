Course

A 5K on the closed roads of the 451-acre US National Arboretum, starting and finishing on the grounds. The route passes garden collections, climbs tree-lined rolling hills and runs alongside streams draining to the Anacostia River. All roads are shut to traffic during the race. Strollers and leashed dogs are permitted.

About

FONA Flowering 5K is a springtime 5K through the U.S. National Arboretum in Washington, D.C., organized by the Friends of the National Arboretum. The course passes the Arboretum's azalea collections in full seasonal bloom.
