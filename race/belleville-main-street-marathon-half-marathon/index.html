Course

Road marathon and half starting and finishing at Public Square on Belleville's Main Street, using Main Street, city roads and about 10 miles of paved trail for the full. Both start at 7:00 am; marathon support ends at 1:30 pm (mile 13 by 10:00 am) and half support at 10:30 am. USATF-certified Boston qualifier.

About

The Belleville Main Street Marathon and Half Marathon take runners through the historic town of Belleville, Illinois, a short drive from St. Louis, Missouri. The half marathon carries designation as the Road Runners Club of America Illinois State Championship Event.
