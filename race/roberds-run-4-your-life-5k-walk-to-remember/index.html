About

The Roberds Run 4 Your Life 5K & Walk to Remember is an annual fundraiser benefiting the Northwest Arkansas nonprofit P.E.A.R.L. The Ben Roberds Scholarship Fund honors the life and work of Ben Roberds, whose career was cut short by cancer at age 37, and creates opportunities for graduates of the Benton County Specialty Court to pursue an accredited 2- or 4-year college or vocational education.
