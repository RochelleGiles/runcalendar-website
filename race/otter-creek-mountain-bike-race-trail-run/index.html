Course

Trail runs at Harmon Lake Recreation Area near Mandan, both starting at 6:00pm. The short loop of 3 to 4 miles runs down the 2-track on the west side of Harmon, joins the end of Epic Loop and returns on Fast & Flowy; the 12 to 13 mile long loop adds Blue Sky and Chutes & Ladders before the same finish.

About

Welcome to the Otter Creek Trail Festival, a two-day celebration of off-road adventure at Harmon Lake Recreation Area near Mandan, North Dakota. Compete in the Otter Creek Trail Run or the Otter Creek Mountain Bike Race — then stick around for camping, food, and family fun at one of North Dakota's most scenic outdoor venues.
