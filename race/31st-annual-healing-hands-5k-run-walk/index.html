Course

The 5K run and walk start and finish at the same location at Mott Community College's Event Center in Flint. Runners set off at 9:00 a.m. and walkers at 9:01 a.m. Free parking and restrooms are available at the start area.

About

The 31st Annual Healing Hands 5K Run/Walk is a community event supporting the Genesee County Free Medical Clinic by providing free healthcare. Participants can choose to run or walk the USATF sanctioned 5K course at Mott Community College in Flint, Michigan.
