Course

Marathon, half and 5K on forest jeep roads through the Kaibab National Forest just south of the Grand Canyon, starting and finishing at the IMAX Visitor Center in Tusayan. Footing ranges from rugged rocky sections to soft and hard-packed dirt, with a rolling final mile on the paved Arizona Trail path. There are no toilets out on forest land; facilities are at the start/finish and halfway point.
