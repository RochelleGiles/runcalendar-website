Course

A road half marathon starting and finishing in Sheffield city centre, from Arundel Gate. The route climbs out to Ringinglow with views over the Peak District before returning. Timed hill-climb and 10K downhill segments are scored within the race.

About

The Sheffield Half Marathon is South Yorkshire's premier running event, drawing thousands of participants through the historic city's picturesque streets. Runners of all abilities can challenge themselves on this scenic course, taking in the stunning views of the surrounding Peak District. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply seeking an unforgettable running experience, the Sheffield Half Marathon is not to be missed.
