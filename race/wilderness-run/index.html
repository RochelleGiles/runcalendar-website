Course

A well-marked cross-country course through Shoal Creek Living History Museum grounds in Hodge Park, crossing fields, wooded paths and rolling hills. Two- and five-mile options are offered for runners and walkers, finishing with soup and campfires.

About

The Wilderness Run is an enjoyable, but also challenging event. As a participant you will follow a well-marked 2- or 5-mile course leading you through Shoal Creek Historical Village, across fields, along wooded paths, up and down hills, and more!
