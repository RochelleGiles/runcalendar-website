Course

A point-to-point 62-mile route along the Cleveland Way from Guisborough Rugby Club to Filey, spending most of its length on coastal cliff tops. It passes through Saltburn, Runswick Bay, Staithes, Whitby, Robin Hood's Bay, Ravenscar and Scarborough, with about 2,500 m of ascent and an 18-hour limit. Seven checkpoints carry water and food, with drop bags at Runswick Bay and Ravenscar.

About

The Hardmoors 60 is an epic 63-mile ultra trail race following the stunning Cleveland Way coastal route. Runners traverse breathtaking cliff tops and pass through charming coastal towns and fishing villages, experiencing the rugged beauty of the North York Moors. The challenging course tests athletes' endurance with significant elevation and a demanding 18-hour time limit.
