Course

A coastal trail event based at Dunwich on the Suffolk coast, offering 10K, half marathon, marathon and ultra distances over moderate terrain. The routes cross forest, moorland, grass and beach. The organisers describe the course as extremely flat by Endurancelife standards.

About

Explore the stunning Suffolk countryside at the EnduranceLife Suffolk trail run. Runners can tackle a 10K, Half Marathon, Marathon, or Ultra distance, all while immersing themselves in the natural beauty of the Dunwich region. This challenging yet rewarding event offers an unforgettable running experience for endurance athletes of all levels.
