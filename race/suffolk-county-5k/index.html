About

The pre-race activities (packet pick-up, race night registration, company gathering etc.) and post-race party (beer-food-music) will take place at Picnic Area #6 within Blydenburgh Park. The 5k run/walk will start and finish at Picnic area #6 and will take place entirely within the confines of beautiful Blydenburgh Park.
