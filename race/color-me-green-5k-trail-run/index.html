About

A 5K trail run through the U.S. National Whitewater Center where participants navigate green powder dye stations, with options to dodge or embrace the color. The event features multiple divisions (Competitive, Recreation, and Kids Fun Run) with awards for the cleanest and greenest finishers, followed by Green River Revival festivities including live music and rafting.
