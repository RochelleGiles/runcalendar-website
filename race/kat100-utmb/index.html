About

The KAT100 by UTMB is an epic mountain running event that takes place across the stunning Kitzbühel Alps. The event features five different race distances ranging from 8km to 173km, offering challenging mountain terrain and beautiful alpine scenery. This prestigious trail running competition takes runners through the most beautiful and demanding peaks of the region, serving as a testament to both running passion and natural beauty.
