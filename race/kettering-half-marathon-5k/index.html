Course

An undulating course starting and finishing at Wicksteed Park, Kettering. The route heads into Kettering town centre, continues through the grounds of Boughton House and follows quiet country lanes back to the park. Half marathon starts at 9:00am and the 5K at 9:20am, with all races finished by 12:30pm.

About

Explore the scenic roads of Northamptonshire at the Kettering Half Marathon & 5K. This closed course event offers both a challenging 13.1-mile half marathon and a 5K option, catering to runners of all levels. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply enjoying the picturesque countryside, this race is sure to provide an unforgettable running experience.
