About

The Bay to Bay Running Festival in Gosford, NSW offers a scenic tour of the picturesque coastal region, with distance options ranging from a 5km fun run to a full 42.2km marathon. Runners of all levels can challenge themselves on the rolling hills and stunning ocean vistas, making this a must-do event for endurance athletes in Australia.
