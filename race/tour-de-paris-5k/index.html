Course

About

Enjoy a scenic run (or walk!) through downtown Paris, Kentucky! Start and end the race at our iconic Eiffel Tower in the heart of Downtown Paris. See Secretariat Park, beautiful historic homes, Stoner Creek, and all the Downtown Shops throughout the course of the race.
