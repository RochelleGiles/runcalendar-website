Course

Point-to-point downhill on the paved recreation path from near Copper Mountain through Ten Mile Canyon to a Frisco Main Street finish. The 11K (6.8 mi) is all downhill; the half marathon adds a lollipop loop past Frisco Adventure Park and the Water Dance neighborhood and is about 75% downhill. Half starts 8:00am, 11K 8:30am; both close 11:30am.

About

The 51th Annual Run the Rockies Road 10k and Half Marathon will feature the traditional Run the Rockies road race course with runners starting near Copper Mountain. Runners will follow the paved recreation path through Ten Mile Canyon. The 10k course will be 100% downhill, and the half marathon course will be 75% downhill.
