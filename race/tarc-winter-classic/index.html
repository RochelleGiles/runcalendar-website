Course

A loop course in the Middlesex Fells Reservation run on the white-blazed Skyline Trail, starting and finishing on Fallon Road, Stoneham. The terrain is rocky and rooty with constant short climbs and descents. Two aid stations per loop, one at the start/finish and one near halfway, roughly 3.5 to 4.5 miles in depending on direction.

About

The TARC Winter Classic follows the rocky, rooted Skyline Trail through Middlesex Fells Reservation in Stoneham, Massachusetts. It offers 8-, 32- and 40-mile options, with proceeds supporting local land conservation.
