Course

Out-and-back on the traffic-free Tissington Trail, a former railway line in the Peak District. The route starts and finishes at Ashbourne Leisure Centre and climbs steadily for the first half towards Parsley Hay before descending on the return. Multi-terrain and undulating, with a six-hour cut-off. The marathon covers the trail twice.

About

The Tissington Trail Marathon & Half Marathon takes place along a traffic-free trail on a former railway line. The route runs 13 miles from Ashbourne to Buxton, surrounded by beautiful countryside, with an uphill section to Parsley Hay and a downhill return to Ashbourne Leisure Centre.
