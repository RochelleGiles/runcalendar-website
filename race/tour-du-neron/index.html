About

The Tour du Néron is a must-do trail running event that takes runners on a scenic journey through the picturesque landscapes surrounding the historic city of Grenoble, France. Challenging yet rewarding, this race showcases the stunning natural beauty of the Néron mountain, offering breathtaking views and an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
