About

Explore the historic Château de Pierrefonds on this scenic trail run. Weaving through the picturesque countryside surrounding the iconic 14th-century castle, this event offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to off-road running, the Trail du Château de Pierrefonds promises an unforgettable challenge and adventure.
