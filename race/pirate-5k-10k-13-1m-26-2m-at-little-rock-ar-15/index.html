Course

Start and finish at Two Rivers Park, 6900 Two Rivers Park Road, Little Rock, Arkansas, on asphalt throughout. Every distance is built from repeated out-and-backs: one for the 5K, two for the 10K, four for the half marathon and eight for the marathon. All events start at 8:00am with a six-hour limit.
