Course

A figure-eight course starting and finishing at Institute Park on Salisbury Street, running past the Grove Street Fire Station and through downtown Worcester. Runners pass under two aerial ladder trucks draping the American flag at both the start and the finish. A 3K walk and a kids race are held alongside the 6K.

About

The Worcester Firefighters 6K follows a figure-eight course from Institute Park past the Grove Street Fire Station and through downtown Worcester. The race honors six firefighters lost in the 1999 Worcester Cold Storage Fire and has raised over $600,000 for local charities including NEADS World Class Service Dogs.
