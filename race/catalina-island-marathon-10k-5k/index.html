Course

The marathon starts at Two Harbors and crosses the island to Avalon on dirt roads and trails, with over 4,300 ft of climbing. The 10K runs Avalon city streets past the golf course and up to the Wrigley Memorial before finishing near the waterfront. The 5K is hilly with views toward Long Beach. A Triple Crown Challenge combines all three.

About

The 48th Annual Catalina Island Marathon, 10K, and 5K offers spectacular views and breathtaking trails on Catalina Island, California's State Off-Road Marathon Championship and oldest trail marathon. Runners experience sweeping ocean views, rolling green hills, and the rugged charm of the Catalina Island Conservancy.
