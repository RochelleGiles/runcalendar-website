Course

A five-mile loop repeated to distance, starting and finishing at St Helena Heritage Park in Canal Fulton. The loop runs south on the Ohio & Erie Canal Towpath to Butterbridge Road and returns on the Olde Muskingum Trail. Packed crushed limestone the whole way apart from short-cut grass through the camping area. Fixed distances, timed formats and relay divisions are all offered.

About

Welcome to the Eagle Up Ultra!!! Unofficially the worlds LARGEST 24 Hour Ultra!! The Eagle Up Ultra began Nine years ago and has become one of the largest 24 hour races worldwide. We are partnered up with Team RWB as our race charity and donate a portion of EVERY registration!! Over the years we have had runners, many veterans and a TON of Team RWB members come here from over 40 states and 3 countries!
