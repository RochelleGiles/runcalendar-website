About

The Great American River Run is a scenic race along the Mississippi River in downtown Memphis, held during Memorial Day weekend as part of the Memphis in May festival. It offers 5K, 10K, and half marathon distances for runners of all skill levels, featuring a fireworks start, bridge challenge prizes, and a post-race party with music, food, and beer at AutoZone Park.
