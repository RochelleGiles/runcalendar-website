Course

Two-lap half marathon starting and finishing near the Colt State Park headquarters in Bristol, taking in views of Narragansett Bay and Bristol Harbour. The route mixes park paths, seawall sections and the East Bay Bike Path. Three aid stations are passed twice each: water only at Stone Bridge and Poppasquash Road, water, Gatorade and gel at Harborview Avenue.

About

The Colt State Park Half Marathon runs through Bristol, Rhode Island's Colt State Park, a waterfront park on Narragansett Bay known for its open fields, stone walls, and shoreline views along Poppasquash Road.
