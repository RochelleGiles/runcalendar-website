About

This is race 1 in a series of races that are to be treated as milestone training runs. We follow a 18 week training schedule designed for runners and running groups that are training for the SATX or BCS Marathon and Half Marathon. These milestone races are designed to keep you on track. They are also used to allow you to meet everyone in the area that is training just like you. This starts early Saturday morning in August at beautiful Tuscany Venue. At the center of this shaded beautiful venue, with&nbsp; expansive grounds, lies a century-old home nestled amidst a picturesque canopy of trees, surrounded by three distinct outdoor patios and an intimate water fountain . You won&rsquo;t want to miss! We have added a Half Marathon 3-person relay to add more excitement and camaraderie to the beginning of running season. Get on your text group, and build your Team!
