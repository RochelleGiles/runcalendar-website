About

All ages and levels are welcome to join us at our 4th Annual 5K! The Alexander Trujillo Foundation is raising funds to support all young adult cancer patients of ages 18-34. Participate with us as we give young adults and their caregivers/families in our community the opportunity to have financial assistance while undergoing cancer treatment.
