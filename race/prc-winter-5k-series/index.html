Course

Each race in the series starts and finishes at the Portland Running Company store in Beaverton, setting off at 9:00am on a road 5K route. Packet pickup is at the store from 7:30 to 8:45am on race morning, and a modified route avoiding the main roads is used when there is snow.

About

Keep those wheels turning and the fire burning all winter long when you join in our seasonal 5K series. This low-key, high-yield trio of races gives you the chance to test your training, improve your times, and compete with yourself.
