About

The Exploring With Grace 5K Race honors Grace Lewis and supports the Exploring with Grace Foundation, promoting mental, physical, and emotional well-being for children and veterans through outdoor and Jiu-Jitsu programs. It features a 5K run/walk, virtual option, post-race celebration with live music, mascots, and beverages.
