About

Families Helping Families of Greater Baton Rouge (FHFGBR) invites you to our Annual Active for Autism 5K, Run/Walk & Family Fun Fest, celebrating awareness, acceptance, and inclusion for individuals affected by Autism Spectrum Disorder. Open to adults and children ages 4 and up. Stroller babies and toddlers are welcome—no registration needed for them!
