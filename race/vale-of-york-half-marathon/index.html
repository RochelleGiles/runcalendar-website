Course

Flat closed-road courses on lanes south of York and north of Selby, starting and finishing near Wistow in North Yorkshire. The half marathon sets off at 9:30am with the 10K following at 9:50am. Both are UKA accurately measured and carry full RunBritain race licences; the exact start and finish lines shift with the parking field used each year.

About

The Vale of York Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging 13.1-mile race through the picturesque countryside of Yorkshire, England. Runners will be treated to stunning views of the region's rolling hills, historic landmarks, and charming villages as they push themselves to the finish line. This popular event is a must-do for any runner seeking a memorable and rewarding half-marathon experience.
