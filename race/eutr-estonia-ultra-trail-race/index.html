About

Now past its 9th running, the Estonia Ultra-Trail Race (EUTR) takes place in Estonia's Ida-Virumaa region, in Narva, with start and finish at the Akkekula (Nut Hill) sports township. Facilities include locker rooms, storage, and an athlete expo.
