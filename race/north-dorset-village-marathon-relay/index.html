Course

Marathon along quiet North Dorset country lanes, starting and finishing at Sturminster Newton High School at 8:30am and passing through ten villages including Hinton St Mary, Marnhull, Stalbridge, Stour Row, Child Okeford and Hammoon. Entry is capped at 400 and a relay runs over the same route.

About

Explore the picturesque villages of North Dorset at the annual North Dorset Village Marathon & Relay. This community-driven event, organized by runners for runners, offers a scenic 26.2-mile course through the stunning countryside surrounding Sturminster Newton. Whether you're tackling the full marathon or participating in the relay, this is a must-do challenge for runners seeking a unique and memorable running experience.
