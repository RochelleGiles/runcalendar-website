Course

A night out-and-back along the Narrowneck firetrail, starting and finishing at Narrow Neck Lookout on Cliff Drive, Katoomba, after runners are walked down the stairs to the trail. The course runs straight out along the ridge line and back on wide, straightforward firetrail. The 14km turns at the 6.88km aid station and the 24.7km continues to the end of the firetrail at 12.2km.

About

The Narrow Neck Night Run is an evening trail race along Narrow Neck Plateau near Katoomba in the Blue Mountains, run after dark with headlamps. Distances are 14km and 24.7km along the exposed cliff-top fire trail.
