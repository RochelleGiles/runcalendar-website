Course

All races start and finish at TEENS, Inc., 151 East Street, Nederland, at about 8,236 feet. The trail half marathon (13.3 mi, 1,408' gain) and 10K (6.3 mi, 712' gain) run through town onto dirt roads and singletrack; the 5K is a road out-and-back to the high school. Half starts 8:00am, 10K 9:00am, 5K after.

About

Trail races starting and finishing at TEENS, Inc. in Nederland, Colorado, at roughly 8,200 feet elevation. The half marathon (13.3 miles) and 10K (6.3 miles) climb out of town with 1,408 and 712 feet of gain respectively, while the 5K is an out-and-back on roads. Leashed dogs are welcome in the 5K only, and no walkers are allowed in the trail races.
