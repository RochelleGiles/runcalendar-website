Course

Single loop starting and finishing at Clyde Primary School, 26 Blyth Street, Clyde. The route crosses the Clyde Dam, follows riverside trail along the Clutha for about 7.5km to a turnaround, passes the Three Miners Vineyard, and returns via the Hidden Highway and the Clyde Bridge. Surface is a mix of riverside trail and closed country road. Aid stations at 4.5km, 10km and 15km.

About

The Clyde Classic is a half marathon, 10km and 5km event based at Clyde Primary School in Central Otago. The half marathon runs as a single loop, crossing the Clyde Dam, following riverside trails along the Clutha River to a turnaround, and returning over the Clyde Bridge on a mix of trail and country road.
