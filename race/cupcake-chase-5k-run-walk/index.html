Course

A flat 5K loop on the canal paths beside Arizona Falls at GR Herberger Park in Arcadia, Phoenix. The route starts on the north canal bank northwest of Indian School Road, crosses at 68th Street and returns to the finish. Mixed paved and dirt surface with minimal elevation change.

About

The Cupcake Chase 5K Run & Walk is an annual event held at Arizona Falls at GR Herberger Park in the Arcadia neighborhood of Phoenix, Arizona. Participants can run or walk to support Labor's Community Service Agency, with post-race cupcakes and community fun.
