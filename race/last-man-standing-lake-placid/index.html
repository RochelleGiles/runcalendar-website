Course

A backyard ultra at Mt Van Hoevenberg, Lake Placid, run in the Lazarus Lake format: one 4.167-mile loop of wooded trail, every hour on the hour, starting and finishing at a central corral. Runners must complete each loop inside the hour to start the next, and the race runs until one runner is left. Awards mark 26.2 miles through to 150 miles.

About

Last Man Standing is a backyard ultra race held on the pristine, wooded trails at Mt. Hovenberg in Lake Placid, New York. Based on the original Backyard Ultra format created by Lazarus Lake, the rules are simple—but finishing is anything but. You must complete one 4.167-mile loop every hour on the hour. Some show up to win, most show up to find out what they're made of.
