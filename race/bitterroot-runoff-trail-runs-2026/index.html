About

Kick off trail racing season in Montana with the Bitterroot Runoff Trail Runs! With 5k, 5 Mile, & 10 Mile courses, there are options for beginners and seasoned veterans alike. All three courses follow private single & doubletrack trails on the Rossignol Ranch outside of Lolo, Montana. After the race, runners will be treated to a homemade brunch and a Big Sky Brewery beverage.
