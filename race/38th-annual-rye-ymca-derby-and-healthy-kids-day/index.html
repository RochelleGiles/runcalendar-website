Course

Road races of 5 miles, 5K and a 1-mile family fun run/walk starting and finishing at the Rye YMCA on Locust Avenue in Rye, New York. The 5-miler is the championship event, and the racing runs alongside a Healthy Kids Day festival with activities, food and music.

About

The Rye Derby is a fundraiser to raise money for the Y Cares Financial Assistance Program which enables the Rye YMCA to keep a powerful promise to the communities they serve: no one will be turned away from a Y program or activity because he or she can't pay.
