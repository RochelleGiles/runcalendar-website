Course

Out-and-back along Lake Pontchartrain on a paved jogging path about nine feet wide, with runners keeping right in both directions. Start and finish at the Kenner lakefront at the end of Williams Boulevard. The 10 Mile course is USATF-RRTC certified. Several aid stations are on course, with a lead bike for each distance group.
