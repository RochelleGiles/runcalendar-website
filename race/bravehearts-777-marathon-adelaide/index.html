Course

Repeated 7km loop starting and finishing at Elder Park on King William Road, Adelaide; longer distances are multiple laps. Water is provided at a minimum of two points on each loop, with portaloos at the event precinct. The race starts at 7:00am with a 1:30pm cut-off.

About

The Bravehearts 777 Marathon Adelaide is one leg of a seven-marathon, seven-state, seven-day series raising funds for child protection charity Bravehearts. The Adelaide leg is run as repeated 7km loops starting and finishing at Elder Park on King William Road, with marathon, half marathon, 14km and 7km options.
