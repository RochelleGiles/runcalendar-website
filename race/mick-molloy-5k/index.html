About

The Mick Molloy 5K is a 5km road race in Oughterard, County Galway, taking runners on a scenic route through the Connemara countryside on quiet roads. The event features professional timing, refreshments and a supportive community atmosphere, and celebrates Mick Molloy, one of Oughterard's finest sporting ambassadors.
