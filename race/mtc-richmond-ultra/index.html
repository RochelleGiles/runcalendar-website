Course

Point-to-point along the Thames Path from Kingston to the Thames Barrier, on urban riverside paths past Richmond Lock, Kew Palace and the central London bridges. The route crosses at Wandsworth Bridge and uses the Greenwich foot tunnel to reach the far bank. Almost flat at 105m of ascent, with two checkpoints serving water, sandwiches and cake.

About

The Richmond Ultra is a challenging 33-mile ultramarathon following the historic Thames Path from Kingston to The Thames Barrier. Runners will experience England's longest river path in this unique endurance event.
