About

Join us on Sunday 29th November in South Park, Darlington. Mental Elf festive fun run is back for 2026! You can choose to walk, jog, skip or run the 5km route. It's a two lap route of beautiful South Park taking in all its main landmarks. The event is open to all ages and abilities - children under 16 must be accompanied at the event by an adult and well behaved dogs on leads are welcome. Festive fancy dress is very much encouraged!
