About

Léman Habitat organizes a solidarity run/walk against cancer in the Parc de la Châtaigneraie in Thonon-les-Bains, with proceeds going to the Léon Bérard Center — a leading hospital in cancer treatment and a renowned research center. Participation and donations support the work of the center's researchers in advancing cancer treatment.
