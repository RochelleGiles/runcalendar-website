About

OTSO UTSM COSTA DAURADA is born from the idea of creating a balanced meeting between a natural environment of great value, such as the Serra de Montsant Natural Park; the people who live in the villages integrated in the territory and the athletes who, with their strong sporting and human commitment, will be the ones who will trace the route that unites them.
