Course

An 8.9-mile loop from the Tram Plaza base area with 3,610 ft of climbing. Runners start on a service road along the creek, climb steeply toward the Mid-Gad chair, then take Rothman Way and the Peruvian Gulch Trail to Hidden Peak before a technical descent and a boulder traverse back to the start. A 2-hour cutoff applies at the Hidden Peak summit.

About

The Cirque Series - Snowbird is a challenging mountain running race starting at Snowbird Tram Plaza, featuring steep climbs up Peruvian Gulch Trail and Ridge Trail to Hidden Peak and Mt. Baldy summit. Runners finish with a technical descent back to the start amid a festival atmosphere with music, food, and vendors.
