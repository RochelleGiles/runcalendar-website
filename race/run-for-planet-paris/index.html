About

Run for Planet Paris is an eco-conscious running event that takes participants on a scenic tour through the heart of the City of Light. Runners will have the chance to experience Paris's iconic landmarks while supporting the fight against climate change. This unique race celebrates the beauty of the planet and the power of community to create positive change.
