Course

Run on the Harold Stevens Athletics Track on Outlook Road in East Coburg, behind the basketball stadium. Entrants circle the track for a fixed time — 24, 12 or six hours — or run a marathon on the same loop.

About

The Coburg 24 Hour Carnival is a 24-hour track ultra hosted by Coburg Harriers at Harold Stevens Athletics Track, where runners rack up as many laps as possible against the clock.
