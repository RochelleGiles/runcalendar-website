About

Ah, Summer Trail Fest Ultra (STFU), Half Marathon, & 5 Mile. This race is a great first timer's ultra with well stocked, frequent aid stations and excessive course markings. The course is 90% gorgeous, well-groomed trails. The race has extremely generous cutoff times, with a 20:00/mile pace for the 50K and 45:00/mile pace for the Half Marathon. The course is also a spectacular race for the seasoned veteran or athlete looking to PR their trail 50K or Half Marathon.
