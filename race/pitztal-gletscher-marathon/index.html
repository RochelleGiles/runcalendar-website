About

Now past its 20th running, the Pitztal Gletscher Marathon (Glacier Marathon) winds through the impressive mountain scenery of the Pitztal valley, starting at the foot of the glacier. It celebrates physical effort, fitness, fighting spirit and camaraderie amid the beauty of the Pitztal mountains.
