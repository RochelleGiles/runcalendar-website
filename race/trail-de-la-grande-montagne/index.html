Course

A mountain trail event in the Ain, based at Le Poizat-Lalleyriat, on entirely natural medium-altitude terrain. Three races start and finish together - the 42km Marathon du Sanglier, run solo or as a relay, the 25km Le Chamois over technical trail with sustained climbing and descending, and the 12km Le Lynx. The event is ITRA-listed and counts as a UTMB World Series qualifier.

About

The Trail de la Grande Montagne is an exciting mountain trail running event offering multiple challenging routes through the scenic landscapes of the Ain region. Participants can choose from four distances ranging from 8 km to a full 42 km marathon, each designed to test runners' agility, endurance, and mountain skills across technical trails and varied terrain.
