About

The Salomon Rondane 100 is a captivating ultramarathon set against the breathtaking backdrop of Norway's Rondane mountains. Runners will tackle a challenging course through rugged alpine terrain, immersed in the stunning natural beauty of this remote region. This event offers a truly unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners seeking an adventure-filled test of their endurance.
