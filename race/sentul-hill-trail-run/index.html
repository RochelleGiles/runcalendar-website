About

Explore the rolling hills and lush trails of Sentul Hill at the annual Sentul Hill Trail Run. This challenging yet scenic event offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in nature, tackling diverse terrain while taking in breathtaking views. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to off-road running, this race promises an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
