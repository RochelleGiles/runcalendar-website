About

The Okinawa Marathon is a prestigious running event held annually in the beautiful island of Okinawa, Japan. The course showcases the stunning natural scenery of the region, including the iconic Okinawan coastline and lush, tropical landscapes. Runners can expect a challenging yet rewarding experience, with a vibrant atmosphere and enthusiastic local support.
