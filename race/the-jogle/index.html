Course

A 17-day, roughly 854-mile stage race from John O'Groats to Land's End, averaging about 50 miles a day. The route is almost entirely on road, with one four-mile trail section on day six, and totals some 16,600 m of ascent.

About

Embark on the iconic JOGLE journey, a 17-day endurance challenge that takes runners from the northern tip of Scotland to the southern coast of England. Traverse the diverse landscapes of the United Kingdom, from rugged highlands to rolling hills, as you push your limits and conquer this legendary ultramarathon route.
