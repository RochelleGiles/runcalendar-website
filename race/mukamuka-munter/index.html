Course

A wilderness loop starting and finishing on the Orongorongo River track in Remutaka Forest Park. Runners follow hard-packed then sandy 4x4 coastal trail around Turikirae Head, turn inland for technical river running up to South Saddle, then descend steep rooted native bush with a major Orongorongo crossing before a fast return on the big bend track.

30K30 km

About

The Mukamuka Munter is a challenging, grassroots event on the rugged south coast, and isolated interior of the Remutaka Forest Park, Wellington. The 30km trail run is designed as a tough, technical adventure, with rocky river running, steep descents, and wild coastlines.
