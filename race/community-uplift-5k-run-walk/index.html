About

The Community Uplift 5K Run/Walk is a fun run/walk event starting at 10AM rain or shine at The Omega Family Resource and Learning Center in East Orange, NJ. Proceeds benefit the Greater Essex Community Uplift Foundation (GECUF) to support community programs.
