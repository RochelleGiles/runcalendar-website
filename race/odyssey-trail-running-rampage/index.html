Course

A loop course starting and finishing at the Discovery Center at Camp Carson in Douthat State Park. The 13-mile mountain loop climbs 2,700ft, most of it in the first three miles, past creeks, waterfalls and rhododendron. Three aid stations per loop: water at mile 3.5, fully stocked at miles 9 and 11.25. Run as one, two or three loops.

About

The Odyssey Trail Running Rampage offers a 50K, marathon, half marathon, or six-mile option on trails around Millboro in Bath County, Virginia. Organized by Odyssey Adventure Racing, the event takes place in the mountains of western Virginia each September.
