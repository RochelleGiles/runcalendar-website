Course

A night race at Eagle Creek Park in Indianapolis on a 6.55-mile loop that is nearly all singletrack trail. One lap for the quarter marathon, two for the half, starting in staggered waves from 6pm. Headlamps are required and there are two water stops on the loop.

About

The Winter Night Trail Run is a January trail race in Indianapolis organized by 131 Events. Runners can choose the half marathon or quarter marathon distance on wooded park trails, with much of the field finishing after sunset.
