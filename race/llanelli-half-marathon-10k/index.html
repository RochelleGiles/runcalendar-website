Course

Starts and finishes in Llanelli on a UKA-licensed, undulating course combining closed roads with the sealed Millennium coastal path. Both the half marathon and the 10K are largely flat along the coastal sections. Water stations are provided on course.

About

The Llanelli Half Marathon & 10K is a scenic coastal race showcasing the stunning landscapes of Carmarthenshire, UK. Runners can choose between the 13.1-mile half marathon or the 10K distance, both offering breathtaking views of the region's rugged shoreline. This event is a must for those seeking a picturesque challenge on the roads.
