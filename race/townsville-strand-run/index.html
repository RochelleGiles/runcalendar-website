About

Explore the picturesque Townsville Strand on foot at the Townsville Road Runners Strand Run. Starting from the scenic Jezzine Park in North Ward, Queensland, this event offers runners a chance to take in the stunning coastal views and vibrant atmosphere of this beautiful seaside city.
