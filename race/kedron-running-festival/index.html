Course

A flat, fast out-and-back along the Kedron Brook Cycleway, with all four events starting and finishing at Shaw Park in Wavell Heights. The paths stay open to other pedestrians and cyclists, so organisers ask runners to skip earphones. Offered as a half marathon, 10km, 5km or 2km family dash.

About

Kedron Running Festival is a community running event in Wavell Heights on Brisbane's northside. Runners can choose a 5km, 10km or half marathon, plus a 2km family dash for younger or first-time participants.
