Course

Starts at 9:30am on the Barclays Radbroke campus near Knutsford and heads out on fully closed country lanes through Over Peover, Snelson and Peover Heath before returning. With only 23 m of elevation across the whole course it is one of the fastest 10Ks in the north west.
