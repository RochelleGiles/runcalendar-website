Course

A figure-eight course run on city streets at night, with the route lit by candles. The start and finish are at J. Fred Johnson Stadium in Kingsport. A 3K run and walk and short youth races are held the same evening, alongside a virtual 8K option.

About

Crazy 8s 8K is a downtown road race in Kingsport, Tennessee, known nationally as one of the fastest 8K courses in the world. A shorter Almost Crazy 3K and youth fun runs share the same morning.
