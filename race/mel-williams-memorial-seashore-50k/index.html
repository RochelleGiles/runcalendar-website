About

The Mel Williams Memorial Seashore 50K runs through First Landing State Park in Virginia Beach, starting and finishing at the 64th Street boat ramp. Roughly a mile is on paved road, with the rest on hard-packed dirt trails through wooded park terrain, designed by veteran ultrarunner John Price.
