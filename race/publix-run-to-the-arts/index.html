About

So much more than any ordinary 5K, the Publix Run to the Arts brings together the best that life has to offer: health and fitness, arts and culture, friends and family, and the scenic beauty of the Fort Myers waterfront. Post-race festivities include a scrumptious breakfast, race medals for the top finishers, and much more.
