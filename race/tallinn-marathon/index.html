About

The Tallinn Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner. Winding through the historic streets of Estonia's capital city, this scenic course offers a unique opportunity to explore the charming architecture and vibrant culture of Tallinn. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, the Tallinn Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
