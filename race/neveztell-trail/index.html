Course

Three trail courses in Brittany: a 10K aimed at trail newcomers, a 15K over the most technical paths with 200m of climb, and a flagship 25K with 350m of climb. A Saturday evening race and a parents-and-children event run alongside.

About

The Neveztell Trail in Nouvoitou is organized by the JSN Athlétisme and ACHV associations, brought together by a dedicated team of volunteers. The event is built around values of sharing and determination among participants and organizers alike.
