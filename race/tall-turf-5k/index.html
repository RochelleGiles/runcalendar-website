About

Tall Turf Ministries is holding its third annual 5K race! We have continued improving our 5k course which integrates several of our trails, allowing runners to see and experience God's creation on our beautiful property located on Campbell Lake, in Oceana County. We are also including a virtual event for those not able to attend!
