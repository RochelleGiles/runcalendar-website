About

The Trail De Noel Ollioules is a winter trail running event that has been held for the past 25 years in the beautiful Rade de Toulon region. Runners can choose from three different courses: the 16.5km Trace des Lutins, the 27km Trail De Noel in duo, and the 31km Défi du Père Noël. The event is known for its stunning scenery and challenging terrain, making it a must-do for trail running enthusiasts.
