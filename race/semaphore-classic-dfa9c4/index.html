About

The Semaphore Classic is a South Australian Road Runners Club race on the Adelaide coast at Semaphore. Runners choose between 5km and 10km on an out-and-back along the Esplanade and Coast Park path, starting and finishing at Point Malcolm Reserve. Dolphins are a regular sight offshore.
