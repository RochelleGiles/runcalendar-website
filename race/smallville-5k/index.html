Course

A cross-country style 5K run 650 feet underground in an active salt mine at 3650 E Avenue G, Hutchinson. Runners go in heats of thirty, and a helmet and headlamp or other light source are required.

About

Smallville 5K runs entirely underground at Strataca, the Kansas Underground Salt Museum in Hutchinson. Runners complete flat loops through the salt mine's tunnels and chambers roughly 650 feet below the surface, a subterranean race distance found nowhere else.
