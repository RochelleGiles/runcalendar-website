About

The Bravehearts 777 Marathon in Perth, Western Australia is the first leg of a remarkable challenge - 7 marathons in 7 states in 7 days. Runners will test their mettle on this iconic course, raising funds to support the vital work of the Bravehearts organization. This unique event offers an unparalleled opportunity for endurance athletes to push their limits while making a difference.
