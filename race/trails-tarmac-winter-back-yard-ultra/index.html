Course

A backyard ultra at Crickhowell in the Black Mountains, run as repeated 4.167-mile loops started on the hour. The loop is half trail and half tarmac, with one gradual climb and a short steep section and otherwise relatively flat, fast terrain. Capped at twelve hours, with an ultra medal at 32 miles.

About

A Backyard Ultra is a last one standing endurance race where competitors must consecutively run a 4.2 mile course in less than one hour, every hour. The race is based in the Black Mountains, with a course that is 50/50 trail and tarmac, featuring only one gradual climb and mostly flat terrain.
