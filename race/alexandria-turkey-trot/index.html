About

The Alexandria Turkey Trot starts at George Washington Middle School in the Del Ray neighborhood and draws thousands of runners and walkers each Thanksgiving morning. The course loops through Del Ray's residential streets, and participants are encouraged to bring canned goods for ALIVE!, a local hunger-relief nonprofit.
