Course

A USATF-certified 5K on the residential roads of Northport, run as a challenging rolling-hills course that takes in 1.6K of the historic Cow Harbor route. Roads are under police traffic control with volunteer marshals, and a water station sits roughly midway near the east end of Oak Street. A shorter untimed Kids Fun Run is also held.

About

The 26th Annual Run for the Health of It is a 5K run/walk event that benefits the Visiting Nurse Service & Hospice of Suffolk. The event features cash prizes for the top finishers, a kids fun run, and a virtual 5K option during National Nurses Week.
