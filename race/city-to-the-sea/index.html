Course

A point-to-point half marathon from 989 Chorro Street in downtown San Luis Obispo out through country backroads and along the Bob Jones Trail to finish at the Pacific at Avila Beach. The 5K starts and finishes at 6464 Ana Bay Road in Avila Beach, briefly joining the Bob Jones Trail, with most of the route in sight of the beach and harbour. Both start at 7:00am.

About

The City to the Sea Half Marathon runs point-to-point from downtown San Luis Obispo to the coast at Avila Beach, with a net-downhill course finishing near the water. A separate Avila Seaside 5K takes place along the coastline.
