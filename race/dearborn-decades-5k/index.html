About

Trivium Racing is excited to once again host our August 5K in Dearborn at Ford Field Park. This race is going to be off the hook as we celebrate the decades! This is a great mid-summer race for all of our racers getting ready for their "A" race in the fall. Our fast and fun USATF-certified course will give you a great shot at your PR! Feeling casual? Bring the family and friends for a morning run or walk and enjoy the community camaraderie, competition, and Dearborn Homecoming festival atmosphere!
