About

Participate in a 10 km race in the heart of our beautiful city! The start and finish will be from the Charles de Gaulle esplanade. You will begin this charming course through the historic center of Istres, which you will leave by the Alderic Chave avenue to offer you a magnificent view of the Etang de Berre. After leaving the banks of one pond, you will find another, this time to go around the Etang de l'Olivier. A pleasant tour with breathtaking viewpoints. To finish in style, you will take the new pedestrian path recently developed, leading you to a triumphant finish!
