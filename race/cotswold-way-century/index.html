Course

Point-to-point 102-mile non-stop ultra following the Cotswold Way National Trail from Chipping Campden Village Hall to the CWC stone in Bath. The hilly route mixes hard stone tracks, green and grassy paths, ploughed fields and some road sections. Food stations and medical support throughout the course.

About

Simply put, it's the Cotswold Way. Start in Chipping Campden at the Village Hall, stop in Bath at Bath Abbey, follow the signs marked "Cotswold Way". Although we may add occasional markings near check points or where the route has more than 1 option, you need to be able to read a map! You'll have 30 hours to complete the event, with intermediate cut-offs along the way.
