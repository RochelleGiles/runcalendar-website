Course

A figure-8 course starting at Nodes 5 and 6 in Dinden National Park. The 19km runs out along Davies Creek Road into Davies Creek Camp, climbs to Turtle Rock and along the ridge, drops down The Hurtle Down the Turtle, crosses the creek past race HQ, then climbs again to Lamb's Head at 1100m before The Jam Down the Lamb and the road back to the finish. The 6km covers the first loop only; the 3km kids' course is an out-and-back toward Turtle Rock.

About

The Turtle and the Lamb is a trail running event on the Mareeba Tablelands in Far North Queensland, organised by Adventure Sport NQ. Runners can choose a 19km, 6km or 3km course through the surrounding bush.
