Course

A flat point-to-point 10 miles on city streets from downtown Pittston, crossing the Susquehanna into West Pittston and running straight down Wyoming Avenue through Exeter, Wyoming, Forty Fort and Kingston. It crosses the Market Street Bridge to finish at Public Square in downtown Wilkes-Barre. Walk and hand cycle divisions run the same course.

About

The Wyoming Valley Run is NEPA's flattest and fastest inclusive road race, presented by Allied Services in partnership with AllOne Charities. The 10-mile course runs from downtown Pittston to Wilkes-Barre, taking in some of the Valley's most iconic views, and is now past its 4th running.
