Course

All three distances start and finish at Kinloch Domain on the northern shore of Lake Taupō. The 21 km climbs the W2K track and loops the Kinloch Headland for views over the lake and the Kaimanawa ranges before descending to boardwalk and lakefront. The 10 km and 5 km follow the clifftop reserve, Whangamata Stream Track and beach trail. Singletrack, farm trail and boardwalk throughout.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of New Zealand's backcountry at the Kinloch Off Road Challenge, part of the renowned Roam Free Trail Series. This off-road running event near Taupō promises an exhilarating challenge for adventurous athletes, with a course that winds through stunning natural landscapes. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or seeking a new outdoor adventure, the Kinloch Off Road Challenge offers an unforgettable experience.
