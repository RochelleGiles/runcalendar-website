Course

Repeating 4-mile (6.4 km) singletrack loop in the Sheyenne National Grassland, starting and finishing at the East Trailhead day-use parking lot. The trail runs through rolling sandhills, oak savanna and tallgrass prairie, with loose sand and cattle tracks underfoot. Runners complete as many laps as they can within the time limit.

About

Oak Leaf Trail 36 Hour is a timed trail ultra held in McLeod, North Dakota, where runners complete as many laps as possible over a 36-hour window. It draws ultrarunners looking for a rural Midwest endurance challenge.
