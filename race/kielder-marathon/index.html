About

The Altra Kielder Marathon and festival events take place in the breathtaking landscapes of the United Kingdom's Kielder Water & Forest Park. Runners can challenge themselves on a scenic course through the region's forests and around the stunning Kielder Reservoir, making this a must-do event for nature-loving endurance athletes.
