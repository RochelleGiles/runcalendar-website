About

The Prosecco Running Festival is a unique running event that traverses the UNESCO World Heritage wine region of Conegliano and Valdobbiadene. Runners experience a spectacular course through picturesque prosecco vineyards, offering a perfect blend of athletic challenge and local wine culture. The event features multiple race distances that allow participants to explore the stunning hills of the Prosecco wine region.
