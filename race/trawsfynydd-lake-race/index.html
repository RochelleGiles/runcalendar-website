Course

Multi-terrain 8.6 mile circuit of Trawsfynydd lake in Eryri/Snowdonia, starting and finishing at the Magnox power station car park. The course mixes tarmac and fast trail, is largely quick with around 175m of ascent and one substantial hill, and has water stations on course. A 1 mile children's race runs alongside it.

About

Explore the stunning Eryri/Snowdonia National Park at the Trawsfynydd Lake Race, an 8.6 mile multi-terrain challenge circling the serene waters of Trawsfynydd lake. This beautiful event offers runners a chance to experience the breathtaking natural landscapes of the United Kingdom's wilderness, making it a must for any adventurous athlete.
