About

One of Chile's largest road races, the Maratón de Viña del Mar runs along the Pacific coastline through Viña del Mar, starting from Reñaca beach. Runners choose the 10K, half marathon or full marathon, all following the shoreline past the resort city's beaches and boardwalks.
