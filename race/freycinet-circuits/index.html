Course

Two circuits are offered, both run simultaneously in a clockwise direction: an 11km circuit via Wineglass Bay and Hazards Beach, and a 29km circuit taking in Mt Graham and Cooks Beach. The 29km route is rough underfoot and marked with cairns in sections, and entrants must be able to finish it within 5 hours 30 minutes. Walkers are not permitted on the 29km.

About

Both circuits will be run simultaneously in a clockwise direction. The Freycinet Peninsula is undoubtedly one of the most beautiful locations in the world. With 29 km of exceptional track interspersed with pristine beaches this trail run will be hard to race with the temptation to stop for a swim or take in the view!
