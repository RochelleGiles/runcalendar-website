Course

Mixed-surface course at Hanging Rock in Woodend, in Victoria's Macedon Ranges about an hour from Melbourne. Runners cover trails, paths and sealed roads, including a lap around the base of the rock formation itself. Distances range from a 2km kids dash to a 22km half marathon.

About

Escape to the stunning Macedon Ranges in Australia for a scenic and challenging off-road running adventure at the iconic Hanging Rock. This scenic trail run offers runners a chance to explore the dramatic natural landscapes and unique geological formations of this stunning location. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, Run the Rock promises an unforgettable running experience.
