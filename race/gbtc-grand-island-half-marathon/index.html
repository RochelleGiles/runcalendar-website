Course

A flat out-and-back along the Niagara River on Grand Island, starting and finishing at the main car park of Beaver Island State Park. More than 85 percent of the route is traffic-free, with six hydration stations offering water and Gatorade and no course time limit.

About

The GBTC - Grand Island Half Marathon is the annual flagship race of the Greater Buffalo Track Club. The event takes place at Beaver Island State Park and is open to runners of all skill levels.
