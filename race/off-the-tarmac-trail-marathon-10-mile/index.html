Course

A looped mountain trail course starting and finishing at Parkwood Outdoors, Dolygaer, near Merthyr Tydfil. The marathon climbs Pen y Fan from Graig Fan Ddu, crosses Fan y Big and finishes over Tor y Foel above Talybont Reservoir, with 1,492 m of ascent; the 10 mile shares the opening section then returns on forestry tracks. Three checkpoints on the marathon and compulsory kit for both.

About

The Off The Tarmac Trail Marathon & 10 Mile starts in Merthyr Tydfil and heads into the foothills of the Brecon Beacons, South Wales. It is part of the wider Off The Tarmac series, which also includes an ultra marathon on the same trails.
