Course

A loop through downtown Westminster, running out along Main Street, up North Avenue to Grey Street, down to W. Abbey Street, up North Isundega, down Anderson Avenue and up Hall Street. A water station sits at the Anderson and Hall intersection. A one-mile fun run accompanies the 5K.

About

Join us for a feel-good 5K with a purpose! The Westminster Police Foundation supports the Westminster Police Department by funding technology, training, and equipment—enhancing officer safety and wellness while strengthening community–police relationships through outreach and engagement. Our First Annual Westminster 5K & Fun Run showcases scenic downtown Westminster: the city's oldest church, historic neighborhoods, and Veteran's Park. We'll finish downtown with a post-race party only the City of Westminster can deliver!
