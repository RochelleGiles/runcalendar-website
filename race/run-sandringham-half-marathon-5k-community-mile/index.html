Course

Starts and finishes inside the Sandringham Estate in Norfolk, passing directly in front of Sandringham House, and runs on a mix of estate roads and trails through the surrounding countryside. Half marathon starts at 10:00, with a 5K and a Community Mile alongside. The full route cannot be run outside race day as it crosses private land.

About

Experience the picturesque Sandringham Estate, the Royal Family's winter retreat, as you tackle the Run Sandringham Half Marathon, 5K, and Community Mile. This scenic event offers runners of all levels the chance to explore the beautiful grounds and challenge themselves on a variety of courses. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or looking to enjoy a leisurely community run, the Run Sandringham event has something for everyone.
