Course

A multi-terrain race at Margam Country Park, Port Talbot, run off-road on park and woodland trails with no public highways used. Distances of 5K, 10K and 20K are offered and are described as approximate. Runners should expect mud, standing water and ice or snow underfoot. The races are chip timed.

About

Red Warrior's March multi-terrain races, in their seventh year: 5K, 10K and 20K on the parkland and woodland trails of Margam Country Park, starting at 11:00. No public roads are used. At this time of year expect standing water, mud and perhaps ice or snow; distances are approximate. Chip-timed, with a medal for every finisher and hot food at the finish. A canicross race runs the same day.
