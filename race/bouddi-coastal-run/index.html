About

The Bouddi Coastal Run is a trail event held in Bouddi National Park on the NSW Central Coast, starting at Killcare Heights. It offers 21km and 14km trail courses plus a 1km kids beach run. The route mixes smooth single trail, clifftop sections, beach sand and rocky ground, and is fully marked with aid stations.
