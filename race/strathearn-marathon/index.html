Course

An officially measured road marathon on the quiet roads of Strathearn, starting and finishing at Cultybraggan Camp near Comrie. After a lap of the camp the route runs southwest through Glen Artney, southeast towards Braco, then north on an old Roman road towards Crieff before returning west to Comrie. About 500 metres of ascent.

About

The Strathearn Marathon is an officially measured road marathon on the quiet roads of Perthshire, run by Strathearn Harriers. It starts and finishes at Cultybraggan Camp near Comrie, heading through Glen Artney towards Braco and Crieff before turning back west to the finish.
