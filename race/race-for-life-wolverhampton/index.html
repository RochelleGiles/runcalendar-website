Course

Two laps of West Park in Wolverhampton, run predominantly on tarmac paths with the start and finish on tarmac. Fairly flat overall, with some inclines and twists and turns. Participants may walk, jog or run at their own pace.

About

Race for Life is Cancer Research UK's non-competitive 5K around West Park, a Victorian park in Wolverhampton with a boating lake. Participants can run, jog or walk, raising money for cancer research.
