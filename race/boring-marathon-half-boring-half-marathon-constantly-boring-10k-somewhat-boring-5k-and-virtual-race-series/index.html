Course

All four distances start from Boring Station Trailhead Park just off SE Dee Street in downtown Boring, on staggered starts from 6:30am for the marathon through 8:30am for the 5K. Aid stations are staffed for the first six hours of each race and the event is chip-timed. No race-day entry.

About

Runners choose from a 5K, 10K, half marathon and full marathon, plus a virtual race series for those entering remotely. All four in-person races start from Boring Station Trailhead Park in Boring, Oregon. The marathon qualifies finishers for the Boston Marathon.
