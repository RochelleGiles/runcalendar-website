About

The Mattoni Running Festival in České Budějovice is a must-do event for runners in the Czech Republic. Held in the historic city center, this vibrant festival features both a scenic marathon route and a range of distances to challenge athletes of all levels. With a lively atmosphere and stunning backdrops, this is an unforgettable running experience not to be missed.
