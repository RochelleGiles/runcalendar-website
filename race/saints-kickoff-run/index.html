Course

A city-centre 5K starting in Champions Square. The route heads toward the French Quarter along Poydras and N Peters, turns near the Bienville Monument, then makes a half lap of the Caesars Superdome before runners enter the Saints team tunnel and finish on the 50-yard line inside the stadium. All road, flat, with a Kids Fun Run also finishing on the field.

About

Presented by Hancock Whitney, the 5K takes runners through the streets of New Orleans each September as the Saints open their season. The race supports local charities and community programs tied to the team.
