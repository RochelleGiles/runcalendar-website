Course

Half marathon, 10K and 5K trail races at Button Farm, Germantown, over twisty singletrack and wide-open farm fields. The 5K and 10K reuse the summer Blue Crab Bolt courses in late-autumn conditions. Support stations sit at the start/finish and mile 1.8, with an extra at mile 5.3 for the half marathon.

About

The Schaeffer Half Marathon, 10K and 5K Trail Run winds through the singletrack of Schaeffer Farm in South Germantown Recreational Park, Maryland, a popular mountain-biking and trail-running network known for its rolling, wooded terrain.
