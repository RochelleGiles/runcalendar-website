Course

All four races start and finish at Wright Park in Middlebury and run on the Trail Around Middlebury. The 19-miler covers the entire TAM across several towns on scenic rolling trail; the 10K takes single-track through Wright Park and the Otter Creek Gorge Loop for about 400 feet of climb; the 5K mixes wide and single track, and a 2-mile fun run follows Otter Creek.

About

TAM Trek follows the Trail Around Middlebury, a community loop trail maintained by the Middlebury Area Land Trust, with distances from a 2-mile fun run up to 19 miles through Vermont forest and farmland.
