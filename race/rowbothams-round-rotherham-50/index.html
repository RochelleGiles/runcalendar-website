Course

A waymarked 50-mile circuit of the South Yorkshire Forest, starting and finishing at Manvers Waterfront Boat Club. The route is undulating but low-lying, with around 800m of climb, and mixes field paths, canal towpath and railway level crossings. Seven staffed checkpoints along the way. Also run as a relay for teams of two to eight, changing at each checkpoint.

About

A 50-mile ultra trail race circumnavigating the whole borough of Rotherham in South Yorkshire, now in its 40th year. The route crosses farmland, woodland and the Trans Pennine Trail on a full loop back to the start.
