Course

A last-one-standing format on a 2km trail circuit at the Harrison County Fairgrounds on County Farm Road. Runners complete one loop every hour for 12 hours, up to 24km. The race runs overnight and headlamps are required. A basecamp with fire pits is provided but there are no staffed aid stations: runners bring their own tents, chairs, food and water.

About

A Dusk-til-Dawn Fundraiser for the Coastal MS Wildcats Track & Field Program! Are you ready to join the frenzy? Lace up your shoes for an overnight endurance challenge like no other. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or a casual walker, the 12hr Frenzy is your chance to push your limits, support the Wildcat team, and experience the Harrison County Fairgrounds under the stars.
