Course

A seven-person relay of roughly 77.7 miles circumnavigating Seneca Lake through the Finger Lakes, run on public roads past inlets, waterfalls and vineyards. Teams start in waves from the lakefront behind the Finger Lakes Welcome Center in Geneva from 5:55am and finish at Lakefront Park. Seven numbered exchange zones ring the lake, with mandatory arrival times at several to keep the field moving.

About

Seneca7 is a 77.7-mile relay run circling Seneca Lake in New York's Finger Lakes region, typically split across seven legs per team. Teams pass through lakeside towns and wine country over the course of a full day of running.
