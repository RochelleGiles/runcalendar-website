Course

Starts and finishes at Morriston Running Track in Elgin and follows the Moray coastline. The organisers describe it as measured, flat and fast, suited to qualifying times. The Chilled 20 joins the marathon course at 6.2 miles and follows the same route to the finish.

About

The Moray Marathon is the only road marathon in the North & East of Scotland held in the Springtime. Set against the stunning landscapes of the United Kingdom's Moray region, this picturesque event offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the beauty of the Scottish countryside. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time racer, the Moray Marathon promises an unforgettable running adventure.
