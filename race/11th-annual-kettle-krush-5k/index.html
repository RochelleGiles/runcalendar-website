About

Run for fitness! Walk for fun! There's a something for everyone at the 11th Annual Kettle Krush 5K run/walk on May 17 at Marietta Square, 1 1Cherokee Street, Marietta. Organized by The Salvation Army Women's Auxiliary, participants, sponsors and donors will be helping The Salvation Army "krush" poverty, homelessness and human trafficking while supporting veterans and youth enrichment in metro Atlanta.
