About

The 20th edition of the Trail de Pleyben, the final stage of the Armorik Trail Challenge, offers a variety of new and challenging courses. Runners can choose from a 44km 'Canal PopKorn' trail for experienced athletes, a 15km 'Radio Lavoir' course with some surprises, and a 5km 'Petites Ondes' route for less experienced runners. The event also includes a family-friendly walking event and children's races, ensuring a fun and inclusive atmosphere.
