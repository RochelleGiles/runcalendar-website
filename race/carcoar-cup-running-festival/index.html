Course

The marathon starts on Belubula Street, Carcoar (8:00am), the half marathon at Crouch Street, Neville (8:45am) and the 10km at Mandurama/Fell Timber Road (9:30am), all running point-to-point into Carcoar. The 6km Carcoar to Creek Dash and the kids 1.8km start in Carcoar. Surfaces are a mix of bitumen and dirt roads over undulating terrain. The marathon also runs as a four-leg relay.

About

Carcoar Cup Running Festival is a hilly marathon, half marathon, 10km and 6km event through the historic village of Carcoar in the NSW Central Tablelands. The festival carries $15,000 in prize money and includes a Saturday pasta dinner and Sunday awards presentation.
