About

3 Días Trébol Trail takes place over three days around Vallehermoso on the volcanic island of La Gomera in the Canary Islands. Runners tackle laurel forest, deep ravines and coastal cliffs on stages from 13K up to a marathon distance, with Garajonay National Park's rainforest as a backdrop.
