Course

All distances run out and back on the Lake Apopka Loop Trail on the lake's Northshore, starting and finishing at Magnolia Park in Apopka. The surface is mostly hard-packed limestone with scattered grass, dirt and gravel. The 30K covers 18.6 miles out and back and the 8-miler turns at the historic Pumphouse; a half marathon is new for 2027.

About

Discover the wild side of running at the AdventHealth Lake Apopka Scenic Trail Run! Step off the pavement and onto a path of pure adventure as you explore one of Florida's hidden gems — the Lake Apopka Loop Trail. Choose your challenge: the 30K, 8 Mile, or 5K distance, each winding along the lake's beautiful shoreline with plenty of opportunities to spot local wildlife.
