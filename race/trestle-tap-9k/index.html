Course

A point-to-point evening 9K starting at the Flat Tire Lounge on South Madison Street in Madrid and finishing in Woodward, run at 18:30. The route follows the paved High Trestle Trail and crosses the landmark half-mile trestle bridge over the Des Moines River valley. The direction reverses each year.

About

The Trestle Tap 9K runs through Madrid, Iowa, along the High Trestle Trail, home to the trail's landmark lighted bridge spanning the Des Moines River valley. The race finishes with a post-run beer tap for participants.
