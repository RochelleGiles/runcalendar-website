About

The great classic run at the beginning of December. Starting from Milly-sur-Thérain, going around the ponds, heading to Bonnières, coming back through Herchies, a total of 17 kilometers offered to the most courageous; an alternative with a 4.5 kilometer race for those who want a first experience of running in competition or for middle-distance specialists.
