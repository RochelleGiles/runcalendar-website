Course

An off-road marathon and half marathon starting and finishing at the CCCU Sports Centre on Pilgrims Way, Canterbury. The routes mix scenic trails, rural footpaths and quiet country lanes through rolling farmland south of the city, ranging from wide runnable track to more technical sections. The half is waymarked; the marathon has minimal waymarking and needs navigation. Four aid stations on the marathon, two on the half.

Marathon42.2 km

About

The Canterbury Trail Marathon & Half Marathon is a trail event around the historic city of Canterbury, Kent, offering both marathon and half marathon distances. Organised by Sporting Events UK, it's part of the county's autumn trail racing calendar.
