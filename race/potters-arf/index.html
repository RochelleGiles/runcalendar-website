Course

A challenging road half marathon through Stoke-on-Trent, taking in the city's towns and surrounding countryside on closed roads. Its defining feature is the famous Heartbreak Hill, which tests runners of all abilities. The finish is in the heart of the city centre with crowds lining the route. Also run as a walk, a relay and a walk relay, with a fun run for ages 5 and up.

About

A half marathon through Stoke-on-Trent, known as the Potteries for its centuries of ceramics manufacturing. The route crosses the six towns that make up the city, past its industrial pottery heritage.
