Course

A 21 km loop starting and finishing at Mountain Creek Campground near Tawonga. Runners climb the Staircase Spur walking track for about 9 km to the Mt Bogong summit, descend Eskdale Spur to Camp Creek Gap, then return along roughly 8 km of 4WD track. Checkpoints sit at the summit and at Camp Creek Gap. The field is capped at 350 and starts at 7:00am.

About

The Mt Bogong Conquestathon is a challenging 21km loop hike or run to the summit of Mount Bogong, the highest mountain in Victoria. The course features 1,410 meters of elevation gain in the first 9km up the Staircase Spur, followed by a descent down Eskdale Spur and Camp Creek Gap back to the start/finish area. This iconic event has been held for over 45 years and is supported by a team of experienced volunteers.
