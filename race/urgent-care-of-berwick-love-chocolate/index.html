Course

A fully paved, USATF-certified out-and-back through the Southbridge neighbourhood of Savannah, kept about 90 percent to one side of a low-traffic road. The marathon runs two laps of the half-marathon course under a five-hour limit, with a Friday-night 5K the evening before. Strollers welcome. Field capped at 600 across all distances.
