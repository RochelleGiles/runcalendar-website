About

The ASICS Los Angeles Marathon is a world-class 26.2-mile race from Dodger Stadium to Century City, showcasing LA's vibrant neighborhoods and iconic landmarks. Hosted by The McCourt Foundation, it supports efforts to cure neurological diseases and empower healthier communities.
