Course

Night running on single-track desert trails in McDowell Mountain Regional Park, all loops run clockwise from a central start and finish. The 75K, 50K and 25K use the Pemberton Trail loop at about 800 feet of gain per 25K lap; the 15K and 7K take the hillier Scenic Trail. The route is marked with orange polka-dot ribbons and reflective medallions.

About

A night trail running event held in McDowell Mountain Regional Park featuring multiple distance options from 7K to 75K. The race is the sixth and final event in the Insomniac Night Trail Run Series, offering runners the opportunity to experience desert trail running under the stars.
