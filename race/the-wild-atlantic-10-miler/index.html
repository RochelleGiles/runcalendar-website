About

The Wild Atlantic 10 Miler, organised by Ballina AC, is an accurately measured 10 mile road race beginning in Belleek in Ballina town. The route passes through Belleek Woods, sections of the Wild Atlantic Way and the Monasteries of the Moy Greenway, and is chipped and timed.
