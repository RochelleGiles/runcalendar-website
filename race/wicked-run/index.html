Course

A loop course entirely inside Maybury State Park, Northville, starting and finishing in the park. The surface is a smooth paved hike and bike trail eight feet wide through forest, with an uphill to the turnaround and a descent to the finish, and two-way traffic at one point. Aid stations every mile with water and Gatorade Endurance.

About

A themed running event held at Belle Isle in Detroit featuring 5K, 10K, and 15K distances on a flat, scenic course with views of Detroit's skyline. Participants are rewarded with a legendary chocolate buffet featuring hot cocoa, chocolate-dipped treats, cakes, muffins, ice cream, and more.
