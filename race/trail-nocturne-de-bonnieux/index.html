About

Explore the enchanting trails of Bonnieux under the glow of the night sky at the Trail Nocturne de Bonnieux. This captivating trail running event takes participants on a scenic journey through the picturesque landscapes of the region, offering a unique and unforgettable running experience. With its stunning natural surroundings and atmospheric nighttime setting, the Trail Nocturne de Bonnieux is a must-do for adventurous trail runners.
