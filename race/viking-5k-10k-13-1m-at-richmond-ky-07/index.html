About

A lapped 5K, 10K and half marathon at Whitehall Park in Richmond, Kentucky, part of the US Road Running series. All distances start together at 8:30 AM and repeat a short out-and-back loop, with finisher medals and a 3 hour 30 minute limit. Entry is online only, with no race-day registration.
