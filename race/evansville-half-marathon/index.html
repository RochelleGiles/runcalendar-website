Course

The half marathon route overlooks the Ohio River and takes in the Greenway, historic Bosse Field, and Garvin and Bayard Parks. The 5 Miler covers the opening miles of the half before splitting off to finish in front of the Ford Center. Both start at 7:00 AM, with a one-mile Kids Dash down Main Street.

About

The Evansville Half Marathon takes runners through Evansville, Indiana, a city on the banks of the Ohio River. Racers can choose between the half marathon and a 5 mile race, both organized by the Evansville Half Marathon nonprofit.
