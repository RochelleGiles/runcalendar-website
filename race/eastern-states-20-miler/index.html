Course

Point-to-point road races along the Atlantic seacoast. The 20 miler starts at Traip Academy in Kittery, Maine and runs through historic Portsmouth and down Route 1A; the half marathon starts at Odiorne Point State Park in Rye, New Hampshire. Both finish at Hampton Beach State Park in Hampton, New Hampshire and are described as flat and fast. Aid stations roughly every 2.5 to 3 miles carry water, Gatorade and toilets.

About

Organized by Millennium Running, this 20 mile and half marathon race starts in Kittery, Maine, on the state's southern seacoast near the New Hampshire border. It has grown into one of the region's notable spring road races.
