Course

A two-loop marathon starting and finishing at Waterfront Park in Burlington, each loop covering the half-marathon distance. The route takes in ten miles of bike path along Lake Champlain with Adirondack views, four city parks and the Church Street Marketplace. Relay teams of two to five split the same course.

About

The Vermont City Marathon and Relay winds through Burlington's downtown streets and lakeside neighborhoods before finishing near the Lake Champlain waterfront. Runners can tackle the full 26.2 miles solo or split the distance with a two-to-five-person relay team.
