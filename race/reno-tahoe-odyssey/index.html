Course

A 178-mile, 36-leg team relay run counter-clockwise as a full loop, starting and finishing at the J Resort Festival Grounds in Reno. Individual legs run roughly 2.6 to 8 miles. Standard teams field 7 to 12 runners and ultra teams six or fewer.

About

The Reno-Tahoe Odyssey is a challenging relay race that takes teams of runners on a 178-mile journey through the beautiful landscapes of Nevada and California. Runners will experience stunning views, diverse terrain, and a true sense of camaraderie as they work together to conquer this epic adventure.
