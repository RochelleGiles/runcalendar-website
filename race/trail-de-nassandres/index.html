About

Explore the picturesque trails of Nassandres at this challenging and scenic running event. Winding through the lush, forested landscapes of the region, the Trail de Nassandres offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in nature and test their endurance on varied terrain. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or seeking a new adventure, this event promises an unforgettable experience.
