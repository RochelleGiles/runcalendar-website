Las Vegas Marathon

The Strip at first light: a marathon, half and 7.02-mile race from the STRAT, through downtown and down Las Vegas Boulevard.

Quick verdict

The Strip at dawn, on foot. All three races start together from the STRAT at 6:30, take in downtown and run south down the Strip past the Venetian, then finish back at the tower. Cool, dry and gently rolling, 2,000 feet up.

The STRATStart & finish

Start and finish at the STRAT, at the north end of the Strip. The marathon takes in downtown and Fremont Street, Springs Preserve to the west and Las Vegas Boulevard south past the Venetian; the half and the 7.02-miler run shorter routes on the same streets.

Three things to know

  1. Collect your bib at Caesars Palace the day before, with photo ID. On race morning be at the STRAT by 5:45: there are no shuttles, and roads around the course close.
  2. Gear check takes only the official bag. You can switch distance, defer to next year or transfer your entry to another runner.
  3. Fall behind the pace and an escort vehicle joins you, but you still finish and you still get your medal.

Desert winter: around 5°C at the start, about 15°C by the afternoon, dry air, and rain on only three days in a typical January.

Worth the trip?

Yes, for the Strip on foot at first light. The altitude and gentle rises make it a course to enjoy rather than a PB hunt, and the expo at Caesars makes a weekend of it.
