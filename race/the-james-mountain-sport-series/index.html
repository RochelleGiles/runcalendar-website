About

The James Mountain Sport Series is a gateway to adventure in the wilderness of New Zealand. The event offers a variety of challenging races, including the 35km 'Mad Cow', the 64km 'Pony Express', the 100km 'Epic XCM', the 20km 'Homestead Half', the 50km 'Stampede Ultra', the 100km 'High Country 100', and the 100-mile 'The Great Southern Alps Miler'. Runners will experience the stunning natural beauty of the St. James region, with scenic mountain landscapes and rugged terrain.
