Course

A 5K and 10K on singletrack hiking trails at Seneca Creek State Park, Montgomery County, both starting and finishing at the expo field in the park. The route takes in lake views and streamside running. Aid stations are on course as well as at the start/finish.

About

The Blue Crab Bolt at Seneca Creek is a trail race organized by EX2 Adventures at Seneca Creek State Park in Gaithersburg, Maryland. Runners choose the 10K or 5K distance across wooded park trails as part of the Blue Crab Bolt trail running series.
