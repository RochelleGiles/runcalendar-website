Course

A point-to-point road race finishing at Cambridge Police Park in East London. The 42.2 km starts at the Berlin off-ramp and the 21.1 km at the Claremont Farm turnoff near Mdantsane, with both distances run on tarred roads into the city.

About

The Buffalo City Marathon is a road marathon and half marathon held in East London, part of the Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality on South Africa's Eastern Cape coast. It's one of the region's established marathon-distance events.
