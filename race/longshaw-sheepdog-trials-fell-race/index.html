Course

A five-mile fell race starting and finishing in the trials field, which is visible from most of the route. The course crosses Burbage, Higger Tor, Over Owler Tor, Owler Tor, Lawrence Field and Longshaw Pastures over woods, rocky paths and occasional bog. Run on the Saturday morning of the Longshaw Sheepdog Trials.

About

An 8km fell race across the National Trust's Longshaw Estate in the Peak District, held alongside the estate's annual sheepdog trials. Runners cross open moorland and gritstone edges above the Derwent Valley.
