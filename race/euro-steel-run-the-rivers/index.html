Course

Two-day trail run on a network of trails through the Maloti-Drakensberg Park grasslands and neighbouring farmland near Underberg. Around 48 km in total over the two days, with roughly 574 m of ascent. The route is 100% GPS-navigated: there is no physical route marking on the ground.

About

A trail race in the Southern Drakensberg near Underberg, South Africa. Distances range from 18km to 39km, crossing river valleys and mountain foothills below the Drakensberg escarpment.
