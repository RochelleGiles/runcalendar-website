Course

A timed loop event on a 2.2 mile circuit of Miller Lake in Little Mulberry Park, following paved path around the lake shore and winding through the surrounding woods and the Karina Miller nature preserve, with about 70 feet of climb per loop. The start and finish area sits by the lake, with a water stop at the loop midpoint.

About

The Thriller Lake Endurance run is a 12, 6, and 3 hour timed loop event on the 2.2 mile loop around Miller Lake at Little Mulberry park in Gwinnett County, GA. We will also host a Trick or Treat style Fun Run in conjunction with this event, allowing participants to complete a single 2.2 mile loop with trick OR treat stations setup along the course.
