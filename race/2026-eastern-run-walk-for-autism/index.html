About

Join us for the 18th Annual Eastern Run/Walk for Autism on Saturday, March 28, at Pitt Community College! Proceeds from the Eastern Run/Walk for Autism will fund local programs of the Autism Society of North Carolina (ASNC). You can make a difference in the lives of children with autism and their families by registering for the Eastern Run/Walk for Autism.
