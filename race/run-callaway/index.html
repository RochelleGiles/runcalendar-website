Course

Runs through Callaway Resort & Gardens near Pine Mountain, taking in the resort's landscapes and holiday light displays. The 5K is run at night through the displays; the 10K and half marathon follow the gardens' roads past resort landmarks. A Pines Challenge combines all three races over the weekend.

About

RUN Callaway offers half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances on the grounds of Callaway Gardens resort near Pine Mountain, Georgia. The race is held in early December, drawing runners onto the property's roads and paths for a winter morning event.
