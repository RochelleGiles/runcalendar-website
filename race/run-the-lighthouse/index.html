Course

An unmarked course in Wilsons Promontory National Park starting and finishing at Tidal River, with runners navigating by map or app. The 6km crosses Tidal River to the Tidal Lookout Track, the 21km follows the coast inland to the Telegraph Track junction, the 42km adds the Mt Oberon climb, the 50km runs to the lighthouse via Waterloo Bay and the 75km does both.

About

Explore the stunning Wilsons Promontory National Park in Victoria at the Run the Lighthouse event. Choose from a range of distances and challenge yourself on the trails while taking in the breathtaking coastal scenery. This unique running experience offers the chance to conquer the iconic Lighthouse and immerse yourself in the natural beauty of this iconic Australian destination.
