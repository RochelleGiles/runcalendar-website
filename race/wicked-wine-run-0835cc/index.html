Course

A 5K run/walk plus a 1K wine walk staged at 3 Palms Action Sports Park in Conroe, Texas, starting and finishing at the venue. The 5K carries two water stations on course and a third at the finish; the 1K passes three wine sampling stations before a final pour at the line.

About

The Wicked Wine Run in Houston, Texas, bills itself as the longest-running and most participated wine run in the country, combining fun, fitness, and wine into a memorable experience for friends and loved ones.
