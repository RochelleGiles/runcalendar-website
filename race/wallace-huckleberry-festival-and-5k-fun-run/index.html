About

Come and immerse yourself in the charm of Historic Downtown Wallace, Idaho, during a weekend brimming with excitement, community spirit, and, of course, huckleberries! Kick off your morning with a delightful Huckleberry Pancake Breakfast, explore a diverse selection of food and craft vendors, enjoy live music, and let the kids have fun with games and events tailored just for them. Join the excitement of the 5K Race and take in the scenic route through the breathtaking landscapes of Wallace.
