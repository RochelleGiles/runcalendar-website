About

A scenic running event through Belle Glade featuring breathtaking views of the area and Lake Okeechobee, with courses starting at the Drawbridge Restaurant, winding through a golf course, and climbing the Herbert Hoover Dike. Part of the annual Black Gold Jubilee festival celebrating the region's agricultural heritage.
