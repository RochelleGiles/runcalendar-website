Course

A point-to-point 10K starting at Carrickfergus Amphitheatre Leisure Centre and running through the town onto the promenade along the Marine Highway and waterfront, overlooking the marina and Belfast Lough, before finishing at the 12th-century Norman castle. Fast and flat on road and promenade, with water stations on the route and at the finish. Junior races over 1 km and 2 km run alongside.

About

An annual 10km road race in Carrickfergus, Antrim, starting outside the Carrickfergus Amphitheatre, with a route along the Marine Highway and waterfront areas, finishing at the 12th Century Norman Castle near Carrickfergus Town centre. Includes a 1km Kids' Fun Run for 4-9 year olds and a 2km Junior Race for 10-14 year olds.
