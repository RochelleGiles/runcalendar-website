Course

Run on the vehicle test track at the GM Milford Proving Ground in Milford. The course is USATF-certified and described by the organiser as more challenging than their flat road courses. A combined 5K plus 10K double entry is offered.

About

Run or walk the famous GM Milford Proving Ground! Think you've got the fastest 5k or 10K around? Put your money where your mouth is and come race on this world famous automobile test track. The course is challenging and beautiful and this is the only chance most mere mortals will get to visit this unique location. We are so thrilled to be partnering again with GM for this one-of-a-kind event!
