About

The Brauereienlauf is more than just a running event - it's a festival with a true folk festival atmosphere. Runners can enjoy the beautiful Franconian Tuscany landscape while passing through the communities of Litzendorf, Strullendorf, and Gundelsheim. The highlight of the event is the aid stations, where local breweries offer a variety of Franconian specialties for participants to enjoy.
