Course

Starts and finishes on the Lord Williams's School playing fields beside Thame Leisure Centre. The relatively flat route runs through Thame's historic town centre and out into the surrounding countryside, returning on the traffic-free Sustrans Phoenix Trail, so nearly half the course is traffic-free.

About

The CPM Thame 10K Road Race is one of Thame's largest mass participation sports events. The 10km course takes runners through the market town of Thame to Towersey village and back along the traffic free Phoenix Trail.
