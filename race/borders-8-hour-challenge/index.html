Course

An 8-hour lapped challenge on trail paths in Borders Forest Park. Runners complete as many 4.366-mile loops as they can, each carrying 480ft of ascent, starting and finishing at the event basecamp. Three laps make a half marathon, six a marathon and seven or more an ultra.

About

The Borders 8-hour Challenge is an endurance running race where entrants complete as many 4.366 mile loops as they can in the 8 hour time limit. The route is on trail paths around Borders Forest Park with 480ft of ascent per lap.
