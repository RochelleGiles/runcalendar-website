About

The Josas Trail is a thrilling off-road running event that takes participants through the stunning, undisclosed wilderness. Runners will be challenged by the diverse terrain and breathtaking natural scenery as they navigate this historic trail. With a rich history and a route designed to push the limits, the Josas Trail offers an unforgettable experience for seasoned trail enthusiasts.
