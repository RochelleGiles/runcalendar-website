About

The Rock N Roll Manila race is a must-do for music-loving runners in the Philippines. Weaving through the vibrant streets of the capital, participants can expect an electrifying atmosphere with live bands and entertainment along the course. This iconic event offers a unique running experience that celebrates the energy and spirit of Manila.
