Course

A 4.2km loop up and down the hill at Pyalong, started on the hour every hour in last-one-standing format until one runner remains. The surface is predominantly grass, soft but uneven, with clusters of small rocks; each lap has two climbs, one very steep descent and about 300m of vertical gain. Runners are allotted a 3m x 3m base camp near the start/finish to refuel between laps.

About

King of The Hill is a last-one-standing race at Pyalong, Victoria. Runners set out on the hour, every hour, on a 4.2km grass loop with roughly 300m of climbing, and continue until only one runner completes a lap within the hour. Entries are capped and the field sells out well ahead of race day.
