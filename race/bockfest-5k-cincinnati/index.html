Course

A 5K loop around downtown Cincinnati, starting at The Banks at 10:00 AM and finishing near the Moerlein Lager House, one of the Bockfest Halls. City streets throughout. Entry includes a post-race party with drink tickets and food.

About

The Bockfest 5K runs through Cincinnati's Over-the-Rhine, celebrating the neighborhood's German brewing heritage as part of the annual Bockfest festival. It's one of the Flying Pig TQL Beer Series races.
