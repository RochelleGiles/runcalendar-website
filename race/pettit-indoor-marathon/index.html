Course

Run entirely indoors at the Pettit National Ice Center, Milwaukee. The marathon is roughly 95 laps of the USATF-certified indoor running track, finishing on the front stretch. One refreshment station at the end of the track with bottle storage and supply tables; runners supply their own bottles. Six-hour limit and a field capped at 130.

About

The Pettit Indoor Marathon & Half Marathon is held at the Pettit National Ice Center in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Our races include the half-marathon, full marathon, and full marathon relay.
