Course

Both courses run on kunanyi/Mount Wellington above Hobart, taking in the Organ Pipes track beneath the dolerite columns, climbing via the Pinnacle Track and descending forested single trail past New Town Falls to finish at the Lost Freight Cafe at The Springs. The 13km Enduro carries 680m of climb; the 5km Sport follows a shorter version.

About

The Organ Pipes Classic is part of the Kunanyi Trail Series, running beneath the dolerite Organ Pipes cliffs on kunanyi/Mount Wellington above Hobart, with 5km and 13km distances.
