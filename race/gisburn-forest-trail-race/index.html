Course

A 10km trail race starting and finishing at the Gisburn Forest Hub near Slaidburn, Lancashire. The fully marked route runs entirely on forestry ground, mixing fast forest track with narrower technical trods and stretches of winding mountain-bike trail through the plantation.

About

The Gisburn Forest Trail Race is a 10km run on the scenic trails, forest tracks and woodland areas of Gisburn Forest. Starting from the Gisburn Forest Hub, the fully marked course suits runners of all abilities.
