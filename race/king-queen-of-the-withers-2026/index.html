Course

A 10 km loop starting and finishing at the Rifle Range car park in the Wither Hills Farm Park above Blenheim. The route climbs farm track and ridge tracks, crosses a footbridge and returns on a long downhill, with wide views over the town and Taylor Pass.

About

Run or fun walk or both this popular 10k hill race in the Wither Hills farm park, Blenheim. Great views over the town across to Taylor Pass and a challenging but fun course attracts participants of all abilities, from first timers giving it a go to some of New Zealand's top runners.
