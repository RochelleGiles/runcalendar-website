Course

Run entirely on the privately owned Hidden Vale trail network at Grandchester, west of Brisbane, which covers more than 80 km of purpose-built track. The trail runs are held on the Saturday of the weekend festival, sharing a start and finish hub at the adventure park. Terrain is rolling bushland singletrack.

About

Experience the ultimate trail running challenge at the Trail Run Epic in the stunning Hidden Vale Adventure Park, Queensland. Conquer a range of distances through the park's diverse natural landscapes, from rolling hills to dense bushland. This exhilarating event offers runners of all levels the chance to push their limits and immerse themselves in the beauty of the Australian outdoors.
