About

Les Foulées du Gois is a road race in Beauvoir-sur-Mer, in the Vendée department of France, crossing the Passage du Gois, a tidal causeway to the Île de Noirmoutier that is submerged at high tide. Distances include 8km, 2.4km and 900m.
