About

Experience the picturesque town of Chepstow, UK, as you conquer the Chepstow Running Festival's challenging yet rewarding courses. With options ranging from a Half Marathon to a 5km, this event offers a distance for every runner, all set against the stunning backdrop of Chepstow Racecourse. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a beginner looking to test your limits, this festival promises an unforgettable running adventure.
