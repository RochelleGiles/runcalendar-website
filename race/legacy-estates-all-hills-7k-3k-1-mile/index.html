About

The Legacy Estates "All Hills" event is the hilly counterpart to our popular Eola Hills "No Hills" race held each November. If Eola is flat, Legacy is not — and that's exactly the point. Set on the stunning Legacy Estate Vineyard in the West Salem hills, this inaugural event challenges runners with rolling vineyard roads, sweeping views, and a course designed to test your legs while rewarding you at the top.
