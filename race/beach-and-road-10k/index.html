Course

Out-and-back 10 km starting at the Pier on Old Orchard Street, Old Orchard Beach, Maine. Runners head out along the sand to the Breaker at Pine Point and return by road, so the start time is set each spring around the tides. Aid station detail is not stated.

About

This 10K in Old Orchard Beach, Maine combines a stretch along the town's long sand beach with a road route through the surrounding streets. Old Orchard Beach is one of Maine's best-known seaside resort towns.
