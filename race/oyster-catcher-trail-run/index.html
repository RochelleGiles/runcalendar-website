Course

A two- or three-day trail race between Gouritz River Mouth and Mossel Bay on the Southern Cape coast, with roughly 15–16km each day across beaches, dunes, cliffs and fynbos. The route uses single-track hiking trails, jeep tracks and beach sections with one aid table per day and is limited to 200 entries.

About

The Oyster Catcher Trail Run follows the Oystercatcher Trail along the Garden Route coastline from Boggoms Bay, near Mossel Bay. Runners cover the route over two or three days, moving across beaches, rocky shoreline and coastal fynbos paths between overnight stops.
