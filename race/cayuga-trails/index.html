Course

A 50K and 50-mile run on singletrack through the gorges around Ithaca, starting and finishing at Y Camp in Robert Treman State Park. The route passes dozens of waterfalls and climbs the signature historic stone stairs through mixed eastern forest. About 95 percent trail and 5 percent paved, with aid stations carrying water, electrolyte drink and assorted food, and drop bags at designated points.

About

Cayuga Trails has often been referred to or named as one of the most scenic courses in the country. It offers miles of singletrack, the pasing of dozens of waterfalls as it winds through gorges, signature historic stone stairs, and mixed eastern forests. It's roughly 95% trail, 5% paved, and only a few miles where you'll overlap in out/back format with other entrants.
