Course

A road course through the town of Sisters, starting and finishing at Luckey's Woodsman on E. Hood Ave. Both the 5K and 10K use town streets and close at 11:30am. Leashed dogs and strollers are permitted.

About

The Lucky Leprechaun 5K/10K is a chip-timed run/walk event held in Sisters, Oregon, featuring live music and an Irish-themed party. It is open to runners and walkers with a family-friendly atmosphere in a small-town setting.
