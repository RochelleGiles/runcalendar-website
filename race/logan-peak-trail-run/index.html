Course

Out-and-back marathon from Hyrum Gibbons Mt. Logan Park in Logan, turning at the Logan Peak summit towers. About a mile of pavement at the start, then single-track and jeep road climbing roughly 6,000 ft and descending the same. Four aid stations with gels, fruit, sandwiches, trail mix and drinks.

About

Experience the thrill and beauty of the Logan Peak Trail Run, a challenging mountain course that winds through gnarly aspen groves and mature pine forests, offering breathtaking views of Cache Valley. This rugged trail features rocky terrain and steep climbs and descents, rewarding runners with sweeping panoramas from the summit of Logan Peak. Whether you're pushing your limits or enjoying the serenity of high alpine landscapes, this race delivers a scenic adventure you won't forget.
