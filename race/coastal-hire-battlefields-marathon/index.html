Course

An out-and-back, undulating course on tarred road through the Battlefields region around Newcastle. Water points are set every 2 km. The 42.2 km route includes roughly 5 km of gravel road; the shorter distances stay on tar.

About

A road marathon based in Newcastle, in the historic Battlefields region of northern KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, site of 19th-century Anglo-Zulu and Anglo-Boer War battles. Distances include 10km, 21.1km and the full marathon over challenging routes.
