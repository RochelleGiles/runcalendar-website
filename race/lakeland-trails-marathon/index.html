Course

A full circuit of Coniston Water in the Lake District on marked footpaths and bridleways, passing Tarn Hows and Beacon Tarn below the Old Man of Coniston, Swirl How and Wetherlam. Mostly hard-packed trail with occasional muddy and technical sections, including stretches of private land opened only on race day. Marathon, half, 10K and 5K all start and finish in Coniston.

About

The Lakeland Trails Marathon offers an incredible trail running experience through the scenic Lake District. Runners traverse a complete circuit of Coniston Water, enjoying panoramic views of surrounding peaks including the Old Man, Swirl How, Wetherlam, and Dow Crags. Described by Runners' World as the 'most scenic trail Marathon in Britain', the event promises a spectacular and challenging route through one of the UK's most beautiful landscapes.
