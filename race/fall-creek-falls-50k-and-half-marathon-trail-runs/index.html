Course

Loop course based at Fall Creek Falls State Park. The half marathon runs one loop finishing at the pavilion by the Betty Dunn Nature Center; the 50K runs the loop twice. Trails are marked with tree blazes. Three aid stations carry water, sports drink, gel and snacks.

About

Come enjoy the beauty of Tennessee's most popular state park. Cooler temps shaded run course, and the highest free-falling waterfall east of the Rocky Mountains are just a few of the attractions of the Fall Creek Falls 50K & Half Marathon Trail Runs. Post-race meal and music for all participants. Centrally located between Nashville, Chattanooga, and Knoxville.
