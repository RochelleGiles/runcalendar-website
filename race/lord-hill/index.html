Course

Trails in Lord Hill Regional Park near Snohomish, starting and finishing at the park's south end on Old Tester Road. Every distance opens with a long climb, and the going turns rough and rocky with slippery bridges and February mud. The 5K peels off early; the 10 mile loop is repeated twice for 20 miles and three times for the 50K. Several aid stations, with hot food at the lap aid.

About

Lord Hill is an early-season trail race at Lord Hill Park, a 1,300-acre regional county park in Snohomish, Washington, now past its 20th running. Runners follow a marked course through miles of park trails that could otherwise be easy to get lost in.
