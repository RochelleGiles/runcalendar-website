Course

The 5K and 15K start together on Main Street in North Myrtle Beach and then split, with the 5K reaching the finish first while the 15K continues further. Both courses are USATF certified, with water stops and mile markers throughout.

About

The Riccobene Dentistry Winter Run is an annual 5K and 15K race held in North Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. The event features a scenic course along the coast and a post-race party with music and refreshments.
