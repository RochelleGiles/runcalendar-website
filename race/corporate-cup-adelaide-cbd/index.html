Course

Start and finish at Karrawirra on Victoria Drive in the Adelaide parklands, beside Lounders Boatshed Cafe. Three distances run: 2.2 km Mini, 4.5 km Corp and 7.2 km Mega. Entrants walk or run any time between 6.00am and a last start at 6.00pm. The Adelaide CBD leg runs fortnightly on Wednesdays across seven rounds.

About

The Corporate Cup Adelaide CBD is a workplace team road race through Adelaide's city streets, with courses of 2.2km, 4.5km and 7.2km for colleagues to run together.
