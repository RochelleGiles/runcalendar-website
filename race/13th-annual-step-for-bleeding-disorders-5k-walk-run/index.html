About

Bleeding disorders may be rare, but their impact is profound on those affected. Individuals diagnosed with conditions such as hemophilia, von Willebrand Disease (vWD), and other genetic platelet disorders often face a daunting and isolating journey. The STEP for Bleeding Disorders event is our largest fundraiser, playing a crucial role in supporting the hundreds of families across South Carolina living with these rare conditions.
