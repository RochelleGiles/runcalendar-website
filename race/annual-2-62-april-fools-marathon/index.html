Course

Single 2.62-mile run and walk, out and back along the Bear Creek Greenbelt in Bear Creek Park, Keller. Start and finish at Pavilion 2, 400 Bear Creek Road, with an evening 6:15pm start. The field is capped at 140 participants.

About

The Bear Creek Running Co. hosts the 2.62 mile April Fools Marathon at Bear Creek Park in Keller, an out-and-back run/walk along the Bear Creek Greenbelt. Registration includes a post-race celebration with food, beverages, and snacks.
