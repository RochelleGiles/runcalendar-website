Course

Two-loop road course on the West Point military grounds, starting on the Plain near Daly Field. Runners head east toward the Hudson, south along Thayer Road, then climb to Michie Stadium and on via Stony Lonesome and Merritt Roads past Keller Army Community Hospital and the Anderson Rugby Complex before finishing on Washington Road. Rolling with sustained climbs.

About

The Fallen Comrades Run is a half marathon and 1-mile kids run held at West Point, dedicated to members of the Long Gray Line who died in war. The event honors their memory while building camaraderie through friendly competition.
