Course

A USATF-certified loop of Flushing Meadows Corona Park, starting and finishing near Queens Theater and passing the Unisphere twice and the Fountain of the Planets. The 5K runs one lap, the 10K two and the half marathon four, with a 3:15 cutoff on the half and hydration stations on course.

About

Come on down to Corona Park and celebrate some late-summer fun. Choose either a 5K or 10K as you make your way through the paths of Flushing Meadows and walk away with some great treats from All Round Foods & Waterloo Sparkling Water!
