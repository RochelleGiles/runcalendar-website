Course

Held at Island Lake State Recreation Area, Commerce Township, on winter park trails. Starts are staggered: the half marathon ruck at 10:30 AM, the half marathon at 11:30 AM and the 5 Mile at 12:00 PM. Each distance is offered as a run or a ruck. About

Winter Trail Fest Eastside is a Trivium Racing event at Island Lake State Recreation Area in Michigan, run on trails in mid-February snow, ice and slush. Runners choose a half marathon or a five-mile race, each available as a run or a weighted ruck, with a late-morning staggered start.
