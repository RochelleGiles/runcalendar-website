About

The Rooney's 5k for the last 19 years has been a fun family event for the serious and not so serious runner. Our Annual Rooney's 5k Walk/Run features our famous French toast breakfast, t-shirts, and more. All proceeds benefit local charities. Held at the Palm Beach Kennel Club.
