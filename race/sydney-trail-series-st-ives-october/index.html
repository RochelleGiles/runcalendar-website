Course

Lapped bush trail racing starting and finishing at St Ives Showground, using the trails of St Ives Wildflower Gardens. The longer courses push into Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park over steep, technical terrain with scenic single trail along Cowan Creek. Distances of 7km, 10km and 24km.

About

Sydney Trail Series St Ives runs multiple bush trail distances from 7km to 24km through the natural bushland around St Ives on Sydney's upper north shore, offering a taste of trail running without leaving the city.
