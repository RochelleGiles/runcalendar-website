About

Zegama-Aizkorri is the most famous mountain marathon in the world, a 42km loop from the Basque village of Zegama over the muddy, misty ridgeline of the Aizkorri massif. A cornerstone of the Golden Trail World Series, it is renowned for fanatical crowds that line the course in any weather.
