Course

A flat, fast three-distance road race in Las Vegas offering 5K, 10K and half marathon. Self-serve water stations sit roughly every 1.5 miles and the event is chip-timed at start and finish. The half marathon carries a 3.5-hour cut-off.

About

Get ready to chase the rainbow at the Lucky 5K/10K/Half Marathon at Bill Briare Park in Las Vegas, where every mile feels like a celebration and a little bit of magic is in the air. This spirited race brings shamrock energy, festive fun, and plenty of good vibes—whether you're flying through the 5K, cruising the 10K, or taking on the bold challenge of the half marathon.
