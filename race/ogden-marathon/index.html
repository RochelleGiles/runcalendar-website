Course

A point-to-point downhill road marathon starting at Dancing Moose Farm in the Wasatch Mountains at 5,423 ft and finishing on Historic 25th Street in Ogden at 4,308 ft. The route runs past Pineview Reservoir and down Ogden Canyon beside the Ogden River on sealed roads. It is USATF certified and a Boston qualifier, and a half marathon, 8.2-mile, 5K and marathon relay run alongside it.

About

The Ogden Marathon is Northern Utah's premier road marathon, with a scenic downhill course starting at 5,423 ft in Ogden Valley and finishing at 4,308 ft on Historic 25th Street. The route passes Pineview Reservoir and Ogden Canyon, and it also offers a half marathon and relay option with strong volunteer support.
