Course

An out-and-back on the Ghost Town Trail, a rail trail near Indiana, Pennsylvania, starting and finishing at Saylor Park in Blacklick. The route climbs on a mild incline on the way out and runs gently downhill on the return. Aid stations carrying pre-packaged food and drink sit every two to three miles: ten on the marathon, five on the half.

About

The InFirst Bank Veteran's Marathon offers marathon, half marathon, and marathon relay distances through Indiana County, Pennsylvania. The race honors military veterans and supports local veteran causes each fall.
