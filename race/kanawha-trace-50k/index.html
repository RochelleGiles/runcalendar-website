Course

A point-to-point trail race on the Kanawha Trace from Fraziers Bottom to Camp Arrowhead in Ona, crossing Putnam, Mason and Cabell counties. The mostly single-track route follows ridges and bridges, through creeks, tunnels and meadows and past a waterfall, on a trail built in 1962 from former bison and Native American paths. The 50K starts at 7:00 am and the 25K and 10K at 9:00 am.

About

A 50K, 25K and 10K trail race on the Kanawha Trace, a backcountry trail system near Ona, West Virginia, organized by West Virginia Mountain Trail Runners through forested ridgeline terrain each March.
