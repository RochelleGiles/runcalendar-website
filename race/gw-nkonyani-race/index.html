About

The GW Nkonyani Race in South Africa offers runners a scenic and challenging experience with distances ranging from 5km to a half marathon. Set in the beautiful Limpopo region, this event adheres to the rules of Athletics South Africa and LIMA, ensuring a well-organized and competitive race for runners of all levels.
