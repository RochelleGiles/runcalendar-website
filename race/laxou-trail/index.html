Course

Two routes through the Forêt de Haye west of Nancy, both starting from the Stade Gaston Lozzia in Laxou. La Belette covers 24 km and Le Marcassin 11 km, on forest trails with short steep climbs and technical descents. The field is capped at around 530 entries.

About

The Laxou Trail is a forest trail race in the Forêt de Haye on the edge of Nancy, in Lorraine. Runners choose a 24 km or an 11 km route on wooded single track and forest paths, with steep climbs and technical sections. Entry is through the Wanatime platform and the field is limited.
