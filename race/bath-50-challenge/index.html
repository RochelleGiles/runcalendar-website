Course

Looped routes of 50km, 29km and 10km from a basecamp at Bath Racecourse, taking in the Royal Crescent, the River Avon and the countryside around the city, with about 1,000m of climb on the full distance. The 50km has rest stops near 12.5km, 25km and 37.5km as well as the finish; shorter options have fewer.

About

An Ultra Challenge event offering 10K, 29K and 50K distances through the countryside around the Georgian city of Bath. The route mixes rolling farmland and villages, raising funds for charity along the way.
