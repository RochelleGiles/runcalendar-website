Course

Start and finish at Festival Park, 5 Fifth Street, Racine, following the Lake Michigan Pathway along the lakefront. The path is ADA-compliant and the organiser states there are no hills, with panoramic lake views, overlooks and seating areas along the way.

About

The Turkey Trot 5K in Racine is a Thanksgiving Day run and walk setting off at 9:00am from Festival Park on the Racine lakefront. Entrants can choose a timed competitive 5K or an untimed recreational option, both following the Lake Michigan Pathway.
