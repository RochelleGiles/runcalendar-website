About

The Trail de Saint-Didier is Les Mollets Pétillants' autumn trail in the Vaucluse village of Saint-Didier, at the foot of the Monts de Vaucluse: 44 km (+1,940 m), 27 km (+1,000 m), a 14 km run solo or as a duo, and a 9 km, all starting and finishing in the village. The 46th edition runs on Sunday 4 October 2026.
