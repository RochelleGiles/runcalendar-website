Course

A two-lap course on flat, wide public streets, starting and finishing at Veterans Park on Loveland Street, Bell Gardens. The route runs via Perry Road, Loveland Street, Suva Street, Scout Avenue and Florence Place, then repeats. Water at the start of the second lap and after the finish. The kids event runs the park perimeter.

About

The Bell Gardens 5K is a community running event featuring 5K run/walk options for adults and those 18 and under, plus a Kids Run. It takes place in Bell Gardens, California, promoting fitness and local participation.
