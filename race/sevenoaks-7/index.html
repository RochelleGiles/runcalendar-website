Course

Seven miles on grass and paths through the rolling hills of Knole Park, Sevenoaks, passing close to Knole House. The route opens on a climb runners call the triangle of death, has a final killer climb near the end and closes with a scenic downhill finish. A 3.5km junior race runs alongside it for ages 9-15.

About

The Sevenoaks 7 is a captivating 7-mile multi-terrain race through the picturesque rolling hills of Knole Park in Kent, United Kingdom. Runners will be treated to stunning countryside views as they navigate this challenging course, making it a must-do event for any trail enthusiast.
