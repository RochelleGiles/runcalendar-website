Course

A 10-mile loop from the Historic Occoneechee Speedway Track, following the Riverwalk section of the Mountains-to-Sea Trail through Gold Park before climbing Occoneechee Mountain for around 700ft of gain. Surfaces run from paved path and gravel access road to rocky, rooted singletrack. Two aid stations are each passed twice.

About

The Occoneechee Mountain Challenge is a 10-mile trail race starting and finishing at the Historic Occoneechee Speedway Track in Hillsborough, NC. The course features the Riverwalk trail, a loop around Occoneechee Mountain with 700 feet of elevation gain, and a mix of paved paths, single-track trails, and gravel roads.
