Course

A lap course at Magnuson Park, Seattle, starting and finishing near the waterfront at 7400 Sand Point Way NE. The 5K is two loops and the 10K is four. The organiser does not state the surface or list aid stations.

About

Running from Gluten is a 5K and 10K organized by the Celiac Guild to support Seattle Children's Hospital's Celiac Program. The race takes place at Magnuson Park in Seattle.
