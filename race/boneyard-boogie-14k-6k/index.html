Course

Two single-track trail courses on Eagle's hillside network, north of the Eagle River. The 14K climbs through pinyon and juniper for about 1,279 ft of gain and is 87% single track, 11% paved road and 2% dirt/gravel. The 6K is 96% single track with roughly 375 ft of gain. Both start and finish in the Town of Eagle.

About

The Boneyard Boogie kicks off the Vail Trail Running Series with a 14K through pinyon groves and juniper shrubs on Eagle's scenic singletrack trails, climbing 1,279 feet to a maximum elevation of 7,718 feet. Both the 14K and 6K start and finish at the Eagle Pool and Ice Rink.
