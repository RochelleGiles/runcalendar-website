Course

Event venue is Sage Court, Baranduda. The 16.3km long course climbs to the Mt Baranduda Ridge Track, crosses open farmland and technical trail, then descends back to the start. The 6.9km short course is a shorter, more flowing alternative on the same trail network. One aid station at 11.5km carries water, fruit and lollies.

About

Nestled in the heart of Victoria's stunning alpine region, the Mt Baranduda Trail Run offers runners a chance to conquer two challenging courses amidst breathtaking natural scenery. Whether you choose the 16km or 7km route, you'll be treated to panoramic vistas and a true test of your endurance as you navigate the rugged terrain of this iconic mountain trail. Part of the renowned Riverina Trail Series, this event is a must-do for any trail running enthusiast.
