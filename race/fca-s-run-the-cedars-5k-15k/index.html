Course

A 15K, 5K and 1-mile walk based at Cedars of Lebanon State Park in Lebanon, Tennessee. Both running courses are USATF certified; the 5K stays within the park while the 15K extends onto surrounding rural roads. Start and finish are on WPA Road near the Cedars Road intersection, with water stops on course.

About

FCA's Run the Cedars 5k/15k is a fundraiser for Wilson County Fellowship of Christian Athletes held at Cedars of Lebanon State Park. The event features scenic routes through the park and surrounding rural roads, with live music, food, and vendors.
