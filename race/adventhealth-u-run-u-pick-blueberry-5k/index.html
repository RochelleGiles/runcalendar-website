Course

A 5K starting at 7am from Southern Hill Farms on Schofield Road, Clermont, running through the farm and along the surrounding clay roads before finishing back at the farm. Finishers get a pancake breakfast with fresh blueberries and can pick their own pint of fruit. A virtual option is also offered.

About

Run, eat, and pick fresh Florida blueberries at the AdventHealth U-Run U-Pick Blueberry 5K! Start your morning with a scenic run through Southern Hill Farms and along Clermont's beautiful clay roads, then celebrate in the sweetest way possible at Southern Hill Farms. After you cross the finish line, enjoy a FREE pancake breakfast, complete with fresh blueberries, and head out into the fields to pick your own pint of Florida-fresh blueberries — on us!
