Course

Start and finish are at the Finch Hatton RSL. The 5 km is a 2.5 km out-and-back crossing Cattle Creek through rainforest and cane paddocks. The 12 km runs over private farmland across five properties, with rainforest, mud, water, sand and rock underfoot, and includes deep creek crossings.

About

The Zach Mach River Run is an off-road event based at the Finch Hatton RSL in Queensland's Pioneer Valley, west of Mackay. The 5 km crosses Cattle Creek and runs through rainforest and cane paddocks; the 12 km crosses private farmland over five properties with mud, water, sand and rock underfoot.
