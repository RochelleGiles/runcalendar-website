Course

Out-and-back courses on the Jingi Wallah – Northern Rivers Rail Trail through the Richmond Valley countryside at Casino. The half marathon covers two laps of the trail, with shorter out-and-backs for the 10km, 5km and 2km. All events start at 7:00am. No aid station detail is published.

About

The Casino Fun Run is a community road race through the Richmond Valley town of Casino, with options from 2km to a half marathon. It is known for its accessible, inclusive course for wheelchair users and people of all abilities.
