Course

All distances start and finish near the clock tower in Schweitzer Village. Courses climb through subalpine forest to ridge tops on rocky two-track access roads and blue-rated single-track, including some rocky downhill mountain-bike trail. The Sky Climb adds a steep off-trail uphill section. Multiple aid stations on course.

About

Race the Wolf is a multi-day mountain trail running event at Schweitzer Mountain Resort near Sandpoint, ID. It features races including a 5 miler, Sky Climb, half marathon, and marathon on challenging terrain with singletrack, two-track, and off-trail sections.
