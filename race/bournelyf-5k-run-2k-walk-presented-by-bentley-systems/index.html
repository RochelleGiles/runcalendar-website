About

The Bournelyf 5K Run and 2K Walk is an evening race in downtown West Chester, Pennsylvania, supporting Bournelyf Special Camp. Walkers complete one loop of the town centre course and runners two, starting at Church and Market Streets. An after-party with prizes follows at a local brewery.
