Course

Starts and finishes on the sands of Abersoch beach on the Llŷn Peninsula, following the coast path past St Tudwal's Islands and Porth Ceiriad. The 20K carries on to Porth Neigwl (Hell's Mouth) before turning back to the beach. Hydration stations, marshals and medical cover are placed around the route.

About

The Abersoch Trail Run takes place on a scenic coastal course in North Wales, starting on the golden sands of Abersoch beach and following the coastal path past Tudwal's Islands, Porth Ceiriad, and Porth Neigwl (Hell's Mouth).
