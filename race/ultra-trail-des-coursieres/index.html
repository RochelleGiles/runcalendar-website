About

The Ultra-Trail des Coursières takes place in the heart of the Monts du Lyonnais, offering a challenging course with panoramic views. The event features several races, including the 104km Ultra-Trail, the 50km Trail, and shorter distances, catering to runners of all levels. The course is designed by experienced ultra-runners, promising an unforgettable experience and lasting memories.
