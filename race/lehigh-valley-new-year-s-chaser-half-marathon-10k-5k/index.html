Course

Three loops of the Ironton Rail Trail at Coplay, Pennsylvania, on a generally flat asphalt surface. The main loop runs 5.25 miles with a spur extending each full lap to 6.55 miles, and elevation stays between roughly 365 and 389 feet. Water and sports drink aid stations sit about every two miles.

About

J's Run for Pancreatic Cancer is a family and pet-friendly 5K run/walk held at Saylor Park in Coplay, PA. The event supports pancreatic cancer awareness and research.
