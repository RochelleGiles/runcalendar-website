Course

Trail races starting and finishing at the Algonquin Shelter in Settlers Cabin Park, Pittsburgh, and run on the Last Trail Standing course in the reverse direction. About 99 percent is wooded trail with rocks, roots and rolling terrain: the 25K is one loop of roughly 2,300 feet of climb and the 50K covers two 15.5-mile loops. Gilbert Love Shelter, 200 yards from the start, serves as the mid-loop aid station.

About

Rise Up Hero uses the iconic Last Trail Standing course started in the opposite direction, so climbs, descents and technical sections hit in new ways. This loop-based trail race at Settlers Cabin Park covers nearly every trail in the park for a true test of grit, endurance and heart.
