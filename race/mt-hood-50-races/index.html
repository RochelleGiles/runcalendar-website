Course

Start and finish at Clackamas Lake Historic Ranger Station. The course runs north on the Miller Trail along the eastern shore of Timothy Lake, joins the Pacific Crest Trail past Forest Road 58 to Frog Lake, returns on the PCT, then loops the northern end of Timothy Lake back to the start. Footing is generally soft with some rocky, rooty sections.

About

The Mt. Hood 50 Races are a classic Pacific Northwest trail running event featuring 50-mile, 50K, and 25K distances with stunning views of Mt. Hood. All races start and finish at the Clackamas Lake Historic Ranger Station near Timothy Lake, utilizing shaded trails including the Pacific Crest Trail.
