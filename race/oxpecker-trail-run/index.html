Course

A two-day stage trail run based at Winterton on the R600, running the flanks of Spioenkop mountain and the banks of the Tugela River in view of the Drakensberg. Saturday covers 21 km and Sunday 15 km on purpose-built, well-maintained singletrack, with the closing kilometres alongside the river.

About

The Oxpecker Trail Run is a trail running event in Winterton, in KwaZulu-Natal's Central Drakensberg. One of the region's most popular trail races, it offers 21km and 15km distances across mountainous terrain near the escarpment.
