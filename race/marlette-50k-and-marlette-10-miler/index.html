Course

Runs from Spooner Lake in Lake Tahoe-Nevada State Park on Sierra singletrack, the 50K climbing through North Canyon to Marlette Lake with views over Lake Tahoe and the Carson Range and eight aid stations. The 10-miler loops Spooner Lake then follows the Tahoe Rim Trail out and back; the 5K and 10K circle the lake.

About

Trail races starting at Spooner Lake in Nevada's Lake Tahoe State Park. The 50K climbs through North Canyon to Marlette Lake on Sierra singletrack with views of Lake Tahoe and the Carson Range; the 10 Miler loops Spooner Lake then runs out and back on the Tahoe Rim Trail. Spooner Lake 10K and 5K options complete the programme.
