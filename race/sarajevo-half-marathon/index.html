About

The Sarajevo Half Marathon is a premier running event in Bosnia and Herzegovina that attracts runners from around the world. The course showcases the beautiful city of Sarajevo, with scenic views of the historic Old Town and the surrounding mountains. The event has a vibrant atmosphere, with local spectators cheering on the participants and creating a memorable experience for all.
