Course

Very flat courses on Sauvie Island, starting and finishing at the Pumpkin Patch, on farm roads past farmland, barns and wildlife. Marathon adds out-and-back sections to the half loop. Seven aid stations carry water, electrolyte and energy gels.

About

Join us for a the best Fourth of July race tradition in the Northwest! Beautiful farm and island setting at the Pumpkin Patch. Always super cute medals, shirts and bibs! SPECIAL COLLABORATION! Ice cream from our local partner, Alden's Organics! Including vegan options!
