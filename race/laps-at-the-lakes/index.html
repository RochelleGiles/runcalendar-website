About

Laps at the Lakes is a timed lapped run at the Lakes Resort Golf and Country Club in Pāuanui, on New Zealand's Coromandel Peninsula. Entrants choose a 6 hour or 12 hour format and cover as many laps as they can in the window, with no minimum distance.
