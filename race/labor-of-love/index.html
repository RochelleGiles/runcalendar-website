Course

Run on Lovell Canyon Road in the Spring Mountains National Recreation Area, starting from the junction with Highway 160 at about 4,500 feet and roughly 30 minutes from the Las Vegas Strip. The sealed road twists through high desert of Joshua trees, pinyon-juniper and sagebrush into the Toiyabe National Forest. Distances run from 5K to 50K on the same corridor.

About

The Labor of Love run takes place in the beautiful Lovell Canyon, just 30 minutes from the Las Vegas Strip. Runners will experience high desert vegetation and a relatively lush Toiyabe National Forest, with temperatures around 10 degrees cooler than the Las Vegas Valley.
