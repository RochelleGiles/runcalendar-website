Course

Four-hour timed trail event on a 1.85-mile loop at Camp Hook in Twin Creek MetroPark, Franklin, Ohio. The loop is about 95 percent rooty trail and 5 percent grass with moderate elevation and one dry creek-bed crossing depending on weather. Start and finish sit in the Parade Field meadow, with a solo run on the Saturday afternoon and a relay on the Sunday morning.

About

Camp Hook 4 Hour Trail Runs at Twin Creek MetroPark in Franklin, Ohio, offers a solo event capped at 60 runners and a team relay capped at 90, with relay teams of two or three and no individual relay entries. Both formats run on a four-hour clock, and pacers are not allowed.
