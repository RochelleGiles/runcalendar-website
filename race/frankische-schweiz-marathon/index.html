About

The Fränkische Schweiz Marathon is a popular running event in the picturesque Franconian Switzerland region. The course showcases the stunning natural landscapes, with runners passing through charming villages and along scenic trails. The event has a welcoming atmosphere and attracts participants of all levels, from elite athletes to recreational runners.
