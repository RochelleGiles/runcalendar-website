About

The Jordan Running Adventure Race is a unique and exceptional event organized by Tendao. It offers a week of travel, visits, and races at various levels, from Xtrem for those seeking an extraordinary challenge to Adventure for those looking to challenge themselves through walking or running. The event features marked routes of 100 km, 83 km, or 60 km in 3 stages, as well as a 185 km non-stop race with a start in Petit Petra, a passage through the ancient site of Petra, and a final stretch in the exceptional Wadi Rum desert. The event is designed for adventure-seekers, ultra-runners, and anyone who wants to discover natural, amazing, and wild places while connecting with local populations.
