About

Lace up your running shoes and head to the picturesque Wicklow coastline for the Wicklow Half Marathon & 10K. Runners and walkers alike rave about the scenic course that hugs the rugged Irish cliffs, offering breathtaking views with every stride. This beloved event is a must-do for anyone seeking a challenging yet rewarding outdoor adventure in Ireland.
