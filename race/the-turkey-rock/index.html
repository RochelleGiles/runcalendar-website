Course

A Thanksgiving morning out-and-back on the crushed limestone basin trail at Floyd Lamb Park, Tule Springs, starting and finishing at Group Area 5. The route runs through open desert with views of the Sheep and Spring Mountains; 10K runners cover the 5K loop twice. Almost flat, starting at 2,462 ft with about 35 ft of gain.
