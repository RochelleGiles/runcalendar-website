Course

Three trail courses in Rotorua's Redwoods / Whakarewarewa Forest. The 7 km passes steam vents, bubbling mud pools and hot springs; the 10 km links the steaming pools of the Whakarewarewa Living Māori Village to manūka-lined trails into the redwoods; the 21 km starts at the Pōhutu Geyser. The finish has drinks, blankets and live music.

About

Run, jog or dance to the finish. Run The Forest is the only reason you need to get up and get going this winter. Three distances, run party vibes, and Rotorua's unique geothermal and Redwood trails. Get the crew together and sign up for the coolest thing you'll do this winter.
