About

The 1LT Robert F. Welch III Run for our Heroes is a 5K and one-mile walk/run in Wylie, Texas, honouring serving military personnel, veterans, police officers and firefighters. Both routes are run on paved roads from Olde City Park, and a ruck march category runs alongside them. Leashed pets are welcome.
