Course

A USATF-certified, fast and flat course circling Grosse Ile, an island in the Detroit River. The half marathon and 15K start at Grosse Ile Middle School and the 5K at the Grosse Ile Naval Hangar, running on quiet island roads with river views throughout.

About

The Rock CF Rivers Half Marathon is our signature spring event—uniting runners, walkers, families, and fighters to push limits and fuel hope. Every step supports the mission of Rock CF: to improve the quality of life for those living with cystic fibrosis through fitness, advocacy, and action.
