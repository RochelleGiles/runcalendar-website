About

The 50th edition of Stracivitanova is a prestigious running event offering multiple race distances in the scenic city of Civitanova Marche. Runners can choose from a half marathon, a 10K non-competitive run, and a family-friendly 4K StraFamily route, making it an inclusive event for runners of all levels.
