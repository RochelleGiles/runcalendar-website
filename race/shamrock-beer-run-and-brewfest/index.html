Course

5K run/walk with beer sampling stations at the start and at each kilometre, finishing with a pint. Packet pickup and staggered wave starts between 1:30 PM and 2:30 PM at Military Park in downtown Indianapolis. Entry is limited to those aged 21 and over.

About

Join us in March for the annual Shamrock Beer Run and Brewfest* at a city near you. Get your Irish on and invite your friends to help celebrate St Patrick's Day. Walk or run the 5K and sample beer along the way. After the 5K, party at the Brewfest with music, food and drinks until sunset.
