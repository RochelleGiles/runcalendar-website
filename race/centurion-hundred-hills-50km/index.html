Course

Two different 25km loops from Stonor Park near Henley-on-Thames, making 50km with about 1,200m of climb across the Chilterns. Roughly 80% trail and 20% road, fully marked with Centurion ribbons and arrows. Five aid stations with gels, electrolyte, fruit, sandwiches and snacks, hot food at the finish, and a nine-hour cut-off. Mandatory kit applies.

About

A 50km trail ultra through the Chiltern Hills from Henley-on-Thames, living up to its 'Hundred Hills' name with constant climbing and descending. Beech woodland and rolling farmland make up most of the route.
