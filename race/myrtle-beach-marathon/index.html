About

The Myrtle Beach Marathon is organized by Capstone Event Group on flat, certified road courses along the South Carolina coast. The 2027 event comprises a marathon, half marathon and an elite invitational, with the 5K suspended, plus a virtual 10K challenge open to in-person entrants.
