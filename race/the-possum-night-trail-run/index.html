Course

The event starts and finishes at Owen Delany Park, 1 Delany Drive, Tauhara, Taupo, and is run after dark on trails. Three distances are offered: 5km, 11km and 21km. Aid stations are at 3km on the 5km course, about 7km on the 11km, and about 8km, 12km and 17km on the 21km, with water, electrolyte and food.

About

The Possum Night Trail Run is an after-dark trail event based at Owen Delany Park in Taupo, New Zealand. Runners and walkers choose 5km, 11km or 21km courses, all completed at night using headlamps, with aid stations on each distance.
