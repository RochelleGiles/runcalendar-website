Course

A fast, scenic 5K through South Haven, starting at the corner of Eagle and Kalamazoo Street and finishing on Water Street beside the Municipal Marina. Run and walk divisions set off together at 10am, with a kids' Frosty Dash following at 11am.

About

Break the Ice is a fundraising 5K run/walk for the South Haven Public Schools Middle and High School Cross-Country teams, offering separate results and awards for runners and walkers. BIGGBY Coffee serves hot drinks at the finish line to keep participants warm.
