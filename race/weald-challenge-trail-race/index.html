Course

Out along the Wealdway and back via the Vanguard Way, starting and finishing at Chiddingly Primary School, Muddles Green. Mostly off-road: it opens with 0.75 miles of quiet country lane, then bridleways and footpaths, with about 2.8 miles of road in total, plus uneven ground and 16 stiles. Checkpoints at 5.6 and 10.2 miles offer water, bananas and oranges.

Half Marathon22.2 km

About

The Weald Challenge Trail Race invites runners to explore the scenic Sussex countryside on a challenging half marathon course. Organized by the Uckfield Runners club, this event offers a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the Weald region through a picturesque off-road adventure. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or seeking a new running experience, the Weald Challenge promises an unforgettable day in the great outdoors.
