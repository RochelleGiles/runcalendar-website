About

The Oak Brook Park District's Pink 5K Run/Walk raises funds to fight breast cancer, with 100% of net registration profits donated to the Hinsdale Hospital Foundation Open Arms Breast Cancer Outreach Fund. The fund supports breast cancer patients and their families, bringing free mammograms to women in need due to lack or insufficiency of insurance.
