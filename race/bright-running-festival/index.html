Course

A trail festival based at Bright in the Victorian Alps, with 10km, 16km, 28km and 50km courses on the mountain trails around the town plus a 2km children's race. The longer distances start together in the morning, with the 10km following later.

About

The Bright Running Festival is a must-attend event for runners seeking a scenic and challenging experience. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Australian countryside, this annual festival offers a variety of race distances to suit all levels, from a family-friendly 5K to a grueling marathon. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a newcomer to the sport, the Bright Running Festival promises an unforgettable day of camaraderie, personal achievement, and breathtaking views.
