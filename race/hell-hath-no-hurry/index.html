Course

Lapped trail race on a 10K loop in a park near Carnegie, Pennsylvania. The 10K runs one lap, the 30K three, the 50K five, and the 50 mile eight laps, run solo or as a relay. All events close at 8pm and participants must be out of the park by sunset.

About

Hell Hath No Hurry is a looped trail race built around community, perseverance, and a great day on the trails. Runners of all abilities are welcome — from first-time trail racers to seasoned ultrarunners looking to take on 50 miles.
