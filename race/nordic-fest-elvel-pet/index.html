About

The Nordic Fest Elveløpet is Decorah's 'river run' race along the Upper Iowa River, held on Saturday morning during the annual Nordic Fest celebrating Scandinavian heritage. It offers scenic 5K run/walk and 15K run courses that highlight the natural beauty of Northeast Iowa.
