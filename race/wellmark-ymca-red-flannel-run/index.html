About

Seen as the unofficial kick-off to the outdoor race season, the Red Flannel Run brings together runners and Y supporters for fun, freezing fitness. It is credited with being the longest consecutively produced race in the Des Moines area, started 35 years ago, with participants encouraged to wear red flannel attire to support the YMCA’s mission.
