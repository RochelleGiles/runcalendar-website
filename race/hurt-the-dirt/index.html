Course

Loop course on hard-packed dirt trail at Luton Park, Rockford, Michigan. The quarter marathon runs one 6.55-mile loop, the half marathon two loops and the full marathon four. Three aid stations sit at 1.4 miles, 4 miles and the 6.55-mile start/finish.
