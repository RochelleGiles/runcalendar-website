Course

A point-to-point course from Bowman Dam along the Crooked River, finishing on the Crook County High School track. Runners in the longer distances are bussed from Ward Rhoden Stadium to the start; the 5K starts at the stadium. A course map is published.

About

The Dam Run is an annual running event held in Prineville, Oregon, United States. It features races starting and finishing near the Bowman Dam at Prineville Reservoir.
