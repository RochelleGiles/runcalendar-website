Course

Start and finish at the Lake Harriet Bandshell, 4135 W Lake Harriet Parkway, Minneapolis, with the course running around Lake Harriet. The 10K starts at 7:30am, the Kids Run at 9:10am and the 5K at 9:30am; the race village opens at 7:00am. Free hot chocolate and coffee are served at the finish and no on-course water stations are listed.

About

Reindeer Run is a holiday-season 10K and 5K in Minneapolis, Minnesota, held each December as one of the city's last races of the year.
