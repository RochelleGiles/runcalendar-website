Course

All three courses start and finish at Speewah Campground and run into Barron Gorge National Park via the Djina Wu track, on a mix of single trail and dirt road through World Heritage rainforest. Steep climbs give way to long flowing descents including the fast, wide Smith's Track. The 17km premier course adds creek crossings at Stoney Creek and a detour to Glacier Rock; 8km and 4km options are also offered.

About

Speewah Legend Foot Race is a trail running event at Speewah in the Cairns hinterland of Far North Queensland, organised by Adventure Sport NQ. Runners can choose a 17km, 8km or 4km course through the tablelands terrain.
