About

Explore the picturesque trails of the Beaume-Drobie region at this challenging and scenic trail running event. Discover stunning landscapes as you wind through forests and up challenging climbs, all while pushing your limits on this unique course. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, this event offers an unforgettable running experience in a breathtaking natural setting.
