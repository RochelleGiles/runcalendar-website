About

The 10 Km De Joinville is a must-run event for any distance enthusiast. Set in the picturesque town of Joinville, this scenic 10K course winds through historic streets and scenic countryside, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels. With a rich history and dedicated local community, this event is sure to provide an unforgettable running adventure.
