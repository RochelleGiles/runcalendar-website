Course

Starts and finishes on the open grass at Yorba Regional Park in Anaheim, with the rest of the 5K run on paved sidewalk through the park and mile markers along the way. Strollers and dogs are welcome, and a kids fun run and virtual option are offered.

About

The Super Warrior 5K is a superhero-themed family run at Yorba Regional Park in Anaheim, hosted by Stripes of a Warrior, a nonprofit that gives financial help to cancer patients. Alongside the 5K it offers a kids fun run and a virtual entry.
