Course

An 11-mile fell race in the Cheviots starting and finishing at Langleeford, taking in The Cheviot and Hedgehope Hill in turn for a total of 3,689 ft of climb. A water station sits at halfway between the two summit climbs and runners collect a wristband on each top. The field is capped at 200; registration 7:30am for a 9:30am start.

About

The Pendulum is a fell race in the Cheviot Hills of Northumberland, starting from Langleeford and taking in both The Cheviot and Hedgehope Hill. It's a tough high-level route across remote border country.
