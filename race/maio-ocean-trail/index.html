About

The Maio Ocean Trail in Cabo Verde offers runners a stunning coastal adventure through the rugged beauty of this island nation. Participants will traverse rocky trails, wind along pristine beaches, and take in breathtaking ocean vistas as they challenge themselves on this unique course. This event is a must for trail enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable running experience.
