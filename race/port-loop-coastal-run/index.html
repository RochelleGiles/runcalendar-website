About

The Port Loop Coastal Run offers runners a unique opportunity to explore the picturesque Inner Harbour of Port Adelaide. This low-key event showcases the area's stunning natural scenery and industrial heritage, providing a scenic and enjoyable running experience for participants of all levels. Runners can look forward to taking in the sights and sounds of this vibrant coastal community.
