Course

A two-lap cross-country style course starting and finishing at the Finnriver cidery and brewery and touring the vineyards. Footing is uneven in places and there are some inclines, but the route suits walkers as well as runners.

About

An annual 5k running and walking event held at Finnriver Farm & Cidery that celebrates community and the beauty of the earth. Participants enjoy scenic views of the farm with cider, donuts, and post-race festivities.
