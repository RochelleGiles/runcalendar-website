Course

A flat city loop: north up Yonge Street, west to Bathurst and down to Lake Ontario, then a long out-and-back along Lake Shore Boulevard to High Park and back along the water past Harbourfront. East across the Don to Leslieville and the Beaches turnaround, and home along Queen Street and Eastern Avenue to a rising final kilometre up Bay Street, finishing outside Old City Hall. About 177 m of climbing in all, most of it in bridge crossings and that last hill.

About

The TCS Toronto Waterfront Marathon is Canada's largest marathon, drawing tens of thousands of runners along a flat, fast course beside Lake Ontario. It also offers half marathon and 5K distances, and serves as a Boston Marathon qualifier.
