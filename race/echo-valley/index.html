Course

Single loop for all distances, starting and finishing at Echo Valley Ski Area above Chelan. Runners head down the main road then turn onto climbing singletrack for a sustained ascent, followed by rolling singletrack through ponderosa pine and a final descent. The surface mixes singletrack with forest road. Aid stations sit at Chaos Corner, the Yurt and Outback.

About

This is a premier Evergreen Trail Run and definitely not one to miss! It's so good you might even want to run it every year! No matter the distance you choose, you'll enjoy endless views towards Lake Chelan and a landscape painted purple and yellow with fields of lupine and balsam root. The winding, nicely graded trails go on endlessly through the ponderosa pine forests.
