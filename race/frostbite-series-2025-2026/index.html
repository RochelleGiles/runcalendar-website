Course

About

Embrace the chill and find your stride with the St. Louis Track Club's annual Frostbite Race Series! A staple of the winter running community, this classic event series takes place in the heart of St. Louis at scenic Forest Park. Whether you're a seasoned runner or looking for a new challenge, our series offers a variety of distances to keep your training on track and your spirits high!
