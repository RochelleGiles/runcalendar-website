About

The 5K/10K Road Race is part of the Sunflower State Games, Kansas' Olympic-style multi-sport festival held in Topeka. Also known as the Cauldron Run, it doubles as the ceremonial opening event, drawing runners of all ages before the rest of the Games get underway.
