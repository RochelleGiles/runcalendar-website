About

This demanding run along the Thames is one of the most popular events in the British ultra running calendar. Runners will enjoy the company of England's largest river as it makes its way past ever-changing scenery, beautiful houses and traditional rowing clubs. Expect everything from tarmac paths to muddy trails on this interesting and challenging course.
