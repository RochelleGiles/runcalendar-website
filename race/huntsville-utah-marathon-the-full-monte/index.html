Course

A point-to-point road course starting in the Monte Cristo area on State Route 39 and finishing at Huntsville Town Park. The route runs on two-lane paved road alongside a willow- and cottonwood-lined river through scenic byway terrain, losing about 3,500 feet from start to finish. Aid stations every two to three miles carry water, Gatorade, gel and fruit.

About

From Monte Cristo to Huntsville Park-the Full Monte is one of the most pristine marathons that Utah has to offer, this course starts in the Monte Cristo area and ends at Huntsville Town Park during the peak of our vibrant autumn colors. This course offers a gentle decline in elevation of 3,500 feet from start to finish.
