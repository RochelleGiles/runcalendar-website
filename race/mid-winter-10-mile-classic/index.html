Course

Ten-mile road race starting and finishing at Cape Elizabeth High School, 345 Ocean House Road, Cape Elizabeth, Maine, run on the town's roads in midwinter. It opens the Maine Track Club Grand Prix series and the field is capped. A virtual option is offered alongside the road race.

About

Organized by the Maine Track Club each January, this 10 mile road race runs along the rocky coast of Cape Elizabeth, Maine. It has become a fixture of the Portland-area winter running calendar, drawing runners despite the cold.
