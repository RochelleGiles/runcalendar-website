About

The 20 km de Montpellier is a road race through central Montpellier, starting and finishing near the Esplanade Charles de Gaulle. It offers a 10 km option and a 2x10 km relay alongside the 20 km, with roughly 200m of climbing on the longer course, with proceeds supporting Handisport and other charities.
