About

The Trail De La Bergere 2 is a running event that takes place in the picturesque town of Gourdon, France. The course offers a challenging and scenic trail through the surrounding countryside, showcasing the natural beauty of the region. Runners can expect a welcoming atmosphere and a well-organized event that caters to both experienced trail runners and those new to the sport.
