Course

A repeated 4-mile loop through Tryon Farm crossing open grassy fields and singletrack dirt trail with only very short sections of pavement; runners cover as many loops as their distance requires. The main aid station sits at the start/finish, with an unmanned water and Gatorade table near the 1.5-mile mark. The 50K starts at 8am and all other distances at 9am, with parking across the road at Head Start of LaPorte County.

About

Tryon Farm 50k is a trail race held at Tryon Farm, a nature preserve community near Michigan City, Indiana, close to the Indiana Dunes. Distances range from a 2-mile fun run up to the 50K, crossing prairie and woodland trails.
