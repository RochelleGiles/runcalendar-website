Course

A marked coastal course starting at Granada Beach, 5100 East Ocean Boulevard, Long Beach. Water and bead stations are placed every two miles. The half marathon starts at 7:00 AM and the 5K and 10K at 7:30 AM.

About

Run for the Beads is a Mardi Gras-themed road event staged by CaliFlow Runs along the Long Beach coastline in California. It offers a 5K, 10K and half marathon on a flat marked course, with bead stations placed alongside the water stops.
