Course

Run in Oil Creek State Park on the Gerard Hiking Trail. The 5-mile course starts at Jersey Bridge, turns toward Boughton Bridge, rejoins the Gerard Trail near the railroad tracks and finishes via Museum Lane in the main parking area. The 13-mile continues further along the Gerard Trail through Miller Farm before returning. Surface is a mix of hiking trail and park road.

About

The Oil Creek 5 & 13 Stacked Trail Races are held at the Drake Well Museum and Park, using the Gerard Hiking Trail and other trails in nearby Oil Creek State Park.
