Course

A 6.5-mile loop in L.L. Stub Stewart Memorial State Park at Buxton, Oregon, repeated up to four times for the quarter, half, three-quarter and full marathon. The loop mixes Pacific Northwest forest, the paved Banks-Vernonia State Trail, gravel roads and winding singletrack with rolling terrain and punchy climbs. Start and finish are at the Hilltop Day-Use Area.

About

The race course is a challenging but fun 6.5 mile loop. In past years the race consisted of a Quarter (one loop) or Half (two loop) marathon. Now we have added a Three Quarter (three loop) & a full Marathon (four loop) option. The race that you want to run in , is the race you decide to finish.
