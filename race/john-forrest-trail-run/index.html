Course

Starts and finishes at the corner of Flora and Park Roads, Hovea, in John Forrest National Park east of Perth, with 13 km and 4.3 km options both off at 7am. There are no drink stations on course, so runners must carry their own water; water is available at the start and finish.

About

The John Forrest Trail Run is a scenic trail running event that takes place in the beautiful John Forrest National Park. Runners can choose between a 13km or 5km course, both of which offer stunning views of the natural surroundings. The event is known for its challenging yet rewarding terrain and friendly, community atmosphere.
