About

Lace up your sneakers and join us for a day of music, movement, and mental health awareness! The Punk Rock Run is a community-driven event supporting the North Shore Youth Council, a vital organization providing mental health counseling services and preserving local history. Your participation will directly impact the lives of young people helping to fund essential programs and expand their Heritage Center. Let's rock out for a cause and make a difference together!
