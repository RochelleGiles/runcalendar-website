A charity run starting and finishing at Marina Barrage on Marina Gardens Drive, Singapore. Flat paved waterfront paths carry a 5km and a 10km route around the reservoir and Marina Bay edge. Organised by Four Seasons Hotel Singapore and the National Cancer Centre Singapore in support of cancer research.
