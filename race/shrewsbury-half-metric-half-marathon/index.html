Course

Both races start and finish at the West Mid Showground. The half marathon takes in the Welsh and English Bridges, Kingsland, Porthill, Coleham, The Quarry, Frankwell and the town centre; the 8.14-mile metric half hugs the River Severn, crossing both bridges and passing The Quarry, Kingsland and Frankwell. UKA licensed and chip timed.

About

A half marathon and metric half marathon through Shrewsbury, the Shropshire county town almost encircled by a horseshoe bend of the River Severn. Runners pass through the historic town centre and surrounding countryside.
