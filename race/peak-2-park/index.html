Course

Point-to-point run and walk from Picnic Point on the Toowoomba escarpment rim, down through the city's parks and gardens to a finish at Lake Annand. The 10 km starts at 7:00am and the 4 km at 7:45am. Multiple water stations are placed on both courses, with fruit and water at the finish.

About

Tackle the challenging terrain of the Peak 2 Park in Toowoomba, Queensland, with a 10km run or a more leisurely 4km walk. This annual community event offers stunning views and a chance to explore the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just looking for a fun outdoor activity, the Peak 2 Park has something for everyone.
