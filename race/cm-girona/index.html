About

The love affair between trail running and Girona continues.The CMG weekend is not just any weekend for Girona. The city is full of trail running along the four rivers. The Girona runners have marked the date in red and proudly welcome the whole family of mountain races to claim the Gavarres massif. From the heart of the old quarter of Girona or from the seafront of Palamós. Choose one of the four routes and discover one of the fastest and most surprising races on the calendar.
