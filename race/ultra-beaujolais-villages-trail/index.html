About

The Ultra Beaujolais Villages Trail is a challenging running event that takes place in the picturesque Beaujolais region of France. The course showcases the stunning natural beauty of the area, with runners traversing through vineyards, forests, and quaint villages. The event attracts both elite and amateur runners, creating a vibrant and supportive atmosphere.
