About

Are you swift enough to outpace the legendary Sasquatch? Get ready to hit the roads right outside of Springfield, MO for the most thrilling warm race of the year – "Running From Squatch!" Step into the realm of the mystical and immerse yourself in the enigmatic ambiance of the Pacific Northwest, right here in Springfield. Whether you believe in the elusive Squatch or simply enjoy the thrill of a chase, this event promises excitement for all.
