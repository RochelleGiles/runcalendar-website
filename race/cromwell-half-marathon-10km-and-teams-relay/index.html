Course

An out-and-back on the Lake Dunstan Cycle Trail, starting and finishing at Alpha Street in Cromwell and turning at the Pisa Moorings footbridge. The trail runs along the lake edge past Matt Connell Wines, Aurum and the 45th Parallel, and is largely flat. Relay teams change over at nine points along the route.

About

Join the Cromwell Half Marathon for a scenic run or walk along the stunning Lake Dunstan Trail! With 10km, half marathon, and team relay options, it's the perfect event for runners and walkers of all abilities to challenge themselves and enjoy a great day out.
