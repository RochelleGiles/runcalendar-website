Course

Out-and-back route starting and finishing at Norco College on Third Street, with a turnaround at Lake Norconian. The lake sits on an active military base, so photo ID is required at check-in and no animals are permitted. The course sometimes stays within Norco College grounds when base access is restricted.

About

The Lake Norconian 5K Run/Walk is an annual community event organized by the Kiwanis Club of Norco Foundation around Lake Norconian. Check-in starts at 6:15 AM with the race beginning at 8:00 AM from the Norco College parking lot.
