Course

Check-in is at Hopkins Pavilion, Hopkins. The course runs over hilly terrain south and west of Hopkins, which the organiser describes as a stiff challenge at a middle distance. The 25K starts at 8:00am and the 8K at 8:15am. A course map is published; surface and aid station detail are not stated. The field is limited to 200 runners.

About

The Ron Daws 25K and 8K is a road race in Hopkins, Minnesota, hosted by Run Minnesota and named for Ron Daws, the 1968 U.S. Olympic marathoner from Minnesota. It offers 25K and 8K distances on local roads.
