Course

An undulating to hilly course on the streets and tracks around Upper Beaconsfield, starting and finishing near the rotunda at Charing Cross. The 6.5km Peake Tower Run climbs about 180m, the 13km double lap about 360m and the half marathon about 600m, and there is a 1km kids dash. Start times run from 7:20am for the half marathon.

About

Tackle the challenging hills and scenic trails of Upper Beaconsfield in this unique running event. Participants will wind their way around the picturesque streets and tracks of this charming Australian town, taking in stunning views from the iconic Upper Beaconsfield Tower. A must-do for runners seeking a scenic and rewarding course.
