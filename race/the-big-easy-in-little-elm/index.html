Course

A 5K and a 1K fun run starting and finishing at Little Elm Park, 701 W Eldorado Parkway, on the shore of Lake Lewisville. Both are evening races, the 1K setting off first, run as part of the town's Mardi Gras festival.

About

The Big Easy in Little Elm, Texas, now past its 12th running, is a Mardi Gras-themed event featuring a 5K and fun walk, a kids' 1K fun run, a crawfish boil, live music, and a hot air balloon glow.
