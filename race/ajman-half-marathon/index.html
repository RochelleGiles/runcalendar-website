About

Ajman's half marathon, from Al Safia Park with the emirate's tourism department: a 21 km, a 10 km, a 5 km and a 2.5 km, all starting at 7 in the morning on flat roads by the corniche. Entries close a few days before the race, or earlier if the field fills.
