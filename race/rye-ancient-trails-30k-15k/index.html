Course

Multi-terrain 30k and 15k starting on Rye high street to the call of the Town Crier and finishing at Rye Sports Centre. The hilly route, with around 1,500 feet of climb, winds through the High Weald Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, taking in historic woodland, orchards and quiet country lanes and linking the Norman churches of Rye, Iden, Beckley, Northiam and Peasmarsh.

About

The Rye Ancient Trails is a stunning multi-terrain race through the High Weald Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty in East Sussex. The route visits historic woodland, trails, quiet country lanes and links the Norman churches of Rye, Iden, Beckley, Northiam and Peasmarsh.
