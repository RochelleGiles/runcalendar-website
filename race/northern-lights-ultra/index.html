About

Venture into the heart of the Arctic at the Northern Lights Ultra, a captivating endurance challenge set against the stunning backdrop of the northern lights. Runners traverse a remote and rugged course through pristine wilderness, pushing their limits under the ethereal glow of the Aurora Borealis. This unique event offers an unforgettable adventure for seasoned ultrarunners seeking an extraordinary experience.
