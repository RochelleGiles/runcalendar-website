Course

Loops through Franklin State Forest's West Rim and West Side trails and the newly designated Head of the Crow State Park in Tennessee. Mostly runnable singletrack with typical rocks and roots and a few short steep climbs, running the rim for several miles past overlooks, a stone bridge and a waterfall. The 50 and 100 milers run two and four full loops.

About

Rim Runner showcases Franklin State Forest's West Rim and West Side trails along with the newly designated Head of the Crow State Park. The course is a big trail loop that runs miles along the rim overlooking the coves on either side. The start time is intentional to get runners to the East side of the course for the sunrise.
