Course

An after-dark trail race on desert single-track loops at the Competitive Track area of McDowell Mountain Regional Park, Fountain Hills. Starts run from 5:00pm, so most of the field finishes by headlamp. One full remote aid station plus a water-only point serve the course, which is marked with reflective medallions.
