Course

A 13.1-mile loop on closed roads starting from Portree, with a gradual climb out of town leading into a fast downhill, then a scenic single-track stretch that often flanks the River Snizort. The 10K starts at the 11 km point of the half marathon course on a dedicated single-track closed road. Water stations are plentiful and both races are chip-timed.

About

The Isle of Skye Half Marathon showcases the stunning natural beauty of Scotland's Isle of Skye. Runners will wind through the island's dramatic landscapes, including rugged coastlines, lochs, and rolling hills. This event offers a unique opportunity to experience the breathtaking scenery of this remote and picturesque region of the United Kingdom.
