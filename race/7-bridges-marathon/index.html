Course

Starts near Coolidge Park on Chattanooga's North Shore and crosses seven bridges - four downtown spans plus three on the North Chick Greenway - on road, concrete and asphalt. Aid stations and bathroom facilities roughly every mile.

About

Race day in Chattanooga starts here. The 7 Bridges Marathon brings you epic river views, a smooth and scenic course, and unbeatable energy from start to finish. Join us for live music, custom medals, Wiki-donuts, and memories that last.
