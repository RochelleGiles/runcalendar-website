About

The Ultra Trail Jack Rabbit (UTJR) is a unique 4-hour, 8-hour, or 12-hour challenge to be completed by running on trails or cross-country skiing under the stars in the beautiful Parc Régional de Val-David/Val-Morin (Secteur Far Hills). This event offers an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts.
