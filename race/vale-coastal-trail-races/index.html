Course

A point-to-point along the Wales Coast Path through the Vale of Glamorgan, with the ultra starting at Penarth Pier and all routes finishing at Ogmore-by-Sea. The course is almost entirely on clifftop trail, passing 19 beaches and taking in the Glamorgan Heritage Coast. Five manned checkpoints on the ultra, three on the 18.5 mile and two on the 10 mile.

About

The Vale Coastal Trail Races follow the Wales Coast Path along the Vale of Glamorgan coastline, with 10-mile, 18.5-mile and 52km ultra options taking in cliffs and beaches between Penarth and Ogmore-by-Sea.
