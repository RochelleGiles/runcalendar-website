About

This trail race takes place entirely within Groton Town Forest and has run annually since 1998, making 2026 roughly its 29th edition. Squannacook River Runners and the Groton Trails Committee organize two distances, 9.5 miles and 3.4 miles, with registration capped at 125 runners per race.
