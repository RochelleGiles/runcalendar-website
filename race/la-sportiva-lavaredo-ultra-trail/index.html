Course

A 120 km loop from Cortina d'Ampezzo through the Dolomites with close to 6,000 m of climbing, passing Lake Misurina and beneath the Tre Cime di Lavaredo. A night start, rocky alpine trails and long descents; the scenery arrives with the dawn.

About

The La Sportiva Lavaredo Ultra Trail by UTMB® is a captivating mountain running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Dolomites in northern Italy. Challenging runners with over 4,500 meters of elevation gain across a scenic 120-kilometer course, this iconic ultramarathon is a must for any trail enthusiast seeking an unforgettable alpine adventure.
