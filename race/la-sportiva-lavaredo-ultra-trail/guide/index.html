Lavaredo Ultra Trail

Cortina d'Ampezzo, Belluno · Italy
TrailAlpine

A 23:00 start on Cortina's main street, sunrise beneath the Tre Cime di Lavaredo, and a great loop of the Dolomites' most famous stone.

Quick verdict

The most beautiful night start in ultrarunning: 120 km out of Cortina at 23:00, timed so the field reaches the Tre Cime at first light. Dolomite scree, 5,800 m of climbing and a lottery that fills in days — the 20th edition runs in 2027.

Course at a glance

5,800mClimbing
30 hrsCutoff
23:00Start

One great loop from Corso Italia: up the valley to Ospitale and Passo Tre Croci in the dark, Misurina's lake at 43 km, then the climb past Rifugio Auronzo for dawn at the Tre Cime. Cimabanche, Malga Ra Stua and the Travenanzes valley fill the day before Col Gallina, Passo Giau at 103 km and Croda da Lago bring you back to Cortina.

Where you'll feel it

📍Passo Giau, 103 km in.

The last big pass lands after a full night and day of running — over 100 km in your legs before its switchbacks even start. Gradients push past 15%, the scree gives nothing back, and Croda da Lago still stands between the summit and Cortina's finish line.

Three things to know

  1. Everything but the 10K fills by lottery: pre-register 7–14 September, draw on the 15th, results in your MyUTMB dashboard — not your inbox. The 120K needs a valid 100K-or-longer UTMB Index and banks four Running Stones toward the Mont-Blanc finals.
  2. The 23:00 start is a spectacle and a problem: you race the entire first night from a standing start. Two headlamps and full mountain kit are mandatory, and checks are random and unannounced.
  3. Cortina books out within days of the lottery results — secure a bed in September, not spring. Venice is the airport, two hours away on a good day, and crews can help only at Cimabanche and Col Gallina.

Late June swings hard: Cortina afternoons around 25°C, the high passes near freezing in the small hours, and the Dolomites' afternoon thunderstorms always in play. The kit list is written for all three at once.

What runners wear

Light for a warm evening in town, with the mountains on your back: taped-seam waterproof, warm layer, leg cover, two lamps. Poles are near-universal on the scree — except the 20K, which bans them.

Typical finish times

Ben Dhiman's 11:49 course best and Courtney Dauwalter's 14:14 led the 2025 edition home; everyone else gets 30 hours, and the tail finishes deep into Saturday night. Across that year's five races, 4,682 of 5,302 starters made it back to Corso Italia.

Worth the trip?

If the lottery smiles, go — dawn at the Tre Cime from inside the race is one of the sport's great moments, and Cortina in race week is the Dolomites at full volume. The 20K and 10K are honest consolations for an unlucky ballot. Book the bed before you own the bib.
