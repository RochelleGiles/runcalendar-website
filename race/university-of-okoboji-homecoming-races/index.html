Course

Point to point road courses around West Lake Okoboji, all finishing along the Arnolds Park Promenade. The 5K starts at Good News Church in Okoboji, the 10K at Methodist Campgrounds and the half marathon at Wahpeton Green Space on Highway 86. Aid stations with water and sports drink sit roughly every 1 to 2 miles; roads stay open to traffic.

About

The University of Okoboji Homecoming Races are held around West Lake Okoboji in northwest Iowa. Runners choose a 5K, 10K or half marathon, each starting at a separate point on the lake and finishing along the Arnolds Park Promenade. The programme also includes an individual and a team triathlon.
