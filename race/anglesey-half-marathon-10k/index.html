About

The Anglesey Half Marathon & 10K is held on a fully closed-road course with breathtaking views of Eryri National Park. The event starts and finishes at the Anglesey Showground, offering a spectacular panorama of Eryri (Snowdonia) rising across the Menai Strait, with the Carneddau and Glyderau peaks forming a dramatic skyline.
