Course

Backyard ultra format: a 6.7km loop starting on the hour, every hour, run entirely within the Nine Mile Camping Ground at Tandur. Runners must complete each lap within 60 minutes and be on the start line for the next hour or they are out; the race ends when one runner remains. The loop is on trail. First aid is at the start/finish only and no crew or personal aid is allowed on the loop.

About

Cobb and Co Backyard Ultra is a last-one-standing race at the Nine Mile Camping Ground in Tandur, Queensland. Runners complete a 6.7km trail loop on the hour, every hour, and must finish each lap within 60 minutes to start the next. The race continues until only one runner remains.
