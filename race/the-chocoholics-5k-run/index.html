About

The Chocoholics 5k is a fun 5k race with a chocolate theme. After the race, we provide chocolatey treats for the runners, and everyone receives yummy chocolate, a custom medal, and a Chocoholics T-shirt. Additionally, we proudly support a national anti-bullying charity, and a portion of your entry fee goes toward this cause.
