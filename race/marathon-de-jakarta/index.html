About

The Marathon De Jakarta is an exhilarating international running event that takes participants on a scenic tour through the vibrant streets and landmarks of Indonesia's bustling capital city. Renowned for its challenging yet rewarding course, this marathon offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the rich culture and energy of Jakarta while pushing their physical limits.
