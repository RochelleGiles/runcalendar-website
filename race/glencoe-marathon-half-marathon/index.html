About

The Glencoe Marathon & Half Marathon are challenging trail races set in the dramatic landscape of the Scottish Highlands. Both races are epic off-road routes that offer spectacular panoramic mountain views, with the Marathon following an ancient route from Ben Nevis to Glencoe.
