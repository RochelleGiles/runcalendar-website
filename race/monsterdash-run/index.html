Course

A loop course around Sloan''s Lake, starting and finishing at the south end corner of the park. The 5K is one loop and the 10K two, run criterium style behind a lead cyclist, with 10K water stops at 1.5, 3.1 and 4.5 miles. The field is capped at 600 runners.

About

The Denver MonsterDash is a costumed Halloween-themed run around Sloan's Lake in Denver, Colorado, featuring 5K and 10K distances alongside a shorter fun run. Runners are welcomed by costumed clowns as part of the race-day tradition.
