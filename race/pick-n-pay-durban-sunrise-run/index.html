Course

Road race starting and finishing at the Amphitheatre on the Durban beachfront, with 10km, 21km and 42km options; the marathon is run as two laps of the half route. A KZN Athletics sanctioned race and a Comrades qualifier. Water points along the route and chip timing throughout.

About

The Pick n Pay Durban Sunrise Run is an early-morning road race in the coastal city of Durban, KwaZulu-Natal. Runners can choose from 10km, 21km and marathon (42km) distances.
