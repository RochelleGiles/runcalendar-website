About

Experience the stunning scenery of Britain's Ocean City at the Run Plymouth Half Marathon & 10K. This iconic event takes runners on a journey through the picturesque streets of Plymouth, offering a challenging yet rewarding course for runners of all levels. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply enjoying the beautiful coastal views, this race is not to be missed.
