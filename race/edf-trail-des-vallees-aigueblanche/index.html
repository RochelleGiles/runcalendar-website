About

The EDF Trail des Vallées d'Aigueblanche is a stunning trail running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of the French Alps. Runners will wind their way through picturesque mountain valleys, challenging themselves on a route that offers stunning vistas and an immersive connection with nature. This event is a must for any trail enthusiast seeking an unforgettable running adventure.
