Course

An off-road run on rugged, unmanicured trails and grass at Mahoney State Park near Ashland. The short course is one loop of about four miles starting at 9:00am; the long course is three loops of the same terrain starting at 8:00am. Conditions are left to nature, so wet and muddy footing is expected.

About

Beer & Bagel Run is an off-road running event held at Eugene T. Mahoney State Park near Ashland, Nebraska. Runners can choose a shorter or half-marathon-distance trail route, finishing with beer and bagels at the aid stations.
