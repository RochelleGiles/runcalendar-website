Course

A flat, fast lapped course at High Lodge in Thetford Forest, almost entirely on woodland trails and tracks. The base lap is 5.4km with just 12m of climb, and runners cover from one to nine laps depending on the distance entered. An aid station sits at the lap point. All distances start together, with a six-hour cut-off for the 09:00 start and four hours for the 11:00 start.

About

A Zig Zag Running multi-distance trail event based in Brandon, on the edge of Thetford Forest. Runners pick from 5K up to full marathon distance along the forest's sandy pine trails.
