Course

Out-and-back courses from the Wai Kai facility at One'ula Beach, Ewa Beach, running Keone'ula Boulevard and Kapolei Parkway before turning for the coast along Papipi Road. Flat and fast, and almost all sealed: the 5K is 100% asphalt, the 10K 87% and the half marathon 94%, the balance packed soft surface.
