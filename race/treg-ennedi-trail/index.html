About

The Treg Ennedi Trail is a challenging running event that takes place in the stunning Ennedi region of Chad. Runners will traverse the majestic landscapes, passing by the Guelta d'Archeï, the Arche de la Lyre, the Arche d'Aloba, and other breathtaking natural wonders. The event offers multiple race distances, allowing both experienced ultrarunners and recreational runners to participate and enjoy the unique atmosphere.
