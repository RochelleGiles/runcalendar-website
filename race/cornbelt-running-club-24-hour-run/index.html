Course

A fixed-time 24 hour run on the track at 3560 North Brady Street, Davenport. Runners lap the track continuously; direction reverses every three hours after an initial four hour block, and volunteers count laps. Organisers supply water and basic aid, but runners arrange their own handlers and supplies. Mechanical assistance is prohibited.

About

The Cornbelt Running Club 24 Hour Run is a fixed-time track ultramarathon in Davenport, Iowa, where runners cover as many laps as they can in 24 hours. The field is capped at 60 runners and the direction of travel reverses periodically through the race.
