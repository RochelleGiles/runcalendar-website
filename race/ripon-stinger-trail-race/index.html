About

The Punk Panther Ripon Stinger Trail Race offers a challenging and scenic trail running experience through wooded valleys and historic sites. The route passes by the world heritage site of Fountains Abbey, Studley Water gardens, and the tranquil River Skell's Valley of the Seven Bridges, featuring beautiful landscapes and historic landmarks.
