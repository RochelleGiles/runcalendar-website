Course

An anticlockwise loop of the Kerry Way from Waterville, County Kerry, over rough mountainside, singletrack and stretches of tarred road. The 43km climbs about 1,240m, running over Coomakista, past Derrynane Beach, Eagle Hill and Lough Currane, with a 5km ridge section above Ballinskelligs Bay. Longer 130km, 86km and 25km races and a kids run share the weekend.

About

Experience the stunning beauty of Ireland's Kerry Way at the Waterville Trail Running Festival. This series of trail races along the Lough Currane / Derrynane loop offers runners the chance to immerse themselves in the region's breathtaking landscapes, from coastal vistas to rolling hills. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable adventure.
