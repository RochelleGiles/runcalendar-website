Course

Starts at the City Beach Kiosk on Challenger Drive, Perth, with ocean views along the way. Both the 8km and the 4km set off at 7:00am. Entries are taken on the morning for anyone who misses the online cut-off.

About

The Founders City Beach Christmas Cup is a road race hosted by the West Australian Marathon Club at City Beach in Perth, with 4km and 8km distances run in the lead-up to Christmas.
