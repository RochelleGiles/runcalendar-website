Course

The 5K and 10K both start and finish at Molly Bowden Memorial Park, 900 W. Nifong Boulevard, Columbia, Missouri. The flat route runs through urban and residential streets in south Columbia and takes in the Memorial Mile, lined with signs and flags honouring fallen Missouri law enforcement officers. Two hydration stations are placed on route.
