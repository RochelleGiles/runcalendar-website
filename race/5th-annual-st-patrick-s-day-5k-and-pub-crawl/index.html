About

Join us for the the fifth annual St. Patrick's Day 5K and Pub Crawl, March 14, 4 PM! The 5K is through the heart of downtown Washington and Washington Park. The route is flat! Registration includes a pub crawl with beer from the local breweries and discounts from other bars. (Participants can choose to register for the pub crawl only as well.)
