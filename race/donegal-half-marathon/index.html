About

The Donegal Half Marathon is one of the most exciting events in the running calendar. The course takes runners through the stunning scenery of Donegal, showcasing the natural beauty of the region. With a welcoming atmosphere and a dedicated community of runners, this event is a must-do for any runner looking for a challenging yet rewarding experience.
