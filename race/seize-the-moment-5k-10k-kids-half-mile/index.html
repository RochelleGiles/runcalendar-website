Course

Starts and finishes at Crocker Park on O Street in downtown Sacramento, offering a 10K, a 5K run/walk and a children's half-mile. The 10K goes at 8:25am and the 5K at 8:30am. The organiser states the course is fully wheelchair and stroller accessible. Surface and aid stations are not described.

About

The 9th Annual Seize the Moment is a combined 5K, 10K, and kids half-mile fun run designed to generate interest, awareness, and promote education about epilepsy, seizures, and SUDEP. The event is open to participants of all ages and is completely wheelchair and stroller accessible.
