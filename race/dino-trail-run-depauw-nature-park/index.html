Course

A 15k and 5k on the trails of DePauw Nature Park, run mostly on crushed stone. Grades are mild apart from a couple of steep pitches of 50-100 feet of climb, and the route passes the park's rock quarry cliffs. The firm surface holds up well in wet spring conditions. Start and finish on West County Road 125 South, Madison Township.

About

DINO Trail Run - DePauw Nature Park is a trail race in Greencastle, Indiana, held within DePauw Nature Park, a former limestone quarry now covered in dense woodland. Runners choose the 5K or 15K distance along the park's wooded trails and quarry overlooks.
