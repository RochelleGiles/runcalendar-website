Course

The marathon and half marathon share a flat oceanfront route on road and coastal path along the Turtle Trail through Mon Repos. The 16 km is a trail run: it starts and finishes on paths, with trails through Barolin Nature Reserve. An 8 km run/walk and 2 km and 4 km junior dashes complete the morning.

About

The Bargara Lighthouse Running Festival in Queensland offers a stunning seaside course perfect for runners of all levels. Participants can take in the breathtaking views of the iconic Bargara Lighthouse as they wind through the coastal trails and beachfront paths. This picturesque event is a must-do for any runner seeking a unique and scenic running experience.
