Course

A 50km point-to-point ultra from Horsham to the Brighton seafront. The route is multi-terrain, running trails and rolling downland before finishing along the seafront promenade. Four aid stations at roughly 10km intervals. Entry is available solo or as a two-person relay.

About

Escape the city and run to the sea at the Run to the Sea Brighton, a 50km ultra marathon on a point-to-point course from Horsham to the iconic coastal town of Brighton. This scenic route takes runners through the picturesque countryside of the South Downs, offering stunning views before the final push to the finish line on the beachfront promenade. An unforgettable challenge for ultrarunners looking to conquer new distances.
