Course

A flat, fast point-to-point run down the Silver Strand Highway with the Pacific on one side and San Diego Bay on the other. The longer distances start from Sunset Park in Coronado and all finish at Silver Strand State Beach on a completely closed, traffic-free road. Half marathon, 10 miler, 12K and 5K are offered.

About

The Silver Strand Half Marathon, 10 Miler, 12K, and 5K run along the Silver Strand in Coronado, California, the narrow strip of sand between San Diego Bay and the Pacific Ocean. Held around Veterans Day, the course passes near the Naval Amphibious Base with water views on both sides.
