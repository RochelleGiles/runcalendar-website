Course

A point-to-point half marathon from Hesperus Church to Ska Brewing outside Durango, run on roads carrying minimal traffic on a Saturday morning. Six aid stations sit roughly every two miles from mile 2.4 with water and Powerade, and snacks around mile 8. Runners slower than 15 min/mile must carry their own water as stations close behind them.

About

The Thirsty 13 Half Marathon is one of the best races in Colorado! The race course is fast and mostly downhill, with gorgeous scenery. The finish line is at Ska Brewing, one of the best breweries in Colorado, where a crowd of spectators cheers you to the finish. You can also choose to add a ticket to the San Juan Brewfest, with over 30 breweries waiting to congratulate you on your run.
