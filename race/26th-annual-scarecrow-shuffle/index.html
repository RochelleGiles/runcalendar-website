About

The Scarecrow Scuttle is a running event featuring 5K and 10K races along with an untimed Family Fun Run held at Old McDonald's Farm in Sackets Harbor, NY. It starts at 10 AM sharp after check-in from 8:45-9:45 AM, rain or shine, with registration supporting local causes.
