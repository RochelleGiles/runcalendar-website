Course

Flat out-and-back road courses along Bayshore Boulevard in Tampa. The half marathon starts at Platt Street and Little Bayshore at 6:00 AM; the 5K starts at Brorein and Franklin Streets and turns around at Gunby Avenue. The 2027 weekend comprises a 15K, 5K, half marathon and 8K plus multi-race challenges.

About

The Publix Gasparilla Distance Classic is the premier, city-signature road running weekend for Tampa, Florida, now past its 49th running. The weekend features four distances plus multi-race challenges: the PGDC 15K, PGDC 5K, the Publix Gasparilla Half Marathon, and the Publix Gasparilla 8K.
