About

The 19th annual Heat the Streets Run & Walk for Warmth is a community event featuring timed 5K and 10K runs, a 2-mile walk, and virtual options. Proceeds benefit local utility assistance programs administered by Dollar Energy Fund to help residents pay their utility bills.
