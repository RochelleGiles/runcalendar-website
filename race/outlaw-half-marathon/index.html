Course

A USATF-certified out-and-back on the backroads around Fredericksburg in the Texas Hill Country, shared in shortened form by the 10K and 5K. Early April brings wildflowers along the rolling route, with longhorns and Hill Country scenery either side of the road.

About

Get your running gear, yall, and make running memories with your friends and family at the Outlaw Half Marathon, 10K & 5K in Luckenbach Texas. Run under the blue Texas skies, along Texas scenic country roads and finish at the famous Luckenbach post office where everybody's somebody! Afterwards, stay and enjoy ice cold beer, eat some delicious food, and listen to some Texas tunes.
