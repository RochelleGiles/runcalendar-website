Course

A 5K and 10K over flowing, moderately technical singletrack at Schaeffer Farms, with stream crossings, log ramps, rocky and rooty sections and stretches of open farm field. Both distances start and finish at the Button Farm Living History Center off Black Rock Road. Portable toilets at the start/finish and at mile 1.8.

About

A trail race on the singletrack network at Schaeffer Farms in Germantown, Maryland, part of the Blue Crab Bolt trail series. The 10K and 5K courses wind through wooded, rolling terrain popular with local mountain bikers and hikers.
