Course

Singletrack trail racing in Tyler State Park, east Texas, starting and finishing at the Blackjack Group Pavilion. The route rolls through dense stands of 100-foot pines over snaking tree roots and deep pine-needle cover, and the organisers rate it 4 out of 5, moderate to strenuous. Aid stations sit at the start/finish plus Old Road Trail, the Group 3 picnic trailhead and Rock Dam.

About

The Piney Woods Ultra runs through Tyler State Park in the East Texas Piney Woods, with 5K, 10K, 25K, and 50K distances winding beneath the region''s namesake pine forest.
