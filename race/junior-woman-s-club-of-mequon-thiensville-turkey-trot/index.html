About

The annual Turkey Trot is a 5K run/walk held on Thanksgiving morning in Thiensville Village Park, serving as the Junior Woman's Club's largest fundraiser. It includes a kids' 50-meter Gobble Gallop dash and supports local charities through proceeds and donations.
