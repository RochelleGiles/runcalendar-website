Course

Runners circle a one-mile loop on a wide, gentle trail of mixed gravel, grass and dirt, with 87 feet of climb per lap. The loop is lit for night running and the direction of travel switches every three hours.

About

Runners will be gathering at Lapham Peak State Park to see how many miles they can cover in the time frame selected; the 12 and 6 hour divisions have day or night options. There is also the "Distance" division, for runners who want to cover a certain distance (such as a marathon, 50K, 50 miles, etc.), but feel they may need more time than the 6 or 12 hours. Join us, and let's see how FAR you can go!
