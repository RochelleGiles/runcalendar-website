About

The Livorno Half Marathon is a must-run event for any seasoned runner or those seeking a scenic coastal challenge. Set against the picturesque backdrop of Livorno, Italy, this historic race winds through the city's charming streets and offers stunning views of the Ligurian Sea. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to experience the vibrant Italian running culture, the Livorno Half Marathon is an unforgettable event.
