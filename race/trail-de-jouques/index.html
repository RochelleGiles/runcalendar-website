About

The 19th edition of the Trail de Jouques is a popular trail running event in the scenic Provence region of France. The race offers several challenging courses through the beautiful countryside, including the 49km 'Big Trail' with over 1,900m of elevation gain and the 29km 'Jouquarde' with a new route and the climb of the Concors. Runners can also choose the 15km 'Course Nature' or the shorter kids' races. The event is known for its well-organized logistics, stunning natural surroundings, and welcoming atmosphere.
