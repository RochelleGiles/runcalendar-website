About

The DoNot Stop Half Marathon is a spring road event in the Milwaukee area, offering 5K, 10K and half marathon races alongside a one-mile run, a kids' dash and a virtual entry option. The courses run through local parks, and youth entries are priced separately to keep the event accessible.
