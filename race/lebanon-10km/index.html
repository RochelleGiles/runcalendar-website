Course

A rolling but very runnable 10KM of two loops on single and double-track trail around Jensen Lake, starting and finishing at the Jensen Lake trailhead in Lebanon Hills Regional Park, Eagan, Minnesota. Total climb is about 200 feet. One water stop sits at roughly 5KM, between the loops, and the race starts at 6:30 p.m.

About

The Lebanon 10KM consists of a fun and very runnable but rolling 10KM course consisting of two loops of single and double-track trail around Jensen Lake. There will be a water stop (and some mad cheering) after your first loop / approximately 5KM into the race. The elevation gain for the race is about 200FT (32 FT per mile).
