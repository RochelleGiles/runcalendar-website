Course

A single-lap road marathon around Lake Windermere, starting at Waterhead and finishing at Brockhole. The route crosses the Brathay, passes High Wray and Esthwaite Water, runs through Near and Far Sawrey to Newby Bridge, then returns up the east shore through Bowness. Roads are partially traffic-free or have a dedicated running lane, and it is hilly in parts.

About

The Windermere Marathon is a single-lap road marathon circling Lake Windermere in the Lake District. It starts at Waterhead near Ambleside and finishes at Brockhole, taking in Esthwaite Water, Newby Bridge and Bowness-on-Windermere. The route is hilly in parts, with around 600 metres of ascent.
