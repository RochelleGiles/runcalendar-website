Course

A counterclockwise loop of Cable Airport, generally flat and fast but with a number of twists and turns around the hangars. Starts and finishes at Mt. Baldy Brewery in Upland, with one water station roughly halfway. A 800m Kid's Takeoff Dash runs alongside.

About

The Taxiway 5K returns for its second year on Saturday, February 28, bringing runners back to one of the most unique race venues in Southern California, Cable Airport in Upland, CA. This afternoon 5K (4:30pm start) starts and finishes in front of Mt. Baldy Brewery, taking runners on a loop around the airport with beautiful mountain views and a chance to see some of the incredible aircraft on display. After crossing the finish line, stick around at the brewery for a post-race celebration featuring live music, food trucks, and the best beer in SoCal.
