Course

A single 3.1-mile loop through the Green Mountain neighbourhood in Lakewood, Colorado, starting and finishing at the Green Mountain High School parking lot. Runners and walkers follow the same sealed-road loop; a basketball knockout tournament runs alongside.

About

The Cayson Pearson Memorial Event is an annual fundraiser to support the Cayson Pearson Memorial Scholarship Fund. The event includes a fun run and a knockout basketball tournament, honoring Cayson's love of sports.
