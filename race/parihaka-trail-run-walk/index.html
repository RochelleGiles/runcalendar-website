About

Explore the stunning Parihaka region of New Zealand during the BDO Parihaka Trail Run/Walk. With distances ranging from a challenging half marathon to a fun 6km run/walk, this event caters to all fitness levels and offers breathtaking views of the rugged natural landscape. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or looking for a scenic outdoor adventure, the Parihaka Trail Run/Walk promises an unforgettable experience.
