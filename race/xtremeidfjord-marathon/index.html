About

Dynafit Hardangervidda Maraton is not a typical marathon on flat asphalt. It takes place in the spectacular mountain terrain of Hardangervidda, offering stunning natural scenery. The course includes challenging climbs that will test your endurance, making this a tough and brutal yet beautiful event. Runners will be rewarded with an unforgettable nature experience.
