Course

Starts and finishes at Mission Point Resort (the 5.7-mile from Straits Lodge), opening on flat paved shoreline road before winding through the car-free island interior on dirt and gravel bike trails and paved roads. Water stations about every 2.5 miles.

About

The Great Turtle Trail Run is one of the most scenic trail events in Michigan, drawing runners and walkers to the interior trails of Mackinac Island during the island's last open weekend of the season.
