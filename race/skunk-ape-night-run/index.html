Course

A 30K loop course in the Green Swamp West Tract. Underfoot the route mixes pasture lanes, mud, dirt and forest roads, single track Florida Trail, sugar sand, fire berms and grassy horse trails. Aid stations sit every 3 to 5 miles, with the Berm Loop aid station passed twice. The race starts at 7:00pm.
