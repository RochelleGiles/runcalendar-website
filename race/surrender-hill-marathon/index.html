Course

All distances start and finish at the corner of Reitz and Kerk Streets in Fouriesburg in the eastern Free State. The event offers a 42.2 km marathon, a 21.1 km half, a 10 km and a 5 km fun run, with the marathon run as a Comrades qualifier.

About

The Surrender Hill Marathon takes place in Fouriesburg, an eastern Free State town in the Maluti foothills right on the Lesotho border. Runners can choose between the marathon, half marathon, 10km, or 5km, on hilly road courses through this mountain-fringed farming district.
