Course

Held at East Point Reserve, Darwin, a beachfront location. Distances are a 2 km and 5 km walk plus a 5 km run. The organiser states the course is wheelchair and pram friendly. Water stations are positioned along both courses and participants are encouraged to bring reusable bottles.

About

Lace up your running shoes and join the Mother's Day Classic in Darwin and the Northern Territory! This scenic community event celebrates mothers and raises funds for breast cancer research in the stunning landscapes of Australia's Top End. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a first-time participant, the Mother's Day Classic offers a fun, family-friendly atmosphere to honor the special women in your life.
