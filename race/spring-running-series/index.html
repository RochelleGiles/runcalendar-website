About

Explore the Best of Brecksville Reservation – a different 4-5 mile course each week – at the Spring Running Series on Wednesday evenings May 6th, 13th, and 20th! Offering a diverse and challenging terrain for runners and hikers at all levels, the series fits easily into many spring training plans – though the trails are so peaceful and scenic that you will forget that you are training!
