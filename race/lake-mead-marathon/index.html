Course

Run entirely within Lake Mead National Recreation Area, starting at Boulder Beach and following the paved River Mountain Loop Trail with views over the lake. Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K share the course, with marathons away at 8:00am and the 5K at 8:40am. The course is USATF sanctioned, measured and marked.
