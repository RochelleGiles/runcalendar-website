Course

Starts at Pfiffner Pioneer Park on the Wisconsin River in Stevens Point and winds through the city trail network and streets, around the University of Wisconsin-Stevens Point campus and past Lake Joanis. Half marathon, 10K and 5K all run on the Saturday; the expo and kids run are held the Friday before.

About

Join the fun, even if you're not running! We need volunteers for the Expo and Kids Run on Friday, Sept. 26, and Race Day on Saturday, Sept. 27. Help with setup, teardown, course support, and kids' activities. There's a role for everyone and it takes a village.
