Course

Backyard ultra run on a 6.7km loop through Brimbank Park in the Maribyrnong Valley at Keilor, with a new lap starting on the hour every hour. Entrants can target 4 yards (25km), 7 yards (marathon distance), 15 yards (100km) or keep going for last one standing, and anyone who fails to finish a loop inside the hour is out. Aid is at the start/finish each lap.

About

The Maribyrnong Valley Backyard Ultra (MVP) is a thrilling "last one standing" event held in the heart of urban Melbourne. Runners will test their endurance on a loop course through the scenic Maribyrnong Valley Park, pushing themselves to their limits in this unique challenge of determination and resilience.
