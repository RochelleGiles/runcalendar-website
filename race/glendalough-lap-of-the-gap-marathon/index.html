About

The Glendalough Lap of the Gap Marathon is a unique road running event held in the stunning Wicklow Mountains National Park in Ireland. Runners will be treated to breathtaking views as they tackle this challenging 26.2-mile course, which winds through the park's dramatic glacial valleys and past the iconic Glendalough monastic site. This is a must-do event for any marathon enthusiast seeking an unforgettable running adventure.
