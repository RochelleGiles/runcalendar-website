Course

A half marathon and a 10K based at Bath Racecourse, crossing hills, stiles, gates, rough tracks and uneven ground with wide views over the Avon valley. The half climbs about 500m; the 10K typically takes runners between one hour and 1hr 45. Aid stations at Weston, North Stoke, Prospect Stile and Kelston Roundhill.

About

A half marathon and ultra 10K through the hills surrounding Bath in Somerset, with the climbs the course is named for. Expect steep gradients and views over the Georgian city and the Avon valley.
