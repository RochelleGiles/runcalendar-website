Course

Four laps of about 5.27 km through Berlin-Reinickendorf. From 2027 the race is run entirely on street: the surface is all asphalt, with no kerbs to climb and no bridges to cross. The 10 km uses a shortened opening lap shared with the fun run, then one full lap alongside the half marathon field.

About

The BORSIG Halbmarathon is a long-running half marathon through Berlin's Reinickendorf district, now past its 27th edition. It pairs a large half marathon field with 10K and 5K races, kept moving each year by more than 100 volunteers along the course.
