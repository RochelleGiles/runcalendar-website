About

The Sonnwendlauf takes place around the Grüntensee reservoir near Oy-Mittelberg in Oberallgäu. The course runs clockwise around the lake with the half marathon completing two laps. With only about 80 meters of elevation gain for the half marathon, runners pass by the dam and St. Sebastian Chapel while enjoying scenic views of the reservoir.
