Course

A senior 10K on Newcastle Town Moor with junior races alongside — 1K for under-11s, 3K for under-13s and under-15s and 5K for under-17s and under-20s. Race HQ is at the Royal Grammar School in Jesmond, about five minutes' walk from the start and finish, where numbers are collected on the day.
