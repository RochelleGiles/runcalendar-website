Course

A six-day, 380 km point-to-point traverse of the mountainous spine of Wales, from Conwy Castle in the north to Cardiff. Runners follow their own line over rough, trackless mountain terrain using a supplied Harvey map. Each day has support points with water and medical cover, and five nights are spent at tented overnight camps.

About

Dragon's Back Race is a multi-day mountain stage race running the length of Wales, from Conwy in the north over Snowdonia, the Cambrians and the Brecon Beacons across six days of rugged, remote terrain.
