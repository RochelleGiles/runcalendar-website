Course

A road loop starting and finishing at Moreau Physical Therapy on Perkins Road, Baton Rouge. The route runs up Perkins Road, left onto E Lakeshore Drive, around toward Dalrymple, right on Magnolia Drive and back along Perkins. Entry is capped at 700.

About

The Shamrock Run is a family-friendly 5K event held on the last mile of the historic Wearin’ of the Green St. Patrick’s Day Parade route in Baton Rouge. Participants enjoy a fabulous after party behind Moreau’s, with the parade rolling immediately after the run.
