Course

Timed looped event at Carkeek Park, Seattle, on a 1.93-mile loop with about 436 feet of climb per lap; runners complete as many loops as they choose within their window. Terrain is rolling trail with steep stair sections and views over the sound. The 12-hour runs 7:00-19:00 and the 6-hour 9:00-15:00. A virtual option is offered.
