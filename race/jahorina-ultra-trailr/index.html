About

The Jahorina Ultra Trail is an ultimate 100-miler race that pushes the limits and chases dreams. The event offers various distance options, including mini, midi, and maxi trails, catering to runners of all levels. The race takes place in the scenic Jahorina region, providing a challenging and rewarding experience for participants.
