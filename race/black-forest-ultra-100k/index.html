Course

Lollipop-shaped 100K from Hyner Run State Park: out the Donut Hole and T-Squared trails onto the Black Forest Trail loop above Pine Creek Gorge (counterclockwise in even years), with over 13,400 ft of gain. Midnight start, 19-hour limit; no crews, pacers or spectators; GPS device with the course GPX is required.

About

The Black Forest Ultra 100k is a brutally challenging 100k trail ultramarathon on the Black Forest Trail in Pennsylvania's Pine Creek Gorge, featuring over 13,400 feet of elevation gain and a strict 19-hour cutoff from a midnight start. No crews, pacers, or spectators are allowed, emphasizing a true wilderness self-supported experience.
