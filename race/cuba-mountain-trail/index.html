About

The CUBA MOUNTAIN TRAIL is a 5-stage, 120 km trail running event in the Escambray mountain range of central Cuba. Runners will discover the beautiful rolling hills, forests, and magical waterfalls of this region closely associated with Che Guevara and the Cuban Revolution. Whether you're a fast-paced challenger or an endurance hiker, this event has something for everyone. After the runs, you'll enjoy the festive Cuban atmosphere, with a colonial city tour of Trinidad and a day trip to a paradise island by catamaran.
