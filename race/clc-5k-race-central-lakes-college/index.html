About

The 5K for Mental Health Awareness, co-hosted by Central Lakes College and Crow Wing Energized, aims to highlight the importance of mental health in the community. Proceeds from the event are directed toward local mental health organizations or nonprofits, as well as a CLC program such as the CLC Food Pantry. This initiative fosters awareness while providing tangible support to CLC students and the local community.
