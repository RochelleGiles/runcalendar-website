Course

A four-race winter training series run by the Ogden Marathon, with rounds on 13 and 27 February, 13 March and 3 April 2027. Each round offers 5K, 10K, 10-mile and half marathon options, and every distance also has a shorter alternative course. Courses differ by round and the venue for the opening round is yet to be set.

About

The Winter Running Series will help prepare you to run any distance in the Ogden Marathon or train for other springtime running events! This four-part training series is supported by race-specific training plans designed by Fleet Feet.
