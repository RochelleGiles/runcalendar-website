Course

A point-to-point road race from Peter Mokaba Stadium in Polokwane to Moria. The 42.2 km and 21.1 km start at 05:30, the 10 km and 5 km at 05:45, with hydration points every 3–5 km and paramedics on course.

About

The A2Z Dynamic Marathon is a road race through Polokwane, Limpopo, with marathon, half marathon, 10km and 5km options and one of the richer prize purses on the South African calendar. It draws elite and club runners each November.
