Course

Track meet on the 400m oval at Civic Stadium (Civic Field), Bellingham. Events are run as seeded and open heats. The headline Gale Pfueller Mile is run over 5,280 feet rather than 1600m. No aid stations.

About

The Gale Pfueller Track Classic is a track meet held in honor of Gale Pfueller, a founding member of the Greater Bellingham Running Club. It features the Gale Pfueller Mile as the main event with heats for middle school, high school, and open categories, plus a 4x400m relay.
