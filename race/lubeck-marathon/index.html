About

The Lübeck Marathon is a must-run event for any dedicated runner. Set in the historic Hanseatic city of Lübeck, this scenic course winds through charming medieval streets and past iconic landmarks like the Holsten Gate. With a rich history dating back decades, the Lübeck Marathon offers a truly unique and memorable running experience.
