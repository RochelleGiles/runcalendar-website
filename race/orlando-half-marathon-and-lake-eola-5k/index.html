Course

Road half marathon starting and finishing on E. Central Blvd in downtown Orlando, ending beside Lake Eola Park. Nine water stops roughly every 1.5 miles, four entertainment zones and a food stop near mile 8.5. The Lake Eola 5K runs the following morning from the same start, and the two can be combined as the DTO 16.2 Challenge.
