About

The Porto Half Marathon is a must-attend event for runners seeking a scenic and historic tour of Portugal's vibrant northern city. Winding through the charming streets and along the Douro River, this race offers a unique opportunity to experience the city's rich culture and picturesque landscapes. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a beginner looking for a new challenge, the Porto Half Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
