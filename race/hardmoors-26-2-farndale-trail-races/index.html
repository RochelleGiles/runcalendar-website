Course

Self-navigated moorland races in the North York Moors, starting and finishing at Hutton le Hole village hall. Routes are neither taped nor signed - runners carry a printed map and route description, plus mandatory kit. The marathon is 29 miles with about 4,200 ft of ascent, the half 16.5 miles with 2,300 ft, the 10k 8.3 miles with 1,130 ft.

About

The Hardmoors 26.2 Farndale Trail Races offer challenging trail running through the scenic North York Moors. The course features stunning views, rugged terrain, and a true test of endurance for runners of all levels. With distances ranging from 8.3 to 29 miles, this event caters to a variety of participants and promises an unforgettable running experience.
