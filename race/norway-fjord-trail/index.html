About

The Norway Fjord Trail is an intense and unique trail running experience through the stunning Sognfjord and Hardanger regions. Participants explore diverse Norwegian landscapes including fjords, snowy plateaus, valleys, and canyons across challenging and technical routes. The event offers both long (60km) and short (41km) trail running options, with additional scenic treks showcasing Norway's breathtaking natural beauty.
