Course

A 7.75-mile loop in Wooster Memorial Park, repeated to make up the 50k, 25k and 12.5k, with roughly 1,000ft of climb per lap. The route is mostly singletrack with technical sections, steep hills, creek crossings and mud. All distances start and finish by the pavilion at the main parking lot.

About

The Super Bull Trail Championships is an annual event featuring races of various distances, including a 50k, 25k, 12.5k, and 5k. The event takes place at Wooster Memorial Park in Wooster, Ohio.
