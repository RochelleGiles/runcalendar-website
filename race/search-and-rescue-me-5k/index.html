Course

A flat two-lap 5 km starting and finishing at the American Legion building in Riverfront Park, Hackettstown, New Jersey. The course runs beside the Musconetcong River over a mix of asphalt, dirt and packed gravel, including a small wooden bridge crossing.

About

A fun timed 5K run/walk to benefit Search and Rescue Teams of Warren County (SARTWC), a 501(c)(3) non-profit dedicated to finding lost or missing persons and wilderness rescues. The event takes place at Riverfront Park in Hackettstown, NJ, with check-in at 8:00am.
