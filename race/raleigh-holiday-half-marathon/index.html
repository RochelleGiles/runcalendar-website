Course

Starts on Crabtree Boulevard for about a quarter mile, then joins the Crabtree Creek Trail out to Anderson Point Park and back. Almost entirely sealed greenway, USATF certified and chip timed. Six aid stations, being three locations passed twice, at Milburnie Road, mid-greenway and Anderson Point Park.

About

The Raleigh Holiday Half Marathon is a December half marathon through Raleigh, North Carolina, capping off the holiday racing season in the Triangle region with a well-supported course through the city.
