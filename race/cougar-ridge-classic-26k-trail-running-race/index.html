Course

Point-to-point 26K (16.1 mi) from Gravity Haus in Vail Village across Vail Mountain to Minturn: 78% singletrack, 16% dirt/gravel road, 6% paved, with 3,439 ft of climbing. 7:00 am start, Eagle's Nest aid cutoff 9:45 am, finish cutoff 1:00 pm; field capped at 250.

About

Enjoy stunning views of Mount of the Holy Cross and golden aspen groves as you traverse across Vail Mountain. The course is a point-to-point race, starting in Vail and finishing in the Town of Minturn.
