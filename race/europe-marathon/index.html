About

The European Marathon began in Aveiro with the aim of creating a great race in Portugal, where sport meets the region's natural and cultural beauty. It is recognized as one of the world's top 100 marathons, and its flat, fast, internationally certified course has become a favorite among runners looking to break records or simply have a unique experience.
