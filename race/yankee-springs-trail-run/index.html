Course

Courses start at the Long Lake Outdoor Center and run the Hall Lake Trail, the Devil's Soup Bowl area and mountain bike trail before rejoining the North Country Trail to the finish. The going is hilly with plenty of rock and root, about 978 feet of climb for the 10k and 1,388 for the 25k, and drains well with no water crossings. The 50k is two laps of the 25k and the 100k is four; aid sits every two to four miles.
