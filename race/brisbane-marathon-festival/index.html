Course

Point-to-point starting on Margaret Street in Brisbane City and finishing inside the City Botanic Gardens. The route crosses the fully closed Story Bridge and follows the river through Kangaroo Point and New Farm. Run on closed urban roads and paths and described as predominantly flat, with aid stations along the course.

About

The Brisbane Marathon Festival in Australia offers runners a diverse range of distances, from the full 42.2km marathon to the family-friendly 2km kids' run. Participants can take in the sights of the city as they race through the streets, with the event featuring both a full marathon and half marathon option for more seasoned athletes.
