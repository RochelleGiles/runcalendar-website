Course

A lapped race on a 5.3km loop through the landscaped parkland and estate grounds of Hockwold Hall near Thetford, among mature trees and open lawns. Runners choose their own distance, from 5K up to ultra, by completing as many laps as they like and must be on their final lap before the six-hour cut-off. Aid stations serve water, squash and cola; the event is cupless.

About

A Zig Zag Running multi-distance trail event based at Brandon Country Park on the edge of Thetford Forest. Runners choose from 5K up to a full marathon along the park's forest trails.
