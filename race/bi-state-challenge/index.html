Course

A two-day, two-state event: day one at Saddle River County Park, Rochelle Park NJ, and day two at Rockland Lake State Park, Valley Cottage NY. Both are flat, paved loop courses. Each day has a single aid station at the finish line, with hydration and nourishment.

About

The XCelsior Marathon is held at Rockland Lake State Park in Valley Cottage, NY, featuring a flat, paved course with scenic views of Rockland Lake. It offers marathon and half marathon distances as part of the Tri-State Challenge.
