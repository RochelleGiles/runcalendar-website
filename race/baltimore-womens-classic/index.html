Course

Start and finish at Power Plant Live! on Market Place in downtown Baltimore. The course carries a one-and-a-half hour time limit, timed from when each wave crosses the start line. Bicycles, pets, skates and scooters are not permitted, though jogging strollers are allowed; headphones are discouraged.

About

The MedStar Health Baltimore Women's Classic 5K is the Mid-Atlantic's largest all-women's 5K and the second-oldest women's-only race in America, now past its 50th running. The course winds through downtown Baltimore streets, finishing with live music and a post-race party.
