About

The Half a Half Marathon is a chip-timed 6.55-mile race through the Rocky River Reservation in the Cleveland Metroparks, starting and finishing at the Emerald Necklace Marina. Known for its fast, picturesque out-and-back course, the race features a 'Fast Last Mile' incentive to motivate runners to finish strong.
