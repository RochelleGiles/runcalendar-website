About

The Aberfoyle 10K is a scenic trail run in the wild and beautiful Queen Elizabeth National Park, Stirling. Starting and finishing in Aberfoyle village, the undulating course covers a mixture of trails, single track and unmade ground, taking in a view of Ben Lomond along the way, and is signposted and marshalled throughout.
