About

The Salomon Bandol Classic is a must-do trail running event in the picturesque region of Bandol, France. Runners can expect a challenging yet breathtaking course through the area's rolling hills and stunning coastal landscapes. With Salomon's renowned event organization, this is an opportunity to experience the beauty of the French Riviera while pushing your limits on the trails.
