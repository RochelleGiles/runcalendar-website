Course

A point-to-point road marathon from Tacoma Narrows Airport to Dune Peninsula Park, with views over Puget Sound and the Olympic and Cascade ranges. The route passes through Point Defiance Park, crosses the Wilson Way Bridge and runs the Thea Foss Waterway Esplanade. Surface is paved throughout with some street crossings; the course is USATF-certified and Boston-qualifying. Aid stations carry water, electrolyte drink and honey products.

About

A weekend running event featuring multiple race distances held over two days in Tacoma, Washington. Runners can participate in a marathon, half marathon, 10K, 5K, or kids 1K, with courses showcasing the Tacoma Narrows Bridge and Dune Peninsula Park.
