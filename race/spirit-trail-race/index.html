Course

A rolling trail course near Buena Vista with views of the Collegiate Peaks. Underfoot it varies from smooth road to rocky single track, marked throughout with pink flags and arrows. Two aid stations sit on the course, with a third at the start/finish.

About

The Spirit Trail Race is a running event that takes place in the beautiful town of Buena Vista, Colorado. The race features a marathon, half-marathon, and 9k distance, and is held during the annual CKS Paddlefest event.
