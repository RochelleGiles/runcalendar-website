Course

Half marathon and 12K starting and finishing on Andover High Street and heading out into the Hampshire countryside past Iron Age hill forts. The half marathon is 84% paved road with the balance on track, run on open roads apart from a single closure on Bridge Street. Aid stations, checkpoints and marshals are set at regular intervals with water and medical cover.

About

The Andover Half Marathon & 12K offers runners a scenic, mixed-terrain challenge through the charming town of Andover and its surrounding countryside. Featuring a 13.1-mile half marathon and a 12K option, this event provides an opportunity to explore the picturesque landscapes of the United Kingdom while pushing your endurance to the limit.
