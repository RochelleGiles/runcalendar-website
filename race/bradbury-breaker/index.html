Course

Two laps of a hilly, technical single-track loop in Bradbury Mountain State Park, Pownal. Forest trail with rocks and roots, traversing the mountainside with repeated climbs and descents. Start and finish at 528 Hallowell Rd. Field capped at 160 runners.

About

The Bradbury Breaker is a 9-mile trail race through Bradbury Mountain State Park in Pownal, Maine, organized by Trail Monster Running. The course climbs the park's network of wooded trails to the summit of Bradbury Mountain.
