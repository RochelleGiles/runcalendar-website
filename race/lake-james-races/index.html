Course

Both races start and finish at Fonta Flora's Whippoorwill Farm in Nebo and run the trails of Lake James State Park. The 7-miler takes in Paddy's Creek, Mills Creek and the Overmountain Victory Trail with a beach crossing and about 425ft of climbing; the 5K loops the farm field then joins the Discovery Trail. Mostly singletrack, with open road crossings.

About

The Lake James 7 Miler & 5k races start and finish at Fonta Flora's charming Whippoorwill Farm in Nebo, NC and take runners on the beautiful trails of Lake James State Park. With stunning views and light rolling hills, both courses are perfect for either a trail beginner or a seasoned runner.
