About

Get ready for the first annual Renegade Rehab Run- an exciting race day event featuring a full marathon, relay marathon, half marathon and 5K. The race will take place on the scenic, paved Suncaost Trail at the Crystal Oaks entrance, running parallel to the western side of the Suncoast Parkway. Professionally managed by DRC Sports, the event will offer multiple divisions for runners of all levels and ages. Sponsors will have the opportunity to engage with the community through marketing events before, during and after the race. With activities and excitement for the whole family, this event promises fun for all- while supporting a meaningful cause in our community.
