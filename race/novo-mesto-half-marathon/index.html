About

The Novo Mesto Half Marathon is a must-do event for runners seeking a scenic and historic challenge. Winding through the charming streets and along the Krka River in the picturesque Slovenian city of Novo Mesto, this race offers a unique opportunity to experience the region's stunning natural beauty and rich cultural heritage.
