About

Dubai's stop on a marathon series that begins in Antarctica, open to anyone: a marathon (eight laps) or a half (four laps) of a flat, traffic-free 5.3 km loop through Expo City Dubai, starting at 2 in the afternoon with a 10 pm cut-off. The day can move if the Antarctic start is delayed by weather; the organiser emails entrants if it does.
