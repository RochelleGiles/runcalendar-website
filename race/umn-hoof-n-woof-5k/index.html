Course

A single 5K run/walk starting outside the University of Minnesota College of Veterinary Medicine on Fitch Avenue, St Paul campus. Packet pickup opens at 7:15am with the event running from 8:30am to 11:00am. Leashed pets are welcome. No route, surface or aid station detail is published.

About

The University of Minnesota College of Veterinary Medicine is excited for another year of the Hoof N Woof 5k! Runners and walkers of all ages and abilities are encouraged to participate, and leashed pets are more than welcome! We hope you'll hang around after to visit booths from our wonderful sponsors and local pet businesses. Be sure to stay for our award ceremony that will take place for the runners and their furry companions as well.
