About

The Bois Guillaume Half Marathon is a popular annual running event that takes place in the town of Bois-Guillaume. The course features a scenic route through the Forêt Verte, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for both seasoned runners and those new to the sport. The event aims to bring the community together in a celebration of fitness and camaraderie.
