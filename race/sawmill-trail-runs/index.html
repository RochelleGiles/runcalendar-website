Course

Start and finish are at the Ranch Hand group picnic area in Staunton State Park. A mountain trail course running between roughly 8,000 and 10,000 ft through high grassy meadows below granite cliffs. Aid stations are provided on course. Time limits are 12 hours for the 50K and 11 hours for the shorter distances.

About

A trail running event presented by the Human Potential Running Series held at Staunton State Park in Pine, Colorado, featuring three distance options across scenic high country terrain. The event combines challenging mountain trails at 8,000+ feet elevation with well-stocked aid stations and themed awards.
