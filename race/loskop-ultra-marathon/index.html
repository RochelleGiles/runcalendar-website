Course

A point-to-point road ultra starting in Middelburg and finishing at Loskopdam, a Forever Resort in Mpumalanga. The 50km rolls over undulating tarmac often likened to the Comrades route, with the last major climb up Varaday's Hill at 48km before the finish. Shorter 21.1km, 10km and 4.9km options run the same corridor, with water tables throughout and physiotherapy at 40km and the finish.
