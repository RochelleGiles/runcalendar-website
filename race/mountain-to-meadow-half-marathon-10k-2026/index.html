About

Join us this June and enjoy our beautiful courses along the Montana-Idaho border! The Mountain to Meadow Half Marathon & 10k follow gravel Forest Service roads starting and finishing from the Lolo Pass Visitor's Center. Runners will pass through flowering meadows, quiet forests, and experience unique views of the Bitterroot Mountains.
