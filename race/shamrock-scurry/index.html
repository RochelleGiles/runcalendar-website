About

Since 1981, the Shamrock Scurry has been raising money to help the hungry and the hurting. Hosted by Killearn Methodist Church, this fun, family-oriented 5K and 1 Mile community event raises over $20,000 annually to help feed and clothe families, provide beds for foster children, and build homes for the homeless.
