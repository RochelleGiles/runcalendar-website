Course

A point-to-point run entirely on paved greenway. The 15K starts at Morningside Park and the 4-Miler at Tyson Park, both finishing at Bearden Elementary School on Kingston Pike. The route follows the Neyland, Third Creek and Bearden greenways. Water and gel are provided at Tyson Park and at the greenway corner by the UT Rec Field.

About

CrossKnox is an annual 15K and 4-mile road race in Knoxville, Tennessee, hosted by the Knoxville Track Club and sponsored by Pilot Travel Centers. Runners choose between the two distances on race day, drawing both competitive and recreational entrants from around the city.
