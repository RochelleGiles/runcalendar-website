About

The Course de la Nive is a running event taking place in the scenic region of Bayonne, located in southwestern France. Participants will experience a challenging and picturesque course through the beautiful Pyrénées-Atlantiques landscape. The event offers runners an opportunity to explore the stunning terrain and enjoy a memorable athletic experience.
