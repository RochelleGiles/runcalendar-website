Course

Start and finish at Cullen Park, 19008 Saums Rd, Houston. The 5K covers one lap of the park course, the 10K two and the 15K three. Packet pickup runs 7:30 to 8:45am, the kids dash goes at 8:55am and the main race starts at 9:00am.

About

The Couple Shuffle is a family-friendly Valentine's race at Cullen Park in Houston, Texas. Entrants choose a one-mile, 5K, 10K or 15K distance, with a children's dash and a virtual option also available.
