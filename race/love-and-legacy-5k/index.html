About

The Summit Area YMCA Love and Legacy 5K is a community event that brings families together for a morning of fitness, camaraderie, and support for YMCA programs. Funds raised support financial assistance for childcare, health initiatives, and mentorship programs like LIVESTRONG® at the YMCA and Achievers.
