Course

Start and finish at River Spirit Casino Resort on Riverside Parkway in south Tulsa. Entrants choose a 5K route or a one-mile walking route; chip timing on the 5K is an optional add-on, and a virtual option is offered.

About

The Komen Tulsa Race for the Cure is a 5K run and one-mile walk at River Spirit Casino Resort, raising funds for Susan G. Komen's breast cancer research and patient support programmes. Participants take part in person or virtually.
