About

The Trail Of The Resistance Fighters is a challenging 100km ultramarathon that takes runners through the historic trails of the French Resistance. Participants will experience the rugged terrain and stunning landscapes that the Resistance fighters traversed, making this event a unique blend of sport and history. The course features over 4,000m of elevation gain, testing the endurance and determination of all who take on this epic challenge.
