About

The Pilot Club Chase 5K is an annual road race in Watkinsville, Georgia, run entirely inside Oconee Veterans Park on the park''s paved jogging path. Hosted by the Pilot Club of Oconee County, it offers a chip-timed 5K alongside a virtual option and raises funds for the club''s community work.
