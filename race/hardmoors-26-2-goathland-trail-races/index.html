Course

Circular marathon, half marathon and 10k routes starting and finishing at Goathland Village Hall on the North York Moors. The marathon covers 27.5 miles with around 4,800ft of ascent. Routes are not marked, so runners carry the route map and description, and a full winter safety kit is mandatory.

About

The Hardmoors 26.2 Goathland Trail Races offer a challenging trail marathon, half marathon, and 10k event in the picturesque village of Goathland, Yorkshire. Runners will experience stunning scenery and a demanding course with significant elevation gain, making this a true test of endurance.
