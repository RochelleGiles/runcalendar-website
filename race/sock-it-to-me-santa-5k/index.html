Course

Starts at Asheville Middle School, 211 S French Broad Ave, and winds through the South Slope and River Arts District. The Snowflake Chaser for children is one loop of the track at 8:30am and the 5K starts at 9:00am. The field is capped at 400 and there is no race-day entry.

About

Get in the holiday spirit and lace up your running shoes for a fabulously festive 5K adventure through downtown Asheville. This chip timed run combines the joy of the season with the satisfaction of supporting a great cause, as we raise funds for the Asheville City Schools Band Programs.
