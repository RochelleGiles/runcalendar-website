About

Le Tacot et la Renarde is a trail running event designed by a passionate team. The course follows the old railway line that the market gardeners from southern Essonne used to supply the Parisian markets, and the Renarde river that flows through the valley. The event is part of the Essonne Nature Races Challenge and offers a fun and challenging experience for runners of all levels, with separate races for children and adults.
