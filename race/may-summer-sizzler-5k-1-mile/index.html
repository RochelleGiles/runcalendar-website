About

The May Summer Sizzler is an evening 5K with a companion one-mile run at Greenway Park in Cleveland, Tennessee, hosted by Terra Running Company. It opens a three-race summer series, and runners who complete all three earn a custom pair of socks. Virtual entries are offered alongside the in-person race.
