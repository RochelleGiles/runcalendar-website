Course

Starts at Hyde Common, Frogham near Fordingbridge, one of the highest points in the New Forest National Park. Routes cross wide open plains and winding forest trails before finishing along Hampton Ridge back to Hyde Common. All routes are fully marked and marshalled with several aid stations carrying refreshments and energy products.

About

Explore the stunning landscapes of the New Forest National Park at the BigSky Trails (AIR) event. This challenging trail running festival offers a range of distances, from an exhilarating 50km Ultra to a scenic 18-mile course, catering to runners of all abilities. Challenge yourself on the diverse terrain and immerse in the natural beauty of this picturesque region.
