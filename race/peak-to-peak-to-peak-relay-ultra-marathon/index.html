Course

28-mile point-to-point course linking three Peak Performance running stores via Omaha's West Papio and Keystone trail systems. Starts at 3606 N 156th Street and finishes at 519 N 78th Street. Four exchange zones split the route for relay teams. The field is capped at 50 relay teams and 50 solo entrants.

About

Introducing to Omaha, NE. - The Peak to Peak to Peak Relay & The Long Marathon (28 Miles). For the first time ever we created a unique event to connect the city from west to east utilizing Omaha's West Papio and Keystone Trail Systems. The Peak to Peak to Peak Relay & Ultra is a 28-mile point-to-point running adventure that links all three metro Peak Performance specialty running stores.
