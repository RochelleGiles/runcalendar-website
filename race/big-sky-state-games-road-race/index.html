Course

A flat, fast course on Thiel Road and River Road following the Yellowstone River, starting and finishing at River's Edge Industrial Park off East Lauralee Lane. The race doubles as the USATF Montana State Championship. The half marathon starts at 6:30am, the 10K at 7:30am and the 5K at 7:40am.

About

The Big Sky State Games Road Race is a fairly flat, fast course on Thiel/River Rd along scenic views of the Yellowstone River and its wildlife. It offers 5k, 10k, and half marathon distances as USATF State Championships for Montana.
