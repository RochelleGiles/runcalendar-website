Course

About

Join us for the third annual Special Steps 5k to celebrate and support our non-profit organization for local adults with exceptional needs. At Special Steps meetings we engage in a wide variety of pursuits, including creative endeavors, athletic activities, and the acquisition of new abilities. Come run, walk, or stroll this 5k with us! All ages and abilities are welcome and accommodated.
