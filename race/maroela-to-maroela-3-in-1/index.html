About

Maroela to Maroela 3-in-1 is a road race in Phalaborwa, Limpopo, close to the Kruger National Park's Maroela area, offering 5km, 10km and 21.1km distances. It is a regular fixture for runners in the Ba-Phalaborwa municipality.
