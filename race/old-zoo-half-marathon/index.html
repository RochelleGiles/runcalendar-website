Course

Half marathon and 12K on the trails around the Old Los Angeles Zoo in Griffith Park, starting and finishing at the old zoo site on Griffith Park Drive. Both routes are relatively flat and largely off-road; the field is capped and has sold out before.

About

The Old Zoo Half Marathon starts and ends at the Old Los Angeles Zoo area in Griffith Park, featuring a half marathon and a 12K course on relatively flat scenic trails. This is the 2nd annual event organized by Ray Endurance Events with limited spots available.
