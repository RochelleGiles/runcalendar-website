Course

Circular 55 mile route starting and finishing at Gulabin Lodge, Spittal of Glenshee, with about 7,450 ft of climb. Roughly 90% is off road on forest trails, rough tracks and open hillside, with short tarmac links on quiet country roads. The finish climbs to the An Lairig pass before dropping back to the lodge.

About

The Cateran Trail Ultramarathon is a scenic 55-mile journey through the stunning landscapes of the Scottish Highlands. Runners will traverse a circular route based from the Gulabin Lodge at the Spittal of Glenshee, immersing themselves in the natural beauty of this remote region of the United Kingdom.
