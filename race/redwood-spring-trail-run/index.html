Course

Run in Redwood Regional Park, starting and finishing at the Chabot Space and Science Center in Oakland. Courses are roughly one third single track and two thirds dirt fire road with very little asphalt, climbing from 480 ft of gain on the 5-mile to 3,550 ft on the 50K. Fully stocked aid stations sit every five to six miles and the event is cupless.

About

The course travels through beautiful Redwood Regional Park. Enjoy the spectacular views along the East Ridge and West Ridge trails and wind through tall redwoods on the popular Stream and Bridle trails.
