Course

A point-to-point route on Whidbey Island mixing coastal roads and trails. Starts at the Captain Whidbey Inn and runs along Madrona Way into Coupeville, crossing to Ebey's Landing and the Kettles Trail System. The marathon adds natural beach and country roads.

About

The Rock Solid Marathon and Half Marathon, now past its second running, takes place in Coupeville, Washington, on Whidbey Island. Both races follow winding coastal roads and scenic trails, starting and finishing at the historic Captain Whidbey Inn, a wooden lodge on Penn Cove.
