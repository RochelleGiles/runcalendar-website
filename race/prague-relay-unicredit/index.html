About

The Prague Relay UniCredit is a must-run event for any avid runner. Set against the stunning backdrop of Prague's historic city center, this team relay challenge tests endurance and teamwork as participants take turns conquering the scenic urban course. Whether you're an experienced racer or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
