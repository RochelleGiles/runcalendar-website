Course

A point-to-point ultra along the Great Glen Way between Fort William and Inverness, finishing with a lap of the 400m track at Inverness Athletics Stadium. The long course covers 73 miles with 8,500ft of climbing; the 42-mile race joins the route at Fort Augustus and takes in around 6,000ft. The Way runs alongside Loch Ness and through the surrounding hills.

About

Conquer the rugged landscapes of the Great Glen in Scotland's ultimate ultra-marathon challenge. This iconic race takes runners through some of the country's most breathtaking scenery, winding along lochs and glens in a true test of endurance. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or seeking your next big adventure, the Great Glen Ultra Marathon promises an unforgettable experience.
