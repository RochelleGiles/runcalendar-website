Course

Based at Mahoney State Park, Ashland, with an 8:00am start and packet pickup from 6:30am. Half marathon, 10K, 5K, 1 mile and an untimed kids dash run on park roads and paths; timed distances are chip-timed with live results. A Nebraska State Park vehicle permit is required for parking.

About

This edition of the Golden Harvest Half Marathon is held in Ashland, Nebraska, near Lincoln, with half marathon, 10K, and 5K options each September. It's the sister race to the Golden Harvest Half Marathon in Omaha.
