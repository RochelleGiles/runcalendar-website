About

Explore the stunning trails of the Chambon region at the annual Trail du Chambon event. This challenging yet rewarding race takes runners through picturesque landscapes, offering a unique opportunity to connect with nature and test their endurance. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail du Chambon promises an unforgettable adventure.
