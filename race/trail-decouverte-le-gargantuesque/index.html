About

Trail Découverte Le Gargantuesque is a trail race in Saint-Pierre-le-Moûtier, in the Nièvre department of Burgundy, organized by USSP Course et Nature. Its full name, 'de Jeanne d'Arc aux Vignes,' reflects a route linking a site associated with Joan of Arc to nearby vineyards, with distances of 10 km, 11 km and 17 km each November.
