About

The Primiero Dolomites Marathon takes runners on a breathtaking journey through the stunning landscapes of the Italian Dolomites. Winding through picturesque villages and challenging mountain terrain, this event offers an unforgettable running experience amidst the dramatic peaks and valleys of this UNESCO World Heritage site.
