About

The 4th annual Coffee Run at Woodward Park offers timed 10K and 5K runs/walks, a non-timed 2-mile option, and a virtual run for all ages and paces. Participants enjoy post-race Starbucks coffee in commemorative mugs, hot chocolate, music, and a family-friendly atmosphere while supporting Valley PBS.
