About

The Frontyard Ultra is a variance on the ever popular backyard ultra. Female runners must consecutively run the distance of 6kms in less than one hour. The race finishes when one person completes one more lap than anyone else. It is an opportunity for females to join together, make new friends, have fun and challenge themselves to something new in a safe environment.
