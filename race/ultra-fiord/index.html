About

Ultra Fiord is a challenging ultra-running event set in the stunning Patagonian landscape of Chile. The course offers runners a chance to experience the region's breathtaking scenery, including glaciers, mountains, and pristine wilderness. The event features multiple distance options, catering to runners of various skill levels, and is known for its welcoming atmosphere and commitment to sustainability.
