Course

A two lap course starting and finishing at Wild Mountain Winery, Taylors Falls. The route runs through the southern vineyards, onto a cross country path east, a dirt road north, then the northern vineyard and onto trails. The ground is uneven with short steep inclines. Aid stations are not described.

About

A chip-timed 5k run/walk event at Wild Mountain Winery featuring scenic vineyard and trail courses with uneven terrain and short steep inclines. Participants receive a t-shirt, collapsible wine glass, finisher medal, and a complimentary pour of wine.
