Course

A 6-hour lapped race at Coombe Abbey Park, Coventry, on a 3.3-mile loop through mature woodland, waterways and open grassland. The majority of the route is stone pathway with a meadow section on open grass, and the loop is relatively flat. Runners set their own distance by lap count, with cafe, toilets and parking at the start/finish.

About

The Swan Challenge is a captivating 6-hour running race through the scenic trails of Coombe Abbey Park in Coventry, United Kingdom. Runners will test their endurance on a challenging 3.3-mile off-road loop, surrounded by the tranquil beauty of this historic parkland. Whether you're seeking a personal best or simply a day of adventure, the Swan Challenge promises an unforgettable running experience.
