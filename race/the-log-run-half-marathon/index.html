Course

A point-to-point course in the Stillwater area that runs mostly downhill, easing into a gentle descent to the finish. All distances are USA Track & Field certified and the race is known for fast times, holding 22 state records. It closes the Run Stillwater season.

About

The Log Run Half Marathon is the classic Stillwater area race going back to 1997 and is the final event of the season in the Run Stillwater race series. It includes a Half, 10 Mile, 10K and 5K.
