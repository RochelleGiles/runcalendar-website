Course

Two distances on concrete paths at Green Hills County Park. The 5K runs two out-and-back repeats of 0.78 miles each way; the half marathon covers six repeats of a 1.09-mile out-and-back. Both start at 9:00 am with a three-hour limit. Parking is at the playground on the north side of the park.

About

The Pirate 5K & 13.1 is a pirate-themed race at Green Hills County Park in Raleigh, North Carolina, offering 5K and half marathon distances on flat concrete out-and-back repeats. Finishers receive themed medals and costumes are encouraged.
