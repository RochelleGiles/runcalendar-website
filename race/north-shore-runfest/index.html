About

This flat 8K/5K starts on Derby Street in downtown Salem, heads out to Salem Willows, and returns past Salem Common before finishing at Notch Brewing. The 8K doubles as a USATF Championship race, and the event includes live music and a post-race festival benefiting Girls on the Run.
