Course

Out-and-back courses starting and finishing at Louisville Community Park. The half marathon is 90% dirt and 10% bike path with 501 ft of gain and major climbs at miles 5.3 and 11.5, finishing with Front Range views. The 10K and 5K are entirely dirt, with 285 ft and 193 ft of gain. Aid stations on all courses.

About

The 13th Annual Louisville Trail Half Marathon, 10K, and 5K returns to the front range, beloved by runners for its scenic trails and welcoming atmosphere. The course is mostly flat with a beautiful, yet challenging hill that creates an epic vista point of the front range mountains as you hit the last mile of the course.
