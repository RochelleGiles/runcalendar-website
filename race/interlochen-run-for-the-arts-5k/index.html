Course

A single 5K on the Interlochen Center for the Arts campus. The route runs on paved and unpaved trails along the shores of Green Lake and Duck Lake, starting and finishing on campus. Packet pickup runs 7:30 to 9:00am for a 9:00am start. No aid station detail is published.

About

Interlochen Center for the Arts hosts this 5K on its campus in northern Michigan. The route follows paved and unpaved trails along the shores of Green Lake and Duck Lake. Entry fees support student scholarships at the academy.
