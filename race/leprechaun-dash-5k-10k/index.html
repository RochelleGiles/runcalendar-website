About

Join us on March 14 at 8:30 a.m. for the annual Leprechaun Dash! This 5K or 10K run raises money for youth-serving opportunities right here in Chatham County. On what will be a fun and challenging course, we'll celebrate St. Patrick's Day and raise money for children in Chatham County. Whether you're tuning up for a spring half-marathon or just want a good goal for a New Year's resolution, this race is for you. Prizes will be awarded for age groups and for the best leprechaun attire.
