Course

All four races start and finish around Rendezvous Pointe in Pinedale, at about 7,185 ft. The half marathon is an out-and-back to Fremont Lake campground on mostly paved roads and bike paths, starting 8:30 AM. The 10K starts at Fremont Lake Lookout with shuttle service and descends back to town. The 5K uses town streets and the bike path toward Willow Lake road.

About

Race to Raise (formerly the Pinedale Half Marathon & 10K) is a community road race in Pinedale, Wyoming, with half marathon, 10K, 5K and one-mile options benefiting local health and community programs through Sublette County's Foundation23.
