Course

Starts on Stirling Range Drive near the corner of Chester Pass Road in Stirling Range National Park, Western Australia. Running options are 5km, 12km and 21km, each paired with a ride leg. The organisers state the events run entirely on gravel roads, with most closed to traffic for the day.

About

The Wild Gravel Run is the running leg of a combined cycling-and-running event in the Stirling Range National Park, Western Australia, with courses of 5km, 12km and 21km on the ranges' gravel roads and trails.
