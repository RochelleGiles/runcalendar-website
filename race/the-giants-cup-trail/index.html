Course

A two-day point-to-point stage run along the Giant's Cup Hiking Trail in the Maloti-Drakensberg Park. Day 1 starts at the old Trading Store, climbs to the Langalibalele Peak saddle at 2158m and finishes at Castleburn Farm; Day 2 crosses rivers to Crane Tarn and descends Gxalingenwa Gorge to Premier Resort Sani Pass. Aid stations and checkpoints sit along both days.
