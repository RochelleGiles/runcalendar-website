Course

A backyard ultra based at the Boyne Valley Community Discovery Centre in Ubobo. The 6.71 km day lap, run from 7am to 6pm, loops through the Bruin Park cattle property on dirt trail with about 65 m of climb; from 6pm the field crosses Railway Terrace to run an out-and-back on the flat Boyne Burnett Inland Rail Trail. Field is capped at 110.

About

The Steven Bradbury event of ultra running. You don't have to be the fastest; just be the Last One Standing. Complete a 6.71km lap within the hour, every hour. Only the runner to complete the last lap within the hour by themselves 'finishes' the race. It's a grassroots event with no shiny medal or event shirt, just an opportunity to challenge yourself to One More Lap.
