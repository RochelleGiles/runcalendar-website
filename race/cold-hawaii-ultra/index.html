Course

Trail courses along the west coast of Jutland, running on small single tracks between the coastal dunes, down onto the beach and through coniferous plantations, heathland and small fishing villages. All distances follow established routes including the West Coast Trail and the old lifeboat road, with the longest adding further sections.

About

Cold Hawaii Ultra is the dream of the ultimate Danish ultra and trail running experience through the wilderness and some of Denmark's most unique, rugged and beautiful nature. The race takes place along the west coast of Jutland, with participants running through coastal dunes, heathlands, and small fishing villages.
