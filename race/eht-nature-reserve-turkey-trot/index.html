About

The EHT Nature Reserve's Turkey Trot, in its 12th year in 2026, is held on the Saturday after Thanksgiving, rain or shine. The 5K runs the reserve's trails, along the sandy edge of its lake and through woodland with plenty of short climbs and descents; a 1-mile nature walk starts at the same time. There is no parking at the reserve: park at Atlantic Christian School on Zion Road, where a shuttle runs from 7:40. Online entries close the evening before, and you can also enter on race morning. Proceeds go to the nature reserve.
