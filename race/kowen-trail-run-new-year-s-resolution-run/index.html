Course

Half marathon and 12 km trail races starting and finishing at Wamboin Community Hall. Both open with 1.3 km of sealed road on Bingley Way before entering Kowen Forest. The half is mostly dirt forestry trail with 2.5 km of single track and 680 m of climbing over Mt Amungula; the 12 km is 8 km of continuous single track in native forest with over 320 m of gain.

About

The Kowen Trail Run: New Year's Resolution Run offers a half marathon on a mix of forestry trails and single track with spectacular views of Canberra and surrounds, or a 12 km course with 8 km of continuous single track in the native forest. The courses provide challenging elevation gains, including an ascent of the formidable Mt Amungula.
