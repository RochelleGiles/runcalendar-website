Course

The 5k and the timed BuffaLoops races are held at Buffalo Park in Flagstaff, with a 7am start. Ultra entrants run a two-mile outer loop, reversing direction every two hours, in 12, 6 and 3 hour formats. Dogs may run the 5k with their owners, and a free kids race is held alongside.

About

This will be the 7th Annual Flagstaff Doggie Dash held at the idyllic Buffalo Park! Runners are welcome to run with their dogs for the 5k distance or run solo. For the endurance monsters, see how many "Buffaloops" you can log in 12,6, or 3 hours!
