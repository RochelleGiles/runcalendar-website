Course

A flat, traffic-free two-lap route through the Milton Keynes countryside, with a stretch along the Grand Union Canal on the first 10-mile lap. From 2027 the second lap is rerouted between Simpson and Woughton-on-the-Green to avoid about 1.5 miles of narrow towpath.

About

The TWIN LAKES 20 is a stunning 10-mile or 20-mile race that will prepare you for your Spring half/marathon experience without having to run on your own. You can enjoy the incredibly scenic, superfast, and traffic-free course through the stunning Milton Keynes countryside. Lapping two beautiful lakes, following the picturesque River Ouzel and the Grand Union Canal, you'll never be far from the water! The course is multi-terrain with about 80% on cycle paths with some gravel paths and the remainder on the canal towpath.
