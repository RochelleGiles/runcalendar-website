About

The Beast of Bryn (Bwystfil Y Bryn) includes 6.5 and 10.5 mile trail races in Bryn, Port Talbot, based from the Bryn Rugby field. The challenging but rewarding routes pass through the mountains of Afan Argoed, with a Mini Beast 1 mile race also offered for children and teenagers up to 18.
