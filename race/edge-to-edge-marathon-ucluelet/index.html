Course

Out-and-back half marathon and 10km on the west coast of Vancouver Island, starting and finishing at the Ucluelet Community Centre. Both routes leave town past the small craft harbour and pick up the Wild Pacific Trail, a packed-gravel coastal path through temperate rainforest with open-ocean and rocky-bluff views; the 10km passes Amphitrite Lighthouse and the half runs the Tofino-Ucluelet Highway past Millstream first.
