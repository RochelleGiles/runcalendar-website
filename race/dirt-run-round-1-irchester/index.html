Course

A multi-terrain lapped route through the Jurassic landscape of Irchester Country Park near Wellingborough, over trails with repeated climbs and mud. The 5K, 10K and 15K share the same loop and runners can switch distance on the day. Trail shoes are strongly recommended.

About

Round one of the Go Beyond Dirt Run winter trail series, held at Irchester Country Park near Wellingborough in Northamptonshire. Runners take on a lapped multi-terrain course over muddy trails and repeated climbs, choosing between 5K, 10K and 15K distances.
