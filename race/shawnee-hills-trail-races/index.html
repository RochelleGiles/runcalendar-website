Course

Trail races through the Shawnee National Forest based at Camp Ondessonk near Ozark, Illinois. Courses run sandstone canyon systems past Jackson Hollow and the Bay Creek Reservoir, taking in Pakentuck, Jackson, Horseshoe and Cedar Falls with views from Salomon's Ridge, and include creek crossings.

About

Shawnee Hills Trail Races is a weekend of trail running at Camp Ondessonk near Ozark, Illinois, for runners and their families and friends alike. Courses run for miles through the Shawnee National Forest, with rustic Camp O cabins on site.
