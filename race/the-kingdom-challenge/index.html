Course

A point-to-point half marathon with over 3,000 feet of elevation change, roughly 60 percent of it on dirt roads. The companion 5K is an in-town run through the four-seasons neighbourhoods of St Johnsbury.

About

The Kingdom Challenge offers a half marathon, half marathon relay, and 5K through the streets of St. Johnsbury, Vermont, the hub town of the state's remote, rural Northeast Kingdom region.
