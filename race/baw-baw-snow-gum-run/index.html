Course

All courses start and finish at Baw Baw Village. Runners follow the Village and Summit Trails to Five Ways, then McMillan Trail through Baragwanath Flat, entering the national park on the Australian Alps Walking Track. Longer distances summit Mt Erica or Mt St Gwinear at The Camp before returning the same way and descending Muellers Track to the village.

About

Venture through the serene alpine village of Mount Baw Baw in the Victorian Alps for the Baw Baw Snow Gum Run. Immerse yourself in the stunning natural landscapes as you navigate the picturesque trails, surrounded by the iconic snow gum trees that give this event its name. This unique running experience offers a breathtaking challenge for runners of all levels.
