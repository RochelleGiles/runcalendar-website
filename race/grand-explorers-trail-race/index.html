Course

Route linking Grand River Park and Grand Ravines County Park via the Idema Explorers Trail along the Grand River. About 2 of the 5K's miles and 4 of the 10K's are paved trail; the rest is dirt or grass with roots and uneven ground. The 10K adds the meadow, dog park, Idema suspension bridge and a stair section. The Kids 1 Mile is paved throughout.

About

Run through two beautiful Ottawa County Parks in one race! The course starts at Grand River County Park with the newly completed Idema Explorers Trail segment that leads you along the Grand River to Grand Ravines County Park. As you enter Grand Ravines, you will see an old covered bridge and go up a slight incline to a spectacular view of the river. If you're lucky, you might even see the resident bald eagles out fishing.
