Course

Three loops of 5km, 10km and 18km on Fair Cape Farm at Durbanville, starting and finishing at the farm. Mixed farm terrain with sections of single track and open views across the property. Water stations are provided on all three routes.

About

This trail run takes place at Kuiperskraal Farm in Durbanville, raising funds for the Fair Cape Cares Foundation. Runners can choose from 5km, 10km and 18km distances across the farmland.
