About

The Patagonian International Marathon is a premier running event set against the stunning backdrop of Torres del Paine National Park in Patagonia, Chile. Runners will experience the breathtaking landscapes of this remote region, including glaciers, lakes, and the iconic Torres del Paine peaks. The challenging course offers an unforgettable adventure for athletes of all levels.
