Course

Point-to-point road race starting on College Street in downtown Asheville and finishing at New Belgium Brewing. The route passes through the historic Montford and North Asheville neighbourhoods, the River Arts District and riverside greenways. Five aid stations on the half, twelve on the marathon, all with water and electrolytes.

About

There's nowhere quite like Asheville, North Carolina. It's an authentic destination where natural beauty and creativity collide. Down her streets, around each neighborhood, and through her greenways lies a vibrant energy and independent spirit that is unrivaled. And whether you run 26.2 or 13.1, you'll love everything she has to offer.
