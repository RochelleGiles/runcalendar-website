About

The Hell on Earth Halloween 5k is the most ghoulish race you'll ever run, or at least the most fun, so whether you are a veteran runner or this is your first 5k we've got a spooky spot for you to enjoy! Family friendly atmosphere for all ages to enjoy the Hell on Earth Halloween 5k promises to leave you haunted with lots of ghoulish good feelings for the holiday!
