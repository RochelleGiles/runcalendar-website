About

The Argory Dusk to Dawn Challenge is a unique running event that takes place in the beautiful countryside of Ireland. Participants will experience the stunning scenery and tranquility of the Argory estate as they challenge themselves to complete this endurance event. The course highlights include winding trails, rolling hills, and breathtaking views. The event's atmosphere is one of camaraderie and personal achievement as runners push their limits from dusk until dawn.
