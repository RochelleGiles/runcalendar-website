Course

A 4.167-mile lollipop loop inside the Prehistoric Trackways National Monument at Las Cruces, run on the hour until one runner is left. The trails are mostly crushed gravel with some elevation change and look out to the Organ Mountains. Twenty-four yards earns a 100-mile buckle; the field is capped at 75 and the event is cupless.

About

The Land of Enchantment Backyard Ultra is a last-one-standing race at Prehistoric Trackways National Monument near Las Cruces, New Mexico, where runners complete a 4.167-mile loop every hour until only one remains.
