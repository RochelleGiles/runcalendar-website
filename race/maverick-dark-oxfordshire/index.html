Course

After-dark trail runs of 17km, 13km and 7km starting and finishing at Stonor Park near Henley-on-Thames, on rooty wooded trails through the estate and the surrounding Chiltern Hills. The course is fully marked and timed. One aid outpost serves the middle and long routes with fruit, cake, crisps, gels and electrolyte; the short route has none.

About

The Maverick Dark Oxfordshire showcases the Chiltern Hills with a challenge for trail runners of every level. Choose from three routes that are dark, spooky, rooty and a lot of fun in the dark.
