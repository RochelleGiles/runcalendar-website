Course

A road race based at Braunton Community College in north Devon, offering a 10 mile route and a shorter 10K. The 10 mile includes two well-known climbs; the 10K keeps the character of the longer course over a shorter distance. Two water stations, mile markers and clear signing throughout, with changing rooms and showers at the venue.

About

The Braunton 10 is a beloved 10-mile and 10km road race organized by the North Devon Road Runners. Set in the picturesque town of Braunton, this event offers a challenging yet scenic course that winds through the charming countryside, making it a must-attend for runners seeking an unforgettable experience.
