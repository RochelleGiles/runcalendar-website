Course

Based at Stanley Park in Estes Park, at roughly 7,500 ft. Saturday is a technical singletrack mountain-bike 50K; Sunday brings a trail 50K and a Heavy Half on the same mountain trail system, with the Epic Double combining both days for 100K. Aid on the 50K sits at miles 5.6, 8, 16, 21.5 and 25.5, and on the Heavy Half at 5.6 and 9.7. All races are cupless.

About

The Epic returns after 6 years in EPIC fashion. This race is our most remote, rugged and challenging of them all. Expect untamed Colorado wilderness, single track trails, monster climbs and EPIC views of Rocky Mountain National Park. The 50k brings 5,700ft of vertical gain over roughly 32 miles.
