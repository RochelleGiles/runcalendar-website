About

The Velvet Hammer 5K and 10K is a road race hosted by Peticolas Brewing Company in Dallas, Texas, named after the brewery's flagship beer. Entrants choose between the 5K and the 10K, and a separate party pass is sold for over-21s who want the post-race celebration without racing.
