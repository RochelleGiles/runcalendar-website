Course

Starts at El Caballo Park in Escondido and runs into Daley Ranch on singletrack and dirt service road. The 10 mile climbs to Stanley Peak for a 360-degree summit view; the 10K and 5K stay lower. Climbing runs from roughly 700ft to 1,775ft depending on distance.

About

Dixon Lake Trail Runs is a trail race around Dixon Lake Recreation Area in Escondido, California, hosted by the Endurance Race Series each December. Runners choose 10 Mile, 10K or 5K distances on the park's lake-view trail network in the hills above the city.
