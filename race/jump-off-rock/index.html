Course

A point-to-point course. The half marathon starts at Jump Off Rock, the highest point in Laurel Park, and descends for about seven miles to join the shared course near the Hendersonville Family YMCA, where the 10K and 5K start. All three distances loop around Lake Osceola and finish on Main Street in downtown Hendersonville. The half has four water stations, the 10K two and the 5K one.

About

Jump Off Rock Half Marathon and 10K starts near Hendersonville, North Carolina, and takes runners past Jump Off Rock, a scenic overlook with mountain views. The rolling course winds through the Laurel Park area on the way to the lookout.
