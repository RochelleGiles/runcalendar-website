Course

Trail races on unpaved ground near Yellowstone National Park. Courses begin on closed double track before joining forest service roads and trail. The organiser describes the terrain as rolling rather than sustained climbing or descending, on a solid footbed of packed dirt and pine needles.

About

A trail race staged by Wolf Pack Adventures near the west entrance to Yellowstone National Park at West Yellowstone, Montana. The half marathon runs on unsealed forest service roads and trail, part of a weekend that also carries shorter and longer distances over the same terrain.
