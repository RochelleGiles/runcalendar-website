About

The Getafe Half Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner. Set against the backdrop of the historic city of Getafe, this challenging 13.1-mile course winds through picturesque streets and offers stunning views of the surrounding countryside. Whether you're a seasoned racer or a newcomer to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
