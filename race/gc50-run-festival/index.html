Course

A four-lap coastal course on the Oceanway from Queen Elizabeth Park, Coolangatta, north to the turn at Kropp Park, Tugun — completely flat, on closed roads and sealed beachfront path. The festival runs across two days: the 50km ultra and a sunset community 5km on the Saturday, with the half marathon and 10km on the Sunday morning.

About

The fisiocrem GC50 Run Festival returns to the beachside town of Coolangatta, Australia for a weekend celebration of running and coastal vibes. Runners can look forward to a variety of race distances, from a 50km ultramarathon to shorter 10km and 5km options, all set against the stunning backdrop of the Pacific Ocean. This annual event is a must for any runner seeking an unforgettable running experience.
