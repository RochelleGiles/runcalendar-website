Course

Circuits starting and finishing at Hawkshead Recreation Ground, with views over Windermere and the surrounding fells. Fully marked and marshalled footpaths and bridleways, mostly good underfoot with short tarmac sections and some steep, rocky descents. Includes the mile-long climb of the Coffin Trail.

About

Explore the picturesque Lakeland Trails in Hawkshead, United Kingdom with challenging 16km and 10km trail runs. Immerse yourself in the stunning natural beauty of the Lake District as you navigate rugged terrain and scenic vistas. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, this event offers an exhilarating experience for all.
