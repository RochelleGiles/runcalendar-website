About

Come and enjoy a USATF certified 5K (GA13120WC) in Historic Downtown Sparta, GA. The John Hancock Academy Rebel Dash 5K benefits the Parent Teacher Organization, which directly supports the activities and operation of John Hancock Academy. Plan to stay after the race to enjoy lots of door prizes and great food and refreshments.
