About

The Afterwork running is a relay running event dedicated to employers and their employees (companies, administrations, local authorities, higher education institutions, non-sporting associations, etc.). It's an opportunity to leave the business world for the world of sports, teamwork, and conviviality. For the most athletic, the stopwatch will be an objective, for the others, it will only be a pretext, the important thing is to participate and share a good time with colleagues.
