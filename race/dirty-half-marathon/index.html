Course

A single 13.1 mile trail race starting from the Unitarian Church in Bend, Oregon. The opening half mile runs on pavement to let the field spread out before the course turns onto dirt roads and single track for the remainder. A GPX file of the route is published by the organiser.

About

The start of the Dirty Half: The first ~1/2 mile leads runners out on pavement, allowing room to find their groove and the crowd to thin out. Soon they will leave the confines of pavement and head towards dirt and single track!
