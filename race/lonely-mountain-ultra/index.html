Course

Trail courses on Mount Canobolas (Gaanha-bula) near Orange, running through the heart of the Canobolas State Forest. The terrain mixes fire trails, woodland and open pasture, with steep climbing on the longer distances. Options run from an 11km up to 100 miles, plus kids' 2km and 4km. About

Lonely Mountain Ultra is a trail ultramarathon through the forests and volcanic peaks of the Canobolas Range near Orange, with distances from 22km to 100 miles. It has hosted the Australian Trail Running Association's long-trail championships.
