About

The 2026 Crete Half Marathon will take place on Sunday, October 4th, 2026, in Arkalochori, Crete, offering an unforgettable experience for runners from Greece and around the world. The Half Marathon follows a scenic circular route through the heart of Crete, passing picturesque villages in the municipality of Minoa Pediadas, as well as olive groves and vineyards. This unique course combines natural beauty with the vibrant energy of local traditions, music, and hospitality.
