Course

An out-and-back road course along the Raquette River on River Rd, starting and finishing at the Potsdam Arena. The route is rolling, USATF-certified and a Boston qualifier. Water and sports drink are offered every 1.5 miles and at the start/finish, with portable toilets at the half and marathon turnarounds.

About

The Raquette River Marathon is a popular annual running event in Potsdam, New York. The course features scenic views along the Raquette River and is USATF certified.
