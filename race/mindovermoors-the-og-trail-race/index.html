Course

A single 6-mile loop through the Fryupdale valley on the North York Moors, starting and finishing at the Yorkshire Cycle Hub. It climbs above the valley before descending on old trails and open moorland.

About

Lace up your trail shoes and get ready to conquer the stunning Fryupdale valley in North Yorkshire at the MindOverMoors - The OG Trail Race. This 6-mile off-road challenge takes runners through the breathtaking landscapes of the British countryside, testing their endurance and connection with nature. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
