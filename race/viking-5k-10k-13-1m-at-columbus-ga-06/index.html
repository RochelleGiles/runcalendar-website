Course

Paved out-and-back loops at Rotary Park, Columbus. The 5K runs a 0.78-mile loop twice and the 10K four times; the half marathon runs a 1.09-mile loop six times. All start at 8:00am with a three and a half hour limit.

About

One of a series of 5K, 10K and half marathon races held at Rotary Park in Columbus, Georgia. All three distances share the same paved out-and-back loop, covered twice for the 5K, four times for the 10K and six times for the half marathon, from a single 8:00 AM start.
