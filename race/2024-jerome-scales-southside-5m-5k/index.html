About

The Jerome Scales Southside 5M & 5K is an Atlanta Track Club event held in College Park, part of the club's Grand Prix Series. Runners take on either five miles or 5K through the College Park neighbourhood and around Woodward Academy. The race honours the legacy of Jerome Scales and is run with the South Fulton Running Partners.
