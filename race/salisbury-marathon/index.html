Course

The half marathon starts near 721 S. Park Drive and runs through Salisbury via Beaver Dam Road, Glen Avenue, Memorial Plaza and North Schumaker Drive, finishing downtown at Main and Division Streets. It shares the marathon course for the first 10.8 miles. Aid stations at miles 3, 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12 with water, sports drink and portable toilets.

About

RunSBY hosts three events on one morning in Salisbury, Maryland: a marathon, a half marathon and a 5K. The marathon is a Boston Marathon qualifier and the half marathon is USATF-certified. All three courses run on city streets through residential and commercial Salisbury and finish downtown at Main and Division Streets.
