About

The Running for Success 5K Run/Walk is held each spring on the Shore Road Promenade at Bay Parkway in Brooklyn, New York. Runners and walkers cover a flat paved out-and-back course beside the water. Proceeds send I.S. 223 students to a summer running camp in Swan Lake, New York.
