Course

Starts and finishes at the Student Fitness Center, 347 S University St, and runs through Uptown Normal and around the Illinois State University campus. One water stop is set on the course, with refreshments afterwards. The 5K is chip timed; strollers are welcome and pets are not.

About

There will be one water stop during the 5K run and refreshments available afterward for all run/walk participants, courtesy of the Town of Normal. The 5K will be chip timed, courtesy of It's Race time, Inc. No pets, please. Strollers welcomed. Parking is available in the School Street Parking Garage off of Beaufort. Virtual participants are encouraged to run and submit times on October 18 or prior.
