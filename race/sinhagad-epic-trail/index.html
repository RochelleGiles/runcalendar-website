About

Conquer the rugged trails of the Sinhagad Fort at the Sinhagad Epic Trail, a challenging and picturesque running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Western Ghats. Runners will navigate through lush forests, scale ancient fortifications, and take in breathtaking vistas as they push their limits on this unforgettable journey.
