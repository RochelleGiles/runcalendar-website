About

The Kasumigaura Marathon is a must-run event for any distance runner. Set against the picturesque backdrop of the Kasumigaura Lake, this scenic course winds through charming Japanese countryside, offering a unique and unforgettable racing experience. Runners of all levels are invited to challenge themselves and soak in the stunning natural beauty of this iconic event.
