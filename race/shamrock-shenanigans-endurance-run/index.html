Course

A timed ultra at Union Meadows Park East, Douglassville, where runners complete as many loops as they can of the berm trail, starting and stopping by the boat ramp. The loop is about 2.4 miles on packed gravel and is wide enough to run three abreast. Five-hour and ten-hour options are offered.

About

A timed endurance run at Union Meadows Park East in Douglassville, Pennsylvania, offered in five-hour and ten-hour formats. Both start and finish at the boat ramp and follow a wide berm trail beside the water. Runners are credited for the laps they complete inside the time limit.
