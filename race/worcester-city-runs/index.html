Course

Half marathon and 10K on courses designed to be fast and as flat as possible, starting and finishing at Croft Road by Worcester Racecourse, ten minutes' walk from Worcester Foregate Street station. The routes run on public roads that are closed or segregated from traffic for the duration of the race. Young athletes' races and a Saturday Families on Track event run alongside.

About

The Worcester City Runs are a celebration of running, featuring a scenic Half Marathon, a fast-paced 10km, and junior events perfect for the whole family. Set in the historic city of Worcester, United Kingdom, this event offers a unique opportunity to explore the city's charming streets and stunning countryside views.
