Course

An out and back with a loop section, starting and finishing at Mt. Timpanogos Park, Orem. The organiser states that 99.9 percent of the route is mountain trail and fire break road. The marathon covers 26.1 miles with seven aid stations; the half covers 13.1 miles with three.

About

An incredibly challenging mountain trail marathon and half marathon course starting at Timpanogos Park with 5,883 feet of elevation gain. The 15th running of this event features 99.9% mountain trails and fire break roads with a 4-hour cutoff at mile 11.39.
