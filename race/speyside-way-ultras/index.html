Course

Point-to-point ultras along the Speyside Way through Moray whisky country to the coast. The 50km starts at Tamdhu Distillery, crosses Ben Aigan and mixes trail, forestry track and road via Fochabers and Spey Bay to finish in Buckie. The 100km starts in Aviemore and joins the 50km route at Tamdhu.

About

An ultramarathon over 50K and 100K distances following the Speyside Way, a long-distance trail through Moray in Scotland's whisky-producing heartland. The route traces the River Spey and old railway paths from Aviemore toward the coast.
