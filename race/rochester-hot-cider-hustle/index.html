Course

Start and finish at Mayo High School, 1420 11th Ave SE, Rochester. The routes run almost entirely on paved paths in the Bear Creek Trail area over gently rolling terrain. The half marathon course is USATF-certified. Water stations are placed at intervals, with sports drink also available on the half marathon course.
