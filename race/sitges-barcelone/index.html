Course

Both races start from Passeig de la Ribera on the Sitges seafront, near Restaurant Kansas, and run the flat coastal roads along the Mediterranean. The 10K quarter marathon sets off at 9:00am and the half marathon at 10:00am. A 5K school race runs alongside the main events.

About

Situated along the stunning Mediterranean coast, the Sitges Barcelone running event offers runners a chance to explore the charming seaside town of Sitges. Participants can look forward to a scenic course that showcases the region's picturesque landscapes and rich cultural heritage. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience for all.
