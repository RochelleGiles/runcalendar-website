Course

The Twinkling Two-Miler starts at Plyler Park on Ocean Boulevard and heads south, turning onto 2nd Avenue North towards the Pier, then left along the Boardwalk and its holiday lights display before looping back to Plyler Park. Sealed streets and boardwalk throughout. The event is untimed, with a raffle in place of awards.

About

The Twinkling Two Miler is an untimed evening run and walk along the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk during Winter Wonderland at the Beach. Participants follow a 1.6-mile route past the oceanfront holiday lights display, starting and finishing at Plyler Park on Ocean Boulevard.
