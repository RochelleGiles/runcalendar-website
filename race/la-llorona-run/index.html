About

Whether you're a seasoned road runner or someone who's curious about getting into the world of trail running, this 10K trail run is perfect for first-timers, offering a great opportunity to explore the exciting world of trail running while keeping things accessible. You'll be surrounded by the natural beauty of McAllister Park's trails—cactus-lined paths, sightings of deer, armadillos, and more!
