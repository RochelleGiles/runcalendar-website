About

The Suttle Lake Trailfest is a trail running event featuring 24-hour, 12-hour, and 6-hour races on a 3.6-mile loop around Suttle Lake with 110 feet of elevation gain per loop. Participants enjoy free camping at South Shore Campground, catered meals, aid stations, and a supportive community atmosphere.
