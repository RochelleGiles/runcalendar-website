Course

Starts and finishes on grass in Lydiard Park, on a mixture of tarmac paths and woodland trails with sections of grass and gravel. The route is hilly and twisting with uneven ground and includes a climb into woodland; a bypass avoids that section and slightly shortens the distance.

About

Race for Life brings Cancer Research UK's 5K fun run to Lydiard Park on Swindon's western edge, with participants running, jogging or walking through the historic parkland surrounding Lydiard House. All money raised goes toward cancer research.
