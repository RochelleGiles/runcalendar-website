About

The Portland Winter Solstice Fun Run offers four challenging and scenic distances through the beautiful coastal town of Portland. The course features highlights including the Cape Nelson Lighthouse, Fawthrop Lagoon, and sections of the Great South West Walk trail system. This unique winter event combines both road and trail running experiences with stunning foreshore views.
