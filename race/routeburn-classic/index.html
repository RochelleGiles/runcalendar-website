Course

A point-to-point run over the Routeburn Track from The Divide to Routeburn Shelter. The 32.2km course climbs from 550m through beech forest to Harris Saddle at 1,292m, passing the Mackenzie Hut checkpoint at 12km, then descends through alpine meadow and gorge sections. The track mixes well-graded path with technical alpine single-track.

About

The Routeburn Classic is a challenging alpine adventure run through New Zealand's stunning Routeburn Track in Mount Aspiring and Fiordland National Parks. Runners traverse spectacular mountain scenery including crystal-clear lakes, majestic peaks, and native beech forests. This iconic race follows one of New Zealand's most famous Great Walks, offering participants a unique opportunity to race through pristine wilderness.
