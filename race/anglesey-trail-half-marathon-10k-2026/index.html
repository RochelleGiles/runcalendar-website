Course

Trail half marathon and 10K based at an event village in Newborough Forest on Anglesey, running single-track forest paths and woodland trails before dropping onto the sand at Traeth Llanddwyn and out towards Llanddwyn Island, with views to the Llyn Peninsula and Snowdonia. Marshalled and signed throughout, with energy stations, support vehicles, bag drop and chip timing.

About

The course takes you through the trees onto the stunning Llanddwyn Island and onwards to beautiful winding woodland trails, which once hosted the Commonwealth Mountain and Ultra Distance Running Championships. You'll catch glimpses of the Llyn Peninsula and the mountains of Eryri (Snowdonia) amongst other incredible views. With loads of single track forest trails, quiet forest views and sandy running trails, there is something for everyone in this race, whether you're an experienced runner, a first timer, or you're more familiar with road running.
