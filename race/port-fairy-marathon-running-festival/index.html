Course

A flat multi-lap course through Port Fairy, taking in the historic streets, the coastline, the wharf and the river frontage. The organisers describe it as wider, car free and free of stairs, and the course holds World Athletics and AIMS certification.

About

The Port Fairy Marathon & Community Running Festival takes runners through one of Australia's most stunning coastal landscapes. Nestled along the picturesque Great Ocean Road, this scenic event offers a challenging yet breathtaking experience for runners of all levels. With sweeping ocean views and charming seaside towns, the Port Fairy Marathon is a must-do on any runner's bucket list.
