Course

A USATF-certified 13.1 miles along the fully paved Friendly City Trail, linking Harrisonburg's parks, golf course paths and neighbourhood streets. Start and finish are at Westover Park, with the first wave away at 7:10 a.m. and a four-hour finishing window. A two-person relay splits the same course.
