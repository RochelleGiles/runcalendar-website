About

The Valentine Run/Walk: Race for Justice, hosted by Legal Services of Northern California (LSNC), is now past its 23rd running on Valentine's Day. The event includes age-appropriate Kids' Fun Runs, a 1.8-mile non-competitive race and a 4-mile timed race, welcoming participants of all ages and abilities, including those using wheelchairs or strollers, with leashed dogs also invited.
