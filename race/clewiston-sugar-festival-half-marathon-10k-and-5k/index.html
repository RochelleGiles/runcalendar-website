About

Kick off your Sugar Festival Saturday with some energy! Our Half Marathon, 10K, and 5K races start the day with big hype, great music, and plenty of friends and family cheering you on. All races start and finish at the Clewiston Sugar Festival grounds, taking runners onto the iconic Lake Okeechobee Scenic Trail for unbeatable views.
