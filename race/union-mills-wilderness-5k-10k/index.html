About

The Union Mills Learning Center presents the Union Mills Wilderness 5K & 10K, now past its fifth running, in Bostic, North Carolina. The 10K takes in scenery along the Second Broad River and the CSX railroad, around the base of Rocky Face Mountain in the Box Creek Wilderness area, while the 5K follows the same course but turns back sooner.
