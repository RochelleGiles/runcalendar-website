Course

Starts and finishes north of the Palladium on City Center Drive in Carmel, running out through Hamilton County, the Carmel parks and the Arts and Design District. Flat to rolling, and marathon runners pass the start/finish line again at halfway before heading out for the second half.

About

Carmel Marathon Weekend offers a marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K through Carmel, Indiana, much of it along the flat Monon Trail rail-corridor through the city's Midtown district. It's one of the largest running weekends in the Indianapolis suburbs.
