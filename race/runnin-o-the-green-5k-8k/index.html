About

Lace up your shoes and bust out your shamrock socks – it's time to get your green on! Runnin' O' the Green 5K & 8K is back with a brand-new route and location: Stones Throw Brewery in Fairhaven Village! Join us for a community classic as we celebrate Bellingham's beautiful greenways and fill the streets with green. Stick around after the run to enjoy the festive atmosphere at Stones Throw Brewery. As always costumes are highly encouraged – the greener the better.
