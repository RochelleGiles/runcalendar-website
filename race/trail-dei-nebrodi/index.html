About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Nebrodi Mountains at the Trail Dei Nebrodi, an exhilarating trail running event in Sicily. Traverse breathtaking landscapes as you challenge yourself on this scenic course, which winds through dense forests, past crystal-clear streams, and over rolling hills. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or looking for a new adventure, this event offers an unforgettable experience in the heart of the Sicilian wilderness.
