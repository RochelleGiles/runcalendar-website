About

The Ultra Trail Stara Planina is a challenging and picturesque ultra-running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Stara Planina mountain range. Runners will tackle a demanding course that winds through remote and rugged terrain, offering a true test of endurance and a chance to experience the breathtaking natural beauty of this region. This event is a must-try for adventure-seeking ultramarathoners.
