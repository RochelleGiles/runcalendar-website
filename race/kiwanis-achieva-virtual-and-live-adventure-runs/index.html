Course

The live races start and finish in the playground area at the north end of Honeymoon Island State Park, Dunedin. All turns are marked and staffed by course monitors, and water stations are placed along both routes. The organiser does not state the surface.

About

The event offers live 5K and 8K (5 mile) runs at Honeymoon Island State Park in Dunedin, FL, starting at 8:30 AM with check-in from 7:00 AM. Participants enjoy a pancake breakfast afterward, with virtual options also available during the week of February 9th.
