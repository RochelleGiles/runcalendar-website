Course

A dog-friendly 5K run or walk based at Boneyard Madison, 1018 Walsh Road, with a 1 mile fun walk and a kids 1 mile walk alongside it. The race is followed by a fundraiser party with food, live music and a raffle.

About

The Fetch 5K is a dog jog and fun walk in Madison, Wisconsin, organized by Fetch Wisconsin Rescue. Runners are welcome to bring leashed dogs along the 5K and 1-mile routes, with proceeds supporting the rescue's adoption programs.
