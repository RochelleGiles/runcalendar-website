Course

Start and finish at Blackwood Mineral Springs, 41 Golden Point Road, Blackwood. An out-and-back trail course that runs about 18 km before joining the Lerderderg Track. Aid stations at the start/finish and O'Briens Crossing carry chips, lollies, fruit, cake, Coke, gels and electrolyte. Cup-free event: runners carry a supplied reusable flexi-cup.

About

The challenging course is on beautiful trails and single-track among the hills, valleys and rough terrain of the Lerderderg Trail between Blackwood and Myrniong. Qualifying criteria applies for the 70km distance. This annual event raises funds for Berry Street.
