About

This evening half marathon runs point-to-point on the paved Old Plank Trail beside Highway 23, starting at Quit Qui Oc Sports Complex in Plymouth and finishing at the Sports Core in Kohler. The flat rail-trail course starts at 6pm to avoid summer heat, with pacers and six water stations along the way.
