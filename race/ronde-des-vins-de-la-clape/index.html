About

The Ronde Des Vins De La Clape is a must-run event for wine enthusiasts and outdoor adventurers alike. Set against the stunning backdrop of the La Clape region, this race takes runners on a scenic journey through vineyards and along rugged coastal trails. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply soaking in the breathtaking views, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
