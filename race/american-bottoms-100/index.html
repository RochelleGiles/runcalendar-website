Course

An out-and-back 110-miler and a point-to-point 55-miler through the American Bottom floodplain near Columbia, Illinois. The route loosely follows the American Discovery Trail and the Kaskaskia-Cahokia Trail along the Mississippi River, passing historic sites. Footing is 76% levee path and 24% sealed road, and organisers describe the course as flat and fast.

About

This is a unique road ultra on a beautiful course, following the Mississippi River, as you run through the historic American Bottom. Race headquarters is in Columbia, Illinois just outside of St. Louis, Missouri. The race offers a 110 mile and a 55 mile option.
