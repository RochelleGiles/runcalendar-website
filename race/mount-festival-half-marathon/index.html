Course

A flat, fast coastal course at Mount Maunganui, run oceanside on the same route as the Tauranga Half Ironman and finishing in Coronation Park. Part road, part trail, with five aid stations on course carrying electrolytes, gels, coke and bananas. Offered as 5 km, 10 km and half marathon.
