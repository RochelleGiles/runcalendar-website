Course

Run on the Ford Britain Trust test track at the Dunton headquarters in Laindon, Essex, a private circuit otherwise closed to the public and almost entirely traffic-free. The 10 mile and 10km races are accredited and chip-timed; a 2.5km fun run also runs on the day. Spectators can watch from the start and finish line, and the course is wheelchair accessible and open to walkers.

About

Test the limits at the Test Track 10 & Fun Run! This unique 10-mile event takes place on the private roads of the Ford Dunton Campus in Laindon, Essex, offering runners an exclusive chance to experience the thrill of a professional test track. Whether you're looking to push your pace or enjoy a leisurely fun run, this event has something for everyone.
