About

The Norn Iron 100 is a stunning 108-mile point-to-point challenge through the rugged beauty of Northern Ireland. Runners will start in the coastal town of Ballintoy, County Antrim and finish in the picturesque town of Gortin, Omagh, taking on the wild and untamed landscapes of the Emerald Isle along the way. This event is a must for any adventurous ultrarunner seeking an unforgettable experience.
