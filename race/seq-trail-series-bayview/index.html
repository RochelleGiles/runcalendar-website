Course

Starts and finishes at the Bayview Conservation Staging Area, 487-503 German Church Rd, Redland Bay. A 7.4km short course and a 10.8km long course are offered, with Strava routes published for each. Professionally timed, with a post-race charity BBQ.

About

SEQ Trail Series: Bayview is a trail running event at Bayview Conservation Park near Redland Bay, southeast of Brisbane. Runners can choose a 10.8km long course or a 7.4km short course through coastal bushland close to Moreton Bay.
