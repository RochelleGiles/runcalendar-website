Course

Start and finish at the Chehalem Cultural Center, 415 E Sheridan Street, Newberg, Oregon. A 5K and a 10K run/walk on town streets, finishing back at the centre for the Camellia Festival. Every finisher is given a camellia or other plant. No route or aid station detail is published.
