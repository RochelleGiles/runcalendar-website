Course

A twelve-hour timed event on a 4.6-mile loop from the Bonita Lakes boat landing around the big lake on a jeep trail road, running 6am to 6pm for as many loops as possible. Rolling, increasingly demanding hills with easy footing. One aid station sits at the start/finish of each loop, plus a roving UTV on course.

About

Circle of The Dragon is a 12-hour timed event at Bonita Lakes in Meridian, Mississippi, run on a 4.6-mile jeep-trail loop from 6am to 6pm. Runners complete as many loops as they can, with Dragon level awarded for reaching the full target. A shorter two-loop Turtle Division is also offered.
