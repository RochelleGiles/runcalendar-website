About

The SL Tennessee Clinton 5k and 1 Mile Fun Run course takes runners along Riverside Drive toward Riverbend Road with flat terrain along the Clinch River. The Start & Finish line will be near 19 Riverside Drive near Lakefront Park in Clinton, TN 37716. The Clinton 5k is a member race of the Run and See Tennessee Grand Prix.
