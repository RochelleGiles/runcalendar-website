About

Eat, Run and have some fun! This is not just another race, this is a Dash for Donuts! This 7k/5k course will be held on paved trails in 370 Lakeside Park. Enjoy some delicious donuts from Donut King halfway through the course to energize you before heading back to the finish line! How many are YOU able to eat at the halfway mark? Each race participant will receive a tee shirt and a finisher medal.
