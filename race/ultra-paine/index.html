About

Ultra Paine is an ultra trail running event in the stunning Patagonia region of Chile. The event offers a variety of distances, from a 50K ultra to a 5K fun run, catering to runners of all levels. The course showcases the breathtaking landscapes of the Patagonian wilderness, with challenging terrain and scenic vistas that make this event a truly unique and unforgettable experience for participants.
