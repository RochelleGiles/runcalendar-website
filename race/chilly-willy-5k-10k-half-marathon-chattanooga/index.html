Course

5K, 10K and half marathon on the flat Tennessee Riverwalk Trail in Chattanooga, starting at Shelter #1 near the dam at 4631 Amnicola Highway. The 5K and 10K are single out-and-backs while the half marathon runs a double loop. Water stations sit roughly every 1.5 miles.

About

Bundle up and get ready to race! The Chilly Willy 5K/10K/Half Marathon is the perfect way to kick off the new year with some frosty fun and a whole lot of miles. Whether you're tackling the 5K, digging deep for the 10K, or braving the full Half Marathon, this winter-themed event brings runners together for a crisp, refreshing challenge that celebrates the season.
