Course

A 50K in Frozen Head State Park starting from Flat Fork Road at 7:00am, on rugged Brushy Mountains trails with numerous hard climbs and descents. The course changes each year and the map is revealed only the night before; GPS is banned. A decision point lets runners drop to a marathon finish, while the full 50K must beat the 8:20pm course close.

About

A 50K held each September in Frozen Head State Park, Tennessee. The course changes every year and is revealed only the night before, GPS devices are banned, and runners face steep climbs through the Brushy Mountains, with a mid-race decision point to drop to a marathon finish or continue for the full 50K.
