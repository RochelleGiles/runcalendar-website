Course

The 5K is run at Elizabethtown Nature Park and is billed by the organisers as a light trail event. Packet pickup opens at 7:00am on race morning and a free entry is offered for children aged 12 and under.

About

A 5K trail run in Elizabethtown, Kentucky, held each fall as part of the town's Heartland Harvest Festival. The community festival celebrates the season with local food, crafts and a fun run through the area.
