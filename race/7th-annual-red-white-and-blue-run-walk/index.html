About

Join us to celebrate America! Runners and Walkers are all welcome!! Participants are encouraged to dress in red, white, and blue! Get the entire family involved and register for either the RWB Run or our free Stars and Stripes Kids' Dash (50 yards)! Participants are encouraged to dress in costume to celebrate our Independence!
