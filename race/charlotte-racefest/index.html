Course

Starts and finishes in the Symphony Park car park at Sharon Road and Carnegie Boulevard in Charlotte's SouthPark. The first and last two miles run on Sharon Road and Carnegie Boulevard, and the route was redesigned to take in the flowering dogwood, North Carolina's state flower.

About

Charlotte Racefest brings a half marathon, 10K, and 1-mile fun run through the streets of Uptown Charlotte, North Carolina. The course winds past the city's skyline before finishing in the heart of downtown.
