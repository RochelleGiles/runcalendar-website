About

Hammonton Rotary's holiday run at the town's Winterfest: a timed 5K and an untimed one-mile fun walk, both starting at 10:00 from the Mount Carmel fairgrounds on Mount Carmel Lane. Costumes and Grinch green are encouraged. Afterwards there is hot cocoa and s'mores, with the Winterfest vendors open around the finish.
