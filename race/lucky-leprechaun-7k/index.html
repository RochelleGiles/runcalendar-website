About

Run or walk your way in St. Paddy's style through the beautiful town of Wauwatosa for our 14th Annual Lucky Leprechaun 7K presented by Festival Foods. Starting and finishing in Hart Park with a 10:00 AM start time, you can sleep in so you are ready for THE BEST Post-Race Party in Milwaukee featuring Live Music at Leff's Lucky Town!
