About

After a decade of winter memories, Hot Dash is celebrating its final finish line on Saturday, March 21. Whether you've been a regular participant or want to be part of the last Hot Dash celebration, this event is for all winter-embracing Minnesotans! Hot Dash is a celebration of the Minnesotan spirit, an event that gets you out of bed to move, connect and party!
