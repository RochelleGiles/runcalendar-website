Course

Two courses, five miles and two miles, both starting on 2nd Avenue North between North 25th and North 24th Streets in downtown Billings. The five-mile starts at 8:00am and the two-mile at 9:00am. Participants may run, walk or use a wheelchair; bicycles, skates, scooters and skateboards are not permitted on course.

About

The Montana Women's Run is a women's road event in downtown Billings offering five-mile and two-mile courses that can be run, walked or completed by wheelchair. It has been held since 1982 and raises money for local women's health services. Virtual entry options are also offered.
