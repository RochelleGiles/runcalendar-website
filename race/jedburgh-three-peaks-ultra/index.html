Course

A 38-mile ultra over three peaks in the Scottish Borders, starting and finishing in Jedburgh and fully route-marked. Checkpoints fall at 10, 17, 21 and 28 miles. A four-leg relay of 10, 7, 11 and 10 miles changes over at Maxton Churchyard and Rhymer's Stone. A waterproof with taped seams is mandatory kit.

About

The Jedburgh Three Peaks Ultra takes in three hill summits in the Scottish Borders, starting and finishing in the market town of Jedburgh. Runners can enter solo over 38 miles or as part of a relay team.
