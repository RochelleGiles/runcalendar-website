Course

The marathon, half marathon and 10km all start and finish at the Glengarry Football and Netball Club, Cairnbrook Road, Glengarry. The course takes in the historic Gippsland Plains Rail Trail.

About

The Traralgon Marathon and Running Festival offers a friendly country atmosphere on the historic Gippsland Plains Rail Trail. The event is ideal for serious and novice runners, with post-race food and socializing opportunities.
