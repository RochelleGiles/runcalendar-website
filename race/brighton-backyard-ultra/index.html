Course

Last-one-standing race in Stanmer Park Nature Reserve at Falmer, Brighton. Runners complete a 4.167 mile (6.7km) out-and-back loop to the top of the park and back within the hour, every hour, until one is left. The route is signed with arrows and caution boards, and hot and cold water, drinks and snacks are available at the venue alongside rest marquees.

About

The Brighton Backyard Ultra is a last one standing endurance race based at Stanmer Park, Brighton. Runners complete a 4.16 miles loop every hour, on the hour until there is one person standing. The course features woods, grass and trail with beautiful views of Stanmer Park Nature Reserve.
