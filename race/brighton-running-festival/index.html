About

The Brighton Running Festival at Decker Park in Brighton, Queensland, includes a half marathon, 10km, 5km and 2km event. Its fast, flat courses run between Brighton and Shorncliffe Pier with views of Moreton Bay throughout, and all finishers receive a themed medal, post-race snacks and a finisher certificate.
