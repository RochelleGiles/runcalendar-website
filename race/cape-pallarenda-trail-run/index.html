Course

All four distances start and finish at Race HQ in Freemason's Park, Cape Pallarenda, 15 minutes from Townsville. The course mixes technical single track, fire trails, steep climbs and beach sections over rocky, dry ground. It carries sweeping views over Cleveland Bay, Magnetic Island and the Coral Sea.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of northern Queensland at the Cape Pallarenda Trail Run, part of the renowned Outer Limits Trail Run Series. Lace up your shoes and challenge yourself on a scenic course that winds through the lush coastal landscapes north of Townsville, showcasing the region's diverse natural wonders. This event offers runners of all levels an opportunity to experience the thrill of trail running in a stunning Australian setting.
