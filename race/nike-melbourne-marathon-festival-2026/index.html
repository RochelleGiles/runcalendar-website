Course

Loop course starting on Batman Avenue and finishing inside the Melbourne Cricket Ground. The route passes Albert Park Lake, Flinders Street Station and the St Kilda beach foreshore on closed city roads; for 2026 the Beach Road section runs in the reverse direction. Aid station locations are announced closer to race day.

About

The iconic Nike Melbourne Marathon Festival returns to Australia's sporting capital. Thousands of participants will take part in five distances from 3km to the full marathon, starting at Batman Avenue and venturing through iconic Melbourne landmarks like Flinders Street Station and the Royal Botanic Gardens with the ultimate finish at the Melbourne Cricket Ground.
