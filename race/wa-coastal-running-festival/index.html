Course

Starts at Beachside Park on Beachside Drive, Burns Beach, and follows the shared Burns Beach coastal path north towards Mindarie. Solo runs are out-and-back, turning to return to the finish; partner relay options alternate legs from the start line. The path is sealed and publicly shared, with several challenging inclines and Indian Ocean views throughout.

About

The WA Coastal Running Festival is held at Burns Beach, north of Perth, with 4km, 8km and 16km solo distances to choose from. The course starts at Burns Beach Foreshore Park and follows the path north towards Mindarie, taking in challenging inclines and Indian Ocean views.
