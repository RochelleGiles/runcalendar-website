Course

Flat dirt trails and tracks through the Angkor temple park, rice paddies and villages outside Siem Reap, passing Angkor Wat, Bayon and the jungle temples. Distances from 16 km to over 100 km; the ground is easy, the heat is not.

About

Explore the ancient wonders of Angkor Uta on this challenging ultra-trail running event. Weave through the lush jungles and rugged landscapes surrounding the iconic Angkor Wat temple complex, immersing yourself in the rich cultural heritage of this remarkable destination. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or a trail enthusiast, the Ultra Trail Angkor offers a truly unique and unforgettable running experience.
