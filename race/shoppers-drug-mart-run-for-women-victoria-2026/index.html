About

The Shoppers Drug Mart® Run for Women is a celebrated annual event series held in cities across Canada, with the Victoria edition taking place at the scenic Elk Lake Regional Park. This event is dedicated to supporting women's mental health, raising funds for the Canadian Mental Health Association, BC Division (CMHA BC). Programs such as "Living Life to the Full" and "Enjoy Your Baby" benefit directly from the proceeds, focusing on mental wellness and postpartum depression prevention.
