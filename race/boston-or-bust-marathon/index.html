Course

Marathon and half marathon run entirely on the paved Aldridge Creek Greenway beside Aldridge Creek and the Tennessee River in South Huntsville. Both start and finish at Ditto Landing Marina and are described as extremely flat and fast. Aid stations carry water, Gatorade and GU gels. Both courses are USATF certified.
