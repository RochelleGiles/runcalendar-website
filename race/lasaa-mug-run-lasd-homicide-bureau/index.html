About

The LASAA Mug Run - LASD Homicide Bureau is an athletic event organized by the Los Angeles Sheriff's Athletic Association to promote health, fitness, and camaraderie among LASD employees, law enforcement, and the public. Started in 1993, it now attracts over 300 runners for friendly competition and community interaction.
