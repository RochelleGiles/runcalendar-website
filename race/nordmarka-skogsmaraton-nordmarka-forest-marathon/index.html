About

Nordmarka Skogsmaraton is a scenic forest marathon taking place in the beautiful woodland trails of Norway. Now in its 33rd year, this event offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the lush green landscapes of Nordmarka. The race provides multiple distance options through picturesque forest terrain, attracting both local and international trail running enthusiasts.
