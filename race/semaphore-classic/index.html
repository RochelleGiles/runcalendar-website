About

The Semaphore Classic is a scenic out-and-back running event along the Esplanade at Semaphore, following the Coast Park route. It offers a fantastic opportunity for runners of all ages to kick off the running year, with a family-friendly atmosphere and the potential bonus of spotting dolphins along the course.
