Course

A night-and-day trail festival in the Snowshoe area of the Monongahela National Forest. The Dark Skies 100K starts at the entrance to Seneca State Forest just after midnight, the Full Moon 50K from Cass Scenic Railroad Park at dawn, and the Stargazer 30K, Twilight 5K and kids mile from Shaver's Center. Run on mountain trails.

About

The West Virginia Trail Fest is a trail ultramarathon at Snowshoe Mountain, West Virginia. Distances range from a 5K up to 100K, including 30K and 50K options, across the Allegheny Mountains of the state's highlands.
