About

The Cookie Classic Run is a Girl Scouts of the Colonial Coast fundraiser held each January in Chesapeake, Virginia, offering chip-timed 10K and 5K options plus an untimed 1-mile fun run. Girl Scout cookies are served to finishers at the end, alongside age-group awards.
