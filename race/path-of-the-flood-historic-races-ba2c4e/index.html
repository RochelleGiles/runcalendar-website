Course

Point-to-point course running from the ruins of the South Fork Dam down the Path of the Flood Trail to Peoples Natural Gas Park in Johnstown. It mixes trail and road sections and passes through the Staple Bend Tunnel, the Coy-McCombie Link and Hope Tunnel. The 14-mile, 8-mile and 5K distances share the same finish.

About

The Path of the Flood Historic Races commemorate the 1889 Johnstown Flood with races following the historic Path of the Flood Trail. Runners can choose from 14-mile, 8-mile, or 5K distances, passing through landmarks like the Staple Bend Tunnel and finishing at Peoples Natural Gas Park.
