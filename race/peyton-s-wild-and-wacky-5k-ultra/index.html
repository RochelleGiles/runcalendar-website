Course

A trail race at Laurel Hill County Park, Mount Pleasant, on a 5K loop repeated up to ten times for the ultra alongside a standalone 5K. The loop carries mud, roots and water crossings. Start and finish are at the park, with an aid station for ultra and relay runners and water at the start/finish.

About

Peyton's Wild and Wacky 5K Ultra, now past its 9th running, honors Peyton Moore, a local runner and track and field standout. The race is held at Laurel Hill County Park in Mount Pleasant, South Carolina.
