About

The Tapped For Sap 25K+ Trail Race is held at Brady's Run Park Lodge in Beaver Falls, Pennsylvania, run as a single loop of about 16 miles. The race is Trail Sisters Approved, with registration limited to 300 entrants. Post-race food includes pancakes, cinnamon rolls and maple syrup, and finishers receive a sleeveless tech shirt.
