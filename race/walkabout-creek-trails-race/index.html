Course

Start and finish are at the Enoggera Dam wall in d''Aguilar National Park near The Gap, Brisbane. The 12.5 km course mixes flatter sections with steep hills on broad fire trail and single track, climbing about 450 m in total. A checkpoint at roughly 9 km carries water and a first aid kit, and runners must carry 500 ml of water from the start.

About

The Walkabout Creek Trails Race is run by the Trail Running Association of Queensland in d''Aguilar National Park, on the edge of Brisbane at The Gap. The 12.5 km course starts and finishes at the Enoggera Dam wall and climbs about 450 m over fire trail and single track. Entry is capped at 100 runners.
