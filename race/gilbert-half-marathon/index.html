About

The Gilbert Half Marathon is run at Verde at Cooley Station in Gilbert, Arizona, offering a half marathon, 10K and 5K on USATF-certified courses with live and virtual entry. It raises funds for the Shun the Sun Foundation, which works on skin cancer prevention and early detection.
