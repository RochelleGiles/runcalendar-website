Course

Lapped out-and-back course starting at North Collier Regional Park, Naples. The 5K and 10K run a 3.11-mile loop once and twice; the half marathon runs a 3.28-mile loop four times. All distances start at 8:00am with a 3 hour 30 minute limit and there is no on-the-day entry.
