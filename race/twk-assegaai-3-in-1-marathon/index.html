About

The TWK Assegaai 3-in-1 Marathon is an annual road event in Piet Retief (eMkhondo), Mpumalanga, hosted by the Assegaai Marathon Club with marathon, half marathon and 10km distances. It is a well-established fixture on the local Mpumalanga racing calendar.
