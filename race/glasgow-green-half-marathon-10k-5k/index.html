Course

Lapped course on a 5km loop of Glasgow Green beside the River Clyde. The 5K runs one lap, the 10K two laps, and the half marathon four laps plus a 1.1km section added at the start. Start and finish are both on Glasgow Green. Route is fully marked with kilometre markers, arrows and marshals; a water station sits on the main riverside path around 4km.

About

The Glasgow Green Half Marathon, 10K and 5K takes runners on a scenic tour through one of the UK's most vibrant cities. Weaving through the lush greenery of Glasgow Green, this event offers a unique opportunity to experience the city's rich history and stunning natural beauty. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this race caters to all levels with distances to suit every participant.
