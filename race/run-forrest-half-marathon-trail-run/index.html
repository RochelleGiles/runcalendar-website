Course

Winter trail courses under the forest canopy of the Otway Ranges, starting and finishing in the hinterland township of Forrest about two hours from Melbourne. The 21km, 10km and 6km all run on the area's established forest singletrack, taking in undulating hills, fern gullies and river crossings.

About

The Run Forrest Trail Run showcases the picturesque trails of the Otway Ranges, offering runners breathtaking natural beauty with undulating hills, flowing rivers, dense fern gullies, and fresh mountain air. Staged in the hinterland township of Forrest, this event highlights the region's world-class trails.
