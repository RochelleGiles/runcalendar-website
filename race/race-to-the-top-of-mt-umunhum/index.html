Course

A 13.35 mile point-to-point climb from the Hacienda entrance of Almaden Quicksilver Park to the summit of Mt Umunhum, gaining 4,767 ft including a steep pitch on the Barlow Trail. Run on established trail and dirt road, with a shuttle back to the start and a six-hour limit.
