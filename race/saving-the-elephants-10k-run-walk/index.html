Course

Both the 5K and 10K start and finish at the Naumburg Bandshell on The Mall in Central Park, New York City, on the park's paved roads and paths. The 5K course is USATF certified. There is one water station on course and another at the finish.

About

An event for everyone, the Saving the Elephants 10K Run/Walk is a chance for you to run, jog or walk in person in Central Park, New York or virtually wherever you are, with the opportunity to help raise valuable funds for our planet's gentle giants.
