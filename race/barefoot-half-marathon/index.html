Course

Point-to-point half marathon on paved roads from Sterling Town Hall south to Shrewsbury, Massachusetts. The route runs Route 12 South for about 5.9 miles, turns onto Route 140 South past the Wachusett Reservoir, then follows Route 70 South before finishing with one lap of an athletic track. Running barefoot is optional; shoes of any kind are permitted.
