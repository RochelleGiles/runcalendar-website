Course

Follows the rim of the Little Colorado River Gorge on Navajo Nation land near Cameron, Arizona, looking down into the canyon. The routes are rocky and challenging with some rugged terrain, particularly on the half marathon. A 10K and a shorter community run are also offered.

About

The Little Colorado River Half Marathon and 10K wind along the canyon rim on Navajo Nation land near Cameron, Arizona, with sweeping views down into the gorge. The race is organized by NavajoYES to support youth programs in the community.
