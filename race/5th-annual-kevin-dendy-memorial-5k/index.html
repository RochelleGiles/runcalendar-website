About

The Kevin Dendy Memorial 5K, now past its 4th running, honors Captain Kevin Dendy of the North Carolina National Guard, who provided medical care to soldiers during a tour in Kuwait and loved the outdoors before his death in a motorcycle accident. Proceeds benefit the Wounded Warrior Project and local community missions.
