About

The Eagle 5K, 10K, & 13.1M is a running event held at Holston River Park in Knoxville, TN, offering 5K, 10K, and half marathon distances on out-and-back courses. Runners receive finisher medals, with races starting at 9:00 AM and a 3.5-hour time limit.
