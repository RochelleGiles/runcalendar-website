Course

Out-and-back on the Rexis Branch of the Ghost Town Trail, starting past the closed gate by the old train at the Rexis trailhead. Runners head 2.5 miles out, turning just past the Red Mill Bridge, on a basically flat rail trail with around 115 ft of gradual climb each way. Run at night on a completely closed course with no road crossings.

About

Come relieve history while running Ghosttown Trail's first ever nighttime race now in its 6th year that brings fun and excitement to all who partake. This 5 mile race will be held on the Rexis Branch, this race is currently the only standalone race held on this section.
