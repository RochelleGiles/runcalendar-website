About

The Do Hard Things challenge starts at Coopers Landing in Columbia, Missouri, offering a 5K walk/run alongside a 9.11-mile run or ruck. Hosted by Do Hard Things Nation, it celebrates determination and resilience while showing support for first responders and veterans.
