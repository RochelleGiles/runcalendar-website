Course

A five-mile road course along the Belmar oceanfront and local streets, with a wheelchair division starting alongside the run. A 90-minute time limit applies, roughly an 18-minute mile. The course is closed to strollers, bicycles, skates and pets, and kids races run the evening before.

About

The 49th Annual Belmar 5 Mile Run is presented by the Goodwill Hose Co., the Borough of Belmar, and the Jersey Shore Running Club along the beautiful Belmar oceanfront and local streets. It is one of the largest road races in New Jersey with over 3000 participants, also featuring kids' races the day before.
