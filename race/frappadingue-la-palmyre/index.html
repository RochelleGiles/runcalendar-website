About

The Frappadingue La Palmyre is an obstacle course race event that takes place in La Palmyre. The event offers two course formats, a shorter 5-6 km course with 15 obstacles and a longer 10-12 km course with 40 obstacles. The event is designed to challenge participants physically and mentally, while also promoting team building and camaraderie.
