Course

About

The Half BraemarAthon, 10 mile and 10km event takes runners on a scenic tour through the stunning Aberdeenshire countryside in the United Kingdom. Boasting beautiful views and a challenging yet rewarding course, this race is a must for runners looking to explore the picturesque landscapes of the region.
