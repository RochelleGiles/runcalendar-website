Course

Held at Lagoon Valley Park, 1 Pena Adobe Road, Vacaville. A single loop of the lagoon on manicured, universally designed trails mixing paved, gravel and dirt surfaces, finishing at the waterfront. Alongside the 5K run there is a 5K roll for wheelchairs, handcycles and duo teams, a half-5K walk and a kids' fun run.

About
