About

Northwest Series is organized by Mainly Marathons, one of the largest race organizers in the US, with races in all 50 states. You can participate in one race or take the challenge to complete the whole series! Mainly Marathons races are designed for runners who run long and run often. All finishers in races longer than half marathon get a custom metal-stamped nameplate!
