Course

Road course starting on Commerce Street in the Frog Level district of downtown Waynesville, climbing to Main Street, passing under the Gateway to the Smokies arch and the Riley Howell memorial tree, then looping through parts of Waynesville and Hazelwood. One aid station at mile 3.1 with water and Gatorade.
