About

The Enduro Des Sables is an iconic coastal running event that brings together passionate runners in a friendly and festive atmosphere. Participants challenge themselves along the sandy beaches of Normandy, experiencing a unique blend of maritime landscape and athletic endurance. This legendary race has become a must-attend summer event that celebrates the spirit of running and community.
