About

The Laufgemeinschaft Saarbrücken invites you to the seventh edition of the saarBRÜCKENlauf. This is a guided run across (almost) all the bridges of the state capital. The event offers two versions: a 10 km run with 7 bridges and a 20 km run with 17 different bridges. Runners will be divided into groups of different paces to navigate the challenging course with ramps, stairs, and frequent direction changes.
