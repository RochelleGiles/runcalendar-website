Course

A flat, fast, scenic course on the Greenbrier River Trail. For 2026 both races switch to out-and-back routes beginning and ending in downtown Marlinton, replacing the former point-to-point format; a marathon and a half marathon are offered. Boston-qualifier certification of the new marathon course is in progress.

About

A marathon and half marathon along the Greenbrier River Trail, the longest rail-trail in West Virginia, following the Greenbrier River near Marlinton. The flat former railroad grade runs through forest and farmland.
