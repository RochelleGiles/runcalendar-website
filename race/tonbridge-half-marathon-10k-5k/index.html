Course

A marked and measured single loop starting at The Judd School in Tonbridge and running out into the Kent countryside. Half marathon, 10K and 5K start at 09:00, 09:30 and 09:45 respectively. Water stations are provided and every kilometre is marked with a distance flag.

About

The Run Kent Tonbridge Half Marathon is a picturesque, closed road, single loop course. Starting and finishing at The Judd School, the 13.1 mile route takes in the sights of Tonbridge town before heading out into the Kent countryside.
