Course

The 5K is run inside Cantigny Park, Wheaton, starting and finishing on the park grounds, with two water stations on course and one at the finish, and a 90-minute limit. The 1K Tasting Walk has three sampling stations plus a final sample at the finish.

About

Join Wicked Wine Run at Cantigny Park, a historic and beautiful space once home to Robert R. McCormick, who dedicated it as a public space for education and recreation. Experience the park's lush grounds during Wicked Wine Run, where you can run or walk through picturesque landscapes and celebrate with wine, music, and friends. Don't miss this unforgettable location with Wicked Wine Run!
