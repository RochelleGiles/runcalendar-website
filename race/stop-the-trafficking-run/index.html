Course

Chip-timed 5K at Purgatory Creek Park in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, on the park's paths. Pre-race speaking from 8:15am, refreshments and anti-trafficking organisation displays at the finish, and free parking in the SW Metro ramp. A virtual option runs over the following week.

About

Join our 13th annual 5K walk/run to raise awareness and funds to end human trafficking. All proceeds will be designated to organizations working to help sexually exploited youth and women.
