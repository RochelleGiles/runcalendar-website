About

Cloverfest is a St Patrick's Day community festival in Columbia Falls, Montana, run by the local Area Chamber of Commerce. A chip-timed 5K and a one mile fun run start from Columbia Falls Junior High School, alongside live music, food trucks and a children's carnival.
