About

Experience the historic charm of the 10 Km de la Cité des Papes in the heart of an ancient city. Run through the picturesque streets and landmarks of this UNESCO World Heritage site, where the Popes once resided. This unique race offers runners a chance to explore the rich cultural heritage of this captivating region.
