Course

5K on Seabrook Island winding through maritime forest, salt marsh, dunes and beach, starting and finishing at St Christopher Camp and Conference Center. Footing is mostly trail with exposed roots, sand, mud and gravel, and some single-file sections under six feet wide. Water refill stations are provided on course.

About

The Barrier Island Earth Day 5K celebrates Earth Day while educating participants about the Lowcountry coastal environment. Held at St. Christopher Camp & Conference Center, it welcomes everyone from competitive runners to casual walkers.
