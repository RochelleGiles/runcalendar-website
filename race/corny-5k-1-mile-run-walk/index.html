About

The Corny 5K & 1 Mile Run/Walk takes place during corn and wheat harvest time in Claysville, raising funds for the restoration of the historic First Lutheran Church building. It's a family-friendly event with a corn theme, and runners are encouraged to wear their corniest outfits.
