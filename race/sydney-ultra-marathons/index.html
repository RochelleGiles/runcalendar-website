Course

All three races start and finish at St Ives Showground and run out through Ku-ring-gai Chase National Park, Garigal National Park, Manly Dam and the surrounding reserves. Genuine bush singletrack and fire trail with steady climbing — about 976m for the 30km, 1,572m for the 50km and 2,914m for the 100km. Courses are marked with signage and trail flags, with marshals at key intersections.

About

The Sydney Ultra Marathon event takes runners through the stunning landscapes of Ku-Ring-Gai and Garigal National Park, offering a dynamic mix of tranquil forest and beautiful waterfront tracks.
