About

The Colterris Wine Run 5k is a chip-timed run and walk through the vineyards of Colterris Winery in Palisade, Colorado. The late-afternoon course crosses more than 75 acres of vines on the bench above the Colorado River. Entry includes a wine pour at the finish, a collapsible glass and a wine-glass finisher medal.
