About

A 5K run and walk through the vineyards of Colterris Winery in Palisade, Colorado. The chip-timed, late-afternoon course crosses more than 75 acres of vines on the bench above the Colorado River. Entry includes a wine pour at the finish, a collapsible glass and a wine-glass finisher medal.
