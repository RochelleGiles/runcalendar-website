Course

Two loops starting on the Madison High School track, running through the Madison Recreation Complex and taking in a short trail section around the Madison Community Garden. The 5K starts at 9am and a kids fun run follows at 10am.

About

The MEF Madison 5K Run/Walk and Kids Fun Run supports the Madison Education Foundation by funding grants for innovative educational programs in local schools. The event takes place at Madison High School.
