About

The Chiclana Half Marathon takes place in the beautiful coastal town of Chiclana de la Frontera. The course allows runners to experience the city streets, marshlands, and incredible views of Sancti Petri beach. Known as the fastest half marathon in the province, the event combines urban running with scenic coastal views while participants can enjoy the local gastronomy and warm climate.
