Course

Start and finish at North Park, 210 Demonbreun Drive, Jackson, Tennessee. Every distance is built from repeated paved out-and-back loops in the park: the 5K and 10K on a 0.78-mile loop, the half marathon on a 1.09-mile loop. All events start at 8:30 am with a three-and-a-half hour limit.
