About

Yo, check out the mic! Lace-up for the 12th Annual Fall Fest, where the beauty of Colorado's great outdoors meets the totally tubular fun of 80s nostalgia! The course takes you along the beloved Big Dry Creek Trail, winding southwest with stunning views of Standley Lake set against the majestic Flatirons. This event, which kicks off the fall season, is also the Endless Series sidekick to Beat the Heat. Come experience the breathtaking scenery in Westminster, CO, and embrace the dope spirit of the 1980s! Word, this race is going to be bad!
