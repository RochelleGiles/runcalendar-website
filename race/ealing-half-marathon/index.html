Course

Starts and finishes in Lammas Park, Ealing, on a fully road-closed route that also passes through Walpole Park. The half marathon sets off at 9:00 and the Ealing Feeling 5K at 9:05, with a one-mile Mini Mile held in the park the day before.

About

The Ealing Half Marathon is a must-do event for runners in West London, featuring a scenic course through the heart of this vibrant community. Touted as the area's biggest running event, this race offers a fantastic challenge for all levels, with stunning views and a lively atmosphere that will inspire you to push towards the finish line.
