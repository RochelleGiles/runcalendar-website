About

The Rooted Acres Endurance Race is a timed trail running event held at Rooted Acres in Woodbine, NJ, where participants complete as many 5.5-mile laps as possible within their chosen timeframe. It features scenic dirt trails with few roots, promoting community and connection to nature through this endurance challenge.
