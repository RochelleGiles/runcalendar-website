Course

A trail course on the newer, very runnable singletrack at Pocahontas State Park. Both races share the same first 10K, after which the half marathon adds a second loop. Water stations sit at roughly 3, 6 and 9 miles and runners are encouraged to carry their own.

About

The Pocahontas Trail Festival offers a 10K or half marathon through Pocahontas State Park in Chesterfield County, Virginia, one of the largest state parks in the commonwealth, with trails winding past lakes and through pine and hardwood forest.
