About

The Michael J. Pitney Farm Run takes place on the picturesque grounds of Pitney Meadows Community Farm. The 5k course is a mixed-surface route, featuring grass and stone dust trails that wind through the farm's open fields. The course is primarily flat with gentle rolling sections, offering a smooth and safe running experience with no road crossings.
