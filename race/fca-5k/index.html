About

The FCA 5K is a family-focused run welcoming runners and walkers to support high school student leadership through the Fellowship of Christian Athletes. Held at Venice High School, it features a chip-timed 5K course along the Inter-Coastal Waterway exercise path ending at athletic facilities.
