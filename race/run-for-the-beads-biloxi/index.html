About

Run for the Beads is a Mardi Gras-themed 5K, with a 1-mile fun run, in Biloxi, Mississippi, hosted annually by GCCA on the Mississippi Gulf Coast. Runners often turn out in beads and costumes, echoing the coast's Carnival season.
