Course

All three races follow the waymarked Wales Coast Path and finish at Mumbles: the Ultra 50 starts and ends at Mumbles Cricket Club, the Ultra Bach from Weobley Castle and the Trail Race from Rhossili. The going takes in rolling cliff tops, soft trail, dunes and hidden coves, with inland woodland and old railway path and a fast flat stretch around Swansea Bay. The Ultra 50 has eight manned checkpoints with hot and cold food and a drop-bag service.

About

Explore the rugged trails and scenic reservoirs of Yorkshire at the Punk Panther Yorkshire Reservoir Dogs event. This off-road running festival based in Otley, West Yorkshire, offers four challenging trail races through the region's stunning natural landscapes. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or seeking an adventurous new challenge, the Yorkshire Reservoir Dogs has something for every outdoor enthusiast.
