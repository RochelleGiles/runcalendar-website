About

Meadows Marathon, an initiative of Edinburgh Students' Charities Appeal (ESCA), is an annual student-organised running event held in the Meadows in Edinburgh to raise money for local, national and international charities. The event has a flat, looped course through the scenic Meadows park, offering a great experience for runners of all abilities.
