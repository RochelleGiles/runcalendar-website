About

The Trail Des Dents Du Midi is an authentic and unique running event that has been taking place for over 50 years. The iconic loop allows participants to discover the mountains at their own pace, with an atmosphere filled with encouragement, smiles, and the simple pleasure of sharing the journey. This is not about setting records, but rather savoring the route, meeting others, and enjoying the beautiful Valais region.
