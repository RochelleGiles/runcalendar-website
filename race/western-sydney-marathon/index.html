Course

Held at the Sydney International Regatta Centre at Castlereagh near Penrith, on a course described as fast and flat around the Western Sydney Lakes. The route mixes paved sections with trail. Marathon, half marathon, 10km, 5km and a 2km fun run all start and finish at the regatta centre, with the first start at 7:00am.
