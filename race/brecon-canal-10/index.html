About

Explore the stunning Brecon Beacons National Park on the Brecon Canal 10, a 10-mile run along a relatively flat course showcasing the area's spectacular scenery. This scenic event offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the Welsh countryside, making it a must-do for those seeking a picturesque and challenging race.
