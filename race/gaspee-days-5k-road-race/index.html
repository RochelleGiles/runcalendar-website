Course

A 5K on closed roads starting on Broad Street at Chiswick Road in Cranston, running south into Warwick along Narragansett Parkway, turning at General Hawkins Drive and heading back north to finish just past the Gaspee Days parade reviewing stand at Rhodes Place. Capped at 2,000 runners with a strict one-hour cutoff and no race-day entry.

About

The Gaspee Days 5K Road Race is part of Rhode Island's annual Gaspee Days celebration in Cranston, commemorating the 1772 burning of the British schooner HMS Gaspee. Runners race through Pawtuxet Village on the weekend of the related parade, making it one of the state's longest-running community 5K traditions.
