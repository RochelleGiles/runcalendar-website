Course

An overnight solo or pairs ultra run from sunset to sunrise, starting and finishing at Goathland Village Hall in the North York Moors. The route crosses good tracks, open moorland and forestry, visiting trig pillars, cairns and crosses as checkpoints. Three refreshment stations; map, compass and headtorch are mandatory.

About

A unique dusk till dawn trail run covering approximately 40 miles in a circular route through the stunning North York Moors National Park. Runners will experience a magical nighttime journey starting and finishing in the picturesque village of Goathland, famously known for its appearances in the TV series Heartbeat and Harry Potter films.
