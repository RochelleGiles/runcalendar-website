Course

A looped ultra hosted by Gulf Winds Track Club each December, run on a 10K loop leaving the grounds of Wakulla Springs State Park and taking in a section of Highway 61, the Wakulla Springs Road. The 50K is USATF certified. Runners repeat the loop to make up their chosen distance.

About

The Tallahassee Ultra Distance Classic is an annual ultrarunning event in Tallahassee, Florida, hosted by the Gulf Winds Track Club each December. Runners choose marathon, 50K or 50 Mile distances in Florida's capital city.
