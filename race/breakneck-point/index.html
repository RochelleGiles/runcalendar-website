Course

Both distances start and finish at University Settlement Camp in Beacon, running jeep trails, historic estate paths and single track that ranges from open to highly technical, including the steep and exposed Breakneck Point climb. The half covers 14.5 miles with about 4,800 feet of climbing and the full 26.4 miles with about 9,000 feet. Aid is at Cold Spring for both, plus two further stops on the full.

About

Breakneck Point provides more vert per mile and technical pitches than all but a handful of courses in the country. It covers jeep trails, single-track, historic estate paths, and winding single-track both open and technical. It features several profile climbs and descents with significant elevation gain and loss, and ranks as the toughest trail marathon in northeast.
