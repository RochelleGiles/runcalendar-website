Course

Evening out-and-back on the paved tram road into Sabino Canyon, Tucson, starting and finishing at 5700 N Sabino Canyon Road. The 7.4-mile run climbs 3.7 miles into the canyon then returns on the same road; a 2-mile fun run turns at one mile. Aid stations with water sit at miles 1.7, 3.4 and 5.1 on the long course, none on the short one. Starts are 5:30pm and 5:40pm.

About

Come join us for one of the most iconic, historic and oldest races in Southern Arizona! Sabino Canyon is located in the Coronado National Forest, is one of the most beautiful desert canyons in the American southwest and a true jewel of the Tucson community. This unique 7.4 mile race is designed to capitalize on the majestic nature of Sabino Canyon to provide a unique opportunity to experience the challenges of a great race in a truly magnificent setting.
