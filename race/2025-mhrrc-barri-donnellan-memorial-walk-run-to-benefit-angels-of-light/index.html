Course

Run in the Village of Millbrook, heading out toward the new Bennett Park. The programme is a 5K run, a 2 mile walk and a half-mile kids fun run, with virtual options for the 5K and 2 mile.

About

Memorial walk/run to celebrate Barri Donnellan's life. All race proceeds will be donated to Angels of Light (501c3) who's mission is to assist children and families living in the Hudson Valley with life threatening illnesses.
