Course

A two-day road weekend in Cambridge: the 5K goes first on the Saturday at 10:00 am, a quick out-and-back along the Charles River starting and finishing at CambridgeSide with Boston skyline views. The half marathon follows on Sunday at 7:00 am, and a Challenge entry covers both.

About

The Cambridge Half Marathon and 5K start and finish at the CambridgeSide shopping center, following a flat, USATF-certified route along Memorial Drive. The race raises funds for local causes including Cambridge Camping and the City of Cambridge Scholarship Fund.
