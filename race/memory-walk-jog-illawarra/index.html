Course

Starts and finishes at Lang Park in the Illawarra, with the start archway on grass before joining the path. The course is fully paved for all distances: a 2.5 km walk, a 6 km walk or jog, and a 9 km timed run.

About

The Memory Walk & Jog: Illawarra is a community event that brings together people to raise awareness and funds for dementia research and support services. The scenic course offers both a walking and jogging option, allowing participants of all fitness levels to enjoy the beautiful Illawarra region and contribute to a meaningful cause.
