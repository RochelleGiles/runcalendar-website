Course

A weighted ruck march at GEODIS Park, 501 Benton Ave, Nashville, on a route of roughly 0.75 mile. Entrants can go solo, in relay teams of two to five or more, or honorary without weight. The event runs rain or shine.

About

Music City Ruck takes place inside Nissan Stadium in Nashville, with a course of about one mile in length. Participants ruck rain or shine as a demonstration of patriotism, camaraderie, resilience and community, going beyond a typical walk or ruck march.
