About

The Funchal Marathon on the stunning island of Madeira offers runners a challenging yet breathtaking course through the historic city of Funchal. Weaving past lush gardens, dramatic cliffs, and the sparkling Atlantic Ocean, this event showcases the natural beauty of this Portuguese paradise. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a first-time racer, the Funchal Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
