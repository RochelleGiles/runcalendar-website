About

Bainbridge Island's premiere spring running event! Runners and walkers enjoy a 10K and 5K through scenic wooded course from Battle Point Park, along the Forest to Sky Trail, and looping among the towering firs of the Grand Forest. Plus a special KIDS FUN RUN within Battle Point Park!
