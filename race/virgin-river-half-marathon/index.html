Course

Starts at the Dixie Convention Center in St. George and runs on paved city trails winding along the Virgin River. The half marathon sets off at 9:00am, the 5K at 9:15am and the 10K at 9:20am, with a fun run later in the morning. Aid stations every two to three miles carry water and Gatorade, with gel at selected stops.

About

The Virgin River Half Marathon follows a paved riverside trail through St. George, Utah's red-rock desert setting along the Virgin River corridor. Half marathon, 10K and 5K distances are offered as part of the St. George Races series.
