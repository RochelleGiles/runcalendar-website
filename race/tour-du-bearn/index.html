About

The Tour du Béarn is a scenic running event that takes participants through the picturesque landscapes of the Béarn region in southwestern France. Runners can expect to be captivated by the rolling hills, charming villages, and stunning mountain vistas as they challenge themselves on this unique course. This event offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
