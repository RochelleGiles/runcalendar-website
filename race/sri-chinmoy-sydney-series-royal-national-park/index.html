Course

Trail races over marathon, half marathon, 10km and 5km, all starting and finishing at Fig Tree Flat (also known as Willow Tree Flat), past Audley in Royal National Park. The courses run on park trails through forest. The marathon starts at 6am and the other distances from 7am. Parking is at the venue, with a shuttle available from Loftus Station.

About

Race 4 of the Sri Chinmoy Sydney Series is a trail event in Royal National Park offering marathon, half marathon, 10km and 5km distances. All races start and finish at Fig Tree Flat near Audley, with the marathon starting at 6am and the shorter distances from 7am. Entries are taken online or on the day.
