About

BK Trail is a mountain running event organized by BAZTANDARRAK, a running club based in Bidarray. The event features multiple challenging mountain courses through the beautiful Basque Country landscapes, offering participants stunning views and demanding elevation gains. The race has built a strong reputation beyond the local borders and emphasizes friendship and conviviality.
