About

Discover the picturesque town of Saint Savinien as you take on the Explor'Run challenge. This unique running event offers a captivating route that winds through the historic streets and scenic countryside, showcasing the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a newcomer to the sport, the Explor'Run Saint Savinien promises an unforgettable experience for all.
