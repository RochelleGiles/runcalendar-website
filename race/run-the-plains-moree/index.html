Course

The Saturday morning races follow the pathways along the Mehi River through the heart of Moree, starting and finishing at Lyle Houlahan Park. The route is predominantly concrete path apart from about 500m of fine gravel on Victoria Terrace. Water, electrolytes, gels, first aid and toilets are provided on course. The 1km and 200m dashes run on the Friday evening.

About

Run the Plains is Moree's community running event on the Gwydir River plains, with courses from 3km to a half marathon plus a 36.1km Triple challenge. Moree is well known across outback NSW for its natural artesian bore baths.
