About

The Last Chance BQ.2 Marathon is a Boston Marathon qualifier held in early September at Fabyan Forest Preserve West in Geneva, IL, featuring a flat, fast, USATF-certified 3.05-mile loop course designed for achieving qualifying times. It supports 300 runners with pace groups targeting 3 minutes under BQ standards, elite aid stations, and a 4:30 time limit.
