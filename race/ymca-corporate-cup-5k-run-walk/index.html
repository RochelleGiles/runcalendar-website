About

The YMCA Corporate Cup is a 5K run/walk event promoting workplace wellness and camaraderie among colleagues, held near Downtown Baton Rouge across from the Cane’s River Center on S. River Road. It features a 3.1 mile course, corporate divisions, fun team and individual options, awards, chip timing, jambalaya cook-off, DJ, and post-race festivities.
