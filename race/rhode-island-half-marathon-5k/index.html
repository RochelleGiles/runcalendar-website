Course

Half marathon and 5K starting and finishing at Pierce Memorial Stadium in East Providence, both USATF-certified and run largely on the sealed East Bay Bike Path with views over Narragansett Bay and the Providence skyline. The half is capped at 2,000 runners and the 5K at 1,500, with a 3.5-hour limit on the half.

About

The Rhode Island Half Marathon & 5K is part of the Rhode Races event series, held in East Providence, Rhode Island. Runners choose between the half marathon and 5K distances, both starting and finishing in the same area of the city.
