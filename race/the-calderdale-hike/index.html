About

Set in the beautiful high Pennine valleys of Calderdale, the routes run over tracks across the surrounding moors with stunning views of the area with some steep ascents and descents. As some of the terrain is harsh, the organising committee recommend that navigation skills are required, especially if the weather is inclement.
