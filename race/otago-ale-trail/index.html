Course

Two point-to-point runs on the crushed-gravel Otago Central Rail Trail. The half marathon starts at Wedderburn and finishes at Waipiata; the 8 km sprint joins the trail at Ranfurly, on the corner of Stuart Road. Grades are gentle throughout, following the old railway formation across the Maniototo.

About

The Otago Ale Trail includes a half marathon and 8km run/walk on the Otago Central Rail Trail. The half marathon runs point-to-point from the rural township of Wedderburn to Waipiata, while the 8km sprint run or walk joins the scenic trail at Ranfurly. All proceeds go directly to the Otago and Southland Rescue Helicopter.
