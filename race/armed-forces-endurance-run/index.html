About

Armed Forces Endurance Run is a timed ultra at Bixler Lake Park in Kendallville, Indiana, north of Fort Wayne, raising support for veterans. Runners lap a three-mile loop around the lake for 6, 12 or 24 hours, and a four-person relay runs alongside the 24-hour event.
