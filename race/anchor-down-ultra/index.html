Course

Timed lap race on a 2.45-mile loop entirely inside Colt State Park, Bristol, with about 70% of each lap on the waterfront along Narragansett Bay and Mill Gut. Each loop is roughly 1.5 miles sealed, 0.9 miles trail and a short grass section, mostly flat with a few rolling rises. Aid every 1.25 miles and access to personal gear at the exchange each lap.

About

An ultramarathon in Bristol, Rhode Island, run as 6, 12, and 24-hour timed events. Known as the smallest state's longest race, runners cover as much distance as possible within the time limit.
