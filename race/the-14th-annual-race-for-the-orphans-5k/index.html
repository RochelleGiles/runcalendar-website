Course

The 5K is run on a USATF-certified course in downtown Newnan and qualifies runners for the Peachtree Road Race. A one-mile fun run and walk called Mia's Mile and a Tot Trot for ages five and under are held alongside it. Route shape, surface and aid station detail are not published.

About

RACE for the Orphans is an annual 5K run and walk through downtown Newnan, Georgia, funding adoption grants for families. It typically draws several hundred entrants; the organiser publishes no route, surface or aid station detail.
