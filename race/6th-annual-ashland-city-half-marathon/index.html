Course

Fast, flat, USATF-certified road half marathon starting and finishing beside the Cumberland River at Riverbluff Park in Ashland City. Five crewed aid stations with water and Gatorade sit at miles 3.3, 4.4, 7.3, 9.3 and 11.5. A three-hour course limit applies, about 13:44 per mile.

About

The Nashville Track Club hosts the Ashland City Half-Marathon, now past its sixth running, on a fast, flat, USATF-certified course starting and finishing beside the Cumberland River at Riverbluff Park in Ashland City, Tennessee.
