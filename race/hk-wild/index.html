Course

Courses start from Tai Po Tau Playground in the north-eastern New Territories, running 19.1km, 33.7km, 51.8km and 73km on the surrounding hill trails. The Grand Slam splits 100km across the two days of the weekend, a 70km leg followed by a 30km leg.
