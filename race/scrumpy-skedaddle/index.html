Course

Starts and finishes at Almar Orchard, 1431 Duffield Rd, Flushing. The route runs country and orchard roads lined with apple trees on a mix of orchard paths and dirt roads, and passes through a working orchard, so expect fallen apples and tractor ruts. Flat throughout. Distances are 10-mile, 10K, 5K and a kids 1K.

About

A fall running event through apple orchards and country roads at Almar Orchards in Flushing, Michigan. Participants enjoy a scenic course with finisher medals, cider glasses, and an all-you-can-eat pancake breakfast with live music.
