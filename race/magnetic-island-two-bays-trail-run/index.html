Course

Point-to-point trail run starting at Magnetic Island State School in Nelly Bay and finishing at Arcadia Village Hotel, traversing the island on technical single track with steps; the 21 km includes beach sections. The route is marked with tape and branded signage. Aid stations provide hydration and snacks, but runners must carry their own bottles.

About

Explore the stunning natural beauty of Magnetic Island on the Outer Limits Adventure Two Bays Trail Run. This challenging course takes runners from one side of the island to the other, offering breathtaking ocean vistas, lush rainforest, and the chance to spot native wildlife along the way. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience in the heart of Queensland's tropical paradise.
