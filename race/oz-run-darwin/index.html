Course

A free Australia Day fun run and walk of 2.5km and 5km, both starting at the Darwin Convention Centre from 6:30am. A Zumba warm-up opens the morning, followed by a Larrakia smoking ceremony and live entertainment.

About

The Oz Run is Darwin's Australia Day community fun run, held along the Darwin Waterfront and paired with a traditional Indigenous smoking ceremony. Runners and walkers can choose the 2.5km or 5km distance.
