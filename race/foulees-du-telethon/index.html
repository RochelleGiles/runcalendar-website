About

Foulées du Téléthon is a charity trail run held in La Crèche, Deux-Sèvres, benefiting the French Télethon fundraiser. Runners choose an 8km or 4km forest loop on mostly natural trails, with the longer distance drawing the bulk of a field of roughly 100 runners.
