About

Experience the vibrant energy of the 10 Km De Fontenay, a beloved annual running event set against the charming backdrop of Fontenay-sous-Bois. Runners of all levels are invited to challenge themselves on a scenic course that winds through the city's picturesque streets and parks, offering a unique opportunity to immerse themselves in the local community and culture.
