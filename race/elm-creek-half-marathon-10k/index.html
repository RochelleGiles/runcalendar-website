About

Run with nature at the Elm Creek Half & 10K! The route takes you on a tour of a beautiful 4,900 acre park over creeks, along several lakes, prairies and in and out of the woods. Runners will likely see a variety of wildlife including eagles, deer, loons and much more. This is a great wilderness run that is only 20 minutes north of the Twin Cities.
