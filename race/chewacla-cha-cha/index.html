Course

A looped trail race in Chewacla State Park, Auburn, with the 5K running one loop and the 10K two. The course is about 90% singletrack over roots, rocks, dirt roads and stream crossings. One on-course aid station carries water, Cerasport and snacks, with more at the finish.

About

The Chewacla Cha Cha is a trail race through Chewacla State Park near Auburn, Alabama, part of the Southern Singletrack Trail Series. Runners choose a 5K or 10K on the park's wooded singletrack trails around Chewacla Lake.
