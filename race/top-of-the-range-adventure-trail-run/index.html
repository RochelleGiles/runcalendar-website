About

Explore the stunning Picnic Point trails in Toowoomba at the Top Of The Range Adventure Trail Run. This exhilarating event offers a range of distances, from a 15km challenge to a 2km kids run, catering to runners of all abilities. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the region as you take on this unique off-road adventure.
