Course

Point-to-point ultra along the waymarked Kintyre Way in Argyll, the 2027 edition running Tayinloan to Tarbert. Terrain varies from hill paths and forestry tracks to beaches and seaside rock, through bluebell woods and gorse. The route is fully waymarked with pale blue posts and markers at one-mile intervals, and relay teams share the same course.

About

The Kintyre Way Ultra challenges runners with a 35-mile mixed terrain course through the scenic Scottish Highlands. Participants will journey from the coastal village of Tayinloan to the historic town of Campbeltown, immersed in the rugged natural beauty of the Kintyre Peninsula. This event offers a unique opportunity to experience the breathtaking landscapes of the United Kingdom.
