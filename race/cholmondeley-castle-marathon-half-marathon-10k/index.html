Course

Marathon, half marathon and 10K run through the 500-acre parkland of Cholmondeley Castle in Cheshire. The routes climb Tower Hill, the highest point on the estate, for panoramic views over the Beeston and Peckforton Hills.

About

Runners enjoy the fabulous private estate at Cholmondeley Castle providing runners of all abilities with the opportunity to enjoy an off-road, scenic and stunning course. Competitors run through the beautiful estate grounds with stunning views of the Peckforton Hills, with varied terrain including track, tarmac and off-road paths.
