Course

A looped course in Greensfelder County Park near Wildwood, Missouri, in the Ozark foothills. More than 80 percent is singletrack, with terrain ranging from steep grades to rolling hills over a mix of roots and rocks. Distances run from 25K up to 100 miles, with a night 50K and a 25K ruck alongside.

About

Hosted each spring at Greensfelder County Park in Wildwood, MO, this race offers various ultramarathon distances on a looped course utilizing top-rated trails in the Ozark foothills. The scenic and technical terrain features more than 80 percent single-track with rolling hills, roots, and rocks.
