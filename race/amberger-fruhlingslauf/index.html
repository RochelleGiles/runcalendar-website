About

The Amberger Frühlingslauf is a beloved annual running event in the charming town of Amberg, offering a scenic route through the picturesque streets and surrounding countryside. Runners of all levels can experience the vibrant local spirit and stunning springtime scenery as they take on this challenging but rewarding race.
