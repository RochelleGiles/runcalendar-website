About

The Laugavegur Ultra Marathon runs the length of Iceland's famous Laugavegur hiking trail, from the geothermal highlands of Landmannalaugar to the green valley of Þórsmörk. The course crosses volcanic desert, glacial rivers and steaming hot springs over roughly 55km.
