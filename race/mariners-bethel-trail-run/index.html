About

The Mariner's Bethel Trail Run sends runners onto trails at Assawoman Wildlife Area near Bethany Beach, Delaware, with 10K and 5K options plus a shorter fun run. Now in its 20th year, it's one of the Delaware coast's longest-running trail events.
