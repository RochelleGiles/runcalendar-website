About

The Turin City Marathon is a must-run event for any seasoned runner or those looking to tackle their first full marathon. Set against the stunning backdrop of the historic city of Turin, Italy, this course winds through picturesque streets and offers a unique challenge for participants. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to experience the thrill of crossing the finish line in this vibrant city, the Turin Marathon is an unforgettable running adventure.
