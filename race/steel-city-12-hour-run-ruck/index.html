Course

A twelve-hour timed event run from 6pm to 6am on a flat 1.52-mile paved loop around Smith Park in Middletown, Ohio. A single aid station sits at the start/finish, and runners may set up their own canopy and supplies beside the loop. A ruck division carries a minimum 25lb pack.

About

The Steel City 12 Hour Run/Ruck is a timed endurance event from 6pm to 6am on a flat 1.52-mile looped paved path around Smith Park in Middletown, OH. Participants aim to complete as many loops as possible, with awards for marathon, 50K, 50 mile, and 100K distances in run or ruck categories.[1]
