Course

Starts and finishes at St Mary's Stadium on Britannia Road, Southampton, on a lapped city-street course. The 10K covers one lap and the half marathon two, with a junior race looping the stadium. Kilometre and mile markers run throughout and an event village with refreshments sits at the finish.

About

The Southampton Running Festival offers a chip timed run through Southampton Common, featuring a scenic course with woodland, grassland, ponds, wetlands, lakes and parkland in the city's central green open space.
