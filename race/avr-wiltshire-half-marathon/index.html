About

The AVR Wiltshire Half Marathon is a chip timed Road Race over a certified course starting in Edington and taking in the beautiful villages of Steeple Ashton and Great Hinton. The race HQ is located at Station Yard, Edington, Wiltshire, BA13 4NT.
