Course

Starts and finishes at Heritage Park in Corpus Christi. The half marathon and eight-mile run use a double out-and-back configuration; the shorter distances are single loops. Surfaces are pavement, crushed granite and, where unavoidable, dirt paths. Multiple aid stations along the route provide water.
