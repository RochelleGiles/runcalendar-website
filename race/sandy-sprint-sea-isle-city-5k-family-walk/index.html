About

The Sandy Sprint Sea Isle City is a 5K run and family walk hosted by the Sandy Rollman Ovarian Cancer Foundation to raise awareness and funds for ovarian cancer research. The event takes place at Excursion Park in Sea Isle City, New Jersey.
