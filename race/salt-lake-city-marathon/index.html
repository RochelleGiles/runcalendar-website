Course

Starts at the Olympic Legacy Bridge on the University of Utah campus and runs down through Salt Lake City's older neighbourhoods to finish downtown, on closed roads throughout with the Wasatch Range in view for much of the way. Marathon, half marathon, 10K, 5K and a 1K kids race share the morning.

About

The Salt Lake City Marathon is a spring road race that starts on the University of Utah campus and drops through the city's historic neighbourhoods to a downtown finish, with the Wasatch Range as a backdrop. Marathon, half marathon, 10K, 5K and kids distances are run on the same morning.
