Course

About

The Bill G. Werner Memorial 5K+ Run is a 3.3-mile road race on the New York Tech campus at Old Westbury, held in memory of Dr. William Werner, a long-serving member of the university's physical therapy faculty. Entry fees support the New York Tech doctor of physical therapy programme and its students.
