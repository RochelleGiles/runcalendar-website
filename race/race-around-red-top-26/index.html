Course

A canicross trail race on the singletrack at Red Top Mountain State Park on Lake Allatoona, run in 1-dog and 2-dog 5K classes. It bills itself as the largest 1-dog canicross race in the United States.

About

Race Around Red Top is a canicross event where participants run trails with their dogs at Red Top Mountain State Park. It offers in-person racing on March 8th and virtual participation options from home trails.
