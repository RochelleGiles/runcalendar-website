About

The Trail In Cote Rotie takes place in the scenic Coteaux d'Ampuis region. This trail running event features multiple distance options through the beautiful wine country terrain, offering participants a unique experience running through the famous Côte-Rôtie vineyards. The event includes both competitive trail races and family-friendly activities including children's races and hiking options.
