Course

Two laps around Bedfordview and Edenvale from Saheti School in Senderwood, east of Johannesburg: a tough first 10 km with several inclines, a downhill past Gillooly's Farm, and a nasty little pull in the final kilometre — twice. 21 km, 10 km and 5 km share the same suburban tarmac. An official Comrades and Two Oceans qualifier, run by the Jeppe Quondam club since 1907.

About

The Jeppe Marathon is an annual road race through Bedfordview and Johannesburg's eastern suburbs, part of the Balwin Run Series. It offers marathon, half marathon, 10km, and 5km distances for runners of every level, with proceeds supporting local running development.
