About

The Kick Cancer Pep Rally and 5K Run is an annual community fundraiser in Schertz supporting cancer research at UT Health San Antonio MD Anderson Cancer Center. Created in 2009 in honour of former councilmember Tony Wilenchik, it pairs a pep rally with a 5K and has raised more than $178,000 to date.
