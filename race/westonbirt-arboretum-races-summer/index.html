Course

Run entirely inside Westonbirt National Arboretum on solid stone forest paths, largely very flat with only a couple of small rises and falls. The routes wind through themed areas holding over 2,500 tree species, with start and finish in the dog-accessible parts of the grounds. Half marathon, 10km, 5km and a one-mile fun run are offered across the weekend.

About

The Westonbirt Arboretum Summer Races are held inside the National Arboretum near Tetbury in Gloucestershire, on flat stone forest paths winding between more than 2,500 species of tree. Runners choose a half marathon, 10km, 5km or one-mile fun run across the two-day weekend.
