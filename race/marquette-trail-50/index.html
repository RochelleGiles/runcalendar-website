Course

A technical single-track trail course in the Marquette hills with substantial climbing. Runners start with a roughly one-mile roll-out loop to spread the field, then take on an 11-mile small loop and the larger 4 Peaks Loop. The trail is marked with orange flags and ribbon. Aid stations are stocked but cupless, so runners must carry their own vessel.
