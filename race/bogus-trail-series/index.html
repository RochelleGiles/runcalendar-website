Course

A three-race summer series on the dirt trails of Bogus Basin, every race starting and finishing on the grass in front of the J.R. Simplot Lodge. Races one and three cover 4.5 miles up Deer Point Trail to Brewers Byway, down Sunshine XC and back on the Bogus Creek Loop. Race two is 3.5 miles over Bogus Creek Loop, Sunshine XC, Brewers Byway and Shindig.

About

The Bogus Trail Series is back and better than ever this summer! Held on the beautiful trails of Bogus Basin, this three week series is a challenging and exhilarating way to hit some trails with other members of the community.
