Course

A flat off-road run around the Chidham Peninsula in Chichester Harbour, an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, on quiet trails and coastal paths with views to Bosham Hoe, Itchenor, East Head and the South Downs. A short foreshore section is crossed at low tide. The 21.3km route climbs 65m and the 16.1km route 50m, starting at 9:15am and 9:30am with a 1:30pm cutoff.

About

Back by popular demand, the Chichester Harbour Trail Run is one of our most-loved events and for good reason. This stunning Chichester running event takes you around the beautiful Chidham Peninsula and along peaceful harbour-side paths, showcasing some of the most spectacular coastal views in the South.
