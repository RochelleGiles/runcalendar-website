Course

City road course through historic San Antonio. The route passes The Alamo, Houston Street, San Pedro Cultural Park, San Fernando Cathedral, the tree-lined King William district, the World Heritage Missions and the River Walk. The race starts at sunrise with a cannon blast. Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K are offered.

About

The Alamo 26.2 Marathon and 13.1 Half Marathon are scenic races through San Antonio's historic landmarks, starting with a cannon blast and musket volley at Hemisfair. Runners cross Travis' line in the sand, passing cultural icons like The Alamo and San Fernando Cathedral on a flat, fast, BQ-certified course.[1][2][4]
