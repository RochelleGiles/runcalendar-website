Course

Starts at the courthouse in the Court District, crosses the Des Moines River into the Historic East Village and circles the State Capitol complex before returning downhill. The 5-mile option extends to the Botanical Center, crossing the Women of Achievement Bridge past the World Food Prize building. Closed city streets.

About

Named as one of the best Turkey Trot's in the country, and 'Top Trot' in Iowa by Runner's World Magazine 5 years in a row, The Des Moines Turkey Trot is Iowa's largest Thanksgiving Day tradition. Join in this holiday in downtown Des Moines, or 'virtually anywhere,' for our 5K, 5 Mile, and Tot Trot events. This is indeed an event for runners, walkers and trotters!
