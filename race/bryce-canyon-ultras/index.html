Course

All distances start and finish at Lucky 7 Ranch in Hatch, Utah, running the northern half and western edge of the Paunsaugunt Plateau between 7,000 and 9,000 ft. The route mixes smooth and rugged trail through hoodoos, forest, canyon and open high country, taking in the Grandview Trail, a hoodoo amphitheatre, a Sevier River traverse and the steep Thunder Mountain descent.

About

Bryce Canyon Ultras takes runners onto forested high-desert trails near Bryce Canyon National Park, part of Aravaipa Running's Ultra Adventures series. Distances range from a half marathon to 100 miles, mostly above 7,000 feet in elevation.
