Course

A point-to-point 50K on marked Chiltern ridgeline trails, running from Berkhamsted Castle to Hampden Meadow in Wendover via Ashridge, Ivinghoe Beacon, Coombe Hill and Whiteleaf. Around 996 m of climbing, capped at 400 runners with a nine-hour limit. Several aid stations along the route.

About

Escape the everyday and challenge yourself on the Chiltern Ridge Classic 50K. This scenic ultra marathon takes runners on a stunning journey through the rolling hills and ridgeline trails of the United Kingdom's Chiltern region. With breathtaking views and a true test of endurance, this event offers an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners and ambitious trail enthusiasts alike.
