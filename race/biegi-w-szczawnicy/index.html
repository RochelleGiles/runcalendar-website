About

Nestled in the picturesque town of Szczawnica, this annual running event offers a unique challenge for athletes of all levels. Runners can expect a scenic course winding through the stunning landscapes of the Pieniny Mountains, providing a breathtaking backdrop to this highly anticipated race. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a newcomer to the sport, the Biegi W Szczawnicy event promises an unforgettable running experience.
