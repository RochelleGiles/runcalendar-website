About

The New River Marathon, Half Marathon & 5K is a scenic running event along the flat, scenic New River. The marathon course features 22.5 miles along the river, while the half marathon has 9.5 miles along the river and the 5K has 2 miles along the river. The event is known for its breathtaking scenery, including the New River, Christmas tree farms, horse farms, and the Blue Ridge Mountains.
