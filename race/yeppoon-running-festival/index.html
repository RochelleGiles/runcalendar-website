Course

A two-day coastal festival based on the Yeppoon main beach foreshore on the Capricorn Coast, with six events from a 1.6km family run up to the marathon. Courses run out along the foreshore and back, flat and sealed for most of the distance, with the longer races starting early on the Sunday.

About

The Yeppoon Running Festival returns to the stunning Yeppoon coastline, offering runners a chance to experience the beauty of this idyllic Australian town. Featuring a variety of race distances, the event promises a scenic and challenging course along the main beach foreshore, making it a must-do for both competitive and recreational runners.
