About

Explore the renowned vineyards of the Chalonaise Coast during the captivating Wine Marathon. Runners will wind through picturesque French countryside, passing historic châteaux and sampling regional wines along the way. This unique event offers an unparalleled opportunity to experience the rich culture and stunning scenery of this renowned wine-growing region.
