Course

A loop around Hauser Lake in northern Idaho, run entirely on paved road with views across the water. The 10K is USA Track & Field certified and a Bloomsday Second Seed qualifier. Walkers and dogs are welcome; the race starts at 10:00am.

About

The Hauser Lake Icebreaker Run is an annual 5K and 10K loop around Hauser Lake in northern Idaho. Walkers and dogs are welcome, with those going slower asked to start toward the back of the pack.
