About

The Joe Duffy BMW Clontarf Half Marathon and 5 mile is Ireland's flattest running event, making it a must-do for those seeking a personal best. Set against the picturesque backdrop of Clontarf, this scenic course is perfect for runners of all levels looking to challenge themselves on a fast, flat route.
