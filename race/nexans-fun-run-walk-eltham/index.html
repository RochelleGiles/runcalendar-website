Course

Off-road loops of the Lake Rotokare track, starting and finishing at Rotokare Scenic Reserve near Eltham. The 2km uses the barrier-free section of track and returns, the 5km is one loop of the lake track and the 9km is two loops. Walkers start at 9:30am and runners at 10:00am after a safety briefing.

About

The Nexans Fun Run/Walk at Eltham is part of Athletics Taranaki's summer series and is run entirely off-road at Rotokare Scenic Reserve. Runners and walkers circuit the bush-clad Lake Rotokare track over 2km, 5km or 9km, with a barrier-free option over the shortest distance.
