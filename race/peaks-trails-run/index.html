Course

Start and finish at the corner of Parker and Dunlop Streets, Dunkeld, beside the Royal Mail Hotel. The 6.4 km is out-and-back to the base of Wurgarri; the 33 km is a loop from Dunkeld; the 20 km combines private trails and the Grampians Peaks Trail. Surfaces vary from river flats and open woodland to technical loose rock, 4WD track and sand. The 50 km has six aid stations.

About

Peaks & Trails is a picturesque trail run starting and finishing in the heart of Dunkeld, Victoria. The event includes sections of the Grampians Peaks Trail and takes runners through the beautiful southern Gariwerd (Grampians) landscape, with trails on both public and private land.
