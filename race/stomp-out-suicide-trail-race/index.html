Course

Run at Withlacoochee River Park, starting and finishing at Pavilion #2. The 5K is one loop of the park's unpaved hiking trails and the 10K covers the same loop twice. The one-mile is an out-and-back mixing hiking trail with paved bike path.

About

Stomp Out Suicide is a trail run/walk at Withlacoochee River Park in Dade City, Florida, raising awareness that suicide is the second leading cause of death for ages 10 to 24. It is organized by A1 Ultra Events with Pasco County Parks, Recreation and Natural Resources and Advent Health, and includes a race shirt, snacks and a finisher award.
