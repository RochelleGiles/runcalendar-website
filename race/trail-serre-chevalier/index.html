About

Trail Serre Chevalier offers four distinct events throughout the year in the stunning Serre Chevalier valley. The events take place in both winter and summer seasons, featuring spectacular routes at the foot of the Ecrins glaciers. The races provide challenging courses in a festive and friendly atmosphere.
