Course

Starts and finishes at Miller's Landing on the Omaha riverfront (151 Freedom Park Rd). The 5K, 10K and half marathon routes run through adjoining local parks and riverfront paths. All timed distances are chip-timed with live results; a 1 mile and kids dash are untimed.

About

The Golden Harvest Half Marathon takes place in Omaha, Nebraska each September, with half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances. It's part of a two-city Golden Harvest series that also runs in Ashland, Nebraska.
