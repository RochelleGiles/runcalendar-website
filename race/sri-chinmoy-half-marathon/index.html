Course

A multi-lap course on the paved pathway around Rockland Lake: a 1.3-mile out-and-back along the south end of the lake, then four clockwise laps of 2.96 miles. Starts and finishes inside Rockland Lake State Park, Congers. USATF-certified and flat, with aid stations every mile.

About

Join us on Sunday, October 25, 2026, 8AM for the Tenth Annual Sri Chinmoy Half-Marathon at Rockland Lake State Park, featuring a scenic, "pancake flat" and fast course around the lake. Set a new PR while taking in the beautiful views!
