Course

Held entirely within Johns Island County Park, on natural, unimproved trails and surfaces throughout the park. Runners choose a 5K, a half marathon or a four-person marathon relay, with a kids dash alongside. Entry includes a shirt, a finisher medal and one adult beverage.

About

The Holy City Hustle is a Charleston County Parks and Recreation race on Johns Island, in South Carolina's Lowcountry. Runners choose a 5K, half marathon or marathon relay, with all distances starting and finishing at a county park on the island.
