Course

Off-road trail races on the shore of Roadford Lake in Devon, starting and finishing at the lake. Courses are lap-based: the half marathon runs two laps of the 10.5K loop, while the 10K, 5K, canicross and children's races run single laps. The organiser describes the terrain as 100 percent off-road.

About

By popular demand we've added an extra Roadford Lake trail race to our schedule. It's run in a different direction to our Autumn race so although it shares a lot of the same trails it feels completely diffferent. There is a 10.5K lap & a 5K lap. The half marathon is two laps of the 10K course.
