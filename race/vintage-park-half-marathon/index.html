About

Join us for Year 9 of this fast and scenic half marathon course with the best post-race food and fun anywhere. If you're not quite ready to tackle the half, we have 2-person relay teams and a 5k, as well as a 1k Kids race for the little ones. Can't make it out, but want all the swag mailed to you? There is a virtual option!
