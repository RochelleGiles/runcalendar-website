About

The Montauk Turkey Run For Fun is a Thanksgiving morning 3 and 6 mile run/walk event held annually in Montauk, NY, starting from the west side of The Circle. Organized by the East Hampton Town Recreation Department, it supports local food pantries and Meals on Wheels, with a history dating back to 1976.
