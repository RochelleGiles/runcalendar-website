Course

A 5K and one-mile starting and finishing on historic 7th Avenue in Hendersonville, running out through the 7th Avenue District and along the Oklawaha Greenway. Both courses are fairly flat and mix pavement with greenway. One water station sits before mile 1 near Sullivan Park.

About

Trick or Trail is a Halloween-themed trail 5K hosted by the City of Hendersonville, North Carolina, run alongside a shorter Spooky Sprint 1 Mile. Runners are encouraged to wear costumes for the occasion.
