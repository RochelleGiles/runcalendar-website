Course

Two laps of a 5k route on Clapham Common. The course runs over a mixture of grass and tarmac paths with inclines and declines just after the Mount Pond; the start and finish are on gravel, and grass and woodland sections can turn muddy and slippery in wet weather.

About

Race for Life's 10K loops around Clapham Common, one of south London's largest and best-known open spaces, not far from the Thames. Participants run, jog or walk at any pace, raising funds for Cancer Research UK.
