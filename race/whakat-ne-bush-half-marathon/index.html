Course

Starts and finishes at Ōhope Christian Fellowship Church, with a 9am start. The route links native bush tracks, gravel roads and sealed stretches through the hills between Whakatāne and Ōhope. All three distances share the same terrain mix and every finisher receives a medal.

About

The Whakatāne Bush Half Marathon is a trail run and walk event organised by the Whakatāne Athletic & Harrier Club. The course navigates lush bush tracks, gravel paths and sealed roads through the landscapes of Whakatāne and Ōhope.
