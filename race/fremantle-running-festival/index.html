About

The Revo Fitness Fremantle Running Festival is a road running event based at Fremantle Oval in Western Australia. It offers a 21.1km half marathon, run solo or as a two to three person relay, plus 10km, 5km and 300m events. It is organised by the West Australian Marathon Club.
