Course

Three colour-graded courses loop through Mundy Regional Park at Lesmurdie: green 4-6km, blue 9-12km and black 12-16km. The routes mix remote trail with road sections and are consistently hilly. Start and finish share a village in the park, marked by alphorns.

About

Explore the stunning Mundy Regional Park in Western Australia during the Perth Trail Summer Series' Swissmurdie event. This picturesque trail run offers three distance options, allowing runners of all abilities to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're seeking a scenic challenge or a serene outdoor adventure, Swissmurdie has something for every trail enthusiast.
