Course

A single-day point-to-point ultra of about 36 miles from Travelodge Shrewsbury Battlefield to the Talbot Hotel in Kidderminster, following the organiser's JOGLE route at a reduced daily distance. Three intermediate checkpoints sit at roughly 8.8, 17.9 and 25.4 miles, with around 2,440ft of climb. Map and compass or GPS and a head torch are required.

About

MTC Beowulf is a 36-mile single-day ultra from Shrewsbury to Kidderminster, run along the same route as Ultra Running Ltd's JOGLE event over a shorter daily distance. Three checkpoints break up the course, which climbs around 2,440ft. Runners carry a map and compass or GPS and a head torch.
