About

The Bidasoa Half Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner. Set against the scenic backdrop of the Bidasoa region, this challenging yet rewarding course takes participants on a journey through picturesque landscapes and historic towns. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply seeking an unforgettable running experience, the Bidasoa Half Marathon promises an unforgettable day on the road.
