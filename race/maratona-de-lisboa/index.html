About

The EDP Lisbon Marathon runs along the Tagus estuary from the western suburbs into the city centre, passing Belém's Jerónimos Monastery and the Discoveries Monument before finishing near the riverfront. The net-downhill course also offers 21.1K and 8K options on the same day.
