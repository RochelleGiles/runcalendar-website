About

The Snowbird Challenge is a winter lap race at Riverwest Preserve in Greenville, South Carolina, run on a 2.62-mile paved loop through a former golf course. Runners pick anything from a five-miler up to a 50K, solo or as a relay team, on a route crossing wooden bridges with waterfall and riverfront views.
