About

The MTC Black Shuck is a challenging 45-mile ultra running event that is part of the Mount to Coast End to End Challenge Series. Runners will traverse a scenic route from Bridgwater to Crediton, experiencing a unique day-long challenge that is part of the larger Myths & Legends of England Series.
