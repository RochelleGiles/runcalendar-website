About

The Dirty Socks 5K Run & Walkathon is an annual fundraising event held by Ellie's Army Foundation to support families facing critical illnesses. It takes place at Aventura Library 5 in Aventura, FL, with start times for walkers at 8:00 am and runners at 8:15 am.
