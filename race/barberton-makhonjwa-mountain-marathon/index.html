About

Barberton Makhonjwa Mountain 4-in-1 Marathon runs through the Barberton Makhonjwa Mountains, a UNESCO World Heritage site containing some of the oldest exposed rock formations on Earth. Entrants choose from 5km, 10km, 21km and 42km distances on the mountainous terrain around Barberton, Mpumalanga.
