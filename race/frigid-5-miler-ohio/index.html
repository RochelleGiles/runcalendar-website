About

On a crisp, icy morning in the heart of winter, runners gathered for the Frigid 5 Miler, a race unlike any other. Join us to remember the day you would brave the cold, test your endurance, and share the course with a most unusual participant: a yeti! The runners are joined by a playful yeti mascot bounding alongside them, waving, cheering, and occasionally darting ahead to stir up laughter.
