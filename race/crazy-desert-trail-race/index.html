About

A trail running event hosted by the San Angelo Road Lizards running club at San Angelo State Park, featuring multiple distance options from 5K to 100K with a 24-hour cutoff. One of the least-expensive ultramarathons in the US, the race is professionally-run with fully-stocked aid stations.
