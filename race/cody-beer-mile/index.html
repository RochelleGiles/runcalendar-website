Course

Four quarter-mile laps at Mentock Park in Cody, with a 12 oz beer drunk in the transition zone before each lap. Entries are solo or as a four-person relay team, with solo races starting at 6 PM and relays at 8 PM.

About

The Cody Beer Mile is a beer mile race in Cody, Wyoming: four quarter-mile laps, each preceded by a beer, organized by Run Cody WY. It's a lighthearted addition to the town's summer running calendar.
