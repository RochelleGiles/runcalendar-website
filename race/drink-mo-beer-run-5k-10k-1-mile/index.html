About

Get ready for a high-energy evening of fun, flavor, and fitness! This flat and fast run loops around a stunning lakeside course and finishes in party mode—right at the heart of our craft beer celebration. Cross the finish line and dive straight into a festival of local microbreweries, tasty bites, and live music.
