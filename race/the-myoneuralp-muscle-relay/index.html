About

The Myoneuralp Muscle Relay is a sporting and solidarity event organized for the benefit of the Telethon to support research on neuromuscular diseases. Organized by students and staff of the University of Lyon 1, the event aims to mobilize the community around this important cause and support families affected by neuromuscular diseases.
