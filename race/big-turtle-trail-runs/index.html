Course

A trail race on the Sheltowee Trace National Recreation Trail through the northern Daniel Boone National Forest, starting and finishing at the Morehead State University Recreation and Wellness Center. The 50K carries six aid stations and the 10 miler two.

About

Big Turtle Trail Runs sends runners onto Kentucky's Sheltowee Trace Trail in the Daniel Boone National Forest near Morehead, with 10-mile and 50K distances through rugged, wooded ridgeline terrain.
