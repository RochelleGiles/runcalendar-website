Course

Road race hosted by uMhlathuze Athletics Club at Empangeni in northern KwaZulu-Natal, offering 42.2km, 21.1km, 10km and a 5km family fun run. The routes run through rolling sugar-cane country with views over the dam and include several sustained hills.

About

A road race in Empangeni, in the uMhlathuze region of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, organised by uMhlathuze Athletics Club and sponsored by Tronox. Distances include 5km, 10km, 21.1km and the full marathon.
