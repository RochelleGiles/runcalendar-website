About

The Les Foulées Lunevilloises is a must-attend running event for those seeking a scenic and historic race experience. Set in the charming town of Lunéville, this annual event offers runners a chance to explore the town's picturesque streets and landmarks as they challenge themselves on the carefully curated course. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this event promises an unforgettable day of running and community.
