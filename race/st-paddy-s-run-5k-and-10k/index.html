About

The St. Paddy's Run 5K and 10K is a popular annual event held in Jacksonville, Florida. The race takes place through the beautiful Evergreen Cemetery, featuring a scenic course with stately oak and magnolia trees. Runners can expect finisher medals, moisture-wicking shirts, and overall and age-group awards.
