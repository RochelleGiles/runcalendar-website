Course

The 5K starts in the back car park of Oyster Bay Brewing Co. and runs a neighbourhood loop out along Audrey Avenue towards Shore Avenue, then through Larabee Avenue, South Street, White Street, East Main Street and McCouns Lane before returning to the brewery to finish. The kids fun run is held separately inside the car park.

About

The Oyster Bay Brewing Company & Matthew Fetzer Foundation 5K Run/Walk & Kids Fun Run is an annual event that benefits the Matthew Fetzer Foundation, a 501c3 non-profit organization that supports children with cancer. The event features a 5K run/walk and a kids fun run, with awards for the top finishers. Participants are encouraged to bring a new toy or gift card for children battling cancer.
