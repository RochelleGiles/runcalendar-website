Course

Starts at the East Cranberry Lake Picnic Area in Deception Pass State Park. The first 0.75 mile is low-traffic paved park road before turning onto mostly singletrack, often rocky and rooty, with short steep hills. The half marathon crosses the Deception Pass Bridge twice; the 10K is a loop south of the bridge. Aid at mile 4 for the 10K, and miles 5.1, 8.2 and 11.0 for the half.

About

Scenic races at Deception Pass State Park feature rugged coastline, steep cliffs, dense forests, and views of the Puget Sound. The half marathon crosses the historic Deception Pass Bridge 180 feet above the water, while both distances cover the park's most beautiful trails.
