Course

A 70km circuit of Lantau Island starting and finishing at Mui Wo, linking Ngong Ping, Tai O and Shui Hau, each of which carries a supported checkpoint. It can be run solo or as a four-stage relay of 14km, 13km, 18km and 20km.

About

Lantau 70 is a trail ultramarathon on Lantau Island, Hong Kong's largest island, starting in the ferry town of Mui Wo and crossing mountainous trails past Lantau Peak and Sunset Peak.
