Course

Five races over two days totalling 42.2 km, staged at separate venues around Warwick. Race 1 is a point-to-point half marathon from Warwick to Yangan on mixed road and dirt; Race 2 is two laps of the Morgan Park horse trials grounds; Race 3 is two laps of the Warwick Aerodrome airstrip; Race 4 is a 5 km down/5 km up trail on a steep hill; Race 5 circuits the Warwick Racecourse.

About

The Warwick Pentathrun challenges runners with a unique five-race format over two days in the beautiful shire of Warwick, just a scenic 2-hour drive southwest of Brisbane, Queensland. Featuring a mix of distances and terrain, this event offers an exciting and rewarding experience for endurance athletes seeking a true test of their multidisciplinary skills. Whether you're a seasoned pentathlete or looking to try something new, the Warwick Pentathrun promises an unforgettable running adventure.
