About

The Mattoni Karlovy Vary Running Festival is a must-experience event for runners of all levels, set against the stunning backdrop of the historic spa town of Karlovy Vary. Offering a range of distances from 5K to marathon, this festival celebrates the beauty of the Czech Republic's picturesque countryside and the thrill of competitive running.
