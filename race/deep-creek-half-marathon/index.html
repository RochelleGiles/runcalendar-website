Course

Starts at the beginning of Last Dollar Road by Telluride Airport, descends to the Deep Creek Corral, then follows mostly singletrack through aspen woods on the Deep Creek Trail to finish at Oak Street park beside the Telluride courthouse. Two aid stations; 10:30 am start with a 3:30 pm finish cutoff.

About

This spectacular trail run will start at the beginning of Last Dollar Road by the Telluride Airport. Racers will make their way down Last Dollar Road to the Deep Creek Corral. At the corral the singletrack starts and racers will enter the Aspen-filled woods lining the deep creek trail on their journey back to the Town of Telluride. The course is approximately 13 miles long, mostly singletrack, and will end back in the town of Telluride. If you enjoy running on golden paths of Fall colors in enjoyable cool temperatures, then this race is perfect for you.
