Course

Courses run on trails within and around Tarra-Bulga National Park, starting at the National Park Visitor Centre in Balook. Routes pass through cool temperate rainforest and fern gullies, including Corrigan's Suspension Bridge. Aid stations vary by distance, from none up to four water points.

About

Known for its cool temperate rainforests, giant Mountain Ash trees, beautiful fern gullies and ancient myrtle beeches, Tarra Bulga is just over 2 hours east of Melbourne, nestled in rolling hills of South Gippsland. Run over the impressive Corrigan's Suspension Bridge, which stretches through the rainforest canopy.
