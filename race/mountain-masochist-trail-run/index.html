Course

About

This 50-mile ultramarathon starts at Montebello Campground and winds through Jefferson National Forest on gravel roads and singletrack before a steep 2.9-mile descent to the finish. First held in 1985, it is one of the oldest ultra-distance trail races in the eastern United States, also offering 50K and 100K distances.
