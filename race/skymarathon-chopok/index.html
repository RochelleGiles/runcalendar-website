About

The Skymarathon Chopok is a challenging mountain running event that takes place in the stunning High Tatras region of Slovakia. Runners will experience breathtaking alpine scenery as they tackle the demanding course, which includes steep climbs and technical terrain. The event is known for its welcoming atmosphere and the sense of accomplishment that comes from conquering this unique mountain challenge.
