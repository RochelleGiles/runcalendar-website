Course

Loop courses on the grass trail system in the Kettle Moraine State Forest Nordic area, running through woods and prairie. The tread is roughly four runners wide and mostly non-technical, with rolling hills. All distances start and finish at the same trailhead; the longest option combines the three shorter loops.

About

The Golden Kettle Trail Series is a collection of incredibly organized and run trail races in the beautiful Kettle Moraine State Forest. Runners can choose from various distances on well-marked trails with aid stations, free photos, and post-race craft beer.
