Course

A three-stage multisport descent of roughly 32 km from the Mt Hutt summit at 2086 m to the Blue Pub in Methven at 300 m. A mass start on the ski slopes runs a 2 km giant-slalom ski or board leg, then 18 km of mountain bike down the rough dirt access road, finishing with a 12 km trail run over road, trail, water and mud. Total descent is about 1786 m; teams and solo entries both race.

About

Conquer the iconic Mt Hutt in New Zealand's South Island at the annual Peak to Pub challenge. This exhilarating race tests both physical and mental fortitude as runners ascend from the mountain's peak to the local pub, making it an achievable challenge for athletes of all abilities.
