Course

Loop course starting and finishing on the town square in downtown Edgefield, South Carolina. Runners leave the square onto the rail trail at Piedmont Tech and follow it around Slade Lake, then return via Highway 25, Macedonia Street, Addison Street and Buncombe Street to the square. Mixed rail-trail and road surfaces; a printed route map is handed out on race morning.

About

The ridge healthcare Foundation awards three $1500 scholarships each year to deserving students of Edgefield county, who are furthering their education in a health care field.
