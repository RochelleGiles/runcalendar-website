Course

A New Year's Eve road race starting and finishing at the ATKV Amphi Theatre in Hartenbos on the Garden Route. The 10km run and walk sets off at 06:00 and a 4km fun run at 06:15, on flat coastal tarmac. Refreshment stations sit at 2.5km, 5km, 7.5km and the finish, with water at 3.5km and 7km.

About

The Oujaarsdraffie is a unique running event held in the picturesque landscapes of South Africa. Runners can look forward to a challenging course that winds through scenic countryside, offering a refreshing escape from the year-end hustle. This event is a must for anyone seeking an exhilarating way to close out the year.
