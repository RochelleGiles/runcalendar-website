About

Les Foulées de Parçay is a running event that takes place in Parçay, France. The event offers a variety of race distances, including a 5km road race, an 11km trail run, and an 18km technical trail run with elevation gain. The event is known for its scenic countryside course, friendly atmosphere, and post-race festivities.
