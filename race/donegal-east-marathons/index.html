About

The Donegal East Marathons on 4 October 2026 includes the popular 3/4 Marathon, Half Marathon and a 12.5km Mini Marathon distance. The fast, scenic route takes in country roads along the River Finn in Ballybofey, Co. Donegal. This race is a great warm up for the Dublin Marathon.
