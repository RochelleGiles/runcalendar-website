About

The 13th Annual Lola's Lake Waconia features a scenic race around Lake Waconia with Half Marathon, 10 Mile, 5K, 10 Mile Relay, and Kids Run options starting from City Square Park. Participants enjoy a post-race party at Lola's Lake House, benefiting Gather & Grow Food Shelf.
