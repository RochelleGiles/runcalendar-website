Course

Day 3 of the three-day Grand Circle Trailfest: a 13.2-mile sand-trail race near Page, Arizona (optional +1 mile) gaining about 1,160 ft, with a 4.5-hour limit and 7:00 am start. For 2026 the day-3 race is billed as Antelope Canyon; days 1–2 race Bryce Canyon and Zion from the Kanab base.

About

A 3-day trail running festival featuring races through Bryce Canyon, Zion, and Horseshoe Bend near Page, Arizona. Day 3 includes a timed race at Horseshoe Bend with post-race exploration, yoga, entertainment, and camping.
