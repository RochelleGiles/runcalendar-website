Course

Trail runs of 5, 10 and 15 km along the caldera rim between Fira and Oia on paths and village lanes, with steep climbs and descents and the volcano's crater below the whole way. Short in distance, big in elevation.

About

Santorini Experience is Santorini's premier sports tourism event and includes running and swimming races, with the magnificent areas of Oia, Imerovigli, Fira and the volcano as a backdrop.
