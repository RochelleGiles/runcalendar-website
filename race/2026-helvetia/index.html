Course

Out-and-back road race through the rural Century Farms country of Washington County, Oregon. All three distances share the first 1.5 miles; the 5K turns first, the 10K at three miles, the half marathon carries on over rolling hills. Starts on Century Blvd by the Gordon Faber Rec Complex and finishes inside Hillsboro Ballpark.

About

The Helvetia Half Marathon, now past its 25th running, offers 5K, 10K and half marathon distances through the countryside of Washington County, Oregon. Runners consider it a popular benchmark tune-up ahead of a fall marathon, drawing families and PR-seekers alike.
