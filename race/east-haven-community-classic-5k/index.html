Course

A flat, fast certified 5K starting and finishing on East Haven Town Green, running out through residential streets and the business district past Town Hall and the historic Old Stone Church. Splits are taken at miles one and two, and an 'Almost a Mile' fun run precedes the main race.

About

This flat, fast certified 5K starts and finishes on East Haven's Town Green, looping through residential streets and the business district. Held each September during the town's Fall Festival, it benefits the Police Athletic League and includes a short kids' fun run.
