Course

Point-to-point routes of 21.8km and 16.1km starting from Alexandra Gardens in Windsor, following the Thames Path downstream past Boveney Lock and the Eton railway viaduct. The terrain is flat riverside path, signed with pink arrows and tape. Water stations and first aid are positioned on course.

About

Starting from the beautiful Alexandra Gardens in Windsor, the Thames Path Trail Run follows one of England's most scenic river routes along the Thames, offering flat, picturesque terrain perfect for runners of all levels. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply enjoying the riverside scenery, this event is a must-add to your running calendar.
