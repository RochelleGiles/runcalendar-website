Course

A last-person-standing backyard ultra on a 4.17-mile loop, with each lap set off on the hour and finished inside the hour to stay in. A separate rucking division covers a 2.6-mile route with a weighted pack. Both start and finish at the amphitheatre in New Town, 3246 Rue Royale Street, Saint Charles, with toilets and a refuelling area between laps.

About

The first annual Backyard Ultra features runners completing a 4.17-mile loop every hour on the hour in a last-person-standing format. Registration is free, with participants contributing $1 per loop to a jar where the winner takes half and the rest supports MSMDS charity.
