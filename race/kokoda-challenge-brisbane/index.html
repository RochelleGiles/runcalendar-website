About

The Kokoda Challenge is a team endurance event testing stamina, teamwork and spirit through Brisbane's D'Aguilar National Park. Teams run or walk courses of 18km, 30km or 48km on fire trails and bush tracks, evoking the spirit of the original Kokoda Track.
