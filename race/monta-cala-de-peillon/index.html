About

Monta Cala de Peillon is a trail race run in and around Peillon, a medieval village perched on a rocky outcrop above Nice in the Alpes-Maritimes. Paired with the Camin des Cabres trail, the route climbs steep paths through the hills behind the Côte d'Azur.
