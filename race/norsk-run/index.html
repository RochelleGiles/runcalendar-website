Course

Out-and-back road run through Mount Horeb as part of the Summer Frolic, over 5 miles or 2 miles. Starts on Parkway Drive beside Grundahl Park and heads south on Blue Mounds Street, the 2-mile turning near Docken Road and the 5-mile near Barton Road, both finishing back at Grundahl Park.

About

The event distance options are a 2-mile run/walk or a 5-mile run. The starting line is on Parkway Drive next to Grundahl Park. Runners and walkers will head south on Blue Mounds Street, with the 2-milers turning back near Docken Road, and the 5-milers turning back near Barton Road. The race finish line is in Grundahl Park.
