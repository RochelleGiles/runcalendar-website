Course

Start and finish at the North Oconee River Greenway Trailhead, North Oconee Access Rd, Athens. The course is run as repeated laps, with water at the finish area at the end of each lap and at the race finish. A six-hour time limit applies.

About

A spooktacular running event featuring multiple race distances with a haunted theme, designed for both competitive and leisure runners. Participants are encouraged to wear costumes for a chance to win special awards, with all finishers receiving medals.
