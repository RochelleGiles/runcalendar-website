Course

The half marathon is two laps and the 10km one lap of a road and shared-path course through Rhodes, Wentworth Point and Sydney Olympic Park, starting and finishing at Wentworth Place outside Marina Square Shopping Centre. Shorter 2km and 500m events use the same precinct. Water and Revvies hydration are provided at stations along the course.

About

The BBBRun is a road and shared-path event at Wentworth Point in Sydney, with a half marathon, 10km run and wheelchair race, 2km race walk and family fun run, and a 500m frame running race. Courses loop through Rhodes, Wentworth Point and Sydney Olympic Park, crossing the Bennelong Bridge. All distances start and finish at Wentworth Place.
