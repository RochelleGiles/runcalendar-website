Course

A 1,250 m closed street loop through the historic centre of Bolzano, run on New Year's Eve. The elite men cover eight laps for 10 km and the elite women four laps for 5 km, with an amateur 5 km over the same circuit and non-competitive and youth races of one to four laps.

About

The South Tyrol Boclassic is a must-run event for any runner seeking a scenic and challenging course through the stunning landscapes of northern Italy. Participants will wind their way through the picturesque towns and valleys of the South Tyrol region, taking in breathtaking alpine views at every turn. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a recreational runner, this iconic race promises an unforgettable experience.
