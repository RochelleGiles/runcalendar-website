Course

All distances start and finish at Alum Creek Park North on paved trails and sidewalks, staying off open roads. The 5K and 10K follow paved paths through the park and along Alum Creek; a one-mile walk and a 100-metre kids dash are also offered.

About

Gather your family and friends and join us on Saturday, September 5 (Labor Day weekend) at Alum Creek Park North in Westerville, Ohio for a family friendly event that includes a 1 mile walk, 5k walk/run, 10k walk/run and a FREE Kids Donut Dash! All participants will receive high quality swag and most importantly a freshly baked Dunkin' donut of their choosing at the finish line.
