About

Therefore International's first Annual Run Walk'N Shine presents opportunity to support international Gospel reach through church planting, Discipleship, Christian Education, Food, Clean Water and Medical Care in West Africa and beyond. Festivities include an open car show, 5K run or walk, 1/2 mile children's run walk, silent auction wood craft and more followed by a Therefore Luncheon.
