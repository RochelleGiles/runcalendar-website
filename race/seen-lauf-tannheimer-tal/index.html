About

The Tannheimer Tal's lakes run from the sports ground at Nesselwängle on the last Saturday of May: a 26.5 km loop on asphalt and gravel round the Haldensee and the Vilsalpsee at about 1,100 m with roughly 190 m of climb, a 12 km, trail runs of 25 km and 12.5 km up to the huts above the valley, a 7 km hobby run and children's runs. Organised by the valley's clubs with the mountain rescue.
