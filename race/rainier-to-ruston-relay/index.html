Course

A point-to-point team relay from the Mount Rainier foothills at Fairfax to the Ruston Way waterfront in Tacoma, split into twelve relay legs. The route follows the Foothills Trail rail-trail and is mostly downhill. The race window runs 6:00am to 7:00pm.

About

The Rainier to Ruston Relay is a scenic team relay race tracing the historic Foothills Trail corridor from the foothills of Mount Rainier to the waterfront in Tacoma, Washington. Teams of 2, 3, 4, or 6 runners cover approximately 50 miles, celebrating National Trails Day with camaraderie, endurance, and stunning Pacific Northwest views.[7][5]
