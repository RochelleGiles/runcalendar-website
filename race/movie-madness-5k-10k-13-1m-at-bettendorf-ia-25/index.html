Course

All three races start and finish at Middle Park in Bettendorf, Iowa, setting off together at 8.30am. The 5K and 10K share one out-and-back loop of 3.11 miles; the half marathon runs a separate 3.28-mile out-and-back four times. A three and a half hour course limit applies.

About

The Movie Madness 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is a themed running event held at Middle Park in Bettendorf, IA, featuring fun costumes and finisher medals for all participants. Races start at 8:30 AM with out-and-back courses along Parkway Dr, including a 3.5-hour time limit.
