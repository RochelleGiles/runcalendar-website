About

The Canalway Classic Spring Peeper is a family-friendly walk/run hosted by Friends of Stark Parks in St. Helena Park, Canal Fulton, Ohio. It offers 2-mile, 5-mile, and combined 2+5-mile distances and serves as a major fundraiser for the organization.
