About

The Bernex Trail Challenge invites runners to explore the stunning natural landscapes of the Bernex region. This challenging trail run features diverse terrain, from winding forest paths to rugged mountain ascents, offering an exhilarating adventure for experienced trail enthusiasts. With breathtaking views and a unique course, the Bernex Trail Challenge promises an unforgettable running experience.
