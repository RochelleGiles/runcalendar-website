About

Please join us for a our Ugly Sweater 5K Run/Walk! The Ugly Sweater run is a 5k run that benefits MCHS Golf Team of Lewisburg, TN. We are excited to host an in person race located at our very own Lewisburg Rec Center. While you run our 5K, you will enjoy the gorgeous scenery of our local golf course. All profits from the 5k will go to Marshall County Golf Team.
