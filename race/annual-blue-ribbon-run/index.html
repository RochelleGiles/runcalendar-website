Course

The 5K run/walk starts and finishes at the Candler Fields parking lot, 528 Prior Street, Gainesville. A free fun run sets off at 1:30pm ahead of the 5K at 2:00pm. The organiser publishes no detail on the route's shape or surface. A virtual 5K option is also offered.

About

The Annual Blue Ribbon Run is a 5K run/walk event that benefits the Edmondson Telford Child Advocacy Center, a children's advocacy center serving Hall & Dawson counties.
