Course

An out-and-back along the sand from the Old Dunsborough boat ramp to Busselton Jetty and back, run under a full moon on a low tide with a 2am start; competitors must stay within three metres of the water's edge. Entered solo over 50 km or as a two-person relay with a 25 km changeover at the jetty.

About

Explore the stunning coastline of Western Australia at the Sandman 50, a 50km running adventure from Old Dunsborough to the iconic Busselton Jetty and back. Soak in the serene ocean views and sand between your toes as you challenge yourself on this picturesque route through seaside towns and pristine beaches. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or seeking a new coastal running experience, the Sandman 50 offers an unforgettable journey along the shores of Australia's southwest.
