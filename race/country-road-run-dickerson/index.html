Course

A 5K starting and finishing near the herb garden at Calleva Farm. The route runs through the farm before an out-and-back on Martinsburg Road, passing hayfields and a large red barn, and ends on a downhill finish.

About

MCRRC's Country Road Run is a 5K along quiet rural roads around Dickerson, Maryland, farm country in northern Montgomery County near Sugarloaf Mountain, run each September by the Montgomery County Road Runners Club.
