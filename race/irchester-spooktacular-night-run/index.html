Course

After-dark run on the woodland trails and tracks of Irchester Country Park, starting on Royal Meadow and finishing on Little Meadow. The surface is mostly hard-packed track, suitable for road or trail shoes. The 2K family fun run sets off at 6pm and the 5K at 7pm; the route is fully marked and marshalled and a head torch is required.

About

A Halloween-themed night-time run through the wonderful woodland trails and tracks of Irchester Country Park. The event features both a 5k race and a 2k Family Fun Run, with the course fully marked and marshaled by costumed volunteers dressed as witches, wizards, goblins, ghosts and ghouls. Participants receive an awesome finishers medal and Halloween-themed sweet treats in this unique nighttime running experience.
