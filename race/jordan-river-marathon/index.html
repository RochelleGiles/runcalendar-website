Course

Marathon and half both start and finish at East River Front Park and run on the paved Jordan River Parkway Trail, passing wetlands, parks, golf courses, ponds and bridges along the river corridor. Bridges carry runners clear of traffic. Aid stations stock water, Gatorade, Honey Stinger products and fruit.

About

Jordan River Parkway Trail is a stunning 40 mile long trail. We have chosen some of the best miles to run on! You will run along this beautiful river for 26.2 miles taking in all of the great views it has to offer. Wetlands, parks, golf courses, ponds, bridges, this course has it all! Passing under and over bridges keeps you out of harms way offering a seamless trail experience that is hard to find on the asphalt these days.
