About

The Tralee 50K Ultra Marathon takes runners on a scenic journey through the stunning landscapes of County Kerry, Ireland. Featuring rolling hills, coastal views, and charming Irish countryside, this challenging ultra-marathon offers an unforgettable experience for endurance athletes seeking a unique running adventure. Hosted in the vibrant town of Tralee, this event is a must for any runner looking to push their limits in a breathtaking setting.
