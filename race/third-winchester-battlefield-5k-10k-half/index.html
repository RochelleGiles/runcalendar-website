About

This 5K, 10K and half marathon starts near 251 First Woods Drive in Winchester and runs scenic routes across the Third Winchester Battlefield, site of a major 1864 Civil War engagement. Organized by Bishop's Events, it also offers a virtual option for runners who cannot attend in person.
