About

A fun and festive annual event that benefits Save A Heart, an organization that raises funds to benefit patients and their families at the Michigan Congenital Heart Center at C.S. Mott Children's Hospital. Funds raised by Save A Heart support families with travel, lodging and other expenses. These funds also support clinical research.
