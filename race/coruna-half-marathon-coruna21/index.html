About

The Coruña21 Half Marathon is a premier running event in the beautiful coastal city of Coruña, Spain. Runners can look forward to a scenic course that showcases the city's stunning architecture and panoramic ocean views. This popular race attracts both local and international participants, making it a must-do event for any seasoned or aspiring half-marathon enthusiast.
