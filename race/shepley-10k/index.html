Course

A certified, accurately measured road 10K on picturesque country lanes around Shepley, starting and finishing at Shepley Cricket Club at 11:00. The anti-clockwise route has a fast start and finish with a hard hilly middle section. A children's fun run and family day follow. Field capped at 200.

About

A 10K road race through the West Yorkshire village of Shepley, part of the Huddersfield Road Series and a club championship race for Holmfirth Harriers Athletics Club, on rolling Pennine lanes near Huddersfield.
