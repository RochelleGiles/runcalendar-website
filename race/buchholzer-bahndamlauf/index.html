About

The Buchholzer Bahndamlauf is a popular running event in the town of Buchholz, Germany. The race takes place along the scenic railway embankment, offering a picturesque course for runners of all levels. With a new 5km Nordic Walking/Walking distance and a dedicated children's care service, the event caters to a diverse audience and creates a fun, family-friendly atmosphere.
