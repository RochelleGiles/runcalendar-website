Course

Point-to-point along the Tasmanian north-west coast, starting at Kings Park below The Nut at Stanley and finishing at the Wynyard Yacht Club. Four legs run through Rocky Cape National Park over singletrack, hard and soft beach, gravel forestry road, 4WD and motorbike track, rail line, rocky reef and farm tracks. Aid at Sisters Beach, Boat Harbour Beach and Table Cape Lighthouse.

About

The Gone Nuts 101 Adventure Race is a point-to-point event across the coastal landscapes of north-west Tasmania, starting near Stanley, now past its 10th running. It challenges runners with a course spanning rugged coastlines and lush forests along Tasmania's scenic terrain.
