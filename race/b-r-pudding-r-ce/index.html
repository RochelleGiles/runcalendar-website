About

Based at the iconic Thornbridge Brewery Tap Room in Bakewell, our Beer and Pudding race is the ultimate trail based treat. Both the long (13.5km) and short (7km) routes have been curated to provide a great variety of Peak District trail running for all. Taking in the picturesque Monsal Trail, surrounding woodland, the River Wye and stunning routes across Derbyshire, it's a feast for the eyes as you race your way back to Bakewell. Stick around to socialise with the Trails For Good team and other runners over an optional pint of local ale and a slice of Bakewell's finest pudding.
