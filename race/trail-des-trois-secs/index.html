About

Trail des Trois Secs takes place in La Ciotat, a coastal town in the Bouches-du-Rhône near Marseille. The event offers a 17 km trail through the surrounding hills, plus a shorter 10 km walking route, and is organized by La Ciotat's tourism office each November.
