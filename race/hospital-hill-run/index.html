About

Join us for the 53rd running of "The Kansas City Running Tradition" and experience the thrill of the Hill! Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your journey, Kansas City's oldest road race offers the perfect blend of challenge and excitement. After you've conquered the Hill, join the city's vibrant and uplifting running community in celebration at Crown Center Square. You'll discover this is more than just a race—it's an unforgettable Kansas City experience.
