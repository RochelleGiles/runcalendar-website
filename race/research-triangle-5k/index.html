About

The Research Triangle 5k is a Corporate FunRun team event held at WakeMed Soccer Park in Cary, North Carolina. Entrants run or walk a packed-gravel cross country course contained within the park, on ground that has hosted ACC championships. The start and finish sit next to the event staging area.
