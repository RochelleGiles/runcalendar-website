Course

Trail run and walk in Washoe County's Rancho San Rafael Park, Reno, starting and finishing at the Peavine Pavilion. The 5K is open to runners and walkers and the 10K to runners only; both follow park trails, with all participants required to finish within 1.5 hours.

About

Welcome to the home of the Polio Purple Pinkie Trail Run – Reno's premier run to benefit the Rotary Foundation's Polio Plus Fund, put on by the Rotary Club of Reno Midtown.
