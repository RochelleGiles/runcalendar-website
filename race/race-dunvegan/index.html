Course

All races start in the grounds of Dunvegan Castle and run out through the woodlands of the MacLeod Estate on mixed terrain. The 10k is undulating and crosses several different surfaces; the 5k, walking 5k and 2k fun run follow an easier course. Water is available at 5k for the 10k race and at the finish for everyone.

About

A 10K and 5K road race through Dunvegan on the Isle of Skye, passing Dunvegan Castle, the historic seat of Clan MacLeod on the shore of Loch Dunvegan. The route also includes a shorter fun run for families.
