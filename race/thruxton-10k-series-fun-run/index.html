Course

A fast, flat, fully closed 10k run on the Thruxton Motor Racing Circuit near Andover, covering roughly two and a half laps of the track. The start is about 1500m from the registration area and the finish is in the pit lane. The 10k route is measured and RunBritain certified; a one-lap fun run of about 4km also runs on the day.

About

Experience the thrill of running on the UK's fastest race track – the iconic Thruxton Motor Racing Circuit near Andover in Hampshire. A series of fast paced, flat and completely safe 10k events taking place in Summer evenings.
