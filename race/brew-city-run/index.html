About

The Brew City Marathon offers a flat and fast USATF certified course through Milwaukee starting at Veterans Park, with distances including marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K. Runners enjoy bratwurst, beer from Broken Bat Brewery, live music, and distance-specific finisher medals at the finish line while supporting the Hunger Task Force.
