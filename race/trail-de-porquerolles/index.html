About

Explore the stunning island of Porquerolles at the Trail Original de Porquerolles, a captivating running event that takes participants on a scenic journey through the region's natural beauty. This challenging trail run offers runners the chance to immerse themselves in the captivating landscapes of this picturesque French island, making it a must-visit event for outdoor enthusiasts and endurance athletes alike.
