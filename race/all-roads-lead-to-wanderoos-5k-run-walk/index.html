Course

The 5K starts and finishes at Wanderoos Ball Park on 70th Avenue near Amery, following the established Wanderoos 5K route. It starts at 8:00am with awards about an hour later. On-site entry opens at 7:15am on race morning.

About

The All Roads Lead to Wanderoos 5K Run/Walk is sponsored by the Wanderoos Area Community Club and local bars to support emergency needs in the Wanderoos area. It kicks off a full day of Independence Day celebrations including a parade, bean bag tournament, kids games, chicken dinner, street dance, and fireworks.
