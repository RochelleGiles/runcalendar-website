About

Let's face it, adulting is hard! Add on the extra pressures we are all facing these days, and we could all use an excuse to unwind and just be silly! Ducky Dash is that excuse! Ducky Dash is where participants run a 5K route around beautiful Lake Herrick in Wheaton, IL. There will also be a fun run/walk edition for less competitive individuals.
