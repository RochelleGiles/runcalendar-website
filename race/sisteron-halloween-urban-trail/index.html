About

Explore the haunting streets of Sisteron during the annual Halloween Urban Trail! This unique running event takes participants on a spooky journey through the historic town, with stunning views and challenging terrain that will test even the bravest of athletes. Runners can expect an unforgettable experience as they navigate the winding alleys and hidden passageways of this picturesque French destination.
