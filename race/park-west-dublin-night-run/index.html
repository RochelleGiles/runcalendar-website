About

The Park West Dublin Night Run is a unique running event that takes place in the scenic Park West area of Dublin, Ireland. Runners will have the opportunity to experience the city at night, taking in the sights and atmosphere of this vibrant neighborhood. The course offers a mix of flat and gently rolling terrain, making it suitable for runners of all levels. Whether you're a seasoned runner or a beginner, the Park West Dublin Night Run promises an unforgettable running experience.
