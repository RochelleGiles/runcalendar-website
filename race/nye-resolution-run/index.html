About

Kick off your New Year's resolutions with the annual Wild Earth NYE Resolution Run! Set against the stunning backdrop of Carrara's Alabaster Fields, this scenic trail run through the lush Queensland countryside is the perfect way to start your year on an active note. With a variety of distance options, this event caters to runners of all levels looking to challenge themselves and enjoy the great outdoors.
