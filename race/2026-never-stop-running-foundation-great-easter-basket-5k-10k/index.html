Course

The 5K is a single USATF-certified loop of Flushing Meadows Corona Park, passing the Unisphere twice and circling the Fountain of the Planets. The 10K is two laps of the same loop, crossing the finish line each time for splits. Parking is at the Queens Museum on Meridian Road.

About

Spend Easter morning running or walking with family and friends at iconic Flushing Meadows Corona Park. Choose between a 5K or 10K and receive cute swag and treats from sponsors.
