Course

Held on the Mt Coot-tha trail network west of Brisbane, starting and finishing at the Legacy Way car park. Courses are entirely off-road on smooth singletrack with rolling climbs and flowing descents; the Surefoot half marathon takes in the more technical trails while the Hightail 12 km is the easier introduction. All distances share the same trails and finish line.

About

The Mt Coot-Tha Trail Festival is a two-day celebration of trail running through Brisbane's iconic backyard. The event features 100% off-road adventures with carefully curated routes across Mt Coot-tha's stunning trails, offering everything from kids' races to a full marathon. With an electric atmosphere and positive vibes, this festival brings together the entire trail running community for an unforgettable weekend of running and celebration.
