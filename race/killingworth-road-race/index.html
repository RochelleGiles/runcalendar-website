Course

Out-and-back four-mile road course starting and finishing at the Killingworth Congregational Church on Route 81. The route passes Lavender Pond Farm before a long, steep climb up Wolf Hollow Road in the third mile. Water is available on course and at the finish, with Gatorade afterwards.

About

A certified 4-mile road race through the rural town of Killingworth, Connecticut, raising funds for local charities each year. The course follows quiet country roads through this small, wooded corner of the state.
