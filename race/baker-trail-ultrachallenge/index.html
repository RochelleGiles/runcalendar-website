Course

A 50 mile point-to-point along the Baker Trail, run solo or as a relay team of up to five. The route mixes woodland singletrack with dirt and paved country roads, waymarked by yellow blazes on trees, poles, fence posts and guard rails, and turns off the road into the woods without warning. Start is 6:30am with a 14 hour cutoff at 8:30pm.

About

The Baker Trail UltraChallenge is a 50 mile ultramarathon along Pennsylvania's Baker Trail, entered either solo or as a relay team of up to five runners. Each edition covers a different section of the trail. Runners set off at 6:30am and have fourteen hours to reach the finish.
