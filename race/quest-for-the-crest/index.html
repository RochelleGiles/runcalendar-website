Course

Three point-to-point courses in the Black Mountains around Burnsville, North Carolina. The 50K takes in four major climbs for around 12,000ft of gain; the 25K covers the last 25km of that course for about 4,800ft; the 11K is a straight climb to the summit of Mt Mitchell for 3,800ft, run with no aid stations.

About

The Quest for the Crest is a challenging point-to-point trail running event that summits Mount Mitchell, the highest point east of the Mississippi River. The course features 3 major climbs and over 12,000 feet of elevation gain.
