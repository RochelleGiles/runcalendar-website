Course

Single-track dirt trail in Bear Creek Lake Park, Lakewood, within sight of the Denver skyline. Both races start and finish at the Pelican Point picnic shelter; the 5 miler runs one lap and the 10 miler doubles it. Entry includes park admission for the day and a post-race finisher meal.

About

The Great Trailhead Foot Race is a trail running event at Bear Creek Lake Park featuring single-track and dirt trails in the shadow of Denver's skyline. Participants can race 5 miles or a double lap of 10 miles, with free beer for those 21+ and a finisher's mug for 10-mile completers.
