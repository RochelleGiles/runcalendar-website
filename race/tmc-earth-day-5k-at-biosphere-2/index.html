Course

Rolling 3.1-mile course starting on the Great Lawn at Biosphere 2, about 3,820 feet elevation. It begins on grass with a gravel stretch and a steep initial descent, passes the Biosphere 2 complex, then follows a historic dirt road section with mountain views before climbing back uphill to finish on the Great Lawn. Mostly pavement with a brief dirt section.

About

Run Tucson hosts the TMC Earth Day 5k Run/Walk on the grounds of Biosphere 2, one of Arizona's most popular tourist destinations. The event combines Earth Day celebration with a scenic run through the historic research facility's campus.
