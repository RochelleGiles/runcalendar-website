About

Nik's 7K Home Run & 1.5 Mile Walk takes place at MercyHealth Sportscore Two in Loves Park, Illinois, followed by an indoor afterparty. It supports young adults aged 18-24 battling cancer, a group that falls outside most pediatric and adult support programs.
