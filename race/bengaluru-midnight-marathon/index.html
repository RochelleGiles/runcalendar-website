About

The Bengaluru Midnight Marathon is a unique running event that takes place under the city's twinkling lights. Runners will traverse the historic streets of India's tech capital, taking in iconic landmarks as they push themselves through the night. This captivating race offers a one-of-a-kind experience for athletes seeking an unforgettable challenge.
