Course

Held in Oak Mountain State Park, starting at the cabins on Tranquility Lake. The 50 mile is one loop and the 100 mile two laps of it, on mountain singletrack through the Belcher Tract with some gentle roadway sections closed to cars. Aid stations every 5 to 7 miles with food and drink; drop bags at the cabin only.

About

A trail ultra event near Pelham, Alabama offering 25K, 50K, 50-mile and 100-mile distances on wooded singletrack trails. Held each December as part of the Southeastern Trail Series, it's one of the state's few winter ultra options for trail runners chasing longer distances.
