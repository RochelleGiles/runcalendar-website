Course

A 5K held at Portland International Raceway on the evening before Thanksgiving, routed through the lights of the Sunshine Division Winter Wonderland display. The start/finish area opens at 4:00 pm, with food and drink available to buy on site. A free kids fun run is held alongside the 5K.

About

The Hood to Coast Turkey Trot is a 5K run on Thanksgiving morning at Portland International Raceway, produced by the Hood to Coast relay organization. It's a flat, family-friendly course before the holiday meal.
