About

The Belfortrail is a running event that takes place in the Belfort region, offering four different trail races: the Belfortrail, the Girotrail, the Savoureuse, and the Collégienne. The event also includes the Illuminée, a festive running event with fun costumes and a celebration of the Belfort Citadel. The event is known for its beautiful and challenging courses, as well as the great organization and enthusiastic volunteers.
