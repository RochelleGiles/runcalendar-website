Course

Starts and finishes at Sir James Mitchell Park in South Perth, with the finish line at the Village in the park. The course follows uninterrupted paved riverside paths along the Swan and Canning Rivers and is fantastically flat - a certified road ultra rather than a trail race. Distances are 50K solo, 50K relay in duos or teams, 21K and 10K.
