Course

Starts and finishes at Amberley Museum in the South Downs, with routes of roughly 22 km, 12 km and 6 km. Trails vary from fire roads and forest paths to farm tracks, with hills and technical sections, and all routes take in part of the South Downs Way. Outposts with snacks and water serve the middle and long routes.

About

A trail running event based at Amberley Museum in West Sussex, easily reached by train from London. Runners choose between roughly 22 km, 12 km and 6 km routes across the South Downs, each taking in a stretch of the South Downs Way. The event is popular and typically sells out ahead of race day.
