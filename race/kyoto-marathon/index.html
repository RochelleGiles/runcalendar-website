About

The Kyoto Marathon is a prestigious running event held in the historic city of Kyoto, Japan. The course takes runners through the city's iconic landmarks, including the Kamo Shrines, Nijo Castle, and the Arashiyama bamboo grove, offering a unique and scenic experience. The event attracts runners from around the world, who come to experience the rich culture and beautiful scenery of Kyoto.
