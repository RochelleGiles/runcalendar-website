About

The Rodnei Ultra Odiseea is an exhilarating ultra-marathon that takes runners on a breathtaking journey through the untamed landscapes of the Rodnei Mountains. Challenging both the mind and body, this epic event invites adventurous athletes to conquer rugged trails and scenic vistas, creating an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners and aspiring endurance enthusiasts alike.
