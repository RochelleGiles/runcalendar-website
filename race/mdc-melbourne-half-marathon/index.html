Course

The half marathon follows the Yarra River and finishes in the Mother's Day Classic event village at Alexandra Gardens, Melbourne. Two start waves depart at 6:45am and 6:50am. Water and toilet stations are positioned throughout, with electrolyte drink at 9.5 km and 12.5 km. The organiser notes terrain and surfaces vary across the course.

About

Experience the inaugural Mother's Day Classic Half Marathon in the heart of vibrant Melbourne, Australia. This new running event invites participants to celebrate the special day while conquering a scenic 21.1km course through the city's iconic landmarks. Runners of all levels will enjoy the festive atmosphere and the chance to support a meaningful cause.
