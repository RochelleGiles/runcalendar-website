Course

A 17-mile loop then an 83-mile out-and-back through the Ouachita Mountains on forest roads and jeep trails with an 8-mile stretch of the Ouachita Trail, about 12,000 ft of cumulative climb. Starts 6:00 am from Hwy 324 near Perryville; 23 fully stocked aid stations; 30-hour limit and Western States qualifier.

About

The Arkansas Traveller 100 is one of the oldest 100-mile ultramarathons in the US, featuring a 17-mile loop followed by an 83-mile out-and-back through the Ouachita Mountains on forest roads, jeep trails, and the Ouachita Trail with 12,000 feet of climb. It offers 23 aid stations, Southern hospitality, and is a Western States qualifier suitable for first-time 100-milers and veterans.[1][7][9]
