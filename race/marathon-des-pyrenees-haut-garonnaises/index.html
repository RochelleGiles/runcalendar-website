About

The Marathon des Pyrénées Haut-Garonnaises is a running event that takes place in the beautiful town of Luchon, a thermal spa town in the Pyrenees mountains. The course offers stunning views of the surrounding peaks and valleys, providing a challenging yet scenic experience for runners.
