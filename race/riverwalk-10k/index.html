About

The Riverwalk 10K is a scenic 10K race starting at Rotary Park on the Riverwalk in Columbus, GA, and finishing near Oxbow Meadows. It is part of the Riverwalk Series organized by the Columbus Roadrunners club, offering awards and post-race amenities.
