About

Celebrate Mother's Day with the Moms Rock 5K & 1 Mile. This event is all about honoring moms everywhere. Mother's Day tradition in Northeastern Ohio that you can't miss! Including a 5K race, 1 Mile walk, a b.a. Sweetie Dasher Dash (50 Meters), and a unique Stroller Division!
