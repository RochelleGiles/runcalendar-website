Course

Two-track and singletrack trail in the hills above Laramie, over sandy flats, rolling hills and steep limestone climbs. The 25K finishes at the Pilot Hill summit at 8,600 feet with views across the Laramie Valley. The route is marked with neon pin flags and signage at trail junctions, and has two aid stations. A 10K was added alongside the 25K.

About

Pilot Hill Classic is billed as the oldest footrace in Wyoming, a trail race in the hills above Laramie with 10K and 25K distances hosted by the High Plains Harriers.
