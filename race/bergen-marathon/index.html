About

Discover the stunning city of Bergen as you take on the challenge of the Bergen Marathon. This iconic race winds through the picturesque streets and scenic fjords of Norway's second-largest city, offering runners a breathtaking backdrop to push their limits. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time racer, the Bergen Marathon promises an unforgettable experience that will leave you in awe of the city's natural beauty.
