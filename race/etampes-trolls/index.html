About

Étampes Trolls is a unique running event that takes place under the stars in the small village of Nesles la montagne. The 12.5km course features 390D+ of elevation gain, challenging runners to conquer the hills and embrace the troll-themed atmosphere. This event is not about time, but rather about taking in the scenic surroundings and enjoying the camaraderie of fellow participants.
