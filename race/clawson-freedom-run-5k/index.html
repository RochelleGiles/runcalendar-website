Course

Certified 5K on the wide, flat, tree-lined streets of Clawson, starting and finishing at the north entrance of Clawson City Park. One water station sits just before the two-mile mark and the route is patrolled by road guards. A 1-mile fun run is held alongside.

About

Clawson's Freedom Run 5K, first held in 1979, is a community tradition featuring an evening race through the tree-lined streets of Clawson starting and finishing at City Park. It includes a free 1-mile kids fun run on the woodchipped path in the park beforehand.
