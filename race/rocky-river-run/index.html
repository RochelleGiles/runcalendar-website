Course

A loop course starting and finishing on Quay Street on Rockhampton's heritage-listed waterfront. The 10 km crosses the Old Fitzroy River bridge, runs towards the boat ramp and racecourse, turns at 4 km, returns through Queens Park, crosses the rail bridge and circuits the Victoria Park sports precinct before finishing on Quay Street.

About

The Rocky River Run is an annual charity fun run in Rockhampton, Queensland, with 2K, 5K, 10K and half marathon options starting and finishing on Quay Street in the riverside precinct. The event doubles as a fundraiser for local organisations and welcomes run, walk and wheelchair participants.
