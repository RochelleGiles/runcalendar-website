Course

Lap racing on a flat, largely shaded paved run/bike trail at Cady Way Park, starting and finishing at a small pavilion. The 50K, marathon and half use laps of about 2.2 miles; the 5K and 10K use 1.55-mile laps. There is one aid station, at the start/finish, and two street crossings per lap.

About

The Sunshine Double is a low-key, casual running event featuring a 50K, marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K each day on a flat, shaded course. It's a great event for first-time runners, with no frills but a fun atmosphere.
