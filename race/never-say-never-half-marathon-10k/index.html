About

This race is all about YOU! Every decision we make has YOU in mind. This is a race designed for everyone from first-timers to seasoned racers. It is a picturesque course with some rolling hills, NINE water stations and six bathroom stops. Tons of volunteers and fans cheering you on along the course. And the ONLY race with a NO-Judgement-Tap-Out Van! The best half marathon and 10k this side of the Mississippi.
