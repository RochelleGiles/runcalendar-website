Course

Starts at Riverside Park, Goulburn. The organiser describes a fully pathed, undulating course surrounding the Wollondilly River. Water stations are provided on each distance: seven on the half marathon, three on the 10 km and three on the 5 km.

About

The Goulburn Half Marathon is a must-do event for runners in Australia. Set against the scenic backdrop of historic Goulburn, this challenging course winds through the city's charming streets and surrounding countryside. With a reputation for excellent organization and a lively community atmosphere, this is a race you won't want to miss.
