Course

The 5K run/walk starts and finishes at the Jewel Tea Pavilion in Barrington and is certified and chip-timed. A 1-mile family fun walk follows later in the morning. Dogs may accompany participants on course.

About

An annual 5K run/walk and 1M fun run/walk. 100% of the proceeds go towards the dogs and cats at our no-kill animal shelter. We are a 501(c)(3), and all donations are tax deductible.
