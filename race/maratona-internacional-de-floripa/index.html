Course

About

The Maratona Internacional De Floripa is a must-attend running event for athletes seeking a stunning seaside challenge. Held in the beautiful city of Floripa, this international marathon treats participants to breathtaking ocean views and a vibrant local atmosphere. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply want to experience the energy of this iconic race, the Maratona Internacional De Floripa promises an unforgettable running adventure.
