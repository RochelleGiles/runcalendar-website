Course

Point-to-point 12km climb from Tavare Park, Harrietville, to the summit of Mt Feathertop via the Bungalow Spur Track. The course is entirely on trail. In poor weather it is shortened to finish at Federation Hut at the 11km mark. No water or food is provided on course; the only water is an untreated tank at Federation Hut. Staggered self-selected starts run between 7am and 8am.

About

The 4 Peaks Bright Alpine Climb: Mt Feathertop is a challenging mountain running event that takes participants up the scenic Bungalow Spur Track to the summit of Mt Feathertop. Runners and walkers navigate a demanding 12km course with a significant 1650m vertical climb, testing their endurance and mountain skills in the beautiful Alpine region of Victoria.
