Course

A five-mile loop around Sleepy Hollow State Park, run at night from a 6pm start on the south side of the main beach car park. The route crosses prairie grass fields, hardwood forest and stands of pine, is mostly flat with about 270ft of climb, and is marked with orange flags. Conditions can be muddy, wet or icy, and entrants must carry a headlamp or torch.

About

Come out and join Michigan Trail Experience for the 4nd annual Full Moon 5. This race is a night race and will begin at 6:00PM. It will be dark at the start of the race, so a headlamp or flashlight is required. The course is a 5-mile loop around Sleepy Hollow State Park. The trail will be well marked with both reflective flags and regular flags. The park contains over 2,600 acres including a river winding its way through the woods. The course will take you through prairie grass fields, hardwood forest, and stands of pine trees. Which may or may not be covered with snow and ice.
