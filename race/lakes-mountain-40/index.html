Course

Forty fell miles from Askham Community Centre with around 3,000m of ascent, taking in Helvellyn and High Street on mountain paths, trails, tracks and the occasional stretch of tarmac. The route is unmarked and needs basic navigation, though GPS is allowed. Three roadside resupply checkpoints plus soup at the finish, a 6am start and most runners home in ten to twelve hours.

About

A 40-mile mountain ultra in the Lake District, starting from the village of Askham and climbing the high fells above Ullswater. Runners face steep, remote terrain across some of Cumbria's toughest ground.
