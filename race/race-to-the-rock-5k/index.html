About

This 5K climbs almost 900 feet from the Best View Motel in Chimney Rock Village to the base of Chimney Rock State Park, finishing with panoramic views over Hickory Nut Gorge and Lake Lure. It closes the Lake Lure Olympiad weekend and is the only day each year the park allows competitive racing on its roads.
