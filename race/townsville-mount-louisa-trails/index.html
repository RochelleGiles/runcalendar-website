About

Explore the scenic Mount Louisa Trails in Townsville, Australia at this captivating running event hosted by the Townsville Road Runners. Runners will traverse through lush greenery and challenging terrain, offering stunning views of the surrounding landscape. This event is perfect for those seeking an immersive outdoor experience and a chance to push their limits on a unique course.
