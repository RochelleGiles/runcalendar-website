Course

A flat multi-terrain route through the grounds of Elvaston Castle Country Park, on a mixture of trail, tarmac and footpath, passing the hall and the Golden Gates. The half marathon runs four laps plus a short out-and-back, the 10k two laps and the 5k one, with 20m, 10m and 5m of elevation respectively.

About

The Derby Running Festival offers a scenic multi-terrain race through the beautiful grounds of Elvaston Castle Country Park. Runners can choose from 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon distances on a flat course that winds through grade II listed grounds, past the iconic hall and famous Golden Gates. The event welcomes runners of all abilities and provides a challenging yet enjoyable running experience in a historic setting.
