Course

All distances start underneath the bridge at The Entrance and finish in Memorial Park, where bag drop is also based. Four aid stations carrying water and electrolyte sit roughly every 2km. Wave starts are used and each runner is timed individually from crossing the start line.

About

Run Fest Central Coast is a premier running event that showcases the stunning natural beauty of the Central Coast region. The course takes runners through picturesque coastal landscapes, offering breathtaking views of the Pacific Ocean. With a range of distances to choose from, this event caters to runners of all levels, creating a vibrant and inclusive atmosphere for the local community and visitors alike.
