Course

An out-and-back 5K and 10K from Boulder Reservoir, both starting at 7:20am and crossing the reservoir dam. The course is flat and fast and finishes on a slight downhill. Water and fuel stations sit at two points on the 5K and six on the 10K.

About

The Boulder Sunset 10K & 5K Run is part of BBSC's biggest endurance event of the summer. Spend your weekend racing with BBSC at the incredible and scenic Boulder Reservoir.
