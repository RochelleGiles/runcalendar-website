Course

An evening 5K and 1K at Desert Color in St. George, starting from The Shores on Akoya Pearl Street. The 1K sets off at 8:30pm and the 5K at 9:00pm, with St. George firefighters spraying runners from fire trucks along the route. One aid station about 1.8 miles in carries water, Gatorade and popsicles, and a pool party at the Desert Color Lagoon follows.

About

Fire Hose Frenzy is a themed 5K and 1K hosted by the City of St. George at The Shores in Desert Color, Utah. Local fire crews add hose-and-water elements along the course, and the race is also run as an evening 'Moonlight' edition. It's a family-friendly community event in the high desert.
