Course

Looped course on the Jericho Horse Trail at Cordesville, South Carolina, mixing typical east-coast trail with fire road. Mostly double track with some tighter singletrack, and sections in the last four miles that tend to be wet. Aid stations every 5 to 7 miles, with hot food at the main station.

About

Hosted by Palmetto Ultras, Frozen:H3 is a trail race on the Jericho Horse Trail outside Charleston, South Carolina, offering distances from 10K to 100 miles with generous cutoff times for all paces.
