Course

Out-and-back road and rail-trail courses starting and finishing at Clare Oval in the Clare Valley wine region. The 5km, 10km and half marathon all run on the sealed Riesling Trail, a former railway line, making for a flat and fast surface.

About

The Clare Valley Running Festival is SARRC's half marathon leg of the Triple Vines Series, held in the Clare Valley wine region of South Australia. Runners choose from 5km, 10km or 21.1km along the scenic Riesling Trail.
