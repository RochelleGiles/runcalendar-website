About

Explore the spooky trails of the Trail De La Nuit D'halloween in an unforgettable nighttime adventure. This unique running event takes participants through a mysterious forest landscape, where the only illumination comes from the glow of jack-o'-lanterns and the stars above. Whether you're looking for a thrilling challenge or simply a chance to experience the magic of Halloween, this trail race promises an unforgettable experience.
