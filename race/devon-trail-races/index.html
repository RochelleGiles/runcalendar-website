Course

Trail races based at Roadford Lake, Broadwoodwidger, in west Devon. The 10K is a single loop and the half marathon covers two laps of the same course, over trails with short sharp climbs and mud underfoot. Canicross waves run at both distances on staggered starts. Chip timed, with first aid on site and caterers serving hot food at the finish.

About

Explore the stunning trails around Roadford Lake in South West Devon at the Devon Trail Races. This scenic event offers runners of all levels the chance to experience the natural beauty of the region's picturesque countryside. Whether you're looking for a challenging ultra or a scenic 10K, the Devon Trail Races have something for everyone.
