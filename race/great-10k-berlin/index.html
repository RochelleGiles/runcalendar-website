About

The Great 10k Berlin is a popular running event held in the heart of the German capital. The scenic course takes runners past iconic landmarks like the Schloss Charlottenburg, offering a unique experience for both elite athletes and recreational runners. The event is known for its vibrant atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators, making it a must-do for any runner visiting Berlin.
