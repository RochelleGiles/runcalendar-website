Course

A two-mile run/walk held around the 120-acre Southern Door School campus at Brussels, Wisconsin. The route crosses compact gravel, wood chips and blacktop as it passes through woods and open fields.

About

Southern Door People Involved in Education (PIE) presents the Southern Door Trail Trot Run/Walk sponsored by the Door County Medical Center and the Southern Door Business Association. All proceeds will benefit the students and staff in the Southern Door School District.
