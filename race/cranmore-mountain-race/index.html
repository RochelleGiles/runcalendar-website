Course

A lapped mountain course starting and finishing at the Arlberg Base Lodge at Cranmore Mountain Resort, climbing and descending the mountain on grassy ski slopes, mountain-bike single track and gravel service roads. The 10K covers two laps, the 5K one. An aid station sits at the start/finish.

About

The Cranmore Mountain Race climbs the trails of Cranmore Mountain Resort in North Conway, New Hampshire. Runners choose the 5K or 10K distance up the ski area's slopes and access roads, with the White Mountains as a backdrop.
