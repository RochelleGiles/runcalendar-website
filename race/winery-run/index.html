Course

Starts and finishes at the Cricket Pavilion at Wandin Estate in Lovedale, running out through the rolling vineyards of the Hunter Valley about two hours north of Sydney. A winter event on vineyard tracks and country roads, with distances from a 5km wander up to a full marathon plus a kids' event.

About

The Winery Run invites runners to explore the picturesque Hunter Valley, with a course winding through the lush vineyards of Wandin Estate in Lovedale, NSW. This unique event offers the chance to combine a challenging run with the opportunity to sample the region's renowned wines and local cuisine. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Winery Run promises an unforgettable experience set against the stunning backdrop of Australia's premier wine country.
