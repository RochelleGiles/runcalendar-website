About

Bring the whole family and join us for the Move Your Mutt 5k Run/Walk! This event raises money for the Eaton County Dog Program. This program supports the kids involved in the Dog Project for the Eaton County Fair. We supply the opportunity for the kids to explore what kind of dog fits into their family's lifestyle, and enables them to become an excellent trainer and caretaker of their dog. Dogs are welcome at this event.
