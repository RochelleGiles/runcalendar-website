Course

Nearly all on good single-track in a large, winding, clockwise loop at Wyandotte County Lake Park, starting and finishing at Shelter 16. Some rocks and roots but not overly technical; the only non-singletrack section crosses the lake dam. The 12K runs a shortened loop, the 25K one loop and the 50K two.

About

A trail ultra in Kansas City, Kansas, hosted by the Wyco Wolfpack running club, with 12K, 25K, and 50K distances looping through the wooded trails of Wyandotte County Lake Park, one of the area's largest natural spaces.
