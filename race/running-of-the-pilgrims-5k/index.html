Course

Thanksgiving morning 5K starting at Plymouth Rock on Water Street, in the DCR Pilgrim Memorial State Park at Plymouth, Massachusetts. The race sets off at 7:30am on sealed waterfront roads, with separate youth, student, adult and senior entry categories.

About

The Running of the Pilgrims 5K is a Thanksgiving-week tradition in Plymouth, Massachusetts, the historic landing site of the Pilgrims. The race is held each November, drawing runners from across the South Shore.
