About

The Brisco Gorseinon 10K & 2K is an epic road race that takes runners through the charming town of Gorseinon, Swansea. This scenic event raises funds to support the work of Morgan's Army and other important local causes, making it a meaningful and rewarding experience for runners of all levels.
