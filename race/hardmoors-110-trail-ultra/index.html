Course

A single-stage 110-mile ultra following the Cleveland Way National Trail around the North York Moors and the Cleveland Heritage coast. From Filey the route runs the coastline through Scarborough, Robin Hood's Bay and Whitby before turning inland over the Cleveland Hills and the moors to finish at Helmsley, with roughly 6,000m of climbing. Eleven intermediate checkpoints, staged cutoffs and a 36-hour limit.

About

The Hardmoors 110 Ultramarathon follows the dramatic Cleveland Way National Trail encircling the North York Moors and the Cleveland Heritage coastline. Competitors will have a time limit of 36 hours to complete the 110 mile single stage trail race, visiting seaside resorts, historic towns, and the beautiful Cleveland Hills and North York Moors.
