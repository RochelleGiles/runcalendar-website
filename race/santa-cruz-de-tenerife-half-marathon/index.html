About

The Santa Cruz de Tenerife Half Marathon offers runners a stunning tour of the historic city of Santa Cruz, capital of the Canary Island of Tenerife. Participants will wind through vibrant streets and along the scenic coastline, taking in breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean and the iconic Mount Teide volcano. This race is a must-do for any runner seeking an unforgettable destination event.
