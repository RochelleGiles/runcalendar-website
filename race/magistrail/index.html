About

The Magis'trail is a challenging and authentic winter trail running event in Luynes, France. The event offers two distances, the Magis'trail (22km/450m+) and the Trail des Vallées (14km/270m+), with respective start times of 8:45 am and 9:30 am. The courses have been largely redesigned, extending further into the trails and woods of the Luynes and Saint Etienne de Chigny communes.
