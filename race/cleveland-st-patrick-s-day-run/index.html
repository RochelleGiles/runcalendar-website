Course

A flat 5K through downtown Cleveland, starting in the Historic Warehouse District and crossing the Detroit-Superior Bridge into Ohio City. The only climbing on the otherwise level city-street course comes over the bridge. Chip-timed, with a 2pm start, and holder of the Guinness World Record for the largest kilt race.

About

Get ready to paint the town green in downtown Cleveland! Join us for this festive, Irish-themed 5K chip-timed race, proudly recognized as the Guinness World Records Largest Kilt Race! Celebrate St. Patrick's Day with a 2PM start — kilts, costumes, and plenty of Irish spirit encouraged!
