Course

A trail half marathon starting and finishing at West End Fields on Penistone Hill, Haworth, running through Bronte Country between Haworth and Hebden Bridge. A challenging out-and-back 10K trail run is held alongside it. Feed stations are placed along the course and all finishers get pie and peas plus a memento. 09:00 start.

About

A half marathon through Brontë country around Haworth and Oxenhope in West Yorkshire, raising funds for Sue Ryder. The route climbs the steep Pennine hills that inspired the Brontë sisters' novels.
