Course

A flat out-and-back along Mission Bay, starting and finishing at Tecolote Shores on the grass beside the Mission Bay Playground. All distances run on paved bayside paths and roads. Aid stations appear roughly every two miles, the first at mile 1.5 with water only and the rest carrying electrolyte drink, gels and bars.

About

Women's running event featuring a flat and fast course around Mission Bay with beautiful bay views, ideal for walkers and runners. Includes half marathon, 10K, 5K, youth dash, and virtual options starting and finishing at Tecolote Shores.
