Course

Based at Tyler Park Center for the Arts in Richboro. The course starts on the Number 1 Lane Trail beside the arts centre and continues through Tyler State Park over a mix of flat and rolling hilly terrain. The 5K is timed from 9:00am and the untimed 2-mile walk sets off at 9:05am. Strollers, wheelchairs and dogs are permitted.

About

Join the 24th annual RunWalkBark4Dreams to help make children's dreams come true! This officially-timed 5K run/walk and 2-mile walk will be held in person in Richboro, PA, and virtually across the country.
