Course

A city-centre 10K starting in Old Market Square and finishing in the grounds of Nottingham Castle. The route heads north along Mansfield Road past Nottingham Girls High School, then along Forest Road East and Church Rock Cemetery before looping the Park Estate. Water stations on course. Minimum age 15.

About

Lace up your running shoes and experience the vibrant heart of Nottingham at the Nottingham 10K. This scenic road race takes runners on a captivating tour through the city's historic streets and landmarks, offering a unique opportunity to explore the city at an exhilarating pace. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting your fitness journey, the Nottingham 10K promises an unforgettable challenge and a celebration of the running community.
