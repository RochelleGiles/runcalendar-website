Course

A point-to-point 5K from Ocean City to Somers Point, starting at the Ocean City Transportation Center at 9th Street and Haven Avenue and finishing in John F. Kennedy Park. Runners cross the Route 52 causeway in the lanes normally given over to cars, with buses shuttling entrants back to the start beforehand and home afterwards.

About

A popular point-to-point 5K race that travels from Ocean City to Somers Point along the Route 52 causeway, offering spectacular views. The course is 3.1 miles (5 kilometers) and features police-controlled roads with transportation provided between the start and finish locations.
