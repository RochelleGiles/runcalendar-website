Course

Based at Ivy Tech in Bloomington with Monroe County Parks and Rec. The 5K runs past Cook Medical to the NW YMCA and back; the 10K carries on to the Karst Park greenway before reversing; the half runs the full new greenway section out to Ellettsville and back. A 1-mile kids fun run is also offered.

About

The Greene & Schultz Hoosier Half Marathon is an annual road running event in Bloomington, Indiana, offering half marathon, 10K and 5K distances for runners of all levels through the college town.
