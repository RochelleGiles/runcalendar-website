Course

Run in the grounds of Temple Newsam, Leeds, starting and finishing on the field in front of Temple Newsam House. The route mixes grass, surfaced paths and woodland paths with varying elevations, and is wheelchair accessible only with assistance because of the inclines and grass sections.

About

Race for Life is Cancer Research UK's charity running event, open to walkers, joggers and runners with no minimum time. This Leeds edition is a 10K through the grounds of Temple Newsam, a historic Tudor-Jacobean estate on the edge of the city.
