Course

A loop around the Ruth Bascom trail network in the greater Alton Baker Park area of Eugene, crossing the Willamette River twice and finishing back at the start. The route runs on paved riverside bike paths with some narrow sections and has minimal elevation gain. Dogs are not permitted because of those narrow stretches. Race-day entry is available from 7 am.

About

Turkey Trot Eugene's 10K and 5K run along the Willamette River at Alton Baker Park on Thanksgiving morning, organized by Level 32 Racing to benefit FOOD for Lane County. It's one of Eugene's longest-running Thanksgiving traditions.
