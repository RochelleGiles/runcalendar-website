Course

A flat city loop from Carrer d'Antonio Ferrandis beside the City of Arts and Sciences: across the Ciutat de les Arts, through the wide avenues of the Ensanche and the Maritime Quarter, then along the Turia gardens to the old town and back to a finish beside the start. No hill worth the name — this is where both half-marathon world records were run.

About

The Valencia Half Marathon is a premier running event in Spain, known for its scenic course through the historic city of Valencia. Runners will experience the vibrant atmosphere and stunning architecture as they make their way along the flat, fast route. The event attracts both elite and amateur athletes, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for all participants.
