Course

Loop routes on Blefjell, mostly on footpaths with some sections across open mountain terrain where there is no path; the final two to three kilometres run on gravel road. Climbing ranges from about 150 m on the 5 km to roughly 3,150 m on the 92 km. The longer routes are left unmarked.

About

Blefjells Beste is an ultrarun event on Blefjell. It has been named this way because the course passes through some of the most beautiful places on 'Store-Ble'. The event offers various race distances to cater to runners of all levels, from a 5km 'Småtroll-løypa' to a challenging 93km 'Storetroll-løypa'. Blefjells Beste is a social and informal event, welcoming all who enjoy moving through the forests and mountains.
