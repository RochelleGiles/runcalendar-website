Course

A lapped event at Rayne on the Flitch Way, run as repeats of a 5km loop from 9:30am. Runners may change direction between laps, and completing a single loop is enough to finish. Entries span 5km, 10km, half marathon, marathon and a six-hour timed option.

About

Join us for the anniversary of our very first H&T event, 9 years ago. 5km, 10km, half, marathon and 6hr timed event on offer. Completing one loop of 5km is all you need to be a finisher and earn that bespoke H&T medal and goody bag! We've kept the option to go either way for your laps. It's always a fun way to start the new running year, so come and join us!
