Course

A flat, sealed park course at Burswood Park on the Swan River. Three options are offered: a 3km walk, a 4.5km walk or jog, and a timed 10km run, all starting and finishing in the park.

About

Memory Walk & Jog Perth is a family-friendly fitness event held in the scenic Charles Paterson Park. Join the community for a morning of fun, exercise, and fundraising to support a meaningful cause. This event is perfect for runners, joggers, and walkers of all ages and abilities.
