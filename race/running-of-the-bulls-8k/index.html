Course

An 8K road race on city streets, winding through downtown Durham, the American Tobacco Campus, Trinity Park and Old North Durham. It finishes with a lap of the warning track inside Historic Durham Athletic Park. The kids runs start at the first base line foul pole.

About

The Running of the Bulls 8K takes runners through the streets of downtown Durham, North Carolina, playing on the city's minor-league baseball team, the Durham Bulls. Hosted by Downtown Durham Inc., the race finishes in the heart of downtown.
