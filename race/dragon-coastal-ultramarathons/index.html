Course

Three point-to-point races down the Wales Coast Path, all finishing at the Norwegian Church on Cardiff Bay Barrage. The 100-mile starts at Worm's Head, Rhossili Bay, the 50-mile at Kenfig Sands and the 30-mile at Dunraven Bay, on clear non-technical trails taking in golden beaches, steep dunes, shingle and Afan Forest singletrack. Ascent is about 3,400m, 1,300m and 460m; aid stations number 12, 7 and 4.

About

The Dragon Coastal Ultramarathons are three epic ultra-trail races along the stunning Wales coast path. Runners will be challenged by the rugged terrain and breathtaking ocean views as they tackle distances from 50K to 100 miles. This unique event offers an unforgettable running adventure in the heart of the United Kingdom.
