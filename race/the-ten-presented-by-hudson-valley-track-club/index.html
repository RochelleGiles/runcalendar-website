Course

Laps of the paved lakeside paths at Rockland Lake State Park on the Hudson. The 10 mile runs three loops and the 10K two, over the same terrain, finishing near Lot 1 by the pavilion. Flat and sealed the whole way.

About

Hudson Valley Track Club proudly presents The Ten, a spring kickoff running event at scenic Rockland Lake State Park. Runners can choose between a 10-mile or a 10K distance, both set on a fast, course perfect for runners chasing spring PRs or building early season fitness.
