About

The SA Track Ultra offers a variety of ultra running challenges on a flat, simple course with aid every 400m. It's perfect for experienced ultra runners looking to test their speed, as well as those new to the ultra world who want the advantage of a clear course. Runners will enjoy the companionship of others throughout the event.
