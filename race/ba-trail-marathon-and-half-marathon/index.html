Course

Point-to-point marathon and half marathon starting at Severna Park High School, with about two miles on residential streets and the remainder on the B&A Trail, an asphalt rails-to-trails path closed to cars. Both distances have only one significant hill. Aid stations carry water and Gatorade, with gels, Vaseline and plasters on the marathon.

About

The B&A Trail Marathon and Half Marathon run along the paved Baltimore and Annapolis Trail, a former rail line connecting Severna Park toward Annapolis, Maryland. The flat, tree-lined course each spring draws runners seeking a fast marathon or half marathon time.
