About

Celebrating its 10th year, the New Rochelle Turkey Trot continues to keep community at the center of its mission! The race is a local thanksgiving tradition – bringing together friends and family to New Rochelle's Historic District. Join us for an active morning followed festivities in the runners' village. The New Rochelle Turkey Trot serves the community by keeping them active and fit through racing and by partnering with local charities as a fundraiser to help their organization financially and by spreading the word about the work they do.
