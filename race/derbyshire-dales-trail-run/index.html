Course

Three trail routes of 21.5km, 15km and 9.1km, all starting and finishing at Bakewell Showground in the Peak District. The course begins on the Monsal Trail beside the River Derwent, passes close to Chatsworth House and through the village of Edensor, then descends before climbing into Manners Wood. Fully signed, with water stations on course.

About

Experience the beauty of the Peak District National Park with the Derbyshire Dales Trail Run, offering three incredible distances: Half Marathon, 15k, and 9k. Set against the breathtaking backdrop of Bakewell Showground, this trail running event showcases the very best of the region's natural scenery.
