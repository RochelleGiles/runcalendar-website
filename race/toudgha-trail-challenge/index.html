About

Toudgha Trail Challenge offers a unique running adventure in the heart of the Toudgha Valley in southeastern Morocco. Participants will experience breathtaking landscapes, including desert tracks, steep mountain trails, and spectacular gorges, while challenging their physical and mental limits. The event combines sport, adventure, discovery, and a celebration of nature and local culture.
