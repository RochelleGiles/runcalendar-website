Course

Trail routes on the historic Baynesfield Estate near Richmond, running along the Mlazi River, through lush plantations and across rolling hills. Three courses are offered, from a 6km short route to an 18km long route with about 469m of climbing. Aid stations run from one on the short course to three on the long; no compulsory kit is required.
