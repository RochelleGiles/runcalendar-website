Course

A 5K run or walk starting and finishing at Bentley Middle School in Burton, Michigan. The route is on roads open to vehicle traffic and crosses the I-69 pedestrian crosswalk twice. As a winter race it can meet snow and ice underfoot.

About

The Burton Race Series is a volunteer‑driven lineup of three runs and walks held throughout the year in Burton, Michigan. Whether you come for fitness, camaraderie, or just a good time, the BRS makes it easy to get moving.
