About

Explore the picturesque trails of the Sainte Fare region at the annual Trail Sainte Fare running event. This scenic race takes participants through stunning natural landscapes, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for trail runners of all levels. Hosted by the local Running Club Faremoutiers, this event is a must-do for those seeking an adventurous outdoor running adventure.
