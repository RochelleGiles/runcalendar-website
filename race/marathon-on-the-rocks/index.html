Course

A point-to-point route from the Archaeological Zone at Palenque to La Chiapaneca restaurant, over gravel, asphalt and single-track jungle trail with about 1,160 m of climb. Water stations sit every 3.5 to 4.5 km and the route is marked with orange flags, kilometre signs and arrows at junctions.

About

The Lost City Marathon is Albatros Adventure Marathons' run through the Chiapas jungle at Palenque, Mexico, on gravel, asphalt and jungle trails past ancient Maya ruins. Marathon and half marathon, with international runners booking a five- or nine-day tour package around race day.
