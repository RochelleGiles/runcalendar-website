About

The Credit Union Cherry Blossom Ten Mile Run circles Washington DC's Tidal Basin and National Mall each spring, timed to the peak cherry blossom bloom. One of the country's most popular road races, the course passes the Jefferson, Lincoln and MLK memorials along the Potomac.
