About

Run to the Moon starts and finishes at the Armstrong Air and Space Museum in Wapakoneta, Ohio, the boyhood home of Neil Armstrong, timed near the anniversary of the Apollo Moon Landing. A NASA astronaut helps start the race and high-fives finishers, with Space Facts signage along the course.
