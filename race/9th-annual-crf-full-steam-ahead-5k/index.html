About

The CRF Full Steam Ahead 5K is an annual running event organized by the Cristian Rivera Foundation to raise awareness and funds for research into Diffuse Intrinsic Pontine Glioma (DIPG), a rare pediatric brain tumor. The event takes place in the Bronx, New York.
