About

La Scintillante is a running event held in the heart of Lyon, France. Participants can choose between a 5km walk/jog or a 9km run, both of which are entirely secure and accessible to all. The event aims to raise funds for cancer research at the Centre Léon Bérard, fostering a spirit of solidarity and hope for those affected by the disease.
