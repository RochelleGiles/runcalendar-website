About

The San Marcos Half Marathon offers 13.1-mile, 10K and 5K distances from Tanger Outlets in San Marcos, Texas. Formerly Moe's Better Half Marathon, it passed to ScallyWompus Events after 33 years. The route runs through prairie landscape with one notable rolling hill.
