About

Both the 3K and 8K start and finish in downtown Shelburne Falls at the historic 1890 Iron Bridge, following scenic, rolling paved roads through the village. Running since 1979, the race benefits Dial/Self Youth and Community Services and ends with free ice cream and beer for finishers.
