About

RiverSea Ultra is a point-to-point ultramarathon from private farmland on the banks of the Rakahuri (Ashley) River to South New Brighton in Christchurch. The roughly 80km route follows stop banks, single track, forest trails and hard-packed beach sand, and can be run solo or as a two or three person relay.
