Course

Loop courses on the wide, well-maintained two-track trails of Independence Oaks County Park on Sashabaw Road, north of Clarkston. The 2.3-mile is flat and fast with lakefront views around Crooked Lake; the 5K adds rolling hills through hardwood forest with bridges and lake views, and the 10K runs two laps of the 5K.

About

Experience one of Southeast Michigan's most scenic trail races! The Crooked Lake Trail Run features well-maintained paths, rolling terrain, and beautiful hardwood forests at Independence Oaks County Park (Clarkston, MI). Whether you're a seasoned runner or a first-timer, there's a perfect distance for you!
