Course

A winter trail loop of about 10.2 miles circling the Twin Mountain massif near Laramie, with roughly 2,000 ft of climbing over rock outcroppings and valleys. Conditions routinely include long stretches of thigh-deep snow and ice; snowshoes and skis are not allowed. The marathon option covers two loops, with full support at the start/finish between them and an unmanned aid station midway.

About

Twin Mountain Trudge is a High Plains Harriers winter trail race near Laramie, Wyoming, held each January with Single Loop and Double Loop marathon distances over snowy, high-altitude terrain.
