About

The Trail Castelpontin is a captivating running event that takes participants on a scenic journey through the picturesque landscapes surrounding a historic castle. Runners will have the opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region as they navigate the challenging trail course, offering a unique and unforgettable running adventure.
