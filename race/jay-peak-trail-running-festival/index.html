Course

A two-day mountain festival on the trails of Jay Peak over Labor Day weekend. Saturday runs three graded 5Ks — Black Diamond, Blue Square and Green Circle — on single track and mowed nordic trails, plus a kids race. Sunday laps the mountain as a 53.1K ultra with over 9,000ft of climbing, a 22-miler and an 11-miler, with aid roughly every three miles.
