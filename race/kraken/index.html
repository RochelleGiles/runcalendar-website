About

Welcome to the Kraken 5k and 10k! We're thrilled to have you join us for this exciting race event! Each runner will receive snacks and refreshments, a stylish t-shirt, and a FINISHERS bottle opener as a token of our appreciation for your participation. We will honor the top three male and female finishers in each age group with awards. Explore various vendor booths and enjoy live music, creating a vibrant atmosphere for all attendees.
