Course

Run on solid stone forest paths through Westonbirt National Arboretum, largely flat with only a few small rises and falls. The route is distance-marked and marshalled throughout. The Saturday races are run at night with torches required; Sunday races are in daylight. The 10K has one feast station, the 5K none.

About

The Westonbirt Arboretum Races Winter event takes place in the stunning Westonbirt Arboretum, a world-renowned tree collection located in Gloucestershire, UK. Runners can enjoy a scenic half marathon or 10km route through the beautiful autumn and winter landscapes of the arboretum, with the chance to see the vibrant changing foliage and diverse tree species.
