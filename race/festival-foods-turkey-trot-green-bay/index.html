Course

A Thanksgiving morning road race based at Johnsonville Tailgate Village at Lambeau Field, where both courses start and finish. The 5 mile sets off on Holmgren Way south of Lombardi Avenue and heads north into west Green Bay neighbourhoods past Lambeau Field; the 2 mile starts north of Mike McCarthy Way and loops south through Ashwaubenon. A dog jog runs on the 2 mile course only.

About

The Festival Foods Turkey Trot – Green Bay is a Thanksgiving Day 2-mile and 5-mile race in Green Bay, Wisconsin, part of a Festival Foods-sponsored trot series held in cities across the state each November.
