Course

An out-and-back 3.1 mile road course starting and finishing at Carson Beach and running along the shoreline on William J. Day Boulevard, with views over the harbour. A single 5K distance is offered, run on a summer evening.

About

This out-and-back 5K starts and finishes at Carson Beach in South Boston, running along William J. Day Boulevard with harbor views. The evening race is organized by the Boston Athletic Association and draws a capped field of local runners.
