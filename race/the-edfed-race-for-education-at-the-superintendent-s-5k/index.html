About

Join the Miami-Dade County Public Schools Foundation for the 11th annual run/walk and family fun day dedicated to promoting health and wellness in Miami-Dade County. Participants include children, parents and guardians, teachers and administrators representing over 200 M-DCPS schools. Proceeds from 5K race registrations benefit participating schools.
