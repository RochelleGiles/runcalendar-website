About

The Ultra Trail Shudao by UTMB is an epic trail running event spanning the millennia-old Shu Roads in northern Sichuan. The route traverses stunning geological landscapes, including Jianmen Pass, ancient cypress forests, and cloud-wrapped terraced fields, starting at Jiange Stadium and ascending to Daping Mountain's summit at 1,073 meters above sea level.
