Jackson Hole Marathon

Jackson, Wyoming · United States

Course

Three point-to-point road courses all finishing at the Teton Village Commons. The marathon starts at Jackson Town Square at 7:30 AM, the Hole Half at the Presbyterian Church of Jackson Hole at 9:00 AM and the quarter marathon at the Village Road Transit Center at 10:00 AM. The race is cupless, so runners carry their own hydration container.

Marathon42.2 km
Hole Half Marathon21.1 km
Quarter Marathon10.6 km

About

The Jackson Hole Marathon offers marathon, half marathon (the Hole Half) and quarter marathon distances through Jackson Hole, Wyoming, with views of the Teton Range along the Snake River valley.
