Course

A predominantly flat course combining public road, sealed shared pathways and the Bay Trail along the Mornington Peninsula foreshore, passing through Rosebud, McCrae, Capel Sound and Rye. Marathon, half, 10km, 5km and a 1km kids race share the route, which suits first-timers, walkers and runners chasing a fast time. The marathon is capped at 150 entries.

About

The Rosebud Running Festival is a premier running event on the Mornington Peninsula, offering a range of distances for runners of all levels. The scenic course takes participants through the picturesque coastal town of Rosebud, showcasing the region's natural beauty. With a vibrant atmosphere and a supportive community, this event is a must-do for runners looking to challenge themselves and enjoy a memorable running experience.
