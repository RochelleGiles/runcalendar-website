About

The McLaren Vale Running Festival is hosted by the South Australian Road Runners Club and starts at McLaren Vale Oval. Runners choose 5km, 10km or a 21.1km half marathon, with courses following the Shiraz Trail rail trail between McLaren Vale and Willunga and crossing several roads.
