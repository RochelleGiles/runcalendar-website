Course

A lapped endurance event on the Grey Abbey Estate in Co. Down, run over a 5.5km loop. Entrants pick a 3-hour, 6-hour or 12-hour window and complete as many loops as they can within it, all starting and finishing at the estate.

About

Nestled in the picturesque countryside of Greyabbey, Co. Down, the Grey Abbey Endurance Challenge invites runners to test their limits on the grounds of the stunning Grey Abbey Estate. This challenging event offers a unique opportunity to explore the historic estate's scenic trails and paths, providing a captivating backdrop for an unforgettable running experience.
