Course

A trail race from Bur-Mil Park in Greensboro, with the half marathon running one complete loop of Lake Brandt and the 4 mile taking a shorter section of the same trail. Aid stations are placed along the course and the field is capped at 500. Ruck and virtual entries are also offered.
