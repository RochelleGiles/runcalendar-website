Course

Lapped course starting and finishing at St Martha Catholic School in Kingwood, run entirely on public concrete greenbelt paths and passing Kingwood Lake. The marathon covers four loops, the half marathon two. Marathon starts at 8:00am, half at 8:15am.

About

The Texas Marathon takes place in Kingwood, Texas, each New Year's Day, offering marathon and half marathon distances through the Houston suburb's forested greenbelt trails.
