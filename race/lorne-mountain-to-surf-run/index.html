About

The Lorne Mountain to Surf is an 8km fun run or walk through the Otway forest ranges, following the St George River track to the Southern Ocean and returning past the Lorne Pier and cliff track to the Surf Life Saving Club. This annual event is a major fundraiser for the Lorne Surf Life Saving Club and its programs.
