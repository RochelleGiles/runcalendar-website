Course

A road course starting on North Shoreline in downtown Corpus Christi, run over a two-day weekend with the 5K on the Saturday and the longer distances on the Sunday. The route takes in views of the New Harbor Bridge, loops through Uptown and Downtown and returns along coastal miles beside Corpus Christi Bay. Organisers describe it as fast and flat with no major hills.

About

The CCTX Marathon runs through downtown Corpus Christi, Texas, along the Gulf Coast. It offers marathon, half marathon, and 5K distances each December, drawing runners from across the Coastal Bend.
