Course

All three distances start at 8:30 am at Whitehall Park in Richmond. The 5K and 10K repeat a 0.78-mile out-and-back twice and four times; the half marathon repeats a 1.09-mile out-and-back six times. A 3 hour 30 minute limit applies to every distance.
