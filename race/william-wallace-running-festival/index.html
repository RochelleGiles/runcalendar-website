Course

A four-race festival based in Stirling. The 70 km and 50 km ultras run scenic loops past the Battle of Bannockburn site, climb into the Campsie Fells and pass Stirling Castle above the River Forth. The 10 km Braveheart is a flat out-and-back beside the Forth crossing Stirling Old Bridge, and the 1 mile Wallace Warriors fun run is flat and traffic free.

About

The William Wallace Running Festival is a multi-distance event in Stirling, Scotland, with options from a 1-mile fun run to a 70km ultra. Named for the Scottish independence hero, it draws runners of all abilities to the historic city.
