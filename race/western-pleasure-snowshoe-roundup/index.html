About

The Western Pleasure Snowshoe Roundup is a snowshoe racing event hosted by Western Pleasure Guest Ranch featuring 5k and 10k races for all ages, plus an untimed Buckaroo race for children 6 and under. It offers recreational and competitive categories on scenic, challenging courses with handcrafted awards and family-friendly amenities.
