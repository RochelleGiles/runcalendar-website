Course

A trail course in Bear Brook State Park, Allenstown, mixing single track, steep climbs and open blueberry fields. Navigation is by GPX file with minimal to no course marking, so runners carry a GPS device. The race is cupless: no cups at aid points, and runners carry their own bottles or flasks.

About

This trail race takes place entirely within Bear Brook State Park, the largest state park in New Hampshire. Runners cover wooded singletrack and forest roads across the park's trail network.
