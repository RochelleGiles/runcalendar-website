Course

A trail run on the Mount Penn trail network, starting and finishing at the Reading Liederkranz club on Spook Lane. The organiser describes loads of rock, roots and downed trees, with fewer sustained climbs and technical descents than their Chilly Cheeks race. Two water stops on course and a covered finish area with food.

About

The 23rd Annual Ugly Mudder is a 12K trail run near Reading, Pennsylvania, hosted by Pretzel City Sports. Runners tackle muddy, wooded singletrack in late winter conditions true to its name, with the club marking over two decades of the event.
