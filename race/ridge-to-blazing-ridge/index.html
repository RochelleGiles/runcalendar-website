Course

Run on a 10.36-mile loop at Oak Mountain State Park near Birmingham, with several long steep climbs and creekside sections. One lap makes the 10.76-mile race and two laps the 21.12-mile race. Two aid stations serve the loop, at Peavine Falls (mile 4.83) and the Red Road water stop (mile 7.84).

About

A trail race in Pelham, Alabama offering 10-mile and 20-mile distances through wooded terrain. It's one leg of the Southeastern Trail Series, a set of trail events that draws regional trail runners across the year.
