About

The Dunboyne 10K Road Race & Fun Run is an annual and popular fixture organized by the Dunboyne Athletic Club in Ireland. Runners of all abilities can enjoy this scenic course through the charming town of Dunboyne, with a challenging route that winds through the local streets and countryside. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply seeking an enjoyable community event, the Dunboyne 10K is an excellent choice.
