Course

About

The Winter Games is a multi-distance winter running event held at Sam Houston Trails Park in Irving, Texas. Runners choose between a half marathon, 10K, 5K or an untimed mile, with a free kids dash and a virtual entry alongside. The half marathon covers two laps of the park course.
