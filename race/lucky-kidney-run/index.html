Course

Out-and-back course starting and finishing on the World's Fair Park Performance Lawn in downtown Knoxville. The route follows the 2nd Creek Greenway and the Neyland Drive greenway before returning to the park. Timed and untimed entry options are offered.

About

The Lucky Kidney Run marks National Kidney Disease Month with a 6K and a one-mile family walk on the greenways around World's Fair Park in Knoxville, Tennessee. Timed and untimed entries are offered, and awards go to top finishers and to the largest and highest-fundraising teams.
