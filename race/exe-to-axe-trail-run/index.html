Course

A point-to-point coastal trail race of 22 miles from Exmouth to Seaton along the South West Coast Path, with roughly 4,000ft of climbing. Checkpoints sit near Budleigh Salterton, Sidmouth and Branscombe. A shorter 11-mile Sid to Axe covers the second half, and the full route can also be run as a 2-4 person relay. Time limit 7 hours.

About

The Exe to Axe Trail Run follows the scenic South West Coast Path from Exmouth to Seaton, offering runners a challenging yet picturesque journey along the rugged Devon coastline. Organized by the Sidmouth Running Club, this event promises stunning ocean vistas, undulating terrain, and the chance to experience the natural beauty of the region.
