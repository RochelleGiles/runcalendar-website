Course

A 2.5-mile grass loop on the cross country course and soccer fields at 3350 E Gans Road, Columbia. Runners repeat the loop for their chosen time band. The start/finish is the only aid station, stocked with water, Tailwind and ultra food, and the event is cupless so runners carry their own containers.

About

The 24 Hour Lions Roar is a 6/12/24 hour race, with a newly added 24 Hour Tag Team Championship event, that is run on a 2.5 mile loop course that includes the D1 NCAA Cross Country Championship Course at Gans Creek Recreation Area in Columbia, MO. A portion of all proceeds from the event goes to Pascale's Pals, a local volunteer organization that benefits the children and families staying at the Children's Hospital in Columbia, MO . A runner friendly course that can challenge runners of all levels. If you enjoy running events where like minded people come together in a high energy atmosphere then you will want to work this into your calendar.
