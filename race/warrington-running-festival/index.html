Course

Starts and finishes at Bank Park on Crosfield Street in Warrington town centre, running out through the town on roads. Half marathon and 10K set off at 9:00, the 5K at 9:15 and the one-mile fun run at 11:30, with the event closing by 12:30. Every kilometre is flagged and marshals are stationed around the course.

About

The Warrington Running Festival Presented by Trek is in its fifth year. Organized by RunThrough Events, this event has grown to over 6,000 participants and features a 5K, 10K, Half Marathon, and Junior races. The event takes place in Bank Park and offers entertainment, activities, and food vendors for runners and spectators alike.
