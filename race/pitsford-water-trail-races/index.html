Course

Both races start and finish at Brixworth Country Park, crossing the dam towards Pitsford before picking up trails above Pitsford Water towards Holcot. The 10k drops down over the causeway and returns on the waterside track; the half marathon continues through Holcot, Hannington, Walgrave, Old and Scaldwell, with a number of stiles between fields, before rejoining the waterside track for the final mile. Footpaths across farmland throughout, trail shoes recommended.

About

Escape the city and explore the stunning trails around Pitsford Water in Northampton at the Pitsford Water Trail Races. This scenic event offers runners of all levels the chance to experience the tranquil beauty of the British countryside, with routes winding through forests and along the banks of the picturesque reservoir. Whether you're looking for a challenging trail run or a scenic day out, this event has something for everyone.
