Course

A trail race at Shongweni Dam in the Valley of 1000 Hills, outer west Durban, run through red cliffs and bushveld. Courses take in rugged riverine sections and steep climbs, with a tunnel on the shorter route. Four distances are offered: 5 km, 12 km, 23 km and 40 km.

About

The 1000 Hills Challenge is a trail race through South Africa's Valley of a Thousand Hills near Cato Ridge, with 5km, 10km, 20km and 30km distances crossing the region's famously rolling hill country.
