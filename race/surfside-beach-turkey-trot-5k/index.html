Course

Starts near the Surfside Beach pier with an ocean view at the gun, then runs out along Ocean Blvd and back through the residential streets of Surfside Beach. Sealed road course, 8:00am start. A free 50-yard Gobbler Kids' Dash follows at 9:00am.

About

The Surfside Beach Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving-morning 5K hosted by the Surfside Beach Rotary Club in Surfside Beach, South Carolina, a Grand Strand town known locally as "The Family Beach."
