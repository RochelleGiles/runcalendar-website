Course

A USATF-certified 5K (GA17079WC) starting at Pemberton Place Greenspace in downtown Atlanta at 8:30 AM. It is one of the last AJC Peachtree Road Race qualifiers of the season, and a virtual entry is offered alongside the in-person race.

About

There are thousands of men, women, and children in Metro Atlanta who face the perilous conditions of winter without a roof over their heads. Your registration fee for the race will cover one night of shelter and services for someone who would otherwise spend the night in the cold, on the streets.
