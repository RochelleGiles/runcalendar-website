About

The Semi De La Nesque is a stunning semi-marathon through the breathtaking Gorges de la Nesque in the south of France. Runners will be treated to panoramic views of the rugged limestone cliffs and lush Mediterranean landscapes as they make their way through this challenging but rewarding course. This event is a must for any trail running enthusiast looking to experience the natural beauty of the region.
