About

On Your Left Fitness and Timing is partnering with the Tucson Bicycle Classic to host this unique 5k in the rolling hills of Oro Valley. The 5k starts and ends at Naranja Park. You will run the same course as the cyclists (who will start their races AFTER the 5k). Once you're done running/walking, there's beer at the finish line (21 and older). Stay and watch some of the action-packed, high-speed cycling races.
