Course

The route starts and finishes at Pi Beta Phi Elementary School on Cherokee Orchard Road and works out of Gatlinburg into the Smoky Mountains. Underfoot it mixes sealed road, gravel road and other uneven ground, with flat stretches, rolling hills and steep climbs. Marathon and half marathon distances are offered as both ruck marches and open runs, alongside a 10K and 5K.

About

The Mountain Man Memorial March honors fallen veterans through marching and running events in Gatlinburg, featuring team and individual categories with heavy and light packs. The route traverses paved roads, gravel, and uneven terrain in the Smoky Mountains with flat stretches, rolling hills, and steep climbs.
