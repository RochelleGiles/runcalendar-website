Course

A repeating 4.167-mile loop at Butterfield Trailhead Regional Park in the Oquirrh Mountains, run in 4, 8 or 12-hour formats. Runners start and finish each lap at the base, where the finish line doubles as the aid station with electrolyte drinks, snacks and food. The single-track loop is marked with pink flags, cones and signs; no poles or artificial aid are allowed.

About

Butterfield Trailhead Regional Park sits in the shadow of Kennecott Mine and the Oquirrh Mountains. This park is new and designed to give access to the new Butterfield Trail system as well as host events both big and small. We will be setup in the event parking which will provide plenty of room for Blitz Backyard runners to get comfortable and settle in for the long and fun day. We hope you come out to push yourself and have a good time with friends and family at the finish line.
