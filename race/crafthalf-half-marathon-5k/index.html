Course

Based at Foothills Brewpub on West 4th Street in downtown Winston-Salem, with the longest city block closed from Poplar to Spring Street for the finish party. Half marathon, 5K and a timed one-mile run on sealed city streets. The event sells out each year.

About

Come celebrate North Carolina craft beer with the Triad's premier spring distance race and community block party! Foothills Brewing & Junction 311 Endurance Sports have partnered to bring you a fun and energetic way to celebrate The State of Southern Beer®. This is a DO NOT MISS event!
