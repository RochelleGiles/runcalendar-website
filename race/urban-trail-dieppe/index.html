About

Explore the scenic urban trails of Dieppe at the annual Urban Trail Dieppe event. This unique race takes runners on a captivating journey through the city's historic streets and picturesque neighborhoods, showcasing the vibrant culture and natural beauty of this charming coastal destination. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or a newcomer to the sport, the Urban Trail Dieppe offers an unforgettable running experience.
