Course

Courses run through Aksarben Village, Elmwood Park and the UNO campus in Omaha. The 10K takes in rolling hills through the surrounding neighbourhoods, while the 5K is flat and the 1-mile is an easy stroll through the village. A kids run and a virtual option are also offered.

About

The Corporate Cup is designed for every type of participant, from walkers to competitive runners. How you participate is up to you! Run our professionally timed 10K or 5K, walk our 5K or 1 Mile routes, or participate in our kids 100-yard dash.
