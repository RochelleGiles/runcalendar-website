Course

Flat, fast courses run almost entirely on the sealed multi-use trails of Gulf State Park at Gulf Shores, Alabama. The marathon and half marathon are held on the Sunday; the Safari 5K and family fun run take place the day before. Combination Dolphin (5K + half) and Shark (5K + marathon) challenges are also offered.
