About

The Bükki Hegyi Maraton (Bükk Mountain Marathon) runs through the Bükk mountain range near Miskolc in northern Hungary. Held in memory of Ádám Kisgyörgy, the event offers marathon, half marathon and shorter distances on forested mountain trails and tracks.
