About

SHAY Boba Run 5K is a fun-filled community 5K celebrating movement, connection, and giving back—topped off with a refreshing boba from Sharetea at the finish line! This unique run/walk, in partnership with Sharetea, brings together runners, walkers, families, and boba lovers of all ages for a meaningful cause.
