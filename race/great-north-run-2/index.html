Course

Point-to-point half marathon from the heart of Newcastle upon Tyne to a seaside finish at South Shields. Around 63,000 runners take part, making it the world's largest half marathon. Half marathon is the only distance offered.

About

The Great North Run is a 13.1 mile half marathon that starts in Newcastle city centre and finishes at the seaside in South Shields. Finishers traditionally celebrate with fish and chips. Entry is allocated by ballot.
