About

Immerse yourself in the enchanting atmosphere of the Course Les Flambeaux, a unique running event nestled in the heart of France. This captivating race takes participants on a journey through the historic streets, offering a glimpse into the region's rich cultural heritage. Runners will be captivated by the mesmerizing sight of flaming torches lining the course, creating a truly unforgettable experience.
