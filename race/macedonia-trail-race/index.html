Course

A loop course in Macedonia Brook State Park, Kent, starting on Macedonia Brook Road with the first four-tenths of a mile on tarmac before turning onto singletrack. The 12K loop climbs Cobble Mountain to a western viewpoint; the 25K runs two loops plus a short out-and-back for over 3,500ft of climb, and the 50K three loops for over 7,000ft.

About

Held at Macedonia Brook State Park in Kent, this Steep Endurance trail race offers 12K, 25K and 50K distances on technical single-track through forested foothills with views of the Catskill and Taconic Mountains. The 50K crosses Cobble Mountain three times for nearly 7,900 feet of climbing.
