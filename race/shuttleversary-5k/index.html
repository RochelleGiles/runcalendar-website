Course

Early-morning 5K run on the cart paths and hard-packed fairways of the Cocoa Beach Golf Course in Florida, with open views over the Banana River and its birdlife. It finishes on the same line as the Cocoa Beach Triathlon, which runs the same day, and the field is capped at 200.

About

Coinciding with the Annual Cocoa Beach Triathlon, this event takes place along the cart path and on the hard-packed fairways of the Cocoa Beach Golf Course. Runners will be greeted with beautiful vistas and sweeping views of the Banana River and bird life before coming across the same finish line as triathletes will pass through just minutes later.
