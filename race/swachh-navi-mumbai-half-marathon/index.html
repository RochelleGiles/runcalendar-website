About

The Swachh Navi Mumbai Half Marathon ties its race day to a civic cleanliness message, in keeping with its "Swachh" (clean) name, and is now in its fifth edition. Runners choose from 21K, 10K, 5K and 3K distances through Navi Mumbai.
