Course

Figure-eight course in Cheyenne Mountain State Park with the start, finish and drop-bag area at the crossing point; the 50K runs two laps. Singletrack through grassy meadows and stands of timber under the granite face of Cheyenne Mountain, described as very runnable with a few well-placed rocks. About 4,000 ft of climbing for the 50K, with three aid stations.

About

The Cheyenne Mountain Trail Race is a trail running event in Cheyenne Mountain State Park near Colorado Springs, Colorado, offering 50K, 25K and shorter mile-based distances across the park's rugged foothill terrain with views of Pikes Peak and the Front Range.
