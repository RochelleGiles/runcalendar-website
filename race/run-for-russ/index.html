Course

A flat, smooth 5K run and walk starting at the corner of Central Avenue and Chino Avenue by the Chino Civic Center, passing the Russ Miller Memorial site on Schaefer Avenue. Bikes, rollerblades and scooters are not allowed on the course.

About

The RUN FOR RUSS is a 5K run/walk event honoring fallen Chino Police Officer Russell M. Miller Sr., killed in the line of duty by a drunk driver on February 1, 2000. It serves as a tribute organized in memory of his service with the Chino Police Department.
