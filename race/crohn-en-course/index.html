About

Crohn En Course is a unique running event that challenges participants to conquer a scenic course while raising awareness for Crohn's disease. Set in the vibrant city of Vaulx-en-Velin, this solidarity-driven race invites runners of all levels to join in the fight against this chronic condition, making it a must-attend event for those passionate about both physical and social impact.
