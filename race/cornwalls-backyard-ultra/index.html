Course

A last-person-standing backyard ultra run from a private farm field at St Eval, with a 4.167-mile loop setting off on the hour, every hour, until one runner remains. The loop takes in Cornish country lanes and a section of the Coast Path at Bedruthan Steps, and is more undulating than hilly.

About

A backyard ultra held near Wadebridge in Cornwall, part of the official Big's Backyard Ultra series. Runners complete a 4.167-mile loop every hour, on the hour, until only one competitor remains.
