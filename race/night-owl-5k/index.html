About

The excitement of competition paired with the thrill of the trails at night make our Night Owl 5K a race to remember! Come dressed in your favorite winter pajamas or festive holiday outfit - a prize will be given for the top 3 best-dressed! The Night Owl 5K starts and finishes at the Lake Haigler entrance, so participants and spectators may order food and drinks from the restaurant, Greenway Canteen, shop for gear and memberships in the visitor center and enjoy popular Greenway trails!
