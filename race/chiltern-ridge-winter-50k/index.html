Course

Single-loop 50K starting and finishing at Berkhamsted Cricket Club, with long stretches along The Ridgeway through the heart of the Chilterns. Woodland trails and ridge paths with wide views. The route is fully marked, the field is capped at 350 runners, and aid stations carry Precision Fuel & Hydration products.

About

The Chiltern Ridge 50K Winter edition takes runners around a stunning loop from Berkhamsted, featuring large sections along The Ridgeway. The route follows classic trails in the heart of the Chilterns with great views and woodland paths.
