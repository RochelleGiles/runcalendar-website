Course

Single 100-mile loop circumnavigating the La Sal Mountains, starting on La Sal Loop Road near Miners Basin about 30 miles from Moab. Alpine singletrack mixes with dirt-road sections, topping out near Mann's Peak at 12,272 ft, with 13 aid stations. The 50-mile and 50K share the same terrain.

About

The Ute 100 loops the La Sal Mountains southeast of Moab on a single 100-mile circuit, with 50-mile and 50K races on the same high terrain. The course tops out near Mann's Peak at 12,272 ft, the highest point reached in any Utah race.
