Course

A trail race starting and finishing near the pool by Hopewell Lake, Elverson. The half marathon uses the Lenape, Buzzards, Raccoon, Mill Creek and Blue/Red trails with over 2,000ft of climbing. The 10K is a lollipop sharing the first 3.2 miles, with aid near miles 1.7 and 4.5.

About

The Big Woods Half Marathon & 10K is a trail race in Elverson, Pennsylvania. The half marathon and 10K distances wind through wooded trails near Hopewell Lake, with the half marathon including significant elevation gain.
