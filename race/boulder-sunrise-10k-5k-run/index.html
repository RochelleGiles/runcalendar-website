Course

Flat out-and-back over the Boulder Reservoir dam on largely paved surface, finishing with a gentle downhill. The 5K carries two water and Gatorade stations and the 10K six, set near each mile marker.

About

The Boulder Sunrise 10k & 5k Run has a reputation of being one of the most scenic courses around. BBSC will provide runners with a supported course. This race is in conjunction with the Boulder Sunrise Triathlon. We've partnered with Your Cause Sports to give each participant an opportunity to raise money for the charity of their choosing!
