Course

A trail race based at Malibu Creek State Park in Calabasas, with all distances starting and finishing there and sharing a network of aid stations. Five distances are run: 100K at 5:30am, 50 mile at 6:00am, and the 50K, marathon and 30K all at 7:00am.

About

Sean O'Brien Trail Runs is a Coastal Trail Runs ultra series through the Santa Monica Mountains near Malibu, offering 30K, 50K, marathon, 50-mile and 100K distances on rugged singletrack with steep climbs and coastal-range views.
