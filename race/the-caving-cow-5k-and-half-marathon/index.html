Course

Trail race staged at Cumberland Caverns outside McMinnville. The half marathon covers five trail loops before an out-and-back passage through the cave, where runners are handed glow sticks; the 5K runs one loop with roughly 470 feet of climb plus the same cave section. A separate road 5K is also offered.
