About

Youghal AC's Youghal Bay Half Marathon & 10K in County Cork follows a flat, fast route through the medieval streets of Youghal, the scenic Front Strand area and the Greenway. The half marathon covers two laps of the route, with the 10km run as a single lap, and full road closures are in place along the course.
