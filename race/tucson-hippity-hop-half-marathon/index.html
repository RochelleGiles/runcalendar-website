Course

Based at Brandi Fenton Park in Tucson, running the park paths and the Loop Trail System on a mix of concrete and asphalt. Flat and stroller friendly, with parts of the course still open to the public. Half marathon, 10K and 5K start in waves, plus a half-mile Cottontail Dash for children.

About

The 2nd annual Tucson Hippity Hop Half Marathon is an Easter-themed running event held at Brandi Fenton Memorial Park. The event offers multiple race distances including a half marathon, 10K, 5K, and Cottontail Dash for kids.
