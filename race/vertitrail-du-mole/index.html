Course

A 13 km mountain race with 1,150 m of climbing, starting from the historic village centre of Saint-Jeoire. Wide tracks give way to a narrow natural trail through old wood-cutting ground and onto the Môle forest road at the alpine pasture, climbing past the Col du Creux to the Bosse de l'Ecutieux. The descent rolls back through the woods to Saint-Jeoire on the old Verticale du Môle line. Drinks at 3 km, food and drink at 8 km.

About

The Vertitrail Du Mole is a challenging mountain running event that takes runners on a scenic course through the beautiful landscapes of the Mole region. The event offers multiple distance options to cater to runners of all levels, from a short but demanding Vertitrail to longer trail running routes. Participants can expect a well-organized event with a focus on authenticity, quality trails, and a welcoming atmosphere.
