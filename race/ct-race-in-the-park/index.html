About

CT Race in the Park is a Mother's Day weekend event at Walnut Hill Park in New Britain, Connecticut, run by the Hartford Marathon Foundation. It offers a 5K run, a shorter walk and a kids fun run, and raises funds for the CT Breast Health Initiative.
