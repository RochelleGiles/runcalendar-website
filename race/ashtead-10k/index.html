Course

An out-and-back 10k on Ashtead Common, a Site of Special Scientific Interest, starting and finishing at Woodfield. The route runs on wide grassy footpaths, bridleways and forest tracks, turning at a halfway point with views over farmland towards Malden Rushett. Undulating, with around 250m of total ascent. A 2km fun run precedes the 10k.
