About

Explore the historic city of Vannes on the Trail des Remparts, a scenic running event that winds through the ancient ramparts and charming streets of this picturesque Brittany town. Runners can immerse themselves in the region's rich culture as they tackle challenging terrain and take in breathtaking views of the coastline and medieval architecture.
