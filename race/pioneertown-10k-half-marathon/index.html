Course

A rugged half marathon and 10K at about 4,400 feet in the Pioneertown Mountains Preserve, on the edge of Joshua Tree National Park in California. The course climbs and drops through high desert with long views out to the Sawtooth Mountains. The half marathon has four water stations and the 10K two.

About

This rugged course features hills and magnificent views of the high desert and Sawtooth Mountains in the Pioneertown Mountains Preserve. Located near Joshua Tree National Park. The run benefits John Wayne Cancer Foundation so every step you take advances the fight against cancer through pioneering cancer research and programs.
