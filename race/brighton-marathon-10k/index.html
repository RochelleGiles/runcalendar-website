About

Lace up your running shoes and head to the stunning seaside city of Brighton for the iconic Brighton Marathon and 10K. Runners can look forward to a scenic course along the beautiful Brighton Beach, with stunning views of the iconic Brighton Palace Pier and the English Channel. This event is a must-do for runners seeking a unique and picturesque running experience in the United Kingdom.
