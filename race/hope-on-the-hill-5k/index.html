About

Hope on the Hill 5K is a fundraiser for the youth at Oak Hill Baptist Church to be able to go to summer camp this summer. We are looking at going to Journey Camp at Toccoa Falls College. They will get the opportunity to hear speakers share about God and how He has changed their lives, worship from the camp band, and many activities during recreation. There have been multiple students saved during this camp over the last couple of years of going there. The students have recently gone and visited in the nursing home and hope to serve both in the church and community more in the coming year.
