Course

Three trail options start from Dunes Restaurant in Hout Bay. The 16km climbs Noordhoek Peak with about 1000m of gain; the 30km adds Karbonkelberg after a Dunes checkpoint; the 42km links Noordhoek Peak, Judas Peak and Karbonkelberg. Staggered starts: 42km at 05:00, 30km at 06:00, 16km at 07:00.

About

Hout Bay Twin Peaks sends runners over the mountains standing on either side of Hout Bay, on the Atlantic edge of the Cape Peninsula. The 16km, 30km and 42km routes climb steeply on rough mountain paths, with long descents back down towards the bay.
