Course

Both races are run at Little River Regional Park in Rougemont, North Carolina, mostly on single track. The 7km route is flagged in blue and the 10-mile route in orange, with volunteers and three aid stations on course. Proceeds go towards maintaining the park and its trail system.

About

The Little River Trail Runs is an in-person trail running event offering 10 mile and 7 kilometer races. It takes place on trails in Little River, Kansas.
