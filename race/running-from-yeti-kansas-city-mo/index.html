About

Running From Yeti is a winter-themed running event series featuring Freddie the Yeti, with participants running, walking, or jogging while enjoying music, treats, and photo opportunities. The Kansas City event supports Easterseals Midwest and offers city-specific swag including a hoodie, finisher's medal, and choice of beanie or thermal headband.
