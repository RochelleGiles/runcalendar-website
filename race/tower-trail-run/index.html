Course

Lapped trail courses at Mount Gambier. The 5km is a single loop, the 21km is two 10.5km laps, the 32km three 10.5km laps and the 56km four 14km laps. The surface mixes singletrack, open trail, technical limestone sections, sealed paths and stairs. Aid stations are provided on the longer distances.

About

The Tower Trail Run in Mount Gambier, South Australia, offers runners a unique challenge through the stunning natural landscapes of the region. This event features an exhilarating 56km ultra-marathon, a half marathon, and shorter 10.5km and 5km fun runs, catering to runners of all abilities. Explore the rugged trails and rolling hills of this picturesque location for an unforgettable running experience.
