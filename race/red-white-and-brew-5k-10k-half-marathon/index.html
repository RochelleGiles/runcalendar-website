Course

Start and finish at The Grainhouse at Raindance National Golf Course, 1779 Raindance National Drive, Windsor. Courses run mostly on the Poudre River Trail plus streets and sidewalks in the Raindance development. Half marathon finishers receive medals and the finish area hosts an after-party at the Grainhouse.
