Course

The 100-mile route starts in Dufton, Cumbria on the Friday and follows the Pennine Way, then the Teesdale Way, finishing along the King Charles III Coastal Path and Cleveland Way into Skinningrove, with about 6,563ft of climbing. The 50-mile covers the second half from Croft-on-Tees and is undulating rather than hilly. Ten checkpoints serve the 100 and four the 50, with hot and cold food.

About

The Charm Bracelet 50 & 100 Ultra is an outstanding long-distance event suitable for both runners and quick walkers. The 100-mile route starts in the beautiful Cumbrian village of Dufton, following the Pennine Way, Teesdale Way, and Cleveland Way, while the 50-mile route covers the second half of the 100-mile course starting in Croft-on-Tees. Both routes offer stunning scenery and a challenging yet rewarding experience for endurance athletes.
