Course

A two-loop road 5K, fast and flat, following the route of the Village of Malverne Memorial Day Parade. It starts and finishes at Connolly Station on Hempstead Avenue. A capped fun run for under-12s is held alongside.

About

The Memorial Day Freedom 5K Run/Walk is a tribute to veterans past and present, following the parade route of the Village of Malverne Memorial Day Parade. It raises funds for American Legion Post #44, Malverne Fire Department, Paws of War, and other veteran-related causes.
