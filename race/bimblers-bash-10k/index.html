Course

An entirely off-road 10K loop through Westwoods in Guilford, starting and finishing at A.W. Cox School. The route links a series of interconnecting single-track hiking trails over rock and root, often wet underfoot, and includes two rock scrambles.

About

A 10K trail race held each spring in Guilford, Connecticut, organized by the Guilford Land Trust. The course crosses the town's protected woodland trails and open-space preserves, raising funds for local conservation.
