About

The Magnetic 7in7 Marathon Event is a multi-day marathon series featuring 7 marathons in 7 consecutive days. Runners can complete one marathon or challenge themselves to run all seven, with each 42.195km course offering scenic views of The Strand, Magnetic Island, and the surrounding beach area.
