Course

A point-to-point downhill race off Fort Mountain on sealed roads, finishing in Chatsworth City Park. The half marathon starts at the Overlook Inn on Highway 52 at about 3,000 feet, opens with an out-and-back over the top that takes in several climbs, then drops more than 2,000 feet over seven miles of 5-10% grade. The 10K and 5K start lower on Highway 52 with five and two miles of descent.
