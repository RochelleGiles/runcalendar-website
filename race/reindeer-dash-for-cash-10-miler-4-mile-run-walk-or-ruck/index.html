About

Follow the Road to a Good Cause! Join us for the 22nd annual Reindeer Dash for Cash 10 Miler & 4 Mile Run, Walk or Ruck. Our patriotic, Christmas-themed run offers a custom designed finisher medal (even for our 4 mile!) keeping in theme with our purpose. Our uniquely designed t-shirt is one you will not want to miss. But, in case you do not want a shirt it is an optional purchase with online registration. It's a win-win.
