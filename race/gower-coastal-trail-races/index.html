Course

Point-to-point races on the Wales Coast Path around the Gower Peninsula, all finishing in Mumbles. The 50-mile ultra starts at Mumbles Cricket Club, the 34-mile Ultra Bach at Weobley Castle and the 23-mile trail race at Rhossili, the latter two reached by coach. Terrain mixes hard paths, cliff tops, soft trails and dunes past Port Eynon, Oxwich, Three Cliffs and Caswell Bay, with up to eight checkpoints.

About

The Gower Coastal Trail Races offer a unique running experience along the stunning Gower Peninsula in Wales. Runners will be treated to breathtaking views of the rugged coastline, sandy beaches, and rolling hills as they navigate the challenging yet rewarding trails. The event caters to runners of all levels, providing a range of distances to choose from, making it an ideal destination for both seasoned trail enthusiasts and those seeking a new adventure.
