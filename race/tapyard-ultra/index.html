Course

A 50km point-to-point between two breweries in Wiltshire and Berkshire, with the start and finish alternating each year between Ramsbury Brewery and Renegade Brewery. The route is 75% off road, mixing woodland trails, paths, canal towpaths and country lanes, with around 314m of climb and four checkpoints roughly 10km apart.

About

The Tapyard Ultra is a 50km point-to-point ultramarathon between two breweries in Wiltshire and Berkshire, run on a mostly off-road course of woodland trails, footpaths, canal towpaths and country lanes. The route reverses direction each year, so the start and finish alternate between the Ramsbury and Renegade breweries.
