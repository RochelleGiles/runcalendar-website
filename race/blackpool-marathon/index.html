About

The Blackpool Festival of Running is an exhilarating annual event that brings together runners of all levels along the vibrant coastal streets of Blackpool. Participants can choose from multiple race distances, enjoying stunning seaside views while challenging themselves in a supportive and energetic atmosphere. The festival celebrates human resilience and athletic achievement, creating a memorable experience for runners and spectators alike.
