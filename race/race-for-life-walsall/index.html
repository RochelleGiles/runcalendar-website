Course

A mixed-surface lapped route through Walsall Arboretum over tarmac paths, woodland trails and flat grass fields. Start and finish are on firm grass that can turn muddy in the wet, and the grass section narrows in places.

About

The Walsall Race for Life loops through Walsall Arboretum, a Victorian park built around a former canal reservoir and famous for its autumn illuminations. Runners, joggers and walkers tackle the 10K, 5K or 3K for Cancer Research UK.
