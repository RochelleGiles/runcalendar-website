About

Join us for the 5th annual race through historic downtown Chillicothe, Ohio's first capital city! The Chillicothe Half Marathon and 5k will begin and end in downtown Chillicothe, just steps away from Fifty West Brewing. Enjoy the choice of a Half Marathon or 5k race distance – that takes you out from Downtown onto the paved trails. This out-and-back style course is made for pase pushing and challenging yourself.
