Course

A Peak District fell race from Hayfield, 8.6 miles with around 1,480ft of climb onto the moors above the village, with a shorter 10k alternative on the day. Start and finish are at the school, where cakes, refreshments and toilets are laid on.

About

A fell race from the Peak District village of Hayfield, climbing into the moorland of the Dark Peak. One of a trio of races staged from the village each year by local fell runners, testing runners on steep, open terrain.
