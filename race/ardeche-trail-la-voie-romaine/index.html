About

Explore the ancient Roman roads of the Ardèche region at the Ardèche Trail La Voie Romaine. This picturesque ultra-trail race takes runners through stunning mountain landscapes, past historic ruins, and along the remnants of the famous Roman roads that once connected the region. With a challenging course and breathtaking scenery, this event is a must for adventurous trail runners seeking a unique running experience.
