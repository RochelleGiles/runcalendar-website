About

The last run of the year and the party of the year. Run or walk 5 km or 10 km on an exclusive route through Steyn City after dark, then stay for the finish-line party in the race village, with DJs and neon lights. Every finisher gets a medal, with professional timing, water points and race photos and video. Gates and registration open at 16:00, the 10 km starts at 18:45 and the 5 km at 19:00. Part of the In-Reach night run series.
