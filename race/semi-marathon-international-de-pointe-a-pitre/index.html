About

The Semi Marathon International De Pointe A Pitre is a must-run event for any serious runner. Set against the stunning backdrop of Pointe-à-Pitre, this iconic international half marathon challenges participants with a scenic and demanding course. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to experience the vibrant local culture, this race is sure to leave a lasting impression.
