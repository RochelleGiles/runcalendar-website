Course

A trail 5K across a privately owned farm managed for conservation and recreation, running through meadows, hardwood forest and long-leaf pine. The start and finish sit on the banks of the Broad River. Walkers are welcome, and the route may change with weather. An optional polar plunge follows.

About

A unique 5K Trail Run through and along bottom land and upland forests. The run starts by following the Broad River and Blue Stone Creek and at the highest elevation offers stunning vistas of the Broad River and forest river valley.
