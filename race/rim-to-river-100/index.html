Course

West Virginia's only 100-mile trail race, an out-and-back through the New River Gorge that crosses from rim to riverbanks several times on a mix of singletrack and rail-trail, passing waterfalls, whitewater, ghost towns and abandoned mines. Entry is by lottery and the time limit is 32 hours.

About

A 100-mile ultramarathon through New River Gorge National Park and Preserve near Oak Hill, West Virginia, run by Rim to River Endurance Co. The course covers gorge rim and riverside trail terrain across the New River Gorge.
