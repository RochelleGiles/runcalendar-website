Course

Steep, technical mountain race on Mt Yonah near Cleveland, Georgia: the 25K summits three peaks (about 5,000 ft of climb) and the 50K six peaks (about 10,000 ft), on rocky, rooty, often muddy or icy trail with scrambling and gravel double-track. Aid roughly every five miles at the trailhead plus a summit water stop.

About

Join Team Summit for the third annual Yonah Mountain Vertical Challenge weekend. Participants will have FOUR options upon registration: 5,000FT of climbing over 3 summits of Yonah, totaling roughly 25km; 10,000FT of climbing over 6 summits of Yonah, totaling roughly 50km; Double UP - Race both events! Saturday and Sunday; 10,000FT 50k Relay - Grab a friend and tag-team Saturdays 10,000FT 50K! Runners may alternate ascents at their leisure.
