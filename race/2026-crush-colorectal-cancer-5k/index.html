About

The CRUSH Colorectal Cancer 5K raises vital community awareness and funds for colorectal cancer research at the Duke Cancer Institute. It brings together patients, families, medical staff, volunteers, and supporters to celebrate walkers and runners while offering community partner exhibitors and screenings.[1]
