Course

Runs from Alton Baker Park on Pre's Trail, Eugene. The 5K trail course follows bark-chip paths past filbert trees, canoe chutes, Autzen Stadium and the Cuthbert Amphitheater, and is almost entirely flat. The road course crosses the Willamette River twice on adjacent riverside paths. An evening event.

About

A unique running/walking tradition with an Irish twist. There will be three race options: 5K Trail, 5K Road, or 10K Road/Trail Combined. 10K participants run both 5K loops (one Road and one Trail), with each participant deciding which loop they will run first. Transition area near the start/finish to be completed prior to completing second 5K. Trail Course is on Pre's Trail and Road Course is on the adjacent River Paths.
