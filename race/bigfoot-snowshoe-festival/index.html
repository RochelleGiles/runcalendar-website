Course

Snowshoe races at Wasatch Mountain State Park in Midway, Utah, on groomed golf-course and single-track mountain loops; the 15K combines a 10K golf-course loop with a 5K mountain loop. Distances range from 5K to 50K, with same-morning entry accepted.

About

The Bigfoot Snowshoe Festival, now past its 18th year, includes snowshoe races ranging from 5K to 50K. Sanctioned by the BFUSSSA, the festival has hosted both the World Snowshoe Championships and the US Snowshoe Championships.
