Course

A low-key, certified, flat and fast marathon and half marathon starting and finishing at Orchard Trail Park in Brooklyn Park, with parking at Champlin Park High School. Both races start at 6:30am and run to an eight-hour limit, with a 2:30pm cutoff.

About

Welcome to the 7th Annual Xenia (pronounced ZEE-Knee-Uh) Avenue Marathon & Half-Marathon. If you're looking for a low key, certified, flat and fast marathon or half-marathon to check off the letter X on your way to completing the alphabet, then look no further.
