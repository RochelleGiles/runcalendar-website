Course

A short, steep mountain race starting and finishing at St Mary's Church in Cilcain, Flintshire. The 7.4 km route climbs 375 m onto the Clwydian hills on open fell and hill paths. The course is only partially marked and local knowledge is an advantage.

About

Cilcain Mountain Race climbs into the Clwydian Range from the village of Cilcain near Mold in north Wales. It is part of the Clwydian Fell Races series and a fixture of the Welsh Fell Runners Association calendar.
