Course

A point-to-point 34.3-mile road run from Skudin Surf on the Long Beach boardwalk to One World Trade Center, passing through Atlantic Beach, the Rockaways and Brooklyn and crossing the Manhattan Bridge. The run begins at 8:46am, with five moments of silence held at the times of the 2001 attacks. Surfaces are boardwalk and city streets.

About

An annual September 11 memorial ultramarathon covering 34.3 miles from the Long Beach boardwalk to the World Trade Center site in Manhattan, honouring the 343 FDNY firefighters lost in 2001. The route passes through Atlantic Beach, the Rockaways and Brooklyn, crossing the Manhattan Bridge, with moments of silence held en route.
