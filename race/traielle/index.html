About

Trai'elle is a unique women-only trail running event that supports Octobre Rose, the breast cancer awareness month. Participants will enjoy scenic routes through the beautiful village of Kaysersberg, exploring its vineyards, forests, and historic castle while promoting a meaningful cause.
