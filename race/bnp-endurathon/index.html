About

BNP Endurathon is a trail race through Borivali National Park (Sanjay Gandhi National Park) in Mumbai, billed by its organisers as the city's toughest run. It sets 25K and 12.5K distances through the park's forest trails, a rare stretch of wilderness inside India's most crowded city.
