About

The Beach to Brother Marathon and Team Relay takes runners from Port Macquarie to North Brother Mountain in the Mid-North Coast of NSW. This point-to-point event traverses beaches, coastal trails, pathways, and scenic headlands, culminating in a unique finish line atop North Brother Mountain.
