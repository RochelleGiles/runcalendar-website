Course

Out-and-back on Wawawai River Road, starting and finishing at Wawawai Landing on the Snake River, with the turnaround near Water Canyon. The organiser states the course is flat, paved and USATF certified. Water stops at miles 2.2, 4, 6, 7.1, 9.1 and 10.9; portable toilets at the start and at miles 2.2 and 4.

About

The Snake River Canyon Half Marathon is a scenic out-and-back race on Wawawai River Road, starting and finishing at Wawawai Landing in Washington, with the turnaround near Water Canyon. The flat, paved course is USATF certified, with water stops along the route.
