Course

Both the 5 km and 2 km start and finish at Kingsford Smith Oval in Longueville, winding through the leafy residential streets of Lane Cove via Kenneth, Woodford, Arabella and Stuart Streets. Sealed roads throughout, closed to traffic for the race, with later finishers moved onto the footpath.

About

The Lane Cove Fun Run is a must-do event for runners of all levels in the heart of Longueville, NSW. Held at the picturesque Kingsford Smith Oval, this community-driven race offers a scenic and challenging course through the local streets and parks. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply want to enjoy a fun day out, the Lane Cove Fun Run has something for everyone.
