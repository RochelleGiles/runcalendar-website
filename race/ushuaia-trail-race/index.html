About

The Ushuaia Trail Race is the southernmost trail running event in Argentina. Runners will experience the stunning landscapes of Tierra del Fuego, including the Beagle Channel, glaciers, and the Andes mountains. The challenging course offers breathtaking views and a unique opportunity to explore the 'End of the World' region.
