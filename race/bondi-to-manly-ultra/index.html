Course

A point-to-point course of about 80km along the Bondi to Manly Walk, tracing Sydney Harbour's foreshore from Bondi Beach around to Manly. The route can be run solo or split as a two-person relay of roughly 40km each or a four-person relay of roughly 20km each.

About

Discover the stunning Bondi to Manly Ultra, an 80km coastal adventure that takes runners on a breathtaking journey along Sydney's famous beaches. Traversing the iconic shoreline between Bondi and Manly, this urban ultra challenges participants with a unique blend of scenic beauty and endurance. Runners will be captivated by the dramatic ocean vistas and vibrant city landscapes as they push their limits in this unforgettable event.
