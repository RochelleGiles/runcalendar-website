About

The Wayne Crime Stoppers Caper Chase is a series of running events including 10K, 5K, and 1 Mile Fun Run held in Goldsboro, NC, to support Crime Stoppers. Participants can choose in-person races or virtual options, with proceeds aiding community safety through anonymous crime tips and rewards.
