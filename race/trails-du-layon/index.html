About

Explore the picturesque Layon region of France at the annual Trails du Layon running event. This scenic trail race winds through rolling vineyards and quaint villages, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels. With a focus on the natural beauty of the local landscape, the Trails du Layon is a must-do event for trail enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable running adventure.
