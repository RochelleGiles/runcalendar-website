Course

A single certified loop starting on Bristol Road outside Chippenham Town FC and finishing on the cricket pitch perimeter at Chippenham Sports Club. The route runs through the town centre before heading out on rural roads through Stanley, the Tythertons, Avon, Kellaways and Langley Burrell. Predominantly flat and fast on open roads with light traffic; drinks stations and marshals throughout.

About

The Chippenham Half Marathon is a fast, accurately measured, and predominantly flat course starting and finishing at Chippenham Sports Club and (next to Chippenham Town Football ground). The route goes through the of the centre of the historic market town of Chippenham and continues the countryside tour through the villages and hamlets of Tytherton Lucas, East Tytherton, Avon, Kellaways and Langley Burrell, before returning to the town for a fast downhill finish into the sports club cricket pavilion. This is an ideal race for both first-timers and experienced runners aiming for a personal best.
