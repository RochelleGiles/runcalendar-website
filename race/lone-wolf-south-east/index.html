Course

A 24-hour lapped event on a 6.7 km loop in Alice Holt Forest near Farnham, Surrey. One lap sets off each hour and runners must complete it inside 60 minutes to stay in. The loop is undulating forest terrain, a mix of stony tracks and single-track paths.

About

Prepare to test your limits at the Lone Wolf South East, a grueling last one standing endurance trail running event in the picturesque Alice Holt Forest near Farnham, UK. This challenging race pushes runners to their physical and mental boundaries as they navigate the rugged terrain, with the ultimate goal of being the last competitor standing. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking your next big adventure, the Lone Wolf South East promises an unforgettable experience.
