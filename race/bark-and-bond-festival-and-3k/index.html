Course

The 3K is run at Westside Park in northwest Atlanta. Dog-and-human activity stations are placed at roughly half-kilometre intervals along the route. Participants set off in staggered waves of about 15 dogs every two minutes from 8:30am.

About

The Bark and Bond Festival and 3K is a dog-friendly run and festival held at Westside Park in Atlanta, raising funds for Pralines Backyard Foundation. Runners and walkers cover a 3K with their dogs, setting off in small staggered waves, and the festival that follows includes demonstrations and stalls.
