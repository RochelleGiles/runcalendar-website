Course

A lap course on rural lanes east of Worcester. Marathon and half start together on Offerton Lane at 9:30am and run through Oddingley, Tibberton, Crowle, Himbleton, Spernal Green and Dunhamstead; the marathon covers two laps, the half splits off at around 11 miles. The 10K starts at 10am and uses the canal towpath for its first 4km before joining the main route. All distances finish at Worcester Rugby Club. Three water stations on the half, seven on the marathon.

About

The Worcester Marathon and Half Marathon return for their 14th event, featuring scenic rural lanes and a route that loops around the beautiful countryside near Worcester. Runners can choose from Marathon, Half Marathon, and 10K distances.
