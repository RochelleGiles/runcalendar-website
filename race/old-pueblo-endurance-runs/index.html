Course

Loop course starting and finishing at Kentucky Camp, a historic mining site in the Santa Rita Mountains; the longer distance covers the loop twice. The route follows the Arizona Trail through rocky, hilly high-desert mountain terrain. Four aid stations per loop: Melendrez Pass at 8mi, Cave Creek at 14.5mi, Gardner Canyon at 21mi and Kentucky Camp at 25mi.

About

Started by a group of local trail runners, all friends, who are still friends, two of whom were married in these mountains. Unknowingly they were actually early pioneers of trail running in Arizona. A sport that has grown by leaps and bounds in recent years.
