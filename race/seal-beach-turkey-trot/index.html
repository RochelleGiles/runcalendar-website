Course

A flat, fast seafront loop starting and finishing at Hennessey's Tavern on Main Street, Seal Beach. The 5K runs west toward the pier then along Ocean Avenue, First Street and Marina Drive, through the park and past the end of the bike path before returning on Ocean Avenue. The 10K covers the same loop twice.

About

The Seal Beach Turkey Trot is an annual Thanksgiving-season road race in Seal Beach, California, a beach town in Orange County. The event offers 10K and 5K distances along with a fun run for kids.
