Course

A single-track trail 10K through High Cliff State Park on the shore of Lake Winnebago. The route starts and finishes at the Lime Kiln Trail car park and takes in rocks, roots, boulders and steady climbing, with close to 500 feet of gain. Entirely off-road.

About

This is a 10k trail run of mostly single track trails. Fun for all leel of runners. No Cut off Time. Run it as a race or challenge yourself with a power hike. No matter what you will have fun and enjoy the challenge and trails. It is designed to take you through the most challenging sections of the High Cliff State Park. There will be rocks, roots, boulders, and hills to face. Total elevation gain is nearly 500 feet. This run will incorporate the true trail running experience.
