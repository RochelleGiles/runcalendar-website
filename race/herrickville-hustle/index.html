About

Celebrate a day of Faith and Freedom. Come run through the beautiful hills of Herrickville with family and friends. Start your morning at the Herrickville Wesleyan Church for the Herrickville Hustle 5k, 7k walk/run. Run for the cause, all proceeds will benefit our local volunteer non-profit services, HOPS Ambulance, Herrickville Fire Department and the Herrickville Wesleyan Church.
