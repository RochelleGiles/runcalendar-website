About

The Graz Marathon is a must-experience running event set against the stunning backdrop of Austria's historic city of Graz. Runners will wind through the charming streets and parks, taking in the city's picturesque architecture and lively atmosphere. This event is a favorite among both local and international participants, offering a challenging yet rewarding course for runners of all levels.
