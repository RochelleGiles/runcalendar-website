Course

Out-and-back races from Tacoma Narrows Airport across the Tacoma Narrows Bridge — "Galloping Gertie" — with views over Puget Sound to Point Defiance, Vashon Island, Mount Rainier and the Olympics. The half marathon crosses the bridge and continues through the old growth forest of the Key Peninsula; the 10K turns after the bridge and the 5K at its midpoint.

About

A half marathon, 10K, and 5K in Gig Harbor, Washington, named after "Galloping Gertie," the original Tacoma Narrows Bridge that famously collapsed in 1940. The course runs through Gig Harbor's waterfront community near the strait.
