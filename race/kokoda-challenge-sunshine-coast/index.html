Course

Teams start and finish at Kenilworth State Community College and cross Imbil State Forest on rugged bush trail with steep climbs, uneven ground and river crossings. The 18 km has two checkpoints, the 30 km three and the 48 km five, with a support crew checkpoint on the 48 km.

About

The Kokoda Challenge team endurance event challenges teams of 2-5 to trek through Imbil State Forest, covering courses with almost 2,000 meters of elevation. Participants cross creeks and wind through dense rainforest while raising funds for local youth programs and honoring Australian soldiers who fought along the Kokoda Track in 1942.
