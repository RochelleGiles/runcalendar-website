Course

A 5K run and walk through Okeeheelee Park in West Palm Beach, starting and finishing at the Micanopy Pavilion and passing the Okeeheelee Nature Center, whose trails wind through 90 acres of flatwood forest and wetland. Proceeds support the Friends of the nature centre.

About

A 5K run/walk event through Okeeheelee Park that supports the Friends of Okeeheelee Nature Center. Participants receive a personalized bib with RFID timing chip, event shirt, finisher medal, and post-race activities including healthy snacks and DJ entertainment.
