Course

Based at the Wairere Road entrance to Totara Park in Manukau, with all courses looping out from and back to that base. The mix is wide hard-packed path and fast single track twisting through native totara bush and green rolling hills. Distances run 7km (141m climb), 11km (195m), 17.5km (362m) and about 21.5km (383m); the two longest are run-only.

About

The Totara Park Trail Run & Walk offers runners and walkers an exciting adventure through Auckland's hidden natural gem. Participants will explore beautiful bushland trails, winding through green rolling hills and experiencing fast, hard single trails. Located just a short distance from Auckland's city centre, this event provides a marvelous escape into the wilderness with multiple course distances to suit different fitness levels.
