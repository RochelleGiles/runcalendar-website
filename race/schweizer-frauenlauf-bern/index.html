About

The Schweizer Frauenlauf Bern is a must-attend running event for women in Switzerland. Set against the stunning backdrop of Bern, this race celebrates the power and spirit of the female runner. With a rich history and a scenic course, the Schweizer Frauenlauf Bern is a truly inspiring experience for athletes of all levels.
