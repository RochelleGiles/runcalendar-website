About

Woodlands Trail Run is held on the grounds of the Woodlands Historic Park, the home of Living Legends where much-loved racehorses enjoy a life of retirement. Runners can visit the historic homestead for a Devonshire tea and do some plane spotting from the end of the Tullamarine runway.
