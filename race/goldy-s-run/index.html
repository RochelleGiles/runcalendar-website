About

Join us at the 16th annual Goldy's Run in Minneapolis and do the 10 Mile, 10K, 5K, Kids Run or the 13.1 Challenge. You can also run for a cause and raise money for the M Health Fairview Masonic Children's Hospital. Your support will go to changing the lives of pediatric patients with heart disease.
