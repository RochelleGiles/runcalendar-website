About

Spectacular trails against a grand natural backdrop: The Madrisa Trail Klosters in the Swiss canton of Graubünden promises trail running in its purest form. The T54 Trail covers 54 kilometers with over 4,000 meters of elevation gain, and with around 90% trail content! A feast for all fans of alpine trail runs.
