About

The Sapanca Ultra is a trail running event that takes place in the beautiful Sapanca region of Turkey. The event offers a variety of race distances, from a 6K team race to a challenging 55K ultra, allowing runners of all levels to participate. The scenic courses wind through the lush forests and along the shores of Lake Sapanca, providing a unique and picturesque running experience.
