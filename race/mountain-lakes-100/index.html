Course

A single ~101-mile loop from Olallie Lake Resort on the Pacific Crest Trail between Mt Jefferson and Mt Hood: 96 miles of singletrack and 5 miles of jeep and gravel road, with technical rocky sections between 3,300' and 5,700'. 14 aid stations, crew access at Olallie Lake, Clackamas (miles 40 and 72) and Olallie Meadows; 8:00am start, 30-hour limit.

About

A single-loop 100-mile race starting and finishing at Olallie Lake Resort in the Oregon Cascades. About 96 of the 101 miles are singletrack with technical, rocky stretches, running between 3,300 and 5,700 feet of elevation. The course is supported by 14 aid stations, with crew access at four points and a 30-hour finish limit.
