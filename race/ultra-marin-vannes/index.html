About

The Ultra Marin Vannes is a stunning trail running event that winds through the picturesque Golfe du Morbihan region in the heart of Brittany, France. Runners will be treated to breathtaking coastal views and challenging terrain as they conquer this epic ultramarathon course. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or looking for your next big adventure, the Ultra Marin Vannes is an event not to be missed.
