About

Explore the trails at the Whiskey Island 5k in Cleveland Metroparks Lakefront Reservation on August 23! Enjoy this timed run/walk while checking out Cleveland Metroparks newest trails including the Whiskey Island Trail and Wendy Park Bridge. Participants receive a custom medal, a t-shirt, and post-race refreshments! A portion of the proceeds benefit Cleveland Metroparks K-9 Unit.
