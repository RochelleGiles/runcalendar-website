About

A prestigious running event featuring multiple distances including a marathon and semi-marathon, both with FFA-Bronze certification qualifying for French championships. The course offers a flat and bucolic route through the Val de Somme region, making it conducive to achieving personal bests. The event is supported by local authorities and organized by dedicated volunteers from Amicale du Val de Somme.
