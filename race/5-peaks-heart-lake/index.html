Course

Shaded singletrack around the spring-fed, heart-shaped kettle lake at Heart Lake Conservation Park, with the start/finish doubling as the main spectator point. Distances run 7.5 km (Sport), 14.6 km (Enduro) and 22.5 km (Grit), plus a 3 km kids' run, each with at least one aid station. The race is cup-free, so runners carry their own vessel, and routes can change with trail conditions.

About

Part of the 5 Peaks Trail Running Series, this race winds through the wooded singletrack trails of Heart Lake Conservation Area near Brampton. Sport, Enduro and Grit distances offer options from a quick 7.5K to a more demanding 22.5K.
