Course

Off-road marathon (26.2 miles, 3,065ft of ascent) and half marathon (14.8 miles, 1,750ft) from Haworth over the West Yorkshire moors, run under Fell Runners Association rules with mandatory kit and many stiles and gates. The full has five feed stations and the half two, stocked with squash, cola, fruit, cake and hot-weather extras. Starts 09:30 (full) and 10:30 (half).

About

The 33rd running of this off-road marathon and half marathon from Haworth, home of the Brontë sisters, climbing onto the Pennine moors of West Yorkshire. Organised by Keighley & Craven Athletics Club.
