About

The Powder Keg Trail Run is a scenic and challenging trail race exploring the trails of the Historic Kings Mansion at Carter Park in Kings Mills, OH. The course features a loop near the Little Miami River, an old dam, steps ascent, and north side park trails, listed as 3.5 miles.
