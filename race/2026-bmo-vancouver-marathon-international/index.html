About

The BMO Vancouver Marathon International is one of Canada's most celebrated running events, drawing participants from around the globe to the scenic city of Vancouver, British Columbia. Established in 1972, the marathon has grown from a modest race around Stanley Park to a world-class event featuring multiple race distances and attracting over 18,000 runners annually. The event is renowned for its breathtaking courses that showcase Vancouver's natural beauty, including oceanfront views, lush parks, and vibrant urban neighborhoods.
