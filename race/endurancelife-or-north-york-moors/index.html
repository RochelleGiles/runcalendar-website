Course

Starts and finishes at Ravenscar, entirely within the North York Moors National Park and using sections of the Cleveland Way. Routes of 10K, half marathon, marathon and ultra cross moorland and grassland past dry stone walls and disused railway lines. Climbing ranges from about 863ft on the 10K to 4,051ft on the ultra, and the marathon and ultra are UTMB qualifiers.

About

Wholly located within the National Park, and utilising sections of the Cleveland Way, this area is superbly suited to trail running. These North Yorkshire 10K, Half Marathon, Marathon and Ultra courses are rich and varied with reminders to Yorkshire's fine heritage all around - from the dry stone walls and disused railway lines, to the hardy sheep flocks up on the moors. The world famous Whitby Abbey is just a few miles up the road, so there is plenty to make a weekend long visit well worthwhile.
