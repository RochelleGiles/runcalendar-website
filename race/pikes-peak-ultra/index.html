Course

Starts and finishes at Bear Creek Regional Park, Colorado Springs, climbing High Drive into Cheyenne Canon and crossing the Seven Bridges Trail to reach Pipeline Trail. Much of the route is crushed granite on sections of the Ring the Peak Trail, and the 50K and 50 Mile add a summit of Mt Rosa. The race is cup-less, with crew-accessible aid stations.

About

Pikes Peak Ultra began as Race Director Justin Ricks' childhood dream to host a race on his home trails, brought to life by Mad Moose Events. It has grown into a showcase of some of the best trails Colorado Springs has to offer.
