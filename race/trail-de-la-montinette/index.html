About

The Trail de la Montinette is a running event that takes place in the scenic town of Arqueves, France. The course offers participants a chance to explore the beautiful countryside and challenging terrain, making it a must-attend event for trail running enthusiasts. The event is known for its welcoming atmosphere and stunning views along the route.
