About

The Mytho Marathon is run at Grado in Friuli Venezia Giulia, on gravel tracks around the lagoon and through its fishing valleys. The programme runs from a 9 km up to a 10-mile competitive gravel race, with a 12.5 km Sunset Run timed to finish in the evening light. Non-competitive Eco Run and Nordic walking options run alongside.
