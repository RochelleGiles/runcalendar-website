About

The Tanuhegyek Trail is a trail running event organized by the Terepfutó Műhely Sports Association, taking place in one of the most beautiful parts of the Balaton Uplands National Park. Depending on the route length, participants can conquer 5, 3, or 1 witness hills, offering spectacular views of Lake Balaton and the autumn-colored Balaton Uplands. The event includes a special dog-friendly category in the shortest 7km distance.
