Course

Circles the base of Mt Beerwah from a start/finish on Mount Beerwah Road in Glass House Mountains National Park. The marathon is two laps of the half marathon course, anti-clockwise then clockwise. Steep trail throughout — about 294m of gain for the 10km, 586m for the half and 1,172m for the marathon. Marked with reflective flagging every 250m; aid stations carry water, electrolyte and food.

About

Beerwah@Daybreak is a road running event at Glass House Mountains in the Sunshine Coast hinterland, with the iconic volcanic peaks as a backdrop. Runners can choose a marathon, half marathon or 10km, all starting at daybreak.
