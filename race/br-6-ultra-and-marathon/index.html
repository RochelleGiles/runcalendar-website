BR-6 Ultra and Marathon

Amherst, New York · United States

Six hours on the clock, run as far as you go

Course

Run as laps of a P-shaped loop of almost 3.25 miles on the traffic-free Amherst Bike Path, starting from the Ellicott Creek Trailway near Northtown Center in Williamsville. The loop is USATF certified at 5.2177 km; the marathon is eight full loops plus 2.195 km and the half four loops plus 1.0975 km, while six-hour entrants cover as many laps as they can. Start, finish and timing stay at one point.

About

Run or walk as far as you can for up to 6 hours. All certified distances are timed concurrently. This contains a USATF-Certified half-marathon and full marathon. You may use your marathon finish time as a Boston qualifier. All timing stops at 2:00 p.m. (Six hours) Partial laps at the end of the race will count, but only if the runner completes the full lap.
