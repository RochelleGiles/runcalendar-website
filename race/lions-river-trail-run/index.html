Course

Trail race based at the Lions River Trading Post in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands, just off the N3. Three looped courses run through indigenous forest: 6km with about 193m of climb, 10km with about 345m, and 18km with about 596m. Water tables are cupless, so runners carry their own hydration.

About

A trail race in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands, South Africa, part of the Midlands trail series. Routes of 6km, 10km and 18km follow trails near Lions River and Lidgetton, through farmland and river valley terrain.
