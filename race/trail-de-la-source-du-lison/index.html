About

The Trail De La Source Du Lison is a captivating running event that takes participants on a scenic journey through the breathtaking landscapes surrounding the source of the Lison River. Runners will traverse challenging trails, immersed in the natural beauty of the region, making this an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts and trail running enthusiasts alike.
