About

Tackle the rugged terrain of the Adamello Ultra Trail, a challenging ultramarathon event set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Italian Alps. Runners will traverse scenic mountain landscapes and conquer grueling ascents as they push their limits on this renowned trail running course. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a thrilling new challenge, the Adamello Ultra Trail promises an unforgettable adventure.
