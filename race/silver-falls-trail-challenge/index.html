Course

Runs the Trail of Ten Falls loop in Silver Falls State Park, starting from the South Falls Day-Use Area near Sublimity, Oregon. The route passes ten waterfalls, going behind two of them, over rocky sections, hundreds of stairs and rolling climbs and descents. Distances are a quarter marathon, half marathon and a 12-mile ruck challenge.

About

The Silver Falls Trail Challenge is a trail race in Silver Falls State Park near Sublimity, Oregon, run on the Trail of Ten Falls. Quarter-marathon, half-marathon and 12-mile ruck divisions climb past ten waterfalls on rocky trail with hundreds of stairs, passing behind two of the falls.
