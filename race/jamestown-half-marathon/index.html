Course

A flat, scenic half marathon starting and finishing at the Amblers House on Jamestown Road in Williamsburg, winding through Jamestown Settlement, circling Jamestown Island and taking in the Greensprings and Virginia Capital Trails. The route mixes road, parkway and improved trail. A 5K and a one-mile Discovery Mile are also offered.

About

An inaugural half marathon, 5K and one-mile race in Williamsburg, near the site of Jamestown, the first permanent English settlement in America. The route passes through the historic triangle area connecting Williamsburg's colonial and Revolutionary-era landmarks.
