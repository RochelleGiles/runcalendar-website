Course

Both races start and finish at the Normandale Lake Bandshell in Bloomington, Minnesota, and loop the lake. Every course is entirely on paved road or path, and the half marathon is USATF certified. Water stations are on course, with sports drink added on the half, and hot apple cider waits at the finish.

About

The Twin Cities Hot Cider Hustle is an autumn half marathon and 5K run around Normandale Lake in Bloomington, Minnesota. Both courses are fully paved and the half marathon is USATF certified. Finishers are met with hot apple cider, caramel apples and a commemorative mug.
