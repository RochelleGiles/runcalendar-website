Course

Repeating laps of a 5km loop through the Western Plantation at the You Yangs, starting and finishing at the Western Plantation carpark on Sandy Creek Road, Little River. Shorter distances finish before sunset, while the 21km, 42km and 50km push on into the night.

About

The Heroes & Villains Trail Run takes place in You Yangs Regional Park near Little River, on the granite hills and bush trails between Melbourne and Geelong. It's part of the Trailsplus You Yangs trail series, held as an evening event.
