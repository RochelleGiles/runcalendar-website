About

Växjö Marathon runs through the southern parts of Växjö, Sweden, past tree-lined avenues, green spaces and lakeside roads along Växjösjön, including the scenic lakeside promenade. Runners pass Lake Trummen, with views across to Teleborg Castle in the distance.
