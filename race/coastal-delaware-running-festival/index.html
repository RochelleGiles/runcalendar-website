Course

A two-day festival on the Delaware coast. The 5K starts and finishes at the Dewey Beach Lions Club on McKinley Street, Dewey Beach; the 9K, half marathon and marathon start and finish at Rehoboth Avenue and the Boardwalk in Rehoboth Beach. Water stops with portable toilets are set at the start, finish and along the course, with Gatorade and gels on the longer distances.

About

The Coastal Delaware Running Festival offers marathon, half marathon, 9K and 5K races through Rehoboth Beach, Delaware. Courses wind past the town's beaches and neighborhoods in this well-known Atlantic boardwalk town on Delaware's coast.
