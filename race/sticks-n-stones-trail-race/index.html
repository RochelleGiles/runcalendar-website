Course

A 5.3km dirt loop around the lake at Christie Lake Conservation Area near Dundas, Ontario, repeated according to distance. The trail is rolling, fast and runnable rather than technical. Two aid points per loop, at about 2km and at the start/finish; the race is cupless.
