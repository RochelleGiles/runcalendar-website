Course

An out-and-back 5K starting and finishing at Adirondack Brewing Company, 33 Canada Street, Lake George, hugging the southern shore of Lake George. The course is nearly flat: the largest climb is only about 60 feet between Beach Road and 9L. Start is 10:00 a.m.

About

The ADK 5k course provides panoramic views of Lake George in early fall. Runners hug the lake in this memorable out and back. Hills are minimal: the biggest (only a 60' climb) occurs on the course between Beach Road and 9L (at Garrisons). After the race, kick back with live music, food trucks, and yard games at Adirondack Brewing Company.
