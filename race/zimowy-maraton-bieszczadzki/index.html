About

The Zimowy Maraton Bieszczadzki is a captivating winter running event that takes participants through the stunning Bieszczady Mountains in southern Poland. Runners will be treated to breathtaking views of snow-capped peaks and serene forests as they challenge themselves on this unique course. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a winter running enthusiast, this event promises an unforgettable adventure.
