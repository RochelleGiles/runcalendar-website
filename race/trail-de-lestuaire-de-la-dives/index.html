About

Explore the stunning Normandy coastline at the Trail de l'Estuaire de la Dives, a captivating trail running event that winds through the picturesque estuaries and valleys of this breathtaking region. Challenge yourself on a scenic course that showcases the natural beauty of the area, from rugged cliffs to lush forests and tranquil waterways.
