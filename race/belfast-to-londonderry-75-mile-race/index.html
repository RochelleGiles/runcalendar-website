About

Embark on an unforgettable journey through the heart of Northern Ireland as you tackle the Belfast to Londonderry 75 Mile Race. Runners will test their endurance, taking on the scenic route from the vibrant city of Belfast to the historic city of Derry/Londonderry. This challenging event offers a unique opportunity to explore the breathtaking landscapes and rich cultural heritage of the region.
