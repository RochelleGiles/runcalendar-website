Course

One-mile races run on the track at Bob Pickens Track & Field in Jackson Park. Adults race in seventeen competitive heats organised by pace, plus open and top divisions for faster runners. The meet is held in the evening under lights with FinishLynx timing. Children race a 100-metre dash or a 400-metre dash.

About

PUMA's Chi-Town Miles is an exciting night of one-mile races on the track! All levels of runners are welcome and will be grouped by pace for different heats that get progressively faster as the night goes on.
