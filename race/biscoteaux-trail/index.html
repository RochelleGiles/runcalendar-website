About

The Biscoteaux Trail is a running event that takes place in France. The event features several race distances, including a 20km Grand Biscoteaux Trail and a 9km Petit BisCoteaux Trail & Nordic Walking. The event also includes children's races and activities. The Biscoteaux Trail is known for its scenic course and supportive atmosphere.
