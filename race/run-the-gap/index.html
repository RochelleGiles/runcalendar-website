About

Explore the stunning Grampians National Park at Run the Gap, an annual trail running event offering 6km, 12km, and half marathon distances. Challenge yourself on the scenic routes winding through the park's rugged mountain landscapes, with breathtaking views around every turn. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience in one of Australia's most beautiful natural settings.
