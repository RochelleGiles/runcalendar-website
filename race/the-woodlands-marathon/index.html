Course

Road courses through The Woodlands Town Center, starting on Lake Robbins Drive at Town Green Park and finishing on The Woodlands Waterway. The marathon course is flat, with a stated 131 ft of total gain, and closes at 1:30 PM (a 14:30 min/mile pace). The weekend also includes half marathon, 10K, 5K and 2K events.

About

The Woodlands Marathon, revived by The Woodlands Marathon Management after a long hiatus, brings a marathon, half marathon, marathon relay, 5K, and 2K Fun Run to The Woodlands, Texas. The organization aims to promote healthy lifestyles and support the local community.
