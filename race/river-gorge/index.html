Course

Highly technical singletrack loops in Prentice-Cooper Wildlife Management Area above the Tennessee River Gorge, starting and finishing at the Mullens Cove parking lot about 30 minutes from downtown Chattanooga. The 6.5-mile runs the Mullens Cove loop; the 10.2 and 20-mile races add the Pot Point loop.

About

The Bad Beard River Gorge 6.5, 10.2, and 20 mile trail races are a Southeastern classic that runs the Mullens Cove and Pot Point loops in the Prentice-Cooper Wildlife Management Area just outside Chattanooga, Tennessee.
