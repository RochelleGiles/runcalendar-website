Course

Trail races on the technical trails at Robinette's Apple Haus near Grand Rapids, Michigan, reputed among the most technical in West Michigan. Options include a 10K loop around the orchard plus 6-hour and 12-hour timed runs on roughly 4-mile loops.

About

The Wicked Worm Trail Races are run on the beautiful trails at the Robinette's Apple Haus. Known for being one of the most technical trails in West Michigan, you'll find the name Wicked Worm is quite fitting.
