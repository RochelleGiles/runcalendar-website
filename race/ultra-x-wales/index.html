Course

Marked trail races in the Bannau Brycheiniog near Abergavenny, over the rolling hills and valleys of the national park. Distances run from 12km to 100 miles across Friday to Sunday, the 110km climbing 4,816m and the 50km 2,166m. Checkpoints carry water and snacks and medical support is on course. Runners can camp at Llangorse Activity Centre on the Friday and Saturday nights.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Brecon Beacons as you take on the challenge of Ultra X Wales. This stunning multi-day ultramarathon event based near Abergavenny, Monmouthshire, promises an unforgettable adventure through the hills and valleys of this picturesque region of the United Kingdom. Runners will be captivated by the breathtaking landscapes and the thrill of pushing their limits in this truly unique endurance event.
