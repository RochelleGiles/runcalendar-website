Course

Half marathon, 10K and 5K courses starting and finishing at Brazos River Park in Sugar Land, Texas, on the southwest edge of Houston. The routes run on a mix of sidewalk, paved trail and gravel trail beside the Brazos River. A short out-and-back kids dash is also held.

About

Start your year with purpose, pride, and the power of community. The 47th annual Lone Star Stampede is a Houston tradition that unites runners, walkers, University of Texas alumni, and supporters for a day of fitness, fun, and giving back.
