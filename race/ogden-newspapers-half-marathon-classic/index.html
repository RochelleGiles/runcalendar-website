Course

A long-standing Wheeling half marathon known for its hilly, undulating course, starting near WesBanco Arena at the Intermodal Center. Run or walk options are offered over the half marathon and 5K, alongside a one-mile fun run, a tiny-tot trot and a dog walk on the Heritage Trail.

About

The Ogden Newspapers Half Marathon Classic runs through Wheeling, West Virginia, along the Ohio River and through the city's hilly Appalachian streets. Held each May as part of the Ogden Newspapers Wellness Weekend, the course is known among local runners as a tough, hilly test.
