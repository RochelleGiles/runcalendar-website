Course

A 4 x 5 km team relay. Each of four runners covers 5 km as two laps of a measured, traffic-free 2.5 km loop inside Victoria Park that uses sections of the Adelaide supercar circuit. One aid station with water sits at the 2.5 km mark. Briefing at 5:45pm, first-leg runners on the line at 5:55pm, start 6:00pm.

About

Gather your team and get ready to tackle the Park Stampede Relay in Australia! This 4 x 5km relay event is the perfect challenge for running groups, employees, friends, and families to come together and put their endurance to the test on scenic local trails. With a focus on camaraderie and community, the Park Stampede offers a unique opportunity to experience the thrill of relay racing in a beautiful natural setting.
