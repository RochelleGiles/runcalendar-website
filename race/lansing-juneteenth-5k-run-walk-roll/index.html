About

Join the Lansing Area Track Club and "Run Tha City 517" for the fourth annual Lansing, Michigan Juneteenth 5k run/walk/roll! Register today to run/walk or roll (strollers and wheel chairs welcome) the race will start at Benjamin Davis Park 5500 Pleasant Grove Rd, Lansing, Mi.48911 and take you through the scenic route of Davis Park.
