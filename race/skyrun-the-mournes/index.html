Course

Three skyrunning routes through the Mourne Mountains, starting in staggered waves. The 50km SkyUltra climbs over 3,500m and starts at 07:00 with a 12-hour limit and two aid stations; the 35km Skyline climbs over 2,700m from 09:00 with an 8-hour limit and one aid station; the 20km SkyTrail climbs over 1,300m from 10:00 with a 4-hour limit and takes in Slieve Donard.

About

Conquer the rugged peaks of Ireland's Mourne Mountains at the SkyRun The Mournes event. This challenging trail running festival offers three distances - the classic 35km Mourne Skyline, the grueling 50km Mourne SkyUltra, and the scenic 20km SkyTrail - all showcasing the breathtaking landscapes of this stunning natural wonder. Whether you're an elite ultrarunner or a trail enthusiast, the SkyRun The Mournes promises an unforgettable adventure.
