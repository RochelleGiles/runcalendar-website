About

The HARP24 is an annual 24 hour endurance race through the scenic Essex countryside. Participants compete to run the most laps in 24 hours, with routes traversing a 5 mile off-road course. This event raises funds for HARP, Southend's homeless charity, supporting around 1,000 local people experiencing homelessness every year.
