Course

Last-one-standing backyard format: a 6.71km trail loop set off on the hour, every hour, from a start and finish base at Bayview Road Caravan Park, Karratha. Surface is a mix of gravel, hard sand and red dirt, flat but with uneven footing. An aid station between laps carries water, coke, electrolytes, fruit, chips and lollies.

About

The Red Dirt Backyard Ultra in Karratha, Western Australia challenges runners to the ultimate test of endurance. This last-one-standing event takes place on the rugged terrain of the Pilbara region, where participants must complete a 6.7km loop course repeatedly until only the most resilient remain. Runners will push their limits amidst the stunning red-hued landscape of Australia's outback.
