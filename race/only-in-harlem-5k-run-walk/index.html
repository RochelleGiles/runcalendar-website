Course

A single 5K loop of Randall's Island in New York City, starting and finishing at Field #10 on Central Road. The route is flat and closed to traffic, and is open to both runners and walkers.

A family-friendly 5K run/walk event celebrating Harlem, welcoming runners of all ages, sizes, and abilities. Proceeds benefit Harlem Run and the Never Stop Running Foundation.
