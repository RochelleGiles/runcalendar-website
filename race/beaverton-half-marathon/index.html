Course

Starts and finishes on SW Gemini and SW Nimbus Avenue outside Portland Running Company in Beaverton. The half marathon and 10K share the first 6.21 miles along the Fanno Creek Path and local bike paths, with half marathoners returning through the start line to run a second loop. The 5K is an out-and-back crossing Hall Blvd with a turnaround on Fanno Creek. Gatorade and GU gels are provided on course.

About

The Beaverton Half Marathon, 10K and 5K run through neighborhoods and parks in Beaverton, Oregon, a Portland suburb. The race has been a fixture of the local running community for over a decade.
