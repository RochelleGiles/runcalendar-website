Course

A night race at Bogue Chitto State Park starting just before midnight from the park Meeting Room. The route is two loops of a scenic dirt trail with some small hills but nothing technical, with the 5-mile and 10-mile options covering one or both loops.

About

The Chupacabra is a trail race in Franklinton, Louisiana, offering 5-mile and 10-mile distances through Washington Parish. It is organized by Q50 Races, a Louisiana trail running series that stages events at parks and wooded venues across the state.
