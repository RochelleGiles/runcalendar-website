About

Experience the love and fun at the Derek Dexter Memorial 5K. Derek was a servant-hearted leader, always putting others before himself—not for recognition, but simply because it was who he was. This 5K is more than just a race; it's a tribute to Derek's unwavering generosity, his love for running, and the countless ways he showed up for others without hesitation.
