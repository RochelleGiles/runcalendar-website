About

Explore the stunning landscapes of the French Alps at the Alpes Verdon Tour, a challenging yet picturesque running event. Runners will traverse the rugged terrain of the Verdon Gorge, one of the most breathtaking natural wonders in the region, offering a truly unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts.
