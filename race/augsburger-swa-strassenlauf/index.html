About

The Augsburger Swa Strassenlauf is a must-attend running event for enthusiasts in the region. Set against the historic backdrop of Augsburg, this street race offers a scenic and challenging course that winds through the city's charming neighborhoods. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a recreational runner, the Augsburger Swa Strassenlauf promises an unforgettable experience for all.
