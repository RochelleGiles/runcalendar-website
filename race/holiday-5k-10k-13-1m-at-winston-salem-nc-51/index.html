Course

A lapped course based at Jamison Park, Winston-Salem, with the 5K, 10K and half marathon each built from repeated laps of the same loop. Start and finish are at the park. Water is available at the finish area at the end of each lap and at the race finish.

About

Festive Holiday 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon held at Jamison Park in Winston-Salem, NC, starting at 8:00 AM. Runners complete out-and-back loops on a scenic course with finisher medals and a 3.5-hour time limit.
