About

Desafío en las Nubes, by UTMX®, is a trail and mountain running event held mainly in the municipality of Xicotepec de Juárez, Puebla, Mexico. The route crosses trails, some of them technical, along with streams, dirt roads and cross-country sections, passing through various checkpoints and aid stations.
