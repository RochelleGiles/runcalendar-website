About

The Loudéac-Pontivy Marathon is a scenic and challenging race through the stunning French countryside. Runners will traverse rolling hills and picturesque villages between the towns of Loudéac and Pontivy, offering a truly unique running experience. This historic event is a must-do for any marathon enthusiast looking to explore the natural beauty of this region.
