About

The Compton Marathon & 16 Mile Challenge are mixed terrain races around the picturesque Berkshire Downs. Both courses follow clearly marked loops that criss-cross the ancient Ridgeway, taking runners along quiet tracks, trails, bridleways, and short road sections.
