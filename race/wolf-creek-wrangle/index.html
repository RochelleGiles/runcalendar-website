Course

Three courses — 5 mile, 7 mile and half marathon — in Wolf Creek canyon in the Bighorn foothills west of Sheridan, all finishing at the historic Eatons' Ranch. The 5 mile climbs the canyon before dropping back to the ranch. Half marathon starts at 8:30 AM and the 5 and 7 mile at 9:00 AM.

About

Wolf Creek Wrangle is a trail race in the foothills of Wyoming's Bighorn Mountains near Sheridan, offering distances from 5 miles up to a half marathon on rolling forest and ranchland trails below the range.
