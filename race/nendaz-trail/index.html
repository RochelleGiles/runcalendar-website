Course

A mountain trail meeting above Nendaz in the Valais, with Rosablanche as the high point. Three adult races: Roi de Balavaux over 15.5 km with 860 m of climb, Reflets Alpins over 30 km with 1,900 m, and the Marathon de la Ros' over 42 km with 3,500 m. Children's courses of 0.5 km to 1.5 km also run.

About

Inalpe Dynafit 2.0 Trail runs through the mountains above Haute-Nendaz, in Switzerland's Valais region, tied to the valley's Inalpe tradition of driving cattle up to the alpine pastures, with trail routes from 15.5K up to the 42K Marathon de la Ros'.
