Course

A single lap of Denver's City Park, starting at 2001 Steele Street, run under the format "one lap as fast as you can go". The race measures roughly two miles, with course records of 8:52 for men and 10:30 for women. A free, untimed quarter mile for children aged 10 and under runs first.

About

The City Park Circuit is a one-lap road race around Denver's City Park, set over roughly two miles. It runs in waves: a free kids quarter mile, an open community wave that welcomes walkers, strollers and dogs, and separate competitive races for women and men.
