Course

A road and rail-trail festival based in Emporium, Pennsylvania, starting at E. 3rd and Chestnut St and running out along the West Creek Industrial Trail through the state's elk country. The marathon is a certified Boston qualifier, with half marathon, 10K and 5K also on offer.

About

Emporium PA, the land of great elks grazing on meadows and throughout the forest. This will make a surefire summer running weekend getaway. Emporium is the "Elk Capital of Pennsylvania" and is located in the heart of the state's wild elk herd, now numbering almost 1,000 majestic animals. We have all distances to make sure you are having fun and challenged at the same time!
