Course

A flat, fast 5km course in Broadwater Parklands on the Gold Coast, run as two loops. Open female, male and mixed teams run 4 x 1.25km legs; the junior mixed relay runs 4 x 2.5km. A Strava route is published for the course.

About

RECC. Run The World Relay is a team running relay held in Southport on the Gold Coast. Teams take on a 4 x 5km relay, with a junior mixed 4 x 2.5km relay also on offer for younger and family entrants.
