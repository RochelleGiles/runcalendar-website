Course

A 60km loop from Tafarn Y Fic in Llithfaen across the Llyn Peninsula to Abersoch and back via the Mariner's Trail. Mixed going over coastal path, beach and gradually ascending lanes, taking in the Steps of Hell below Yr Eifl, the Nant Gwrtheyrn descent, Morfa Nefyn, Pwllheli and Llanbedrog Headland. Four fully stocked vegan-friendly aid stations plus two water stations; relay teams welcome.

About

Tackle the rugged terrain of North Wales in the Coast2Coast Winter Ultra, a challenging 60km multi-surface ultra marathon. Runners will push their limits as they traverse a stunning natural landscape, taking on hills, trails, and coastal paths in this beautifully brutal event. With a focus on camaraderie and the thrill of the challenge, this race offers an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners.
