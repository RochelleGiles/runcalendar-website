About

Explore the picturesque trails of the Brie region at the Trails de la Brie des Morin, an exhilarating running event that takes participants through the stunning French countryside. Challenging yet rewarding, this event offers runners a unique opportunity to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the Brie des Morin area, making it a must-try for trail enthusiasts.
