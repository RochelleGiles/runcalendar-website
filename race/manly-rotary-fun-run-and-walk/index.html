Course

Three loop courses start and finish on the Manly beachfront. The 2 km follows the flat promenade footpath to North Steyne and suits prams and wheelchairs. The 5 km leaves the ocean side near Manly Surf Club along The Corso and the oceanfront path, with short sharp climbs past the old hospital toward North Head. The 10 km adds East Esplanade, North Head Scenic Drive, the Q Station loop and the Memorial Walkway.

About

The Manly Rotary Fun Run and Walk is a community event organized by the Rotary Club of Manly. The event offers three course options - a 10km Runners Challenge, a 5km Fun Run and Walk, and a 2km Fun Run and Walk. The courses feature scenic beachfront views and some challenging hill climbs, making it a great event for runners and walkers of all levels. All proceeds from the event go directly to local charitable causes, making it a meaningful way to get active and give back to the community.
