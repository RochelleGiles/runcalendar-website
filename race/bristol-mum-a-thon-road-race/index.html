Course

Road courses through the quiet neighborhoods of northeast Bristol, starting and finishing at St. Paul Catholic High School on Stafford Avenue. There is a climb before mile 1, flat middle miles and a downhill finish, and the course runs facing traffic. An untimed Monster Mile is held alongside the 8 mile, 8K and 5K.

About

This road race in Bristol, Connecticut has run annually since 1970, making it one of the longest-running community road races in the state. It offers 5K, 8K, and 8-mile distance options within a single event.
