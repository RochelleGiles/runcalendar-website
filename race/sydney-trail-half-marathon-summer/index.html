Course

A 21km trail half marathon starting and finishing at Lake Parramatta Reserve, mixing waterfront trail, technical singletrack and fire trail past a waterfall and under eucalypt canopy. 386m of climbing, 6:30am start and a four-hour cutoff.

About

The Summer Edition of the Sydney Trail Half Marathon takes runners along bush trails around Sydney, one of several seasonal editions held through the year alongside the series' Autumn, Winter and Night editions.
