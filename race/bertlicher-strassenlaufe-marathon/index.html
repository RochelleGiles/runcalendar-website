About

The Bertlicher Straßenläufe is one of Germany's oldest running events, reaching its 125th edition in Herten, North Rhine-Westphalia. The Ruhr-region race offers distances from 5km up to the marathon, including a half marathon and 30km option, run through the streets of Herten.
