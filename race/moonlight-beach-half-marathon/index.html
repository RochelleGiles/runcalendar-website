About

The Moonlight Beach Half Marathon and 10K, now past its 3rd running, starts and finishes at Moonlight State Beach in Encinitas, California, with views of the Pacific Ocean and lagoon. Finishers receive a medal and t-shirt, with chip timing and cash prizes for top finishers and best St. Patrick's-themed costumes.
