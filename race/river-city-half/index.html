Course

The half starts and finishes at Westhampton Green on the University of Richmond campus, crossing the James River twice via the Huguenot and Nickel bridges, through Westover Hills with a steep climb, past the Carillon, then Windsor Farms and River Road back to campus. Six water stops at miles 2.1, 4, 6.5, 8.4, 10 and 11.8. The 5K is an out-and-back along River Road over the Huguenot Bridge.

About

The Virginia Credit Union River City Half and 5K start at the University of Richmond and wind through city neighborhoods and riverfront parks. Held each March, the race benefits fifteen local charity partners and offers a virtual option.
