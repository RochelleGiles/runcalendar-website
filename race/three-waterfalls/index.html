Course

A technical trail run through the Krantzkloof gorge at Kloof, starting on Kloof View Road and taking in the Nkutu, Nkonka and mPhithi waterfalls, with Uve Falls seen from the Eastern Ridge. The route crosses grassland and coastal scarp forest with rocky, exposed sections including The Gulley and a sheer rock face. It drops to about 216m in the gorge and climbs to 547m, finishing over the escarpment climb known as The Wall.
