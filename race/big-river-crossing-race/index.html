Course

The course crosses the Big River Crossing, the longest public pedestrian bridge over the Mississippi, linking Memphis with the Arkansas side. The half marathon is fully paved on the Arkansas side, while the 5K runs along the river through Tom Lee Park. The start is at Martyrs Park at 8:30 AM.

About

The Big River Crossing Race in Memphis, Tennessee, crosses the Mississippi River on the Big River Crossing pedestrian bridge, the longest such bridge over the river. The race offers 5K, 10K and half marathon distances along the riverfront.
