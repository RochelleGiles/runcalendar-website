Course

Point-to-point from the Bass Pro Shops Grand Entrance on S Campbell Avenue to the John A. and Genny Morris Conservation Center on W Sunshine in Springfield. Mostly flat with a few rolling hills, running through neighbourhoods, paved trails, part of historic Route 66 and past Missouri State University and downtown. Water and Powerade roughly every two miles.

About

The Bass Pro Shops Conservation Marathon is part of the Bass Pro Shops Fitness Series in Springfield, Missouri. It includes a full marathon, the Cohick Half Marathon, and a 5K, drawing runners from across the Ozarks each November.
