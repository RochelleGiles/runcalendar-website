Course

A paved loop half marathon starting and finishing at Memorial Park on the east side of Eldora. The route follows the River's Edge Trail along the Iowa River Greenbelt through Pine Lake State Park to Steamboat Rock and back. A walk division starts an hour ahead of the run.

About

The River's Edge Trail Half Marathon follows Eldora's River's Edge Trail along the Iowa River in central Iowa. Runners taking on the half marathon distance follow the trail's riverside scenery from start to finish.
