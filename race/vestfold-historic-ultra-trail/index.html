About

VHUT is a trail ultrarun that goes through some of the best terrain in northern Vestfold. Only 6% asphalt for the long course! Our goal is to offer the runners a proper trail experience, the motto is 'demanding but fair'. The course goes through 2 counties and no less than 5 municipalities, and along the way the runners pass many places of local historical significance.
