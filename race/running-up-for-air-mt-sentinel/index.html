Course

A four-mile lap with about 1,900 ft of climb, starting at the M-Trail car park in Missoula. Each lap climbs the M switchbacks, then the steep Northwest Ridge Trail to the summit of Mt Sentinel, descending by the Boy Scout Trail and M Trail to the start. A small aid station sits at the summit. Runners repeat the lap as often as they can inside a 3, 6, 12 or 24 hour limit.

About

Running Up for Air (RUFA) Mt. Sentinel is a 3, 6, 12, or 24-hour winter endurance challenge on Mt. Sentinel (5,158’ elevation), the iconic mountain on the edge of Missoula. Participants ascend the mountain as many times as possible to raise awareness and funds for Climate Smart Missoula's efforts to improve air quality.
