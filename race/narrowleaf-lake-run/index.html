Course

A flat lakefront course at Boyd Lake State Park, Loveland, starting and finishing in the park. The organiser states the route is on paved walkways with about half a mile off road on the south loop. Aid stations are placed along the route, the number varying by distance.

About

Brisk autumn running event at Boyd Lake State Park with five distance options including kids run, 15K, 10K, 5K, and 1 Mile Pumpkin race. The flat course features paved walkways, minimal elevation gain, lake views, wildlife, and fun post-race festivities.
