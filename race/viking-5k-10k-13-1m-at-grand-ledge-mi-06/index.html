Course

Lapped out-and-back course at Mount Hope Park, Grand Ledge, Michigan. The 5K and 10K use a 0.78-mile out-and-back segment run two and four times respectively; the half marathon repeats a 1.09-mile out-and-back segment six times. All entries are online only, with no race-day registration.

About

The Viking 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is held at Mount Hope Park in Grand Ledge, Michigan, featuring out-and-back courses with finisher medals for all participants. The event starts at 8:30 AM with a 3.5-hour time limit and no on-site registration.
