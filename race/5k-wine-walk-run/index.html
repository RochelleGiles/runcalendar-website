Course

Two laps of a mostly cross-country route through the vineyards at Fence Stile Vineyards, Winery & Distillery, starting and finishing on the winery grounds. Runners who prefer not to take the vines a second time can step out after the first lap and finish on the grounds.

About

The 5K Wine Walk/Run is held at Fence Stile Vineyards & Winery in Excelsior Springs, Missouri, combining a run or walk through the vineyard with tastings of the winery's wines.
