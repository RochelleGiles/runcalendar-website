Course

A road half marathon and 10K starting from the Quays Shopping Centre in Newry and finishing at Quayside Close Community Centre in Drumalane. Both distances set off at 10am and are open to runners aged 18 and over.

About

The Newry City Half Marathon & 10K is a popular running event hosted by the Newry City Runners club. The courses are known to be flat and fast, making them perfect for personal bests. The event is well-organized and has a great atmosphere, attracting runners from across the region.
