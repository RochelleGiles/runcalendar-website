About

This annual 5K run/walk and kids fun run honors fallen heroes from the Union City Police Department, including Officer Ricardo Fernandez. The event starts and finishes at Washington Park in Union City, NJ, with prizes for top finishers and guaranteed T-shirts for pre-registered participants.
