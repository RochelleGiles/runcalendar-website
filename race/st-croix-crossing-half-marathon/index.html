About

The St. Croix Crossing Half Marathon starts in western Wisconsin and ends in downtown Stillwater, Minnesota. This fully paved course features scenic tree-lined roads, picturesque farms, the Loop Trail, and crosses the St. Croix Crossing bridge, finishing at the historic Stillwater Lift Bridge.
