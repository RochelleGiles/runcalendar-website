Course

All events start and finish at Les Peart Oval, Pool Road, Mount Beauty, in the Upper Kiewa Valley. The programme mixes road and path courses (3 km, 10.6 km, 21.1 km) with a separate 18 km trail run. Drink stations are provided on course, but runners are asked to carry their own bottles.

About

One of Australia's most scenic foot races, the Mt Beauty Running Festival is a fun-filled day with something for the whole family. Runners can enjoy the beautiful autumn colors in the Upper Kiewa Valley as they take on distances ranging from a 3k to an 18k trail run.
