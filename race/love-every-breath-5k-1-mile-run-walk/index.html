About

Pulmonary fibrosis (PF) is a disease that causes scarring in the lung, which makes it difficult for patients to breathe. Despite its devastation, there unfortunately remains no cure for those with pulmonary fibrosis. This event aims to fundraise for the Pulmonary Fibrosis Foundation, which has demonstrated long-standing support for research and patients with PF!
