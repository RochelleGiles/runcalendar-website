About

Run FORE Cancer takes place at Scottsdale Silverado Golf Club's Club House, offering three distances: the Front 9/5K, the Full 18/8K and a 1-mile Survivor Walk. It began as a tribute run in memory of Kathy Maxwell, who lost her battle with sarcoma cancer.
