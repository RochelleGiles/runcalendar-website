Course

A four-day festival of running across Tameside, Oldham and Glossop made up of four races: a trail 10K at Daisy Nook, a six-mile fell race at Stalybridge, an out-and-back half marathon on the Longdendale Trail, and a closing seven-mile road race starting and finishing at Hyde Town Hall. Terrain ranges from trail and fell to tarmac. Runners may enter single races or the full four-day challenge.

About

The Tour is a four-day running festival across Tameside, Oldham and Glossop, with a different race each day on the fringe of the Peak District. Distances range from 10K to half marathon, and many runners return each day for the full multi-stage challenge.
