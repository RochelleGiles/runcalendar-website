About

Join us at Vasona Lake Park in Los Gatos for the JW House Hope Run, a 5K and 10K supporting families in medical crisis run by the JW House Student Board. Both races begin at 9:00 AM at the Pier Parking Lot and follow a scenic course along the lake and the surrounding park trails. We are open to all runners, walkers, and teams! The course is fully accessible and stroller-friendly, perfect for all families and participants of all levels of experience. All proceeds benefit JW House and help keep families together during medical emergencies.
