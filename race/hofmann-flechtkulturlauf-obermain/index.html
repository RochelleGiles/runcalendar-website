About

The HOFMANN-Flechtkulturlauf Obermain is a popular running event in Lichtenfels, Germany. The flat course follows the 'Path of Wicker Culture' and is suitable for runners of all levels, from beginners to professionals. The event is part of the larger Lichtenfels Wine Festival, offering a great opportunity for companies and authorities to organize a team-building activity for their employees.
