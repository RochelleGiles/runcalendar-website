Course

The half marathon and 5K both start on Michigan Avenue in front of the Lansing Center, taking in Michigan Ave, Grand River Ave, the MSU campus and the Lansing River Trail before finishing in downtown Lansing near Grand Ave and Adado Park. Kids races run a one-mile course on the river trail from the Impression 5 Science Center.

About

The Capital City River Run is held each September in Lansing, Michigan, offering a half marathon, half marathon relay, 5K and kids races. The courses run through downtown Lansing, past the Michigan State campus and along the Lansing River Trail beside the Grand River.
