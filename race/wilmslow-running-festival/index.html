About

The Wilmslow Running Festival offers runners a scenic tour through the picturesque Cheshire countryside, featuring the iconic Wilmslow Half Marathon and a challenging 10K distance. This annual event is a must for runners seeking a beautiful and rewarding race experience in the heart of the United Kingdom.
