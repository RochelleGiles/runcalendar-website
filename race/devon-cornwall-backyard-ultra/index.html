About

The Devon & Cornwall Backyard Ultra is a unique last-one-standing endurance challenge set in the picturesque landscapes of southwest England. Runners will test their limits as they navigate the scenic trails of Newnham Park in Plymouth, taking on laps of a 4.167-mile loop until only one competitor remains. This event promises an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners seeking an extraordinary test of their mental and physical fortitude.
