Course

An eight-hour lapped challenge on a 4.366-mile coastal loop taking in sanded beach, rocky coastline, forest trail and grass fields. Runners complete as many laps as they can: three laps makes half-marathon distance, six a marathon, seven or more an ultra.

About

An eight-hour timed running challenge on trails around Gullane on Scotland's East Lothian coast, near the Gullane Bents beach and dunes. Runners complete as many laps of the coastal loop as possible within the time limit.
