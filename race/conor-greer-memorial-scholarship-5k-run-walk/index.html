About

A 5k run/walk to benefit the Conor Greer Memorial Scholarship. This scholarship was created to recognize multisport athlete(s) and NHS members graduating from Liberty-Benton. All funds raised will go directly to the scholarship account to enable multiple rewards each graduation year. The race will begin and end at the High School football stadium on Saturday April 11th. Start time is scheduled for 9am.
