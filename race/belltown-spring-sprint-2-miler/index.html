Course

A two-mile run and walk through the neighbourhoods surrounding East Hampton High School, on sealed streets with no cross-country trails or hills, and volunteers posted at every turn to keep runners on course. The kids run is held entirely on the high school track.

About

The Belltown Spring Sprint is a community 2-mile run and walk in the Belltown neighborhood of East Hampton, Connecticut, with a kids' fun run alongside the main event. It's a low-key early-summer race for local families and casual runners.
