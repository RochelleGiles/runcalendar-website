Course

Run through Minerva Hills National Park above Springsure in the Queensland Central Highlands. The 32km takes in all four mountain lookouts, with 17km, 10km and 5km options and an untimed 1.6km fun run. Participants may walk, jog or run at their own pace.

About

Springsure Mountain Challenge is a community trail run in the Central Highlands of Queensland, with distances from 1.6km to 32km across the hills around the town of Springsure, for runners of all abilities.
