Course

A downhill point-to-point 5K on the paved streets of Bel Air, starting on Main Street and running out through residential roads. Only Main Street and Lee Way are closed to traffic, with marshals and police at the major intersections and the route marked in white chalk. A "Fit For Duty" 5K in turnout gear and a one-mile fun run are also held.

About

The Bel Air Town Run is a 5K and one-mile road race through the streets of Bel Air, Maryland, organized by the Harford County Running Club. It's a community event that draws local runners and families of all ages each summer.
