Course

All distances run in and around Kapiolani Park in Honolulu. The 5-mile Turkey Trot loops Kapiolani Park and Triangle Park from a 7:30 am start, the 3.6-mile run and walk goes twice around Kapiolani Park at 7:40 am, and the 1.8-mile fun run circles Queen Kapiolani Park at 7:50 am. A self-paced virtual 10K runs alongside.
