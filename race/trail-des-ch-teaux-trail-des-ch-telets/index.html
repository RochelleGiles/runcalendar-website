About

A December trail race from Sion, in the Swiss canton of Valais, run on the same day as the town's Christmas race. The 32 km Trail des Châteaux climbs to the Château de Tourbillon and loops through Savièse, Arbaz and Grimisuat; the 14 km Trail des Châtelets has 700 m of climbing and a 300-runner cap.
