Course

The 10K starts just west of the toll gates on the New Jersey side, crosses the Ben Franklin Bridge westbound, turns on the Philadelphia side and returns eastbound, then runs through Camden streets and around Wiggins Marina to finish on the Camden Athletic Complex track. Surfaces are bridge lanes, city streets, brick promenade, paved path and track, with a 90 minute limit. The 2 Mile walk is an out-and-back to the bridge's 1-mile marker.

About

The Cooper Norcross Run the Bridge is a 10K road race in Camden, New Jersey, with a course that crosses the Benjamin Franklin Bridge over the Delaware River into Philadelphia.
