Course

All races start and finish near the Meyer Amphitheatre on Flagler Drive in downtown West Palm Beach, on the Intracoastal waterfront. The marathon, half marathon and marathon relay run on the Sunday; the 5K and 10K are held the day before.

About

The Palm Beaches Marathon is an annual December race through West Palm Beach, Florida, offering marathon, half marathon, 10K, 5K and relay distances. The race draws runners from across Florida for a winter marathon weekend on the state's southeast coast.
