Course

Four and eight mile routes on the singletrack network of Forest Ridge Park, Wake Forest, a 587-acre peninsula beside Falls Lake. Parts of the course run on the remains of the abandoned "ghost road" stretch of NC Route 98, cut off when the lake was created in 1981.

About

The Ghost Road Trail Run is a trail running event held at Forest Ridge Park in Wake Forest, NC, featuring courses that include single-track trails and sections of the abandoned ghost road North Carolina Route 98. Races are limited in participants due to parking constraints at the 587-acre park on Falls Lake.
