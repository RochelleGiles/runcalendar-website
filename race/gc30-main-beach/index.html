About

The fisiocrem GC30 is a road and trail run on The Spit at Main Beach, Gold Coast, starting and finishing at Doug Jennings Park. Entrants choose a 30 km, 15 km or 7.5 km course, split roughly half sealed road and half hard-packed trail along the coastal reserve.
