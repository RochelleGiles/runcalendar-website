About

The Wolverhampton Half Marathon and 10K race offers runners a scenic tour through the historic streets of Wolverhampton, United Kingdom. Challenging yet rewarding, this event is a must for runners seeking an unforgettable running experience in the heart of the Midlands. With a mix of urban landscapes and picturesque countryside, this race promises an exhilarating challenge for athletes of all levels.
