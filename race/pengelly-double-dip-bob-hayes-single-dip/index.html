Course

Both races start at River Bowl East on the south side of the Clark Fork River near the UM footbridge at 8:00 am. The Pengelly Double Dip is a tall half marathon-plus with 3,400 ft of elevation gain and is rated very challenging; the Bob Hayes Single Dip is a 10K with what the organisers call the toughest first mile of any 10K, easing off afterwards for the best views in town.

About

The Pengelly Double Dip and Bob Hayes Single Dip are trail races in Missoula. The Double Dip is a half marathon-plus with 3,400 ft of elevation gain, rated very challenging, while the Single Dip is a 10K known for a tough opening mile that eases into some of the best views in town. Both offer in-person and virtual participation, with mimosas and pizza waiting at the finish.
