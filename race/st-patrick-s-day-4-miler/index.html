About

For 24 years, the St. Patrick's Day 4 miler has been a local tradition which kicks off the new running season. For the past 10 years, the net race proceeds of approximately $40,000 have been used to support the community outreach programs of St. Patrick's Church. Those in need in our community are provided food, clothing and showers thanks to a group of volunteers led by St. Patrick's Church in Binghamton.
