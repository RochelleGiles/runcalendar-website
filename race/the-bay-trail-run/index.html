About

The Bay Trail Run invites runners to explore the picturesque Nelson region of New Zealand, with multiple distance options to challenge every skill level. Participants will be treated to incredible coastal views as they wind their way along the scenic Bay Trail, making this a must-do event for trail running enthusiasts.
