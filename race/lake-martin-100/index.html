Course

Two 25.09-mile loops through the private Russell Forest Trail System on the shores of Lake Martin, run as a 50 miler (two laps) or a 50K (one lap plus a partial loop). The singletrack is runnable and non-technical by Southeast standards, though there are roots and stretches of fist-sized granite. Each loop climbs over 3,000 ft in constant short rolling hills.

About

A trail ultramarathon near Alexander City, Alabama offering 50K and 50-mile distances along the wooded shores of Lake Martin. Held each March as part of the Southeastern Trail Series, it draws ultrarunners from across the Southeast.
