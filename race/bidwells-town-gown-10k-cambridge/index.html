Course

Starts and finishes on Midsummer Common, running on closed city-centre roads through the heart of Cambridge past the historic university colleges and along the River Cam. A 3K route for ages 9-15 follows a shortened version of the same closed-road course. Both are marshalled and signed throughout.

About

The Bidwells Town & Gown 10K in Cambridge is an annual fundraiser for Muscular Dystrophy UK, offering runners a unique opportunity to race through the historic streets and landmarks of this iconic university city. Featuring a scenic course that winds past picturesque colleges and the River Cam, this event promises an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
