Course

A 5K and a 1-mile run from Skyline Camp and Retreat Center on Sandhill Road, Almont, entirely off public roads on wooded trail, open field and a private dirt road. Expect steep climbs and descents plus rocks, roots and other natural obstructions underfoot. The 5K starts at 9am, the mile ten minutes later.

About

September will bring runners, walkers, amblers, and wanderers to the woods and fields of Almont's beautiful Camp Skyline. Skyline boasts 200 acres, including the highest point in Lapeer County.
