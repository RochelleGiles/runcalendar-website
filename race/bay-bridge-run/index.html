About

The Bay Bridge Run is a 10K road race crossing the eastbound span of the Chesapeake Bay Bridge, starting in Anne Arundel County and finishing on Kent Island in Queen Anne's County. It is organized by Corrigan Sports and held each November, drawing runners for its post-race celebration on the island.
