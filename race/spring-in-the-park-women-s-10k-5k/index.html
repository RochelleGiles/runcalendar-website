About

Spring in the Park is a women's race held in the Cleveland Metroparks, starting from the South Mastick Picnic Area in Fairview Park, Ohio. The event offers a 10K, a 5K, a 5K ruck and a one-mile run and walk, with a course map published via RideWithGPS.
