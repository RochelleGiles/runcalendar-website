About

Lanzarote International Marathon is held on the sunny Island of Lanzarote, Canary Islands, in December with average air temperature around 21ºC, under blue sky and usually sunny weather runs along the east coastline of the Island. Truly for all ages, abilities form amateurs to professional runners. The race is held in a friendly, open and warm local atmosphere.
