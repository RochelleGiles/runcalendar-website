Course

All four races start and finish at The Morrison Center on the Boise State campus and run out along the flat, scenic Boise Greenbelt beside the river. The marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K Spud Run all share the greenbelt route.

About

Be a part of our biggest race of the year! Running the Famous Idaho Potato Marathon offers a unique opportunity to experience Boise's scenic beauty while challenging yourself on a well-organized, supportive course. Join the fun with the 5K or 10K distances or push your limits with the half or full marathon. Connect with fellow runners and celebrate Idaho's famous spuds!
