Course

A lapped route starting and finishing at Sawyer Point Park, 705 East Pete Rose Way, Cincinnati. The 10K runs two laps and the 15K three laps of the same circuit. Surface and aid stations are not stated by the organiser.

About

The Couple Shuffle is a Valentine-themed road event staged by Bodies Race Company at Sawyer Point Park on the Cincinnati riverfront. Runners choose a 5K, 10K or 15K built from laps of the same circuit, with a kids dash alongside.
