Course

An out-and-back road course starting on Wilson Street outside the Burnie City Council Chambers. Runners head south to the Bass Highway, east to a 5km turnaround, then back west along the highway before turning into Wilson Street for the final 300m to the finish. Three drink stations sit at 2.5km, 5km and 7.5km, with a two-hour limit for the 10km. 5km and 800m junior events also run.

About

The Burnie 10 is a road running event on Tasmania's north-west coast, offering 10km, 5km and 800m junior distances. The 10km is an out-and-back course starting on Wilson Street in central Burnie and running east along the Bass Highway to a 5km turnaround, with three drink stations and a two-hour time limit.
