About

Eaton County Parks and Recreation will host our 8th Annual Timber Trails 5K at scenic Fitzgerald Park. This run/walk event is routed on a slightly challenging course with ranging elevations over grass, gravel roads, and wooded trails. A perfect way for outdoor lovers to enjoy a brisk fall morning in one of the most beautiful parks in Mid-Michigan. Proceeds from the event will benefit trail development within Eaton County Parks. All ages are encouraged to join!
