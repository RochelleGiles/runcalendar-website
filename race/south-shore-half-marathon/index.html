Course

An out-and-back half marathon on a paved recreational path along the Lake Michigan shore, starting and finishing at the South Shore Pavilion, 2900 South Shore Drive, Milwaukee. Two start waves are offered, 8.15am for runners needing three hours or more and 9am for everyone else.

About

The South Shore Half Marathon is a half marathon in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, organized by the Badgerland Striders running club along the Lake Michigan shoreline near the Bay View neighborhood's South Shore Park.
