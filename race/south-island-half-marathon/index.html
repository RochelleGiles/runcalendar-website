Course

A fast, flat certified course looping around Lake Hood just outside Ashburton, taking in lake views and passing through home and garden settings and reserves. The half marathon is offered in run, hybrid and walk options alongside a 7km one-sixth marathon and a 3km Kidz Dash. The course is measured by a World Athletics-certified road measurer and graded with AIMS.

About

The South Island Half Marathon is a premier running event in New Zealand's scenic South Island. The course takes runners through stunning landscapes, showcasing the region's natural beauty. With a welcoming atmosphere and a dedicated community of participants, this event offers an unforgettable running experience.
