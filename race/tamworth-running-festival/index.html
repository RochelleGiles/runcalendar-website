Course

All races start at No.1 Oval in Tamworth, offering a marathon, half marathon, 10km, 5km fun run and a 6km community walk on sealed city roads and paths. Water stations sit at 3km, 5km, 8km, 11km and 15km as well as the finish.

About

The Tamworth Running Festival is an annual running celebration in Australia, offering a variety of distances to challenge athletes of all levels. From a scenic full Marathon to more approachable 10K and 5K options, this event has something for every runner seeking an exciting new adventure.
