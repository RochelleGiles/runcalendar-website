About

The Wicklow Way Race is a challenging ultra-running event that takes runners along the scenic Wicklow Way from Clonegal, Carlow to Marlay Park, Dublin. The race features over 3,000 meters of elevation gain and a maximum time allowance of 21 hours, making it an extreme test of endurance and mountain running skills.
