Course

All events start and finish at the Century Center in downtown South Bend, with routes winding through the city centre, along the St Joseph River and out through local neighbourhoods. Half marathon, 10K, 5K run and a 5K fitness walk are offered.

About

Sunburst Races brings marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K distances through South Bend, Indiana, with runners finishing on the University of Notre Dame campus near the Golden Dome, a tradition for the region's runners each spring.
