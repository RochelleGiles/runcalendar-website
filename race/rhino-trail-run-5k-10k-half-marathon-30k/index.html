Course

Off-road trail race in the 1,500-acre Mount Airy Forest in Cincinnati, starting and finishing at the forest playground. The 5K has its own route; the half marathon covers two loops of the 10K route and the 30K covers three. Races start at 8:00 AM with bib pickup from 7:30.

About

A themed trail running event held in Mt. Airy Forest, Cincinnati's largest park with nearly 1,500 acres of beautiful off-road trails. The race welcomes runners and walkers of all levels, with finisher medals, costume awards, and a supportive community atmosphere.
