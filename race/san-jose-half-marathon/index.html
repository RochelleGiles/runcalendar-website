Course

All races start at Santana Row in San Jose. The half marathon is a flat and fast road course, run alongside an 8K of just under five miles on the Sunday. A Speed City Mile is held on the Saturday and a kids run covers 200m to half a mile by age group.

About

The San Jose Half Marathon sends runners through the streets of downtown San Jose and Silicon Valley, with Half Marathon, 8K, and 1 Mile options. The event benefits Special Olympics Northern California and draws a large local field each June.
