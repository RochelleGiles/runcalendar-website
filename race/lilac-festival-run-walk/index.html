Course

Run entirely on the paved roads of Mackinac Island, where no cars are permitted. The start is on Main Street in front of Wyndemere Park; the route climbs through the wooded island interior on Truscott, Huron and Garrison roads before dropping to the shore road with views of the lake and the Mackinac Bridge, finishing in the island school yard.

About

The Lilac Festival Run & Walk kicks off the Mackinac Island Lilac Festival each summer. The course runs entirely on the island's paved, car-free roads through tree-covered terrain, with lake views and the Mackinac Bridge on the horizon.
