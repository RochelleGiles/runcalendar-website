Course

Flat, fast routes on the Leeds to Liverpool Canal towpath and compact trail, starting and finishing at Aireville Park in Skipton. Distances run out-and-back or lapped depending on length, from 10K up to a marathon and ultra of two laps plus 10km. Feed stations roughly every three miles and waterside views throughout.

About

A multi-distance trail event from 5K up to marathon, starting in Skipton on the edge of the Yorkshire Dales. Routes climb into the surrounding limestone countryside before returning to the market town.
