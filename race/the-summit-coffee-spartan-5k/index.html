Course

Starts and finishes at The Village Green on South Main Street in Davidson, North Carolina, run through the town centre alongside the Davidson Town Day celebration. Entries are taken at 5K and a separate student 5K for ages 1 to 18, both setting off at 8:00am.

About

The Summit Coffee Spartan 5K is an annual 5K race held in conjunction with the Davidson Town Day Celebration to benefit the Community School of Davidson's Athletic Department. 100% of proceeds support student athletes at the school.
