Course

A mix of jeep road and singletrack on the rims northwest of Moab, starting and finishing at the Seven Mile parking lot near Arches National Park. The race opens with a climb up a jeep road before rim trails open out to Canyonlands National Park and the La Sal Mountains. Cupless event: runners carry their own cup and refill at dispensers on course.

About

Moab's Red Hot Ultra, held each February by Mad Moose Events, is one of the longer-running ultramarathons in the Moab, Utah desert trail scene. Distances range from 15K to 50 miles across red-rock canyon terrain outside town.
