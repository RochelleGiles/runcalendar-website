Course

All three races are out-and-back courses on the Ashuwillticook Rail Trail, starting and finishing at the Adams Visitors Center on Hoosac Street. Water stops sit at every even mile from 2 through 24, with additional nutrition offered from mile 6 onwards. A free kids' race is also held.

About

Steel Rail Races includes a marathon, half marathon and 8K, each an out-and-back course on the Ashuwillticook Rail Trail starting at the Adams Visitor Center in the Berkshires. Organized by the Berkshire Running Foundation, the event raises funds for trail upkeep.
