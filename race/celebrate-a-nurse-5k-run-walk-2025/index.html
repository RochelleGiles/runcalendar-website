About

Join us for the 4th annual Celebrate a Nurse 5K, which helps fund scholarships for nursing students at Madison College's Reedsburg campus. Just ahead of National Nursing Week, the event brings together participants from Madison College, Reedsburg, surrounding communities and local healthcare organizations.
