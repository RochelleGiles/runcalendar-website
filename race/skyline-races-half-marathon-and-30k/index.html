Course

Point-to-point route starting on the Skyline Trail along a sandstone ridge above Billings and finishing at Two Moon Park. It joins Skyway Drive over rolling hills, then follows the Alkali Creek Trail beside the creek. The surface is mostly paved with some gravel near Two Moon Park, with moderate elevation change. Parking is at Centennial Ice Arena with a shuttle to the start.

About

The Skyline Races take runners along the Rimrocks above Billings, Montana, on a point-to-point course that finishes at Two Moon Park. The route is mostly paved with moderate elevation change and opens out over the Yellowstone Valley and, on a clear day, the Beartooth Mountains. Both a half marathon and a 30K are offered.
