Course

Race headquarters, start and finish are at Lake Chabot Marina, Castro Valley, on the fire roads and trails of the East Bay Regional Park ridgeline. The day runs a 100K, the Firetrails 50 mile and the Golden Hills Marathon over the same terrain. Aid stations are set along the course.

About

The Dick Collins Firetrails 50 runs point-to-point through East Bay Regional Park District land, from the Oakland hills into Castro Valley on redwood and oak-shaded fire trails. It also hosts 100K and marathon distances on the same course.
