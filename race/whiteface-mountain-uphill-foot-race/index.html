Course

An 8 mile uphill-only race up the Whiteface Mountain Veterans' Memorial Highway, starting at the junction of Route 431 and Route 86 and finishing near the summit of New York's fifth-highest peak. The course is sealed highway the whole way and climbs about 3,500 feet, opening out to views across the Adirondacks, New England and Canada.

About

The Whiteface Mountain Uphill Foot Race is an 8-mile road race up the Whiteface Veterans Memorial Highway, climbing from Wilmington, New York to near the summit of Whiteface Mountain in the Adirondacks.
