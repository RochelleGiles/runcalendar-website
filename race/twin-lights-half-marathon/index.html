About

The Twin Lights Half Marathon starts and finishes at Good Harbor Beach in Gloucester as part of the YuKanRun race series. The 13.1-mile course is USATF certified, and runners can pair it with the Twin Lobster Half Marathon held in June as a discounted two-race bundle.
