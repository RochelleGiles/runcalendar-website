About

The PKO Wrocław Marathon is a must-run event for seasoned runners and newbies alike. Winding through the historic streets of Wrocław, this picturesque course offers a unique challenge with its mix of flat stretches and gentle hills. Runners can soak in the vibrant atmosphere of this lively Polish city as they push towards the finish line.
