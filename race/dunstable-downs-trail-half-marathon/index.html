Course

Trail half marathon across the National Trust's Dunstable Downs estate, starting and finishing at Dunstable Downs at 09:45. The route takes in sweeping ridge views with fast descents and tough climbs, marked throughout with arrow markers and orange ribbons. Capped at 250 runners with a four-hour limit.

About

Explore the rolling chalk ridge trails of the Dunstable Downs Estate during this rugged half marathon challenge. Runners will traverse the scenic National Trust parkland, taking in panoramic views over the Bedfordshire countryside. This off-road adventure offers a unique test of endurance and trail running skills.
