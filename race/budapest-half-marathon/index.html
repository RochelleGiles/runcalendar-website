About

The Budapest Half Marathon is a must-do event for runners seeking an unforgettable European adventure. Weaving through the stunning architecture and historic landmarks of Hungary's capital, this race offers a unique opportunity to experience the vibrant culture and stunning scenery of Budapest. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time half-marathoner, the Budapest Half Marathon promises an exhilarating challenge and an unparalleled running experience.
