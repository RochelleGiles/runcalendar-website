About

The Sainte Trail Urbain is the perfect opportunity to combine sport and heritage. The City of Saint-Etienne opens the doors to its most emblematic places: the Mining Museum, the Geoffroy Guichard Stadium, the Cité du Design, and many others. Trail running, Nordic walking, or hiking are offered to explore the alleys and numerous stairs of the city.
