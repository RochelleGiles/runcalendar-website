About

The Susan G. Komen Minnesota Race for the Cure is a 5K run/walk held in Eagan, Minnesota, alongside a one-mile walk option. Entry fees and fundraising support breast cancer research, patient navigation and advocacy programmes across the state. The field is made up largely of teams, survivors and families rather than competitive runners.
