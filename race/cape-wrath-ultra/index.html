About

Embark on an unforgettable 400km journey through the breathtaking Scottish Highlands at the Cape Wrath Ultra, an 8-day ultra running expedition that challenges participants to conquer the rugged and captivating terrain of this remote region of the United Kingdom. This exceptional event offers runners the chance to immerse themselves in the stunning natural beauty of the Scottish landscape while pushing their physical and mental limits to the fullest.
