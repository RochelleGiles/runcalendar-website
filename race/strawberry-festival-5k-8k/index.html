Course

Both races start at the Court House steps on State Street and run through the village of Albion, New York, and out along the historic Erie Canal. The 5K covers 3.14 miles and the 8K five miles, and both courses are certified.

About

The signature event that opens the Albion Strawberry Festival each year. This event carries on the tradition of its founder Judy Christopher and offers a 5K and 8K option with both races certified.
