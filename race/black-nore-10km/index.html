Course

A 10K starting at Portishead Open Air Pool in the Lake Grounds and running along the edge of the Bristol Channel past Black Nore Lighthouse and through Redcliffe Bay. Terrain is mixed - grass, mud, rock and tarmac on undulating coastal path - with a steep climb at halfway where the water station sits, a shorter climb near the sailing club, then a fast finish along the esplanade.
