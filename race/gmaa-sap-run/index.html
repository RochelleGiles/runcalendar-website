Course

Certified 10K out and back on muddy, hilly country roads through rural Westford, Vermont. Starts at Westford Elementary School, heads south on Brookside Road then Pettingill Road, turns at mile 3 and returns the same way to finish in the school driveway. Course certified VT21003JK.

About

The GMAA Sap Run is a 10K road race in Westford, Vermont, hosted by the Green Mountain Athletic Association during the state's maple sugaring season in late March.
