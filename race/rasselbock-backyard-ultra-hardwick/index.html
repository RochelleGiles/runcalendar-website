Course

A single 4.16-mile (6.71km) loop of the Hardwick Hall Estate, repeated on the hour from a fixed starting corral. The lap mixes estate trails, woodland and farmland tracks with small amounts of tarmac, and climbs about 69m each time round.

About

Explore the stunning grounds of Hardwick Hall Estate in Chesterfield at the Rasselbock Backyard Ultra. This unique running event challenges participants to complete laps of an undisclosed course, with the last runner standing declared the winner. Runners of all abilities are invited to test their limits in this captivating backyard-style ultramarathon.
