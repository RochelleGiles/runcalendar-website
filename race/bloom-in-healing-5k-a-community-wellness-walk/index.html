About

Bloom in Healing is a community wellness walk rooted in faith, prayer, encouragement, and connection. Come walk, breathe, and bloom with us. This free event was created as a gentle space to pause, breathe, and move forward—together. Bloom in Healing is a community wellness walk designed for individuals and families who may be carrying stress, grief, anxiety, or simply the weight of life and need a moment to reset. If you're feeling overwhelmed or in need of fresh air and encouragement, this space is for you. Throughout our time together, we'll walk at an easy pace while sharing moments of prayer, encouragement, reflection, and connection. This experience is not about performance or pressure—it's about presence, community, and taking intentional steps toward healing and wholeness.
