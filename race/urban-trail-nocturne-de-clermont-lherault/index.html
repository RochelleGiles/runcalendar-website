About

Discover the enchanting Urban Trail Nocturne de Clermont-l'Hérault, a captivating night race that winds through the historic streets and scenic landscapes of this charming French town. Runners will be treated to a unique and atmospheric experience as they navigate the challenging trails under the glow of the moon, taking in the stunning natural beauty that surrounds them.
