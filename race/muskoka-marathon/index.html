Course

Flat out-and-back courses along the Muskoka River from Santa's Village in Bracebridge, with hydration stations every 2.5 km. The 10K runs at 7:30am and the 5K at 9:30am on the Saturday; the marathon, a double out-and-back, goes at 7:30am on the Sunday with the half at 8:00am. The marathon is a certified Boston qualifier.

About

The Muskoka Marathon runs through Bracebridge, Ontario, in the heart of Muskoka cottage country. Marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K options wind along the region's lakes and forested roads each October.
