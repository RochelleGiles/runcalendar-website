About

The River to Bay 5K Classic is organised by the Kiwanis Club of Greater Toms River and run on a USATF-certified course at Island Heights, New Jersey. It pairs a timed 5K with a 5K health walk plus one-mile run and walk options, and proceeds support local children's charities.
