Course

Paved-path 10K starting at Mohawk Harbor in Schenectady and finishing beside the Rivers Casino and Landing Hotel courtyard. The only real climb is about 60 ft between miles 1.0 and 1.5, after which the course runs downhill for two miles and then stays flat. A 1:30 cutoff applies.

About

The Electric City 10K, now past its sixth running, is the first major Capital District road race of the spring season, staged from Mohawk Harbor in Schenectady, New York, with an energetic, festive atmosphere.
