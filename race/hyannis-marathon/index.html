About

The Hyannis Marathon offers a USATF-certified, Boston-qualifying course on Cape Cod, with the marathon run as two loops and the half as one. The flat, paved route passes Craigville and Kalmus beaches, Hyannis Harbor, and Lewis Bay, with a 10K option on a separate course.
