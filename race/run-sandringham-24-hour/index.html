Course

A 24-hour race on a five-mile multi-terrain looped trail around the Sandringham Estate, running from noon Saturday to noon Sunday. Entrants complete as many laps as they can, solo or in relay pairs and teams of up to 12. Head torches are mandatory from 20:15. An event village provides food and drink, with official timing and medical cover throughout.

About

A major event in the East Anglian running calendar, Run Sandringham 24 will attract a large field of runners of all abilities to the Winter Residence of The Royal Family, to run a 24 Hour Race around the Sandringham Estate and surrounding trails, while raising thousands of pounds for good causes.
