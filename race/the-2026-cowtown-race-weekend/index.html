Course

Races start at the Will Rogers Memorial Center in Fort Worth's Cultural District, run north to the Historic Stockyards, through downtown and the Near Southside to the TCU campus, then return along the Trinity River through Trinity Park to finish at Will Rogers. The weekend spans ultra, marathon, half marathon, 10K and 5K races over two days.

About

The Cowtown Marathon, now past its 48th running, is the largest multi-event road race in North Texas, based at Will Rogers Memorial Center in Fort Worth, Texas. It has an estimated $10.4 million annual economic impact and includes one of the largest youth races in the nation, with multiple virtual options available.
