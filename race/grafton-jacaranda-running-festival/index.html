About

The Jetts Grafton Jacaranda Running Festival is held in Grafton, northern New South Wales, during the city's annual Jacaranda Festival. The event starts at Memorial Park on Prince Street and offers 21km, 10km, 5km and 2km distances. Entry is online through Event Wizards and closes in the week before race day.
