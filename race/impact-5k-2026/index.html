Course

A scenic 5K route through Hahamongna Watershed Park in Pasadena, signed throughout and marshalled by Impact alumni. Water aid stations sit on course and an EMT is posted at the start/finish. The route may be altered if the park floods.

About

Get ready to travel back in time! Join us for an inspiring and impactful day at the second annual Impact Drug & Alcohol Treatment Center 5K Run/Walk, set to take place on SUNDAY FEBRUARY 22nd, at the picturesque Hahamongna Watershed Park in Pasadena. Our 5K Run/Walk will be 80s themed, and we encourage all participants to show up in their best 1980s attire.
