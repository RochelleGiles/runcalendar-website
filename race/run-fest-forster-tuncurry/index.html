Course

Based at the Forster Beach event precinct, with the course crossing the bridge between Forster and Tuncurry on the NSW mid-north coast. Aid stations sit roughly every 2km and some path sections are narrow and closed to prams. Distances run from a 3km family event to the full marathon.

About

Soak in the picturesque coastal views as you take on the RunFest Forster-Tuncurry, a running celebration held at the stunning Forster Main Beach. With a range of events to choose from, this event caters to runners of all levels, offering the perfect opportunity to challenge yourself while immersing in the vibrant local community.
