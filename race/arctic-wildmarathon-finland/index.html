About

The Arctic Marathon takes you to the most authentic of Finnish Lapland. The race starts from the village square of Santa Claus village, in Rovaniemi, where exactly the line of the Arctic Circle is located. In addition to running, the organization offers experiences such as trip on sleds pulled by Huskies, ice pool swim, sauna or seeing the Northern Boreal Lights while staying in 4* Finnish-style bungalows with sauna, all meals and all the comforts.
