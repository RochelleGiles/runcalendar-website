Course

Three options from Wolf Junction on the Glade Top Trail in the Mark Twain National Forest: a one-mile gravel road event, a 4.25-mile out-and-back trail run, and a 10.25-mile trail loop. The course is marked and practice days are offered beforehand. No aid station detail is published.

About

The Glade Top Trail Run is an annual event that takes runners across creeks, loose rocks, steep inclines and declines, and some downed trees in the National Forest. The race offers distances of 4.25 miles and 10.25 miles.
