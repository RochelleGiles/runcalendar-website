Course

Loops of the Blue Hills Reservation in Milton, Massachusetts, starting and finishing at Houghton's Pond. The 50K and 50-mile courses run on twisty, rocky singletrack and are left unmarked, so runners navigate for themselves from maps. Entry is free, with donations invited to the Blue Hills Trailside Museum.

About

This 50K and 50-mile ultra starts and finishes at Houghton's Pond in the Blue Hills Reservation, covering rocky, root-strewn, largely unmarked singletrack. Organized by the Trail Animals Running Club since the 1990s, it is among the earliest true trail ultras in Massachusetts.
