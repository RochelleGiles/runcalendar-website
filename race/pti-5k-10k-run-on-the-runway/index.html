About

The PTI 5k/10k Run on the Runway is held on the airport's 9,000 foot parallel runway. This run benefits The Second Harvest Food Bank of Northwest NC, Greensboro Urban Ministry and Open Door Ministries of High Point. All are invited to come out to run or walk the runway. The 5k/10k on the Runway provides a smooth and flat surface. The entire race course will provide a close up view of the airport and surrounding area from the airfield. The course is wheelchair accessible and baby stroller friendly.
