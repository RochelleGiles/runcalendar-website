Course

A 6.71km lap repeated on the hour under backyard ultra rules, starting from Barge Showgrounds in Whangārei. The loop runs across maintained showground fields into neighbouring sheep paddocks, mixing grass, farm track, single trail, gravel road and short sealed sections, and passes creekside native bush. Each lap climbs roughly 100m.

About

Runners seeking a unique challenge will find it at the Kings Backyard Ultra, a grueling test of endurance set in the picturesque Barge Showgrounds in Whangārei, New Zealand. This innovative event requires participants to complete a 6.7km loop every hour until they can no longer maintain the pace, pushing the limits of human performance in a stunning natural setting.
