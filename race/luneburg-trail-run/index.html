Course

Starts at the Lüneburg church in northern KwaZulu-Natal with 5km, 10km, 23km and 65km options. The 65km circles Ncaga mountain, opening with a steep 4km climb and taking in cattle trails, rough ground, 4x4 and farm tracks, several water crossings and technical rocky descents before a final 3km on old tar road.

About

Luneburg Trail Run is a trail running event in the Luneburg area of KwaZulu-Natal, offering distances from 5km up to a 65km ultra. It is listed among races recognised by the American Trail Running Association.
