About

The Black Chamber of Commerce of Palm Beach County's MLK5K and Divine Nine 5K Challenge celebrates the life and legacy of Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. All of South Florida is invited to this family-friendly race that draws a diverse crowd of 400+ men, women, and children to chase Dr. King's dream of equality, good health, and economic equity.
