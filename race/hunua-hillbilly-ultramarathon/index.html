Course

Trail courses through the Hunua Ranges south of Auckland, run largely on native-bush singletrack with sustained climbs. Three distances share the terrain: a 60km ultramarathon, a 42.2km trail marathon and a 22km run/walk, alongside a Kokako Kids Dash. Well-stocked aid stations are provided on course.

About

Come and join us for a beautifully brutal trail journey through the Hunua Ranges' stunning native bush and experience an event like no other. Tackle the original 60km Ultramarathon, a course which demands some serious respect! Or join us for the Trail Marathon or 22km course options which still deliver a fair share of challenge of their own, plus plenty single trail bliss.
