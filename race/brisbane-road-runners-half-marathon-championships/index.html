About

Explore the bustling streets of Brisbane at the Brisbane Road Runners: Half Marathon Championships. This annual club event challenges runners with a 21.1km course, showcasing the city's vibrant energy and scenic landscapes. Whether you're an experienced half-marathoner or looking to conquer your first, this championship race promises an unforgettable running experience.
