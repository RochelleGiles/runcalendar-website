About

This USATF-certified 5K winds through residential neighborhoods in Woodbridge, Connecticut, starting from the JCC of Greater New Haven campus. Now in its 15th year, it also includes a free kids costume race and a 2-mile family walk/run, benefiting local Jewish community programs.
