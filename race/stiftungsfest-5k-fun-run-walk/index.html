Course

Starts and ends at the NYA Legion Pool Park, following the paved trail system and streets of Norwood Young America over two or three challenging hills. A water station sits at 1.5 miles, with splits at the 1 and 2-mile markers. The Kids 1/2 Mile loops the park perimeter.

About

Want to run? Or enjoy a good walk? This race is for you. Stiftungsfest will be timing runner/walkers at 8 a.m. Racecourse starts and ends at the NYA Legion Pool Park and runs along the paved trail and streets of Norwood Young America. The 5K includes a t-shirt for the first 200 participants.
