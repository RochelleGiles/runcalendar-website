Course

A cross-country race at Richmond Park in Grand Rapids, run on mowed grass, packed dirt and gravel. The route is marked and cyclists lead the runners around it. The 5K is chip timed with team-based scoring; a one-mile community fun run is untimed.

About

Running IS a team sport, and the Brian Diemer Family of Races Cross Country Team Challenge allows you and four (or more) of your friends to experience it all together. This event resembles a high school-style cross country meet, using standard scoring and a 5K course.
