About

Join the Friends of Birthright at the 25th Annual Run for Life and Learning benefitting Birthright Counseling, St. Louis! This in-person event offers something for everyone including a chip-timed 6K Run/Walk, 1 mile run/walk and 0.25 mile youth run with FredBird®! Donations and sponsorships will fund the education of Birthright clients in financial need, enabling them to make better lives for themselves and their children.
