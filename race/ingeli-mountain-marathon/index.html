Course

A four-course mountain trail event on the ANEW Resort Ingeli Forest estate outside Kokstad, on the KwaZulu-Natal/Eastern Cape border. Routes link winding forest singletrack, dirt jeep tracks and open grassy paths over wetlands and streams, climbing to one of the highest points of the Ingeli Mountain. Aid stations range from one on the 6km to four on the 40km marathon, which carries a compulsory kit list.

About

The Ingeli Mountain Marathon takes runners through the Ingeli Forest highlands above Kokstad in southern KwaZulu-Natal, with distances from 6km up to a full 40km mountain marathon.
