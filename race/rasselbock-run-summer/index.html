About

Escape the bustle of everyday life and join the Rasselbock Run through the lush, verdant trails of Sherwood Pines Forest in Nottinghamshire. This unique summer event invites runners of all levels to immerse themselves in the serene beauty of this historic woodland, offering a refreshing and rejuvenating experience amidst the tranquil surroundings.
