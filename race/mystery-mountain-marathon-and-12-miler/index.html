Course

A loop from the lake at Fort Mountain, opening with a flat lap of the lake before the rocky Big Rock trail joins the Gahuti trail and narrow, cambered singletrack past the mysterious rock wall. The marathon adds a steep power line climb and the mostly double-track 301 loop, about 5,500ft of climb with eight aid stations; the 12-miler has four.

About

Marathon and 12-mile trail races traverse difficult, sometimes technical but stunningly beautiful trails with panoramic views of fall foliage on surrounding mountains. The course features significant elevation gain over single track, rugged mountain bike, and smooth horse trails around Fort Mountain, passing a mysterious ancient rock wall.
