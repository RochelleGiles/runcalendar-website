Course

A three-day stage race totalling about 60 miles on mountain trails around Chattanooga. Day one is a 20-mile technical loop on Signal Mountain, day two an 18-mile point-to-point at North Chickamauga Creek, and day three a 22-mile loop on Lookout Mountain. Each stage starts at 8:00am from its own trailhead, and the field is capped at 100 runners.

About

The Chattanooga Mountains Stage Race is a three-day trail stage race across the ridges above Chattanooga, Tennessee, organized by Wild Trails. Runners tackle three point-to-point stages, each in the 18-to-22-mile range, with cumulative time deciding the overall winner.
