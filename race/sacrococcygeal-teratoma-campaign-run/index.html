About

Sacrococcygeal Teratoma Campaign Run is a road race in Mokopane, Limpopo, held to raise awareness and funds for sacrococcygeal teratoma, a rare congenital tumour affecting newborns. Entrants can choose 5km, 10km or 21.1km distances through the town.
