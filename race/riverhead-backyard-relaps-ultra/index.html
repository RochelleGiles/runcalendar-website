Course

A last-one-standing race on a 6.7km loop through Riverhead Forest, north-west of Auckland. The lap runs mostly along the lower valley floor through commercial pine forest with about 90m of climbing, including a short singletrack section and a bridge crossing. A new lap starts on the hour until one runner remains.

About

The Riverhead Backyard ReLaps Ultra is an extreme endurance event where runners complete a 6.7km lap every hour until only one runner remains. Participants must finish each lap within one hour, with the race continuing until a single runner can complete a lap, testing the ultimate limits of human endurance and mental resilience.
