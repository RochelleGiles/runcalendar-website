About

The 14th annual St. Paul Village Community Walk & 5K Run is a fundraiser held at McDougle Middle School in Chapel Hill, NC. It supports the development of St. Paul Village, an affordable housing project in the historic Rogers Road Neighborhood, with proceeds donated to local causes.
