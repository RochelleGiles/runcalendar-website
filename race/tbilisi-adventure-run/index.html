About

Explore the heart of Georgia's vibrant capital at the Tbilisi Adventure Run. This off-road race takes runners through the city's historic streets, scenic parks, and challenging trails, offering a unique mix of urban adventure and natural beauty. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or looking for a new challenge, the Tbilisi Adventure Run promises an unforgettable experience.
