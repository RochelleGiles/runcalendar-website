Course

A 40.5-mile loop starting and finishing in Buxton, Derbyshire, with roughly 5,800 ft of ascent. The route is about 65% trail and 35% road across the High Peak, with six staffed checkpoints along the way.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Peak District at the High Peak 40 Challenge, an exhilarating ultra-marathon starting and finishing in the charming town of Buxton. Challenge yourself to conquer 40 miles of rolling hills and stunning vistas as you push your limits through this scenic corner of the United Kingdom.
