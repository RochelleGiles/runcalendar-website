About

Lace up your running shoes and join the Cook 5km Summer Series Run/Walk in Hamilton, New Zealand. This popular Tuesday night event is part of a series that takes place during daylight savings, offering runners and walkers a chance to enjoy the summer weather and scenic city streets. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to get active, this friendly community gathering is not to be missed.
