Course

A pancake-flat 10km starting and finishing at Baths Island, Windsor, about 150 yards from the brewery taproom. The route runs on towpaths and footpaths with some short road sections, roughly half off-road, with just 33m of elevation gain and one water refill station. A canicross wave is also offered.

About

The Windsor & Eton 10km is a flat multi-terrain race organised by Racing Line Running, starting from Baths Island on the Thames at Windsor. The route runs largely on towpaths and footpaths with short road sections, and a two-hour cut-off makes it walkable. A canicross wave runs alongside the main field.
