Course

Trail races starting and finishing at the Leavenworth Ski Hill Area on Ski Hill Drive, climbing to the ridgelines north of town for sweeping Cascade views. The 25k loops the north side of Tumwater Mountain over alpine ridge and fast forest service road for about 3,700ft of gain; the 50k runs it twice for roughly 7,500ft. A beginner-friendly Town 5k and a kids race complete the programme.

About

The Leavenworth Trail Fest is a trail running festival featuring multiple mountain races including 55k, 27k divisions, and a 5k event at the Leavenworth Ski Hill Area. It offers fast-flowing trails with significant vertical gain, stunning views of the Eastern Cascade Mountains, and a post-race festival atmosphere.[1][3][6]
