Course

Single-lap 5k at Stoneleigh Park near Kenilworth, starting and finishing on grass in the Grand Ring by Farmers Fayre. About one kilometre is on grass and the remainder on tarmac paths. In poor weather an alternative route skips the grass section and is roughly a kilometre shorter.

About

This 5K Race for Life is held at Stoneleigh Park, the Warwickshire showground that once hosted the Royal Show. Runners, joggers and walkers take part at any pace, raising money for Cancer Research UK.
