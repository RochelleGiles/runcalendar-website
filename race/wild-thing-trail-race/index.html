About

Wild Thing Trail Race runs through Mass Audubon's Pleasant Valley Wildlife Sanctuary in Lenox, Massachusetts, in the Berkshires. Runners choose a 5K or 10K on wooded sanctuary trails past ponds and wetlands typical of the reserve.
