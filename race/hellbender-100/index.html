About

Hellbender 100 is a 100-mile trail ultramarathon based in Old Fort, North Carolina, climbing the steep Blue Ridge escarpment above town. Named for the Appalachian hellbender salamander, the course is known for extreme elevation gain through the surrounding mountain forest.
