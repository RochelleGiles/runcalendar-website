Course

Multi-terrain courses through the St Giles Estate in Cranborne Chase, all starting and finishing at St Giles House near Wimborne before winding through the heart of the estate. Underfoot is compact gravel, trail sections, farm tracks and grass with small areas of tarmac. Drinks stations are provided on course, and the weekend includes live music and camping.

About

Discover the stunning trails of Dorset at the Garmin UK Trail Running Festival. Set in the picturesque St Giles Estate, this event offers a range of distances to challenge runners of all levels, from a marathon to a 5km, plus junior races. Immerse yourself in the natural beauty of the countryside and test your endurance on this unforgettable running adventure.
