Course

Point to point from Mountain Creek over Mt Bogong, Victoria's highest peak, descending T-Spur to Big River then climbing Duane's Spur to the Bogong High Plains. The main aid station is at Langford Gap, after which the course crosses the high plains, drops to the Cobungra River and climbs Swindler's Spur to finish at the Mt Hotham summit. Offered at 29 km, 35 km and 64 km.

About

The Bogong to Hotham Rooftop Run is a challenging point to point ultra marathon from Mountain Creek to the summit of Mt Hotham. Runners climb massive Mt Bogong, descend through rugged terrain, cross high plains, and face significant elevation changes. This is considered the toughest race mile-for-mile in Australia, offering spectacular mountain views and an incredible trail running experience.
