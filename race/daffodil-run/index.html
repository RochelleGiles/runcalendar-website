About

If you are looking for a scenic Willamette Valley road race, the Daffodil Run will not disappoint. The 8K run begins eight miles northwest of Junction City at the historic Long Tom Grange. Daffodils surround you as Ferguson Road weaves through the Coast Range's eastern foothills.
