About

The Amicale des sapeurs-pompiers de Corrençon en Vercors offers 2 exceptional trails in the heart of the Vercors. Come and discover the varied terrain (trails, meadows, paths, etc.) of this beautiful region, in a friendly and warm atmosphere, typical of this small village at the end of the world.
