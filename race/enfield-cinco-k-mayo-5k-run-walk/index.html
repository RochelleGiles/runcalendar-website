About

The Cinco K Mayo 5K Run/Walk is an annual road race in Enfield, Connecticut, staged from Moody Road beside the Enfield Town Annex. Entries are taken as either a 5K run or a 5K walk, both setting off at 9:00am on a USATF-certified course.
