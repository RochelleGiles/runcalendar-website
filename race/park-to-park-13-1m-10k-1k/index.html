Course

A road course starting and finishing at Heritage Square on Ruth Street in Sulphur. The route runs through the city past the Henning Cultural Center, West Calcasieu Cameron Hospital and the historic City Courthouse. Time limits are 3.5 hours for the half marathon and 2 hours for the 10K.

About

The Park to Park race weekend in Sulphur, Louisiana features a half marathon, 10K and 1K organized with Race Pace Multisport. The 2027 edition is scheduled for February 20-21, with time limits of 3.5 hours for the half and 2 hours for the 10K.
