Course

Trail races starting and finishing at Larmer Tree Gardens on the Rushmore Estate, crossing chalk valleys, woodland and tree-lined avenues in the Cranborne Chase National Landscape. The surface mixes open grassland, woodland and estate roads. Courses are built from repeating loops: the 10km is one lap, the half marathon one larger lap, the 20 miler both, and the marathon two half-marathon laps. Feast stations range from one on the 10km to seven on the marathon.

About

The Larmer Tree Marathon, 20 Miler, Half Marathon & 10K is a scenic running event set in the beautiful Larmer Tree Gardens in the UK. The course takes runners through the stunning Wiltshire countryside, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants. With a range of distances to choose from, the event caters to runners of all abilities, creating a welcoming and inclusive atmosphere.
