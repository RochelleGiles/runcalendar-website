About

The iconic Santa in the City 5km fun run/walk takes in all the best London landmarks and is suitable for all abilities. The route starts and finishes at Paternoster Square, by the majestic St Paul's Cathedral. Every runner is provided with a 5-piece Santa Suit and a medal.
