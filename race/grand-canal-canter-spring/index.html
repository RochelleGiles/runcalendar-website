Course

Run entirely on the Grand Western Canal path, starting from Sampford Peverell in Devon. The surface is hard-packed gravel towpath and the route is close to flat, with under 90 ft of ascent in the half marathon and under 180 ft in the marathon.

About

Explore the picturesque Mid Devon countryside on the Grand Canal Canter Spring edition. This scenic running event offers a range of distances, from a challenging Marathon to a lively 10K, allowing runners of all levels to enjoy the tranquil winding paths along the historic Grand Canal. Whether you're chasing a new personal best or simply seeking a beautiful running adventure, this event has something for every runner.
