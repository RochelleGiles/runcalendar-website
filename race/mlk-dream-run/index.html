About

The MLK Dream Run is an annual fundraiser for the Soul District Business Association in Portland. A Saturday kickoff brings live music, food and a vendor market, and the USATF-certified race day on Sunday offers 5K, 10K and 15K run/walk options through the Soul District, starting and finishing at Portland Community College's Cascade Campus.
