Course

A USATF-certified 5K route through Prospect Park, Brooklyn. Runners gather at the Grecian Center (the Peristyle) at Park Side Avenue and Parade Place, with day-of bib pickup from 7:30am and the start at 9:15am, rain or shine. A virtual option runs over the following week.

About

An annual 5K run/walk organized by NOBLE NY Chapter in partnership with Power in Wellness Health Consultants. The event honors the legacy of NYPD Detective Dillon H. Stewart, who was shot and killed in the line of duty on November 28, 2005, with proceeds supporting the NOBLE NY Chapter scholarship established in his memory.
