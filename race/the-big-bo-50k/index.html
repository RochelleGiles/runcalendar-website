Course

Flat out-and-back on the paved B&O rail trail, starting and finishing at Daredevil Brewing Co. in Speedway, Indiana. The route runs west through Marion and Hendricks County before turning back toward the start. 50k, 25k, 10k and 5k are offered; the 50k has an eight-hour limit.
