Course

Starts and finishes at the Saint Clair Vineyard Kitchen on Selmes Road outside Blenheim. The 21.1 km winds through the vineyard rows, over short stop banks and along the Wairau River, with the Wither Hills and Richmond Ranges in view. Mainly flat but uneven underfoot: under 4 km is on sealed road. A 12 km runs the same country.

About

The Saint Clair Vineyard Half Marathon runs through the Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Noir blocks of Marlborough in autumn, starting and finishing at the Saint Clair Vineyard Kitchen near Blenheim. Runners choose 21.1 km or 12 km, walking or running, with food from local producers and live music on course.
