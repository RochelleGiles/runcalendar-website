Course

A point-to-point 5K starting at 6150 Kavanaugh Boulevard and running down the heart of Little Rock along Kavanaugh, past the Little Rock Zoo, to a finish on the east side of War Memorial Stadium. Runner's World has rated it one of the fastest 5K courses in the country.

About

An Independence Day 5K through Little Rock, Arkansas, hosted by Fleet Feet Little Rock. Runners chase a fast holiday-morning time before the city's July 4th celebrations get underway.
