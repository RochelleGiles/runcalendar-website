Course

Organised by City of Henderson Parks and Recreation. The 5K run and 1-mile walk start at Cornerstone Park, 1600 Wigwam Pkwy, and use the Union Pacific Railroad Trail. A trail clean-up follows from 11:00 to noon at the park and along the trail. Aid station and surface detail are not published.

About

Celebrate Earth Day on the Union Pacific Railroad Trail with a 5K run and 1-mile walk starting at 10am, followed by a trail and park clean-up from 11am to noon. Unused bibs, t-shirts, and medals are recycled from previous events, welcoming all fitness levels.
