Course

A 5K over the roads, grass fields and wooded trails of the Beulah Beach Camp and Retreat Center on West Lake Road. The course is mostly flat with some small hills, chip timed, with water stops on course. A separate kids fun run uses the roads only. The 5K starts in the evening at about 6:00pm.

About

The Beulah Beach 5K is a run/walk through the roads and trails of Beulah Beach Camp and Retreat Center, welcoming both veteran racers and first-timers. The race features water stops, post-race food and drinks, professional timing, posted results, and age-group awards. Proceeds support funding for a new Beulah Beach Pier.
