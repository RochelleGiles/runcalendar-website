Course

A Thanksgiving morning road race based at the Sunnyview Expo Center on East County Road Y, Oshkosh. A 5 mile run and a 2 mile fun run/walk set off in waves from 8am, with 5 milers first, then 2 mile runners, then walkers, and dogs at the back. The dog jog runs on the 2 mile course only.

About

The Festival Foods Turkey Trot – Oshkosh is a Thanksgiving Day 2-mile and 5-mile race in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, part of a Festival Foods-sponsored trot series held in cities across the state each November.
