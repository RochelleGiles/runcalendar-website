Course

Circular 44 mile (71km) road-based ultra around the Firth of Forth, about 85% tarmac and 15% trail. The route crosses two of the Forth bridges and links the Fife Coastal Path with the John Muir Way, split into four stages of 10.16, 14.10, 10.28 and 9.76 miles. Four checkpoints with water and snacks; 11 hour limit. Run solo or as a relay team of two or four.

About

Explore the stunning Firth of Forth estuary on the Forth Ultra, a challenging 44-mile (71km) circular ultra marathon through the beautiful Scottish countryside. This unique event offers runners the chance to conquer a scenic yet demanding course, passing historic landmarks and bridges along the way. Whether running solo or as part of a relay team, the Forth Ultra promises an unforgettable experience for endurance athletes.
