Course

Point-to-point run on the Coast Track through Royal National Park, from Otford to Bundeena. Thirty kilometres of trail, beach and sand running, with rocky sections, cliff-tops, bushland, rainforest and steep climbs. Aid stations are set up along the route.

About

Experience the breathtaking beauty of the New South Wales coastline at the Coastal Classic, a 30km trail run from Otford to Bundeena. Winding through one of Australia's most scenic natural landscapes, this event offers runners the chance to challenge themselves on a stunning coastal course.
