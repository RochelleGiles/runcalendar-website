Course

A 10.5-mile anti-clockwise loop starting and finishing in Scoriton village on the southern edge of Dartmoor, with over 550m of ascent. The route takes in Pupers Hill, Snowdon and Ryders Hill along with the Red Lake volcano, crossing three rivers, a clapper bridge and two bogs on open moorland. One aid station at 6.3 miles offers drinks and snacks.
