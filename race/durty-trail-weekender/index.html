Course

Held around the Bowhill Country House estate in the Scottish Borders on largely off-road ground. The routes mix hard-packed paths with muddy trail, linking forest tracks and open hill, and carry steep climbs and fast descents. The 5K and 10K have no cut-off; the longer distances do.

About

The Durty Trail Weekender is a celebration of off-road running in one of the most scenic corners of the Scottish Borders, featuring routes with hard packed paths, muddy trails, challenging climbs, and exhilarating descents.
