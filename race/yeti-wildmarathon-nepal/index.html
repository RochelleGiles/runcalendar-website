Course

Point-to-point Himalayan marathon through the Annapurna Conservation Area, starting near 4,000m and finishing around 3,200m. Roughly 75% dirt track and 25% single track, with 256m of climb against 2,800m of descent; some sectors exceed 30% gradient downhill though the going is not highly technical. The half marathon is flatter and less technical.

About

Run in the impressive circuit of the Annapurna Natural Park with Wildmarathon's Yeti Marathon. The Annapurna circuit is considered one of the most beautiful itineraries in the world, and the marathon passes through some of the most spectacular and picturesque places and villages in Nepal.
