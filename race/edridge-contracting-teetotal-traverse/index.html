Course

Trail courses on the Teetotal tracks at St Arnaud, starting and finishing on Beechnest Drive near Alpine Lodge. The half marathon and 13km mix technical single track, easier and gravel single track and a short stretch of forestry road, both taking in a steep 1km climb of about 220m at the 2km mark. The 8km stays mostly flat on gravel and rougher single track.

About

The Edridge Contracting Teetotal Traverse is a challenging trail event for runners and walkers located in the beautiful Teetotal Flats / Big Bush Conservation Area on the edge of the Nelson Lakes National Park. The event offers a Half Marathon run, 13km run, and an 8km run/walk through this little-known Conservation Area, offering a day inhaling some pristine alpine air.
