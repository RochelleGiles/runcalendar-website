About

The Superhero 5K and Family Walk is a costumed run and walk in Wichita, Kansas, benefiting Wichita's Littlest Heroes. The charity supports local children facing serious pediatric diagnoses through financial assistance, hospital visits and family events. A one mile family fun walk is held alongside the timed 5K.
