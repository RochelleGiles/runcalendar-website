About

The Cyprus Marathon is a renowned running event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the picturesque landscapes of the island nation. Held in the heart of Cyprus, this race offers a unique opportunity to experience the country's rich history and stunning natural beauty while challenging yourself physically. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a dedicated runner seeking a new adventure, the Cyprus Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
