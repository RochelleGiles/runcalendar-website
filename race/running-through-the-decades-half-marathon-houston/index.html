About

Get ready to lace up your neon high-tops, tease up that hair, and break out your best 80's threads for Running Through the Decades – The 1980s Edition! This totally tubular event will take you on a nostalgic run through the raddest decade ever — complete with a pre-race costume contest, 80's music pumping all morning, and retro vibes everywhere.
