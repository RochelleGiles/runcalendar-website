Course

Starts and finishes at the Petco Park tailgate lots in downtown San Diego. All distances run through the East Village; the 10K and 15K extend into Balboa Park, Golden Hill Park and its neighbourhood, passing the Fig Tree Garden and the Hi, Hello, Hola mural. The 5K starts at 7:00 am, the 10K and 15K at 8:00.

About

The SKECHERS Hot Chocolate Run brings a 15K and 5K through downtown San Diego, with every finisher rewarded with a signature chocolate fondue station and toppings bar at the finish line.
