About

Explore the picturesque Côtes de l'Orbe region in Switzerland at the Semi-Marathon des Côtes de l'Orbe. This challenging yet scenic half-marathon course winds through rolling hills and vineyards, offering stunning views of the surrounding countryside. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable experience for all participants.
