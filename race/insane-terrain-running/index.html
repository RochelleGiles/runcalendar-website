About

Conquer the Insane Terrain Running event in Doddington, March, where you'll navigate an exhilarating off-road obstacle course that challenges both your physical and mental limits. Traverse through rugged landscapes, tackle heart-pumping obstacles, and immerse yourself in an adrenaline-fueled experience that will push you to your absolute best.
