About

A 5K trail race run at night under the lights on the Shelby Farms Cross Country Course in Memphis. A 2K fun run sets off first at 6:15 p.m., with the 5K following at 6:40 p.m. Both races are open to all ages, and food trucks serve at the venue through the evening.
