Course

Both races start and finish on the beach behind the Flora-Bama lounge. The half marathon heads east into Florida for 10.5K before turning back; the 5K heads west into Alabama for 2.5K before returning. The whole course is run on sand.

About

Flora-Bama's Annual Beach Run/Walk for America's Warriors is a beach race starting and ending at the Flora-Bama Lounge, with the entire course on white sandy beaches. The event supports the Special Operations Warrior Foundation through 100% of registration fees, honoring families of fallen special operators.
