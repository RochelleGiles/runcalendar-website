About

The Osaka Women's Marathon is a prestigious annual running event that celebrates the strength and determination of female athletes. The course takes runners through the vibrant city of Osaka, showcasing its iconic landmarks and scenic landscapes. With a welcoming atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators, this event offers a unique and empowering experience for participants of all levels.
