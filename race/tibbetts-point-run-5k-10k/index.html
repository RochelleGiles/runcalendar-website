About

The Tibbetts Point Run is a fast and flat "out-and-back" 5k/10k course that starts right at the heart of the village of Cape Vincent. Run along the mighty St. Lawrence Seaway while enjoying spectacular views of the river. 10k runners will go all the way out to Tibbett's Point Lighthouse and back, while the 5k runners will have a turnaround halfway on the route. It's one of the most scenic routes in Upstate NY! Walkers are welcome to sign up for the "TPR Mile Walk" and will also receive a Finisher Medal and T-shirt.
