Course

All on trail paths through Devilla Forest in Fife. Runners repeat a 4.37-mile lap with about 205ft of climb, completing as many as possible within eight hours; three laps makes a half marathon, six a marathon and seven or more an ultra. The same loop is used throughout, with a grace period to finish a final lap.

About

The Devilla 8-hour Challenge invites endurance runners to test their limits in the stunning Devilla Forest of Fife, UK. Participants will navigate a scenic course through the forest, with the goal of covering as much distance as possible over the 8-hour duration. This unique event promises an immersive and rewarding experience for seasoned ultrarunners seeking a new adventure.
