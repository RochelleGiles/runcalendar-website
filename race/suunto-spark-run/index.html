Course

The 2026 edition moves off the trails onto Sai Kung's roads, billed as the "Devil's Course". Individual races start at the Pak Tam Chung PHAB site over 21.6km, 10km and 5km, with climbing from 169m on the 5km to 713m on the half. A four-leg charity relay of about 2.5km per runner starts at Man Yee Au.
