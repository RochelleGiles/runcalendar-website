About

The Geelong Running Festival holds a marathon, half marathon, half marathon wheelchair race and a 6.5km community run on the Sunday, with kids' and all-abilities 1-mile races the day before. The traffic-free course runs through the streets of Geelong, Victoria, and out along the Corio Bay waterfront.
