Course

A flat, fast evening loop through Dublin Docklands, starting near the Samuel Beckett Bridge. It runs to the Matt Talbot Bridge, crosses to the North Quay and out to the 3 Arena. The finish is at Capital Dock on Sir John Rogersons Quay.

About

The Docklands 5K in Dublin, Ireland offers runners a flat, fast loop through the city's vibrant Docklands area. This scenic 5km race provides a perfect opportunity to experience the lively atmosphere and stunning waterfront views of this historic district.
