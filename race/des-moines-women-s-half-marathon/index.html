About

The Des Moines Women's Half Marathon is a unique and empowering event held annually at Jasper Winery, inviting women of all ages and abilities to run 13.1 miles through downtown Des Moines streets, trails, and parks. It features a weekend of races including a Saturday 5K and Sunday Half Marathon, 10K, and Team Relay, with post-race festivities like live music, wine, and swag.
