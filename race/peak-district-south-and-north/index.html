Course

A two-day trail ultra based in Edale: 50km on the Saturday, 43km on the Sunday, or 93km across both. The routes tour the White Peak and Dark Peak using the Pennine Way and Pennine Bridleway, climbing 1,439m on day one and 1,649m on day two. Not waymarked beyond existing finger posts, so navigation is required; GPX files are issued in advance and there are several refreshment points on course.

About

A two-day ultra through the Peak District National Park, starting from Edale at the southern end of the Pennine Way. Runners cover the moorland and gritstone edges around Kinder Scout across consecutive days, tackling either leg or both back to back.
