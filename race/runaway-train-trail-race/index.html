Course

Point-to-point trail race starting outside Shepton Mallet and finishing in the town market place. The route crosses open fields, grass trails, rough tracks and some tarmac footpath, passing three viaducts and a tunnel, and is often muddy and stony. Marshals with first aid are stationed at Ham Woods quarry, Croscombe and below Maesbury Castle.

About

The Runaway Train Trail Race takes runners on a rugged journey through the scenic Windsor Hill railway path in the United Kingdom. Featuring a post-race celebration in the charming Shepton Mallet market place, this event offers a unique trail running experience for adventurous athletes.
