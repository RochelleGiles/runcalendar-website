Course

Point-to-point on trail from Byron Herbert Reece Farm near Blairsville to Amicalola Falls State Park, with roughly 16,000 ft of climbing and 16,000 ft of descent. Starts at 5:00 am, and every runner must carry a 1 lb railroad spike the whole way. A sub-24-hour finish qualifies for the Western States lottery.

About

The Georgia Death Race is a 74-mile mountain ultramarathon through the Blue Ridge Mountains near Blairsville, Georgia, following remote ridge and forest trails with extensive elevation gain and a strict time cutoff.
