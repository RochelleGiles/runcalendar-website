Course

Run at the Shell V-Power Motorsport Park at The Bend in South Australia, on the racing circuit itself. The half marathon covers laps two and three of the full GT circuit, while the 10km and 5km use sections of the International circuit. A kids dash runs alongside.

About

The Bend Running Festival is held at The Bend Motorsport Park in Tailem Bend, South Australia, with runners covering the circuit and its surrounds over 5km, 10km and half marathon distances, plus a 1km kids dash.
