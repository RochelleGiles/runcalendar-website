Course

A road-closed loop through Frome town centre, starting and finishing on the Old Showfield. The half marathon extends into the Somerset countryside past Nunney Castle while the 10K and 5K stay within the town. Water is provided at 5K and at the finish.

About

The Frome Running Festival is a road-closed running event in Somerset offering 5K, 10K and half marathon distances. All three races start and finish on the Old Showfield in Frome, with the 5K and 10K held within the town and the half marathon heading out into the surrounding countryside.
