About

Fleet Feet and the Columbus Track Club invite any and all, adults and children alike to join us for the fun, fast and FREE Gary Smith All-Comers XC Meet!! Plus, we have a race for the kid in all of us... meaning you, parents, coaches, and post-collegiates! What better way to send off summer than with a quick two-mile race at the fastest cross country meet this side of the Scioto!
