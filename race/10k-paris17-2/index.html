About

The 10km ONATERA Paris17 is a unique running event taking place in the 17th arrondissement of Paris. Runners will enjoy a flat and fast course through the wide, tree-lined streets of the district, discovering the cultural and architectural heritage of the area. The event promotes sport, health, and wellness, offering participants a chance to run with friends, family, or colleagues in a convivial atmosphere.
