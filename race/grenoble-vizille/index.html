About

The Grenoble-Vizille run is a scenic and historic event that winds its way through the stunning landscapes of the French Alps. Runners will enjoy picturesque views as they challenge themselves on a course that connects the vibrant city of Grenoble with the charming town of Vizille. This race offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
