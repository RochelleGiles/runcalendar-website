Course

A circuit of the spa town of Harrogate on mostly country paths, taking in the wooded Nidd Gorge and the valleys around the town. Three distances are offered: 14.3 miles, 20.3 miles and a 34.3-mile ultra, the longest adding the Knaresborough Round loop.

About

The Harrogate Hustle Trail Race invites runners to explore the stunning off-road trails of North Yorkshire, with three thrilling courses to choose from. Set in the picturesque town of Harrogate, this event offers a unique challenge for trail enthusiasts, combining scenic rural landscapes with the vibrant character of this historic destination.
