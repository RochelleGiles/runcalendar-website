About

As part of the lead-up to our exciting NC Sports Con, we invite you to join us in downtown Wendell, NC, for an unforgettable 5K experience that supports a great cause! Put on by Pace Yourself Run Company, located in downtown Wendell, the race will raise funds for Lake Myra Elementary School, helping to enhance educational opportunities for local students.
