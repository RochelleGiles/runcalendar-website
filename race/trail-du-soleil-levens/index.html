About

The Trail du Soleil Levens sets off from the village of Levens in the Nice backcountry, climbing summits such as Mont Férion and the Col du Dragon with views toward the Mercantour range and the Mediterranean coast. Six options run from a 6km beginner loop to a 60km ultra gaining roughly 3,700 metres, alongside a non-competitive hike for those who prefer to walk the route.
