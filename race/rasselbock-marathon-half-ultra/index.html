Course

Looped forest routes at Sherwood Pines, Nottinghamshire, starting and finishing in the arena area event village. The half marathon runs one 13.1-mile loop and the marathon two, on trails and fire roads with about 250m of climb per lap; an 8.5km mini route cuts out the south side of the forest. Nine aid stations on the marathon and four on the half, with water, sports drink, fruit and sweets. Not wheelchair accessible.

About

Explore the enchanting Sherwood Pines Forest in Nottinghamshire at the Rasselbock Marathon, Half Marathon, and Ultra. This scenic event offers runners a chance to immerse themselves in the tranquil woodland surroundings, with challenging yet rewarding courses that wind through the heart of this historic region. Whether you're seeking a full marathon, half marathon, or an ultra-distance challenge, the Rasselbock has an option to suit every runner's ambitions.
