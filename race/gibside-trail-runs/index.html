Course

Two evening trail runs over the 600-acre Gibside estate, on woodland, garden and countryside paths through an 18th-century designed landscape. The route passes the neo-classical chapel, restored stable block and the hall ruin. The 10km starts at 7.10pm and the 5km at 7.15pm, with refreshments at the finish.

About

Trail runs through Gibside, a National Trust estate near Rowlands Gill known for its 18th-century landscaped grounds, including the Column to Liberty and Gibside Chapel. Routes follow woodland and parkland paths across the estate.
