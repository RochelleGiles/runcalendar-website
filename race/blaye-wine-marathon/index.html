About

The Blaye Wine Marathon takes runners on a scenic journey through the renowned vineyards of Blaye, France. Participants will have the opportunity to experience the region's rich winemaking heritage while challenging themselves on a picturesque course. This unique event offers a one-of-a-kind running experience for both wine enthusiasts and avid marathoners.
