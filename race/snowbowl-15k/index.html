Course

A 9.3-mile loop from the Montana Snowbowl base area. The first 4.65 miles climb on dirt and double-track roads, followed by a 4.65-mile descent on the Beargrass Highway single track. Total climbing is about 2,150 feet, with the same descending. The field is capped at 250.

About
