Course

A 5K loop starting and finishing at the American Legion on Prospect Place in Danville. The course is largely flat with a few short hills. The race starts at 11:00 am; strollers are welcome but pets are not permitted.

About

An annual 5K road race held in Danville, Illinois to honor veterans. The event features challenging terrain and post-race festivities, with proceeds benefiting the American Legion Post 210.
