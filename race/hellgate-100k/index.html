About

Hellgate 100K++ is a point-to-point trail ultramarathon through Virginia's Blue Ridge Mountains, crossing the Appalachian Trail and Blue Ridge Parkway four times. Founded by ultrarunner David Horton in 2003, the race includes nine aid stations and finishes at Camp Bethel near Nace, Virginia.
