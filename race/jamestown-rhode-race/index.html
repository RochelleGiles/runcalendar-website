Course

Half marathon circling almost all of Conanicut Island from Fort Getty, past Mackerel Cove and down into Jamestown Village, then up the east shoreline and back along the western side past windmills, estuaries and farms. Paved roads apart from a short unpaved stretch before mile 2, with a long climb around miles 9 and 10.

Half Marathon21.1 km

About

The Jamestown Rhode Race half marathon circles Conanicut Island, the town of Jamestown's home in the middle of Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island, with water views across the bay toward Newport for much of the route.
