About

Explore the picturesque Trail de Grésigne, a challenging and rewarding trail running event set against the backdrop of the Grésigne Forest. Runners will traverse rugged terrain, winding through lush greenery and past stunning natural landscapes, offering an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts. This event is a must-try for trail runners seeking an adventure-filled day in the great outdoors.
