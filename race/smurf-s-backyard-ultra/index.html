Course

A backyard ultra on a 6.7km loop of the Smithfield Mountain Bike Trails at Smithfield Mountain Bike Park, north of Cairns. One lap starts on the hour, every hour, and the race runs until a single runner is left; a Mini Backyard option caps the field at six laps.

About

Smurf's Backyard Ultra is an intense endurance event set in the tropical landscape of Cairns, Far North Queensland. Runners tackle a challenging 6.7km lap of the famous Smithfield Mountain Bike Trails, completing a lap every hour until they can no longer continue. The event offers a unique atmosphere with a Saturday night party featuring food, drinks, and live music, making it a standout endurance experience in Far North Queensland.
