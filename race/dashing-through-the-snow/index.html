Course

The 5K starts at the North Dakota State Fair Event Center and loops around the fairgrounds and Roosevelt Park area. The 1 mile is run Amazing Race style, with challenge stations along the way for trivia and festive tasks. A team entry is offered over the 1 mile distance.

About

Dashing Through the Snow is a festive 5K and 1-mile fun run in Minot, North Dakota, held each November ahead of the winter holidays. Runners and walkers of all paces are welcome on the wintry course.
