Course

Trail running over the mountains and through the valleys of the Zuikerkop game farm near Clocolan, with a 6 km colour fun run and 11 km and 20 km trail races. Most routes cross the game farm itself, so wildlife sightings are common. Mountain-bike distances share the venue on the same day.
