Course

A 26.3-mile trail marathon through Letchworth State Park, starting from Visitor Center Road at Mount Morris and running the gorge trails with eight stocked aid stations and checkpoints. Two- and four-person relay teams share the same course. The companion Vale of Three Falls 5K, 10K and 15K are out-and-back runs on a seasonal gravel road from the Parade Grounds, on stone, gravel and broken asphalt.

About

The Sehgahunda Valley ("Vale of Three Falls"), which encompasses the portion of the Genesee River Valley, was comprised of several villages settled by the Seneca People. Letchworth State Park spans over 14,000 acres, with miles and miles of trails - all with varying degrees of technicality to challenge both experienced runners and casual hikers. Many of the trails that make up this race are the very trails used by the Seneca Indians to access the Genesee River, which bisects this valley, giving rise to its nickname "The Grand Canyon of the East."
