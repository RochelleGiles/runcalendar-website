About

The 17th Annual Rockets Foundation 5K and Kids Fun Run is a fundraiser benefiting St. Mary's Catholic School in Mount Carmel, Illinois. The event features a 5K race, kids fun run for ages 10 and under, prizes for top finishers, and door prizes.
