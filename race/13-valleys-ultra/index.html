Course

Runs over three days in the Lake District National Park, linking valleys on mountain trails around the lakes. Distances range from the 21 km 2 Valleys (439 m, 8-hour cut-off) through 58.1 km, 110.6 km and the full 185.7 km 13 Valleys with over 7,000 m of ascent and a 46.5-hour limit. Feed stations vary from one to twelve by route; all courses except the 7 and 13 Valleys are fully waymarked.

About

The Altra 13 Valleys Ultra celebrates the 13 unique valleys of the Lake District National Park, which are part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site. The event links all these valleys together through spectacular trail running around magnificent lakes and valleys.
