Course

A fast, flat course on smooth park paths linking the historic grounds of Exhibition Park with the open expanse of Town Moor, run in closed parkland with the Newcastle skyline in view. Every kilometre is marked with distance flags and marshalled throughout. Half marathon, 10K, 5K and a junior race start in staggered waves from 9:30am.

About

A half marathon, 10K and 5K starting from Exhibition Park and running out across Newcastle's Town Moor, one of the largest inner-city commons in Europe, before finishing back in the park.
