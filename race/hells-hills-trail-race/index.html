Course

Single-track loops at Rocky Hill Ranch near Smithville, running through pine forest and wildflower meadows with stream crossings and small bridges. The profile is a constant roller-coaster of short, steep hills rather than long climbs. The 10K and 25K run one loop, the 50K two 15.5-mile laps and the 50 mile three, with aid every 2.8-4.5 miles.

About

Hells Hills Trail Race is a trail ultramarathon at Rocky Hill Ranch near Smithville, Texas, offering 10K, 25K, 50K, and 50-mile distances on hilly, wooded singletrack with the repeated climbs that give the race its name.
