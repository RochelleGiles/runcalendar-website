Course

New Year's Day five-miler starting and finishing at Houghton School in Sterling, Massachusetts. The route is mostly out and back on gently rolling paved roads, passing scenic farmland with views towards Sterling Airport. A one-mile kids' fun run is held alongside it.

About

The Freezer 5 Mile Road Race is a New Year's Day tradition in Sterling, Massachusetts, drawing runners out each January 1st for a cold-weather start to the year.
