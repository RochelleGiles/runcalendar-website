About

Organized by VGA Compiègne, the Imperial Race is the 10K of Compiègne, now past its 4th running. The start and finish are in the same location, on the Paul Petitpoisson stadium track, with the course following the Guynemer route along the Oise to the Pont Neuf, through the city centre, up the Royal Avenue and finishing on the circular road around the grounds of the castle.
