About

The Māngere Bridge Fun Run, Walk, Stroll & Stride is a family event with 5km, 10km and half marathon distances, based at Naomi and Bill Kirk Park. The course takes in the village and the Kiwi Esplanade, and the half marathon continues through Ambury Farm.
