Course

Run on the Cowboy Trail out of Valentine, Nebraska. The vast majority of the course is smooth packed earth, with the in-town sections paved and one bridge crossing decked in wood. Aid stations are placed along the route.

About

The Valentine Marathon runs along the Cowboy Trail out of Valentine, Nebraska, and benefits the Valentine Community Schools Foundation. It offers marathon, half marathon and 10K distances.
