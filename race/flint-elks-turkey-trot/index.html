About

Join us for the Flint Elks Turkey Trot 5K Run/Walk at the scenic Flint Elks Golf Course in Grand Blanc! This family-friendly event is the perfect way to kick off the Thanksgiving season with some fresh air, fitness, and festive fun. Runners and walkers of all ages and abilities are welcome. Whether you're chasing a new PR or enjoying a relaxed stroll before the holiday feast, this course winds through the beautiful Flint Elks Golf Course grounds with soft well maintained grass, gentle rolling hills, paved cart paths, and crisp fall vibes.
