About

Climb to the summit of Africa at 5,895m and participate in the Kilimanjaro Trail Festival in a sporty and friendly atmosphere. The Rongai route is one of the most beautiful and less crowded paths to the top. After the summit, enjoy a local running event with 10km or 21km options. An optional safari in the Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater completes the program.
