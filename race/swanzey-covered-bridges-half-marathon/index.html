Course

A counter-clockwise road loop starting and finishing at Monadnock Regional Middle-High School in Swanzey. The route takes in the town's historic covered bridges before returning on paved roads. Six water stations are on course, with alternating stations also serving Gatorade.

About

Known as Elijah's Race, this half marathon winds through the countryside of Swanzey, New Hampshire, passing several of the town's historic covered bridges. The course is a local favorite for its scenic, small-town route and post-race food.
