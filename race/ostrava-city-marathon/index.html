About

The Ostrava City Marathon is a must-run event for any dedicated distance runner. Weaving through the historic streets of Ostrava, this picturesque course showcases the vibrant culture and stunning architecture of this Czech city. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply seeking an unforgettable running experience, the Ostrava City Marathon promises an exhilarating challenge in a beautiful setting.
