About

The Portumna Forest Marathon & Ultra in County Galway, Ireland offers runners a truly unique experience on a scenic course through the lush, wooded landscape. Whether you're looking to conquer a full marathon or an ultra-distance challenge, this event has a distance to suit every level of runner. Explore the natural beauty of Portumna Forest as you push yourself to the finish line.
