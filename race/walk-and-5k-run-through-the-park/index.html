About

At The Friends of Einstein's Annual Walk and 5K Run Through the Park, dedicated runners and walkers enjoy a morning of family fun before a heart-pumping stroll around Norristown Farm Park. Whether you're on a team or registering as a single guest, this is a wonderful chance for the community to come together to support patients receiving cancer care at Jefferson Einstein Montgomery Hospital.
