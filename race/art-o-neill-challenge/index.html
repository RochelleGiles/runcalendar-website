About

The Art O'Neill Challenge is an exhilarating 60km road and mountain race through the stunning landscapes of Ireland. Starting at Dublin Castle, runners will conquer a challenging course that winds through scenic countryside before finishing in the picturesque Glenmalure valley. This unique event offers an unforgettable adventure for endurance athletes seeking an exceptional running experience.
