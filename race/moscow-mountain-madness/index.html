Course

A mountain course following the ridge of Moscow Mountain, mostly technical single-track with stretches of fast logging road and significant climbing. The five-mile has one aid station at three miles; the half marathon has three, at 5.5, 9.5 and 10.75 miles; the 50K is aided frequently and runs to cut-offs on a ten-hour limit. Entry is pre-registration only, with no race-day sign-up.

About

The Moscow Mountain Madness Run began in 1978 and was reinstated in 1992 after a hiatus. Originally open only to runners, it briefly welcomed a bike race before returning to its running-only format, and now includes a half-marathon course alongside the classic distances.
