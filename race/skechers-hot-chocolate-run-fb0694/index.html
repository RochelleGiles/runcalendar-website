About

The SKECHERS Hot Chocolate Run St. Louis offers a 5K and 15K starting and finishing at Kaufman Park in downtown St. Louis. The route passes Energizer Park, Union Station and Saint Louis University, with the 15K extending through Washington Avenue, Ballpark Village and Soulard, and finishers receive hot chocolate.
