Course

A paved 3.1-mile course at Lost River Cave in Bowling Green, running the Greenways trails and out through the Shawnee neighbourhood. The finish leads into the Cavern Nite Club, the cave's underground ballroom, where the post-race celebration is held.

About

A winter 5K race held at Lost River Cave in Bowling Green, Kentucky that promotes fitness and supports the restoration of vital wildlife habitats in the park. The event features a scenic 3.1-mile run through the prairie with a celebration inside the cave.
