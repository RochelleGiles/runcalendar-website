Course

Starts and finishes on Gateway Road off 30th Street inside Garden of the Gods Park. The road courses run on park roads closed to traffic, passing the Siamese Twins, Kissing Camels and Balanced Rock; a separate five-mile course is run on trail. Water stops number one on the 5K, four on the five-mile road, three on the five-mile trail and seven on the Double Take.

About

Take 5 in the Garden of the Gods is held in Garden of the Gods Park, Colorado Springs, starting and finishing on Gateway Road off 30th Street. It offers a 5K road race, a five-mile road race, a five-mile trail race and the Double Take 9.5-mile road-and-trail race. The park roads used are closed to traffic.
