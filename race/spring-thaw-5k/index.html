Course

A 5K run and walk and a 10K run starting at 10am from 1132 Larsen Park Rd in Sioux City, on the Missouri River waterfront. A virtual entry is offered alongside, which entrants may complete on trail, sidewalk or treadmill. The organiser publishes no route map or surface detail.

About

The Spring Thaw is a 5K running race organized by Missouri River Runners in Sioux City, Iowa. It takes place at 1085 Council Oak Dr, offering a fun early spring running event.
