About

Explore the historic streets of this charming French town at the annual Les Foulées Des 4 Portes running event. Featuring a scenic course that winds past the iconic four gates of the old city walls, this community-driven race offers a unique opportunity to experience the local culture and beautiful architecture while challenging yourself physically.
