About

Runmaröloppen is a running event in the heart of the Stockholm archipelago. It's a day of running, food, ice cream, swimming, and a great atmosphere along the beautiful courses. The races are timed to coincide with the Vaxholm boats, making it a perfect day trip from Stockholm City.
