About

The Breffni 3 Province Challenge is a unique running event that allows participants to choose from 7 different courses traversing three Irish provinces: Ulster, Connaught, and Leinster. Now in its 25th year, this event stands out as the only race in Ireland offering such a diverse range of courses in a single day, making it a special challenge for runners.
