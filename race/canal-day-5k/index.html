Course

Waterfront 5K starting at the Buzzards Bay Recreation Area on Main Street in Buzzards Bay, Massachusetts, and running out along the Canal Service Road beside the Cape Cod Canal. The surface is sealed service road throughout. The race starts at 9:15am as part of Cape Cod Canal Day, with food trucks, vendors and entertainment following from 11am.
