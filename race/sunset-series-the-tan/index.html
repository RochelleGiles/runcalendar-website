Course

Run on the Tan track circling Melbourne's Royal Botanic Gardens, starting on Linlithgow Avenue. The 3.8 km is one lap starting at 6:30pm and the 7.7 km is two laps starting at 7:00pm. One drink station sits at about the 2.5 km mark.

About
