About

The Nordholzer Windmühlenlauf is a unique running event that takes place against the picturesque backdrop of historic windmills. Runners will wind their way through the charming town, taking in the sights and sounds of this quaint German locale. With a course designed to challenge and delight, this event is a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable experience.
