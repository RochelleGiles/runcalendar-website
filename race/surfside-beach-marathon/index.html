Course

Out-and-back beach course between Surfside Beach and the San Luis Pass, starting and finishing at Stahlman Park Pavilion; marathoners cover the loop twice. Water stations sit every 1.5-2.5 miles, and tides can force minor course zig-zagging. The marathon has no formal cutoff.

About
