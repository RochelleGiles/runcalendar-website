About

Experience the stunning Cornish countryside at the An Res Hellys 10 Mile Race. This multi-terrain challenge takes runners through the picturesque town of Helston and the surrounding rolling hills, offering breathtaking views and an unforgettable running adventure. Whether you're seeking a scenic off-road test or simply want to explore the beauty of Cornwall, this event is not to be missed.
