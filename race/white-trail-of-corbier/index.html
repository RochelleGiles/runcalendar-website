About

A twilight trail race across snow-covered slopes at Le Corbier, part of the Sybelles ski area in the Savoie Alps. Runners choose the 11.5km Trail des Aiguilles or the 5km Trail des Etoiles, finishing by headlamp as darkness falls over the mountains.
