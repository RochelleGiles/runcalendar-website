About

The Dad Bod Summer Sweat 5K embraces the heat and highlights the importance of Men's Mental Health Month. We're proud to partner with NAMI Gwinnett to champion mental wellness, support community programs, and break the stigma surrounding men seeking help. This race celebrates dads, father figures, brothers, uncles, mentors — and every runner committed to better physical AND mental health.
