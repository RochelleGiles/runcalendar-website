Course

A 38km skyrace with 2,800m of ascent, starting and finishing at Stickle Barn in Great Langdale and run on technical single-track from start to finish. The route crosses the central Lakeland fells and valleys, summits Scafell Pike and takes in the Great Slab descent off Bowfell. Fully marked with two feed stations and manned mountain checkpoints; front runners go under four hours, the limit is twelve.

About

The Scafell Sky Race is a challenging 40km trail running event in the stunning Lake District of the United Kingdom. Runners will tackle over 2,800m of elevation gain as they traverse the rugged terrain and iconic peaks of the Scafell range. This event offers a true test of endurance and mountain running skills, with breathtaking views and a rewarding sense of accomplishment for all who take on the challenge.
