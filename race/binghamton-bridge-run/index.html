Course

A road half marathon weaving through downtown Binghamton and the historic west-side neighbourhoods, crossing four bridges and passing under a fifth. Aid stations with water, sports drink and toilets sit at miles 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9.5, 12 and the finish. A two-person relay splits the half at roughly 6.5 miles each.

About

The Greater Binghamton Bridge Run is the largest premier running event in the Greater Binghamton area, featuring a USATF certified half marathon, relay, and 5K races. The scenic, mostly flat course crosses four bridges with beautiful views of the Susquehanna and Chenango Rivers.
