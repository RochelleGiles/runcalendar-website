Course

Paved road loop starting near Community Drive and Highway 36, running counter-clockwise around Lake Estes with a short out-and-back on Mall Rd. Five aid stations on the half marathon course; the 5K circles Lake Estes on paved road with one aid station.

About

A half marathon and 5K held at Estes Park, Colorado, on the doorstep of Rocky Mountain National Park. Both races run on paved roads around Lake Estes, with the 5K held on the Friday and the half marathon on the Saturday. An Elk Double option combines the two.
