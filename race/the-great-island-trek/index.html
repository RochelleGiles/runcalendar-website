The Great Island Trek

Picnic Bay, Queensland · Australia
TrailCoastal

Coastal trek across Magnetic Island, ending at Nelly Bay

Course

A fully supported, non-competitive point-to-point trek across Magnetic Island, with bag transfer from start to finish and a chartered bus back afterwards. Four courses of 25km, 18km, 12km and a 5km all-ability route, with fruit and hydration stations on course. Routes can change for high tides or fallen trees.

About

Join us for a fully supported, non-competitive adventure on the stunning Magnetic Island! Experience the island's breathtaking beauty as you explore at your own pace, and create unforgettable memories with friends in this tropical paradise.
