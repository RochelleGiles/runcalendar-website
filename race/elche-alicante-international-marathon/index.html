Course

Point-to-point marathon of 42.195 km linking the two main cities of Alicante province, starting in Elche and finishing in Alicante. The route is virtually flat with a slight net downhill from Elche to Alicante, favouring fast times, and carries several refreshment stations along the way.

About

The Elche Alicante International Marathon is a comprehensive running event that takes participants on a journey between the two cities of Elche and Alicante. The course highlights the scenic coastal landscapes and historic landmarks, creating an unforgettable atmosphere for runners of all levels.
