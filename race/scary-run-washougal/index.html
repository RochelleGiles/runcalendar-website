Course

A flat evening race on a single 5K loop at Captain William Clark Park in Washougal, run once, twice or three times for the 5K, 10K and 15K. Every lap passes through a lit "Spooky Forest" with views over the Columbia River. All races start at 4:00pm from Washougal City Hall, with a cheer zone at the venue.
