Course

A loop course at Shaffer Bend Recreation Area near Marble Falls, linking the Equine, Lakeview, Turkey and Homestead trails on dirt, sand and rock. Distances from 5K to 50 miles are built from repeats of the same rolling 10-mile loop, which climbs about 590 ft. Start, finish and the single aid station are all at the same point.

About
