About

The 15 Km De San Sebastian is a key event in the running calendar, catering to a wide range of athletes. The race features a fast, urban course that takes runners through the most iconic sights of the beautiful city of San Sebastian. Whether your goal is to set a personal best, prepare for longer races, or simply get started in running, this event has something for everyone.
