Course

Road race starting on Cliff Avenue in Cromer, opposite North Lodge Park, and finishing on Tucker Street in the town centre. Marathon and half share the first 10km before splitting, and the half rejoins the marathon course for the closing six miles. Water roughly every 5km, seven stations for the marathon and three for the half.

About

Experience the stunning Norfolk countryside at the Norfolk Marathon & Half Marathon. This picturesque road race takes runners through the charming villages and rolling hills of the United Kingdom's East Anglia region, offering a scenic and challenging course for both marathon and half marathon distances. With a beautiful backdrop and a welcoming atmosphere, this event is a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable running adventure.
