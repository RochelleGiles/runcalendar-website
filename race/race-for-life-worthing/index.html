Course

Starts and finishes at Beach House Grounds and heads west along Marine Parade on the Worthing seafront promenade, over a 2.5km loop. The surface is tarmac and hardstanding with sections along the sea wall, and a water refill station sits at halfway.

About

Cancer Research UK's Race for Life 5K is held at Beach House Grounds, a park right on Worthing's seafront on the West Sussex coast. Runners and walkers follow a flat, traffic-free course near the promenade to raise money for cancer research.
