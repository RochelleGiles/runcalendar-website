Course

The half marathon starts at the Isle of Mull Hotel in Craignure and the 10k at the Fishnish road end, both finishing at the Salen Hotel in Salen. Point-to-point on the island, run as a friendly event open to all abilities.

About

The Isle of Mull Half Marathon & 10K is a friendly annual road race for all levels and abilities. The half marathon course is flat and fast, starting near the nine-hole golf course outside Craignure.
