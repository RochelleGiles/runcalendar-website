Course

Both races finish at the entrance to the Environmental Learning Center in Black Moshannon State Park. The 10K is a loop from the dam on Black Moshannon Road, turning up Huckleberry Hill and Cassanova Road on sealed surfaces. The 5K runs out and back along West Side Road by the lake, turning at the Bog Trail and climbing a grass bank to Cassanova Road.

About

An annual 5K and 10K run with a separate 5K walk, hosted by the YMCA of Centre County at Black Moshannon State Park near Philipsburg, Pennsylvania. The 5K run starts at 9am, the walk at 9.15am and the 10K at 10am, with check-in under the pavilion at Concession and Black Moshannon Roads.
