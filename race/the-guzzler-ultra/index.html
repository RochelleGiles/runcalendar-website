About

The Guzzler Ultra is Brisbane's very own trail ultra; presented by Those Guys Events and made possible by Brissie's thriving trail running community. 99% trails and 100% fun, you'll run past Brisbane's oldest reservoirs in the order they were built. Along the way, you'll be well looked after by the wonderful, crazy and energetic groups that populate Brisbane's trail scene.
