About

The Mill Stone 50K and 10M is a trail race hosted by Rock Hill Striders at Anne Springs Close Greenway in Fort Mill, SC, featuring three 10.5-mile loops for the 50K and one loop for the 10M. Runners are encouraged to touch the historic Mill Stone landmark on the technical single-track course with creek crossings and potential mud.
