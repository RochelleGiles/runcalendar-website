Course

Based at 2245 Clute Road outside Cortland in the Finger Lakes hills, with 100 mile, 50 mile and 50K options plus a 100 mile relay. The route runs on singletrack forest trail with steep ascents and descents and significant cumulative gain.

About

Virgil Crest Ultras takes place near Cortland, New York, in the hilly Finger Lakes region. The race offers 50K, 50-mile, and 100-mile distances on rugged trail, known among ultrarunners as one of the toughest courses in the eastern US.
