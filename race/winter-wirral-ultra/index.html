Course

A 36-mile circular route starting and finishing at Hooton Memorial Hall, following the Wirral Circular Trail anticlockwise around the peninsula coast before returning along the Wirral Way for the final 12 miles. Mixed road and trail underfoot and partially self-navigational, with extra signage added. Five checkpoints with water and snacks; 12-hour cutoff.

About

The Winter Wirral Ultra is a challenging 36-mile circular route that explores the beautiful Wirral Peninsula. The event offers a unique mixed terrain experience suitable for both road and trail runners and walkers, featuring a scenic course that follows the Wirral Circular Trail in an anticlockwise direction.
