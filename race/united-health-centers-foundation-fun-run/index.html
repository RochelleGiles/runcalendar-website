About

The UHC Foundation Fun Run, now past its 3rd running, is held in the Art of Life area of Woodward Park in Fresno. It offers a 10K run, 5K run, 2-mile walk, mascot race, and kids' races, with proceeds supporting medical, dental, vision, and specialty care at United Health Centers.
