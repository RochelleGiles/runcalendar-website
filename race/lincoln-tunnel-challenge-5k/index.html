Course

An out-and-back run through the Lincoln Tunnel, mostly below the Hudson and crossing between two states. The start and finish are at the toll booths in Weehawken, with a cone turnaround on the New York side. Run entirely on the tunnel roadway. USATF certified.

About

The Lincoln Tunnel Challenge 5K runs through Weehawken, New Jersey, near the Lincoln Tunnel's New Jersey entrance, with views across the Hudson River to the Manhattan skyline. The race raises funds for Special Olympics New Jersey.
