About

The 11th Annual SJWC Snow Shoe Shuffle is a winter-themed 2-mile run/walk event featuring snowshoes. It includes a 2 Mile Run, 2 Mile Competitive Walk, and a free Fun Run, benefiting community causes like the American Heart Association.
