Course

A road race starting and finishing at Rietondale High School on the corner of 18th Avenue and Pierneef Street in Pretoria. Four distances run the jacaranda-lined suburban streets north of the city: 42.2km, 21.1km, 10km and a 5km fun run. Entry is free for runners aged 80 and over.

About

The Jacaranda City Challenge runs through Pretoria, South Africa's 'Jacaranda City', named for the purple-blossomed trees that line its streets each October and November. Runners can choose the marathon, half marathon, 10km, or 5km.
