Course

Out-and-back on the cycle and walking paths alongside the Christchurch Southern Motorway, starting and finishing at Canterbury Agricultural Park on Curletts Road, Hillmorton. The course is flat and vehicle-free, with one traffic control point at Marsh's Road. Drink stations sit at approximately 4 km, 9 km, 12 km and 17 km.

About

The Christchurch Motorway Half Marathon will see the runners run the Christchurch Southern Motorway on the cycle and walking paths. There are drink stations at the approximate 4km, 9km, 12km and 17km marks.
