About

The La Boucle Festival is a must-attend event for running enthusiasts. Set against a stunning, undisclosed backdrop, this annual race offers a challenging and scenic route that showcases the region's natural beauty. Runners of all levels can experience the thrill of this vibrant community celebration of endurance and camaraderie.
