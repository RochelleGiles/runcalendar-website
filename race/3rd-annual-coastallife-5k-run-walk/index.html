About

The CoastalLIFE 5K Run/Walk is held on the campus of Coastal Carolina University in Conway, SC, supporting the CoastalLIFE program for students with intellectual and developmental disabilities. It welcomes runners and walkers with events including a 5K, 1-mile fun run, kids' dash, and virtual option.
