Course

Two-lap (21km) and single-lap courses in Bowling Green Bay National Park, starting and finishing at the Alligator Creek day-use area about 30km south of Townsville. The route mixes fast open trail, technical single track, stairs and several creek crossings on undulating walking trails and power line tracks, with rocky, uneven gravel underfoot in places.

About

The Elliot's Revenge Trail Run takes place in Bowling Green Bay National Park, where rugged mountains rise from salt plains and mangrove forests. The course follows designated walking trails and power line tracks with undulating terrain, offering trails well-suited for beginners and newcomers to trail running.
