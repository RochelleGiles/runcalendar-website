About

The Grossglockner Ultra Trailr is a challenging and awe-inspiring running event that takes place in the stunning Austrian Alps. Runners will traverse breathtaking mountain landscapes and conquer the mighty Grossglockner, Austria's highest peak, on this epic ultramarathon adventure. With a focus on the natural beauty of the region, this event promises an unforgettable experience for trail running enthusiasts.
