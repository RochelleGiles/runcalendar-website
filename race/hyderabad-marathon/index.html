About

The NMDC Hyderabad Marathon is an annual road race in Hyderabad, Telangana, titled after its sponsor NMDC and run for its 15th edition in 2026. Four distances are offered: a full marathon, a half marathon, a 10K and a 5K, plus a kids' dash.
