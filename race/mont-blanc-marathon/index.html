Course

A 42 km loop from Place du Triangle de l'Amitié: rolling valley trails to Le Tour, the steep, technical Aiguillette des Posettes (2,185 m), a technical descent to Vallorcine, the balcony traverse to La Flégère, a final climb to Planpraz and the long descent into Chamonix. 2,540 m of climb and descent.

About

The Mont Blanc Marathon is a breathtaking trail running event set against the stunning backdrop of the French Alps. Runners will test their endurance as they tackle challenging terrain and take in awe-inspiring views of the iconic Mont Blanc massif. This legendary race is a must for any adventurous trail enthusiast.
