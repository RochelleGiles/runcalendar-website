About

The Lace Up Against Stigma 5K Run and 1 Mile Walk is hosted by Prevention Plus of Burlington County to reduce stigma around substance use disorder and mental health issues. Proceeds fund prevention education programs and support community initiatives at Lenape High School in Medford, NJ.
