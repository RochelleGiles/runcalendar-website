About

A spring city run along the Groene Loper, the tree-lined boulevard laid over Maastricht's buried A2 motorway. A 5 km, a 10 km and a half marathon on the same Sunday morning, with kids' runs and a business run alongside; the finish is at the MVV stadium. Pre-entry closes in mid-April or when the field is full.
