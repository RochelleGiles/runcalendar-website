About

The Trail Du Caillou association provides access to several Trail and walking formats departing from Sebourg in the Valenciennois region (Hauts-de-France). The event aims to showcase the natural surroundings of Sebourg, the Bettrechies quarry between the Mormal state forest and the Hogneau valley, as well as the neighboring Belgian municipalities and the Caillou qui bique park. The event is one of the few sporting competitions of this format in the region.
