About

The Baker's Dozen Half Marathon and Half Dozen 10K take place at Brady's Run Park in Beaver Falls, Pennsylvania, celebrating generations of baking history with the Baker family. Runners enjoy baked goods, swag and the high-energy atmosphere of a Get Fit Families event.
