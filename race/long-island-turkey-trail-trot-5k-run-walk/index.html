Course

A 5K run/walk on the trails of Bethpage State Park, starting and finishing at the Polo Field. Hydration and refreshments are at the finish line, with toilets a short walk from the start and finish areas. A free kiddie dash starts 30 minutes before the main race, and a virtual option is also offered.

About

Bring the family to the annual Long Island Turkey Trail Trot 5K at Bethpage State Park! Run or walk through the friendly trails then enjoy post race treats from All Round Foods & Waterloo Sparkling Water. Guaranteed fun for the whole flock - This is the perfect trail run/walk for the whole family!
