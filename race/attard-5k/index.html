About

The Attard 5K is a scenic running event through the charming streets and hills of Attard, Malta. Organized by the Malta Marathon team, this race offers a challenging yet rewarding course for runners of all levels, with stunning views of the island's stunning countryside. Whether you're a local or visiting Malta, the Attard 5K is a must-do event for any runner seeking an unforgettable experience.
