Course

A 17.5km point-to-point run along the edge of the Blue Mountains escarpment, linking Wentworth Falls, Leura and Katoomba. The route follows cliff top tracks and bushland with short village sections, taking in exposed escarpments, forest trail, waterfalls and a great many stairs, and climbs around 700m overall.

About

The Grand Cliff Top Race is a 17.5km trail run along the escarpment above Wentworth Falls in the Blue Mountains, following clifftop tracks with views over the Jamison Valley. It is a newer, beginner-friendly addition to the region's trail running scene.
