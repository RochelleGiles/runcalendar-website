Course

A flat two-loop circuit starting on the seafront at Port Neuf, running the Allee du Mail to the quays of the Vieux Port, past its towers and on through the city centre. Roads throughout, with the closing metres on the athletics track at the Stade A. Bouffenie in the race village.

About

The Semi-Marathon de La Rochelle is a long-standing road half marathon on the Atlantic coast of France, now in its mid-forties as an event. Runners set off from the seafront at Port Neuf and pass the towers of the old harbour before finishing on the athletics track. A 5km race and a two-person relay run alongside it.
