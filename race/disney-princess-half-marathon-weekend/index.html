About

The Disney Princess Half Marathon Weekend is a runDisney event at Walt Disney World Resort in Florida, with a 5K, 10K, half marathon and the Fairy Tale Challenge, which combines the 10K and half for 19.3 miles over two days. Races start at 5:00 AM and travel through the resort's theme parks.
