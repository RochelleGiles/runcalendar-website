Course

A point-to-point 80 mile route along the St Edmund Way from Manningtree in Essex to Brandon in Norfolk, with registration from 8am and a 10am start. Four checkpoints fall at roughly 17, 28, 45 and 63 miles. The organiser lists the checkpoint locations and finer course detail as still being finalised.

About

Explore the picturesque landscapes of Essex, Suffolk, and Norfolk during the St Edmund Way Ultra Marathon. This 80-mile winter race challenges runners to conquer the historic St Edmund Way, immersing them in the serene countryside and rich cultural heritage of eastern England. Whether you're seeking a scenic adventure or a grueling test of endurance, this event promises an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners.
