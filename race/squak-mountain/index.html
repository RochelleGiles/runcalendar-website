Course

Loop course starting and finishing at the main trailhead, 21492 SE May Valley Rd. All distances open with a sustained climb; the 12K branches to the Summit aid station while half marathon and 50K runners summit the southeast peak. The half adds loops on the north side and west peak, and the 50K repeats the main loop twice. The descent passes the Bullitt homestead on technical trail.

About

A trail race series on Squak Mountain in Issaquah, Washington, run over 12K, half marathon and 50K distances on rugged singletrack. The terrain is steep and technical, with sustained climbs to the mountain's peaks and aid stations spaced across each course. Evergreen Trail Runs organises it; online entry closes at noon on the Friday before race day.
