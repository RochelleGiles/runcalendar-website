About

Explore the urban landscape of Chaumont on the Urban Trail Chaumont, a unique running event that takes participants through the city's historic streets and hidden pathways. With a challenging yet rewarding route, this race offers runners a chance to discover the vibrant culture and architecture of this captivating destination.
