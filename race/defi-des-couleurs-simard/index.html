Course

A three-day trail festival at Mont-Sainte-Anne near Beaupré, Québec, timed for the autumn colours. Trail distances run 5km (nocturne), 11km, 17km, 26km, 32km and 42km, with easier forest-trail routes of 6.5km, 10km and 21km, plus MSA Uphill and Up & Down mountain races and a 1km children's cross-country. Routes take in Mont Ferréol and the Rivière Jean Larose.

About
