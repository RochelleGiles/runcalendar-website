Course

An out-and-back 5K starting and finishing at the WaterOne lobby on Renner Boulevard in Lenexa. The route runs from WaterOne's water tower to the City of Olathe's water tower and back. A course map is published as a PDF; surface and aid station detail are not stated.

About

The Tower 2 Tower 5K is a benefit run from WaterOne offices to a water tower and back, celebrating National Drinking Water Week. It raises funds for Water For People to provide clean water and sanitation services worldwide.
