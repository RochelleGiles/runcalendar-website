About

Join A Cure in Sight on Sunday, January 11, 9AM-12PM, to walk or run with us. We will be taking steps to find a cure for a rare eye cancer, ocular melanoma. Ocular Melanoma is estimated by the American Cancer Society to have roughly 3000 new cases each year nationwide. Bring your friends and family to the race day–all ages welcome to walk or run with us.
