About

An annual 5K run held in conjunction with the Greening Up the Mountains Festival in Sylva, NC, celebrating Appalachian heritage and environmental stewardship around Earth Day. The festival attracts upwards of 12,000 attendees and features arts, crafts, live music, and local vendors.
