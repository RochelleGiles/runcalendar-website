Course

A 5K fun run and walk along Milwaukee's lakeshore, held as part of the Milwaukee Irish Fest. The course starts and finishes at Urban Park, with on-site registration from 8:00 a.m. and the race starting at 10:00 a.m.

About

The Arthritis Foundation's Run/Walk to Milwaukee Irish Fest bills itself as the longest-running heritage festival 5K fun run, following Milwaukee's lakeshore. Entry includes admission to Irish Fest itself, with Irish entertainment and a costume contest alongside the race.
