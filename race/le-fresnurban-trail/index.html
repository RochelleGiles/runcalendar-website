About

Explore the picturesque town of Fresnay-sur-Sarthe at the Fresn'Urban Trail, a unique running event that winds through historic streets and scenic countryside. Runners of all levels can challenge themselves on this diverse course, which offers breathtaking views of the Alpes Mancelles region. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
