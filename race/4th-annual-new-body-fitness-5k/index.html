About

A chip-timed 5k run/walk event in Tipton, Iowa featuring finisher medals for all participants and awards for top overall and age group finishers. The event includes a Kids Sprint and is part of the Tipton Chamber of Commerce's Kick-Off to Summer celebration with food vendors, games, and music.
