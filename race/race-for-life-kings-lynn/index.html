Course

Run in the grounds of Houghton Hall, an 18th-century Norfolk estate, starting and finishing on grass behind the hall beside the car park. The terrain is rough and bumpy, almost all grass with a short section of stony path, and can turn soft or muddy in wet weather.

About

Race for Life's Kings Lynn event is held at Houghton Hall, an 18th-century Norfolk stately home set in a working deer park. Cancer Research UK's charity 3K is run, jogged or walked across the grounds, raising money for cancer research.
