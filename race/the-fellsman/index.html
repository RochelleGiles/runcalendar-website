Course

A 61-mile point-to-point traverse of the Yorkshire Dales from Ingleton to Threshfield, crossing rugged fells and open moorland with more than 11,000 feet of climb. Twenty-five checkpoints provide food and drink along the way. A 30-mile Fellsman Dart runs from Redshaw to Threshfield with 13 checkpoints and 4,200 feet of climb. Strong navigation is required on both routes.

About

A historic 61-mile fell race across the Yorkshire Dales from Ingleton, with a shorter 30-mile option. Runners navigate remote moorland and rough fell terrain, often through the night, with numerous checkpoints.
