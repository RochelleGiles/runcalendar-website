About

Mountain Do Fim do Mundo is an extreme snow running challenge in the Argentine Patagonia, taking place at the Cerro Castor ski center in Ushuaia. Participants can tackle distances of 42K, 20K and 10K through pure snow between forests, trails and high mountains with temperatures that can reach -10°C. The event offers a unique opportunity for personal challenge in extreme cold conditions at the world's southernmost city.
