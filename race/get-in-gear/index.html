Course

Start and finish at Minnehaha Falls Regional Park, Minneapolis, with a finish festival at the park. The organiser describes the 10K as circling the river. Start times are 8:10am for the 10K, 8:25am for the 5K, 9:00am for the half marathon and 9:45am for the children's Sprocket Scamper. Course maps are published closer to race day.

About

Get in Gear is a spring road race weekend in Minneapolis presented by Twin Cities Marathon, with 5K, 10K and half marathon options. It's a popular season-opening race for Twin Cities runners building toward summer.
