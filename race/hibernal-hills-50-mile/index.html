Course

A single 51-mile loop starting and finishing at Ditcham Park School in Hampshire, 99% trail and 1% road. The route follows the Staunton and Monarch's Ways before climbing onto the South Downs Way for the return. Runnable but undulating, with around 6,400 ft of climbing, six well-stocked aid stations and a fully marked course.

About

The Hibernal Hills 50 mile is our new, season opening race and, is a great one to kick the year off. This is a single looped event starting and finishing at the stunning location of Queen Elizabeth Country Park in Hampshire. The stunning route is runnable, but undulating with some bigger, longer hills throughout, and around 6400 ft of climbing. The first 50km of the route is along the picturesque Staunton and Monarchs Ways before working up onto the South Downs Way back towards the finish at QECP.
