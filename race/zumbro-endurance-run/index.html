About

The Zumbro Endurance Run is a challenging and scenic ultra-marathon event held in the beautiful Zumbro Bottoms region of Minnesota. Runners can choose from distances of 100 miles, 50 miles, 34 miles, and 17 miles, each offering a unique and rewarding experience.
