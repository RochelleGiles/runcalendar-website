About

The Sand Creek Trail Run takes place at the Sand Creek Conservation District in Decatur, Illinois, on trails within the Macon County Conservation District's Sand Creek Trails. The course is fast rolling terrain, and race profits benefit the Special Operations Warrior Foundation.
