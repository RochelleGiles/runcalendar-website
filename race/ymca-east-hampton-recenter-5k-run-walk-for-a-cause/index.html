Course

A 5K starting and finishing at East Hampton Main Beach on Ocean Avenue, off at 9am rain or shine. The route is published as a MapMyRun course. A virtual option runs 27 September to 4 October for those who cannot get there.

About

An annual 5K run/walk event that is part of the YMCA 5K Series, benefiting local individuals and families in need with access to the Y's healthy living, youth programs and support services. The event takes place at Main Beach in East Hampton, NY.
