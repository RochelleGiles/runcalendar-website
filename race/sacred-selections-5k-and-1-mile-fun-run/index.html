Course

A chip-timed 5K on the streets of Temple Terrace, just outside Tampa, with a one-mile fun run starting 45 minutes later. Both distances finish at the same venue and carry finisher medals, with age-group awards on the 5K.

About

Sacred Selections is a 5K and one-mile fun run through the streets of Temple Terrace, near Tampa, raising funds to help children find forever homes through adoption. All proceeds go directly toward adoption costs, and those unable to attend in person can still contribute online.
