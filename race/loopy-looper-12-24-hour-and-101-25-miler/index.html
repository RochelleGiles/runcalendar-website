Course

A timed ultra on a flat, paved 3.75-mile loop around Cooper River Park, starting and finishing at Jack Curtis Stadium in Pennsauken, New Jersey. Twelve- and twenty-four-hour entries are run solo or as four-person relays, alongside a fixed 101.25-mile option of 27 loops. Food and hydration sit at the start/finish, with a second hydration point at the loop midpoint.

About

The Loopy Looper is a timed ultra marathon event held on a 3.75-mile flat paved loop around Cooper River Park in Pennsauken, NJ. Runners complete as many loops as possible in 12 or 24 hours, with a fixed 101.25-mile (27-loop) distance for 100-mile finishers, plus relay and glow-up options.
