About

Trail La Charolaise is an annual trail running event featuring multiple race distances including 21km and 11km options. The event includes a free 1.5km children's race without rankings, making it a family-friendly running festival. Participants receive finisher gifts with the first 200 registrants getting special surprise gifts.
