About

Rand Road Warriors' marathon in its 38th year, from Edenvale Sports Club in J.P. Bezuidenhout Park. The marathon and the 10-miler start at 06:00 and the 5 km fun run at 06:15, all on measured routes through Edenvale's suburban streets with mat-to-mat timing. The marathon counts as a Comrades and Two Oceans qualifier.
