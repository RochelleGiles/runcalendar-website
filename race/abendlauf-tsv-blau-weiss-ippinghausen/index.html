About

The Abendlauf Tsv Blau Weiss Ippinghausen is an annual running event held in the town of Wolfhagen-Ippinghausen, Germany. The event features a 10km, half marathon, and full marathon distance, all taking place on scenic, paved forest trails. The event is known for its beautiful natural surroundings and welcoming community atmosphere.
