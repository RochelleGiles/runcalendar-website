About

The Hamburg Half Marathon, sponsored by PSD Bank, is a premier running event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the historic streets of Germany's second-largest city. With a challenging yet rewarding course, this race attracts both seasoned marathoners and recreational runners looking to conquer the 13.1-mile distance. Participants can expect a lively atmosphere and stunning views as they make their way through this vibrant urban landscape.
