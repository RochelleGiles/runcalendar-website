About

The Trail Trashed Ultra runs the 601 and McCullough trails in the hills above Henderson, Nevada, mixing wide groomed trail with challenging single-track. The course offers spectacular views of Las Vegas, Henderson, and the surrounding mountains, valleys, and ravines, with loud music and high-energy volunteers along the way.
