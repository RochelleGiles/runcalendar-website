Course

A 24-hour race run as repeats of the 1.5032-mile inner loop around Hinson Lake, starting and finishing at the footbridge by the dam in the main car park. The loop is sand and gravel maintained trail through woods, crossing 16 wooden footbridges including a 300-foot span over the lake. Marked with chem-lights overnight.

About

Hinson Lake 24 Hour Ultra Classic is a fixed-time ultramarathon in Rockingham, North Carolina, where runners complete laps around Hinson Lake for 24 hours straight. The event has run for decades, among the longest-standing ultras in the state.
