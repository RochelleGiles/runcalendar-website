Course

A night trail run in Beavers Bend State Park on rocky, rooty dirt single-track, taking in the Beaver Creek, Lookout Mountain, Deer Crossing and Skyline trails. Both distances finish at the main area; the 11-mile field is shuttled to a separate start. Three aid stations serve water, electrolytes and hot and cold food.

About

Beaverfoot is a night trail race held in Broken Bow, Oklahoma, in the forested hills of McCurtain County. Runners choose an 11 mile or 22 mile distance run after dark.
