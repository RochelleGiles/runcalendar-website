Course

Starts and finishes at the Autry Museum on Western Heritage Way in Griffith Park. The half marathon opens with four flat miles on compact trail, rolls through the Pony Ride and Merry Go Round areas over miles five to seven, then runs mostly flat past Travel Town and the zoo. The 5K is flat, circling the perimeter of the golf course.

About

The Griffith Park Run offers a half marathon and a 5K through Griffith Park in Los Angeles, starting and finishing at the Autry Museum. The half marathon mixes compact park trail with rolling sections past Travel Town and the zoo, while the 5K circles the golf course perimeter.
