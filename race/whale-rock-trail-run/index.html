About

A trail running event on the bushland tracks around Pennant Hills in northern Sydney, offering a 17 km Challenge Run, a 12 km Adventure Run and a 6.5 km Fun Run plus kids' athletics. Courses use a mix of fire trail and singletrack, with bridge crossings and boulder sections on the longest distance.
