Course

An undulating trail circuit of about 13 miles starting and finishing on Corfe Common at Corfe Castle. The route heads out towards Worth Matravers and joins the coast path with sea views before turning back to Corfe, with only a few minor hills on the return. No dogs, buggies or wheelchairs; minimum age 17.

About

The Beast Trail Race in the picturesque Dorset countryside offers runners a challenging 13-mile journey through some of the region's most breathtaking landscapes. Tackle rugged terrain and steep climbs while taking in panoramic views of the Jurassic Coast, making this a must-do event for trail enthusiasts looking for an unforgettable running experience.
