About

The Big Five Marathon runs through Entabeni Game Reserve in Limpopo, unfenced among elephant, rhino, buffalo, lion and leopard, with armed rangers and a helicopter providing safety cover. The loop course crosses savanna terrain with about 540 metres of elevation change, and marathon and half marathon distances are offered.
