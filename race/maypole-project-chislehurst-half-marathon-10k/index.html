Course

Multi-terrain and undulating. The route mixes roads that are not wholly closed to traffic with footpaths that are narrow in places, and the organiser warns of numerous tree roots through the woodland sections. Exact start, finish and aid station positions are not published.

About

A half marathon and 10K through Chislehurst in south-east London, raising funds for the Maypole Project children's charity. The route loops through the suburb's commons and residential streets.
