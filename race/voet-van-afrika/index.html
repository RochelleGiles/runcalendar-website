Course

The marathon crosses the Overberg mountains and farmland through Napier to finish in Bredasdorp, largely on tar. The half marathon starts with 4 km of gravel near Napier before joining the marathon route on tar into Bredasdorp. The 10 km is a loop from Bredasdorp with stiff climbs at start and finish; the 27 km and 13.5 km trail runs use farm roads and fynbos single track.
