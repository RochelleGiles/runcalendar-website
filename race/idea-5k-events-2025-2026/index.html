About

IDEA Public Schools holds an annual 5K run and walk with a healthy living expo in each of its large regions, including San Antonio, Texas, where it is staged at the S.T.A.R. Soccer Complex. A 1K is offered alongside the 5K, open to students, families and the wider community.
