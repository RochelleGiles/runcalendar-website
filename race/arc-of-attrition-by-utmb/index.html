Course

Four races on the South West Coast Path in Cornwall over a winter weekend: 173km with 5,507m of climb, 82.7km with 2,708m, 49.4km with 1,458m and 22.1km with 744m. The 100-mile sets off on the Friday, the 100K on the Saturday and the 50K and 20K on the Sunday. Rugged cliff path, relentless climbs and whatever the winter delivers.

About

A winter ultramarathon along Cornwall's South West Coast Path, starting from Porthtowan. Runners face relentless coastal climbs, sea fog and storms in January, with distances from 20K up to 100 miles along cliff-top trail.
