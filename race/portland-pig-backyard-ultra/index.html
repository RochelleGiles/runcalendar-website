About

The Portland Pig BackYard Ultra is a unique last-person-standing endurance challenge set on the picturesque Isle of Portland in Dorset, UK. Runners will test their limits as they complete laps of a looped course, with the event continuing until only one participant remains. This challenging event promises an unforgettable experience for seasoned ultrarunners seeking a true test of their mental and physical fortitude.
