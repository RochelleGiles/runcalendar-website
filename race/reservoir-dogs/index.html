About

Punk Panther's Yorkshire Reservoir Dogs is a trail ultramarathon run around the reservoir network above Otley in West Yorkshire, with distances of 33km, 51km and 80km. The exact route is kept secret until race day, taking in moorland and reservoir-side trails across the Yorkshire countryside.
