Course

The 5K starts and finishes at the Asheville Area Chamber of Commerce on Montford Avenue. The route runs through the Montford neighborhood via Hill Street, Courtland Avenue, Pearson Drive, Rosewood Avenue, Hawthorne Lane, Tacoma Street and Santee Street.

About

The Chamber Challenge is a 5K run/walk celebrating workplace wellness through friendly competition between Asheville businesses. It encourages participation from co-workers, family, and friends, whether walking or running.
