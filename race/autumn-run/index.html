Course

A point-to-point course starting west of town at Cottonwood Lake Campground and dropping past the lake and through aspen groves into Buena Vista, finishing at McPhelemy Park. The route mixes gravel roads with paved sections of CR 306 and West Main Street. Three fuel stations carry water, GU Roctane Hydration and sweet and salty snacks.

About

The Autumn Run is held each year on the third weekend in September, in and around the historic, friendly, and picturesque town of Buena Vista, Colorado. Nestled in the upper Arkansas River Valley at the base of the Collegiate Peaks, the Autumn Run has one of the most beautiful courses in Colorado.
