Course

A 44-mile point-to-point along the South West Coast Path from Lizard Point, England's southernmost tip, to Land's End, its westernmost - the quarter of the compass that gives the race its name. The going is rock, mud and coastal trail with innumerable steep climbs and descents, about 5,531 feet of ascent in total, and exposed Atlantic weather. Fully waymarked throughout. Entry is solo or as a relay pair or quad.

About

The Classic Quarter is an epic ultra marathon traversing the stunning South West Coast Path from Lizard Point to Land's End. Runners challenge themselves across 44 miles of challenging coastal terrain, facing steep climbs, dramatic scenery, and unpredictable Atlantic weather. This legendary event tests runners' mental and physical endurance along one of the most spectacular coastal routes in the United Kingdom.
