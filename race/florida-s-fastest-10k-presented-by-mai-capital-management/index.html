About

We are warming up for our ninth annual Apryle Showers Run – Florida's Fastest 10K in Ponte Vedra (Nocatee), FL. The ALL NEW COURSE is great for runners of all levels and proceeds benefit the Apryle Showers Foundation, restoring hope for families living with all types of cancer. Along with the usual 10K age divisions, we are proud to offer a ruck division.
