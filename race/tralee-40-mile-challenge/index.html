About

The Tralee 40 Mile Challenge takes runners on a scenic tour through the picturesque town of Tralee and the stunning countryside of North Kerry, Ireland. Featuring both road and trail running, this event offers a unique challenge for endurance athletes seeking to push their limits in a beautiful natural setting. With stunning views and a welcoming local community, the Tralee 40 Mile Challenge is a must-do event for any serious distance runner.
