Course

A USATF-certified half marathon starting on Commerce St beside Wild Wing Cafe and finishing at Founder's Park Pavilion in Johnson City, on moderately rolling roads through the city's neighbourhoods. Teams of two to four can split it as a relay, with exchanges at Local Motion, Southside Elementary and the Tomy Thai car park. Three water stations on course.

About

The Bluegrass Half Marathon is a popular annual running event in Johnson City, Tennessee. Runners can choose to participate in the half marathon, a 2-4 person relay, or a virtual option.
