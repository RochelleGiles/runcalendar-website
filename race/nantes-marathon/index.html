About

The Nantes Marathon is a must-experience event for runners seeking a scenic and historic challenge. Weaving through the charming streets of Nantes, France, this marathon showcases the city's medieval architecture, picturesque riverfront, and vibrant culture. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to soak in the lively atmosphere, the Nantes Marathon offers an unforgettable running adventure.
