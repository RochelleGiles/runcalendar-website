Course

Start and finish at Place Bellerive in Lausanne, on the northern shore of Lake Geneva. The marathon route runs out through Pully, Paudex, Lutry, Cully, Rivaz, Saint-Saphorin and Corseaux to Vevey before returning. Toilets are provided at the start and finish and at many points on route.

About

The Lausanne Marathon offers a marathon, half marathon and 10km along the northern shore of Lake Geneva in Switzerland. All distances start and finish at Place Bellerive in Lausanne, with the longer routes running out through the Lavaux vineyard villages towards Vevey.
