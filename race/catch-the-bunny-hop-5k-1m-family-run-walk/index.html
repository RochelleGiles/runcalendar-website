About

Bring the family for a hoppin' good time at Wayne Bunker Family Park for Catch the Bunny, Hop 5K & 1M Family Run/Walk. Join us in supporting Slightwork Girls Flag Football at this event. We're committed to keeping girls in the game, with a portion of race proceeds going back to support their program.
