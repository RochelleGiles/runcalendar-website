About

The Keswick Half Marathon takes runners on a scenic, challenging 13.1-mile journey around the stunning Derwentwater lake, with an added loop into the picturesque Newlands valley. Set in the heart of the United Kingdom's beautiful Lake District, this event offers a unique opportunity to experience the region's breathtaking landscapes while testing your endurance on an unforgettable course.
