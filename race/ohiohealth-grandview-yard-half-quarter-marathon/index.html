Course

Starts and finishes at Ray E. DeGraw Park in Grandview Yard, touring Grandview Heights, Marble Cliff, Upper Arlington and the Ohio State University campus on road. The half marathon course stays open and fully supported for 3 hours 45 minutes; runners off pace at 8.9 miles are rerouted onto an 11-mile course and still finish.

About

The OhioHealth Grandview Yard Half & Quarter Marathon will feature a distance for everybody: OhioHealth Grandview Yard Half Marathon (13.1 miles), OhioHealth Quarter Marathon (6.55 miles) and the Purple Heart 5K (3.1 miles). The OhioHealth Grandview Yard Half Marathon (13.1 miles), Quarter Marathon (6.55 miles) & Purple Heart 5K (3.1 miles) starts and finishes at Ray E. DeGraw Park in Grandview Yard and feature tours of Grandview Heights, Marble Cliff, Upper Arlington and The Ohio State University.
