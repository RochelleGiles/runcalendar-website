Course

A mix of closed roads and hard-surfaced woodland paths through the ancient woodland of Burnham Beeches in South Bucks. The half marathon runs two laps and the 10K a single loop, both starting and finishing at Caldicott School in Farnham Common. The terrain is undulating with gentle hills, and water stations on course use paper cups with taps in the race village.

About

The Burnham Beeches Half Marathon & 10km is run on a mix of closed roads and hard-surfaced paths through the Burnham Beeches ancient woodland in South Bucks. The event features routes starting and finishing at Caldicott School, with clearly marked courses well marshalled by Burnham Joggers.
