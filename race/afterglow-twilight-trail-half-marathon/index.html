About

The Afterglow Twilight Trail Half Marathon in Victoria takes runners on a magical journey along the Surf Coast Walk, offering stunning ocean views and a one-of-a-kind twilight experience. Explore the rugged beauty of Australia's coastline as you challenge yourself on this scenic trail course, making memories that will linger long after the event.
