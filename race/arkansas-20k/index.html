Course

Starts and finishes at the Boys and Girls Club, 1810 Citizens Drive, Benton. A single 20km loop on a course the organisers rate more difficult than most half marathons, with most of the elevation gain packed into the first five miles. Aid stations at roughly two-mile intervals. An early start is offered an hour before the main field.
