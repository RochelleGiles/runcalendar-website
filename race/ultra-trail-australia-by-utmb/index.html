Course

Trail races based at Scenic World and central Katoomba in the Blue Mountains, running along cliff tops, through valleys and across ridges, including the 951-step Furber Steps. The programme spans UTA11 (11.5 km, 731 m+), UTA22 (22 km, 1,200 m+), UTA50 (51 km, 2,400 m+), UTA100 (101 km, 4,400 m+) and the 161 km UTA Miler (7,200 m+).

About

HOKA Ultra-Trail Australia by UTMB is a four-day trail festival based at Scenic World in Katoomba, in the Blue Mountains of New South Wales. Runners cover clifftops, valleys and ridgelines on distances from 11 km to a 161 km miler, with the 100 km finishing up the 951 Furber Steps.
