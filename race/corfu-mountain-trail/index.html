About

The Corfu Mountain Trail is an extreme adventure on the island of Corfu, offering four unique races of varying distances and difficulty levels. Runners can challenge themselves on the 103km CMT Ultra, the 44km Rain Trail, the 24km Olive Tree, or the 12km Quick Trail, all while taking in the stunning natural beauty of the island.
