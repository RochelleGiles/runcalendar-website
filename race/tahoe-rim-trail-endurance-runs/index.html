Course

Run on mountain singletrack along the Tahoe Rim Trail above Lake Tahoe, crossing Spooner Nevada State Park and Forest Service land near Diamond Peak. The 100 mile carries roughly 18,000-18,500 ft of climbing at an average elevation of 8,300 ft, with a 36-hour limit. 100K, 50K and 23K options share the same trails.

About

The Tahoe Rim Trail Endurance Runs follow high-altitude singletrack on the Tahoe Rim Trail above Lake Tahoe, climbing through Sierra Nevada forest and alpine terrain near Carson City, Nevada, with 50K, 100K and 100 mile distances.
