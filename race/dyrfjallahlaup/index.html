About

Dyrfjallahlaup is a mountain running event exploring the majestic Dyrfjöll mountains, known as 'Door Mountains'. The race offers runners a spectacular route through unique landscape featuring gigantic tuff boulders, meadows, and fresh water ponds, with elevation reaching over 1000 meters providing breathtaking views of the surrounding area.
