About

The Tralee Half Marathon in the picturesque County Kerry, Ireland offers runners a stunning coastal course through historic towns and scenic countryside. This popular event is the culmination of the 2026 Tralee Marathon Series, providing an exciting challenge for both seasoned and aspiring half-marathon competitors.
