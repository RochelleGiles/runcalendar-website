Course

A 16.7 mile loop at Murphy-Hanrehan Park Reserve in Savage, Minnesota, run once for the 25K and three times for the 50 miler. The ground mixes rolling wooded trail, prairie, two miles of singletrack, a beaver dam crossing and short gravel and asphalt sections at the south end. Four aid stations per lap carry water, snacks, firepits and toilets.

About

Surf the Murph is a trail running event held at Murphy-Hanrehan Park in Savage, MN, featuring looped courses on official trails. Distances include 25K (1 loop), 50K (2 loops), marathon (2 loops), and 50 miles (3 loops) of a 16.8-mile loop.
