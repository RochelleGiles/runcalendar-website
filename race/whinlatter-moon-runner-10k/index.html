Course

A fully waymarked 10K run entirely within Whinlatter Forest, starting at 6pm in darkness with head torches required. The very hilly route mixes hard-packed gravel forest roads, twisting single dirt track and occasional muddy bridleway. Suitable for novice and experienced trail runners alike.

About

Whinlatter Forest is England's only true mountain forest. Set within the Lake District and home to stunning views, fantastic walks and trail running, exhilarating mountain biking, rare wildlife and adventure play. The Whinlatter Moon Runner 10K Head Torch Trail Race takes place in the winter months when a crisp ground frost is common. Beams of moonlight breaking through the trees can create a magical atmosphere while you run along the sparkling surface.
