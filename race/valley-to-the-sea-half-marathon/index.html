Course

Point-to-point half marathon from Iao Valley in Wailuku down to the Maui Humpback Whale Sanctuary in Kihei, flat to downhill with only short uphill sections. The 5K and 10K are coastal loops starting and finishing at the Whale Sanctuary in Kihei with ocean views throughout.

About

The Valley to the Sea Half Marathon starts at the Iao Valley Needle above Wailuku on Maui and runs down to the coast, with 10K and 5K distances offered alongside the half. Finishers gather on the beach, and a shuttle to the start line is available to entrants on request.
