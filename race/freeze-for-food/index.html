Course

Starts and finishes at the Vilas Park Shelter on Vilas Park Drive, Madison. The 5K run/walk goes at midday and the 10K at 1:00pm, and entrants can take on both. Now in its 46th year, it raises funds for Open Doors for Refugees.

About

The Freeze for Food has been a labor of love for the RPCVs of Madison. Through the years it has been passed on from member to member to manage as a fundraiser for food security projects. After the International Calendar began making a profit (around 1992), a certain amount from calendar sales has been allocated to supporting the expenses of the Freeze.
