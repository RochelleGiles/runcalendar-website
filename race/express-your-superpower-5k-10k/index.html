Course

The course runs entirely within Etowah River Park and Heritage Park, starting at 600 Brown Industrial Blvd, Canton. The race is not chip-timed; finishers complete a finish card at the line recording name, age, bib number and gender.

About

The 11th Annual Express Your SuperPower 5K/10K, Tot Trot, and Fun Run is presented by the Northwest Georgia Breastfeeding Coalition and Georgia Breastfeeding Coalition. Participants run a fast, flat course entirely within Etowah River Park and Heritage Park in Canton, Georgia, wearing capes to support the MedMilk Project for Georgia's first milk bank.
