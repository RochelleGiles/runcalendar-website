About

The Maira Occitan Trail takes runners on a captivating journey through the picturesque Occitan region of Italy. Winding through stunning mountain landscapes, this event offers a unique opportunity to explore the rich cultural heritage and breathtaking natural beauty of the area. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, the Maira Occitan Trail promises an unforgettable experience.
