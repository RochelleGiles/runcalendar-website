About

The Oktoberfest Road Race offers 5-mile and 5K courses through the Waltham area, starting and finishing at Mighty Squirrel Brewery. Now in its eighth year, the race is organized by the RACE Cancer Foundation and ends with an after-party featuring live music and beer.
