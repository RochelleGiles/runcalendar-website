About

L'Hivernale des Lacs is held in Orthez and traditionally opens the season of off-stadium races in the Pyrénées-Atlantiques department. It draws runners from across the Béarn, Landes, Gers, Basque, and Bigorre areas for a run after the year-end holidays.
