Course

A 10K at Thornes Park, Wakefield, run as a double lap of the 5K route. The terrain mixes tarmac, grass and woodland paths over rolling hills with some steep inclines. Start and finish are on fairly flat, firm grass that can turn soft in wet weather.

About

Race for Life's 10K is held at Thornes Park in Wakefield, a park close to the city centre with a lake and sports facilities. Runners, joggers and walkers take part at their own pace, with proceeds going to Cancer Research UK.
