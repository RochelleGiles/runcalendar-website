About

The Corrida de Dieppe is an annual running event that takes place in Dieppe, France. The event features various race distances for runners of all ages and abilities, creating a lively and inclusive atmosphere. The course showcases the scenic coastal town, allowing participants to enjoy the picturesque views while challenging themselves.
