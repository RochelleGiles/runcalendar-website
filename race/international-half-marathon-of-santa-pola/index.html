About

Explore the stunning coastal scenery of Santa Pola as you take on the International Half Marathon. This picturesque seaside town hosts a world-class running event, challenging athletes with a scenic route that showcases the region's natural beauty. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Santa Pola International Half Marathon promises an unforgettable racing experience.
