Course

A 15K trail race through the North Zone of Mount Jumbo, on city conservation and open space land that serves as winter range for the local elk herd. It starts and finishes at the Hellgate Soccer Fields on Lincoln Hills Drive. The in-person field is capped at 300, with a separate virtual option.

About

The Mount Jumbo Elk Ramble 15K climbs Mount Jumbo, a grassland peak overlooking Missoula, Montana, that is preserved as winter range for elk and closed to the public each winter to protect the herd.
