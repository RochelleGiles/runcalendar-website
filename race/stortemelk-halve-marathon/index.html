About

The Stortemelk Halve Marathon is a popular running event held on the island of Vlieland in the Netherlands. Runners can choose from distances of 21.1 km, 10 km, and 5.5 km, with the courses taking them through the island's beautiful forests, dunes, and paved roads. The event also features a 'Ministortelon' race for children up to 12 years old, making it a fun and inclusive experience for the whole family.
