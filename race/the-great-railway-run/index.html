About

Explore the scenic Irish countryside on The Great Railway Run, a 25km road race starting in the historic city of Cork and finishing in the picturesque town of Carrigaline. This unique event offers runners the chance to experience Ireland's stunning landscapes and rich railway heritage as they wind their way through rolling hills and charming villages.
