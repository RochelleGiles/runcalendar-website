Course

A 24-hour event at Pemberton Park in which a 5K trail loop sets off every hour on the hour from 6pm Friday to 5pm Saturday; runners complete as many loops as they choose, solo or in four-person teams. A well-stocked aid station sits at the start/finish, and overnight camping is included.

About

The Pemberton 24 challenges runners to complete a 5K loop through Pemberton Historical Park in Salisbury, Maryland, every hour on the hour for up to 24 hours. Missing a lap eliminates you, while finishing early just means a longer wait for the next round.
