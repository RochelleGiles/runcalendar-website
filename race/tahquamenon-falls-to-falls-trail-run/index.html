Course

A technical single-loop 9-mile trail run in Tahquamenon Falls State Park, starting near the trailhead east of Tahquamenon Brewery. The route follows the connector to the nature trail, then the NCT (blue blazes) to a pedestrian bridge, completes a counter-clockwise island loop and returns to the upper falls. Field is capped at 100. One aid station at the pedestrian bridge (about mile 4) is passed twice, with water, Gatorade and snacks.

About

The Tahquamenon Falls to Falls Trail Run runs from Upper Tahquamenon Falls to Lower Tahquamenon Falls and back, in Tahquamenon Falls State Park near Paradise, Michigan. It starts and finishes near the trailhead just east of the Tahquamenon Brewery, where runners can celebrate afterward with a meal or drink.
