Course

All distances start and finish on Cormier Road outside the EPIC Event Center at 2351 Holmgren Way, Green Bay. The marathon, half marathon and both relays set off at 7am, the 5K at 7.45am and a kids dash at 10am.

About

The Cellcom Green Bay Marathon offers marathon, half marathon and 5K events through Green Bay, Wisconsin each May. The route follows the Fox River past downtown landmarks before runners finish inside an NFL stadium. Green Bay sits on a bay of Lake Michigan.
