About

The Bélier Blanc is an annual running event that takes place in the stunning setting of La Clusaz, France. The event features multiple race distances, allowing runners of all levels to participate and experience the breathtaking mountain scenery. The event is known for its unique atmosphere, with runners enjoying the event under the starry skies of the Aravis region.
