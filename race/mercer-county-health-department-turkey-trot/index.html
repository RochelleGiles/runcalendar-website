About

The Mercer County Health Department's 5K on the Saturday before Thanksgiving, starting and finishing at its office on NW 7th Street in Aledo. The route takes in a couple of hills in the first mile, then a gentle grade into a flat run to the finish. Chip-timed; proceeds support the department's client assistance fund.
