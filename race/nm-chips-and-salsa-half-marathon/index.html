Course

A flat loop course through the North Valley and Los Ranchos de Albuquerque. Start and finish are on the Southwestern Indian Polytechnic Institute campus on Coors Boulevard NW. Seven fluid stations carrying water and Powerade sit roughly every two miles, with restrooms at the start, finish and mile 6.

About

The NM Chips and Salsa Half Marathon runs along the Rio Grande bosque trails in Los Ranchos de Albuquerque, New Mexico, with half marathon, 10K and 5K distances finishing with a chips-and-salsa fiesta.
