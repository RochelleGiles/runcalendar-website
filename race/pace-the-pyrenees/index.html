Course

Starts and finishes at the Goldfields Recreation Reserve in Beaufort, heading around the lake and out onto trail roads through the Trawalla State Forest. All distances share the same start and finish. The 50km and 5km run on the Saturday, with the 25km, 10km and a 2km kids race on the Sunday.

About

Pace the Pyrenees is a trail ultra based in Beaufort, Victoria, running through the Pyrenees Ranges with distances from a 2km kids' dash up to a 50km ultra.
