Course

Starts and finishes at Fairmont Resort on Sublime Point Road, Leura, heading into Blue Mountains National Park towards Lilian's Bridge where the 11 km and 16 km courses split. Both run on single trail and stairs with views of Wentworth Falls and Mt Solitary. Aid at Conservation Hut (water, fruit) and Chester Rd (water, fruit, electrolyte); runners carry their own 500 ml and no cups are provided.

About

Experience our most breathtaking short course race, starting and finishing at the majestic Fairmont Resort. The course winds through the Blue Mountains National Park on single trails and stairs, offering runners some of the most magnificent views of Wentworth Falls and Mt. Solitary.
