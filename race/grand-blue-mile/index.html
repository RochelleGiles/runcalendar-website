About

The Grand Blue Mile, presented by Wellmark Blue Cross & Blue Shield, is a one-mile street run through Downtown Des Moines that has drawn participants from dozens of states and several countries. Designed for all ages and abilities, it brings together competitive and elite runners with recreational participants and families to celebrate personal achievement and healthy habits.
