About

Asha-Jyothi (AJ) is a non-profit organization dedicated to serve the needs of underprivileged kids education and healthcare. Please join us for this fun and family September run/walk. The course will have hydration stations with course marshals to make it a great place for run/walk. After the race, there will medal ceremony for the kids (1-mile finishers), free food and coffee for everyone. This event is more than Run/walk, it`s all-singing, all-dancing at the end.
