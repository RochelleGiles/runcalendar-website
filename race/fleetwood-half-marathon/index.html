Course

An out-and-back half marathon along Fleetwood's seafront, starting and finishing on the promenade. The route is fast and flat on road and promenade, with the Irish Sea and Morecambe Bay alongside for much of the way. Water stations at 3, 6, 9 and 12 miles supply 500 ml bottles.

About

The Fleetwood Half Marathon is a road race that runs along Fleetwood's Outer Promenade and Esplanade with great views over Morecambe Bay. The 13.1 mile course is very flat and accurately measured, offering a perfect race for runners of all abilities with a fun and friendly atmosphere.
