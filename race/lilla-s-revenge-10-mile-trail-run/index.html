Course

Ten-mile trail run starting and finishing at Jugger Howe Beck on the North York Moors, following part of the Lyke Wake Walk long-distance footpath across open moorland. The route visits Lilla Howe, site of the 7th-century Lilla Cross, and Louvern Howe, believed to be a Neolithic or Bronze Age burial site.

About

Traverse the stunning landscapes of the North York Moors National Park in Silverback Trails' Lilla's Revenge 10 Mile Trail Run. This challenging off-road course winds through rugged terrain, offering breathtaking views and an exhilarating test of endurance for adventurous runners. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or seeking a new outdoor challenge, Lilla's Revenge promises an unforgettable running experience.
