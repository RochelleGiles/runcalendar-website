About

The Full Moon Trail Series highlights some of Northeast Ohio's most scenic trails within the Cleveland Metroparks and Cuyahoga Valley National Park. Suited to experienced trail runners and newcomers alike, these Tuesday night races align with the summer's full moons for an evening of running, camaraderie and nature.
