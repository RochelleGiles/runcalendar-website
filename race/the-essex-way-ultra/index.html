Course

A point-to-point 82-mile ultra following the full length of the Essex Way, starting at Epping tube station on the Friday evening and finishing at the lighthouse in Harwich. The terrain is mixed trail, footpath, track and road with around 3,469ft of ascent. There are five checkpoints, one drop bag, GPS tracking and race medical support, under a 24-hour cut-off.

About

The Essex Way Ultra is an ultra marathon that covers the full 82 miles of the beautiful Essex Way trail. This point to point course starts at Epping tube station in the city of London and finishes at the High Lighthouse in the historic port of Harwich.
