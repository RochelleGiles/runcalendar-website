About

The Milk Run is a capped 5K held on the Minnesota State Fairgrounds as part of the Kickoff to Summer at the Fair weekend. Runners loop the fairgrounds from a start and finish near the International Bazaar. Entries are limited and the race has sold out within days of opening.
