Course

A point-to-point 47-mile route along the Thames Path from The Catherine Wheel at Henley-on-Thames to the finish at Kingston upon Thames. Mixed riverside terrain of towpath, open common and meadow, passing Marlow Lock, Windsor Racecourse, Windsor Castle and Runnymede. Three checkpoints with water and nutrition at 9.5, 21.5 and 35.4 miles.

About

The MTC Windsor Ultra is an ultramarathon along the Thames Path, England's longest river path. Runners will traverse 47 miles from Henley to Kingston, experiencing a challenging and scenic route through the beautiful Thames countryside.
