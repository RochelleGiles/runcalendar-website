About

The Tamborine Mountain Relays & Marathon is a unique running event held in a beautiful mountain setting. The course features a 7km loop combining bitumen, footpath, grass and trail surfaces that participants complete multiple times. Runners can tackle the full marathon solo or participate in teams of 2, 3 or 6 people, making it an inclusive event that caters to different fitness levels.
