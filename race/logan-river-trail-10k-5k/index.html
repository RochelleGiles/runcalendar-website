Course

The course follows the Logan River Trail on an 8 foot wide concrete path with no elevation change, crossing the river several times by bridge on a shaded route. Both distances start and finish at Willow Park on West 700 South. The 5K follows the blue line on the course map and the 10K the blue and red lines.

About

The race follows the beautiful Logan River Trail on an 8' wide concrete path with no elevation changes. You'll run through a shaded course crossing the Logan River several times by bridge. This is a favorite running trail in Logan. Don't miss out!
