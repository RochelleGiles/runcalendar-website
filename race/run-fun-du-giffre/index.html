About

Explore the stunning mountain scenery of the Giffre Valley at the annual Run & Fun du Giffre event. This scenic trail race offers a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region, with routes winding through picturesque villages and alongside serene alpine streams. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or a newcomer to the sport, the Run & Fun du Giffre promises an unforgettable running adventure.
