Course

A four-mile loop starting and finishing at Duck Creek Park Lodge in Davenport, running through Duck Creek Park, the surrounding neighbourhood and along the bike path before heading back to the lodge. The course is flat and sealed, and both a run and a walk category start together at 1:00 p.m.

About

The CBRC Chili Chase is a 4-mile run/walk event organized by the Cornbelt Running Club in Davenport, Iowa. The race starts at 1:00 p.m. and loops through Duck Creek Park, bike paths, and surrounding neighborhoods on a mostly flat course.
