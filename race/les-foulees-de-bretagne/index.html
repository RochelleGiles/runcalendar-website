About

Discover the rich cultural heritage of Brittany at the annual Les Foulées de Bretagne running event. Set against the picturesque backdrop of the region, this race invites runners to explore scenic routes while immersing themselves in the vibrant local traditions. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a newcomer to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
