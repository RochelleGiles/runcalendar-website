About

A trail through the Quincy wine appellation in the Cher, with 28K, 13K and 8K routes rolling through vineyards and the gently wooded countryside of the Loire Valley. Quincy is one of the oldest appellations in the Loire, known for its Sauvignon Blanc whites.
