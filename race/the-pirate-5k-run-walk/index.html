About

The 4th Annual Pirate 5k Run/Walk is a fun event in Lincolnton, NC, where participants are encouraged to dress in pirate costumes. The race starts at St. Luke's Episcopal Church and follows a scenic course including the Lincolnton Rail Trail, welcoming runners and walkers of all ages with awards and finisher medals.
