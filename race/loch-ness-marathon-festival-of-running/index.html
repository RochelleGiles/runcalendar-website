Course

Point-to-point along the south-eastern shore of Loch Ness from the hills near Whitebridge to Inverness. A downhill opening, a long lochside stretch with the water beside you, then a stiff climb out of Dores around 30 km before the descent into the city and the finish at Bught Park.

About

The Loch Ness Marathon & Festival of Running is a renowned running event set in the stunning Scottish Highlands. Runners can enjoy a challenging yet scenic course that winds along the banks of the iconic Loch Ness, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains and countryside. The event has a vibrant atmosphere, with enthusiastic crowds cheering on the participants throughout the race.
