Course

Both courses start and finish at Mt Difficulty Winery. The 25km is an out-and-back over vineyard verges, untracked mountainside, sheep tracks and 4WD tracks, returning on a different trail through the Bannockburn Sluicings, with aid stations at 6.5km and 16km. The 44km follows the 25km route then climbs to the Mt Difficulty summit and descends a different ridge, with five aid stations.

About

The Mount Difficulty Ascent is an adventure run that takes participants on a challenging course through the scenic Otago region of New Zealand. The course features a mix of terrain, including dirt, mud, rock, and snow, making it a true test of endurance and skill. The event is known for its stunning mountain views and the camaraderie among the runners.
