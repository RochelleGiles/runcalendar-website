Course

Out-and-back single-track on the Ice Age Trail, starting and finishing at the historic Oleson Cabin trailhead. Courses are flagged by colour — 3.6 miles red, 7.0 miles green, 12.0 miles blue — with the Golden Gauntlet combining all three for 22.6 miles. An aid station sits at the Highway 12 road crossing, and the terrain grows more technical with distance.

About

The Golden Kettle Trail Series - Oleson Cabin is a trail running event featuring three looped courses starting and finishing at the historic Oleson Cabin in Whitewater, Wisconsin. Participants enjoy a stress-free start between 7:00 AM and 12:00 PM, live music, craft beer for those 21+, and a commitment to trail sustainability.
