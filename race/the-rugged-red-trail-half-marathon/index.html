Course

A single-loop trail half marathon set in the Red River Gorge, described by the organisers as very challenging and technical: roots, rocks, creek crossings, steep inclines and declines, stair steps and uneven trail throughout. A virtual option is also offered.

About

A trail half marathon in Slade, Kentucky, at the gateway to the Red River Gorge in the Daniel Boone National Forest. The course climbs through the Gorge's sandstone cliffs, natural arches and forested ridgelines.
