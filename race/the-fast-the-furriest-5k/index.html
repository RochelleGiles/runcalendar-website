About

Join us for this inaugural race supporting The Betty J. Martin L.I.F.E. House For Animals, Inc! We are excited to welcome runners and walkers of all abilities to this fun new race as we close out winter. Come run/walk a 5K on the scenic paved trails at Lakeview Park in Frankfort, Kentucky as we support our furry friends through the Betty J. Martin L.I.F.E. House For Animals.
