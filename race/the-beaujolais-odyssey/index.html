About

The Beaujolais Odyssey is a unique running adventure through the picturesque vineyards of the Beaujolais region in France. Runners will wind their way through rolling hills and charming villages, immersed in the rich history and culture of this renowned winemaking area. This event offers an unforgettable experience for those seeking a scenic and challenging road race.
