Course

All distances start and finish in downtown Helena and climb quickly from historic streets onto rugged single track in the Mount Helena and Mount Ascension urban parks, which rise about 1,400 feet above the valley. The 50K traverses five ridges and summits two peaks.

About

Montana's trail race season begins in May at the Don't Fence Me In Trail Run. Explore Helena's South Hills and the lands and trails protected by Prickly Pear Land Trust. Don't Fence Me In is more than a race – it's a grassroots celebration of community, connection, and Happy Trails.
