Course

Loop course based at the Meadows parking lot in Ward Pound Ridge Reservation, Cross River. Mostly wooded trail mixing technical singletrack with wide carriage roads: the 25K is one loop, the 50K two loops of that course, and the 50-miler two loops of a 25-mile course. Two to three aid stations on course, plus start/finish for the 50K and 50M.

About

Trail races of 25K, 50K and 50 miles at Ward Pound Ridge Reservation, a 4,000-acre preserve at Cross River, New York, about 50 miles north of New York City. The courses run on wooded trails combining technical singletrack with wide carriage roads, run as repeated loops.
