About

The Kosice Peace Marathon is a prestigious running event held annually in Kosice, Slovakia. The course takes runners through the historic city center, showcasing the beautiful architecture and vibrant atmosphere. Participants can choose from the full marathon, half marathon, or relay options, making it an inclusive event for runners of all levels.
