Course

The runs start on the Washington side of the Columbia River and cross to Cascade Locks, following the Historic Columbia River Highway State Park Trail with the river directly underfoot. The 10K is an out and back, aided every 1 to 1.5 miles, and the half marathon allows a generous five-hour cutoff for walkers.

About

A destination running event along the Historic Columbia River Highway State Park Trail, starting on the Washington side and crossing the scenic Bridge of the Gods. It features a Half Marathon, 10K, Twilight 5K, and Kids' Community 1K, with post-race festivities including music and catered lunch.
