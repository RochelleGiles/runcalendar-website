Course

Loop course starting and finishing at Riverside Park, Goulburn. All distances (21.1km half marathon, 10km, 5km and 2km Family Dash) use a fully pathed, undulating route around the Wollondilly River on sealed path. Water and Tailwind stations are on course at 3, 5, 9.6, 11.5, 13.5, 18 and 20km for the half marathon and 2.5, 7 and 8.7km for the 10km.

About

The Goulburn Running Festival is based at Riverside Park in Goulburn, New South Wales, offering a 21.1km half marathon, 10km, 5km and a 2km Family Dash. All distances follow a fully pathed, undulating loop alongside the Wollondilly River, with water and Tailwind stations on course.
