Course

An evening 10km starting and finishing at Silent Pool car park in Albury, near Guildford. The route follows part of the North Downs Way and the forest of Netley Heath before descending to Shere village, mixing wide trails with singletrack. Free post-run beer at the finish.

About

A Running Adventures 10K through the Surrey Hills AONB near Guildford, taking in the Silent Pool, the clear spring-fed pond at the foot of the North Downs. The route crosses rolling chalk countryside around Shere and Albury.
