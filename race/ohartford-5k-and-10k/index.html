Course

A road 5K and 10K through Hartford's Coltsville Historic District, finishing at midfield inside Trinity Health Stadium, home of Hartford Athletic. A quarter- and half-mile FitKids run is held alongside, and a virtual 5K is also offered.

About

A 5K and 10K road race through downtown Hartford, Connecticut, capping the Hartford Marathon Foundation's four-race St. Patrick's Day series. Thousands of runners turn out each March to close out the shamrock-themed race weekend.
