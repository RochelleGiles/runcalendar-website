Course

The 5K starts at Tiger Stadium in San Jacinto at 8:00 am, run alongside a Unified Games day for local students. The organiser publishes a course map but no detail on surface, route or aid stations. Stadium parking closes at 7:45 am, when parking moves to the Tiger Lane lot.

About

A community 5K in San Jacinto, California, run alongside a Unified Games day for local students. The course is open to runners and walkers of all ages, and the event has now reached its fourteenth edition.
