About

Leg It! for LARCC is a fundraising 5km walk/run in aid of Cancer Support Sanctuary LARCC, held in LARCC's grounds in Multyfarnham. LARCC is funded almost entirely by public donations and fundraising events to meet the cost of the services it offers. The event is timed and AAI sanctioned.
