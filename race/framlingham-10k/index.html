Course

An undulating two-lap road course starting in Framlingham Market Square and finishing on the Sports Club fields. The route passes the castle and takes in country lanes, with the short but steep Market Hill climbed twice. A Little Leaders junior race of about 2K runs alongside for under-15s.

About

A Framlingham Flyers 10K road race that circles the town beneath its 12th-century castle, made famous beyond Suffolk by Ed Sheeran's song of the same name. A junior fun run runs alongside for younger family members.
