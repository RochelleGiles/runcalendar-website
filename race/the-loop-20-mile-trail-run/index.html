Course

Loop starting and finishing at Broadsands Beach in Torbay, running the South West Coast Path out along the cliffs before returning on inland countryside and woodland trails. Rollercoaster coastal going including the steep double dip at Mansands and Scabbacombe beaches. Three aid stations with water, coke, squash, flapjacks, crisps and other snacks; a route map is mandatory kit.

About

The Loop! 20 Mile Trail Run is a challenging mixed terrain route on the South West Coast Path and countryside trails. The course starts on the beach at Broadsands Beach and traverses through scenic landscapes, including woodland paths, coastal trails, and historic areas, with approximately 4000ft of ascent.
