Course

Point-to-point downhill road race dropping from Guardsman Pass at the top of Big Cottonwood Canyon to Cottonwood High School in Murray. The half marathon starts about ten miles up the canyon just below the Mill D parking area and finishes at the same place. Aid stations roughly every two miles carry water, Gatorade, gels and fruit, with medical support and sag wagons on course.

About

REVEL Big Cottonwood starts high in the Wasatch Mountains' Big Cottonwood Canyon and descends toward Salt Lake City, part of the REVEL series of steep net-downhill road races. Marathon and half marathon distances share the canyon course, finishing in the valley below Murray.
