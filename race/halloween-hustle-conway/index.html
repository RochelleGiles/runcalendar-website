Course

USATF-certified 5K and 10K plus a 1-mile, all starting at 4 Elm St in downtown Conway. Flat and scenic on sealed roads through the historic centre, heavily signed with mile markers throughout. The 10K carries two water stops and the 5K one, each led out by a cyclist.

About

The Halloween Hustle is a costume-friendly 10K, 5K and one-mile race hosted by Coastal Race Productions in Conway, South Carolina, a Waccamaw River town, each October. It's a fall favorite for local run clubs and families.
