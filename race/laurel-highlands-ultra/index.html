Course

Point-to-point ultra on the Laurel Highlands Hiking Trail, starting at Ohiopyle, Pennsylvania. The 70 miler runs the full trail to Seward; the 50K finishes at the 31-mile mark near Route 31. Relay teams cover the same courses. Entirely on hiking trail, with fields capped at 210 for the 70 miler and 250 for the 50K.

About

The Laurel Highlands Ultra is held each year on the second Saturday in June. The 70.5 mile race is now past its 46th running, and the 50K is past its 20th running.
