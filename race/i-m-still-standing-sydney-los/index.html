About

I'm Still Standing Sydney is a unique Last One Standing format ultra race that challenges runners to push their limits in the scenic Heffron Park in Maroubra, Australia. Competitors must keep moving forward on a looped course until they are the last runner still standing, making this an exhilarating test of endurance and mental fortitude for ultrarunners.
