About

The Festival de Trails EEC Engie is an ultra trail running event showcasing the natural beauty of New Caledonia. The event features multiple trail running distances and routes through scenic trails, including passages by wind turbines and solar farms. It promotes ecological awareness and supports local community initiatives.
