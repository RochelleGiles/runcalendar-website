About

The Blackfoot Ultra is a trail ultramarathon through the Blackfoot Recreation Area near Sherwood Park, Alberta, on forested trails east of Edmonton. Runners choose from 25K, 50K, 50-mile and 100K distances on trails within the recreation area.
