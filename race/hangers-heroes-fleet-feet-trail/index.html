Course

Based at Bagwell Farm Touring Park in Chickerell near Weymouth, Dorset. The courses are multi-terrain, rural and hilly, with route markers throughout: the 10K is a single circular lap, the half marathon an out-and-back and the marathon a circular lap. Routes are not traffic-free, and anyone retiring is given free transport back to the finish.

About

Explore the stunning Dorset countryside at the Hangers Heroes' Fleet & Feet Trail, offering a 10km, half, or full marathon run or walk. Challenge yourself on this scenic trail through rolling hills and picturesque landscapes, hosted by the inspiring Hangers Heroes organization. An event not to be missed for outdoor enthusiasts and runners seeking a memorable adventure.
