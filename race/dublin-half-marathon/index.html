About

The Dublin Half Marathon is a must-run event for distance enthusiasts, offering a scenic tour through the heart of Ireland's vibrant capital city. Runners can expect a challenging yet rewarding course, winding past iconic landmarks and picturesque neighborhoods. Whether you're an experienced half-marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, this event promises an unforgettable running experience in the historic city of Dublin.
