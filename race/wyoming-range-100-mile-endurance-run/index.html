Course

A point-to-point 104.4-mile wilderness run south to north through the Bridger-Teton National Forest, from the Fish Creek Trailhead near Big Piney to a finish near Hoback. High-country trail throughout, with 28,700 ft of climbing and 31,000 ft of descent across nine climbs of over 1,000 ft. The high point of 11,266 ft comes early at mile 9.7; the finish at 6,085 ft is the lowest point on course.

About

A 100-mile mountain ultramarathon through the Wyoming Range within Bridger-Teton National Forest, near Big Piney, Wyoming. The remote course climbs through high-altitude backcountry trail with steep, sustained elevation changes typical of the range.
