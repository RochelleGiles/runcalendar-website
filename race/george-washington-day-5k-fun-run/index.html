About

The George Washington Day 5K & Fun Run is hosted by the Warwick Historical Society on a USA Track & Field certified course starting and finishing at Veterans Memorial Park in Warwick, NY. This family-friendly event features a 5K race through the village's charming streets past historic landmarks, with chip timing and age-group awards.
