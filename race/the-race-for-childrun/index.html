About

The Race for ChildRUN is a fundraiser for The Children's Center located on the SUNY Morrisville Campus. It's a family friendly event that offers a challenging 5K race option with a fun 1 Mile run/walk option for participants of all ages. The event is sponsored by local businesses and all proceeds benefit the child care tuition scholarships at The Center.
