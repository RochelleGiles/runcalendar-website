About

ZTA's 37th Annual Franklin 5K is a 5K race organized by the Zeta Tau Alpha sorority at UNC Chapel Hill to support breast cancer awareness and local charities including the ZTA Foundation, UNC Lineberger Comprehensive Cancer Center, and the Arc of the Triangle. The event features a festive atmosphere with early packet pickup available the day before.
