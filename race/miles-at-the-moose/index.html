About

A 5K run/walk held on the Moose Ridge Golf Course at South Lyon, Michigan, with proceeds going to the Rogel Cancer Center breast cancer programme at University of Michigan Medicine. The course runs the fairways and cart paths over rolling ground, and a 1K kids' run is held alongside it.
