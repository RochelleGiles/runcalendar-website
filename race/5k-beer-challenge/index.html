Course

Two laps of a 2.5km circuit at Bowdon Rugby Club, running through wild fields, woodland and paths. Inflatable and non-inflatable obstacles are spread around the loop - a tyre run, hay bales, a crawler and a walk-the-line - and drinking stations sit roughly every kilometre. Runners choose a start time slot of 12pm, 1pm, 2pm or 3pm.

About

The 5K Beer Challenge in Altrincham, Cheshire offers a unique twist on the classic running event. Participants will enjoy a scenic route through the leafy suburbs and countryside of Bowden, with the added incentive of sampling local craft beers along the way. This is a must-try event for runners seeking a fun, social challenge.
