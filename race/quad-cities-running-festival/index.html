Course

The half marathon starts by the big bicycle statue between Cherry and Linn Streets in Port Byron and follows an out-and-back route through the Quad Cities. The 5K starts at Hampton Heritage Center near Empire Park. Both courses carry water and electrolyte stations at multiple miles.

About

Join us on Monday, May 26th for the Quad Cities Running Festival, a scenic, point-to-point run along the Great River Trail on the Mississippi (from Port Byron to East Moline, Illinois). Offering Half Marathon, 5K, and Kids Race routes.
