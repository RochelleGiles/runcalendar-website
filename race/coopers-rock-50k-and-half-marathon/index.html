Course

A rocky, scenic trail race in Coopers Rock State Forest east of Morgantown, starting at the Overlook Area with views over the Cheat Canyon and Cheat Lake and past towering boulder formations. The 50K climbs around 5,000 feet, the half marathon about 2,000; neither course repeats loops.

About

A 50K and half marathon on trail through Coopers Rock State Forest near Bruceton Mills, West Virginia, with the 50K starting at night. The forest's main overlook looks down onto the Cheat River Canyon.
