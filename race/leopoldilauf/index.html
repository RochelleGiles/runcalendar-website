About

The Leopoldilauf is a running event organized by the LCC Wien (Laufclub Concordia Wien) in Austria. The event features 7km, 14km, and half marathon distances, with a flat and traffic-free course along the Prater Hauptallee. The race is AIMS-measured and offers finisher medals for all participants.
