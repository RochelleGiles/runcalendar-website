About

The Marathon Rund Um Den Winterstein is a traditional running event in Hessen that combines a volksmarathon with scenic landscape running. The challenging route takes participants over the Steinkopf summit at 518 meters, offering panoramic views of the Mainebene, Taunus, and Wetterau regions.
