About

The Molten Mohawk Niner will start and finish in Hattiesburg, Mississippi at Thomley's Christmas Tree Farm (50 Hegwood Road). Athletes can choose the run the challenging nine mile course or the flat 4 mile course on paved roads. Once completing the distance, runners will gather back at Thomley's Christmas Tree Farm socializing around GIANT bonfires and watching our artist fire up their glass and metal creating works of art.
