Course

Timed-format winter race on a 2-mile loop at Silver Springs Park Lodge in Stow, Ohio. Runners start between 10am and noon and complete as many loops as they wish within the window; the 4 Hour option awards the most loops, the 3 x 2 Mile scores the three fastest. The course drains well in winter conditions.

About

Ready for something unique? Perfect start of the year with Two Mile Loops and rest between loops - off the clock! Choose between the fastest 3 loops or the most loops in 4 hours. Plus enjoy chili and other offerings inside our heated space after or during a break.
