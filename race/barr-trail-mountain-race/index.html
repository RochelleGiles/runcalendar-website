Course

A 12.6-mile out-and-back on Pikes Peak's Barr Trail. Starts at the Cog Railway Station in Manitou Springs at 6,570 ft, climbs to Barr Camp at 10,200 ft, then turns and descends to finish at the Barr Trailhead, for about 3,630 ft of gain. The race is cup-less, so runners carry their own cup or reservoir.

About

A mountain race on Colorado's legendary Barr Trail, climbing from Manitou Springs toward Pikes Peak along the same route used for the Pikes Peak Ascent. Runners face a steady climb through pine forest with sweeping Front Range views before turning back for the descent.
