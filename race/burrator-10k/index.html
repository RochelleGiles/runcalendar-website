Course

A fast, flat 10K almost entirely on tarmac around Burrator Reservoir, with only about 90 metres of climbing. Runners start on the dam bridge roughly 1km south-east of the visitor centre, complete one full lap of the reservoir and about two thirds of a second. Kids' 1K and 2K trail races run earlier in the evening as out-and-backs, uphill out and downhill home.

About

The Burrator 10K offers runners a fast, flat 10km route that is almost entirely on tarmac around the scenic Burrator Reservoir in Yelverton, United Kingdom. This event provides a picturesque and challenging course for both seasoned runners and those looking to test their 10K abilities in a beautiful natural setting.
