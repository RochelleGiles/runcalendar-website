Course

Club races start from the SBSC Hall at Orleigh Park, West End, Brisbane, and are held fortnightly on Sunday mornings. The programme runs from 5 km up to 30 km during the peak season. A Strava route is linked but no surface or aid station detail is published.

About

Explore the vibrant streets of Brisbane as the Brisbane Road Runners Club hosts its annual running extravaganza. Offering a range of distances from 5K to 20K, this event caters to runners of all levels, providing a scenic and challenging course through the heart of the city. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a newcomer to the sport, this race promises an unforgettable experience for the whole running community.
