About

The Trail du Couserans takes place in Saint-Girons, Ariège, within the Parc Naturel Régional des Pyrénées Ariégeoises. Courses include the Trail des Falaises, Trail Les 4 Seigneurs and Liberté Trail, ranging from about 11km to 31km through the Pyrenean foothills.
