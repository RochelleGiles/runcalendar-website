Course

Out-and-back on a single route, starting and finishing at Riverside Park in Morgan City, Utah. The course combines flat sections with rolling hills. Aid stations appear every other mile from mile 2, carrying water, Gatorade and gels. Roads are not closed and remain open to traffic.
