About

A trail running event held at San Pedro Valley Park in Pacifica, CA, featuring 1,150 acres of coastal mountain environment nestled in the Santa Cruz Mountains. Runners choose from three distances (10K, Half Marathon, or 30K) on moderately technical dirt trails of Mt. Montara and surrounding foothills.
