Course

Loops of ATV trail across the Arizona Strip desert, based out of Mesquite, Nevada, with wide-open vistas and rolling hills past viewpoints such as Thelma & Louise. The 50K loop climbs about 1,580 ft and the 50-mile loop about 2,450 ft, with the 100K and 100 Mile repeating them. Aid stations sit every 4-7 miles.
