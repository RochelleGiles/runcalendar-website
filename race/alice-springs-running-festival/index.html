Course

All distances start and finish at Simpsons Gap in Tjoritja / West MacDonnell National Park, west of Alice Springs. The route runs up Darken Drive to the ranger station then back down the Simpsons Gap path, retracing the same way out and back. Undulating, with red range views for most of the distance.

About

The Alice Springs Running Festival returns to the stunning Red Centre of Australia, offering runners a chance to experience the breathtaking landscapes of the outback. Featuring a marathon, half marathon, and 10km run or walk, this event promises an unforgettable running adventure for all levels of participants.
