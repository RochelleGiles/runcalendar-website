About

The Urban Trail de la Butte Montmartre offers runners a unique challenge through the iconic streets and hills of Montmartre in Paris. Weaving past historic landmarks and stunning city views, this urban trail event promises an exhilarating experience for runners of all levels. Participants can expect an unforgettable adventure as they conquer the legendary Butte Montmartre.
