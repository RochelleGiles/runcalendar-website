Course

An out-and-back coastal route starting and finishing at Brighton Marina. Runners cross the Marina Wall then head east along the Undercliff Path towards Rottingdean, returning along the Overcliff above the English Channel. Low elevation gain of 65-75 m. Fully signed, with water stations on course; runners carry their own cup or bottle.

About

Explore the stunning Brighton Marina on the BrighTEN 10-miler or 10k run. Soak in picturesque views of the English coast as you wind through the marina and surrounding areas in this scenic trail event. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, the BrighTEN offers a beautiful and challenging route for all levels.
