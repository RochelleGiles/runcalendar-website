About

Explore the picturesque town of Vorra as you wind through historic chapels and scenic countryside at the annual Kapellenlauf running event. This unique race showcases the natural beauty of the region, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a newcomer to the sport, the Kapellenlauf Vorra is an event not to be missed.
