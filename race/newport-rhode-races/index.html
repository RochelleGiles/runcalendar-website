Course

Out-and-back road marathon and half marathon starting and finishing at Easton's Beach, running the craggy Newport shoreline past ocean vistas and historic mansions before turning inland through Middletown and returning along the waterfront. A 5K is also offered. Roads stay open to traffic with minor restrictions.

About

Newport Rhode Races is a marathon, half marathon and 5K weekend hosted in Newport, Rhode Island, a historic seaside city on Narragansett Bay known for its Gilded Age mansions, sailing harbor, and colonial-era streets.
