Course

Road courses on the north shore of Lake Pontchartrain, starting and finishing under moss-covered live oaks in Mandeville. The half marathon and 10 miler wind through the historic homes and lakefront streets of Old Mandeville and are flat throughout. The Jockamo IPA 5K is run the day before, on the Saturday.

About

The Northshore Half Marathon, 10 Miler and 5K start in Mandeville on the north shore of Lake Pontchartrain, taking runners through the city's lakefront streets and neighborhoods before finishing back near the water.
