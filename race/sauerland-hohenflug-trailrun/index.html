About

Explore the stunning Sauerland region of Germany at the Sauerland Hohenflug Trailrun. This challenging trail running event takes participants on a journey through the area's rolling hills and lush forests, offering breathtaking views at every turn. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Sauerland Hohenflug Trailrun promises an unforgettable adventure in the great outdoors.
