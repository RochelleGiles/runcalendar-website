About

Come out and join us for our 28th Annual Harvest 5k-10k run/walk & Kids Miler. As the oldest race in the Plainfield, this event has averaged around 800 to 900 people each year and has been put on by the Plainfield Shorewood Area Chamber of Commerce. Proceeds from the race each year are donated back out in the Plainfield and surrounding communities to non-profits and other organizations.
