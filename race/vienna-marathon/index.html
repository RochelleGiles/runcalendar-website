Course

Starts on the Reichsbrücke over the Danube, runs the long straight of the Prater Hauptallee, out past Schönbrunn Palace and back along the Ring to finish at Heldenplatz in the heart of the city. Flat and fast, with the imperial architecture as the backdrop.
