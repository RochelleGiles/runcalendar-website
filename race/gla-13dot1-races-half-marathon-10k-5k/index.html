Course

All three distances start and finish at the Town Square Terrace in Green Lake, on a hilly lakeside route around Big Green Lake. The half marathon goes at 7:30 AM and the 10K and 5K at 8:00 AM, under a 3.5-hour limit. Strollers and walkers are welcome, with music and awards on the terrace afterwards.

About

Kick off Labor Day Weekend in scenic Green Lake with your choice of a Half Marathon, 10K, or 5K —all starting and finishing at the Town Square Community Center Terrace. Our race courses wind through historic downtown, past local shops and parks, and along the breathtaking shoreline of Big Green Lake.
