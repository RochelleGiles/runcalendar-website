Course

A 5K, 10K and half marathon on the paved paths around White Rock Lake in Dallas, Texas. All three distances start and finish at Norbuck Park, 200 N Buckner Blvd. The lakeside route is flat and fast, with pace groups offered for the half marathon from 1:45 to 3:45.

About

Too Cold To Hold offers half marathon, 10K and 5K distances at White Rock Lake in Dallas, part of the Run Project race series. It brings together runners of all levels, with a post-race party and event swag for finishers.
