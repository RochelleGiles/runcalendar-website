About

The Sharland Cycles 5K Fun Run/Walk Series offers a relaxed, community-focused evening event in the beautiful New Zealand countryside. Runners and walkers of all abilities are invited to enjoy scenic routes and a friendly atmosphere at this weekly series hosted by the Waimea Harriers club. Whether you're training for a bigger race or just looking to stay active, this is the perfect midweek workout.
