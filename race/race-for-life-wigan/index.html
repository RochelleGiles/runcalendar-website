Course

A single 5K lap starting and finishing near Haigh Hall in Haigh Woodland Park. The route drops through woodland towards the Plantation Gates and climbs back to the finish, on tarmac paths that are occasionally uneven and can be slippery in wet weather. There are steep sections and no step-free alternative.

About

Race for Life's Wigan event runs through Haigh Woodland Park, the wooded former estate of the Earls of Crawford shaped by centuries of coal mining. Runners, joggers and walkers cover the course to raise money for Cancer Research UK.
