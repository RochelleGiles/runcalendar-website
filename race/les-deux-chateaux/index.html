About

Les Deux Châteaux is organized by VGA Compiègne. The 17km course runs mostly on a flat cycle path through the Compiègne forest, linking the Château de Pierrefonds to the Petitpoisson stadium in Compiègne. The 11km route starts in Vieux Moulin and joins the same course as the 17km, while the roughly 15km walk starts at the Petitpoisson stadium and follows forest paths.
