Course

Based at the Lake Zorinsky boat ramp in south-west Omaha, with packet pickup from 6:30am, a kids dash at 7:55am and the main start at 8:00am. Half marathon, 10K, 5K and 1 mile options run on marked routes around the lake and are chip-timed with live results.

About

Spooky Sprint is a Halloween-themed half marathon, 10K, and 5K in Omaha, Nebraska, held each October. It's part of a run of seasonal races in the city that also includes Hot Cider Hustle and Turkey Trails.
