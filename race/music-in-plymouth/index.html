About

This spectacular summer event has welcomed Plymouth residents and neighbors from around the Twin Cities (and beyond!) for more than 50 years. While a generous portion of the cost associated with presenting Music in Plymouth is provided by the City of Plymouth, the majority of the cost is still borne by corporate sponsors and individual donors. Your support can help make Music in Plymouth a Minnesota summertime extravaganza for generations to come!
