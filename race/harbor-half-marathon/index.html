Course

A half marathon, two-person relay and 4-mile race on the Corpus Christi bayfront, all starting at 7.30am. The route mixes city streets with Corpus Christi Beach and stretches of coastal wetland, passing the Harbor Bridge, Nueces Bay, the USS Lexington, the Texas State Aquarium and the working Port of Corpus Christi.
