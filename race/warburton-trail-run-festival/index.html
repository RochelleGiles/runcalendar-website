Course

Three-day trail festival in the Warburton Valley, run on singletrack. Most distances start and finish in Warburton; the 50 km Lumberjack Run starts at Powelltown, about 30 minutes' drive away. Courses take in Mt Donna Buang and the Redwoods. The 3 km Lilo Derby is a river float on inflatables rather than a run.

About

The Warburton Trail Run Festival is a celebration of the wild singletrack life in the beautiful Warburton Valley. Runners can take on a variety of distances, from the ultra-challenging 50km to the more approachable 14km and 8km options. The event features the unique Lilo Derby, where runners race down the river on inflatable floats, adding a fun and quirky element to the trail running experience.
