Course

Two short road courses through downtown St. George, starting by the pickleball courts at 200 S and 400 E and following the Dixie Days parade route. The 2K turns around at 400 E; the 4K continues to 164 W 100 S, where the aid station and bathrooms sit. Entirely on sealed city streets.

About

The Dixie Days Dash is a short community 2K and 4K run held in St. George, Utah, during the city's annual Dixie Days festival. It is organized through the St. George Running Center as a family-friendly event alongside the week's other festivities.
