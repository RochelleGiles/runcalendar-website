Course

Trail routes of 15 km, 25 km and 55 km on the Oak Valley Estate in the Elgin Valley, run largely on flowing single track normally reserved for mountain biking. Climbs are sustained, with about 430 m of ascent on the 15 km, 755 m on the 25 km and 1 289 m on the 55 km, and the routes look out over the Groenland mountains. Each distance is capped at 50 entries.
