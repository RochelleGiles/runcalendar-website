About

Race for the Dome brings hundreds of fans together to race around Houston's Astrodome, the 'Eighth Wonder of the World,' with a 5K road race and a Kids 1K fun run. Participants are encouraged to dress in vintage gear or their best Dome wear to celebrate the stadium's legacy.
