Course

A USATF-certified 5K on the streets of Montclair. The start is in front of Rand Park on North Fullerton Avenue and the finish is outside Montclair Bread Company. The route runs south to Walnut Street, crosses the railroad via Christopher Street to Watchung Avenue, loops through Montclair and Chestnut streets and returns via Christopher and Walnut. Doughnuts at the finish.
