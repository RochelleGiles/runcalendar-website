Course

Point-to-point five-mile social run in Raleigh, North Carolina, starting at Snoopy's on Hillsborough Street beside Meredith College and finishing at Snoopy's on Wake Forest Road. The road route is net downhill and passes the site of the former downtown Snoopy's. A safety briefing precedes the 2.30pm start.

About
