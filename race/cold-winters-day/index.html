Course

Both the 10K and 5K run flat and fast on sealed roads off North Trenholm Road, with turns marked in flour and staffed water stops, monitored throughout by police and volunteers. A downhill Snowflake Youth Fun Run runs alongside.

About

Cold Winter's Day is an annual 10K and 5K hosted by Strictly Running in Columbia, South Carolina's Trenholm Road area, held the Saturday after Christmas alongside a Snowflake Youth Fun Run for kids.
