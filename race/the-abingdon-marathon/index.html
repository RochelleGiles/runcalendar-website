About

The Abingdon Marathon is Oxfordshire's premier marathon and one of the oldest in the UK, offering runners a scenic tour through the charming countryside surrounding this historic town. With a reputation for excellent organization and a friendly, welcoming atmosphere, this event is a must for marathon enthusiasts looking to experience the best of British running culture.
