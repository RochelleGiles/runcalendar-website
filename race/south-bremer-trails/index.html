Course

Start and finish at Kimbolton Wines cellar door, Langhorne Creek. Flat courses over hard dirt road and sandy track through vineyard and farmland, with roughly 1 km of bitumen. The long course loops past Chilton Scrub towards North Bremer before a final loop and a hard 400 m of sand. Aid stations: three on the long, two on the medium, one on the short. Field capped at 250.

About

South Bremer Trails is a unique running event set in the pristine Langhorne Creek Wine Region. The course traverses through picturesque vineyards and scrubland, offering runners challenging terrain with stunning scenic views. Participants can expect a mix of back-roads and dirt tracks that test their endurance while showcasing the region's natural beauty.
