Course

A USATF-certified loop course inside Flushing Meadows Corona Park, starting on the south side of the Queens Theatre. The 5K runs a single loop and the half marathon four, with runners crossing the finish mats on each lap for split times. Portable and park toilets are available on course.

About

An annual running event held on Mother's Day featuring a 5K and Half Marathon in Queens, NY. The race includes a free Kiddie Dash and is organized by the Never Stop Running Foundation.
