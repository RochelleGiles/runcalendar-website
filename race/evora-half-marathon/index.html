About

The Evora Half Marathon is a running event held in the historic city of Evora, Portugal. The event features a half marathon (21.1 km) as well as a 10 km race and a 5 km walk, catering to runners of all levels. The course takes participants through the charming streets and landmarks of Evora, a UNESCO World Heritage site, providing a scenic and memorable experience for participants.
