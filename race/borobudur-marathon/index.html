About

The Borobudur Marathon will bring back a 42.195 kilometer running race, a 21.097 kilometer half marathon, and a 10 kilometer race, all held at the Lumbini Park Complex near the Borobudur Temple. This event offers runners the chance to experience the beauty and cultural significance of the Borobudur region.
