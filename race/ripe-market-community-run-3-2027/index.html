About

The third Community Run at the Ripe Market and the first of the season under lights: 15 km and 10 km from 5 in the afternoon, the 5 km at 5:05 and the 2.5 km at 5:10, chip-timed on the flat loops of Academy Park. Entries close the evening before, or earlier if the run fills.
