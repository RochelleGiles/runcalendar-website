Course

Starts and finishes at Cave Garden in central Mount Gambier with assembly on Watson Terrace. The 3.2 km runs south along Bay Road and back; the 6.8 km adds a full circuit of Blue Lake on John Watson Drive, and the 10.4 km goes twice around the lake before returning. All distances are on sealed road.

About

The Blue Lake Fun Run offers four distances -- 3.2K, 6.8K, 10.4K and a half marathon -- through Mount Gambier in South Australia's Limestone Coast region. Organised by the local Rotary club, the event doubles as a community fundraiser near the city's landmark volcanic crater lake.
