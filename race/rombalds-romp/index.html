Course

An 8.1-mile multi-terrain fell race with 1,214ft of climb, starting and finishing on Keighley Road about half a mile from race HQ at Darwin Gardens car park in Ilkley. The route climbs the back of the moor on rough rocky tracks and heathery paths with roughly a mile of road, and includes forest sections of tree roots and small wooden bridges that are slippery when wet. 11:00 start.

About

A trail race across Rombalds Moor above Ilkley in West Yorkshire, also known as Ilkley Moor, crossing open heather moorland with sweeping views across Wharfedale and the Aire Valley.
