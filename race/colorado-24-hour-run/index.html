Course

A certified flat one-mile dirt loop with essentially no elevation gain, run from the Colorado Youth Outdoors Swift Ponds Campus in Fort Collins. Solo runners take on 24, 12 or 6 hours, and relay teams of up to six can share any of the three formats.

About

A 24-hour running event on a flat, one-mile dirt loop at Colorado Youth Outdoors' Swift Ponds Campus in Fort Collins. Participants can choose to run for 24 hours, 12 hours, or 6 hours and accumulate as many miles as possible.
