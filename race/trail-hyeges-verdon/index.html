About

The Trail Hyeges Verdon is a running event that takes place in the beautiful Verdon region of France. The event offers several race distances, including 12km, 26km, and 45km, allowing runners of all levels to participate. The course showcases the stunning natural landscapes of the Verdon Gorge, providing a challenging and scenic experience for participants.
