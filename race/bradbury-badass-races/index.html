Course

Lapped course at Bradbury Mountain State Park, Pownal, Maine, covering both sides of the park via Tryon Mountain, the powerlines and the Elwood Loop. The 25 km runs one 15.5-mile loop of classic Maine singletrack and the 50 km runs it twice, over tough hills, rocks and roots with very little flat. About half a mile of paved road and a quarter mile of dirt road are included.

About

Run entirely within Bradbury Mountain State Park in Pownal, Maine, this ultra offers 25K and 50K distances on the park's rocky, rolling singletrack trails, a course well known among Maine trail runners for its technical footing.
