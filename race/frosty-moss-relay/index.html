Course

A point-to-point team relay across the Olympic Peninsula, the 80-mile Full Moss running from the Sol Duc River to Blyn and the 30-mile Mini Moss starting at Ediz Hook. The route mixes the paved Olympic Discovery Trail with about 25 miles of forest singletrack on the Adventure Route. It passes Lake Crescent, the McPhee Tunnel and the Elwha and Dungeness river bridges.

About

The Frosty Moss Relay is an approximately 80-mile team relay race consisting of 15 legs on a mix of pavement and trail along the Olympic Discovery Trail and Adventure Route, starting at Camp Creek Trailhead and finishing in Blyn. It includes a 30-mile Mini Moss Relay with 8 paved legs starting at Ediz Hook in Port Angeles and a 50K individual race following the Mini Moss course.
