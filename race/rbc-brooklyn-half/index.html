Course

A point to point half marathon starting beside the Brooklyn Museum and finishing on the Coney Island boardwalk, running through Prospect Park on the way. Ten fluid stations sit roughly every 1.18 miles, alongside fourteen misting stations and ten medical stations.

About

The RBC Brooklyn Half is a half marathon race organized by New York Road Runners that takes runners on a tour through Brooklyn, from Prospect Park to the Coney Island boardwalk. The race features scenic views of the East River waterfront and historic Brooklyn neighborhoods.
