Course

All three races start and finish at Hartletts Park in the middle of Hook. The 10 mile and 10K run out on rural roads through Rotherwick and Newnham; the 2.5 mile fun run adds an extra lap of the field at the start.

About

Lace up your sneakers and join the Hook Fun Run & Road Races in the United Kingdom! This exciting event offers a variety of distances, including a 10K, 10-mile, and a 2.2-mile Fun Run, catering to runners of all levels. Whether you're aiming for a new personal best or simply want to enjoy the scenic route, this community-driven race is sure to get your heart pumping.
