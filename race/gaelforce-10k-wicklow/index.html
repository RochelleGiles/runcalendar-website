About

Explore the rugged beauty of County Wicklow during the Gaelforce 10K Wicklow Trail Run. This challenging yet scenic event is part of the renowned Gaelforce 10K Trilogy, offering runners a chance to conquer trails amidst the stunning landscapes of Ireland's Garden County. Whether you're an experienced trail enthusiast or seeking a new adventure, this race promises an unforgettable experience.
