About

Discover the historic Semi-Marathon Meuse Grande Guerre et 10 km de Verdun, a captivating running event that takes you on a journey through the picturesque landscapes of Verdun, France. Runners can challenge themselves on the scenic routes, while immersing in the region's rich World War I heritage. This event offers a unique opportunity to experience the beauty and history of the Meuse River valley.
