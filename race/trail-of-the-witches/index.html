About

The Trail of the Witches is organized by TriO Events, which has extensive experience managing outdoor and multisport competitions, including trail running and triathlon. The event takes place in Spotorno, Italy, offering courses of 41.5K, 16.2K and 9.4K, while maintaining its existing character.
