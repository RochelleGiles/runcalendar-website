About

Experience the challenging and scenic Townsville North Ward Hills run, starting from the picturesque Queens Park in North Ward. This event offers runners a chance to test their endurance on the rolling hills and enjoy stunning views of the surrounding landscape. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a newcomer to the sport, this run is sure to provide an unforgettable challenge and adventure.
