About

The Hartshorne Memorial Masters Mile is one of the premier masters indoor running events in the country, attracting national and international talent aged 40 and up on an IAAF-certified track at Cornell University's Barton Hall. It features fully automatic timing, elite heats with rabbits, custom swag, and a post-race awards ceremony.
