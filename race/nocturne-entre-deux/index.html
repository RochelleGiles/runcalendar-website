About

The Nocturne Entre Deux is a 10km night running event held in the town of Entre-Deux on the island of Réunion. The race takes place on the streets of the town, offering a unique atmosphere with the event starting and finishing on the Place de la Liberté. Runners can expect a scenic course with views of the surrounding mountains and forests.
