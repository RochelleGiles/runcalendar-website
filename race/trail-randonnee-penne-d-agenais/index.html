About

The Trail Randonnee Penne D Agenais is a challenging hiking event that takes participants through the scenic valleys and hills around the village of Penne-d'Agenais. The course offers stunning views of the Boudouyssou and Lot valleys, with several steep climbs and descents that test the endurance of hikers. The event is known for its beautiful natural surroundings and the opportunity to explore the local countryside.
