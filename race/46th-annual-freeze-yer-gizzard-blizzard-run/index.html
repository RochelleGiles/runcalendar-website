About

The Freeze Yer Gizzard Blizzard Run is the zaniest winter running event in North America held in International Falls, Minnesota, known as the Icebox of the Nation. Participants can choose the 5K or 10K, or run both, with the legendary Gizzard Girls assisting and cheering.
