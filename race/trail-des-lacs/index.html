About

After creating beautiful sporting events over the past fifteen years that have been a great success with up to 1,300 runners, we have chosen to take a wilder path. We are proud to have taken our event to such heights and we want to continue to discover the joy of walking the paths of 'our corner', but we prefer to change the direction and continue to evolve differently. The event offers a mix of trail running and hiking with various distance options, allowing participants to choose their own adventure.
