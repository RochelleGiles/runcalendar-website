Course

A single counter-clockwise loop run almost entirely on trails in Hells Gate State Park, taking in named features such as Devils Slide and Gunpowder Ridge. Both the 4 mile and 11K start in the upper Jack O'Connor Center car park and head south, with steady climbing throughout. The event is self-supported with no water stations on course. A free kids 1 mile also runs.

About

Edge of Hell is a chip-timed race at Hells Gate State Park in Lewiston, Idaho, offering an 11K, 4-mile, and kids' 1-mile distance along the Snake River.
