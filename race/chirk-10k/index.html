Course

A single-lap 10K on fully closed roads starting and finishing on Station Road in Chirk, next to Caffi Wylfa. The route mixes fast flat sections with challenging inclines, taking in the grounds around Chirk Castle and surrounding countryside lanes. There is a 90-minute cut-off.

About

The Chirk 10K is a road race through the village of Chirk on the Wales-England border in Wrexham county, close to the National Trust's Chirk Castle and the Ceiriog Valley. It draws local club and charity runners each summer for a route through this border countryside.
