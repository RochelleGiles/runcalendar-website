Course

A point-to-point road marathon touring Orange County, running from the oceanfront streets of Newport Beach through bayside Costa Mesa and the neighbourhoods of Santa Ana. The marathon and half start early Sunday morning; the 5K and Kids Run are held the previous evening and morning.

About

The OC Marathon runs through Newport Beach and Costa Mesa in Orange County, California, offering marathon, half marathon, 5K, and kids run distances. The certified course passes coastal neighborhoods near the Pacific shoreline.
