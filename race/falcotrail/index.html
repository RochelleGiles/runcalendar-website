About

The Falco Trail is a prestigious running event organized by the Quípar Sports Club. The event aims to promote sports and showcase the beautiful natural landscapes of the Northwest region, especially around the town of Cehegín. The challenging trail course offers runners a chance to experience the stunning terrain and scenery of this part of Spain.
