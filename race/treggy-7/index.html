About

Launceston Town Square is the starting point of this 7 mile circular race, which begins at 10am. Registration takes place beforehand in the Town Hall. The first mile heads down hill out of the town and onto undulating rural roads. The first water station is approximately 3.5 miles, adjacent to Tregadillett Primary School after a half mile climb to the village. After leaving the village at 4 miles, the last 3 miles are mainly downhill with the second water station at 5.5 miles where a fantastic finish line in the grounds of Launceston Castle awaits you.
