Course

All distances start beside the gazebo in Comfort Park, near the centre of town, and head out into the Texas Hill Country. The roads roll rather than climb, running shady streets with access to Cypress Creek and the Guadalupe River and taking in a low water crossing before returning to the park.

About

River Road Run is a road race in Comfort, Texas, part of the Texas Hill Country Series organized by ScallyWompus Racing. Runners choose half marathon, 10 mile, 10K or 5K distances on rolling roads through the Hill Country near Comfort Park.
