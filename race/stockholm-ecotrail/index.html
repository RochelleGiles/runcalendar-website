About

Explore the natural beauty of Stockholm at the annual Ecotrail event. This eco-friendly trail running race takes participants on a stunning journey through the city's lush forests and picturesque landscapes, offering a unique opportunity to experience the Swedish capital's green spaces. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or a nature enthusiast, the Stockholm Ecotrail is a must-do event that will leave you invigorated and inspired.
