Course

All courses are out and back on the Ice Age Trail, mostly singletrack and flagged by colour for each distance. The trail is not highly technical but carries rocks and roots. Start and finish at the Palmyra Educational Learning Center, with parking and a shuttle from St Marys Catholic Church. One cupless aid station with water, electrolytes and snacks.

About

Choose from any of our three courses - Short, Middle or Long, or challenge yourself and run them all! Golden Gauntlet option is all three courses - long, mid, then short. You must arrive to start at 7am if you choose this option and will be required to check-in after each loop/course. You must be off course by noon.
