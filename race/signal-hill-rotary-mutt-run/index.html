About

A fun run and walk event along the scenic Long Beach shoreline starting at the Granada Launch Ramp at Rosie’s Dog Beach, welcoming participants with or without dogs on leashes. Proceeds support Long Beach Animal Care Services (70%) and Signal Hill Rotary community projects (30%), with an adoption event at the finish line.
