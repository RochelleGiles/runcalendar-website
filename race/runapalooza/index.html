Course

The USATF-certified half marathon starts and finishes in Asbury Park, running through Interlaken, Allenhurst, Deal, Ocean Grove, Bradley Beach and Avon-by-the-Sea. The 5K is flat, starting and finishing in Asbury Park past Convention Hall, the Stone Pony and Madam Marie, with a boardwalk finish. The relay is a 26-mile point-to-point from Seaside Heights to Asbury Park along ocean-front boardwalks.
