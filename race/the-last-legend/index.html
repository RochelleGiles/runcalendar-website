Course

A repeating 2.5km trail loop through Woodlands Historic Park at Greenvale, over rolling, undulating ground. Each lap sets off on a countdown and the time allowed per lap tightens as the day goes on. All runners start together at 8:00am, with 5km, 10km, 25km and 42km entries alongside the open-ended last-one-standing option.
