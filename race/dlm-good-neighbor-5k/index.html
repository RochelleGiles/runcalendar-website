About

Together with locally owned and operated Up & Running, our annual 5K is a family-friendly run/walk aimed at bringing neighbors together! Proceeds benefit the fight against hunger and will be distributed to The Foodbank. We also are grateful for the generous vendor donations that'll be loaded directly on The Foodbank truck race morning. All 5K participants will receive a Killer Brownie® treat at the finish line!
