Course

A point-to-point road course that starts in the hills at Ojai, about 700 ft above sea level, and runs downhill to finish at the Ventura Pier. The marathon is USATF-certified and a well-known Boston qualifier; a half marathon, 10K, 5K and kids 1K are also offered.

About

The Mountains 2 Beach Marathon is a point-to-point race from Ojai down to the Pacific Ocean in Ventura, California, with a net elevation drop that makes it one of the fastest marathon courses on the West Coast. Half marathon, 10K, and 5K options share parts of the same downhill route.
