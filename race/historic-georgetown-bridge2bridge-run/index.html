About

The Bridge2Bridge Run is more than a race - it's a celebration of community, purpose, and fun in the heart of Historic Georgetown, SC. Whether you're a seasoned runner, a first-time participant, or a spirited supporter, this annual event brings people together from near and far to run, walk, and connect.
