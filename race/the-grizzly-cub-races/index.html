Course

A brutal multi-terrain race starting on Seaton beach and finishing on the Esplanade. Runners head along the coast, climb to Beer Head and drop down a technical stony path into Branscombe, then face a cold river crossing, a mile of beach running and the climb known as the Stairway to Heaven. The 20-mile Grizzly adds steeply cambered hills and the notorious bogs before rejoining the 9-mile Cub route home. Entry is by ballot.

About

The Grizzly & Cub Races challenge runners with a demanding multi-terrain course through the picturesque Axe Valley of Devon, UK. Covering over 20 miles and 3,000 feet of ascent, this event tests the mettle of both experienced trail runners and those looking to push their limits on an epic adventure. Whether you tackle the full Grizzly or the shorter Cub route, stunning views and a true sense of accomplishment await.
