Course

Races start and finish at Santa Rita Park in Durango, alongside the Animas River. A 10 mile, a 5K and a free one mile children's race are run. The organiser describes it as the longest continuously run road race in Colorado, held every year since 1978.

About

The Narrow Gauge 10 Mile starts in downtown Durango, Colorado, tracing the route of the historic Durango & Silverton narrow-gauge railroad through the Animas River valley before finishing back in the historic downtown district.
