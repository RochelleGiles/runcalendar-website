Course

A 5K and 10K trail run over the pastures and discovery trail of Thorncroft Equestrian Center in Malvern, Pennsylvania, with creek crossings and a finish through the centre's 40-stall barn. A one-mile fun run/walk is held alongside.

About

We invite you back to our beautiful 70-acre property to experience a community health and wellness celebration perfect for the beginner to experienced mover. After-party includes live music, food trucks (food available for purchase by credit card), beer garden, (complimentary for participants 21+), and health and wellness vendors. We welcome you to experience the beauty of the Farm and everything it has to offer to our community. So lace up, stay active, and get ready for another fun neighborhood party!
