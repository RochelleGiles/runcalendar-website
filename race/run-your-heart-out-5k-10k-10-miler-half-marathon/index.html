Course

Start and finish at Alamitos Beach on the Long Beach beach path, which stays open to other users. The 5K runs one lap of a 5K route and the 10K runs it twice; the 10-Miler is two laps of a 5-mile route and the half marathon is that 5-mile route twice plus the 5K route. One stocked water station on course plus a finish aid station with water, electrolytes, gels and fruit.

About

This event offers runners 5K, 10K, 10-Miler, and Half Marathon distances starting at Alamitos Beach in Long Beach, CA. It features early bib pickup, gear check, medals for all finishers, and a course closing after 3 hours 20 minutes for longer distances.
