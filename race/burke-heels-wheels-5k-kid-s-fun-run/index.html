Course

A 5K run and walk starting and finishing at the Burke campus, 785 Mamaroneck Avenue, White Plains, New York, on a course where wheelchair athletes, runners and walkers compete together. Adaptive athletes and their guides start at 9:45 am and the 5K field at 9:50 am. A free kids fun run at 9:30 am offers a 100-yard dash for ages 4 to 6 and a quarter-mile for ages 7 to 9.

About

Wheelchair athletes, runners, walkers and families all compete together at the annual Heels & Wheels 5K Road Race & Walk and Kids Fun Run. The Heels & Wheels 5K is unique in that it is one of the only events of its kind that allows participants of all ability levels to compete together in a supportive environment. Community members are invited to cheer on the athletes.
