Course

An out-and-back 5K starting at the HUB patio outside the Husky Union Building on the University of Washington campus. The route runs down Rainier Vista and joins the paved Burke-Gilman Trail, looping the waterfront path around Union Bay Natural Area at the halfway point before returning.

About

For the first time ever, the UW Womxn's Rugby Club will be hosting a 5k around the beautiful campus of UW! Bib pick up will be from 9am to 9:50am and the race will start at 10am. This race is for UW students, potential students, alum, friends, family, those not affiliated with UW at all, runners, non runners, walkers, speed walkers, supporters of womxn's sports, participators of sports, and those who simply want to take a nice trip around UW's campus on a beautiful Saturday. You are welcome to run, walk, trot, cartwheel, or piggyback a friend for this 5k -all are welcome!
