About

Explore the historic streets of Geneva at the annual Balexert 20 km of Geneva! This scenic race winds through the city's charming neighborhoods and iconic landmarks, offering runners a unique tour of this picturesque Swiss destination. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a beginner looking for a new challenge, the Balexert 20 km provides an unforgettable running experience.
