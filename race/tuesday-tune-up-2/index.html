About

The Shoe Clinic Tuesday Tune Up is a weekly fun run/walk on Tuesday evenings across October and November, with this week's event on 13 October 2026. Choose from a 2km, 3km, 4km or 5km distance for runners and walkers. The meeting point is on the Embankment Road car park opposite the Westshore Beach Inn, registration from 5:30pm for a 6:00pm start. Spot prizes afterwards at the Beach Inn. The series is a fundraiser for the Napier Harrier Club.
