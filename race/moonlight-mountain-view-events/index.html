Course

Single-day event based at Moonlight Hall in the Grey Valley, about 30 minutes from Greymouth. Foot options are a 3.4km social walk and a 10km run/walk described as varied and mostly flat, using farm track and rural roads. The programme also includes mountain bike, biathlon and triathlon events.

About

The Moonlight Mountain View Events offers a range of events and courses suitable for the whole family, including a Social Walk, 22km Mountain Bike, 10km Walk, Biathlon, and Triathlon. This event promises an awesome day out in the beautiful New Zealand countryside.
