About

The Hearts With A Mission Superhero Run is a family-friendly event where participants run or walk in superhero costumes at Valley of the Rogue State Park to support at-risk youth and families. It features a 1-Mile Fun Run, timed 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon, along with a costume contest, kid zone, and food trucks.[1][3]
