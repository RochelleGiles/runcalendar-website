Course

About

The Newcastle Cross Country King of the Mountain is a 27km off-road out-and-back from the summit of Mt Sugarloaf down to Wakefield and back up again. It runs on bush track and fire trail rather than road, with aid stations carrying cola, water and bananas, and hot soup for finishers.
