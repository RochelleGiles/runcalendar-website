About

The Nature Marathon is a scenic running event in the historic town of Třeboň, offering multiple race distances through the beautiful landscape of the national park. Runners can choose from marathon, half marathon, 10 km, and 5 km races, making it accessible to runners of all skill levels. The event provides a unique opportunity to explore the picturesque Czech countryside while challenging oneself athletically.
