Course

A looped course through the River Bend Nature Center trail system, running through the sugar bush along Racoon Trail out to the Straight River. The start and finish is in the prairie and the longer distances repeat the same loop. The routes are trail rather than road, with water stations and toilets set around the loop.

About

An annual trail running event held at River Bend Nature Center in Faribault, Minnesota, featuring multiple race distances from 1 mile to 50 kilometers. All participants receive a free pancake brunch and the event supports environmental education, outdoor recreation, and natural resource conservation.
