Course

Starts and finishes at Twin Bridges Reserve in the Aniseed Valley south of Nelson, climbing into the Tasman Pine Forests estate on private forestry roads. The 10km has an aid station at the top of Meads Road at 6km; the 20km has two. A thermal top and waterproof jacket are compulsory for both.

About

The Aniseed Ascent Trail Run is a trail race set in the Aniseed Valley, just outside Nelson. Runners choose from a 20km or 10km course through the Tasman Pine Forests estate, following private dirt forestry roads through pine-clad hills and dramatic terrain.
