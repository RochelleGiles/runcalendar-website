About

The Vineland Grand Prix XC Series is South Jersey's only free race series featuring weekly 5K cross country races every Wednesday night at Campanella Park in Vineland, NJ. It is family-friendly, beginner-friendly, welcomes all paces, and is sponsored by the City of Vineland Recreation Department with chip-timed results.
