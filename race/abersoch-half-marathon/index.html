Course

Half marathon starting in Abersoch and finishing on the sands of Abersoch Beach. The route loops through the villages of Llanengan, Llangian and Sarn Bach, predominantly on roads, before a final kilometre along the beach. Drink stations at miles 5.7, 8.7 and 11.3 and at the finish, with marshals and traffic management throughout.

About

The Abersoch Half Marathon is a stunning seaside running event set against the picturesque backdrop of the Welsh coast. Runners will wind their way through charming villages and along the breathtaking Llyn Peninsula, taking in unparalleled views of the Irish Sea. This challenging yet rewarding course is a must for any runner seeking a unique and memorable half marathon experience.
