About

Discover the Cheetah Run, a thrilling 5K and 1-mile race through the stunning Fota Wildlife Park in County Cork, Ireland. Challenge yourself on this unique course that winds through the habitats of majestic cheetahs and other exotic animals. An unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
