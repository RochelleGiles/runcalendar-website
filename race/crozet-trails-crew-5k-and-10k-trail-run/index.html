Course

The 5K starts in Crozet Park and follows a rustic trail loop thick with roots, rocks and ruts before returning to the park. The 10K loops out near the Lickinghole basin and crosses Lickinghole Creek, where runners can expect wet feet. A family fun run of about 0.75 miles is also offered.

About

The Crozet Trails Crew 5K and 10K wind through the trail network of Crozet, Virginia, a small town at the foot of the Blue Ridge Mountains near Charlottesville. Proceeds support the nonprofit that builds and maintains the area's trails.
