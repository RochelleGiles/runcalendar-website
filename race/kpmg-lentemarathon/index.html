About

Celebrate spring with the KPMG Lentemarathon. Whether you're taking on the challenge of the full or half marathon, running the 10K or 5K, joining the relay, or participating in the Kids Run with your child – there's a distance for everyone. The start and finish are once again located in Stadshart Amstelveen, where thousands of spectators create an amazing atmosphere filled with music, good vibes, and delicious food.
