Course

Point-to-point down the length of the Cape Peninsula. The marathon starts in Green Point at sunrise and finishes at the historic Simon's Town Naval Sports Ground; the half marathon starts in Bergvliet and covers roughly 15 km of road along the False Bay coastline to the same finish. Entirely on road.

Marathon42.2 km

About

The Cape Peninsula Marathon is a long-running club event on Cape Town's Cape Peninsula, organised by Celtic Harriers. It offers marathon and half marathon distances and has been part of the Western Province racing calendar for decades.
