Course

Four distances based at Lawson Park in Mudgee. The marathon weaves through the valley past vineyards, wineries and farmland, with water stations and a wine station. The half marathon climbs north out of town past wineries before descending to the finish. The 10km is an out-and-back along the Cudgegong River with a climb to the turnaround, and the 5km stays flat on park paths and the riverbank.

About

The Mudgee Running Festival offers a relaxed and friendly atmosphere perfect for first-time runners while still challenging experienced marathoners. The courses wind through the picturesque Mudgee valley, passing vineyards, wineries, farmland, and grazing cattle with enthusiastic volunteers cheering participants along.
