Course

Fixed-time races held on the synthetic 400m track at Campbelltown Athletics Centre, Leumeah. Competitors circle the track for the duration of their race, with lanes allocated per event: 48hr and 24hr in lanes 1-4, 12hr in lanes 3-4, and 6hr, 3hr and 1hr in lanes 5-6. Aid stations provide basic supplies and runners are advised to bring their own nutrition. The field is capped at 240 entrants.

About

The Southern Sydney Track Ultra is a fixed-time track race on the synthetic 400m track at Campbelltown Athletics Centre, Leumeah. Runners and race walkers choose from 48, 24, 12, 6, 3 and 1 hour events, each allocated set lanes. Entry is capped at 240 competitors across all formats.
