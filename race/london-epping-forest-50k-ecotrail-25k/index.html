Course

An out-and-back route starting at Wanstead Flats and heading north through east London's green spaces into Epping Forest. The trail and woodland course is hilly and often muddy at this time of year. Three aid stations serve the route, which the 25K shares in shortened form.

About

The London Epping Forest 50K and EcoTrail 25K events offer runners the opportunity to explore the beautiful Epping Forest in London. The 50K course takes participants through the heart of the forest, showcasing its stunning natural landscapes, while the 25K EcoTrail event provides a more accessible option for runners looking to experience the tranquility of this urban oasis. Both events promise an unforgettable running experience amidst the serene surroundings of Epping Forest.
