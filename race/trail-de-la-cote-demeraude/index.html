About

Explore the stunning Emerald Coast of France at the Trail de la Cote d'Emeraude, a challenging and picturesque trail running event. Weaving through the rugged coastal landscapes, this race offers runners a unique opportunity to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of this captivating region. Whether you're a seasoned trail enthusiast or seeking a new adventure, the Trail de la Cote d'Emeraude promises an unforgettable running experience.
