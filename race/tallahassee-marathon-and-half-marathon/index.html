Course

Starts on Monroe Street beside the Florida Capitol, outside the Leon County Courthouse, and finishes in the College Town district on West Madison Street, so start and finish are separate. Marathoners run a second lap, turning onto Madison at 6.4 miles, and include an out-and-back on the St. Marks Trail; the route passes the FSU and FAMU campuses. Aid stations every 2 to 3 miles, some with gels.

About

The Tallahassee Marathon and Half Marathon is a premier running event in the heart of Florida's capital city. Runners will experience a scenic course that showcases the city's historic landmarks, vibrant neighborhoods, and beautiful natural surroundings.
