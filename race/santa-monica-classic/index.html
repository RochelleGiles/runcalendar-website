Course

City road races along the Santa Monica seafront. The 10K starts and finishes at Ocean Avenue and Santa Monica Blvd at 7:00am; the 5K starts at Ocean Avenue and Broadway at 8:30am and finishes at the same Santa Monica Blvd line. A half-kilometre Kids Run follows on Ocean Avenue, and the finish festival is held at the 3rd Street Promenade.

About

The Santa Monica Classic is a 10K and 5K road race through Santa Monica finishing near the iconic Santa Monica Pier. Held every September, it raises funds for the McCourt Foundation's youth fitness programs.
