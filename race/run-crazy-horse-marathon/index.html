Course

Point-to-point from Crazy Horse Memorial to a downtown finish in Hill City, South Dakota. USATF-certified marathon and half marathon plus a 5-person relay; aid stations roughly every 3 miles; the finish line stays open 7 hours. A Fast 5K and Kids 1K round out the weekend.
