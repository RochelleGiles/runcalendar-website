About

The Sault International Festival of Races is a two-day event in the twin cities of Sault Ste. Marie, spanning the US-Canada border. Saturday's 5-Mile Bridge Run crosses the International Bridge between Ontario and Michigan, while Sunday brings a half marathon, 5K run and 5K walk. The bridge closes to through traffic for a set window during the Saturday race.
