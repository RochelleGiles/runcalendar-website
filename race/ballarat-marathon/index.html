Course

The marathon is two laps of a World Athletics certified 21.1 km loop, starting at Ballarat Town Hall on Sturt Street. The flat sealed course runs through Victoria Park and around Lake Wendouree past the city's historic buildings. Thirteen drink stations sit at roughly 3 km intervals with water, electrolyte, gels and toilets.

About

The Ballarat Marathon is a premier road running festival showcasing the scenic routes of Ballarat. Runners can experience a challenging and picturesque course through the historic city and surrounding landscapes. The event offers multiple race distances to accommodate runners of all skill levels.
