About

Welcome to Sinister 7 Ultra — a race that may be the greatest challenge of your life. The 100-mile, 50-mile, and 50km courses will take you through the most rugged, remote and beautiful terrain in Alberta's stunning Rocky Mountains. With over 6,600m of elevation gain across the 100-mile course, this race will punish those who are not prepared.
