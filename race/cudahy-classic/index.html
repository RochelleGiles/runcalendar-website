Course

A mostly flat, fast course starting at Sheridan Park Picnic Area #1 in Cudahy, running north and south through Sheridan Park and along the Oak Leaf Trail with views over Lake Michigan. The five mile covers one loop and the ten mile two, with a one mile children's race alongside them.
