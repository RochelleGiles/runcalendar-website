Course

Three single-track trail distances - 22K, 11K and 5K - on the Prison Hill trails above Carson City, hubbed at Silver Saddle Ranch. The 22K climbs roughly 3,150 ft via Dead Truck Canyon, Odyssey Loop, North Loop and the South Climb before The Pacifier descent. The 22K has aid at 8 and 11.5 miles; the 5K and 11K have none. Field capped at 200.

About

We have three fun and challenging distances to ESCAPE from Prison Hill on! Choose your distance and dig into your lowest hill-climb-gear and your lightest descent flow on the 22K, 11K or 5K. It's a mindbending adventure you don't want to miss!
