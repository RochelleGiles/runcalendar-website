About

La Pyjama Party Trail is a unique night running event featuring multiple distance options and a playful atmosphere. Runners are encouraged to wear pajamas and use headlamps while exploring nocturnal trail routes. The event combines adventure, fun, and athletic challenge in a festive nighttime setting.
