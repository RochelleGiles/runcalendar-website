About

The 30 km des rives à Boucherville follows the St. Lawrence riverside in Boucherville, Quebec, starting from the Parc de la Rivière-aux-Pins. Distances range from 3K up to the marquee 30K, with the event raising funds for local causes like Nature-Action Québec.
