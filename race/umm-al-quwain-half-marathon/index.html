About

Umm Al Quwain's half marathon, in its third year, from the beachfront on Sheikh Ahmed Bin Rashid Al Moalla Street: an elite half and 10K at dawn, then the open half marathon at 6:45, a 10K, a 5K and a one-mile run through the morning. Flat coastal roads in the smallest and quietest of the emirates.
