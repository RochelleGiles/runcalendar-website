About

Embark on a scenic journey across the stunning landscapes of North Wales at the North Wales Pilgrim Trail Marathon. This challenging yet captivating event takes runners on a winding route through coastal trails and picturesque countryside, offering breathtaking views and an unforgettable running experience. Whether you're seeking a unique challenge or simply want to immerse yourself in the natural beauty of this region, this marathon is a must-add to your running calendar.
