Course

An accurately measured 10K based at Norman Park Athletics Track off Hayes Lane in Bromley. The route mixes track and park pathway: 270m plus four laps of the track, then four laps of the park, finishing with 300m around the track. Water stations on course, with changing rooms, toilets and baggage at the track.
