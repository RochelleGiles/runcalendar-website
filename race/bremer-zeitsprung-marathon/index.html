About

The Bremer Zeitsprung Marathon is a unique running event that takes participants on a captivating journey through the historic city of Bremen, Germany. Runners will have the chance to experience the city's rich cultural heritage as they tackle this challenging 26.2-mile course, which winds through picturesque neighborhoods and past iconic landmarks. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, the Bremer Zeitsprung Marathon promises an unforgettable running adventure.
