Course

A single 7.6-mile climb up the Mount Washington Auto Road, starting at the base and finishing at the 6,288-foot summit, with about 4,650 feet of ascent and a maximum grade of 22 percent. Entries are allocated by lottery. There is one water refill station midway, with medical support at the base and the summit.

About

The Mount Washington Road Race climbs 7.6 miles straight up the Auto Road to the 6,288-foot summit of New Hampshire's highest peak. It's an uphill-only course with no flat or downhill sections, long considered one of the toughest road races in the country.
