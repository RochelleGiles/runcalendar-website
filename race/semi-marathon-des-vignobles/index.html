About

Explore the picturesque vineyards of the Vignobles region in this stunning semi-marathon event. Runners will wind through charming French countryside, passing by historic châteaux and lush vineyards, offering a truly unique running experience. This annual race is a must for any endurance athlete seeking a scenic challenge.
