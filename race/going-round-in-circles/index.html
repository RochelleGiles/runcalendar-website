Course

A self-navigated route of around 16 miles taking in all 27 stations on the original Circle line in central London. Runners choose clockwise or anticlockwise on the day and follow a GPX file and what3words points rather than course markings. Relatively flat. Registration is at The Volunteer pub on Baker Street.

About

A unique running challenge that follows London's Circle Line, where participants run between all 27 underground stations on the Circle Line. Runners can participate individually or in teams, traveling either anticlockwise or clockwise while collecting station selfies.
