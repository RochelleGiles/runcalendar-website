About

The Innocent Half Marathon offers runners a perfect mid-training challenge along scenic Edinburgh routes. The course traverses cycle paths and quiet roads, including the historic Innocent Railway, passing under Arthur's Seat with a relatively flat profile. Participants can enjoy a well-marked route while supporting three Scottish children's charities.
