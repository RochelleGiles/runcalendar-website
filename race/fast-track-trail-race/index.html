Course

A single-loop dirt singletrack course in George Wyth State Park, Waterloo, running through woods, prairie and lakefront sections. The trail is smooth and runnable with gentle rollers and no major climbs; the 25K covers the same loop twice.

About

The Fast Track Trail Race runs through George Wyth Memorial State Park in Waterloo, Iowa, a wooded park along the Cedar River. Runners choose between 13K and 25K trail distances through the park's forest and river-bottom terrain.
