Course

Both races start and finish at Veterans Pier on a paved boardwalk along the shore. The 5K is a single out-and-back with one signed turnaround; the half marathon runs four loops with two turnaround checkpoints. Water stations sit at the turnarounds and at the finish for half marathoners.

About

Join us this fall season for a great experience at Veterans Pier in Brooklyn! Choose between a HM or a 5K & enjoy the sights & sounds of this paved boardwalk run/walk. Afterward, pick up your well earned pie and some yummy treats from All Round Foods and Waterloo Sparkling Water!
