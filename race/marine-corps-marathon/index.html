Course

About

Known as The Peoples Marathon, the Marine Corps Marathon starts in Arlington, Virginia and tours the monuments and memorials of Washington DC before a stirring uphill finish at the Marine Corps War Memorial. Organised by the US Marine Corps each October, it is one of the largest marathons in the world without prize money.
