Course

A point-to-point run through Groenkloof, starting at Pretoria Rugby Club on Totius Street and finishing at the CBC Old Boys Club on George Storrar Drive. Part road race, part music festival: on the 10 km and 21 km routes a split lets you take the DJ-powered Party Lane (still full distance) or the Fast Lane straight to the finish, with a race village at the end.

About

HOKA Half Pretoria is part of the HOKA HALF Runfest series, pairing a half marathon, 10km, and 5km road race through Pretoria with a live music festival at the finish line, drawing runners looking for a festival atmosphere alongside their race.
