Course

Certified five-mile road course through Branford's beaches, parks and residential neighbourhoods. At least four water stations are placed on the five-mile course and one on the health walk. Medical spotters cover both events, with an ambulance stationed at the finish area. Packet pickup is at the Branford Town Green.
