Course

A point-to-point 69km run from Glamis Castle to Scone Palace across the Sidlaw hills, with buses from Scone Palace at 6.30am and an optional 7.30am early start. The route crosses trails and exposed hill tops and can be very wet, with water at checkpoints and drop bags carried between them.

About

Conquer the rugged Sidlaw hills in Scotland during the Sidlaw Ultra, a scenic 69km winter ultra marathon. Immerse yourself in the breathtaking landscapes as you challenge yourself on this exhilarating journey through the United Kingdom's picturesque countryside. With stunning vistas and a demanding course, the Sidlaw Ultra promises an unforgettable adventure for seasoned ultrarunners.
