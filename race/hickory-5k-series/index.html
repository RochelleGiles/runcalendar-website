About

Throughout the year, the Hickory Parks, Recreation & Sports Tourism Department hosts a series of 5K races in varying locations across the city to encourage runners to go outdoors and explore different parks and trail systems in Hickory. Each race is unique and poses its own challenges and triumphs.
