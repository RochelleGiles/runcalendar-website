Course

Starts at Webb Community Park on Mansfield Webb Road in Arlington, with the 10K run as a double out-and-back. The organiser describes the surface as a mix of pavement, crushed granite and some unavoidable dirt paths. A 5K, a one mile run and a kids' dash share the same park start, and dessert tickets are redeemed at the finish line.

About

The Dessert Dash is a Fort Worth-area run held in Arlington, Texas, with a 10K, 5K and 1-mile fun run. The dessert-themed event welcomes runners and walkers of all levels and finishes with sweet treats.
