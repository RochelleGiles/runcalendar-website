Course

A four-mile out-and-back loop through and around the trails and roads of Forest Park, starting and finishing at Victory Field Track. The route takes in wooded horse trails, with split times at each mile marker and water stations on course.

About

Annual 4-mile road and trail race organized by Forest Park Runners Club, starting and finishing at Victory Field Track in Forest Park, Queens. The course features a 2-mile out-and-back loop with two miles on roads and two miles on trails.
