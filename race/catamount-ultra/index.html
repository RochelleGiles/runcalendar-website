Course

A 25K loop starting and finishing at the Trapp Family Lodge outside Stowe, run mostly on wide, hard-packed dirt trail through highland pasture and hardwood forest in the foothills of Mount Mansfield. Each loop carries about 2,500 feet of climb and the same again in descent; the 50K covers the loop twice.

About

The Catamount Ultra follows sections of Vermont's historic cross-country Catamount Trail through the Green Mountains near Stowe. Runners cover 25K or 50K of singletrack, forest roads, and climbs with significant elevation gain typical of Vermont trail racing.
