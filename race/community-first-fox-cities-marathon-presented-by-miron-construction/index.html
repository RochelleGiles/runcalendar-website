Course

Marathon and half run point-to-point from Midway Road, Menasha to Riverside Park, Neenah on paved roads and paved trail, crossing the 1,600-foot Trestle Trail Bridge. The 5K and 10K start and finish at Riverside Park on flat residential streets on Saturday; the marathon events run Sunday from 7:00am with a 2:00pm course close.

About

The Community First Fox Cities Marathon presented by Miron Construction is a premier running event in Wisconsin. The race features a variety of distances, including the full marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K, as well as a kids fun run and other activities.
