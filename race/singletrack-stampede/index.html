Course

A singletrack trail race in Cheeseboro and Palo Comado Canyons, within the Santa Monica Mountains National Recreation Area. The course twists and turns along the established No Name Trail Run route and climbs a set of steep switchbacks, with aid stations at roughly 4.7 and 10.3 miles. A 10K and a 21K are offered.

About

A trail race in the Cheeseboro and Palo Comado Canyons of the Santa Monica Mountains, offering 10K and 21K distances. Two aid stations serve the longer course. The finish area includes medals, breakfast tacos and a beer garden for over-21s.
