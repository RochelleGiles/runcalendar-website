About

Join us for the RBC Race for the Kids on Saturday, August 15 for a 3 Mile, 5 Mile, 10 Mile or Kids Run. This run is open to everyone no matter your fitness level. Enjoy the beautiful course along the Mississippi River. Race starts and finishes at the Guthrie Theater.
