About

The Semi Marathon De Solastranden is a picturesque running event set against the stunning backdrop of Norway's Solastranden coastline. Runners will enjoy a challenging yet scenic course that winds through charming coastal towns and offers breathtaking views of the North Sea. This race is a must-do for any endurance athlete seeking a unique running experience.
