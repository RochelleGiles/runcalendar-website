Course

Out-and-back laps on paved paths at Ben Geren Regional Park. The 5K and 10K run a 1.56-mile out-and-back two and four times respectively; the half marathon repeats a 2.18-mile out-and-back six times. All distances start together at 8:30am with a 3 hour 30 minute course limit.

About

The Pirate 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is held at Ben Geren Regional Park in Fort Smith, AR, featuring out-and-back courses with finisher medals and a best costume contest. Races start at 8:30 AM with a 3.5-hour time limit and no on-site registration.
