Course

A looped course of roughly 6.2 miles through the wooded Musser Scout Reservation along the Unami Creek, mostly double-track technical trail with some single-track, over roots, rocks, dirt and mud with hills throughout. One aid station serves carbohydrates, fruit, water, Gatorade and soda, with hot food available. Parking sits about a quarter-mile from the start/finish.

About

A timed endurance race at Musser Scout Reservation on the Unami Creek in Pennsburg, Pennsylvania. Runners complete as many 6.2-mile loops as they can within 12, 6 or 4 hours, on mostly double-track technical trail over roots, rocks and mud. A 6-mile hike option runs alongside the timed events.
