Course

Run along the Brisbane Valley Rail Trail. The 100 mile starts at Yarraman Heritage Centre and runs the full trail to Brassall, finishing at Ipswich Grammar School playing fields. The 200 mile starts at Ipswich Grammar the previous day, runs out to Yarraman and returns with the 100 mile field. The 100km starts at Harlin and the 50 mile at Toogoolawah, both finishing at Ipswich Grammar.

About

The Brisbane Valley Rail Trail 100s is a challenging ultramarathon event that takes runners along the entire Brisbane Valley Rail Trail, covering distances of 200 miles, 100 miles, 100 km, 50 miles, and a marathon. The course showcases the beautiful natural scenery of the region, with participants experiencing a variety of terrain and landscapes throughout the event.
