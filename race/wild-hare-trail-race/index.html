Course

Lapped trail race on a 7.8-mile loop, with 50-milers running a modified first lap then five full laps. The course mixes mountain-bike style singletrack of twists, dips and tight turns with fast open field and a few climbs over the bluff. Aid stations at the start/finish and midway.

About

The Wild Hare Trail Race is an annual event held at Bluff Creek Ranch in Warda, Texas, hosted by Tejas Trails. Runners choose from 10K, 25K, 50K, and 50 Mile distances across the ranch's rural trails.
