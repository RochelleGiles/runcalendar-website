About

Le Tour du Nipalou is now past its 25th running, held in Lorcières in the Margeride massif of Auvergne. Distances range from 700m to 53km, with the 21km and 53km serving as qualifiers for the French Trail Running Championships; the 53km also counts toward the National Trail Running Challenge Mountain category and qualifies for the Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc.
