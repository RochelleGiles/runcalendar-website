Course

The half marathon starts at 8am from Bayside Park on North Shoreline Boulevard, on the Corpus Christi bayfront, with a quarter marathon, 5K, one-mile run and kids' dash alongside it. Aid stations are spread along the route and the finish opens into a rally tent with live music and refreshments. A Dia de los Muertos costume contest is held before the start.

About

The Day of the Dead Half Marathon in Corpus Christi is a Dia de los Muertos-themed race with half marathon, quarter marathon, 5K, and 1-mile distances through the coastal city of Corpus Christi, Texas.
