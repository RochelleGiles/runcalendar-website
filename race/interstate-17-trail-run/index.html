Course

A four-mile loop run four times — three counterclockwise, one clockwise — from State Line Lookout in Closter, preceded by a one-mile out-and-back on closed road. Rocky technical singletrack, rock scrambles, boulder fields and cliff switchbacks above the Hudson, with over 5,000 ft of climbing. Aid stations at miles 1, 5, 9 and 13.

About

Interstate 17 Trail Run is a 17-mile trail race starting at State Line Lookout in Closter, opening with a mile out-and-back on a closed road before four four-mile loops of rocky, technical singletrack along the Hudson River Palisades, including boulder scrambles and steep cliff switchbacks.
