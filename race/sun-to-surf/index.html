About

Welcoming participants of all abilities to experience the unique beach vibes of the treasured Eastern Bay region, and enjoy a slice of ocean paradise! Whether you're looking to nail a personal best on our FuelMe 21.1km and Takutai 10km courses, or wanting to embrace our amazing Kiwi summer with family and friends in the SunFM 5km or The Wilde Whippet kids' 1km fun social distances, Sun to Surf ticks has an event for everyone.
