About

The Glenbeigh Glencar Marathon is a road race on the Ring of Kerry between the villages of Glenbeigh and Glencar, Co. Kerry, part of the Glenbeigh Summer Festival. Distances range from 5K to full marathon through this scenic stretch of Kerry.
