Course

A 22km point-to-point run starting at the Cedar Flats second picnic area and finishing at a clearing opposite the Maiala picnic area on Mount Glorious. The route climbs a steep ridgeline firebreak, crosses to the England Creek checkpoint, then ascends single track to the Mount Glorious summit before descending a stone-lined western ridge trail. Four checkpoints: three self-help water points and one manned station at England Creek.

About

Take on the Mount Glorious Trail Race – Tough, Beautiful, Unforgettable! Starting at Cedar Flats, the race takes you across the top of Northbrook Mountain, through the lush terrain of England Creek, and into the pristine rainforest at the summit of Mount Glorious. Finish your adventure at the clearing opposite the picturesque Maiala picnic area, where the sense of accomplishment will be unmatched.
