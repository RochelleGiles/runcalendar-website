Course

An out-and-back through Veterans Park, starting and finishing in front of the War Memorial at the top of the E. Mason Street hill. The 5K runs about 3.37 miles and the 10K covers two laps of roughly 6.26 miles. One aid station serves the course.

About

The Vets Day 5K & 10K honors veterans and active-duty service members with runs through Veterans Park, starting and finishing at the Milwaukee War Memorial. Participants join a push-up challenge for Project 22 and enjoy post-race awards and hot chocolate.
