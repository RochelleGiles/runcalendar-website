About

The 13th edition of the Valberg Trail, organized by the Club des Sports des Portes du Mercantour de Valberg (CSPM) and Mermonts organization. The event offers three courses of 13km, 21km, and 42km, with the 42km course being part of the Challenge Trail 06.
