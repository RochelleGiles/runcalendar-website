About

The Beneva Montreal Marathon is a must-run event for seasoned runners and newcomers alike. Weaving through the vibrant streets of Montreal, this scenic course offers a unique challenge as participants take in the city's iconic landmarks and stunning riverside views. With a rich history and a reputation for an exceptional race experience, the Beneva Montreal Marathon is a bucket-list event for any distance runner.
