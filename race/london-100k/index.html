Course

An urban ultra built from four 25km routes radiating north, south, east and west from a single central London headquarters, so every option starts and finishes in the same place. The full 100km links all four; runners can also take one 25km leg or a 50km pairing. The route passes more than 100 London landmarks along streets, parks and hidden city paths. Checkpoints with water, snacks and fruit at the start and midpoint of each 25km section.

About

The London 100K is Central London's premier urban ultra marathon, challenging runners with a 100km course through the heart of the historic city. This unique event offers an unparalleled opportunity to experience the iconic landmarks and vibrant atmosphere of the United Kingdom's capital while pushing the limits of endurance.
