Course

A single loop starting and finishing at Arundel Football Club in West Sussex, running out through Houghton Forest, Eartham Wood, Angmering Park and Wepham Woods before returning along the River Arun. Rolling South Downs hills on grass trails, farm tracks and forest paths, about 3,200 ft of climb; 99% trail, 1% road.

About

The UK Ultra Springtide 50K is a stunning single-loop Spring run through the historic town of Arundel in West Sussex. Runners will be treated to beautiful countryside views as they challenge themselves on this picturesque 50K course. Whether you're looking to push your limits or simply enjoy the tranquil surroundings, the Springtide 50K is a must-do event for ultrarunners and half marathoners alike.
