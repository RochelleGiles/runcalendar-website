Course

A point-to-point ultra of roughly 48 miles following the Peddars Way north from Knettishall Heath Nature Reserve to Holme-next-the-Sea on the Norfolk coast. The route mixes footpaths, bridleways and rural lanes, is mostly good underfoot and relatively flat, and is straightforward to navigate. Three checkpoints support the course, with hot food at the finish.

About

The Peddars Way Ultra Marathon is a challenging event that takes runners along the historic Peddars Way in the United Kingdom. The course offers stunning countryside views and a unique opportunity to experience this ancient Roman road. With a welcoming atmosphere and a focus on camaraderie, the Peddars Way Ultra is a must-do for endurance enthusiasts.
