About

Olne Spa Olne is a challenging trail running event that takes participants through scenic trails and paths in the Liège region. The 71 km course offers a demanding route with significant elevation gain of 2296 meters, testing runners' endurance and trail running skills. Participants experience a semi-self-sufficient trail run that celebrates the spirit of running and the beautiful Belgian countryside.
