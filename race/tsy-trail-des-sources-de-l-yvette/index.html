About

The TSY Trail offers a beautiful nature trail running event in the heart of the Chevreuse Valley. Participants explore scenic trails and technical routes through the picturesque landscape of Levis Saint Nom, experiencing a family-friendly and convivial atmosphere that attracts both recreational runners and high-level competitors.
