Course

A 28 km mountain race weaving around the Helvellyn massif, starting at Patterdale. The route links runnable climbs through the Glenridding mines with technical single track, scree descents and knife-edge ridgelines, and takes in five graded scrambles.

About

The Pinnacle Ridge Extreme is a challenging 28km trail race with 2,700m of vertical gain in the stunning Lake District of the United Kingdom. Runners will tackle technical terrain and breathtaking scenery as they push themselves to the limit on this extreme sky running event.
