About

The Fukuoka Marathon is a renowned running event held annually in the vibrant city of Fukuoka, Japan. Known for its scenic coastal route and enthusiastic spectators, this marathon offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the rich culture and stunning natural beauty of the region. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a passionate runner seeking a new challenge, the Fukuoka Marathon is an event not to be missed.
