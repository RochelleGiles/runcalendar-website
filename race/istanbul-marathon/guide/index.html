Istanbul Marathon

Fatih, Istanbul · Türkiye
RoadHistoricMarathon · 15K

The only marathon that runs you out of Asia and into Europe — and it does it in the first ten minutes.

Quick verdict

You cross from Asia to Europe at kilometre one, on a bridge closed to traffic since three in the morning. Then Istanbul spends eight kilometres showing off and twelve making you pay for it. Spectacular at both ends, lonely in the middle.

Course at a glance

2Continents
1 kmBridge crossed at
6 h 20Cutoff

Point-to-point with a long coastal spur. Over the July 15 Martyrs' Bridge from the Asian side, past Dolmabahçe at 6 km, over the Galata Bridge at 9 km, past the Spice Bazaar and Topkapı, then west along the Marmara shore to a turnaround at 28 km and back to finish on the Hippodrome beside the Blue Mosque.

Where you'll feel it

📍The Marmara shore road, kilometres 20 to 35.

Dead flat, wide, exposed seafront highway with almost no spectators and nothing to break the wind off the sea. You run out on it knowing you have to run back, watching the returning runners across the carriageway for kilometres.

Best chance to push

Kilometres 5 to 13: Dolmabahçe, the Galata Bridge, the Spice Bazaar, Topkapı. The postcard stretch, the loudest crowds, and early enough that you can still look up.

Three things to know

  1. You cannot simply pay to enter. The marathon needs a qualifying time — a 42 km inside 6:20 or a half inside 3:25 from the last two years — and it has to be on your profile before you can register.
  2. The 6:20 cutoff is nine gates, not one line. You must clear 10 km by 1:32, 20 km by 3:04, 30 km by 4:36 and 40 km by 6:08. Stopping for bridge photographs is how people get pulled at a checkpoint.
  3. There is no organiser bus from the tourist districts. Your bib gives you free public transport, and the only shuttle runs from Üsküdar to Beylerbeyi between seven and eight. Plan your own way to the Asian side.

Race-day weather

Early November averages 11 to 17°C, with a one-in-four chance of rain and steady wind off the water. Near-ideal for running — but you will be standing on an exposed bridge approach for a long time first.

What runners wear

Shorts and short sleeves once moving, with a throwaway layer for the pens. Nothing technical needed; the wind on the seafront matters more than the temperature, so someone to tuck in behind is worth more than a jacket.

Typical finish times

Winners run close to 2:10, and the course records — 2:09:44 and 2:18:35 — have stood since 2019 and 2018. The mass field is slower than most big-city marathons: the median man runs about 4:43, and 4,568 of 4,982 starters finished in 2025.

Worth the trip?

For the bridge and a finish inside Sultanahmet, yes — and at €120 at the dearest tier it costs a fraction of a Major. Just know the middle twelve kilometres are an empty seafront motorway, and that you need a qualifying time to be there at all.
