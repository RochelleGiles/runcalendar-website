Course

The only marathon run on two continents: starts on the Asian shore, crosses the 15 July Martyrs Bridge over the Bosphorus into Europe, then follows the Bosphorus and the Golden Horn waterfront into the old city to finish at Sultanahmet beside the Blue Mosque. Downhill off the bridge, flat along the water, with a few short rises near the end.

About

The Istanbul Marathon is a renowned annual running event that takes place in the vibrant city of Istanbul, Turkey. Runners from around the world come to experience the unique course that showcases the city's iconic landmarks, including the Bosphorus Bridge, Sultanahmet Square, and the historic Topkapi Palace. The event is known for its lively atmosphere and enthusiastic spectators, creating an unforgettable experience for participants.
