Course

A 6.71km alpine trail loop at Falls Creek mixing grassy ski slopes, dirt road and mountain-bike singletrack, with strong climbs and flowing descents. Expect tree roots, rocks, mud and small water crossings; the course is fully marked and marshalled with a two-hour cut-off.

About

Falls Creek Easter Fun Run is a 5km community run held in the alpine village of Falls Creek, Victoria, as part of the resort's Easter Festival weekend, drawing runners and families to the high-country village.
