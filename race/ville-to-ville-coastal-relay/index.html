Course

A point to point relay of about 68 miles from Summerville to Mount Pleasant, South Carolina, split into 12 legs of 3 to 9 miles. Teams of three to six run through historic Charleston, the barrier islands and Lowcountry marshland before finishing at the Lawn at Ferry Wharf with views over the Cooper River. Staggered starts begin at 5:00am.

About

The Ville to Ville Coastal Relay celebrates the South Carolina Lowcountry, spanning roughly 70 miles from Summerville to Mount Pleasant. Teams will wind through historic Charleston, barrier islands, and marshland before finishing with a scenic view of the Cooper River.
