Course

A rural road course around Coatesville, about ten minutes drive north of the Albany exits from the Northern Motorway, run traffic free with views back to the Sky Tower. Aid stations sit roughly every 4 to 5km, water at the first table and sports drink at the second. The event is based at Coatesville Reserve.

About

The Goodr Coatesville Half Marathon offers runners a scenic countryside experience with stunning rural views. The course features a downhill finish in its final kilometers, making it an appealing option for those seeking a fast time. Set in the beautiful Coatesville countryside, this classic event combines picturesque landscapes with a runner-friendly course.
