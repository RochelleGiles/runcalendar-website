Course

Starts on the Brillion High School athletic fields, crossing grass onto the Mike Ariens Friendship Trail, then loops through the wooded trail and finishes back at the start line. Water stations and spectator viewing points sit along the trail. A 1K untimed kids race is also offered.

About

The Eagles Wings 5K, now past its 14th running, welcomes both serious competitors and casual walkers in Brillion, Wisconsin. The route winds through the wooded Mike Ariens Friendship Trail.
