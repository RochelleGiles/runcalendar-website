About

The Birell 10K Night Race Prague is a popular running event that takes place in the heart of the historic city of Prague. Runners will enjoy a scenic course that showcases the city's iconic landmarks, including the Charles Bridge and Prague Castle. The night-time setting creates a unique and vibrant atmosphere, making this a must-do event for runners seeking an unforgettable experience.
