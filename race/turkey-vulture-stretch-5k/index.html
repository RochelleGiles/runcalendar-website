Course

A road 5K through Ely, Nevada, starting and finishing at Steptoe Park. The route runs down Avenue K, along East 13th Street, East Madison Avenue, California and Grant Avenues, then back to Avenue K for the finish. Entry is online only with no race-day registration.

About

Every March the turkey vultures return to Ely after a relaxing winter in South America. This run is to honor these not-so-pretty birds and welcome them and spring back to Ely! The route takes you past the Ely Cemetery where you have a chance of seeing a turkey vulture with its wings outstretched in a tree.
