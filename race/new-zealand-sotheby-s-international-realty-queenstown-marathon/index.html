Course

Point-to-point from The Avenue at Millbrook Resort to the Queenstown Recreation Ground, with 70% of the course on smooth hard-packed trails and 30% on road. The route passes Arrowtown, Lake Hayes, the Shotover River and Lake Wakatipu before finishing in central Queenstown.

About

A destination running weekend held each November in the Queenstown Lakes district, with four races — the marathon, half marathon, 10km and a 2.2km kids run — all finishing together at the Queenstown Recreation Ground. Start lines are staggered through the morning from Millbrook, Coronet Fields and Frankton, and every course ends in central Queenstown.
