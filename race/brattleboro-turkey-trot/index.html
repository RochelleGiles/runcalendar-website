Course

A three-mile road race on Upper Dummerston Road, starting and finishing near the Brattleboro Country Club. The road stays open to traffic, so runners keep to the left. A one-mile kids' race is held beforehand.

About

The Brattleboro Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving Day 3-mile road race in Brattleboro, Vermont, hosted by the Red Clover Rovers running club in the Connecticut River valley.
