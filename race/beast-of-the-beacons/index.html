Course

A trail race in the Brecon Beacons National Park starting and finishing at Henderson Hall in Talybont on Usk. The route crosses forests, mountains, rivers and reservoirs, with harsh climbs, rocky paths and soft forest trail underfoot. The 40 mile option covers the course twice, the second lap in reverse. Three refuel stations serve food and drinks, with marshals and a medical team on course.

About

Tackle the rugged terrain of the Brecon Beacons National Park in the Beast of the Beacons, a challenging trail running event with 40, 20, and 10-mile course options. Immerse yourself in the majestic landscapes of this stunning region as you push your limits on this unique running adventure.
