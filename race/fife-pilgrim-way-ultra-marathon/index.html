Course

Point-to-point 90K along the Fife Pilgrim Way, starting at North Queensferry at 6am and finishing at St Andrews Cathedral, with about 1,200m of climb and a 15 hour limit. The route passes through Dunfermline, Lochore, Markinch and Ceres on a mix of path and countryside track.

About

Immerse yourself in the rich history and stunning landscapes of Scotland's Fife Pilgrim Way during this challenging 90km ultramarathon. Runners will embark on a point-to-point journey from North Queensferry to the historic town of St Andrews, passing through picturesque villages and coastal trails that offer breathtaking views of the Firth of Forth. This event is a must for adventurous ultrarunners seeking an unforgettable experience.
