About

The Shawshank Hustle is a 4.6 mile run/walk event held at the Ohio State Reformatory, the filming location of The Shawshank Redemption. The race features a fun atmosphere, unique giveaways, a finisher's medal, and a course on closed city streets with rolling hills from the Reformatory to Downtown Mansfield and back.
