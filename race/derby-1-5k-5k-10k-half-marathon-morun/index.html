Course

Starts and finishes at Markeaton Park in Derby on an undulating mix of surfaced path and trail. The 5k, 10k and half marathon lap the park; the course is buggy-, wheelchair- and dog-friendly. A 1.5k Mini MoRun is also offered.

About

MoRunning brings everyday people together with a focus on inspiring you, your family, your kids and your friends to have the best time running! When you MoRun, you will be inspired to achieve your goals, run with a smile, meet incredible people, and help raise awareness and fundraising for the Movember charity.
