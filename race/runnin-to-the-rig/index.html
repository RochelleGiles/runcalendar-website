Course

A 5K run and walk around the Bakersfield High School campus, starting on Morrow Field for runners and Griffith Field Stadium for walkers. A 2K walking option tours historic spots on campus, including the Harvey Auditorium stage. A kids 5K and a shorter, slower 5K option are also offered.

About

Embrace the BHS Legacy and Join us for our 2nd Runnin' To The Rig 5k Run/Walk! We invite you to participate with us in this FUNdraising endeavor to build community and raise funds. Our 5k course is designed as a nostalgic run/walk throughout our historic campus ending near the famous football tunnel in Griffith Stadium. Our 2K walk will meander around campus and even through some of the buildings.
