Course

Off-road trail races starting and finishing at Van Community Centre, Caerphilly, crossing fields and woodland with a marked, marshalled route. The 10K sets off at 10:00am and the 5K at 10:30am, with a notable downhill single-track section. Two-hour time limit and walkers welcome.

About

The Caerphilly Trail Races offer runners a thrilling multi-terrain experience through beautiful countryside. Participants can enjoy spectacular scenery, varied terrain including fields and woods, with routes featuring hills and a great downhill single-track section.
