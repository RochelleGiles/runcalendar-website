Course

Three trail routes through Table Mountain National Park above Cape Town, taking in city, coastline and mountain. The King Protea covers 43 km, the Stately Protea 22 km and the Pincushion Protea 11 km, each climbing steadily onto the mountain before dropping back to the city side.

About

The Cape Town Trail Marathon takes runners through mountain trails around Cape Town in the Western Cape, with distances of 43km, 22km and 11km. It is one of the city's most established trail running events, drawing entrants from across South Africa.
