Course

The 21.1km and 8.5km start at the Tauranga Yacht and Power Boat Club, Sulphur Point, cross the Harbour Bridge and run via Totara Street, Coronation Park and the Pilot Bay waterfront, finishing on sand at Mount Main Beach. The half adds Harbour Drive and a loop of the Daisy Hardwick Track, with the bridge the only hill. The 5km starts and finishes on Mount Main Beach. Surfaces are road, boardwalk, gravel and beach.

About

The Tauranga City to Surf offers 21.1km and 8.5km courses starting at the Tauranga Yacht and Power Boat Club at Sulphur Point, plus a 5km starting and finishing on Mount Main Beach. Runners cross the Harbour Bridge and follow Totara Street, Coronation Park and the Pilot Bay waterfront. All courses finish on the sand at Mount Main Beach.
