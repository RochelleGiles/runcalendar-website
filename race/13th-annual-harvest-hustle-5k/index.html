Course

The 5K starts and finishes in the parking lot of Notre Dame College Prep High School, 7655 W Dempster St, Niles. A youth 5K run/walk runs alongside the open event. Every participant collects a participation medal at the race.

About

The Harvest Hustle 5K in Niles raises money and collects food donations to benefit Niles Family Services Food Pantry, supporting families in need throughout the community.
