Course

A point-to-point down the John Day River valley on Highway 19. The half marathon starts a quarter mile northwest of Service Creek and finishes in Spray; the 10K and 5K start 6.2 and 3.1 miles west of town on the same road. The paved course rises just twelve feet from start to finish. Liquids are available at four points along the way plus the finish. The highway is not closed to traffic.

About

Now in its seventh decade, the Eastern Oregon Half Marathon is Oregon's oldest half marathon, held each May in the small town of Spray on the John Day River in eastern Oregon.
