About

Odisseia Ultra Trail Run is celebrating its 5th edition as one of Brazil's premier trail running experiences. The event features challenging multi-day races through the scenic terrain of Urubici with significant elevation gains. With 100% approval ratings from previous editions, participants are transported into a parallel world of intense emotions and memorable trail running experiences.
