Course

13.5km course with 800m of vertical climb, starting at Nightingale Bros Alpine Produce, Wandiligong, as Day 4 of the 4 Peaks Bright Alpine Climb. On-the-day registration opens 6:15am and runners self-select a start time between 7am and 8am in five-minute intervals. No aid station on course; fruit, water and electrolyte are provided at the finish.

About

Mystic Hill is a 13.5km trail run with 800m of vertical climb. The event starts at Nightingale Bros Alpine Produce in Wandiligong and is part of the 4 Peaks multi-day trail running event.
