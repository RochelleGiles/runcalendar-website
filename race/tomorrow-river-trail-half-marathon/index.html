About

In its 2nd year, the Tomorrow River Trail Half-Marathon is a 13.1 mile race starting in Plover and finishing at Central Waters Brewing Co. in Amherst with the majority of its course being on the beautiful Tomorrow River Trail. The trail is crushed limestone and makes up around 9.5 miles of the course weaving through Wisconsin farmland and alleys of trees along the ridge.
