About

The Carlingford 5K is a scenic evening road race set in the breathtaking village of Carlingford, Co. Louth, Ireland. Runners will enjoy stunning views of Carlingford Lough and the Cooley Mountains as they make their way through this charming coastal town. This event is a must for anyone seeking a picturesque and memorable running experience.
