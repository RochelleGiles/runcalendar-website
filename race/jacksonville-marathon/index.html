Course

Starts and finishes at The Bolles School on San Jose Boulevard, with the finish line inside the school stadium track. The USATF-certified marathon, half marathon and 5K run on flat Jacksonville roads, with digital clocks at every mile and water and Gatorade every two miles. The marathon is a Boston qualifier.

About

The Ameris Bank Jacksonville Marathon brings marathon, half marathon and 5K distances to Jacksonville, Florida each December. Title-sponsored by Ameris Bank, the race draws runners from across North Florida for a winter weekend in the self-styled River City.
