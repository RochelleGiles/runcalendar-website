About

A trail running event at Lake MacBride State Park in Solon, Iowa, offering 5K, 10K, and half marathon distances on crushed limestone and single-track trails. The event features clear course marking, support stations, custom bibs, rotating race items, post-race food, and free beer from Singlespeed Brewery.
