About

Ruee Des Fadas Rocbaron is a unique adventure festival filled with mud, obstacles, and unforgettable experiences. Participants will tackle over 30 crazy obstacles, from traversing electrical wires to climbing cargo nets, all while surrounded by a lively festival atmosphere with food trucks, drinks, and music.
