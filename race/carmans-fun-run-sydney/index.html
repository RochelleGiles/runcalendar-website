Course

A flat, fast road course based at Metcalfe Park, Pyrmont, on Sydney Harbour. The half marathon runs a new two-lap out-and-back; a 10K, 5K and Pink Lady 5K walk share the same precinct.

About

Carman's Fun Run Sydney offers 5K, 10K and half marathon options through the city each spring, with entry proceeds supporting the Breast Cancer Network Australia's work for people affected by breast cancer.
