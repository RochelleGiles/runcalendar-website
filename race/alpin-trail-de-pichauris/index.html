About

The Alpin Trail De Pichauris is a challenging and scenic trail running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of the French Alps. Runners will navigate a picturesque course through rugged mountain terrain, testing their endurance and rewarding them with stunning vistas at every turn. This event offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes seeking an adventure in the great outdoors.
