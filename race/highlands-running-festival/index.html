Course

Starts and finishes at Oxley College Bray Fields in Burradoo. The course is fully pathed, following the Bong Bong Common Track along riverside paths through the Southern Highlands. Distances are a 21.1 km half marathon, 10 km, 5 km and a 2 km Family Dash, all chip timed with water stations on course.

About

The Highlands Running Festival in Burradoo, NSW offers a scenic and challenging course through the stunning Southern Highlands. With events ranging from the 2km Family Dash to the grueling Half Marathon, this festival caters to runners of all abilities seeking an unforgettable experience amidst the region's picturesque landscapes.
