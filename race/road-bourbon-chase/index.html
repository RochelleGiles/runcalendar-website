Course

206-mile point-to-point road relay from Jim Beam Distillery in Clermont through Kentucky bourbon country to Lexington Brewing and Distilling in Lexington. Teams of 12 (or 6 ultra, plus a 6-runner single-day sprint option) cover legs averaging 17.2 miles per runner over two days; all runners must be 21 or older.
