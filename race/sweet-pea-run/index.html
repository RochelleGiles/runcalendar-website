Course

Both races start and finish on Main Street in downtown Bozeman. The 5K mixes road and trail, following Bozeman Creek past the community gardens and historic Willson Avenue to a flat Main Street finish. The 10K heads east and south of town with a climb of over a mile and further rise toward the MSU campus before merging with the 5K for the final mile.
