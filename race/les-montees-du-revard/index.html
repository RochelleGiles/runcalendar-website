About

Conquer the stunning slopes of the Revard mountain range in this captivating trail running event. Runners will be treated to breathtaking alpine views as they tackle challenging ascents and technical descents through the picturesque French countryside. A must-do for any trail enthusiast looking to push their limits in a stunning natural setting.
