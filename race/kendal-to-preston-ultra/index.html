About

Explore the picturesque landscapes of England's North West on the Kendal to Preston Ultra, a challenging 50-mile point-to-point ultramarathon. This scenic route takes runners through the stunning Lake District and along the scenic Fylde Coast, offering a truly unforgettable running experience. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or looking to push your limits, the Kendal to Preston Ultra promises an adventure you won't soon forget.
