About

Between sport, nature and history, this event will allow you to discover the emblematic Châteaux of the Loire, and will immerse you in a majestic setting listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Epopée Royale is not just a sports challenge, it is also a Solidarity Epopée - with two events for the benefit of the association 'Les invincibles' raising funds for research on Charcot's Disease.
