About

A timed ultra held on a measured loop at Felixstow Reserve on the River Torrens in inner Adelaide. Entrants choose a 24, 12, 6 or 3 hour event and cover as much distance as they can within it, running through the night in the longer options. The course is flat, shaded and AIMS certified.
