About

Explore the stunning Riverhead Forest on a women-only trail running adventure at the Trail LoopHER in Auckland, New Zealand. This scenic event offers a challenging yet rewarding course through the heart of the lush forest, providing a unique opportunity to connect with nature and fellow female runners. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail LoopHER promises an unforgettable experience.
