Course

Road courses based at Eastern Illinois University's O'Brien Stadium in Charleston, with every distance finishing inside the stadium. Staggered starts send the 10K away at 7:50am, the 5K at 8:15am and the one-mile at 9:30am. Toddler and infant dashes are run on the 50-yard line of O'Brien Field.

About

Family-friendly event featuring chip-timed 10K, 5K, and 1-mile fun run/walk at EIU’s O’Brien Stadium in Charleston, IL. Includes finisher medals, awards, food trucks, and kids' activities with proceeds benefiting the SBL Dental program.
