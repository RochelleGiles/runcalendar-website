Course

A flat, single-lap 50km starting and finishing at Summerleaze Park in Maidenhead, linking the Thames Path, the Jubilee River and the Green Way. About 75% is off road, with roughly 235m of climbing over the whole course, and the route is fully signed. Four checkpoints at about 10km intervals carry cola, water and sweet and savoury snacks. The cutoff is set to local sunset.

About

Challenge yourself to Beat the Sunset in this captivating 50K ultra marathon set against the picturesque backdrop of Maidenhead, UK. Runners will race to make it to the finish line before darkness falls, pushing their limits in a unique test of endurance. This event promises an unforgettable experience for ultrarunners seeking a thrilling challenge.
