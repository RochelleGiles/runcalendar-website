About

The Run Your Outback Trail Festival is a multi-day running event in Robin Falls, Northern Territory. Choose from 7km, 18km, 28km and 56km distances, plus a 1km kids' run, on outback trail terrain of fire-break dirt roads and rocky, hilly single track.
