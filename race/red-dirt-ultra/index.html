Course

Singletrack trail ultra on the Sandstone Trail in Kisatchie National Forest, Louisiana — loose and hardpacked sand, stream crossings and longleaf pine. Loop format: 50K one small loop, 100K small plus one big loop, 100-mile three big loops; six aid stations around the small loop.

About

Join us on the beautiful Sandstone Trail in Kisatchie National Forest for the seventh edition of the Red Dirt Ultra. This rugged trail consist of loose and hardpacked sand, stream crossings, climbing and descending, and long runnable sections through the long leaf pines.
