Course

A Thanksgiving morning trail run on dirt trails at Lake Cunningham, starting and finishing by the marina entrance on State Street, Omaha. The 5K and 10K share a loop course; the half marathon runs the 5K course first, then a longer loop around the lake. Three aid stations serve the half — one water only, two with water and Tailwind. The race is cupless, so runners carry a bottle.

About

Feast and Feathers is a Thanksgiving-morning trail race in Omaha, Nebraska, with half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances. It's billed as one of the city's best Thanksgiving trail run traditions, drawing runners out before the holiday meal.
