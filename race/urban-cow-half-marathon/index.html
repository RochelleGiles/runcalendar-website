Course

A road half marathon through central Sacramento, taking in Capitol Mall, DOCO, Old Sacramento, the Tower Bridge and the Sacramento River waterfront. The half runs on the Sunday; a 5K through William Land Park is held on the Saturday. Aid stations are provided on course.

About

The Urban Cow Half Marathon is a half marathon and 5K through the streets of Sacramento, California, held each September as part of Urban Cow Weekend, one of the city's longtime running traditions.
