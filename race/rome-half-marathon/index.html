Course

A single loop through the historic centre of Rome on closed city roads, passing the Colosseum, the Circus Maximus, the Baths of Caracalla and Piazza Navona. Alongside the half marathon the organiser runs a non-competitive 5K, a relay and a business team race. Around 10,000 runners are expected, most of them from outside Italy.

About

The Wizz Air Rome Half Marathon is a prestigious 21.0975 km race through the historic center of Rome. The route takes runners past iconic landmarks including the Colosseum, Circus Maximus, Baths of Caracalla, and Piazza Navona. In its second edition, it set the record for Italy's largest half marathon with 22,304 registered participants, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the eternal city's great beauty while competing.
