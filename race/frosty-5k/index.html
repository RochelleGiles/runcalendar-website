Course

About

The Frosty 5K is Moberly Parks and Recreation's winter run and walk through the Christmas lights display in Rothwell Park, Missouri, now in its 29th year. The race starts in the evening from the Rothwell Park Lodge, with entry taken online, at the MPRD office or on the night.
