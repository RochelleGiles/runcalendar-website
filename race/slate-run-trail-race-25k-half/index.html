Course

Technical singletrack through the remote mountains of Tiadaghton State Forest, starting at 9:00am from the Manor Fork area. Both distances share the first nine miles, which include one long climb, with an aid station at the Manor Fork camps; the half then returns by road while the 25K carries on over technical trail via the Old Supply Trail. Stream crossings and open vistas throughout.

About

A 25K/Half Marathon trail race through the remote and rugged mountains of Tiadaghton State Forest. The course will take runners through the Algerine Wild Area and Red Run sections of the Black Forest Trail that are full of technical single track, streams, and awesome vistas.
