Course

Five mile out-and-back on the Brampton Valley Way from Scotland Road in Market Harborough, run twice through the 422m Oxendon Tunnel. The surface is hard-packed track suitable for road or trail shoes, with ruts and puddles inside the tunnel. A head torch is mandatory. The Night Run starts at 7.30pm on the Thursday and the Dawn Run at 6.30am the next morning.

About

The Tunnel Vision Night and Dawn Runs offer a unique running experience, with participants tackling a 5-mile out-and-back course that takes them through the long and dark Oxendon Tunnel. The night run starts at 7:30 PM, while the dawn run begins at 6:30 AM, providing runners with the option to experience the tunnel in different lighting conditions. The event is suitable for both experienced athletes and less experienced runners looking for a challenge.
