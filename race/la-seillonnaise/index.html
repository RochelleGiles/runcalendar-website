About

La Seillonnaise is a series of nature runs in the Seillon forest (Ain) and a Nordic walk, organized by CAP SEILLON. The event offers a 17km run with 2 aid stations and a finish line aid station, a 10km run (Challenge Agglo) with 1 aid station and a finish line aid station, and a 10km Nordic walk/hike that is not timed. The proceeds from the three races are donated to the Rêves association, which fulfills the dreams of very seriously ill children.
