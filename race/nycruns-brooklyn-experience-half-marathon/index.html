Course

Starts in Williamsburg and Greenpoint in north Brooklyn, then follows the East River waterfront beneath the Williamsburg, Manhattan and Brooklyn bridges before turning inland along Fulton Street and Flatbush Avenue to finish in Prospect Park. The USATF-certified road course has nine fluid stations, split timing points and medical support on route.
