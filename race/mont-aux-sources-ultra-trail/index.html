About

Mont-Aux-Sources Ultra Trail runs through Royal Natal National Park in the Drakensberg, part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site. First held in 1992, it is one of South Africa's oldest trail races, with 21km and 50km routes and proceeds supporting local conservation.
