About

Ring O' Fire is a three-day, 130-mile coastal ultramarathon that circumnavigates Ynys Mon (Anglesey) in north Wales, following the island's coastal path. Runners cover the route in stages across a long weekend, supported by checkpoints and bag transport. A relay option and a 50-mile Firelighter race run alongside the main event.
