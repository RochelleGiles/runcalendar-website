Course

A loop through paved, tree-lined residential streets in Highland, described as slightly hilly, starting and finishing on the St. Paul Catholic Church grounds at Ninth and Lemon Streets. Water stops, mile markers and marshalled intersections serve the 10K, 5K and 2K.

About

The St. Paul Strassenlauf is a running event that takes place as part of the St. Paul Kirchenfest in Highland, Illinois. The event features 10K, 5K, and 2K races through the tree-lined residential streets of Highland.
