Course

Held at A.W. Marion State Park near Circleville on rolling wooded terrain, running along the Hargus Lake shoreline inside the 310-acre park. The six-hour timed event starts at 8:00am and the half marathon, 10K and 5K all start at 10:00am. Loop configuration, surface split and aid station detail are not published.

About

The Hargus Lake Trail Race offers trail running events including 1/2 Marathon, 10K, 5K, 6 Hours of Power, and 20K Relay at A.W. Marion State Park along Hargus Lake. The courses feature rolling woodlands, shoreline trails with color-coded markings, and aid station support.
