About

The Julian Alps Trail Run by UTMB is the biggest Slovenian trail running event, hosting more than 5,000 runners from around the world. The courses present the true charm of trail running in Slovenia — single trails, beautiful views and the breathtaking nature of the Julian Alps and Triglav National Park.
