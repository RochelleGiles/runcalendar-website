Course

Held at the Redbanks property in Nugent, about 30 minutes from Hobart Airport, over hills, bush trails and mud with obstacles set along the way. The 7km runs as a timed competitive wave and an untimed fun wave, with a 3.5km junior option for ages 10 and over.

About

The Rugged Race is a mud run and obstacle course event held in rural Nugent, south-east Tasmania, with a 7km competitive course, a 7km fun run and a junior 3.5km option.
