Course

An out-and-back 5K on the paved river trail beside the Guadalupe River, starting and finishing at Riverside Nature Center in Kerrville. The route is largely shaded with water stops along the way.

About

The 11th Annual 5K Run/Walk for the Riverside Nature Center is on track. Our initial postponement was due to flood recovery. Now that the River Trail is open, we are ready to roll. Grab your honey and Run for Riverside on February 14th.
