About

The Palmanova Half Marathon is a prestigious running event taking place in the unique UNESCO World Heritage star-shaped fortress city of Palmanova. Runners will experience a super-fast course that passes through the historic borough of Clauiano, offering a blend of scenic urban and countryside running. The event features not only the main half marathon but also additional activities like a School Relay and a non-competitive 5 or 10 km march.
