About

Lace up your trail shoes and take on the Orion Mercury 10 Mile Race, a challenging cross-country trek through the scenic Epping Forest just outside of London. Hosted by the Orion Harriers running club, this off-road adventure offers runners a chance to test their mettle on rolling hills and technical terrain. Whether you're chasing a new personal best or simply looking to explore the great outdoors, the Orion Mercury is sure to deliver an unforgettable running experience.
