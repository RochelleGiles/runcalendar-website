Course

The 10K starts and finishes at Royston Wellness and Community Park, 179 Skelton St, Royston, Georgia, and runs as one large loop around the Royston area. The organiser publishes a course map but no surface or aid-station detail. A virtual option is offered alongside the in-person race.

About

10K run/walk through Royston, GA, where participants donate new or gently used coats to help those in need this winter season. Finishers receive hot cocoa, a t-shirt, and a medal.
