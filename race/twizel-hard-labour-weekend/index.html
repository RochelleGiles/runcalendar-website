Course

The Pyramid Run is the running discipline of the weekend, starting and finishing at Ben Ohau Station near Twizel. The 21km half marathon is a loop with out-and-back sections, crossing Darts Bush and Fraser Streams and climbing 290m over the Pyramid Saddle. Surface is a short section of sealed road plus farm tracks, singletrack and 4WD tracks. Four drink stations on the half marathon course; 10km and 5km kids options also run.

About

The Meridian Twizel Hard Labour Weekend is a multi-day event in New Zealand's Mackenzie Country combining a kayak race, a mountain bike race and the off-road Pyramid Run. Competitors enter individual disciplines or complete all three to earn Hard Labourer status. The Pyramid Run offers 21km and 10km distances starting at Ben Ohau Station.
