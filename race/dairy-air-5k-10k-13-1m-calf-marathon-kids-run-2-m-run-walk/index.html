Course

Farm-themed races based at Delaware Valley University in Doylestown, with all distances starting and finishing on the campus. The 5k, 10k and 2-mile routes overlap across the university grounds and surrounding roads, while the 13.1-mile Calf Marathon runs its own longer loop. The courses pass within a few feet of the university farm's animal barns.

About

Dairy Air Run Fest is a family friendly event with a distance for EVERY level of runner or walker! Each distance (5k, 10k, 13.1M calf marathon, Kids' Run & 2 mile fun run) will take runners & walkers within a few feet of just about every farm animal you can think of!
