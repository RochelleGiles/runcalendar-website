Course

A USATF-certified 15K road course starting and finishing in downtown Schenectady at 8:30am. The route winds through the historic Stockade district, Central Park and the Vale Cemetery, on sealed city streets and park roads with rolling sections through the park.

About

The Stockade-athon 15K road race takes place in Schenectady, New York, finishing through the historic Stockade District, one of the oldest neighborhoods in the United States, settled by the Dutch in the 17th century.
