Course

A 10km road race starting and finishing on Beach Road at King's Beach, Humewood, with a 07:00 start. The route takes in city landmarks and is lined with entertainment stations including dancers, marimba bands and drummers. Two refreshment stations are provided on route, with tog bag service and a finisher's medal at the finish.
