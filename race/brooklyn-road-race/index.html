Course

A road race starting and finishing at Brooklyn Design Square in Pretoria, on the corner of Veale and Bronkhorst Streets. Four distances are offered — 33km, 21.1km, 10km and 5km — on suburban tarmac through the eastern suburbs. Organised by Pretoria Marathon Club with mat-to-mat timing.

About

The Brooklyn Road Race is a long-standing club road race through the Brooklyn area of Pretoria, offering distances from a 5km up to a 33km for experienced runners, alongside 10km and 21km options.
