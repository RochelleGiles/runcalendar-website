Course

Starts outside Parkview Field on Ewing Street in downtown Fort Wayne. The 10K runs through historic neighbourhoods and past landmark churches, while the 4 Mile finishes on the field inside the ballpark. The half marathon is USATF certified, with water stops and live entertainment on course.

About

The Fort4Fitness Fall Festival brings a half marathon, 10K and 4 mile race to downtown Fort Wayne, Indiana. Organized by the Fort4Fitness nonprofit, the fall festival is part of a broader push to promote healthy, active living across the city.
