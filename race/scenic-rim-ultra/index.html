Course

Loop course starting and finishing at Lilybrook Recreation Area (Western Trail Head) on Beaudesert-Boonah Road, about an hour south of Brisbane. The route crosses four mountains, the Dugandan Range and Wyaralong Dam on dirt road, single track and dense forest, much of it private trail normally closed to the public. Staffed checkpoints support the course.

About

Explore the stunning Scenic Rim region at the Scenic Rim Ultra, a challenging yet breathtaking trail running event. Winding through lush forests and across rolling hills, this picturesque course offers runners an unforgettable experience amidst the natural beauty of the Australian landscape. Whether you're a seasoned ultrarunner or a trail running enthusiast, the Scenic Rim Ultra promises an adventure you won't soon forget.
