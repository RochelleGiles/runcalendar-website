About

The last of the Rockford Road Runners' four Tour de Trails races, run every winter since 2013: twelve miles through the Severson Dells, Funderburg and Coleman forest preserves, starting at 10:00. Expect late winter underfoot, which can mean ice, snow, mud and cold. Each race is entered on its own, or all four together as the series.
