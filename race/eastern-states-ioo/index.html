About

A 103-mile single-loop ultramarathon starting and finishing at Waterville in the Pennsylvania Wilds, circumnavigating the Pine Creek watershed. The course carries more than 20,000 feet of climbing on remote mountain trails, with crew-access aid stations around the loop.
