About

The Minnehaha 15K & 5K trail race takes place on the private trails of Camp Manatoc, normally closed to the public, featuring technical single-track paths, hills, and Minnehaha Falls. Hikers are welcome, and participants can combine it with the Leave No Trace Half Marathon for an extra challenge.
