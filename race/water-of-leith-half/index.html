Course

A point-to-point half marathon following the Water of Leith pathway from a farm track off Harlow Road just outside Balerno to Coalhill in Leith. The route is mostly off-road, well marked and marshalled at key road crossings, but roads are not closed to traffic. It is run as a handicap, with the slowest starters setting off first.

About

The Water of Leith Half Marathon is a point-to-point race tracing part of Edinburgh's Water of Leith, the river that flows from the Pentland Hills to the historic port of Leith. It is organised by Blast Running.
