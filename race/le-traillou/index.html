About

Le Trailou is a scenic trail running event set in the beautiful Aveyron region, overlooking the stunning Gorges du Tarn. Participants will traverse two ecological zones and a Natura 2000 site, experiencing the rich natural beauty of the area while challenging themselves on challenging trail routes.
