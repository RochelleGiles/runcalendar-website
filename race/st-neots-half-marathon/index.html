Course

A single-lap half marathon on gently undulating country roads around St Neots, starting on Cromwell Road five minutes' walk from race HQ at Ernulf School. A three-mile downhill finish makes for seriously fast times. Fully marshalled and policed, with drink stations at roughly 3, 6, 9 and 11 miles plus gel and nutrition drops. Capped at 2,000 runners and usually sells out quickly.

About

St Neots Half Marathon runs through this market town in Cambridgeshire, which sits on the banks of the River Great Ouse. The route takes in the town and its riverside surroundings.
