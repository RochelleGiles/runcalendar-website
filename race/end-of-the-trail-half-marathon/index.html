Starts and finishes at the End of the Trail statue in Mooney Grove Park, Visalia. Runners cover roughly a mile inside the park before heading out the back gate onto flat, closed country roads to a turnaround. Aid stations are staffed by local high school runners, with DJs and live bands on course.
