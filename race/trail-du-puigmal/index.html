About

The Trail Du Puigmal is a thrilling mountain running event that takes place in the picturesque Pyrenees mountains. Runners will challenge themselves on a rugged trail course that showcases the stunning natural beauty of the region, with breathtaking views of the iconic Puigmal peak along the way. This event is a must-try for adventurous trail runners seeking an unforgettable high-altitude experience.
