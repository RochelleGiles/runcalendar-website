About

The Reims Champagne Run is a must-do event for runners looking to experience the picturesque vineyards and historic city of Reims, France. Participants can soak in the stunning scenery as they race through the heart of the Champagne region, with the iconic Notre-Dame Cathedral serving as a breathtaking backdrop. This unique running event offers a chance to indulge in the local culture and, of course, the world-famous bubbly.
