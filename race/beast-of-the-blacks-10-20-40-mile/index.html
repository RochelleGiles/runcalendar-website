Course

A lapped mountain trail race starting and finishing in Crickhowell, Powys, in the Black Mountains of Wales. The route crosses forest trails, mountain tops, river crossings and ancient churches; the 40 mile runs the 20-mile loop twice, the second lap in reverse. Climbing runs from 1,535 ft on the 10 mile to 8,924 ft on the 40.

About

Conquer the rugged terrain of the Black Mountains in Wales at the Beast of the Blacks, a challenging trail running event offering 40, 20, and 11-mile courses. Traverse through the majestic landscapes of this stunning region, pushing your limits amidst breathtaking natural beauty. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a thrilling new adventure, the Beast of the Blacks promises an unforgettable journey for runners of all abilities.
