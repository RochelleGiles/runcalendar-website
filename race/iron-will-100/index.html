Course

Flat, fast river trail with roughly 75% shade, starting and finishing on Highway 57 north of Junction City. The 100 mile runs ten out and back legs, the 50 mile five, and the 50K a short out and back followed by three longer ones. Manned aid stations every five miles.

About

A 100-mile trail ultra, with 50K and 50-mile options, near Junction City, Kansas, built as an adaptive-friendly race welcoming runners of varying abilities. The course winds through the trail network around Milford Lake.
