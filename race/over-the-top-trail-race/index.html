Course

Starts and finishes at Centennial Park in Pocatello, with all four distances held on trails in the City Creek Recreation Area. Footing is rocky, narrow and steep, with exposed sections high on the half marathon and 30K. Singletrack makes up roughly 75 to 98 percent depending on distance, and the race is paperless, so runners carry their own bottle or cup.

About

Over the Top Trail Race is hosted by the Pocatello Running Club in the hills above Pocatello, Idaho, offering 5K, 10K, half marathon, and 30K distances on singletrack trail.
