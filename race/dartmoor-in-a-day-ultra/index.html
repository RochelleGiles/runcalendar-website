Course

Point-to-point 50km ultra from Okehampton across open Dartmoor to a finish at Ivybridge Community College, passing Postbridge, Bellever Tor and the West Dart stepping stones. Almost entirely off road over wild open moor. A 30km option starts at Postbridge and covers the second half. Checkpoints with refreshments at Postbridge and Combestone Tor.

About

Conquer the rugged terrain of Dartmoor National Park in a single day at the Dartmoor In A Day Ultra. This challenging event takes runners on a captivating journey through the park's diverse landscapes, from rolling hills to windswept moorlands. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a thrilling new challenge, this race promises an unforgettable adventure in the heart of one of England's most breathtaking natural wonders.
