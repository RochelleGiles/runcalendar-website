Course

All trail races start at Blue Lake (Lake Tikitapu), Rotorua, with 8:00am starts. About

The XTERRA Rotorua Festival is a premier multi-sport event in New Zealand that draws top athletes from around the world. Set in the stunning natural landscapes of Rotorua, this off-road triathlon challenges competitors with a rugged course through lush forests, across crystal-clear lakes, and up challenging mountain trails.
