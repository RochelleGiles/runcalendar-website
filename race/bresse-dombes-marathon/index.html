About

The Bresse Dombes Marathon takes runners on a picturesque journey through the rolling hills and tranquil lakes of the Bresse Dombes region. This scenic French marathon offers a challenging yet rewarding course, showcasing the natural beauty of the Ain department. Runners will be captivated by the stunning landscapes and charming local towns along the way, making this event a must-do for any marathon enthusiast.
