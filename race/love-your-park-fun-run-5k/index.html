About

Join us for a community celebration that brings neighbors together for a great cause — supporting the North of the River Recreation Foundation's Youth Scholarship Fund! Every step you take helps local children and families access recreation programs, sports, and camps that build confidence, connection, and lifelong memories. Whether you're running the 5K, walking the Fun Run, or cheering from the sidelines, this event is all about loving our parks, loving our hearts, and giving back to the parks that make our neighborhoods thrive.
