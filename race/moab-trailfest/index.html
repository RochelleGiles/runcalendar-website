Course

A three-day stage event based at Old Spanish Trail Arena: Pritchett Arch 13.5 mi with steep climbs and class-3 scrambles, Gemini Bridges 16.75 mi on slickrock and sandy singletrack of the Horsethief system, and Porcupine Rim 14.6 mi on rocky doubletrack. Each stage starts 7:00am with a 4.5-hour cutoff.

About

Moab Trailfest is a multi-day trail running festival featuring moderate back-to-back races on dirt trails in the stunning landscapes of Moab, Utah. It offers opportunities for runners of all levels to explore new trails, enjoy family activities, and connect with outdoor enthusiasts amid the natural beauty of the American Southwest.
