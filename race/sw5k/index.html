About

Each summer as a part of our Outdoor Sundays, we host a competitive 5K race, 5K walk, and a family-friendly one-mile fun run. There are prizes for various age groups for children, women, and men. Our morning run is at the end of summer and will hopefully feature cool temperatures in our course through the neighborhoods and trails.
