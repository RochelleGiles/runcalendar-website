About

The Courses Des 3 Villes Soeurs is a renowned running event that takes participants on a scenic tour through the charming towns of the French Côte d'Opale region. Featuring both a race and a walking event, this historic competition offers runners of all levels the chance to experience the stunning coastal landscapes and vibrant local culture. Whether you're seeking a challenging course or a leisurely adventure, the Courses Des 3 Villes Soeurs promises an unforgettable experience for endurance enthusiasts.
