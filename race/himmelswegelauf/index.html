About

The Himmelswegelauf is a unique running event that explores the archaeological and cultural heritage of the Nebra region. Runners can experience historic sites like the Nebra Sky Disc location, the Goseck Sun Observatory, and other ancient cultural landmarks while participating in various running distances.
