About

The MRT 5k to 50k is a trail running event held at the Statesville Soccer Complex in North Carolina, featuring distances from 5k to 50k in 5k increments. It is part of a championship series in which participants accumulate points across multiple races for prizes and awards in overall, masters, and age-group categories.
