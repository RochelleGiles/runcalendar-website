About

The Horikiri Ecomarathon offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of Japan's Arakawa region. This eco-friendly event takes participants on a scenic course through lush forests and alongside tranquil waterways, showcasing the area's commitment to sustainability. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a casual runner, the Horikiri Ecomarathon promises an unforgettable adventure in a stunning setting.
