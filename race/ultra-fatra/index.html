About

An extreme trail running event traversing the main ridge of Veľká Fatra National Park. Runners experience challenging mountain terrain featuring rocky cliffs, mountain meadows, and pristine natural reserves. The event offers a unique connection with nature, providing participants with breathtaking views and a sense of freedom in an untouched landscape.
