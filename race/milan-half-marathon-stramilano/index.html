About

The Milan Half Marathon Stramilano is a prestigious running event that takes place in the heart of Milan, Italy. The course showcases the city's iconic landmarks and offers a scenic route through the historic streets. Runners can expect a lively atmosphere and a challenging yet rewarding experience as they take on the 21.1 km distance.
