Course

Starts and finishes at Monte Sano Lodge above Huntsville and runs on the mountain's singletrack. The going is extremely rocky, with mud and creek crossings, and close to 3,900 feet of climbing across the 50K. It is one of Alabama's oldest continuously run trail ultras.

About

A trail ultramarathon on Monte Sano Mountain in Huntsville, Alabama, organized by the Huntsville Track Club each January. It's one of Alabama's oldest continuously run trail ultras, prized by regional trail runners for its demanding singletrack.
