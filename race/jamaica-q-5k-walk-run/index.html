About

Jamaica, Queens is known as a vibrant hub of strength, community, and culture. This USATF-certified 5K run is designed to promote a meaningful community connection through a day of fitness and physical activity. The course route will promote attention on Downtown Jamaica's commercial corridors and highlight historic landmarks throughout the neighborhood.
