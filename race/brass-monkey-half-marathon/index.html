Course

A flat, fast certified road half marathon looping south from York Racecourse, with start and finish at the Ebor Stand. Three water stations on course and water at the finish; the course is suitable for wheelchair athletes and there are no toilets out on the route.

About

A flat, fast half marathon through the Vale of York in January, named for the bitter winter cold. Renowned among UK runners as one of the quickest courses in the calendar, it draws a large field chasing personal bests each year.
