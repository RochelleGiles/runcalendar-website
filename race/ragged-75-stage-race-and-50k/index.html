Course

A three-day stage race on the Sunapee Ragged Kearsarge Greenway around Lake Sunapee, with the Sunday stage also run as a standalone 50K. Stage 1 goes from Ragged Mountain over Mt Ragged and Mt Kearsarge to Wadleigh State Park; stage 2 crosses Mt Sunapee to Sunapee Middle High School; stage 3 returns to Ragged Mountain. Technical single track, double track, fire roads, dirt and some pavement, with limited aid.

About

The Ragged 75 is a multi-day trail stage race based at Ragged Mountain in Danbury, New Hampshire, with a standalone 50K option for runners tackling a single day. The event covers rugged central New Hampshire singletrack over three days.
