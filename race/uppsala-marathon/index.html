About

The Uppsala Marathon is a must-experience running event set against the picturesque backdrop of Sweden's historic university city. Runners will wind their way through charming streets and parks, taking in stunning medieval architecture and beautiful natural scenery along the course. With a reputation for excellent organization and a welcoming community, this event is a fantastic choice for marathoners seeking a memorable European running adventure.
