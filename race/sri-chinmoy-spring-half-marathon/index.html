Course

A flat 2.5km loop marked with cones in North Hagley Park, Christchurch, repeated according to distance. Registration and the finish are near Victoria Lake, and a single hydration station on the loop carries water and electrolyte drink.

About

The Sri Chinmoy Spring Half Marathon, 10km and Kids' 2.5km races are based at Hagley Park in Christchurch, on a flat 2.5km loop marked with cones inside Hagley Park North. The events suit the whole family in a parkland setting along the park's spring pathways.
