Course

Lapped night trail race from the Pass Mountain Trailhead staging area in Usery Mountain Regional Park, Mesa. Mostly smooth runnable desert singletrack - Noso, Blevins, Moon Rock, Levee, Spillway and Cat Peaks trails - with a few rollers and rocks. The 20K and 30K run two loops, the 5K and 10K one, and every lap passes the start/finish. Main aid at start/finish plus Levee Aid on course.

About

Experience the beauty of Usery Regional Park on an array of fun and fast courses at the Punisher Night Trail. Our 20K distance is perfect for night trail runners ready to step up from the 10K and the challenging 30K will push those ready for a new challenge.
