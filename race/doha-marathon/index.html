About

The Doha Marathon by Ooredoo is now past its 13th running, drawing a large field for a vibrant atmosphere with activities for families. Starting and finishing at Hotel Park, the marathon route follows Doha's picturesque corniche, offering a scenic and safe experience for runners and spectators.
