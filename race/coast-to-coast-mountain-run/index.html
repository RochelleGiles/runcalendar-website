About

Traverse the rugged landscapes of New Zealand's Southern Alps in the Coast to Coast Mountain Run. This challenging event takes runners on a scenic journey through the heart of the country, showcasing the stunning natural beauty of the region. Prepare to be awed by the towering peaks, pristine rivers, and lush native forests that define this iconic running experience.
