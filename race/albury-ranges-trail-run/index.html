Course

Starts and finishes at Bonnie Doon Park, West Albury. The course uses single track and mountain-bike trails including the Powerlines descent. The long course is 14.8 km and the short 5 km, starting at 8:00am and 8:30am. Coffee and pastries are available at the start/finish; no aid stations are described.

About

The Albury Ranges course is a trail runner's dream featuring singletrack heaven. The course is packed with long, grindy climbs, smooth-flowing MTB trails, and includes the infamous 'Powerlines' descent that challenges runners' quads. This fast and technical course offers one of the most varied and exhilarating experiences in the series.
