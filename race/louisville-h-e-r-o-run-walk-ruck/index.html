About

The Louisville Honor Earned, Remembrance Owed (H.E.R.O.) Run/Walk/Ruck, now past its 4th running, honors those who died in battle and those lost at home to the psychological wounds of war. Proceeds benefit Volunteers of America Mid-States and the University of Louisville's Salute to Service Scholarship Fund.
