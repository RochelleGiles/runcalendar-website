About

Le Saint Max Trail is a nature trail running event taking place in the Grésivaudan valley, on the heights of Pontcharra. The course features wooded trails and open paths offering magnificent panoramic views. Organized by the Saint Max Trail association, the event's proceeds benefit the children of Saint-Maximin school.
