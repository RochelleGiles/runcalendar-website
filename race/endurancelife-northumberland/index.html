Course

The only linear course in the Coastal Trail Series, starting at Bamburgh and running south along the Northumberland coast. Terrain is sand and dunes with rocky cliff sections and views of Bamburgh Castle and the offshore islands. Four distances share the route, with 10K, half, marathon and ultra options.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of Northumberland's dramatic coastline during the EnduranceLife Northumberland trail run. Offering 10K, half marathon, full marathon, and ultra-distance options, this event invites runners of all levels to experience the stunning natural scenery and challenge themselves on varied terrain. Whether you're seeking a scenic race or an epic adventure, the EnduranceLife Northumberland promises an unforgettable running experience.
