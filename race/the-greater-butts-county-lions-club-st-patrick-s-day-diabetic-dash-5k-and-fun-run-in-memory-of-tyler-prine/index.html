About

The Greater Butts County Lions Club St. Patrick's Day Diabetic Dash 5K and Fun Run, held in memory of Tyler Prine at Red Devil Hill Athletic Complex in Jackson, Georgia, raises funds for diabetes care for Butts County residents, including support for patients at Rock Springs Clinic and diabetic supply boxes for school nurses.
