Course

A flat, single-lap multi-terrain 10K starting and finishing at Wetherby Racecourse, which serves as race HQ, with parking on site. The race starts at 14:00 and has no cut-off time. Entry on the day is not available and the course is not suitable for wheelchairs.

About

A 10K road race organised by Wetherby Runners AC through the Yorkshire market town of Wetherby, on the banks of the River Wharfe. The course is popular with local clubs and known for good post-race hospitality.
