Course

Point-to-point half marathon starting at Highland Park in Washington, UT and finishing at Bloomington Park in St. George. Runners drop roughly 500 feet over the first four miles, then follow a gradual decline for the remaining nine miles along the paved Virgin River Trail. Aid stations at miles 2.25, 4, 6, 8.5, 10.5 and 11.75 with water, sports drink and gel.

About

The Southern Utah Half Marathon is a beautiful route starting at Highland Park in Washington, UT offering amazing views of the sun rising over Zions National Park in the distance. The race will proceed quickly downhill through Washington City to the scenic Virgin River Trail. This paved trail offers a gradual decline grade for the remaining 9 miles of the race.
