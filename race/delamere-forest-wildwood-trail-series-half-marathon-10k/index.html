Course

Starts and finishes at the Visitor Centre at Linmere in Delamere Forest, Cheshire. The half marathon runs two laps and the 10K one, on a mix of well-marked forest paths and more technical trail with steep climbs and muddy sections through dense woodland. Half marathon starts at 9:30 and the 10K at 10:00, both with a 1:30pm cut-off; canicross options run alongside each.

About

The Delamere Forest Wildwood Trail Series is home to the Cheshire Half Marathon and 10K, held on the trails of Delamere Forest, one of Cheshire's picturesque running locations. The event welcomes runners of all abilities to its forest routes.
