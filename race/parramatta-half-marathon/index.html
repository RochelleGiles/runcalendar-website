About

The Parramatta Half Marathon is Sydney's newest major running event, taking participants on a scenic tour through the vibrant heart of Western Sydney. Runners will experience the energy of Parramatta's bustling streets and parks, with the iconic Parramatta River providing a stunning backdrop along the course. This event is a must for any runner seeking an unforgettable urban challenge in one of Australia's fastest-growing cities.
