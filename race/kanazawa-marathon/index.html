About

The Kanazawa Marathon is an international full marathon event taking place in the historic city of Kanazawa. Runners will experience a scenic course that begins at the Shiinoki Cultural Complex and winds through the beautiful landscapes of Ishikawa Prefecture. The event offers a challenging 42.195 km route approved by JAAF and AIMS, attracting runners from around the world.
