About

The Kassel Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner. Set in the picturesque city of Kassel, Germany, this scenic course winds through historic landmarks and beautiful natural landscapes, offering a truly unique and unforgettable running experience. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a newcomer to the distance, the Kassel Marathon is sure to challenge and inspire you.
