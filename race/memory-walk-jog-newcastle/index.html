About

Explore the scenic Griffith Park in Newcastle, Australia during the annual Memory Walk & Jog event. This family-friendly fitness challenge combines a leisurely walk and invigorating jog to raise funds for a meaningful cause, creating a morning of fun, activity, and community spirit.
