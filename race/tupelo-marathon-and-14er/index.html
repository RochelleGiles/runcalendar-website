Course

An out-and-back on rolling country roads outside Tupelo, starting at 5:00am before sunrise. The 14.2-mile option follows the same route and splits from the marathon at mile 13. Water stops are set along the way.

About

The Tupelo Marathon and 14er is an annual road race in Tupelo, Mississippi, featuring a full marathon and a 14.2-mile companion distance. Both courses run through the city, drawing local and regional runners each September.
