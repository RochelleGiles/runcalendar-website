About

The Wicklow Hospice Half Marathon & 10K offers runners a scenic journey through the lush Avondale Forest Park in Rathdrum, Ireland. Participants can immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the Wicklow countryside while supporting the important work of the Wicklow Hospice. This event is a must-do for runners seeking a picturesque and charitable running experience.
