Course

Run entirely on the Steven Burke Cycle Track at Barrowford, a flat sealed 1.1 km circuit, with the whole 5K start to finish on the track. The organiser bills it as one of the three fastest 5Ks in the UK. There are no entries on the day and no waiting list once it fills.

About

The Natterjack Barrowford Fast 5K takes place on the Steven Burke Cycle Track. This track is one of the flattest tracks in UK and the setting of previous UK road race records. This race is the perfect opportunity to push your limits, achieve personal best times and have the opportunity to compete with elite runners.
