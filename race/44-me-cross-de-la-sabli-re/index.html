About

This cross-country event starts at the Parc Forestier de Viroflay Rive Gauche, opposite the cemetery on Route du Pavé de Meudon, and sends runners along marked paths through the Versailles-Meudon state forest. Adults choose a short or long course, with shorter distances for children.
