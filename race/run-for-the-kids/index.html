About

The Run for the Kids is a community event in Melbourne, raising funds for the Royal Children's Hospital. This annual race brings together runners of all levels to support a worthy cause and experience the vibrant energy of the city. Participants can enjoy the scenic route while contributing to the well-being of local children.
