Course

An out-and-back trail half marathon starting at the Nower on the western edge of Dorking, climbing from the gun. The route takes footpaths and bridleways past Westcott, climbs Wolvens Lane to the Plough at Coldharbour, then ascends to Leith Hill Tower, the highest point in south-east England, before returning the same way. About 1,800 ft of climbing.

About

The Leith Hill Half Marathon is a cross-country race from Dorking to the top of the highest point in south-eastern England, Leith Hill - and back. This is a beautiful but challenging, hilly trail run in the Surrey Hills with 13.1 miles of trails and 1,800 feet of overall elevation.
