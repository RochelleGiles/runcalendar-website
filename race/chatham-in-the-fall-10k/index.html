Course

Uses the certified Chatham Harbor Run 10K route on Cape Cod. The start is about two tenths of a mile from Monomoy Regional Middle School, 425 Crowell Rd, Chatham, with a 9:00 am gun and awards in the school gymnasium. A 2 x 5K team relay is run over the same course.

About

Now in its 7th year, this Chatham race uses the certified Chatham Harbor Run 10K route on Cape Cod. Proceeds benefit Sharing Kindness and the WCOD Stuff-A-Bus toy drive, and finishers gather afterward at The Squire in downtown Chatham.
