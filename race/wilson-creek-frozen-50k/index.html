Course

About

The Wilson Creek Frozen 50k stages winter trail races — 50K, 20-mile and 10-mile — on the Wilson Creek trail system in Idaho's Owyhee foothills, 30 minutes south of Nampa. Courses cross the Reynolds Creek gorge along the China Ditch and climb to the summit of Wilson Peak in January conditions.
