About

The Trail Du Granit is part of the Trails Anjou - Logémaine Challenge, which aims to highlight local organizations, volunteers, remarkable sites, and geographical features of each region or municipality crossed, as well as the athletes, regardless of their level. The event offers trail running courses ranging from 8 to 34 km, united by the same spirit of opening up a living and running environment to both licensed and non-licensed runners.
