Course

Starts at Margaret McKenny Campground and follows the road to the Campground trail, then the McKenny Trail out to the Fall Creek Day Use Area, which is the 25K turnaround and a water-only aid station. The 50K continues on toward Capitol Peak before returning to finish at Margaret McKenny.
