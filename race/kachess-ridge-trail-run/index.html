Course

A point-to-point mountain trail race starting near Lake Kachess and finishing at Salmon La Sac. Runners climb to rolling ridgeline and alpine meadow, summit the Thorp Mountain Lookout at 5,853 ft, then drop into the Cooper River valley for the run out. Sections are steep and technical. Both a 50K and a 50 mile are offered.

About

A Central Cascades trail ultra out of Easton, Washington, with 50K and 50 Mile options on forested singletrack near Lake Kachess and the Snoqualmie Pass corridor, organized by Evergreen Trail Runs.
