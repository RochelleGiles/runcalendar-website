Course

Trail races run mostly on the well-maintained Speyside Way, an old railway line through the whisky region, with forest tracks, short sections of minor road and some hilly ground. All distances finish at Glenfiddich Distillery in Dufftown with staggered starts at different distilleries, and the final 7km from Craigellachie to Dufftown climbs gradually.

About

The Dramathon follows part of the Speyside Way through Moray, Scotland, past whisky distilleries along the River Spey to a finish in Aberlour. Choose the Wee Dram 10K, Half Dram, Full Dram marathon, or the We Dram relay.
