About

The Connor Luke Hayes Memorial 5K is an annual road race in Calhoun, Georgia, hosted by the CLStrong Foundation. It starts and finishes at the Calhoun City Recreation Department park, with a kids fun run following. Proceeds fund the foundation and raise awareness of childhood neuroblastoma.
