About

La Ronde des Vignobles is a festive 11km running and walking event through the beautiful wine region of Cuers. The course winds through vineyards and wine estates, featuring wine tastings, regional product sampling, and live music orchestras along the route. This unique event requires 100% of participants to wear costumes and focuses on fun rather than competition, with no timing or rankings.
