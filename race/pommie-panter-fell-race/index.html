Course

A 5.8-mile fell race with about 876 feet of climb, starting at Youlgrave Village Well on Moor Lane and finishing at The George on the main street. The ground is mixed and rough: trails, open fields, country lanes, a swamp, and limestone descents that turn slippery without warning. Only partially marked and marshalled, so some navigation is expected. Entry on the day is available.

About

The Pommie Panter Fell Race is a traditional Peak District running event that takes runners on a challenging 5.8-mile course with 876 feet of climb. The terrain includes rough trails, open fields, and a touch of country lane, with some technical sections and a swamp. The race is partially marked and marshalled, so local knowledge and navigation skills can be an advantage.
