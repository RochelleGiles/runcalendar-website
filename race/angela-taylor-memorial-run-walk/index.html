About

The Angela Taylor Memorial Run/Walk commemorates the life of Angela Rose Taylor who died in the 1986 Russell Street bombing. This community event welcomes runners and walkers of all ages and fitness levels, supporting Victoria Police Blue Ribbon Foundation's work in improving emergency facilities.
