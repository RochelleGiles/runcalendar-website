About

A unique winter trail running adventure in the stunning Mercantour region, offering two challenging routes for trail runners and hikers. Participants can compete in solo or duo categories, experiencing the beautiful snowy landscapes while embracing the values of trail running: mutual support, solidarity, and personal challenge.
