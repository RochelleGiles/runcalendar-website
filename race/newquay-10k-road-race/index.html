Course

A fast road 10K starting and finishing at Newquay Sports Centre at 9am. A hill comes just after the 1 mile marker, with a water station around 3 miles, after which the course is largely downhill or flat. A final climb arrives just before 6 miles and runs to the finish. Part of the Cornish Grand Prix Road Race Series.

About

The Newquay 10K Road Race is a fast, fairly flat course with one Cornish lane hill within the first mile. The race attracts over 700 runners and is part of the popular Cornish Grand Prix running series.
