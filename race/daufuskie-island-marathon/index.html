Course

Marathon and half tour Daufuskie Island from Haig Point, reached by ferry from Hilton Head, on a USATF-certified course past the Haig Point Lighthouse, Freeport, the Historical Society and Tabby Circle, with stretches overlooking the Atlantic. Marathon starts 8:30 a.m., half 9:30 a.m., with a 4:30 p.m. course close.

About

The Daufuskie Island Marathon and Half Marathon are run on a USATF-certified course on car-light Daufuskie Island, South Carolina, reached by the Haig Point ferry. The route passes the Haig Point Lighthouse, Freeport and the island's historic sites with Atlantic Ocean views, and both races must finish by 4:30 p.m.
