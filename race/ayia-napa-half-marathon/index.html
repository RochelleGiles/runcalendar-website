About

Ayia Napa Half Marathon is an open invitation to runners from all over the world to take part in a friendly yet amazing race. The course is relatively flat and fast, making it a great opportunity to set a personal record or run a fast time for your first half marathon. Runners can also discover one of the best holiday resorts with golden sands and scenic landscape.
