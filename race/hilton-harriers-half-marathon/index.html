Course

Tarred out-and-back road race starting and finishing at Grace College on Hilton College Road. The 21.1km runs out through Hilton Gardens and includes a 3km circuit inside the Hilton College grounds, with six refreshment stations; the 10km covers the first 5km of that route and returns. A separate 5km fun run is held entirely on forest trail.

About

A road race organised by Hilton Harriers Athletic Club in Hilton, a small town in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands, South Africa. It includes a 5km fun run, 10km and half marathon distances, drawing runners from across the Midlands region.
