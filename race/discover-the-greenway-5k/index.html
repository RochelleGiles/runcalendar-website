About

Discover the Greenway 5K+ is a 3.5-mile race run entirely along the Wolf River Greenway in Memphis, through landscapes the Wolf River Conservancy works to protect. The course passes under mature tree cover and alongside restored habitats in one of the city's most vital natural corridors.
