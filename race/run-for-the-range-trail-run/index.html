Course

Half marathon, 10K and 5K on the dirt roads and singletrack of the 1,500-acre Fall Creek Ranch outside Granbury. Rolling hills, creek crossings, native grassland, wooded stretches and overlooks make up about 220ft of elevation change on uneven natural terrain; trail shoes are recommended. Bulk water and hydration stations are set up for refills.

About

Run for the Range is a trail race on Fall Creek Ranch, a 1,500-acre conservation property near Granbury, Texas. Runners choose a half marathon, 10K or 5K over dirt ranch roads and singletrack, crossing creeks and native grassland. Entry proceeds support land conservation work on the ranch.
