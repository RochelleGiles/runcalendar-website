Course

Mountain course from Wandiligong traversing Mystic Mountain and Goldmine Ridge. The organiser describes single trail, steep trail, technical trail and no trail, with rocky escarpments, fern-lined spurs and hands-in-the-dirt scrambling; the Xtreme takes in four separate ridge ascents. Aid stations: four on the Xtreme, two on the Traverse, one on the Summit.

About

The Wandi Cross is a thrilling mountain running challenge set amidst the picturesque valley of Wandiligong, just minutes from the scenic town of Bright, Victoria. Runners will be treated to stunning natural landscapes as they test their endurance on this unique course. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Wandi Cross promises an unforgettable running adventure.
