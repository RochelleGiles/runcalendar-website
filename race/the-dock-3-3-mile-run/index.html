Course

A point-to-point 3.3 mile race on the eastern tip of Long Island, starting at the Montauk Post Office on South Euclid Avenue and finishing at The Dock Bar and Grill on West Lake Drive. There is no shuttle back to the start.

About

The Dock 3.3 Mile Run is an annual event in Montauk, New York that raises funds for the Montauk Food Pantry & Senior Center. Runners will start at the Montauk Post Office and finish at The Dock Bar & Grill, where they can enjoy refreshments and prizes.
