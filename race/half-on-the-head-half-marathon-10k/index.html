About

Explore the stunning Kerryhead loop in Ireland during the Half on the Head Half Marathon & 10K. This scenic event offers runners the choice of a half marathon or 10K course, both showcasing the breathtaking coastal views that make this region a runner's paradise. Whether you're looking for a challenging endurance test or a picturesque run, the Half on the Head has something for every level of runner.
