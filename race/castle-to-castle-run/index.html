About

Explore the rugged beauty of Northern Ireland's County Antrim on the Castle to Castle Run. This unique event offers both an Ultra Marathon and Marathon, allowing runners to experience the stunning landscapes and historic castles that dot the region. Challenge yourself on this unforgettable journey through the Emerald Isle.
