Course

Runs through Brasilia's monumental centre on courses the organisers describe as wide, flat and with minimal elevation change. The 5 km and 10 km go off on the Saturday evening, with the marathon starting at 5am and the half marathon at 6am on the Sunday.

About

The Maratona Monumental de Brasília offers marathon, half marathon, 10K, and 5K distances and is one of the largest road running events in Brazil and South America, drawing thousands of athletes from many countries. It holds the Silver Permit from the Brazilian Athletics Confederation, is an AIMS member, and carries World Athletics international endorsement.
