About

The Trail Des 3 Rochers is an off-road marathon event that takes runners through a challenging and scenic trail course. The event features multiple distance options, allowing participants to choose the challenge that best suits their fitness level. The course highlights the natural beauty of the region, offering runners a unique and immersive running experience.
