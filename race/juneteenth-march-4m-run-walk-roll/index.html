About

The Juneteenth March is an annual 4-mile run/walk/roll event in Central Park, New York City, commemorating the emancipation of African-American slaves through health, wellness, and economic initiatives. It starts at 10:00 AM from Terrace Drive near the Naumburg Bandshell, following a route around the Harlem Loop through Seneca Village.[1]
