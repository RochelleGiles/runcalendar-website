About

The Toy Drop 5K, 10K and 15K in Loveland, Colorado is held in partnership with Realities for Children, with participants encouraged to donate a toy along the run through the city's roads and bike paths. Realities for Children serves kids in Larimer County who have suffered abuse or neglect, or are at risk, distributing donations to dozens of partner agencies.
