Course

Team relay in which one runner per team is on course at a time, rotating through three trails of 6 km, 9 km and 12 km. Based at Kangaroobie, Princetown, over private land — farmland paddocks and bush and forest trails — and run across two days including a night shift. Teams of four to eight; roughly 54 km per runner and 216 km per team.

About

The Kangaroobie Base Camp Ultra Relay is an intimate, team-based running event held on private land near Princetown, Victoria. Runners take on 6, 9, and 12km laps through muddy paddocks and magical forest trails, sometimes led by a kelpie or dodging cows and a bull. Between runs, teams support each other with food, rest, and camaraderie. It's an adventure and quirky challenge that feels like being invited to a community Christmas party.
