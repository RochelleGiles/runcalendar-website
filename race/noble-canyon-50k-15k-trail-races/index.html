Course

Trail races starting and finishing at 8631 Pine Creek Road, Pine Valley in the Laguna Mountains east of San Diego. The route is largely runnable singletrack through Noble Canyon with sustained climbs and long views. Aid stations are provided on course.

About

The Noble Canyon 50K and 15K run through Cleveland National Forest outside Pine Valley in the Laguna Mountains, following the singletrack Noble Canyon Trail through pine forest and high-desert chaparral.
