About

The East Yorkshire Half Marathon & 10K offer a scenic, closed-road course starting and finishing at the historic Burton Constable Hall in Skirlaugh. Runners can enjoy a flat, fast route through the picturesque East Yorkshire countryside, making this an ideal event for both experienced half-marathoners and 10K enthusiasts.
