Course

All three courses start and finish at the Diamond in the centre of Hollidaysburg and stay within the borough limits. Runners head out along Allegheny Street and return on Walnut Street through the historic district. The 6K and 10K add loops past Legion Park, the high school, Tiger Stadium and the junior high.

About

The Hollidaysburg Diamond Dash is a community running and walking event hosted by the Blair Regional YMCA, starting at the Hollidaysburg Diamond. It features scenic courses through historic Hollidaysburg Borough, including landmarks like Legion Park, schools, and the Blair County Courthouse.
