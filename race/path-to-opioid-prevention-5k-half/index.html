Course

The 5K starts and finishes at Riverdale City Offices, running out along Weber River Drive onto the riverside path to a footbridge and back. The half marathon starts in Uintah, covers about three miles of city streets, then joins the Centennial Trail along the Weber River Parkway to Fort Buenaventura Park and back. A water station sits on Parker Drive.

About

The Path to Opioid Prevention is a half marathon and 5K race held to educate on preventing opioid and substance misuse. Both races start and finish at Riverdale City Offices, with the half marathon bussed to Uintah and run on Weber Pathways trails through scenic hills.
