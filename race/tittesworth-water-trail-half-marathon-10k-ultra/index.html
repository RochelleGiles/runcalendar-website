Course

Three trail routes of 10K, 22K and 50K around Tittesworth Reservoir and the southern Peak District, over rugged trails and moorland tracks with punchy climbs and wide hill views. Courses are marked with trail flags, tags and arrows, with marshals at major junctions and several aid stations on route; GPX files are issued for self-navigation. All three finish beside the water.

About

Explore the stunning South Peak District at the Tittesworth Water Trail Half Marathon, 10K & Ultra. Runners will tackle challenging, scenic courses that wind through the beautiful Tittesworth Water nature reserve in the United Kingdom. This event offers a range of distances to suit runners of all abilities, from the half marathon to the grueling ultra.
