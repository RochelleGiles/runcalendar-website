Course

Flat, fast, USATF-certified courses starting and finishing at Webber Park in Minneapolis and running along Victory Memorial Drive. The 10K goes at 8:00 AM, the 5K at 9:30 AM and a half-mile kids race at 10:20 AM, with a double-header option covering both. Strollers are not allowed in the 10K.

About

A Labor Day morning race series on Victory Memorial Drive in north Minneapolis, starting and finishing at Webber Park. Runners can enter a flat USATF-certified 5K or 10K, take on both as a double header, or bring children for the half-mile kids race.
