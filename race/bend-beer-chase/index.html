Course

A roughly 57-mile team relay of 12 legs, each four to eight miles, starting and finishing at Drake Park in downtown Bend. The route heads out toward Redmond over a mix of trails and rural back roads before returning. Breweries host the leg exchange points and the finish is at a riverside festival.

About

The Bend Beer Chase is a roughly 57-mile team relay in Bend, Oregon, run over 12 legs by teams of one to six runners. The course starts and finishes at Drake Park and links trails with rural roads, with breweries hosting the exchange points.
