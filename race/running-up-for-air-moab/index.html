Course

Repeating loop from the Moab Rim Trailhead on Kane Creek Boulevard. Runners climb the rocky singletrack Stairmaster Trail, about 0.95 miles, and descend the Moab Rim Trail, a slickrock 4WD track of similar length, completing as many loops as possible within their time window. One aid station at the start and finish, reached roughly every two miles.
