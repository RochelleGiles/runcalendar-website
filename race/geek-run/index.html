Course

A 5km loop along the river through shady forest in the City of Norwood, Payneham & St Peters, starting and finishing at the Drage Reserve car park in Felixstow. Shorter distances cover part of the loop and longer distances repeat it. There is one aid station on course, plus a bag drop. The run starts at 4pm and walkers are accepted.

About

The Geek Run is a unique evening running event celebrating sci-fi culture and the inner geek in all participants. Set along a scenic river course in the City of Norwood, Payneham, & St Peters, the event offers multiple distance options and a fun, inclusive atmosphere for runners and walkers alike.
