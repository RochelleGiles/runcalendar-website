Course

A fast, flat course on the tarmac roads of the Snetterton motor racing circuit in Norfolk, looping the track itself. Both the 10k and the 5k use the same circuit, run in the evening with the 10k off at 18:50 and the 5k at 19:00.

About

Run Snetterton 10K & 5K, organised by Good Running Events, welcomes runners of all abilities to loop the iconic Snetterton Race Track in Norfolk on its flat tarmac surface. It's a fast course well suited to chasing a personal best, held on a weekday evening.
