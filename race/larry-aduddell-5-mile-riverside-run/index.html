Course

A five-mile point-to-point on the sealed River Parks trail system beside the Arkansas River. The course starts on the Jenks Pedestrian Bridge and runs north along the trail to finish at 56th Street. One water stop sits near halfway, with water, Gatorade and fruit at the finish.

About

The Larry Aduddell 5-Mile Riverside Run is a 5-mile road race in Jenks, Oklahoma, following the Arkansas River near the city's Riverwalk district. Jenks sits along the river just south of Tulsa. The race is a local spring tradition drawing runners from the Tulsa metro area.
