Course

A repeating 5K cross-country loop at Citronelle, Alabama, run twice for the 10K, five times for the 25K and ten times for the 50K. The loop takes in woods, meadows, creek crossings and hills.

About

The Puppy Creek Challenge is a trail race through rural Citronelle, Alabama, with 10K, 25K and 50K distances on wooded trails near Puppy Creek, organized with support from Port City Pacers.
