About

The Bay City Fraternal Order of Police Lodge 103 & Consumers Energy proudly presents the 7th annual Runnin' with the Law Race Series. Participate in one of the many run/walks through the nature trail of Hampton Twp. Stick around after for the Public Safety Showcase where you can meet & greet with local Law Enforcement and local Businesses/Sponsors. Proceeds benefit the Fraternal Order of Police Lodge 103
