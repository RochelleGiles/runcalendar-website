Course

A single 2-mile loop entirely inside Mehaffey Park in Loveland. The first 50 feet or so are on grass before the route joins the park's paved paths, which carry the rest of the way. Start and finish are both within the park.

About

Come join us on Thanksgiving morning for a 2-mile fun run! The event will be held at Mehaffey Park. We will have a fun course, music, vendors, and more! Come dressed in your best Turkey Day outfit. Every participant will receive a memorable campfire mug and bib. Shirts can be added on during registration. This event will benefit KidsPak. We will be accepting food and money donations to help fill their shelves and a portion of the proceeds will go to them too.
