About

The Mongolie Trail Adventure is a two-week sports, cultural, and emotional adventure through the stunning landscapes of Mongolia. Participants will have the opportunity to discover the steppes, the Gobi Desert, and the Naadam Festival, one of the country's most important cultural events. The event features 8 stages of trail running or hiking, allowing participants to immerse themselves in the breathtaking scenery at their own pace.
