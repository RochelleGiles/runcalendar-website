Course

Courses run around Rampart Reservoir near Woodland Park, mixing single-track with forest roads at up to 9,500 ft, with views of Pikes Peak and the Front Range. The 55 mile climbs Rampart Range Road then loops the reservoir, the 60K is point-to-point, and the 50K, 24K and 15K are out-and-backs. Start and finish are at Buffalo Lodge Bicycle Resort.

About

We're having a Ram Party! Come party with us.
