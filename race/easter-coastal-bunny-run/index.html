About

Hop along the picturesque Semaphore coastline in South Australia at the Easter Coastal Bunny Run. This fun-filled event invites runners of all ages to don their best bunny ears and enjoy scenic ocean views while celebrating the spring season. Whether you're looking for a family-friendly 5K or a more challenging 10K, this coastal race is sure to delight runners of all levels.
