Whistler Half Marathon

Whistler, British Columbia · Canada
TrailMountain32KM · Half Marathon · 10KM · 5KMEntries open Fri 1 Jan 2027

Lost Lake gravel and the Valley Trail under the Coast Mountains: a half, a 32K, a 10K and a 5K from Whistler Olympic Plaza on the first Saturday of June.

Quick verdict

A mountain-resort half on soft ground. Three-quarters of the course is smooth, non-technical gravel around Lost Lake, the rest paved Valley Trail, with 295 m of climb spread through rolling park trails. You start and finish in Whistler Olympic Plaza with the peaks in view. 2026 sold out.

Course at a glance

7:45 amStart
4 h 30Course open
Olympic PlazaStart & finish

From Olympic Plaza onto the Valley Trail and into Lost Lake Park, where the gravel trails roll and twist for most of the distance before the paved trail brings you back into the village. The 32K takes the same ground further, adding short punchy climbs and singletrack for 700 m of gain, with Rainbow Mountain and Mount Currie ahead of you.

Three things to know

  1. Entries open on 1 January at 7 am Pacific, and 2026 sold out. The first hundred in each distance pay the early price.
  2. Bib pick-up is on the Friday afternoon in Olympic Plaza. Your entry plants a tree, gives $5 to Crohn's and Colitis Canada, and gets you a locally designed medal and a post-race massage.
  3. Transfers to another runner or another distance are free until late May. Withdrawing refunds half, unless you took the refund protection when you entered.

Early June in the valley: a cool start near 10°C, warming towards the low 20s by the time the half finishes, and rain on the trails a real possibility.

Worth the trip?

Yes, for a half on gravel under the Coast Mountains with the village at your feet afterwards. Soft ground and rolling climb make it a day to enjoy rather than a road PB; come for the 32K if you want the mountain views earned.
