About

The 1 North Trail Run is the first event held at 1 North Adventure Trails in Blanchard, LA, featuring boundary roads, jeep trails, and freshly cut paths. Participants can choose between 3 mile and 5 mile trail races starting at 8:30 a.m., with post-race burgers and awards for overall and age group winners.
