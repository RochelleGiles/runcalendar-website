Course

Half marathon starting on Thames Street in downtown Bristol, running past historic homes, the town common and the America's Cup museum before following the coastline through Roger Williams University and finishing on the water at Independence Park. Mostly sealed, with some unpaved shell paths along the shore. A two-person relay splits the course at the university.

About

The Independence Rhode Race is a half marathon and two-person relay held in Bristol, Rhode Island around the Fourth of July. The course winds through the historic seaside town, home to the nation's oldest continuous Independence Day celebration.
