Course

Traffic-free, flat marathon on the footpath and cycle track along the River Exe and the Exeter Canal, starting at 8:45am. The course is measured and certified and carries London Marathon Good for Age qualification. A Community 5 Mile starts at 9:00am and runs along Exeter Quayside and through Riverside Valley Park to the same finish at Haven Banks.

About

The Exeter Live Better Marathon & Community 5 Mile, organized by the Exeter City Community Trust, offers runners a unique opportunity to explore the historic city of Exeter, United Kingdom. Participants can challenge themselves on the 26.2-mile marathon course or opt for the more community-focused 5-mile run, both showcasing the city's beautiful landmarks and scenic countryside. This event is a must-do for runners seeking a memorable running experience.
