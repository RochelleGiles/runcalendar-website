About

The GO2 Dallas 5K Walk/Run is held at Reverchon Park and on the Katy Trail in Dallas, Texas, as part of the GO2 for Lung Cancer national series. A kids dash, music and family activities run alongside the 5K, and all ages and abilities are welcome.
