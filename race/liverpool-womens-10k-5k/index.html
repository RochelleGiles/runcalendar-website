Course

A women-only 10K and 5K run entirely within and around Sefton Park in south Liverpool. The 5K starts at 9:30am and the 10K at 10:30am, both chip-timed and marshalled by an all-female team. The route stays inside the park, so the surrounding drives are not closed to traffic.

About

Liverpool Women's 10K & 5K takes place in Sefton Park, a Victorian park on Merseyside, with the 5K starting first followed by the 10K. It is organised by BTR Liverpool.
