About

Explore the picturesque Lake Wendouree Gardens in Ballarat at the Memory Walk & Jog, a family-friendly event combining fitness and fundraising. Lace up your running shoes and join the community for a morning of camaraderie and scenic routes, all while supporting a meaningful cause. This event offers an engaging way to stay active and make a difference.
