About

The Lions Club Vision 5K is organised by the West Iredell Lions Club in Statesville, North Carolina, pairing a 5K run with a 1 mile vision walk from Simonton Road. Entry fees fund eye exams and glasses for Iredell County residents and support programmes for the visually impaired.
