About

The Strides for Safety 5K is a run/walk event held at Kestrel Point Shelter in Winton Woods Park to raise awareness for children with food allergies. Participants enjoy a scenic 3.1-mile course through trails, lake views, and forest, with awards for top finishers and the most spirited food allergy family.
