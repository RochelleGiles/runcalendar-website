About

Join us for an extraordinary 5K adventure through scenic trails where every step counts in our fight against childhood cancer. Inspired by Marshall Staff's courageous journey, this heartwarming 5k adventure combines the thrill of trail running with a powerful purpose. Whether you're a seasoned runner or weekend warrior, your participation helps illuminate the path toward better treatments and brighter futures for young warriors battling cancer. Together, we'll create footprints of change and memories that last a lifetime.
