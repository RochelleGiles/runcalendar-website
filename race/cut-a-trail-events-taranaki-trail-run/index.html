Course

Run entirely off-road in Lake Mangamahoe Forest near New Plymouth, using forestry tracks, walking and bridle tracks, mountain bike trails and gravel roads. The half marathon is one full lap and the quarter marathon covers the first half of it; a 5 km option shares the same forest trails.

About

Cut a Trail Events is dedicated to providing Taranaki and beyond with trail running and walking events. Hosted and organised by two New Plymouth Boys' High School teachers, it showcases the region's natural environment for participants.
