Course

Coastal road half marathon starting and finishing at Misquamicut, running out through the Watch Hill neighbourhoods and Weekapaug before returning along the shoreline. A two-person relay splits the same course into 5.1 and 8 mile legs, and the flat 5K runs out and back along Winnapaug Pond.

About

The Surftown Half Marathon, Relay and 5K starts in Westerly, Rhode Island, a beach town on the state's southern shore known locally as Surftown. Runners can choose the half marathon solo, split it as a two-person relay, or race the 5K.
