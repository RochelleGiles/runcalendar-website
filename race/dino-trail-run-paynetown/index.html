Course

The 15K runs a single loop on the Pate Hollow Trail beside Paynetown State Recreation Area, rugged and remote singletrack through Hoosier National Forest with lake views and sustained climbing. The 5K uses a shorter section of the same trail. The organiser rates it 4 of 5 for both hills and surface difficulty.

About

DINO Trail Run - Paynetown is a trail race in Bloomington, Indiana, held at Paynetown State Recreation Area on the shore of Lake Monroe. Runners choose the 5K or 15K distance through the park's wooded singletrack.
