Course

Three road races over the May bank holiday weekend in three Mid Sussex towns: a 10 mile at East Grinstead, a 10 mile at Haywards Heath and a 10K at Burgess Hill. Runners can enter any one race or all three for the combined marathon-distance total. Family fun runs and a chip-timed one mile run alongside.

About

The Mid Sussex Marathon Weekend offers an unforgettable running experience, with a thrilling mixed-terrain event over three days. Runners can tackle the full marathon distance or choose from individual town races, catering to all levels from first-timers to seasoned marathoners. The event is set against the beautiful landscapes and vibrant community atmosphere of the Mid Sussex region.
