About

Keeping with NEWBSANITY core values, the Extreme Ravine will be the gnarliest 5K you have ever done! We will also feature 15K and 12 hour divisions that will complete multiple laps of the 5K course. The course is laid out on the southern trail system of the NEWBSANITY Adventure Center. Expect radical elevation changes on varying terrain, creek crossings, swamps, rocks, roots, ruts, and oh, did we mention extreme elevation changes?
