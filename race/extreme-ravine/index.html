Course

About

Extreme Ravine is a trail race at the Newbsanity Adventure Center near Binghamton, New York. Runners choose a single-lap 5K, a three-lap 15K or a 12-hour division on the same loop. The course crosses creeks, swamp and rocky, rooted ground with repeated sharp elevation change.
