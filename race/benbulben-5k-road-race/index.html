About

The Benbulben 5K Road Race in County Sligo, Ireland offers runners a scenic, flat, and fast course with the iconic Benbulben mountain as a stunning backdrop. Hosted by Sligo Athletic Club, this picturesque event is a must for those seeking a scenic road race experience.
