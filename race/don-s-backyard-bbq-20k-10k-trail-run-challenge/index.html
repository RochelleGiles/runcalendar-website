Course

Run on the groomed trails of Fort Yargo State Park over rolling hills, starting and finishing at Entrance B, 210 South Broad Street, Winder. The 20K is four separate 5K segments, each capped at 45 minutes with recovery time between; the 10K is two such segments. The field is capped at 225.

About

We're excited to present the 6th Annual Don's Backyard BBQ 20K/10K Trail Run Challenge at Fort Yargo State Park (Entrance B)! Your Race Registration will include (but not limited to) a BBQ Meal, Beverage, and a Super Soft Tri-Blend Polyester Shirt (Along with an Unforgettable Trail Running Experience)!
