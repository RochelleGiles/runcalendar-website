About

The Banja Luka Marathon is an exciting running event that combines the spirit of determination, endurance, and community. Runners will embark on an extraordinary journey that goes beyond the typical racing experience, becoming part of the legacy and true heroes of Banja Luka. The course takes runners through the historic city center and along the captivating Vrbas River, offering an ideal setting for personal records with its exceptionally flat profile.
