Course

Mountain trail races starting and finishing at the Cody Archery Range, about ten minutes east of Cody off Highway 14. Distances are 1 mile, 6 miles, 16 miles and 50K. The short courses are flowy with hill climbing; the 16 mile is hilly throughout and the 50K adds a more mellow second half. Conditions can include snow, and bear spray is advised.
