About

The Grand Canyon of the South Ultras take place at Breaks Interstate Park, where the Russell Fork River has carved the deepest gorge east of the Mississippi. Runners choose a 50K or a series of 10K loops through the rugged canyon terrain on the Virginia-Kentucky border.
