About

The Running For Warriors is a 5K event organized by Veterans Freedom Shows and Events, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit supporting veteran causes like Central Valley Honor Flight and Wreaths Across America. Participants can join competitive and fun runs to honor veterans while enjoying a community-focused race day.
