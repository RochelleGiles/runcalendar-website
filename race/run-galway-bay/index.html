Course

Starts at Nimmo's Pier in the Claddagh, Galway, and follows the flat coastline of Galway Bay out through Salthill and Blackrock and back. Marathon, half marathon and 10K start at 08:30, 09:30 and 11:30 respectively, with water stations on course and at each finish.

About

Run Galway Bay is the largest multi-distance running event in Galway City. The flat course takes in the fabulous coastline of Galway Bay from The Claddagh to Salthill, Blackrock and back, offering breathtaking views of the Clare mountains and the wild Atlantic Ocean.
