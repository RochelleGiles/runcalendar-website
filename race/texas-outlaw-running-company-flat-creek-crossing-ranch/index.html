Course

All distances start and finish at Flat Creek Crossing Ranch, Johnson City, on a 15.5-mile loop of dirty, rocky single and wide track. Each loop climbs about 1,400ft; the 100K runs four laps and the 50K two. Aid sits at miles 7.7, 10.8, 12.2, 13.7 and 15.5, with the first 7.8 miles remote and unsupported.

About

The Texas Outlaw Running Company event at Flat Creek Crossing Ranch offers a variety of distances, including a 5K, 12K, 25K, 50K, and 100K. The course features flat, rocky, and single-track trails with an elevation gain of 1400ft for the 15-mile loop. Participants can expect historic average temperatures of 65-84 degrees.
