Course

All four distances start and finish at the Smith Wellness Center on the Iowa Lakes Community College campus on College Drive, Emmetsburg. The routes run around or close to Five Island Lake, with a 1.7-mile fun run, 5K, 10K and half marathon on offer.

About

Take part in the 46th Annual Smith Wellness Center O'Round the Loch Run. Whether you are wanting to run for fun, or want to finish first - the O'Round the Loch Run is your place to kick-off your running season. Held each year in conjunction with the Emmetsburg St. Patrick's Day Celebration you are sure to find a good time.
