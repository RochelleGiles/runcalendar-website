Course

All three races start and finish near the Wellsville City Tabernacle at 100 E 100 S in Wellsville, Utah, run on Labor Day morning. The event offers a 10K, a 5K and a Kids K, with same-day registration available on race morning.

About

This legendary race is the unofficial kickoff to Wellsville's Founders Day Celebration, and it's been bringing the community together for nearly 50 years of fun, fitness, and tradition. With around 500 runners showing up strong, it's one of the valley's favorite ways to celebrate!
