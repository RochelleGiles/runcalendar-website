About

The Salisbury 10 Mile is the flagship event of the City of Salisbury Athletics & Running Club, offering a challenging yet picturesque route through the historic city and surrounding countryside. Runners can look forward to a scenic tour of Salisbury's iconic landmarks and rolling hills, making this a must-do event for any distance enthusiast in the United Kingdom.
