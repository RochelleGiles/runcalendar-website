Course

A point-to-point traverse along the ridge line of the Bridger range, described by the organiser as one of the most technical trail runs in the United States. It involves sustained climbing and descending, unstable footing and miles of exposure along the ridge, with weather that can turn quickly. The field is capped at 250 runners.
