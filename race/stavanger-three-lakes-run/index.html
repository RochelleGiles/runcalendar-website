About

The Stavanger Three Lakes Run is one of Norway's coolest running events. Participants get to experience the excitement of running with over 5,200 others, following skilled pacers to help them maintain the right pace. The scenic trail takes runners on a sightseeing tour of some of Stavanger's best offerings, with beautiful lakes, varied terrain, and a lively atmosphere with music and cheering crowds.
