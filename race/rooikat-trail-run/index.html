Course

Held at Entle Private Game Reserve in the Elands River Valley near Gqeberha. Three routes of 8km, 12km and 20km run on flowing single track through the reserve, taking in sustained climbs, descents and river crossings. Runners share the reserve with game.

About

Rooikat Trail Run is a trail running event near Gqeberha in the Eastern Cape, organised by Big O Event Solutions. Distances on offer are 8km, 12km and 20km.
