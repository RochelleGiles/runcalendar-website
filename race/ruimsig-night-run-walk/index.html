About

Get ready to experience Ruimsig after dark. The Ruimsig Night Run / Walk brings runners and walkers together for an evening of movement, community and energy under the lights at Ruimsig Country Club. Whether you are chasing the 5 km, taking on the 10 km, or simply looking to get some kilometres in, the route is open to everyone. Headlamp on, the 10 km starts at 19:00 and the 5 km at 19:05, with registration from 17:00. One of the In-Reach night run series, hosted monthly at Steyn City, Ruimsig, Eagle Canyon and Jackal Creek.
