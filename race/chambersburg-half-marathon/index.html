Course

Start and finish are both at CAMS South Middle School, 1151 E McKinley St, Chambersburg. The course is run entirely on roads, described by the organiser as curvy and hilly, and is open to traffic throughout. It is USATF certified. There is no race-day entry, and strollers, pets and in-ear listening devices are not permitted.

About

The Chambersburg Half Marathon is a hilly, curvy road race starting and finishing at CAMS South Middle School in Chambersburg, Pennsylvania, organized by the Chambersburg Road Runners Club.
