Course

Start and finish at Tom Bennett Park, 400 Cypress Creek Boulevard, Bradenton, Florida. The 5K loops around the park's ponds on mixed terrain of grass, crushed shell and asphalt, with one water station on route. A 0.25 km fun run is held alongside it.

About

This leg of the RUN Manatee 5K series, presented by RWP Runs, is held at Tom Bennett Park in Bradenton. The series takes runners through parks, beaches, and preserves across Manatee County.
