Course

Both races start and finish at Little Pine State Park, Waterville. Routes use the Mid State, Spiked Buck, Tiadaghton, Pesto and Panther Run trails, with the 25K adding the Torbert Trail, and both distances finish along Panther Run. The 25K starts at 9:00am and the 10K at 9:30am, with bib pickup from 7:30am at the main pavilion.

About

The event will be held in mid-October, right at the peak of fall foliage. Both courses will use the Mid State, Spiked Buck, Tiadaghton, Pesto, Panther Run and of course, the infamous Torbert Trail to keep things entertaining. To reward runners after Torbert, we'll be sending you down one of the nicest downhill runs to the finish on Panther Run. Just a reminder that these are not beginner courses. Please train accordingly as the 25K has over 5000 feet of vertical and the 50K has about 8000. Cut-offs are in place to ensure a daylight finish.
