About

Loop The Hooch is a 50k/25k running race featuring a winding 5 kilometer gravel/dirt surface loop along the Chattahoochee River at Chattahoochee Pointe Park. The fast and flat course is ideal for personal records and first-time ultramarathoners, with options for solo participants or relay teams of up to 5 runners.
