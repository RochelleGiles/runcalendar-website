About

Welcome to the Swifty Fun Run! It is a themed running event where fans and fitness enthusiasts come together to run and celebrate the amazing person and music of Taylor Swift. This event offers a range of distances to suit every participant's level of fitness and ambition: a 1-mile jog/walk, 5K, 10K or Half Marathon.
