Course

A one mile run at the Nebraska State Capitol on South 13th Street in Lincoln, with parking in the lot south of the Capitol. It is run in six heats split by grade and gender, covering second grade and under, third and fourth grade, and fifth and sixth grade. Start and finish points are not described.

About

Known as midwest America's largest children's run, the Mayor's Run is a 1-mile kids' race held in Lincoln, Nebraska. The event gives away 30 bikes and donates $1 from every entry to a local charity.
