Course

A multi-race road series run by Memphis Runners Track Club across Memphis-area parks and streets, with paired races at each distance. Venues include Cancer Survivors Park in East Memphis, W.J. Freeman Park in Bartlett, Shelby Farms Park, Meeman-Shelby Forest State Park and downtown Memphis. Most races start at 7am.

About

The Lane Purser Memorial Road Race Series is a season-long set of road races around Memphis, Tennessee, offering distances from the 5K to the half marathon across several race days spread through the year for local runners.
