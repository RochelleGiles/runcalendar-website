About

The Joe Koziarz 5K Run/Walk is a long-standing event that benefits local organizations such as the Southampton Town PAL and the Koziarz Hurricane Scholarship Fund. The race takes place in the picturesque town of Westhampton Beach and features a variety of race distances for runners and walkers of all levels.
