About

Ida Ultra is a challenging running event in the beautiful and rugged Kaz Mountains of Turkey. The course takes runners through olive groves and scenic villages, offering a unique experience to explore this stunning region. With several distance options, the event caters to both seasoned ultrarunners and those seeking a memorable adventure.
