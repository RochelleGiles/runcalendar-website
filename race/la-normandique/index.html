About

Explore the picturesque landscapes of Normandy at the annual La Normandique running event. This scenic race takes participants on a journey through the region's charming villages and rolling hills, offering a unique challenge for runners of all levels. With a stunning backdrop of historic landmarks, La Normandique promises an unforgettable experience for endurance enthusiasts.
