Course

Two short, steep trail courses at Robinette's Trails in Grand Rapids, Michigan, starting at 9:00am: Short & Brutal (3.1 miles) or Long & Difficult (3.4 miles). Runners pick one course on entry; the organisers bill it as the toughest 5K in the country.

About

The Dirty Duel is the only trail race in the U.S. that offers you a choice of courses: Short & Brutal (3.1 miles/5K) or Long & Difficult (3.4 miles). Choose your own adventure!
