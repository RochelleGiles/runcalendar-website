About

The Hillbilly Half Race Series is run during the Hillbilly Days festival in downtown Pikeville, Kentucky, offering 5K, 10K and half marathon distances alongside a free kids fun run. The 10K covers city streets while the half marathon extends onto a wide paved and gravel path. Proceeds go to Run for the Kids.
