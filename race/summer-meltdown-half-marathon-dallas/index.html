About

The Summer Meltdown Half Marathon - Dallas is part of a multi-city race series held across Texas and Oklahoma. The Irving event offers a half marathon, quarter marathon, 5K, and 1-mile option, all starting and finishing within the same morning window, with a bundle discount for runners doing multiple cities.
