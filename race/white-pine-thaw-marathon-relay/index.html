Course

An out-and-back on the White Pine Trail, starting and finishing at Vitale's Restaurant in Comstock Park, Michigan. The route runs north through Belmont and Rockford to a turnaround near Russell Road by Cedar Springs. The trail is not closed to traffic and crosses public roads, most of them quiet. Water and toilets are at the three exchange zones.

About

The White Pine Thaw Marathon Relay will be Sunday, March 22nd, starting in Comstock Park, Michigan, on the southern most section of the Fred Meijer White Pine Trail. It's held annually on the second Sunday of March. Competitors form teams of runners and on race day, will each take a leg of the relay, covering 13.1 miles, north to Russel Road, where the course returns back south to Comstock Park.
