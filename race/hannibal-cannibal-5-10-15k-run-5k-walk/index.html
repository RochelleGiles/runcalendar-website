Course

Out-and-back on closed roads near the river, with runners keeping to the right-hand lane. All 5K, 10K and 15K runners climb Lover's Leap, a steep hill; the 15K adds a side trip up it plus a timing gate at Mark Twain Cave. Water at the 5K and 10K turnarounds, the top of Lover's Leap and the finish, with first aid at the Leap summit and the finish.

About

The Hannibal Cannibal is a beloved annual running event that brings the community together to promote a healthy lifestyle. The event features a variety of race distances, catering to runners of all levels.
