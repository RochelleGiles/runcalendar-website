Course

Trail courses in the woods at Camp Hebron, mixing flat, fast singletrack with rolling hills, a minor stream crossing, rocks and roots and one hard climb, for about 360ft of gain. Both the 5K and 10K are run, and a 5K hike option is offered; the finish is followed by the camp's baked oatmeal, bacon and buns with fruit and drinks.

About
