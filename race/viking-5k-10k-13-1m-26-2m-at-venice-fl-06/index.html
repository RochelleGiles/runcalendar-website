Course

An out-and-back at Shamrock Park, Venice, on a mix of concrete and asphalt. The 5K and 10K run a 1.56-mile out-and-back once and twice; the half marathon and marathon run a 1.64-mile out-and-back four and eight times. All start at 8:00am with a six-hour limit.
