Course

A point-to-point 50 miler on the Knobstone Trail, Indiana's longest footpath, running north from Deam Lake State Park to Delaney Creek Park. Narrow winding singletrack over rocky ridges and through hardwood forest, with steep ascents, technical descents and several creek crossings.

About

Knobstone 50 is a 50-mile trail ultramarathon in Borden, Indiana, run on the Knobstone Trail, widely regarded as the most rugged footpath in the state. The course follows its steep, wooded ridgelines through southern Indiana's hill country.
