Course

The 5K is a certified rolling course starting on the roads in Pickens and spending its final 1.5 miles on the Doodle Trail, finishing at Pickens Doodle Trail Park. The 15K starts separately in Easley outside the Law Enforcement Center and runs the rail-trail corridor toward Pickens. No aid station detail is published.

About

The Doodle Trail 5k and 15k Challenge is held at Pickens Doodle Park in downtown Pickens, SC, featuring a certified 5k loop on a rolling course and a 15k challenge starting in Easley. It serves as the final race of the Pickens County 5k Series with awards for overall, masters, and age groups.
