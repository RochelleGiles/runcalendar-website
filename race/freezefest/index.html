About

The Freezefest 5K race has become a traditional "must run" event for Eastern Iowans. If you are ready, shake off the cabin fever and get out and hit the roads for a fun 5K. There's a post-race social immediately following the run at the Prairie Oak Lodge. Stick around for lots of food, music, your favorite beverages, and door prizes.
