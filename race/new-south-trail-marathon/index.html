Course

Run through the trail system at the US National Whitewater Center in Charlotte, with the marathon doubling the half marathon loop. Aid stations at roughly 4.2, 8.1 and 11.2 miles plus the start/finish. Trails stay open to other runners, hikers and bikers throughout.

About

A trail running event offering full marathon (26.2 miles) and half marathon (13.1 miles) options on the Whitewater Center's trail system in Charlotte, NC. The course is 100% unpaved trails with moderate elevation gain.
