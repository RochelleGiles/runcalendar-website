Course

A six-mile out-and-back that drops into the New River Gorge and back out. Starting on the south rim on Fayette Station Road, runners descend Burma and Ames Heights roads about 800 feet, cross the New River at the bottom, then climb 800 feet to the far rim, with a steepest pitch around 20%. A short trail section leads to a finish overlook at Adventures on the Gorge.

About

A 6-mile race in the New River Gorge near Fayetteville, West Virginia, running from one rim of the gorge to the other through New River Gorge National Park and Preserve each May, crossing some of the park's rugged terrain.
