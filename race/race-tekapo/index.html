Course

Trail racing on the shores of Lake Tekapo/Takapō in the Mackenzie Basin, with seven distances from a 2 km kids dash to a 50 km ultra. The 50 km starts at 6am under the dark skies of the Aoraki Mackenzie International Dark Sky Reserve, with the lake and the surrounding snow-capped ranges in view for much of the course.

About

The Merrell Race Tekapo returns to the stunning setting of Lake Tekapo/Takapō in New Zealand, offering runners a range of distances from a 50km ultra to a 2km kids' race. With breathtaking mountain and lake views, this event is a must-do for runners seeking a scenic and challenging experience.
