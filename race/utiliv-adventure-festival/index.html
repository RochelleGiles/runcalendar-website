About

The Utiliv Adventure Festival is a unique event held in the stunning Faroe Islands, a paradise for trail runners and adventure-seekers. The race routes will take you through the wild and wind-swept islands, never more than 4km from the sparkling Atlantic Ocean. Participants will have the opportunity to explore the best of the 18 islands, experiencing the breathtaking natural beauty, welcoming local culture, and a range of adventure activities.
