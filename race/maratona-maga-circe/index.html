Course

Point-to-point coastal marathon: the 42.195 km and 29 km start at the port in San Felice Circeo and run the scenic shoreline to finish in Piazza del Comune in Sabaudia. The 10.6 km and a 2.5 km family walk start and finish in that same square. The course is certified and carries World Athletics Bronze label status.

About

The Maratona Maga Circe is organized by the In Corsa Libera association under FIDAL, with the patronage of the municipalities of Sabaudia and San Felice Circeo, and is now past its 6th running. The certified course is part of the World Athletics Global Calendar and the FIDAL national calendar as a Bronze Label race.
