Course

A chip-timed, partially road-closed course beside the River Thames, starting and finishing at Cowey Sale Car Park at Walton-on-Thames. The route mixes road and riverside path, heading east along the southern side of Desborough Island under Walton Bridge before returning to the finish arch. Half marathon at 09:00, 10K at 10:00, 5K at 11:15 and a junior race at noon.

About

Take on a 5k, 10k or Half Marathon with a scenic route set against the backdrop of Walton Bridge. With a mix of the town-centre and rural, riverside scenery, these routes have something for everyone. Great for beginners and experienced runners alike, this race has people coming along for the outstanding atmosphere.
