About

Explore the picturesque countryside surrounding Newcastle Airport and Dinnington at the Newcastle Half Marathon & 10K. Runners will enjoy a road-closed course that winds through beautiful lanes, providing a scenic and challenging experience for all levels. This event is a must for those seeking a memorable running adventure in the United Kingdom.
