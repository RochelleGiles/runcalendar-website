About

Explore the stunning coastal town of Pacasmayo as you take on the Pacasmayo Marathon. This historic race winds through picturesque beachfront scenery, offering runners a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of Peru's northern region. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a first-time racer, the Pacasmayo Marathon promises an unforgettable challenge and a chance to create lasting memories.
