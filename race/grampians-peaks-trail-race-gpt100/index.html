Course

Point-to-point 162km course on the Grampians Peaks Trail, from Mt Zero Picnic Area in the north to Dunkeld in the south. Mostly single track, alternating thin rocky uneven surface with smoother dirt, plus technical rocky sections and scrambling descents; one paved road section at the Mt William summit. Eleven major aid stations at road crossings, trailheads, Halls Gap and campgrounds.

About

The Grampians Peaks Trail Race (GPT100) runs 162km point-to-point along the Grampians Peaks Trail in Gariwerd, Victoria, from Mt Zero Picnic Area in the north to Dunkeld in the south. The course is mostly rocky single track with eleven major aid stations. A stage race, team relay and 50km event run over the same weekend, based at Halls Gap.
