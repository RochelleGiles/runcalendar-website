About

A 5K run, 3K walk, and kids' 1/4 mile fun run held in historic Miller Park, the surrounding neighborhood, and Miller Park Zoo in Bloomington, Illinois. The event supports the Miller Park Zoological Society's mission of fundraising and community outreach.
