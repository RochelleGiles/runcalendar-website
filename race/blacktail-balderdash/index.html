Course

An off-trail footrace starting and finishing at Blacktail Mountain Ski Area, using ski cuts, open forest and untracked ground with views of the Swan Range and Glacier National Park. All three courses include unmarked sections between checkpoints. Green is about 5 miles and 3,000 feet of gain, Blue 10 miles and 5,000 feet, Black 20 miles and 10,000 feet.
