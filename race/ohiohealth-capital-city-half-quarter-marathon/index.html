Course

Road race through Columbus, Ohio, with the finish and celebration area at High and Town Streets. Eight refueling stations serve the half marathon course, four the quarter marathon and one the 5K, offering water and Powerade Endurance Formula, with energy gel at one half marathon station. The course stays open four hours.

About

Since 2004, the OhioHealth Capital City Half Marathon has been the choice for thousands of runners and walkers, showcasing the City of Columbus and its diverse neighborhoods and entertainment districts to runners and walkers from all over the region. Each year, Cap City hosts more than 12,000 runners and walkers, plus spectators and volunteers. The annual economic impact is estimated at $7.5 million.
