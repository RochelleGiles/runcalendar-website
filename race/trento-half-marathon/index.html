About

The Trento Half Marathon is a scenic running event that takes place in the historic city of Trento, Italy. The course showcases the city's monumental architecture, including the Buonconsiglio Castle, and follows the gentle slopes along the Adige River, making it a fast and suitable course for runners looking to achieve their personal best times.
