About

Explore the stunning trails of the Pyrenees at the Trail Des Gypaetes, a challenging and picturesque ultramarathon event. This renowned race takes runners through the heart of the Lourdes region, offering breathtaking views of the iconic Gypaète Barbu (Bearded Vulture) and the surrounding mountain landscapes. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or seeking a new adventure, the Trail Des Gypaetes promises an unforgettable endurance challenge.
