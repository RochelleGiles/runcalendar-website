Course

A closed-road half marathon starting and finishing on Tewkesbury High Street, running through the historic town past the Abbey and along the riverside. Water stations are spaced along the route, with marshals and medical teams on course and an event village at the finish.

About

A half marathon through Tewkesbury, the historic Gloucestershire town where the River Severn meets the River Avon. The flat course follows the river valley past Tewkesbury Abbey and into the surrounding countryside.
