Course

A USATF-certified course through Flushing Meadows Corona Park, passing the Unisphere twice and circling the Fountain of the Planets, on paved park roads. Start and finish are near Queens Theater. A free kids dash starts at 9:00am and the main race at 9:30am.

About

Pi is the ratio of the circumference of any circle to the diameter of that circle, regardless of the circle's size. In celebration of Pi day and our historical mathematicians, we will run this 3.14 mile circular course to remember it by! After, grab yourself some goodies from All Round Foods & Waterloo Sparkling Water!
