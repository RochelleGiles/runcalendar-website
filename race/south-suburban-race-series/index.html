Course

The series race falling early in the year is the Super Sunday Fun Run 5K, which starts at Reynolds Landing on South Santa Fe Drive and follows the Mary Carter Greenway along the Platte River Trail. Start time is 10:00 a.m. The wider series runs six races across the year at 5K, 8K and 10K.

About

The South Suburban Race Series consists of six races over the calendar year. Participants can earn points at each race, which go toward overall scoring. Each male and female who accumulates the most points will be awarded prizes and be crowned Race Series Champion. Participation in the series is free.
