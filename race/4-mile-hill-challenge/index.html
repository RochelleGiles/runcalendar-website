About

The 4 Mile Hill Challenge is a trail running event held at Audubon Center at Debs Park featuring rolling hills on enclosed historic park trails. It offers a 4 Mile Run for all levels and a 2K Youth Hill Challenge, starting at the park entrance on Griffin Avenue.
