Course

Point-to-point mountain trail ultra starting and finishing at Jug Mountain Ranch, just south of McCall, through remote high country far from aid. A Hardrock 100 and Western States 100 qualifier. Offers a 100-miler and a 20-miler.

About

The Idaho Mountain Trail Ultra Festival, known as IMTUF, is a mountain trail ultramarathon based in McCall, Idaho. The event offers 100-mile and 20-mile distances through the rugged terrain of the Payette National Forest.
