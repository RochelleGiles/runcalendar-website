Course

Unmarked trail races from Huntcliff School in Saltburn-by-the-Sea on the Cleveland Way and coastal paths. The marathon is about 28 miles with roughly 4,000 ft of ascent and must be navigated from downloadable maps and route descriptions; the half is 14.5 miles and the "10k" 7.5 miles.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of North Yorkshire's coastline at the Hardmoors 26.2 Saltburn Trail Races. Runners can choose from a Trail Marathon, Half Marathon, or 10km distance, all showcasing the stunning natural landscapes of this picturesque region. Challenge yourself on this scenic trail running adventure, with stunning ocean views and challenging terrain that will test your endurance and resilience.
