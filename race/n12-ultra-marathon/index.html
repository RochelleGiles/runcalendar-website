Course

A road event based on the N12 corridor in the North West. The 50 km ultra runs point-to-point from Klerksdorp Recreation Centre to Olen Park Rugby Stadium in Potchefstroom; the 21.1 km, 10 km and 5 km all start and finish at Olen Park. Hydration stations sit roughly every 3 km, and the route is close to flat between the two venues.

About

The N12 Ultra Marathon is a road ultramarathon in Klerksdorp, North West, following sections of the N12 national route. It offers 50km, half marathon, 10km and 5km distances for club and social runners.
