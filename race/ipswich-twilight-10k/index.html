Course

Starts and finishes on Portman Road, home of Ipswich Town FC. The road route winds from the stadium through the main shopping area and the historic centre, passing the Corn Exchange and Town Hall.

About

The Ipswich Twilight 10K is an evening road race through Ipswich, Suffolk, organised by Ipswich JAFFA Running Club as part of the town's twilight race series.
