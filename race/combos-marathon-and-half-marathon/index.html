About

The COMBOS® Marathon and Half Marathon offer a flat, spectator-friendly course in Albany, Georgia, open to hand cyclists, runners, and walkers with a 7-hour time limit. Runners enjoy aid stations every two miles stocked with Combos®, water, Gatorade, fruit, and gels, plus a weekend of events including a downtown street festival.
