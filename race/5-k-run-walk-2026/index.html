Course

A USATF-certified 5K (course #SC17032DW) starting at the Little River Waterfront on Mineola Avenue, South Carolina. The route has very few turns and finishes along the waterfront, with one water stop on course. Runners, walkers and stroller pushers are all welcome.

About

A 5K run/walk event hosted by the Rotary Club of Little River featuring a course with minimal turns that finishes along the waterfront. The event welcomes both runners and walkers and is part of the Rotary Crown Series for 2026.
