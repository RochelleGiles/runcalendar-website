Course

A single 5K loop repeated for the chosen distance: one lap for the 5K, three for the 15K and five for the 25K. Run in Midland City Forest on wide wooded trails rather than single track, including a bridge crossing. An aid station with water and snacks sits about 0.75 miles into each lap and is passed twice per loop. The course is marked with LED light markers.

About

The Snow Moon Trail Run takes place in Midland's City Forest on wide-track cross country ski trails and mountain bike trails, marked with small LED lights for night navigation. Runners choose from 5K (1 lap), 15K (3 laps), or 25K (5 laps) options on a 5K loop course with an aid station featuring a bonfire.
