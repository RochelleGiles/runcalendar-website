Course

A three-day festival of races around Lake Tahoe, with 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon spread over Friday to Sunday and a 72-mile ultramarathon that circles the lake. The Sunday marathon starts at 7am and finishes, like the other Sunday races, at Lakeside Beach in South Lake Tahoe.

About

The Lake Tahoe Marathon takes place in South Lake Tahoe, California, with routes tracing the shoreline of Lake Tahoe, including the half marathon's path along scenic Emerald Bay. Distances range from 5K to a 72-mile ultra.
