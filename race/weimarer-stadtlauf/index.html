About

Now in its 35th year, the Weimarer Stadtlauf is an 11K road race with a 2x5.5K relay option through Weimar, the Thuringian city famed as the home of Goethe and Schiller and listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It draws runners of all abilities each October.
