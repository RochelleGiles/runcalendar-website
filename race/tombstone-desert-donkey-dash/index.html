About

The Tombstone Desert Donkey Dash brings the Wild West to life with a full day of family-friendly fun. As part of Tombstone's legendary Ed Schieffelin Days, the fun doesn't stop with the races — there's plenty to enjoy all day long. You can try your hand at old-school skills like hand drilling, test your grit in mucking competitions, gallop your way through hobby horse races, and yes… even compete in the legendary poop pick-up contest. It's silly, spirited, and guaranteed to bring out laughs from kids and grown-ups alike.
