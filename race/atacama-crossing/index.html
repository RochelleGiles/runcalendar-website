About

Atacama Crossing is a 250km, multi-stage, self-supported footrace across the Atacama Desert in northern Chile, one of the driest places on Earth. Runners cross salt flats, canyons and high-altitude desert terrain over roughly a week of stages, carrying their own food and gear.
