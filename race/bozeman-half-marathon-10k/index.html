Course

Point-to-point downhill courses starting in the southern foothills of the Gallatin Valley and finishing on Main Street in Bozeman. Both the half marathon and the 10K are USATF certified, with mountain views along the route.

About

The Bozeman Half Marathon and 10K take runners through Bozeman, Montana, a city in the Gallatin Valley surrounded by the Bridger and Gallatin mountain ranges. Both distances start and finish in the same Montana college town.
