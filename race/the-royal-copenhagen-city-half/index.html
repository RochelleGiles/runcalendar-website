Course

A point-to-point half marathon from Copenhagen International School in Nordhavn to the Royal Arena in Ørestad, passing Kastellet, Amalienborg, Nyhavn, Kgs. Nytorv, the Black Diamond and Lille Langebro. Ultra-flat, under 36m of climb, on 93.4 percent even asphalt.

About

The Royal Copenhagen City Half is a must-run event for distance runners looking to experience the vibrant heart of Denmark's capital. Weaving through the historic streets and iconic landmarks of Copenhagen, this scenic half-marathon offers a unique opportunity to explore the city's rich culture and architecture while challenging your endurance. With an engaging atmosphere and stunning backdrops, the Royal Copenhagen City Half is a truly memorable race for runners of all levels.
