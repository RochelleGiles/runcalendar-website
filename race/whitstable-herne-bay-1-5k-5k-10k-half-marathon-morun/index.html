Course

Seafront runs starting and finishing at Hampton Pier Recreation Ground, Herne Bay, on an out-and-back along the coast between Herne Bay and Whitstable. The surface is predominantly hard promenade apart from the grass village hub, with some narrow sections. Half marathon, 10K, 5K and a 1.5K mini run start in sequence through the morning; buggy and dog friendly.

About

The Whitstable Herne Bay MoRun is a beloved annual event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the picturesque coastal towns of Whitstable and Herne Bay. Offering 1.5k, 5k, 10k, and half marathon distances, this community-driven race caters to runners of all levels, with stunning views of the Kent countryside and the opportunity to support a meaningful cause.
