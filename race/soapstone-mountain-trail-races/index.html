Course

The half marathon starts and finishes at Reddington Rock Riding Club on Handel Road, running dirt road and singletrack before joining the blue-blazed Shenipsit Trail, with an out-and-back to Hopkins Road and two ascents of Soapstone Mountain past the fire tower. Two aid stations sit at the Gulf Road crossings. The 6K uses singletrack and dirt road through forest with no aid.
