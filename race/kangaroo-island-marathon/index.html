About

The Kangaroo Island Marathon has welcomed runners from around Australia and overseas, run inside a closed Flinders Chase National Park. The course follows undulating roads through post-bushfire regenerating bush, with an exposed coastal section giving cliff-top views over the Southern Ocean and a boardwalk out to the iconic Remarkable Rocks.
