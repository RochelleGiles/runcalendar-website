Course

Set in the Santa Monica Mountains above Agoura Hills. The Chesebro Half climbs from rustic ranches and canyons through meadows to a summit panorama, the Pure Water 10K runs the tree-lined streets of Old Agoura, and the one-mile is held around Chumash Park. Stack Challenge entries combine two of the races.

About

The Great Race of Agoura Hills is a spring running festival in the Santa Monica Mountains north-west of Los Angeles. It offers the Chesebro Half Marathon, a 10K, 5K and one-mile alongside combined Stack Challenge entries. The half marathon climbs through ranch land and canyons to a summit viewpoint.
