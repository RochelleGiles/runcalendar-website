Course

An out-and-back ultra along the Deeside Way, starting from the Duthie Park car park in Aberdeen, running out to a marshal check-in at Banchory and returning by the same route. Entries open at midnight on Hogmanay.

About

Explore the scenic Deeside Way in northeast Scotland at the D33 Ultra Marathon. This approximately 33-mile challenge follows the historic Deeside Railway Line, offering runners a scenic tour of the beautiful Aberdeenshire countryside. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or tackling your first long-distance event, the D33 promises an unforgettable adventure through the heart of this stunning region.
