About

The Grasse Par Hameaux is a unique running event that takes participants on a scenic tour through the charming hamlets surrounding the picturesque town of Grasse. Runners will experience the rich history and culture of this renowned perfume capital as they wind through quaint villages and stunning landscapes, making this a must-do event for any endurance athlete seeking an unforgettable running adventure.
