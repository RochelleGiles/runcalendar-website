About

The Ecomarathon is an environmentally conscious running event inspired by German sustainability practices. The event features solar-powered timing systems, recycled race bibs, and bicycle lead vehicles to minimize environmental impact. A portion of proceeds goes to charity, making it both an athletic and socially responsible event.
