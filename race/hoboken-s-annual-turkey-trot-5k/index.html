Course

Starts and finishes at Pier A Park on Sinatra Drive and runs along Hoboken's waterfront, with views across to the Manhattan skyline. The 5K is USATF-certified and starts at 10:00am; a one-mile fun run goes off at 9:00am.

About

The Hoboken Turkey Trot is a family-friendly 5K run hosted by Winning Events Group on a USATF-certified course along Sinatra Drive. It includes a 1 Mile Fun Run, professional photography, and supports community traditions with hot chocolate and festivities.
