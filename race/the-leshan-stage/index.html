About

The Leshan Stage is a multi-day ultra trail race in Sichuan, China, near Leshan — home to the Giant Buddha, the world's largest stone Buddha carved into a cliff at the confluence of three rivers. The route covers 55K and 100K distances through the surrounding mountains.
