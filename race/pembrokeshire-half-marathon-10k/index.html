Course

Both races start and finish in the village of Dale at 9am, running on relatively quiet country lanes above the Milford Haven Waterway. The 10K has one steep hill; the half marathon is undulating with most of its climbing in the second half. The route gives views of St Ann's Head, Skokholm Island and two lighthouses.

About

The Pembrokeshire Half Marathon & 10K takes place on relatively quiet country lanes, offering spectacular views of the Milford Haven Waterway and Skokholm Island. The event starts and finishes in the beautiful village of Dale.
