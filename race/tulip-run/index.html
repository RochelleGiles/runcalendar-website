About

Now in its 40th year, the annual Tulip Run invites runners of all levels to a fast, scenic course at the Skagit Regional Airport. Choose from 5K, 2-mile, or 10K options, with clearly marked routes and attentive monitors ensuring a smooth experience. Embrace the spirit of spring in the Valley. Register today and join the fun!
