Course

Races start and finish at the Anderson Complex in Stoughton and include sections of off-road trail. A half marathon, 5K and 2-mile Lil' Run are offered, with separate running and walking divisions in the half. Strollers, wheelchairs and other mobility aids are welcome; pets are not permitted.

About

The Syttende Mai Classic encompasses a Half Marathon Run, Half Marathon Walk, 5K Run, and 2-Mile Lil' Run. Strollers, wheelchairs, and other mobility aids welcome. No pets allowed. Off-road trails are included.
