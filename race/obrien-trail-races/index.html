Course

All races start at the William O'Brien State Park Visitor Center, 16821 O'Brien Trail N, Marine on St Croix. The 50K covers two 25K loops through the park's upper and south sections and Jackson Meadows. The 5K and 10K use wide grass trails with one substantial hill and a flat finish. The 25K course was listed as still to be determined at the time of checking.

About

O'Brien Trail Races run through William O'Brien State Park in Marine on St. Croix, Minnesota, with 5K, 10K, 25K and 50K options on wooded trails above the St. Croix River. The park's mix of prairie, woods and river bluffs makes for a varied ultra-distance day.
