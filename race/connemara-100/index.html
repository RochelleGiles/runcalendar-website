About

The Connemara 100 on the West Coast of Ireland is a 100 mile road race with a fabulous course taking participants through the stunning Connemara region. The course loops through Letterfrack, the Inagh Valley, Maam Cross, Leenane, and Roundstone before returning to Clifden.
