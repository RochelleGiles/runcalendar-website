Course

Run inside Vicksburg National Military Park on the park's sealed avenues. The 10K starts near the Visitor Center, follows Confederate Avenue to the Louisiana Monument and loops back via Pemberton and Union Avenues; the 5K walk climbs Louisiana Hill before an out-and-back on the same avenues. Both finish at the Visitor Center; the one mile Cannonball Run is held at the Hampton Inn.

About

The Vicksburg Run Thru History is an annual 10K road race held in the Vicksburg National Military Park. Runners will experience the historic sites and landscapes of the Vicksburg Campaign during the Civil War.
