Course

Point-to-point starting at Monarto Safari Park and finishing at Sturt Reserve, Murray Bridge. The 54 km, 35 km and 26 km routes travel via the Lavender Federation Trail and Kinchina Conservation Park to the finish. The 10 km and 5 km are run as the Zoo Runs. Aid station detail is not given.

About

The Federation Ultra Trail Marathon traverses the Lavender Federation Trail and Kinchina Conservation Park in South Australia. The 56km ultra starts from Monarto Safari Park and finishes at the Murray Bridge riverfront, and can be run solo or in a relay team of four.
