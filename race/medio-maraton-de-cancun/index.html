About

The Medio Maratón de Cancún runs along Boulevard Kukulcán through the Zona Hotelera, the narrow strip of beachfront resorts between the Caribbean Sea and Nichupté Lagoon. Distances include 5K, 10K and half marathon along Mexico's Caribbean coast.
