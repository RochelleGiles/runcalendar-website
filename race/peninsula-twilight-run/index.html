About

The Peninsula Twilight Run offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the beauty of Perth's Burswood Peninsula at dusk. This scenic 5km or 10km course starts and finishes at the Western Australian Marathon Club, providing a picturesque and challenging route for runners of all levels. Join fellow enthusiasts for an unforgettable evening of fitness and camaraderie in this stunning coastal setting.
