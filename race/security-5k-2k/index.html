About

The Mission 500 Security 5K/2K is an annual fundraising run/walk held at ISC West in Las Vegas, attracting security professionals to support children in need across the US, Canada, and Puerto Rico. Funds from registration and sponsorships aid Mission 500's charitable initiatives, with additional events like care pack builds enhancing community impact.
