Course

A two-lap cross-country style 5K touring the vineyard at Indian Creek Winery on North McDermott Road, Kuna, starting and finishing at the winery. The route has several turns and uneven ground, so it is not one for a personal best, and runners may stop after one lap. Two wave starts run at 9am and 11am.
