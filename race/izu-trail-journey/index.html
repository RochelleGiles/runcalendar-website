About

Izu Trail Journey is a roughly 70km trail race across Japan's Izu Peninsula in Shizuoka Prefecture, climbing through mountain forest with stretches of coastal scenery. It is known as one of the toughest one-day trail events near Tokyo, with heavy cumulative elevation gain.
