Course

Point-to-point half marathon from the Wrest Point car park in Sandy Bay to the pinnacle of kunanyi/Mt Wellington, climbing 1,271 m via Sandy Bay Road, Davey Street, Huon Road and Pillinger Drive, with several drink stations en route. The 10 km Point to Pub follows the same roads to finish at the Fern Tree Tavern.

About

Point to Pinnacle starts at sea level at Wrest Point and climbs 1,271m over 21.1km to the summit of kunanyi/Mount Wellington, with a shorter Point to Pub option finishing at Fern Tree. Held annually for around three decades, the main race is capped at 3,200 entrants.
