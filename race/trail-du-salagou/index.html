About

The Trail Du Salagou is a must-do event for trail running enthusiasts. Nestled in the stunning landscape of the Salagou region, this challenging course takes runners through rugged terrain, past striking red rock formations, and along the shores of a serene lake. Whether you're seeking a scenic adventure or a test of your endurance, the Trail Du Salagou promises an unforgettable running experience.
