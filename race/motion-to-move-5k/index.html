About

The Motion to Move 5K is a run and walk staged by the Tallahassee Bar Association's Young Lawyers Section as a fundraiser. It starts at 9:00 am on Drayton Drive in Tallahassee, Florida, and is open to runners and walkers alike. The first edition was held in January 2026.
