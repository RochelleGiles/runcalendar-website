Course

Starts and finishes at the Potterville High School track on East Main Street. A single re-fuelling station is placed so that participants pass it twice, at roughly the one-mile and two-mile marks. Police escort the lead runners and volunteers manage traffic.

About

This 5K takes place during Potterville's annual Gizzard Fest, with a different theme each year. Participants receive race gear, medals, trophies and gift bags amid a lively, competitive atmosphere.
