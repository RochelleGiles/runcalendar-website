Course

Courses follow the Historic Columbia River Highway out of Hood River on part paved trail, part small-town backroads, finishing riverside at the festival grounds. Marathon and half run Sunday with the 5K and 10K Saturday; aid stations every 1-3 miles.

About

Get ready for an unforgettable weekend of racing and celebration in Oregon's breathtaking Columbia River Gorge. Whether you're running with your best friends or using Saturday's 5K & 10K as a warmup for Sunday's Half or Full Marathon, this is your chance to experience one of the most scenic race courses in the country! Every race finishes riverside at our festival grounds, where good vibes, great food, and full celebration await!
