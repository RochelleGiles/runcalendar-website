About

The Hunters Point Parks Conservancy is proud to partner with the Woodside-Sunnyside Runners, Run LIC, and the LIC Post to put on the annual LIC Waterfront 5K. This event takes runners and walkers through the streets and waterfront parks of Long Island City and is an extremely popular family-friendly event that draws casual and competitive runners alike.
