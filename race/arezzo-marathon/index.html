About

The Arezzo Marathon is a must-attend event for running enthusiasts, set against the picturesque backdrop of the historic city of Arezzo. Runners can look forward to a challenging course that winds through the city's charming streets and scenic countryside, offering a unique opportunity to experience the rich cultural heritage of this stunning Italian destination.
