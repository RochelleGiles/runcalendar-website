About

Explore the picturesque Chatsworth estate in Derbyshire during the Chatsworth 10K, a challenging mixed-terrain race through the stunning grounds of this historic property. Challenge yourself on a course that winds through scenic countryside, offering runners an unforgettable running experience in a beautiful natural setting.
