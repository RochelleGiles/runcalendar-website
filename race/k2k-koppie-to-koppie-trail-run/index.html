Course

Trail race at Die Bosveld, Doornpoort, north of Pretoria, on a 2,000-hectare property of indigenous bush, rocky paths and steep koppies past Ndebele stone kraal ruins. Four named distances from 7km to a 50km ultra, with water points on route.
