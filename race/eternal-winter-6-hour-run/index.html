Course

Six hours on a 2.5-mile loop through the dirt and paved trails of Greenbury Point Nature Center, Annapolis. The route mixes wooded trail with street sections on Bullard Boulevard and Hooper High Road and runs along Beach Road beside the Severn River. Run fat-ass style: no course marshals, no markers and no aid provided.

About

The Eternal Winter 6-Hour Run is a timed ultra hosted by the Annapolis Striders each January in Annapolis, Maryland. Runners complete as many laps of a looped course as possible within the six-hour window.
