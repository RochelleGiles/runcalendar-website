Course

A figure-eight loop starting at the Wellhouse on Spring Street in Wartrace and finishing in front of the Walking Horse Hotel. The route uses quiet streets in Wartrace and Bell Buckle plus back roads, including gravel sections and fords. Aid stations are at miles 7.5, 13.1, 18, 23.5, 29, 35 and 39, with water jugs placed between them.

About

The Strolling Jim 40 Miler is a road ultramarathon on rural backroads around Wartrace, Tennessee, first run in 1979 and among the oldest 40-mile races in the country. The route is named for a champion Tennessee Walking Horse and crosses rolling Middle Tennessee countryside.
