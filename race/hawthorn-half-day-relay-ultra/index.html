Course

A timed event run on a scenic 5K loop of mixed trail and road in Terre Haute, starting and finishing at Runners Village. Both races run 7:00am to 7:00pm, with a half-mile loop used for the final 30 minutes. Race headquarters has drinks and snacks, and campground restrooms and water stations sit along the loop.

About

Hawthorn Half Day Relay & Ultra is a timed relay and ultra race in Terre Haute, Indiana, now past its 20th running. Teams and solo runners complete looped courses over a half-day timed format.
