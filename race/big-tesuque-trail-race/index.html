Course

A single out-and-back on forest service road. The course starts at the Aspen Vista trailhead at 10,000 feet on Hyde Park Road, winds up to the towers on Tesuque Peak at 12,000 feet, then returns the same way, for about 2,000 feet of climb. It is timed for the peak of the aspen colour, with food and coffee at the finish.

About

The Big Tesuque Trail Race climbs into the Santa Fe National Forest above Santa Fe, New Mexico, on a 12-mile mountain trail toward Tesuque Peak, reaching well above 10,000 feet through aspen and conifer forest.
