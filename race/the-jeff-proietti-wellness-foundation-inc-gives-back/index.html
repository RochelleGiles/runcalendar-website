Course

A loop course around Solvay High School at 900 Woods Road, Solvay, New York. The programme is a 5–10k run, walk or ruck plus a 2k for students and beginners, and the event can also be completed virtually in your own neighbourhood.

About

The Jeff Proietti Foundation strives to inspire families & individuals to embrace fitness and wellness, while raising awareness and funds for scholarships that empower high school students from Solvay and West Genesee to pursue careers in health and wellness.
