About

Leap 4 The Lily Pad, now past its 13th running, raises funds for The Lily Pad Cafe's operating costs at the Methodist Rehabilitation Center Green Space in Ridgeland, Mississippi. The Lily Pad employs and trains individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities, helping them gain local employment and showcase their abilities.
