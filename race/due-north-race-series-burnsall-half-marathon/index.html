About

Explore the stunning Yorkshire Dales at the Due North Race Series Burnsall Half Marathon. This picturesque race winds through the charming village of Burnsall, offering runners breathtaking views of the River Wharfe and the surrounding countryside. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or a newcomer to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running experience.
