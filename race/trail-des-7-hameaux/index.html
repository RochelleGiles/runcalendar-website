About

Trail des 7 Hameaux is a looped trail race around Magny-les-Hameaux in the Yvelines, passing the historic Port Royal des Champs abbey and seven surrounding hamlets. It offers 12.5km and 23.5km distances over notoriously leg-sapping terrain.
