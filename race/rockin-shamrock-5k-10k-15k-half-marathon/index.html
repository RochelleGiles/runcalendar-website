Course

Out-and-back loops on the paved beach path at Belmont Shore, Long Beach, starting and finishing on East Ocean Boulevard. The half marathon runs three 4.36-mile loops; the 5K and 10K use shorter loops on the same path. Aid at the turnaround and start/finish with water, Gatorade and gels. The course is open to the public.

About

The Rockin' Shamrock event offers 5k, 10k, 15k, and Half Marathon races on an open course in Woodley Park, Van Nuys. Participants enjoy custom finisher medals, goodie bags, free photos, videos, and a fun, family-friendly atmosphere with snacks along the course.
