Course

New Year's Day five-mile road race starting and finishing in Bristol, running through Colt State Park and along Poppasquash Road, with parts of the route on an untreated bike path that can be uneven or icy in winter. Run old-school: no chip timing, no aid stations, runners carry what they need, but results are timed and scored.

About

The Hangover Classic 5 Miler is Bristol, Rhode Island's traditional New Year's Day road race, drawing runners out on January 1st to shake off the night before along the town's historic streets near Bristol Harbor.
