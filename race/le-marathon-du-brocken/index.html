About

The Harz-Gebirgslauf is a scenic mountain running event through the picturesque landscape of Harz National Park. Featuring multiple race distances including a marathon over North Germany's highest mountain, the Brocken, this event attracts nearly 4,000 running enthusiasts each year. Established in 1978, it has become one of the most popular running events in the region.
