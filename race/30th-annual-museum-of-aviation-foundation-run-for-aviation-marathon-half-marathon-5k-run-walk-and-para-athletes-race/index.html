Course

All races are run on Robins Air Force Base at Warner Robins, Georgia, on sealed roads within the installation. The marathon course is USATF certified and serves as a Boston Marathon qualifier. Marathon, half marathon, 5K and a para athlete race with hand cycles are offered.

About

The Museum of Aviation Foundation's Run for Aviation, now past its 29th running, offers a marathon, half marathon, 5K run/walk, and para athletes race at the Museum of Aviation. All races run on Robins Air Force Base, and the marathon course is USATF certified as a Boston Marathon qualifier.
