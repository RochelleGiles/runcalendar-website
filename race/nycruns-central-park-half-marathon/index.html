About

Take a wintry tour of the park's spectacular rolling hills and test your fitness going into the spring season. Running Central Park in the winter is an off-season treat. The park is less crowded and the views are more spectacular than other times of the year. Join us for this popular annual half marathon and get swept up in the magic of running in one of the city's most beloved spaces.
