Course

Starts at Porth Penrhyn in Bangor and follows the Snowdonia Slate Trail through the old quarry landscape. The 147km route climbs over 3,900m and has eight aid stations, a drop bag and a 36-hour limit; the 80km Ultra Tour of the Quarries climbs 2,300m with four aid stations and a 24-hour cutoff.

About

An epic ultra running adventure through the stunning Snowdonia landscape, featuring two challenging trail routes that explore the historic slate quarry region. Runners will experience breathtaking mountain views and challenging terrain across two distance options: a 147km full trail ultra and an 80km quarry highlights route.
