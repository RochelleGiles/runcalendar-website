About

The Medal Madness 5K & 10K is a fun running event held at Cypress Grove Park in Orlando, Florida, featuring scenic out-and-back courses. Participants in the 5K complete the course twice and the 10K four times, with finisher medals awarded to all finishers within the 2-hour time limit.
