About

The November Midweek Mile & Dash is a free youth running event held under the lights at Midtown High School's Eddie S. Henderson Stadium in Atlanta. It features a timed mile for ages 6-14 and a 50-meter dash for ages 6 and under, with a track and field zone for fun activities.
