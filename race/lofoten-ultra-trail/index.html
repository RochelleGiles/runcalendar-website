About

The Lofoten Ultra-Trail crosses the dramatic mountain peaks and fjords of Norway's Lofoten Islands, with distances from 12km to 100 miles. Courses climb steep coastal ridgelines with views over turquoise fjords and fishing villages under the midnight sun.
