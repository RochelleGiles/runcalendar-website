Course

Certified road race on Redlands city streets over half marathon, 10K and 5K, plus a Kinderdash. The start is in front of the post office on Brookside Avenue. The half runs through Prospect Park, the Redlands Country Club area and Smiley Heights, finishing at the Redlands Bowl. Aid stations: two on the 5K, four on the 10K and seven on the half.

About

The 43rd Annual Run Through Redlands is a beloved community event that brings runners and walkers of all levels together for a day of fitness, camaraderie, and celebration. With a variety of race distances, this event caters to participants of all ages and abilities, making it a must-attend for anyone who loves the thrill of the run.
