About

The Kieler Förde Marathon takes runners on a scenic tour through the charming city of Kiel, Germany, showcasing the stunning Kieler Förde waterfront. This historic event promises a challenging yet rewarding course, attracting runners from around the world to experience the unique coastal atmosphere of this picturesque destination.
