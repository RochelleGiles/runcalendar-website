Course

Flat 5K and one-mile courses set on and around the Cuyahoga Community College Western Campus at 11000 Pleasant Valley Road in Parma, Ohio. The campus grounds are level throughout, making it a fast, beginner-friendly route. A timed 5K, a 5K walk and a one-mile walk are offered.

About

The Parma Run-Walk for Pierogies benefits local charities and community projects each year. The first 600 participants receive a signature t-shirt, on-site pierogies from Perla Pierogies, and a certificate for free pierogies, plus icy towels to cool off at the finish.
