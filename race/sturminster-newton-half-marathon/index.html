Course

A road half marathon on open roads, starting at the top of Station Road and running out through Manston, Margaret Marsh, Stour Row and Todber before returning via Moorside and Hinton St Mary along an avenue of trees to finish on the high school playing field. Seven water stations on route plus one at the finish. A multi-terrain 5K fun run runs alongside.

About

The Sturminster Newton Half Marathon is a picturesque and challenging race through the rolling hills of Dorset. Runners will enjoy stunning countryside views as they tackle this scenic 13.1-mile course, which winds through the charming town of Sturminster Newton. With a reputation for a friendly atmosphere and enthusiastic local support, this event is a must-do for any half marathon enthusiast.
