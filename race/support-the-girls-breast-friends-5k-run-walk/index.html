Course

A paved, mostly flat 5K starting and finishing at Stickmen Brewing in Tualatin, Oregon, run or walked between 9am and noon. Suitable for all abilities and ages, with a free entry for children 13 and under and a virtual option. Finishers refuel with pizza and a drink at the brewery.

About

The third annual 5K run/walk fundraiser benefits Breast Friends, a nonprofit supporting breast cancer patients and survivors in Oregon and Washington. Over 4,000 women in Oregon are diagnosed with breast cancer annually, and this event ensures no one goes through the journey alone.
