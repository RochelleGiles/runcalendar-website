Course

Run on rugged trails in the Jackson-Washington State Forest outside Brownstown, with all distances starting and finishing at the forest. The 100K can be run solo or as a 2-6 person relay. Aid stations are well stocked and the 100K carries live tracking.

About

Jackson County Ultras is a trail ultramarathon held in Jackson-Washington State Forest near Brownstown, Indiana. The 50K and 100K distances cross the forest's steep, knob-studded terrain, among the hilliest trail running in the state.
