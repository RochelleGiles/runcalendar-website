Course

A single 5km loop starting at Jezzine Park and running via Cook Street and The Esplanade to Soroptimist Park, along the boardwalk to Kissing Point lookout, then down to Jezzine Way East, around the rockpool and along The Strand before finishing at Jezzine Way East. It is held on public paths and footpaths with no road closures.

About

The Townsville Road Runners annual Australia Day Fun Run supports the Brighter Lives Townsville Hospital Foundation, helping to enhance the hospital and patient experience at Townsville Hospital and Health Service.
