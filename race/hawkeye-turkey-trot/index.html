About

The Hawkeye Turkey Trot is a Thanksgiving morning 5K held in Iowa City, Iowa, starting and finishing at Shelter #6 in City Park. A shorter Herky Turkey Tot Trot for children is run alongside the main race, making it a family fixture on Thanksgiving Day.
