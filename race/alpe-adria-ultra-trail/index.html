About

The Alpe Adria Ultra Trail is a stunning mountain running event that takes participants through the breathtaking landscapes of the Alpe Adria region. Runners will traverse alpine meadows, forests, and challenging mountain terrain, immersed in the natural beauty of this remarkable setting. This challenging yet rewarding ultra-distance race promises an unforgettable adventure for seasoned trail runners.
