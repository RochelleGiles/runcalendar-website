Course

Trail courses based at Queen Elizabeth Park, Paekākāriki. The 14 km starts at Whareroa Beach, runs under the expressway into Whareroa Farm, climbs through forest onto a mountain bike link track, then descends past ponds and the Yankee trail to a beach finish. The trail marathon starts at the Ramaroa Rooms. The festival also carries mountain bike and duathlon events.

About

XTERRA Wellington Festival, an offroad festival packed full of goodness on and off the trails. A completely unique, off-road duathlon, mountain bike and trail running experience with spectacular views, 'the best downhill of any XTERRA on the planet' and true Kiwi hospitality to make you feel right at home.
