Course

A single flat loop starting and finishing at The Rocks in Sydney. The route runs down Hickson Road, crosses Pyrmont Bridge, follows Darling Harbour and comes back through Barangaroo to the finish. Sealed roads and harbourside promenade throughout, with no significant hills; the Harbour 5 covers a shortened version of the same loop.

About

The Real Insurance Harbour 10 is set against the backdrop of Sydney's harbour and can be enjoyed by walkers and runners of all abilities. The Harbour 10 is a fast, flat 10km course with harbour views, while the Harbour 5 suits those trying a fun run for the first time.
