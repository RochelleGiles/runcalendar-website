About

The Goosebumps 6/24 Hour Run/Walk is a fun, unique fundraiser hosted by the River City Running Club. You can walk or run as much—or as little—as you'd like within the 6- or 24-hour timeframe. It's the perfect chance to challenge yourself at your own pace: walk a single loop and hang out, or push for a half, full, or even an ultramarathon distance. You set the goal!
