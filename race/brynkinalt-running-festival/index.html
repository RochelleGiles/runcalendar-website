Course

Two days of 5K and 10K trail racing on the private Brynkinalt Estate near Chirk, Wrexham, with canicross on the Saturday and standard running on the Sunday. The routes cover the whole estate on a mix of fast footpaths, grassed sections and the occasional stony stretch, with some climbing and views across the parkland.

About

The Brynkinalt Running Festival offers 10K and 5K road routes through the grounds of Brynkinalt Estate near Chirk, on the Wales-England border. The event is organised as a charity fundraiser, drawing runners from across the region each spring.
