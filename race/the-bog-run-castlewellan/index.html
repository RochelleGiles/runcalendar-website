About

The Bog Run Castlewellan is a 5km untimed fun run in support of children and young people impacted by cancer across the island of Ireland. The event offers 2 courses; a senior course for those 12 years + and a junior course open to 8-14 year olds with accompanying adult. The course involves crawling through bushes, climbing over trees and wading through mud pits.
