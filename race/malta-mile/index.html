Course

A single-lap road mile starting at the Town of Malta Community Center. The route runs north on Bayberry, then south on Route 9 around the 'four corners' roundabout, finishing near Ellsworth Commons. Flat village streets throughout, starting at 10 AM alongside the Veterans Appreciation Day Parade.

About

The Malta Mile is a 1-mile race starting at 10 AM in front of the Town of Malta Veteran's Appreciation Day Parade, beginning north of the Malta Community Center on Bayberry Drive and looping around the Dunning/Route 9 and 67 roundabout before finishing at Ellsworth Commons. Proceeds support the maintenance of bike paths during winter months by the Roundabout Runners Club.
