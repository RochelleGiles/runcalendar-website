Course

Start and finish at Bluewater Fitness in Niceville, Florida. The 5K is a fast out-and-back down White Point Road; the 10K is a scenic loop with bay views through the Bluewater Bay neighbourhoods, turning on sand at the end of Maxwell Gunter Road, with a 90-minute limit. A kids fun run and an after-party at the gym round out the day.

About

The 5K and 10K Hunger Run/Walk benefits Niceville Sharing and Caring, with proceeds going to support the local community in and around Niceville, Florida.
