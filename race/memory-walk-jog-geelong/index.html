About

Memory Walk & Jog Geelong is a family-friendly fitness event held at the picturesque Eastern Park in Geelong. Participants can enjoy a leisurely walk or energetic jog while raising funds for a meaningful cause. This community event offers a fun and rewarding way to get active and make a difference.
