About

Explore the scenic Beaujol Trail Morgon, a unique running event that winds through the renowned Beaujolais wine region of France. Runners will be captivated by the stunning vineyards, rolling hills, and historic villages that set the stage for this challenging yet rewarding trail race. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, the Beaujol Trail Morgon offers an unforgettable running experience.
