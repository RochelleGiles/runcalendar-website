Course

A fast single-lap half marathon starting at the Malkars training centre on Torsåsgatan in Kalmar, running past the Tegelviken allotments and Stensö fishing harbour, then along Kalmarsundsparken with Kalmar Castle in view. The route carries on past the museum and out along Ängöleden with views over the Kalmarsund and the Öland bridge, turning at Djurängen and returning through Skälby to the finish. Drinks stations at 5, 10, 15 and 18 km and at the line. A 5 km race is run alongside it.

About

The Kalmar Malkars 21km is a fast and scenic half marathon that takes runners through the beautiful city of Kalmar. The course showcases the city's historic landmarks, including Kalmar Castle, and offers stunning views of the Kalmarsund. The event also features a 5km race, making it accessible for runners of all abilities.
