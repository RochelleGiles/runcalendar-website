About

Run Madtown isn't just a race — it's Madison's ultimate kickoff to summer! Picture this: a weekend packed with vibrant energy, flamingo flair, and a community that knows how to have fun. Whether you're running fast, walking proud, or just soaking in the sights, you'll experience the best of Madtown with music, festive cheer, and unforgettable vibes.
