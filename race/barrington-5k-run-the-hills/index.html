Course

Starts and finishes at the Presbyterian Church of Barrington on Brinker Road in Barrington Hills, running out over the rolling roads that give the race its name. Water stations sit at roughly the one and two-mile marks and the course closes at 10:30am.

About

The Barrington 5K Run the Hills is an annual community event that brings together runners and walkers of all ages and abilities to enjoy a scenic course through the picturesque Barrington Hills. The event benefits several local charity organizations, making it a meaningful and impactful experience for participants.
