About

Join us for the INCLUDEnyc 5k, our annual event that brings together runners and community supporters to champion inclusion for New York's young people with disabilities. Sign up as an individual or form a team with family and friends. All proceeds go to INCLUDEnyc. This is a fully accessible event. Participants will receive a medal.
