About

Join us for GoGirl Run in beautiful Columbia, MO! This women-only race features a half marathon and a 5K option, celebrating the strength, resilience, and camaraderie of women. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this event promises a supportive atmosphere filled with fun, friendship, and empowerment.
