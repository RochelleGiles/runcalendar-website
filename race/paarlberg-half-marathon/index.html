Course

Road race from De Poort in Southern Paarl offering 21.1km, 10km and 5km. The half marathon takes in the traditional gravel section of almost 9km along the Jan Phillips drive on the slopes of Paarlberg, with views over the Paarl Valley. The 10km and 5km are flat and suited to all abilities.

About

The Paarlberg Half Marathon takes place around Paarl Mountain, one of the world's largest granite outcrops, in the Western Cape winelands. Runners can choose from 5km, 10km and 21km distances.
