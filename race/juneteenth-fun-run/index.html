Course

Start and finish at The Trailhead, 1221 Theodore Wirth Parkway, in Theodore Wirth Park. The route follows flat, paved park paths and is stroller friendly. The event is untimed, though a clock runs at the finish. The half-mile kids run starts at 9:00am and the 5K at 9:15am.

About

The Loppet Foundation hosts this Juneteenth Fun Run in partnership with community organizations, welcoming all to the 5K or half-mile run. Juneteenth commemorates the emancipation of enslaved African Americans, honoring history and celebrating freedom.
