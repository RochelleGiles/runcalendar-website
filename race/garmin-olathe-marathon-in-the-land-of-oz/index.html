Course

Start and finish at Garmin World Headquarters, 1200 E. 151st Street, Olathe, Kansas. The route mixes Olathe streets with sections of trail. Aid stations are placed roughly every 1.5 to 2.5 miles. There is no race-day registration.

About

The Garmin Olathe Marathon in the Land of Oz is a Boston qualifier held at Garmin World Headquarters, featuring a USATF-certified course through Olathe's streets and trails. Race weekend includes a full marathon, half marathon, 10K, and Kids Marathon (Munchkin Marathon) for all ages.
