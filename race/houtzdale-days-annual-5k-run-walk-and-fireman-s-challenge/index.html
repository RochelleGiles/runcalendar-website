Course

The 5K run/walk and the Fireman's Challenge, contested in full turnout gear, share one course starting and finishing at 601 Good Street in Houtzdale, Pennsylvania. The route runs slightly long at 3.3 miles rather than the standard 3.1.

About

The Houtzdale Days 5K Walk/Run and Fireman's Challenge is an annual event during our Houtzdale Days celebration. Participants are awarded medals for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place for Male and Female in each category.
