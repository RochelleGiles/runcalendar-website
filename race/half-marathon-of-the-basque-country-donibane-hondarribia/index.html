Course

A point-to-point half marathon from France into Spain, starting at 10:00 in the Plaza Ferdinand Foch in Saint-Jean-de-Luz and finishing in Arma Plaza at Hondarribia, beside the Parador Carlos V. The 21.1 km route is measured for official half marathon homologation.

About

The Donibane-Hondarribia is a 21.1 km half marathon run across the French-Spanish border in the Basque Country. Runners set off from Saint-Jean-de-Luz on the French side and finish in the old town of Hondarribia in Spain. The course is measured for official half marathon homologation.
