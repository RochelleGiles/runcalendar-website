About

Guiding Light was proud to host our fourth annual 5k Run/Walk. Guiding Light is a donor-funded Christian non-profit dedicated to helping men and women recover from addiction and the associated chaos of a life consumed by substance abuse. All race proceeds supported Guiding Light programming!
