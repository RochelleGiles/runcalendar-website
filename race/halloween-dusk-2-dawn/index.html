Course

Runners complete as many 7km laps as possible over 12 hours, from 6pm to 6am. The lap is a forest trail circuit in state forest at Telegraph Point, returning to the start/finish area at the end of each lap.

About

Coastal Track and Trail Runners' Halloween Dusk 2 Dawn is a unique night running event where participants run as many laps as possible of a 7km course over 12 hours, starting at 6pm and finishing at 6am.
