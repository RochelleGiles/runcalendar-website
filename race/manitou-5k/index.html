Course

A flat, fast 5K starting and finishing at Fields Park, Manitou Springs. The route runs east along the Manitou Creekwalk past a historic bridge and the Manitou arch, crosses a tiled bridge back through Fields Park, then follows the upper creekwalk to Memorial Park before returning downhill to a grass finish.

About

3rd annual Manitou 5K! Come celebrate the beauty of Manitou Springs! Race starts and ends in Fields park. Run along the rolling creek, view the art along the Creekwalk, loop the beautiful parks full of murals, sculptures, disc golf, skateparks and more. The path is flat and accessible to all. Run/walk. Bring your family, bring your neighbor, bring your friends!
