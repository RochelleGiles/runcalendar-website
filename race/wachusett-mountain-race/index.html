Course

Starts in the ski lodge car park at 499 Mountain Road, Princeton, Massachusetts, at the base of Mount Wachusett. The three-mile race climbs to the summit, while 10K entrants take their split at the top and carry on back down to a finish at the bottom of the mountain.

About

The Wachusett Mountain Race, first held in 1995, offers a 3-mile uphill road climb scored for the USATF New England Mountain Running Circuit, or a 10K that continues back down the mountain on roads and about 20% unpaved trails.
