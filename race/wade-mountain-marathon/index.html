Course

Marathon, half marathon and 10K on low-traffic North Alabama trails around Monte Sano, starting and finishing at Spragins Hollow Road NW in Huntsville. Billed as the region's only trail marathon. The race is cupless, so runners carry their own bottle or cup; a post-race meal is provided.

About

The Wade Mountain Marathon is a trail marathon with 10K and half marathon options, run through Wade Mountain Nature Preserve in Huntsville, Alabama — a rugged, forested preserve popular with local trail runners.
