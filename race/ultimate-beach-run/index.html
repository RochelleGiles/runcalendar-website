Course

The course follows the sealed Marine Parade Coastal Pathway at Mount Maunganui, starting at the Cenotaph, with each distance using its own turnaround point. The 12 km is two laps of the 6 km course. Aid stations and medical support are placed along the route, and the pathway suits wheelchairs and prams.

About

Experience the ultimate coastal adventure at the Ultimate Beach Run in Mount Maunganui, New Zealand. Challenge yourself with 3km, 6km, or 12km routes along the picturesque Marine Parade Coastal pathway, as the sun rises over the stunning beaches and rugged cliffs. This event offers a truly unique running experience for all levels.
