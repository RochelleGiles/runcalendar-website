Course

Two options, a 10 km Challenge and a 5 km Fun Run, both starting at the base of Mount Marlay in Stanthorpe and climbing the mountain, given as 919 m above sea level at an average 8% gradient. Bib collection from 5:30am, 10 km starts at 7:00am and 5 km at 7:30am. Surface and aid stations are not described.

About

The Mt Marlay Fun Run is a must-do event for runners in Stanthorpe, Queensland. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Granite Belt region, this community race is part of the renowned Stanthorpe Apple & Grape Harvest Festival, offering a unique opportunity to experience the local culture and natural beauty. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this event promises an unforgettable running adventure.
