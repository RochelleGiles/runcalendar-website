About

The Starlight Stroll is a glow-in-the-dark themed 5K and 1 mile run/walk/roll event for all ages and abilities, held at dusk as a fundraiser for Winona ORC. Participants are encouraged to bring glow sticks and enjoy a health-promoting community event starting at 6:05 p.m. at Lake Park Lodge.
