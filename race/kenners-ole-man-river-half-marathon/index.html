About

Kenner's Ole Man River Half Marathon, 10K and 5K follow the Mississippi River levee path in Kenner, Louisiana, just outside New Orleans. The flat, paved route offers open river views along Jefferson Parish's levee trail, finishing near the Kenner riverfront.
