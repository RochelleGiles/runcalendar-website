About

Explore the stunning island of Oleron as you challenge yourself on the Ultra Trail de l'Île d'Oléron. This scenic trail running event takes participants through the diverse landscapes of this French coastal region, from rugged cliffs to lush forests. Runners of all levels will be captivated by the stunning natural beauty that unfolds along the course.
