Course

A trail loop based at the Red Gum camp site, Euroka Clearing, in Blue Mountains National Park at Glenbrook. The course opens with a lap of the clearing, climbs the Goat Track to Mount Portal Lookout, drops onto single track at Camp Fire Creek and runs on to Red Hands Cave and Nepean Lookout before returning on fire trails. Distances are 25km, 34km and 42.2km, with five aid stations.

About

The Glenbrook Marathon is a trail race through Blue Mountains National Park, run by Running Wild NSW, with 25km, 34km and full marathon distances on rugged bushland trails known as a Blue Mountains trail-running favourite.
