Course

A single clockwise lap of the Tan Track in Melbourne, starting and finishing at the Pillars of Wisdom in Kings Domain Gardens. The course is 3.827 km on the Tan's gravel path with distance markers every 250 m. The run starts at 10:30am, with check-in from 8:30am.

About

The Big Melbourne Kilt Run is a unique and spirited event held as part of the annual Melbourne Tartan Festival. Runners of all levels are invited to don their finest kilts and race through the streets of this vibrant Australian city, celebrating Scottish heritage and community. This one-of-a-kind running experience offers a fun and festive atmosphere for participants.
