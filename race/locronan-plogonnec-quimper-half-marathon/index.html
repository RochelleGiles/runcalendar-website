About

The Locronan Plogonnec Quimper Half Marathon is a scenic and historic running event through the beautiful Brittany countryside. Runners will wind their way past charming villages, medieval churches, and stunning coastal landscapes on this challenging yet rewarding course. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply seeking an unforgettable running adventure, this event promises an unparalleled experience.
