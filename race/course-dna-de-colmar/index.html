About

The Course Dna De Colmar is a must-experience running event in the historic city of Colmar, France. Organized by the renowned LOnZA team, this race offers runners a unique opportunity to explore the charming streets and stunning architecture of this picturesque Alsatian town. With a route designed to showcase the city's captivating DNA, this event promises an unforgettable running adventure for participants of all levels.
