Course

A USATF-certified 5K winding through the slightly hilly paths of Lisle Community Park, lit gently by luminaria. The 5K run/walk starts at 6:45 p.m. from the Sea Lion Aquatic Park car park, with a 50-yard tot dash and a half-mile youth run setting off from other points in the park.
