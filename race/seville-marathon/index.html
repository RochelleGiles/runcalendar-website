Course

A single-lap road course through the streets of Seville, with the start and finish in the city. The organiser states the route has only 38 turns and 10 metres of elevation gain, making it exceptionally flat. The field is capped at 20,000 runners.

About

The Seville Marathon is a must-run event for any avid runner, set against the stunning backdrop of Spain's historic city of Seville. Boasting a flat, fast course that winds through the city's picturesque streets and landmarks, this marathon offers an unforgettable running experience for participants of all levels. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply soaking in the vibrant atmosphere, the Seville Marathon is an event you won't want to miss.
