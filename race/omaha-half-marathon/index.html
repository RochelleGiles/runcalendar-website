Course

Starts and finishes at Rahui Te Kiri Reserve, Omaha, with the start line on Taumata Road at Mangatawhiri Drive. The 21km is a modified loop of the southern peninsula plus an out-and-back to Point Wells. Surfaces include sealed road, concrete dune paths, about 2km of beach sand, a 150m boardwalk and 700m of gravel on the Matakana Cycle Trail. Four drink stations at 2.6km, 5.9km, 10.5km and 19.2km.

About

The Omaha Half Marathon starts from Rahui Te Kiri Reserve in the beachside settlement of Omaha, north of Auckland. The 21km course loops the southern peninsula and adds an out-and-back to Point Wells, mixing sealed road, dune paths, about 2km of beach sand and gravel on the Matakana Cycle Trail. Shorter 10km, 5km and 2km events are also held.
