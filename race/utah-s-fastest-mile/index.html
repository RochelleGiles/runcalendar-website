About

Utah's Fastest Mile is back—and it's faster than ever. Hosted in beautiful North Ogden, this point-to-point downhill mile takes runners down 1050 E with a blazing 350 feet of elevation drop. Guided and secured by North Ogden Police, this is a rare, safe, fully-supported opportunity to fly.
