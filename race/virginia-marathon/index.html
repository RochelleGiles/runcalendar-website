Course

Run on paved roads with some gravel sections through the horse country of Loudoun and Fauquier counties, past farms and rolling open countryside. The marathon and the 19.65-mile Merikos take in Bull Run Mountain and carry substantial climbing; the half, 10K and 5K are gentler.

About

The Virginia Marathon is run through the horse and wine country around Middleburg in Loudoun and Fauquier counties. Alongside the marathon it offers the 19.65-mile Merikos, a half marathon, a 10K and a 5K on rolling paved and gravel roads.
