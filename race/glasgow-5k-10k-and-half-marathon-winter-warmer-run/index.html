Course

A flat lapped course starting and finishing at Nelson's Monument in Glasgow Green. The 5K is one lap, the 10K two laps, and the half marathon four laps plus an additional 1.1km section. The route is mainly on surfaced paths with one short trail section, and is wheelchair and buggy friendly.

About
