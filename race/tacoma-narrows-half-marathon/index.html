Course

An out-and-back half marathon from Cheney Stadium along the paved Scott Pierson Trail, over a pedestrian bridge across Highway 16 and through the Skyline neighbourhood before crossing the Tacoma Narrows Bridge and returning to finish with a lap of the stadium warning track. Aid stations sit roughly every 2 miles. The 5K and 10K share the start, finish and stadium lap.

About

A half marathon, 10K and 5K along Tacoma, Washington's waterfront, tracing Puget Sound near the Tacoma Narrows Bridge. Organized by the Tacoma City Marathon Association, the course showcases views across the Narrows toward the Kitsap Peninsula.
