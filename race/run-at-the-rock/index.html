Course

Loop course starting and finishing at Cedarock Park, Burlington. The first 0.6 miles is wide road, gravel and paved trail before the route turns onto forested single track and wide equestrian trails, moderately technical with roots, rocks and climbs. The half marathon runs two loops; the 5K and 10K run one. Aid stations sit every 1 to 2 miles.

About

A trail running event held annually at Cedarock Park since 2005, featuring 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon distances on single-track and equestrian trails. Participants receive a race hoodie, hot soup, live music, and compete in an awards ceremony with giveaways.
