About

The Orcas Island 100 is a Rainshadow Running race on Orcas Island, Washington, covering 100 miles as four 25-mile loops from Camp Moran with roughly 26,000 ft of climbing. Entry is first come, first served, and runners have 36 hours to finish, supported by 15 aid stations.
