Course

Starts at the Bromsgrove end of Timberhonger Lane on Whitford Road and follows the lane for about 3 km, then takes in two 2 km loops before climbing back to Sanders Park and finishing with a run across the parking field. A picturesque country-lane road course with a couple of testing hills.

About

The Timberhonger 10K Road Race is a popular running event in the United Kingdom. The course takes runners through scenic countryside, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for participants. With a vibrant atmosphere and a supportive community, this event is a must-attend for runners of all levels.
