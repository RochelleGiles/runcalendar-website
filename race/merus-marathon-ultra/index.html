Course

Trail courses inside Merus Adventure Park in Palo Duro Canyon, Texas, running over sand, sandstone, red dirt, rock, stone and clay. The canyon rim stands at 3,412ft and the park's low point at 2,500ft, with individual climbs generally between 100 and 500ft. Distances run from a youth trail run up to 52.4 miles.

About

Set against the stunning backdrop of the Palo Duro Canyon, our marathon offers an unforgettable race experience through some of nature's most breathtaking landscapes. From towering cliffs to panoramic vistas, each mile is an adventure, combining scenic beauty with challenging terrain. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply soaking in the natural surroundings, this marathon is about pushing your limits while connecting with the outdoors and fellow runners.
