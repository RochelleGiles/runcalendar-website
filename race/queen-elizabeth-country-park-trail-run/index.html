Course

Starts and finishes inside Queen Elizabeth Country Park on its hard-packed off-road trail network. The route climbs out through ancient woodland at Head Down to meet the South Downs Way, loops back via the Milky Way trail and crosses War Down on tree-lined gravel tracks. Water and on-course refreshments are provided. Three distances of 21.6 km, 15.2 km and 7.9 km.

About

Explore the stunning trails of the Queen Elizabeth Country Park in the heart of the United Kingdom at this exciting off-road running event. Offering a Half Marathon, 15km, and 8km distance, runners of all levels can challenge themselves on the scenic, undulating courses through ancient woodland and open countryside. This is a must-do event for trail enthusiasts seeking a scenic and rewarding running experience.
