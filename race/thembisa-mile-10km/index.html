Course

A road race starting and finishing at Olifantsfontein Sports Ground on the edge of Tembisa, with a 07:00 start. The programme pairs a 10km road race with a one-mile elite street race, plus race walk and junior categories. It carries one of the larger prize purses on the Gauteng road calendar.

About

The Thembisa Mile & 10km is a community road race held at Olifantsfontein Sports Ground near Midrand, Gauteng, combining a fast street mile with a competitive 10km and a significant prize purse for top finishers.
