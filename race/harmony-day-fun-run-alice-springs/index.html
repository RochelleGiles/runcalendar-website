Course

A free, untimed out-and-back run and walk starting on the Simpsons Gap footpath across from Flynn's Grave, west of Alice Springs, followed by morning tea. Participants are encouraged to wear orange or national dress to mark Harmony Day.

About

Held each year on Harmony Day, this Alice Springs fun run and walk celebrates the town's cultural diversity. Organised by the Alice Springs Running and Walking Club, it is open to runners and walkers of all abilities.
