Course

Starts and finishes at Strictly Running in Columbia, routing through the Shandon neighbourhood and Historic Melrose. The certified 5K is mostly flat with one hill at the start and is professionally timed. An untimed one-mile community walk is held alongside it.

About

The March for Meals 5K and 1-Mile Senior Walk is now past its 22nd running in Columbia. The event raises funds for Senior Resources and Meals on Wheels of Richland County.
