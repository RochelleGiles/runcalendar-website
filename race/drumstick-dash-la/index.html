About

Drumstick Dash LA is a Thanksgiving Day run and walk in North Hollywood's NoHo Arts District, raising money for Hope the Mission. The 10K and 5K start together at 8:30 from Chandler Boulevard, with the Lil Gobbler 1K kids' run at 7:45. 2027 is the 12th edition.
