About

The Inverness Half Marathon takes runners on a flat, scenic on-road route through the picturesque Highland capital of Inverness, United Kingdom. Boasting stunning views of the River Ness and historic landmarks, this event offers a scenic challenge for runners of all levels. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to experience the beauty of the Scottish Highlands, the Inverness Half Marathon is not to be missed.
