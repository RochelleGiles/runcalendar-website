About

The Wodonga Athletics Club 10,000m Championship is a prestigious annual event held at the Wodonga Athletics Complex. Runners of all abilities can test their endurance on the fast, flat course, with the Pearce Family Trophy awarded to the champion. This community-driven race is a must for distance running enthusiasts in the region.
