Course

Last-one-standing backyard ultra on a 6.7 km loop at the Telegraph Station Historical Reserve, Alice Springs, run anti-clockwise. The surface is mostly single track over varied outback terrain. Runners must complete each loop within the hour to continue. Shared aid stations are provided; no personal aid may be carried out on a loop and poles are not allowed.

About

The Red Centre Wringer Backyard Ultra is a unique running event held in the heart of Australia's outback. Runners will challenge themselves on a scenic course through the rugged terrain of the Red Centre, surrounded by the stunning natural beauty of the region. This event is designed to push participants to their limits, testing their endurance and mental fortitude as they compete against the clock and the elements.
