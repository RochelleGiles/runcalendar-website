About

Ronkonkoma Rotary's Run Around the Lake is Long Island's largest veteran race, a fundraiser supporting veteran causes at Lake Ronkonkoma, NY. It honors LT Michael P. Murphy and features a half marathon, 4-mile run, fun run, and virtual options with medals, t-shirts, and amenities.
