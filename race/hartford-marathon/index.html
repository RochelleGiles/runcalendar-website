Course

The marathon starts at the Connecticut State Capitol on Capitol Avenue and finishes just past the Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Arch in Bushnell Park. Runners head through West Hartford and Elizabeth Park, cross Founders Bridge into East Hartford and the rural roads of South Windsor before returning to the city. Half marathoners split off around mile 8 for a riverside route.

About

Connecticut's largest marathon starts at the State Capitol and finishes near the Soldiers & Sailors Memorial Arch in Bushnell Park, crossing the Founders Bridge into East Hartford and South Windsor along the way. A companion half marathon splits off around mile eight for a shorter riverside route, alongside a 5K that stays within downtown.
