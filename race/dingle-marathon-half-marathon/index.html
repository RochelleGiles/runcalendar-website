About

The Dingle Marathon & Half Marathon takes place on one of the most scenic courses in Ireland. The route follows the breathtaking Dingle Peninsula coastline, offering magnificent views of the Blasket Islands. Runners and walkers can enjoy the natural beauty of the Wild Atlantic Way, with the course uniquely free of vehicular traffic for this event.
