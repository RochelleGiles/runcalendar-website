Course

Repeated 7km loop with start and finish at the event precinct at Bowen Park, Barton, Canberra; longer distances are multiple laps. Water is provided at a minimum of two points on each loop. The marathon starts at 7:00am, half marathon 7:30am, and the 14km and 7km at 8:00am, with a 1:30pm cut-off.

About

The Bravehearts 777 Marathon Canberra is one leg of a seven-marathon, seven-state, seven-day series supporting child protection charity Bravehearts. Runners complete repeated 7km loops from an event precinct at Bowen Park, Barton, choosing marathon, half marathon, 14km or 7km.
