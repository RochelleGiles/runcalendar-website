Course

A monthly series rather than a single race, run on the second Thursday from March to October with evening starts between 6:00 and 6:30pm. Venues rotate around Eugene and Springfield, including Alton Baker Park, Pre's Trail, Dorris Ranch, Maurie Jacobs Park and Lane Community College. Surfaces vary by venue between paved, gravel and bark trail.

About

The Oregon Track Club hosts an annual Run/Walk series, kept interesting by varying the location in the Eugene/Springfield area, the distance (1 mile, 4K, 5K, 8K to 10K), and the surface (paved, gravel, bark trail, or hybrid). Run/Walks start at 6:00 or 6:30pm on the second Thursday of the month from March through October.
