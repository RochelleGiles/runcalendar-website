Course

Races start and finish at Wheaton City Park, a block from East Main Street in the centre of the town. A 5K and a one-mile course are offered on sealed town streets, with separate breast cancer survivor waves. A dinner and auction are held at the high school the evening before.

About

The Power of Pink Foundation's Walk, Run and Auction began as a Pink Night during the local rodeo before growing into an annual Walk/Run. The event has raised significant funds to support local women battling breast cancer.
