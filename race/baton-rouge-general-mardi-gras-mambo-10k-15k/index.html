About

The Baton Rouge General Mardi Gras Mambo 10K/15K is a popular annual running event that celebrates the spirit of Mardi Gras in the heart of Louisiana. Runners can choose between the 10K or 15K distances and enjoy a festive atmosphere with music, costumes, and a post-race celebration.
