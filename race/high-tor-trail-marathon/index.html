Course

Starts and finishes at Naples Community Park. The route runs through downtown Naples then into the High Tor Wildlife Management Area on the Finger Lakes Trail. Aid stations sit near miles 3, 7 and 13, with a further station at the marathon turnaround, carrying water, hydration mix, gels and snacks.

About

This half marathon and marathon trail run explores the town and trails that make the scenic backdrop of Naples, NY. Starting with a romp through the vibrant downtown, runners then climb to explore the High-Tor Wildlife Management Area. This half marathon loop for has a great mix of good warm-up terrain, challenging climbs, and groovy downhills. This community driven event has generous aid stations and is great for runners and spectating families alike.
