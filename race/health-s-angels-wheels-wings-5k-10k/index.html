About

The proceeds from the Wheels & Wings 5K & 10K will go directly toward advancing research and finding a cure for Multiple Sclerosis (MS) via Health's Angels Cycling Team. By participating in this event, you are helping to fund groundbreaking studies, innovative therapies, and vital resources that support individuals living with MS. Your contribution will empower scientists and medical professionals to make strides toward ending this disease, while also providing hope and care for those affected. Together, we can drive progress and make a meaningful difference in the fight against MS.
