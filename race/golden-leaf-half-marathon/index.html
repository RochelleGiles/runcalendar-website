Course

Point-to-point from Fanny Hill in Snowmass Village to Koch Park in Aspen, 13.3 miles over mountain trails and roads. The central Government Trail section is singletrack dirt with rocks and roots through aspen forest, starting near 8,650 ft and topping out at 9,488 ft with 1,768 ft of climbing. Two aid stations, at mile 4 and after mile 8.

About

The Golden Leaf Half Marathon is a point-to-point trail race from Snowmass Village to Aspen, Colorado, timed each September to catch the valley's turning aspens.
