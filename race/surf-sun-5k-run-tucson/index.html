Course

A flat out-and-back along the Rillito River in Tucson, starting and finishing at Michael Perry Park on South Pantano Parkway at 8:30am. Water stations sit roughly every 1.5 miles. A virtual option is offered alongside the 5K.

About

The Surf & Sun 5K brings beach vibes to Tucson's winter with a scenic course along the Rillito River at Curtis Park. Participants receive custom finisher medals and age group awards despite the chilly weather.
