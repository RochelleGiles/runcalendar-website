Course

Trail courses at South Kitsap Regional Park, Port Orchard. The 5K is one full loop through wooded areas of the park, passing the skate park, batting cage, playground and live steamer trains, and is described as mostly flat. The 1 Mile is a similarly flat wooded trail route. Chip timed, start and finish in the park.
