About

Lace up your running shoes and join the Bunbury Runners Club for their annual Australia Day Fun Run in Western Australia. This scenic 5km run/walk and 10km run offers a chance to celebrate the country's national holiday while taking in the picturesque coastal views of Bunbury. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just starting out, this community-focused event promises a fun and festive atmosphere for all.
