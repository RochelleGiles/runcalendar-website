About

Top Form Athletics Club's coastal road race along Victoria Road. The 30 km starts at Camps Bay High School at 05:30 and runs out towards Hout Bay and back; the 15 km starts at Mainstream Mall in Hout Bay at 07:00 and runs one way. Both finish at the Glen Country Club in Camps Bay. Entries through iRace.
