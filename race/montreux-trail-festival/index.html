About

The Montreux Trail Festival in the heart of Switzerland offers runners a breathtaking challenge through stunning alpine scenery. Explore the trails around the picturesque town of Montreux, with routes catering to all skill levels. This popular event is a must for any trail running enthusiast looking to experience the beauty of the Swiss Alps.
