Course

A navigation challenge rather than a marked race. Teams of two are blindfolded and bussed to an unknown drop point around Christchurch, then find their own way back to the finish at Halswell Quarry — no course, no directions and no GPS. Roughly 21km depending on the route a team picks, capped at 100 teams.

About

A unique team-based running adventure challenge where pairs of blindfolded runners are dropped at mystery locations around Christchurch and must navigate approximately 22.5-30km to the finish line at Halswell Quarry without maps, GPS, or navigation devices. Teams must stay together and rely on basic awareness and teamwork to complete the race.
