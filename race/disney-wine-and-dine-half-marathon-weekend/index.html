Course

A 13.1 mile route through Walt Disney World Resort that runs through the World Showcase at EPCOT, with Disney characters and entertainment along the way. The weekend also offers a 5K and a 10K. Half marathon finishers get after-hours access to the post-race party at the EPCOT International Food and Wine Festival.

About

Disney Wine & Dine Half Marathon Weekend is a nighttime rundisney event at Walt Disney World in Orlando, Florida, finishing inside Epcot during the Epcot International Food & Wine Festival. Distances include a 5K, 10K, and half marathon.
