Course

A mountain trail 100K starting and finishing at Willamette Pass Ski Area, 70 miles south-east of Eugene in the Oregon Cascades. The course summits Fuji Mountain at 13.7 miles for the first view of Waldo Lake and climbs Maiden Peak above 7,800 feet. Aid stations sit 4.9 to 7.5 miles apart, with three covering the remote final 24 miles.

About

A 100K trail ultramarathon near Oakridge, Oregon, circling Waldo Lake in the Cascade Mountains' Waldo Lake Wilderness. It's known informally as the "Where's Waldo 100K."
