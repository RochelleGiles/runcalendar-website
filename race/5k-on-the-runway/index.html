Course

A single 5K run on runway 10/28 at Tampa International Airport, Florida, starting at 8:00 am. The grooved concrete runway surface is flat but demanding, and the organisers warn it is very hard going for a manual wheelchair. The field is capped at 2,000 and chip timed.

About

TPA's annual 5K on the Runway benefits United Way Suncoast. UWS elevates early learning, energizes middle school and high school students and empowers families through financial stability. We hope you'll give back to the Tampa Bay community by joining us on the runway.
