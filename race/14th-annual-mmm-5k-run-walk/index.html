About

Are you looking for a way to get outdoors and do some good this spring? Lace up your tennis shoes and join MMM as they host the 14th Annual Mary Miller Memorial 5K Walk/Run. The event will take place Saturday, April 26 at 9 a.m. at Miami Meadows Park in Milford. Funds raised through this event will be used to support the MMM food pantry, directly impacting families in Greater Milford and Clermont County.
