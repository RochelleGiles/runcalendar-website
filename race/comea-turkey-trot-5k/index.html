Course

An out-and-back 5K starting and finishing at the COMEA Shelter on West Lincolnway in Cheyenne. Runners follow West Lincolnway onto the Cheyenne Greenway path, turn around near Optimist Park and return to the shelter. A kids Gobble Wobble fun run for ages 3-12 sets off at 8:45 AM ahead of the 9:00 AM 5K start.

About

The COMEA Turkey Trot 5K and Kids' Gobble Wobble is a Thanksgiving-season fun run through Cheyenne, Wyoming, raising funds for COMEA House & Shelter, which provides shelter and support to people experiencing homelessness.
