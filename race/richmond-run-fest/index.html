Course

Marathon, half marathon and 10K all start inside Kew Gardens, with the half and marathon finishing at a music-festival finish in the heart of Richmond upon Thames. Courses are England Athletics certified and qualify for London and Boston. The 10K runs on the Saturday; the marathon, half and Kids & Family Mile on the Sunday.

About

The Richmond RUN-FEST is a multi-day running event set within the Royal Botanical Gardens. Runners can enjoy scenic routes passing historical sites like Ham House, Richmond Palace, Teddington Lock, and the River Thames, with courses that are flat and traverse path, road, gravel, and grass terrains.
