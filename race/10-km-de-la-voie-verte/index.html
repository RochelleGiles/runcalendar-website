About

The 10 Km De La Voie Verte offers runners a scenic journey through the beautiful countryside of Haute-Loire. Participants will experience a unique route along the Voie Verte, crossing the Sarcenas viaduct with panoramic views and traversing a 300-meter illuminated tunnel. The event welcomes both experienced runners and novices, providing a memorable and inclusive running experience.
