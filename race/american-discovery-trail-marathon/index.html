Course

The marathon is point-to-point from Palmer Lake Regional Park to America the Beautiful Park near downtown Colorado Springs, following the New Santa Fe Trail through Monument and the Air Force Academy before joining the Pikes Peak Greenway. The half marathon and 10K are out-and-back from America the Beautiful Park along the Greenway. Surfaces mix concrete, asphalt and crushed granite, with soft shoulders beside some concrete.

About

The American Discovery Trail Marathon is a trail race in Colorado Springs, Colorado, following sections of the cross-country American Discovery Trail. Runners choose the 10K, half marathon, or full marathon distance on natural-surface trail.
