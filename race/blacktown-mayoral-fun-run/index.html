About

The Blacktown Mayoral Fun Run in Sydney's Blacktown, NSW, includes 2.5km and 5km distances. Based in Nurragingy Reserve, the course winds through natural landscapes, the Aboriginal heritage garden and the Chang Lai Yuan Chinese gardens, with prizes for best dressed and finisher medals for all participants.
