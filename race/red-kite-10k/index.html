Course

A single-lap off-road trail 10K starting and finishing at Dipton Jubilee Centre near Stanley, County Durham. The route twists through ancient woodland, is rocky underfoot in parts and includes a stream crossing, with a short section of faster path. The course is fully marked and marshalled.

About

A challenging off-road 10K trail race at Dipton near Stanley, County Durham, hosted by Derwent Valley Trail Runners. The route runs through ancient woodland on rocky, twisting trails and includes a stream crossing, with entries open to runners aged 18 and over.
