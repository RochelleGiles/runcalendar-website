About

The Greenway Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging race that winds through the picturesque countryside. Runners will enjoy stunning views as they tackle this 13.1-mile course, which showcases the natural beauty of the region. Whether you're an experienced half-marathoner or looking for your next big running adventure, the Greenway Half Marathon is an event you won't want to miss.
