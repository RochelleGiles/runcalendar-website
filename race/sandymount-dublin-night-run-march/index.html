About

The Sandymount Dublin Night Run offers a unique running experience under the city lights. Runners can choose between a 5km single lap or a 10km double lap course, making it suitable for all fitness levels. This annual event in Ireland provides an exciting opportunity to explore the streets of Dublin at night.
