About

A 4-day trail running stage race through Tasmania's wild landscapes, including coastal trails, alpine peaks, ancient rainforests, and dramatic sea cliffs. Runners can choose between a longer 'Devil' course or a shorter 'Quoll' course to explore these diverse environments.
