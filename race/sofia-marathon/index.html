About

The Sofia Marathon is a renowned running event set against the stunning backdrop of Bulgaria's capital city. Participants can look forward to a challenging course that winds through the city's historic streets and picturesque landmarks, offering a unique experience for runners of all levels. With a rich history and a growing reputation, the Sofia Marathon is a must-do event for any avid marathoner.
