Course

A road course through downtown Cincinnati, running along the banks of the Ohio River and through the Transit Center beneath Second Street. The 5K and 10K start together and split on Freedom Way at about mile 3. Water stops are on course.

About

The Cincinnati Cyclones are proud to present the Frozen 5K/10K to benefit the Cincinnati Cyclones Foundation. Each year, thousands of brave runners take to the streets in support of youth hockey programs in our community. Our unique race takes you on a course along the Banks of the Ohio River and through Cincinnati's Transit Center underneath second street!
