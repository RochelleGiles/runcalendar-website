Course

Two point-to-point road courses finishing together at Rawhiti Domain in New Brighton. The 12km starts from Latimer Square in central Christchurch; the 6km starts from Porritt Park. Both are run or walked, and the event finishes at the beach end with a festival atmosphere.

About

Every year, thousands of people come together to have fun & get active running City2Surf Christchurch's epic course! Whether you're here to challenge yourself, have fun with friends, or soak in the amazing atmosphere, this is the perfect way to get active and celebrate our incredible community.
