Course

Lapped trail race in Brazos Bend State Park with start and finish at the Elm Lake Pavilion. The course is 99.9% flat and mixes hard crushed rock with soft hiking trails under tree canopy and smooth horse trails. Distances are 100 miles, 100km, marathon and half marathon.
