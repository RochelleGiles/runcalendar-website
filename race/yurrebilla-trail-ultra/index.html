Course

A point-to-point trail run across the western face of the Adelaide Hills, starting at Belair Railway Station and finishing at Foxfield Oval in Athelstone. The route links a series of national parks and conservation reserves below the Mt Lofty summit, using bush single track with some fire tracks and access roads. Drop bags can be left at Cleland Conservation Park and Norton Summit.

About

The Yurrebilla 56km and 28km trail ultras cross a series of national parks and conservation reserves in the Adelaide Hills, taking in gorges, waterfalls and wildflowers within 12km of the Adelaide CBD. The South Australian Road Runners Club-run course uses narrow bush trails throughout.
