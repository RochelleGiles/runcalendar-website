About

This event features two races - the 20km Trail de Figuerolles with 700m of elevation gain, and the 10km Ronde de St Mitre Les Remparts (mini trail) with 300m of elevation gain. The courses are 100% off-road, taking runners through the scenic landscapes of Provence, including wild beaches, wooded areas, and garrigue. This is a great introduction to trail running with technical sections and hills that allow participants to admire the beautiful Provençal scenery.
