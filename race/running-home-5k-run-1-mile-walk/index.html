About

Join us for Zukin Realty's Running Home 5K! Bring the entire family for this fun event and enjoy a spring morning in the West Chester Borough! Running Home 5K seeks to raise community awareness and financial support for North Star of Chester County. North Star is a 501(c)3 non profit organization that seeks to help single working parents towards stability and financial independence.
