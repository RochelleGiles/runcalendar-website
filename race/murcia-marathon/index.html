About

The TotalEnergies Marathon Murcia Costa Cálida is a stunning seaside running event set against the picturesque backdrop of Spain's sunny Murcia region. Boasting a fast, flat course that winds through historic city streets and along the stunning Mediterranean coastline, this marathon offers runners an unforgettable experience and the chance to explore the vibrant culture of this beautiful part of the country.
