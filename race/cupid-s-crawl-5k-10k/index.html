About

Celebrate love and fitness at Greenville's sweetest race! Run solo, with your sweetheart, or as a team in our 5K or 10K. Every finisher earns a medal! Both courses start and finish at Elm Street Park and are USATF-certified. Proceeds support Special Olympics NC and Law Enforcement United, making every step count for a great cause.
