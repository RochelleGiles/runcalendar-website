Course

Both the 5K and the 10K start and finish at DuSable Harbor, 111 N Jean Baptiste Pointe du Sable Lake Shore Drive, Chicago. The organiser states the courses run entirely on the Chicago Lakefront Trail and sidewalks, following the waterfront rather than city streets. A Kids Dash is held alongside.
