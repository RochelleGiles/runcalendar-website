About

The Berlin Road Race Generalprobe is a running event that serves as a dress rehearsal for the spectacular BMW BERLIN-MARATHON. The half marathon on a 2-lap course through Berlin's neighborhoods promises 21 km of adrenaline and is the ultimate test of marathon preparation. Runners can also choose the 10.5 km quarter marathon for a more relaxed late summer afternoon. The event offers a fun atmosphere for the whole family, with the Bambini races for kids between 500 and 1,000 meters.
