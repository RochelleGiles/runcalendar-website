Course

A 5km loop starting on Park Road by the Noosa Heads Surf Club, heading toward the National Park and returning via the boardwalk to Noosa Main Beach, then out toward the river mouth before finishing back at the surf club. Sealed road and boardwalk throughout, with one water aid station at the halfway point near the Groyne.

About

Noosa Breakfast Fun Run is a 5km community event held in Noosa Heads as part of the Noosa Triathlon festival week. It offers a relaxed morning run through the resort town for locals and visitors ahead of the main triathlon weekend.
