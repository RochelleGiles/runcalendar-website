Course

Trail racing through St. Croix State Park and the Kettle River valley, crossing forest, riverbank and prairie on a largely flat course. The 50K and longer routes climb a historic fire tower mid-race — twice for the 100K — with a vertigo option for anyone who would rather not. Cut-offs are generous, and food and camping are on site.

About

Fire Tower Trail Races exist to help you achieve your goals in a safe, welcoming, and accessible running event! We have generous cut offs, a flat course, food, camping, and friendly smiles. If you're new to trail running, or you've been around and looking for a small old-school style event Fire Tower is for you.
