Course

A challenging but runnable course on canopied single track through the trail system of Fort Clinch State Park, circling behind the historic fort. Starts are staggered, with the 50K at 8:00am, the 30K at 10:00am and the 10K at 1:00pm, and all runners must be finished by 7:00pm.

About

The Florida Roots Trail Series stops at Fort Clinch State Park on Amelia Island, sending runners along sandy coastal trails past the historic Civil War-era fort in Fernandina Beach. Racers choose 10K, 30K, or 50K, with the longer distances built from repeated loops of the park's trail network.
