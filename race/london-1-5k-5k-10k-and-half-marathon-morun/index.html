Course

Starts and finishes at East Sheen Gate in Richmond Park and laps the park on mainly tarmac paths. The course is hilly: one lap for the 5k, two for the 10k and four for the half marathon. A 1.5k is also offered.

About

MoRunning brings everyday people together with a focus on inspiring you, your family, your kids and your friends to have the best time running! When you MoRun, you will be inspired to achieve your goals, run with a smile on your face, meet incredible people, and help raise awareness and fundraising for the Movember charity.
