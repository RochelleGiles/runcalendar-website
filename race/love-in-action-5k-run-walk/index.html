About

The McGarry "Love in Action" Foundation, Inc. was created in honor of Kris and Joe McGarry, who were foster parents in Nassau County for over thirty years. The foundation seeks to raise awareness and funds to set up foster children for success after aging out of the system, as well as provide financial support to families dealing with cancer diagnoses.
