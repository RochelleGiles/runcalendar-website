Course

Starts and finishes at Memorial Park on Woodward Avenue in Royal Oak, Michigan. A chip-timed 5K run/walk sets off at 9:00am, with a kids fun run at 9:45am and a virtual option over the same distance. The organiser publishes a course map but does not describe the route or surface.

About

The Lucky Loop 5K celebrates the return of baseball season, raising funds and awareness to promote baseball at the Lucky Corner in Royal Oak, Michigan. In-person participants are entered into a raffle for door prizes.
