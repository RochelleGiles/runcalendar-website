Course

The 5K starts at the Broadway entrance of Toledo Zoo, runs through the neighbourhoods west of the zoo, then returns onto zoo grounds via the Tembo Trail to finish in the Main Plaza. A fun run for ages 12 and under is held entirely within the grounds, as is a non-competitive, ADA-accessible walk starting near the polar bear fountain.
