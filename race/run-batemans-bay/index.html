About

Run Batemans Bay is part of the vibrant Batemans Bay Triathlon Festival, offering runners a scenic coastal course through the picturesque town of Tomakin, New South Wales. With stunning ocean views and a supportive community atmosphere, this event is a must-do for runners seeking a memorable and picturesque running experience.
