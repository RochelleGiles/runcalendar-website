Twin Cities Twilight Relays

Course

A four person team relay in which each runner completes an identical 3.25 mile loop of Bde Maka Ska, about 13 miles per team. The start and finish are at Thomas Beach on Bde Maka Ska, with a relay festival at the venue. The race starts at 6:00pm.
