About

Grand Mesa Ultras are high-elevation trail races on the largest flat-topped mountain in the world, featuring technical rocky ridges, smooth single track, wildflowers, meadows, lakes, streams, and forests. Races include 50M, 55K, and 30K distances with stunning views from Crag Crest summit at 11,200 feet.[3]
