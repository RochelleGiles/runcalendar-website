About

The White River 4k and 8k are both great events for the casual snow shoe enthusiast to the endorphin junkie racer. Both events begin and end at the White River Sno-Park between Government Camp and Mt Hood Meadows on Hwy 35. The 4k is a single loop traveling through the upper White River canyon. The 8k is 2 loops on the same course. Warm snacks and beverages post race with medals, awards and x-dog raffles of unknown hijinks.
