About

The Royal Gorge Groove Trail Runs follow the rim of the Royal Gorge some 900 feet above the Arkansas River, starting and finishing at Royal Gorge Ranch and Resort near Cañon City, Colorado. Distances run from 5K up to 50K, with aid stations spaced along the longer courses.
