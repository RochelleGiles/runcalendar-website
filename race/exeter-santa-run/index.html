About

The Exeter Santa Run welcome participants of all ages and abilities to lace up their running shoes, put on a Santa Suit, and run together for a jolly good cause. Based from Princesshay Square in the city centre, there are two distances available for families to come together, celebrate the festive season and support charitable activities in the community.
