Course

Trail race on the North Shore Trail along the shore of Lake Grapevine. The singletrack ranges from soft dirt paths to technical, rocky sections, and the finish follows the lake bank before a steep wooden staircase. Distances are 15K, 30K and 50K.

About

The Rockledge Rumble is a trail ultramarathon on the North Shore Trail at Rockledge Park, on the shore of Lake Grapevine, Texas. Runners choose between 15K, 30K, and 50K distances along wooded, rocky lakeside trails.
