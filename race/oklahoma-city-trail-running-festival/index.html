Course

Run on singletrack at Bluff Creek Park in Oklahoma City, with short punchy climbs, exposed tree roots and wooded terrain. Longer distances repeat a 10.2-mile loop with about 50 feet of gain each; the 5K uses its own loop. Three aid stations serve the course, including one at the start/finish. The event is cupless, so runners carry their own bottles.

About

The Oklahoma City Trail Running Festival takes place on singletrack trails around Lake Stanley Draper in Oklahoma City. Runners can choose 5K, 15K, 35K, or 50K distances through the wooded lakeside terrain.
