About

The Cracovia Marathon, held since 2002, is the most important running event in Krakow and the largest spring marathon race in Poland. The 42.195 km course attracts runners from all over the world, making it one of the most international Polish marathons with a high participation of foreign athletes. The Cracovia Marathon is part of the Gold Label Series 42 circuit.
