About

Waddle, run, or sprint your icicles off at the Penguin Pace 5K! Embrace the chill and join us for a fun winter run that'll warm your heart (and maybe your toes). Whether you're racing for time or just along for the frosty fun, this cool-weather tradition is the perfect way to shake off winter blues. Costumes and penguin pride encouraged! Then stick around for our Hot Cocoa Party — the sweetest way to celebrate your finish!
