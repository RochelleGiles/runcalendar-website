Course

A flat, fast 5K road race through Columbia's Shandon neighbourhood, starting and finishing at Strictly Running on Devine Street. Water stops are placed on course, with water, Gatorade, bananas, grapes and strawberries at the finish. A non-competitive Ice Cream Youth Run, one turn and no hill, is held alongside.

About

A 5K road race held in the evening in Columbia, SC, featuring an Ice Cream Youth Run for younger participants. The event includes cash prizes for top finishers meeting specific time standards and concludes with an award ceremony and refreshments.
