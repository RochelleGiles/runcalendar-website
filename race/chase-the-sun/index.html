Course

A summer evening trail race at The Blue Pool in the Dorset Purbecks, on ancient woodland trails with hills and castle viewpoints along the ridges, lapping the water at the start and finish. The 7-mile has one aid station at about 5 miles; the 3-mile is flatter and takes in less of the castle. Food, music and a drink by the water afterwards.

About

Chase the Sun is an exhilarating mid-week evening trail race set against the picturesque backdrop of The Blue Pool in Dorset. Runners can choose between a challenging 7-mile or a more accessible 3-mile course, offering a unique opportunity to experience the stunning natural beauty of the region as the sun dips below the horizon.
