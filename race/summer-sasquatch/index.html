Course

Trail races at Salt Fork State Park on a 10.4-mile loop over rolling hills: the 50K runs three loops, the 20 mile two, and the 10 mile one. The organiser warns the course is muddy and hard on shoes. The 10 mile is offered on both days of the weekend, the longer distances on the Saturday only.

About

Bring your sense of adventure and "get squatchy" with us at the Summer Sasquatch 50k, 20 & 10M trail run at Salt Fork State Park, Ohio's largest park and home of Ohio's largest creature. This challenging race will test your limits with a 10.4-mile loop course over the rolling hills of the park's hiking and bridle trails.
