Course

A fully closed-road 10K starting and finishing at the Porthcawl Pavilion on the Promenade, taking in panoramic views across Newton Bay, Trecco Bay and Sandy Bay, a loop through Trecco Bay Holiday Park, the shopping district on John Street and Rest Bay, before a sprint finish along the Promenade past the Porthcawl Lighthouse. Water stations are on course. Junior races and a toddler dash follow the main race.

About

The Porthcawl 10K takes runners on a scenic tour of the picturesque seaside town of Porthcawl in Wales. This closed-road event offers a challenging yet rewarding course, with stunning coastal views and a lively atmosphere that makes it a must-do for runners seeking an unforgettable experience.
