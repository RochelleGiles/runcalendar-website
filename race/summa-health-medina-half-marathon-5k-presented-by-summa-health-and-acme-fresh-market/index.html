Course

The half marathon and 5K follow mapped routes through Medina, both starting and finishing in town. Water and Gatorade stations are set roughly every 1.5 miles, with portable toilets on course and medical tents at the start/finish and at mile seven. The half marathon also carries a competitive walk division.

About

Run Medina is a runner-focused event that offers quality swag and race experiences while staying connected to the community. The goal is to provide the 'big race' feel in the quaint, small town of Medina, with a race hosted by volunteer runners for the runners.
