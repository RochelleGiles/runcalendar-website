About

Bryan/College Station Habitat for Humanity and the Faith in Action Committee invite you to our annual Run for the House at Lake Walk Pavilion! This 5K run/2K walk welcomes all ages and fitness levels—whether you're racing for time or just here to support affordable housing in our community. Proceeds help build safe, decent homes for local families in need.
