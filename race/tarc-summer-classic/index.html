About

This trail ultra runs mostly singletrack and gravel through forested Noon Hill and Shattuck Reservations along the Charles River in Medfield. The 50K and 40-mile distances repeat a hilly 10-mile loop with about 1,000 feet of climbing each lap, organized by the Trail Animals Running Club.
