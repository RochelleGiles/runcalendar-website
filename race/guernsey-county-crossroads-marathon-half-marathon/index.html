Course

Starts on Southgate Parkway beside the trailhead at 134 Southgate Parkway, Cambridge, Ohio, and finishes near the historic Guernsey County courthouse. Runners head through historic Cambridge and north to Northwood Cemetery on flat roads with a few hills, then join the concrete and paved Great Guernsey Trail out to Lore City and back. Marathon runners have eight hours, half marathoners four.

About

This event is meant to showcase historic downtown Cambridge, Ohio and The Great Guernsey Trail and will consist of a marathon, a half-marathon and a 5k distance. This will be Chip timed race with timing services provided by Muskingum Multisports. Proceeds raised at this event will support the Guernsey County Community Development's (CDC) Great Guernsey Trail and The Cambridge Area Chamber of Commerce. After the race join us for the Finish Line Festival courtesy of Cambridge Main Street.
