About

La Ligne droite is a popular evening race organized in the streets of Lucé, offered by the Aclam (Athlé Chartres Lucé Mainvilliers), the leading athletics club in Eure-et-Loir, in partnership with the City of Lucé. The 5 km race is bronze labeled by the FFA.
