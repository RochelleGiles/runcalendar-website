About

The Bemidji Blue Ox Marathon is a beloved annual event that brings runners together in the charming city of Bemidji, Minnesota. Participants can choose from a variety of race distances, including the full marathon, half marathon, 26K lake loop, and shorter distances, ensuring there's something for runners of all levels.
