About

MS Walk Run + Roll Sydney is a fundraising event for people living with multiple sclerosis, based at Metcalfe Park in Pyrmont. Participants walk, run or roll a 5 km or 10 km course taking in the Pyrmont harbour waterfront. The event is open to all abilities, including wheelchair users and those with prams.
