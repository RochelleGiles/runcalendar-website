Course

Roughly circular half marathon starting and finishing at Riverside Drive open space, at the junction with Ham Street in Richmond upon Thames, at 8:30am. Most of the route follows the tarmac Thames towpath through Ham, Richmond, Twickenham and Kingston, turning at Kingston Bridge, with some road running through residential streets. Four water stations at about 2.8, 6.8, 8.5 and 11.4 miles.

About

The Ranelagh Half Marathon is an annual road half marathon with a fast and flat course starting from Ham, through Richmond and Twickenham via the Thames Towpath, down to Kingston Bridge and back up into Ham. It's open to all runners, whether from a local club or running their first half marathon.
