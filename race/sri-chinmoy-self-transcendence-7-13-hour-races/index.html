Course

Fixed-time races on a 1.54-mile loop at Magnuson Park, Seattle, with the 13-hour starting at 6am and the 7-hour at noon. The lap mixes pavement, gravel and chip surface with some wooded single track and a few hills, giving stretches the feel of a trail run, and looks out over Lake Washington.

About

Our timed loop running races make it easy to pace yourself while having your liquids, food and gear are nearby, simplifying your day. All the races are in an enthusiastic, cheerful and meditative atmosphere. The 1.54-mile loop course offers views of Lake Washington and the backwoods of Seattle's most scenic Magnuson Park.
