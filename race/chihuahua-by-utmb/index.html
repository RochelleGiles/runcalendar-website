About

Chihuahua by UTMB races through the Copper Canyon region of northern Mexico, ancestral home of the Rarámuri, famed for their long-distance running culture. Courses range from the 21K Napurega to the 118K Rarámuri, crossing canyon trails and mountain terrain near Cerocahui and Batopilas.
