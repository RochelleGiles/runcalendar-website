About

The Ostend Half Marathon is a must-run event for any endurance enthusiast. Held in the picturesque coastal city of Ostend, this race offers runners a breathtaking seaside course that showcases the charming architecture and stunning ocean views. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply looking to enjoy the scenic route, the Ostend Half Marathon promises an unforgettable running experience.
