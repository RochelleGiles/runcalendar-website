Course

Three trail courses in Walyunga National Park on the Darling Scarp north-east of Perth, run entirely on the park's natural trails. Green, Blue and Black options reach their first water point at 2.1 km, 7.6 km and 12 km respectively, with more water at the finish. Courses are shaded in parts and hilly throughout.

About

Explore the stunning Walyunga National Park in Western Australia at the Wallygrunta running event, part of the Perth Trail Summer Series. Choose from three challenging course distances and immerse yourself in the breathtaking natural landscapes of this unique location. Whether you're a seasoned trail runner or new to the sport, the Wallygrunta offers an unforgettable running experience.
