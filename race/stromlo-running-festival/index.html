Course

Six distances (42.2km, 30km, 15km, 10km, 5km and a 2.5km kids run) start from an event hub at Stromlo Forest Park, a purpose-built running and cycling precinct. The marathon is a single 42.24km loop starting and finishing at the hub, taking in the eastern and northern slopes, a loop of the western side of Mt Stromlo and two ascents of the summit; it is marshalled and marked throughout.

About

Stromlo Running Festival offers marathon, 30km, 15km, 10km and 5km distances at Stromlo Forest Park, a purpose-built running and cycling precinct on the edge of Canberra. The festival partners with the Indigenous Marathon Foundation and has set record entry numbers in recent years.
