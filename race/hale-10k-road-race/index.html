Course

A fully road-closed, traffic-free 10K starting in Hale village, South Manchester, crossing the railway level crossing and running the length of the high street. The route then heads into the Ashley countryside on quiet lanes before entering the south gates of Ashley Hall to finish in the grounds. A 3K race is also held.

About

The Watersons Hale 10K is a race on closed roads starting in the picture-book village of Hale, South Manchester. The course leads runners through the village centre before heading into the picturesque countryside of Ashley, weaving through country lanes and finishing in the grounds of Ashley Hall.
