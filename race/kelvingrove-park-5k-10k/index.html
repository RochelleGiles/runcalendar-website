About

Kelvingrove Park 5K & 10K is run on the flat, closed paths of Kelvingrove Park in Glasgow's West End, near the Kelvingrove Art Gallery and the River Kelvin. Runners choose the 5K or 10K distance, part of the Up and Running Events series.
