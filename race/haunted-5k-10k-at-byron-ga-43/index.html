About

The Haunted 5K & 10K is a Halloween-themed running event held next to the Tennis Courts in Byron, GA, featuring out-and-back courses, finisher medals, and awards for best costumes. Runners must register in advance on RunSignup with packet pickup starting 30 minutes before the 8:00 AM start.
