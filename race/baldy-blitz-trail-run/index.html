Course

An out-and-back on unmarked, unsupported mountain trail. It starts at the M trailhead and climbs to the Baldy summit at 4.1 miles, then returns by the same route. Snow, mud, rain and unpredictable mountain weather are all typical. A racer shuttle runs from the Gallatin County Fairgrounds.

About

The Baldy Blitz Trail Run climbs Baldy Mountain above Bozeman, Montana, following the popular M-to-Baldy trail into the Bridger range foothills. The 8-mile out-and-back is organized by the Big Sky Wind Drinkers running club.
