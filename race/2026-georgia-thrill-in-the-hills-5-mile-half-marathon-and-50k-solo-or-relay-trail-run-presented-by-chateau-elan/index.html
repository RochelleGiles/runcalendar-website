Course

Lap course in Fort Yargo State Park, Winder, Georgia, on singletrack and doubletrack with rolling hills, roots and some technical sections. The 21K runs one lap; the 50K runs two laps plus a five-mile loop, with solo and relay divisions.

About

Thrill in the Hills is a trail race staged by Dirty Spokes Productions in Fort Yargo State Park in Winder, Georgia. Runners choose between a 50K (solo or relay), a 21K and a five-mile race on singletrack and doubletrack park trails with rolling, occasionally technical terrain.
