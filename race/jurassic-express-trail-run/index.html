Course

A point-to-point coastal trail run starting on Swanage beachfront and finishing at Corfe Castle, where runners board a private vintage steam train back to Swanage. The route runs on firm chalk paths and sandy tracks past Old Harry Rocks and Studland, with three distances of 10.5km, 16.5km and 21km climbing 273m to 473m.

About

The Jurassic Express Trail Run offers a spectacular coastal trail running experience along the stunning Jurassic Coast. Runners can choose from three distances while enjoying breathtaking views of Old Harry Rocks, Studland, and Corfe Castle. The unique event combines scenic trail running with a nostalgic vintage train ride back to the finish, creating an unforgettable running adventure.
