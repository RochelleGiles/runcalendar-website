Course

A lapped course of about 1.5km around the Barrabadeen peninsula at Tinaroo Dam, with runners passing the start line between laps. Entrants choose to run for 30 minutes or 60 minutes. The loop takes in a 100m water wade, hay bales, a gate crawl, Nuisance Hill, a beach run, flowing single track, a boulder hop, monkey bars and a slip and slide.

About

Dirty 30 Sweaty 60 is a trail running event held at Tinaroo Dam on the Atherton Tablelands in Far North Queensland. It is organised by Adventure Sport NQ, the group behind several trail races across the region.
