Course

Loop course around the Massanutten Mountains, starting and finishing in Woodstock, Virginia, through George Washington National Forest and around Fort Valley. The surface is 62% dirt and gravel road, 22% trail and singletrack, and 16% paved. Roughly 14,000 feet of climbing. Twenty-four aid stations are spaced four to six miles apart, with a medical check at Elizabeth Furnace at mile 75.

About

The Old Dominion 100 Mile Endurance Run has crossed Virginia's mountain and countryside trails since 1979, starting at the Shenandoah County Fairground in Woodstock. It is one of the sport's oldest 100-milers and part of the historic Grand Slam of Ultrarunning, with a 28-hour cutoff and a silver buckle for sub-24-hour finishers.
