About

Our mission is to enrich the lives of children by investing in programs, facilities, and services that provide children the opportunities and resources to grow, learn, and play in a safe and healthy environment. Keeping in line with the spirit of Mark, his love of the game, his love of friends and his love of activity, the MAK Memorial Fund has hosted events designed to engage the spirit of children to provide for children.
