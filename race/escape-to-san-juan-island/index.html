Course

A point-to-point half marathon starting at South Beach inside San Juan Island National Historical Park. The route climbs about 900 feet over three main ascents, at roughly miles 0 to 1.6, 3 to 5.3 and 9 to 10.8, with views over the Salish Sea and across to the Olympic Mountains.

About

Run where the sea meets the sky–at San Juan Island. The course is stunning, and the island is a wonderful place to spend a weekend. Bring your family, run the race, and then stay to explore the island and make memories together.
