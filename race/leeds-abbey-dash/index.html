Course

A single-lap 10K through central Leeds, starting on Wellington Street at 09:30 and finishing in the city centre. The route is an urban road course with water stations at intervals and fixed first aid points alongside them. Pacers are provided. Selected as the England Athletics 10K Championship event.

About

After being organised by Age UK for nearly 40 years, the Leeds Abbey Dash is now delivered by Run For All, continuing this well-loved 10K as a world-class event in the city.
