About

The Bay Run is one of Sydney's favourite community fun runs, with a scenic 7km loop and a 2km kids' run. The flat course circles Iron Cove Bay, passing through Leichhardt, Rozelle, over the Iron Cove Bridge and back through Drummoyne, Five Dock and Haberfield, bringing together families, school kids and elite runners.
