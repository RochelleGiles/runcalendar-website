About

Explore the picturesque trails of Senlis at the annual Trail de Senlis event. This challenging yet rewarding race takes runners through the stunning natural landscapes surrounding the historic French town, offering a unique blend of scenic beauty and athletic accomplishment. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, the Trail de Senlis promises an unforgettable running adventure.
