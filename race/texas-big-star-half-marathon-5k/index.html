Course

Half marathon, 5K and 1-mile fun run based at the Collin College Frisco Campus, 9700 Wade Blvd, Frisco, Texas. The half marathon starts at 7:00am and the 5K at 7:15am, with a separate wheelchair and adaptive half marathon at 6:55am. Both main courses are certified. About

The Texas Big Star Half Marathon & 5K takes place in Frisco, Texas, offering half marathon and 5K distance options. Proceeds benefit the Frisco Chamber Foundation and the Collin College Foundation.
