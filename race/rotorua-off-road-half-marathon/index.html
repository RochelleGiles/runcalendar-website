Course

Loop course starting and finishing at the Waipa Recreation Area in Whakarewarewa Forest. The route crosses a bridge onto Pohaturoa Road, passes mud pools on pine-needle trails, climbs forest roads through the Lion Trail to a high point at Red Tank Road, then descends through the Redwoods back to Waipa. The half marathon is two laps. Four aid stations on the 21.1 km, two on the 10.5 km.

About

Lace up your trail shoes and get ready for an unforgettable adventure at the Rotorua Off Road Half Marathon. This stunning event takes runners through the breathtaking Whakarewarewa Forest in New Zealand, offering a challenging and scenic course that winds through lush native bush and past volcanic landscapes. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, this race promises an exhilarating experience in one of the country's most beautiful natural settings.
