About

The World Down Syndrome Day 5K/10K/Kids Fun Run takes place the Sunday after World Down Syndrome Day each year. It celebrates all things “321” from the unique 5K race distance (3.21 miles) to registration fees, with options for everyone including a “Sleepy Sunday” virtual participation.
