About

The Ultra Trail Forillon is a captivating trail running event set against the stunning backdrop of the Forillon National Park. Runners will challenge themselves on a rugged course that winds through the park's dramatic landscapes, offering breathtaking views of the Gulf of St. Lawrence. This event is a must for adventurous trail enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable running experience.
