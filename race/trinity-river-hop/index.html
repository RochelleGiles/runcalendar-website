Course

Starts at the Fort Woof dog park lot in Gateway Park, heading east on Disposal Road onto the paved Trinity Trails beside the river. An out-and-back looped course, flat and fast, mostly concrete and asphalt: one loop for the 5K, two for the 10K and three for the half marathon.

About

The Trinity River Hop is a fun running event with bunny-themed touches along the beautiful, wildflower-lined Trinity River trails in Fort Worth. It features flat out-and-back courses starting at Fort Woof Dog Park parking lot for all fitness levels.
