About

The Verona Half Marathon, also known as the Romeo & Giulietta Run, takes runners on a picturesque journey through the historic city of Verona, the setting of Shakespeare's iconic love story. Participants can experience the charm of this romantic destination while challenging themselves with a 13.1-mile course that showcases the city's stunning architecture and scenic landscapes.
