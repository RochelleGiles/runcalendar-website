About

Le Défi des Vignes is a unique running event that combines sports and local wine heritage. Participants will run through the picturesque landscape of La Londe, traversing vineyards, streams, and forest clearings in the beautiful Provence region. The course offers runners a scenic journey through the natural terroir of the area, showcasing the local viticultural landscape.
