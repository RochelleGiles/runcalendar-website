About

A 10K road race held in Otsiningo Park in Binghamton, NY, with races held every Saturday in January. The series spans four weeks with free entry for Triple Cities Runners Club members and $10 for non-members, with pie prizes awarded to overall male, female, and non-binary winners each week.
