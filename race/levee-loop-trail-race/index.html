Course

Looping course on the Levee Loop Trail beside the Brodhead Creek, starting at the trailhead in Dansbury Park on Day Street, East Stroudsburg. Underfoot is a mix of grass, gravel and sidewalk with a short section of road. Offered as a timed 10K, a timed 5K or a 5K walk, all away at 8:00am.

About

The Levee Loop Trail Race is an annual event that takes runners on a scenic course along the Levee Trail in East Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania. Participants can choose from various distances to challenge themselves.
