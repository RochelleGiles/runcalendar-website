Course

A flat two-lap loop starting and finishing at Warland Reserve on the Esplanade, run on sealed roads and shared pathways around Encounter Bay with less than 100 m of elevation change. Aid stations with water and sports drink sit at Kent Drive, Franklin Parade, Kent Reserve and the Esplanade.

About

The Victor Harbor Half Marathon offers runners a spectacular coastal race around Encounter Bay with million-dollar views of the South Australian coastline. This popular event provides a fast, picturesque course that attracts runners looking to achieve personal best times while enjoying stunning ocean scenery. The race features multiple distance options and a supportive, friendly atmosphere that welcomes runners of all levels.
