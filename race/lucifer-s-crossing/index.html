Course

Trail loop starting and finishing at the Old Y Camp in Robert H. Treman State Park. All distances open with a rolling 1.5-2 mile wooded ascent of the Rim Trail; the 5K turns at a stone staircase and returns on singletrack. Longer distances drop through the Lucifer Falls gorge, climb the Red Pine Trail, pass the Old Mill and finish on the Finger Lakes Trail. The half runs the 10K loop twice.

About

Lucifer's Crossing takes in some of the finest scenery and flowing trails in our region. Each distance starts with a roughly 1.5 - 2 mile rolling ascent of the wooded Rim Trail. At the top of a historic and steep stone staircase 5k runners will take a left to return on the singletrack Finger Lakes Trail. 10k and Half Marathon Runners will drop down through the Lucifer Falls gorge, a majestic amphitheater. A left will take you up a steep ascent of the Red Pine Trail, looping back around for a passing of the historic Old Mill, and leaving you with a couple mile mostly descent along the flowing and fast Finger Lakes Trail Singletrack.
