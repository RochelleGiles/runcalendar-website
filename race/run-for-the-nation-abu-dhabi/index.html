About

The Zayed Charity Run's Abu Dhabi event, in its second year as Run for the Nation: a 10K, a 5K and a 3K starting at Wahat Al Karama and finishing at ERTH Abu Dhabi, organised with the Abu Dhabi Sports Council. The organisers expect more than 20,000 runners. A charity run, not a marathon, whatever the name of the series.
