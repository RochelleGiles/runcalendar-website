About

Get ready for the BCSD Pajama Run 5K. Slip into your favorite pajamas and join us for a fun-filled event to support BCSD students and educators. Proceeds from the BCSD Pajama Run will go toward Camperships: Giving BCSD students the opportunity to attend Camp KEEP, an unforgettable outdoor education experience, and Teacher Grants: Funding programs that support student achievement, technology, chess, mini-grants, and reading incentives.
