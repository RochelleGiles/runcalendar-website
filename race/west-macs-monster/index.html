Course

A trail festival run on the Larapinta Trail in Tjoritja (West MacDonnell) National Park, west of Alice Springs. Distances from 5km to 231km follow rocky, technical singletrack through ancient desert ranges on the traditional lands of the Western and Central Arrernte people. The longer events run point-to-point over multiple days.

About

The West Macs Monster is an extraordinary trail running event traversing the spectacular Larapinta Trail in Tjoritja National Park. Runners will explore one of the most breathtaking landscapes in Central Australia, challenging themselves through ancient terrain that promises a transformative experience. The event offers an opportunity to run through vast, expansive landscapes that will test runners' physical and mental limits.
