Course

A 50km women's ultra on the Llyn Peninsula in north-west Wales. The route is fully marked and marshalled with traffic management in place, and SheUltra ambassadors walk, hike and run alongside participants. Aid stations carry vegan options, sanitary products and breastfeeding facilities. There are no cut-offs and the event is run at each participant's own pace.

About

At SheUltra™, we're all about creating a fun and supportive environment while making a difference in the journey against cancer. Join us for exciting and impactful fundraising events that bring hope, strength, and support to women affected by cancer. We believe in the power of community and the importance of standing together in the face of adversity.
