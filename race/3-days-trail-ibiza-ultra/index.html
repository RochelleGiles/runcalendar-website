Course

Three-stage trail race held over consecutive days on Ibiza. Stage one is a 10 km night run, stage two offers a choice of ultra trail, marathon, half marathon or 10 km, and stage three is a 10 km daytime run. Run on the island's trails and listed on the Spanish athletics federation (RFEA) calendar.

About

3 Days Trail Ibiza is a multi-stage trail running event that explores the beautiful island of Ibiza. Runners experience three unique stages across diverse terrain, showcasing the island's natural beauty, history, and culture. The event offers multiple race distances and allows participants to discover Ibiza's stunning landscapes from coastal paths to mountain trails.
