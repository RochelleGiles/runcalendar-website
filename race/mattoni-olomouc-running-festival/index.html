About

The Mattoni Olomouc Running Festival is a popular annual running event in the historic city of Olomouc, Czech Republic. The event features a 5K race through the picturesque streets and landmarks of the city, offering runners a chance to experience the vibrant atmosphere and scenic beauty of Olomouc.
