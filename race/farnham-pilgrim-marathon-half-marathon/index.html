Course

A trail marathon and half marathon following the Pilgrims Way and North Downs Way through the Surrey Hills, starting and finishing at Myrtle's Courtyard on the Hampton Estate. The route passes five churches, an abbey, two manor houses, ponds, old mills and vineyards, reaching St Martha's Church via Pewley Down at the marathon halfway point.

About

A trail marathon and half marathon starting from Farnham, climbing into the Surrey Hills Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The route follows tracks and bridleways across the hills surrounding the town.
