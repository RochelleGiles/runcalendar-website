About

The firefighters from the La Grave de Peille fire station in the Alpes-Maritimes organize the 'Christian Risso Challenge' trail run each June, in tribute to their former fire chief, who died in the line of duty. The event offers two courses: a shorter one for walkers and beginner runners, and a more technical one for experienced trail runners with elevation gain and scenic views.
