About

The Parma Half Marathon is a popular running event that takes place in the heart of the historic city of Parma, Italy. The race offers a scenic course that winds through the beautiful streets and landmarks of this renowned city, known for its rich cultural heritage and delicious cuisine. Runners can choose from a variety of distances, including the 21 km competitive half marathon, the 10 km competitive race, and the 5 km charity walk, making it an event suitable for runners of all levels. The event also features a range of complementary activities, such as open museums and art exhibitions, creating a well-rounded weekend of sport, culture, and enjoyment for participants and spectators alike.
