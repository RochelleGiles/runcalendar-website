Course

Run through downtown Rossford, with a festival opening at 8:00am and the 5K starting at 9:00am. The course is deliberately accessible, so participants can walk, run, push or roll it, and most finish in 25 to 50 minutes. A quarter-mile Little Star Run is held for the youngest children.

About

Each season comes to an end with a celebratory 5K. This closing event gives program participants of all abilities a tangible sense of accomplishment. Crossing the finish line instills confidence through completion and is a joyful moment program participants always remember!
