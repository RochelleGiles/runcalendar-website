About

The CDA Marathon offers a Boston Marathon certified road race through the scenic paths and streets of Coeur d’Alene, Idaho, featuring stunning lake views. It includes multiple distances: Marathon with early start, Half Marathon, 10K, and 5K, starting from McEuen Park.
