About

The Cross de Chapet is a must-do event for avid runners looking to explore the scenic countryside around the historic town of Chapet. This challenging cross-country race takes participants on a scenic tour through rolling hills and picturesque landscapes, offering a unique running experience outside the typical road race. Whether you're aiming for a personal best or simply want to enjoy the beautiful surroundings, the Cross de Chapet is sure to be a highlight on any runner's calendar.
