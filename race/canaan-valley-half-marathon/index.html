Course

Run entirely within Canaan Valley State Park on park roads and paved golf-course cart paths. The half marathon covers the front nine on the first lap and the back nine on the second (about 820 ft of climb); the 10K rolls around the golf course and the 5K has short climbs near each end. A half-mile kids run is also offered.

About

A half marathon, 10K and 5K on the roads of Canaan Valley in the Allegheny Mountains near Davis, West Virginia - one of the highest valleys of its size in the eastern United States, held every spring.
