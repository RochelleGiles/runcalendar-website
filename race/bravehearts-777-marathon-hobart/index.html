About

The Bravehearts 777 Marathon Hobart is one leg of a seven-marathon, seven-state, seven-day series supporting child protection charity Bravehearts. Runners complete repeated 7km loops from an event precinct at Bellerive Beach Park, in front of Ninja Stadium, with a 7:30am start and a 2:00pm cut-off.
