Course

Two-park 10K on entirely traffic-free paths. Starts at 11am in Hyde Park, runs through Kensington Gardens where runners complete two short laps, then returns to Hyde Park to finish on the south side of the Serpentine at The Old Football Pitches. Passes Kensington Palace, the Italian Gardens, Long Water and the Peter Pan statue. UKA course accuracy certificate. A 3K Fun Run runs alongside.

About

Experience the Serpentine New Year's Day 10K Race and 3K Fun Run, a unique opportunity to welcome the new year with a scenic run through the stunning green spaces of Hyde Park and Kensington Gardens in the heart of the United Kingdom. This traffic-free event offers runners of all levels the chance to start the year with a refreshing and invigorating challenge amidst the beautiful surroundings of these iconic London landmarks.
