Course

The course uses the Boise Greenbelt path system along the Boise River. The organiser notes the river can be unpredictable and the route may be altered accordingly. About

Boise Half Marathon is an annual running event held in Boise, Idaho. The race takes place on the Boise Greenbelt, offering runners a scenic course along the Boise River.
