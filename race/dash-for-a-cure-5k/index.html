Course

Starts and finishes at the London Farmer's Market on West Dixie Street. The flat 5K runs out along Whitley Street and South Dixie Street to a turnaround at Southeast Kentucky Farm Supply, then loops the outer perimeter of Veteran's Memorial Park. A water stop in the park is passed twice.

About

The Dash For A Cure 5K at the London Farmer’s Market supports Breast Cancer Awareness and the Baptist Health Karlyle Keith Young Cancer Fund. It features a 5K Run/Walk, 1 Mile Fun Walk, FREE Kid's Dash, post-race food, prizes, and a pink-out theme.
