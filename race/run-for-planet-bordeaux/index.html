About

Run through the historic streets of Bordeaux at the annual Run For Planet event. This eco-friendly race celebrates the city's commitment to sustainability, offering runners a scenic tour past iconic landmarks while raising funds for local environmental initiatives. Lace up your shoes and join the community of passionate athletes making a difference for the planet.
