Course

A flat 5K and 1-mile road race through downtown Las Vegas. Both distances start on Fremont Street at the northwest corner of 9th and Fremont and finish on the southeast corner of the same intersection, with start and finish festivities held on the Llama Lot between 9th and 10th Streets.

About

Vegas Strong 5K/1M is a community fun run in Las Vegas held in remembrance of the victims of a mass shooting on the Las Vegas Strip. Runners and walkers of all paces join to honor survivors and first responders.
