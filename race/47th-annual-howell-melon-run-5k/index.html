About

A 5K and one-mile evening race in downtown Howell, Michigan, held as part of the Howell Melon Festival. Organised by Howell Recreation, it welcomes runners, walkers and racers using wheelchairs and other assistive devices, with a short Melon Roll for children aged two to five.
