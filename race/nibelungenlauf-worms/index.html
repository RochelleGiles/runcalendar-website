About

The Nibelungenlauf is a vibrant running event held in the historic Nibelungen city of Worms. Participants enjoy a scenic route along the Rhine River, near the Hagen monument and Kriemhild's Rose Garden. The event attracts runners from around the world, offering a unique blend of sport and cultural experience in the heart of the Rhein-Neckar region.
