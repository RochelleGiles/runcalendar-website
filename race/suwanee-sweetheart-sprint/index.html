About

The Rotary Club of North Gwinnett is pleased to host the 15th Annual Suwanee Sweetheart Sprint 5k and Fun Run. The race is a Peachtree Road Race qualifier and boasts tons of fun including, medals for each age group, fastest dog, most senior finisher and a Valentine themed costume contest. The Fun Run where the kiddos will earn their own shirt and finisher ribbon will be around the town green and take place between the 5k and awards ceremony . Come early and take part in DJ and mascot fun and a great pre-race warm up. After the race enjoy some hot chocolate, post-race snacks and some fun with your friends and family at the photo booth! This is a pet-friendly event, so bring along your furry friends too!
