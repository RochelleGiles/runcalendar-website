Course

All four distances start and finish in the meadow off Alpine Loop near Frank Kinney Park in South Eugene, with staggered starts from 10am. Runners open with a loop on the Rexius Trail then climb the Amazon Headwaters Trail to Fox Hollow Road; longer races add the Dillard Road connector, Willamette spurs and Blanton Heights. Five aid stations sit at major trailhead junctions.

About

Autumn Trails Eugene is the fifth event in the Run Big Trail Series, featuring trail races for all fitness levels on the Ridgeline Trail system near Frank Kinney Park in South Eugene. Courses are organic out-and-back routes with rolling ascents and descents, supported by five aid stations stocked with water, GU Brew, energy gels, and snacks.
