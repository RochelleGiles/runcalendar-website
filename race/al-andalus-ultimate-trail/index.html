About

The Al Andalus Ultimate Trail is an exhilarating running event that takes participants on a stunning journey through the breathtaking landscapes of the Iberian Peninsula. Immerse yourself in the rich history and culture of the region as you conquer challenging trails and soak in the picturesque vistas. This truly unique race offers an unforgettable experience for adventurous trail runners.
