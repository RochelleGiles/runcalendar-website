About

Kick off Valentine's weekend with a brisk winter run at Half Fast Half. Stay warm inside the Vadnais Heights Elementary gym for packet pickup, then choose your distance—5K, 10.5K, or a certified half marathon—on gently rolling, snowy roads. Aid stations keep you moving, and a pancake-and-sausage breakfast awaits after the finish. A classic Minnesota winter half marathon experience for every pace.
