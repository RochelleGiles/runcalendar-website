About

Dalmacija Ultra Trail® is an annual multi-distance trail running event held in the Dalmatian region of Croatia. The event features several race distances, including the challenging 104.2km Mountain DUT, the 56.2km Sea DUT, the 29.1km Ethno DUT, the 19.48km River DUT, and the 10.16km Easy DUT.
