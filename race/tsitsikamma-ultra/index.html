Course

A point-to-point 50 km trail run from Storms River Petroport to Covie in the Tsitsikamma. The route follows single-track through indigenous forest, coastal paths and gravel roads, with stream crossings and technical root and rock sections. Two fully stocked aid stations sit at 29 km and 40 km, with water points at Sleepkloof Hut and Heuningbos Hut.

About

The Tsitsikamma Ultra is a 50km trail race through the indigenous forests of the Tsitsikamma coast near Storms River, part of South Africa's Garden Route. Runners follow forest trails within this stretch of ancient Afromontane forest, one of the last of its kind in the country.
