Course

An out-and-back course from Garryowen Drive on Black Mountain Peninsula, finishing at John Cardiff Close. The route runs entirely on sealed bike and shared paths with no road crossings, circling West Basin and crossing Scrivener Dam, with views over Lake Burley Griffin. The half marathon continues past Curtin ovals to Woden before turning. Water stations sit at about 4 km and 8 km.

About

The Weston Creek Half Marathon and 10km Fun Run is hosted each year by the Weston Creek Athletics Club in Canberra. Both courses run on sealed bike and shared paths around Lake Burley Griffin and West Basin, with no road crossings. The undulating route takes in Black Mountain Peninsula, Scrivener Dam and views across the lake.
