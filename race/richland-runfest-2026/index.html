Course

Starts and finishes at Columbia Point Marina Park on the Columbia River in Richland. The half marathon and 10K use looped sections so runners pass some aid stations twice; three aid stations sit near miles 3, 4.5/12 and 6.5/10. The organiser notes it is not a flat course.

About

Richland RunFest is presented by 3 Rivers Road Runners at Columbia Point Marina Park in Richland, Washington. The event offers a half marathon, 10K, and 5K, in-person or virtually, with a unique medal for runners who complete all three distances.
