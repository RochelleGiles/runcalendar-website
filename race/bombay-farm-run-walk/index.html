Course

Run on working farms at Bombay, with the course following farm tracks and crossing paddocks. The terrain is mixed, with flat tracks, undulating ground and some hills. Distances are 5 km and 10 km, walked or run; off-road buggies are permitted on the 5 km and dogs are not allowed.

About

Escape the city and explore the scenic Bombay countryside at the annual Bombay Farm Run & Walk. This off-road adventure features 5km and 10km routes winding through rolling hills, lush farmland, and picturesque landscapes of New Zealand. Whether you're a seasoned runner or casual walker, this community-driven event promises a fun, refreshing outdoor experience for all.
