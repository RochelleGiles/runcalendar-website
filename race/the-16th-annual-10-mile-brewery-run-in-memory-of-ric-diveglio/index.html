About

The 10 Mile Brewery Run in Memory of Ric DiVeglio boasts a scenic, flat USATF certified course, and the best post race celebration on Long Island. Participants will race ten miles and then enjoy a variety of complimentary Blue Point beer, post-race food, and live music.
