About

Lokmat Maha Marathon Nashik is the Nashik leg of the multi-city Maha Marathon series run by the Lokmat media group across Maharashtra, alongside sister editions in Kolhapur, Thane and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. It offers 21K, 10K, 5K and 3K distances.
