Course

Courses tour Jekyll Island under live-oak canopies, past salt marshes, open beaches and through the Jekyll Island Club National Historic Landmark district. The 10K and 5K run Saturday, the marathon and half Sunday, and every event carries a seven-hour time limit.

About

Running on Jekyll is a one of a kind experience. All four course will showcase the natural beauty of the island and take runners past historic landmarks. Runners will pass under the canopies of the majestic live oaks, past the beauty of the salt marshes, and wide open beaches. The course will also transport runners to an earlier time when they pass through the Jekyll Island Club National Historic Landmark.
