Course

Routes of 10.8km, 15.5km and 21.3km start and finish at Plumpton Racecourse near Lewes. Each opens with a flat tree-lined section of about 3.5km before climbing onto the South Downs Way, with long descents and open views. On-course water and refreshment stations carry first aid cover.

About

Experience the stunning natural beauty of the South Downs National Park at the Sussex Downs Trail Run. This picturesque off-road event takes runners on a scenic journey through rolling hills, lush meadows, and tranquil woodland, offering a challenging yet rewarding trail running adventure. Whether you're an experienced trail enthusiast or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable day in the great outdoors.
