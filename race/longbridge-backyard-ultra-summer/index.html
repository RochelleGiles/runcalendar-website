Course

A last-one-standing backyard ultra from Church Farm, Litlington, in which runners start a new 4.2-mile (6.78 km) lap on the hour, every hour. The loop mixes single paths and farm access roads over natural chalky flint and gravel tracks; trail shoes are recommended.

About

The Longbridge Backyard Ultra is an intriguing running challenge set on private farmland in East Sussex. Runners test their endurance by completing laps of an undisclosed course, with the goal of outlasting their competitors in this unique "last person standing" format. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or looking to push your limits, the Longbridge promises an unforgettable and rewarding experience.
