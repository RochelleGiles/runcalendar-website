Course

The course starts and finishes at Happy Valley Elementary School, running a full sidewalk route out to the middle school and back. The 5K is one lap and the 10K two, with a timing mat recording a 5K split on the second lap. The route is measured by the USATF calibrated-bicycle method.

About

The Happy Valley Fun Run and Walk, now past its 46th running, is presented by the Happy Valley Elementary PTO. It's a community event for walkers and runners of all abilities that raises funds for Happy Valley Elementary School.
