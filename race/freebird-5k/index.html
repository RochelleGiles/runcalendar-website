About

Join us for the 2nd Annual Freebird 5K Run/Walk honoring the life & legacy of Charles "Charlie" O'Connell. The race features a flat/fast and scenic course at Coldstream Park/Legacy Trail, commemorative unisex sized T-shirts, 5K Run/Walk, 1 Miler (untimed) Fun Walk & Virtual Option, awards, free race photos, and more.
