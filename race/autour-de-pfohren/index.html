About

Nestled in the picturesque countryside, the Autour de Pfohren running event offers a unique and scenic route through the charming town of Pfohren. Runners can immerse themselves in the local culture and natural beauty as they wind their way through the course, making this a must-attend event for those seeking a truly memorable running experience.
