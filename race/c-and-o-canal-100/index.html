About

The C&O Canal 100 runs along the historic Chesapeake & Ohio Canal towpath, starting and finishing at Camp Manidokan near Knoxville, Maryland. Its flat profile makes it one of the more approachable courses for first-time 100-mile finishers, and it serves as a Western States 100 qualifier.
