About

A 5K run/walk and 1-mile walk event held at Washington Lake Park in Sewell, NJ, organized by the Jazmine T. Bryant Brighter Day Fund. The event supports mental health awareness and raises funds to provide scholarships and resources for those in minority communities struggling with mental health issues and substance abuse.
