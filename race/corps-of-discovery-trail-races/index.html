Course

Trail races starting and finishing at the Lewis & Clark Trailhead in Weldon Spring. The 5-mile runs the Clark Trail, the 8-mile the Lewis Trail, and the half marathon combines both loops, over hardwood-lined ridges with hollow-to-ridge climbs and river views. Aid stations are spaced through each course.

About

The Corps of Discovery Trail Races are hosted by the St. Louis Track Club on the Lewis and Clark Trails in the Weldon Spring Conservation Area, Missouri. Runners choose a 5-mile, 8-mile or half marathon over wooded ridge-and-hollow singletrack above the Missouri River.
