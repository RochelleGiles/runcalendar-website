Course

A JAAF-certified loop course starting and finishing at the Funatsuhama Hirahama parking lot in Fujikawaguchiko, circling Lake Kawaguchi and Lake Saiko beneath Mt Fuji. The full marathon starts at 9:00am and carries a 6-hour gross-time limit with cut-off points along the way.
