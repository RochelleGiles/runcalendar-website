Course

Multi-lap course starting and finishing at the SBSC clubhouse in Orleigh Park, West End, on a 5.275km loop beside the Brisbane River. Entrants run one lap (5.275km), two laps (10.55km) or four laps (21.1km); Ekiden relay teams of two to six runners share eight laps for the 42.2km marathon. All distances start at 6:00am.

About

The BRRC Ekiden Relay Marathon starts at the SBSC clubhouse in Orleigh Park, West End, on a 5.275km loop beside the Brisbane River. Runners enter 5.275km, 10.55km or 21.1km, or join a team of two to six for the 42.2km Ekiden relay over eight laps. All distances start at 6:00am.
