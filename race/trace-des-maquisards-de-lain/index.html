Course

The races are based at Oyonnax in the Ain and are run largely at night, linking maquis sites of the French Resistance: the Camp de Chougeat, the Cize viaduct, Nantua, the Echallon meadow and Lac Genin. Distances run from 7km to 100km, the 100km also as a two- or four-person relay. The organiser publishes no surface or aid-station detail.

About

La Trace des Maquisards is a night trail event based at Oyonnax in the French Ain, run across two days each February. Races range from 7km to 100km, with two- and four-person relay options over the 100km. The routes link wartime maquis sites including the Camp de Chougeat, the Cize viaduct, Nantua and Lac Genin.
