About

The Simpson Desert Ultra (SDU) is an exhilarating ultra marathon event set in the heart of the Australian Outback. Runners will embark on a challenging journey through the rugged and breathtaking landscapes of the Simpson Desert, immersing themselves in the raw beauty of this remote region. This event promises an unforgettable adventure for seasoned ultrarunners seeking an exceptional experience.
