Course

A trail festival run on the mountain paths of Table Mountain National Park, with the finish in central Cape Town. Six distances share the weekend — 100 miles, 100km, 55km, 35km, 23km and 16km — over rocky singletrack, contour paths and long climbs onto the massif. The wider event runs across race week in mid-November.

About

Ultra-Trail Cape Town is part of the Ultra-Trail World Tour, taking runners through Table Mountain National Park and along the Cape Peninsula. Distances range from 23km to a 100-mile race, crossing fynbos slopes, coastal cliffs and mountain passes above Cape Town.
