Course

A 240km point-to-point ultramarathon from Twofold Bay near Eden on the far south coast of NSW to Charlotte Pass, by way of the summit of Mt Kosciuszko. About 60 runners are accepted and have up to 46 hours to finish.

About

Coast to Kosci is a 240km ultramarathon from Twofold Bay at Eden, on the New South Wales coast, to the summit of Mount Kosciuszko, Australia's highest peak, run in stages over several days through changing terrain.
