About

The 54th edition of the Wolfgangseelauf, one of Austria's most scenic running events, will take place on October 17-18, 2026. The race offers a variety of distances, including the Salzkammergut Marathon, along the beautiful shores of Lake Wolfgang. Runners can expect a challenging yet rewarding course with stunning mountain views and a lively atmosphere.
