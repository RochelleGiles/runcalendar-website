Course

Run through the vineyards at Fulkerson Winery above Seneca Lake in Dundee, New York. A 5K run/walk loops the estate on grass and farm tracks between the vines, with a shorter 1K tasting walk offered alongside. The finish area hosts a wine tasting festival.

About

The Wicked Wine Run takes participants on a fun-filled run or walk through the scenic vines of Fulkerson Winery, a past New York Sauvignon Blanc Winery of the Year, followed by a celebration of wine, music and friends in the tasting room.
