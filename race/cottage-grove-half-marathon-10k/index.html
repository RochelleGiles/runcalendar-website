Course

A point-to-point half marathon on the paved Row River Trail, starting at Dorena School and finishing at Trailhead Park, with runners bused to the start from Bohemia Park. Net 240 ft downhill, with four aid stations roughly every 2 to 3 miles. The 10K is an out-and-back from Trailhead Park with one aid station at 3.1 miles.

About

The Cottage Grove Half Marathon & 10K takes place along the beautiful Row River Trail, a paved path through forests and meadows. The half marathon starts at Dorena School and finishes at Trailhead Park in Cottage Grove, with a net 240-foot descent.
