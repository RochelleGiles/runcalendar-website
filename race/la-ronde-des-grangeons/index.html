About

Now past its 27th running, La Ronde des Grangeons in Ambérieu-en-Bugey has grown substantially since its first year, with the help of many volunteers. The event offers various race distances, including 10 km, 16 km and 28 km, plus a festive 15 km walk, for runners of all levels on scenic routes.
