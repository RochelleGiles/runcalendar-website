Course

An out-and-back 5K starting and finishing on South Catalina Avenue in Riviera Village, between Palos Verdes Boulevard and South Elena Avenue. The turnaround is near the Elks Lodge at Catalina Avenue and Torrance Boulevard, where a water station is placed. The route runs on city streets close to the Pacific shoreline.

About

An annual Fourth of July morning race through the streets of Redondo Beach, California, pairing a timed 5K with a community walk. The event has been a local Independence Day tradition for more than three decades.
