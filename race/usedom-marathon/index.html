About

The Usedom Marathon is a picturesque running event held on the idyllic German island of Usedom. Runners can look forward to a scenic course that showcases the island's stunning natural beauty, from its pristine beaches to its lush forests. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a first-time racer, the Usedom Marathon offers a unique and unforgettable running experience.
