Course

A winter point-to-point 50K across New River Gorge National Park, starting in front of the Fayetteville courthouse and finishing at Hawks Nest State Park. The route runs high-ridgeline single track with expansive views, passes waterfalls and drops to the New River beneath the gorge bridge, sharing much of the Rim to River 100 course. Four fully stocked aid stations; 10-hour limit.

About

A 50K trail ultramarathon through New River Gorge National Park and Preserve near Fayetteville, West Virginia, held each February by Rim to River Endurance Co on gorge rim and riverside trail through the winter woods.
