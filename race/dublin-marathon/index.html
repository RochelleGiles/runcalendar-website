Course

A single loop of the city from Leeson Street: a downhill start through St Stephen's Green and the Liberties, the climb up Patrick Street to Christchurch, over the Liffey and into Phoenix Park at 6K along Chesterfield Avenue, out through Castleknock and Chapelizod to halfway at Dolphin's Barn, then Crumlin and Terenure before the long drag up Clonskeagh and Roebuck Roads to 35K. Downhill on Fosters Avenue and flat along the Stillorgan and Merrion Roads to finish at the Pepper Canister Church. Mostly flat with two real climbs, the second where it hurts.

About

The Dublin Marathon is a prestigious annual running event that takes place in the heart of Ireland's capital city. Runners will experience the vibrant atmosphere of Dublin as they navigate a scenic course that showcases the city's iconic landmarks and historic neighborhoods. With a reputation for its enthusiastic spectators and warm hospitality, the Dublin Marathon is a must-do event for runners seeking an unforgettable race experience.
