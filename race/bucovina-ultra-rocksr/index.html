About

Chase peaks in Romania's last great wilderness. From a fast 15 km dash to a relentless 114 km odyssey, six courses thread rocky ridges, a UNESCO untouched forest, medieval sheepfolds, the legendary Devil's Mill Gorge and, towering above all, the natural citadels of Lady's Rocks. Expect steep climbs, technical single-tracks and raw mountain weather. Every kilometer is a chance to forge your path through Europe's wild frontier.
