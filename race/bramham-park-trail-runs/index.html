Course

Well-established trail paths around the 1,200-acre Bramham Park estate between Leeds and Wetherby, passing the 18th-century house, its statues and woodland views. All three distances start and finish in the grounds on staggered starts, and canicross runners are welcome with one dog each.

About

A trail run through the grounds of Bramham Park, a historic stately home estate near Wetherby better known for hosting international horse trials and the Leeds Festival. The route follows parkland tracks around the estate.
