Course

Starts and finishes at The Summit Sport and Recreation Park at Mount Barker. Runners cover half a lap of the park before heading out and up the surrounding hills, including Whinery Hill, on mixed farm and reserve terrain; the 5 km route turns at Ngeringa Vineyards. Distances are 21.1 km, 10.55 km, 5 km and a free kids' dash.

About

Conquer the Summit is a community running event set in the stunning Adelaide Hills at Mt Barker, Australia. This annual fun run challenges participants to push their limits as they ascend the picturesque summit, rewarding them with breathtaking panoramic views. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or a beginner seeking a scenic challenge, Conquer the Summit offers an unforgettable running experience.
