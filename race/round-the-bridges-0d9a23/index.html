Course

A flat city loop starting and finishing at Garden Place in the Hamilton CBD. The 12 km runs north up Victoria Street, joins the Waikato River path at Rostrevor Street, crosses Fairfield Bridge, heads south along River Road past Hamilton Gardens and returns over Cobham Bridge; the 6 km cuts back at Victoria Bridge. Surface is sealed throughout, mixing urban streets with riverside paths.

About

Lace up your sneakers and join the crowds for Lugton's Round the Bridges, Hamilton's most iconic fun run! This scenic course winds through the heart of the city, offering stunning views of the Waikato River and its historic bridges. Whether you're a seasoned runner or just looking for a fun day out, this event is not to be missed.
