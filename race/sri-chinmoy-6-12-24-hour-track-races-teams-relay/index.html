Course

Held on the floodlit, World Athletics certified 400 m Rekortan track at AUT Millennium Stadium in Auckland. Runners and race-walkers complete as many laps as they can within 6, 12 or 24 hours, with a 24-hour six-person team relay also offered. Lap counting and refreshments are provided trackside throughout.

About

The Sri Chinmoy 6-12-24-hour individual races are open to all runners & race-walkers eager to 'challenge the pride of frightening distance'. Participants can run, walk, eat, drink, and take breaks to discover their own potential and achieve their goals in 6, 12, or 24 hours. The event also includes a 24-Hour Teams Relay where teams of 6 runners/walkers can compete.
