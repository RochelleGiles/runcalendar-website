About

Lace up your trail shoes and get ready for the Red Yeti Rampage, a fall trail run hosted by the Chippewa Valley Nordic Ski Team (The Red Yetis) at Tower Ridge! Choose from the 5K, 10K, or new for 2026: the 15K Yeti Challenge route — all begin and end at the Chalet. Hikers/Walkers/Trekkers are encouraged as well! Every entrant enjoys a hearty post-race lunch! Afterwards, stick around for awards, door prizes, refreshments, and great community vibes. Whether you're pushing for a PR or just soaking in the scenery, it's a race for all levels!
