Course

A single loop on the 250-acre South Mountain Christian Camp property at Bostic, North Carolina. The race starts and finishes outside the Joy Center, opening on a wide gravel road before narrowing onto trails, and mixes gravel road, grassy lakeshore and wide dirt trail over rolling wooded hills. The course is marked a month ahead with paint, flags, cones and signs.

About

A family-friendly 5K trail race held at South Mountain Christian Camp near Bostic, North Carolina, with entries benefiting the camp. Runners cover a single loop of gravel road, grassy shoreline and wooded dirt trail over rolling terrain. A free kids' fun run for under-12s runs alongside the main event.
