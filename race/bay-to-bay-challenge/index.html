Course

A point-to-point coastal road race on Cape Town's Atlantic seaboard. The 30 km starts at Camps Bay High School sports field and the 15 km at Mainstream Mall in Hout Bay; both finish at Glen Country Club on Victoria Road, Clifton.

About

Bay to Bay is a long-running coastal road race organised by Top Form Athletic Club along Cape Town's Atlantic seaboard, starting and finishing in Camps Bay. It offers 30km and 15km distances.
