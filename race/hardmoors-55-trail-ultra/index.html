Course

Point-to-point from Guisborough to Helmsley across the North York Moors and Cleveland Hills. About 95% of the 55-mile route follows the well-signed Cleveland Way National Trail, leaving it only near Sutton Bank and close to the finish. Starts at Guisborough Rugby Club and finishes at Helmsley Sports Club, with a 16-hour limit.

About

The Hardmoors 55 is a challenging 55-mile trail ultra through the stunning Yorkshire countryside, taking runners from the historic town of Guisborough to the picturesque Helmsley. Tackle rugged terrain and breathtaking views as you push your limits on this classic British ultra-marathon event.
