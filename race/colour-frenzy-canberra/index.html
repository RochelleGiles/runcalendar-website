Course

A non-competitive, untimed course through Weston Park in Canberra, with colour powder stations along the route. The fun run starts at 9:30am with arrival from 8:30am; participants walk, jog or run at their own pace and most choose to walk.

About

Colour Frenzy Canberra is a 5km colour-powder fun run held at Weston Park in Yarralumla, on the shore of Lake Burley Griffin, with colour stations bursting along the route. It's a family-friendly, non-competitive event.
