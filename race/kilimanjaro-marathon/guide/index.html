Kilimanjaro Marathon

Moshi, Kilimanjaro · Tanzania
RoadMountainMarathon · Half Marathon · 5KEntries open

Africa's biggest road race, at the foot of its highest mountain — and it climbs for the first eight kilometres.

Quick verdict

It front-loads its hill: 290 metres of climbing in the first eight kilometres, straight towards the mountain. Then it sends you onto twenty kilometres of dirt road, alone, in rising heat. Altitude is a red herring here. Heat is the whole story.

Course at a glance

290mClimb in first 8 km
20 kmOn dirt
6 hrsCutoff

Start and finish inside MoCU Stadium in Moshi. Uphill north towards Kilimanjaro for eight kilometres, dirt through farms and schools, then the long descent down Lema Road. At 19 km the marathon breaks from the half, right past Moshi Club and over the Old German Bridge, looping back through an avenue of shade into the stadium.

Where you'll feel it

📍Out of Moshi, the first eight kilometres.

Not on the climb — on the dirt loop from 19 to 39 kilometres. The seven thousand half-marathoners have peeled off and gone home, you are alone on unpaved road, and it is late morning in a place that averages thirty-four degrees.

Best chance to push

The Lema Road descent from about ten kilometres, and the last three on tar under the shade trees into the stadium. Both give back real time if you did not spend it on the opening climb.

Three things to know

  1. Check the date on anything you read. The 2027 race moved to Sunday 7 February, six weeks earlier than 2026, and several travel sites still advertise March. Entries are not open yet.
  2. The cutoff is a clock time, not an elapsed one — everything closes at 12:30. The marathon starts 06:30 and gets six hours; the half starts 07:00 and gets five and a half. Half-marathon walkers have almost no buffer.
  3. Roughly forty-seven percent of the marathon is unpaved, graded and watered beforehand to hold the dust down. With 290 metres of climbing in the first eight kilometres, the plan writes itself: start slow, treat the descent as recovery.

Race-day weather

19–34°CAfrican autumn
6:30amStart
830mAltitude

February in Moshi means 19°C at the gun and a daily maximum near 34°C. The race starts at first light because that is the only cool part of the day. Kilimanjaro is usually visible early and clouded over by late morning.

What runners wear

Light and pale, with a cap and sunglasses for the exposed dirt. Carry your own fluid for the back half. Road shoes are fine — the dirt is graded, not technical.

Typical finish times

The fastest recent times are 2:18:52 and 2:50:33, both from 2026 — slow for an East African field, which tells you what 290 metres of early climbing and twenty kilometres of dirt cost. Medals go to the first 950 marathon finishers, and everyone is done by 12:30.

Worth the trip?

The 25th running, thirteen thousand runners, and a crowd that treats it as a national occasion; its own name for itself is the People's Race. Go for that and for the mountain. Not for a time, and not without building in a safari or a climb.
