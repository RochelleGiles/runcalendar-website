Course

Road marathon starting and finishing at MoCU Stadium in Moshi, in the shadow of Kilimanjaro, with a hilly out-and-back profile. The half marathon starts at Sokoine Road Gate and the 5km fun run at the foot of the stadium on Uru Road, both finishing in the stadium. Run under Athletics Tanzania and a Comrades qualifier.

About

The Kilimanjaro Marathon is an annual running event held in Moshi, Tanzania. It features a full marathon, half marathon, and 5K race, attracting runners from around the world to compete in the shadow of Mount Kilimanjaro.
