Course

Trail races on the interconnected tracks of Hahamongna Watershed Park in the Pasadena foothills. The 5K uses mixed-use truck trail and gains about 306 feet; the 10K adds fire road for roughly 400 feet; the half marathon is the hard one, rocky singletrack with about 1,855 feet of climbing including El Prieto Canyon and Brown Mountain Road. Courses are marked with flour arrows and have aid stations.

About

The Pasadena Trail Half & 5K/10K is a trail running event in La Cañada Flintridge, California, organized by the Pasadena Running Company, with half marathon, 10K and 5K distance options on local trails in the foothills of the San Gabriel Mountains.
