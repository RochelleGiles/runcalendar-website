Course

Two evening 5K events run inside Rosehill Cemetery, Chicago's largest burial ground at 350 acres. The chip-timed run starts at 7:00pm and the fun run/walk at 7:15pm. The candlelit course loops the Victorian-era grounds past monuments and headstones and includes several sharp turns. A medic is on site.

About

The 13th annual Rosehill Cemetery 'Crypt' 5K Run/Walk takes place on the historic grounds of Chicago's largest 350-acre Victorian-era Rosehill Cemetery. Participants run through a candlelit course at night with two options: a chip-timed 5K run or a fun run/walk.
