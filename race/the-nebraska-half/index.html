Course

A flat loop course around Zorinsky Lake in West Omaha, starting and finishing at the lake boat ramp off the 156th and F Street entrance. The route follows sealed, partly shaded lakeside paths and is easy for spectators to move around. Start is 8:00am.

About

The Nebraska Half is held at Zorinsky Lake in West Omaha. This flat and fast course winds around the scenic lake with shaded trails and is very spectator friendly, with the start and finish at the Zorinsky Lake Boat Ramp.
