Course

A waterfront course inside Fort Totten Park on Little Neck Bay, starting and finishing at the landmarked Officers Club at 208 Totten Ave. The route loops out onto Little Bay Path towards the Throgs Neck Bridge and back to the Fort. USATF-certified and chip timed.

About

A Halloween-themed 5K foot race and kids' fun run held on a waterfront course in historic Fort Totten Park on Little Neck Bay in Bayside. The USATF certified course passes through the Historic District with buildings from the late 1800s and includes a loop to the Throgs Neck Bridge.
