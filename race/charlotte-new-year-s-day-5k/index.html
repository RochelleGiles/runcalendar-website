Course

Starts on the campus of UNC Charlotte, heads out along the Toby Creek Greenway and finishes back on campus. The 5K begins at 10:00am with a Kids Dash at 11:00am. It can be entered together with the 31 December NOON Year Eve 5K as the New Year Double.

About

The Charlotte New Year's Day 5K is an annual race held on New Year's Day in University City, Charlotte, featuring a 5K run and a Kids Dash. Participants receive a long sleeve shirt, beer for 21+, coffee, lunch, and finisher medals, with VIP upgrades available.
