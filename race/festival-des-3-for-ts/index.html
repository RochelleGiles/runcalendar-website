About

The Festival Des 3 Forets is an annual running event that takes place in Mériel, France. The event features several race distances, including an ultra-marathon, marathon, and shorter trail runs, allowing runners of all levels to participate. The course showcases the beautiful natural landscapes of the surrounding forests, providing a unique and scenic running experience.
