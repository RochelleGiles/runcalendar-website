Course

Mountain trail courses in the Valle Morobbia above Bellinzona, Ticino, run on alpine paths and mule tracks. The K33 "Trail del lupo" climbs high above the valley and the K14 "Trail della croce" covers a shorter loop, with a 6 km popular walk and a kids' run alongside them. A separate Mannaro vertical race is also held.

About

The Bancastato Morobbia Trail climbs through Ticino's Morobbia Valley above Bellinzona, Switzerland, with routes ranging from a short family walk to the demanding 33km 'Trail del Lupo' (Wolf Trail). The event also includes a vertical kilometre race up to the Mannaro ridge.
