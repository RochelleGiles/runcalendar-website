About

The Lumberjack Pancake Run features a Silver Dollar Mile and Flapjack 5K along the flat, fast out-and-back course on the Dequindre Cut in Detroit, starting at the Outdoor Adventure Center. Participants enjoy a pancake breakfast, custom medals, and lumberjack-themed prizes as part of Lumberjack Days.
