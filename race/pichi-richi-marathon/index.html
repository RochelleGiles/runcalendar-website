About

Explore the stunning Pichi Richi region of South Australia at the Pichi Richi Marathon. This point-to-point road marathon takes runners on a scenic journey from Port Augusta to Quorn, showcasing the area's unique landscapes and historic railway heritage. Whether you're an experienced marathoner or a first-time runner, the Pichi Richi Marathon offers a challenging and rewarding experience.
