Course

Point-to-point half marathon starting at Cabrillo National Monument on the tip of Point Loma, running along San Diego Bay, Harbor Island and the Embarcadero, past the Star of India and through downtown before finishing in Balboa Park. The 5K is a separate flat loop through Balboa Park. Sealed roads throughout.

About

America's Finest City Half Marathon starts at Cabrillo National Monument on Point Loma and finishes in Balboa Park, taking runners along San Diego Bay and through downtown neighborhoods. A companion 5K also finishes in the park.
