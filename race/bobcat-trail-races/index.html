Course

Loop courses in Palmer Park from the Maizeland Road trailhead, on technical singletrack, rolling hills and rocky bluffs in the middle of Colorado Springs. Staggered starts from 7:00 am (50K) to 8:30 am (5K); courses marked with orange ribbon.

About

Bobcat Trail Races showcase Palmer Park, one of Colorado Springs' best city parks, with views of the city and Pikes Peak from its rocky bluffs. Formerly a Mad Moose Event, the race is now hosted by Aravaipa Running.
