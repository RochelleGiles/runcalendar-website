Course

About

The Big House 5K, owned and operated by the Michigan Athletic Department, has a mission of raising funds and awareness for local non-profit beneficiaries. The race begins on State Street, courses through the University of Michigan's central campus, and then finishes on the 50-yard line of Michigan Stadium.
