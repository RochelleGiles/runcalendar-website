Course

Both distances run the Pacific Crest Trail and the Gorge 400 trail before crossing the Bridge of the Gods into Cascade Locks, finishing with a loop of Thunder Island beside the Columbia River. The 50K is a point-to-point lollipop starting and finishing at Marine Park; the 25K starts at Wyeth Trailhead, with a shuttle, and finishes at Marine Park. Full-service aid stations with medical support and radio cover the course.
