Course

Point-to-point 20 km road race from near the CEPAC Velodrome in Marseille to the seafront at Cassis, starting at 09:00. The route climbs the Col de la Gineste before descending through the edge of the Calanques National Park to the finish. Run on closed roads and registered with the French athletics federation; over 800 volunteers staff the course with relief and massage points.

About

Marseille-Cassis is a 20km point-to-point road race from Marseille over the Col de la Gineste to the harbour at Cassis. The climb out of the city dominates the first half before the descent past the Calanques National Park. It is one of the largest mass-participation races in southern France.
