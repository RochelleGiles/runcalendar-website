Course

Held on the trails around Valley Lodge in Magaliesburg, in the Cradle of Humankind, alongside a two-day mountain-bike weekend. Three routes are offered: 5 km and 10 km run/walk options and a 15 km run for runners only, on flowing farm and hillside trail.
