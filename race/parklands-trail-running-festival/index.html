Course

Off-road course at Lizard Log in Western Sydney Parklands, Abbotsbury, running bushland trail, soft grass and undulating open ground. Four distances are offered — 32 km (644 m climb), 21.1 km (367 m), 11 km (205 m) and 6 km (136 m). The paths stay open to other walkers and cyclists during the event.

About

Join us at Lizard Log in Western Sydney Parklands for the second edition of the Parklands TRAIL Running Festival! We're back in Sydney's biggest backyard with four stunning distances to choose from — 32k, 22k, 11k, or 6k — offering something for everyone.
