About

Held the Friday after Thanksgiving, this race offers a half marathon and 7.5-miler, each with a ruck division, on the trails of the Cane Creek Mountains Natural Area in Snow Camp, North Carolina. Virtual options run the same week, and in-person entry stays open until race morning.
