About

The Basalt Half Marathon & Relay, now past its 48th running, starts with a quarter mile on a dirt road before descending about 1,000 feet along the paved Frying Pan Road to finish at Basalt Middle School. Four aid stations are placed along the course, and each mile is clearly marked.
