About

The Dungarvan 10 Mile is a fast, flat race that takes runners on a scenic tour of the historic town of Dungarvan, Ireland. Organized by the West Waterford Athletics Club, this event offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels, with stunning coastal views and a course designed for personal bests.
