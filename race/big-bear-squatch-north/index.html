Course

A lollipop course starting and finishing at the Anthony Wayne Recreation Area at Bear Mountain, New York. The half marathon runs two loops, the gentler 7.5-mile loop and then the 10K loop; rugged technical singletrack with steep climbs, cliffs and scrambling sections. Around 2,600ft of gain for the 13.1 and 1,400ft for the 10K.

About

Sassquad and the New York-New Jersey Trail Conference present the Big Bear SquaTCh North, now past its 4th running, at Harriman State Park near Bear Mountain, New York. The 10K and half-marathon trail event is known to sell out, so early registration is recommended.
