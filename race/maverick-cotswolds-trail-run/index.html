Course

All five distances start and finish at Sudeley Castle in Winchcombe and follow the Cotswold Way and Winchcombe Way through rolling hills, honey-stone villages and patchwork farmland. The going is hard-packed established trail — flowing bridleways, ancient woodland, open ridge lines — broken by steep climbs that reward power-hiking and fast descents. Aid outposts carry fruit, cake, crisps, gels and electrolyte; the ultra and marathon have four each.

About

Explore the rolling hills and picturesque landscapes of the Cotswold Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty at the Maverick Cotswolds Trail Run. Winding through scenic trails and charming villages, this event offers a unique challenge for runners seeking an unforgettable adventure in the heart of the British countryside.
