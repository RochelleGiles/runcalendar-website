About

The Half Marathon of Foligno is a must-run event for any endurance enthusiast. Set against the picturesque backdrop of Foligno's historic city center, this half marathon takes runners on a scenic tour through the charming streets and landmarks of this ancient Italian town. With a challenging yet rewarding course, the Half Marathon of Foligno promises an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
