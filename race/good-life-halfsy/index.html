Course

A point-to-point, net-downhill half marathon through Lincoln, passing parks, water features, tree-lined boulevards and city landmarks. The route uses a mix of streets, sidewalks and trails. Fluid stations, energy gels, medical support and toilets are spread along the course, with five cheer stations and live entertainment. A four-hour limit applies.

About

Good Life Halfsy is a half marathon held in Lincoln, Nebraska each November. The name nods to Nebraska's "Good Life" state slogan, and the race is one of the city's later-season road events before winter.
