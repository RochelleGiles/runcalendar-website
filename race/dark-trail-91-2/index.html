About

The Dark Trail is a nighttime trail running and Nordic walking event taking place almost entirely on forest paths. Participants run or walk with headlamps and may encounter torch-lit sections through 'the desert'. The non-timed event features marshals along the route for safety and encouragement, concluding with a welcoming buffet for all participants.
