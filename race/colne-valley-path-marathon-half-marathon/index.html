Course

A point-to-point trail run along the Colne Valley Path, self-navigated by GPS file or written instructions rather than marshalled. The marathon starts at Great Yeldham and the half at Greenstead Green, both finishing at Cymbeline Meadows in Colchester. Aid stations and checkpoints are stocked at regular intervals, and the race is cupless so runners carry their own.

About

The Colne Valley Path Marathon & Half Marathon run on point-to-point off-road courses along the Colne Valley Path. Marathon runners start from Stambourne and pass through Great Yeldham and the ancient Yeldham Oak, while half marathon runners join the route at Greenstead Green, with both distances finishing at Cymbeline Meadows in Colchester.
