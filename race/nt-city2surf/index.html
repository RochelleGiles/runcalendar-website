About

The NT City2Surf in Darwin is a must-do event for runners of all levels. Featuring a 13km City2Surf run, a 4km mini surfer run or walk, and a Kids' mile, this event showcases the vibrant city and stunning natural surroundings. Whether you're chasing a personal best or simply looking to enjoy the scenic course, the NT City2Surf promises an unforgettable running experience.
