Course

The run follows an out-and-back route along the Irrigation Festival parade course through Sequim, starting and finishing at the Shipley Center parking lot. A virtual option is also offered for those running remotely.

About

The Sequim Irrigation Festival Run Series offers 5K and 1-mile races for walkers and runners as part of the annual festival. It is a timed out-and-back event starting at 11am from the old JC Penney parking lot.
