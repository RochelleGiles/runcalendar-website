Course

A single 5K loop contained entirely within Cheltenham Racecourse, home of the Cheltenham Festival. The surface is a mix of grass, woodchip and made paths, with the start and finish on tarmac in front of the grandstand. The route may be run, jogged or walked.

About

Cancer Research UK's Race for Life 5K is held at Cheltenham Racecourse, world-famous home of the Cheltenham Festival jump racing. Runners and walkers cover a flat course around the racecourse grounds, raising money for cancer research.
