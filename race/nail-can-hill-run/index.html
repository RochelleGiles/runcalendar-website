About

Nestled in the heart of Albury, New South Wales, the Nail Can Hill Run invites adventurous runners and walkers to tackle a thrilling mix of running, walking, and mountain biking on the scenic trails of the local hills. This unique event offers a chance to explore the great outdoors and challenge yourself on the rugged terrain, making it a must-try for fitness enthusiasts seeking an unforgettable experience.
