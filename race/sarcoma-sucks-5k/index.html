About

Help us take the fight to Sarcoma! This 3.1 mile run/walk is in memory of Jenny Harman, Jeff Grose, and Faye Grose, supporting the Move For Jenn Foundation. All three were taken far too soon by different forms of Sarcoma. Jenny's birthday was March 20th so we are having a 5k race with a .1 mile memorial cooldown walk for a total of 3.2 miles! Our mission is to foster community support and awareness for Sarcoma research through this annual charity run.
