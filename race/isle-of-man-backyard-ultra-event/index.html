Course

A last-one-standing backyard ultra held at Archallagan Plantation on the Isle of Man. Runners set off on the hour, every hour, from 12pm to complete a 4.167-mile loop, and must finish each yard within the hour to start the next. The race ends when one runner remains.

About

The Isle of Man Backyard Ultra is a last-one-standing race held at Archallagan Plantation. Runners set off together every hour, on the hour, to complete a 4.167-mile loop, and must finish inside the hour to start the next one. The race ends when a single runner remains.
