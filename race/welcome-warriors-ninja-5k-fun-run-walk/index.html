About

The Welcome Warriors Ninja 5K Fun Run/Walk is a fundraiser for Welcome Sports Ministry, an outreach of Welcome Lutheran Church providing free martial arts and self-defense lessons for all ages. The timed run/walk passes open pastures and farm animals in a countryside setting.
