Course

A single counter-clockwise lap of Green Lake on the inner paved path for about 2.8 miles, then out of the loop to circle the play fields. Start and finish are at the Green Lake Community Center basketball courts on the east side of the park. The course is flat and fully paved.

About

A 5K running and walking event in Seattle, Washington organized by Hi5K. The event is themed around Super Bowl Sunday and welcomes both runners and walkers of all levels.
