Course

Start and finish at Typhoon Texas Waterpark Houston on Katy Fort Bend Road. The course is run entirely on paved road and the half marathon is USATF certified. All water stations carry water, with sports drink added on the half marathon course. Starts are staggered: half 7:15 a.m., 5K 7:45 a.m., Kid's K 9:45 a.m.

About

The 12th annual Katy Half Marathon & 5K presented by Houston Methodist is part of the Run Houston! Race Series. The event features runner's choice event shirts, finisher medals, Voodoo doughnuts, and finish line beer (21+).
