About

The Flanders Beer Marathon Festival is a unique running event that takes runners on a scenic tour through the heart of Belgium's renowned beer country. Participants can enjoy the region's world-class brews while challenging themselves on a challenging course that showcases the picturesque Flanders countryside. This event offers an unforgettable experience for runners seeking a one-of-a-kind running adventure.
