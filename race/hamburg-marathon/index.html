Course

A road marathon through central Hamburg, taking in the wide boulevards of the inner districts and the Hanseatic waterfront along the harbour, the Elbe and the Alster. The route is marked by a blue line runners follow the whole way. A half marathon and a marathon relay share the course.

About

Germany's largest spring marathon, held in Hamburg each April and one of the country's biggest road races. The programme covers a marathon, a half marathon and a marathon relay. The course is flat and fast, and regularly draws a deep international field.
