Course

Three days of racing on a private working farm on the edge of the Pilliga, over gravel farm roads and rocky sandstone trails beneath sandstone escarpments. The 10 km runs Friday, the 50 km Saturday and the 20 km Sunday, with a Grand Slam covering all three. Aid stations sit at roughly 5 km on the 10 km, 8.5 km on the 20 km, and four points on the 50 km.

About

The Pilliga Ultra in Coonabarabran, NSW is a 50km ultra marathon through the rugged, wild, and beautiful terrain of the Pilliga Forest. Runners will traverse diverse landscapes, from dense bushland to open plains, as they challenge themselves in this remote and captivating part of Australia. This event offers an unparalleled opportunity to experience the natural wonder of the Pilliga.
