About

Trail Urbain de Sète, known as the Défi du Mont Saint-Clair, is a UTMB Index-labelled urban trail race through the streets and hillside of Sète on the Mediterranean coast. Runners climb Mont Saint-Clair on courses of 8 km, 13 km or 20 km, combining coastal views with the town's urban terrain.
