Course

A single-loop 5K held entirely inside the Norfolk Showground site, with the start and finish areas on grass. The route mixes flat tarmac paths with grass sections that carry a few inclines, and is relatively flat overall. Organisers note the ground can turn soft or muddy in wet weather.

About

This 5K Race for Life is held at the Norfolk Showground on the outskirts of Norwich. Entrants run, jog or walk the course at their own pace, with all funds raised going toward Cancer Research UK's work.
