Course

A lapped trail course on soil with no pavement, starting and finishing at 1037 Trice Cemetery Rd, Thomaston. One lap for the 5K, three for the 15K and ten for the 50K, all setting off together at 9:00am with a nine hour cutoff. The race is cupless, so runners carry their own bottle or pack.

About

Get out of the city and join us for the first annual Soil Mates Trail Race. Run on beautiful trails with fresh air, fun running mates, and no pavement over rich Georgia soil. Running on soil is good for the soul and your soles! Whether you're a road runner who is trail curious, a seasoned trail runner, or a weekend hiker, this race offers options for all levels.
