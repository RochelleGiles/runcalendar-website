Course

Both distances start in downtown Ashland, Virginia and run through the historic town centre, with the course running alongside the railway that splits the main street. The half marathon has a 3.5 hour limit and the 5K a 1.5 hour limit.

About

This half marathon and 5K loop through downtown Ashland, known as "The Center of the Universe," passing historic homes and running alongside the town's active railroad tracks. The half marathon has a 3.5-hour time limit and the race is organized by Sports Backers.
