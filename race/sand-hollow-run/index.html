Course

Both races start at the north end of Transition and run out to Lakeside Dr, passing alongside the dam for about a mile before continuing on paved road beside Sand Hollow Reservoir. The 5K turns at half a mile of road, the 10K at two miles, and both return by the same route. Aid stations with water and Gatorade sit at each mile, and a hill leads into the finish.

About

The Sand Hollow 10K and 5K Run is a multisport event where you will be racing alongside athletes of all levels, on the same run course as the Sand Hollow Triathlon. This endurance event is family-friendly, and the venue is exceptional! Experience picturesque views of the red sandstone and deep turquoise waters creating an unforgettable day! The course's elevation will test your endurance, and you will have to race up a hill before reaching the finish line. Sand Hollow makes for a can't miss RACE-cation. With nearby attractions like Zion, Moab and Sand Hollow Golf Course, you'll have more adventures awaiting after the race!
