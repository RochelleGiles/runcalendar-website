About

QUERROIGTRAIL is a trail-running event in Portbou, the coastal town on Catalonia's Costa Brava at the French border. Three distances — 9km, 15km and 23km — climb into the hills above the coast, passing the Torre de Querroig watchtower and the area's most emblematic viewpoints.
