Course

A flat loop course at Damian Leeding Memorial Park in Oxenford, on the northern Gold Coast, running around Regatta Waters Lake. The organiser describes it as relatively easy and accessible. Distances are a 21 km half marathon, 12 km, 8 km, a 4 km run and a 4 km walk, with a virtual option alongside.

About

The Chempro Endo-End Fun Run is an annual fundraiser to raise awareness and funds for Endometriosis Australia. Situated around the picturesque Regatta Waters Lake in Oxenford, the event features a relatively easy and flat loop route through the Damian Leeding Memorial Park, one of the more scenic parks on the Northern Gold Coast.
