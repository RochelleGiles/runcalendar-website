About

A 4.7-mile road race through the town of Manchester, Connecticut, drawing an elite field alongside thousands of local runners each Thanksgiving morning. It has grown into the largest race in the state and among the largest Thanksgiving Day races in the country. Proceeds support muscular dystrophy research and about 18 local charities.
