Course

A two-day stage trail run based at Laerskool Mariepskop in Limpopo, half an hour from Hoedspruit. Day one climbs mountain terrain on the Blyde River Canyon escarpment; day two crosses bushveld at Moholoholo Mountain View, where buffalo, hippo, leopard and plains game are on the reserve. Four route lengths run the same two stages: 70 km, 44 km, 30 km and 20 km.

About

The Kruger2Canyon Challenge is a multi-stage trail race based in Hoedspruit, Limpopo, between the Kruger lowveld and the Blyde River Canyon escarpment. Stages range from 20km to 70km, with a strong conservation and community focus.
