About

The Erfurt Marathon is a long-standing annual running event in the heart of Germany. The race takes place on a scenic course around the city, offering runners a chance to explore the historic streets and landmarks of Erfurt. With a focus on charity and community, the event brings together runners of all levels to support local causes and enjoy the vibrant atmosphere.
