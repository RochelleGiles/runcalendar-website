About

The Magaluf Half Marathon is one of the premier running events in Mallorca. The course features scenic routes through the beautiful coastal town, offering runners a dynamic experience with entertainment along the way. Participants can enjoy the vibrant atmosphere and local hospitality as they take on the challenge of the 21km or 10km distances.
