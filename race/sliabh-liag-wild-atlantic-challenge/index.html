About

The Sliabh Liag Wild Atlantic Challenge invites runners to experience the breathtaking beauty of Ireland's dramatic Donegal coastline. Held along the iconic Sliabh Liag cliffs, this exhilarating event offers a unique opportunity to conquer challenging terrain while taking in the rugged splendor of the Wild Atlantic Way. Runners will be captivated by the stunning vistas and invigorating sea air as they push themselves to the limit in this unforgettable coastal challenge.
