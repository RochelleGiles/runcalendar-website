Course

A USATF-certified single-loop half marathon that crosses the Texas-Arkansas state line, starting at W Broad Street and Texas Boulevard in Texarkana. About four miles run on ten-foot-wide asphalt trails through two city parks, with the remainder on coned-off city streets. Several long inclines. Aid stations near miles 2.3, 3.5, 5, 6.5, 8, 9.8 and 11.

About

The Run the Line Half Marathon is a unique opportunity to run in one half marathon in Texas and Arkansas on a USATF certified single-loop course. The course includes 10-ft wide asphalt trails that wind through residential neighborhoods on city streets.
