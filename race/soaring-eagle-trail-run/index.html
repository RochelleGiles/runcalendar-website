Course

Loops entirely within Soaring Eagle Park, starting and finishing at the same point. The 5K and 10K split at 0.74 miles and half-marathoners run the 10K loop twice. Surfaces mix the wide Pipeline Trail with narrow, rooty and rocky singletrack through forest. Aid at 4.5 miles on the 10K; at 4.5, 6.5 and 11 miles on the half.

About

Nestled in Sammamish, Washington, Soaring Eagle Trail Run features trails in Soaring Eagle Regional Park, including the wide Pipeline Trail and winding singletracks through lush forests. The event offers 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon distances on mostly flat, well-graded courses with roots and rocks.
