Course

A flat, fast course starting and finishing at Riverwalk Crossing Park on Court Street and running out through historic downtown Jacksonville. The 5K, 10K and 15K share the route, and the event is explicitly run/walk/roll, welcoming athletes of all abilities.

About

Entering its 12th year, the Run With Your Heart 5K/10K/15K/Virtual is a major fundraiser for Ainsley's Angels in Eastern Carolina and Ainsley's Angels in America. This race brings both the local community and the entire nation together to promote the message of inclusion. Together, we shall accomplish so much more than we ever could alone!
