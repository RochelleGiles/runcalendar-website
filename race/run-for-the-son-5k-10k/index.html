Course

Begins and ends in the Burnt Hickory Baptist Church car park, 5145 Due West Road, Powder Springs. The 5K and 10K run a rolling road loop on Due West and Burnt Hickory Roads. Both are chip-timed with a gun start, and a one-mile fun run is also offered.
