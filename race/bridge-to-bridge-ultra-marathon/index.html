About

The Bridge to Bridge Ultra is a point-to-point run across the Canterbury lowlands, finishing on Raven Quay in Kaiapoi. The 60km starts at Waimakariri Golf Club, with the 30km, 15km and 5km joining the same route from later start points. It is held the day before the Bridge to Bridge mountain bike race.
