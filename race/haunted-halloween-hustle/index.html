Course

Out-and-back courses on the paved Pumpkinvine trail, shaded by mature trees and almost entirely traffic-free apart from a few marshalled road crossings. Start and finish are at the Abshire Park pavilion in Goshen. Portable toilets are placed along the route, with hot cocoa, soup, coffee, cookies and fruit at the finish.
