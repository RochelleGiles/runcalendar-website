About

The annual Tarmonbarry 3/4 Marathon, Half Marathon and 8km is on 4 October 2026 in County Roscommon. Races start from Tarmonbarry village. Each race route is a one lap course, starting in the village, going out into the scenic quiet rural roads and finishing back in the village beside St. Barrys GAA clubhouse.
