About

Urgent Run Paris is a charitable running event that raises funds for toilet construction in developing countries, offering 5km and 10km races to support the World Toilet Organization's mission. Proceeds have funded toilet construction projects in Senegal, Ivory Coast, Benin, Togo, Mali, and Madagascar.
