Course

Repeated laps of a 6.706km loop starting and finishing at the Spencer Park campground, Spencerville. Each lap must be started on the hour and completed within the hour. The route includes coastal dunes, with the first lap timed to reach the dunes at sunrise around 8am.

About

The Krayzie Midwinter Backyard Ultra is a backyard ultra held at Spencer Park, Spencerville, near Christchurch, New Zealand. Runners complete a 6.706km lap each hour and use the remaining time to rest. The race ends when one runner completes a lap that no one else finishes.
