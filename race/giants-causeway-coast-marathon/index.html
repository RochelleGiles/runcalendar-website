About

The Giant's Causeway Coast Marathon runs along Northern Ireland's Causeway Coast between Portballintrae and the Giant's Causeway World Heritage Site, with Marathon, Half Marathon, 10K and Ultra Marathon distances organised by 26 Extreme.
