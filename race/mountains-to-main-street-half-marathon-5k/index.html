Course

A point to point half marathon from Travelers Rest to downtown Greenville along the Swamp Rabbit Trail, finishing at the TD Stage. A 5K is also held. Aid station positions and the surface breakdown are not published; the organiser links a MapMyRun route.

About

Join us at the 10th Annual Mountains to Main Street Half Marathon & 5K in Greenville, South Carolina. This scenic race takes runners through the beautiful Blue Ridge Mountains and downtown Greenville.
