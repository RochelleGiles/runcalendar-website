About

The Sunshine Coast Marathon Festival offers runners a scenic tour of the picturesque Australian coastline. With events ranging from the full marathon to a 2km fun run, this inclusive festival caters to runners of all abilities. Soak in the stunning natural beauty of the Sunshine Coast as you conquer your distance and celebrate your achievement.
