Course

One continuous loop starting and finishing at Skyline Farm, 95 The Lane, North Yarmouth, Maine, running the farm's trails and the adjoining Yarmouth Water District land. Terrain is varied with a few hills. Aid station detail is not stated.

About

Held each September at Skyline Farm in North Yarmouth, Maine, this 5K trail run and walk crosses working farmland recognized as an official American Trail Running Association race, drawing local runners for a low-key morning on the farm.
