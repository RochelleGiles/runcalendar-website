Course

All distances start and finish at Maple Grove Senior High stadium. The half marathon and 10K combine city streets with paved trails and carry hills at several points; the half runs through Elm Creek Park Reserve and around Rice Lake. The 5K follows roughly the first two miles of the 10K course. Half and 10K are USATF-certified and chip-timed.

About

The Maple Grove Nature Run offers a half marathon, 10K and 5K starting and finishing at Maple Grove Senior High in Minnesota. The courses combine city streets with paved trails through Elm Creek Park Reserve and around Rice Lake. It is run by the Maple Grove Lions as a community fundraiser.
