Course

About

United PDX Soccer Club, one of Oregon's largest youth soccer organizations, is excited to host its Second Annual 5K Run/Walk! By participating, you'll help support nearly 3,000 youth soccer players, ages 4 to 19, as they pursue their passion for the game. This event promises a fun and active way to engage with your community while contributing to a great cause.
