Course

Half marathon, 10K and 5K courses starting and finishing at Coyote Hills Regional Park in Fremont, California. Surfaces vary between dirt trail, paved path and wooden boardwalk across the marsh, and total climbing is modest. Aid stations run from one on the 5K to three on the half marathon.

About

This Fremont park has "hills" in the name, but the climbing is minimal at these runs by the bay. You will find an abundance of scenic views and variety of surfaces from dirt to paved to the wood boardwalks running through the marsh!
