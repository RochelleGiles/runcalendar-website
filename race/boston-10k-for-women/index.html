Course

About

Now in its fifth decade, this out-and-back 10K starts at the Boston Public Garden, crosses the Charles River into Cambridge via Memorial Drive, and finishes near Boston Common. It was once the largest women's-only 10K in the country, and six-time champion Lynn Jennings won the inaugural 1977 race.
