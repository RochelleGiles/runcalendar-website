Course

A two-day event in Sackets Harbor. Saturday's 5K and 5-mile races start and finish at the brewery in the village; Sunday's longer races, including the half marathon and the signature 18.12-mile Challenge, are run at Sackets Harbor Battlefield on the Lake Ontario shore. Distances can be combined into multi-race challenge entries.
