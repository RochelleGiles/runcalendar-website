Course

One lap anti-clockwise undulating route on B class roads from Monikie Country Park, near Dundee. The 10K uses the first 3km of the half marathon route before turning back to the park for its remaining 7km. Half marathon starts at 10:30am.

About

The Angus Ham Half Marathon is a scenic road race set in the picturesque Monikie Country Park near Dundee, Scotland. Runners will wind through rolling hills and lush forests, making this a must-do event for those seeking a challenging yet rewarding half marathon experience in a stunning natural setting.
