About

Embark on a captivating journey through the historic Canal du Midi in southern France. This unique running event offers runners the chance to experience the serene beauty of this UNESCO World Heritage site, with a route that winds along the picturesque waterway and through charming towns. Whether you're a seasoned runner or seeking a memorable adventure, the Canal du Midi Experience promises an unforgettable challenge and a chance to immerse yourself in the rich cultural heritage of the region.
