About

Join us for the Fairfield Fresh Market & Deli Cross Country run (3 x 3km loops) at Bicentennial Park in Townsville. This event offers a short 3km course and a longer 9km course, perfect for runners of all levels. With the scenic backdrop of the park, this cross country run promises an enjoyable and challenging experience.
