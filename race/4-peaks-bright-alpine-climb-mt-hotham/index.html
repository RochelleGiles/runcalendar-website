Course

Point-to-point 15km course starting at Tavare Park, Harrietville and finishing at Diamantina Hut near Mt Hotham, with 1867m of vertical climb. The route follows the Bon Accord Track beside the river, climbs a steep spur to the treeline, then crosses the Razorback on alpine singletrack. There is no aid on course; water and fruit are provided at the finish only.

About

Mt Hotham is day three of the four-day 4 Peaks Bright Alpine Climb series. The 15km point-to-point course starts at Tavare Park in Harrietville, climbs the Bon Accord Track and a steep spur to the treeline, then crosses the Razorback to finish at Diamantina Hut, gaining 1867m. There is no aid on course, so entrants must be self-sufficient.
