About

The Trail Des Dahus Sallanchards is a multi-format trail running event set in the beautiful landscapes near Mont Blanc. Offering various race distances for different skill levels, the event features a semi-nocturnal duo trail, 24-hour ultimate challenge, and courses ranging from 7.5 km to 26 km with significant elevation gains. More than just a race, it's a festive weekend celebration of trail running with local artisan village, music, and community spirit.
