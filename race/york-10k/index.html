Course

Flat and fast city 10K starting and finishing on Knavesmire Road beside York Racecourse. The closed-road route runs through the historic centre, passing the ancient city walls, Clifford's Tower and the minster before returning to the racecourse. Water stations handing out 330ml bottles are positioned along the course.

About

The York 10K is a must-do event for runners in Yorkshire and beyond. Weaving through the historic streets of the city, this scenic 10K course showcases the stunning architecture and vibrant atmosphere that make York a premier running destination. With a reputation for excellent organization and a lively post-race celebration, the York 10K is a fantastic way to experience the rich culture and community of this iconic northern city.
