Course

Starts and finishes at Gull Lake High School on N 34th Street, Richland. The course winds through the streets neighbouring the Gull Lake schools and the surrounding countryside, with aid stations along the route. The 10K runs the 5K loop twice, with separate start and finish chutes. A 1K fun run/walk is held the evening before.

About

Kathy's Run for Cancer is actually based off of an event that started nearly a decade ago by a group of passionate citizens. They found a way to combine their love for running and connecting with the community in an annual road race. Reach out to our organizers to learn more about this Summer's 5K and 10K races.
