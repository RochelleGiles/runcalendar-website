Course

Both races start and finish at Park Place on the corner of 17th and Central Avenue in downtown Ashland, going off at 8:00am. The 5K runs the Central/Carter Avenue course; the 10K branches onto 13th Street and crosses the blue bridge to Ohio and back twice before returning via Carter Avenue and 21st Street to the finish. City streets throughout.

About

A 10K run and 5K run/walk staged as part of the Summer Motion festival in Ashland, Kentucky. Both races start and finish at Park Place in the middle of town, and the 10K crosses the Ohio River bridge twice before returning through the city streets to the line.
