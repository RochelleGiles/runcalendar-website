About

Plunge into the nocturnal world of the Vallée de Joux! The Nocturnal Trail invites you to live a unique experience: running by the light of headlamps, on wild trails, through deep forests, gentle ridges, and silhouettes of spruce trees. Over 10 km, this accessible but demanding course offers an unforgettable sensory immersion, where the sounds of the night replace the noise of the day, and the effort reveals new emotions. A natural, preserved, and authentic playground.
