Course

Combination of loops and out-and-back on the Pinhoti Trail in northwest Georgia, starting near Snake Creek Gap and finishing near Dalton. Entirely singletrack over rock gardens, ridgelines and pine forest, taking in Keown Falls and John's Mountain. Longer distances repeat loops through the Dry Creek trail system, returning through the same aid stations.

About

The Georgia Jewel is a trail ultramarathon in the mountains near Dalton, Georgia, with 100-mile, 50-mile, 35-mile, and 18-mile distance options. The rugged, technical course crosses forested ridgelines in the Appalachian foothills of northwest Georgia.
