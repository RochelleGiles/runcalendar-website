About

The AXA Raheny 5 Mile is Ireland's premier road race, attracting top runners from across the country to compete on a scenic course through the charming Dublin suburb of Raheny. Firmly established as a must-do event on the Irish running calendar, this popular race offers a challenging yet enjoyable challenge for runners of all abilities.
