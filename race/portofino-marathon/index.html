About

The Portofino Marathon is a scenic running event that takes place in the picturesque coastal town of Portofino, Italy. The course winds through the charming streets and offers stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea and the surrounding hills. Runners will experience the unique atmosphere of this historic Italian destination, making this a truly memorable event for both participants and spectators.
