Course

A rugged course of about 24 km on fire trails and walking tracks weaving through Hamilton Island's undulating interior, with sustained climbs and descents. It can be run solo or split between a team of two or three. Junior events of 3 km, 1.5 km and 500 m run alongside.

About

Tackle the hilly terrain of Hamilton Island's interior at the annual Hilly Half Marathon. This scenic 21.1km event takes runners on a challenging course through the island's winding roads, fire trails, and walking tracks, showcasing the region's stunning natural beauty. With a mix of uphill and downhill sections, this race offers a unique test of endurance for both seasoned and aspiring half-marathon runners.
