Course

Point-to-point from the Baird Center on Wisconsin Ave through downtown Milwaukee and along Lake Michigan, crossing the Hoan Bridge near miles 7.5 and 23.5; over 40% of the course runs beside the lake. Marathon and half share the first 10 miles, both with 6.5-hour limits from the 7:00 am start; the 5K runs Saturday.

About

Experience Milwaukee at its absolute best. The Milwaukee Lakefront Marathon course delivers unmatched views of the city skyline, lakefront parks, and the natural beauty that defines the Midwest. Runners cross the iconic Hoan Bridge, a once-a-year opportunity that can only happen at the Lakefront Marathon, offering sweeping, unforgettable views of Lake Michigan and downtown Milwaukee. More than 40% of the course runs alongside Lake Michigan, showcasing miles of shoreline, waterfront parks, and open water that make this one of the most scenic marathon and half marathon courses in the Midwest.
