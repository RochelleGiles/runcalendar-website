About

Run for Your Life (RFYL) is an annual charity 5k/10k Fun Run/Walk & Health Fair organized by the students at PNWU. As future health professionals, students aim to bring the community together to promote healthy lifestyles. This event has become a way for PNWU students to contribute to the greater Yakima area by providing a family-friendly, healthy, and safe activity.
