Course

Marathon, half marathon and 5K all start at 7:00am from Cotter Schools, 181 Mabel Street, Cotter. The organisers describe a flat, fast course running along the White River. The field is capped at 800 entrants across all three distances.

About

White River Marathon is a road race in Cotter, Arkansas, following the White River, a renowned trout fishing destination in the Ozarks known as Trout Capital USA. The event offers marathon, half marathon, and 5K distances.
