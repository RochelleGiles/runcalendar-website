About

The Ultra Trail Qingdao is a unique 336-kilometer ultra trail running event in northern China. Designed by trail running expert Zhang Youliang, this challenging race showcases Qingdao's stunning coastal landscape, connecting urban and rural environments. Runners will experience the city's bay, peninsula, and island terrains while exploring the beautiful summer climate of Qingdao.
