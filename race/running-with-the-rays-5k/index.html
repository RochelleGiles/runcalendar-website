About

The Running with the Rays 5K is a community race presented by Webull, starting on 1st Avenue South through downtown St. Petersburg, routing through Al Lang Stadium, and finishing at Tropicana Field. Registration includes a medal, t-shirt, timing chip, race bib, and tickets to a Rays game and Rowdies match.
