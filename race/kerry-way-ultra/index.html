About

The Kerry Way Ultra is a challenging and scenic ultra-marathon that winds through the stunning landscapes of Ireland's Kerry region. Runners will traverse rugged mountain trails, lush forests, and picturesque coastal paths as they push their limits on this historic route. With breathtaking views at every turn, this event offers an unforgettable adventure for endurance athletes seeking an extraordinary running experience.
