About

Get ready for the 10th annual Mardi Crawl, where we blend a wonderful winter run with a festive Mardi Gras celebration, Colorado-style! Starting from the award-winning Apex Center in Arvada, you can choose your challenge: relatively gentle 5K and 10K courses, or a more challenging half marathon course. All races take you along the acclaimed Ralston Creek Trail, featuring shady, creekside beauty through Arvada's picturesque open spaces and charming golf course communities.
