Course

The course starts and finishes at Kellogg Zaher Park in Las Vegas and follows the paved park paths and the adjacent trail system. It is mostly flat, gaining around 130 feet from a start elevation of 2,543 feet. The 10K covers the 5K loop twice.

About

This Mother's Day weekend event features 10K, 5K, and 1-mile races at Floyd Lamb Park in Las Vegas, celebrating moms with tech shirts, medals, and flowers for mothers. 100% of net proceeds support Helping Hands of Vegas.
