About

The 19th annual Wisco Mile will be at Wisconsin Lutheran High School (forever nicknamed Wisco). We renovated the Wisco Athletic Complex with a brand new track and scoreboard. We are excited to showcase it for the distance running community of the Midwest. Join us on a fun and eventful evening as we witness history being made.
