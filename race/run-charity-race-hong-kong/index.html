Course

All distances start and finish at the BBQ Site Area 2 in Tai Tam Country Park, reached via Parkview on Hong Kong Island. The 15km and 8km races go off first in the morning, followed by shorter junior distances of 3km and 1.5km; 8km and 15km can also be run as a pair.
