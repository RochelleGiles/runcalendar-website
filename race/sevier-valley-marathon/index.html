Course

A point-to-point, net-downhill marathon starting at the Big Rock Candy Mountain trailhead on Historic Utah Highway 89 at 5,803 feet and finishing at the south end of Richfield near Cove View Golf Course at 5,327 feet. The route follows the Candy Mountain Express bike path through canyon and valley. Nine aid stations carry water, food, electrolytes and restrooms. The course is USATF certified.

About

The course is USATF certified and a Boston Marathon Qualifying race! The route follows the Candy Mountain Express bike path and winds through the beautiful Marysvale Canyon and Sevier Valley.
