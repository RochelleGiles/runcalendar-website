About

A 5K run and walk at Okeeheelee Park in West Palm Beach, raising money for the Mary Murray Scholarship at Young Singers of the Palm Beaches. Both distances start together at 8:00 AM from the Micanopy Pavilion, with age-category medals and a pancake breakfast afterwards.
