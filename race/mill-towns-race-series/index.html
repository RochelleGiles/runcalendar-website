Course

A looping course along the Cannon River between Dundas and Northfield, starting and finishing at Memorial Park in Dundas. The marathon runs four loops, the half marathon two and the 10K one. USATF-certified and Boston-qualifying, with water stops on course.

About

The Mill Towns Race Series offers chip-timed marathon, half marathon and 10K distances, all run on a flat, scenic course. The marathon is a Boston-qualifying, certified course. Take the stress out of racing toward your next PR or running your first half marathon or marathon!
