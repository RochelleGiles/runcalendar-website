About

The Vincennes Forest Half Marathon is a scenic and challenging race that takes runners through the lush, historic Vincennes Forest just outside of Paris. Boasting stunning views of the French countryside, this event offers a unique opportunity to experience the natural beauty of the region while pushing your limits on a well-designed course. Whether you're a seasoned runner or new to the sport, the Vincennes Forest Half Marathon promises an unforgettable running adventure.
