Course

Two loop courses from Eagles Nest, Hyner, on singletrack through the Pennsylvania mountains, taking in ridge vistas and stream crossings. The 25K has three major climbs and descents for about 4,000ft of gain; the 50K has five and roughly 6,500ft. Fields are capped at 1,000 for the 25K and 500 for the 50K.

About

The Hyner View Trail Challenge is a grueling backcountry test through the Pennsylvania mountains, praised by elite ultrarunner Zach Miller as one of the most fun courses in the world. Both the 25K and 50K rank among the toughest trail events in the region.
