Course

A 5-mile fell race with 1,250ft of climb above Hayfield in the Peak District, starting and finishing at the bottom of Morland Road with registration and prizegiving at the Sycamore Inn. Late-November conditions are part of the character — frequent mud, often rain, occasionally snow and ice. Entry is a few pounds, cheaper for FRA members.

About

A fell race starting in Hayfield, a village on the edge of the Peak District's Kinder Scout plateau, run each November as part of the Hayfield Fell Races series.
