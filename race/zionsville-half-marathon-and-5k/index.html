Course

Starts in the heart of Zionsville, running past the shops and over the historic bricks of Main Street before heading out beyond the town limits into open countryside. The USATF-certified half then joins the flat, fast Rail Trail back to the finish at Lions Club Park; the 5K stays close to downtown.

About

The Zionsville Half-Marathon & 5K finishes on Zionsville's historic brick Main Street in the town's downtown district. Runners can choose the half marathon or 5K distance through this Indianapolis suburb.
