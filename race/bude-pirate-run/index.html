Course

A multi-terrain coastal loop of about eight miles starting and finishing at Maer Down, Crooklets, with roughly 370m of climb. The course mixes coast path, fields and woodland with a couple of short road sections, a ford crossing at Coombe and a steep climb out of Duckpool. Water is available at Stowe Barton around mile three, Sandymouth around mile six, and at the finish.

About

A themed 8-mile multi-terrain race around Bude, the Cornish seaside town known for its beaches and 19th-century canal. Run each September by Bude Rats running club, the route combines coastal paths with inland lanes.
