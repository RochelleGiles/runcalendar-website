Course

An out-and-back half marathon starting and finishing beside the Bideford AAC clubhouse, running the countryside beside the River Torridge towards Great Torrington. The outward leg is on road and the return follows the Tarka Trail, including a tunnel. Start is at 10.30am, with a medal and t-shirt for finishers.

About

A half marathon organised by Bideford Amateur Athletic Club, starting from this historic Devon port town on the River Torridge estuary. Now in its fourth decade, it's one of the longest-running road races in North Devon.
