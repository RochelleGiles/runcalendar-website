About

The Halbmarathon Ingolstadt is a must-run event for any avid runner. Held in the picturesque city of Ingolstadt, this half marathon takes participants on a scenic tour through the historic streets and along the banks of the Danube River. With a challenging yet rewarding course, the Halbmarathon Ingolstadt offers an unforgettable running experience for athletes of all levels.
