Course

An obstacle course event held on natural terrain at Barossa Helicopters, Lyndoch, South Australia. The 10km course carries more than 30 obstacles and the 5km more than 15, among them A-frames, rope traversals, monkey bars, walls, sandbag carries and tyre walls. Mud is a feature; long pants and running or hiking shoes are advised.

About

True Grit Barossa is a military-inspired obstacle course race held in the Barossa wine region at Lyndoch, South Australia, with 5km and 10km distances combining running with obstacles.
