About

The 39th edition of the Cross des Coteaux de Crolles event features a 24km trail run with 1500m of elevation gain, an 11km cross-country race with 400m of elevation gain, and a 11km walking route. The event also includes children's races from 0.5km to 3km. The course takes runners through the scenic Crolles hills, offering stunning views of the valley.
