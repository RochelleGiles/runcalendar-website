Course

All distances start at Rio Grande Park in central Aspen, Colorado, on an Independence Day morning schedule. The programme is a 5-mile race, a 5K and an untimed 1-mile family and canine run/walk.

About

Boogie's Buddy Race is an Aspen Independence Day tradition where runners, families and dogs gather to kick off the Fourth of July with purpose and community spirit.
