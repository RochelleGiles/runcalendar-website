About

The Trail De Consolation takes place in the spectacular natural amphitheater of Val de Consolation. The course features forests, rivers, waterfalls, cliffs and historic heritage sites, offering runners a unique blend of natural beauty and historical significance. This sporting adventure provides participants with an exceptional experience in the heart of a remarkable site.
