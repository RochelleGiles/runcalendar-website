Course

Single-track racing in the Blue Mountains at Jasper Mountain Reserve, built from three loops: Red at 3.3 miles, Green at 4.4 and Black at 5.4. The half marathon runs all three once for 2,570 feet of climb and the marathon twice for 5,140 feet, over rugged ground with steep descents. A 4.4-mile family run and a four-person 52.4-mile relay use the same trails.

About

Jasper Mountain Trail Fest is a fundraising event that benefits Jasper Mountain Reserve, an environmental 501(c)3 non-profit organization to improve and enhance wildlife habitats for rare and endangered flora and fauna native to the Blue Mountains in the Southeast corner of Washington State. Come and enjoy the beautiful natural scenery with the knowledge that your signup funds will benefit the conservation of the reserve.
