Course

A fast, flat loop around the lake at Pegasus Town, north of Christchurch, taking in water views, walkways and the surrounding streets. The half marathon, a 9 km run or walk and a 2.5 km Kidz Dash for ages 7 to 13 all start and finish on Pegasus Main Street.

About

The Canterbury Half Marathon is a popular running event in New Zealand's South Island. The scenic course takes runners through the picturesque Canterbury region, showcasing the area's natural beauty. With a welcoming atmosphere and enthusiastic local support, this event is a must-do for runners of all levels.
