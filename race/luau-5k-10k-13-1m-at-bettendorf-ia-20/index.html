Course

All three races run a repeated out-and-back from Middle Park in Bettendorf, starting at 8:30 a.m. The 5K covers the loop once, the 10K twice and the half marathon four times. A course time limit of three and a half hours applies.

About

The Luau 5K, 10K, and Half Marathon is a fun running event held in Bettendorf, IA, featuring out-and-back courses with finisher medals for all participants. Runners are encouraged to wear costumes for a Best Costume award, with packet pickup and parking at Middle Park.
