About

Manhattan Running Company's spring race in Tuttle Creek State Park, in its eighth year in 2027. The marathon is four laps of a 6.55-mile park loop and the half is two, on a USATF-certified course with less than 100 feet of climb per lap; the 5K loops the campground and a 1 mile is open to all. Marathon and half marathon relays run the same loops with a timing bib handed over at the start and finish. Every finisher gets a medal, and the race benefits the Manhattan Parks and Recreation Foundation.
