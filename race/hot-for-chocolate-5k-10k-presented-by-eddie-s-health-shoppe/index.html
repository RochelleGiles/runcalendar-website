Course

Both the 5K and the 10K start and finish at World's Fair Park on Worlds Fair Park Drive in downtown Knoxville, with a 9:00am start for each. The organiser describes views over the city; chocolate treats and a finisher medal are given at the finish.

About

A Valentine's Day-themed running event featuring 5K and 10K distances starting from World's Fair Park in downtown Knoxville. Participants receive chocolate treats and other finisher gifts at the finish line.
