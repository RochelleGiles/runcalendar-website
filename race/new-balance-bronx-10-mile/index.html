Course

A ten-mile out-and-back through the Bronx, starting on the Grand Concourse near Yankee Stadium and finishing close to 161st Street. Runners head north to Mosholu Parkway, turn back at two U-turns, then return south down the Grand Concourse. Water and Gatorade stations line the route, with a self-serve filtered-water refill station near mile 8.
