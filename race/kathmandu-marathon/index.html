About

The Kathmandu Marathon is a captivating running event set against the breathtaking backdrop of Nepal's vibrant capital city. Weaving through historic landmarks and scenic urban landscapes, this race offers runners a unique opportunity to experience the rich culture and natural beauty of Kathmandu. Whether you're a seasoned marathoner or a passionate runner seeking an unforgettable adventure, the Kathmandu Marathon promises an unforgettable challenge and an immersive journey through this enchanting destination.
