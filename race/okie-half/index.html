Course

Both the half marathon and the 5K start and finish inside a restored historic aeroplane hangar at the Hatbox Events Center in Muskogee. The courses are flat and USATF certified, run on sealed surfaces.

About

The Okie Half is an annual half marathon and 5K held in Muskogee, Oklahoma, part of the Green Country region's racing calendar. The event offers both distances for runners of varying experience levels.
