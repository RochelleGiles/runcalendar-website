Course

A road 5K through Northfield village, starting in front of the Northfield Savings Bank and finishing at Depot Square. Mile markers are posted along the route and a water station sits at halfway. A one-mile kids' race on Route 12 runs alongside it.

About

The Northfield 5K Run/Walk is a community road race hosted by Central Vermont Runners in Northfield, Vermont, doubling as a Vermont Senior Games event with a One Mile Kids' Race alongside the main run.
