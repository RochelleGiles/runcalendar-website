About

The Ardennes Mega Trail is a unique adventure in the Ardennes massif of France. The 5 races, from the most accessible to the most demanding, traverse forest paths and often unseen singles through the Meuse and Semoy valleys. Runners will be immersed in the magnificent landscapes of the Ardennes region, including the challenging Dames de Meuse and Côte de Madagascar slopes.
