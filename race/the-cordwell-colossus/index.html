Course

A single 20-mile trail race starting and finishing at Holmesfield Village Hall near Dronfield, on a mix of highly runnable trail and lane with stretches of single track and trod. Around 950 m of ascent. Two fully stocked checkpoints on route, with more refreshments at the village hall for finishers.

About

The Cordwell Colossus is a challenging summer trail run on the edge of the Peak District in North Derbyshire, United Kingdom. Runners will test their endurance on a scenic course through the rugged terrain, with stunning views of the surrounding countryside. This event promises an unforgettable adventure for outdoor enthusiasts.
