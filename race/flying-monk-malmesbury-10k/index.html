Course

The race starts at 10:30 from Malmesbury Victoria FC and runs a single 10K loop on the country roads around the historic town. The route is spectator friendly with distance markers every kilometre, and free cider or a mimosa is offered at the finish.

About

The Flying Monk Malmesbury 10k is a mixed-terrain race on the quiet country roads around Malmesbury in Wiltshire. The undulating route includes about 1.5km of off-road running along the Old Fosse Way, the oldest Roman road in the country.
