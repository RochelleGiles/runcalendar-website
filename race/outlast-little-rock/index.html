Course

A six-hour timed challenge on the Burns Park Equestrian Trail in North Little Rock. Runners set out on a 5K trail lap every hour on the hour and must finish each lap inside its hour to stay in; the loop is dirt trail with rocks, roots and occasional pavement. The event runs 7:30am to 1:30pm.

About

A six-hour trail endurance challenge held at Burns Park in North Little Rock, Arkansas. Runners complete a 5K lap every hour on the hour, dropping out when they can no longer finish a lap within the hour, with awards for reaching four, five or all six laps.
