Course

A six-hour lapped trail event at Holme Pierrepont Country Park and Watersports Centre east of Nottingham, run as repeats of a 5.44km loop. Four laps make a half marathon, eight a marathon and nine or more an ultra. The surface is compacted gravel path with a stretch of natural wetland path, mud, roots and puddles, and a section alongside the rowing lake. Pretty flat, with two alternate loops available when the wetlands flood.

About

Explore the picturesque Holme Pierrepont Country Park near Nottingham during the Pierrepont Plod Spring, a 6-hour running challenge that puts your endurance to the test. Navigate the scenic trails and embrace the crisp, invigorating atmosphere as you push your limits in this unique winter running event.
