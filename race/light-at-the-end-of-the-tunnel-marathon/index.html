About

The Light at the End of the Tunnel Marathon and Half Marathon start inside the historic Snoqualmie Tunnel near North Bend, Washington, then follow the old rail grade downhill along the Palouse to Cascades Trail. The net-downhill course draws runners chasing a fast time.
