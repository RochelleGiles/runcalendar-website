About

The first of the Rockford Road Runners' four Tour de Trails races, run every winter since 2013: three miles on the trails of Blackhawk Springs Forest Preserve, starting at 10:00. Expect winter underfoot, which can mean ice, snow and cold. Each race is entered on its own, or all four together as the series.
