Course

An out-and-back 9.06K from Bridge View Park in St. Ignace, heading west along Boulevard Drive for 4.53 km before turning back. The surface is hard-packed limestone gravel with some potholes, and the route looks out over the Mackinac Bridge and the water. Water and Gatorade are at the turnaround, with food and drinks at the finish.

About

Join Yooper Running Company on the Sunday before Labor Day to celebrate the Yooper Area Code of 906 with a 9.06K. The race takes place along one of the most gorgeous, stunning, and little-known views of the Mackinac Bridge at 9:00am.
