Course

A four-day, 184-mile stage race along the Thames Path from Kemble Station near the river's Cotswold source to the Thames Barrier at Greenwich. The daily stages run 57, 49, 47 and 33 miles, entered solo or as a relay team. Checkpoints on each stage provide water and light nutrition.

About

The MTC Thames Challenge is a captivating multi-stage ultrarunning event that takes participants on an unforgettable journey along the banks of England's iconic River Thames. Over the course of four days, runners will cover 184 miles, immersing themselves in the stunning natural landscapes and historic landmarks that line this legendary waterway.
