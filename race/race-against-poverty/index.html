Course

A 5K run and walk on roads in Chambersburg, with traffic control at crosswalks; walkers use the footpaths and follow normal pedestrian rules at junctions. A one mile children's race and a free half mile Kids Dash run alongside it.

About

The Race Against Poverty is an opportunity to take steps as a community against the poverty that grips many of our own. Poverty impacts the entire community and requires community solutions. Together we can build a stronger future for all!
