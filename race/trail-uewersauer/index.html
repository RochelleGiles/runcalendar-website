About

Trail Uewersauer takes its name from the Upper Sûre (Uewersauer) lake in Luxembourg's Éislek region, looping through the forests and hills around Lac de la Haute-Sûre near Heiderscheid. Distances range from a 10K up to a 57K ultra through this lake and forest landscape.
