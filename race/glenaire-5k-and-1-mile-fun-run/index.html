Course

Start and finish at 5000 Glenhope Court, Cary, North Carolina. The 5K is a fast, rolling USA Track and Field certified road course through the neighbourhoods near downtown Cary, with a short stretch on the Higgins Greenway and one water stop near the two-mile mark. The one-mile fun run is an out-and-back through the community.

About

Glenaire Life Plan Community is proud to present the 10th annual Glenaire 5K and 1 Mile Fun Run. The race will start and finish at the Community located at 5000 Glenhope Court in Cary.
