About

El Vaquero Loco ('the crazy cowboy') is a high-altitude mountain ultramarathon out of Afton, Wyoming, with 50K and 31K distances through the Salt River Range above the Star Valley. Expect steep climbs, alpine terrain and the thin air typical of Wyoming's high country.
