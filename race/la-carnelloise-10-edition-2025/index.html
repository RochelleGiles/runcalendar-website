About

The La Carnelloise 10 is a must-run event for any avid runner, set against the picturesque backdrop of the Carnelle region in France. This historic 10-mile race takes participants on a scenic tour through the countryside, offering a challenging yet rewarding experience for runners of all levels. Whether you're seeking a new personal best or simply a scenic day of running, the La Carnelloise 10 is sure to impress.
