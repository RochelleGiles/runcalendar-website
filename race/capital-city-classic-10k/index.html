Course

A 10K through downtown Little Rock, starting at Markham and Broadway and finishing about half a mile away at Capitol and Louisiana. A kids 1 mile out-and-back crossing the Broadway Bridge is held alongside it. Wheelchair and handcycle categories are offered.

About

The Capital City Classic 10K is Arkansas' oldest 10K, run through downtown Little Rock. It features a free Kids' Mile, prize money for elite finishers, and pace teams ranging from 40 minutes to a 'Party Pace' group of two hours, welcoming runners and walkers of all abilities.
