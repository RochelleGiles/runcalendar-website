Course

Trail races around Loch Ness in the Scottish Highlands. The 100 miler starts in Inverness with roughly 4,000m of climb and a 40 hour cut-off; the 110km single and multi-stage, the 50km and the 25km all start at Dores, and the 60km at Fort Augustus. The 16km Dash sets off from Dores at noon on the Sunday. For 2027 the routes run the opposite way round the loch.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Scottish Highlands at Ultra X Scotland, a challenging multi-stage ultra marathon and trail running event. Tackle distances ranging from 12km to 110km across two days, immersing yourself in the region's breathtaking landscapes. Whether you're a seasoned ultra-runner or looking to push your limits, this event promises an unforgettable adventure.
