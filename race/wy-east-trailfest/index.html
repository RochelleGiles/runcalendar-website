Course

Mountain trail courses on the flanks of Mt. Hood, based at Mt. Hood Meadows ski area. The point-to-point 50-mile and 50K run from Underhill Site and Surveyors Ridge over dirt singletrack, historic rocky doubletrack and volcanic rock trails to the Meadows lodge; shorter Sunday races stay near the resort.

About

Wy'east Trailfest is a celebration of mountain trail running featuring Mt. Hood's illustrious alpine terrain. Wildly beautiful, dynamic and challenging 50 miler, 50K, 28K & 14K mountain trail courses reach Oregon's alpine rooftop at 8000' on Saturday, followed by more cruisy and scenic Half Marathon & 8K (5 miler) trail races on Sunday for those looking for a more approachable trail experience.
