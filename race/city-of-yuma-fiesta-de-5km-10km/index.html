About

The City of Yuma Fiesta de 5km & 10km takes place on trails within West Wetlands Park in Yuma, Arizona, starting from the Playa Linda area. The course is mostly paved or groomed trail with some rugged sections, and pre-registration is required, with no same-day entry. Runners celebrate afterward with a post-race fiesta of chips and salsa.
