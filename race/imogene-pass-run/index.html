Course

A 17.1-mile point-to-point mountain race from the corner of 4th Avenue and Main Street in Ouray, over Imogene Pass, down to a finish in Telluride. Checkpoints with cutoffs sit at Upper Camp Bird (7.6 mi) and the pass summit (10 mi); the finish closes at 2:30pm after the 7:30am start. Weather can include subfreezing temperatures, snow and fog.
