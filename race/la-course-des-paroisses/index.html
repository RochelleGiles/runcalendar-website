About

La Course des Paroisses is a unique trail running event that takes place in the remarkable Esterel forest. Organized by the parishes of Saint-Raphaël and the Aumônerie Association, the event has attracted over 500 runners and mobilized a hundred volunteers in recent editions. This is not just a sporting challenge, but also an opportunity to support the young people of the Saint-Raphaël chaplaincy, who can participate in camps, meaningful evenings at the chaplaincy, and benefit from a welcoming and well-maintained facility throughout the year.
