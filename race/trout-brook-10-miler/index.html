Course

A 10-mile singletrack race in Willow River State Park, Hudson, starting near the registration area. The route leaves the wide park trails for tight, technical lines through the trees, with real climbs, a stair section and a waterfall. One aid station sits at roughly the 4.2-mile midpoint.

About

Get ready to tackle some serious terrain at the Trout Brook 10-miler, a challenging trail race through Willow River State Park. This race will take you off the wide-open park trails and onto various single-track trails that will test your skills. The course includes hills, a stair climb, and breathtaking views of a majestic waterfall. This race is ideal for experienced trail runners seeking a new challenge or anyone looking to explore the beautiful, less-traveled areas of Willow River State Park. Take advantage of this opportunity to push yourself out of your comfort zone and experience the thrill of a proper trail race.
