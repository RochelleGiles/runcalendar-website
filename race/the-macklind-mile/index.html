Course

Straight one-mile road race down Macklind Avenue in the Southampton neighbourhood of St. Louis. The start line sits at the intersection of Macklind and Sublette and the finish is at 5352 Devonshire Avenue. A quarter-mile kids dash is run on the same street.

About

The Macklind Mile is a fast, family-friendly, downhill one-mile road race held annually in South City, St. Louis. It features multiple waves including competitive, recreational, elite, kids 1/4 mile dash, and dog mile events with live music and vendors.
