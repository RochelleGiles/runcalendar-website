About

The Trail de la Dent du Chat climbs from Le Bourget-du-Lac up to the Dent du Chat, the 1,390m rocky summit overlooking the Lac du Bourget near Chambéry. The 27km route gains around 1,500m, alongside a shorter 12km option.
