Course

Out and back courses starting and finishing at Fairhaven Park, Bellingham. The half marathon and 10K follow the Interurban Trail above Chuckanut and Samish Bay, turning at the Lost Lake parking lot; the 3K/6K runs in the Hundred Acre Woods. Aid stations sit roughly every two miles, with seven on the half marathon.

About

Run Like a Girl is a half marathon, 10K and 3K/6K based at Fairhaven Park in Bellingham. The half marathon and 10K follow the Interurban Trail along Bellingham Bay, while the 3K/6K runs through the Hundred Acre Woods.
