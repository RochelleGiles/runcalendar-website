Course

All races start at Slalom Plaza, Falls Creek. The 100 mile course covers 160km with 7,400m of ascent and descent over six major climbs: Mt Feathertop, Mt Hotham, Mt McKay, Spion Kopje, Mt Nelse and Mt Bogong. Course maps are published as Avenza and PDF downloads. Surface breakdown, aid station detail and finish location are not stated.

About

Conquer the rugged peaks of the Victorian Alps at the Alpine Challenge, one of Australia's most demanding and awe-inspiring mountain trail runs. Set against the breathtaking backdrop of Falls Creek, this multi-day event tests the limits of even the most seasoned ultrarunners with its technical terrain and relentless climbs. Runners who dare to take on this challenge will be rewarded with stunning vistas and a profound sense of accomplishment.
