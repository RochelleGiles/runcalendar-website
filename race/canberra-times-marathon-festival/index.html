Course

A road marathon on closed roads starting at King George Terrace, Parkes. It loops Parliament House and Telopea Park, runs via Kings Avenue and State Circle to Yarralumla and Weston Park, crosses Commonwealth Avenue Bridge with turnarounds at Glenloch Interchange and Morshead Drive, then takes a second Telopea loop to the finish. Aid stations sit every 2.5-3km.

About

The Canberra Times Marathon Festival is a premier running event in the heart of Australia's capital city. Runners will experience a scenic course that showcases the city's iconic landmarks and natural beauty. With a range of distances to choose from, the event caters to runners of all levels, creating a vibrant and inclusive atmosphere.
