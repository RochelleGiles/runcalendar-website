Course

A scenic South Bay road 10K with a split start at Ardmore Avenue & 4th Street and Valley Drive & 3rd Street; the two groups merge at 15th Street after half a mile. The route runs through downtown and residential Manhattan Beach and finishes in the lower parking lot just south of the Manhattan Beach Pier. Rolling, rated moderate to slightly difficult. No race-day entry.

About

The Manhattan Beach 10K is a longtime beach-city road race in the South Bay of Los Angeles, with a course through Manhattan Beach neighborhoods near the Strand and the Pacific coastline. Now in its fifth decade, it's one of the area's oldest running events.
