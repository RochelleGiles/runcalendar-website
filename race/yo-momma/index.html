Course

Run on the Pioneer Trail near Middleburg in northeast Florida. The trail is split into sections of varied terrain, with Section 4 (2.7 miles) holding some of the most vertical and technical ground in the state. The 50K and trail marathon share the same trail system.

About

The 4th annual ( This ain't yo ) Momma's 50k & Trail Marathon, Sombrero Running Events likes to have fun and enjoy the beautiful scenery of this majestic trail here in NEFL. The Pioneer Trail IS NOT YOUR TYPICAL FLORIDA TRAIL, and the reason for that is each section has its own diverse style of terrain with Section 4 ( 2.7 miles ) having some of the most vertical and technical terrain you can find in Florida.
