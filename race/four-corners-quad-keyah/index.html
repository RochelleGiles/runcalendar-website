About

The Four Corners Quad Keyah is a four-day running event at the Four Corners Monument near Teec Nos Pos, Arizona. Participants run a marathon or half marathon in a different state each day from December 3–6, choosing between a one-mile paved circuit and 5.25-mile trail loops, with the series capped at 150 runners.
