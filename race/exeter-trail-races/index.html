Course

Lapped races in the grounds of Escot Park at Ottery St Mary, Devon. The course is all off-road with short sharp climbs and muddy sections: one lap of about 10.5 km and 170 m of climb for the 10K, two laps and around 340 m for the half marathon. Canicross races run alongside both.

About

The Exeter Trail Races offer runners a chance to explore the stunning trails and landscapes of Escot Park. Weaving through this picturesque countryside setting, the event showcases the very best of the region's natural beauty. Whether you're an experienced trail runner or new to the sport, this event promises an unforgettable running experience in the heart of the United Kingdom.
