Course

Both races start at Riverview Park on Barton Avenue. The 8K is a road course through the historic Riverview neighbourhood on the North Shore and is known for its challenging hills; the accompanying one-mile is flat and fast through the streets beside the Chattanooga Golf and Country Club.

About

Established in 1968, The Chattanooga Chase is this area's oldest active competitive road race. The event features an 8k and 1-mile race through the scenic and historic Riverview neighborhood on the North Shore.
