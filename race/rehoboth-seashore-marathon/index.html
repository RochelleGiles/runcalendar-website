Course

Marathon and half marathon start together on Rehoboth Avenue at the bandstand at 7:00am and finish on 4th Street behind Rehoboth Beach Running Co. Fields are capped at 1,100 for the marathon and 2,200 for the half. Entry is online only, with no registration at packet pickup or on race day.

About

A road marathon and half marathon in the seaside town of Rehoboth Beach, Delaware, held every December. The course runs through the coastal community, giving runners an off-season look at one of the state's most popular beach towns before finishing near the shore.
