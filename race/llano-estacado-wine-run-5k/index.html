Course

Start and finish at Llano Estacado Winery, 3426 FM 1585, Lubbock, Texas. The route is mostly cross-country style on grass, flat but with many turns through the vineyards and uneven footing, and includes a pass through the wine cellar. Suited to runners and walkers alike.

About

The Llano Estacado Wine Run 5k is a timed 5k run/walk event held at Llano Estacado Winery in Lubbock, TX, featuring a flat cross-country course through vineyards. Participants receive a t-shirt, shatterproof wine glass, finisher medal, race bib, and a pour of wine.
