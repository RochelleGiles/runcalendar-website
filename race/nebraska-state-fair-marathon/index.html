Course

All distances start and finish on the Nebraska State Fairgrounds in Grand Island, with a 6:30am start and a six-hour course limit for the marathon. The marathon is a certified Boston qualifier on a flat course. Marathon, half, 4-person marathon relay, 5K and kids 1-mile are offered, plus push-rim wheelchair and handcycle divisions.

About

The Nebraska State Fair Marathon runs through Grand Island on a course billed as one of the flattest and fastest in the country. Runners can choose the marathon, half marathon, 5K, or a marathon relay, with a kids' fun mile rounding out race weekend.
