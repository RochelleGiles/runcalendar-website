About

The Teal Diva 5K is a run and walk at McAlpine Park in Charlotte, North Carolina, raising funds for women affected by gynecologic cancer. Entrants choose a timed 5K, an untimed option for ages 12 and under, or a virtual 5K, with a short inflatable dash held after the finish.
