Course

About

A scenic 5K race through Papago Park and into the Phoenix Zoo's ZooLights display, featuring a commemorative t-shirt, hot cocoa, and ZooLights admission. The event also includes a 1-mile leisure loop for families and walkers, with activities at the lakeside area and awards for top finishers.
