Course

A twilight race on the packed snow paths of the La Rosière ski area in the Tarentaise, starting from the Les Eucherts snow front. Three routes climb the mountain: 5.3 km with 244 m of ascent, 10.5 km with 431 m and 20.1 km with 862 m. The 20 km sets off at 5:15pm and the shorter races at 6:00pm, so headlamps are compulsory.

About

The Trail Blanc de La Rosière is a winter evening trail race in the French Alps, run on the snow-covered paths of the La Rosière resort above Bourg-Saint-Maurice. Runners choose a 5, 10 or 20 km route climbing from the Les Eucherts snow front, finishing in the dark with headlamps. Entry is inexpensive and bib numbers are limited.
