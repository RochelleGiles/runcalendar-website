Course

A point-to-point road marathon through central Milan, starting on Corso Sempione and finishing in Piazza del Duomo. Ten refreshment points, one roughly every 5km plus two extra, with a 6 hour 30 minute time limit. A relay marathon, family run and dog run share the day.

About

The Milan Marathon is a premier running event in the heart of Italy's fashion capital. Participants can expect a scenic course that winds through the city's historic streets and landmarks, offering a unique challenge and unforgettable experience for runners of all levels. With a rich history and a vibrant atmosphere, the Milan Marathon is a must-attend event for anyone seeking an exceptional running adventure.
