About

Explore the stunning Dolomite mountains at the Hero Südtirol Dolomites, a captivating running event that takes participants on a challenging journey through the heart of this UNESCO World Heritage site. With breathtaking vistas and a course that winds through picturesque villages, this race offers a truly unique and unforgettable experience for runners of all levels.
