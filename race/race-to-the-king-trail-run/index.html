Course

Point-to-point ultra along the South Downs Way through the South Downs National Park, finishing at West Dean Gardens. The Coastal 50k loops around Chichester Harbour and Bosham; the Castle 50k passes Arundel Castle, and the longer distances combine both loops. Fully signposted with regular pit-stops and hot food at halfway.

About

Conquer the rolling hills and breathtaking vistas of the South Downs Way and Monarch's Way in the Race to the King Trail Run, an unforgettable ultramarathon experience in the heart of the United Kingdom. Runners will be captivated by the stunning natural landscapes as they push their limits on this challenging yet rewarding course.
