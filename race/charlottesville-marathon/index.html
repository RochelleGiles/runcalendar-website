About

The Charlottesville Marathon is a spring road race in Charlottesville, Virginia, offering marathon, half marathon and 8K distances. All three courses start and finish in downtown Charlottesville and run on quiet roads and bike paths along the Rivanna River and through the surrounding neighbourhoods.
