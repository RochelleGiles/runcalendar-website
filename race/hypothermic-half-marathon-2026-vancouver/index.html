About

The Hypothermic Half Marathon is a winter running event organised by the Running Room, held annually in multiple Canadian cities. Known for its festive atmosphere and scenic courses, the Vancouver edition starts and finishes at the Stanley Park Pavilion, a landmark nestled within one of the city's most beautiful green spaces.
