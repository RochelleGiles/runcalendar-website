About

The Hueston Woods Trail Race is a multi-day trail running event held at Hueston Woods State Park in Ohio, featuring various distances over two days on single-track trails, pavement, and grass. Organized by the Wolf Pack, it highlights the park's limestone and shale bedrock with frequent fossil finds.
