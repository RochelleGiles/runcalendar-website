About

The Angkor Wat International Half Marathon runs through the Angkor Archaeological Park in Siem Reap, Cambodia, passing dozens of centuries-old temples including Angkor Wat itself. Half marathon, 10km, 5km and 3km distances are offered on mostly flat park roads.
