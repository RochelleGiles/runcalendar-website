Course

Run on the Tan track around the Royal Botanic Gardens in Melbourne, starting and finishing at Alexandra Avenue. Entrants complete either one lap (3.8km) or two (7.6km) at their own pace, with marshals at the foot and top of the Anderson Street hill.

About

Celebrate diversity and community at the vibrant Melbourne Pride Run, taking place in the lush Botanical Gardens. Runners of all levels are invited to join in the festivities and show their pride, while enjoying a scenic course and a welcoming atmosphere.
