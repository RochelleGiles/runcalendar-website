About

The SVE Hamburg Half Marathon is a must-run event for avid runners seeking a scenic and challenging race experience. Set against the backdrop of Hamburg's iconic landmarks, this half-marathon course winds through the city's historic districts and picturesque waterfront, offering participants a unique opportunity to explore the vibrant heart of this German metropolis.
