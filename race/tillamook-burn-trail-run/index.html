Course

Two courses on singletrack in Tillamook State Forest, based at Reehers Camp. The 50 mile on Saturday is point to point; the 55K on Sunday is a lollipop. The route runs through fir forest past waterfalls, stream crossings and fire killed snags. Full service aid stations are provided but their positions are not published.

About

The Tillamook Burn Trail Run is a two day trail event based at Reehers Camp in the Tillamook State Forest, Oregon. Runners take on a point to point 50 mile on the Saturday or a 55K lollipop on the Sunday, on singletrack through fir forest, waterfalls and stream crossings. The name recalls the wildfires that swept the forest last century.
