Course

A one mile race on the outdoor track at Phoenix High School, 745 N Rose St, Phoenix, Oregon, run as four laps starting and finishing at the same point. The first start is at 6:00pm, with five staggered heats from 6:15pm grouped by estimated pace. No aid stations are described.

About
