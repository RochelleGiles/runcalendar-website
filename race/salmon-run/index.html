Course

A 43-mile point-to-point ultra following the River Exe upstream from Exmouth to Dulverton on the edge of Exmoor. Shorter community relay legs of two to seven miles are run alongside the full distance. The day opens with a gathering at Sideshore in Exmouth and runners reach Dulverton from mid-afternoon.

About

An ultramarathon following the River Exe upstream from Exmouth on the Devon coast towards Dulverton on the edge of Exmoor, tracing the valley from tidal estuary to the moor where the river rises. A 5K is also held alongside the ultra.
