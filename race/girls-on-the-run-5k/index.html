Course

Flat 5K starting and finishing at Wesley Bolin Plaza, 1700 W Washington St, in downtown Phoenix, with cheer zones along the route. A 1-mile option is also offered. The start is 8:00 with packet pickup from 6:00. No route map, surface detail or aid station information is published.

About

A public 5K running event open to all ages, genders, and abilities, featuring soft event shirts, sparkly medals, and a flat course with cheer zones. The event celebrates girls from Girls on the Run and Heart & Sole programs alongside community participants, followed by a festival with music, activities, and empowerment activities.
