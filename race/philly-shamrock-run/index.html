Course

A 5K starting and finishing on South Broad Street in the Philadelphia Navy Yard, looping via Admiral Peary Way, League Island Blvd and Kitty Hawk Ave. Flat paved roads throughout, with only a few feet of elevation change. The field is capped at 3,500 runners.
