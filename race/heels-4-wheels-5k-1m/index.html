Course

Accessible 5K and one-mile run/walk at Desert Mountain Park on Hawes Road in Queen Creek, Arizona, benefiting the Spina Bifida Association of Arizona. Each distance runs in a Heels division for runners and walkers and a Wheels division for wheelchairs and assisted walkers. The park stays open during the event and strollers, wagons and leashed dogs are welcome, but skates, skateboards and motorised devices are not.

About

Heels 4 Wheels is an accessible 5K and one-mile run/walk at Desert Mountain Park in Queen Creek, Arizona. Each distance runs a Heels division for runners and walkers and a Wheels division for wheelchair users and assisted walkers. Proceeds buy adaptive cycles through the Spina Bifida Association of Arizona.
