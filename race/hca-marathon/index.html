Course

The marathon and half marathon set off together at 10:00 on Sunday morning, based around Odense Sports Park and Flakhaven in the city centre. A 10 km race, 'Run the last 10', covers the closing stretch of the marathon route, and there are MiniMarathon races for children.

About

The HCA Marathon transforms Odense into a festive running scene, inviting runners, spectators and the whole city to take part. Centred on Hans Christian Andersen's home city, the event combines community and energy in a shared running experience.
