Course

A backyard ultra at High Lodge in Thetford Forest, run as repeated 4.167-mile loops until one runner remains, with 6-hour and 12-hour timed options alongside. The loop is wide, flat and firm underfoot on mostly gravel and grass forest footpaths with one small rise, and is fully signed.

About

Our famous highly competitive Summer Suffolk Backyard Ultra Event. Run 4.167 miles every hour on the hour until only one person remains who can complete 1 "Yard" (loop) in the 1 hour time limit. Held at High Lodge Thetford Forest in Suffolk. Chip timing and a £1000 prize for the winner.
