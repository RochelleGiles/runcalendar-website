Course

A relentless uphill from Lithia Park in Ashland at 1,900 ft to the summit of Mt Ashland at 7,500 ft, a total gain of more than a mile. The route climbs continuously from town into the Siskiyous with no descent.

About

The Mt. Ashland Hill Climb is a challenging uphill race starting in Lithia Park, Ashland, and ascending over 13 miles with more than a mile of vertical gain to the summit of Mt. Ashland at 7,533 feet. Open to runners, hikers, and mountain bikers, it features aid stations, a festive summit party, and supports community trail efforts.
