Course

Road routes through Prestwood village in Buckinghamshire, with the 10k and 5k both starting and finishing at Prestwood Gym and Fitness Centre on Honor End Lane. The 10k has a water station at the end of Hangings Lane, about halfway. Roads are not closed and there are uneven grass and tarmac sections. A 2k children's race runs alongside.

About

Run Prestwood is a beloved annual running event that takes place in the picturesque village of Prestwood, Buckinghamshire. Runners of all levels can enjoy the scenic countryside routes and charming local atmosphere that have made this event a favorite among the running community for over 25 years. Whether you're looking for a fun day out or a challenging race, Run Prestwood offers an unforgettable running experience.
