About

The Annual E-race Stigma 5k will be held on Sunday, May 17th in downtown Adrian, Michigan. The race starts Downtown at the Farmers Market. We encourage people of all abilities to register and will hold a shortened kids dash for younger participants. All kids dash participants will receive a medal.
