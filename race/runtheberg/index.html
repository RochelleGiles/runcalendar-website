Course

A mountain trail race in the Central Drakensberg with four distances: 65 km (~2,600 m climb), 42 km (~1,700 m), 26 km (~850 m) and 13 km (~250 m). The two longer races are run on GPS self-navigation with a GPS device compulsory; the 25 and 13 km courses are marked.

About

A trail running festival based in Bergville, on the doorstep of the northern Drakensberg in KwaZulu-Natal, with distances ranging from a half marathon up to a 65km ultra.
