About

The Red Warrior Challenge offers a thrilling multi-terrain adventure through the picturesque Margam Country Park in Port Talbot, UK. Runners will test their endurance and agility as they tackle a variety of natural obstacles and challenging terrain, immersed in the stunning Welsh countryside. This unique event promises an unforgettable experience for outdoor enthusiasts and competitive athletes alike.
