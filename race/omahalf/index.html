Course

Starts and finishes at Stinson Park in Aksarben Village, Omaha, with an 8am start. The course joins the paved Keystone Trail system for a flat out-and-back. Half marathon, 10K and 5K are offered; the half marathon carries a 3 hour 15 minute cutoff.

About

OmaHalf is a half marathon, 10K, and 5K held in Omaha, Nebraska each October, organized by the team behind the Omaha Marathon and the Nebraska Half Marathon series. It's designed as a simpler, more relaxed alternative to full marathon weekend.
