Course

About

The Barley's St. Patrick's Day 5K combines pizza, beer, and running for a charitable cause in Knoxville, Tennessee. The course starts at Barley's Taproom and Pizzeria in the Old City and finishes at Covenant Health Park, with a VIP 5K option offering an exclusive post-race party.
