Course

USATF-certified 10K and 5K plus a Memorial Mile, all starting and finishing at Barefoot Landing Resort on Thorny Oyster Way in North Myrtle Beach. The roads are not closed to traffic, so runners are asked to stay alert throughout. Course maps are published on MapMyRun and every distance has a virtual equivalent.

About

The Pink Ribbon Run is a family-friendly event offering 10K, 5K, and Memorial Mile races, with a challenge option to complete all three for an extra medal. It raises funds and awareness for breast cancer research, treatment, and local charities while celebrating survivors.
