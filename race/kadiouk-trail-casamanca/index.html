About

The Kadiouk Trail Casamanca is a captivating running event set against the stunning landscapes of Casamance, Senegal. Runners will traverse picturesque trails through lush forests and along the banks of the Casamance River, offering a truly immersive experience in the heart of West Africa. This annual race promises an unforgettable challenge for both seasoned trail runners and those seeking a unique adventure.
