Course

All distances start and finish at the corner of Pinnacle Septimus and Pinnacle Station Roads. Fire roads, single trail and uneven ground through Mia Mia State Forest, opening with a steep climb and a fast descent. The 14km and 24km include creek crossings; the 24km passes a historic homestead and gains 553m. Water stations are set at intervals.

About

Explore the rugged beauty of the Australian bush at the Captain's Crossing Trail Run near Mackay, Queensland. This off-the-beaten-track event is part of the thrilling Off The Beaten Track Trail Run Series, offering runners a chance to challenge themselves on scenic trails winding through the stunning natural landscape.
