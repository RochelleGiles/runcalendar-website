Course

The run starts at 8600 Ward Parkway, Kansas City, by 86th Terrace on the east side of the Ward Parkway Center. Alongside the 5K and 10K there is a one-mile walk, a downhill mile run setting off at 8:30am, and a Tiny Turkey Trot for children twelve and under. Course maps for each distance are published on Garmin.

About

The Ward Parkway Thanksgiving Day Run is an annual event featuring a 5K, 10K, Downhill Mile, Mile Walk, and Tiny Turkey Trot for kids 12 and under. All participants receive a long-sleeve thermal shirt and a 6" Tippin's pie as their finisher medal.
