About

The Sachsenlauf Coswig is a must-attend running event that takes participants on a scenic journey through the picturesque German town. With a rich history and a challenging yet rewarding course, this race offers an unforgettable experience for runners of all levels. Explore the charming streets and landmarks of Coswig as you push yourself towards the finish line.
