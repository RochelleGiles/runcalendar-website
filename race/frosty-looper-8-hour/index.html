Course

Timed eight-hour event on a 3.75-mile paved loop around Cooper River Park, with runners completing as many laps as they can inside the window. Start and finish at Jack Curtis Stadium, 1301 North Park Drive, Pennsauken. The course is mostly flat. A single aid and hydration station sits at the start and finish in the stadium, with no mid-course support.

About

Frosty Looper is an 8-hour timed race on a 3.75-mile paved loop course at Cooper River Park. Runners complete as many loops as possible within the time limit on this mostly flat, fast course.
