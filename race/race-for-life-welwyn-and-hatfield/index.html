Course

A 5k lap of Stanborough Park in Welwyn Garden City. The start and finish are on grass, and the route follows a mixture of grass, tarmac and gravel paths, taking in uphill and downhill grassy slopes. Grass sections can become muddy and slippery in wet weather.

About

Race for Life Welwyn and Hatfield takes place at Stanborough Park, set around a pair of lakes used for sailing and canoeing. The 5K route follows the parkland paths, raising funds for Cancer Research UK.
