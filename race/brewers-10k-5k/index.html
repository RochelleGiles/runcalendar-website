Course

A chip-timed, closed-road 10K and 5K starting and finishing at the Pirelli Stadium, home of Burton Albion. The route flows out through Stretton and Rolleston-on-Dove with wide views over the Staffordshire countryside. Every kilometre is marked with distance flags, with marshals and well-placed water stations throughout.
