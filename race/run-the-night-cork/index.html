About

Run the Night Cork is an all-ages, community night run through the vibrant streets of Ireland's historic city. This 5-mile event, based at the popular Marina Market, offers a unique opportunity for runners of all abilities to experience the city under the glow of the evening lights. Runners can look forward to a lively atmosphere and scenic views as they make their way through the heart of Cork.
