Course

Start and finish at Kamiak High School in Mukilteo, on a gently rolling course open to runners and walkers. The 5K has no hydration stop on course; the 10K has a single aid station at mile four with sealed water bottles. Water dispensers are available at the start and finish.

About

The Inspiring Hope Run is a 5K/10K event that takes place in Mukilteo, Washington. The event aims to raise funds and awareness for various charitable organizations, including the Susan G. Komen Foundation, Code 4 NW, Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center, Special Olympics of WA, and the UW Family Autism Center Scholarship Fund.
