About

The Super Bowl 5K is a fun run/walk event at Washington Park in Denver, featuring football-themed activities like a 40-yard dash, kids fun run, costume contest, and custom finisher medals. Part of the RunDenver Series produced by HAL Sports, it welcomes families and competitive runners with swag, free photos, and awards.
