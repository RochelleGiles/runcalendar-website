Course

Starts at Bare Methodist Church in Morecambe and runs along the promenade and out towards the shore near Red Bank Farm before returning. The half marathon covers the loop once and the marathon twice; the 10K shares the opening 6.2 miles and finishes back at the start.

About

Morecambe Marathon, Half Marathon & 10K runs along the seafront of Morecambe Bay on England's northwest coast, part of the Lancaster Race Series with all three distances starting together.
