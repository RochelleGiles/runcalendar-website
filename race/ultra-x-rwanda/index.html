About

Ultra X Rwanda is an event that takes place over two days around northern Rwanda in the districts of Burera and Musanze. It offers two events: a 110km two stage event and a 50km single stage race. The race takes place near the Volcanoes National Park (where the famous mountain gorillas are based) and the twin lakes: Lake Ruhondo and Lake Burera. This course will take you on a sensory journey, over gentle hills and valleys, through traditional coffee and banana fields with lush, jungle like vegetation and over a stunning lake peninsula.
