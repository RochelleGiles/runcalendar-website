Course

The course runs alongside the Canning River in Gosnells from start to finish, on extremely well shaded riverside pathway. Marathon, half marathon and 10 km options are offered, with a six-hour cutoff on the marathon and finisher medals for the two longer distances.

About

The Canning River RunningWorks Festival offers runners a unique experience through the scenic landscapes of Gosnells, Western Australia. Weaving along the picturesque Canning River, this community-driven event celebrates the joy of running with a range of distances to challenge athletes of all levels. Whether you're seeking a fast-paced 10K or a more leisurely 5K, the RunningWorks Festival promises an unforgettable day of fitness, camaraderie, and appreciation for the great outdoors.
