Course

Circular trail race of about 17.5 miles (1,700 ft of ascent) from Fylingdales Village Hall in Robin Hood's Bay, following the Cleveland Way over coastal path and moorland. Checkpoints at Ravenscar (4.5 and 12.5 miles) and Hayburn Wyke (8 miles); 8-hour time limit.

About

The Hardmoors 15 is a circular route of approximately 17.5 miles, starting and finishing in the picturesque village of Robin Hood's Bay. This winter ultra event offers stunning views of the North York Moors and a challenging course with over 1,700 feet of ascent.
