Course

A one-way 70-mile journey through Hadrian's Wall country, starting at Carlisle Castle and finishing on the Gateshead and Newcastle Quayside across the Millennium Bridge. The route mixes fields, trails and urban sections, passing through Hexham and Newburn. It is fully waymarked with orange arrows and supported by five stocked pit stops, bag transport and paramedic cover.

About

Conquer the legendary Hadrian's Wall at the Rat Race The Wall Ultramarathon, a 70-mile fully supported and way-marked journey through the historic landscapes of northern England. Challenge yourself to this epic ultra-distance run, immersing in the rugged beauty of the region as you follow in the footsteps of Roman soldiers. Whether you're an experienced ultrarunner or seeking a transformative endurance challenge, this event promises an unforgettable adventure.
