Course

Trail races on and around the Winwick Hall Estate at Winwick, Northamptonshire, over grassland, country paths and hard-packed trail with some short climbs and fast descents. Start and finish at Winwick Hall, with the 5k and 10k off at 9.30am and a 2k children's race at 11.15am. A water station sits at halfway on the 10k, with water and first aid at the finish.

About

Explore the picturesque Winwick Hall Estate on a scenic trail running adventure. Offering 10km, 5km, and a kids 2km race, this event promises stunning views and a fun challenge for runners of all levels. Located in the heart of the United Kingdom, the Winwick Hall Trail Races provide an idyllic setting to test your endurance and connect with nature.
