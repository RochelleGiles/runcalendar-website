About

The Gold Coastal Run is part of the Coastal Fun Runs series in Adelaide, starting and finishing at Grange, South Australia. The July event offers 7km, 14km and 21.1km options along the metropolitan coast. Five dollars from every entry fee is donated to the series charities.
