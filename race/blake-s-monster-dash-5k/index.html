Course

An evening 5K on gravel trails through a family-owned apple orchard and a haunted cornfield, passing the haunted hayride and zombie paintball scenes with live actors. The whole route is gravel and grass; strollers allowed, dogs not.

About

A 5K run/walk on gravel trails around Blake Farm's 800-acre property, featuring haunted hayride and zombie paintball scenes with live actors. Participants wear costumes and receive a donut, cider, t-shirt, pie pumpkin, and admission to Spookyland attractions after the race.
