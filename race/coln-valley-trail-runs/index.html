Course

All three courses run in the Coln River valley between Coln St Aldwyns and Bibury, starting and finishing at Yew Tree Lodge. The 5km and 10km climb out of the valley then cross gently undulating grass pasture, the 10km splitting at 3km for a figure-of-eight towards Bibury; both are 95% on trail with a water and refreshment station. The 2km is a cross-country fun run over grassy fields with hay bale hurdles and stream crossings.

About

The Coln Valley Trail Runs include a 2km, 5km and 10km set along the Coln River valley between Coln St Aldwyns and Bibury, Gloucestershire. The 5km and 10km follow gently undulating grass pasture, with the 10km taking a figure-of-eight route past Bibury's Arlington Row cottages, almost entirely on marked trail. The 2km is a cross-country fun run for kids over grassy fields with hay bale hurdles.
