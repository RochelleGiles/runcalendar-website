About

The Plzeň 30K, 20K, and Marathon on the Athletic Track is a running event held annually in Plzeň, Czech Republic. The event features races of various distances, including a full marathon, on a track in the city. The event is known for its scenic course and welcoming atmosphere, attracting runners from across the country and beyond.
