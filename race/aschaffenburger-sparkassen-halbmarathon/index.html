About

The Aschaffenburger Sparkassen-Halbmarathon runs through the Bavarian city of Aschaffenburg and is now in its 23rd edition. It pairs the half marathon with an Inklusionslauf, an inclusion run open to runners with disabilities, making it one of the region's more accessible race days.
