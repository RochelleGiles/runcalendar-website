Course

Starts and finishes at Riverview Park on Corning Avenue in Des Moines, with the kids dash at 06:55 and the main races from 07:00. Distances run entirely within the park and along the adjacent riverside paths — 15K, 10K, 5K and one mile — keeping the course off local streets. A virtual option is also offered.
