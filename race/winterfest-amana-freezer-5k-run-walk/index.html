Course

About

The Amana Colonies Freezer 5K Run/Walk is an annual winter event held during Winterfest, starting at 10 AM from the Carding Studio at Hotel Millwright in Amana to East Amana. It welcomes runners and walkers of all levels regardless of weather conditions.
