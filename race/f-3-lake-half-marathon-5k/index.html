About

Experience Chicago's Original Winter Half Marathon at the 17th Annual F^3 Lake Half Marathon & 5k! As one of the few independently-owned large races in Chicago, the F^3 Lake Half Marathon & 5k has become a beloved event in the Chicagoland running community. Every year, thousands of runners gather in the warmth of Soldier Field's United Club before braving the wind and weather along the lakefront course. This event is a must-attend for anyone looking to stay active and fit in the new year or preparing for a spring marathon.
