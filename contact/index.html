We read everything

Questions, ideas, corrections, bug reports, a race we should know about — one address reaches a human: team@runcalendar.com. We usually reply within one to two business days.

✉️  team@runcalendar.com

For everything: app questions and support · a wrong date or detail on a race · feature ideas · press · partnerships · privacy requests. If it's about a specific race, include the race name and country and we'll be twice as fast.

Reporting a bug? In the app, go to Profile and long-press the version number at the bottom — it copies your app version and device details to paste into your email. That one line usually saves a whole round of questions.

Listing your event is free, always. Use Add your event for a race we don't have yet, or claim your listing to confirm dates and details at the source.
