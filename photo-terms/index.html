Version 1 · Effective: 12 November 2026 · RunCalendar is operated by Net Project Limited

The short version

These terms cover photographs that an event organiser shares with RunCalendar for that event's pages. You keep ownership of every photograph. We choose between two and four, crop them, credit them to your event, and show them only on your event's pages in the RunCalendar app and on runcalendar.com. We remove any photograph within 7 days of your request. Neither of us pays the other anything. The detail follows.

1. Agreement

These terms are an agreement between Net Project Limited, which operates RunCalendar ("we" or "us"), and the event or organisation named on the form at runcalendar.com/photos ("you"). By ticking the box on that form, you accept these terms on behalf of your event or organisation, and you confirm that you are authorised to do so. One acceptance covers every event you name on the form. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle the details you give us.

2. Your photographs stay yours

You keep ownership of, and copyright in, every photograph you share with us. You grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to store, copy, resize, crop and display the photographs on your event's pages for as long as they are published under these terms.

3. Where your photographs appear

Your photographs appear only on your event's pages: the event's page in the RunCalendar app and on runcalendar.com, including the full-screen view of each photograph. We will not use them in event listings or search results in the app, in our App Store listing, in advertising, email or social media, or on any other event's page, unless you agree to it separately in writing.

runcalendar.com is a public website, so search engines and link-preview services may show or keep copies of a page that carries your photographs. Those copies are outside our control. We will always remove the photographs from our own pages when you ask, under section 9.

4. How we choose and present them

We choose which photographs to publish, between two and four for each event, and the order in which they appear. We may crop and resize them. We will not add text, logos, filters or colour, or change what a photograph shows. Each photograph is credited to your event or organisation. Where the photographer must be credited, we will add their name in the full-screen view of that photograph. We may decline to publish a photograph, or take one down, at our discretion.

5. Photo guidelines

Every photograph you share must meet these guidelines, which also appear on the form:

  • Two to four photographs per event, taken on race day or on the course.
  • You hold the rights to each photograph, or have the photographer's permission to share it.
  • Photographs must be free of logos, text, watermarks and promotional graphics.
  • Groups and crowds are welcome. No individual should be the main subject without their consent, and no child should be the main subject.
  • RunCalendar selects, crops and credits each photograph to your event, and removes any photograph within 7 days of your request.
  • Landscape photographs (wider than tall) of at least 2,000 pixels on the long side are preferred. Real photographs only: no graphics, collages or AI-generated images.

6. What you confirm

By sharing photographs with us, you confirm that:

  • you own them, or the photographer has given you permission to share them with us on these terms;
  • the people in them took part in or attended your event on terms that allow event photography to be published, or have otherwise agreed to it;
  • no photograph has a child as its main subject without the consent of a parent or guardian; and
  • they contain no logos, text, watermarks or promotional graphics, and nothing unlawful, misleading or offensive.

If any of this stops being true for a photograph, you will tell us promptly, and we will take it down.

9. Taking photographs down

You can ask us to remove any or all of your photographs at any time by emailing team@runcalendar.com. We will remove them from the RunCalendar app and from runcalendar.com within 7 days. If a person in a photograph asks us to remove it, we will do so and let you know. Our licence to a photograph ends when it is removed.

10. No fee, no endorsement

Neither of us pays the other anything under these terms. Publishing your photographs is not an endorsement of your event, and it does not change how your event is listed, ordered or featured. Featured races and race guides remain our editorial choice.
